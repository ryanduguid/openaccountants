# Estonia tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the taxpayer type, residence facts, income sources and relevant period. Check any treaty that assigns residence or limits the source country's tax.

## Territorial reach

A resident individual generally accounts for Estonian and foreign income. A nonresident accounts for the Estonian-source income specified in the Act. An Estonian company generally pays income tax when it distributes profit, including deemed distributions, and on specified items such as fringe benefits and non-business expenses. A foreign company's Estonian permanent establishment can be taxed on profit withdrawn from the establishment and other specified items. Check relief where a company distributes qualifying profit already taxed abroad. [Income Tax Act, §§ 6–7, 29 and 48–53](https://www.riigiteataja.ee/et/akt/TuMS) · [Tax and Customs Board on profit distributions](https://www.emta.ee/en/business-client/taxes-and-payment/income-and-social-taxes/income-tax-and-basic-exemption)

## Residence

An individual is resident if their place of residence is in Estonia or they spend at least 183 days there in 12 consecutive calendar months. For a person arriving in Estonia who meets the stay test, residence ordinarily starts on the first day of arrival in that period. The Estonian Tax and Customs Board may record the change retrospectively. Check resident status under the treaty if both countries claim residence. A legal person established under Estonian law is generally resident, subject to the limited partnership fund exception. A European company or European cooperative society with its seat registered in Estonia also qualifies. [Income Tax Act, § 6](https://www.riigiteataja.ee/et/akt/TuMS) · [Tax and Customs Board on residence](https://www.emta.ee/en/private-client/foreigner-non-resident/tax-residency/determining-residency)

## Taxpayer types

An individual who carries on a business uses the individual business-income rules. Companies, including general and limited partnerships, apply the statutory distribution rules. Limited partnership funds have separate rules. For a foreign trust or other unincorporated pool of assets, identify who derives the amount and how the law of the place of formation treats the arrangement. Section 6(3) extends nonresident treatment to certain foreign pools and associations that lack legal personality but are taxed as legal persons where formed. It excludes contractual investment funds from that extension. [Income Tax Act, §§ 6(3), 14, 50 and 56³](https://www.riigiteataja.ee/et/akt/TuMS)

## Currency

Prepare statutory annual accounts in euros (EUR), Estonia's official currency. Record the exchange-rate basis for foreign-currency amounts in the relevant return. [Accounting Act, § 15(5)](https://www.riigiteataja.ee/akt/RPS) · [Tax and Customs Board income-tax guidance](https://www.emta.ee/en/business-client/taxes-and-payment/income-and-social-taxes/income-tax-and-basic-exemption)

## Assessment periods

Ordinary personal income tax follows the calendar year. The ordinary company distribution-tax period is the calendar month. The associated return is generally due by the 10th day of the following month. Keep the company's 12-month financial year and its annual accounts separate from the monthly tax periods. [Income Tax Act, §§ 3 and 54](https://www.riigiteataja.ee/et/akt/TuMS) · [Accounting Act, § 13](https://www.riigiteataja.ee/akt/RPS)

## Responsible authorities

The Estonian Tax and Customs Board (Maksu- ja Tolliamet) administers ordinary income taxes and customs. Use e-MTA for returns. If circumstances affecting your individual tax residence change, you must notify the Board and submit Form R under § 6(6) of the Income Tax Act. [Tax and Customs Board](https://www.emta.ee/en) · [Residence determination and Form R](https://www.emta.ee/en/private-client/foreigner-non-resident/tax-residency/determining-residency)

## Fictional intake

A consultant has a home abroad and spends 190 days in Estonia across parts of 2 calendar years. Count the days over the relevant 12 consecutive calendar months and document the date of arrival. Check treaty residence before deciding which foreign fees enter the Estonian return.
