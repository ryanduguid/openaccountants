# Saint Kitts and Nevis VAT, imports, property and formation

Source-cited reference checked on 11 November 2026. Amounts are in Eastern Caribbean dollars (EC$) unless stated otherwise. Establish the island, transaction date, taxpayer and applicable instrument before calculating or filing. A qualified local practitioner must review live work.

## VAT on supplies and imports

The federal Value Added Tax Act covers taxable supplies in Saint Christopher and Nevis by taxable persons and non-exempt imports of goods or services. The supplier ordinarily accounts for domestic output VAT, the importer pays on goods and the recipient accounts for taxable imported services. The 2017 revision sets the standard rate at **17%**. It also provides for **10%** on specified accommodation, tour and restaurant supplies to the extent prescribed. Check regulations 9–11 for the qualifying charges and business conditions. Section 41(8) caps input deductions attributable to these reduced-rate supplies at their reportable output VAT for the period. [Value Added Tax Act, ss 26–27 and 41 and subsidiary regulations](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Act17TOC/Ch-18_47-Value-Added-Tax-Act.pdf)

Inland Revenue's guidance for 2025 applied **13%** to supplies previously at 17% from **1 January to 30 June 2025** and confirmed the return to **17% on 1 July 2025**. Use the correct time of supply for the boundary. The guidance also lists commercial rentals at 10%. Confirm the operative instrument and eligible lease class before applying that treatment. The 2024 Order No. 24 concerns 4 designated days in August and December 2024. The instrument for the six-month 2025 reduction remains unconfirmed in this reference. [IRD first-half 2025 guidelines](https://www.sknird.com/value-added-tax-vat-reduction-guidelines-for-the-first-half-of-2025/) · [IRD return to 17%](https://www.sknird.com/the-inland-revenue-department-confirms-return-of-vat-rate-to-17-effective-july-01-2025/) · [SRO 24 of 2024, s 3](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Annual-Laws/2024/SROs/SRO-24-of-2024-Value-Added-Tax-Special-VAT-Reduction-No_-2-Order.pdf)

The 2026 Inland Revenue notices describe **5%** on eligible tangible goods from participating sellers on **17 April and 28–29 August**, with vehicles excluded and other product exclusions. The October government notice reconfirms **11 and 19 December** as announced dates. The April schedule says vehicles are to be included for December. Obtain the operative December terms, including any vehicle value limits and business reporting, before quoting the reduced rate. Verify participation and product eligibility for each sale. [IRD April notice](https://www.sknird.com/upcoming-discounted-value-added-tax-rate-day-on-april-17-2026/) · [IRD August notice](https://www.sknird.com/inland-revenue-department-announces-discounts-vat-rate-days-in-august-2026-and-opens-applications-for-businesses/) · [2026 schedule](https://www.sknis.gov.kn/2026/04/10/government-of-st-kitts-and-nevis-announces-discounted-vat-rate-days-dvrd-for-2026-easter-dvrd-set-for-april-17-summer-and-christmas-dates-confirmed/) · [13 October government notice](https://www.sknis.gov.kn/2026/10/13/federal-cabinet-approves-special-duty-free-concessions-for-the-festive-season/)

### Registration, supply and filing

- **Registration.** Under the 2017 revision's section 13, the ordinary tests use taxable supplies above EC$150,000 over the previous 12 months or fewer, or a reasonable forecast above that amount over the next 365 days. Apply within 14 calendar days of the relevant period boundary. The separate 3-month test requires actual taxable supplies above EC$37,500 and a reasonable forecast above EC$150,000 over that period plus the next 9 months. The application is due within 14 calendar days of the end of month 3. For commercial and timeshare leases and prescribed or designated professional services, use EC$96,000 and EC$24,000 respectively. Check mixed supplies, related persons, the narrower non-resident measure, separate timing for auctioneers and public entertainment and the Comptroller's voluntary registration criteria. Confirm any subsequent threshold order and the current prescribed services before registration. [VAT Act, ss 13–17](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Act17TOC/Ch-18_47-Value-Added-Tax-Act.pdf)
- **Time and place.** Test the earliest applicable delivery or availability of goods, completion of services, invoice or receipt of consideration, subject to the special rules for periodic supplies and other transactions. Apply section 37 by supply type. Goods ordinarily follow the delivery or availability rule, and services the supplying place of business. Exceptions include services connected with immovable property and specified professional, digital and other supplies where the recipient uses or obtains the benefit. Apply the separate export rule for qualifying services supplied from a local place of business. Check the current treatment of platforms, non-resident suppliers and imported services before filing. [VAT Act, ss 36–37 and 119–120](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Act17TOC/Ch-18_47-Value-Added-Tax-Act.pdf) · [IRD first-half 2025 guidelines](https://www.sknird.com/value-added-tax-vat-reduction-guidelines-for-the-first-half-of-2025/)
- **Exempt and zero-rated items.** Classify the supply against the applicable schedule and item description. Sections 31–35 distinguish exempt imports, zero-rated imports and supplies and exempt supplies. The Second Schedule includes qualifying exports with evidence requirements. The Third Schedule contains specified exempt financial services and other categories. Determine input recovery separately. Deduct qualifying input VAT attributable to taxable supplies with the invoice, adjustment note or import evidence required by sections 40–41. Apportion mixed inputs as applicable and check disallowances, special financial-institution rules and reduced-rate limits. [VAT Act, ss 31–35 and 39–45, First to Third Schedules](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Act17TOC/Ch-18_47-Value-Added-Tax-Act.pdf)
- **Returns and evidence.** The statutory tax period is a calendar month. File within 15 calendar days after period end, including a nil period, and pay by the original return due date. A filing extension under section 48 does not extend the payment deadline. Inland Revenue's guidance shifts weekend or public holiday due dates to the next working day and identifies the error correction form and supporting explanation. Verify the current filing channel, receipt, adjustment treatment and any deadline notice. Display the registration certificate, show VAT-inclusive prices, issue the required invoices or receipts and retain the records for 6 years after the relevant period ends. [VAT Act, ss 21, 44–48, 51, 79–81 and 112](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Act17TOC/Ch-18_47-Value-Added-Tax-Act.pdf) · [IRD post-registration instructions](https://www.sknird.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/IRD-VAT-What-must-I-do-after-successful-registration.pdf) · [IRD April 2026 filing reminder](https://www.sknird.com/announcements-upcoming-events/)

### Customs declaration and valuation

For a commercial entry establish the tariff classification, origin and preference evidence, customs value, applicable duty, excise, surcharge, restrictions and relief. Keep the commercial invoice, transport and insurance evidence and any origin certificate or permit with the entry and payment evidence. Customs links ASYCUDA World and its tariff search from its public site. The 2020 Customs Act revision uses the sale-for-export transaction value with statutory adjustments and alternative methods where transaction value cannot be used. Cap. 20.06 provides a **6%** Customs Service Charge on cost, insurance and freight, subject to applicable relief, and duty at the relevant schedule rate. [Customs Act, declaration provisions and First Schedule, paras 3–9](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Revised-Acts-of-St-Kitts-and-Nevis/Revised-Acts-of-St-Kitts-and-Nevis-2020/Ch-20_04-Customs-Act.pdf) · [Customs Tariff Act, ss 3–6](https://www.skncustoms.com/pdfs/Ch-20_06-Customs-Tariff-Act.pdf) · [Customs and Excise](https://skncustoms.com/)

Declare taxable goods and pay import VAT at entry under sections 10–11. For section 29, add any insurance and freight absent from customs value, applicable duty, excise, environmental and other fiscal charges and the Customs Service Charge to the customs value. Count each amount once. Test exemptions under sections 31–33. A recipient liable for taxable imported services generally furnishes a declaration and pays within 15 calendar days after the import period. Services incidental to imported goods form part of the goods import. Check consideration, fair market value for certain related parties and the separate time-of-import rules. [VAT Act, ss 5(13), 10–12 and 26–33](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Act17TOC/Ch-18_47-Value-Added-Tax-Act.pdf)

The April 2026 Customs memorandum and the 14 April government announcement use **20 April to 31 July 2026** for the bunker surcharge exclusion and specified gasoline excise and service charge reductions. The August extension applies from **1 August through 30 September**, also confirmed by the November government account. The August release gives **30 April** for the original start. For entries in that disputed interval, establish the rule from the applicable Customs instructions and entry. Qualifying alternative energy equipment had a separately announced period through **31 December 2026**. For **1 December 2026 to 31 January 2027**, Cabinet announced the first **400 lb** of gift food packages free of duties and taxes and **EC$675** off cost, insurance and freight for qualifying non-commercial imports. Confirm eligible goods, limits and entry procedures with Customs. [Customs memorandum, 17 April](https://www.skncustoms.com/pdf1/economic%20relief.pdf) · [April government announcement](https://www.sknis.gov.kn/2026/04/14/prime-minister-dr-terrance-drew-announces-major-economic-relief-measures-to-ease-cost-of-living/) · [August extension](https://www.sknis.gov.kn/2026/08/04/government-extends-temporary-relief-measures-to-cushion-rising-fuel-and-shipping-costs/) · [November update](https://www.sknis.gov.kn/2026/11/09/prime-minister-drew-urges-frank-discussion-on-global-diesel-prices-and-the-relief-government-can-provide/) · [Festive concessions](https://www.sknis.gov.kn/2026/10/13/federal-cabinet-approves-special-duty-free-concessions-for-the-festive-season/)

**Fictional ordinary import.** EC$1,000 customs value already includes insurance and freight. Assume a 10% tariff rate, the 6% service charge, standard VAT, no other charges and no relief. Duty is EC$100 and the service charge is EC$60. The VAT base is EC$1,160 and 17% VAT is EC$197.20. The 3 amounts total EC$357.20. The assumed tariff is for arithmetic only. Any input deduction is a separate test. [Customs Tariff Act, ss 3–4](https://www.skncustoms.com/pdfs/Ch-20_06-Customs-Tariff-Act.pdf) · [VAT Act, ss 27–29 and 40–41](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Act17TOC/Ch-18_47-Value-Added-Tax-Act.pdf)

## Property valuation, annual tax and transfers

Obtain the property's island, valuation list entry, land and improvement values, class or mixed-use allocation, exemption, demand notice and notice dates. St Kitts administers annual property tax under Cap. 20.32. Nevis uses its Property Tax Ordinance Cap. 6.13(N). Apply the assessed taxable valuation and the rate for each applicable class. For St Kitts, Inland Revenue's residential example deducts EC$80,000 and applies 0.2%. On a fictional EC$330,000 residential valuation meeting that example's conditions, the taxable amount is EC$250,000 and tax is EC$500. Confirm the valuation, class and concession on the notice. [St Kitts Property Tax Act, Parts III–VI](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Act02and09TOC/Ch-20_32-Property-Tax-Act.pdf) · [IRD St Kitts residential example](https://www.sknird.com/property-tax/) · [Nevis Ordinance, ss 43–45](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Ord02and09TOC/Ch-06_13N-Property-Tax-Ord.pdf)

- **St Kitts, 2026.** The June SMARTS rollout applied to St Kitts. The June notices showed current-year assessments only. Obtain a separate arrears balance for reconciliation. Inland Revenue says tax was due **30 June** and later encouraged payment by **31 July** whilst interest and penalties had yet to be applied. Keep the June statutory due date distinct from that administrative reminder. [IRD SMARTS notice, 3 June 2026](https://www.sknird.com/inland-revenue-department-administers-property-tax-in-smarts-for-2026/) · [IRD property reminder](https://www.sknird.com/)
- **Nevis, 2026.** Section 54 sets **30 June** as the ordinary deadline, subject to its exceptions. Inland Revenue announced **30 September 2026** for Nevis during the island-wide revaluation and directed owners to its e-government portal for demand notices. For a formal valuation complaint, section 73(1) in the 2009 revision uses 30 days after the sending date shown on the valuation notice and directs filing to the Secretary of the Board. The June announcement describes 30 days to object after the certificate. Check the operative instrument, stated dates and filing channel promptly. [Nevis Ordinance, ss 54 and 72–73](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Ord02and09TOC/Ch-06_13N-Property-Tax-Ord.pdf) · [Nevis extension, 8 June 2026](https://nia.gov.kn/property-tax-payment-deadline-extended-to-september-30-in-nevis/)
- **Nevis rate and exemption evidence.** SRO 3 of 2024 replaces 0.156% with **0.2%** in regulation 3(a)(i) from **1 January 2025**. Inland Revenue's undated table still prints 0.156% for a residential component. The Nevis 2025 budget speech, para 173, describes 0.2% on residential property with market value of **EC$2 million or more** from **1 June 2025**. Obtain the principal rates regulations, any later instrument and the assessment before selecting the rate, class or effective date. The Premier's 8 June 2026 statement describes the then-existing EC$80,000 exemption and a proposal to raise it. Verify the enacted threshold and any revaluation relief separately. [SRO 3 of 2024](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Revised-Ordinances-of-Nevis/Revised-Ordinances-of-Nevis-2024/SRO-3-of-2024-N-Property-Tax-Tax-Rates-Amendment.pdf) · [IRD rates table](https://www.sknird.com/property-tax-rates-and-exemptions/) · [Nevis 2025 budget, para 173](https://nia.gov.kn/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Final-Budget-Address-2025.pdf) · [June 2026 revaluation statement](https://nia.gov.kn/premier-brantley-steps-being-taken-to-cushion-impact-on-homeowners-amid-property-tax-revaluation/)

For transfers, identify the instrument, market value, consideration, parties and location before applying the Stamps Act Schedule. The **2020 revision** reflects Act 5 of 2015 and places **10%** on the vendor on ordinary land sales, using the higher of consideration and value. The older 2009 revision gives **12%**. Under the 2020 baseline, a fictional ordinary sale for EC$300,000 with a statutory value of EC$320,000 and no applicable relief gives vendor duty of EC$32,000. The 2020 revision also shows **10% of consideration** payable by the vendor or transferor on condominium sales. Paragraph (f), replaced by Act 25 of 2011, keeps **12% of market value** on the vendor or transferor for Special Development Areas and the South East Peninsular. Separate entries cover gifts, transmission on death and public vendor transactions, including the paragraph (k) National Housing Corporation exception. Sections 60–62 address the principal instrument, land-rich company shares and passing vendor duty to the purchaser. [Stamps Act, 2020 revision, ss 52–62 and First Schedule](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Revised-Acts-of-St-Kitts-and-Nevis/Revised-Acts-of-St-Kitts-and-Nevis-2020/Ch-20_40-Stamps-Act.pdf) · [2009 revision](https://eservicesskn.sknird.com/UserFiles/TaxLib/200427022708.pdf) · [Stamps Amendment Act 25 of 2011, s 2](https://www.sknird.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/04/No.-25-of-2011-Passed-on-30th-September-2011.pdf)

Verify subsequent amendments, exemptions and the land holding licence under Cap. 10.01 separately. Inland Revenue's S-10 instructions require transaction and property particulars, declared consideration and duty and the applicable signatures. Use the current submission requirements and keep actual identity documents out of working files supplied to the assistant. [Aliens Land Holding Regulation Act, ss 3–4](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Act02and09TOC/Ch-10_01-Aliens-Land-Holding-Regulation-Act.pdf) · [IRD S-10 instructions](https://www.sknird.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/STAMP-DUTY-S-10-FORM-INSTRUCTIONS.pdf)

## Special regimes

### Virtual asset businesses

Apply sections 2–4 of the Virtual Asset Act to the service and where it is offered or operated. The covered activities are fiat or virtual asset exchange, transfers, custody or administration and financial services related to issuance or sale, when carried out for another person. The Act applies to services to residents or from the Federation. The registration prohibition in section 4 as replaced in March 2021 refers to business offered or operated in or from the Federation. Confirm the treatment of an offshore provider serving residents before it begins activity. Mere holding for one's own account falls outside the provider activity described in section 2. The definition excludes digital representations of fiat currency and securities. Test securities rules and tax classification separately. For a registrant participating in a public issue or sale, check the prospectus and amendment requirements in sections 10–12. The 2021 continuation and application periods addressed operators already trading when that amendment commenced. [Act 1 of 2020, ss 2–4 and 10–12](https://www.fsrc.kn/law-library/virtual-assets/763-act-1-of-2020-virtual-asset-act/file) · [Act 8 of 2021, ss 3–5](https://www.fsrc.kn/law-library/virtual-assets/765-virtual-assets-amendment-act-8-of-2021/file)

Use the Schedule to the Virtual Asset Business Regulations, published **22 June 2026**. Its repeal provision expressly replaces the 2022 forms regime. Regulation 3 requires ownership and fitness evidence, constituting documents for legal persons, a business plan and evidence of capital, liquidity, resources and controls. The Authority has **90 days from receipt of a complete application** to decide. Provide requested information or seek an extension within **15 days**. Reconcile the existing-business accounts requested by regulation 3 with the separate periods and documents in the Schedule before submission. The June 2026 amendment adds senior management to application particulars, beneficial owners to specified fitness tests and proliferation financing to the controls. The significant shareholder definition from June 2024 also captures a controlling shareholder and specified holdings or voting rights above **10%**. An offshore registrant must maintain an ordinarily resident principal representative under section 9(9). [Interpretation Act, s 22](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Revised-Acts-of-St-Kitts-and-Nevis/Revised-Acts-of-St-Kitts-and-Nevis-2017/Ch-01_02-Interpretation-Act.pdf) · [SRO 13 of 2026, regs 3–6, repeal and Schedule](https://www.fsrc.kn/law-library/virtual-assets/1622-sro-no-13-of-2026-virtual-asset-business-regulations/file) · [Act 8 of 2026, ss 3–5](https://www.fsrc.kn/law-library/virtual-assets/1617-act-no-8-of-2026-virtual-asset-act-amendment/file) · [Act 9 of 2024, s 3](https://www.fsrc.kn/law-library/virtual-assets/1585-virtual-asset-amendment-act-2024/file) · [Act 8 of 2021, s 6](https://www.fsrc.kn/law-library/virtual-assets/765-virtual-assets-amendment-act-8-of-2021/file)

- **Fees and renewal.** The Schedule replaced in December 2021 sets **EC$54,000** for application and **EC$135,000** for registration. Amended section 14 provides for a calendar year registration fee, the same fee on renewal and a late charge of **EC$100 per day** after **31 January**. Registration lapses where the fee remains unpaid 3 months after the renewal due date. Confirm the charge period for a first registration and the Authority's current instructions before payment. [SRO 47 of 2021](https://www.fsrc.kn/law-library/virtual-assets/839-sro-47-of-2021-virtual-asset-amendment-of-schedule-order-2021/file) · [Act 8 of 2021, s 10](https://www.fsrc.kn/law-library/virtual-assets/765-virtual-assets-amendment-act-8-of-2021/file)
- **Client assets and reports.** Reconcile client liabilities to funds and custody balances. Section 9A requires qualifying escrow equal to **15%** of client funds held, quarterly reporting on account numbers, values and escrow and local copies of accounting records and annual financial statements. Consider section 9's local asset or evidence requirement, and maintain adequate capital and liquidity under regulation 6 and any additional written requirement. Track approval of business changes, transfer information, insurance and reports directed by the Authority. Regulation 27 measures the **7-year** retention period from the day each record was obtained or produced. Amended section 13 permits the Authority to require separate virtual asset activity reports from financial institutions. [Act 1 of 2020, s 9](https://www.fsrc.kn/law-library/virtual-assets/763-act-1-of-2020-virtual-asset-act/file) · [Act 8 of 2021, s 7](https://www.fsrc.kn/law-library/virtual-assets/765-virtual-assets-amendment-act-8-of-2021/file) · [SRO 13 of 2026, regs 6, 13 and 24–28](https://www.fsrc.kn/law-library/virtual-assets/1622-sro-no-13-of-2026-virtual-asset-business-regulations/file) · [Act 8 of 2026, s 7](https://www.fsrc.kn/law-library/virtual-assets/1617-act-no-8-of-2026-virtual-asset-act-amendment/file)

The amendment published **19 June 2026** adds an independent test of operations and financial crime controls under section 9(11), addresses the compliance certificate for the opinion the Authority may require under section 12A and permits an Authority-appointed external audit at the registrant's expense where necessary. The industry standards' paragraph 28A provides for a comprehensive independent audit every 2 years under a risk-based approach. The September 2022 amendment calls for approved reports to be submitted to the Commission. Confirm the applicable audit cycle and any directions for the registrant. [Act 8 of 2021, s 8](https://www.fsrc.kn/law-library/virtual-assets/765-virtual-assets-amendment-act-8-of-2021/file) · [Act 8 of 2026, ss 5–6](https://www.fsrc.kn/law-library/virtual-assets/1617-act-no-8-of-2026-virtual-asset-act-amendment/file) · [2020 industry standards amendment, para 28A](https://www.nevisfsrc.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/Financial-Services-Implementation-of-Industry-Standards-Amendment-Regulations-2020.pdf) · [SRO 26 of 2022, s 6](https://www.nevisfsrc.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/SRO-26-of-2022-Financial-Services-Implemenatation-of-Industry-Standards-Amendment-Regulations-2022.pdf)

### Gaming

Identify the federal or Nevis licence and the covered activities. Under federal Gaming Control Act section 13A, inserted by the amendment published **19 June 2026**, a licensed or registered gaming entity must notify the Commission within **21 days** of specified changes to ownership, control, shareholders, directors or senior management and supply the required questionnaire and particulars for new persons. Retain the gaming records required by section 36. Nevis Online Gaming Ordinance 2 of 2025 provides for a separate licensing authority and commencement by Order. Its regulations expressly took effect on **1 July 2025** and prescribe fees and application evidence. Under the Ordinance, preserve financial records for at least **5 years** after the relevant business relationship ends and submit the required financial statements within **3 months** of year end. Verify the commencement Order, licence conditions, fees and the interaction with federal gaming and tax rules.
