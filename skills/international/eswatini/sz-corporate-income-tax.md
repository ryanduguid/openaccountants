# Eswatini Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax rates and base

Eswatini levies company tax on income from a source within or deemed within the country. The headline rate was reduced to 25% for year-ends after 31 December 2024. Withholding taxes apply to a range of payments, especially to non-residents.

- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 25% of taxable income for 2025 declarations (years of assessment ending from 30 June 2025), down from the 27.5% applied since 1 July 2013; s 6(3) lets the Minister fix the Third Schedule rates annually  _(Eswatini Revenue Service, Income Tax Return Submission and Payment Notice for tax year end 2025, para 5 — https://www.ers.org.sz:8000/whatsnew-file/1753959572719-FULL_PAGE___Income_Tax_Returns_Notice_2025.pdf ; Eswatini Revenue Service, Rates and Thresholds — https://www.ers.org.sz/IncomeTax/RatesandThres ; Income Tax Order, 1975 (as amended), Eswatini Revenue Service consolidation, s 6(3) and Third Schedule Part I — https://www.ers.org.sz:8000/documents/IncomeTaxOrder1975.pdf)_
- **Corporate tax base** — All income received by or accrued to the company from a source within or deemed to be within Eswatini, whatever the recipient's residence; capital receipts are excluded unless s 7 brings them in  _(Income Tax Order, 1975 (as amended), Eswatini Revenue Service consolidation, s 7 (definition of gross income) — https://www.ers.org.sz:8000/documents/IncomeTaxOrder1975.pdf)_
- **Development Approval Order incentive rate** — A reduced rate (commonly 10%) may apply to approved enterprises granted Development Approval Order status (approx — confirm current incentive terms)  _(Income Tax (Development Approval Order) provisions / Income Tax Order, 1975)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends (non-resident)** — 15% non-resident shareholders tax; 12.5% where the dividend is paid to a company incorporated in Botswana, Lesotho, Namibia or South Africa that is not a subsidiary or branch of a company outside those countries; a treaty may reduce the rate further, and the tax is remitted by the 15th of the following month  _(Income Tax Order, 1975 (as amended), Eswatini Revenue Service consolidation, s 21(2) — https://www.ers.org.sz:8000/documents/IncomeTaxOrder1975.pdf ; Eswatini Revenue Service, Withholding Taxes — https://www.ers.org.sz/IncomeTax/Withholding)_
- **Withholding tax on interest (non-resident)** — 10% of interest accruing to a non-resident, withheld by the resident debtor and payable within 14 days of accrual  _(Income Tax Order, 1975 (as amended), Eswatini Revenue Service consolidation, ss 27 and 32 (non-residents tax on interest) — https://www.ers.org.sz:8000/documents/IncomeTaxOrder1975.pdf ; Eswatini Revenue Service, Withholding Taxes — https://www.ers.org.sz/IncomeTax/Withholding)_
- **Withholding tax on royalties and management fees (non-resident)** — 15% of the gross royalty and 15% of the gross management charge paid to a person not ordinarily resident or carrying on business in Eswatini, as a final tax; practitioner summaries report that a 5% refund may be claimed where a treaty applies  _(Income Tax Order, 1975 (as amended), Eswatini Revenue Service consolidation, s 32B(1)(b) — https://www.ers.org.sz:8000/documents/IncomeTaxOrder1975.pdf ; Eswatini Revenue Service, Withholding Taxes — https://www.ers.org.sz/IncomeTax/Withholding)_
- **Withholding tax on non-resident contractors/professionals** — 15% of payments to a non-resident person under an agreement for construction operations, excluding the direct cost of materials, on account of the non-resident's liability; the ERS also lists a 15% withholding on other payments to non-resident persons  _(Income Tax Order, 1975 (as amended), Eswatini Revenue Service consolidation, s 59(3) — https://www.ers.org.sz:8000/documents/IncomeTaxOrder1975.pdf ; Eswatini Revenue Service, Withholding Taxes — https://www.ers.org.sz/IncomeTax/Withholding)_
- **Withholding tax on entertainers and sportspersons (non-resident)** — 15% of the gross remuneration or receipts of public entertainers and sportspersons not ordinarily resident in Eswatini, deducted by the local agent or promoter  _(Income Tax Order, 1975 (as amended), Eswatini Revenue Service consolidation, s 32A — https://www.ers.org.sz:8000/documents/IncomeTaxOrder1975.pdf ; Eswatini Revenue Service, Withholding Taxes — https://www.ers.org.sz/IncomeTax/Withholding)_
- **Corporate return filing deadline** — Set by the Commissioner General's annual notice under s 33: for the 2025 year of assessment all non-individual entities file and pay by 31 October 2025, and clients with other approved tax years within four months after their financial year end  _(Eswatini Revenue Service, Income Tax Return Submission and Payment Notice for tax year end 2025, para 4 — https://www.ers.org.sz:8000/whatsnew-file/1753959572719-FULL_PAGE___Income_Tax_Returns_Notice_2025.pdf ; Income Tax Order, 1975 (as amended), Eswatini Revenue Service consolidation, s 33(1) — https://www.ers.org.sz:8000/documents/IncomeTaxOrder1975.pdf)_
- **First provisional tax installment** — Within six months from the start of the year of assessment (1 July) or of the approved financial year  _(Eswatini Revenue Service, Self Assessment — https://www.ers.org.sz/IncomeTax/SelfAssessment ; Income Tax Order, 1975 (as amended), Eswatini Revenue Service consolidation, s 58A and Fourth Schedule — https://www.ers.org.sz:8000/documents/IncomeTaxOrder1975.pdf)_
- **Second provisional tax installment** — Not later than the last day of the year of assessment or approved financial year; a third payment settling the balance is due by the return submission date  _(Eswatini Revenue Service, Self Assessment — https://www.ers.org.sz/IncomeTax/SelfAssessment ; Income Tax Order, 1975 (as amended), Eswatini Revenue Service consolidation, s 58A and Fourth Schedule — https://www.ers.org.sz:8000/documents/IncomeTaxOrder1975.pdf)_
- **Tax audit lookback period** — Departmental practice is to audit going back four years; there is no statute of limitations  _(Income Tax Order, 1975 (Eswatini Revenue Service consolidation — https://www.ers.org.sz:8000/documents/IncomeTaxOrder1975.pdf); the four-year audit practice is as reported `[RESEARCH GAP — the statute's text was not read for this change; the secondary summary that carried the figure is no longer cited]`)_
