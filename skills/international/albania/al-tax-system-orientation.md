# Albania tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the taxpayer, source of income, residence facts and tax period. Check treaty treatment where applicable.

## Territorial reach

Albania taxes residents on taxable worldwide income. A nonresident generally accounts for taxable worldwide income attributable to an Albanian permanent establishment and other taxable Albanian-source income. For example, the source rules include pay owed by a resident employer even when the work takes place abroad. Check the source of each receipt, the domestic permanent establishment tests and any treaty limits. [Income Tax Law 29/2023, arts 2, 4 and 5](https://tatime.gov.al/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Ligj-Nr.-29-2023-Per-Tatimin-mbi-te-Ardhurat-i-ndryshuar.pdf)

## Residence

An individual is generally resident if they have a settled home in Albania during the tax year, spend at least 183 days there in that year or have their centre of vital interests there. Count the dates of entry and exit as days of presence. Also check the rules for an Albanian government employee or official posted abroad and for a place of business or habitual residence in Albania. The latter test has an exception where the individual has a permanent home abroad throughout the tax year and no permanent home in Albania. Under the 2026 rules, a special provision treats artists who are not Albanian citizens as nonresidents for 24 months from first entry, regardless of their days there.

An entity is resident if established in Albania or managed and controlled there at any time during the tax year. Management and control are deemed to be in Albania if the board meets there or at least 2 of the statutory tests are met. Those tests concern where day-to-day decisions are made, the residence of at least 50% of board members or managers and ownership of at least 50% of capital or voting rights by Albanian residents. Check treaty residence separately. [Income Tax Law, arts 8 and 28](https://tatime.gov.al/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Ligj-Nr.-29-2023-Per-Tatimin-mbi-te-Ardhurat-i-ndryshuar.pdf) · [Law 81/2025, arts 2 and 8](https://tatime.gov.al/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Ligj-Nr-81-2025-Per-disa-ndryshime-dhe-shtesa-ne-ligjin-nr.-29-2023-Per-tatimin-mbi-te-ardhurat-i-ndryshuar.pdf)

## Taxpayer types

Individuals include sole traders and self-employed people. Check the business and employment income rules, including the applicable self-employment declaration. General and limited partnerships, limited liability companies and joint-stock companies are within the corporate income tax regime. The entity definition can also cover unincorporated structures and foreign trusts. Classify the actual arrangement and check statutory exemptions. [Income Tax Law, arts 3, 7, 12 and 27](https://tatime.gov.al/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Ligj-Nr.-29-2023-Per-Tatimin-mbi-te-Ardhurat-i-ndryshuar.pdf) · [Law 81/2025, art 4](https://tatime.gov.al/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Ligj-Nr-81-2025-Per-disa-ndryshime-dhe-shtesa-ne-ligjin-nr.-29-2023-Per-tatimin-mbi-te-ardhurat-i-ndryshuar.pdf)

## Currency

The Albanian lek (ALL) is the domestic currency. Income tax declarations use lek amounts. Keep the original currency amount and evidence of any conversion required by the applicable form or accounting rule. [Bank of Albania currency guidance](https://www.bankofalbania.org/About_the_Bank/Core_functions/Currency_issue.html) · [General Income Tax Instruction 26/2023 and return annexes](https://www.qbz.gov.al/pdf/908874e6fdeea0aafc8e7cdd4d905784040360c74d35be2d87d16068aaf527a4.pdf)

## Assessment periods

The tax year runs from 1 January to 31 December. Identify separately any withholding instalment, advance payment or annual declaration required for the income category and taxpayer. [Income Tax Law, arts 6 and 56–65](https://tatime.gov.al/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Ligj-Nr.-29-2023-Per-Tatimin-mbi-te-Ardhurat-i-ndryshuar.pdf)

## Responsible authorities

The General Directorate of Taxes (Drejtoria e Përgjithshme e Tatimeve) and its regional offices administer national income taxes. Use the competent tax office or electronic filing channel for the registration, return or request. Check the municipality for local taxes and fees. [Tax Administration procedures and Law 9920](https://tatime.gov.al/legjislacioni/procedurat-tatimore/) · [Tax Administration registration guidance](https://tatime.gov.al/regjistrimi/) · [General Income Tax Instruction 26/2023](https://www.qbz.gov.al/pdf/908874e6fdeea0aafc8e7cdd4d905784040360c74d35be2d87d16068aaf527a4.pdf)

## Fictional intake

An individual with no Albanian employer moves to Tirana and spends 182 days there during the tax year. Check their home, business and centre of vital interests before treating the person as a nonresident. If a foreign company controlled by the same person starts holding board meetings in Albania, assess that company's status separately.
