# Brazil tax system

> General orientation for 2026. Confirm the taxpayer, regime, transaction and relevant state or municipality with a qualified Brazilian tax professional before applying these rules.

Amounts are in Brazilian reais (BRL). The Receita Federal administers federal taxes. Check state and municipal obligations separately, particularly during the consumption-tax transition.

## Income tax

- **Individuals and residence.** Residence can arise on arrival, including through qualifying immigration or employment status, or when a temporary entrant reaches 184 days in Brazil within a period of up to 12 months. Check the earlier trigger and the rules for permanent departures and return. Residents generally bring Brazilian and foreign income into scope. Non-residents generally face Brazilian tax on Brazilian-source income under the rule for that payment or gain. Check treaties, final withholding and foreign tax relief separately. [IN SRF No. 208/2002, arts. 2–3 and 7–27](https://normas.receita.fazenda.gov.br/sijut2consulta/link.action?idAto=15079) · [Receita Federal residence guidance](https://www.gov.br/receitafederal/pt-br/assuntos/meu-imposto-de-renda/preenchimento/dsdp/nao-residente)
- **2026 individual rates.** The ordinary progressive income tax (IRPF) table has 0%, 7.5%, 15%, 22.5% and 27.5% bands. From January 2026 a separate reduction can eliminate ordinary monthly tax on qualifying taxable income up to BRL 5,000 and tapers to zero at BRL 7,350. The 2027 annual assessment for calendar 2026 has a separate reduction up to BRL 60,000, tapering to zero at BRL 88,200. Check the reduction against the tax calculated, and apply the separate high-income minimum tax, dividend withholding and special income rules where relevant. [Receita Federal 2026 tables](https://www.gov.br/receitafederal/pt-br/assuntos/meu-imposto-de-renda/tabelas/2026) · [Law No. 15,270/2025](https://legis.senado.gov.br/norma/41758136/publicacao/41762089)
- **Companies.** Ordinary federal company income tax (IRPJ) is 15%, with a 10% additional charge on the taxable base above BRL 20,000 multiplied by the number of months in the assessment period. The social contribution on net profit (CSLL) is generally 9% for ordinary companies, with other rates for specified financial entities. Determine the actual, presumptive or other eligible basis before computing the tax. Check the 2026 changes to presumptive coefficients and any distinct Simples Nacional rules. [IN RFB No. 1,700/2017](https://normas.receita.fazenda.gov.br/sijut2consulta/link.action?idAto=81268) · [Receita Federal 2026 guidance on reduced benefits](https://www.gov.br/receitafederal/pt-br/centrais-de-conteudo/publicacoes/perguntas-e-respostas/beneficios-fiscais/perguntas-e-respostas-reducao-dos-incentivos-e-beneficios-tributarios-v5-final.pdf/%40%40download/file)

## Consumption taxes and filing

- **2026 transition.** The statutory test rates are 0.9% for federal CBS and 0.1% for state IBS. Collection can be waived when the taxpayer meets the applicable reporting requirements. Continue checking existing federal PIS/Cofins and IPI, state ICMS and municipal ISS for the period. Federal transition advances in 2027 and the ICMS and ISS transition runs from 2029 to 2032 before the new model applies fully in 2033. Select the electronic document and effective date by transaction. The joint act published on 13 November 2026 moved specified electronic service invoices, including certain platform services, to 1 March 2027. [Complementary Law No. 214/2025, arts. 343–348](https://planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/leis/lcp/lcp214.htm) · [Receita Federal transition guidance](https://www.gov.br/receitafederal/pt-br/acesso-a-informacao/acoes-e-programas/reforma-tributaria-do-consumo/entenda) · [Joint Act No. 7/2026](https://www.cte.fazenda.gov.br/portal/exibirArquivo.aspx?conteudo=ouucTgkRXRE%3D)
- **Filing.** Confirm the annual individual filing calendar applicable to calendar 2026 and any separate departure return. For a company required to file digital accounting records (ECD) or its digital tax record (ECF), the ordinary following-year deadlines are the last working day of June or July respectively. Check event-driven deadlines, any extensions and the separate payment calendar. [Receita Federal ECD guidance](https://www.gov.br/receitafederal/pt-br/acesso-a-informacao/perguntas-frequentes/sped/ecd/ecd/qual-e-o-prazo-para) · [Receita Federal ECF guidance](https://www.gov.br/pt-br/servicos/entregar-escrituracao-contabil-fiscal)
