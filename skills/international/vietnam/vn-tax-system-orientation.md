# Viet Nam tax-system orientation

Identify the taxpayer, income category and relevant period before applying a rate or exemption. The scope of charge differs between an individual, an enterprise formed in Viet Nam and a foreign enterprise.

## Taxable income and source

Resident individuals are subject to personal income tax (PIT) on taxable income from Viet Nam and abroad. Non-resident individuals are subject to PIT on taxable income arising in Viet Nam. The legislation generally commenced on 1 July 2026. Apply its rules for resident employment and business income from the 2026 tax period. Check the applicable treaty separately. [Personal Income Tax Law 109/2025/QH15, arts 2 and 29, consolidated through Law 09/2026/QH16](https://vanban.chinhphu.vn/?classid=2629&docid=218215&pageid=27160)

An enterprise established under Vietnamese law is within corporate income tax (CIT) for taxable income from Viet Nam and abroad. A foreign enterprise with a permanent establishment in Viet Nam is within CIT on taxable Vietnamese and foreign income attributable to that establishment. Other Vietnamese-source taxable income can be taxable without attribution to the establishment or when the enterprise has no permanent establishment. Check the domestic source rule and any treaty limit by income category. [Corporate Income Tax Law 67/2025/QH15, arts 2–3](https://congbao.chinhphu.vn/tai-ve-van-ban-so-67-2025-qh15-45557-57755?format=pdf) · [Consolidated corporate income tax law](https://vanban.chinhphu.vn/?classid=2629&docid=218265&pageid=27160)

## Individual residence

The presence test is at least 183 days in a calendar year or in the 12 consecutive months from first presence. Count the arrival and departure dates. Count an arrival and departure on the same day once. A registered regular home or residential leases totalling at least 183 days in the tax year can also bring the individual within the residence rule. A person with a regular home there and fewer than 183 days of presence in the tax year is resident unless they can establish residence elsewhere under the decree. Use any applicable treaty tie-breaker separately. [PIT Law, art 2](https://vanban.chinhphu.vn/?classid=2629&docid=218215&pageid=27160) · [Decree 253/2026/NĐ-CP, arts 4–5](https://vanban.chinhphu.vn/?docid=218684&pageid=27160&typegroupid=4)

## Taxpayer type

A registered partnership company (công ty hợp danh) acquires legal personality on registration and comes within the Vietnamese enterprise rules. Check partners' own income separately. An individual or household business can instead fall under the PIT business-income provisions. Determine the entity's legal form and who earns the income before choosing the return. [Enterprise Law consolidated in 67/VBHN-VPQH, art 177](https://congbao.chinhphu.vn/tai-ve-van-ban-so-67-vbhn-vpqh-45865-58272?format=pdf) · [CIT Law, art 2](https://congbao.chinhphu.vn/tai-ve-van-ban-so-67-2025-qh15-45557-57755?format=pdf) · [PIT Law, arts 3 and 7](https://vanban.chinhphu.vn/?classid=2629&docid=218215&pageid=27160)

## Currency

Use Vietnamese dong (VND) for ordinary tax declarations and computations. Where legislation authorises foreign currency, identify the provision and use the prescribed conversion or payment rule for that transaction. Check the goods import or export exchange-rate rule under customs legislation separately. [Tax Administration Law 108/2025/QH15, art 12](https://vanban.chinhphu.vn/?classid=1&docid=216541&pageid=27160) · [Decree 252/2026/NĐ-CP, art 14](https://datafiles.chinhphu.vn/cpp/files/vbpq/2026/7/252-ndcp.signed.pdf)

## Assessment periods

PIT finalisation normally follows the calendar year. If an individual spends fewer than 183 days in Viet Nam in the first calendar year, but reaches 183 days in the first 12 consecutive months from arrival, that 12-month period is the first finalisation period. Some income is assessed by taxable event. Annual CIT follows the calendar year or the enterprise's financial year as prescribed. Check the applicable period and filing rules for foreign enterprises and first or final short periods. [PIT Law, art 6](https://vanban.chinhphu.vn/?classid=2629&docid=218215&pageid=27160) · [Decree 252/2026/NĐ-CP, art 9](https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/toan-van-nghi-dinh-252-2026-nd-cp-huong-dan-thi-hanh-luat-quan-ly-thue-119260715155021635.htm) · [CIT Law, art 5](https://congbao.chinhphu.vn/tai-ve-van-ban-so-67-2025-qh15-45557-57755?format=pdf)

## Tax authority

The Tax Department (Cục Thuế) is part of the Ministry of Finance. It operates through central, provincial and local levels under the structure effective 1 October 2026. Identify the competent office and use the official electronic filing service where applicable. [Decree 376/2026/NĐ-CP](https://vanban.chinhphu.vn/?docid=219754&pageid=27160) · [Government structure and three levels](https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/co-cau-to-chuc-moi-cua-bo-tai-chinh-tu-1-3-2025-119250228144039911.htm) · [Electronic tax service](https://thuedientu.gdt.gov.vn/)

## Worked example

A consultant first arrives on 1 September and spends 120 days in Viet Nam before the end of that calendar year. They spend another 63 days there in the remainder of the first 12 months from arrival. The total is 183 days. Use the first 12 consecutive months for their first PIT finalisation and check the treaty before determining the tax on income from abroad.
