# Vietnam Company Formation & Entity Choice

> **The previous version cited a single commercial host for every substantive
> fact, and two of those facts had stopped being true.** It sent readers to a
> ministry that no longer exists, and it billed an annual fee that Vietnam has
> abolished. It also had no trace of the **beneficial-owner regime**, which has
> been a registration requirement for every Vietnamese company since 1 July
> 2025. The entity types and the capital rules survived; what follows re-sources
> them and adds what was missing.

## 1. The law as it now stands

The Law on Enterprises 2020 has been amended twice, and the registration decree
under it has been replaced. A 2025 or 2026 transaction is governed by all three.

| Instrument | Date | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Law on Enterprises No. 59/2020/QH14** | 17 Jun 2020 | The base law, in force 1 Jan 2021 |
| Law No. 03/2022/QH15 | 2022 | First amendment |
| **Law No. 76/2025/QH15** | 17 Jun 2025 | Second amendment, **in force 1 July 2025** |
| **Decree No. 168/2025/NĐ-CP** | 30 Jun 2025 | Business registration; **in force 1 July 2025**, replacing Decree 01/2021/NĐ-CP |

- **Law 76/2025 amends, it does not replace** — Its preamble states it amends
  and supplements the Law on Enterprises No. 59/2020/QH14 *"đã được sửa đổi, bổ
  sung một số điều theo Luật số 03/2022/QH15"*. Read the three together  _(Law No. 76/2025/QH15, preamble and art. 2 — https://dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn/vn/Pages/ChiTietVanBan.aspx?vID=27042)_
- **Decree 168/2025 replaced the registration decree** — It governs dossiers,
  procedures, business-household registration, the one-stop-shop and online
  registration, replacing Decree 01/2021/NĐ-CP and Decree 122/2020/NĐ-CP  _(Decree No. 168/2025/NĐ-CP — https://vanban.chinhphu.vn/?pageid=27160&docid=214334)_

## 2. Entity types

Foreign investors most commonly use a limited liability company (single- or
multi-member) or a joint-stock company.

- **Multi-member LLC (công ty TNHH hai thành viên trở lên)** — *"an enterprise
  that has **between 2 and 50** institutional and individual members"*. Liability
  is limited to the capital contributed  _(Law on Enterprises 2020, art. 46(1))_
- **Single-member LLC (công ty TNHH một thành viên)** — One owner, individual or
  organisation; liability limited to charter capital  _(Law on Enterprises 2020, art. 74)_
- **Joint-stock company (công ty cổ phần)** — Minimum 3 shareholders, no maximum;
  capital divided into shares; may issue securities ((approx — confirm against
  art. 111(1); this figure was not re-read from the statute for this pass, and the
  only full texts reachable from this network were image-only scans))  _(Law on Enterprises 2020, art. 111 (as described at [vietnam-briefing.com](https://www.vietnam-briefing.com/doing-business-guide/vietnam)))_
- **Private enterprise (doanh nghiệp tư nhân)** — One individual owner with
  **unlimited** liability ((approx — confirm against art. 188; not re-read from
  the statute for this pass))  _(Law on Enterprises 2020, art. 188 (as described at [vietnam-briefing.com](https://www.vietnam-briefing.com/doing-business-guide/vietnam)))_
- **Dissolution for falling below the minimum** — A company that no longer has
  the minimum number of members or shareholders for **06 consecutive months**
  without converting its form is dissolved. Law 76/2025 rewrote this limb  _(Law No. 76/2025/QH15 art. 1(23), amending art. 207(1)(c) — https://dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn/vn/Pages/ChiTietVanBan.aspx?vID=27042)_

## 3. Charter capital — and the 90-day rule has an exception the guide omitted

- **No fixed statutory minimum for most sectors** — Registered charter capital
  must be adequate for the business; conditional sectors such as banking,
  insurance, real estate and finance carry their own minimums ((approx —
  confirm the sector minimums against the governing sectoral law))  _(Law on Enterprises 2020; Law on Investment No. 61/2020/QH14 (as described at [vietnam-briefing.com](https://www.vietnam-briefing.com/doing-business-guide/vietnam)))_
- **90 days — but the clock stops for assets in transit** — Members contribute
  *"within 90 days after the grant of the enterprise registration certificate,
  **excluding the time for transportation or import of assets contributed as
  capital and performance of administrative procedures for asset ownership
  transfer**"*. The previous version gave the 90 days without the exclusion, which
  understates the deadline for anyone contributing imported plant or real
  property  _(Law on Enterprises 2020, art. 47(2))_
- **Under-contributing is now a named prohibited act** — Law 76/2025 rewrote the
  prohibitions to catch *"kê khai khống vốn điều lệ thông qua hành vi không góp
  đủ số vốn điều lệ như đã đăng ký mà không thực hiện đăng ký điều chỉnh vốn điều
  lệ"* — declaring sham charter capital by failing to contribute the registered
  amount **without registering a reduction** — and deliberate mis-valuation of
  contributed assets. Missing the deadline and doing nothing is the offence; the
  cure is to register the adjustment  _(Law No. 76/2025/QH15 art. 1(5), amending art. 16(4)–(5) — https://dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn/vn/Pages/ChiTietVanBan.aspx?vID=27042)_

## 4. Beneficial owners — a registration duty the guide had no trace of

From **1 July 2025** every Vietnamese company must identify, declare and keep
current the individuals who actually own or control it. This is the single
largest change to Vietnamese company formation since the 2020 Law, and it was
absent from the previous version entirely.

- **Who counts** — A beneficial owner is *"cá nhân có quyền sở hữu trên thực tế
  vốn điều lệ hoặc có quyền chi phối đối với doanh nghiệp đó"* — an individual
  with actual ownership of the charter capital **or** control over the
  enterprise. State-capital representatives are excluded  _(Law No. 76/2025/QH15 art. 1(1)(d), inserting art. 4(35) — https://dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn/vn/Pages/ChiTietVanBan.aspx?vID=27042)_
- **A standing duty on the company** — Collect, update and retain beneficial-owner
  information, and supply it to the competent state authority on request  _(Law No. 76/2025/QH15 art. 1(2), inserting art. 8(5a) — https://dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn/vn/Pages/ChiTietVanBan.aspx?vID=27042)_
- **It goes in the registration dossier** — The beneficial-owner list is now part
  of the dossier for LLCs and partnerships (arts. 20(3), 21(3)) and for
  joint-stock companies (art. 22(3)), and is declared to the provincial Business
  Registration Authority  _(Law No. 76/2025/QH15 art. 1(7)–(9); Decree 168/2025/NĐ-CP art. 18(1) — https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/toan-van-nghi-dinh-168-2025-nd-cp-ve-dang-ky-doanh-nghiep-119250702175708554.htm)_
- **What must be listed** — *"họ, tên; ngày, tháng, năm sinh; quốc tịch; dân tộc;
  giới tính; địa chỉ liên lạc; tỷ lệ sở hữu hoặc quyền chi phối"* plus the
  individual's legal-document details. Note that **ethnicity and gender** are
  required fields, which surprises foreign-invested groups building the register  _(Law No. 76/2025/QH15 art. 1(11)(c), inserting art. 25(5) — https://dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn/vn/Pages/ChiTietVanBan.aspx?vID=27042)_
- **Changes must be notified** — A change in beneficial-owner information is a
  notifiable change to the Business Registration Authority, except for listed
  companies and companies registered for securities trading  _(Law No. 76/2025/QH15 art. 1(13), amending art. 31(1)(c) — https://dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn/vn/Pages/ChiTietVanBan.aspx?vID=27042)_
- **The register is an anti-money-laundering tool** — Competent state agencies
  may require the information from the National Business Registration
  Information System *"để phục vụ công tác về phòng, chống rửa tiền"*, free of
  charge  _(Law No. 76/2025/QH15 art. 1(14), inserting art. 33(1a) — https://dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn/vn/Pages/ChiTietVanBan.aspx?vID=27042)_
- **Retained five years past the end of the company** — The registration
  authority keeps beneficial-owner information *"ít nhất 05 năm kể từ ngày doanh
  nghiệp giải thể, phá sản"*  _(Law No. 76/2025/QH15 art. 1(26), inserting art. 216(1)(h) — https://dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn/vn/Pages/ChiTietVanBan.aspx?vID=27042)_
- **Existing companies: no standalone filing, but do not wait to prepare** — A
  company registered **before** 1 July 2025 supplies the information *"đồng thời
  tại thời điểm doanh nghiệp thực hiện thủ tục đăng ký thay đổi nội dung đăng ký
  doanh nghiệp… gần nhất"* — at its next registration-change or notification —
  unless it asks to do so earlier. There is no separate deadline, so the
  obligation bites at the next routine filing, whenever that falls  _(Law No. 76/2025/QH15, art. 3(1) transitional — https://dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn/vn/Pages/ChiTietVanBan.aspx?vID=27042)_

## 5. Who may not establish or manage a company

- **Cadres, civil servants and public employees — with a new carve-out** — Still
  barred under art. 17(2)(b), *"trừ trường hợp được thực hiện theo quy định của
  pháp luật về khoa học, công nghệ, đổi mới sáng tạo và chuyển đổi số quốc gia"*.
  Law 76/2025 opened the same exception to the capital-contribution ban in art.
  17(3)(b). Anyone advising a university or state-institute spin-out needs the
  science-and-technology legislation, not just the Law on Enterprises  _(Law No. 76/2025/QH15 art. 1(6), amending art. 17(2)(b) and 17(3)(b) — https://dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn/vn/Pages/ChiTietVanBan.aspx?vID=27042)_
- **Acceptable personal identification** — thẻ Căn cước, thẻ Căn cước công dân,
  Hộ chiếu, or other lawful personal identification  _(Law No. 76/2025/QH15 art. 1(1)(c), amending art. 4(16) — https://dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn/vn/Pages/ChiTietVanBan.aspx?vID=27042)_

## 6. Registration — the authority has moved, and the ERC has a statutory clock

- **It is the Department of Finance now, not Planning and Investment** — The
  provincial Business Registration Authority is *"Cơ quan đăng ký kinh doanh
  thuộc **Sở Tài chính** tỉnh, thành phố trực thuộc trung ương"*. The Ministry of
  Planning and Investment was merged into the Ministry of Finance in 2025, and
  the National Business Registration Portal itself now carries *"Bản quyền thuộc
  Bộ Tài chính"*. **A reader sent to a provincial Department of Planning and
  Investment is being sent to a body that no longer exists**  _(Decree 168/2025/NĐ-CP, art. 20(1) — https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/toan-van-nghi-dinh-168-2025-nd-cp-ve-dang-ky-doanh-nghiep-119250702175708554.htm)_
- **High-tech park companies register with the park** — The management board of a
  high-tech park registers enterprises, branches and representative offices
  within it  _(Decree 168/2025/NĐ-CP, art. 20(1) — https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/toan-van-nghi-dinh-168-2025-nd-cp-ve-dang-ky-doanh-nghiep-119250702175708554.htm)_
- **Commune-level registration body** — Phòng Kinh tế (communes and special
  zones) or Phòng Kinh tế, Hạ tầng và Đô thị (wards and the Phú Quốc special
  zone) under the commune-level People's Committee. District-level registration
  offices are gone  _(Decree 168/2025/NĐ-CP, art. 20(2) — https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/toan-van-nghi-dinh-168-2025-nd-cp-ve-dang-ky-doanh-nghiep-119250702175708554.htm)_
- **ERC in 03 working days** — *"Trong thời hạn 03 ngày làm việc kể từ ngày nhận
  hồ sơ đăng ký doanh nghiệp, Cơ quan đăng ký kinh doanh cấp tỉnh xem xét tính
  hợp lệ của hồ sơ và cấp Giấy chứng nhận đăng ký doanh nghiệp"*. This is the
  statutory clock on the ERC step alone — not on the whole foreign-investment
  timeline, which the IRC step below dominates  _(Decree 168/2025/NĐ-CP, art. 33 — https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/toan-van-nghi-dinh-168-2025-nd-cp-ve-dang-ky-doanh-nghiep-119250702175708554.htm)_
- **Investment Registration Certificate (IRC)** — Required for foreign-invested
  projects before company registration ((approx — confirm the triggering
  thresholds and exemptions against the Law on Investment No. 61/2020/QH14))  _(Law on Investment No. 61/2020/QH14 (as described at [vietnam-briefing.com](https://www.vietnam-briefing.com/doing-business-guide/vietnam)))_
- **Typical timeline, foreign-invested LLC** — Roughly 1 to 3 months including
  IRC and ERC for a standard, non-conditional business ((approx — confirm;
  varies by sector and locality, and this is a practitioner estimate, not a
  statutory period))  _(Law on Investment No. 61/2020/QH14 (as described at [vietnam-briefing.com](https://www.vietnam-briefing.com/doing-business-guide/vietnam)))_
- **Foreign shareholders of a JSC: notify within 03 working days** — An unlisted
  joint-stock company must notify the Business Registration Authority within
  **03 working days** of obtaining or changing the name, nationality, passport
  number, contact address and shareholding of a foreign individual or corporate
  shareholder  _(Law No. 76/2025/QH15 art. 1(22), amending art. 176(3) — https://dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn/vn/Pages/ChiTietVanBan.aspx?vID=27042)_

## 7. The annual business licence fee no longer exists

- **Abolished from 1 January 2026** — *"Chấm dứt việc thu, nộp lệ phí môn bài từ
  ngày 01 tháng 01 năm 2026."* The previous version listed the lệ phí môn bài as
  a live recurring cost of running a Vietnamese company. **It is not one.** From
  2026 there is no fee and no môn bài declaration to file  _(Nghị quyết 198/2025/QH15, art. 10(7) — https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/chinh-sach-dac-biet-phat-trien-kinh-te-tu-nhan-ho-tro-tai-chinh-tin-dung-thue-phi-le-phi-uu-dai-lua-chon-nha-thau-119250518063622747.htm)_
- **The implementing decree** — Decree No. 362/2025/NĐ-CP, signed 31 December
  2025 and in force **1 January 2026**, details the Law on Fees and Charges and
  carries the abolition into the fee regulations  _(Decree No. 362/2025/NĐ-CP — https://vanban.chinhphu.vn/?pageid=27160&docid=216389)_
- **For periods up to and including 2025 the fee did apply** — It was set by
  Decree 139/2016/NĐ-CP as amended by Decree 22/2020/NĐ-CP. The previous
  version's "VND 1,000,000 to VND 3,000,000 based on registered charter capital"
  also mis-stated the bands: **VND 1,000,000 was not a charter-capital band** but
  the rate for branches, representative offices, business locations and other
  economic organisations, while a company paid VND 3,000,000 above VND 10 billion
  of charter capital and VND 2,000,000 at or below it ((approx — confirm against
  Decree 139/2016 art. 4; both decrees are now spent, and this was not read from
  the decree itself))  _(Decree 139/2016/NĐ-CP art. 4, as amended by Decree 22/2020/NĐ-CP — repealed with effect from 1 Jan 2026)_

## 8. Ongoing obligations after formation

- **Annual statutory audit for foreign-invested enterprises** — Annual financial
  statements must be audited by an independent auditor ((approx — confirm the
  scope against the Law on Independent Audit No. 67/2011/QH12 and its
  implementing decree))  _(Law on Independent Audit No. 67/2011/QH12 (as described at [vietnam-briefing.com](https://www.vietnam-briefing.com/doing-business-guide/vietnam)))_
- **Annual financial statement filing — within 90 days of year-end** — Audited
  statements go to the tax, statistics and licensing authorities ((approx —
  confirm against the Law on Accounting No. 88/2015/QH13 and Decree
  174/2016/NĐ-CP))  _(Law on Accounting No. 88/2015/QH13 (as described at [vietnam-briefing.com](https://www.vietnam-briefing.com/doing-business-guide/vietnam)))_
- **Private-placement bonds: a leverage cap** — An issuer's total liabilities,
  including the bonds to be issued, must not exceed **5 times owner's equity** on
  the audited financial statements for the year preceding issue. State-owned
  enterprises, real-estate project issuers, credit institutions, insurers,
  reinsurers, insurance brokers, securities companies and fund managers are
  carved out to their own sectoral rules  _(Law No. 76/2025/QH15 art. 1(19)(b), inserting art. 128(3)(c1) — https://dangkykinhdoanh.gov.vn/vn/Pages/ChiTietVanBan.aspx?vID=27042)_

## 9. What this replaces

| The guide said | The statute says |
| --- | --- |
| LLC 2–50 members, cited to `vietnam-briefing.com` | **Confirmed** — art. 46(1), *"between 2 and 50"* |
| Capital contributed "within 90 days of issue of the ERC" | Incomplete: 90 days **excluding** transport/import of contributed assets and ownership-transfer formalities — art. 47(2) |
| ERC "issued by the provincial **Department of Planning and Investment**" | **Wrong body.** The Business Registration Authority sits in the **Sở Tài chính** (Department of Finance) — Decree 168/2025 art. 20(1) |
| No ERC timeline given | **03 working days** from receipt of the dossier — Decree 168/2025 art. 33 |
| Annual business licence fee "VND 1,000,000 to VND 3,000,000" | **Abolished from 1 Jan 2026** — Resolution 198/2025/QH15 art. 10(7); and the VND 1,000,000 tier was never a charter-capital band |
| Registration governed by unnamed decree | **Decree 168/2025/NĐ-CP** from 1 Jul 2025, replacing Decree 01/2021/NĐ-CP |
| Law on Enterprises 59/2020 cited unamended | Amended by Law 03/2022/QH15 and **Law 76/2025/QH15** (in force 1 Jul 2025) |
| *nothing* | **Beneficial-owner regime**: definition, dossier item, notifiable changes, AML access, 5-year retention, transitional rule — Law 76/2025 |
| *nothing* | Under-contributing registered capital without registering a reduction is a **named prohibited act** — art. 16(5) |
| *nothing* | Civil servants' bar now carries a **science-and-technology carve-out** — art. 17(2)(b) |
| *nothing* | Unlisted JSCs notify foreign-shareholder details within **03 working days** — art. 176(3) |
| *nothing* | Private-placement bonds capped at **5× owner's equity** — art. 128(3)(c1) |

Every figure above except those marked "(approx — confirm)" was read from the
text of Law No. 76/2025/QH15 published by the National Business Registration

47(2) of the Law on Enterprises 2020 were read in an English translation rather
