# Vietnam Payroll & Social Contributions

## Compulsory insurance and payroll withholding

Vietnam requires compulsory social insurance (SI), health insurance (HI) and unemployment insurance (UI) on employee salaries, split between employer and employee. The 2024 Social Insurance Law took effect 1 July 2025. Employers also withhold PIT from payroll monthly.

- **Employer total compulsory insurance contribution** — 21.5% of gross salary (SI 17.5% + HI 3% + UI 1%)  _(Law on Social Insurance No. 41/2024/QH15 (in force 1 July 2025), art. 34 — https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Bao-hiem/Law-41-2024-QH15-on-Social-Insurance-622824.aspx ; Vietnamese text on hcc.nghean.gov.vn — https://hcc.nghean.gov.vn/tin-tong-hop/toan-van-luat-so-41-2024-qh15-bao-hiem-xa-hoi-1457.html; Decree 58/2020/NĐ-CP on contributions to the occupational accident and disease fund, art. 4 — https://caselaw.vn/van-ban-phap-luat/347686-nghi-dinh-so-58-2020-nd-cp-ngay-27-05-2020-cua-chinh-phu-quy-dinh-ve-muc-dong-bao-hiem-xa-hoi-bat-buoc-vao-quy-bao-hiem-tai-nan-lao-dong-benh-nghe-nghiep; Law on Health Insurance as amended by Law No. 51/2024/QH15 (in force 1 July 2025), art. 13 — https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Bao-hiem/Luat-Bao-hiem-y-te-sua-doi-2024-505750.aspx; Law on Employment No. 74/2025/QH15 (in force 1 January 2026), art. 33 — https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Bao-hiem/Law-74-2025-QH15-Employment-673744.aspx)_
- **Employee total compulsory insurance contribution** — 10.5% of gross salary (SI 8% + HI 1.5% + UI 1%)  _(Law on Social Insurance No. 41/2024/QH15 (in force 1 July 2025), art. 33 — https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Bao-hiem/Law-41-2024-QH15-on-Social-Insurance-622824.aspx ; Vietnamese text on hcc.nghean.gov.vn — https://hcc.nghean.gov.vn/tin-tong-hop/toan-van-luat-so-41-2024-qh15-bao-hiem-xa-hoi-1457.html; Law on Health Insurance as amended by Law No. 51/2024/QH15 (in force 1 July 2025), art. 13 — https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Bao-hiem/Luat-Bao-hiem-y-te-sua-doi-2024-505750.aspx; Law on Employment No. 74/2025/QH15 (in force 1 January 2026), art. 33 — https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Bao-hiem/Law-74-2025-QH15-Employment-673744.aspx)_
- **Employer social insurance (pension/survivorship 14% + sickness/maternity 3% + occupational 0.5%)** — 17.5%: the Social Insurance Law sets 3% to the sickness and maternity fund and 14% to the pension and survivorship fund, and the occupational accident and disease fund takes 0.5% of the salary fund (0.3% for approved low-risk employers)  _(Law on Social Insurance No. 41/2024/QH15 (in force 1 July 2025), art. 34.1 — https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Bao-hiem/Law-41-2024-QH15-on-Social-Insurance-622824.aspx ; Vietnamese text on hcc.nghean.gov.vn — https://hcc.nghean.gov.vn/tin-tong-hop/toan-van-luat-so-41-2024-qh15-bao-hiem-xa-hoi-1457.html; Decree 58/2020/NĐ-CP on contributions to the occupational accident and disease fund, art. 4.1 — https://caselaw.vn/van-ban-phap-luat/347686-nghi-dinh-so-58-2020-nd-cp-ngay-27-05-2020-cua-chinh-phu-quy-dinh-ve-muc-dong-bao-hiem-xa-hoi-bat-buoc-vao-quy-bao-hiem-tai-nan-lao-dong-benh-nghe-nghiep)_
- **Employee social insurance (pension/survivorship)** — 8% of the salary on which social insurance is paid, to the pension and survivorship fund  _(Law on Social Insurance No. 41/2024/QH15 (in force 1 July 2025), art. 33.1(a) — https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Bao-hiem/Law-41-2024-QH15-on-Social-Insurance-622824.aspx ; Vietnamese text on hcc.nghean.gov.vn — https://hcc.nghean.gov.vn/tin-tong-hop/toan-van-luat-so-41-2024-qh15-bao-hiem-xa-hoi-1457.html)_
- **Health insurance (employer 3% / employee 1.5%)** — 4.5% total: the law caps the monthly contribution at 6% of salary with the employer paying two thirds and the employee one third, and Decree 146/2018/NĐ-CP art. 7 sets the rate at 4.5% `[RESEARCH GAP — the decree was read through secondary summaries only]`  _(Law on Health Insurance as amended by Law No. 51/2024/QH15 (in force 1 July 2025), art. 13.1(a) — https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Bao-hiem/Luat-Bao-hiem-y-te-sua-doi-2024-505750.aspx; Decree 146/2018/NĐ-CP, art. 7)_
- **Unemployment insurance (employer 1% / employee 1%)** — 2% total: the employee's premium is capped at 1% of monthly salary and the employer's at 1% of the monthly salary fund of insured employees, with State support of up to 1%  _(Law on Employment No. 74/2025/QH15 (in force 1 January 2026), art. 33.1 — https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Bao-hiem/Law-74-2025-QH15-Employment-673744.aspx)_
- **Salary cap for SI and HI contributions** — 20 times the reference level, the Government-set amount that replaces the basic salary for contributions and benefits (equal to the basic salary of VND 2,340,000 while that salary stands, so about VND 46,800,000 per month) (confirm the current reference level)  _(Law on Social Insurance No. 41/2024/QH15 (in force 1 July 2025), arts 7 and 31.1 — https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Bao-hiem/Law-41-2024-QH15-on-Social-Insurance-622824.aspx ; Vietnamese text on hcc.nghean.gov.vn — https://hcc.nghean.gov.vn/tin-tong-hop/toan-van-luat-so-41-2024-qh15-bao-hiem-xa-hoi-1457.html)_
- **Salary cap for unemployment insurance contributions** — 20 times the regional minimum wage (Region 1 cap approx VND 106,200,000 per month) VND/month (approx - confirm current Region 1 minimum wage)  _(Law on Employment (as described at [vietanlaw.com](https://vietanlaw.com/vietnam-employee-social-insurance-contribution-rate-2025/)))_
- **Payroll PIT withholding** — Employers withhold PIT monthly under the progressive schedule (residents) or at 20% flat (non-residents)  _(Law on Personal Income Tax No. 04/2007/QH12 as amended (consolidated text 103/VBHN-VPQH, 2025), arts 22 and 26; Law on Personal Income Tax No. 109/2025/QH15 (in force 1 July 2026, applying to residents' employment and business income from the 2026 tax year), arts 9 and 21 — https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Thue-Phi-Le-Phi/Law-109-2025-QH15-personal-income-tax-687756.aspx)_
- **Payroll PIT remittance / declaration deadline** — Monthly by the 20th of the following month, or quarterly by the last day of the first month of the next quarter where eligible; payment falls due with the declaration  _(Law on Tax Administration No. 38/2019/QH14, arts 44.1 and 55.1 — https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/thue-phi-le-phi/Law-38-2019-QH14-Tax-administration-425672.aspx)_
- **Foreign employees and social insurance** — Foreign nationals working in Vietnam under employment contracts of at least 12 months join compulsory social insurance (and health insurance), except intra-company transferees and the other cases listed in the law  _(Law on Social Insurance No. 41/2024/QH15 (in force 1 July 2025), art. 2.2 — https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Bao-hiem/Law-41-2024-QH15-on-Social-Insurance-622824.aspx ; Vietnamese text on hcc.nghean.gov.vn — https://hcc.nghean.gov.vn/tin-tong-hop/toan-van-luat-so-41-2024-qh15-bao-hiem-xa-hoi-1457.html)_
