# Vietnam Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax rates

- **Standard CIT rate** — 20%; 15% for enterprises with total annual revenue up to VND 3 billion and 17% from over VND 3 billion to VND 50 billion, measured on the preceding period's revenue  _(Law on Corporate Income Tax No. 67/2025/QH15 (in force 1 October 2025, applying from the 2025 tax period), art. 10 — https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Doanh-nghiep/Law-67-2025-QH15-on-Corporate-Income-Tax-664714.aspx)_
- **Reduced rate - revenue not exceeding VND 3 billion** — 15%  _(Law No. 67/2025/QH15; [Vietnamese Government, policy portal](https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/thue-suat-thue-thu-nhap-doanh-nghiep-moi-ap-dung-tu-1-10-2025-119250730082233732.htm))_
- **Reduced rate - revenue over VND 3 billion to VND 50 billion** — 17%  _(Law No. 67/2025/QH15; [Vietnamese Government, policy portal](https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/thue-suat-thue-thu-nhap-doanh-nghiep-moi-ap-dung-tu-1-10-2025-119250730082233732.htm))_
- **Which year's revenue decides the band** — The **immediately preceding** tax period's total revenue, not the current year's. The statute reads *"Doanh thu làm căn cứ xác định doanh nghiệp thuộc đối tượng được áp dụng thuế suất 15% và 17% ... là tổng doanh thu của kỳ tính thuế thu nhập doanh nghiệp trước liền kề"* -- the revenue used to determine eligibility for the 15% and 17% rates is the total revenue of the immediately preceding corporate income tax period. A company that grew past VND 3 billion this year is still on last year's band this year. The Government is to issue detailed regulations on how revenue is determined; the treatment of a **newly established** enterprise with no preceding period is not settled on that page, so confirm it before applying a reduced rate in a first year  _(Law No. 67/2025/QH15; [Vietnamese Government, policy portal](https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/thue-suat-thue-thu-nhap-doanh-nghiep-moi-ap-dung-tu-1-10-2025-119250730082233732.htm))_
- **Special rate - oil and gas** — 25% to 50% depending on the project/contract (approx - confirm by project)  _(Law No. 67/2025/QH15; [Vietnamese Government, policy portal](https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/thue-suat-thue-thu-nhap-doanh-nghiep-moi-ap-dung-tu-1-10-2025-119250730082233732.htm))_
- **Special rate - rare mineral extraction** — **50%**, reduced to **40%** where the mine lies in an area of difficult socio-economic conditions  _(Law No. 67/2025/QH15; [Vietnamese Government, policy portal](https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/thue-suat-thue-thu-nhap-doanh-nghiep-moi-ap-dung-tu-1-10-2025-119250730082233732.htm))_
- **Tax base** — Assessable income = taxable income (revenue less deductible expenses, plus other income) less tax-exempt income and losses carried forward; resident companies are taxed on worldwide income  _(Law on Corporate Income Tax No. 67/2025/QH15 (in force 1 October 2025, applying from the 2025 tax period), arts 2 and 7 — https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Doanh-nghiep/Law-67-2025-QH15-on-Corporate-Income-Tax-664714.aspx)_

The standard CIT rate is 20%. **Law No. 67/2025/QH15**, passed 14 June 2025, took effect on **1 October 2025 and applies to the 2025 tax period onward** -- not merely from October. It repealed Law No. 14/2008/QH12. It introduced reduced rates for micro and small enterprises based on annual revenue, and the revenue that decides the band is the **immediately preceding** tax period's, not the current one. Oil, gas and certain mineral activities are taxed at higher special rates.

## Withholding taxes and filing deadlines

- **Withholding tax on dividends paid to a corporate shareholder** — 0%: income distributed from capital contributions, share purchases, joint ventures or associations with domestic enterprises after corporate income tax has been paid is tax-exempt income of the recipient  _(Law on Corporate Income Tax No. 67/2025/QH15 (in force 1 October 2025, applying from the 2025 tax period), art. 4.7 — https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Doanh-nghiep/Law-67-2025-QH15-on-Corporate-Income-Tax-664714.aspx)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends paid to an individual** — 5% PIT on income from capital investment, withheld by the payer  _(Law on Personal Income Tax No. 04/2007/QH12 as amended (consolidated text 103/VBHN-VPQH, 2025), art. 23; Law on Personal Income Tax No. 109/2025/QH15 (in force 1 July 2026, applying to residents' employment and business income from the 2026 tax year), art. 12 — https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Thue-Phi-Le-Phi/Law-109-2025-QH15-personal-income-tax-687756.aspx)_
- **Foreign Contractor Tax (FCT) — how the charge works** — Vietnam's main charge on non-residents is not a classic dividend, interest and royalty withholding but **FCT**, which applies where a Vietnamese entity contracts with a foreign contractor that has no licensed presence in Vietnam. Under the direct method the Vietnamese payer withholds **both a VAT component and a CIT component** at deemed rates on the contract value. The three CIT-portion lines below give the common cases; the table in the next bullet is the full set  _(Circular 103/2014/TT-BTC on the tax obligations of foreign organisations and individuals doing business in or earning income from Vietnam, arts 12.2 (VAT: 5% services, machinery leasing and insurance, 3% production, transport and construction with materials, 2% other) and 13.2 (CIT: 1% goods, 5% services, 10% restaurant, hotel and casino management, 2% construction, transport and other business, 0.1% securities, 5% loan interest, 10% royalties) — https://caselaw.vn/van-ban-phap-luat/264707-thong-tu-103-2014-tt-btc-huong-dan-thuc-hien-nghia-vu-thue-ap-dung-doi-voi-to-chuc-ca-nhan-nuoc-ngoai-kinh-doanh-tai-viet-nam-hoac-co-thu-nhap-phat-sinh-tai-viet-nam-do-bo-truong-bo-tai-chinh-ban-hanh; its corporate income tax provisions were replaced from 12 March 2026 by Circular 20/2026/TT-BTC art. 7 `[RESEARCH GAP — reviewer to confirm the deemed rates against Circular 20/2026, whose text was not read]`)_
- **FCT deemed rates by activity, in full** — This guide carried the CIT portion for interest, royalties and services and not the rest, and the VAT component nowhere. Distribution or supply of goods: **1% CIT**, VAT 1% or exempt. Services and e-commerce: **5%–10% CIT**, VAT 5%–10% (so the 5% service line below is the bottom of a range, not a fixed rate). Construction and installation: **2% CIT**, VAT 3%–5%. Transportation: **2% CIT**, VAT 3%, 0% international. Interest: **5% CIT**, VAT exempt. Royalties: **10% CIT**, VAT exempt or 5%. Securities transfer: **0.1% CIT**, VAT exempt  _(Circular 103/2014/TT-BTC on the tax obligations of foreign organisations and individuals doing business in or earning income from Vietnam, arts 12.2 (VAT: 5% services, machinery leasing and insurance, 3% production, transport and construction with materials, 2% other) and 13.2 (CIT: 1% goods, 5% services, 10% restaurant, hotel and casino management, 2% construction, transport and other business, 0.1% securities, 5% loan interest, 10% royalties) — https://caselaw.vn/van-ban-phap-luat/264707-thong-tu-103-2014-tt-btc-huong-dan-thuc-hien-nghia-vu-thue-ap-dung-doi-voi-to-chuc-ca-nhan-nuoc-ngoai-kinh-doanh-tai-viet-nam-hoac-co-thu-nhap-phat-sinh-tai-viet-nam-do-bo-truong-bo-tai-chinh-ban-hanh; its corporate income tax provisions were replaced from 12 March 2026 by Circular 20/2026/TT-BTC art. 7 `[RESEARCH GAP — reviewer to confirm the deemed rates against Circular 20/2026, whose text was not read]`)_
- **FCT scope and credit** — FCT targets payments originating in Vietnam, with an exception for the pure supply of goods or services consumed **outside** Vietnam, so where the contract is performed and consumed decides whether FCT arises at all. The withheld VAT component can generally be claimed as an input credit subject to conditions — note that `vietnam-vat.md` states the VAT portion is generally **not** creditable, so the two guides do not agree and the position should be settled with a Vietnamese adviser before either is relied on. Treaty relief reduces the CIT component: France gives 0% on interest, most partners 10%, and royalty rates run 5%–20% by partner  _(Circular 103/2014/TT-BTC on the tax obligations of foreign organisations and individuals doing business in or earning income from Vietnam, arts 1, 4, 12 and 13 — https://caselaw.vn/van-ban-phap-luat/264707-thong-tu-103-2014-tt-btc-huong-dan-thuc-hien-nghia-vu-thue-ap-dung-doi-voi-to-chuc-ca-nhan-nuoc-ngoai-kinh-doanh-tai-viet-nam-hoac-co-thu-nhap-phat-sinh-tai-viet-nam-do-bo-truong-bo-tai-chinh-ban-hanh; its corporate income tax provisions were replaced from 12 March 2026 by Circular 20/2026/TT-BTC art. 7 `[RESEARCH GAP — reviewer to confirm the deemed rates against Circular 20/2026, whose text was not read]`; `vietnam-vat.md`)_
- **Foreign Contractor Tax on interest paid abroad (CIT portion)** — 5% (may be reduced by an applicable double tax agreement)  _(Circular 103/2014/TT-BTC on the tax obligations of foreign organisations and individuals doing business in or earning income from Vietnam, art. 13.2 — https://caselaw.vn/van-ban-phap-luat/264707-thong-tu-103-2014-tt-btc-huong-dan-thuc-hien-nghia-vu-thue-ap-dung-doi-voi-to-chuc-ca-nhan-nuoc-ngoai-kinh-doanh-tai-viet-nam-hoac-co-thu-nhap-phat-sinh-tai-viet-nam-do-bo-truong-bo-tai-chinh-ban-hanh; its corporate income tax provisions were replaced from 12 March 2026 by Circular 20/2026/TT-BTC art. 7 `[RESEARCH GAP — reviewer to confirm the deemed rates against Circular 20/2026, whose text was not read]`)_
- **Foreign Contractor Tax on royalties paid abroad (CIT portion)** — 10% (may be reduced by an applicable double tax agreement)  _(Circular 103/2014/TT-BTC on the tax obligations of foreign organisations and individuals doing business in or earning income from Vietnam, art. 13.2 — https://caselaw.vn/van-ban-phap-luat/264707-thong-tu-103-2014-tt-btc-huong-dan-thuc-hien-nghia-vu-thue-ap-dung-doi-voi-to-chuc-ca-nhan-nuoc-ngoai-kinh-doanh-tai-viet-nam-hoac-co-thu-nhap-phat-sinh-tai-viet-nam-do-bo-truong-bo-tai-chinh-ban-hanh; its corporate income tax provisions were replaced from 12 March 2026 by Circular 20/2026/TT-BTC art. 7 `[RESEARCH GAP — reviewer to confirm the deemed rates against Circular 20/2026, whose text was not read]`)_
- **Foreign Contractor Tax on services (deemed CIT portion)** — **5%–10%**, not a flat 5%. This line read 5% alone; services and e-commerce sit in a 5%–10% band on the CIT side and 5%–10% on the VAT side, so the activity has to be identified before a rate is quoted  _(Circular 103/2014/TT-BTC on the tax obligations of foreign organisations and individuals doing business in or earning income from Vietnam, art. 13.2 — https://caselaw.vn/van-ban-phap-luat/264707-thong-tu-103-2014-tt-btc-huong-dan-thuc-hien-nghia-vu-thue-ap-dung-doi-voi-to-chuc-ca-nhan-nuoc-ngoai-kinh-doanh-tai-viet-nam-hoac-co-thu-nhap-phat-sinh-tai-viet-nam-do-bo-truong-bo-tai-chinh-ban-hanh; its corporate income tax provisions were replaced from 12 March 2026 by Circular 20/2026/TT-BTC art. 7 `[RESEARCH GAP — reviewer to confirm the deemed rates against Circular 20/2026, whose text was not read]`)_
- **FCT remittance deadline** — Within 10 days of each payment to the foreign contractor (declaration-on-each-payment method)  _(Law on Tax Administration No. 38/2019/QH14, art. 44.3 — https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/thue-phi-le-phi/Law-38-2019-QH14-Tax-administration-425672.aspx; Circular 103/2014/TT-BTC on the tax obligations of foreign organisations and individuals doing business in or earning income from Vietnam, art. 4 — https://caselaw.vn/van-ban-phap-luat/264707-thong-tu-103-2014-tt-btc-huong-dan-thuc-hien-nghia-vu-thue-ap-dung-doi-voi-to-chuc-ca-nhan-nuoc-ngoai-kinh-doanh-tai-viet-nam-hoac-co-thu-nhap-phat-sinh-tai-viet-nam-do-bo-truong-bo-tai-chinh-ban-hanh)_
- **Quarterly provisional CIT payment deadline** — By the 30th day of the first month of the following quarter (no quarterly return required)  _(Law on Tax Administration No. 38/2019/QH14, art. 55.1 — https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/thue-phi-le-phi/Law-38-2019-QH14-Tax-administration-425672.aspx)_
- **Annual CIT finalisation return and final payment deadline** — Last day of the 3rd month after the calendar or fiscal year-end; the balance is paid by the same date  _(Law on Tax Administration No. 38/2019/QH14, arts 44.2(a) and 55.1 — https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/thue-phi-le-phi/Law-38-2019-QH14-Tax-administration-425672.aspx)_

Vietnam taxes cross-border payments to foreign parties mainly through the Foreign Contractor Tax (FCT) regime, which bundles VAT and CIT/PIT. Dividends paid to corporate shareholders are generally not subject to further withholding; payments to individuals are. Treaty relief may reduce these rates.
