# Kyrgyzstan tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the taxpayer, where income arose, residence facts and period. Check the applicable treaty and foreign-tax relief.

## Territorial reach

Personal income tax reaches Kyrgyz and foreign income of Kyrgyz citizens and resident noncitizens who have a Kyrgyz residence permit or kairylman status. Other resident noncitizens and ordinary nonresident noncitizens fall within the Kyrgyz-source rules. Check the statutory exception for a nonresident employee of a foreign-registered branch or representative office of a domestic organisation. Kyrgyz-source income can be taxable when paid abroad.

For an in-scope domestic organisation or individual entrepreneur, profit tax generally covers Kyrgyz and foreign income. Apply art 207(2) to a business operating outside Kyrgyzstan before using that rule. Foreign organisations with a Kyrgyz permanent establishment and in-scope nonresident individual entrepreneurs are covered on Kyrgyz-source income. For a foreign organisation without a permanent establishment, check whether the Kyrgyz-source payment gives the tax agent a withholding obligation. [Tax Code, arts 185–186, 207–209 and 249](https://cbd.minjust.gov.kg/112340/edition/57656/ru)

## Residence

An individual generally qualifies as resident with at least 183 days during a consecutive 12-month period ending in the tax period, or whilst serving the Kyrgyz state abroad. The count includes time abroad for medical treatment, business travel and study. Check the exclusions for diplomatic and consular status, qualifying international organisation employment and family members.

A domestic organisation is a legal person created under Kyrgyz civil law. Classify a foreign organisation and any registered branch or representative office separately. Use the permanent establishment rules for foreign operations and check the applicable treaty. [Tax Code, arts 24–28](https://cbd.minjust.gov.kg/112340/edition/57656/ru)

## Taxpayer types

An individual entrepreneur is a natural person who carries on business without forming an organisation. Ordinary business income falls within the profit-tax regime unless a special regime applies. A registered full or limited partnership is a legal person. For a contractual simple partnership, check which tax regimes the participants may use and identify the participant appointed to keep separate accounts for the joint activity. [Tax Code, arts 25–26, 183¹, 185 and 207](https://cbd.minjust.gov.kg/112340/edition/57656/ru) · [Law on Business Partnerships and Companies, arts 1–2](https://cbd.minjust.gov.kg/4-632/edition/1231390/ru)

## Currency

Tax obligations are ordinarily settled in the Kyrgyz som (KGS). A foreign organisation with no characteristics of a Kyrgyz permanent establishment may settle in a foreign currency under the Cabinet's rules. [Tax Code, art 67](https://cbd.minjust.gov.kg/112340/edition/57656/ru)

## Assessment periods

Ordinary personal income tax and profit tax use the calendar year. Check the separate filing and payment schedules for withholding, instalments and special regimes. [Tax Code, arts 188 and 211](https://cbd.minjust.gov.kg/112340/edition/57656/ru)

## Responsible authorities

The State Tax Service and its territorial bodies administer national taxes. Check the competent office for registration, returns and withholding. Refer import and customs questions to the competent customs authority. [Tax Code, arts 55–59](https://cbd.minjust.gov.kg/112340/edition/57656/ru) · [State Tax Service](https://sti.gov.kg/)

## Fictional intake

A noncitizen spends enough time in Kyrgyzstan to meet the day count and receives consulting income from abroad. Establish whether the consultant is an individual entrepreneur, identify the income source and ask about a Kyrgyz residence permit or kairylman status before deciding which rules apply.
