# Saint-Barthélemy Tax System Orientation

Saint-Barthélemy sets territorial taxes under its own Code des contributions. Fiscal domicile can still bring a taxpayer within the French national regime. Identify both the taxpayer's domicile and the source, activity or transaction before applying territorial rules. [Organic statute, art LO6214-4][CGCT] · [IEDOM, 2025 annual report, pp 36–38][REPORT]

## Residence and source

Under article LO6214-4, individuals can be treated as fiscally domiciled in Saint-Barthélemy only after residing there for at least five years. Legal entities qualify after locating their effective management there for at least five years, or once effective management is there and the entity is controlled directly or indirectly by individuals who meet the five-year residence requirement. The statute treats individuals and entities that fail those tests as fiscally domiciled in mainland France. French-department taxes can apply alongside territorial taxes on Saint-Barthélemy-source income or wealth. [Organic statute, art LO6214-4 I][CGCT]

The organic statute provides for a capped credit on income from the other territory before a double taxation convention enters into force. The French 2026 foreign-income return notes apply that provision to tax actually paid in Saint-Barthélemy, limited to the corresponding French tax. Check the income category and the taxpayer's facts before claiming relief. [Organic statute, art LO6214-4 I bis][CGCT] · [DGFiP, 2026 form 2047 notes][CREDIT]

## Taxpayer type

The territorial system has no general individual income tax or ordinary corporate profit tax. The Collectivité's non-taxation attestation describes the absence of a local personal income tax. The CFAE is an annual business contribution with its own scope rules for local seats and establishments. Companies, individuals and property owners can also encounter transaction-specific territorial taxes. French-department taxation and social contribution rules require their own checks. [Collectivité, non-taxation attestation][ATTEST] · [Senate report on the organic-law agreements][SENATE] · [IEDOM, 2025 annual report, pp 36–38][REPORT] · [CFAE legislative reference](bl-cfae-legislative-reference.md)

## Currency and assessment periods

The official currency is the euro (EUR). Apply the timetable of the relevant obligation. Deliberation 2024-075 CT states that CFAE becomes due on 1 January of the tax year and is payable by 31 March. Check the current period, exemptions and required filings against the legislative reference. For a regular release into free circulation, the droit de quai debt arises when the declaration is accepted. Monthly deferred payment is available subject to authorisation. [IEDOM, currency][CURRENCY] · [CFAE legislative reference](bl-cfae-legislative-reference.md) · [Code des contributions, art 13 bis][CODE] · [Collectivité, deferred payment form][DEFERRAL]

## Headline taxes and authorities

The September 2024 code annex states a 5% ordinary droit de quai on the customs value of imported goods. It specifies 8% for motor vehicles and 30% for motor vehicles designed and built to carry goods with an authorised gross weight over 14 tonnes. The 2025 IEDOM report, published in October 2026, reports the same rates. Check the current tariff, classification, valuation and any exemption with the Collectivité's Service des droits de quai. Property registration and tourist accommodation are subject to separate territorial rules. Contact the Collectivité for local obligations and use the French tax administration for any national return. [Code des contributions, art 13][CODE] · [IEDOM, 2025 annual report, pp 37–38][REPORT] · [Collectivité, Service des droits de quai][DEFERRAL] · [DGFiP, 2026 form 2047 notes][CREDIT]
