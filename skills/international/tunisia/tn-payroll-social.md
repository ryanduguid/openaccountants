# Tunisia Payroll & Social Contributions

## Social security (CNSS) and payroll — 2025

Employers and employees contribute to the national social-security fund (CNSS) on gross salary, with employers carrying the larger share plus a sector-based work-accident levy. A new unemployment-fund contribution of 0.5% from each side applies from 1 January 2025.

- **Employer social-security contribution** — 17.07% of gross salary (Reduced to 16.57% for industrial wholly-exporting companies; includes the 0.5% unemployment-fund share) `[RESEARCH GAP — the 16.57% general-regime rate is as reported; the CNSS rate schedule was not read]`  _(Loi n° 2024-48 du 9 décembre 2024 portant loi de finances pour l'année 2025 (JORT n° 149, Arabic text), Ministry of Finance, art. 17 (0.5% on the employer, on payroll declared to the CNSS) — https://www.finances.gov.tn/sites/default/files/2024-12/LF2025.pdf)_
- **Employee social-security contribution** — 9.68% of gross salary (Includes the 0.5% unemployment-fund share) `[RESEARCH GAP — the 9.18% general-regime rate is as reported; the CNSS rate schedule was not read]`  _(Loi n° 2024-48 du 9 décembre 2024 portant loi de finances pour l'année 2025 (JORT n° 149, Arabic text), Ministry of Finance, art. 17 (0.5% on the employee) — https://www.finances.gov.tn/sites/default/files/2024-12/LF2025.pdf)_
- **Work-accident / occupational-disease contribution** — 0.4% to 4% of gross salary, depending on sector (employer-borne) `[RESEARCH GAP — rates as reported; the CNSS schedule was not read]`  _(CNSS contribution rules, as reported)_
- **Unemployment Insurance Fund contribution** — 0.5% from employer and 0.5% from employee (within the totals above), levied on the payroll declared to the CNSS to finance the fund for loss of employment for economic reasons  _(Loi n° 2024-48 du 9 décembre 2024 portant loi de finances pour l'année 2025 (JORT n° 149, Arabic text), Ministry of Finance, art. 17 — https://www.finances.gov.tn/sites/default/files/2024-12/LF2025.pdf)_
- **Contribution wage base** — Gross salary including most benefits in cash and in kind; no general statutory ceiling (approx — confirm any sector caps)  _(CNSS contribution rules)_
- **Payroll income-tax withholding (retenue à la source)** — The employer withholds IRPP at source on salaries each month under the progressive scale, after the social security contribution and the 10% professional expense deduction capped at TND 2,000  _(Ministère des Finances, Aperçu général sur la fiscalité (système fiscal tunisien) — https://www.finances.gov.tn/fr/apercu-general-sur-la-fiscalite)_
- **CNSS remittance frequency** — Quarterly declaration and payment of social-security contributions (approx — confirm; some employers report monthly)  _(CNSS contribution rules)_
- **Withheld IRPP remittance** — Withheld salary tax remitted to the DGI monthly with the employer's declaration: within the first 15 days of the following month for natural persons and the first 28 days for legal persons  _(Code de l'IRPP et de l'IS, 2026 edition (Direction Générale des Impôts, jibaya.tn), art. 52 IV — https://jibaya.tn/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Code_IRPP_IS_2026_fr.pdf)_
