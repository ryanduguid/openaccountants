# Peru Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax rates and base

- **General taxation framework** — Resident companies are taxed on worldwide income at a standard 29.5% rate; non-resident companies are taxed on Peruvian-source income. Special regimes exist for small and micro enterprises and for the agriculture sector. Confirm regime eligibility with a Peruvian accountant.
- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 29.5 percent of net income (art. 55)  _(TUO de la Ley del Impuesto a la Renta (SUNAT consolidated text, amendments to August 2025), art. 55 — https://www.sunat.gob.pe/legislacion/renta/ley/capvii.pdf ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/peru/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Tax base** — Net income (worldwide for residents; Peruvian-source for non-residents) after deductible expenses  _(TUO de la Ley del Impuesto a la Renta (SUNAT consolidated text, amendments to August 2025), arts. 6 and 7 — https://www.sunat.gob.pe/legislacion/renta/ley/capii.pdf ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/peru/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **MYPE Tributario (small enterprise) graduated rate** — 10% on net income up to 15 UIT and 29.5% on the excess, for domiciled taxpayers whose net income in the previous year did not exceed 1,700 UIT  _(Decreto Legislativo 1269 (Régimen MYPE Tributario), SUNAT text, Arts 1 and 5 — https://www.sunat.gob.pe/legislacion/mypeIR/dl1269.pdf)_
- **Special income regime (RER)** — 1.5% of monthly net income from third-category activities, for taxpayers whose annual net income stays within S/ 525,000 and whose fixed assets (other than premises and vehicles) do not exceed S/ 126,000  _(TUO de la Ley del Impuesto a la Renta (SUNAT consolidated text), Capítulo XV, Arts 117 to 120 — https://www.sunat.gob.pe/legislacion/renta/ley/capxv.pdf)_
- **Agribusiness sector preferential rate** — 15% for 2021 to 2030 where net income does not exceed 1,700 UIT; larger agribusinesses pay 15% in 2021 to 2022, 20% in 2023 to 2024, 25% in 2025 to 2027 and the general rate from 2028  _(Ley 31110 (régimen laboral agrario y de incentivos), gob.pe text, Art. 10 — https://cdn.www.gob.pe/uploads/document/file/1535274/Ley%2031110.pdf)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends** — 5 percent (On distributions to non-domiciled companies and individuals and to resident individuals, withheld by the paying company under art. 73-A; no tax on dividends between resident companies)  _(TUO de la Ley del Impuesto a la Renta (SUNAT consolidated text, amendments to August 2025), arts. 54(a) and 56(e) — https://www.sunat.gob.pe/legislacion/renta/ley/capvii.pdf ; art. 73-A — https://www.sunat.gob.pe/legislacion/renta/ley/capx.pdf ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/peru/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on interest to non-residents (general)** — 30 percent (Interest on external loans that fail the art. 56(a) conditions, the excess over the permitted rate, and interest paid to related parties)  _(TUO de la Ley del Impuesto a la Renta (SUNAT consolidated text, amendments to August 2025), art. 56(j) — https://www.sunat.gob.pe/legislacion/renta/ley/capvii.pdf ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/peru/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on qualifying foreign loan interest** — 4.99 percent (Cash loans must be shown to have entered Peru, the parties must be unrelated, and the annual rate must not exceed the prevailing preferential rate of the lender's market plus 3 points)  _(TUO de la Ley del Impuesto a la Renta (SUNAT consolidated text, amendments to August 2025), art. 56(a) — https://www.sunat.gob.pe/legislacion/renta/ley/capvii.pdf ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/peru/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on royalties to non-residents** — 30 percent  _(TUO de la Ley del Impuesto a la Renta (SUNAT consolidated text, amendments to August 2025), art. 56(d) — https://www.sunat.gob.pe/legislacion/renta/ley/capvii.pdf ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/peru/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on technical assistance to non-residents** — 15 percent (The local user must obtain and file with SUNAT an audit firm's report certifying that the technical assistance was provided; art. 56(f) sets the detailed conditions)  _(TUO de la Ley del Impuesto a la Renta (SUNAT consolidated text, amendments to August 2025), art. 56(f) — https://www.sunat.gob.pe/legislacion/renta/ley/capvii.pdf ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/peru/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Monthly income tax advance (pago a cuenta)** — Monthly prepayments equal to the greater of the month's net income multiplied by the prior year's coefficient (prior-year tax divided by prior-year net income) and 1.5% of the month's net income (art. 85)  _(TUO de la Ley del Impuesto a la Renta (SUNAT consolidated text, amendments to August 2025), art. 85 — https://www.sunat.gob.pe/legislacion/renta/ley/capxi.pdf ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/peru/corporate/tax-administration)_
- **Annual corporate return filing/payment deadline (TY2025)** — 26 March to 10 April 2026 by last RUC digit (13 April for Buenos Contribuyentes) for companies outside Ley 31940; 27 May to 9 June 2026 by last RUC digit (10 June for Buenos Contribuyentes) for MYPEs and individuals covered by Ley 31940  _(Resolución de Superintendencia N.° 000386-2025/SUNAT, Arts 5 and 6 — https://www.sunat.gob.pe/legislacion/superin/2025/000386-2025.pdf)_
