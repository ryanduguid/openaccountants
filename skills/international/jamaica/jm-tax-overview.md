# Jamaica Tax Overview

## Jamaica tax system at a glance

Jamaica taxes income on a residence basis and runs a value-added tax called General Consumption Tax (GCT). Tax Administration Jamaica (TAJ) administers most direct and indirect taxes. Figures below are for the 2025 year of assessment and are an unverified draft pending review by a licensed Jamaican accountant.

- **Principal tax authority** — Tax Administration Jamaica (TAJ)  _([Revenue Administration Act](https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/))_
- **Currency** — Jamaican dollar (JMD / J$)  _(Bank of Jamaica Act)_
- **Year of assessment / tax year** — Calendar year (1 January to 31 December); income tax is calculated on a calendar year basis and returns fall due on 15 March of the following year  _(Tax Administration Jamaica, Technical Advisory 042025/01/IT-TA, Increase in Income Tax Threshold effective April 1, 2025 to December 31, 2028 — https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/documents/10194/52231399/Technical_Advisory_Threshold+_042025.pdf/c4adbf81-8ddd-9a1b-9e8b-8bdc431162df ; Tax Administration Jamaica, Income Tax forms page (IT02 and IT07 filing dates) — https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/web/guest/income-tax1)_
- **Basis of taxation** — Residents are taxed on worldwide income; a non-resident is taxed on income earned in Jamaica, at 25% unless a double taxation agreement sets a lower rate, with no tax-free threshold  _(Tax Administration Jamaica, Non-residents and Income Tax — https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/documents/10181/123967/non-residents-and-income-tax-final-document.pdf/7bfdd5c6-eb64-926e-e5c3-ea296e5f4831 ; residents: Income Tax Act (Jamaica), as reported; the Act's consolidated text is not in the Ministry of Justice statute library, s 5)_
- **Headline personal income tax rates** — 0% up to the threshold; 25% on statutory income above the threshold up to JMD 6,000,000; 30% on statutory income above JMD 6,000,000  _(Tax Administration Jamaica, Individual Income Tax Rates, Threshold and Exemptions 2013-2028 (updated April 2025) — https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/documents/10194/53547865/Income+Tax+Rates+Thresholds+and+Exemption+2013+-2028+Updated+April+2025.pdf/3c1961e5-3cad-2ef4-789a-91c74a1e904c ; Tax Administration Jamaica, Payroll Taxes and Statutory Contributions (Employee / Employer), April 2025 edition — https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/documents/10194/53547865/Payroll+Taxes+and+Statutory+Contributions+%28Employee+Employer%29.pdf/bef9095b-fb2d-db4a-5de4-b19405bd8631)_
- **Personal income tax-free threshold (2025)** — JMD 1,799,376 per annum (JMD 149,948 monthly) from 1 April 2025, giving an effective JMD 1,774,554 for the 2025 year of assessment; the phased increases continue with JMD 1,902,360 from 1 April 2026 (effective JMD 1,876,614 for 2026) and JMD 2,003,496 from 1 April 2027  _(Tax Administration Jamaica, Technical Advisory 042025/01/IT-TA, Increase in Income Tax Threshold effective April 1, 2025 to December 31, 2028 — https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/documents/10194/52231399/Technical_Advisory_Threshold+_042025.pdf/c4adbf81-8ddd-9a1b-9e8b-8bdc431162df ; Jamaica Information Service, Increase in Income Tax Threshold Now in Effect (14 April 2026) — https://jis.gov.jm/increase-in-income-tax-threshold-now-in-effect/)_
- **Headline corporate income tax rates** — 25% for unregulated companies (from 1 January 2013); 33 1/3% for regulated companies, meaning those regulated by the Financial Services Commission, Bank of Jamaica, Office of Utilities Regulation or Ministry of Finance; 30% for building societies  _(Jamaica Information Service, New Threshold and Corporate Income Tax Rate January 1 (2013) — https://jis.gov.jm/new-threshold-and-corporate-income-tax-rate-january-1/ ; Tax Administration Jamaica, Income Tax FAQ — https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/income-tax-faq ; Tax Administration Jamaica, IT02 Notes and Instructions — https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/documents/10181/106853/notes_and_instructions.pdf/0e07453f-7925-4475-ab0e-c5457a8b3214)_
- **Value-added tax (GCT)** — Yes — General Consumption Tax (GCT), standard rate 15% since 1 April 2020 (16.5% before), 25% on telephone services and instruments; registration threshold JMD 15,000,000 of annual supplies from 1 April 2025  _(Tax Administration Jamaica, Quick Guide to GCT (amended March 2021) — https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/documents/10194/21679950/GCT_Quick_Guide_to_GCT_%28Amended%29_032021.pdf/6a23364a-3cbe-ddb4-8de4-e9fb691af3e8 ; Tax Administration Jamaica, General Consumption Tax page (threshold effective April 1, 2025) — https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/general-consumption-tax1)_
- **Annual income tax return deadline (individuals and companies)** — 15 March of the year following the year of assessment (Forms IT01 and IT02)  _(Tax Administration Jamaica, Income Tax forms page (IT02 and IT07 filing dates) — https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/web/guest/income-tax1 ; Tax Administration Jamaica, Income Tax for Individuals and Businesses (April 2019) — https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/documents/10181/106853/Income+Tax+for+Individuals++Businesses+042019.pdf/f3d77ed8-eba4-0698-180f-8dd0293a640f)_
- **Estimated/quarterly tax payment dates** — The estimated return (Form IT07) is due 15 March and the instalments on 15 March, 15 June, 15 September and 15 December  _(Tax Administration Jamaica, Income Tax for Individuals and Businesses (April 2019) — https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/documents/10181/106853/Income+Tax+for+Individuals++Businesses+042019.pdf/f3d77ed8-eba4-0698-180f-8dd0293a640f ; Tax Administration Jamaica, Income Tax forms page (IT02 and IT07 filing dates) — https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/web/guest/income-tax1)_
