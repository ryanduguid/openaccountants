# Jamaica Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax rates and base

- **Corporate tax classification and residence basis** — Jamaica applies different corporate rates depending on whether a company is 'regulated' (e.g. financial institutions) or 'unregulated'. Companies are taxed on worldwide income if resident.  _(Jamaica Information Service, New Threshold and Corporate Income Tax Rate January 1 (2013) — https://jis.gov.jm/new-threshold-and-corporate-income-tax-rate-january-1/ ; Tax Administration Jamaica, IT02 Notes and Instructions — https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/documents/10181/106853/notes_and_instructions.pdf/0e07453f-7925-4475-ab0e-c5457a8b3214)_
- **Unregulated company rate** — 25% from 1 January 2013 (reduced from 33 1/3%)  _(Jamaica Information Service, New Threshold and Corporate Income Tax Rate January 1 (2013) — https://jis.gov.jm/new-threshold-and-corporate-income-tax-rate-january-1/)_
- **Regulated company rate** — 33 1/3% for companies regulated by the Financial Services Commission, Bank of Jamaica, Office of Utilities Regulation or Ministry of Finance  _(Jamaica Information Service, New Threshold and Corporate Income Tax Rate January 1 (2013) — https://jis.gov.jm/new-threshold-and-corporate-income-tax-rate-january-1/ ; Tax Administration Jamaica, IT02 Notes and Instructions — https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/documents/10181/106853/notes_and_instructions.pdf/0e07453f-7925-4475-ab0e-c5457a8b3214)_
- **Building society rate** — 30%  _(Tax Administration Jamaica, Income Tax FAQ — https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/income-tax-faq)_
- **Life assurance company rate** — 25% (life assurance companies file the IT04 return)  _(Income Tax Act (Jamaica), as reported; the Act's consolidated text is not in the Ministry of Justice statute library, s 30 (rates for companies); Tax Administration Jamaica, IT02 and IT04 Notes and Instructions — https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/documents/10181/106853/notes_and_instructions.pdf/0e07453f-7925-4475-ab0e-c5457a8b3214)_
- **Renewable energy IPP rate** — 25% for independent power producers with 75%+ renewable generation `[RESEARCH GAP — reviewer to confirm the renewable-generation condition in the Income Tax Act or its 2013 amendment]`  _(Income Tax Act (Jamaica), as reported; the Act's consolidated text is not in the Ministry of Justice statute library, s 30 (rates for companies))_
- **Tax base** — Worldwide income for tax-resident companies; Jamaica-source income for non-residents  _(Income Tax Act (Jamaica), as reported; the Act's consolidated text is not in the Ministry of Justice statute library, s 5 (charge to tax); Tax Administration Jamaica, Non-residents and Income Tax (non-residents are taxed on income earned in Jamaica) — https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/documents/10181/123967/non-residents-and-income-tax-final-document.pdf/7bfdd5c6-eb64-926e-e5c3-ea296e5f4831)_
- **WHT on dividends to non-residents** — 15% under the single dividend rate announced in the 2025/26 Budget, reduced from 25% for non-resident individuals and 33 1/3% for non-resident companies; EY reports it effective 1 April 2025  _(Jamaica Information Service, Single Tax Rate on Dividends to Attract Investors (14 March 2025) — https://jis.gov.jm/single-tax-rate-on-dividends-to-attract-investors/ ; effective date: EY Tax Alert 2025-0742)_
- **WHT on dividends to residents** — 15%, withheld at source under s 38 of the Income Tax Act since 1 April 2013; not withheld on distributions to a resident company holding more than 25% of the voting rights  _(Jamaica Information Service, House Passes Income Tax (Amendment) Act (31 July 2013) — https://jis.gov.jm/house-passes-income-tax-amendment-act/)_
- **WHT on interest to non-residents** — 25% where no double taxation agreement applies, which is the rate Tax Administration Jamaica states for payments to non-residents; practitioners report 33 1/3% for non-resident companies `[RESEARCH GAP — reviewer to confirm the company rate in the Income Tax Act]`. This line previously read 15%, a treaty-level rate  _(Tax Administration Jamaica, Non-residents and Income Tax — https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/documents/10181/123967/non-residents-and-income-tax-final-document.pdf/7bfdd5c6-eb64-926e-e5c3-ea296e5f4831)_
- **WHT on royalties to non-residents** — 33 1/3% to non-resident companies and 25% to non-resident individuals (non-treaty), as reported; Tax Administration Jamaica states 25% where no double taxation agreement applies and treaty rates of 10% to 12.5% `[RESEARCH GAP — reviewer to confirm the company rate in the Income Tax Act]`  _(Tax Administration Jamaica, Non-residents and Income Tax — https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/documents/10181/123967/non-residents-and-income-tax-final-document.pdf/7bfdd5c6-eb64-926e-e5c3-ea296e5f4831)_
- **WHT on management fees to non-residents** — 33 1/3% to non-resident companies and 25% to non-resident individuals (non-treaty), as reported; Tax Administration Jamaica states 25% where no double taxation agreement applies and treaty rates of 10% to 12.5% `[RESEARCH GAP — reviewer to confirm the company rate in the Income Tax Act]`  _(Tax Administration Jamaica, Non-residents and Income Tax — https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/documents/10181/123967/non-residents-and-income-tax-final-document.pdf/7bfdd5c6-eb64-926e-e5c3-ea296e5f4831)_
- **WHT remittance deadline** — Within 14 days after the end of the calendar month in which the payment is made (Income Tax Act s 41(1)); unremitted deductions are increased at up to 50% per annum under s 41(2)  _(Tax Administration Jamaica, Employer's Guide (December 2019), PAYE Audits, Interest and Penalties — https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/documents/10194/14559577/Employers_guide_13122019.pdf/9d09b82e-e215-6aaa-72c8-7b8a3dd7f061)_
- **Corporate income tax return deadline** — Annual return (Form IT02) due 15 March of the following year, filed online  _(Tax Administration Jamaica, Income Tax forms page (IT02 and IT07 filing dates) — https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/web/guest/income-tax1)_
- **Corporate estimated tax** — Declaration of estimated income (Form IT07) due 15 March, with instalments due 15 March, 15 June, 15 September and 15 December  _(Tax Administration Jamaica, Income Tax forms page (IT02 and IT07 filing dates) — https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/web/guest/income-tax1 ; Tax Administration Jamaica, Income Tax for Individuals and Businesses (April 2019) — https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/documents/10181/106853/Income+Tax+for+Individuals++Businesses+042019.pdf/f3d77ed8-eba4-0698-180f-8dd0293a640f)_
- **Asset tax (financial institutions)** — 0.25% on taxable assets of deposit-taking institutions, securities dealers and insurers `[RESEARCH GAP — reviewer to confirm the current rate: a cut to 0.125% announced for 2021 was reported as not implemented]`  _(Assets Tax (Specified Bodies) Act, Ministry of Justice statute library — https://laws.moj.gov.jm/library/statute/the-assets-tax-specified-bodies-act)_
