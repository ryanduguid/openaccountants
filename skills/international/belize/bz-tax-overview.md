# Belize Tax Overview

Belize Tax Service (BTS) administers employee income tax, business tax and general sales tax (GST). The amounts are in Belize dollars (BZD) unless indicated.

## Income periods and source

The ordinary income-tax basis year is the calendar year. The employee charge applies to income accruing in or derived from Belize even if received elsewhere. For the employee relief in section 16, residence includes domicile or presence in Belize for more than 182 days in aggregate in the basis year. Confirm special-period and cross-border cases separately. [Income and Business Tax Act, Cap. 55, ss 2 and 16(6)](https://agm.gov.bz/uploads/laws/63976e6032352_Cap_55_Income_and_Business_Tax_Act.pdf) · [Income and Business Tax (Amendment) Act No. 40 of 2024, s 3](https://media.bts.gov.bz/uploads/Act_No_40_of_2024_Income_and_Business_Tax_Amendment_Act_2024_b6bf496b66.pdf)

## Employee income tax

The ordinary employee rate is 25% of chargeable income. Under the amendment in force from 1 January 2025, resident employees with total annual income below BZD 29,000 are exempt. Those above BZD 29,000 receive a BZD 20,000 basic deduction. Above BZD 29,000 through BZD 32,000, a credit starts at BZD 2,250 less 75% of total income above BZD 29,000. The Act uses strict inequalities at BZD 29,000. For a resident employee whose only income is employment income, the current BTS203 return calculates zero tax at exactly BZD 29,000. [Act No. 40 of 2024, ss 3, 4 and 34](https://media.bts.gov.bz/uploads/Act_No_40_of_2024_Income_and_Business_Tax_Amendment_Act_2024_b6bf496b66.pdf) · [BTS203 for the 2025 basis year onward, cells Y39, Y53 and Y57](https://media.bts.gov.bz/uploads/BTS_203_Income_Tax_Return_For_Employees_Fillable_Effective2025_c69fde410f.xlsx)

## Business tax

Ordinary taxable business receipts are taxed on gross receipts at activity-specific rates. Examples include 1.75% for other trade or business and 6% for a profession, vocation or occupation. The 3% real-property receipts category excludes real estate business. The amended income-tax provision specifies separate chargeable-income rates of 40% for petroleum operations, payable in US dollars, and 3% for approved Designated Processing Areas. [Cap. 55, Part X and Schedule IX](https://agm.gov.bz/uploads/laws/63976e6032352_Cap_55_Income_and_Business_Tax_Act.pdf) · [Act No. 40 of 2024, ss 3 and 6](https://media.bts.gov.bz/uploads/Act_No_40_of_2024_Income_and_Business_Tax_Amendment_Act_2024_b6bf496b66.pdf)

## GST

The standard rate is 12.5%. Ordinary registration considers whether taxable supplies reach or are expected to reach BZD 75,000 over the relevant 12 months. Short trading periods and specific activities have separate registration rules. Registered persons ordinarily file and account for GST within 15 days after the end of each calendar month. [General Sales Tax Act, Cap. 63, ss 2, 8–10, 22–24 and 30–31](https://agm.gov.bz/uploads/laws/63976e60334b6_Cap_63_General_Sales_Tax_Act.pdf) · [General Sales Tax (Amendment) Act No. 34 of 2024, s 4](https://media.bts.gov.bz/uploads/Act_No_34_of_2024_General_Sales_Tax_Amendment_Act_2024_5aa922a419.pdf)

## Filing

- An annual income tax return, where required, is ordinarily due on the last day of the third month after the basis year, normally 31 March. Check any authorised extension. [Cap. 55, s 31](https://agm.gov.bz/uploads/laws/63976e6032352_Cap_55_Income_and_Business_Tax_Act.pdf)
- Ordinary business tax returns and payment are generally due by the 15th following each month. Section 109 provides separate timing where receipts are solely rents, royalties, premiums or other revenue receipts from real property. [Cap. 55, s 109](https://agm.gov.bz/uploads/laws/63976e6032352_Cap_55_Income_and_Business_Tax_Act.pdf) · [BTS guides](https://bts.gov.bz/guides/)
