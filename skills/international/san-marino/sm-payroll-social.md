# San Marino Payroll & Social Contributions

> **The previous version carried the 2023 rates and called them current.** It was
> right that its own totals did not add up, and it was right to say so. The cause
> is now known: San Marino legislated a **seven-year ramp** in 2022, so a single
> "current" contribution rate does not exist. The employee first-pillar rate of
> 5.90% and the employer's 16.60% are the **2023** column of a table that runs to
> 2029. Both had already moved twice by 2025.

## 1. The statutory table — Legge 29 novembre 2022 n.157, art. 21(4)

Art. 21 raises the rates by 0.50% a year and then prints the resulting
progression. These are the figures as the statute prints them.

**Primo pilastro — the mandatory Fondo Pensioni**

| | 2023 | 2024 | **2025** | **2026** | 2027 | 2028 | 2029 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| a carico del dipendente (employee) | 5.90% | 6.40% | **6.90%** | **6.90%** | 6.90% | 6.90% | 6.90% |
| a carico del datore (employer) | 16.60% | 17.10% | **17.60%** | **17.60%** | 17.60% | 17.60% | 17.60% |
| **Total** | 22.50% | 23.50% | **24.50%** | **24.50%** | 24.50% | 24.50% | 24.50% |

**FONDISS — the mandatory second pillar** (established by Legge 6 dicembre 2011 n.191)

| | 2023 | 2024 | **2025** | **2026** | 2027 | 2028 | 2029 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| a carico del dipendente (employee) | 2.00% | 2.00% | **2.00%** | **2.50%** | 3.00% | 3.50% | 4.00% |
| a carico del datore (employer) | 2.00% | 2.00% | **2.00%** | **2.50%** | 3.00% | 3.00% | 3.00% |
| **Total** | 4.00% | 4.00% | **4.00%** | **5.00%** | 6.00% | 6.50% | 7.00% |

_(Legge 29 novembre 2022 n.157, art. 21(4) — https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/streaming-video-consiglio/documento17136203.html)_

- **How the ramp is written** — The first pillar rises 0.50% a year from 1 January
  2023 to a cumulative **+1.5%** on each side, which is why it stops moving after
  2025. FONDISS rises 0.50% a year from 1 January **2026**, to a cumulative +2%
  for the employee and **+1.0%** for the employer — so the two sides stop being
  equal from 2028  _(Legge 157/2022, art. 21(1) and 21(3) — https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/streaming-video-consiglio/documento17136203.html)_
- **Two employer branches were cut to help pay for it** — From 1 January 2023 the
  employer's **Fondo malattie** contribution falls by **0.40%**, and from 1
  January 2024 the **Fondo Assegni Familiari** contribution falls by **0.60%**.
  Any figure for those two branches carried from before 2023 is too high  _(Legge 157/2022, art. 21(2) — https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/streaming-video-consiglio/documento17136203.html)_

## 2. Why the previous version's totals could not be made to add up

The old block stated an employee total of "approximately 8.3%" alongside
components of 5.9 + 2.0 + 0.5. The arithmetic problem was real but the diagnosis
was not available without the statute:

- **At 2025 rates the two pillars alone are 6.90 + 2.00 = 8.90%** — already more
  than the 8.3% total the guide asserted, before any unemployment or family
  allowance contribution is added. For 2026 the two pillars are 6.90 + 2.50 =
  **9.40%**. A total below the sum of two of its own components is not a rounding
  question.
- **The employer's 16.6 + 2.0 was the 2023 column.** At 2025 it is 17.60 + 2.00 =
  **19.60%** before the other branches, and the same in 2026 at 17.60 + 2.50 =
  **20.10%**.
- **Do not reconcile these by adjusting a component.** The earlier note said so
  and it was right. What it could not know is that the components and the total
  were describing **different years**.

## 3. What is still not verified, and why

The other branches — unemployment, health and accident, the Social Services Fund,
and the employee's combined unemployment and family-allowance contribution — are
**not set by Legge 157/2022** and are not reproduced here as figures.

> **The ISS publishes the schedule, and it publishes it as an image.** The
> Istituto per la Sicurezza Sociale is the body that collects these contributions,
> and its Ufficio Contributi issues an annual circular titled *"adeguamento
> redditi minimi ed aliquote contributive"* — the 2026 one is on its site. Every
> such circular read for this pass is a **scanned PDF with no text layer** (2.2 MB
> for eight characters of extractable text), and no OCR was available, so the
> full branch-by-branch table could not be read. [RESEARCH GAP — reviewer to
> obtain the current ISS circular and replace the branch rates below. The two
> pillars above come from the statute and do not need re-checking; the branches
> do.]  _(Istituto per la Sicurezza Sociale, Ufficio Contributi, Ispettorato e Fondiss — https://www.iss.sm/on-line/home/servizi-generali-e-amministrativi/settore-previdenza/ufficio-contributi-ispettorato-e-fondiss.html)_

- **Employer — unemployment insurance** — 1.9% ((approx — confirm against the
  current ISS circular; not in Legge 157/2022 and not verified))  _([Istituto per la Sicurezza Sociale (ISS) contribution regulations](https://remotepeople.com/countries/san-marino/hire-employees/payroll-tax/))_
- **Employer — health and accident insurance** — 4.0% ((approx — confirm; and
  note art. 21(2) cut the Fondo malattie employer contribution by 0.40% from 1
  January 2023, so a pre-2023 figure is too high))  _([Istituto per la Sicurezza Sociale (ISS) contribution regulations](https://remotepeople.com/countries/san-marino/hire-employees/payroll-tax/))_
- **Employer — Social Services Fund** — 1.0% ((approx — confirm))  _([Istituto per la Sicurezza Sociale (ISS) contribution regulations](https://remotepeople.com/countries/san-marino/hire-employees/payroll-tax/))_
- **Employee — unemployment & family allowance** — 0.5% ((approx — confirm; and
  note art. 21(2) cut the Fondo Assegni Familiari contribution by 0.60% from 1
  January 2024))  _([Istituto per la Sicurezza Sociale (ISS) contribution regulations](https://remotepeople.com/countries/san-marino/hire-employees/payroll-tax/))_

**No employer or employee total is stated in this guide.** Stating one would mean
adding statutory figures to unverified ones and presenting the sum as a single
number, which is how the previous version's totals came to disagree with their
own components.

## 4. Self-employed, and the categories with their own rates

- **Lavoratori autonomi — first pillar** — The compulsory Fondo Pensioni
  Lavoratori Autonomi rate rises 0.50% a year from 1 January 2023 to a cumulative
  **+2.5%**. The printed progression gives commercianti, liberi professionisti and
  artigiani **22.50% (2023), 23.00% (2024), 23.50% (2025), 24.00% (2026), 24.50%
  (2027)**  _(Legge 157/2022, art. 22(1) and 22(4) — https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/streaming-video-consiglio/documento17136203.html)_
- **Lavoratori autonomi — FONDISS** — Rises 0.50% a year from 1 January **2028**,
  to a cumulative +1%  _(Legge 157/2022, art. 22(2) — https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/streaming-video-consiglio/documento17136203.html)_
- **Artisans lose family allowances and short sickness in 2029** — Their Fondo
  Assegni Familiari contribution falls 0.50% a year from 1 January 2023 through
  2028, and from 1 January 2029 it is **zeroed**, with the family-allowance and
  short-sickness entitlements abolished for that category at the same time. This
  is a benefit change, not only a rate change  _(Legge 157/2022, art. 22(3) — https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/streaming-video-consiglio/documento17136203.html)_
- **Working pensioners: a pure-solidarity contribution** — Where a pensioner's
  work does not exceed **twenty hours a week**, the art. 31 rate is reduced to
  **25%**, of which **18% falls on the employer and 7% on the worker**. The same
  reduced rate applies to a collaboration engaged to train the pensioner's
  replacement, for at most twelve months  _(Decreto Delegato 28 marzo 2024 n.76, art. 6(2)–(3), amending Legge 157/2022 art. 31 — https://www.bollettinoufficiale.sm/on-line/RicercaBU?operation=getDocBU&id=236d78f5d4b85f53b95385c46d68dab4f416fb79)_
- **Pensioner directors** — An Amministratore Unico or Presidente drawing an
  ordinary pension pays a pure-solidarity rate of **30.5%**, calculated on **20%**
  of income received; an Amministratore Operativo or contracted member in the
  same position pays 30.5% on **60%** of income. Neither may be computed on less
  than the minimum income set for the self-employed  _(Decreto Delegato 28 marzo 2024 n.76, art. 3 — https://www.bollettinoufficiale.sm/on-line/RicercaBU?operation=getDocBU&id=236d78f5d4b85f53b95385c46d68dab4f416fb79)_

## 5. Withholding and remittance

- **Payroll income-tax withholding (IGR)** — Employers withhold Imposta Generale
  sui Redditi from salaries at source and remit it to the Ufficio Tributario  _(Legge 16 dicembre 2013 n.166 (Imposta Generale sui Redditi) (as described at [remotepeople.com](https://remotepeople.com/countries/san-marino/hire-employees/payroll-tax/)))_
- **Contribution and withholding remittance** — Periodic, typically monthly
  ((approx — confirm the exact due dates against the ISS circular; not verified))  _([Istituto per la Sicurezza Sociale (ISS) contribution regulations](https://remotepeople.com/countries/san-marino/hire-employees/payroll-tax/))_
- **Unpaid contributions now suspend the licence** — Where contributions to ISS or
  FONDISS go unpaid for **13 months**, or the arrears exceed **€100,000**, the
  operator's licence to trade is suspended. Access to any pension benefit, and to
  a FONDISS drawdown, also requires arrears to be cleared first  _(Legge 3 marzo 2025 n.30, Capo II — https://www.gov.sm/pub2/GovSM/dam/jcr:f1d046ac-b8d5-4cf8-ae74-22f122f7ae53/17150774L030-2025.pdf)_
- **13th-month salary** — A mandatory 13th-month payment applies and is included
  in the contribution and withholding base ((approx — confirm against the
  applicable collective agreement))  _([San Marino labour law / collective agreements](https://www.papayaglobal.com/countrypedia/country/san-marino/))_

## 6. What this replaces

| The guide said | The statute says |
| --- | --- |
| Employee first pillar **5.90%** | The **2023** figure. 2025 and after: **6.90%** — art. 21(4) |
| Employer first pillar **16.60%** | The **2023** figure. 2025 and after: **17.60%** — art. 21(4) |
| Employee FONDISS 2.00% | Right for 2023–2025; **2.50%** in 2026, rising to 4.00% by 2029 |
| Employer FONDISS 2.00% | Right for 2023–2025; **2.50%** in 2026, capped at 3.00% from 2027 |
| Employee total "approximately **8.3%**" | Impossible: the two pillars alone are **8.90%** in 2025 and **9.40%** in 2026. No total is stated here |
| Employer total "approximately **27.4%**" | Not stated here — it mixed a 2023 pension figure with branches of unknown vintage |
| A single set of "current" rates | A **seven-year ramp**, printed year by year in the statute |
| *nothing* | Employer **Fondo malattie −0.40%** from 2023 and **Assegni Familiari −0.60%** from 2024 — art. 21(2) |
| *nothing* | Self-employed first pillar rising 22.50% → 24.50%, and **artisans' family allowances abolished in 2029** — art. 22 |
| *nothing* | Working pensioners at **25%, split 18/7** under twenty hours a week — DD 76/2024 |
| *nothing* | Unpaid contributions **suspend the trading licence** at 13 months or €100,000 — Legge 30/2025 |

The two pillar tables were read from the text of Legge 29 novembre 2022 n.157 as
published by the Consiglio Grande e Generale. Before quoting them, the law's
**errata corrige** of 1 December 2022 and **Decreto Delegato 28 marzo 2024 n.76**
were both checked against art. 21: the errata corrige only substitutes one
office's name, and the decree abrogates art. 23(4), replaces art. 27 and reduces
the art. 31 rate, leaving art. 21 and its progression intact. The branch rates in
§3 remain unverified and are marked as such.
