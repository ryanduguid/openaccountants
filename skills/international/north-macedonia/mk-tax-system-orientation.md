# North Macedonia tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the taxpayer, source of income, residence facts and tax period. Check treaty treatment where applicable.

## Territorial reach

Resident individuals are taxed on taxable income from North Macedonia and abroad. Nonresident individuals are taxed on income earned in North Macedonia. The profit tax covers resident legal persons' domestic and foreign business profits and profits from local operations of a foreign person's permanent establishment. Separately check withholding on specified payments to foreign legal persons and any applicable treaty. [Personal Income Tax Law, arts 3, 5 and 7](https://www.ujp.gov.mk/files/attachment/0000/1466/ZAKON_ZA_DANOKOT_NA_LICEN_DOHOD_-_Azuriran_so_Sl.vesnik____274___od_20.12.2022.pdf) · [Profit Tax Law, arts 4–6 and 20–23](https://www.ujp.gov.mk/files/attachment/0000/0965/Zakon_za_danok_na_dobivka_199_2023.pdf)

## Residence

An individual is resident if they have a permanent residence in North Macedonia or stay there for at least 183 days in any 12-month period. Add continuous and interrupted stays. A legal person is resident for profit tax purposes if established in North Macedonia or if its head office is there. Check any treaty tie-breaker and whether a residence certificate is needed. [Personal Income Tax Law, arts 5 and 8](https://www.ujp.gov.mk/files/attachment/0000/1466/ZAKON_ZA_DANOKOT_NA_LICEN_DOHOD_-_Azuriran_so_Sl.vesnik____274___od_20.12.2022.pdf) · [Public Revenue Office April 2026 guidance](https://www.ujp.gov.mk/files/attachment/0000/1627/08-3061_1_________________________________________________________________14.04.2026.pdf) · [Profit Tax Law, art 4](https://www.ujp.gov.mk/files/attachment/0000/0965/Zakon_za_danok_na_dobivka_199_2023.pdf)

## Taxpayer types

Sole traders and individuals carrying on independent activities fall under the personal income tax rules. Registered general and limited partnerships are trade companies with legal personality and can fall within the profit tax charge on resident legal persons. The Profit Tax Law also provides separate treatment for covered non-profit bodies and qualifying small trade companies. Check the particular entity and its activities. [Personal Income Tax Law, arts 6 and 19](https://www.ujp.gov.mk/files/attachment/0000/1466/ZAKON_ZA_DANOKOT_NA_LICEN_DOHOD_-_Azuriran_so_Sl.vesnik____274___od_20.12.2022.pdf) · [Law on Trade Companies, arts 19–20 and 25](https://aso.mk/wp-content/uploads/2019/05/LAW-ON-TRADE-COMPANIES_02feb2016.pdf) · [Profit Tax Law, arts 4, 4-a and 31–36](https://www.ujp.gov.mk/files/attachment/0000/0965/Zakon_za_danok_na_dobivka_199_2023.pdf)

## Currency

The Macedonian denar (MKD) is the domestic currency. Report denar amounts where the tax form requires them. Retain the original foreign-currency figures and evidence of the exchange rate used. [National Bank Law, arts 25 and 29](https://www.nbrm.mk/content/%D0%97%D0%B0%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%BD-%D0%B7%D0%B0-%D0%9D%D0%B0%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%B4%D0%BD%D0%B0%D1%82%D0%B0-%D0%91%D0%B0%D0%BD%D0%BA%D0%B0-16-25.pdf) · [Public Revenue Office April 2026 guidance](https://www.ujp.gov.mk/files/attachment/0000/1627/08-3061_1_________________________________________________________________14.04.2026.pdf)

## Assessment periods

The annual personal income tax period is the calendar year, with advance tax on specified categories during the year. The ordinary profit tax period is also the calendar year. A taxpayer that operated for only part of the year uses that shorter period for profit tax. Check any separate short-period rule for a sole trader's annual calculation. [Personal Income Tax Law, arts 4 and 97](https://www.ujp.gov.mk/files/attachment/0000/1466/ZAKON_ZA_DANOKOT_NA_LICEN_DOHOD_-_Azuriran_so_Sl.vesnik____274___od_20.12.2022.pdf) · [Profit Tax Law, arts 38–39](https://www.ujp.gov.mk/files/attachment/0000/0965/Zakon_za_danok_na_dobivka_199_2023.pdf)

## Responsible authorities

The Public Revenue Office (Uprava za javni prihodi) administers personal income and profit taxes, including the residence certificate and the profit tax return. Check the competent municipal office for property taxes. [Personal Income Tax Law, art 8](https://www.ujp.gov.mk/files/attachment/0000/1466/ZAKON_ZA_DANOKOT_NA_LICEN_DOHOD_-_Azuriran_so_Sl.vesnik____274___od_20.12.2022.pdf) · [Profit Tax Law, art 39](https://www.ujp.gov.mk/files/attachment/0000/0965/Zakon_za_danok_na_dobivka_199_2023.pdf) · [Ministry of Finance Property Taxes Law](https://finance.gov.mk/wp-content/uploads/2009/02/%D0%BF%D1%80%D0%B5%D1%87%D0%B8%D1%81%D1%82%D0%B5%D0%BD-2018-.pdf)

## Fictional intake

An individual without a permanent home in North Macedonia spends 100 days there before 31 December and 83 after it within the same 12 months. The combined 183 days meet the domestic stay test. Check the treaty position and assign each receipt to the applicable calendar year.
