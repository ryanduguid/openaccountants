# Germany tax system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record residence or establishment facts, the kind of taxpayer, the source and currency of each amount and the calendar or business year. Check income tax, trade tax, VAT and customs separately.

## Territorial reach

An individual with unlimited income tax liability generally comes within charge on worldwide income. For an individual with limited liability, check domestic income under § 49. Corporation tax covers all income of an entity within § 1 KStG, subject to the applicable rules, and domestic income of an entity within § 2 no. 1 KStG. Check statutory inclusions for specified activities in Germany's share of the exclusive economic zone and continental shelf. Establish the source, activity and any treaty relief before computing tax. [Einkommensteuergesetz (EStG), §§ 1–2](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/BJNR010050934.html) · [EStG, § 49](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__49.html) · [Körperschaftsteuergesetz (KStG), §§ 1–2](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/kstg_1977/BJNR025990976.html)

## Residence

An individual with a dwelling maintained for use in Germany or a habitual abode there is generally subject to unlimited income tax. Under § 9 AO a continuous stay of more than 6 months generally establishes habitual abode from its start, with short interruptions disregarded. Check the exception for stays exclusively for visits, recreation, recuperation or similar private purposes of no more than 1 year. Check the separate rules for some public employees abroad and eligible non-residents who apply. For a company, use the statutory seat or centre of executive management for domestic liability and check any applicable treaty. [EStG, § 1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__1.html) · [Abgabenordnung (AO), §§ 8–11](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ao_1977/BJNR006130976.html) · [KStG, § 1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/kstg_1977/__1.html)

## Taxpayer types

An individual sole trader reports the applicable business or professional income. A company generally makes its own corporation tax return. For an ordinary business partnership, identify the partners' shares and any remuneration included under § 15(1) EStG. Check whether the partnership validly elected corporation tax treatment under § 1a KStG. For a foreign trust or similar arrangement, establish its legal and tax classification. Check entity-level liability under § 1 KStG and potential attribution under § 15 AStG, including the applicable exceptions. [EStG, §§ 15 and 18](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/BJNR010050934.html) · [KStG, § 1a](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/kstg_1977/__1a.html) · [Außensteuergesetz (AStG), § 15](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/astg/__15.html)

## Currency

The euro (EUR) is the currency. The Commercial Code requires covered annual financial statements to be prepared in euro. For foreign amounts, document the exchange rate, date and rule applicable to the tax return and keep the supporting calculation. [Council Regulation (EC) No 974/98, arts 1a and 2 and Annex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/1998/974/2026-01-01/eng) · [Handelsgesetzbuch, § 244](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/hgb/__244.html)

## Assessment periods

The ordinary income and corporation tax assessment period is the calendar year. A business may determine profit for a different accounting year. Check the allocation rule that applies to the taxpayer. For ordinary commercial businesses within § 4a(2)(2) EStG and companies within § 7(4) KStG, the profit of a business year that differs from the calendar year generally belongs to the calendar year in which it ends. Check any approval required to change the year. [EStG, § 25](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__25.html) · [EStG, § 4a](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__4a.html) · [KStG, § 7](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/kstg_1977/__7.html)

## Responsible authorities

The state tax offices (Finanzämter) ordinarily administer income and corporation tax. The Federal Central Tax Office (BZSt) handles assigned federal functions. Federal customs offices handle customs and import VAT. Confirm the relevant office and any separate municipal trade tax obligations. [Fiscal Administration Act (FVG), §§ 5 and 17](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/fvg_1971/) · [Federal Ministry of Finance, German tax administration](https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Content/DE/Downloads/Broschueren_Bestellservice/steuerverwaltung-in-deutschland.html) · [Federal Ministry of Finance, customs responsibilities](https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Web/DE/Themen/Zoll/Aufgaben-und-Struktur/aufgaben-und-struktur.html)

## Fictional intake

A company managed in Germany closes its books on 30 September 2026 and trades with a customer abroad in US dollars. Record the company's seat and management, business and assessment periods, source and treaty facts and the exchange-rate evidence. Separately determine whether a customs, VAT or withholding obligation arises.
