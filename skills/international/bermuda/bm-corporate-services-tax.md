# Bermuda corporate services tax

Check the supplier, the customer and each service before applying Bermuda's corporate services tax to gross earned revenue. Start with `workflow-base`. This guide covers selected statutory provisions checked on 20 November 2026. Establish the law in force for the requested tax period and any applicable notice or decision. Treat missing authority or facts as **Needs Input** for each affected result. [Corporate Services Tax Act 1995, ss 2–7][cst]

## Provider, customer and service

Section 3 charges the provider on gross earned revenue from qualifying services provided to or on behalf of an exempted undertaking. A provider can be an individual, company, partnership, association or other body of persons. The definition excludes an exempted undertaking from the provider class. Verify each party's status for the relevant period. [Corporate Services Tax Act, ss 2–3][cst]

The customer definition includes an exempted company, a permit company with a valid permit, an exempted partnership and an exempted LLC. For an exempted LLC, confirm that it does not meet the local LLC requirements, was formed or recognised as exempted under the LLC Act and has that status in its certificate of formation. The LLC limb entered the Companies Act definition on 1 October 2016. [Companies Act 1981, s 2(1)][companies] · [Limited Liability Company Act 2016, ss 21 and 258 and Schedule para 1(1)][llc] · [Commencement Notice BR 96/2016, s 2][llc-start]

| Service | What to establish |
|---|---|
| Administration, management, secretarial work or provision of a registered office | Identify the services actually supplied to or on behalf of the exempted undertaking. |
| Director work | The director must be an owner, officer or employee of a business that is a provider of corporate services. |
| Resident representative | Establish an appointment within the CST definition and that the functions serve the exempted undertaking's trade or business. The definition refers to Companies Act s 130, Exempted Partnerships Act s 17 and Companies Act s 136A principal representatives. |
| Accounting and financial services | Check the exclusion for the audit of financial statements. |

The exclusions also cover banking business transacted in Bermuda with and through a bank licensed under the Banks and Deposit Companies Act 1999 and management of an insurance company by a local company. The banking licence reference took effect on 1 January 2000. [Corporate Services Tax Act, s 2][cst] · [BR 81/1999, s 1 and Schedule][bank-order]

The CST definition of resident representative names specific appointments. An appointment solely under LLC Act s 26(5) needs its own legal basis before classifying that component. Assess any registered office or administrative work under its own service limb. Obtain records that establish the service and earned revenue for each qualifying component. [Corporate Services Tax Act, ss 2–3][cst] · [Limited Liability Company Act, s 26(5)][llc]

## Register, return and pay

| Obligation | Ordinary statutory timing |
|---|---|
| Apply in the form and manner the Tax Commissioner specifies | A subsequent entrant applies within **30 days** of becoming a provider. The agency links **Form C1**, which asks for the date the provider commenced supplying corporate services. |
| Submit the return specifying gross earned revenue | Within **15 days** after the end of each tax period. The Act defines successive periods of 3 calendar months from April to June 1995. |
| Pay the tax due to the Tax Commissioner | Within **15 days** after the end of the same period. |

Confirm the actual date each obligation arises and any applicable variation. Verify the current submission route, return form and payment channel directly for the relevant period. Form C1 is the registration application. [Corporate Services Tax Act, ss 2 and 5–6][cst] · [Government Form C1][c1] · [Government payment information][payment]

Section 6(3) makes unexcused failure to apply for registration an offence punishable on summary conviction by a fine up to $1,000. A late-payment penalty has a separate basis. Section 7 applies the Taxes Management Act 1976 with necessary adaptations. Use the specific 30-day registration rule in the CST Act. Determine how any other provision applies to CST and obtain the controlling notice or decision before changing a date or amount. [Corporate Services Tax Act, ss 5–7][cst] · [Taxes Management Act, ss 6–9 and 21–25][tma]

| Procedure to check | Published Taxes Management Act provision |
|---|---|
| Return timing or correction | Section 7 requires a written notice for the specified return variations. A section 8 exemption depends on a certificate and its conditions. Under section 9, permission is needed to correct a return. If the correction reveals more tax payable, pay the excess with that return. Section 9 sets separate conditions for remittance. |
| Time to pay or late payment | Section 21 permits discretionary payment extensions or instalments. Default on an instalment makes the outstanding amount payable immediately. Section 22 sets out the late-payment penalty and discretion to remit it. Check the actual instruction, applicable basis and dates. |
| Dispute | Under section 23, the written objection states grounds and ordinarily has a 30-day clock from service of the relevant decision, determination or assessment. Section 25 provides a separate 30-day appeal clock from service of the objection decision, subject to time allowed by the Tribunal, with a written notice and the prescribed fee. Verify service, the applicable fee and the case's procedural requirements. |

[Taxes Management Act, ss 7–9 and 21–25][tma]

## Rate and calculation

The original Act set a 4% rate. The 2013 amendment replaced it with 6% from 1 April 2013. The 2015 amendment replaced 6% with **7%** from 1 April 2015. The posted consolidated section 4 gives 7% of gross earned revenue. Check the applicable version and dates before calculating. [Original Act, s 4][original] · [2013 amendment, ss 2–3][rate13] · [2015 amendment, ss 2–3][rate15] · [Consolidated Act, s 4][cst]

1. Identify the provider, supported customer status and qualifying services for the period.
2. Reconcile the gross earned revenue derived from those services. Obtain evidence for mixed fees and the period in which each amount was earned. Resolve currency conversion and rounding where needed.
3. Multiply the supported gross earned revenue by the applicable statutory rate. Hold any total that depends on unclassified or unallocated fees or missing period authority.
4. Check the return, payment and registration clocks separately. Record the authority and applicable notice or decision for any variation.

[Corporate Services Tax Act, ss 2–7][cst]

| Facts | Expected handling |
|---|---|
| For the quarter ending 30 September 2026, assume the posted rules apply throughout, a qualifying provider earns BMD 10,000 entirely from eligible administration for an exempted company and no variation applies. | Conditional tax is BMD 700. Under the ordinary clocks the return and payment fall on 15 October 2026. The registration clock needs the provider's commencement date. |
| A confirmed exempted undertaking offers management services to another exempted undertaking. | Section 2 excludes the supplier from the CST provider class. Confirm that status for the period before closing this branch. |
| A supplier gives one price for a registered office and work under an LLC Act s 26(5) resident representative appointment. The customer has confirmed exempted LLC status. | Assess the registered office under its own limb. Hold classification of the representative work and any amount that depends on an unsupported fee allocation. |
| The Commissioner allows instalments and the provider assumes its return date has also moved. | Verify the payment terms under section 21 and the return obligation under CST section 5. Any change to filing needs its own applicable authority. |
