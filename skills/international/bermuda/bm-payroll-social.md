# Bermuda Payroll & Social Contributions

## Payroll tax

- **Payroll tax overview** — Payroll tax is Bermuda's principal tax on employment income, levied under the Payroll Tax Act 1995 and the Payroll Tax Rates Act 1995. It has an employer portion and an employee portion (which the employer may deduct from pay but remains liable to remit). Remuneration is capped at BMD 1,000,000 per person per year. Rates are set annually in the Budget; the values below should be confirmed against the gov.bm tables for the 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2026 year.
- **Governing legislation** — Payroll Tax Act 1995 and Payroll Tax Rates Act 1995  _(Payroll Tax Act 1995; Payroll Tax Rates Act 1995 (https://www.gov.bm/payroll-tax))_
- **Taxable remuneration cap** — BMD 1,000,000 per person per annum; remuneration above the cap is not taxed, and from 1 April 2026 dividends from non-listed companies are taxable as remuneration only above a BMD 20,000 deductible per person per year  _(Government of Bermuda, Calculating Payroll Tax for the period 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027 — https://www.gov.bm/calculating-payroll-tax-2026 ; Government of Bermuda, Payroll Tax page (Office of the Tax Commissioner) — https://www.gov.bm/payroll-tax)_
- **Tax structure** — Two portions — an employer portion on gross taxable remuneration at a rate set by the employer's class, and an employee portion calculated across five progressive bands; the employer may deduct the employee portion but remains liable for the whole tax  _(Government of Bermuda, Calculating Payroll Tax for the period 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027 — https://www.gov.bm/calculating-payroll-tax-2026)_
- **Employer portion rate (standard, large employers)** — From 1 April 2026: 9.50% where annual payroll exceeds BMD 1,000,000; 9.75% for all exempt undertakings; 7.00% where payroll is above BMD 500,000 up to BMD 1,000,000  _(Government of Bermuda, Calculating Payroll Tax for the period 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027 — https://www.gov.bm/calculating-payroll-tax-2026)_
- **Employer portion rate (small employers)** — From 1 April 2026: 0.5% where annual payroll is below BMD 200,000; 2.00% from BMD 200,000 to 350,000; 4.75% above BMD 350,000 up to 500,000; 0% for new start-ups (four quarters), registered charities, approved schools and new-hire remuneration; 1.00% for educational, sporting or scientific institutions; 1.75% for self-employed horticulturists; 4.00% for hotels and restaurants with payroll of BMD 350,000 or more; 5.00% for fashion, shoe, jewellery and perfume retailers above BMD 500,000  _(Government of Bermuda, Calculating Payroll Tax for the period 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027 — https://www.gov.bm/calculating-payroll-tax-2026)_
- **Employee portion — Band 1 (first BMD 48,000)** — 0.25% on the first BMD 48,000 of annual remuneration for 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027  _(Government of Bermuda, Calculating Payroll Tax for the period 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027 — https://www.gov.bm/calculating-payroll-tax-2026)_
- **Employee portion — Band 2** — 7.75% on remuneration from BMD 48,001 to 96,000  _(Government of Bermuda, Calculating Payroll Tax for the period 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027 — https://www.gov.bm/calculating-payroll-tax-2026)_
- **Employee portion — band structure** — Progressive (marginal) across five bands up to the BMD 1,000,000 cap: 0.25% to BMD 48,000; 7.75% to 96,000; 10.75% to 200,000; 11.50% to 500,000; 12.50% to 1,000,000  _(Government of Bermuda, Calculating Payroll Tax for the period 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027 — https://www.gov.bm/calculating-payroll-tax-2026)_
- **Payroll tax filing & payment frequency** — Quarterly returns and payment to the Office of the Tax Commissioner, due 15 April, 15 July, 15 October and 15 January (a 15-day grace period after each quarter end); exempted companies and employers with annual remuneration above BMD 200,000 must file online at etax.gov.bm  _(Government of Bermuda, Calculating Payroll Tax for the period 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027 — https://www.gov.bm/calculating-payroll-tax-2026 ; Government of Bermuda, Payroll Tax page (Office of the Tax Commissioner) — https://www.gov.bm/payroll-tax)_
- **Social insurance (contributory pension) total contribution** — BMD 75.30 per week (combined employer + employee)  _(Contributory Pensions Act 1970 (https://www.gov.bm/department/social-insurance))_
- **Social insurance split** — Employer pays 50% (BMD 37.65/week) and employee pays 50% (BMD 37.65/week) for workers aged 16-64  _(Contributory Pensions Act 1970 (https://www.gov.bm/department/social-insurance))_
- **Social insurance — employees aged 65+** — Only the employer portion is due; no employee contribution  _(Contributory Pensions Act 1970 (https://www.gov.bm/department/social-insurance))_
- **Social insurance remittance deadline** — Employer billed monthly; payment due by the last Friday of that month  _(Contributory Pensions Act 1970 (https://www.gov.bm/department/social-insurance))_
- **Occupational (private) pension contribution** — Mandatory for Bermudians/spouses under the National Pension Scheme; typically 5% employer + 5% employee of pensionable earnings (approx — confirm current rate) percent (approx — confirm current rate)  _(National Pension Scheme (Occupational Pensions) Act 1998 (https://www.gov.bm/bermuda-pension))_
