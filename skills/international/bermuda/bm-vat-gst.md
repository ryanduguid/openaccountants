# Bermuda VAT / GST

## VAT / GST in Bermuda

The inherited guide reported no VAT, GST or general sales tax and therefore no registration threshold, return or reverse charge. Current affirmative primary evidence for those conclusions has not been established in this reconciliation. Treat them as **Research Gap / Needs Input**, not verified inapplicability. A customs duty or a sector fee is a separate question. [Tax-system evidence boundary](bm-tax-overview.md#claims-still-requiring-evidence)

- **VAT / GST / general sales tax:** prior absence claim unverified; obtain current affirmative primary evidence before a conclusion.
- **VAT registration threshold:** the prior ‘not applicable’ conclusion depends on establishing the absence claim; do not invent a threshold or report zero.
- **VAT return / filing frequency:** the prior absence conclusion remains unverified. It does not establish that no customs or other indirect-tax return exists.
- **Reverse charge:** the prior ‘not applicable’ conclusion remains conditional on resolving the VAT/GST evidence gap.
**Other indirect charges.** The [OTC inventory](https://www.gov.bm/department/tax-commissioner) lists sector taxes and duties and [Customs](https://www.gov.bm/department/customs) assesses duty and other taxes on arriving goods and passengers. Customs is not an exhaustive account of indirect charges. The retained tariff and allowance figures below are **Needs Input** until their separate consumption-tax reconciliation is complete; this update does not validate them.

- **Retained customs duty summary** — Customs import duty is levied on most goods imported into Bermuda at the rate for the goods' tariff code in the Bermuda Customs Tariff 2026; the most common rate is 25%, building materials carry 10% from 1 July 2025 and motor vehicle parts are duty free  _(Government of Bermuda, Importing and exporting (Bermuda Customs Tariff 2026) — https://www.gov.bm/business/importing-and-exporting ; Government of Bermuda, Tax and Duty Reductions Take Effect From July 1, 2025 — https://www.gov.bm/articles/tax-duty-reductions-take-effect-july-1-2025)_
- **Personal import duty allowance** — BMD 300 of accompanied personal goods for returning residents from 1 July 2025 (BMD 200 before), BMD 50 for visitors, plus 200 cigarettes, 50 cigars, 0.5 kg of tobacco, one litre of wine and one litre of spirits per arriving passenger  _(Government of Bermuda, Tax and Duty Reductions Take Effect From July 1, 2025 — https://www.gov.bm/articles/tax-duty-reductions-take-effect-july-1-2025 ; Government of Bermuda, Duty free allowances — https://www.gov.bm/duty-free-allowances ; visitor allowance (as reported))_
