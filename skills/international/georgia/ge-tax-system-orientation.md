# Georgia tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the taxpayer, where income arose, residence facts and period. Check the applicable treaty and foreign-tax relief.

## Territorial reach

Start by identifying the income source under art 104. A resident individual's income outside Georgian sources is generally exempt from personal income tax. A nonresident generally falls within Georgian income tax on Georgian-source income, through a permanent establishment or by the rules for income outside it. Work done in Georgia can have a Georgian source when the customer or payment account is abroad.

For an ordinary company, profit tax generally arises on distributions and specified transactions, including certain non-business expenses, free transfers and excess representation expenses. A foreign enterprise's Georgian permanent establishment has its own taxable-event and attribution rules. Check the relevant withholding and treaty rules for income outside a permanent establishment. [Tax Code, arts 79–82, 97–104 and 134](https://new.matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/download/1043717/248/ge/pdf)

## Residence

An individual generally becomes resident for the whole tax year if present in Georgia on at least 183 days during any continuous 12 calendar months ending in that year. Check qualifying time outside Georgia and excluded periods in Georgia under art 34. Part of a qualifying day counts as a day. Days used to establish residence in the preceding period are excluded from the next period's test. Georgian state service abroad and residence granted under the separate statutory rules can also establish residence.

An enterprise is Georgian when its place of business or management is in Georgia, or both are. Identify the legally determined place of business and the place where management functions are exercised. Review domestic status separately from a permanent establishment test and any treaty rule. [Tax Code, arts 22, 27–29 and 34](https://new.matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/download/1043717/248/ge/pdf)

## Taxpayer types

An individual entrepreneur is a natural person. Check whether an ordinary or special business regime applies. A general or limited partnership registered under the Law on Entrepreneurs is a company and legal person. An unincorporated partnership or comparable joint undertaking can also be an enterprise for the Tax Code. For such a partnership, check its profit-tax events and the separate rules for its members' allocated income, withholding and losses. [Law on Entrepreneurs, arts 2 and 8](https://www.matsne.gov.ge/en/document/view/5230186) · [Tax Code, arts 20–21, 36, 83–93 and 143](https://new.matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/download/1043717/248/ge/pdf)

## Currency

The Georgian lari (GEL) is the domestic currency. Identify the tax conversion date for each foreign-currency transaction and preserve the original amount and exchange-rate evidence. [National Bank Organic Law, art 34](https://new.matsne.gov.ge/en/document/download/101044/67/en/pdf) · [Tax Code, art 73](https://new.matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/download/1043717/248/ge/pdf)

## Assessment periods

Ordinary annual individual income tax follows the calendar year where a self-assessed return is required. Ordinary profit-tax events are reported monthly. Check the separate periods for taxpayers subject to the annual profit model and for any special business regime. [Tax Code, arts 97, 135 and 153](https://new.matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/download/1043717/248/ge/pdf)

## Responsible authorities

The Revenue Service and its authorised units administer national taxes. Use the Revenue Service to verify registration, returns, withholding and customs procedures. Check the relevant municipality's decisions where local tax settings apply. [Tax Code, arts 6 and 48–49](https://new.matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/download/1043717/248/ge/pdf) · [Revenue Service](https://www.rs.ge/)

## Fictional intake

A Georgian resident consultant works from Tbilisi for an overseas company and is paid into an overseas account. Check the statutory source of the service income using the work and contract facts before applying the exemption for income outside Georgian sources.
