# Cape Verde Tax Overview

## Cape Verde tax system at a glance

Cape Verde (Cabo Verde) levies a progressive personal income tax, a corporate income tax, and a VAT (IVA). The tax year is the calendar year and the currency is the Cape Verdean escudo (CVE). This is an unverified source-cited draft for tax year 2025.

- **Tax year** — Calendar year for companies (CIRPC art 10(1), subject to the exceptions in that article for a different tax period) and for individuals (CIRPS art 85)  _(Código do IRPC (Lei n.º 82/VIII/2015) as republished by Lei n.º 116/IX/2021, art 10(1) — https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/54571/64342/Altera%C3%A7%C3%B5es+C%C3%B3digos+Tribut%C3%A1rios_Lei116-IX-2021%2C+de+2+fevereiro.pdf/26a33c81-cb9a-a813-a842-f4152024574c ; Código do IRPS (Lei n.º 78/VIII/2014) as republished by Lei n.º 116/IX/2021, art 85 — https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/54571/64342/Altera%C3%A7%C3%B5es+C%C3%B3digos+Tribut%C3%A1rios_Lei116-IX-2021%2C+de+2+fevereiro.pdf/26a33c81-cb9a-a813-a842-f4152024574c)_
- **Currency** — Cape Verdean escudo (CVE), pegged to the euro at approximately 110.265 CVE = 1 EUR  _(Banco de Cabo Verde)_
- **Tax authority** — Direcao Nacional de Receitas do Estado (DNRE) / Direccao Geral dos Impostos (DGI)  _(Direccao Nacional de Receitas do Estado)_
- **Residence basis of taxation** — Residents are taxed on worldwide income and non-residents only on Cabo Verde-source income; a company is resident when its seat or effective management is in Cabo Verde, and an individual when present for more than 183 days, consecutive or not, in any 12-month period or with a habitual home there on 31 December  _(Código do IRPC (Lei n.º 82/VIII/2015) as republished by Lei n.º 116/IX/2021, arts 2(4) and 6(1) — https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/54571/64342/Altera%C3%A7%C3%B5es+C%C3%B3digos+Tribut%C3%A1rios_Lei116-IX-2021%2C+de+2+fevereiro.pdf/26a33c81-cb9a-a813-a842-f4152024574c ; Código do IRPS (Lei n.º 78/VIII/2014) as republished by Lei n.º 116/IX/2021, arts 20 and 21 — https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/54571/64342/Altera%C3%A7%C3%B5es+C%C3%B3digos+Tribut%C3%A1rios_Lei116-IX-2021%2C+de+2+fevereiro.pdf/26a33c81-cb9a-a813-a842-f4152024574c)_
- **Headline personal income tax (IRPS) rates** — Progressive 16.5% up to CVE 960,000, 23.1% from CVE 960,000 to CVE 1,800,000 and 27.5% above, applied on aggregation (the declarative method); employment income is normally settled by progressive final withholding  _(Código do IRPS (Lei n.º 78/VIII/2014) as republished by Lei n.º 116/IX/2021, art 46(1) — https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/54571/64342/Altera%C3%A7%C3%B5es+C%C3%B3digos+Tribut%C3%A1rios_Lei116-IX-2021%2C+de+2+fevereiro.pdf/26a33c81-cb9a-a813-a842-f4152024574c ; Código do IRPS (Lei n.º 78/VIII/2014) as republished by Lei n.º 116/IX/2021, art 47(1) — https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/54571/64342/Altera%C3%A7%C3%B5es+C%C3%B3digos+Tribut%C3%A1rios_Lei116-IX-2021%2C+de+2+fevereiro.pdf/26a33c81-cb9a-a813-a842-f4152024574c)_
- **Headline corporate income tax (IRPC) rate** — 20% for taxpayers under organised accounting from 1 January 2026  _(Lei n.º 69/X/2025, de 31 de dezembro (Orçamento do Estado para 2026), art 29, replacing CIRPC art 84(1) — https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/5793427/Lei+que+aprova+o+OE+2026.pdf/f9c15473-419c-0817-2d7d-dd46b4912fbc ; Código do IRPC (Lei n.º 82/VIII/2015) as republished by Lei n.º 116/IX/2021, art 84(1) (22% in the 2021 republication) — https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/54571/64342/Altera%C3%A7%C3%B5es+C%C3%B3digos+Tribut%C3%A1rios_Lei116-IX-2021%2C+de+2+fevereiro.pdf/26a33c81-cb9a-a813-a842-f4152024574c)_
- **Does VAT/GST exist** — Yes; Imposto sobre o Valor Acrescentado (IVA) at a standard rate of 15%  _(Código do IVA (Lei n.º 21/VI/2003, republication), art 17(1) — https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/54571/64715/Lei+n%C2%BA+21+-+2003+-+Republica%C3%A7%C3%A3o+o+C%C3%B3digo+do+IVA+com+altera%C3%A7%C3%B5es.pdf/39072edf-457a-adc8-3133-554a65079487)_
- **Annual corporate income tax return deadline** — The last day of May of the following year (Modelo 1B, filed electronically), whether or not that day is a working day  _(Código do IRPC (Lei n.º 82/VIII/2015) as republished by Lei n.º 116/IX/2021, art 102(1) — https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/54571/64342/Altera%C3%A7%C3%B5es+C%C3%B3digos+Tribut%C3%A1rios_Lei116-IX-2021%2C+de+2+fevereiro.pdf/26a33c81-cb9a-a813-a842-f4152024574c ; Direção Nacional de Receitas do Estado, Calendário Fiscal 2026 — https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/54571/0/Calendario_Fiscal_2026_PEC_Vers%C3%A3o_Atualizado_110626.pdf/7f524ec2-1b35-2f33-8936-42e8b752516e)_
- **Special regime for micro and small companies** — Tributo Especial Unificado (TEU) of 4% of turnover for micro and small companies under the REMPE regime, replacing IRPC, VAT, the fire tax and the employer's social security contribution; paid quarterly with the Modelo 107  _(Regime Jurídico Especial das Micro e Pequenas Empresas (Lei n.º 70/VIII/2014) as republished by Lei n.º 116/IX/2021, arts 24(3) and 25(1) — https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/54571/64342/Altera%C3%A7%C3%B5es+C%C3%B3digos+Tribut%C3%A1rios_Lei116-IX-2021%2C+de+2+fevereiro.pdf/26a33c81-cb9a-a813-a842-f4152024574c ; Código do IRPC (Lei n.º 82/VIII/2015) as republished by Lei n.º 116/IX/2021, art 84(2) — https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/54571/64342/Altera%C3%A7%C3%B5es+C%C3%B3digos+Tribut%C3%A1rios_Lei116-IX-2021%2C+de+2+fevereiro.pdf/26a33c81-cb9a-a813-a842-f4152024574c ; Direção Nacional de Receitas do Estado, Calendário Fiscal 2026 — https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/54571/0/Calendario_Fiscal_2026_PEC_Vers%C3%A3o_Atualizado_110626.pdf/7f524ec2-1b35-2f33-8936-42e8b752516e)_
- **Social security authority** — Instituto Nacional de Previdencia Social (INPS)  _(Instituto Nacional de Previdencia Social (INPS))_
