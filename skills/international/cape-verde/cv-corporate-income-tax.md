# Cape Verde Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax (IRPC)

- **Corporate income tax (IRPC)** — Corporate income tax in Cape Verde is the Imposto sobre o Rendimento das Pessoas Colectivas (IRPC). Resident companies and permanent establishments are taxed on adjusted accounting profit; non-residents without a PE face withholding on Cape Verde-source income.
- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 20% on taxable profit for taxpayers under organised accounting from 1 January 2026 (22% in the 2021 republication of art 84, amended by the budget laws for 2023 to 2025)  _(Lei n.º 69/X/2025, de 31 de dezembro (Orçamento do Estado para 2026), art 29, replacing CIRPC art 84(1) — https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/5793427/Lei+que+aprova+o+OE+2026.pdf/f9c15473-419c-0817-2d7d-dd46b4912fbc ; Código do IRPC (Lei n.º 82/VIII/2015) as republished by Lei n.º 116/IX/2021, art 84(1) (22% in the 2021 republication) — https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/54571/64342/Altera%C3%A7%C3%B5es+C%C3%B3digos+Tribut%C3%A1rios_Lei116-IX-2021%2C+de+2+fevereiro.pdf/26a33c81-cb9a-a813-a842-f4152024574c)_
- **Municipal fire-brigade surcharge (Taxa de Incendio)** — 2% in Praia and Mindelo municipalities, giving an effective rate of about 20.40% %  _(Codigo do IRPC (Corporate Income Tax Code) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cabo-verde/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Special single tax for micro and small companies (SST)** — 4% of the gross turnover of the period, paid quarterly with the Modelo 107 by the end of the month after the quarter; it replaces IRPC, VAT, the fire tax and the employer's social security contribution  _(Regime Jurídico Especial das Micro e Pequenas Empresas (Lei n.º 70/VIII/2014) as republished by Lei n.º 116/IX/2021, arts 24(3) and 25(1) — https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/54571/64342/Altera%C3%A7%C3%B5es+C%C3%B3digos+Tribut%C3%A1rios_Lei116-IX-2021%2C+de+2+fevereiro.pdf/26a33c81-cb9a-a813-a842-f4152024574c ; Código do IRPC (Lei n.º 82/VIII/2015) as republished by Lei n.º 116/IX/2021, art 84(2) — https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/54571/64342/Altera%C3%A7%C3%B5es+C%C3%B3digos+Tribut%C3%A1rios_Lei116-IX-2021%2C+de+2+fevereiro.pdf/26a33c81-cb9a-a813-a842-f4152024574c ; Direção Nacional de Receitas do Estado, Calendário Fiscal 2026 — https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/54571/0/Calendario_Fiscal_2026_PEC_Vers%C3%A3o_Atualizado_110626.pdf/7f524ec2-1b35-2f33-8936-42e8b752516e)_
- **Tax base** — Accounting profit computed under local accounting rules, adjusted for tax purposes  _(Codigo do IRPC (Corporate Income Tax Code) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cabo-verde/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Pillar Two global minimum tax** — A qualified domestic minimum top-up tax (Imposto Mínimo Global, IMG) of 15% applies from 2026 to constituent entities in Cabo Verde of multinational or large domestic groups with annual revenue of EUR 750 million or more; the top-up equals the difference between 15% and the group's effective rate in Cabo Verde, with the detailed rules left to separate legislation  _(Lei n.º 69/X/2025, de 31 de dezembro (Orçamento do Estado para 2026), art 101 — https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/5793427/Lei+que+aprova+o+OE+2026.pdf/f9c15473-419c-0817-2d7d-dd46b4912fbc)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends (resident)** — Full relief in force on profit distributions  _(Codigo do IRPC (Corporate Income Tax Code) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cabo-verde/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on interest (resident)** — 20%, or 10% on interest from public debt securities, bonds and similar instruments (art 85(1)(a)); PwC reports 5% for bonds listed on the stock exchange  _(Código do IRPC (Lei n.º 82/VIII/2015) as republished by Lei n.º 116/IX/2021, art 85(1) — https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/54571/64342/Altera%C3%A7%C3%B5es+C%C3%B3digos+Tribut%C3%A1rios_Lei116-IX-2021%2C+de+2+fevereiro.pdf/26a33c81-cb9a-a813-a842-f4152024574c ; PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cabo-verde/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on royalties (resident)** — 20% on capital income as defined in the CIRPS, including royalties; the withholding is final except for taxpayers under organised accounting, for whom it is a payment on account  _(Código do IRPC (Lei n.º 82/VIII/2015) as republished by Lei n.º 116/IX/2021, art 85(1) and (2) — https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/54571/64342/Altera%C3%A7%C3%B5es+C%C3%B3digos+Tribut%C3%A1rios_Lei116-IX-2021%2C+de+2+fevereiro.pdf/26a33c81-cb9a-a813-a842-f4152024574c)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends/interest/royalties (non-resident, non-treaty)** — Final withholding at the CIRPS rates: 20% on capital income, with 10% for the items the CIRPS lists at that rate; there is no option to aggregate  _(Código do IRPC (Lei n.º 82/VIII/2015) as republished by Lei n.º 116/IX/2021, art 86(1)(a) — https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/54571/64342/Altera%C3%A7%C3%B5es+C%C3%B3digos+Tribut%C3%A1rios_Lei116-IX-2021%2C+de+2+fevereiro.pdf/26a33c81-cb9a-a813-a842-f4152024574c ; Código do IRPS (Lei n.º 78/VIII/2014) as republished by Lei n.º 116/IX/2021, arts 50 and 52 — https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/54571/64342/Altera%C3%A7%C3%B5es+C%C3%B3digos+Tribut%C3%A1rios_Lei116-IX-2021%2C+de+2+fevereiro.pdf/26a33c81-cb9a-a813-a842-f4152024574c)_
- **Withholding tax on services (non-resident)** — 15% unless reduced by tax treaty %  _(Codigo do IRPC (Corporate Income Tax Code) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cabo-verde/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Annual return filing deadline** — The last day of May of the following year, filed electronically (Modelo 1B), whether or not that day is a working day; a company with a non-calendar tax period files by the last day of the fifth month after its year end  _(Código do IRPC (Lei n.º 82/VIII/2015) as republished by Lei n.º 116/IX/2021, art 102(1) and (2) — https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/54571/64342/Altera%C3%A7%C3%B5es+C%C3%B3digos+Tribut%C3%A1rios_Lei116-IX-2021%2C+de+2+fevereiro.pdf/26a33c81-cb9a-a813-a842-f4152024574c ; Direção Nacional de Receitas do Estado, Calendário Fiscal 2026 — https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/54571/0/Calendario_Fiscal_2026_PEC_Vers%C3%A3o_Atualizado_110626.pdf/7f524ec2-1b35-2f33-8936-42e8b752516e)_
- **Advance payments (standard regime)** — Three instalments due at the end of March, August and November of the tax year, at 30%, 30% and 20% of the previous year's tax; the 2026 calendar lists 31 March, 31 August and 30 November  _(Código do IRPC (Lei n.º 82/VIII/2015) as republished by Lei n.º 116/IX/2021, art 96(1) and (2) — https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/54571/64342/Altera%C3%A7%C3%B5es+C%C3%B3digos+Tribut%C3%A1rios_Lei116-IX-2021%2C+de+2+fevereiro.pdf/26a33c81-cb9a-a813-a842-f4152024574c ; Direção Nacional de Receitas do Estado, Calendário Fiscal 2026 — https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/54571/0/Calendario_Fiscal_2026_PEC_Vers%C3%A3o_Atualizado_110626.pdf/7f524ec2-1b35-2f33-8936-42e8b752516e)_
