# Cape Verde Personal Income Tax

## Personal income tax (IRPS) bands and rates

- **Band 1 — taxable income up to CVE 960,000** — 16.5%  _(Código do IRPS (Lei n.º 78/VIII/2014) as republished by Lei n.º 116/IX/2021, art 46(1)(a) — https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/54571/64342/Altera%C3%A7%C3%B5es+C%C3%B3digos+Tribut%C3%A1rios_Lei116-IX-2021%2C+de+2+fevereiro.pdf/26a33c81-cb9a-a813-a842-f4152024574c)_
- **Band 2 — taxable income CVE 960,000 to 1,800,000** — 23.1%  _(Código do IRPS (Lei n.º 78/VIII/2014) as republished by Lei n.º 116/IX/2021, art 46(1)(b) — https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/54571/64342/Altera%C3%A7%C3%B5es+C%C3%B3digos+Tribut%C3%A1rios_Lei116-IX-2021%2C+de+2+fevereiro.pdf/26a33c81-cb9a-a813-a842-f4152024574c)_
- **Band 3 — taxable income over CVE 1,800,000** — 27.5%  _(Código do IRPS (Lei n.º 78/VIII/2014) as republished by Lei n.º 116/IX/2021, art 46(1)(c) — https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/54571/64342/Altera%C3%A7%C3%B5es+C%C3%B3digos+Tribut%C3%A1rios_Lei116-IX-2021%2C+de+2+fevereiro.pdf/26a33c81-cb9a-a813-a842-f4152024574c)_
- **Tax-free threshold (annual net income exemption)** — Taxable income up to CVE 220,000 a year is exempt as the minimum subsistence amount  _(Código do IRPS (Lei n.º 78/VIII/2014) as republished by Lei n.º 116/IX/2021, art 46(2) — https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/54571/64342/Altera%C3%A7%C3%B5es+C%C3%B3digos+Tribut%C3%A1rios_Lei116-IX-2021%2C+de+2+fevereiro.pdf/26a33c81-cb9a-a813-a842-f4152024574c)_
- **Non-resident taxation** — Non-residents are taxed only on Cabo Verde-source income, by final withholding at the rates in arts 47 to 51 without the option to aggregate, or by compulsory return where withholding cannot apply  _(Código do IRPS (Lei n.º 78/VIII/2014) as republished by Lei n.º 116/IX/2021, arts 20(2) and 52 — https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/54571/64342/Altera%C3%A7%C3%B5es+C%C3%B3digos+Tribut%C3%A1rios_Lei116-IX-2021%2C+de+2+fevereiro.pdf/26a33c81-cb9a-a813-a842-f4152024574c)_
- **Resident scope** — Residents are taxed on worldwide income; an individual is resident when present for more than 183 days, consecutive or not, in any 12-month period, or with a habitual home in Cabo Verde on 31 December  _(Código do IRPS (Lei n.º 78/VIII/2014) as republished by Lei n.º 116/IX/2021, arts 20(1) and 21 — https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/54571/64342/Altera%C3%A7%C3%B5es+C%C3%B3digos+Tribut%C3%A1rios_Lei116-IX-2021%2C+de+2+fevereiro.pdf/26a33c81-cb9a-a813-a842-f4152024574c)_
- **Residence test** — Individual is resident if present in Cape Verde more than 183 days in a tax year, or with a permanent home there suggesting intent to keep it as habitual residence (approx — confirm)  _(Codigo do IRPS (Personal Income Tax Code))_
- **Employment income withholding (PAYE-equivalent)** — Employers withhold IRPS monthly at source from salaries under the retencao na fonte rules  _(Codigo do IRPS (Personal Income Tax Code))_
- **Deduction — mandatory social security contributions** — Compulsory contributions paid by the employee to social security institutions (8.5% of gross pay under the general regime) are deducted from gross employment income when it is aggregated  _(Código do IRPS (Lei n.º 78/VIII/2014) as republished by Lei n.º 116/IX/2021, art 28(2)(a) — https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/54571/64342/Altera%C3%A7%C3%B5es+C%C3%B3digos+Tribut%C3%A1rios_Lei116-IX-2021%2C+de+2+fevereiro.pdf/26a33c81-cb9a-a813-a842-f4152024574c ; INPS, Obrigações (contribution rates by regime) — https://inps.cv/obrigacoes/)_
- **Personal deductions/allowances** — Documented health, education and housing expenses, plus dependant allowances, may reduce taxable income under the declarative method (approx — confirm)  _(Codigo do IRPS (Personal Income Tax Code))_
- **Annual IRPS return filing deadline** — 31 May of the year following the tax year (declarative method) (approx — confirm)  _(Codigo do IRPS (Personal Income Tax Code))_
- **Final withholding method** — Employment income is taxed by progressive final withholding under art 71, with aggregation optional; capital and other categories are also settled by final withholding, so most individuals file no annual return  _(Código do IRPS (Lei n.º 78/VIII/2014) as republished by Lei n.º 116/IX/2021, arts 19(2)(a) and 47(1) — https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/54571/64342/Altera%C3%A7%C3%B5es+C%C3%B3digos+Tribut%C3%A1rios_Lei116-IX-2021%2C+de+2+fevereiro.pdf/26a33c81-cb9a-a813-a842-f4152024574c)_
