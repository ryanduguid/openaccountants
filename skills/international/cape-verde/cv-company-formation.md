# Cape Verde Company Formation & Entity Choice

## Entity types and incorporation

The most common business vehicles in Cape Verde are the Sociedade por Quotas (Lda) and the Sociedade Anonima (SA). Companies can be incorporated through the one-stop shop Casa do Cidadao under a fast-track procedure. The capital, pay-up, reserve and audit rules are now taken from the Código das Sociedades Comerciais as published in the Boletim Oficial. The fee rows still rest on a commercial source and are marked as such.

> **The code this guide cited was repealed in 2019.** Every capital rule below used
> to be attributed to the *Código das Empresas Comerciais* (Decreto-Legislativo n.º
> 3/99, de 29 de março). Books II and III of that code were **expressly revoked** by
> the **Código das Sociedades Comerciais**, approved by **Decreto-Legislativo n.º
> 2/2019**, published in **Boletim Oficial n.º 80, I Série, de 23 de julho de 2019**,
> together with a new Código Comercial (Decreto-Legislativo n.º 1/2019) in the same
> bulletin. The same article also revoked **Portaria n.º 17/2013, de 14 de março,
> *"que fixa os montantes mínimos do capital social"*** — the instrument that set
> minimum share capital. Article 4 of the enacting decree commences the code ninety
> days after publication; practitioners date it to 22 October 2019, and anything
> turning on the exact day should be checked against the counting rule.
>
> The rules below are now read from the gazette text itself. **Read that text with
> Retificação n.º 133/2019** (Boletim Oficial n.º 101, 1.º Suplemento, I Série, de
> 30 de setembro de 2019): the code *"saiu de forma inexata"* and five articles were
> formally corrected, two of them by an order of magnitude. See the audit-threshold
> row below.
>
> _(Decreto-Legislativo n.º 2/2019 — https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/View/42379; full bulletin PDF — https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/Download/2861; Retificação n.º 133/2019 — https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/View?id=43751)_

- **Sociedade por Quotas (Lda)** — Private limited company. Capital is divided into quotas, which may be of different amounts; the members are jointly and severally liable for the entradas agreed in the articles, but not for the company's debts except where article 173 has been used. The firm must end in *"Limitada"* or *"Lda"*  _(Código das Sociedades Comerciais, arts. 172 and 174 — https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/Download/2861)_
- **Sociedade Anonima (SA)** — Public limited company. Capital is divided into shares and each member's liability is limited to the shares subscribed. It is formed with a **minimum of two shareholders**, save where the law expressly provides otherwise; co-owners of one holding count as a single party. The firm must end in *"sociedade anónima"* or *"S.A."*  _(Código das Sociedades Comerciais, arts. 234 and 236 — https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/Download/2861)_
- **Sociedade Unipessoal por Quotas** — A one-member Lda is a distinct statutory form. Only the company's assets answer for its debts. An ordinary Lda may not have a *sociedade unipessoal por quotas* as its sole member  _(Código das Sociedades Comerciais, arts. 229–233 — https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/Download/2861)_
- **Minimum share capital (Lda)** — **There is no statutory minimum.** Article 172(2): *"O montante do capital social é livremente fixado no contrato de sociedade, correspondendo à soma das quotas subscritas pelos sócios."* The floor is indirect: **each quota must have a nominal value of at least CVE 100**, and its value must be divisible by CVE 1. Sector laws may still impose a minimum for regulated activities  _(Código das Sociedades Comerciais, arts. 172(2) and 185(2) — https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/Download/2861)_
- **Minimum share capital (SA)** — **There is no statutory minimum either.** Article 237(1) fixes capital freely in the articles, expressed in national currency. The indirect floor is that **all shares carry the same nominal value, which may not be below CVE 1,000**, unless the statutes provide for shares without nominal value. An SA formed **by public subscription** is the exception: the founders must subscribe and **fully pay** shares whose nominal values total at least **CVE 2,500,000**  _(Código das Sociedades Comerciais, arts. 237 and 240(2) — https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/Download/2861)_
- **Capital pay-up on formation** — **Lda: at least half.** Article 176(2) allows the articles to defer *"não mais de metade"* of the cash entradas, and the deferral may not run beyond **three years** from the date of the contract. **SA: at least 30%.** Article 238(2) allows **70%** of the nominal value of cash-subscribed shares to be deferred, for a period fixed in the articles and capped at **five years**. Neither form admits contributions in industry. In both cases the cash actually paid must be deposited with a credit institution in an account in the company's name, and the deposit document produced when the registration of the incorporation is applied for  _(Código das Sociedades Comerciais, arts. 176–177 and 238 — https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/Download/2861)_
- **Legal reserve** — A constraint that matters more in practice than the absent capital minimum. An **Lda** must build a legal reserve of **not less than CVE 200,000**. An **SA** must build one of **a fifth of its share capital, and never less than CVE 2,500,000**, setting aside a twentieth of profits each year until it is complete, and **profits may not be distributed** until that minimum is reached or restored. Cite the SA rule as **art. 254(2) and (3)**: as printed in the Boletim Oficial, articles 253 and 254 begin at paragraph 2 and article 255 at subparagraph b), with no paragraph 1 or subparagraph a) anywhere on the page. The rectification did not touch it, so a reader looking for "article 254(1)" will not find one  _(Código das Sociedades Comerciais, arts. 184(1) and 254(2)–(3) — https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/Download/2861)_
- **One-stop shop incorporation** — *Empresa no Dia* is a **public service**, described by the Imprensa Nacional de Cabo Verde as allowing the incorporation of **Sociedades Anónimas and/or Sociedades por Quotas** *"de forma imediata, no próprio dia"* from a staffed Casa do Cidadão counter. Its stated design is that **eight separate interactions with the administration** — legal, fiscal, social and labour — are replaced by a single counter  _(Imprensa Nacional de Cabo Verde, Empresa no Dia — https://incv.cv/empresanodia/)_
- **Incorporation timeline** — **The operator says same day.** The previous version gave "approximately 1 to 2 weeks (faster via the one-stop shop)" on a commercial source; INCV's own service page says incorporation through *Empresa no Dia* happens **immediately, on the day**. Treat any longer estimate as describing steps outside that service rather than the incorporation itself  _(Imprensa Nacional de Cabo Verde, Empresa no Dia — https://incv.cv/empresanodia/)_
- **Notary fees** — Approximately CVE 5,000 to 15,000 for drafting and authenticating statutes CVE ((approx — confirm))  _(as described at [expanship.com](https://www.expanship.com/cv/blog/incorporation-requirements-in-cabo-verde))_
- **Commercial registration fees** — Approximately CVE 2,500 plus 0.2% stamp duty on capital where applicable CVE ((approx — confirm))  _(as described at [expanship.com](https://www.expanship.com/cv/blog/incorporation-requirements-in-cabo-verde))_
- **Statutory audit — read the gazette with the rectification** — An **Lda** without a supervisory body must appoint a certified auditor for the statutory audit once **turnover exceeds CVE 100,000,000 *or* the workforce exceeds fifty**. Boletim Oficial n.º 80 published this as *"10.000.000$00 (dez milhões de escudos) e/ou … superior a dez"*; **Retificação n.º 133/2019 replaced both figures and the conjunction**. Anyone reading the original gazette PDF alone gets the threshold wrong by a factor of ten and the headcount by a factor of five. Where an Lda does have a supervisory body, a *conselho fiscal* must include a certified auditor and a *fiscal único* must be one  _(Código das Sociedades Comerciais, art. 222, as rectified — https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/View?id=43751)_
- **Large SAs** — In a **sociedade anónima de grande dimensão** the certified auditor is a separate body and does not sit on the *conselho fiscal*, which must include an independent member qualified in audit or accounting. A company is of large dimension if, for two consecutive years, it reaches **turnover above CVE 200,000,000** or **total net assets above CVE 150,000,000**. Both figures come from the rectification: the gazette had printed *"200.000$00 (duzentos milhões de escudos)"* — digits and words contradicting each other — and CVE 15,000,000  _(Código das Sociedades Comerciais, arts. 239 and 342(5), as rectified — https://boe.incv.cv/Bulletins/View?id=43751)_
- **Chartered accountant requirement** — A chartered accountant must be appointed at incorporation under the fast-track procedure and for ongoing bookkeeping/tax filings. **The Companies Code is not the source of this**: its only accounting-professional requirements are the audit rules in the two rows above. If the obligation exists it comes from tax or registration law ((approx — confirm))  _(as described at [addleshawgoddard.com](https://www.addleshawgoddard.com/en/doing-business-in-africa/africa-countries-a-z-list/cape-verde/))_
- **Core annual compliance** — Annual IRPC return by 31 May, periodic VAT returns, monthly INPS and IRPS withholding remittances, and annual financial statements  _(Codigo do IRPC (Corporate Income Tax Code) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cabo-verde/corporate/tax-administration)_
