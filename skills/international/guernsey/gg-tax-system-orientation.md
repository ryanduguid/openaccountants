# Guernsey tax system orientation

The Guernsey Revenue Service administers income tax for Guernsey, Alderney and Herm. Guernsey uses sterling. Check the relevant island before applying Guernsey rules elsewhere in the Bailiwick. [Revenue Service, returns][PERSONAL] · [Locate Guernsey, currency][CURRENCY]

## Residence and income source

The ordinary year of charge is the calendar year. Section 3 generally treats an individual as resident at 91 days in the year, or 35 days in the year with 365 days during the preceding four years. Sole residence also requires fewer than 91 days in each other jurisdiction in that year. Principal residence can follow from 182 days in the year, or 91 days in the year with 730 days in the preceding four years. Check the separate permanent arrival test, midnight rule and exceptions. [Income Tax (Guernsey) Law, 1975, s 3 excerpt][LAW3] · [Revenue Service, residence][RES]

Solely or principally resident individuals generally come within the charge on worldwide income. Resident-only taxpayers generally also pay on worldwide income and may elect for the standard charge instead. Non-residents generally come within the charge on Guernsey income, subject to the type of income and applicable relief. Determine any treaty residence and source rules separately. [Locate Guernsey, tax residence][RESBASE] · [Revenue Service, non-resident returns][PERSONAL]

## Individuals and companies

The ordinary individual rate is 20% of taxable income. The 2026 standard personal allowance is £15,200. Qualifying allowances and deductions are withdrawn by £1 for each £5 of calculated income above £85,000, subject to conditions. Check residence, entitlement and the statutory income basis before using either figure. [Revenue Service, rates and allowances][RATES] · [2026 monetary values, p 1][MONETARY] · [2026 budget resolutions][BUDGET]

For companies, check registration, control and central management and control under section 4, including the conditions for any foreign residence exception. Ordinary company income is generally taxed at 0%. Specified activities, including banking and designated financial services, face an intermediate 10% rate. A 20% rate applies to categories including Guernsey property income, certain utilities and qualifying Guernsey retail business where taxable retail profits exceed £500,000. Identify the income stream and any applicable special regime separately. [Guernsey Revenue Service submission, s 4][CORES] · [Locate Guernsey, company taxation][CORP]

## Transactions and filing

Guernsey has no general VAT or GST in force for 2026. Check customs and excise separately. In October 2026 the States directed implementation of a GST package subject to conditions, including operational income tax, contribution and benefit measures. Check the implementing legislation and commencement before applying the reform. [Locate Guernsey, economy][VAT] · [Customs and Excise][CUSTOMS] · [States resolutions, 2 October 2026][GST]

The Revenue Service gives 30 November 2026 as the deadline for 2025 personal and corporate returns. Guernsey-registered companies file online, and eligible individuals can use MyGov or the paper return. Check the Revenue Service's separate agency and non-resident instructions if the taxpayer lives abroad. [Revenue Service, personal and agency returns][PERSONAL] · [Revenue Service, corporate returns][CORPRET]
