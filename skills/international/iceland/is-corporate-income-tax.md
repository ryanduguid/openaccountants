# Iceland Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax rates and base (2025)

- **Corporate income tax rate — limited liability companies (hf./ehf.)** — 20% percent for the 2025 income year (a temporary 21% applied to 2024 income, assessed in 2025)  _(Skatturinn, Helstu tölur og prósentur 2025 (key figures 2025, Icelandic) — https://www.skatturinn.is/einstaklingar/helstutolur/2025/ ; Skatturinn, Álagningarseðill og forsendur 2025 (assessment of legal entities 2025 for the 2024 operating year, Icelandic) — https://www.skatturinn.is/atvinnurekstur/framtal-og-alagning/alagningarsedill-og-forsendur/2025/)_
- **Income tax rate — partnerships and other legal entities (incl. general partnerships taxed as separate entities)** — 37.6% percent for the 2025 income year (38.4% applied to 2024 income, assessed in 2025) for partnerships taxed as separate entities, estates and the other legal entities in the upper bracket; these entities pay 22% on dividends received from limited companies  _(Skatturinn, Helstu tölur og prósentur 2025 (key figures 2025, Icelandic) — https://www.skatturinn.is/einstaklingar/helstutolur/2025/ ; Skatturinn, Álagningarseðill og forsendur 2025 (assessment of legal entities 2025 for the 2024 operating year, Icelandic) — https://www.skatturinn.is/atvinnurekstur/framtal-og-alagning/alagningarsedill-og-forsendur/2025/)_
- **Tax base** — Taxable income less the deductions allowed for the business (Act No. 90/2003 Articles 7, 31 and 61, as summarised by Skatturinn); worldwide income for resident entities  _(Skatturinn, Atvinnurekstur: Tekjuskattur (income tax of legal entities, Icelandic) — https://www.skatturinn.is/atvinnurekstur/skattar-og-gjold/tekjuskattur/)_
- **Non-resident companies** — Taxed at the same rates on Icelandic-source income and permanent establishment profits; a foreign service provider without a permanent establishment may apply for exemption under a double taxation convention, and withholding on dividends can be reduced or refunded on application  _(Skatturinn, Companies: Foreign service providers — https://www.skatturinn.is/english/companies/foreign-service-providers/ ; Skatturinn, Companies: Dividends — https://www.skatturinn.is/english/companies/dividends/)_
- **Inter-company dividends** — Dividends received by a resident company are effectively exempt — a deduction equal to received dividends generally eliminates tax (approx — confirm conditions)  _(Act No. 90/2003 on Income Tax (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/iceland/corporate/income-determination))_
- **Tax loss carryforward** — Losses may be carried forward for up to 10 years; no carryback (approx — confirm 10-year period)  _(Act No. 90/2003 on Income Tax (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/iceland/corporate/deductions))_
- **Withholding tax on dividends to non-residents** — **20%** for non-resident companies (21% for 2024), reduced under double taxation conventions; where tax was withheld at the full rate the recipient applies for a refund of the difference on form RSK 5.43, and EEA-resident limited companies have a refund route percent  _(Skatturinn, Companies: Dividends — https://www.skatturinn.is/english/companies/dividends/)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends to a resident company** — **22%** withheld at source on dividends paid to resident companies (the capital income withholding rate) and credited against the company's assessed taxes; higher than the 20% charged on a dividend leaving the country percent  _(Skatturinn, Álagningarseðill og forsendur 2025 (assessment of legal entities 2025 for the 2024 operating year, Icelandic) — https://www.skatturinn.is/atvinnurekstur/framtal-og-alagning/alagningarsedill-og-forsendur/2025/ ; Skatturinn, Helstu tölur og prósentur 2025 (key figures 2025, Icelandic) — https://www.skatturinn.is/einstaklingar/helstutolur/2025/)_
- **Withholding tax on interest to non-residents** — **12%**, for corporate and individual recipients alike. The 13% that PwC publishes is a **2024-only** rate: Skatturinn states plainly that "interest income tax rate is 12% except for 2024, then it is 13%", and KPMG's Icelandic Tax Facts 2026 gives 12%. An earlier revision of this guide read the PwC/statute gap as an unsettled rate and deducted at 13% on the rule of taking the higher-tax position. That rule is for rates genuinely in dispute; this one was simply dated, and the authority settles it percent  _(Act No. 90/2003 on Income Tax; Skatturinn (skatturinn.is/english/companies/interests); KPMG Icelandic Tax Facts 2026)_
- **Withholding tax on royalties to non-residents** — 22% percent for companies, whether limited or unlimited (individuals pay 22% income tax plus 14.94% municipal tax, 36.94% in total); reduced under treaties  _(Skatturinn, Companies: Royalties, lease etc. — https://www.skatturinn.is/english/companies/royalties-lease-etc/)_
- **Heads beyond these three: checked, not settled** — Whether Iceland withholds on service and consultancy fees, on artists, entertainers and sportspersons, or on rent paid to non-residents is **not answered by the source this guide cites**: PwC's Iceland withholding page covers dividends, interest and royalties plus a treaty matrix and nothing else. That is a gap in the evidence, not a finding that no such head exists — the two read identically from here. Ask Skatturinn or the Act itself before telling a payer that a service fee to a non-resident carries no Icelandic withholding  _(Act No. 90/2003 on Income Tax; Skatturinn)_
- **Corporate tax year** — Calendar year; other fiscal years require approval of the Internal Revenue Directorate  _(Act No. 90/2003 on Income Tax (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/iceland/corporate/tax-administration))_
- **Corporate tax return deadline** — 31 May of the year following the income year; accountants and bookkeepers filing for clients can obtain extensions to 30 September (2025 filing season)  _(Skatturinn, Opnað fyrir skil á skattframtali lögaðila 2025 (company tax return filing notice, 31 January 2025, Icelandic) — https://www.skatturinn.is/um-rsk/frettir-og-tilkynningar/opnad-fyrir-skil-a-skattframtali-logadila-2025)_
- **Advance corporate tax payments** — Monthly instalments of the previous year's assessed taxes fall due on the 1st of each month except January and October (PwC reports 8.5% a month from February to September) percent  _(Skatturinn, Álagningarseðill og forsendur 2025 (assessment of legal entities 2025 for the 2024 operating year, Icelandic) — https://www.skatturinn.is/atvinnurekstur/framtal-og-alagning/alagningarsedill-og-forsendur/2025/ ; PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries, Tax administration (monthly rate) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/iceland/corporate/tax-administration)_
- **Final corporate tax balance** — Any balance after the assessment, issued at the end of October, is payable on 1 November and 1 December  _(Skatturinn, Álagningarseðill og forsendur 2025 (assessment of legal entities 2025 for the 2024 operating year, Icelandic) — https://www.skatturinn.is/atvinnurekstur/framtal-og-alagning/alagningarsedill-og-forsendur/2025/)_
