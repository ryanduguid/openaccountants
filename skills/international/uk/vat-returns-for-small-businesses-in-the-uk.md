# VAT returns for small businesses in the UK

## VAT returns for small businesses in the UK

VAT returns for small businesses in the UK: how I do it

If you run a small business in the UK and you're VAT registered, you need to file a VAT return regularly — usually every quarter. This is how I handle it for clients, step by step.

## Who this is for

This is for VAT-registered small businesses doing a straightforward, routine VAT return.

It's not for you if there's anything unusual going on partial exemption, cross-border sales, group VAT registrations, or anything that looks messy. Those go to a specialist, not a quick guide.

## Before I start, I ask the client

What VAT scheme are you actually on?
When's this return due?
Do you have everything: sales, purchases, bank statements, invoices for the period?
Anything unusual happen this quarter? (sold an asset, imported/exported goods, wrote off a bad debt, etc.)

## How I do it

Get the records first. Don't start classifying anything until you have the full picture for the period.
Reconcile against the bank statements. Sales and purchases need to match what actually moved through the account.
Sort everything by VAT rate. This is where mistakes creep in, so take your time.
Work out the numbers. What's owed, what can be reclaimed, and the net VAT due (or due back).
Check it over before you submit. A second pass catches the errors a first pass misses.
File it through the proper channel.
Keep everything. Records and proof of submission, in case anyone ever asks.
