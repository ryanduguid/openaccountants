# United Kingdom tax system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the residence and taxpayer category for each period, where the income or gain arises, the currency and which authority administers the tax.

## Territorial reach

The United Kingdom comprises Great Britain and Northern Ireland under the Interpretation Act definition. The Channel Islands and the Isle of Man sit outside that definition. Individuals resident in the UK generally account for UK and foreign income and gains on the arising basis. Non-residents generally account for UK income and can have chargeable gains on UK land. From 6 April 2025, qualifying new residents may claim relief for eligible foreign income and gains within their first 4 tax years of UK residence after at least 10 consecutive tax years of non-UK residence. Check claim eligibility and amount separately. [Interpretation Act 1978, Schedule 1](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1978/30/schedule/1) · [HMRC, UK residence and tax](https://www.gov.uk/tax-foreign-income/residence) · [HMRC, 2026 foreign income and gains guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/foreign-income-and-gains-fig-regime-self-assessment-helpsheet-hs266/hs266-foreign-income-and-gains-fig-regime-2026)

A UK-resident company generally falls within corporation tax on income wherever its profits arise, subject to applicable relief and exemptions. For a non-UK-resident company check UK permanent establishment profits, UK land development and UK property income under s 5, and check the separate territorial charge on gains. Section 1313 deems specified continental shelf exploration or exploitation profits to arise through a UK permanent establishment in the cases it describes. [Corporation Tax Act 2009, s 5](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2009/4/section/5) · [Corporation Tax Act 2009, s 1313](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2009/4/section/1313)

## Residence

For each individual's tax year, apply the Statutory Residence Test. Check the automatic overseas tests, then the automatic UK tests and, where required, the sufficient ties test. Check whether split-year provisions apply on arrival or departure and document the days, work and ties. A company is generally UK-resident if incorporated in the UK or if its central management and control is in the UK. A treaty can treat a company as non-resident under s 18. [Finance Act 2013, Schedule 45](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2013/29/schedule/45) · [HMRC, Statutory Residence Test](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/rdr3-statutory-residence-test-srt/guidance-note-for-statutory-residence-test-srt-rdr3) · [HMRC, company residence](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/international-manual/intm154050)

## Taxpayer types

Identify the individual carrying on a sole trade, the company subject to corporation tax, the partners responsible for tax on their shares of ordinary partnership profits or the trustees and beneficiaries. The nominated partner manages the partnership return. A partner may itself be a company. Who is taxable on trust income depends on the trust type and the way income is received or distributed. Check special partnership and trust rules separately. [HMRC, business partnerships](https://www.gov.uk/set-up-business-partnership) · [HMRC, trusts and income tax](https://www.gov.uk/trusts-taxes/trusts-and-income-tax) · [Corporation Tax Act 2009, s 5](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2009/4/section/5)

## Currency

Use pounds sterling (GBP) for the UK tax result. Corporation Tax Act 2010 s 5 requires a company's income and chargeable gains for an accounting period to be calculated and expressed in sterling. Sections 6 to 9 and 9C set out how the rule applies in specified non-sterling accounts and gains cases. Use the conversion rule applicable to each item and retain the exchange-rate evidence. [Corporation Tax Act 2010, s 5](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2010/4/section/5)

## Assessment periods

The income tax year runs from 6 April to 5 April. A corporation tax accounting period begins and ends under ss 9 to 12, with the normal end at the earliest applicable event, including the end of 12 months or the company's accounting date. Check whether long accounts require more than one return and which dates apply for VAT, payroll or devolved taxes. [Income Tax Act 2007, s 4](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2007/3/section/4) · [Corporation Tax Act 2009, Part 2 Chapter 2](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2009/4/part/2/chapter/2)

## Responsible authorities

His Majesty's Revenue and Customs (HMRC) administers UK income tax, corporation tax and national insurance. It collects Scottish Income Tax and the Welsh Rates of Income Tax under the UK income tax system. Revenue Scotland administers Land and Buildings Transaction Tax, Scottish Landfill Tax and, from 1 April 2026, Scottish Aggregates Tax. The Welsh Revenue Authority administers Welsh Land Transaction Tax and Landfill Disposals Tax. Identify the tax and authority by transaction and location. [Commissioners for Revenue and Customs Act 2005, s 5](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2005/11/section/5) · [HMRC, Scottish Income Tax](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/service-level-agreement-for-operation-of-scottish-income-tax-by-hmrc/service-level-agreement-for-operation-of-scottish-income-tax-by-hmrc) · [Revenue Scotland](https://revenue.scot/about-us) · [Welsh Government, 2026-27 budget explainer](https://www.gov.wales/sites/default/files/publications/2026-01/final-budget-2026-2027-explainer.pdf)

## Fictional intake

Assume a UK-incorporated company buys land in Wales and keeps its accounts in euros. Confirm UK corporation tax residence and the accounting period, identify the applicable sterling conversion method and review any UK property income. Check Land Transaction Tax separately with the Welsh Revenue Authority. Verify the transactions, rates, elections and filing dates before preparing returns.
