# Jordan Payroll & Social Contributions

## Social security and payroll withholding

Employers in Jordan must register employees with the Social Security Corporation (SSC) and withhold both social security contributions and income tax from payroll. Contributions are split between employer and employee and capped by a monthly wage ceiling.

- **Employer social security contribution** — 14.25% of gross insurable salary percent  _(Social Security Law No. 1 of 2014 (https://www.ssc.gov.jo))_
- **Employee social security contribution** — 7.5% of gross insurable salary percent  _(Social Security Law No. 1 of 2014 (https://www.ssc.gov.jo))_
- **Total social security contribution** — 21.75% of gross insurable salary (14.25% employer + 7.5% employee) percent  _(Social Security Law No. 1 of 2014 (https://www.ssc.gov.jo))_
- **Employee contribution breakdown** — 6.5% pension (old-age, disability, death) + 1% unemployment percent  _(Social Security Law No. 1 of 2014 (https://www.ssc.gov.jo))_
- **Employer contribution coverage** — Pension, maternity, unemployment and work-injury insurance  _(Social Security Law No. 1 of 2014 (https://www.ssc.gov.jo))_
- **High-risk occupation surcharge** — Extra 1% on the employer contribution for high-risk jobs percent  _(Social Security Law No. 1 of 2014 (https://www.ssc.gov.jo))_
- **Maximum insurable monthly wage (ceiling)** — JOD 3,668 for employees who joined on/after 1 May 2010 (2025 figure) JOD  _(Social Security Law No. 1 of 2014 (https://www.ssc.gov.jo))_
- **Minimum insurable wage** — Equal to the national minimum wage (JOD 260 per month) JOD ((approx — confirm current minimum wage))  _(Social Security Law No. 1 of 2014 (https://www.cxcglobal.com/global-hiring-guide/jordan/payroll-and-benefits-in-jordan/))_
- **Payroll income tax withholding (PAYE-equivalent)** — Employers withhold income tax from employment income at the rates and procedures in the executive instructions and remit it to the ISTD within 30 days of payment  _(Income Tax Law No. 34 of 2014 as amended by Law No. 38 of 2018 (ISTD English translation), art. 12/A/4 and 12/E — https://www.istd.gov.jo/ebv4.0/root_storage/en/eb_list_page/income_tax_law_no._%2838_%29_of_2018.pdf)_
- **Social security remittance deadline** — Contributions due monthly, typically by the 15th of the following month ((approx — confirm))  _(Social Security Law No. 1 of 2014 (https://www.ssc.gov.jo))_
