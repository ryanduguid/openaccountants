# Jordan Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax rates and base

- **Standard CIT rate (most companies)** — 20 percent  _(Income Tax Law No. 34 of 2014 as amended by Law No. 38 of 2018 (ISTD English translation), art. 11/B/1 — https://www.istd.gov.jo/ebv4.0/root_storage/en/eb_list_page/income_tax_law_no._%2838_%29_of_2018.pdf ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jordan/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **CIT rate — banks** — 35 percent  _(Income Tax Law No. 34 of 2014 as amended by Law No. 38 of 2018 (ISTD English translation), art. 11/B/3 — https://www.istd.gov.jo/ebv4.0/root_storage/en/eb_list_page/income_tax_law_no._%2838_%29_of_2018.pdf ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jordan/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **CIT rate — telecom, insurance/reinsurance, financial intermediation, electricity, mining** — 24 percent (main telecommunication companies, electricity generation and distribution, basic material mining, financial intermediaries and financial companies, financial leasing, insurance and reinsurance)  _(Income Tax Law No. 34 of 2014 as amended by Law No. 38 of 2018 (ISTD English translation), art. 11/B/2 — https://www.istd.gov.jo/ebv4.0/root_storage/en/eb_list_page/income_tax_law_no._%2838_%29_of_2018.pdf ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jordan/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **CIT rate — industrial companies** — 20 percent standard rate. The 2018 amendment cut the tax due on industrial activities by 25%, 20%, 15%, 10% and 5% in 2019 to 2023 (50%, 30%, 20%, 10% and 5% for pharmaceuticals and clothing) for no more than five years (art. 11/C); the consolidated text carries no reduction after 2023, so confirm any current industrial relief with the ISTD before applying a rate below 20 percent  _(Income Tax Law No. 34 of 2014 as amended by Law No. 38 of 2018 (ISTD English translation), art. 11/B/1 and 11/C — https://www.istd.gov.jo/ebv4.0/root_storage/en/eb_list_page/income_tax_law_no._%2838_%29_of_2018.pdf ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jordan/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Development / free zone rate** — 5% on development zone income from transformational industry with at least 30% local value added and 10% on other development zone activities (art. 11/I/1 and 2); the Council of Ministers may lower these; free zone activities inside the zone bear the rate for the activity or person (art. 11/I/4), with the zone legislation's sales tax and customs exemptions  _(Income Tax Law No. 34 of 2014 as amended by Law No. 38 of 2018 (ISTD English translation), art. 11/I — https://www.istd.gov.jo/ebv4.0/root_storage/en/eb_list_page/income_tax_law_no._%2838_%29_of_2018.pdf)_
- **Tax base** — Jordanian-source income regardless of where it is paid (art. 3/A); a resident's foreign income that originates from Jordanian money or deposits, and a Jordanian company's foreign branch income, are taxed at 10% with no deductions (art. 3/B)  _(Income Tax Law No. 34 of 2014 as amended by Law No. 38 of 2018 (ISTD English translation), art. 3/A and 3/B — https://www.istd.gov.jo/ebv4.0/root_storage/en/eb_list_page/income_tax_law_no._%2838_%29_of_2018.pdf ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jordan/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Foreign branch net income** — 10 percent (Total net income of a Jordanian company's branch outside the Kingdom, per its audited financial statements, at a flat rate with no deductions)  _(Income Tax Law No. 34 of 2014 as amended by Law No. 38 of 2018 (ISTD English translation), art. 3/B/2 and 3/B/3 — https://www.istd.gov.jo/ebv4.0/root_storage/en/eb_list_page/income_tax_law_no._%2838_%29_of_2018.pdf ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jordan/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **National contribution tax (companies)** — National Solidarity Account contribution on taxable income: banks and electricity companies 3%; basic material mining 7%; financial intermediaries, financial and financial leasing companies 4%; telecommunication, insurance and reinsurance companies 2%; all other legal persons 1%  _(Income Tax Law No. 34 of 2014 as amended by Law No. 38 of 2018 (ISTD English translation), art. 11/F — https://www.istd.gov.jo/ebv4.0/root_storage/en/eb_list_page/income_tax_law_no._%2838_%29_of_2018.pdf ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jordan/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends** — Dividends from resident companies are exempt income (art. 4/A/8), except when received by banks, main telecommunication, basic mining, insurance, reinsurance, financial intermediation, financial and financial leasing companies; no withholding applies to exempt dividends paid to residents or non-residents  _(Income Tax Law No. 34 of 2014 as amended by Law No. 38 of 2018 (ISTD English translation), art. 4/A/8 and 12/B — https://www.istd.gov.jo/ebv4.0/root_storage/en/eb_list_page/income_tax_law_no._%2838_%29_of_2018.pdf ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jordan/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on interest/commissions (paid by banks/financial companies)** — 5% for natural persons and 7% for legal persons on deposit interest, commissions and participating deposit profits paid by banks and financial companies  _(Income Tax Law No. 34 of 2014 as amended by Law No. 38 of 2018 (ISTD English translation), art. 12/A/2 — https://www.istd.gov.jo/ebv4.0/root_storage/en/eb_list_page/income_tax_law_no._%2838_%29_of_2018.pdf ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jordan/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on non-resident services (imported services / royalties)** — 10% of any taxable income paid to a non-resident, withheld on the earlier of the due or payment date and remitted within 30 days; the executive instructions may make it a final tax (art. 12/B). PwC adds the national contribution on top  _(Income Tax Law No. 34 of 2014 as amended by Law No. 38 of 2018 (ISTD English translation), art. 12/B and 12/E — https://www.istd.gov.jo/ebv4.0/root_storage/en/eb_list_page/income_tax_law_no._%2838_%29_of_2018.pdf ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jordan/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on resident professional services** — 5% of fees paid by a resident legal person to resident doctors, attorneys, engineers, certified public accountants, experts, consultants, agents, brokers, arbitrators and similar service providers  _(Income Tax Law No. 34 of 2014 as amended by Law No. 38 of 2018 (ISTD English translation), art. 12/A/1 — https://www.istd.gov.jo/ebv4.0/root_storage/en/eb_list_page/income_tax_law_no._%2838_%29_of_2018.pdf ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jordan/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Advance income tax payments** — Two payments where business gross income in the previous tax period exceeded JOD 1,000,000, each due within 30 days after the end of each half of the tax period and equal to 40% of the tax computed on that half's financial statements (or on the last tax return where there are none)  _(Income Tax Law No. 34 of 2014 as amended by Law No. 38 of 2018 (ISTD English translation), art. 19/B and 19/C — https://www.istd.gov.jo/ebv4.0/root_storage/en/eb_list_page/income_tax_law_no._%2838_%29_of_2018.pdf ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jordan)_
- **CIT return filing and final payment deadline** — Return and payment of the tax balance within the four months after the end of the tax period (30 April for calendar-year companies)  _(Income Tax Law No. 34 of 2014 as amended by Law No. 38 of 2018 (ISTD English translation), art. 17/A and 18/A — https://www.istd.gov.jo/ebv4.0/root_storage/en/eb_list_page/income_tax_law_no._%2838_%29_of_2018.pdf ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jordan)_
