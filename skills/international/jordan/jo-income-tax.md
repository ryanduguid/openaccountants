# Jordan Personal Income Tax

## Personal income tax rates and bands

- **First JOD 5,000 of taxable income** — 5 percent  _(Income Tax Law No. 34 of 2014 as amended by Law No. 38 of 2018 (ISTD English translation), art. 11/A/1 — https://www.istd.gov.jo/ebv4.0/root_storage/en/eb_list_page/income_tax_law_no._%2838_%29_of_2018.pdf ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jordan/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **Second JOD 5,000 (5,001-10,000)** — 10 percent  _(Income Tax Law No. 34 of 2014 as amended by Law No. 38 of 2018 (ISTD English translation), art. 11/A/2 — https://www.istd.gov.jo/ebv4.0/root_storage/en/eb_list_page/income_tax_law_no._%2838_%29_of_2018.pdf ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jordan/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **Third JOD 5,000 (10,001-15,000)** — 15 percent  _(Income Tax Law No. 34 of 2014 as amended by Law No. 38 of 2018 (ISTD English translation), art. 11/A/3 — https://www.istd.gov.jo/ebv4.0/root_storage/en/eb_list_page/income_tax_law_no._%2838_%29_of_2018.pdf ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jordan/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **Fourth JOD 5,000 (15,001-20,000)** — 20 percent  _(Income Tax Law No. 34 of 2014 as amended by Law No. 38 of 2018 (ISTD English translation), art. 11/A/4 — https://www.istd.gov.jo/ebv4.0/root_storage/en/eb_list_page/income_tax_law_no._%2838_%29_of_2018.pdf ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jordan/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **JOD 20,001 to 1,000,000** — 25 percent  _(Income Tax Law No. 34 of 2014 as amended by Law No. 38 of 2018 (ISTD English translation), art. 11/A/5 — https://www.istd.gov.jo/ebv4.0/root_storage/en/eb_list_page/income_tax_law_no._%2838_%29_of_2018.pdf ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jordan/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **Top personal income tax rate** — 30 percent on taxable income above JOD 1,000,000  _(Income Tax Law No. 34 of 2014 as amended by Law No. 38 of 2018 (ISTD English translation), art. 11/A/6 — https://www.istd.gov.jo/ebv4.0/root_storage/en/eb_list_page/income_tax_law_no._%2838_%29_of_2018.pdf ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jordan/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **National contribution tax (individuals)** — 1% of annual taxable income exceeding JOD 200,000  _(Income Tax Law No. 34 of 2014 as amended by Law No. 38 of 2018 (ISTD English translation), art. 11/F/1/f — https://www.istd.gov.jo/ebv4.0/root_storage/en/eb_list_page/income_tax_law_no._%2838_%29_of_2018.pdf ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jordan/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **Personal (taxpayer) exemption** — JOD 9,000 (from 2020; JOD 10,000 for 2019)  _(Income Tax Law No. 34 of 2014 as amended by Law No. 38 of 2018 (ISTD English translation), art. 9/A/1 — https://www.istd.gov.jo/ebv4.0/root_storage/en/eb_list_page/income_tax_law_no._%2838_%29_of_2018.pdf ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jordan/individual/deductions)_
- **Family / dependents exemption** — JOD 9,000 for the taxpayer's dependents regardless of their number (from 2020)  _(Income Tax Law No. 34 of 2014 as amended by Law No. 38 of 2018 (ISTD English translation), art. 9/A/2 — https://www.istd.gov.jo/ebv4.0/root_storage/en/eb_list_page/income_tax_law_no._%2838_%29_of_2018.pdf ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jordan/individual/deductions)_
- **Additional exemption (medical, education, rent, housing interest/murabaha)** — JOD 1,000 for the taxpayer, JOD 1,000 for the spouse and JOD 1,000 per child up to JOD 3,000, against medical, educational, rent, home loan interest and murabaha expenses  _(Income Tax Law No. 34 of 2014 as amended by Law No. 38 of 2018 (ISTD English translation), art. 9/A/3 — https://www.istd.gov.jo/ebv4.0/root_storage/en/eb_list_page/income_tax_law_no._%2838_%29_of_2018.pdf ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jordan/individual/deductions)_
- **Maximum total exemption per family** — JOD 23,000 for the taxpayer and spouse together  _(Income Tax Law No. 34 of 2014 as amended by Law No. 38 of 2018 (ISTD English translation), art. 9/C — https://www.istd.gov.jo/ebv4.0/root_storage/en/eb_list_page/income_tax_law_no._%2838_%29_of_2018.pdf ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jordan/individual/deductions)_
- **Charitable / donations deduction** — Donations to public bodies and municipalities are deductible in full (art. 10/A); subscriptions and donations to approved religious, charitable, humanitarian, scientific, environmental, sport or professional bodies and to political parties are deductible up to 25% of taxable income (art. 10/B)  _(Income Tax Law No. 34 of 2014 as amended by Law No. 38 of 2018 (ISTD English translation), art. 10 — https://www.istd.gov.jo/ebv4.0/root_storage/en/eb_list_page/income_tax_law_no._%2838_%29_of_2018.pdf ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jordan/individual/deductions)_
- **Residence test** — A natural person who has effectively resided in Jordan for at least 183 days in the tax period, consecutively or not, is resident  _(Income Tax Law No. 34 of 2014 as amended by Law No. 38 of 2018 (ISTD English translation), art. 2, definition of resident natural person — https://www.istd.gov.jo/ebv4.0/root_storage/en/eb_list_page/income_tax_law_no._%2838_%29_of_2018.pdf ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jordan/individual/residence)_
- **Non-resident taxation** — Non-residents are taxed only on income generated in or from Jordan (art. 3/A); taxable income paid to a non-resident bears 10% withholding at source, which the executive instructions may treat as final (art. 12/B)  _(Income Tax Law No. 34 of 2014 as amended by Law No. 38 of 2018 (ISTD English translation), art. 3/A and 12/B — https://www.istd.gov.jo/ebv4.0/root_storage/en/eb_list_page/income_tax_law_no._%2838_%29_of_2018.pdf ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jordan/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **Return filing and payment deadline** — Return and payment within the four months after the end of the tax period (30 April for calendar-year taxpayers)  _(Income Tax Law No. 34 of 2014 as amended by Law No. 38 of 2018 (ISTD English translation), art. 17/A and 18/A — https://www.istd.gov.jo/ebv4.0/root_storage/en/eb_list_page/income_tax_law_no._%2838_%29_of_2018.pdf ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jordan)_
