# Serbia tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the taxpayer, where income arose, residence facts and period. Check any applicable double-tax treaty and relief.

## Territorial reach

Serbia taxes residents on income or profits from Serbia and abroad. For a nonresident individual, identify income earned in Serbia, including work performed or rights exercised there. For a nonresident legal entity, identify profit attributable to a Serbian permanent establishment and any payments or gains subject to separate nonresident provisions. Check the domestic permanent establishment test, its exceptions and the applicable treaty. [Personal Income Tax Law, arts 7–8](https://www.mfin.gov.rs/sr/propisi-1/zakon-o-porezu-na-dohodak-gradjana-1) · [Corporate Income Tax Law, arts 2–5 and 40](https://www.mfin.gov.rs/sr/propisi-1/zakon-o-porezu-na-dobit-pravnih-lica-1)

## Residence

An individual is resident if they have their residence or centre of business and life interests in Serbia, or spend at least 183 days there in a 12-month period beginning or ending in the relevant tax year. Count part-days, apart from time spent in transit. Check the statutory arrival, departure and posting rules. A legal entity is resident if established in Serbia or if its actual management and control are there. Determine treaty residence separately where 2 jurisdictions claim residence. [Personal Income Tax Law, art 7](https://www.mfin.gov.rs/sr/propisi-1/zakon-o-porezu-na-dohodak-gradjana-1) · [Corporate Income Tax Law, arts 2–3](https://www.mfin.gov.rs/sr/propisi-1/zakon-o-porezu-na-dobit-pravnih-lica-1)

## Taxpayer types

An individual's business income falls under the self-employment provisions. Check the actual-profit rules and any permitted flat-rate method. Companies and other profit-making legal entities are subject to corporate income tax. A general or limited partnership registered under the Serbian Companies Act is a company with legal personality. Cooperatives and non-profit bodies can also be within the corporate charge where they sell goods or supply services for a fee. Check the rules for payments to owners and the classification of any foreign arrangement. [Personal Income Tax Law, arts 31–33](https://www.mfin.gov.rs/sr/propisi-1/zakon-o-porezu-na-dohodak-gradjana-1) · [Corporate Income Tax Law, arts 1 and 40](https://www.mfin.gov.rs/sr/propisi-1/zakon-o-porezu-na-dobit-pravnih-lica-1) · [Companies Act, arts 2–3 and 8](https://www.apr.gov.rs/upload/Portals/0/privredna%20drustva/stvarni_vlasnici/Company_Law.pdf)

## Currency

The Serbian dinar (RSD) is the domestic currency. Tax collection is generally in dinars. Retain the original amounts and the exchange-rate evidence supporting values reported in dinars. [National Bank of Serbia Law, arts 53–54](https://www.nbs.rs/export/sites/NBS_site/documents-eng/propisi/zakoni/law_nbs.pdf) · [Tax Procedure and Tax Administration Law](https://www.mfin.gov.rs/sr/propisi-1/zakon-o-poreskom-postupku-i-poreskoj-administraciji-0809-1)

## Assessment periods

The ordinary personal assessment is based on the calendar year. Check separate collection and filing rules for each income category and for annual personal income tax. The ordinary corporate tax period is the calendar year, with shorter or special periods for events such as commencement, cessation, restructuring, liquidation or bankruptcy. A qualifying entity may obtain tax authority approval for a different 12-month period, subject to the prior financial reporting approval and the minimum 5-year use rule. [Personal Income Tax Law, arts 2 and 87](https://www.mfin.gov.rs/sr/propisi-1/zakon-o-porezu-na-dohodak-gradjana-1) · [Corporate Income Tax Law, art 38](https://www.mfin.gov.rs/sr/propisi-1/zakon-o-porezu-na-dobit-pravnih-lica-1)

## Responsible authorities

The Serbian Tax Administration (Poreska uprava) administers national income taxes. Identify the competent office and filing channel for the return or decision, including any request for a non-calendar corporate tax period. Check local tax administration separately for taxes within local jurisdiction. [Tax Procedure and Tax Administration Law, arts 2a and 160](https://www.mfin.gov.rs/sr/propisi-1/zakon-o-poreskom-postupku-i-poreskoj-administraciji-0809-1) · [Tax Administration annual personal income tax guidance](https://purs.gov.rs/upload/media/2026/4/17/760713/Your_tax_official_-_Annual_personal_income_tax_april_26.pdf) · [Corporate Income Tax Law, arts 38 and 63](https://www.mfin.gov.rs/sr/propisi-1/zakon-o-porezu-na-dobit-pravnih-lica-1)

## Fictional intake

An individual is present in Serbia for 100 days before year-end and 83 days in the first half of the next year. Check which 12-month periods begin or end in each year, exclude time spent in transit and check the centre-of-interests and arrival rules. Then classify the person's Serbian and foreign receipts for each period.
