# Turkey Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax (2025)

Turkish corporate income tax applies a flat 25% rate (30% for banks and certain financial institutions). From 2025 a domestic minimum corporate tax and Pillar Two top-up rules also apply. Resident companies are taxed on worldwide income.

- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 25% %  _(Corporate Tax Law No. 5520 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/turkey/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Financial-sector corporate income tax rate** — 30% % (banks, financial leasing, factoring, financing, insurance, capital-market institutions)  _(Corporate Tax Law No. 5520 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/turkey/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Domestic minimum corporate tax** — Effective 1 Jan 2025: corporate tax must be at least 10% of corporate income before certain exemptions/deductions; pay the higher of standard vs minimum calculation %  _(Corporate Tax Law No. 5520 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/turkey/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Pillar Two global minimum tax** — 15% QDMTT for MNE groups with consolidated revenue of EUR 750m or more; IIR from 2024 fiscal years, UTPR from 2025  _(Corporate Tax Law No. 5520 (Pillar Two / GloBE rules) (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/turkey/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Tax base** — Net accounting profit adjusted for non-deductible expenses, exemptions, deductions and prior-year loss carryforwards  _(Corporate Tax Law No. 5520 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/turkey/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Loss carryforward** — Tax losses may be carried forward up to 5 years; no carryback  _(Corporate Tax Law No. 5520 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/turkey/corporate/deductions))_
- **Withholding tax — dividends** — 15% on dividends paid to resident/non-resident individuals and non-resident companies (raised from 10% by Presidential Decree No. 9286, 22 Dec 2024) %  _(Corporate Tax Law No. 5520; Presidential Decree No. 9286 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/turkey/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **Withholding tax — interest** — **10%** on interest paid to non-residents under domestic law. The "10% over two years, 15% otherwise" split is a **treaty** rule, not a domestic one — PwC carries it as a footnote against particular treaties (Austria, Luxembourg, Portugal, Korea), and applying it to a lender outside those treaties over-deducts by 5 points. Turkish withholding rates sit in Presidential Decrees rather than in the Law itself and are changed often, so check the decree in force on the payment date, not the rate you used last year. Interest on cross-border lending by a **licensed non-resident bank or financial institution** is withheld at **0%** under Decree No. 2009/14593 art. 1/5-a; confirm the lender qualifies before deducting nothing, because an under-deduction is the payer's own liability % (0% financial-institution rate — confirm the lender's licence and the decree in force)  _(Corporate Tax Law No. 5520 art. 30; Presidential Decree No. 2009/14593 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/turkey/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **Withholding tax — royalties** — 20% on royalties paid to non-residents (treaty rates may reduce) %  _(Corporate Tax Law No. 5520 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/turkey/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **Withholding tax — professional services** — **20%** on professional services (consulting, audit, legal and similar) paid to a non-resident, reduced to **17%** for certain copyright-related work percent  _(Corporate Tax Law No. 5520 art. 30)_
- **Withholding tax — commercial rent** — **20%** on rent for a workplace, applied to the **gross** rent rather than the net percent  _(Corporate Tax Law No. 5520 art. 30; Income Tax Law No. 193 art. 94)_
- **Withholding tax — progress payments on multi-year construction** — **5%** on progress payments to contractors, domestic and foreign alike, for construction and repair work spanning more than one calendar year; this applies to payments made from 1 April 2025 for projects outside the exceptions. A **1%** rate applies to certain long-term construction and repair projects — confirm whether a given project qualifies before applying it, because the difference is four points on every progress payment percent  _(Corporate Tax Law No. 5520 arts. 15 and 30; Income Tax Law No. 193 art. 94)_
- **Withholding tax — payments to harmful-tax-competition jurisdictions** — **30%** on all sorts of payments to corporations established or operating in countries the Turkish Council of Ministers regards as undermining fair tax competition. This overrides the ordinary rates above and applies by reference to the payee's jurisdiction, so check the payee's country before concluding a lower rate applies percent  _(Corporate Tax Law No. 5520 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/turkey/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **Where these rates come from, and what to confirm** — The dividend, interest, royalty and 30% lines are PwC-sourced. The professional services, rent and construction progress payment heads are not: PwC's Turkey withholding page covers only dividends, interest and royalties plus a treaty matrix, so the rest were taken from Turkish practitioner sources and PwC Turkey's own bulletins. Turkish rates sit in Presidential Decrees and move often, so confirm each against the decree in force on the payment date  _(Corporate Tax Law No. 5520 art. 30; Presidential Decrees)_
- **Inter-company dividends (resident to resident)** — Dividends between resident companies are generally exempt (participation exemption); no dividend WHT on distributions to resident corporations  _(Corporate Tax Law No. 5520 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/turkey/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **Quarterly advance (provisional) tax** — Advance corporate tax declared and paid quarterly by the 17th of the second month following each quarter; credited against annual liability  _(Corporate Tax Law No. 5520 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/turkey/corporate/tax-administration))_
- **Annual CIT return & payment deadline** — Filed by end of the 4th month after year-end (30 April for calendar-year filers); tax payable by the end of that month  _(Corporate Tax Law No. 5520 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/turkey/corporate/tax-administration))_
