# Turkey Tax Overview

## Turkish tax system at a glance (2025)

Turkey (Türkiye) operates a national tax system administered centrally, with progressive personal income tax, a flat corporate income tax, and a broad-based VAT. The figures below reflect the 2025 calendar tax year. This is an unverified source-cited draft pending review by a licensed Turkish accountant (Yeminli Mali Müşavir / Serbest Muhasebeci Mali Müşavir).

- **Standard tax year** — Calendar year (1 January – 31 December)  _(Income Tax Law No. 193 (Gelir Vergisi Kanunu) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/turkey)_
- **Currency** — Turkish lira (TRY)  _(Central Bank of the Republic of Türkiye)_
- **Tax authority** — Turkish Revenue Administration (Gelir İdaresi Başkanlığı, GİB)  _(Gelir İdaresi Başkanlığı (Revenue Administration) — https://www.gib.gov.tr)_
- **Residence basis (individuals)** — Residents taxed on worldwide income; non-residents taxed on Turkish-source income only  _(Income Tax Law No. 193 — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/turkey/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **Residence basis (companies)** — Resident entities taxed on worldwide income; non-resident entities taxed on Turkish-source income only  _(Corporate Tax Law No. 5520 (Kurumlar Vergisi Kanunu) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/turkey/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Headline personal income tax rate range** — 15% to 40% (progressive) %  _(Income Tax Law No. 193; Income Tax General Communiqué No. 329 — https://orbitax.com/news/country/article/Turkey-Updates-Individual-Inco-57797)_
- **Headline corporate income tax rate** — 25% (standard); 30% for financial-sector entities %  _(Corporate Tax Law No. 5520 — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/turkey/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Does VAT exist?** — Yes — VAT (KDV) standard rate 20% %  _(Value Added Tax Law No. 3065 (Katma Değer Vergisi Kanunu) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/turkey/corporate/other-taxes)_
- **Corporate income tax return deadline** — By the end of the 4th month after fiscal year-end (30 April for calendar-year taxpayers)  _(Corporate Tax Law No. 5520 — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/turkey/corporate/tax-administration)_
- **Annual individual income tax return deadline** — 1–25 March of the following year; tax paid in two equal installments (March and July)  _(Income Tax Law No. 193 — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/turkey/individual/tax-administration)_
- **VAT return frequency and deadline** — Monthly; filed by the 24th and paid by the 26th of the following month  _(Value Added Tax Law No. 3065 — https://www.avalara.com/us/en/vatlive/country-guides/asia/turkey/turkey-vat-compliance-and-rates.html)_
