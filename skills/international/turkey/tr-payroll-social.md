# Turkey Payroll & Social Contributions

## Social security & payroll (2026)

Employers withhold income tax and social security from wages and remit to the tax office and the Social Security Institution (Sosyal Güvenlik Kurumu, SGK). The 2026 figures below are set under Social Insurance Law No. 5510 and the annual minimum-wage/SSI determinations.

- **Employee social security contribution** — 14% of gross wages within the ceiling: 9% invalidity, old-age and survivors insurance (MYÖ) plus 5% general health insurance (GSS)  _(Social Insurance and General Health Insurance Law No. 5510 (consolidated text, mevzuat.gov.tr), art. 81(1)(a) and (f) — https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.5.5510.pdf)_
- **Employee unemployment insurance** — 1% of gross wages within the ceiling  _(Unemployment Insurance Law No. 4447 (consolidated text, mevzuat.gov.tr), art. 49(1) — https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.5.4447.pdf)_
- **Employer social security contribution** — 21.75% of gross wages from 1 January 2026: 12% MYÖ (11% until 2025; Law 7566 raised the total MYÖ rate from 20% to 21%), 7.5% GSS and 2.25% short-term insurance branches (2% until Law 7524 of 2024). Private-sector employers who file their monthly returns and pay on time have two points of the MYÖ employer share met by the Treasury (art. 81(1)(ı)), giving 19.75%; the Treasury share was five points when this guide was first written, four points before Law 7566 and two points from 2026. Until October 2026 this guide read 20.75% and 15.75%  _(Social Insurance and General Health Insurance Law No. 5510 (consolidated text, mevzuat.gov.tr), art. 81(1)(a), (c), (f) and (ı), with footnotes 141, 142 and 149 — https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.5.5510.pdf)_
- **Employer unemployment insurance** — 2% of gross wages within the ceiling  _(Unemployment Insurance Law No. 4447 (consolidated text, mevzuat.gov.tr), art. 49(1) — https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.5.4447.pdf)_
- **State unemployment insurance contribution** — 1% of gross wages (State share); Law 7577 of 2 April 2026 lets the President raise or lower the State share by up to half  _(Unemployment Insurance Law No. 4447 (consolidated text, mevzuat.gov.tr), art. 49(1) — https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.5.4447.pdf)_
- **SSI earnings floor (2026)** — 33,030.00 TRY/month for 2026: the daily floor is the gross daily minimum wage of 1,101 TRY, taken for 30 days; 2025: 26,005.50 TRY/month  _(Social Insurance and General Health Insurance Law No. 5510 (consolidated text, mevzuat.gov.tr), art. 82(1) — https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.5.5510.pdf ; Minimum Wage Determination Commission Decision No. 2025/1 of 23 December 2025, Resmî Gazete 26 December 2025, No. 33119, item 2 — https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2025/12/20251226-6.pdf)_
- **SSI earnings ceiling (2026)** — 297,270.00 TRY/month for 2026: 9 times the daily floor, taken for 30 days (Law 7566 raised the multiple from 7.5 to 9 from 1 January 2026); 2025: 195,041.40 TRY/month at 7.5 times  _(Social Insurance and General Health Insurance Law No. 5510 (consolidated text, mevzuat.gov.tr), art. 82(1) and footnote 156 — https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.5.5510.pdf)_
- **Minimum wage (2026)** — TRY 33,030.00 gross per month for 1 January to 31 December 2026 (1,101 TRY per day); net 28,075.50 TRY after the 14% SGK and 1% unemployment premiums, because wage tax and stamp tax on the minimum wage are exempt; 2025: TRY 26,005.50 gross / TRY 22,104.67 net  _(Minimum Wage Determination Commission Decision No. 2025/1 of 23 December 2025, Resmî Gazete 26 December 2025, No. 33119, items 2 and 4 — https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2025/12/20251226-6.pdf ; Income Tax Law No. 193 (consolidated text, mevzuat.gov.tr), art. 23(18) — https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.4.193.pdf)_
- **Wage income tax withholding (PAYE-equivalent)** — Employers withhold income tax monthly on cumulative wages for the year using the art. 103 tariff (15% to 40%; for 2026 the 27% band for wages runs from 400,000 to 1,500,000 TRY and the 40% rate applies above 5,300,000 TRY); the tax attributable to the net minimum wage (gross minimum wage less employee social security and unemployment premiums) is exempt each month (art. 23(18))  _(Income Tax Law No. 193 (Gelir Vergisi Kanunu, consolidated text with the 2026 amounts, mevzuat.gov.tr), arts. 23(18), 94(1), 103 and 104 — https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.4.193.pdf ; Income Tax General Communiqué Seri No. 332, Resmî Gazete 31 December 2025, No. 33124 (5th repeated edition), art. 3(3) — https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2025/12/20251231M5-30.pdf)_
- **Stamp tax on wages** — 0.759% (binde 7.59) of gross wages and similar service payments; the minimum-wage portion is exempt  _(Stamp Tax Law No. 488 (Damga Vergisi Kanunu, consolidated text, mevzuat.gov.tr), attached table (1), section IV/1-b — https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.5.488.pdf)_
- **Payroll withholding remittance** — Wage tax and stamp tax withheld are reported in the monthly withholding and premium service return (muhtasar ve prim hizmet beyannamesi) by the 23rd of the following month (art. 98, combined with the SGK premium declaration under art. 98/A) and paid by the 26th (art. 119); these are the statutory dates and GİB may align the filing day with the payment day by circular. SGK premiums are declared on the same combined return  _(Income Tax Law No. 193 (Gelir Vergisi Kanunu, consolidated text with the 2026 amounts, mevzuat.gov.tr), arts. 98, 98/A and 119 — https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.4.193.pdf ; SGK premiums: Social Insurance and General Health Insurance Law No. 5510 (as reported))_
- **Severance pay ceiling (2025)** — 46,655.43 TRY (per year of service, tax-exempt cap) (approx — semi-annual ceiling; confirm period)  _(Labour Law No. 1475 (severance provisions) (https://turkishlaborlaw.com/2025-payroll-parameters/))_
