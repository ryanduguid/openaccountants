# Costa Rica tax system

> General orientation for 2026. Confirm the taxpayer, accounting period and transaction with a qualified local tax professional before applying these rules.

Amounts are in Costa Rican colones (CRC). The Ministry of Finance administers the profits tax (impuesto sobre las utilidades), tax on employment income and value added tax (IVA). [Income Tax Law No. 7092, art. 1](https://www.pgrweb.go.cr/DOCS/NORMAS/1/NOVIGEN/L/1980-1989/1985-1989/1988/2AD9/160D1A.HTML) · [Ministry of Finance TRIBU-CR](https://www.hacienda.go.cr/TRIBU-CR.html)

## Income tax

- **Individuals and residence.** The ordinary presence test counts more than 183 days in the relevant fiscal period, continuous or intermittent, including entry and exit days. Certain brief absences of up to 30 consecutive calendar days count unless foreign tax residence is established by certificate. Special domicile rules apply to certain public officials abroad. Profits tax generally covers Costa Rican-source income. An exception covers specified foreign passive income of non-qualified entities in multinational groups. [Income Tax Regulations, art. 10](https://www.pgrweb.go.cr/DOCS/NORMAS/1/VIGENTE/D/2020-2029/2020-2024/2021/176F8/14A9D8.HTML) · [Income Tax Law, art. 1](https://www.pgrweb.go.cr/DOCS/NORMAS/1/NOVIGEN/L/1980-1989/1985-1989/1988/2AD9/160D1A.HTML)
- **Individual rates.** For 2026, the gross monthly salary scale starts with an untaxed band through CRC 918,000. The marginal rates are 10% to CRC 1,347,000, 15% to CRC 2,364,000, 20% to CRC 4,727,000 and 25% above that. The ordinary annual scale for individuals with business income starts with an untaxed band through CRC 6,244,000 of taxable income. It then applies 10% to CRC 8,329,000, 15% to CRC 10,414,000, 20% to CRC 20,872,000 and 25% above that. Employment or pension income can reduce the untaxed band for business income. [Decree No. 45333-H, arts. 1 and 4](https://www.imprentanacional.go.cr/gaceta/?date=05%2F12%2F2025) · [Income Tax Law, art. 15(c)](https://www.pgrweb.go.cr/DOCS/NORMAS/1/NOVIGEN/L/1980-1989/1985-1989/1988/2AD9/17D02A.HTML)
- **Companies and small business.** The standard rate for legal persons is 30% of taxable income. Qualifying legal persons with gross income up to CRC 119,174,000 apply graduated rates to annual net income. These are 5% to CRC 5,621,000, 10% to CRC 8,433,000, 15% to CRC 11,243,000 and 20% above that. Check separate tax reductions for eligible newly operating micro and small businesses. [Income Tax Law, art. 15(a)–(b)](https://www.pgrweb.go.cr/DOCS/NORMAS/1/NOVIGEN/L/1980-1989/1985-1989/1988/2AD9/17D02A.HTML) · [Ministry of Finance 2026 scales](https://www.hacienda.go.cr/docs/TramosRenta2026.pdf)

## IVA and filing

- **IVA.** The standard rate is 13%. Reduced rates and exemptions apply to specified supplies. Ordinary monthly declarations and payment are due by the 15th calendar day of the following month, subject to deadline adjustments. Registered taxpayers generally still file for a month with no tax to pay. Check any applicable credit-sale deferral separately. [IVA Law No. 6826, arts. 10, 11 and 27](https://www.pgrweb.go.cr/DOCS/NORMAS/1/VIGENTE/L/2010-2019/2015-2019/2018/156A8/180B1D.HTML)
- **Filing.** The ordinary profits-tax year runs from 1 January to 31 December. File the return and pay the balance within 2 months and 15 calendar days of year end, ordinarily by 15 March for a calendar-year filer. Check special fiscal periods, partial payments and any adjustments to the deadline through the Ministry of Finance. [Income Tax Law, arts. 20 and 21](https://pgrweb.go.cr/scij/Busqueda/Normativa/Normas/nrm_texto_completo.aspx?nValor1=1&nValor2=10969&nValor3=109669&param1=NRTC&strTipM=TC) · [Ministry of Finance annual filing notice](https://www.hacienda.go.cr/docs/CP11-2026.pdf)
