# Malta tax system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record who receives each amount, the source, where foreign amounts are received, the taxpayer's residence and domicile and the applicable basis year.

## Territorial reach

An individual ordinarily resident and domiciled in Malta is generally taxable on worldwide income and chargeable gains. For a resident individual eligible for the ordinary remittance basis, include Maltese income and chargeable gains and foreign income received in Malta. Foreign capital gains are outside that basis even if received in Malta. Check the long-term or permanent residence rules, the spouse rule and any special regime before using the remittance basis. A non-resident individual generally comes within charge on Maltese-source income and chargeable gains, subject to exceptions. Maltese companies generally account for worldwide income and chargeable gains. For a foreign company, establish residence, domicile and the extent of any Maltese-source activity and remittances. Article 4(1)(iv) treats licensed or otherwise authorised activity in the exclusive economic zone as carried out in Malta and specifies how the transfer of assets situated there is treated. [Income Tax Act, arts 4, 5 and 5A](https://legislation.mt/eli/cap/123/20260310/eng/pdf) · [MTCA, remittance basis, paras 1.2–1.6](https://mtca.gov.mt/docs/default-source/documents/mtca-guidelines-on-the-remittance-under-the-income-tax.pdf) · [MTCA, corporate tax](https://mtca.gov.mt/business-tax/corporate/corporate_tax)

## Residence

For an individual, identify actual residence and reasonable temporary absences. MTCA treats presence of more than 183 days in a year as residence and treats someone who comes to establish residence as resident from arrival. Ordinary residence and domicile require separate facts. For a body of persons, determine where management and control of its business take place. The statute expressly includes companies incorporated in Malta, with historical commencement rules in the definition. Check any treaty for dual residence. [Income Tax Act, art 2](https://legislation.mt/eli/cap/123/20260310/eng/pdf) · [MTCA, tax residence](https://mtca.gov.mt/personal-tax/individual/tax-residence)

## Taxpayer types

An individual sole trader reports business income in their individual return. A company accounts for its own taxable income. For a partnership outside the tax definition of company, the applicable partnership return and the partners' own reporting are separate. Each partner reports the share to which they are entitled. Check any company-treatment election under art 27(6) and the deemed election for specified older partnerships. For a trust, identify income deemed derived by the trustee or the beneficiary, the trustee's tax responsibilities and any eligible company-treatment election. [Income Tax Management Act, art 27](https://legislation.mt/eli/cap/372/eng/pdf) · [Income Tax Act, arts 2 and 27B–27D](https://legislation.mt/eli/cap/123/20260310/eng/pdf) · [MTCA, partnership returns](https://mtca.gov.mt/partnership-income-tax-returns)

## Currency

The euro (EUR) is Malta's currency from 1 January 2008. The ordinary individual return uses euro. For a company in a fiscal unit, check the reporting rule tied to the denomination of the principal taxpayer's share capital. Keep the currency, conversion evidence and the method used for the applicable return. [Council Regulation (EC) No 974/98, arts 1a and 2 and Annex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/1998/974/2026-01-01/eng) · [MTCA, individual return booklet for YA 2026](https://mtca.gov.mt/docs/default-source/documents/personal-tax/individual/tax-return-booklet-individuals/mtca-tax-booklet-2026-eng-17-03.pdf) · [MTCA, fiscal unit guidance, Other considerations B](https://mtca.gov.mt/docs/default-source/documents/guidelines-in-relation-to-the-consolidated-group-%28income-tax%29-rules.pdf)

## Assessment periods

The year of assessment is a calendar year. For an ordinary individual, distinguish income of the preceding basis year from the year of assessment. For example, the 2026 individual return covers basis year 2025. For a company, the normal basis period is the financial year ending in the calendar year before the year of assessment. Verify any approved change of accounting date and the applicable filing and payment dates. [MTCA, YA 2026 individual return](https://mtca.gov.mt/news-results-page/2026/05/15/personal-income-tax-return---year-of-assessment-2026-%28basis-year-2025%29) · [MTCA, company income tax return cycle](https://mtca.gov.mt/business-tax/corporate/Income_Tax)

## Responsible authorities

The Commissioner for Tax and Customs administers and collects income tax, VAT, customs and excise and the applicable duties on documents and transfers through the Malta Tax and Customs Administration. MTCA also collects social security contributions. Identify the separate obligation and filing channel for each. [MTCA, functions](https://mtca.gov.mt/1/top-level-menu-bar/about-us/general-information)

## Fictional intake

An individual moves to Malta in September to establish residence, earns foreign dividends and sells an overseas investment. Record arrival, domicile, ordinary residence and any status affecting the remittance basis. Separate the dividends from the disposal proceeds, trace amounts received in Malta and identify the correct basis year before computing tax.
