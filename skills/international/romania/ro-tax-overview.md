# Romania Tax Overview

## The Romanian tax system at a glance

- **Tax year** — Calendar year (1 January to 31 December); art. 16(1) makes the fiscal year the calendar year for corporate taxpayers, who may opt for a different fiscal year under art. 16(5)  _(Fiscal Code, Law No. 227/2015 (ANAF consolidated text with methodological norms, updated to Government Emergency Ordinance 38/2026), art. 16(1) — https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/Cod_fiscal_norme_2023.htm)_
- **Currency** — Romanian leu (RON)  _(Romanian Fiscal Code (Law 227/2015))_
- **Tax authority** — Agenția Națională de Administrare Fiscală (ANAF — National Agency for Fiscal Administration)  _([Romanian Fiscal Procedure Code (Law 207/2015)](https://www.anaf.ro))_
- **Basis of taxation for residents** — Romanian legal persons, and foreign legal persons resident through their place of effective management, are taxed on profit from any source in or outside Romania (art. 14(a)); resident individuals are taxed on income from any source (art. 59(1)(a) and (b)); non-residents are taxed only on Romania-source income  _(Fiscal Code, Law No. 227/2015 (ANAF consolidated text with methodological norms, updated to Government Emergency Ordinance 38/2026), arts. 14(a) and 59(1) — https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/Cod_fiscal_norme_2023.htm)_
- **Headline personal income tax rate** — 10% flat rate on the taxable income of each category (art. 64(1))  _(Fiscal Code, Law No. 227/2015 (ANAF consolidated text with methodological norms, updated to Government Emergency Ordinance 38/2026), art. 64(1) — https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/Cod_fiscal_norme_2023.htm)_
- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 16% (art. 17)  _(Fiscal Code, Law No. 227/2015 (ANAF consolidated text with methodological norms, updated to Government Emergency Ordinance 38/2026), art. 17 — https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/Cod_fiscal_norme_2023.htm)_
- **Does VAT exist?** — Yes — VAT (TVA, taxa pe valoarea adăugată): standard rate 21% (art. 291(1)) and reduced rate 11% (art. 291(2)) from 1 August 2025, replacing 19% and 9%; the former 5% rate in art. 291(3) is repealed  _(Fiscal Code, Law No. 227/2015 (ANAF consolidated text with methodological norms, updated to Government Emergency Ordinance 38/2026), art. 291(1) to (3) — https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/Cod_fiscal_norme_2023.htm)_
- **Annual personal income & social contributions return deadline** — 25 May of the following year for the Declarația Unică on income tax and social contributions (art. 122)  _(Fiscal Code, Law No. 227/2015 (ANAF consolidated text with methodological norms, updated to Government Emergency Ordinance 38/2026), art. 122 — https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/Cod_fiscal_norme_2023.htm)_
- **Annual corporate income tax return deadline** — 25 June of the following year (Form 101, art. 42(1)) for calendar-year taxpayers, with quarterly tax paid by the 25th of the first month after quarters I to III (art. 41(1)); this guide gave 25 March until October 2026  _(Fiscal Code, Law No. 227/2015 (ANAF consolidated text with methodological norms, updated to Government Emergency Ordinance 38/2026), arts. 41(1) and 42(1) — https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/Cod_fiscal_norme_2023.htm)_
- **EU / VAT regime context** — Romania is an EU member state; VAT follows the EU VAT Directive (intra-Community supplies, OSS/reverse charge apply)  _(EU VAT Directive 2006/112/EC; Romanian Fiscal Code (Law 227/2015))_
