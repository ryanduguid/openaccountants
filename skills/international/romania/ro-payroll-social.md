# Romania Payroll & Social Contributions

## Social contributions and payroll withholding

- **CAS — employee social (pension) contribution** — 25% of gross salary (art. 138(a))  _(Fiscal Code, Law No. 227/2015 (ANAF consolidated text with methodological norms, updated to Government Emergency Ordinance 38/2026), art. 138(a) — https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/Cod_fiscal_norme_2023.htm)_
- **CASS — employee health contribution** — 10% of gross salary (art. 156)  _(Fiscal Code, Law No. 227/2015 (ANAF consolidated text with methodological norms, updated to Government Emergency Ordinance 38/2026), art. 156 — https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/Cod_fiscal_norme_2023.htm)_
- **Income tax withheld on salary** — 10% of the net base: gross salary less CAS and CASS and the deductions the Code allows (art. 64(1)(b))  _(Fiscal Code, Law No. 227/2015 (ANAF consolidated text with methodological norms, updated to Government Emergency Ordinance 38/2026), art. 64(1)(b) — https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/Cod_fiscal_norme_2023.htm)_
- **CAM — employer labour insurance contribution** — 2.25% of gross salary (art. 220³(1))  _(Fiscal Code, Law No. 227/2015 (ANAF consolidated text with methodological norms, updated to Government Emergency Ordinance 38/2026), art. 220³(1) — https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/Cod_fiscal_norme_2023.htm)_
- **Additional employer CAS — special/hard working conditions** — Employers pay an extra 4% for 'condiții deosebite' (particular working conditions) and 8% for 'condiții speciale' (special working conditions) as defined in Law 263/2010 (art. 138(b) and (c)); this guide had the two labels the other way round until October 2026  _(Fiscal Code, Law No. 227/2015 (ANAF consolidated text with methodological norms, updated to Government Emergency Ordinance 38/2026), art. 138(b) and (c) — https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/Cod_fiscal_norme_2023.htm)_
- **Combined employee deduction** — About 41.5% of gross before the personal deduction: 25% CAS, 10% CASS and 10% income tax on the remaining 65% (6.5% of gross); illustrative computation from arts. 64, 138 and 156  _(Fiscal Code, Law No. 227/2015 (ANAF consolidated text with methodological norms, updated to Government Emergency Ordinance 38/2026), arts. 64(1)(b), 138(a) and 156 — https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/Cod_fiscal_norme_2023.htm)_
- **Gross national minimum wage** — RON 4,050 per month from 1 January 2025 to 30 June 2026; RON 4,325 per month from 1 July 2026 under Government Decision 146/2026  _(Government Decision 146/2026 (Official Gazette, 13 March 2026), as reported by StartupCafe — https://startupcafe.ro/salariul-minim-2026-in-monitorul-oficial-hg-146-96219)_
- **Minimum CASS base for self-employed / non-salary income** — For rent, investment and other non-salary income the annual CASS base is 6, 12 or 24 gross minimum wages, according to whether the year's income reaches 6, 12 or 24 minimum wages (art. 170(3) and (4)); income from independent activities follows the separate base in art. 170(1)  _(Fiscal Code, Law No. 227/2015 (ANAF consolidated text with methodological norms, updated to Government Emergency Ordinance 38/2026), art. 170(3) and (4) — https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/Cod_fiscal_norme_2023.htm)_
- **Payroll tax & contribution remittance deadline** — Withheld income tax and social contributions are declared and paid with Form 112 by the 25th of the month following the month for which the income is paid (art. 147(1)); the employers listed in art. 147(4) may file quarterly  _(Fiscal Code, Law No. 227/2015 (ANAF consolidated text with methodological norms, updated to Government Emergency Ordinance 38/2026), art. 147(1) and (4) — https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/Cod_fiscal_norme_2023.htm)_
- **Construction / agri-food sector reliefs** — Repealed: art. 60 points 5 and 7, which carried the construction and agri-food income tax exemptions, are marked repealed in ANAF's consolidated Code; the text does not show the repeal date  _(Fiscal Code, Law No. 227/2015 (ANAF consolidated text with methodological norms, updated to Government Emergency Ordinance 38/2026), art. 60 points 5 and 7 — https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/Cod_fiscal_norme_2023.htm)_
