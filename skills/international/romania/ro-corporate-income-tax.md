# Romania Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax rates and regimes

- **Overview of CIT and turnover regimes** — Companies are subject to a standard 16% corporate income tax (CIT) on worldwide profit, with a 1% micro-enterprise turnover regime for small companies meeting strict conditions and additional turnover-based taxes for large firms, banks and energy companies. Withholding taxes apply to dividends, interest and royalties, subject to EU directives and tax treaties.
- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 16 percent  _(Romanian Fiscal Code (Law 227/2015) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/romania/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **CIT tax base** — Accounting profit adjusted for non-deductible expenses and non-taxable income; resident companies taxed on worldwide profit  _(Romanian Fiscal Code (Law 227/2015) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/romania/corporate/income-determination)_
- **Micro-enterprise turnover tax rate** — 1% of revenues (in lieu of 16% CIT) for qualifying small companies percent  _(Romanian Fiscal Code (Law 227/2015) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/romania/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Micro-enterprise revenue threshold** — **EUR 100,000** (RON equivalent) from 1 January 2026. It was EUR 250,000 for 2025 and EUR 500,000 before that, so a company reads its eligibility off the prior year: the 2026 test is revenue at 31 December 2025 against EUR 100,000. Crossing it moves the company to ordinary 16% CIT from the quarter it happens  _(Romanian Fiscal Code (Law 227/2015), as amended)_
- **Micro-enterprise eligibility conditions** — Must have at least one employee, not operate in banking/insurance/gambling/oil-gas, and meet shareholder-holding limits across related entities  _(Romanian Fiscal Code (Law 227/2015) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/romania/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Minimum turnover tax (IMCA)** — 0.5% minimum tax on adjusted revenues for companies with prior-year turnover above EUR 50 million (scheduled for elimination from 2027) percent  _(Romanian Fiscal Code (Law 227/2015) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/romania/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Additional turnover tax — credit institutions** — Banks pay a special turnover tax in addition to standard CIT (rate increased under 2025/2026 reforms — confirm current rate) ((approx — confirm))  _(Romanian Fiscal Code (Law 227/2015) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/romania/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Nightclubs & gambling operations CIT floor** — Greater of 5% of relevant revenue or 16% of taxable profit percent  _(Romanian Fiscal Code (Law 227/2015) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/romania/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends to non-residents** — **16%** on dividends distributed from **1 January 2026**, raised from 10% by Law 141/2025 (Official Gazette No. 699/2025, 25 July 2025), and it applies to every recipient alike — resident companies, individuals and non-residents. **The transitional rule decides most answers being asked in 2026:** interim dividends distributed during 2025 out of the 31 March, 30 June or 30 September 2025 interim statements stay at **10%**, including when they are regularised in 2026; a distribution out of the **31 December 2025 annual statements is 16%**, because those statements cannot be approved until after 1 January 2026. Ask which financial statements the distribution is based on before quoting a rate. Still 0% under the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive where a 10%+ holding is held continuously for at least a year; treaty rates may reduce it percent  _(Romanian Fiscal Code (Law 227/2015) as amended by Law 141/2025, Official Gazette No. 699/2025; EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive 2011/96/EU)_
- **Withholding tax on interest & royalties to non-residents** — 16% standard; 0% under the EU Interest & Royalties Directive where conditions are met; treaty rates may reduce percent  _(Romanian Fiscal Code (Law 227/2015); EU Interest & Royalties Directive 2003/49/EC — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/romania/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on management and consultancy services** — **16%**, and the scope rule is the one to notice: it applies **irrespective of where the services are rendered**. A consultant working entirely outside Romania for a Romanian payer is inside the charge, so "the work happened abroad" is not an answer here percent  _(Romanian Fiscal Code (Law 227/2015) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/romania/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on other services rendered in Romania** — **16%** on income from services provided in Romania. Outside management and consultancy, the place of performance does matter percent  _(Romanian Fiscal Code (Law 227/2015) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/romania/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on commissions** — **16%** on commissions paid to non-residents percent  _(Romanian Fiscal Code (Law 227/2015) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/romania/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Exempt from withholding** — Interest on Romanian **government bonds** and revenue from **international transportation** are exempt. Worth stating alongside the rates, because an omitted head reads the same as an exempt one and this guide carried neither percent  _(Romanian Fiscal Code (Law 227/2015) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/romania/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Penalty withholding rate** — 50% WHT on certain payments to jurisdictions without an information-exchange agreement, where transactions are artificial percent  _(Romanian Fiscal Code (Law 227/2015) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/romania/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Annual CIT return & payment deadline** — 25 March of the following year (Form 101) for calendar-year taxpayers; quarterly advance payments due by the 25th of the month following each quarter  _(Romanian Fiscal Code (Law 227/2015) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/romania/corporate/tax-administration)_
