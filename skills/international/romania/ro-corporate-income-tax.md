# Romania Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax rates and regimes

- **Overview of CIT and turnover regimes** — Companies are subject to a standard 16% corporate income tax (CIT) on worldwide profit, with a 1% micro-enterprise turnover regime for small companies meeting strict conditions and additional turnover-based taxes for large firms, banks and energy companies. Withholding taxes apply to dividends, interest and royalties, subject to EU directives and tax treaties.
- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 16% (art. 17)  _(Fiscal Code, Law No. 227/2015 (ANAF consolidated text with methodological norms, updated to Government Emergency Ordinance 38/2026), art. 17 — https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/Cod_fiscal_norme_2023.htm)_
- **CIT tax base** — Fiscal result = accounting revenue less accounting expenses, minus non-taxable income and fiscal deductions, plus non-deductible expenses (art. 19(1)); Romanian legal persons are taxed on profit from any source in or outside Romania (art. 14(a))  _(Fiscal Code, Law No. 227/2015 (ANAF consolidated text with methodological norms, updated to Government Emergency Ordinance 38/2026), arts. 14(a) and 19(1) — https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/Cod_fiscal_norme_2023.htm)_
- **Micro-enterprise turnover tax rate** — 1% of revenues in place of 16% CIT (art. 51(1)); the former 3% band in art. 51(1¹) is repealed  _(Fiscal Code, Law No. 227/2015 (ANAF consolidated text with methodological norms, updated to Government Emergency Ordinance 38/2026), art. 51(1) — https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/Cod_fiscal_norme_2023.htm)_
- **Micro-enterprise revenue threshold** — **EUR 100,000** (RON equivalent) from 1 January 2026. It was EUR 250,000 for 2025 and EUR 500,000 before that, so a company reads its eligibility off the prior year: the 2026 test is revenue at 31 December 2025 against EUR 100,000. Crossing it moves the company to ordinary 16% CIT from the quarter it happens  _(Fiscal Code, Law No. 227/2015 (ANAF consolidated text with methodological norms, updated to Government Emergency Ordinance 38/2026), art. 47(1)(c) — https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/Cod_fiscal_norme_2023.htm)_
- **Micro-enterprise eligibility conditions** — Tested at 31 December of the prior year: revenue up to EUR 100,000, share capital held by persons other than the State and local authorities, not in dissolution followed by liquidation, and at least one employee (art. 47(1)(c), (d), (e) and (g)); shareholders holding over 25% may designate only one company to apply the regime (art. 47(1)(h)); banking and the other sectors listed in art. 47(3) are excluded  _(Fiscal Code, Law No. 227/2015 (ANAF consolidated text with methodological norms, updated to Government Emergency Ordinance 38/2026), art. 47(1) and (3) — https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/Cod_fiscal_norme_2023.htm)_
- **Minimum turnover tax (IMCA)** — 1% of adjusted revenues (IMCA = 1% x (VT - Vs - I - A)) as a minimum for companies with prior-year turnover above EUR 50 million (art. 18¹(1) and (3)); applies until 31 December 2026 (art. 18¹(17)); this guide said 0.5% until October 2026  _(Fiscal Code, Law No. 227/2015 (ANAF consolidated text with methodological norms, updated to Government Emergency Ordinance 38/2026), art. 18¹(1), (3) and (17) — https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/Cod_fiscal_norme_2023.htm)_
- **Additional turnover tax — credit institutions** — Art. 18¹(2), which imposed the additional turnover tax on credit institutions, is marked repealed in ANAF's consolidated Code (updated to GEO 38/2026); the text does not show the repeal date, so check the amending act before advising a bank  _(Fiscal Code, Law No. 227/2015 (ANAF consolidated text with methodological norms, updated to Government Emergency Ordinance 38/2026), art. 18¹(2) heading — https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/Cod_fiscal_norme_2023.htm)_
- **Nightclubs & gambling operations CIT floor** — Where the CIT due on night bar, night club, discotheque and casino activities is below 5% of their revenue, 5% of that revenue is due (art. 18)  _(Fiscal Code, Law No. 227/2015 (ANAF consolidated text with methodological norms, updated to Government Emergency Ordinance 38/2026), art. 18 — https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/Cod_fiscal_norme_2023.htm)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends to non-residents** — **16%** on dividends distributed from **1 January 2026**, raised from 10% by Law 141/2025 (Official Gazette No. 699/2025, 25 July 2025), and it applies to every recipient alike — resident companies, individuals and non-residents. **The transitional rule decides most answers being asked in 2026:** interim dividends distributed during 2025 out of the 31 March, 30 June or 30 September 2025 interim statements stay at **10%**, including when they are regularised in 2026; a distribution out of the **31 December 2025 annual statements is 16%**, because those statements cannot be approved until after 1 January 2026. Ask which financial statements the distribution is based on before quoting a rate. Still 0% under the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive where a 10%+ holding is held continuously for at least a year; treaty rates may reduce it percent  _(Romanian Fiscal Code (Law 227/2015) as amended by Law 141/2025, Official Gazette No. 699/2025; EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive 2011/96/EU)_
- **Withholding tax on interest & royalties to non-residents** — 16% (art. 224(4)(d)); 10% for individuals resident in an EU member state or a treaty state (art. 224(4)(c¹)); exempt where the EU Interest and Royalties Directive conditions in art. 229(1) are met; treaty rates may reduce  _(Fiscal Code, Law No. 227/2015 (ANAF consolidated text with methodological norms, updated to Government Emergency Ordinance 38/2026), arts. 224(4)(c¹) and (d) and 229(1) — https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/Cod_fiscal_norme_2023.htm)_
- **Withholding tax on management and consultancy services** — **16%**, and the scope rule is the one to notice: it applies **irrespective of where the services are rendered**. A consultant working entirely outside Romania for a Romanian payer is inside the charge, so "the work happened abroad" is not an answer here percent  _(Fiscal Code, Law No. 227/2015 (ANAF consolidated text with methodological norms, updated to Government Emergency Ordinance 38/2026), arts. 223(1) and 224(4)(d) — https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/Cod_fiscal_norme_2023.htm)_
- **Withholding tax on other services rendered in Romania** — **16%** on income from services provided in Romania. Outside management and consultancy, the place of performance does matter percent  _(Fiscal Code, Law No. 227/2015 (ANAF consolidated text with methodological norms, updated to Government Emergency Ordinance 38/2026), arts. 223(1)(k) and 224(4)(d) — https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/Cod_fiscal_norme_2023.htm)_
- **Withholding tax on commissions** — 16% on commissions paid to non-residents (art. 224(4)(d))  _(Fiscal Code, Law No. 227/2015 (ANAF consolidated text with methodological norms, updated to Government Emergency Ordinance 38/2026), arts. 223(1) and 224(4)(d) — https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/Cod_fiscal_norme_2023.htm)_
- **Exempt from withholding** — Interest on public debt instruments is exempt (art. 229(1)(a)); international transport and its ancillary services are outside the charge on services performed in Romania (art. 223(1)(k))  _(Fiscal Code, Law No. 227/2015 (ANAF consolidated text with methodological norms, updated to Government Emergency Ordinance 38/2026), arts. 223(1)(k) and 229(1)(a) — https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/Cod_fiscal_norme_2023.htm)_
- **Penalty withholding rate** — 50% where income under art. 223(1)(b) to (g), (i), (k), (l) and (o) is paid into an account in a state that has no information-exchange instrument with Romania and the transaction is artificial (art. 224(4)(c))  _(Fiscal Code, Law No. 227/2015 (ANAF consolidated text with methodological norms, updated to Government Emergency Ordinance 38/2026), art. 224(4)(c) — https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/Cod_fiscal_norme_2023.htm)_
- **Annual CIT return & payment deadline** — Annual return (Form 101) by 25 June of the following year (art. 42(1)), with the annual settlement paid by the same date; quarterly CIT is declared and paid by the 25th of the first month after quarters I to III (art. 41(1)); this guide gave 25 March until October 2026  _(Fiscal Code, Law No. 227/2015 (ANAF consolidated text with methodological norms, updated to Government Emergency Ordinance 38/2026), arts. 41(1) and 42(1) — https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/Cod_fiscal_norme_2023.htm)_
