# Romania tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the taxpayer type, residence facts, income sources and relevant period. Check any treaty that assigns residence or limits the source country's tax.

## Territorial reach

Romanian-resident individuals generally account for worldwide income. Nonresident individuals account for items Romania can tax under the applicable source rules, including specified employment, business and Romanian immovable-property income. Romanian corporate income taxpayers, including foreign entities resident through their place of effective management, generally account for worldwide profits. For a foreign enterprise operating through a Romanian permanent establishment, identify profits attributable to the establishment and check any other Romanian-source receipts separately. [Fiscal Code, arts 12–14, 36–37 and 58–59](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/171282)

## Residence

Individual residence can arise through Romanian domicile, a Romanian centre of vital interests, more than 183 days in any consecutive 12 months ending in the calendar year concerned or the Romanian-citizen public-service posting branch. For an incoming individual meeting the days condition, article 59(2¹) generally starts the worldwide scope on arrival under domestic law. Check treaty residence and the applicable ANAF arrival or departure procedure separately.

Check whether a company is Romanian under the statutory formation or EU-seat rules. For a foreign legal person's place of effective management, article 7 point 18 requires genuine, substantive economic operations and at least one of the specified links. These are the taking of strategic decisions for the company as a whole in Romania or Romanian residence of at least 50% of its executive directors or board members under the provision. Check the ANAF determination and the treaty where relevant. [Fiscal Code, art 7 points 18 and 28, arts 8¹, 59 and 230](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/171282) · [ANAF, 2026 individual residence guidance](https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Cluj/cj_rezidentaPF_18iun2026.pdf)

## Taxpayer types

An individual conducting an independent activity accounts for that income under the personal rules. An ordinary limited liability or joint-stock company is a corporate taxpayer. Check separately whether an eligible microenterprise has entered the turnover-tax regime. An association without legal personality ordinarily allocates profit or loss to its participants in the agreed proportions, subject to the special rules for different participant types. Keep the written agreement and any applicable tax registration.

For a Romanian `fiducie`, check whether the settlor is also the beneficiary. Under article 30(1), in that corporate case the trustee keeps separate records and reports the administered income and expenses to the settlor. The separate beneficiary branch and individual tax rules can change who accounts for a transfer or receipt. Classify an arrangement established abroad under the relevant provisions and treaty. [Fiscal Code, arts 13, 30, 47–52, 67, 125 and 126](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/171282)

## Currency

Romanian accounts use the Romanian leu (RON). Foreign-currency transactions are recorded in both RON and the foreign currency. Apply the exchange-rate provision for the particular tax item and period. [Accounting Act, art 3](https://www.primariadeva.ro/images/uploads/documente/LEGEA_82.pdf) · [Fiscal Code, art 9](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/171282)

## Assessment periods

The ordinary individual income-tax year and corporate tax year are calendar years. An eligible corporate taxpayer with a different financial year may elect the corresponding modified tax year under article 16(5). Keep the election, transition dates and the relevant bookkeeping period. A microenterprise has a separate calendar-year rule. [Fiscal Code, arts 16, 50 and 63](https://legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocument/171282)

## Responsible authorities

The National Agency for Fiscal Administration (ANAF) administers ordinary national income taxes. Check the competent central tax office and its online filing instructions. The Romanian Customs Authority handles import customs administration. Local fiscal authorities administer applicable local taxes. [ANAF](https://www.anaf.ro/) · [Romanian Customs Authority](https://www.customs.ro/) · [Fiscal Procedure Code, arts 29, 30 and 37](https://static.anaf.ro/static/10/Anaf/legislatie/Cod_Procedura_Fiscala_2023.htm)

## Fictional intake

A person arriving in Romania stays 192 days in a qualifying 12-month period and receives freelance income from abroad. Record first arrival, travel and the location of the work, then check domestic scope from arrival and any treaty position. For a separate Romanian company using a non-calendar financial year, confirm the article 16 election before using that year for income tax.
