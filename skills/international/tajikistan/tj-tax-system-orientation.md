# Tajikistan tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the taxpayer, where income arose, residence facts and period. Check the applicable treaty and foreign-tax relief.

## Territorial reach

Residents generally account for taxable income from Tajikistan and abroad. Nonresidents generally account for Tajikistan-source income. For a nonresident carrying on business through a Tajik permanent establishment, identify domestic-source income connected with the establishment and the deductions allowed for that period. Other domestic-source receipts may be subject to withholding or a separate rule for asset disposals. Check any treaty and foreign-tax relief. [Tax Code, arts 17, 181–182, 222 and 239](https://andoz.tj/docs/kodex/TaxKodex.pdf)

## Residence

An individual meets the ordinary presence test with more than 182 days in Tajikistan in a 12-month period beginning or ending in the calendar year. Check the first and last days of residence when residence changes between years. Tajik citizens qualify under separate provisions, including government service abroad. Certain applicants for citizenship or permanent residence qualify if they have no permanent home abroad. Diplomatic, international organisation and foreign government service exceptions can apply. A legal entity is resident if formed under Tajik law or if its principal governing body is in Tajikistan. [Tax Code, art 14(2)–(8)](https://andoz.tj/docs/kodex/TaxKodex.pdf)

## Taxpayer types

Classify individuals, individual entrepreneurs and legal persons before applying the income tax rules. An individual entrepreneur operates without a legal entity under a patent or certificate. A written joint ownership or business agreement without a separate legal entity generally taxes participants on their shares, presumed equal when the shares cannot be determined. A foreign entity other than an individual is treated as an enterprise for income tax unless it establishes that it participates in joint ownership under art 94. [Tax Code, arts 14, 94 and 180](https://andoz.tj/docs/kodex/TaxKodex.pdf)

## Currency

Use the Tajik somoni (TJS) and generally convert foreign currency transactions at the National Bank of Tajikistan's official rate on the transaction date. Where no official rate is available, check the US-dollar cross-rate rule. Check art 90 for prior written approval to use an average rate and the separate conditions for an approved functional currency. [Tax Code, art 90](https://andoz.tj/docs/kodex/TaxKodex.pdf) · [National Bank of Tajikistan](https://nbt.tj/en/)

## Assessment periods

The ordinary income tax period is the calendar year for legal persons and for individual income without Tajik withholding, subject to the Code's exceptions. Withheld employment income ordinarily has a calendar-month tax period. Check the current corporate instalments and reporting dates separately. [Tax Code, arts 240–243](https://andoz.tj/docs/kodex/TaxKodex.pdf)

## Responsible authorities

The Tax Committee under the Government of the Republic of Tajikistan and its territorial offices administer ordinary national taxes. Check the relevant office for registration, declarations and certificates. Use the Customs Service for import procedures and the National Bank for official exchange rates. [Tax Code, arts 162–163](https://andoz.tj/docs/kodex/TaxKodex.pdf) · [Tax Committee](https://andoz.tj/) · [Customs Service](https://customs.tj/)

## Fictional intake

Two suppliers agree in writing to share a Tajik project without forming a new legal entity. Identify their shares and the evidence of joint ownership before applying art 94 or classifying a foreign participant as an enterprise.
