# Federated States of Micronesia Tax System Orientation

Load `workflow-base`. The national income tax law separates wages and salaries from business gross revenue. A separate national regime applies to major corporations within Title 54, Chapter 3, and Customs administers import taxes. Check obligations in Chuuk, Kosrae, Pohnpei or Yap separately. The Department of Finance and Administration's Customs and Tax Administration administers the national taxes covered here. [Title 54, chs 1 and 3][CH1] · [Major corporation rules][CH3] · [Customs and Tax Administration][CTA]

## Residence and income source

For ordinary national wages and gross revenue, start with the activity and source tests in Chapter 1. The wage charge applies to employees as defined, with rules for self-filing when an employer has neither a local place of business nor a responsible local agent. Section 124 presumes all compensation for services performed partly inside and partly outside the Federated States to be locally earned. Section 142 similarly presumes a mixed-source business's gross revenue is local, with an application for apportionment on the Secretary's prescribed form. Chapter 1 supplies no general individual day-count test for these charges. [Title 54, ss 112, 121, 124, 137–139 and 141–143][CH1] · [Public Law 21-250][PL21250]

## Taxpayer type

Identify the employment relationship under the statutory common-law test. The ordinary business definition applies to qualifying undertakings regardless of whether they are incorporated, and includes statutory exclusions. Check Chapter 3 separately for companies within the major-corporation definition in s 313. Its income charge and gross revenue tax exemption each have their own conditions. [Title 54, ss 112 and 141–143][CH1] · [Public Law 21-250][PL21250] · [Title 54, ss 313 and 321–323][CH3]

## Currency

The legal tender is the United States dollar (USD). Confirm the tax-specific conversion method for foreign-currency amounts under the rules for the particular tax. [Finance Management Regulations, s 4.2.1][USD]

## Assessment periods

Ordinary resident employer withholding returns and payments and ordinary business gross revenue returns and payments are generally due on 30 April, 31 July, 31 October and 31 January for the preceding quarter. Section 138 also sets dates for employees required to file under s 137. For major corporations, use the entity's fiscal year and Chapter 3 return and payment rules. Quarterly reporting alone does not determine how a wage rate band is allocated across pay periods. [Title 54, ss 132, 137–139 and 143][CH1] · [Title 54, ss 341 and 351–352][CH3]

## Headline rates

Section 121 states 6% on the first USD 11,000 of wages and salaries and 10% on the excess, with a separate low-wage refund rule under s 122. Confirm the applicable period and withholding method before applying the band. Section 141 states USD 80 per year on taxable gross revenue up to USD 10,000 and 3% on the excess, with a separate refund procedure for the exemption for annual gross revenue of USD 2,000 or less. Use the amended definition of taxable gross revenue for permitted deductions. Public Law 21-09 sets a 21% initial rate band for major corporations for years ending on or after 31 March 2020 and higher bands for larger amounts. The 2026 bill proposing new major corporation bands for years ending on or after 31 March 2027 requires an enactment check before use. [Title 54, ss 121–123 and 141][CH1] · [Public Law 21-250][PL21250] · [Public Law 21-09][PL2109] · [Bill 24-163][BILL]
