# Madagascar Tax Overview

## Madagascar tax system at a glance

Madagascar levies income tax, corporate tax, salary tax (IRSA) and VAT (TVA) under the Code des impôts, administered by the Direction Générale des Impôts, which publishes the consolidated code and the Code des procédures fiscales after each Loi de finances. This is an unverified draft for tax year 2025, with the 2026 changes noted, and must be reviewed by a licensed Malagasy accountant.

- **Currency** — Malagasy ariary (MGA, written Ar in the code)  _(Code des impôts 2026 (Loi de finances 2026), art. 01.03.16 — https://www.impots.mg/explorer?path=/legislation/Codes%20et%20Manuels/CDI-LFI%202026.pdf)_
- **Tax authority** — Direction Générale des Impôts (DGI), Ministère de l'Économie et des Finances  _(DGI — https://www.impots.mg/)_
- **Standard tax / fiscal year** — Calendar year (31 December); a company may close its financial year on 30 June or another date, with the return due by the 15th of the fourth month after the close  _(Code des procédures fiscales 2026, art. I-11 — https://www.impots.mg/explorer?path=/legislation/Codes%20et%20Manuels/CPF-LFI%202026.pdf)_
- **Basis of taxation** — Residents are taxable on income from any source; non-residents on Malagasy-source income, including salary for work performed in Madagascar  _(Code des impôts 2026 (Loi de finances 2026), art. 01.03.04 and 01.03.05 — https://www.impots.mg/explorer?path=/legislation/Codes%20et%20Manuels/CDI-LFI%202026.pdf)_
- **Headline top personal salary-tax (IRSA) rate** — 20% in 2025; 25% above MGA 4,000,000 a month from 2026  _(Code des impôts 2026, art. 01.03.16 — https://www.impots.mg/explorer?path=/legislation/Codes%20et%20Manuels/CDI-LFI%202026.pdf ; Code des impôts 2025, art. 01.03.16 — https://www.impots.mg/explorer?path=/legislation/Codes%20et%20Manuels/CDI%20_%20LFI%202025.pdf)_
- **Headline corporate income tax (IR) rate** — 20%  _(Code des impôts 2026 (Loi de finances 2026), art. 01.01.14 I-A — https://www.impots.mg/explorer?path=/legislation/Codes%20et%20Manuels/CDI-LFI%202026.pdf)_
- **Does VAT/GST exist?** — Yes — Taxe sur la Valeur Ajoutée (TVA) at a standard rate of 20%  _(Code des impôts 2026 (Loi de finances 2026), art. 06.01.12 — https://www.impots.mg/explorer?path=/legislation/Codes%20et%20Manuels/CDI-LFI%202026.pdf)_
- **Annual corporate/individual income tax return deadline (31 Dec year-end)** — 15 May following the close of the financial year  _(Code des procédures fiscales 2026, art. I-11 — https://www.impots.mg/explorer?path=/legislation/Codes%20et%20Manuels/CPF-LFI%202026.pdf)_
- **VAT filing frequency** — Monthly — return and payment due by the 15th of the following month  _(Code des procédures fiscales 2026, art. I-104 — https://www.impots.mg/explorer?path=/legislation/Codes%20et%20Manuels/CPF-LFI%202026.pdf ; deadline per https://manao.mg/fr/declaration-mensuelle-tva)_
- **Turnover threshold for the 'real' tax regime / VAT liability** — MGA 400,000,000 annual turnover  _(Code des impôts 2026 (Loi de finances 2026), art. 01.01.13 and 06.01.04 — https://www.impots.mg/explorer?path=/legislation/Codes%20et%20Manuels/CDI-LFI%202026.pdf)_
