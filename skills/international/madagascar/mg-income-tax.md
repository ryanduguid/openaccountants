# Madagascar Personal Income Tax

## Salary income tax (IRSA) bands and rates

IRSA (impôt sur les revenus salariaux et assimilés) is a monthly withholding on employment income under Title III of the Code des impôts. The figures below are read from the consolidated codes that the Direction Générale des Impôts publishes for the Loi de finances 2025 and the Loi de finances 2026. The 2026 code adds a 25% band above MGA 4,000,000 a month and leaves the other bands, the minimum and the dependant reduction unchanged.

- **IRSA band 1 — up to MGA 350,000 / month** — 0%  _(Code des impôts 2026 (Loi de finances 2026), art. 01.03.16 A — https://www.impots.mg/explorer?path=/legislation/Codes%20et%20Manuels/CDI-LFI%202026.pdf)_
- **IRSA band 2 — MGA 350,001 to 400,000 / month** — 5%  _(Code des impôts 2026 (Loi de finances 2026), art. 01.03.16 A — https://www.impots.mg/explorer?path=/legislation/Codes%20et%20Manuels/CDI-LFI%202026.pdf)_
- **IRSA band 3 — MGA 400,001 to 500,000 / month** — 10%  _(Code des impôts 2026 (Loi de finances 2026), art. 01.03.16 A — https://www.impots.mg/explorer?path=/legislation/Codes%20et%20Manuels/CDI-LFI%202026.pdf)_
- **IRSA band 4 — MGA 500,001 to 600,000 / month** — 15%  _(Code des impôts 2026 (Loi de finances 2026), art. 01.03.16 A — https://www.impots.mg/explorer?path=/legislation/Codes%20et%20Manuels/CDI-LFI%202026.pdf)_
- **IRSA band 5 — MGA 600,001 to 4,000,000 / month** — 20% (for 2025 the 20% band had no upper limit)  _(Code des impôts 2026, art. 01.03.16 A — https://www.impots.mg/explorer?path=/legislation/Codes%20et%20Manuels/CDI-LFI%202026.pdf ; Code des impôts 2025, art. 01.03.16 — https://www.impots.mg/explorer?path=/legislation/Codes%20et%20Manuels/CDI%20_%20LFI%202025.pdf)_
- **IRSA band 6 — above MGA 4,000,000 / month, from 2026** — 25%; introduced by the Loi de finances 2026 and absent from the 2025 scale  _(Code des impôts 2026 (Loi de finances 2026), art. 01.03.16 A — https://www.impots.mg/explorer?path=/legislation/Codes%20et%20Manuels/CDI-LFI%202026.pdf)_
- **Tax-free monthly threshold** — 350,000 MGA; the monthly base is rounded down to the nearest hundred ariary  _(Code des impôts 2026 (Loi de finances 2026), art. 01.03.16 — https://www.impots.mg/explorer?path=/legislation/Codes%20et%20Manuels/CDI-LFI%202026.pdf)_
- **Minimum IRSA payable** — 3,000 MGA per month regardless of income; the dependant reduction cannot take the tax below this floor  _(Code des impôts 2026 (Loi de finances 2026), art. 01.03.16 and 01.03.19 — https://www.impots.mg/explorer?path=/legislation/Codes%20et%20Manuels/CDI-LFI%202026.pdf)_
- **Reduction per dependant** — 2,000 MGA per dependant per month for taxpayers whose taxable income exceeds MGA 350,000; dependants are the taxpayer's or spouse's children under 21 at 1 January (under 25 if studying, no age limit if disabled) and the other persons listed in the article, none with separate income  _(Code des impôts 2026 (Loi de finances 2026), art. 01.03.19 — https://www.impots.mg/explorer?path=/legislation/Codes%20et%20Manuels/CDI-LFI%202026.pdf)_
- **Deductibility of mandatory social contributions** — Employee pension contributions (CNaPS) are deductible up to 10% of the gross pay subject to withholding; employee contributions to a company health organisation or health insurer (OSTIE and similar) up to 2% of gross salary  _(Code des impôts 2026 (Loi de finances 2026), art. 01.03.09 — https://www.impots.mg/explorer?path=/legislation/Codes%20et%20Manuels/CDI-LFI%202026.pdf)_
- **Non-resident salary income** — Residents are taxable on employment income from any source; non-residents on Malagasy-source employment income, which includes pay for work performed in Madagascar and pay from an employer in Madagascar; staff of NGOs, international bodies, embassies and projects are taxable whether resident or not  _(Code des impôts 2026 (Loi de finances 2026), art. 01.03.02, 01.03.04 and 01.03.05 — https://www.impots.mg/explorer?path=/legislation/Codes%20et%20Manuels/CDI-LFI%202026.pdf)_
- **IRSA collection mechanism** — Withheld by the employer at each payment and remitted within the first 15 days of the following month; employers under the synthetic regime may remit every two months  _(Code des procédures fiscales 2026, art. I-20 and I-21 — https://www.impots.mg/explorer?path=/legislation/Codes%20et%20Manuels/CPF-LFI%202026.pdf)_
- **Dependant reduction for individuals taxed on business income (IR)** — 24,000 MGA a year per dependant  _(Code des impôts 2026 (Loi de finances 2026), art. 01.01.16 — https://www.impots.mg/explorer?path=/legislation/Codes%20et%20Manuels/CDI-LFI%202026.pdf)_
- **Annual filing for individuals with business income under the synthetic regime** — Impôt synthétique declared and paid by 31 March of the following year, with advance payments due by 31 March and 30 June of the current year  _(Code des procédures fiscales 2026, art. I-17 — https://www.impots.mg/explorer?path=/legislation/Codes%20et%20Manuels/CPF-LFI%202026.pdf)_
