# Madagascar VAT / GST

## Value-added tax (TVA)

- **Value-added tax (TVA) overview** — Madagascar applies a value-added tax (Taxe sur la Valeur Ajoutée, TVA) at a standard rate of 20%. Registration is mandatory once annual turnover reaches MGA 400 million; returns are filed monthly. The provisions below are read from the consolidated Code des impôts for the Loi de finances 2026, Title VI  _(Code des impôts 2026, art. 06.01.04, 06.01.12 and 06.01.16 — https://www.impots.mg/explorer?path=/legislation/Codes%20et%20Manuels/CDI-LFI%202026.pdf)_
- **Standard TVA rate** — 20%  _(Code des impôts 2026 (Loi de finances 2026), art. 06.01.12 — https://www.impots.mg/explorer?path=/legislation/Codes%20et%20Manuels/CDI-LFI%202026.pdf)_
- **Zero rate on exports** — 0% on exports of goods and services  _(Code des impôts 2026 (Loi de finances 2026), art. 06.01.12 — https://www.impots.mg/explorer?path=/legislation/Codes%20et%20Manuels/CDI-LFI%202026.pdf)_
- **Reduced rate — butane gas** — 10% on imports and sales of butane gas and its containers (customs headings 2711.13 00 and 7311.00 00)  _(Code des impôts 2026 (Loi de finances 2026), art. 06.01.12 — https://www.impots.mg/explorer?path=/legislation/Codes%20et%20Manuels/CDI-LFI%202026.pdf)_
- **Exempt supplies** — The exemption list in art. 06.01.06 includes school and training fees, services of the health professions, medicines and medical equipment, inputs and equipment for agriculture, and educational books and printed matter  _(Code des impôts 2026 (Loi de finances 2026), art. 06.01.06 — https://www.impots.mg/explorer?path=/legislation/Codes%20et%20Manuels/CDI-LFI%202026.pdf)_
- **VAT registration threshold** — 400,000,000 MGA (mandatory at annual turnover excluding tax of MGA 400,000,000 or more); a non-resident provider making taxable supplies in Madagascar is liable whatever its turnover  _(Code des impôts 2026 (Loi de finances 2026), art. 06.01.04 — https://www.impots.mg/explorer?path=/legislation/Codes%20et%20Manuels/CDI-LFI%202026.pdf)_
- **Filing frequency and deadline** — Monthly return; the tax is computed by the taxpayer for each monthly period, and the return and payment are due by the 15th of the following month  _(Code des procédures fiscales 2026, art. I-104 — https://www.impots.mg/explorer?path=/legislation/Codes%20et%20Manuels/CPF-LFI%202026.pdf ; Code des impôts 2026, art. 06.01.16 — https://www.impots.mg/explorer?path=/legislation/Codes%20et%20Manuels/CDI-LFI%202026.pdf ; deadline per https://manao.mg/fr/declaration-mensuelle-tva)_
- **VAT payable computation** — Output TVA on the period's operations less deductible input TVA, declared on the monthly return  _(Code des impôts 2026 (Loi de finances 2026), art. 06.01.16 and 06.01.17 — https://www.impots.mg/explorer?path=/legislation/Codes%20et%20Manuels/CDI-LFI%202026.pdf)_
- **VAT credits and refunds** — Excess input tax is a credit carried forward; refunds may be claimed by free-zone companies, export professionals, approved lessors and any registered business making investments, with the export refund prorated to export turnover  _(Code des impôts 2026 (Loi de finances 2026), art. 06.01.23 and 06.01.24 — https://www.impots.mg/explorer?path=/legislation/Codes%20et%20Manuels/CDI-LFI%202026.pdf)_
- **Non-resident service providers (reverse charge)** — A provider with no establishment in Madagascar must accredit a local representative; failing that, the recipient of the service computes and remits the TVA on a separate return within the same deadline, and may deduct it under the normal rules if registered  _(Code des impôts 2026 (Loi de finances 2026), art. 06.01.09 bis — https://www.impots.mg/explorer?path=/legislation/Codes%20et%20Manuels/CDI-LFI%202026.pdf)_
