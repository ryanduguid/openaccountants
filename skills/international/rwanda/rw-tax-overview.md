# Rwanda Tax Overview

## Rwanda tax system at a glance

Rwanda's taxes are administered by the Rwanda Revenue Authority (RRA) and largely codified in Law No. 027/2022 of 20/10/2022 establishing taxes on income (the Income Tax Law). A multi-year reform package (2025-2030) is phasing the corporate rate down and pension contributions up.

- **National tax authority** — Rwanda Revenue Authority (RRA)  _([Law establishing the Rwanda Revenue Authority](https://www.rra.gov.rw))_
- **Currency** — Rwandan franc (RWF)  _([RRA general guidance](https://www.rra.gov.rw))_
- **Standard tax period (fiscal year)** — Calendar year, 1 January to 31 December; the Minister may authorise a different 12-month period on written request  _(Law nº 027/2022 of 20/10/2022 establishing taxes on income (Official Gazette nº Special of 28/10/2022), Rwanda Revenue Authority copy, Art. 8 — https://www.rra.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Income_Tax_law_of_2022.pdf)_
- **Basis of taxation** — Residents (individuals with a permanent residence or habitual abode in Rwanda or present for 183 days or more in the tax period; entities established under Rwandan law or effectively managed in Rwanda) are taxed on worldwide income with a foreign tax credit; non-residents are taxed only on Rwandan-source income  _(Law nº 027/2022 of 20/10/2022 establishing taxes on income (Official Gazette nº Special of 28/10/2022), Rwanda Revenue Authority copy, Arts 4, 6 and 7 — https://www.rra.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Income_Tax_law_of_2022.pdf)_
- **Top personal income tax rate** — 30 percent (on annual taxable income above RWF 2,400,000; 0% to RWF 720,000, 10% to RWF 1,200,000 and 20% to RWF 2,400,000)  _(Law nº 027/2022 of 20/10/2022 establishing taxes on income (Official Gazette nº Special of 28/10/2022), Rwanda Revenue Authority copy, Art. 14 (table 2) — https://www.rra.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Income_Tax_law_of_2022.pdf)_
- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 28 percent (Article 48 as replaced by Law 051/2023, in force on its publication on 14 September 2023; 30% before that)  _(Law nº 051/2023 of 05/09/2023 amending Law nº 027/2022 (Official Gazette nº Special of 14/09/2023), Rwanda Revenue Authority copy, Art. 2 (replacing Art. 48) — https://www.rra.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/LAW_AMENDING_THE_INCOME_TAX_LAW_of_2022.pdf ; Law nº 027/2022 of 20/10/2022 establishing taxes on income (Official Gazette nº Special of 28/10/2022), Rwanda Revenue Authority copy, Art. 48 — https://www.rra.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Income_Tax_law_of_2022.pdf)_
- **Does Rwanda have VAT?** — 18 percent (Yes — standard VAT rate)  _(Law nº 049/2023 of 05/09/2023 establishing value added tax, Rwanda Revenue Authority copy, Art. 4 — https://www.rra.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/LAW_ESTABLISHING_VALUE_ADDED_TAX.pdf)_
- **Annual income tax return deadline** — Declaration and payment not later than 31 March of the year following the tax period (within three months after the end of an approved non-calendar period)  _(Law nº 027/2022 of 20/10/2022 establishing taxes on income (Official Gazette nº Special of 28/10/2022), Rwanda Revenue Authority copy, Arts 9 and 10 — https://www.rra.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Income_Tax_law_of_2022.pdf)_
- **Monthly PAYE and VAT remittance deadline** — By the 15th day of the month following the period  _([Tax Procedures Law](https://www.rra.gov.rw))_
- **Digital services tax (DST)** — 1.5 percent of the gross revenues sourced from Rwanda of a company supplying digital services in Rwanda with a substantial national presence (Article 50 bis, inserted by Law 014/2025 in force on its publication on 29 May 2025); a Ministerial Order defines the taxable services, substantial national presence and the registration, declaration and payment procedures  _(Law nº 014/2025 of 27/05/2025 amending Law nº 027/2022 (Official Gazette nº Special of 29/05/2025), Rwanda Revenue Authority copy, Art. 5 (inserting Art. 50 bis) — https://www.rra.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Law_amending_the_2022_Income_Tax_May_2025.pdf)_
