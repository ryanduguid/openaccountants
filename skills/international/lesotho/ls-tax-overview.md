# Lesotho tax overview

Identify the taxpayer, type of income, locations and assessment year. Resident taxpayers generally include income from Lesotho and abroad. Non-resident taxpayers generally include Lesotho-source income. Apply the source rule for the income category and check any exemption or treaty. Foreign employment income of a resident individual has a specific exemption if it is chargeable to tax in the foreign country. [Income Tax Act, ss 17 and 103–104][act]

## Residence and taxpayers

An individual can be resident if they have a normal home in Lesotho and spend part of the assessment year there, or are present for more than 182 days in any consecutive 12-month period that overlaps the assessment year. The Act also covers government officials posted abroad and persons who are otherwise resident. Check the arrival and departure rules in subsections 5(2) and 5(3) before assigning residence for a particular period. [Income Tax Act, s 5][act]

A company is resident if formed under Lesotho law, managed and controlled in Lesotho or carrying on most of its operations there. For income tax, a Lesotho branch of a non-resident company is treated as a separate resident company. The ordinary charge distinguishes individuals, companies and trustees. Partners are taxed on their shares, with a separate partnership return. Check the legal form, actual residence and applicable rates for the taxpayer. [Income Tax Act, ss 4, 6 and 75][act]

From 1 April 2025 through 31 March 2026, the ordinary resident individual scale applies 20% to the first LSL 74,040 of chargeable income and 30% to the excess. The annual non-refundable personal credit is LSL 11,640. The ordinary company rate outside special categories is 25%. The 2014 amendment set a 10% rate on manufacturing income, replacing the former 0% rate on specified exports outside the Southern African Customs Union. Standard-rated supplies bear 15% VAT. Check tax credits, withholding and the classification of the company and supply separately. [Income Tax (Amendment) Act 2014, ss 1–3][amendment] · [Legal Notice 50 of 2025, regs 1 and 3–4][band-notice] · [RSL 2025-26 individual tax structure][individual-rates] · [RSL tax rates][standard-rates]

## Currency, period and filing

The currency is the Lesotho loti (LSL), plural maloti. The Central Bank reports a 1:1 peg to the South African rand. Confirm the exchange method and date for any foreign amount. [Central Bank of Lesotho, currency][currency] · [Central Bank of Lesotho, country information][peg]

The ordinary 2025-26 assessment year ran from 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2026. A company may use a different 12-month period with the Commissioner's approval. When a return is required, the ordinary filing rule runs to the last day of the third month after the assessment year. Revenue Services Lesotho (RSL) announced 30 June 2026 as the ordinary filing deadline. Check any return exception, approved accounts period, extension and payment obligation separately. [Income Tax Act, ss 3, 49 and 128–131][act] · [RSL 2026 filing season notice][filing]

Use RSL's filing services and check the [individual income tax guide](ls-income-tax.md), [company tax guide](ls-corporate-income-tax.md) and [VAT guide](ls-vat-gst.md) for the appropriate regime. Verify the applicable concessions, classification and date for the taxpayer. [RSL income tax services][rsl]
