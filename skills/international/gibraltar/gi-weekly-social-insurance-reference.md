# Gibraltar weekly Social Insurance published-profile reference

> **General reference only.** This Tier 2 draft illustrates the published ordinary ER weekly contribution profile. It does not establish an actual contribution base, complete current law, annual liability, lawful deduction, remittance or complete payroll. Guide presence and component checks do not establish comprehensive jurisdiction acceptance.

## Scope and source gates

Load both workflow bases in full. Preserve all 4 universal outputs and 18 checks, all 5 payroll outputs and 12 checks, all 12 mandatory payroll country slots, shared refusals and the exact topical refusals below. Every required logical output must remain identifiable. This companion leaves the country contract incomplete; no incomplete prescribed financial output is permitted. A standalone contribution position or remittance reconciliation also requires the social-contributions-workflow-base and its complete country contract; this payroll reference cannot fill it by implication.

The fictional illustration stipulates an ordinary registered employee aged 30 throughout one complete Monday–Sunday contribution week wholly within July–December 2026, one employer, an ordinary contract of service, at least 15 service hours that week and the published earnings-related ER profile. It excludes D1 detached workers and every other class in the administrative table. There is no student or vacation employment, casual or family exception, military or specified Crown service, multiple-principal agent, temporary entertainment work, self-employment, voluntary cover, foreign work or coordination, age/status transition, statutory early retirement, maternity leave, no-service/remuneration exception, benefit period, alternative arrangement, advance, arrears, instalment, termination or disputed payment. These are fictional stipulations, never defaults for missing facts. The arithmetic boundary amounts do not establish lawful minimum wages or actual legal insurability.

**The actual earnings basis remains unresolved.** Insurance Act section 2(7) delegates the calculation or estimation of earnings and remuneration. Social Insurance (Benefits) Regulations regulation 3 expressly invokes that subsection and limits the calculation to net remuneration or profit, excluding specified statutory deductions and reasonable employment expenses. The later contribution Schedule expressly says 'Gross Earnings'. The title 'Benefits' does not justify ignoring regulation 3's Act-wide wording; its net rule must not silently replace the later contribution wording either. Resolve their scope, amendments and applicable legal basis with the reviewer before deriving any actual base. A payslip label, net bank receipt or generic earnings definition cannot settle this relationship.

**The printed Employment Injuries table is not an additional charge.** The retained Employment Injuries consolidation still prints an older combined table with a 20% employer rate. Legal Notice 2008/042 regulation 2 expressly replaces its Schedule 1 with Insurance Act Schedule 1 'as amended from time to time'. This explains the replacement mechanism; do not add the old table to the later table or treat both as independent payable schedules. Complete subsequent amendment coverage and the actual applicable profile still require verification. The consolidation's amendment notes also record LN2010/114 amending its Schedule 1 through the Social Security (Open Long-Term Benefits Scheme) (Contributions) (Amendment) Order 2008 (LN2008/046), and the printed Schedule apportions contributions across the Open Long-Term Benefits Scheme and Medical (Group Practice Scheme) Acts. Neither that Order nor those Acts is retained here; that link and any fund allocation remain held under H01 and H08.

The Schedule states annual as well as weekly contribution minima and maxima. Their amounts are not exactly interchangeable through multiplication or division by 52. This draft makes no annual reconciliation, year-to-date exhaustion, 53-week adjustment, refund, carry-forward or remaining-limit calculation. Likewise, no authority is established here for converting monthly or annual salary to a weekly base, daily apportionment, payday allocation or actual rounding. Those matters remain held even when the published-profile arithmetic is shown.

## Intake and weekly-profile mechanics

Reuse relevant redacted records before grouped questions. Establish the legal employer/employee, reviewer-held registration readiness, age throughout each week, actual service, hours, contractual status, other employment, student/foreign/exceptional status, every pay component and its dates, the full source chain and base authority, contribution class, annual position, deductions, payments and special arrangements. Present the profile and wait for confirmation before actual classification. Keep identifiers, bank information and signatures with the reviewer. Confirmation of facts cannot supply missing law.

1. **Qualify insurability separately.** Insurance section 3 imports insurable employment under the EI Act, with its exclusions. Special Classes regulation 5 and Schedule disregard specified employment of less than 15 hours per week per employer; exactly 15 hours is outside that particular disregard. Vacation-student disregard and Contributions regulation 14B's insured-person student exemption are different provisions; do not turn either into a universal employer exemption or extend an Insurance exclusion automatically to every combined component. Foreign payment alone does not establish a Director-approved exemption or resolve UK/EU coordination. No coverage rule is inferred from Brexit alone.
2. **Qualify the week and amount independently.** The published arithmetic input G is a stipulated positive rational weekly amount, in source-scale pounds, for the exact complete contribution week. It is not derived from an actual payslip, monthly/annual amount, net receipt or Benefits regulation 3. All included components must be aggregated in that one fictional G before applying each share's bounds. No missing amount, class or exception receives a factual default.
3. **Apply each contribution floor and ceiling to its own percentage result.** For the selected published weekly profile only, E = min(40.79, max(14.34, 0.10 × G)) and R = min(56.22, max(31.98, 0.18 × G)). These bounds are contribution amounts, not one common earnings band. Keep E and R separate; T = E + R is only a combined reference total. Neither a single 28% clamp nor the stale EI employer 20% rate reproduces this profile. Apply the weekly profile separately to separately stipulated weeks; do not combine their earnings into one week.
4. **Preserve exact precision.** Keep G and both percentage results exact before comparison, then preserve exact E, R and T. The rational crossings are G = 143.40 and 407.90 for E and G = 533/3 and 937/3 for R. A fractional result printed below is not a lawful wage deduction or filing amount. No rounding, floor to pence, annual/52 conversion or monthly-table substitution is authorised here.
5. **Keep liability and recovery distinct.** Insurance section 7 initially puts both shares on the employer, prohibits recovery of its own share and qualifies recovery of the insured person's contribution. Its no-pecuniary-remuneration proviso and maternity-week rule cannot be ignored. Actual recovery needs the complete applicable incorporated regulations and facts. Employee consent does not make the employer's own share deductible. The published-profile total is not authority to debit it all from wages.
6. **Resolve collection before filing.** The retained incorporated rules address approved schedules, first-employer treatment, advances, termination and death. Their monthly/weekly recording and quarterly-return wording must not be collapsed into one invented deadline. EI regulation 4(8) literally refers to an employer schedule signed by the 'self-employed person'; Insurance regulation 5(2) names EI regulation 12 whilst the retained benefit-deduction clause is regulation 11. These source anomalies, original-page clarification, current forms/channels, dates and arrangements remain held. No return, remittance or statutory calendar is completed from this profile.
