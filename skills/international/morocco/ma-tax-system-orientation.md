# Morocco tax system

> General orientation for 2026. Confirm the taxpayer, accounting period and transaction with a qualified Moroccan tax professional before applying these rules.

Amounts are in Moroccan dirhams (MAD). The Direction générale des impôts (DGI) administers the taxes described here. Use the consolidated 2026 General Tax Code (CGI) and check the effective period for each rule. [2026 General Tax Code](https://www.finances.gov.ma/Publication/dgi/2025/CGI-2026-FR.pdf) · [Finance Law No. 50-25](https://www.finances.gov.ma/Publication/db/2025/BO_7465-bis_fr.pdf)

## Income tax

- **Individuals and residence.** A permanent home in Morocco, a centre of economic interests there, or presence exceeding 183 days in any 365-day period can establish Moroccan tax domicile. Domiciled individuals generally include Moroccan and foreign income. Others generally include Moroccan-source income, and treaty taxing rights can also apply. The ordinary annual bands apply 0% through MAD 40,000, then 10% to MAD 60,000, 20% to MAD 80,000, 30% to MAD 100,000, 34% to MAD 180,000 and 37% above that. Check specific rates, withholding, deductions and credits by income class. [CGI, arts. 23 and 73](https://www.finances.gov.ma/Publication/dgi/2025/CGI-2026-FR.pdf)
- **Companies.** Article 5 taxes companies on income connected with Moroccan property, activities and profit-making transactions or allocated to Morocco by treaty. Non-resident companies can also be taxable on specified gross receipts. For accounting periods opening in 2026, the ordinary company rate is 20%, or generally 35% when net profit is at least MAD 100 million. The threshold is a rate test for the company, with exceptions and rules that can keep a higher rate after profit falls. Specified credit institutions, insurers and similar entities generally use 40%. Check the applicable transitional rule, exclusions and minimum contribution before calculating the liability. [CGI, arts. 5, 15, 19, 144 and 247 XXXVII-A](https://www.finances.gov.ma/Publication/dgi/2025/CGI-2026-FR.pdf)

## VAT and filing

- **VAT.** The ordinary rate is 20%. Article 99 lists transactions at 10% and specifies whether deduction is available. Check exemptions and transitional rules separately. Monthly reporting generally applies when the prior year's taxable turnover is at least MAD 1 million or when a person carries out taxable transactions without an establishment in Morocco. Check the quarterly rules and the separate regime for eligible non-resident suppliers of remote services. [CGI, arts. 89–99, 107–108 and 115 bis](https://www.finances.gov.ma/Publication/dgi/2025/CGI-2026-FR.pdf)
- **Filing.** Where an annual individual return is required under article 82, the ordinary deadline is before 1 March of the following year for non-professional income and before 1 May for business income under the actual or simplified net result regimes. An ordinary company declares its taxable result within 3 months of the close and generally makes 4 provisional payments during the year. Check filing exemptions, special categories and any official extension. [CGI, arts. 20, 82, 86 and 170](https://www.finances.gov.ma/Publication/dgi/2025/CGI-2026-FR.pdf)
