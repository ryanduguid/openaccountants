# Slovenia tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the taxpayer type, residence facts, income sources and relevant period. Check any treaty that assigns residence or limits the source country's tax.

## Territorial reach

Slovenian-resident individuals and corporate taxpayers generally account for Slovenian and foreign income under their respective laws. Nonresidents account for Slovenian-source amounts. For a foreign company, distinguish profit attributable to a Slovenian permanent establishment from Slovenian payments within the statutory withholding regime. Check the source rule for the type of income and any applicable treaty. [Personal Income Tax Act, arts 5 and 8–14](https://pisrs.si/pregledPredpisa?id=ZAKO4697&print=1) · [Corporate Income Tax Act, arts 4, 6–8, 12 and 70](https://pisrs.si/pregledPredpisa?id=ZAKO4687)

## Residence

Check an individual's officially registered permanent address, habitual abode, centre of personal and economic interests, presence for more than 183 days in a tax year and special statutory cases. The tests are alternatives and the statutory exceptions can apply. Check article 7 where an applicable treaty treats the person as solely resident elsewhere. A corporate taxpayer is resident if its seat or place of effective management is in Slovenia. Identify where the key administrative and commercial decisions are made, then review any treaty. [Personal Income Tax Act, arts 6–7](https://pisrs.si/pregledPredpisa?id=ZAKO4697&print=1) · [Corporate Income Tax Act, art 5](https://pisrs.si/pregledPredpisa?id=ZAKO4687)

## Taxpayer types

An individual carrying on independent business ordinarily accounts for business income under the personal income-tax rules. A domestic or foreign legal person is generally within the corporate income-tax regime, which also covers specified unincorporated foreign companies or associations that fall outside the personal income-tax law. Check a foreign partnership under this rule before attributing income to members. For a foreign trust, review its legal form and check the corporate taxpayer tests, the rights to income and assets and any statutory attribution rule. The personal income rule on transfers of income rights can leave the income attributable to the transferor unless there is an accompanying unlimited and irrevocable transfer of the income-producing asset. [Personal Income Tax Act, arts 16(5) and 46](https://pisrs.si/pregledPredpisa?id=ZAKO4697&print=1) · [Corporate Income Tax Act, art 3](https://pisrs.si/pregledPredpisa?id=ZAKO4687)

## Currency

Use euros (EUR) for ordinary tax reporting. For personal income and expenses in another currency, the general rule uses the Bank of Slovenia rate for the day the income arises or the expense is incurred, subject to specific rules. Statements under Slovenian Accounting Standards use the euro as their presentation currency. For corporate filings, start with the accounts under the applicable standards and reconcile them to the filing currency and tax base. [Personal Income Tax Act, art 16(6)](https://pisrs.si/pregledPredpisa?id=ZAKO4697&print=1) · [Corporate Income Tax Act, art 12](https://pisrs.si/pregledPredpisa?id=ZAKO4687) · [Companies Act, art 54](https://pisrs.si/pregledNpb?idPredpisa=ZAKO4291&idPredpisaChng=ZAKO9437) · [Slovenian Accounting Standards 2024, framework](https://si-revizija.si/datoteke/standardi/3310/srs-2024-e-knjiga.pdf)

## Assessment periods

Personal income tax uses the calendar year. Corporate income tax also defaults to the calendar year. A corporate taxpayer can elect its different business year if the tax period is no longer than 12 months, notify the tax authority and keep the choice for at least 3 years. The ordinary corporate return is due within 3 months after the relevant period ends. Check the transition return when the period changes. [Personal Income Tax Act, art 15](https://pisrs.si/pregledPredpisa?id=ZAKO4697&print=1) · [Corporate Income Tax Act, art 10](https://pisrs.si/pregledPredpisa?id=ZAKO4687) · [Tax Procedure Act, art 358](https://pisrs.si/pregledPredpisa?d-49682-p=1&id=ZAKO4703)

## Responsible authorities

The Financial Administration of the Republic of Slovenia (FURS) administers income taxes, social contributions, customs and excise. For business tax filings use eDavki. Record the competent office and check the process for the specific return or registration. [FURS responsibilities](https://www.gov.si/en/state-authorities/bodies-within-ministries/financial-administration/about-the-administration/) · [eDavki for businesses](https://www.fu.gov.si/en/business_events_businesses/edavki_electronic_tax_management_system)

## Fictional intake

A company has a Slovenian registered seat, meetings abroad and a financial year ending 30 June. Check both corporate residence tests and any treaty. Confirm the alternate tax-year notification before using 30 June as the tax period end.
