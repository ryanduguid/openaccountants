# Slovenia Tax Overview

## Slovenian tax system at a glance

- **Overview** — Slovenia is a euro-area EU member state with a progressive personal income tax, a flat corporate income tax, and a full VAT system. Taxes are administered by the Financial Administration of the Republic of Slovenia (FURS).
- **Standard tax year** — Calendar year for individuals (ZDoh-2 art. 15(1)); companies use the calendar year or a business year that differs from it (ZDavP-2 art. 358(2) and (3))  _(Personal Income Tax Act (ZDoh-2, consolidated text, PisRS), art. 15(1) — https://pisrs.si/pregledPredpisa?id=ZAKO4697 ; Tax Procedure Act (ZDavP-2, consolidated text, PisRS), art. 358(2) and (3) — https://pisrs.si/pregledPredpisa?id=ZAKO4703)_
- **Currency** — Euro (EUR)  _(Bank of Slovenia / euro adoption (2007))_
- **Tax authority** — Financial Administration of the Republic of Slovenia (FURS / Finančna uprava Republike Slovenije)  _([Financial Administration Act (ZFU)](https://www.fu.gov.si/))_
- **Individual taxation basis** — Residents are taxed on all income with a source in or outside Slovenia; non-residents on income with a Slovenian source (art. 5)  _(Personal Income Tax Act (ZDoh-2, consolidated text, PisRS), art. 5(1) and (2) — https://pisrs.si/pregledPredpisa?id=ZAKO4697)_
- **Company taxation basis** — A company with its seat or place of effective management in Slovenia is a resident (art. 5(1)); residents are taxed on their profit, non-residents on the profit of a Slovenian permanent establishment (art. 12(1)) and by withholding on Slovenian-source income (art. 70)  _(Corporate Income Tax Act (ZDDPO-2, consolidated text, PisRS), arts. 5(1), 12(1) and 70 — https://pisrs.si/pregledPredpisa?id=ZAKO4687)_
- **Top personal income tax rate (2025)** — 50% on net annual base above the top threshold. The Act's scale runs 16%, 26%, 33%, 39% and 50% over base thresholds of EUR 8,500, 25,000, 50,000 and 72,000, which the Government indexes each year; the payroll guide carries the 2025 and 2026 indexed amounts  _(Personal Income Tax Act (ZDoh-2, consolidated text, PisRS), art. 122(1) — https://pisrs.si/pregledPredpisa?id=ZAKO4697)_
- **Corporate income tax rate (2024-2028)** — 22% for the 2024 to 2028 tax years under art. 64 of the Act on reconstruction, development and provision of financial resources (ZORZFS); the general rate in ZDDPO-2 art. 60 remains 19%  _(Corporate Income Tax Act (ZDDPO-2, consolidated text, PisRS), art. 60 — https://pisrs.si/pregledPredpisa?id=ZAKO4687 ; Financial Administration of the Republic of Slovenia, corporate income tax page (rate under ZORZFS art. 64) — https://www.fu.gov.si/davki_in_druge_dajatve/podrocja/davek_od_dohodkov_pravnih_oseb_ddpo/)_
- **VAT in force** — Yes — standard rate 22%; reduced rate 9.5% for the supplies in Annex I and a special reduced rate of 5% (art. 41)  _(Value Added Tax Act (ZDDV-1, consolidated text, PisRS), art. 41(1) and (2) — https://pisrs.si/pregledPredpisa?id=ZAKO4701)_
- **Personal income tax annual assessment** — FURS sends residents an informative calculation (informativni izračun) by 31 May; it becomes the assessment unless the taxpayer objects within 15 days of service; a resident not served by 15 June must file a return by 31 July  _(Tax Procedure Act (ZDavP-2, consolidated text, PisRS), art. 267(3) and (6) — https://pisrs.si/pregledPredpisa?id=ZAKO4703)_
- **Corporate income tax return deadline** — Within three months after the end of the calendar year (31 March for calendar-year taxpayers) or of the business year where the tax period follows it  _(Tax Procedure Act (ZDavP-2, consolidated text, PisRS), art. 358(2) and (3) — https://pisrs.si/pregledPredpisa?id=ZAKO4703)_
