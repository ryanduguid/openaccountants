# Australia: transfer pricing, documentation and reporting

Establish the entity, residence, permanent establishments, transaction,
period and actual conditions before deciding an Australian TP adjustment
or reporting duty. Load workflow-base and transfer-pricing-workflow-base
v1.1. Preserve the 4 universal outputs, 5 TP outputs and 10 domain checks.
This reference supports selected statutory analysis; unresolved country
requirements or engagement evidence prevent a completed filing package.
It remains Tier 2 pending professional review.

## Application and arm's length conditions

ITAA 1997 Subdivision 815-B substitutes arm's length conditions where
actual conditions differ and the entity obtains the specified TP benefit.
The benefit can involve taxable income, a tax loss, a tax offset or interest
or royalty withholding tax. Apply every section 815-120 condition,
including its residence/PE cross-border table and dual-resident treaty
rule. A related-party label, a foreign address or a transaction amount
alone does not prove liability or an exemption. [ITAA9, 815-105 to 815-120][ITAA9]

Section 815-125 requires the most appropriate and reliable method, or
combination, having regard to the actual functions, assets, risks,
contracts, property/services, economic circumstances and business
strategies. Differences need a reliable comparability adjustment or a
reason the comparison remains reliable. Section 815-130 starts with
actual commercial or financial relations and applies its substance,
reconstruction or absence conditions separately. [ITAA9]

CUP, resale price, cost plus, TNMM and profit split are available under
the OECD guidance selected by section 815-135. Traditional methods over
profit methods, and CUP over another method, have a preference only where
the alternatives are equally reliable. Missing data does not automatically
select TNMM. A tested party follows reliable application and comparables,
usually the less complex party, rather than a fixed business label.
Section 815-135 identifies the OECD guidance last amended on **20 January
2022** and any prescribed documents; check the applicable regulations and
period before use. [ITAA9, 815-125 to 815-135][ITAA9]; [OECD, 2.2-2.5, 3.18-3.19][OECD]

Subdivision 815-C addresses PE attribution from the entity's actual income
and expenditure on the statutory distinct-and-separate-entity basis.
It has special foreign-bank-branch conditions. Section 815-235 gives
priority to the pre-**22 July 2010** Article 7/commentary material it
specifies, then the section 815-135 documents subject to prescribed rules.
Do not automatically use the later OECD functionally separate entity
approach. Subdivision 815-D modifies application to trusts' net income and
partnership net income or loss. [ITAA9, 815-205 to 815-310][ITAA9]

Section 815-140's thin-capitalisation interaction is conditional. Do not
infer that TP establishes deductibility under Division 820 or that a debt
limit proves an arm's length price. CFC attribution, Part IVA, diverted
profits tax, debt deduction creation, withholding and actual treaties
require their own current sources and facts. A consequential adjustment
under section 815-145 is a separate fair-and-reasonable decision with
request, notice and objection rules. Sections 815-150 and 815-240 have
special **7-year** amendment provisions measured from the assessment
notice, with a separate consequential-adjustment exception; do not import
an ordinary 2- or 4-year clock. [ITAA9]

## Documentation, CBC reporting and the 2026 IDS

Keep these obligations separate:

| Obligation | Rule and evidence |
|---|---|
| General records | Apply the taxpayer's record duties and retention/review extensions. Special TP documentation does not replace them. |
| Special penalty documentation | TAA Schedule 1 sections 284-250 and 284-255 do not themselves compel preparation or retention. To rely on RAP treatment, records must exist **before actual return lodgement**, be in English or readily accessible and convertible into English, explain 815-B/C application or non-application and guidance consistency, and make the required methods, conditions and consequences readily ascertainable. Records are necessary but not sufficient for RAP. [TAA2] |
| CBC statements | Test sections 815-355 to 815-380 for each statement; foreign parent reporting alone does not clear the local entity. [ITAA9] |
| IDS | Use the actual entity's **2026** return triggers and instructions. It is a disclosure schedule, not the substantive TP law or a universal TP exemption. [IDS-GET] |

A CBC reporting parent is not an individual, meets the control condition
and has annual global income of at least **AUD 1 billion**. Test the
accounting-consolidated or notional listed CBC group and its exceptions;
CBC and SGE status are different tests. The section 815-355 trigger uses
CBC status during a period overlapping the **previous income year**, a
specified Australian residence/trust/partnership/PE connection during the
reporting year, and the exemption/regulation conditions. [ITAA9, 815-355,
815-370 to 815-380][ITAA9]; [ITAA10, 960-555 to 960-575][ITAA10]

Where required, the 3 statements correspond to the Master File, Local File
and CbC report and are due within **12 months after the reporting period**.
A written replacement-period notice, exemption for an entity or statement,
class exemption and lodgement deferral have separate conditions. Establish
the approved form, current Australian Local File schema/short form,
electronic route and any exchange/exemption arrangement for each actual
statement. An internal workflow Local File is not proof that a statutory
Local File has been filed. [ITAA9, 815-355 to 815-365][ITAA9]

The IDS can be triggered for companies, partnerships, trusts, AMITs and
attribution CCIV sub-funds. The instructions include overseas interest or
royalty amounts, specified foreign interests or branches, thin capitalisation
and debt deduction creation, as well as international related-party
transactions exceeding **AUD 2 million**, including property and loan
balances. Use the entity-specific questions; not every trigger applies
identically to every return. A below-threshold dealing does not itself
exclude substantive TP or every IDS trigger. Own branches are not separate
international related parties for this schedule. [IDS-GET]; [IDS-DEFS]

Section A question 1b permits skipping specified questions only when Part A
of the Local File is lodged **at the same time as the return**, on its
conditions. Question 2b's **AUD 5 million** and **50% of aggregated
turnover** tests also require a small business entity that is not an SGE.
Neither is an unconditional schedule exemption or PCG 2017/2's different
small-taxpayer test. Use the actual return deadline and all applicable
sections. The 2026 instructions remove specified 2024-25 transitional
reporting concessions; do not copy the old edition. Actual IDS fields,
RTP schedules and public CBC reporting remain separate source gates.
[IDS-A, questions 1-2][IDS-A]; [IDS-NEW]

## Benchmarking and currency

Record the transaction, selected method, indicator, tested party, search
source/date, independence and geographic filters, rejected comparables,
adjustments and financial reconciliation. Geography is one comparability
factor: foreign comparables are not automatically unsuitable, and a
particular commercial database is not made acceptable by its name alone.
Obtain actual reproducible data. Never invent entities, ratios or an
industry margin. [ITAA9, 815-125][ITAA9]; [OECD, 3.18-3.19, 3.34-3.38][OECD]

A single reliable point or full reliable range may be appropriate.
Interquartile narrowing is conditional on residual comparability defects
and a sizeable number of observations; neither IQR nor median is automatic.
Review the documentation annually. OECD paragraph 5.38 allows a tax
administration to permit searches every **3 years** where conditions are
unchanged, whilst requiring annual comparable financial updates. It is
not an automatic Australian entitlement to reuse a search for 3 years.
CbC aggregates do not replace transaction-level benchmarking. [OECD,
3.55-3.62, 5.25, 5.37-5.38][OECD]

Use AUD for the selected Australian tax computations. Translate underlying
amounts before calculating a net amount except where the applicable rule
provides otherwise. Under the ordinary section 960-50 table:

- Item 6 translates ordinary income at receipt if received at or before
  derivation; otherwise at derivation.
- Item 8 translates deductions other than Division 40 amounts at payment
  if paid at or before becoming deductible; otherwise when deductible.

Use section 960-55's commencement/transitional conditions. These selections
do not resolve every table item, functional currency choice, forex gain,
accounting translation or reporting-form field. [ITAA10, 960-50, 960-55][ITAA10]

The ATO FX page directs daily rates to the RBA and identifies its monthly
and annual data. It permits a reasonable external source for an unlisted
currency. Record the actual observation date, source, currency pair and
quote direction. Apply an average only after its legal/practical conditions
are verified; a published annual or year-end rate is not automatically the
rate for every TP amount. Hold an unsupported conversion. [FX]

## Simplified record keeping: conditional compliance options

PCG 2017/2 is an ATO resource-allocation approach. Eligible dealings still
need self-assessed compliance with 815-B/C/D and eligibility evidence.
The PCG does not itself satisfy Subdivision 284-E or create a statutory
safe harbour. Elect/disclose the option as instructed for the relevant
IDS/CBC reporting and retain the general records. [PCG, 1-15][PCG]

| Selected option | Conditions to check together |
|---|---|
| Small taxpayers | Australian economic group turnover **below AUD 50 million**, no sustained loss/restructure, combined royalty/licence/R&D dealings **no more than AUD 500,000**, specified services **no more than 15% of turnover**, not a distributor and self-assessed compliance. Excluded royalty/R&D, financial and capital transactions retain their own analysis. [PCG, 16-19, 76-103][PCG] |
| Distributors | Turnover **below AUD 50 million**, the required **3%** weighted profit-before-tax ratio over the specified **3 years**, no restructure, the royalty/R&D conditions and self-assessed compliance. Apply all exclusions and definitions. [PCG, 22-27, 76-103][PCG] |
| Low-value services | Actual qualifying supportive services; combined received/provided charges **no more than AUD 2 million**, or, above that, received charges **no more than 15% of group expenses** and provided charges **no more than 15% of group revenue**. Service expenses must not exceed **25% of pre-service profit**. Received mark-up **no more than 5%** and provided mark-up **at least 5%**, with the other loss/restructure, compliance and eligibility conditions. [PCG, 28-43, 76-103][PCG] |

Use all relevant direct and indirect service costs, excluding pass-through
costs from the mark-up base. For the selected expenses test, pre-service
profit is total income minus (total expenses minus all intra-group service
expenses). Identify the Australian economic group and the PCG definitions;
an isolated entity's turnover or a 5% label alone does not establish eligibility.
[PCG, 39-43, 76-103][PCG]

The OECD low-value services approach is separately conditional on adoption,
benefit tests, service/cost-pool eligibility, allocation keys and supporting
documentation. OECD encouragement about withholding on a mark-up does not
itself change Australian withholding law. Annual loan rates, materiality,
technical services and other PCG options require the relevant option and
year to be checked; this reference supplies no loan safe-harbour rate.
[OECD, 7.43-7.65][OECD]; [PCG]

## TP penalties: establish liability before calculating

Apply TAA Schedule 1 section **284-145(2B)**: the Commissioner must amend
an assessment or serve the specified withholding notice to give effect to
815-B/C, with additional income tax or withholding tax payable. Under
284-150(4), the scheme shortfall is that additional tax, not the transfer
price or profit adjustment. Avoid double counting under 284-150(5).
A self-adjustment or price difference alone does not establish this penalty.
[TAA2]

Section 284-165 excludes liability where the scheme shortfall is at or
below the taxpayer's reasonably arguable threshold. Section 284-90(3)
uses the greater of **AUD 10,000 and 1% of return-basis income tax payable**
for the ordinary case, or **AUD 20,000 and 2% of return-basis net income**
for a trust/partnership; separate statutory branches must be selected where
applicable. Sections 284-165(2)-(4) additionally test the specified trust or
partnership **net-income/loss adjustment**, with nil-amount rules. Do not
collapse these exceptions into a single partner's tax-shortfall comparison.
[TAA2]

Once liability remains, split the shortfall by reasonably arguable matters:

| Section 284-160(3) purpose branch | Non-RAP portion | RAP portion |
|---|---|---|
| Sole or dominant purpose of obtaining a TP benefit | **50%** | **25%** |
| That purpose branch does not apply | **25%** | **10%** |

RAP is a legal standard under 284-15, not a box checked by writing a report.
Apply sections 284-224, 284-220 and 284-225 separately where their conditions
are established. For 284-155(2), calculate BPA + BPA × (increase percentage
minus reduction percentage); do not compound these 2 adjustments in series.
Then apply 284-155(3)'s SGE doubling only on its status/period and non-RAP
conditions. A mixed-matter case needs a documented legal treatment of
those conditions. Remission under 298-20 is separate and discretionary;
RAP does not automatically make the penalty nil. [TAA2]

Failure-to-lodge penalties, general statement penalties, interest, notices,
unit values, SGE multipliers and procedural review rights are separate.
Verify their actual obligation, period and current sources before an amount;
a CBC parent's size alone is insufficient. Do not substitute a fixed
maximum or a good-faith remission example for those tests.

## Advance pricing arrangements

PS LA 2015/4, revised **29 October 2025**, describes unilateral, bilateral
and multilateral APAs. Its usual arrangement term is **3-5 years**, not a
processing guarantee. The 3 stages are early engagement, application and
monitoring compliance. The taxpayer submits an early engagement form;
formal application follows invitation. Plans are agreed for engagement and
conclusion. An APA depends on agreed conditions, critical assumptions and
annual compliance reporting. [APA-PS, 1A-3C, 9A-10E][APA-PS]; [APA]

Use the actual programme, treaty and case requirements for acceptance,
rollback, fees, timing, renewals and cancellation. This selected review
establishes no universal fee or **12-24 month** completion promise. A full
APA application remains pending its complete procedure and case facts.
TR 95/23 is not the current general procedure; PCG 2019/1 concerns inbound
distributor risk rather than replacing PS LA 2015/4.

## Reviewer intake, refusals and outputs

Confirm entity type/residence and all PEs; income/reporting periods;
group accounting structure and previous-year CBC/current SGE evidence;
all relevant dealings, contracts and actual conduct; financial statements;
method alternatives and actual comparable data; currency observations;
record completion and return dates; reporting notices/exemptions; and
any adjustment, tax shortfall, purpose, RAP and disclosure evidence.
Use synthetic or redacted working data. Credentials, identifiers and
actual submission remain with the authorised professional.

| Missing or disputed matter | Handling |
|---|---|
| Operative law, country slot, reporting schema or special regime | Needs Input; obtain the source before the affected output. |
| Functions, comparables, actual rate or reliable adjustment | Hold the arm's length or conversion conclusion; no factual default. |
| PCG eligibility, CBC exemption or foreign exchange filing relief | Hold the claimed relief; record all unverified conditions. |
| Historical period or amendment/notice clock | Select the relevant law and dates; do not back-apply 2026 figures. |
| Documentation, RAP, purpose, SGE or shortfall threshold | Hold the penalty conclusion; do not assume a nil or maximum amount. |

These holds apply at any cash amount. Flag every proposed TP adjustment,
penalty, exemption or statutory filing conclusion for professional review;
there is no invented statutory or reviewer cash safe harbour. Conservative
defaults concern presentation of missing evidence, never invented tax facts.
Preserve the base refusal catalogue and verbatim messages. This reference
adds no new refusal code and authorises no filing, payment or tax position.

Keep a transaction/source ledger, functional and method analysis,
benchmark/search record, conversion schedule, separate documentation and
filing matrix, PCG conditions sheet and penalty decision trail. Map each
conclusion to source, period, evidence, calculation and unresolved condition.
Extend the reviewer brief with the missing country slots and an owner for
each action. Preserve every base output even when marked incomplete; no
Local File, Master File or CBC conclusion is ready whilst its gate is open.

## Fictional positions and checks

All amounts below are invented AUD unless stated. Stipulated conditions
are not findings about an actual taxpayer.

| Position | Working and bounded conclusion |
|---|---|
| Ordinary company; return-basis tax AUD 600,000; statutory TP scheme tax shortfall AUD 10,000 | Threshold = max(10,000, 1% × 600,000) = **10,000**. Equality satisfies 284-165(1); no 284-145(2B) penalty. This does not cancel tax or interest. |
| Same company; shortfall AUD 100,000; gateway met, all non-RAP, no sole/dominant purpose, not SGE, no other adjustment | Threshold exceeded; BPA = 25% × 100,000 = **25,000**. Remission is not assumed. |
| Same shortfall; AUD 60,000 non-RAP and AUD 40,000 RAP, no purpose branch; no other adjustments or doubling stipulated | BPA = 25% × 60,000 + 10% × 40,000 = **19,000**, not 10% of the whole shortfall. |
| Trust net income on return AUD 2 million; beneficiary's scheme shortfall exceeds its own threshold; specified trust adjustment AUD 40,000 | Trust threshold = max(20,000, 2% × 2,000,000) = **40,000**. If 284-165(2)'s other conditions apply, equality gives the separate trust exception. The trust adjustment is not the beneficiary's additional tax. |
| Qualifying service costs AUD 100,000 plus AUD 20,000 pass-through; provided mark-up 5%; all other PCG conditions stipulated | Mark-up = 5% × 100,000 = **5,000**; charge including pass-through = **125,000**. This arithmetic alone cannot establish PCG eligibility or deductibility. |
| Ordinary foreign income FC 1,000 received before derivation; applicable receipt-date quote stipulated at FC 2 per AUD 1 | Item 6 selects receipt; 1,000 ÷ 2 = **AUD 500**. Invert the operation for a reciprocal quote. The invented rate is not an actual historical rate or permission to average. |
| Previous-year CBC status unknown, current turnover exceeds AUD 1 billion | Hold the reporting conclusion. Current turnover alone cannot establish previous-year CBC status or a statement exemption. |
| A distributor has no reliable comparable data | Hold the benchmark and arm's length result; TNMM, 3% or an industry label cannot fill the gap. |

Self-check the statutory gateway, complete cross-border test, separate PE
and trust/partnership branches, method reliability, benchmark and currency
evidence, each reporting duty and deadline, every PCG condition, tax versus
profit shortfall, separate exceptions, matter-level RAP and adjustments.
Reconcile figures across all outputs and retain all 10 domain checks.
