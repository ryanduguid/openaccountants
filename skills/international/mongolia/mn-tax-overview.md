# Mongolia Tax Overview

## Mongolian tax system at a glance

Mongolia taxes residents on worldwide income and non-residents on Mongolian-source income, administered by the Mongolian Tax Administration (MTA). The headline taxes are personal income tax, corporate income tax (CIT), VAT and social insurance.

- **Tax year** — Calendar year (1 January to 31 December)  _(General Taxation Law of Mongolia, as reported `[RESEARCH GAP — the statute's text was not read for this change; the secondary summary that carried the figure is no longer cited]`)_
- **Currency** — Mongolian Tugrik (MNT)  _(Bank of Mongolia)_
- **Tax authority** — Mongolian Tax Administration (MTA / Tatvaryn Yeronkhii Gazar)  _([General Taxation Law of Mongolia](https://mta.gov.mn))_
- **Basis of taxation** — Residents taxed on worldwide income; non-residents taxed only on Mongolian-source income  _(Corporate Income Tax Law; Personal Income Tax Law, as reported `[RESEARCH GAP — the statute's text was not read for this change; the secondary summary that carried the figure is no longer cited]`)_
- **Headline personal income tax rate** — 10% progressing to 20% for residents under the 2022 scale (10% to MNT 120 million, 15% to 180 million, 20% above) through 2026; flat 20% for non-residents (art. 21.2.5). The law of 26 June 2026 re-enacts art. 21.1: from 1 January 2027 a 0% band to MNT 9,504,000 is added (then 10% to 120 million, MNT 11,049,600 plus 15% to 180 million, MNT 20,049,600 plus 20% above), and from 1 January 2028 the band from MNT 9,504,000 to 24 million is taxed at 1%  _(Law on Personal Income Tax (Хувь хүний орлогын албан татварын тухай, 22 March 2019, consolidated on legalinfo.mn), art. 21.1 and 21.2.5 — https://legalinfo.mn/mn/detail?lawId=14410)_
- **Headline corporate income tax rate** — 10% on the first MNT 6 billion of taxable income and 25% above it through 2026; 1% small-business rate on revenue up to MNT 300 million outside the excluded sectors (art. 20.2.7). The law of 26 June 2026 re-enacts art. 20.1 from 1 January 2027: 10% to 6 billion, MNT 600 million plus 15% from 6 to 10 billion, MNT 1.2 billion plus 25% above 10 billion  _(Law on Corporate Income Tax (Аж ахуйн нэгжийн орлогын албан татварын тухай, consolidated on legalinfo.mn), art. 20.1 and 20.2.7 — https://legalinfo.mn/mn/detail?lawId=14407)_
- **Does VAT exist?** — Yes — VAT applies at a standard rate of 10%  _(Law on Value Added Tax (Нэмэгдсэн өртгийн албан татварын тухай, consolidated on legalinfo.mn), art. 11.1 — https://legalinfo.mn/mn/detail?lawId=11227)_
- **Annual personal income tax filing deadline** — 15 February of the following year  _(Personal Income Tax Law of Mongolia, as reported `[RESEARCH GAP — the statute's text was not read for this change; the secondary summary that carried the figure is no longer cited]`)_
- **Annual corporate income tax filing deadline** — 10 February after the end of the tax year  _(Corporate Income Tax Law of Mongolia, as reported `[RESEARCH GAP — the statute's text was not read for this change; the secondary summary that carried the figure is no longer cited]`)_
- **VAT return filing/payment deadline** — Monthly — the withholding agent transfers the VAT on the month's sales to the single treasury account and reports it by the 10th of the following month (art. 16.1)  _(Law on Value Added Tax (Нэмэгдсэн өртгийн албан татварын тухай, consolidated on legalinfo.mn), art. 16.1 — https://legalinfo.mn/mn/detail?lawId=11227)_
