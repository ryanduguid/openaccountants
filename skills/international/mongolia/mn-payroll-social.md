# Mongolia Payroll & Social Contributions

## Social insurance and payroll withholding

- **Employer social insurance contribution** — 12.5% to 14.5% of payroll for 2024 to 2026: 10.5%, 11.5% or 12.5% under the General Law on Social Insurance art. 18.1 table (pension 8.5%, benefit 1.0%, unemployment 0.5%, and industrial accident and occupational disease insurance at 0.5%, 1.5% or 2.5% by risk class) plus 2.0% health insurance under the Health Insurance Law. The law of 2 July 2026 sets the social insurance totals at 10.4%, 11.3% or 12.3% from 1 January 2027 (accident 0.3%, 1.2% or 2.2%; unemployment 0.6%)  _(General Law on Social Insurance of 7 July 2023 (Нийгмийн даатгалын ерөнхий хууль, consolidated on legalinfo.mn), art. 18.1 table and its amendment note — https://legalinfo.mn/mn/detail?lawId=16760148379551 ; health insurance share (as reported))_
- **Employer contribution breakdown** — 8.5% pension, 1.0% benefit, 0.5% unemployment (0.6% from 2027) and 0.5%, 1.5% or 2.5% industrial accident and occupational disease insurance by risk class (0.3%, 1.2% or 2.2% from 2027) under art. 18.1, plus 2.0% health insurance under the Health Insurance Law. This guide gave 0.2% unemployment and 0.8% to 2.8% accident insurance until October 2026  _(General Law on Social Insurance of 7 July 2023 (Нийгмийн даатгалын ерөнхий хууль, consolidated on legalinfo.mn), art. 18.1 table and its amendment note — https://legalinfo.mn/mn/detail?lawId=16760148379551 ; health insurance share (as reported))_
- **Employee social insurance contribution** — 11.5% of salary: 8.5% pension, 0.8% benefit and 0.2% unemployment under art. 18.1 (9.5%) plus 2.0% health insurance under the Health Insurance Law  _(General Law on Social Insurance of 7 July 2023 (Нийгмийн даатгалын ерөнхий хууль, consolidated on legalinfo.mn), art. 18.1 table and its amendment note — https://legalinfo.mn/mn/detail?lawId=16760148379551 ; health insurance share (as reported))_
- **Employee contribution monthly cap** — MNT 910,800 per month (cap applies once monthly salary exceeds MNT 7,920,000)  _(Social Insurance Law of Mongolia, as reported `[RESEARCH GAP — the statute's text was not read for this change; the secondary summary that carried the figure is no longer cited]`)_
- **Payroll PIT withholding (PAYE-equivalent)** — Employers withhold PIT at the progressive resident scale (10% to MNT 120 million, 15% to 180 million, 20% above, through 2026) and at a flat 20% for non-residents. The law of 26 June 2026 re-enacts art. 21.1: from 1 January 2027 a 0% band to MNT 9,504,000 is added (then 10% to 120 million, MNT 11,049,600 plus 15% to 180 million, MNT 20,049,600 plus 20% above), and from 1 January 2028 the band from MNT 9,504,000 to 24 million is taxed at 1%  _(Law on Personal Income Tax (Хувь хүний орлогын албан татварын тухай, 22 March 2019, consolidated on legalinfo.mn), art. 21.1 and 21.2.5 — https://legalinfo.mn/mn/detail?lawId=14410)_
- **PIT withholding remittance deadline** — By the 10th of the following month (art. 25.6)  _(Law on Personal Income Tax (Хувь хүний орлогын албан татварын тухай, consolidated on legalinfo.mn), art. 25.6 — https://legalinfo.mn/mn/detail?lawId=14410)_
- **Social insurance remittance** — Employers withhold the employee share from each month's pay and pay it with the employer share within that month (art. 21.1); the monthly contribution report is filed electronically by the 5th of the following month, moving to the next working day when the 5th falls on a weekend or public holiday (art. 25.1)  _(General Law on Social Insurance of 7 July 2023 (Нийгмийн даатгалын ерөнхий хууль, consolidated on legalinfo.mn), arts. 21.1 and 25.1 — https://legalinfo.mn/mn/detail?lawId=16760148379551)_
