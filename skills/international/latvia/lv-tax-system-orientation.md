# Latvia tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the taxpayer type, residence facts, income sources and relevant period. Check any treaty that assigns residence or limits the source country's tax.

## Territorial reach

A Latvian resident individual generally accounts for Latvian and foreign income. A nonresident accounts for income in the statutory Latvian-source categories. Latvia's ordinary corporate tax base includes distributed and deemed distributed profit arising from domestic or foreign taxable objects. A Latvian permanent establishment of a foreign enterprise applies the rules to attributable profit withdrawn from the establishment and other statutory objects. Consider withholding for separately specified payments to nonresident entities. [Personal Income Tax Act, ss 2–3](https://likumi.lv/ta/id/56880-par-iedzivotaju-ienakuma-nodokli/) · [Corporate Income Tax Act, ss 2, 4–5](https://likumi.lv/ta/id/292700)

## Residence

A person is generally resident if they have a declared place of residence in Latvia or spend at least 183 days there in any 12 months beginning or ending in the tax year. Latvian citizens employed abroad by the Latvian Government are also residents. The Act has specific arrival and departure rules for residence under the stay test. An entity is resident if formed and registered under Latvian law, or required to be formed and registered under it. Check any applicable treaty and retain the facts and any residence certificate. [Taxes and Fees Act, s 14](https://likumi.lv/ta/id/33946-par-nodokliem-un-nodevam/) · [State Revenue Service treaty procedures](https://www.vid.gov.lv/en/application-tax-convention-and-rebates)

## Taxpayer types

An individual carrying on economic activity generally starts under the personal income tax rules. A Latvian limited liability company and an ordinary partnership fall within the corporate regime, subject to the statutory exceptions. Check whether a partnership profit-share payment is treated as equivalent to a dividend. For a trust formed abroad, record the legal arrangement, who receives or controls the income and the nature and source of each payment. Apply the relevant taxpayer and charging provisions before classifying the amount. [Personal Income Tax Act, ss 2 and 11](https://likumi.lv/ta/id/56880-par-iedzivotaju-ienakuma-nodokli/) · [Corporate Income Tax Act, ss 1–2 and 4–5](https://likumi.lv/ta/id/292700)

## Currency

Keep statutory accounts in euros (EUR). For foreign-currency amounts, use the qualifying accounting exchange rate at the start of the transaction day. The ordinary source is the European Central Bank's euro reference rate. Check the statutory fallback, sector exceptions and year-end retranslation separately. [Accounting Act, s 7](https://likumi.lv/ta/id/324249-gramatvedibas-likums/)

## Assessment periods

Ordinary personal income tax follows the calendar year. The ordinary corporate tax period is the calendar month, or a quarter for a taxpayer entitled to record source documents quarterly under the Accounting Act. Check any special annual regime separately. A taxpayer can generally omit a return for a period with no taxable object, except the period covering the final month of the financial year. [Personal Income Tax Act, s 3](https://likumi.lv/ta/id/56880-par-iedzivotaju-ienakuma-nodokli/) · [Corporate Income Tax Act, ss 3 and 17](https://likumi.lv/ta/id/292700)

## Responsible authorities

The State Revenue Service (Valsts ieņēmumu dienests) administers ordinary national income and corporate taxes and customs. Use its Electronic Declaration System for filing and for the available residence certificate service. [State Revenue Service](https://www.vid.gov.lv/en) · [State Revenue Service treaty procedures](https://www.vid.gov.lv/en/application-tax-convention-and-rebates)

## Fictional intake

A Latvian company keeps its 2026 trading profit in the company but pays a personal bill for an owner. Check whether the payment is a taxable non-business expense in the relevant period. Keep the retained trading result and any year-end return requirement in the records.
