# Iraq Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax rates and base

- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 15% flat rate at all income levels (no progressive scale)  _(Income Tax Law No. 113 of 1982 as amended, Arabic text published by the General Commission for Taxes, flat 15% rate for companies — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/uploads/Najwan/law/Income-Tax-Law-No.-113-of-1982-Ar.pdf ; General Commission for Taxes, income tax guide (daleel dharibat al-dakhl: rates, allowances and filing) — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/%D8%AF%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%B6%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D8%AE%D9%84/)_
- **Oil & gas sector corporate tax rate** — 35% (income from contracts with foreign oil companies, branches, offices and subcontractors in oil/gas production and related industries)  _(Law No. 19 of 2010 imposing income tax on foreign oil companies contracting in Iraq, General Commission for Taxes copy (scanned) — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/uploads%2FNajwan%2Flaw%2F-%20%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86%20%D9%81%D8%B1%D8%B6%20%D8%B6%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A8%D8%A9%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D8%AE%D9%84%20%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89%20%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%AA%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%86%D9%81%D8%B7%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D8%AC%D9%86%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A9%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%AA%D8%B9%D8%A7%D9%82%D8%AF%D8%A9%20%D9%84%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%85%D9%84%20%D9%81%D9%8A%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%82%20%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%20(19)%20%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9%202010.pdf)_
- **Tax base** — Net profit from Iraq-source income, determined under the Iraqi unified accounting system (GAAP)  _(Income Tax Law No. 113 of 1982 as amended (to Coalition Provisional Authority Order 49 of 2004), Arabic text published by the General Commission for Taxes, arts 2 (sources of income), 3 (tax on the income of the year preceding the assessment year) and 8 (deductions) — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/uploads/Najwan/law/Income-Tax-Law-No.-113-of-1982-Ar.pdf)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends** — 0% (dividends paid out of taxed profits are not taxed again in the shareholder's hands: a company registered in Iraq deducts the tax paid or payable on its income from the dividends it distributes, limited to dividends on which that tax was paid)  _(Income Tax Law No. 113 of 1982 as amended (to Coalition Provisional Authority Order 49 of 2004), Arabic text published by the General Commission for Taxes, art. 15(1) — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/uploads/Najwan/law/Income-Tax-Law-No.-113-of-1982-Ar.pdf)_
- **Withholding tax on interest to non-residents** — 15% (a resident who pays interest on bonds, mortgages, loans, deposits or advances to a person outside Iraq is responsible for the tax, withholds it at payment and gives the recipient a statement of the gross amount, rate and net paid; banks are exempt from this withholding). The 15% follows the flat company rate that Coalition Provisional Authority Order 49 of 2004 wrote into art. 13 `[RESEARCH GAP — the 1982 text of art. 19 named 20% for payers other than banks; reviewer to confirm the rate now applied under the amended article]`  _(Income Tax Law No. 113 of 1982 as amended (to Coalition Provisional Authority Order 49 of 2004), Arabic text published by the General Commission for Taxes, arts 13 and 19(1) and (2) — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/uploads/Najwan/law/Income-Tax-Law-No.-113-of-1982-Ar.pdf)_
- **Withholding tax on interest between residents** — 1.8% to 10% (approx — confirm exact rate by instrument) `[RESEARCH GAP — the Income Tax Law sets no banded rate on interest between residents; the band is reported by practitioners and was not traced to a statute or instruction]`  _(Income Tax Law No. 113 of 1982 as amended (to Coalition Provisional Authority Order 49 of 2004), Arabic text published by the General Commission for Taxes — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/uploads/Najwan/law/Income-Tax-Law-No.-113-of-1982-Ar.pdf)_
- **Withholding tax on royalties (KRI)** — 15% applied to a 75% deemed-profit base (for goodwill, trademark, patent, copyright and logo contracts (Kurdistan Region deemed-profit method; confirm federal treatment)) `[RESEARCH GAP — Instruction No. 7 of 11 April 2022 of the Kurdistan Regional Government Ministry of Finance and Economy is reported as the source of the deemed-profit schedule; its text was not located]`  _(Kurdistan Regional Government Ministry of Finance and Economy, Instruction No. 7 of 2022 (as reported; text not located))_
- **Withholding tax — non-upstream oil & gas contractors** — The Ministry of Oil withholds 35% of amounts due to foreign oil companies, their branches and subcontractors, and the foreign company withholds 7% of gross payments to its subcontractors, both remitted to the General Commission within 30 days as deposits settled at the final assessment; practitioners report a 3.3% retention on other (non-upstream) contracts `[RESEARCH GAP — the instrument setting the 3.3% retention was not located]`  _(Instructions No. 5 of 2011 facilitating Law No. 19 of 2010 on foreign oil companies (Arabic), General Commission for Taxes copy, Art. 4(1) and (2) — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/uploads/Najwan/law/%D8%AA%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA%20%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%205%20%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9%202011.pdf)_
- **Corporate return filing deadline** — 31 May of the assessment year (a 10% penalty on the tax due applies to later filing)  _(General Commission for Taxes, income tax guide (daleel dharibat al-dakhl: rates, allowances and filing) — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/%D8%AF%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%B6%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D8%AE%D9%84/)_
- **Corporate tax payment deadline** — Within 21 days of notification of the assessment (the taxpayer's signature on the assessment memorandum counts as notification of the amount and due date); a 5% addition applies after those 21 days and doubles if the tax is still unpaid 21 days later. This line previously read 'within 3 days of the assessment date', which the statute does not support  _(Income Tax Law No. 113 of 1982 as amended (to Coalition Provisional Authority Order 49 of 2004), Arabic text published by the General Commission for Taxes, arts 44 and 45 — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/uploads/Najwan/law/Income-Tax-Law-No.-113-of-1982-Ar.pdf)_
- **Advance / instalment payments** — No quarterly or advance payment requirement during the tax year: tax is assessed on the income of the preceding year and falls due on notification, and the authority may allow payment by instalments on a written request, with every instalment falling due if one is more than 21 days late  _(Income Tax Law No. 113 of 1982 as amended (to Coalition Provisional Authority Order 49 of 2004), Arabic text published by the General Commission for Taxes, arts 3, 44 and 46 — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/uploads/Najwan/law/Income-Tax-Law-No.-113-of-1982-Ar.pdf)_
- **Mandatory tax inspection** — A tax inspection is required to determine the liability and issue a tax clearance certificate: the authority investigates the taxpayer's real income and its sources, may suspend related transactions at other departments until the tax is paid, and may require up to 10% of amounts due to contractors to be withheld until their clearance is confirmed  _(Income Tax Law No. 113 of 1982 as amended (to Coalition Provisional Authority Order 49 of 2004), Arabic text published by the General Commission for Taxes, art. 28(2) to (4) — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/uploads/Najwan/law/Income-Tax-Law-No.-113-of-1982-Ar.pdf)_
- **Statute of limitations** — 5 years (extendable by the authority in certain cases): the authority assesses on real income where a person was not assessed, or was under-assessed, in the last assessment year or any of the five years before it, and a failure to notify within that period does not defeat the right  _(Income Tax Law No. 113 of 1982 as amended (to Coalition Provisional Authority Order 49 of 2004), Arabic text published by the General Commission for Taxes, art. 32 — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/uploads/Najwan/law/Income-Tax-Law-No.-113-of-1982-Ar.pdf)_

Iraq applies a flat 15% corporate income tax on all profit levels, with a special 35% rate for the oil and gas production sector. Tax is assessed on Iraq-source income and is finalised through a mandatory tax inspection.
