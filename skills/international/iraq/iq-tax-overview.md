# Iraq Tax Overview

## Iraq tax system at a glance

- **Territorial income tax system administered by GCT; KRI separate administration** — Iraq taxes income on a territorial basis under the Income Tax Law, administered by the General Commission for Taxes. The economy is oil-dependent and the Kurdistan Region (KRI) runs a separate tax administration with its own rates and procedures.
- **Tax (assessment) year** — Calendar year (1 January to 31 December); tax is assessed on the income of the year preceding the assessment year  _(Direct Deduction Instructions No. 1 of 2007 under art. 61(4) of the Income Tax Law, General Commission for Taxes copy, art. 1 (financial year 1 January to 31 December) — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/uploads/pdf/Law/%D8%AA%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA%20%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%201%20%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9%202007.pdf ; General Commission for Taxes, income tax guide (daleel dharibat al-dakhl: rates, allowances and filing) — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/%D8%AF%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%B6%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D8%AE%D9%84/)_
- **National currency** — Iraqi dinar (IQD)  _(Central Bank of Iraq Law (Coalition Provisional Authority Order 56 of 2004), art. 31 (the national monetary unit is the dinar), English text published by the National Investment Commission — https://investpromo.gov.iq/?p=1067)_
- **Federal tax authority** — General Commission of Taxes (الهيئة العامة للضرائب), under the Ministry of Finance. It publishes at `tax.mof.gov.iq` and gives `info@tax.mof.gov.iq` as its contact  _(General Commission of Taxes — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/)_
- **Governing statute** — Income Tax Law **No. 113 of 1982, as amended** (قانون ضريبة الدخل رقم 113 لسنة 1982 المعدل). The GCT's own income-tax guide names this as the law in force and states that the Minister of Finance may propose the tax scale and the level of allowances within the State budget  _(General Commission of Taxes — دليل ضريبة الدخل — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/دليل-ضريبة-الدخل/)_
- **Exemptions** — Set out in **article 7** of the Income Tax Law, granted for social, economic or political reasons or to avoid double taxation  _(General Commission of Taxes — دليل ضريبة الدخل — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/دليل-ضريبة-الدخل/)_
- **Deductions** — A taxpayer may deduct what was spent to obtain the income in the year it arose, where supported by acceptable documents: interest on loans used to obtain the income, rent, sums spent maintaining plant and machinery, and life-insurance premiums among others  _(General Commission of Taxes — دليل ضريبة الدخل — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/دليل-ضريبة-الدخل/)_
- **Kurdistan Region tax administration** — The Kurdistan Region of Iraq (KRI) operates a separate tax authority with distinct rates and procedures: the Directorate of Income Tax of the KRG Ministry of Finance and Economy administers, for example, a 5% direct deduction from monthly salaries above IQD 1,000,000  _(Kurdistan Regional Government services portal, Income Tax (Direct Deduction) — https://services.gov.krd/en/node/610)_
- **Basis of taxation** — Resident Iraqis are taxed on income arising in Iraq or abroad, wherever received; non-residents on income arising in Iraq, whether or not received there; non-Iraqis resident in Iraq are not taxed on income arising outside Iraq  _(Income Tax Law No. 113 of 1982 as amended (Arabic), General Commission for Taxes copy, Art. 5(1) to (3) — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/uploads/Najwan/law/Income-Tax-Law-No.-113-of-1982-Ar.pdf ; Direct Deduction Instructions No. 1 of 2007 (Arabic), General Commission for Taxes copy, Art. 1(1)(c) to (e) — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/uploads/Najwan/law/%D8%A3%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%82%D8%B7%D8%A7%D8%B9%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B6%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A8%D8%A9%20%D8%A8%D8%B7%D8%B1%D9%8A%D9%82%D8%A9%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%82%D8%B7%D8%A7%D8%B9%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%B4%D8%B1%20%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%201%20%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9%202007.pdf)_
- **Top personal income tax rate** — 15% on annual income above IQD 1,000,000 (3% to 250,000, 5% to 500,000, 10% to 1,000,000)  _(General Commission for Taxes, income tax guide (daleel dharibat al-dakhl: rates, allowances and filing) — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/%D8%AF%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%B6%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D8%AE%D9%84/ ; Income Tax Law No. 113 of 1982 as amended, Arabic text published by the General Commission for Taxes, art. 13 (rates) — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/uploads/Najwan/law/Income-Tax-Law-No.-113-of-1982-Ar.pdf)_
- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 15% flat rate at all income levels (no progressive scale)  _(Income Tax Law No. 113 of 1982 as amended, Arabic text published by the General Commission for Taxes, flat 15% rate for companies — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/uploads/Najwan/law/Income-Tax-Law-No.-113-of-1982-Ar.pdf ; General Commission for Taxes, income tax guide (daleel dharibat al-dakhl: rates, allowances and filing) — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/%D8%AF%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%B6%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D8%AE%D9%84/)_
- **Oil & gas sector corporate tax rate** — 35% (income from contracts with foreign oil companies, branches, offices and subcontractors in oil/gas production and related industries)  _(Law No. 19 of 2010 imposing income tax on foreign oil companies contracting in Iraq, General Commission for Taxes copy (scanned) — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/uploads%2FNajwan%2Flaw%2F-%20%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86%20%D9%81%D8%B1%D8%B6%20%D8%B6%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A8%D8%A9%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D8%AE%D9%84%20%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89%20%D8%B4%D8%B1%D9%83%D8%A7%D8%AA%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%86%D9%81%D8%B7%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A3%D8%AC%D9%86%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%A9%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%AA%D8%B9%D8%A7%D9%82%D8%AF%D8%A9%20%D9%84%D9%84%D8%B9%D9%85%D9%84%20%D9%81%D9%8A%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%B1%D8%A7%D9%82%20%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%20(19)%20%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9%202010.pdf)_
- **Value added tax (VAT)** — Iraq has no broad-based VAT; a selective sales tax applies to specified goods and services (mobile and internet services, car imports, air tickets, tobacco and alcohol under the 2015 instructions, and first-class restaurant and hotel services under a 1997 resolution)  _(Instructions No. 5 of 2015 on imposing and collecting the sales tax, issued under art. 33(a) of the 2015 Federal Budget Law (Official Gazette 4363 of 4 May 2015), Ministry of Justice English translation, arts 1 to 4 — https://www.moj.gov.iq/upload/pdf/%D8%AA%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA%20%D9%81%D8%B1%D8%B6%20%D9%88%D8%AC%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%8A%D8%A9%20%D8%B6%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A8%D8%A9%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%AA.pdf)_
- **Corporate return filing deadline** — 31 May of the assessment year; the Commission's guide fixes a 10% penalty on tax due where the income tax return is not filed on or before 31 May  _(General Commission for Taxes, income tax guide (daleel dharibat al-dakhl: rates, allowances and filing) — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/%D8%AF%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%B6%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D8%AE%D9%84/)_
- **Individual return filing deadline** — 31 May of the assessment year: every registered or unregistered person with taxable income files a return before the first day of June unless the authority calls for it earlier by written notice or publication, and the authority may extend the period for a legitimate excuse. This line previously read 31 March, the date in the 1982 text (before the first day of April), which the amended law published by the General Commission replaced  _(Income Tax Law No. 113 of 1982 as amended (to Coalition Provisional Authority Order 49 of 2004), Arabic text published by the General Commission for Taxes, art. 27(2) and (3) — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/uploads/Najwan/law/Income-Tax-Law-No.-113-of-1982-Ar.pdf)_
