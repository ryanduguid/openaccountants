# Iraq VAT / GST

## Sales tax (no broad-based VAT)

Iraq does not operate a broad-based VAT or GST. Instead, a selective sales tax (introduced in 2015) applies to specified goods and services at varying rates. An Iraqi accountant should confirm current rates, as the schedule has changed over time and broader VAT reform has been discussed.

- **Broad-based VAT/GST** — None — Iraq has no general value added tax or goods-and-services tax; the sales tax instructions reach only mobile and internet services, car imports, air tickets, tobacco and alcohol  _(Instructions No. 5 of 2015 on imposing and collecting the sales tax, issued under art. 33(a) of the 2015 Federal Budget Law (Official Gazette 4363 of 4 May 2015), Ministry of Justice English translation, art. 1 First — https://www.moj.gov.iq/upload/pdf/%D8%AA%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA%20%D9%81%D8%B1%D8%B6%20%D9%88%D8%AC%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%8A%D8%A9%20%D8%B6%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A8%D8%A9%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%AA.pdf; Arabic text on the General Commission for Taxes site — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/uploads/Najwan/law/%D8%AA%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA%20%D9%81%D8%B1%D8%B6%20%D9%88%20%D8%AC%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%8A%D8%A9%20%D8%B6%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A8%D8%A9%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%AA%20-%20Copy%202.pdf)_
- **Sales tax — alcohol and tobacco (cigarettes)** — 300% of the customs value of imported cigarettes, tobacco products and alcohol, collected by the tax units at the border; a local producer collects the tax and remits it quarterly  _(Instructions No. 5 of 2015 on imposing and collecting the sales tax, issued under art. 33(a) of the 2015 Federal Budget Law (Official Gazette 4363 of 4 May 2015), Ministry of Justice English translation, art. 4 Third — https://www.moj.gov.iq/upload/pdf/%D8%AA%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA%20%D9%81%D8%B1%D8%B6%20%D9%88%D8%AC%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%8A%D8%A9%20%D8%B6%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A8%D8%A9%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%AA.pdf)_
- **Sales tax — mobile recharge cards and internet** — 20% of the price of the card or of the later-payment bill for mobile and internet services, added to the price shown to the consumer and remitted quarterly  _(Instructions No. 5 of 2015 on imposing and collecting the sales tax, issued under art. 33(a) of the 2015 Federal Budget Law (Official Gazette 4363 of 4 May 2015), Ministry of Justice English translation, arts 2 and 3 — https://www.moj.gov.iq/upload/pdf/%D8%AA%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA%20%D9%81%D8%B1%D8%B6%20%D9%88%D8%AC%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%8A%D8%A9%20%D8%B6%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A8%D8%A9%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%AA.pdf)_
- **Sales tax — travel tickets** — 15% on air tickets for travel inside or outside Iraq, collected by Iraqi Airways, the other national carriers and travel agencies  _(Instructions No. 5 of 2015 on imposing and collecting the sales tax, issued under art. 33(a) of the 2015 Federal Budget Law (Official Gazette 4363 of 4 May 2015), Ministry of Justice English translation, arts 1 Sixth and 4 Second — https://www.moj.gov.iq/upload/pdf/%D8%AA%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA%20%D9%81%D8%B1%D8%B6%20%D9%88%D8%AC%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%8A%D8%A9%20%D8%B6%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A8%D8%A9%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%AA.pdf)_
- **Sales tax — cars** — 15% of the customs value of imported cars, collected by the tax units at the border crossings  _(Instructions No. 5 of 2015 on imposing and collecting the sales tax, issued under art. 33(a) of the 2015 Federal Budget Law (Official Gazette 4363 of 4 May 2015), Ministry of Justice English translation, art. 4 First — https://www.moj.gov.iq/upload/pdf/%D8%AA%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA%20%D9%81%D8%B1%D8%B6%20%D9%88%D8%AC%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%8A%D8%A9%20%D8%B6%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A8%D8%A9%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%AA.pdf)_
- **Sales tax — deluxe / first-class restaurants and hotels** — 10% (approx — confirm current rate) `[RESEARCH GAP — the levy on first-class and deluxe restaurant and hotel services dates from Revolutionary Command Council Resolution No. 36 of 1997 and is outside the 2015 instructions; the resolution's text was not located]`  _(Revolutionary Command Council Resolution No. 36 of 1997 (as reported; text not located))_
- **VAT registration threshold** — Not applicable — there is no VAT registration regime; the sales tax falls on the listed goods and services and is collected by the supplier or at the border  _(Instructions No. 5 of 2015 on imposing and collecting the sales tax, issued under art. 33(a) of the 2015 Federal Budget Law (Official Gazette 4363 of 4 May 2015), Ministry of Justice English translation, arts 1 to 4 — https://www.moj.gov.iq/upload/pdf/%D8%AA%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA%20%D9%81%D8%B1%D8%B6%20%D9%88%D8%AC%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%8A%D8%A9%20%D8%B6%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A8%D8%A9%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%AA.pdf)_
- **Reverse charge mechanism** — Not applicable — no VAT system means no VAT reverse-charge mechanism  _(Instructions No. 5 of 2015 on imposing and collecting the sales tax, issued under art. 33(a) of the 2015 Federal Budget Law (Official Gazette 4363 of 4 May 2015), Ministry of Justice English translation, art. 1 First (scope of the sales tax) — https://www.moj.gov.iq/upload/pdf/%D8%AA%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA%20%D9%81%D8%B1%D8%B6%20%D9%88%D8%AC%D8%A8%D8%A7%D9%8A%D8%A9%20%D8%B6%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A8%D8%A9%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%A8%D9%8A%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%AA.pdf)_
