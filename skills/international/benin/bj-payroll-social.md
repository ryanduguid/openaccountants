# Benin Payroll & Social Contributions

## Benin Payroll & Social Contributions

## Social security and payroll contributions

Employers in Benin contribute to the Caisse Nationale de Sécurité Sociale (CNSS) and pay an employer payroll tax (Versement Patronal sur les Salaires, VPS). Employees contribute to the CNSS pension. Employers also withhold IRPP from salaries (PAYE-equivalent).

- **Employer CNSS contributions (total)** — Approximately 15.4% of gross salary (6.4% pension + 9% family allowances) percent  _(Code de Sécurité Sociale (Bénin) — CNSS (as described at [lloydsbanktrade.com](https://www.lloydsbanktrade.com/en/market-potential/benin/taxes)))_
- **Employer pension contribution** — 6.4% of gross salary percent  _(Code de Sécurité Sociale (Bénin) — CNSS pension (as described at [lloydsbanktrade.com](https://www.lloydsbanktrade.com/en/market-potential/benin/taxes)))_
- **Employer family allowance contribution** — 9% of gross salary percent  _(Code de Sécurité Sociale (Bénin) — prestations familiales (as described at [lloydsbanktrade.com](https://www.lloydsbanktrade.com/en/market-potential/benin/taxes)))_
- **Employer industrial injury insurance (risques professionnels)** — 1% to 4% of gross salary, depending on risk level percent  _(Code de Sécurité Sociale (Bénin) — accidents du travail (as described at [lloydsbanktrade.com](https://www.lloydsbanktrade.com/en/market-potential/benin/taxes)))_
- **Employee CNSS pension contribution** — 3.6% of gross salary percent ((approx — confirm))  _(Code de Sécurité Sociale (Bénin) — CNSS pension salarié (as described at [rivermate.com](https://www.rivermate.com/guides/benin/taxes)))_
- **Employer payroll tax (Versement Patronal sur les Salaires, VPS)** — 4% of gross salaries percent (**reduced to 2% for private education establishments**. Charged on anyone, individual or company, paying salaries, emoluments or accessory remuneration)  _(Code Général des Impôts (Bénin) 2021, arts. 211 and 214 — https://www.impots.finances.gouv.bj/code-general-des-impots/)_
- **VPS base** — Gross salaries, emoluments and other remuneration, **including benefits in cash and in kind** valued under art. 51, less anything expressly exempted  _(Code Général des Impôts (Bénin) 2021, art. 213 — https://www.impots.finances.gouv.bj/code-general-des-impots/)_
- **VPS exemptions** — The State and local authorities; non-profit public services and offices; diplomatic missions and international organisations; taxpayers on the taxe professionnelle synthétique; **newly incorporated companies in their first year of activity**; **any employer, for two years, on pay for an employee's first job** from the hiring date provided the employee is declared to the CNSS; promoters of sporting or artistic activity on pay to professional sportspeople and artists; and pay to interns within art. 50  _(Code Général des Impôts (Bénin) 2021, art. 212 — https://www.impots.finances.gouv.bj/code-general-des-impots/)_
- **VPS payment** — Assessed on the same return as the tax on salary income and paid to the competent tax office on the same terms and deadlines  _(Code Général des Impôts (Bénin) 2021, art. 215 — https://www.impots.finances.gouv.bj/code-general-des-impots/)_
- **CNSS contribution ceiling** — Pension contributions are capped at a monthly salary ceiling set by CNSS ((approx — confirm current ceiling amount))  _(Code de Sécurité Sociale (Bénin) — plafond CNSS)_
- **PAYE-equivalent withholding (IRPP/ITS)** — Employer withholds IRPP on salaries (Impôt sur les Traitements et Salaires) monthly under the progressive barème  _(Code Général des Impôts (Bénin) — retenue sur traitements et salaires (as described at [rivermate.com](https://www.rivermate.com/guides/benin/taxes)))_
- **Remittance deadline — withheld salary tax** — Within the first ten days of the month following the month the salary relates to, in cash or by certified cheque to the Directeur Général des Impôts, with a monthly return in duplicate (electronic filing permitted)  _(Code Général des Impôts (Bénin) 2021, art. 182 — https://www.impots.finances.gouv.bj/code-general-des-impots/)_
- **Remittance deadline — CNSS contributions** — Set by the Code de Sécurité Sociale, not the CGI, and not checked against the primary source here ((approx — confirm CNSS deadline separately))  _(Code de Sécurité Sociale (Bénin) — CNSS)_
- **ORTB broadcasting levy withheld with salary tax** — XOF 1,000 from the March salary and XOF 3,000 from the June salary, on top of the barème; employees within the first (0%) band are exempt from the XOF 3,000 charge  _(Code Général des Impôts (Bénin) 2021, art. 142(3) — https://www.impots.finances.gouv.bj/code-general-des-impots/)_
