# Papua New Guinea Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate tax rates

- **Corporate tax rates overview** — Resident companies are taxed at a flat 30% on trading profits. For the 2025 tax year the Income Tax Act 1959 and the Rates Act 1984 still apply: a non-resident company pays 48%. From the 2026 tax year the Income Tax Act 2025 taxes a non-resident company's PNG permanent establishment at 30% plus 15% non-resident tax on its repatriated profit. Commercial banks and resource projects have their own rates  _(Internal Revenue Commission, Corporate Tax Rates — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/taxes/businesses-and-employers/corporate-tax-rates ; Income Tax Act 2025, ss 11, 14 and 71 and Schedule 1, IRC certified copy — https://static.irc.gov.pg/2025/October/0uvVvY-media-certifiedincometaxact2025.pdf)_
- **Resident company tax rate** — 30% of taxable income in 2025 (Rates Act 1984, s 6 and Schedule 4) and from 2026 (Income Tax Act 2025, Schedule 1 Part I cl (5)(d), "any other company")  _(Income Tax, Dividend (Withholding) Tax and Interest (Withholding) Tax Rates Act 1984 (consolidated to 2021), s 6 and Schedule 4, listed on Internal Revenue Commission, Legislation Home — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/know-your-taxes/legislation-home ; Internal Revenue Commission, Corporate Tax Rates — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/taxes/businesses-and-employers/corporate-tax-rates ; Income Tax Act 2025, Schedule 1 Part I cl (5), IRC certified copy — https://static.irc.gov.pg/2025/October/0uvVvY-media-certifiedincometaxact2025.pdf)_
- **Non-resident company (branch) rate** — 2025 tax year: 48% of the PNG-source taxable income of a non-resident company (Rates Act 1984, Schedule 4; the IRC's corporate rates page). From the 2026 tax year: 30% on the taxable income of the PNG permanent establishment plus 15% non-resident tax on the PE's repatriated profit for the year (s 14(1)(b); the formula in s 71 takes the PE's net profit less income tax and the change in its net assets), giving about 40.5% where the whole after-tax profit is repatriated. The non-resident tax is due with the income tax (s 137(2))  _(Internal Revenue Commission, Corporate Tax Rates — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/taxes/businesses-and-employers/corporate-tax-rates ; Income Tax Act 2025, ss 14(1)(b), 71 and 137(2) and Schedule 1 Part I cl (5)(d) and (9), IRC certified copy — https://static.irc.gov.pg/2025/October/0uvVvY-media-certifiedincometaxact2025.pdf)_
- **Commercial bank rate** — Fiscal year 2025: 40% on taxable income under K300 million and 44% on taxable income over K300 million (Schedule 4.11 as replaced from 1 January 2025). Fiscal year 2026 onwards: 35% under K300 million, and on the excess over K300 million 43% in 2026 falling by one point a year to 35% in 2034 and later years; the Income Tax Act 2025 repeats the same schedule  _(Income Tax, Dividend (Withholding) Tax and Interest (Withholding) Tax Rates (2025 Budget) (Amendment) Act 2024 (No. 17 of 2024), s 2, PacLII — https://www.paclii.org/pg/legis/num_act/itdtaitr2025ba2024808/ ; Income Tax Act 2025, Schedule 1 Part I cl (5)(c), IRC certified copy — https://static.irc.gov.pg/2025/October/0uvVvY-media-certifiedincometaxact2025.pdf)_
- **Overseas shippers (non-resident transport)** — 2025: a deemed taxable income of 5% of gross income taxed at the 48% non-resident company rate, an effective 2.4% of gross. From 2026: international transportation income tax of 2.4% of the gross amount a non-resident derives for carrying passengers, livestock, mail or goods embarked or loaded in PNG for a place outside PNG, a final tax; transit passengers and transhipped goods are excluded  _(Internal Revenue Commission, Corporate Tax Rates — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/taxes/businesses-and-employers/corporate-tax-rates ; Internal Revenue Commission, Withholding Taxes — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/know-your-taxes/withholding-taxes ; Income Tax Act 2025, s 15 and Schedule 1 Part I cl (13), IRC certified copy — https://static.irc.gov.pg/2025/October/0uvVvY-media-certifiedincometaxact2025.pdf)_
- **Mining / petroleum / gas project rate** — 2025 (IRC schedule): resident mining 30%, non-resident mining 40% (reduced from 48% from 1 January 2006), petroleum 45% with a 30% incentive rate for projects from a prospecting licence granted between 1 January 2003 and 31 December 2007 whose development licence was granted by 31 December 2017, gas 30%; mining dividends bear 10% dividend withholding tax. From 2026 the Income Tax Act 2025 taxes a resource licensee at 30% and imposes a 30% additional profits tax, with resource-project income tax paid on the licensee's own estimate in instalments  _(Internal Revenue Commission, Taxation of Companies — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/taxes/businesses-and-employers/taxation-of-companies ; Income Tax Act 2025, Part VIII Division 1 and Schedule 1 Part I cl (22), IRC certified copy — https://static.irc.gov.pg/2025/October/0uvVvY-media-certifiedincometaxact2025.pdf)_

## Tax base, withholding taxes and administration

- **Tax base and administration overview** — The tax base is worldwide income for resident companies, less allowable deductions. Companies file annually following the calendar year. PNG's withholding regime is **not** limited to dividends, interest and royalties — see the non-resident tax heads below  _(Income Tax Act)_

> **Read this before computing any PNG withholding: from the 2026 tax year the
> question is not "what kind of payment is this?" but "does the recipient have a
> PNG permanent establishment?"**
>
> The Income Tax Act 2025 replaces the old Act for tax years commencing on or
> after **1 January 2026** (the 2026 calendar year for a standard taxpayer; a
> substituted accounting period approved by the IRC shifts that). It splits
> non-residents in two:
>
> - **With a PNG permanent establishment** — taxed by **assessment at 30% on the
>   net taxable income attributable to the PE**, not by withholding on gross.
>   This is the change that catches people: a non-resident service provider who
>   used to suffer 15% Foreign Contractors Withholding Tax on gross revenue from
>   a prescribed contract may now be filing a PNG return instead.
> - **Without a PNG permanent establishment** — **Non-Resident Tax (NRT)**,
>   withheld on the **gross** amount as a final tax.
>
> The Act sets presence limits that decide the question, and three of them are
> short enough to be crossed by an ordinary engagement: a **consulting services
> PE** at more than **183 days** in any 12-month period; a **substantial
> equipment PE** at more than **90 days**; and a **construction, assembly or
> installation PE** — including supervisory activity, and including connected
> activities performed by an **associate** — at more than **90 days**. A fixed
> place of business (office, factory, warehouse, mine site) and an agent who
> regularly negotiates or signs contracts also create a PE.
>
> Count the days before quoting a rate. (Income Tax Act 2025, ss 8, 14(4) and (5)
> and 165, IRC certified copy,
> https://static.irc.gov.pg/2025/October/0uvVvY-media-certifiedincometaxact2025.pdf)
- **Corporate tax base** — Resident companies are taxed on worldwide income less allowable deductions; non-resident companies on PNG-source income only, with passive income taxed by withholding. Under the Income Tax Act 2025 taxable income is total assessable income less allowed deductions (s 17), and a non-resident's income that is attributable to a PNG permanent establishment is taxed by assessment rather than by non-resident tax (s 14(4)(b) and (5))  _(Internal Revenue Commission, Taxation of Companies — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/taxes/businesses-and-employers/taxation-of-companies ; Internal Revenue Commission, Corporate Tax Rates — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/taxes/businesses-and-employers/corporate-tax-rates ; Income Tax Act 2025, ss 9, 14 and 17, IRC certified copy — https://static.irc.gov.pg/2025/October/0uvVvY-media-certifiedincometaxact2025.pdf)_
- **Withholding / non-resident tax on dividends** — 2025: 15% dividend withholding tax on dividends paid by a resident company (10% for mining companies), deducted from dividends to residents and non-residents alike and remitted by the 21st of the following month. It is a final tax for non-residents and for resident individuals, and a credit for a resident company against the tax on dividends it pays itself, usable for seven years. From 2026: 15% non-resident tax on a dividend paid to a non-resident (30% where a non-profit body or former non-profit body pays it out of exempt income), and 15% withholding on a dividend a resident company pays to a resident individual, partnership or trust; treaty rates may apply  _(Internal Revenue Commission, Withholding Taxes — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/know-your-taxes/withholding-taxes ; Internal Revenue Commission, Taxation of Companies — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/taxes/businesses-and-employers/taxation-of-companies ; Income Tax Act 2025, ss 14(1)(a), 151(1) and 152 and Schedule 1 Part I cl (9)(e) and (f), (15) and (16), IRC certified copy — https://static.irc.gov.pg/2025/October/0uvVvY-media-certifiedincometaxact2025.pdf)_
- **Withholding / non-resident tax on interest** — 2025: 15% interest withholding tax on most interest derived by residents or from PNG sources, an interim tax for residents and a final tax for non-residents. From 2026: 15% non-resident tax on interest paid to a non-resident, and 15% withholding on interest a resident company or PNG permanent establishment pays to a resident individual, partnership or trust; treaty relief may apply  _(Internal Revenue Commission, Withholding Taxes — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/know-your-taxes/withholding-taxes ; Income Tax Act 2025, ss 14(1)(a) and 151(4) and Schedule 1 Part I cl (9)(d) and (17), IRC certified copy — https://static.irc.gov.pg/2025/October/0uvVvY-media-certifiedincometaxact2025.pdf)_
- **Withholding / non-resident tax on royalties** — 30% where the recipient is an associate of the payer and 10% for any other non-resident recipient, in 2025 and from 2026 alike; from 2026 a 5% withholding also applies to prescribed royalty payments under s 150; treaty rates may apply  _(Internal Revenue Commission, Withholding Taxes — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/know-your-taxes/withholding-taxes ; Income Tax Act 2025, s 150 and Schedule 1 Part I cl (9)(c) and (14), IRC certified copy — https://static.irc.gov.pg/2025/October/0uvVvY-media-certifiedincometaxact2025.pdf)_
- **Withholding / non-resident tax on technical fees** — 2025: 15% Foreign Contractor Withholding Tax on all payments under a prescribed contract (construction, installation, use of substantial equipment, professional or consultancy services), and 17% Management Fee Withholding Tax on management fees paid to a non-resident for services performed outside PNG (reduced to 10% for UK, Malaysian and Indonesian residents, 15% for Fiji, and nil for China, Germany and South Korea under the IRC's treaty table, effective 1 February 2021). From 2026: 15% non-resident tax on a "technical fee", defined as a fee for administrative, management, technical, professional or consultancy services, where the non-resident has no PNG permanent establishment  _(Internal Revenue Commission, Withholding Taxes — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/know-your-taxes/withholding-taxes ; Income Tax Act 2025, s 3 (definition of "technical fee"), s 14 and Schedule 1 Part I cl (9)(d), IRC certified copy — https://static.irc.gov.pg/2025/October/0uvVvY-media-certifiedincometaxact2025.pdf)_
- **Withholding / non-resident tax on annuities** — 15% of the gross annuity paid to a non-resident from the 2026 tax year, reduced by the capital component of a purchased annuity (s 25)  _(Income Tax Act 2025, ss 14(1)(a) and 25 and Schedule 1 Part I cl (9)(d), IRC certified copy — https://static.irc.gov.pg/2025/October/0uvVvY-media-certifiedincometaxact2025.pdf)_
- **Withholding / non-resident tax on natural resource amounts** — 15% of the gross amount from the 2026 tax year where the amount relates to taking minerals, petroleum or other resources from PNG land or waters. A distinct NRT head, separate from the resource-project income tax regimes  _(Income Tax Act 2025, ss 9(h) and 14(1)(a) and Schedule 1 Part I cl (9)(d), IRC certified copy — https://static.irc.gov.pg/2025/October/0uvVvY-media-certifiedincometaxact2025.pdf)_
- **Withholding / non-resident tax on insurance premiums** — 2025: 4.8% on premiums paid to non-resident insurers. From 2026: 3% of the **gross premium** for the insurance or reinsurance of a risk in PNG. The low headline rate is on gross, not on profit  _(Internal Revenue Commission, Withholding Taxes — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/know-your-taxes/withholding-taxes ; Income Tax Act 2025, ss 9(f) and 14(1)(a) and Schedule 1 Part I cl (9)(a), IRC certified copy — https://static.irc.gov.pg/2025/October/0uvVvY-media-certifiedincometaxact2025.pdf)_
- **Withholding / non-resident tax on entertainers** — 10% of the gross amount a non-resident entertainer, sportsperson or group derives from a performance in PNG from the 2026 tax year, applied to the gross amount even where an entity receives it  _(Income Tax Act 2025, s 14(1)(c) and (3) and Schedule 1 Part I cl (9)(b), IRC certified copy — https://static.irc.gov.pg/2025/October/0uvVvY-media-certifiedincometaxact2025.pdf)_
- **Foreign Contractors Withholding Tax (FCWT) — being displaced, not simply continued** — Under the **old** Act a non-resident party to a *prescribed contract* suffered **15% FCWT on gross** payments, deducted by the PNG resident payer, and since 1 January 2017 could not elect to lodge a return and be assessed on net profit instead; an individual performing personal services in PNG was taxed under salary or wages tax rather than FCWT. The Income Tax Act 2025 has no FCWT head: a non-resident acting through a **permanent establishment** is assessed at **30% on the net taxable income** of that PE, and a non-resident without a PE pays 15% non-resident tax on technical fees. Do not quote 15% FCWT for a 2026 engagement without first testing the PE day-counts above  _(Internal Revenue Commission, Withholding Taxes (Foreign Contractor Withholding Tax) — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/know-your-taxes/withholding-taxes ; Income Tax Act 2025, ss 8 and 14(4) and (5), IRC certified copy — https://static.irc.gov.pg/2025/October/0uvVvY-media-certifiedincometaxact2025.pdf)_
- **Business income payments withholding — a resident-facing head** — A paying authority deducts **10%** from contract payments to **local** contractors in prescribed industries unless the contractor produces a **Certificate of Compliance** (a **nil withholding authority** under the 2025 Act). The industries are building, construction or road works; road transport of goods; servicing or repairing motor vehicles; construction of items of joinery that are fixtures in a structure; and security services, which Schedule 7 of the 2025 Act restates in detail. The amount withheld is held as a credit against the contractor's income tax. From 2026 a 5% withholding also applies to payments for prescribed products. This is not a non-resident tax and is easily missed by a payer who has checked only the NRT table  _(Internal Revenue Commission, Business Payments Tax and Income Reporting System — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/taxes/businesses-and-employers/business-payments-tax ; Income Tax Act 2025, s 153, Schedule 1 Part I cl (19) and (20) and Schedule 7, IRC certified copy — https://static.irc.gov.pg/2025/October/0uvVvY-media-certifiedincometaxact2025.pdf)_
- **Resident recipients are not all at the non-resident rate** — 2025: dividend withholding tax applies to resident companies (as a credit) and resident individuals (final), and interest withholding tax is an interim tax for residents; royalties and management fees paid to residents carry no withholding. From 2026: a resident company withholds 15% from dividends and interest paid to resident **individuals**, partnerships and trusts, but not from dividends paid to a resident **company**; there is no withholding head for royalties or technical fees paid to residents beyond the 5% prescribed-royalty and 10% business income payment heads. Read the recipient's residence and entity type before applying a rate from the NRT table  _(Internal Revenue Commission, Withholding Taxes — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/know-your-taxes/withholding-taxes ; Income Tax Act 2025, ss 150, 151 and 153 and Schedule 1 Part I cl (14) to (20), IRC certified copy — https://static.irc.gov.pg/2025/October/0uvVvY-media-certifiedincometaxact2025.pdf)_
- **Treaty rates** — PNG has ten double tax agreements in force: Australia, Canada, China, Fiji, Indonesia, Korea, Malaysia, New Zealand, Singapore and the United Kingdom; the German treaty is signed but not in force. Treaty rates differ by treaty and by head (the IRC's management fee table runs from nil to 17%), so read the treaty before applying a rate. Treaty relief applies to NRT on gross; it does not help a non-resident who has crossed into PE taxation on net, and the 2025 Act treats a treaty permanent establishment as a PE  _(Internal Revenue Commission, International Tax Agreements — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/know-your-taxes/International-Tax-Agreements ; Internal Revenue Commission, Withholding Taxes — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/know-your-taxes/withholding-taxes ; Income Tax Act 2025, ss 8(6) and 14(4), IRC certified copy — https://static.irc.gov.pg/2025/October/0uvVvY-media-certifiedincometaxact2025.pdf)_
- **Capital gains tax — new from 1 January 2026** — PNG had **no CGT regime at all** before the Income Tax Act 2025. The Act imposes a narrow, transaction-based CGT at **15%** on the gain on disposal of a taxable asset: a resource right, information relating to a resource right, a membership interest in an entity where more than 50% of the interest's value derives from such assets at any time in the 365 days before the disposal, or an option over any of them (s 83). The person **disposing** of the asset furnishes a CGT return by the **21st of the month after the disposal** (or the stamping due date if earlier) and pays the tax on the same date (ss 145 and 146); where the disposer is a non-resident the acquirer withholds 10% (s 152(5)), and a resource licensee must report a change of 10% or more in its beneficial ownership within 15 days and is liable as agent for a non-resident disposer's tax (s 102). An asset acquired before 1 January 2026 takes its **fair market value at commencement** as cost unless the owner elects and substantiates actual cost by the return date (s 164(10)); the Act contains **no indexation**, so nominal inflationary gains are taxed. See `pg-income-tax` for the individual-side treatment  _(Income Tax Act 2025, ss 16, 81 to 83, 102, 145, 146, 152(5) and 164(10) and Schedule 1 Part I cl (18) and (21), IRC certified copy — https://static.irc.gov.pg/2025/October/0uvVvY-media-certifiedincometaxact2025.pdf)_
- **Corporate tax year** — Calendar year ending 31 December; a substituted accounting period requires the Commissioner General's permission  _(Internal Revenue Commission, Taxation of Companies — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/taxes/businesses-and-employers/taxation-of-companies)_
- **Corporate return deadline** — For 2024 and earlier years the Commissioner General's annual gazette notice under s 223 of the Income Tax Act 1959 set the dates: 2024 returns were due on or by 28 February 2025, or a later date under the tax agent lodgement extension program (two months after year end for a substituted accounting period), with a court penalty of K500 to K5,000 plus K50 a day for failing to lodge; the notice for 2025 returns is gazetted each January. From the 2026 tax year the Act fixes the dates: a company's return is due within 6 months after year end, or within 9 months where a registered tax agent prepares it (s 135(1)); a non-resident's PE return must include its repatriated profit (s 135(2))  _(National Gazette No. G7 of 7 January 2025, Lodgement of 2024 Income Tax Returns, Parts E and F, PacLII — http://www.paclii.org/pg/other/PGGovGaz/2025/7.pdf ; Income Tax Act 2025, s 135, IRC certified copy — https://static.irc.gov.pg/2025/October/0uvVvY-media-certifiedincometaxact2025.pdf)_
- **Corporate tax payment** — 2025: provisional tax is assessed by the Commissioner General from the last return lodged and paid in three equal instalments on or before 30 April, 31 July and 31 October, credited against the year's assessment; the balance of assessed tax is due within 30 days of the notice of assessment. From the 2026 tax year: three provisional instalments are due by the last day of the month after the end of the third, sixth and ninth months of the tax year (30 April, 31 July and 31 October for a calendar year), each one third of the most recent assessed liability multiplied by the regulated uplift factor less tax withheld (s 138), and the balance is due on the return due date for a self-assessment return or on the date in the notice of assessment otherwise (s 137)  _(Internal Revenue Commission, Taxation of Companies — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/taxes/businesses-and-employers/taxation-of-companies ; Internal Revenue Commission, Tax Information — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/know-your-taxes/tax-information ; Income Tax Act 2025, ss 137 and 138, IRC certified copy — https://static.irc.gov.pg/2025/October/0uvVvY-media-certifiedincometaxact2025.pdf)_
