# Papua New Guinea Tax Overview

## PNG tax system at a glance

Papua New Guinea taxes income under the Income Tax Act, administered by the Internal Revenue Commission (IRC). It operates a calendar tax year, has both personal income tax and a 10% GST, and uses a residence-based system under which residents are taxed on worldwide income.

- **Tax year** — 1 January to 31 December; a company lodges on a 31 December year end unless the Commissioner General permits another period  _(Internal Revenue Commission, Taxation of Companies — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/taxes/businesses-and-employers/taxation-of-companies)_
- **Currency** — Papua New Guinea kina (PGK / K)  _(Central Banking Act)_
- **Tax authority** — Internal Revenue Commission (IRC)  _(Income Tax Act — https://irc.gov.pg/)_
- **Basis of taxation** — Residents are taxed on worldwide income; non-residents only on Papua New Guinea-source income, with passive income subject to withholding  _(Internal Revenue Commission, Taxation of Companies — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/taxes/businesses-and-employers/taxation-of-companies ; Internal Revenue Commission, Corporate Tax Rates — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/taxes/businesses-and-employers/corporate-tax-rates)_
- **Top personal income tax rate** — 42% on the part of taxable income above K250,000 (resident and non-resident tables from 1 January 2024)  _(Internal Revenue Commission, Salary and Wages Tax (rates from 1 January 2024) — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/know-your-taxes/salary-wages-tax ; Income Tax (Salary or Wages Tax) (Rates) (2025 Budget) (Amendment) Act 2024 (No. 18 of 2024), IRC copy — https://media.irc.gov.pg/2025/January/Mj8vk8-document-incometax-salaryorwagestaxratesact2024.pdf)_
- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 30% of taxable income for a resident company; 48% for a non-resident company under the Rates Act consolidated to 2021  _(Income Tax, Dividend (Withholding) Tax and Interest (Withholding) Tax Rates Act 1984 (consolidated to 2021), IRC copy, s 6 and Schedule 4 — https://media.irc.gov.pg/2021/December/S8b5xv-document-Income-Tax-Dividend-Withholding-Tax-and-Interest-Withholding-Tax-Rates-Act-Consolidat.pdf ; Internal Revenue Commission, Corporate Tax Rates — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/taxes/businesses-and-employers/corporate-tax-rates ; PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/papua-new-guinea/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Does PNG have GST/VAT** — Yes; Goods and Services Tax at 10% of the value of goods and services sold or imported, with registration compulsory above K250,000 annual turnover and monthly returns due on the 21st  _(Internal Revenue Commission, Goods and Services Tax — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/taxes/businesses-and-employers/goods-services-tax ; Internal Revenue Commission, Key Tax Dates — https://irc.gov.pg/key-tax-dates)_
- **Individual income tax return deadline** — 28 February following the year of income (30 June if lodged by a registered tax agent)  _(Income Tax Act — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/papua-new-guinea/individual/tax-administration)_
- **Personal tax-free threshold** — K20,000: the first K20,000 of a resident's annual salary or wages is taxed at 0% under the tables from 1 January 2024, then 30% to K33,000, 35% to K70,000, 40% to K250,000 and 42% above  _(Internal Revenue Commission, Salary and Wages Tax (rates from 1 January 2024) — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/know-your-taxes/salary-wages-tax)_
