# Papua New Guinea Tax Overview

For tax assessed by reference to a tax year, apply the Income Tax Act 2025 to years starting on or after 1 January 2026. Check the start date before applying the current rules to a company with a substituted accounting period. [Income Tax Act 2025, ss 164–165][ITA]

## Residence

An individual can be resident by residing in Papua New Guinea, by domicile there unless they have a permanent place of abode abroad, or through at least 183 days in the tax year. The day-count test has an exception if the usual abode is abroad and the individual has no intention of taking up local residence. It counts part days as whole days and includes specified temporary absences. A Papua New Guinean citizen employed by the government and posted abroad can also be resident. The first or last day of physical presence can limit residence in the year of arrival or departure. [Income Tax Act, s 10][ITA]

## Income and territorial scope

Ordinary assessable income includes worldwide income for a resident and Papua New Guinea-source income for a non-resident. The Act defines source separately for employment, business and other categories. Check the permanent establishment and similar sales or activity rules before attributing a non-resident's business profits. Exempt amounts and income subject to specified separate or final taxes are outside ordinary assessable income. [Income Tax Act, ss 9 and 18][ITA]

## Taxpayer type

A company can be resident by incorporation, creation or formation in Papua New Guinea or by central management and control there. Section 10 also specifies how certain trusts and superannuation funds qualify as resident companies. A partnership can be resident by formation or management and control. It files a return separately from its partners, who account for their shares. Trust residence can arise by settlement, establishment or administration in Papua New Guinea or when the trust is the estate of a deceased resident individual. [Income Tax Act, ss 10, 58–67 and 135][ITA]

## Currency

Use Papua New Guinea kina (PGK). Translate at the Bank of Papua New Guinea rate on the date the amount is taken into account. The Commissioner General can give prior written permission for a tax-year average rate for business income and related deductions or written permission to use a qualifying functional currency. [Income Tax Act, s 52][ITA] · [Bank of Papua New Guinea currency][BPNG]

## Assessment periods

For the normal calendar year, count from 31 December. Required ordinary returns are due within 3 months for individuals, partnerships and trusts, 6 months for companies or 9 months for companies whose return a registered tax agent prepares. Check s 136 for exceptions, including some employees and non-residents taxed through specified final charges. The Act generally ties payment to filing for self-assessed liability and to the due date in the notice for an assessment issued by the Commissioner General. [Income Tax Act, ss 135–138][ITA] · [IRC company filing guidance][COMPANY]

## Tax authority and headline rates

The Internal Revenue Commission administers income tax and Goods and Services Tax (GST). The ordinary resident annual individual scale starts at zero through K20,000. The non-resident individual rate for that band is 22% under the 2025 Act. Both ordinary annual scales reach a top marginal rate of 42% above K250,000. The ordinary company rate is 30%, with separate rates for specified entities and a 15% charge on repatriated profits of a non-resident's Papua New Guinea permanent establishment. The IRC gives 10% standard GST and ordinary monthly return and payment by the 21st of the next month. [Income Tax Act, ss 11 and 14 and Schedule 1][ITA] · [IRC GST guidance][GST]
