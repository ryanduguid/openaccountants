# Austria Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax (Körperschaftsteuer)

- **Overview** — Austrian corporations pay a flat 23% CIT on worldwide income (residents) or Austrian-source income (non-residents), with a minimum annual CIT even in loss years. A participation exemption shields most qualifying intercompany dividends.
- **Standard CIT rate** — 23% (applies whether profits are retained or distributed)  _(Körperschaftsteuergesetz 1988 (KStG), RIS consolidated text, § 22 Abs. 1 — https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/1988/401/P22/NOR40269396)_
- **Minimum CIT — GmbH** — EUR 500 per year (EUR 125 per full calendar quarter), being 5% of a quarter of the EUR 10,000 statutory minimum share capital  _(Körperschaftsteuergesetz 1988 (KStG), RIS consolidated text, § 24 Abs. 4 Z 1 — https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/1988/401/P24/NOR40269397 ; GmbH-Gesetz (GmbHG), RIS consolidated text, § 6 Abs. 1 — https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/rgbl/1906/58/P6/NOR40258368)_
- **Minimum CIT — AG** — EUR 3,500 per year (EUR 875 per full calendar quarter), being 5% of a quarter of the EUR 70,000 statutory minimum share capital  _(Körperschaftsteuergesetz 1988 (KStG), RIS consolidated text, § 24 Abs. 4 Z 1 — https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/1988/401/P24/NOR40269397 ; Aktiengesetz 1965 (AktG), RIS consolidated text, § 7 — https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/1965/98/P7/NOR12040509)_
- **Tax base** — Worldwide income for corporations with their seat or place of management in Austria (unlimited liability); Austrian-source income only for other corporations (limited liability)  _(Körperschaftsteuergesetz 1988 (KStG), RIS consolidated text, § 1 Abs. 2 and 3 — https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/1988/401/P1/NOR40254333)_
- **Participation exemption (domestic dividends)** — Profit shares of any kind from participations in Austrian corporations are exempt, with no minimum holding or holding period  _(Körperschaftsteuergesetz 1988 (KStG), RIS consolidated text, § 10 Abs. 1 Z 1 — https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/1988/401/P10/NOR40218459)_
- **International participation exemption** — Dividends and capital gains from an international participation (at least 10% of a comparable foreign corporation held without interruption for at least one year) are exempt, subject to the switch-over and anti-abuse rules in the same section  _(Körperschaftsteuergesetz 1988 (KStG), RIS consolidated text, § 10 Abs. 2 and 3 — https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/1988/401/P10/NOR40218459)_
- **WHT on dividends to non-residents** — 27.5% KESt for individuals; 23% where the recipient is a corporation; exempt at source for EU parent corporations holding at least 10% for an uninterrupted year, and reducible under treaties  _(Einkommensteuergesetz 1988 (EStG), RIS consolidated text, § 27a Abs. 1 — https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/1988/400/P27a/NOR40263362 ; Einkommensteuergesetz 1988 (EStG), RIS consolidated text, § 93 Abs. 1a — https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/1988/400/P93/NOR40254915 ; Einkommensteuergesetz 1988 (EStG), RIS consolidated text, § 94 Z 2 — https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/1988/400/P94/NOR40274899)_
- **WHT on interest to non-residents** — 0% for non-resident corporations and for individuals resident in a state with automatic information exchange, both excluded from the limited liability; 25% (bank deposits) or 27.5% (other Austrian interest) otherwise  _(Einkommensteuergesetz 1988 (EStG), RIS consolidated text, § 98 Abs. 1 Z 5 — https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/1988/400/P98/NOR40205210 ; Einkommensteuergesetz 1988 (EStG), RIS consolidated text, § 27a Abs. 1 — https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/1988/400/P27a/NOR40263362)_
- **WHT on royalties to non-residents** — 20% before treaty or EU Interest and Royalties Directive relief  _(Einkommensteuergesetz 1988 (EStG), RIS consolidated text, § 99 Abs. 1 Z 3 — https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/1988/400/P99/NOR40274901 ; Einkommensteuergesetz 1988 (EStG), RIS consolidated text, § 100 Abs. 1 — https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/1988/400/P100/NOR40254931)_
- **§99 EStG withholding on commercial or technical consultancy** — **20%** where a non-resident provides *kaufmännische oder technische Beratung* in Austria — market research, strategy development, technology transfer and the like. This is the head most often missed, because it is not on the secondary withholding summaries this guide once cited  _(§99 EStG — https://www.usp.gv.at/en/themen/steuern-finanzen/einkommensteuer-ueberblick/weitere-informationen-est/internationales-steuerrecht/pflichten-bei-inlandsaktivitaeten-auslaendischer-unternehmer.html)_
- **§99 EStG withholding on hiring-out of labour (Arbeitskräftegestellung)** — **20%**. A non-Austrian employer leasing staff into Austria is within the charge, and the Austrian recipient of the leased staff is the one who must withhold  _(§99 EStG; BMF, *International Hiring-out of Labour* — https://www.bmf.gv.at/en/topics/taxation/double-taxation-agreements/international-hiring.html)_
- **§99 EStG withholding on supervisory board remuneration** — **20%** on Aufsichtsrat fees and on certain partnership shares  _(§99 EStG — https://www.usp.gv.at/en/themen/steuern-finanzen/einkommensteuer-ueberblick/weitere-informationen-est/internationales-steuerrecht/pflichten-bei-inlandsaktivitaeten-auslaendischer-unternehmer.html)_
- **§99 EStG withholding on artists, entertainers, sportspeople, lecturers, authors and architects** — **20%**, where the activity is **performed in Austria**. The test is where the performance happens, not where the contract sits  _(§99 EStG — https://www.usp.gv.at/en/themen/steuern-finanzen/einkommensteuer-ueberblick/weitere-informationen-est/internationales-steuerrecht/pflichten-bei-inlandsaktivitaeten-auslaendischer-unternehmer.html)_
- **§99 rate exceptions and remittance** — The §99 rate is 20% generally but **27.5%** for the income described in §99(1)(6) and (7). Amounts withheld in a calendar month are remitted in a **single payment by the 15th of the following month**. A net-basis alternative exists under §99(2) where documented expenses are deducted — confirm the applicable net rate against the current §99(2) text before electing it (approx — the net-basis rate was not confirmed for this entry)  _(§99 EStG — https://www.usp.gv.at/en/themen/steuern-finanzen/einkommensteuer-ueberblick/weitere-informationen-est/internationales-steuerrecht/pflichten-bei-inlandsaktivitaeten-auslaendischer-unternehmer.html)_

> **Why these five heads were missing, and what it says about the source.**
>
> The three bullets above (dividends, interest, royalties) are exactly what
> PwC's Austria withholding-taxes page carries. That page does not mention §99
> EStG at all. So a guide built by reading it faithfully, citing it correctly,
> and verifying every figure against it would still miss the entire §99 charge —
> and §99 is the head an Austrian business is most likely to trip over, because
> it catches ordinary consultancy and staff leasing rather than passive income.
>
> This is the same shape as Turkey and Thailand elsewhere in this corpus:
> checking a figure against the source a guide cites cannot tell you about a
> figure the guide does not state. The §99 rates here come from the Austrian
> business service portal (USP) and the Federal Ministry of Finance (BMF)
> instead.
- **Heads beyond these three: checked, not settled** — Whether Austria withholds on commercial or technical consulting fees, on supervisory board fees, or on other service payments to non-residents is **not answered by the source this guide cites**: PwC's Austria withholding page establishes no categories beyond dividends, interest and royalties/licences, and says so. That is a gap in the evidence and not a finding that no such head exists — the two read identically from here. Check §98 and §99 EStG, or ask an Austrian adviser, before telling a payer that a consulting fee to a non-resident carries no Austrian withholding  _(Einkommensteuergesetz (EStG))_
- **Group taxation (Gruppenbesteuerung)** — Available: the results of group members are attributed to the group parent, which must hold more than 50% of the member's capital and voting rights  _(Körperschaftsteuergesetz 1988 (KStG), RIS consolidated text, § 9 Abs. 4 — https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/1988/401/P9/NOR40263406)_
- **CIT advance payments** — Quarterly prepayments due 15 February, 15 May, 15 August, 15 November  _(Körperschaftsteuergesetz 1988 (KStG), RIS consolidated text, § 24 Abs. 3 Z 1 — https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/1988/401/P24/NOR40269397 ; Einkommensteuergesetz 1988 (EStG), RIS consolidated text, § 45 Abs. 2 — https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/1988/400/P45/NOR40174045)_
- **CIT return filing deadline** — End of April of the following year (end of June when filed electronically via FinanzOnline); the tax office may extend the deadline on application, and represented taxpayers obtain longer extensions under the quota arrangement  _(Bundesabgabenordnung (BAO), RIS consolidated text, § 134 Abs. 1 and 2 — https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/1961/194/P134/NOR40263545)_
