# Austria Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax (Körperschaftsteuer)

- **Overview** — Austrian corporations pay a flat 23% CIT on worldwide income (residents) or Austrian-source income (non-residents), with a minimum annual CIT even in loss years. A participation exemption shields most qualifying intercompany dividends.
- **Standard CIT rate** — 23% percent (applies whether profits are retained or distributed)  _(Körperschaftsteuergesetz (KStG) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/austria/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Minimum CIT — GmbH** — EUR 500 per year (EUR 125 per full quarter) EUR  _(Körperschaftsteuergesetz (KStG) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/austria/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Minimum CIT — AG** — EUR 3,500 per year (EUR 875 per full quarter) EUR  _(Körperschaftsteuergesetz (KStG) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/austria/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Tax base** — Worldwide income for companies with seat or place of effective management in Austria; Austrian-source income only for others  _(Körperschaftsteuergesetz (KStG) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/austria/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Participation exemption (domestic dividends)** — Dividends between Austrian corporations are generally tax-exempt regardless of holding size  _(Körperschaftsteuergesetz (KStG) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/austria/corporate/income-determination)_
- **International participation exemption** — Dividends and capital gains exempt where holding is at least 10% held for 1+ year in a qualifying foreign corporation (subject to switch-over/anti-abuse rules)  _(Körperschaftsteuergesetz (KStG) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/austria/corporate/income-determination)_
- **WHT on dividends to non-residents** — 27.5% (individuals) / 23% (corporations) before treaty or EU Parent-Subsidiary relief percent  _(Einkommensteuergesetz (EStG) / Körperschaftsteuergesetz (KStG) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/austria/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **WHT on interest to non-residents** — Generally 0% for non-resident corporations; 25% (bank deposits) / 27.5% (Austrian bonds) can apply to individuals percent  _(Einkommensteuergesetz (EStG) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/austria/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **WHT on royalties to non-residents** — 20% before treaty or EU Interest & Royalties Directive relief percent  _(Einkommensteuergesetz (EStG) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/austria/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **§99 EStG withholding on commercial or technical consultancy** — **20%** where a non-resident provides *kaufmännische oder technische Beratung* in Austria — market research, strategy development, technology transfer and the like. This is the head most often missed, because it is not on the PwC withholding page this guide otherwise cites  _(§99 EStG — https://www.usp.gv.at/en/themen/steuern-finanzen/einkommensteuer-ueberblick/weitere-informationen-est/internationales-steuerrecht/pflichten-bei-inlandsaktivitaeten-auslaendischer-unternehmer.html)_
- **§99 EStG withholding on hiring-out of labour (Arbeitskräftegestellung)** — **20%**. A non-Austrian employer leasing staff into Austria is within the charge, and the Austrian recipient of the leased staff is the one who must withhold  _(§99 EStG; BMF, *International Hiring-out of Labour* — https://www.bmf.gv.at/en/topics/taxation/double-taxation-agreements/international-hiring.html)_
- **§99 EStG withholding on supervisory board remuneration** — **20%** on Aufsichtsrat fees and on certain partnership shares  _(§99 EStG — https://www.usp.gv.at/en/themen/steuern-finanzen/einkommensteuer-ueberblick/weitere-informationen-est/internationales-steuerrecht/pflichten-bei-inlandsaktivitaeten-auslaendischer-unternehmer.html)_
- **§99 EStG withholding on artists, entertainers, sportspeople, lecturers, authors and architects** — **20%**, where the activity is **performed in Austria**. The test is where the performance happens, not where the contract sits  _(§99 EStG — https://www.usp.gv.at/en/themen/steuern-finanzen/einkommensteuer-ueberblick/weitere-informationen-est/internationales-steuerrecht/pflichten-bei-inlandsaktivitaeten-auslaendischer-unternehmer.html)_
- **§99 rate exceptions and remittance** — The §99 rate is 20% generally but **27.5%** for the income described in §99(1)(6) and (7). Amounts withheld in a calendar month are remitted in a **single payment by the 15th of the following month**. A net-basis alternative exists under §99(2) where documented expenses are deducted — confirm the applicable net rate against the current §99(2) text before electing it ((approx — the net-basis rate was not confirmed for this entry))  _(§99 EStG — https://www.usp.gv.at/en/themen/steuern-finanzen/einkommensteuer-ueberblick/weitere-informationen-est/internationales-steuerrecht/pflichten-bei-inlandsaktivitaeten-auslaendischer-unternehmer.html)_

> **Why these five heads were missing, and what it says about the source.**
>
> The three bullets above (dividends, interest, royalties) are exactly what
> PwC's Austria withholding-taxes page carries. That page does not mention §99
> EStG at all. So a guide built by reading it faithfully, citing it correctly,
> and verifying every figure against it would still miss the entire §99 charge —
> and §99 is the head an Austrian business is most likely to trip over, because
> it catches ordinary consultancy and staff leasing rather than passive income.
>
> This is the same shape as Turkey and Thailand elsewhere in this corpus:
> checking a figure against the source a guide cites cannot tell you about a
> figure the guide does not state. The §99 rates here come from the Austrian
> business service portal (USP) and the Federal Ministry of Finance (BMF)
> instead.
- **Heads beyond these three: checked, not settled** — Whether Austria withholds on commercial or technical consulting fees, on supervisory board fees, or on other service payments to non-residents is **not answered by the source this guide cites**: PwC's Austria withholding page establishes no categories beyond dividends, interest and royalties/licences, and says so. That is a gap in the evidence and not a finding that no such head exists — the two read identically from here. Check §98 and §99 EStG, or ask an Austrian adviser, before telling a payer that a consulting fee to a non-resident carries no Austrian withholding  _(Einkommensteuergesetz (EStG))_
- **Group taxation (Gruppenbesteuerung)** — Available — profits/losses of group members can be pooled at the group parent; requires a financial connection of more than 50%  _(Körperschaftsteuergesetz (KStG) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/austria/corporate/group-taxation)_
- **CIT advance payments** — Quarterly prepayments due 15 February, 15 May, 15 August, 15 November  _(Bundesabgabenordnung (BAO) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/austria/corporate/tax-administration)_
- **CIT return filing deadline** — 30 April of the following year (30 June via FinanzOnline); represented taxpayers may obtain longer extensions  _(Bundesabgabenordnung (BAO) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/austria/corporate/tax-administration)_
