# Austria Tax Overview

## Austrian tax system at a glance

- **Tax year** — Calendar year; taxpayers keeping books and corporations obliged to keep accounts may adopt a financial year that departs from the calendar year, with the profit taxed in the calendar year in which it ends  _(Einkommensteuergesetz 1988 (EStG), RIS consolidated text, § 2 Abs. 5 — https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/1988/400/P2/NOR40246516 ; Körperschaftsteuergesetz 1988 (KStG), RIS consolidated text, § 7 Abs. 5 — https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/1988/401/P7/NOR40254334)_
- **Currency** — Euro (EUR)  _(Austrian national currency (Eurozone member))_
- **Tax authority** — Finanzamt Österreich (Federal Ministry of Finance / Bundesministerium für Finanzen), filings via FinanzOnline  _(Bundesabgabenordnung (BAO) — https://www.bmf.gv.at)_
- **Residence basis of taxation** — Residents taxed on worldwide income; non-residents taxed on Austrian-source income only  _(Einkommensteuergesetz 1988 (EStG), RIS consolidated text, § 1 Abs. 2 and 3 — https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/1988/400/P1/NOR40274820)_
- **Top personal income tax rate** — 55 percent (on income above EUR 1,000,000, for the 2016 to 2029 calendar years)  _(Einkommensteuergesetz 1988 (EStG), RIS consolidated text, § 33 Abs. 1 (2025 text) — https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/1988/400/P33/NOR40269378)_
- **Personal income tax-free threshold** — 13,308 EUR for 2025 (the 20% band then runs to EUR 21,617); the indexed 2026 tariff starts at EUR 13,539  _(Einkommensteuergesetz 1988 (EStG), RIS consolidated text, § 33 Abs. 1 (2025 text) — https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/1988/400/P33/NOR40269378 ; Einkommensteuergesetz 1988 (EStG), RIS consolidated text, § 33 Abs. 1 (current text, 2026 figures) — https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/1988/400/P33/NOR40279893)_
- **Corporate income tax (CIT) rate** — 23 percent (flat)  _(Körperschaftsteuergesetz 1988 (KStG), RIS consolidated text, § 22 Abs. 1 — https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/1988/401/P22/NOR40269396)_
- **VAT (Umsatzsteuer) in force** — Yes — standard rate 20%, reduced rates 13% and 10%  _(Umsatzsteuergesetz 1994 (UStG), RIS consolidated text, § 10 Abs. 1 to 3 — https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/1994/663/P10/NOR40278068)_
- **Annual personal income tax return deadline** — End of April of the following year (end of June if filed electronically via FinanzOnline); the tax office may extend the deadline on application, and longer extensions apply where a tax adviser files under the quota arrangement  _(Bundesabgabenordnung (BAO), RIS consolidated text, § 134 Abs. 1 and 2 — https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/1961/194/P134/NOR40263545)_
- **Annual corporate income tax return deadline** — End of April of the following year (end of June via FinanzOnline); represented taxpayers can extend under the quota arrangement (typically into the following year)  _(Bundesabgabenordnung (BAO), RIS consolidated text, § 134 Abs. 1 and 2 — https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/1961/194/P134/NOR40263545)_
- **Flat tax on capital investment income (KESt)** — 27.5% (25% on interest from bank deposits and other non-securitised claims against credit institutions)  _(Einkommensteuergesetz 1988 (EStG), RIS consolidated text, § 27a Abs. 1 — https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/1988/400/P27a/NOR40263362)_

Austria taxes residents on worldwide income and operates a self-assessment-with-assessment system administered through FinanzOnline. The figures below are 2025 headline parameters; an Austrian accountant should confirm each before reliance.
