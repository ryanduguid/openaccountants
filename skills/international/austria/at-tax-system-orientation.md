# Austria tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record where the person has a home or usually stays, where the business is established or managed, the legal form, the source of each income item and the accounting period.

## Territorial reach

Individuals with unlimited liability generally pay income tax on domestic and foreign income under the Income Tax Act. Corporations with unlimited liability generally pay corporation tax on domestic and foreign income under the Corporate Income Tax Act. Limited liability covers the items listed in the applicable Act. For individuals, § 98 includes specified income from work, Austrian business activity and Austrian real estate. For foreign corporations, check the category and charge under §§ 1 and 21 of the Corporate Income Tax Act. Apply domestic exemptions and treaty limits to the item in question. [Income Tax Act 1988, §§ 1 and 98](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/geltendefassung.wxe?abfrage=bundesnormen&gesetzesnummer=10004570) · [Corporate Income Tax Act 1988, §§ 1 and 21](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/GeltendeFassung.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004569&ShowPrintPreview=True)

## Residence

An individual generally comes within unlimited liability through an Austrian home or habitual abode. A home is a dwelling kept in circumstances indicating that the person intends to retain and use it. Habitual abode depends on a stay that is more than temporary. A stay of more than 6 months normally establishes unlimited liability back to the start of the stay under § 26(2). Check the relevant exceptions and treaty residence, including the domestic second-home regulation where applicable. A covered entity generally comes within unlimited corporate liability if its seat or management is in Austria. Management means the centre of its senior business direction. [Income Tax Act 1988, § 1](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/geltendefassung.wxe?abfrage=bundesnormen&gesetzesnummer=10004570) · [Federal Fiscal Code, §§ 26–27](https://ris.bka.gv.at/GeltendeFassung.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10003940&ShowPrintPreview=True) · [Second-Home Regulation](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/GeltendeFassung.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=20003027) · [Corporate Income Tax Act 1988, § 1](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/GeltendeFassung.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004569&ShowPrintPreview=True)

## Taxpayer types

A sole trader accounts for business income under the individual rules. Partners in an ordinary trading partnership such as an OG or KG generally account for their shares in their own tax assessments after the partnership result is established. An Austrian GmbH or AG is a separate corporate taxpayer. For a foreign trust, check the deed, who can exercise control and who has the economic ownership of the income-producing assets. Check separately whether payments from a foreign asset pool comparable to a private foundation fall under § 27(5)(7). [Income Tax Act 1988, §§ 23 and 27](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/geltendefassung.wxe?abfrage=bundesnormen&gesetzesnummer=10004570) · [Business Service Portal, taxation of entities](https://www.usp.gv.at/themen/steuern-finanzen/einkommensteuer-ueberblick/weitere-informationen-est/besteuerung-von-gesellschaften.html) · [Ministry of Finance, US trusts and Austrian foundations](https://findok.bmf.gv.at/findok/iwg/44/44920/44920.1.pdf) · [Administrative Court, Ra 2024/13/0131](https://ris.bka.gv.at/Dokumente/Vwgh/JWT_2024130131_20260129L00/JWT_2024130131_20260129L00.html)

## Currency

Austria uses the euro (EUR). Annual accounts within § 193 of the Commercial Code are prepared in EUR. Identify foreign-currency transactions and retain the rates used to translate them into the accounts and any separately prescribed tax amounts. [Commercial Code, § 193](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/geltendefassung.wxe?ShowPrintPreview=True&abfrage=bundesnormen&gesetzesnummer=10001702)

## Assessment periods

The ordinary income tax period is the calendar year. Eligible individual businesses and corporations can use a different financial year under their respective statutes. Where the ordinary shifted-year rule applies, attribute the profit to the calendar year in which the financial year ends. Check the short-period and change-of-year rules separately. [Income Tax Act 1988, § 2](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/geltendefassung.wxe?abfrage=bundesnormen&gesetzesnummer=10004570) · [Corporate Income Tax Act 1988, § 7](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/GeltendeFassung.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10004569&ShowPrintPreview=True)

## Responsible authorities

Finanzamt Österreich handles ordinary income tax, corporation tax and VAT for taxpayers within its jurisdiction. Finanzamt für Großbetriebe covers taxpayers meeting its statutory criteria. Both have nationwide jurisdiction. Zollamt Österreich handles customs. [Federal Fiscal Code, §§ 60–63](https://ris.bka.gv.at/GeltendeFassung.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10003940&ShowPrintPreview=True) · [Business Service Portal, responsibilities of the tax offices](https://www.usp.gv.at/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuerliche-rechte-und-pflichten/weitere-informationen-zu-steuerlichen-rechten-und-pflichten-als-unternehmen/zustaendigkeiten-der-finanzaemter.html)

## Fictional intake

An individual with homes in 2 countries receives a share of an Austrian KG's profit and a payment from a foreign trust. Record the facts for domestic and treaty residence, obtain the partnership's profit allocation and read the trust documents before deciding who accounts for its income or distributions.
