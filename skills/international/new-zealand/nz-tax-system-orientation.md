# New Zealand tax system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the person or entity, residence, income source, currency, income year and any approved balance date. Confirm the specific tax and authority before filing.

## Territorial reach

The general statutory geographical definition includes the islands and territories in the Realm of New Zealand except the Cook Islands, Niue, Tokelau and the Ross Dependency. Check for a different statutory or treaty definition where relevant. The Income Tax Act imposes income tax on taxable income. Section BD 1 excludes exempt income, excluded income and defined non-residents’ foreign-sourced income from assessable income. The last category has a specific exception for relevant trustee income under s HC 25(2). Use ss YD 4–5 to classify and apportion New Zealand source where required. [Legislation Act 2019, ss 9 and 13](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2019/58/en/latest/DLM7298125/) · [Income Tax Act 2007, ss BB 1 and BD 1](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2007/97/en/2026-04-01/parts/DLM1512313/#DLM1512361) · [Income Tax Act 2007, ss YD 4–5](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2007/97/en/2026-04-01/parts/DLM1520573/#DLM1523142)

## Residence

For an individual, check the permanent place of abode and the number of days present. Section YD 1 generally treats more than 183 days of presence in a 12-month period as residence, with the statutory backdate. Ending residence under the day-count rule requires more than 325 days of absence in a 12-month period and the permanent-place-of-abode test must also be considered. Check the part-day rules and the provisions for government service abroad, recognised seasonal workers and non-resident visitors. The non-resident visitor provisions in ss YD 1(13)–(14) and YD 1B were inserted on 1 April 2026. For a company, each of incorporation, head office, centre of management and exercise of control by its directors can establish residence in New Zealand. Verify any applicable treaty separately. [Income Tax Act 2007, ss YD 1–2](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2007/97/en/2026-04-01/parts/DLM1520573/#DLM1523134)

## Taxpayer types

Distinguish individuals including sole traders, companies, partners and trustees. Partnerships and look-through companies have separate statutory transparency provisions. Confirm status and each owner’s share before assigning income or deductions. For trusts, classify trustee and beneficiary income under subpart HC and examine the settlor and residence facts separately. [Income Tax Act 2007, subparts HB, HC and HG](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2007/97/en/2026-04-01/parts/DLM1517125/#DLM3676672)

## Currency

The monetary unit is the New Zealand dollar (NZD). Section YF 1 gives the general conversion rule when the Income Tax Act requires a foreign-currency amount to be taken into account in NZD and no more specific conversion rule applies. Identify the prescribed rate method and conversion date for the particular amount. [Decimal Currency Act 1964, s 5](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/1964/0027/latest/whole.html) · [Income Tax Act 2007, s YF 1](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2007/97/en/2026-04-01/parts/DLM1520573/#DLM1523167)

## Assessment periods

The standard income year runs from 1 April to 31 March. An eligible taxpayer may use a different balance date with the Commissioner’s consent under s 38. Reconcile the elected income year to the tax year and check any transition period. Use the separate return period for GST and the applicable reporting period for employer obligations. [Income Tax Act 2007, s YA 1](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2007/97/en/2026-04-01/parts/DLM1520573/#DLM1520575) · [Tax Administration Act 1994, s 38](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/1994/166/en/2026-04-01/#DLM351171) · [Inland Revenue, balance dates](https://www.ird.govt.nz/income-tax/income-tax-for-businesses-and-organisations/balance-dates)

## Responsible authorities

The Commissioner of Inland Revenue has care and management of taxes covered by the Inland Revenue Acts. Identify the Inland Revenue return or assessment for the relevant tax and period. The New Zealand Customs Service has separate responsibilities for GST and duty on goods at the border, including assessment under the import rules. Verify the point of collection for the actual supply or import. [Tax Administration Act 1994, s 6A](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/1994/166/en/2026-04-01/) · [Goods and Services Tax Act 1985, s 12](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/1985/141/en/2026-04-01/sections/DLM83048/)

## Fictional intake

Assume a New Zealand-incorporated company imports goods, receives foreign-currency payments and has an approved 30 June balance date. Record incorporation as a domestic residence ground and confirm the approval and income year. Identify the Inland Revenue obligations for corporate income and the separate Customs assessment for the imports. Determine which conversion rule applies to each amount. Confirm any treaty, rates, deadlines, classifications and concessions from the relevant sources before completing a filing.
