# Burkina Faso Company Formation & Entity Choice

## Entity types and incorporation

> **The uniform act has been read, and both figures are in it — attached to two
> different things.** Burkina Faso and Gabon are both **OHADA** member states, so
> their company law is not national law: it is the same *Acte uniforme relatif au
> droit des sociétés commerciales et du groupement d'intérêt économique* (AUSCGIE),
> adopted at Ouagadougou on **30 January 2014** and published in the **Journal
> Officiel de l'OHADA, numéro spécial du 4 février 2014**. This guide cited the
> AUSCGIE for a SARL minimum capital of *"at least the par value of one share
> (5,000 FCFA)"*; `ga-company-formation` cited the same instrument for **1,000,000
> FCFA**. **Article 311 contains both numbers, and the guide attached the wrong one
> to minimum capital:**
>
> *"**Sauf dispositions nationales contraires**, le capital social doit être d'un
> million (1.000.000) de francs CFA au moins. Il est divisé en parts sociales égales
> dont la valeur nominale ne peut être inférieure à cinq mille (5.000) francs CFA."*
>
> So the uniform minimum capital is **1,000,000 FCFA** and **5,000 FCFA is the
> minimum nominal value of a single part sociale** — a floor on the denomination of
> the units, not on the capital.
>
> **An earlier version of this note guessed wrongly and is corrected here.** It said
> the 2014 revision "is understood to have left the SARL's minimum capital to each
> member state", so that neither figure belonged to the uniform act. Both do. What
> the revision did is subtler and matters more: it opened article 311 with **"sauf
> dispositions nationales contraires"**, so a member state **may** displace the
> 1,000,000 default by its own legislation. **Article 387's SA minimum of 10,000,000
> FCFA carries no such clause** and is not displaceable.
>
> [RESEARCH GAP — what is not settled is whether **Burkina Faso** has enacted
> contrary national provisions under article 311. The uniform default is stated
> below as the default it is. A reviewer with the Burkinabè company legislation
> should confirm whether it displaces the 1,000,000 figure; the SA and par-value
> rows need no such check.]
>
> _(Acte uniforme relatif au droit des sociétés commerciales et du GIE, adopté le 30 janvier 2014 — Journal Officiel de l'OHADA, n° spécial du 4 février 2014: https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974)_

- **Governing company law** — The **OHADA Uniform Act on Commercial Companies and Economic Interest Groups (AUSCGIE)**, adopted 30 January 2014 at Ouagadougou. It applies directly in every member state; the rules below are the uniform text, and only article 311 admits a national variation  _(AUSCGIE 2014 — https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974)_
- **SARL (Société à Responsabilité Limitée)** — A company in which the members answer for the company's debts only up to their contributions, and whose rights are represented by *parts sociales*. **It may be instituted by a single natural or legal person**, or between two or more. The name must be immediately preceded or followed, in legible characters, by *"société à responsabilité limitée"* or *"S.A.R.L."*  _(AUSCGIE 2014, arts. 309–310 — https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974)_
- **SA (Société Anonyme)** — A company in which the shareholders answer for the company's debts only up to their contributions and their rights are represented by shares. ***"La société anonyme peut ne comprendre qu'un seul actionnaire."*** The name must carry *"société anonyme"* or *"S.A."* **and the mode of administration** under article 414  _(AUSCGIE 2014, arts. 385–386 — https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974)_
- **Single-member companies generally** — A commercial company may be created by one person, the *associé unique*, by written act, in the cases the Act provides  _(AUSCGIE 2014, art. 5 — https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974)_
- **SARL minimum share capital** — **1,000,000 FCFA**, and this is the only capital figure in the Act that national law may displace: article 311 opens *"sauf dispositions nationales contraires"*. Check Burkinabè legislation before relying on it FCFA  _(AUSCGIE 2014, art. 311 — https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974)_
- **Minimum nominal value of a part sociale (SARL)** — **5,000 FCFA**. The capital is divided into **equal** parts sociales and none may be denominated below this. This is a floor on the units, not on the capital — the two were previously conflated FCFA  _(AUSCGIE 2014, art. 311 — https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974)_
- **SARL pay-up on formation** — The parts sociales must be **subscribed in full**. Those representing contributions in kind must be **paid up in full**; those representing cash are paid up on subscription to **at least half** of their nominal value, with the balance in one or more instalments **within two years** of registration in the RCCM  _(AUSCGIE 2014, art. 311-1 — https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974)_
- **SARL contributions in kind — when a valuer is compulsory** — The statutes must value each contribution in kind. A ***commissaire aux apports*** is required once the value of a contribution in kind, **or of all of them taken together**, exceeds **5,000,000 FCFA**; for *avantages particuliers* the valuation is always controlled FCFA  _(AUSCGIE 2014, art. 312 — https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974)_
- **SARL deposit of funds** — The founder must deposit the paid-up funds **immediately**, against receipt, with a bank or any other duly approved credit or **microfinance** institution, into an account in the name of the company in formation, or with a notary. Unless national provisions say otherwise, payment and deposit are recorded by a notary of the registered-office district in a *déclaration notariée de souscription et de versement*, and the funds are **unavailable until RCCM registration**  _(AUSCGIE 2014, arts. 313–314 — https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974)_
- **SA minimum share capital** — **10,000,000 FCFA**. Article 387 carries **no national-derogation clause**, so unlike the SARL figure this one is uniform across every OHADA state. The nominal amount of the shares is **freely fixed by the statutes** and must be a whole number FCFA  _(AUSCGIE 2014, art. 387 — https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974)_
- **SA subscription and pay-up** — The capital must be **entirely subscribed before the date the statutes are signed**. Shares representing cash contributions are paid up on subscription to **at least one quarter** of their nominal value, the balance falling due within a period not exceeding **three years** from RCCM registration  _(AUSCGIE 2014, arts. 388–389 — https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974)_
- **Registration one-stop-shop** — CEFORE (Centre de Formalités des Entreprises), Maison de l'Entreprise du Burkina Faso  _([CEFORE business registration procedures](https://www.usemultiplier.com/burkina-faso/company-registration))_
- **Beneficial ownership disclosure** — Founders must identify and declare ultimate beneficial owner (UBO) information at the point of CEFORE registration  _([CEFORE business registration procedures](https://www.expanship.com/bf/blog/incorporation-requirements-in-burkina-faso))_
- **Incorporation timeline** — Registration through CEFORE typically completed within a few days to a couple of weeks ((approx — confirm))  _([CEFORE business registration procedures](https://www.usemultiplier.com/burkina-faso/company-registration))_
- **Incorporation cost** — Government administrative registration fees under ~50 EUR; total professional/notary budget commonly 1,000–1,500 EUR (excluding share capital) ((approx — confirm))  _([CEFORE business registration procedures](https://www.expanship.com/bf/blog/incorporation-requirements-in-burkina-faso))_
- **Core annual compliance** — Annual financial statements (SYSCOHADA), annual IS return, monthly TVA returns, IUTS and CNSS declarations, and renewal of business registrations  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI); SYSCOHADA accounting framework)_
