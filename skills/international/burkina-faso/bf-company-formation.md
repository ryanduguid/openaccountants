# Burkina Faso Company Formation & Entity Choice

## Entity types and incorporation

Burkina Faso applies the OHADA AUSCGIE together with national provisions where
the uniform act permits them. Article 311 sets a default SARL capital of
FCFA 1,000,000, subject to contrary national provisions. Burkina Faso exercised
that power: article 1 of Décret n°2016-314 of 3 May 2016 replaces article 3 of
Décret n°2014-462 with a rule allowing members to set SARL capital freely in the
articles. The FCFA 5,000 minimum nominal value per share under AUSCGIE article
311 still applies. An ordinary SARL with one such share can therefore have
FCFA 5,000 capital; the OHADA default is not its national minimum.

[National decree, arts. 1–2](https://police.gov.bf/index.php/infos-utiles/textes-officiels/category/3-decrets?download=26:decret-capital-minimum);
[AUSCGIE, art. 311](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974).

- **Governing company law**: The **OHADA Uniform Act on Commercial Companies and Economic Interest Groups (AUSCGIE)**, adopted 30 January 2014 at Ouagadougou. It applies directly in member states, with national rules where it permits them, including the Burkina Faso SARL capital decree cited above  _(AUSCGIE 2014: https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974)_
- **SARL (Société à Responsabilité Limitée)** — A company in which the members answer for the company's debts only up to their contributions, and whose rights are represented by *parts sociales*. **It may be instituted by a single natural or legal person**, or between two or more. The name must be immediately preceded or followed, in legible characters, by *"société à responsabilité limitée"* or *"S.A.R.L."*  _(AUSCGIE 2014, arts. 309–310 — https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974)_
- **SA (Société Anonyme)** — A company in which the shareholders answer for the company's debts only up to their contributions and their rights are represented by shares. ***"La société anonyme peut ne comprendre qu'un seul actionnaire."*** The name must carry *"société anonyme"* or *"S.A."* **and the mode of administration** under article 414  _(AUSCGIE 2014, arts. 385–386 — https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974)_
- **Single-member companies generally** — A commercial company may be created by one person, the *associé unique*, by written act, in the cases the Act provides  _(AUSCGIE 2014, art. 5 — https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974)_
- **SARL share capital**: members set it freely in the articles under Décret n°2016-314, art. 1. Each share must have a nominal value of at least **FCFA 5,000** under AUSCGIE art. 311. The FCFA 1,000,000 uniform default is displaced in Burkina Faso. [National decree](https://police.gov.bf/index.php/infos-utiles/textes-officiels/category/3-decrets?download=26:decret-capital-minimum).
- **Minimum nominal value of a part sociale (SARL)**: **5,000 FCFA**. The capital is divided into **equal** parts sociales and none may be denominated below this. Apply this share-value rule together with the national capital decree  _(AUSCGIE 2014, art. 311: https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974)_
- **SARL pay-up on formation** — The parts sociales must be **subscribed in full**. Those representing contributions in kind must be **paid up in full**; those representing cash are paid up on subscription to **at least half** of their nominal value, with the balance in one or more instalments **within two years** of registration in the RCCM  _(AUSCGIE 2014, art. 311-1 — https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974)_
- **SARL contributions in kind — when a valuer is compulsory** — The statutes must value each contribution in kind. A ***commissaire aux apports*** is required once the value of a contribution in kind, **or of all of them taken together**, exceeds **5,000,000 FCFA**; for *avantages particuliers* the valuation is always controlled FCFA  _(AUSCGIE 2014, art. 312 — https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974)_
- **SARL deposit of funds** — The founder must deposit the paid-up funds **immediately**, against receipt, with a bank or any other duly approved credit or **microfinance** institution, into an account in the name of the company in formation, or with a notary. Unless national provisions say otherwise, payment and deposit are recorded by a notary of the registered-office district in a *déclaration notariée de souscription et de versement*, and the funds are **unavailable until RCCM registration**  _(AUSCGIE 2014, arts. 313–314 — https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974)_
- **SA minimum share capital**: **10,000,000 FCFA** for an ordinary SA under article 387. Public offerings and regulated activities require separate capital checks. The nominal amount of the shares is **freely fixed by the statutes** and must be a whole number FCFA  _(AUSCGIE 2014, art. 387: https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974)_
- **SA subscription and pay-up** — The capital must be **entirely subscribed before the date the statutes are signed**. Shares representing cash contributions are paid up on subscription to **at least one quarter** of their nominal value, the balance falling due within a period not exceeding **three years** from RCCM registration  _(AUSCGIE 2014, arts. 388–389 — https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974)_
- **Registration one-stop-shop** — CEFORE (Centre de Formalités des Entreprises), Maison de l'Entreprise du Burkina Faso  _([CEFORE business registration procedures](https://www.usemultiplier.com/burkina-faso/company-registration))_
- **Beneficial ownership disclosure** — Founders must identify and declare ultimate beneficial owner (UBO) information at the point of CEFORE registration  _([CEFORE business registration procedures](https://www.expanship.com/bf/blog/incorporation-requirements-in-burkina-faso))_
- **Incorporation timeline** — Registration through CEFORE typically completed within a few days to a couple of weeks (approx — confirm)  _([CEFORE business registration procedures](https://www.usemultiplier.com/burkina-faso/company-registration))_
- **Incorporation cost** — Government administrative registration fees under ~50 EUR; total professional/notary budget commonly 1,000–1,500 EUR (excluding share capital) (approx — confirm)  _([CEFORE business registration procedures](https://www.expanship.com/bf/blog/incorporation-requirements-in-burkina-faso))_
- **Core annual compliance** — Annual financial statements (SYSCOHADA), annual IS return, monthly TVA returns, IUTS and CNSS declarations, and renewal of business registrations  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI); SYSCOHADA accounting framework)_

## Annual company reporting under the 2014 AUSCGIE

The revised AUSCGIE took effect on **5 May 2014**. The following rules address
annual SARL and SA decisions and commercial-company registry deposits.
[OHADA company law register](https://www.ohada.org/droit-des-societes-commerciales-et-du-gie/)

- **SARL annual meeting.** The annual ordinary general meeting must take place
  within **6 months after the financial year closes**. The managers may ask the
  competent court for an extension. [AUSCGIE, articles 347 and 348](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974#page=86)
- **SA annual meeting.** The ordinary general meeting must take place at least
  once a year, within **6 months after the financial year closes**, subject to
  an extension by court decision. [AUSCGIE, article 548](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974#page=133)
- **Sole-member companies.** The sole shareholder of an SA takes the annual
  ordinary decisions within **6 months after the financial year closes** and
  records them in minutes kept in the company's archives. Article 347 applies
  these sole-member decision rules to a SARL, with the exceptions it specifies.
  [AUSCGIE, article 347](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974#page=86),
  [articles 558 to 560](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974#page=135)
- **Deposit of annual accounts.** A commercial company must deposit the prior
  year's annual financial statements with the RCCM in the state of its
  registered office **within the month following approval** by the competent
  body. If approval is refused, a copy of the refusal decision must be deposited
  within the same period. [AUSCGIE, article 269, pages 65 and 66](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974#page=65)

For example, for a multi-member SARL with a **31 December 2025** year-end, the
ordinary annual meeting is due by **30 June 2026**, subject to a court extension.
Track the RCCM deposit deadline from the actual approval decision. Record the
national tax-return deadline separately. The current registry filing method,
fees, applicable accounting and audit rules and any sector-specific obligations
still need verification. [AUSCGIE, articles 269 and 348](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974)
