# Central African Republic Personal Income Tax

## Personal income tax (IRPP) bands and rates

> **This guide's twelve rows were sourced entirely to one HR platform until September
> 2026. They have now been checked against the Code itself.** The **Ministère des Finances
> et du Budget** publishes the ***Code Général des Impôts, version 2017, édition officielle
> mise à jour 2023/DGID*** — 442 pages, clean text — on
> [finances.gouv.cf](https://finances.gouv.cf/index.php/documentations).
>
> **The five bands are exactly right. Four other rows are not**, and the pattern is the
> one this corpus keeps finding: a number that exists in the Code, attached to the wrong
> rule.
>
> | Row as it stood | What the Code says |
> |---|---|
> | Five bands, 0/8/15/28/40% | **Confirmed** verbatim — article 86 |
> | "on annual income" | **Wrong base** — article 86 applies the scale to *revenu global net*, after a flat **30%** professional-expenses abatement and social contributions |
> | XAF 378,000 tax-free threshold | **Confirmed**, and it is also an express exemption in article 35 |
> | Deduction of ~XAF 50,000 per dependant, max 3 | **Not in the Code** |
> | Non-resident withholding "flat 15% on CAR-source income" | **Narrower** — article 166 bis 1 |
> | Capital gains 15% resident / 20% non-resident | **No such rates.** Individuals' gains are an ordinary IRPP income category |
> | Filing deadline 31 May | **30 April** — article 87 |

- **IRPP band 1 (tax-free threshold)** — 0% on *revenu net annuel* up to XAF 378,000  _([CGI art. 86](https://finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2023-05/CGI%20RCA%202017%20mise%20%C3%A0%20jour%202023.pdf))_
- **IRPP band 2** — 8% from XAF 378,001 to 1,680,000  _([CGI art. 86](https://finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2023-05/CGI%20RCA%202017%20mise%20%C3%A0%20jour%202023.pdf))_
- **IRPP band 3** — 15% from XAF 1,680,001 to 3,360,000  _([CGI art. 86](https://finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2023-05/CGI%20RCA%202017%20mise%20%C3%A0%20jour%202023.pdf))_
- **IRPP band 4** — 28% from XAF 3,360,001 to 5,040,000  _([CGI art. 86](https://finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2023-05/CGI%20RCA%202017%20mise%20%C3%A0%20jour%202023.pdf))_
- **IRPP band 5 (top rate)** — 40% above XAF 5,040,000  _([CGI art. 86](https://finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2023-05/CGI%20RCA%202017%20mise%20%C3%A0%20jour%202023.pdf))_
- **⚠ The bands apply to net global income, not to salary** — Article 86 is explicit: the tax is *"déterminé par application du barème annuel ci-dessous au **revenu global net**, c'est-à-dire déduction faite des cotisations sociales et des allocations familiales ainsi que de l'abattement pour frais professionnels **fixé forfaitairement à 30%**"*. So before the scale is applied, gross pay is reduced by a **flat 30% professional-expenses abatement** (art. 38) and by **family allowances and contributions to the State, the CNSS and supplementary employee retirement schemes** (art. 39). Running the bands against gross salary over-states the tax by a wide margin, and this guide gave no hint that a base adjustment existed  _([CGI arts. 38, 39 and 86](https://finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2023-05/CGI%20RCA%202017%20mise%20%C3%A0%20jour%202023.pdf))_
- **The bands sit under a heading — "D. Salariés"** — Articles 82 to 86 divide liquidation of the IRPP by class of taxpayer. Article 86's scale is the one for **employees**. Article 84 taxes individuals' *revenus de capitaux mobiliers* at **15% of gross distributed income**, and article 85 applies a **3%** précompte to sales of diamonds, gold and precious stones to buying offices. Picking the barème for a non-salary case is a category error the guide's flat presentation invited  _([CGI arts. 82–86](https://finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2023-05/CGI%20RCA%202017%20mise%20%C3%A0%20jour%202023.pdf))_
- **Tax-free threshold** — XAF 378,000. It appears twice: as the 0% band in article 86, and as an express exemption in **article 35, item 17** — *"le revenu net imposable inférieur ou égal à 378 000 F CFA"* is exempt income. Note the article 86 table prints the band's upper bound as "378.0000", with an extra zero; the following row begins at 378.001 and article 35 gives 378 000, so the boundary is XAF 378,000  _([CGI arts. 35 and 86](https://finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2023-05/CGI%20RCA%202017%20mise%20%C3%A0%20jour%202023.pdf))_
- **⚠ No dependant deduction is in the Code** — This guide claimed *"approximately XAF 50,000 per dependent, up to a maximum of 3 dependents"*, marked *(approx — confirm)*. The Code contains **no occurrence of *personne à charge*, *quotient familial* or a domestic *charges de famille* relief** — the phrase appears only inside two double-taxation treaties reproduced in Livre IV, as a non-discrimination clause about reliefs the *other* State grants. The deductions from employment income are the ones in articles 38–39 above. **A dependant allowance may exist in a loi de finances outside the Code; it has not been found, and it is no longer stated here as though it had been**  _([CGI arts. 38–39](https://finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2023-05/CGI%20RCA%202017%20mise%20%C3%A0%20jour%202023.pdf))_
- **Benefits in kind have a fixed valuation scale** — Article 37 values them as a percentage of **gross taxable salary**: water, electricity and gas **3%**; **rent at its real value**; housing **10%**; food **25% per person, capped at XAF 60,000 per month**, with children under 18 counting as half; **5% per domestic servant**; **10% per company vehicle**. The guide carried none of this  _([CGI art. 37](https://finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2023-05/CGI%20RCA%202017%20mise%20%C3%A0%20jour%202023.pdf))_
- **There is a floor under taxable pay** — Article 36: the amount of salaries, indemnities and emoluments taken into account **can in no case be lower than the guaranteed inter-professional minimum wage (SMIG)** set by the collective labour agreement  _([CGI art. 36](https://finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2023-05/CGI%20RCA%202017%20mise%20%C3%A0%20jour%202023.pdf))_
- **Non-resident withholding — 15%, but on a narrower head than this guide said, and it is final** — Article 166 bis 1: the single précompte rate is **3%** on local purchases and services, rents and imports. **Remuneration for services paid abroad** bears withholding at **15%**, *"sous réserve des conventions internationales"*, and that withholding is a ***prélèvement libératoire*** — a final levy. The same 15% final levy reaches lessors of vehicles who are registered for neither the patente nor the impôt global unique. Article 166 bis 1 also confirms the **15% on gross distributed investment income**. "Flat 15% withholding on CAR-source income" was too broad: the general précompte is 3%  _([CGI art. 166 bis 1](https://finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2023-05/CGI%20RCA%202017%20mise%20%C3%A0%20jour%202023.pdf))_
- **⚠ Capital gains are not taxed at a separate rate** — This guide gave 15% for residents and 20% for non-residents. Article 1 lists *"plus-values réalisées par les personnes physiques et assimilées"* as one of the categories **making up global net income**, and article 55 says individuals' gains — on sale, exchange, partition, expropriation, contribution to a company or liquidation, over movable or immovable property or rights of any kind — are *"soumis à l'IRPP"*. They are an ordinary IRPP category, not a schedular rate. **No 20% non-resident capital gains rate was found anywhere in the Code.** The 15% that does exist is article 84's rate on *revenus de capitaux mobiliers*, a different head that is easy to mistake for capital gains  _([CGI arts. 1, 55 and 84](https://finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2023-05/CGI%20RCA%202017%20mise%20%C3%A0%20jour%202023.pdf))_
- **Five capital gains exemptions the guide omitted entirely** — Article 56 exempts gains on: the **principal residence**; the **first** disposal of a dwelling used as a secondary residence or of a let property; **agricultural land**; property **belonging to retired persons**; and **expropriation for public utility, conditional on reinvesting the gain**. Article 57 sets the base as sale price or market value less acquisition price, the latter increased for immovables by construction, reconstruction, extension, renovation or improvement spending since acquisition, where not already deducted and not rental in character  _([CGI arts. 56–57](https://finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2023-05/CGI%20RCA%202017%20mise%20%C3%A0%20jour%202023.pdf))_
- **Securities in property-rich companies are treated as immovable gains** — Article 55: gains on disposal for consideration of securities or corporate rights in a company whose assets **consist principally of immovables** or of rights over them are assimilated to immovable gains  _([CGI art. 55](https://finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2023-05/CGI%20RCA%202017%20mise%20%C3%A0%20jour%202023.pdf))_
- **A minimum IRPP applies to the non-salaried, and the figure is striking** — Article 82: the minimum IRPP rates are **0.3% for agricultural activities** and **1.85% for other activities**, and *"dans tous les cas"* the minimum IRPP payable is **XAF 300,000 for agricultural activities** and **XAF 1,850,000 for others**. Article 83 exempts from the minimum non-salaried persons with the income described in articles 13, 31 and 53. **Recorded as the Code states it.** XAF 1,850,000 is a very large floor for an individual, and it mirrors the 1.85% rate digit for digit; article 133 imposes the identical pair of figures for companies, so the Code is at least internally consistent. Whether it is a drafting slip is **not resolved here**  _([CGI arts. 82–83 and 133](https://finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2023-05/CGI%20RCA%202017%20mise%20%C3%A0%20jour%202023.pdf))_
- **⚠ Annual return deadline — 30 April, not 31 May** — Article 87: every IRPP taxpayer must file a detailed annual return of income and profits, classified by category, with the tax office of their place of residence or the office designated for the purpose, ***"au plus tard le 30 Avril de l'année fiscale suivant celle de la réalisation des revenus déclarés"***. This guide said 31 May, marked *(approx — confirm)*. It is a month out  _([CGI art. 87](https://finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2023-05/CGI%20RCA%202017%20mise%20%C3%A0%20jour%202023.pdf))_
- **A separate foreign-asset declaration is due on the same date** — Article 88 (L.F. 2018): anyone domiciled or habitually resident in the Central African Republic who keeps movable property **outside** the country must file, within the article 87 deadline, a detailed return stating the nature and value of that property and the income from it  _([CGI art. 88](https://finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2023-05/CGI%20RCA%202017%20mise%20%C3%A0%20jour%202023.pdf))_
- **Individual residence test** — Generally tax-resident if domiciled or with principal place of abode/economic interests in CAR (French-code criteria) ((approx — confirm)). **Still unverified:** the Code's own liability article was not read, so this row remains as it stood  _(Code Général des Impôts (CAR))_

### Finance Act follow-up, 12 September 2026

Selected operative provisions of the Ministry's 2024, 2025 and 2026 Finance Acts,
and the 2024 and 2025 amending Acts, have now been checked. No amendment to the
employee scale, professional allowance or article 87 annual deadline was identified
in those provisions. The page-by-page coverage and remaining limits are recorded
in `docs/ACCURACY-METHODOLOGY.md`. This does not establish that every subsequent
instrument or every page of the Acts has been reviewed.

The 2024 Act, article 11, creates a *Livre des Procédures Fiscales*. Its article 53
requires declarations within the deadlines set by the tax laws. Article 54 permits
an exceptional filing extension on a written request made at least seven days
before the deadline: the administration has three days to respond, silence counts
as acceptance, and only one extension of up to 30 days may be granted. An extension
does not stop late-payment interest. These provisions do not replace the ordinary
30 April date above. [Finance Act 2024, PDF pages 35 and 46–47](https://www.finances.gouv.cf/sites/default/files/2024-03/Loi%20de%20Finances%202024.pdf).

The 2024 Act's article 43, headed *Des abattements sur salaires*, concerns a
reduction of remuneration charged to the State budget. It does not establish a
general IRPP dependant allowance or replace the professional-expenses deduction.
The later Acts retain earlier Finance Act provisions unless expressly repealed.
[Ministry Finance Act register](https://finances.gouv.cf/finances/les-lois-de-finances-ldf).

> **Still unverified:** article 3 and the *personnes imposables* sub-section; the
> *impôt global unique* regime; the simplified and normal real-profit regimes
> in articles 60–61; and a complete review of the new procedures code, implementing
> instruments and amendments outside the provisions recorded in the methodology.

Individuals are subject to the Impôt sur le Revenu des Personnes Physiques (IRPP) on a progressive scale applied to net global income.
