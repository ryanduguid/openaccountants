# Qatar Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax rates and base

- **General overview** — Qatar applies a flat 10% corporate income tax to the share of profits attributable to non-Qatari/non-GCC ownership, on a territorial (Qatar-source) basis. Petroleum operations and certain pre-2010 agreements carry higher rates.
- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 10% of taxable income  _(Income Tax Law No. 24 of 2018 and its Executive Regulations, as amended by Law No. 11 of 2022, General Tax Authority 2024 English edition, art 9 — https://www.gta.gov.qa/assets/pdf/Income%20Tax%20Law%20EN%202024.pdf)_
- **Oil and gas operations rate** — The rate and conditions in the petroleum and petrochemical agreements apply, provided the rate is in all cases not less than 35%  _(Income Tax Law No. 24 of 2018 and its Executive Regulations, as amended by Law No. 11 of 2022, General Tax Authority 2024 English edition, art 9(1) — https://www.gta.gov.qa/assets/pdf/Income%20Tax%20Law%20EN%202024.pdf)_
- **Pre-2010 special agreements** — Rates fixed in agreements with the government or its agencies concluded before the Law came into force continue to apply; 35% applies where the agreement sets no rate  _(Income Tax Law No. 24 of 2018 and its Executive Regulations, as amended by Law No. 11 of 2022, General Tax Authority 2024 English edition, art 9(3) — https://www.gta.gov.qa/assets/pdf/Income%20Tax%20Law%20EN%202024.pdf)_
- **Qatari / GCC ownership exemption** — No tax on the gross income of Qatari natural persons or of legal persons wholly owned by Qataris, nor on the share of a resident legal person's profits attributable to Qatari owners; nationals of other GCC states resident in Qatar receive the same treatment, so only the foreign-owned share is taxed (state-owned petroleum and petrochemical interests excepted)  _(Income Tax Law No. 24 of 2018 and its Executive Regulations, as amended by Law No. 11 of 2022, General Tax Authority 2024 English edition, art 4(13) to (15) and the Executive Regulations' GCC nationals clause — https://www.gta.gov.qa/assets/pdf/Income%20Tax%20Law%20EN%202024.pdf)_
- **Tax base** — Territorial — taxable income arising from sources in the State during the tax year  _(Income Tax Law No. 24 of 2018 and its Executive Regulations, as amended by Law No. 11 of 2022, General Tax Authority 2024 English edition, art 2 — https://www.gta.gov.qa/assets/pdf/Income%20Tax%20Law%20EN%202024.pdf)_
- **Domestic Minimum Top-Up Tax (Pillar Two)** — 15% minimum effective tax for multinational groups with consolidated revenue of EUR 750 million or more in two of the four preceding fiscal years, for fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025  _(General Tax Authority, Global Minimum Tax page — https://www.gta.gov.qa/en/global-minimum ; Council of Ministers Resolution No. 2 of 2026 issuing the Pillar Two rules under Law No. 22 of 2024, art 2 and art 1.1 — https://www.gta.gov.qa/assets/pdf/EN%20Qatar%20Pillar%20Two%20Regulations%20Decision%20No%202%20of%202026.pdf ; Income Tax Law No. 24 of 2018 and its Executive Regulations, as amended by Law No. 11 of 2022, General Tax Authority 2024 English edition, art 34, as amended, and Chapter VII bis added by Law No. 22 of 2024 — https://www.gta.gov.qa/assets/pdf/Income%20Tax%20Law%20EN%202024.pdf)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends** — 0%: dividends are not among the payments listed for withholding at source  _(Income Tax Law No. 24 of 2018 and its Executive Regulations, as amended by Law No. 11 of 2022, General Tax Authority 2024 English edition, art 9(2) — https://www.gta.gov.qa/assets/pdf/Income%20Tax%20Law%20EN%202024.pdf)_
- **Withholding tax on interest (to non-residents)** — 5% final withholding on the gross amount, subject to tax agreements  _(Income Tax Law No. 24 of 2018 and its Executive Regulations, as amended by Law No. 11 of 2022, General Tax Authority 2024 English edition, art 9(2) — https://www.gta.gov.qa/assets/pdf/Income%20Tax%20Law%20EN%202024.pdf)_
- **Withholding tax on royalties (to non-residents)** — 5% final withholding on the gross amount, subject to tax agreements  _(Income Tax Law No. 24 of 2018 and its Executive Regulations, as amended by Law No. 11 of 2022, General Tax Authority 2024 English edition, art 9(2) — https://www.gta.gov.qa/assets/pdf/Income%20Tax%20Law%20EN%202024.pdf)_
- **Withholding tax on technical/service fees & other payments to non-residents** — **5%** final withholding on the gross amount of commissions and fees for services performed wholly or partly in the State, paid to non-residents for activities unrelated to a permanent establishment in Qatar; the Authority treats a service as performed in Qatar if any work needed to complete it, such as data collection or site inspection, is done on Qatari soil. Amounts owed to non-government payees are deemed paid 12 months after falling due; no withholding applies to holders of a tax card or Qatar Financial Centre registrants. Withheld tax is remitted before the 16th day of the following month  _(Income Tax Law No. 24 of 2018 and its Executive Regulations, as amended by Law No. 11 of 2022, General Tax Authority 2024 English edition, art 9(2) and the Executive Regulations' remittance provision — https://www.gta.gov.qa/assets/pdf/Income%20Tax%20Law%20EN%202024.pdf ; General Tax Authority, Taxes information page, withholding tax — https://www.gta.gov.qa/en/taxes-info)_
- **CIT return filing & final payment deadline** — Within four months from the end of the accounting period, with payment of the tax due; the Authority may extend the deadline by up to four months on a request made at least 60 days before it  _(Income Tax Law No. 24 of 2018 and its Executive Regulations, as amended by Law No. 11 of 2022, General Tax Authority 2024 English edition, art 11 and the Executive Regulations' return provision (within four months from the end of the tax year or accounting period) — https://www.gta.gov.qa/assets/pdf/Income%20Tax%20Law%20EN%202024.pdf)_
