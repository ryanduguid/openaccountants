# Luxembourg tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the individual's home and habitual stay, the entity's statutory seat and central administration, the legal form, each income source and the books' closing date.

## Territorial reach

Resident individuals generally account for domestic and foreign income. Article 156 limits the domestic income within the charge for nonresidents. It includes specified amounts from Luxembourg real estate, work and businesses with a local permanent establishment or permanent representative. Resident corporate taxpayers generally account for worldwide income. A nonresident entity within art 160(1) is subject to corporate income tax on the domestic income defined in art 156. Apply exemptions and applicable treaty limits after establishing the domestic charge. [Income Tax Act, arts 2 and 156–161](https://impotsdirects.public.lu/dam-assets/fr/legislation/LIR/texte-coordonn-en-vigueur-au-1er-janvier-2026-ver-08052026.pdf)

## Residence

An individual is ordinarily resident if their fiscal domicile or habitual abode is in Luxembourg. Apply those domestic tests to the facts and establish the dates if the person moved. A covered collective body such as a limited liability company is resident if either its statutory seat or its central administration is in Luxembourg. If 2 states claim residence, check the treaty before determining how to report the income. [Income Tax Act, arts 2 and 159](https://impotsdirects.public.lu/dam-assets/fr/legislation/LIR/texte-coordonn-en-vigueur-au-1er-janvier-2026-ver-08052026.pdf)

## Taxpayer types

Sole traders generally account for profit under the individual rules. A company such as an SA or SARL generally pays corporate income tax. Ordinary general and limited partnerships, including an SCS or SCSp, are generally transparent for income tax. Account for each partner's allocated share and check the municipal business tax separately. The reverse-hybrid rule can charge qualifying arrangements on otherwise untaxed income under art 168quater.

For a trust or fiduciary contract, obtain the deed and trace who is entitled to the income and which assets, parties and payments are within Luxembourg's tax reach. Check whether income is assessable directly to another taxpayer or whether the arrangement falls in an entity or dedicated-assets category under arts 159–160. Check the separate AED beneficial ownership registration requirements for express trusts and fiduciary contracts. [Income Tax Act, arts 159–160, 168quater and 175](https://impotsdirects.public.lu/dam-assets/fr/legislation/LIR/texte-coordonn-en-vigueur-au-1er-janvier-2026-ver-08052026.pdf) · [Trust and Fiduciary Contracts Act of 27 July 2003](https://legilux.public.lu/eli/etat/leg/loi/2003/07/27/n4/jo) · [Guichet.lu, registration of trusts and fiduciary contracts](https://guichet.public.lu/fr/entreprises/gestion-juridique-comptabilite/gestion/fiducie/declaration-fiducie-trust.html)

## Currency

Luxembourg uses the euro (EUR). Check the Direct Tax Administration's foreign-currency balance sheet circular if an eligible entity keeps its books in another currency. It sets conditions for calculating taxable income in that currency and prescribes conversion into EUR for tax purposes. Keep the supporting rates and conversion workings. [Direct Tax Administration, foreign-currency balance sheet circular](https://impotsdirects.public.lu/dam-assets/fr/legislation/legi18/LGA60-LIR-23-3-ICC39-EVAL59-IFORT-49-06072018.pdf)

## Assessment periods

The individual and corporate assessment year is the calendar year. An individual commercial operator generally closes their accounts at year end, with other annual closing dates available under specified conditions. When a corporation required to keep regular accounts validly closes them on another date, use the accounting year ending within the relevant calendar year. Check the rules for a change of accounting date or a cessation separately. [Income Tax Act, arts 1, 17, 158 and 163](https://impotsdirects.public.lu/dam-assets/fr/legislation/LIR/texte-coordonn-en-vigueur-au-1er-janvier-2026-ver-08052026.pdf)

## Responsible authorities

The Direct Tax Administration (ACD) administers individual and corporate income tax and collects municipal business tax on behalf of municipalities. The Registration Duties, Estates and VAT Authority (AED) handles VAT and registration duties and keeps the register of trusts and fiduciary contracts. [ACD responsibilities](https://impotsdirects.public.lu/fr/profil/attributions.html) · [Guichet.lu, municipal business tax](https://guichet.public.lu/fr/entreprises/fiscalite/impots-benefices/benefices-patrimoine/impots-divers/impot-commercial-communal.html) · [AED register](https://pfi.public.lu/fr/blanchiment/registre-fiducies-trusts.html)

## Fictional intake

A resident sole trader becomes a partner in a Luxembourg SCSp and receives a payment under a foreign trust. Record the period of residence, obtain the partnership allocation and read the trust deed and payment records before assigning each amount to a taxpayer and period.
