# Ecuador Tax Overview

Ecuador uses the US dollar (USD). The Servicio de Rentas Internas (SRI) administers national taxes. The ordinary income tax year runs from 1 January to 31 December. [Ley de Régimen Tributario Interno (LRTI), art. 7](https://www.sri.gob.ec/o/sri-portlet-biblioteca-alfresco-internet/descargar/12c884d5-c8d9-4171-becc-1513b990b87a/LEY_DE_REGIMEN_TRIBUTARIO_INTERNO_LRTI.pdf) · [Central Bank currency notice](https://www.bce.fin.ec/los-criptoactivos-no-son-una-moneda-de-curso-legal-ni-un-medio-de-pago-autorizado-en-ecuador/) · [SRI income tax guide](https://www.sri.gob.ec/impuesto-renta)

## Income tax

- **Residence and income scope.** An individual is generally resident after at least 183 calendar days in Ecuador in one fiscal year, including sporadic absences. Article 4.1 also covers periods spanning 2 fiscal years and tests based on economic or family ties. A company formed in Ecuador under Ecuadorian law is generally resident. Residents generally include taxable Ecuadorian and foreign income. Non-residents generally pay on Ecuadorian-source income. Confirm the source rules, qualifying temporary residence regime, treaty relief and foreign tax credits before applying this distinction. [LRTI, arts. 2, 4.1 to 4.3, 8 and 49](https://www.sri.gob.ec/o/sri-portlet-biblioteca-alfresco-internet/descargar/12c884d5-c8d9-4171-becc-1513b990b87a/LEY_DE_REGIMEN_TRIBUTARIO_INTERNO_LRTI.pdf)
- **Individuals.** In 2026, the ordinary annual scale starts at 0% on taxable income through USD 12,208. The top marginal rate is 37% on the portion above USD 109,956. Apply the other bands and check eligible reductions using the SRI table for the year. [SRI 2026 table, Resolution NAC-DGERCGC25-00000043](https://www.sri.gob.ec/o/sri-portlet-biblioteca-alfresco-internet/descargar/58a7f4f6-ab51-48b6-b9ff-a8e97e1a28ef/Tablas%20de%20c%C3%A1lculo%20de%20Impuesto%20a%20la%20Renta.pdf)
- **Companies.** The general corporate income tax rate is 25% on the taxable base. Article 37 increases it by 3 percentage points in specified ownership or disclosure cases. Check the affected share of the base and any special regime before applying a rate. [LRTI, art. 37](https://www.sri.gob.ec/o/sri-portlet-biblioteca-alfresco-internet/descargar/12c884d5-c8d9-4171-becc-1513b990b87a/LEY_DE_REGIMEN_TRIBUTARIO_INTERNO_LRTI.pdf) · [SRI guidance for the 2025 return](https://www.sri.gob.ec/detalle-noticias?idnoticia=1284&marquesina=1)

## VAT, filing and payroll

- **VAT (IVA).** The general rate applied in 2026 is 15% under the continuation rule in the regulation. Article 65 sets the statutory base rate at 13% and allows the executive to adjust it within the statutory range. Check the rate in force at the supply date, including zero-rated and other specially rated supplies. [LRTI, art. 65](https://www.sri.gob.ec/o/sri-portlet-biblioteca-alfresco-internet/descargar/12c884d5-c8d9-4171-becc-1513b990b87a/LEY_DE_REGIMEN_TRIBUTARIO_INTERNO_LRTI.pdf) · [SRI Circular NAC-DGECCGC25-00000006](https://www.sri.gob.ec/o/sri-portlet-biblioteca-alfresco-internet/descargar?id=236482f4-6125-42fd-b073-62c99d08233d&nombre=NAC-DGECCGC25-00000006.pdf) · [SRI Circular NAC-DGECCGC26-00000002](https://www.sri.gob.ec/o/sri-portlet-biblioteca-alfresco-internet/descargar?id=ee088145-61f4-4926-8f32-646ec8369f79&nombre=NAC-DGECCGC26-00000002.pdf)
- **Filing.** Where a return is required, ordinary annual income tax deadlines fall in March for individuals and April for companies, generally by the ninth RUC or cédula digit, as applicable. Special taxpayers and other categories can have different dates. For VAT returns presented from June 2026, the SRI generally requires filing and full payment in the same step. Check the resolution’s exception for active registered goods exporters and their direct suppliers and any later system changes. [SRI income tax deadlines](https://www.sri.gob.ec/impuesto-renta) · [SRI Resolution NAC-DGERCGC26-00000016](https://www.sri.gob.ec/o/sri-portlet-biblioteca-alfresco-internet/descargar?id=2c63daa5-084c-48cb-b70f-961dd23dc248&nombre=NAC-DGERCGC26-00000016.pdf)
- **Social insurance.** IESS lists 9.45% employee and 11.15% employer contributions for ordinary private-sector employees, or 20.60% combined. Confirm the pay base, worker category and any other employer charges separately. [IESS contribution guidance](https://www.iess.gob.ec/afiliacion-cobertura-pf/)
