# Ecuador Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax rates and base

- **General corporate income tax rate** — 25% on the taxable base of companies formed in Ecuador, branches and permanent establishments of foreign companies  _(LRTI art. 37, Codificación 2004-026 consolidated to 2 July 2021 — https://www.bolsadequito.com/uploads/normativa/normativa-relacionada/ley-organica-regimen-tributario-interno.pdf; SRI, Impuesto a la Renta — https://www.sri.gob.ec/impuesto-renta)_
- **Rate with tax-haven shareholders** — 28% (the company rate plus three points where the ownership chain holds a resident of a tax haven or low-tax jurisdiction and the beneficial owner is an Ecuadorian tax resident, or where the company has not reported its ownership)  _(LRTI art. 37 — https://www.bolsadequito.com/uploads/normativa/normativa-relacionada/ley-organica-regimen-tributario-interno.pdf; SRI, Impuesto a la Renta — https://www.sri.gob.ec/impuesto-renta)_
- **Reinvestment-of-profits reduced rate** — Reduced rate (general rate minus 10 percentage points) on profits reinvested in qualifying productive assets, subject to conditions (approx — confirm conditions)  _(Ley de Régimen Tributario Interno (LRTI))_
- **Tax base** — Net taxable income: gross income less exempt income and deductible costs/expenses (gastos deducibles)  _(Ley de Régimen Tributario Interno (LRTI))_
- **Income tax advance / minimum** — Companies may be subject to an income-tax advance payment regime (anticipo del impuesto a la renta) (approx — confirm computation)  _(Ley de Régimen Tributario Interno (LRTI))_

The general corporate income tax (Impuesto a la Renta de sociedades) rate is 25%, with higher rates where tax-haven shareholders are involved. Companies are taxed on net taxable profits after deductible expenses.

## Withholding taxes and filing deadlines

- **Withholding on dividends to non-residents** — 25% applied to 40% of the dividend (effective ~10%): the taxable income is 40% of the dividend distributed and the distributing company withholds at the general non-resident rate; distributions to another Ecuadorian company or permanent establishment are not taxed, and the top individual rate applies where ownership is unreported  _(LRTI art. 39.2 numerals 1, 2, 4 and 6 — https://www.bolsadequito.com/uploads/normativa/normativa-relacionada/ley-organica-regimen-tributario-interno.pdf)_
- **Withholding on interest to non-residents** — 25% (the company rate under art. 39); no withholding and no Ecuadorian tax on interest paid on external financing from foreign financial institutions or qualified specialised lenders, and on government and multilateral loans, within the maximum reference rates set by the Junta de Política y Regulación Monetaria y Financiera  _(LRTI arts. 39 and 13 numeral 3 — https://www.bolsadequito.com/uploads/normativa/normativa-relacionada/ley-organica-regimen-tributario-interno.pdf)_
- **Withholding on royalties / technical services to non-residents** — 25% (the company rate on non-resident income without a specific withholding percentage, withheld at source; 37% where the recipient is in a tax haven, low-tax or preferential regime)  _(LRTI art. 39 — https://www.bolsadequito.com/uploads/normativa/normativa-relacionada/ley-organica-regimen-tributario-interno.pdf)_
- **Domestic income tax withholdings** — Various rates apply to local payments (commonly 1%–10% depending on the payment type) (approx — confirm by payment type)  _(Reglamento para la Aplicación de la LRTI; SRI withholding resolutions)_
- **Corporate return filing deadline** — April of the year following the tax year; specific day determined by the 9th digit of the RUC  _(Reglamento para la Aplicación de la LRTI)_
- **Tax treaty relief** — Reduced withholding may apply under Ecuador's double tax treaties and the Andean Community (CAN Decision 578)  _(CAN Decision 578; applicable double tax treaties)_

Cross-border payments to non-residents are generally subject to a 25% withholding, subject to treaty relief. Dividends to non-residents are taxed on a reduced base.
