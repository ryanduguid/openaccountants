# Djibouti Company Formation & Entity Choice

## Entity types and incorporation in Djibouti

Djibouti is absent from the [OHADA member-state register](https://www.ohada.org/les-etats-membres-de-lohada/).
The AUSCGIE and SYSCOHADA therefore cannot be treated as applicable solely by
OHADA membership. The former capital, shareholder and accounting instructions
cited those instruments and have been withdrawn pending a check of national law.

Before advising on an incorporation, obtain Djibouti's applicable Code de commerce
and registration requirements and confirm:

- Permitted entity forms, shareholder limits and minimum capital.
- The registry, required documents, capital deposit and incorporation steps.
- The accounting framework, annual filing duties and any free-zone rules.
- Current official fees and processing arrangements.

The commercial sources previously cited are research leads only:
[entity forms](https://www.expanship.com/dj/blog/types-of-companies-in-djibouti.html)
and [incorporation requirements](https://www.expanship.com/dj/blog/incorporation-requirements-in-djibouti.html).
Do not substitute an OHADA default for an unverified Djibouti rule.
