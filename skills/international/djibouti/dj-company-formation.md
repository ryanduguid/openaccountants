# Djibouti Company Formation & Entity Choice

## Entity types and incorporation in Djibouti

> **Djibouti is not an OHADA member state, and this guide was built as though it
> were.** OHADA's own register of *Les Etats membres de l'OHADA* lists seventeen:
> Bénin, Burkina Faso, Cameroun, Centrafrique, Comores, Congo, Côte d'Ivoire,
> Gabon, Guinée, Guinée Bissau, Guinée Équatoriale, Mali, Niger, RDC, Sénégal,
> Tchad and Togo. **Djibouti is not on it.**
>
> Every row below that cites the *Acte uniforme relatif au droit des sociétés
> commerciales et du GIE* is therefore citing an instrument that **does not apply
> in Djibouti**. The entity names (SARL, SA, RCCM) are shared across francophone
> civil-law systems and may well be right; what is wrong is the **authority given
> for them**. A figure can survive its citation being wrong, and none of these has
> been checked against a source that governs Djibouti.
>
> [RESEARCH GAP — Djibouti's own **Code de commerce** is the missing document.
> Until it is read, treat every capital amount, shareholder limit and registration
> rule below as unsourced rather than as OHADA law.]
>
> _(OHADA, Les Etats membres de l'OHADA — https://www.ohada.org/les-etats-membres-de-lohada/)_


Company law in Djibouti is **national law, not OHADA law** — see the note below. The SARL (limited liability company) is the most common form; the SA (public limited company) suits larger ventures. Formation runs through the Centre de Formalites des Entreprises (CFE) and the commercial register (RCCM).

- **Limited liability company** — Societe a Responsabilite Limitee (SARL) — most common; limited liability. The "1 to 50 partners" ceiling is **not an OHADA rule** (the AUSCGIE sets no maximum for a SARL) and, since Djibouti is not an OHADA state, it must come from Djiboutian law or from nowhere ((approx — confirm))  _(as described at [expanship.com](https://www.expanship.com/dj/blog/types-of-companies-in-djibouti.html))_
- **Public limited company** — Societe Anonyme (SA) — for larger businesses, higher capital and governance requirements  _(as described at [expanship.com](https://www.expanship.com/dj/blog/types-of-companies-in-djibouti.html))_
- **Other forms** — Sole proprietorship (entreprise individuelle), branch of a foreign company, and free-zone entities are also available ((approx — confirm))  _(unsourced — the OHADA attribution this row carried does not apply to Djibouti)_
- **Minimum share capital — SARL** — "Approximately DJF 1,000,000" is **the OHADA article 311 figure with the currency relabelled**: 1,000,000 *francs CFA* is the uniform-act default for member states, and Djibouti is not one. The Djibouti franc amount is unknown DJF ((approx — confirm against Djiboutian law))  _(as described at [expanship.com](https://www.expanship.com/dj/blog/incorporation-requirements-in-djibouti.html))_
- **Free-zone company capital** — Higher capital requirement applies for free-zone companies (reported around USD 70,000) USD ((approx — confirm)) USD (approx — confirm)  _(Djibouti free-zone regulations (https://www.womenconnect.org/web/djibouti/business-registration/-/asset_publisher/ObbONgnQXwfM/content/le-processus-de-creation-d-entreprise))_
- **Registration authority** — Centre de Formalites des Entreprises (CFE) — single window; entry in the Registre du Commerce et du Credit Mobilier (RCCM)  _(Djibouti commercial registry rules, as described at [expanship.com](https://www.expanship.com/dj/blog/incorporation-requirements-in-djibouti.html))_
- **Incorporation steps** — Reserve/verify company name; draft statutes (notarised or private seal); deposit capital in a Djiboutian bank; register with RCCM/CFE; obtain business licence; register for tax with the DGI  _(Djibouti commercial registry rules, as described at [expanship.com](https://www.expanship.com/dj/blog/incorporation-requirements-in-djibouti.html))_
- **Incorporation timeline** — Around 3 days once all documents are complete ((approx — confirm)) days (approx — confirm)  _(Djibouti commercial registry rules (CFE single window) (https://www.womenconnect.org/web/djibouti/business-registration/-/asset_publisher/ObbONgnQXwfM/content/le-processus-de-creation-d-entreprise))_
- **Minimum shareholders — SARL** — 1 (single-member SARL permitted); the "maximum 50" needs a Djiboutian source, not an OHADA one ((approx — confirm))  _(as described at [expanship.com](https://www.expanship.com/dj/blog/types-of-companies-in-djibouti.html))_
- **Core annual compliance — accounts** — The claim that accounts follow the **OHADA accounting system (SYSCOHADA)** has the same defect as the rest: SYSCOHADA is an OHADA uniform act and Djibouti is not a member. Confirm which framework Djiboutian law actually prescribes ((approx — confirm))  _(unsourced for Djibouti)_
- **Core annual compliance — tax filings** — Annual corporate tax return by 31 March; monthly VAT, ITS and CNSS declarations; business licence (patente) renewal ((approx — confirm))  _(Code General des Impots (Djibouti))_
