# Angola Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax (Imposto Industrial)

- **Overview of Imposto Industrial** — Corporate profits are taxed under the Imposto Industrial (Corporate Income Tax) Code at a standard 25%. Resident companies are taxed on worldwide profits and non-residents on profits attributable to an Angolan permanent establishment. Investment income (dividends, interest, royalties) is taxed separately under the Investment Income Tax (IAC).
- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 25%  _(Código do Imposto Industrial (Lei n.º 19/14 as amended by Lei n.º 26/20), art. 64 n.º 1 — https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2020/lei-n-o-26-20-de-20-de-julho/; AGT, Impostos — https://agt.minfin.gov.ao/PortalAGT/#!/servicos-fiscais//impostos)_
- **Reduced rate for agriculture, aquaculture, forestry and similar** — 10% on income exclusively from agricultural, aquaculture, beekeeping, poultry, livestock, fishing and forestry activities, timber excluded  _(Código do Imposto Industrial (Lei n.º 19/14 as amended by Lei n.º 26/20), art. 64 n.º 2 — https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2020/lei-n-o-26-20-de-20-de-julho/)_
- **Higher rate for banking, insurance, telecommunications and oil** — 35% on income from the banking and insurance sector, telecommunications operators and Angolan oil companies as defined in Decreto Legislativo Presidencial n.º 3/12  _(Código do Imposto Industrial (Lei n.º 19/14 as amended by Lei n.º 26/20), art. 64 n.º 3 — https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2020/lei-n-o-26-20-de-20-de-julho/)_
- **Tax base** — Taxable profit is the result shown by the accounts prepared under the Plano Geral de Contabilidade, the difference between all income and gains and all costs and losses of the year, adjusted under the code  _(Código do Imposto Industrial (Lei n.º 19/14 as amended by Lei n.º 26/20), arts. 12 to 14 — https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2014/lei-n-o-19-14-de-22-de-outubro/)_
- **General regime threshold** — The simplified regime covers Imposto Industrial taxpayers inside the VAT exclusion regime (turnover or imports below AOA 25,000,000) other than public companies, financial institutions, special-regime companies, telecommunications operators and branches or subsidiaries of foreign companies; everyone else is in the general regime, which also starts once the limit is exceeded in two years  _(Código do Imposto Industrial (Lei n.º 19/14 as amended by Lei n.º 26/20), art. 58 — https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2020/lei-n-o-26-20-de-20-de-julho/; Código do IVA (Lei n.º 7/19 as amended and republished by Lei n.º 14/23), art. 60 — https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2023/lei-n-o-14-23-de-28-de-dezembro/)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends (Investment Income Tax, IAC)** — 10% on profits distributed to partners or shareholders and on profits repatriated by permanent establishments (Section B, art. 9 n.º 1 a)), withheld by the paying entity; distributions to an Angolan-resident corporate shareholder are exempt (art. 13); reductions under the Private Investment Law or the Código dos Benefícios Fiscais are not read here `[RESEARCH GAP — reviewer to confirm]`  _(Código do Imposto sobre a Aplicação de Capitais (Decreto Legislativo Presidencial n.º 2/14), arts. 9, 13, 27 and 29 — https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2014/decreto-legislativo-presidencial-n-o-2-14-de-20-de-outubro/)_
- **Withholding tax on interest (Investment Income Tax, IAC)** — 15% on interest on loans and credit facilities (Section A, art. 3); 10% on interest from bonds, bank deposits, treasury bills and bonds and central bank securities (Section B, art. 9 n.º 1 c), j), k), l)); 5% for listed securities with a maturity of three years or more  _(Código do Imposto sobre a Aplicação de Capitais (Decreto Legislativo Presidencial n.º 2/14), art. 27 — https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2014/decreto-legislativo-presidencial-n-o-2-14-de-20-de-outubro/)_
- **Withholding tax on royalties (Investment Income Tax, IAC)** — 10% on royalties (Section B, art. 9 n.º 1 i)), withheld by the payer; incentive reductions not read here `[RESEARCH GAP — reviewer to confirm]`  _(Código do Imposto sobre a Aplicação de Capitais (Decreto Legislativo Presidencial n.º 2/14), arts. 9, 27 and 29 — https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2014/decreto-legislativo-presidencial-n-o-2-14-de-20-de-outubro/)_
- **Withholding tax on services (general)** — **6.5%** withheld by the paying entity on services rendered by Imposto Industrial taxpayers with their seat, effective management or permanent establishment in Angola, remitted by the last business day of the following month; it is a **provisional** payment credited at the final assessment, and any excess is carried to later years. Services rendered **accidentally by entities without seat, domicile, permanent establishment or effective management in Angola** are taxed under the special regime of arts. 71 to 73 at a **final 15%** since Lei n.º 26/20 (the 6.5% final rate quoted by older summaries is superseded)  _(Código do Imposto Industrial (Lei n.º 19/14 as amended by Lei n.º 26/20), arts. 64 n.º 6, 67, 71 and 73 — https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2020/lei-n-o-26-20-de-20-de-julho/)_
- **Annual return / final payment deadline (general regime)** — Last business day of May of the following year (Declaração Modelo 1 and payment)  _(Código do Imposto Industrial (Lei n.º 19/14 as amended by Lei n.º 26/20), arts. 51, 69 n.º 1 b) and 70 — https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2020/lei-n-o-26-20-de-20-de-julho/)_
- **Annual return / final payment deadline (simplified regime)** — Last business day of April of the following year (Modelo de Declaração Simplificada and payment)  _(Código do Imposto Industrial (Lei n.º 19/14 as amended by Lei n.º 26/20), arts. 61, 69 n.º 1 a) and 70 — https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2020/lei-n-o-26-20-de-20-de-julho/)_
- **Provisional / advance payment** — Self-assessed provisional payment by the last business day of August at 2% of the sales of the first six months of the year (sales not subject to withholding at source), credited against the final assessment  _(Código do Imposto Industrial (Lei n.º 19/14 as amended by Lei n.º 26/20), art. 66 — https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2020/lei-n-o-26-20-de-20-de-julho/)_
