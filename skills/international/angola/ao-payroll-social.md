# Angola Payroll & Social Contributions

## Social security and payroll withholding

- **Employer social security contribution** — 8% of the worker's contributory remuneration  _(Decreto Presidencial n.º 227/18 (contribution regime of the Protecção Social Obrigatória), art. 12 n.º 1 — https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2018/decreto-presidencial-n-o-227-18-de-27-de-setembro/)_
- **Employee social security contribution** — 3% of contributory remuneration  _(Decreto Presidencial n.º 227/18 (contribution regime of the Protecção Social Obrigatória), art. 12 n.º 1 — https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2018/decreto-presidencial-n-o-227-18-de-27-de-setembro/)_
- **Employee social security contribution (retired employees)** — 8% for a worker already retired who returns to work, with the employer's 8% unchanged  _(Decreto Presidencial n.º 227/18 (contribution regime of the Protecção Social Obrigatória), art. 12 n.º 2 — https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2018/decreto-presidencial-n-o-227-18-de-27-de-setembro/)_
- **Contribution base** — The worker's gross remuneration: every cash payment due under the employment relationship, with pay in kind converted to its cash value; the decree lists the excluded payments and sets no ceiling  _(Decreto Presidencial n.º 227/18 (contribution regime of the Protecção Social Obrigatória), art. 13 — https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2018/decreto-presidencial-n-o-227-18-de-27-de-setembro/)_
- **Payroll income tax withholding (PAYE)** — The paying entity assesses Group A tax monthly, after the art. 7 deductions, by applying the progressive table annexed to the code (0% to 25% in the table replaced by Lei n.º 18/24 for 2025)  _(Código do Imposto sobre os Rendimentos do Trabalho (Lei n.º 18/14 as amended by Lei n.º 28/20), arts. 10 and 16 — https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2014/lei-n-o-18-14-de-22-de-outubro/; Lei n.º 18/24, Anexo I — https://www.ucm.minfin.gov.ao/cs/groups/public/documents/document/aw41/mdm1/~edisp/minfin5035043.pdf)_
- **IRT remittance deadline** — By the end of the month following the month of payment, to the competent tax office  _(Código do Imposto sobre os Rendimentos do Trabalho (Lei n.º 18/14 as amended by Lei n.º 28/20), art. 11 n.º 1 — https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2014/lei-n-o-18-14-de-22-de-outubro/)_
- **Social security remittance deadline** — Monthly, by the 10th day of the month following the month the contributions relate to; late payment bears interest of 1% a month on the principal  _(Decreto Presidencial n.º 227/18 (contribution regime of the Protecção Social Obrigatória), arts. 15 and 16 — https://lex.ao/docs/presidente-da-republica/2018/decreto-presidencial-n-o-227-18-de-27-de-setembro/)_
- **Social security coverage** — The compulsory regime covers sickness, maternity, occupational risks (accidents and occupational disease), invalidity and old age, death, unemployment and family charges; unemployment protection is delivered mainly through active employment measures  _(Lei n.º 7/04 (Lei de Bases da Protecção Social), art. 18 — https://angolex.com/paginas/leis/lei-de-bases-da-proteccao-social.html; AGT, Impostos — https://agt.minfin.gov.ao/PortalAGT/#!/servicos-fiscais//impostos)_

Angola's social security (INSS) is funded by combined employer and employee contributions on gross remuneration, covering family, pension, and unemployment protection. Employers also operate IRT withholding (PAYE) on employees' salaries.
