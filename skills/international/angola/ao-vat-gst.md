# Angola VAT / GST

## Value-Added Tax (IVA)

Angola operates a VAT (Imposto sobre o Valor Acrescentado, IVA) with a standard rate of 14%, introduced in 2019 and subsequently amended. Reduced rates apply to specific goods, services, and the Cabinda province, and a captivation (VAT withholding) regime substitutes for a traditional reverse charge in many cases.

- **Standard VAT rate** — 14 percent on imports, supplies of goods and services  _(Código do IVA (Lei n.º 7/19 as amended and republished by Lei n.º 14/23), art. 19 n.º 1 a) — https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2023/lei-n-o-14-23-de-28-de-dezembro/; AGT, Impostos — https://agt.minfin.gov.ao/PortalAGT/#!/servicos-fiscais//impostos)_
- **Reduced rate on certain food goods and agricultural inputs** — 5 percent on imports and supplies of the widely consumed food goods and agricultural inputs listed in Annexes I and II of the code  _(Código do IVA (Lei n.º 7/19 as amended and republished by Lei n.º 14/23), art. 19 n.º 1 d) — https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2023/lei-n-o-14-23-de-28-de-dezembro/)_
- **Reduced rate on hotel and restaurant services** — 7 percent, available only to providers that register their properties and vehicles, issue invoices through electronic invoicing systems and have filed their prior-year returns  _(Código do IVA (Lei n.º 7/19 as amended and republished by Lei n.º 14/23), art. 19 n.º 1 c) and n.º 2 — https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2023/lei-n-o-14-23-de-28-de-dezembro/)_
- **Simplified VAT regime rate** — 7 percent (on turnover)  _(Código do IVA (Lei n.º 7/19 as amended and republished by Lei n.º 14/23), art. 19 n.º 1 b) — https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2023/lei-n-o-14-23-de-28-de-dezembro/)_
- **Reduced rate in Cabinda Province** — 1% on imports and supplies of goods under the special tax regime of Cabinda Province, except the Annex III goods, which carry the general rate; the 2% figure quoted by older summaries for port and water services is not in art. 19 `[RESEARCH GAP — reviewer to confirm]`  _(Código do IVA (Lei n.º 7/19 as amended and republished by Lei n.º 14/23), art. 19 n.º 1 e) — https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2023/lei-n-o-14-23-de-28-de-dezembro/)_
- **Standard (general) VAT regime threshold** — Turnover or imports of AOA 350,000,000 or more in the previous year; manufacturers above AOA 25,000,000 in the previous 12 months; and voluntary adherents  _(Código do IVA (Lei n.º 7/19 as amended and republished by Lei n.º 14/23), art. 62 — https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2023/lei-n-o-14-23-de-28-de-dezembro/)_
- **Simplified VAT regime turnover range** — Turnover or imports of AOA 25,000,000 or more and below AOA 350,000,000 in the previous year; below AOA 25,000,000 is the exclusion regime  _(Código do IVA (Lei n.º 7/19 as amended and republished by Lei n.º 14/23), arts. 60 and 69.º-A — https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2023/lei-n-o-14-23-de-28-de-dezembro/)_
- **VAT return filing frequency** — Monthly periodic declaration with its annexes, submitted electronically; simplified-regime taxpayers file a monthly simplified declaration  _(Código do IVA (Lei n.º 7/19 as amended and republished by Lei n.º 14/23), arts. 44 and 69.º-B — https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2019/lei-n-o-7-19-de-24-de-abril/; https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2023/lei-n-o-14-23-de-28-de-dezembro/)_
- **VAT payment deadline** — The periodic declaration is due by the last day of the month following the month it covers and the system issues the payment note with it; VAT not captured by the customer is handed over by the invoice issuer by the last business day of the month after receipt  _(Código do IVA (Lei n.º 7/19 as amended and republished by Lei n.º 14/23), arts. 30 and 44 — https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2019/lei-n-o-7-19-de-24-de-abril/; https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2023/lei-n-o-14-23-de-28-de-dezembro/)_
- **VAT captivation (withholding) regime** — The State and its bodies and oil investor companies capture the whole of the VAT on supplier invoices, and the central bank, commercial banks, insurers and reinsurers and telecommunications operators capture 50%, at receipt of the invoice, paying it to the State instead of the supplier; the captured tax is credited in the supplier's return  _(Código do IVA (Lei n.º 7/19 as amended and republished by Lei n.º 14/23), arts. 21 and 29 — https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2019/lei-n-o-7-19-de-24-de-abril/; https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2023/lei-n-o-14-23-de-28-de-dezembro/)_
