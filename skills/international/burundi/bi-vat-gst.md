# Burundi VAT / GST

## 1. The rates: VAT law, article 19

Article 19 of the VAT law as revised by loi n° 1/10 du 16 novembre 2020 sets three
rates and no others.

| Rate | Name | Applies to |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| **18%** | *taux ordinaire* | The taxable base generally |
| **10%** | *taux intermédiaire* | (a) agricultural inputs; (b) agricultural products **processed in Burundi**; (c) imports and local purchases of **foodstuffs on a list set by ministerial ordinance**; (d) products and services supplied by **hotel establishments** |
| **0%** | *taux particulier* | (a) exports and assimilated operations; (b) **international transport services on the ministerial list**, other than services ancillary to transport; (c) intermediate operations and transactions on products **destined for export** |

_(Loi n° 1/10 du 16 novembre 2020 portant modification de la loi n° 1/12 du 29 juillet 2013 portant révision de la loi n° 1/02 du 17 février 2009 relative à la TVA, art. 19: https://www.obr.bi/images/LOI_N1_10_DU_16_NOVEMBRE_2020_PORTANT_MODIFICATION_DE_LA_LOI_N1_12_DU_29_JUILLET_2013_PORTANT_REVISION_DE_LA_LOI_N1_02_DU_17_FEVRIER_2009_PORTANT_INSTITUTION_DE_LA_TAXE_SUR_LA_VALEUR_AJOUTEE_TVA_.pdf)_

- **The 10% band is not "essential goods"**: the previous version described it that
  way and marked it "(approx: confirm scope)". The article's four limbs are
  narrower and more specific than that phrase suggests: two of them are
  agricultural, one is a **list the Minister publishes separately**, and the fourth
  is the **hotel sector**, which is not an essential good by any reading  _(VAT law, art. 19: https://www.obr.bi/images/LOI_N1_10_DU_16_NOVEMBRE_2020_PORTANT_MODIFICATION_DE_LA_LOI_N1_12_DU_29_JUILLET_2013_PORTANT_REVISION_DE_LA_LOI_N1_02_DU_17_FEVRIER_2009_PORTANT_INSTITUTION_DE_LA_TAXE_SUR_LA_VALEUR_AJOUTEE_TVA_.pdf)_
- **Sugar sits at 10% by a separate provision**: the Finance Act 2026/2027 abolishes
  the consumption tax on sugar, whether imported or produced locally, sets **VAT on
  it at 10%** and customs duties at **25%**  _(Loi de Finances 2026/2027 (loi n° 1/10 du 30 juin 2026), art. 90: https://www.obr.bi/images/LOI_DE_FINANCES_2026-2027_PROMULGUEE_compressed.pdf)_
- **Zero-rating of exports was right, and international transport was missing**: the
  previous version had exports "(approx: confirm)" and said nothing about
  international transport, which is the limb that matters to freight and airline
  operators  _(VAT law, art. 19: https://www.obr.bi/images/LOI_N1_10_DU_16_NOVEMBRE_2020_PORTANT_MODIFICATION_DE_LA_LOI_N1_12_DU_29_JUILLET_2013_PORTANT_REVISION_DE_LA_LOI_N1_02_DU_17_FEVRIER_2009_PORTANT_INSTITUTION_DE_LA_TAXE_SUR_LA_VALEUR_AJOUTEE_TVA_.pdf)_
- **The 10% foodstuff list and the 0% assimilated-export and international-transport lists are set by
  ministerial ordinance**, not by the law. The applicable ordinances were not read here  _(VAT law, art. 19, final paragraph: https://www.obr.bi/images/LOI_N1_10_DU_16_NOVEMBRE_2020_PORTANT_MODIFICATION_DE_LA_LOI_N1_12_DU_29_JUILLET_2013_PORTANT_REVISION_DE_LA_LOI_N1_02_DU_17_FEVRIER_2009_PORTANT_INSTITUTION_DE_LA_TAXE_SUR_LA_VALEUR_AJOUTEE_TVA_.pdf)_

## 2. Registration: the thresholds are set annually, not by the VAT law

- **The VAT law delegates the threshold**: art. 37: *"Le seuil d'assujettissement
  d'office est fixé par ordonnance du Ministre."* This is why a threshold cited to
  "the VAT Law" is unfalsifiable: the law contains none  _(VAT law, art. 37: https://www.obr.bi/images/LOI_N1_10_DU_16_NOVEMBRE_2020_PORTANT_MODIFICATION_DE_LA_LOI_N1_12_DU_29_JUILLET_2013_PORTANT_REVISION_DE_LA_LOI_N1_02_DU_17_FEVRIER_2009_PORTANT_INSTITUTION_DE_LA_TAXE_SUR_LA_VALEUR_AJOUTEE_TVA_.pdf)_

For budget year **2026/2027**, article 271 of the Finance Act fixes them:

| Trigger | Threshold |
| --- | ---: |
| **Compulsory** registration (*assujettissement d'office*): taxable turnover reaching this in an accounting year | **BIF 25,000,000** |
| **Optional** registration (*assujettissement par option*) | **BIF 5,000,000** |
| **Automatic** liability: local purchases **and/or imports** in a fiscal year, excluding the value of investments and personal effects | **over BIF 50,000,000** |
| **Automatic** liability: stock of raw materials, finished or semi-finished products or merchandise | **over BIF 50,000,000** |

_(Loi de Finances 2026/2027 (loi n° 1/10 du 30 juin 2026), art. 271: https://www.obr.bi/images/LOI_DE_FINANCES_2026-2027_PROMULGUEE_compressed.pdf)_

- **The purchases and stock tests are the ones businesses miss**: they do not look
  at sales at all. A trader with modest turnover but more than **BIF 50,000,000** of
  local purchases, imports **or stock on hand** is automatically liable to VAT and is
  compulsorily moved into the **medium or large taxpayer** category. Neither test
  appeared in the previous version  _(Loi de Finances 2026/2027, art. 271: https://www.obr.bi/images/LOI_DE_FINANCES_2026-2027_PROMULGUEE_compressed.pdf)_
- **Liability starts before registration does**: art. 38: compulsory liability takes
  effect on the **first day of the month following** the month in which the threshold
  was reached, and the person is liable for the tax from that date ***"qu'elle ait
  introduit ou pas la demande d'enregistrement"***: whether or not they have applied
  to register. The Finance Act repeats the timing: invoicing VAT begins the first day
  of the month after the threshold is reached  _(VAT law, art. 38, and Loi de Finances 2026/2027, art. 271: https://www.obr.bi/images/LOI_N1_10_DU_16_NOVEMBRE_2020_PORTANT_MODIFICATION_DE_LA_LOI_N1_12_DU_29_JUILLET_2013_PORTANT_REVISION_DE_LA_LOI_N1_02_DU_17_FEVRIER_2009_PORTANT_INSTITUTION_DE_LA_TAXE_SUR_LA_VALEUR_AJOUTEE_TVA_.pdf)_
- **The application is due by the 15th of the following month**: art. 39, with the
  administration required to issue the registration certificate within **fifteen
  calendar days** of receiving a properly completed form  _(VAT law, art. 39: https://www.obr.bi/images/LOI_N1_10_DU_16_NOVEMBRE_2020_PORTANT_MODIFICATION_DE_LA_LOI_N1_12_DU_29_JUILLET_2013_PORTANT_REVISION_DE_LA_LOI_N1_02_DU_17_FEVRIER_2009_PORTANT_INSTITUTION_DE_LA_TAXE_SUR_LA_VALEUR_AJOUTEE_TVA_.pdf)_
- **Deregistration**: article 67 permits an application from the month following
  24 consecutive months below the applicable threshold. A force-majeure request
  may be made earlier. Falling below the threshold alone does not cancel registration.
  _(VAT law, art. 67: https://www.obr.bi/images/LOI_N1_10_DU_16_NOVEMBRE_2020_PORTANT_MODIFICATION_DE_LA_LOI_N1_12_DU_29_JUILLET_2013_PORTANT_REVISION_DE_LA_LOI_N1_02_DU_17_FEVRIER_2009_PORTANT_INSTITUTION_DE_LA_TAXE_SUR_LA_VALEUR_AJOUTEE_TVA_.pdf)_

## 3. Filing, imports and penalties

- **Monthly returns, by the fifteenth**: returns and payment are due to the
  competent service of the tax administration no later than the **fifteenth day** of
  the following month. The previous version had this right and marked it "(approx ;
  confirm)"  _(VAT law, arts. 52 and 54: https://www.obr.bi/images/LOI_N1_10_DU_16_NOVEMBRE_2020_PORTANT_MODIFICATION_DE_LA_LOI_N1_12_DU_29_JUILLET_2013_PORTANT_REVISION_DE_LA_LOI_N1_02_DU_17_FEVRIER_2009_PORTANT_INSTITUTION_DE_LA_TAXE_SUR_LA_VALEUR_AJOUTEE_TVA_.pdf)_
- **The import VAT base includes duties but excludes the 3% income-tax advance** ;
  art. 18 builds the taxable base on imports from the customs value plus duties and
  border taxes of every kind, **excluding** the *prélèvement forfaitaire* levied as an
  advance of income tax at **3% of the customs value**: and that advance itself does
  not apply to **fuel**. So the 3% is a real cost on importers but is **not** part of
  what VAT is charged on  _(VAT law, art. 18: https://www.obr.bi/images/LOI_N1_10_DU_16_NOVEMBRE_2020_PORTANT_MODIFICATION_DE_LA_LOI_N1_12_DU_29_JUILLET_2013_PORTANT_REVISION_DE_LA_LOI_N1_02_DU_17_FEVRIER_2009_PORTANT_INSTITUTION_DE_LA_TAXE_SUR_LA_VALEUR_AJOUTEE_TVA_.pdf)_
- **Consumption tax sits inside the VAT base**: *"La taxe de consommation de toute
  nature est incluse dans la base taxable de la TVA"*  _(Loi de Finances 2026/2027, art. 90: https://www.obr.bi/images/LOI_DE_FINANCES_2026-2027_PROMULGUEE_compressed.pdf)_
- **Electronic communications: a 20% specific tax replaces VAT**: the Finance Act
  levies a specific tax of **20%** on the fees for value-added services in the
  electronic communications sector and ***abolishes VAT on those services***. A guide
  that applies 18% VAT to them would be wrong for 2026/2027  _(Loi de Finances 2026/2027, art. 187: https://www.obr.bi/images/LOI_DE_FINANCES_2026-2027_PROMULGUEE_compressed.pdf)_
- **Penalties**: articles 55–56 provide penalties of 100% of VAT evaded,
  rising to 200% on repetition, for the specified electronic-invoicing breaches.
  They are not a general penalty rate for every VAT error. Articles 59–60 and
  the following provisions address separate filing, payment and invoicing
  offences. Their detailed penalty ladder needs review before application.
  _(VAT law, arts. 55–60 ff.: https://www.obr.bi/images/LOI_N1_10_DU_16_NOVEMBRE_2020_PORTANT_MODIFICATION_DE_LA_LOI_N1_12_DU_29_JUILLET_2013_PORTANT_REVISION_DE_LA_LOI_N1_02_DU_17_FEVRIER_2009_PORTANT_INSTITUTION_DE_LA_TAXE_SUR_LA_VALEUR_AJOUTEE_TVA_.pdf)_
