# Trinidad and Tobago Personal Income Tax

## Personal income tax rates and bands

- **Chargeable income basis and two-tier rate** — Personal income tax is charged on chargeable income (assessable income less allowances and deductions). A two-tier rate applies, with a higher 30% rate on chargeable income above TTD 1,000,000.
- **Rate on chargeable income up to TTD 1,000,000** — 25%, with effect from 1 January 2017  _(Inland Revenue Division, Self Employed/Sole Trader page — https://www.ird.gov.tt/self-employed-sole-trader)_
- **Rate on chargeable income over TTD 1,000,000** — 30% on every dollar over TTD 1,000,000  _(Inland Revenue Division, Self Employed/Sole Trader page — https://www.ird.gov.tt/self-employed-sole-trader)_
- **Personal allowance (tax-free threshold)** — TTD 90,000 per year from income year 2023 (TTD 84,000 for 2021 and 2022; TTD 72,000 for 2018 to 2020)  _(Inland Revenue Division, Individual Income Tax Deductions and Allowances by Income Year 2018 to 2023 — https://www.ird.gov.tt/Media/Default/IRDForms/I-Individual_Income_Tax_Deductions_and_Allowances--2023.pdf)_
- **Personal allowance for non-residents** — Not available, except to non-resident individuals aged 60 or over receiving a pension accrued or derived from Trinidad and Tobago  _(Inland Revenue Division, Individual Income Tax Deductions and Allowances by Income Year 2018 to 2023 — https://www.ird.gov.tt/Media/Default/IRDForms/I-Individual_Income_Tax_Deductions_and_Allowances--2023.pdf)_
- **Non-resident rates on TT-source income** — Same 25% / 30% scale applies to chargeable income; passive income may instead suffer final withholding tax  _(Income Tax Act (Chap. 75:01) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/trinidad-and-tobago/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **Residence test** — Individual is resident if present in Trinidad and Tobago for at least 183 days in the year of income, or otherwise ordinarily resident ((approx — confirm))  _(Income Tax Act (Chap. 75:01) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/trinidad-and-tobago/individual/residence)_
- **National Insurance (NIS) contributions** — Employee NIS contributions are deductible in computing chargeable income  _(Income Tax Act (Chap. 75:01) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/trinidad-and-tobago/individual/deductions)_
- **Tertiary education expenses deduction** — Up to TTD 72,000 per year from income year 2019 (TTD 60,000 in 2018) for tertiary education at institutions outside the region, for the taxpayer, spouse or child, with one return trip a year allowed  _(Inland Revenue Division, Individual Income Tax Deductions and Allowances by Income Year 2018 to 2023 — https://www.ird.gov.tt/Media/Default/IRDForms/I-Individual_Income_Tax_Deductions_and_Allowances--2023.pdf)_
- **Approved pension/annuity/NIS deduction cap** — Contributions to approved pension funds, annuities and NIS deductible up to TTD 60,000 per year combined from income year 2022 (TTD 50,000 before)  _(Inland Revenue Division, Individual Income Tax Deductions and Allowances by Income Year 2018 to 2023 — https://www.ird.gov.tt/Media/Default/IRDForms/I-Individual_Income_Tax_Deductions_and_Allowances--2023.pdf)_
- **First-time homeowner allowance** — Up to TTD 30,000 per year from income year 2022 (TTD 25,000 for 2018 to 2021) for five years from acquisition, for property acquired on or after 1 January 2011 and constructed or purchased  _(Inland Revenue Division, Individual Income Tax Deductions and Allowances by Income Year 2018 to 2023 — https://www.ird.gov.tt/Media/Default/IRDForms/I-Individual_Income_Tax_Deductions_and_Allowances--2023.pdf)_
- **Individual return filing deadline** — Within four months after the end of the year of income, so by 30 April  _(Income Tax Act (Chap. 75:01), Ministry of the Attorney General consolidation to Act 7 of 2016, s 76(1) — https://rgd.legalaffairs.gov.tt/laws2/alphabetical_list/lawspdfs/75.01.pdf)_
- **Self-employed quarterly instalments** — One quarter of the estimated tax on or before 31 March, 30 June, 30 September and 31 December, with the balance due by 30 April of the following year  _(Income Tax Act (Chap. 75:01), Ministry of the Attorney General consolidation to Act 7 of 2016, s 79(1) — https://rgd.legalaffairs.gov.tt/laws2/alphabetical_list/lawspdfs/75.01.pdf ; Inland Revenue Division, Self Employed/Sole Trader page — https://www.ird.gov.tt/self-employed-sole-trader)_
- **Business levy on self-employed** — 0.6% of gross sales or receipts other than emolument income, where it exceeds the income tax liability; PwC reports the levy applies above TTD 360,000 of gross receipts  _(Income Tax Act (Chap. 75:01), Ministry of the Attorney General consolidation to Act 7 of 2016, s 5A(1) — https://rgd.legalaffairs.gov.tt/laws2/alphabetical_list/lawspdfs/75.01.pdf ; Inland Revenue Division, Self Employed/Sole Trader page — https://www.ird.gov.tt/self-employed-sole-trader ; TTD 360,000 threshold: PwC — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/trinidad-and-tobago/individual/other-taxes)_
