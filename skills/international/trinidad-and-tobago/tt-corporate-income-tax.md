# Trinidad and Tobago Corporate Income Tax

## Corporation tax rates and base

- **Standard corporation tax rate** — 30% percent  _([Corporation Tax Act (Chap. 75:02)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/trinidad-and-tobago/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Banks and petrochemical companies rate** — 35% percent  _([Corporation Tax Act (Chap. 75:02)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/trinidad-and-tobago/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Petroleum production companies rate** — 50% (standard); 30% for deep-sea production percent  _([Petroleum Taxes Act (Chap. 75:04)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/trinidad-and-tobago/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **SMEs newly listed on the TT Stock Exchange** — 0% for first 5 years, then 15% for the next 5 years percent ((approx — confirm))  _([Corporation Tax Act (Chap. 75:02)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/trinidad-and-tobago/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Special Economic Zone entities rate** — 15% percent  _([Trinidad and Tobago Special Economic Zones Act](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/trinidad-and-tobago/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Tax base** — Chargeable profits (accounting profit adjusted for tax) plus chargeable short-term capital gains  _([Corporation Tax Act (Chap. 75:02)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/trinidad-and-tobago/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Business Levy** — 0.6% of gross revenue/receipts, payable where it exceeds the corporation tax liability (exempt below TTD 360,000 turnover) percent  _([Corporation Tax Act (Chap. 75:02)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/trinidad-and-tobago/corporate/other-taxes))_
- **Green Fund Levy** — 0.3% of gross income, non-deductible, payable quarterly percent  _([Miscellaneous Taxes Act (Chap. 77:01)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/trinidad-and-tobago/corporate/other-taxes))_
- **Withholding tax on dividends to non-residents** — **3%** to a non-resident parent company (one holding a controlling interest, usually over 50% of the voting power) and **8%** to any other non-resident company or individual, before treaty relief (e.g. 0% within CARICOM). Not a flat 10%  _([Income Tax Act (Chap. 75:01)](https://www.ird.gov.tt/international-tax/Withholding-Tax-Guide); Board of Inland Revenue Withholding Tax Guide)_ %  _([Income Tax Act (Chap. 75:01)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/trinidad-and-tobago/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **Withholding tax on interest to non-residents** — **15%**, to non-resident individuals and non-resident companies alike, before treaty relief. Interest falls under "payments" in the Board of Inland Revenue guide, alongside royalties and other payments, all at 15%. The 8% and 3% figures belong to distributions, not to interest  _([Income Tax Act (Chap. 75:01)](https://www.ird.gov.tt/international-tax/Withholding-Tax-Guide); Board of Inland Revenue Withholding Tax Guide)_ %  _([Income Tax Act (Chap. 75:01)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/trinidad-and-tobago/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **Withholding tax on royalties to non-residents** — 15% (non-treaty); reduced under double tax treaties percent  _([Income Tax Act (Chap. 75:01)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/trinidad-and-tobago/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **Withholding tax on payments to residents** — 0% on dividends, interest and royalties paid to resident companies/individuals  _([Income Tax Act (Chap. 75:01)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/trinidad-and-tobago/corporate/withholding-taxes))_

> **Trinidad has two withholding categories, not a list of heads. Do not read
> the three bullets above as the whole charge.**
>
> Section 50(1) charges withholding on anything paid to a person who is neither
> resident in nor carrying on business in Trinidad and Tobago, and section 51
> sorts it into exactly two boxes:
>
> - **Distributions** — dividends, distributions of assets, and **branch profits
>   remitted or deemed remitted**. **3%** to a parent company, **8%** otherwise.
>   Branch profits are charged on after-tax profits *whether remitted or not*,
>   except to the extent the branch reinvests them in Trinidad and Tobago to the
>   Board's satisfaction (replacing fixed assets does not count as reinvestment).
> - **Payments** — everything else, at a flat **15%**. Section 51 defines this
>   to include *interest, discounts, annuities or other annual or periodic
>   sums*; **rentals**; **royalties**; **management charges or charges for the
>   provision of personal services and technical and managerial skills**; and
>   **premiums, commissions, fees and licences** — with premiums paid to
>   insurance companies and contributions to pension funds and schemes carved
>   out.
>
> The practical consequence is that a **management charge, a professional fee, a
> rental or a licence fee** to a non-resident carries the same 15% as interest
> and royalties, and none of those heads appears anywhere else in this guide. An
> agent that enumerates dividends, interest and royalties and stops will
> under-withhold on the majority of an ordinary intercompany invoice.
>
> **Withhold on the gross.** The Board's guide defines "payment" as *"a payment
> without any deductions whatsoever"*, so the 15% runs on the full invoice
> value, not on a net or an expense-recovered amount.
>
> Remit within **30 days** of the payment or distribution. Treaty relief may
> reduce a rate; where a treaty rate is *higher* than the statutory rate, the
> statutory rate applies  _([Income Tax Act (Chap. 75:01) ss. 50–51](https://www.ird.gov.tt/international-tax/Withholding-Tax-Guide); Board of Inland Revenue *Withholding Tax Guide*)_

- **Withholding tax on management charges, professional fees and other "payments"** — **15%** to non-residents, on the gross, under the s.51 "payment" definition (management charges, personal services, technical and managerial skills, commissions, fees, licences)  _([Income Tax Act (Chap. 75:01) s.51](https://www.ird.gov.tt/international-tax/Withholding-Tax-Guide))_
- **Withholding tax on rentals to non-residents** — **15%** on the gross rental, as a s.51 "payment"  _([Income Tax Act (Chap. 75:01) s.51](https://www.ird.gov.tt/international-tax/Withholding-Tax-Guide))_
- **Withholding tax on annuities and discounts to non-residents** — **15%**, as s.51 "payments" alongside interest  _([Income Tax Act (Chap. 75:01) s.51](https://www.ird.gov.tt/international-tax/Withholding-Tax-Guide))_
- **Withholding tax on insurance premiums to non-residents** — **15%** where within the s.51 "premiums" head; premiums paid to insurance companies, and contributions to pension funds and schemes, are excluded  _([Income Tax Act (Chap. 75:01) s.51](https://www.ird.gov.tt/international-tax/Withholding-Tax-Guide))_
- **Withholding tax on branch profits remittance** — **3%** to a parent company / **8%** otherwise, charged on after-tax branch profits **whether remitted or not**, less amounts reinvested in Trinidad and Tobago to the Board's satisfaction (replacement of fixed assets does not qualify)  _([Income Tax Act (Chap. 75:01) s.50(1)](https://www.ird.gov.tt/international-tax/Withholding-Tax-Guide))_
- **Corporation tax return deadline** — 30 April following the end of the income year  _([Corporation Tax Act (Chap. 75:02)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/trinidad-and-tobago/corporate/tax-administration))_
- **Quarterly tax instalments** — Payable on 31 March, 30 June, 30 September and 31 December; balance due by 30 April  _([Corporation Tax Act (Chap. 75:02)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/trinidad-and-tobago/corporate/tax-administration))_
