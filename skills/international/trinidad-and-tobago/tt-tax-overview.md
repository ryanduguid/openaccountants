# Trinidad and Tobago Tax Overview

## 2026 income year

The Inland Revenue Division (IRD) of the Ministry of Finance administers income tax, corporation tax and value added tax (VAT). Individuals generally use the calendar income year. Companies determine their accounting terminal date and apply the corporation tax accounting-period rules. [Income Tax Act, sections 2 and 6](https://laws.gov.tt/ttdll-web/revision/download/106658?type=act) · [Corporation Tax Act, section 4](https://laws.gov.tt/ttdll-web/revision/download/106656?type=act) · [IRD responsibilities of corporations](https://www.ird.gov.tt/corporations)

### Personal income tax

- **Tax base.** Section 5 generally brings income from Trinidad and Tobago and abroad into charge. A person who is not ordinarily resident or not domiciled in Trinidad and Tobago is generally taxed on foreign income to the extent received in the country. Income from employment or an office exercised in Trinidad and Tobago is treated as local income even when paid abroad. Check treaty and withholding rules for cross-border income. [Income Tax Act, section 5](https://laws.gov.tt/ttdll-web/revision/download/106658?type=act)
- **Allowance and rates.** The ordinary resident personal allowance is TTD 90,000 per income year from 1 January 2023. Apply 25% to the first TTD 1,000,000 of chargeable income and 30% to the excess. For example, TTD 140,000 before the personal allowance, with no other deductions or credits, leaves TTD 50,000 of chargeable income and TTD 12,500 of income tax. [Income Tax Act, section 18 and Third Schedule](https://laws.gov.tt/ttdll-web/revision/download/106658?type=act) · [Finance Act 2022, section 8(a)](https://www.ttparliament.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/a2022-21g.pdf) · [IRD allowances](https://www.ird.gov.tt/individual/deductions-and-required-supporting-documents)
- **Filing and payment.** When a return is required it is ordinarily due by 30 April following the income year, as is any balance due on ordinary quarterly estimated tax. Check the exceptions to the return requirement in section 76 and the instalment rules in section 79. [Income Tax Act, sections 76 and 79](https://laws.gov.tt/ttdll-web/revision/download/106658?type=act) · [IRD filing deadlines](https://www.ird.gov.tt/etax/non-logged_in_returns/faqs)

### Corporation tax

- **Tax base.** A resident company is ordinarily controlled in Trinidad and Tobago, where its mind or management is ordinarily situated. Corporate residence and the source of profit determine the tax base. Resident companies are generally taxed on worldwide profits, and non-resident companies are generally taxed on Trinidad and Tobago profits. [Corporation Tax Act, sections 2 and 3](https://laws.gov.tt/ttdll-web/revision/download/106656?type=act)
- **Rates.** The ordinary company rate is 30%. Licensed banks and specified petrochemical and other energy businesses have separate First Schedule treatment. Check company type before applying the ordinary rate. Business Levy and Green Fund Levy have separate bases and may also apply. [Finance Act 2017, section 7](https://www.ttparliament.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/a2017-15g.pdf) · [Corporation Tax Act, section 3A and First Schedule](https://laws.gov.tt/ttdll-web/revision/download/106656?type=act) · [IRD responsibilities of corporations](https://www.ird.gov.tt/corporations)
- **Filing and payment.** The ordinary annual return and balance of tax are due by 30 April after the income year. Companies subject to instalments pay during the year. Check current IRD notices for any year-specific extensions to the ordinary individual or company return deadline. [Corporation Tax Act, section 19](https://laws.gov.tt/ttdll-web/revision/download/106656?type=act) · [Income Tax Act, sections 76 and 79](https://laws.gov.tt/ttdll-web/revision/download/106658?type=act) · [IRD filing deadlines](https://www.ird.gov.tt/etax/non-logged_in_returns/faqs)

### Value added tax

The ordinary VAT rate is 12.5%. Test commercial supplies against the TTD 600,000 registration threshold in force from 1 January 2023. Section 21 looks at both the previous 12 months and the next 12 months beginning with the month of supply, with a separate averaging test for a new business. The Act uses a 'not more than' test for the ordinary exception, whilst the IRD registration page also asks businesses at TTD 600,000 in the preceding period to apply. Confirm the registration position with the IRD if exactly at the boundary. [VAT Act, sections 7 and 21–22](https://www.ird.gov.tt/Media/Default/IRDLegislation/Ch2024_75.06-Value-Added-Tax-Act--2024.pdf) · [Finance Act 2022, section 9(a)](https://www.ttparliament.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/a2022-21g.pdf) · [IRD VAT rate](https://www.ird.gov.tt/VAT) · [IRD registration guidance](https://www.ird.gov.tt/VAT/registration)

The standard tax period is 2 months, according to the category the IRD assigns. The return and net payment are generally due by the 25th of the month following the end of the period. For example, TTD 10,000 before VAT on a standard-rated supply gives TTD 1,250 of VAT and an invoice total of TTD 11,250. [VAT Act, sections 30–34](https://www.ird.gov.tt/Media/Default/IRDLegislation/Ch2024_75.06-Value-Added-Tax-Act--2024.pdf) · [IRD return guidance](https://www.ird.gov.tt/vat-submission-of-the-vat-return)
