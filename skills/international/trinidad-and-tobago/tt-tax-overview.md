# Trinidad and Tobago Tax Overview

## Trinidad and Tobago tax system at a glance

- **Tax year (year of income)** — Calendar year: the period of twelve months commencing on 1 January  _(Income Tax Act (Chap. 75:01), Ministry of the Attorney General consolidation to Act 7 of 2016, s 2, definition of 'year of income' — https://rgd.legalaffairs.gov.tt/laws2/alphabetical_list/lawspdfs/75.01.pdf)_
- **Currency** — Trinidad and Tobago dollar (TTD / TT$)  _(Central Bank of Trinidad and Tobago)_
- **Tax authority** — Inland Revenue Division (IRD), Ministry of Finance  _(Inland Revenue Division — https://www.ird.gov.tt/)_
- **Individual residence basis** — Residents are taxed on worldwide income; a person not ordinarily resident or not domiciled in Trinidad and Tobago is taxed on foreign-source income only to the extent received in the country, and non-residents on Trinidad and Tobago-source income  _(Income Tax Act (Chap. 75:01), Ministry of the Attorney General consolidation to Act 7 of 2016, s 5(1) and (2) — https://rgd.legalaffairs.gov.tt/laws2/alphabetical_list/lawspdfs/75.01.pdf)_
- **Corporate residence basis** — Resident companies are taxed on profits accruing in or derived from Trinidad and Tobago or elsewhere, whether or not received in the country; non-resident companies only on profits accruing in or derived from Trinidad and Tobago  _(Corporation Tax Act (Chap. 75:02), Ministry of the Attorney General consolidation to Act 10 of 2016, s 3(1) — https://rgd.legalaffairs.gov.tt/laws2/alphabetical_list/lawspdfs/75.02.pdf)_
- **Headline personal income tax rates** — 25% on chargeable income up to TTD 1,000,000 and 30% on every dollar above, with effect from 1 January 2017  _(Inland Revenue Division, Self Employed/Sole Trader page — https://www.ird.gov.tt/self-employed-sole-trader)_
- **Headline corporation tax rate** — 30% from income year 2018 (25% to TTD 1,000,000 and 30% above for 2017); 35% for petrochemical and natural gas liquefaction, processing, transmission and distribution companies and petroleum wholesalers under First Schedule para 3  _(Inland Revenue Division, Corporations page — https://www.ird.gov.tt/corporations ; Corporation Tax Act (Chap. 75:02), Ministry of the Attorney General consolidation to Act 10 of 2016, s 3 and First Schedule para 3 — https://rgd.legalaffairs.gov.tt/laws2/alphabetical_list/lawspdfs/75.02.pdf)_
- **Value Added Tax** — Yes — VAT applies at a standard rate of 12.5%  _(Value Added Tax Act (Chap. 75:06) — https://www.ird.gov.tt/VAT)_
- **Annual individual income tax return deadline** — Within four months after the end of the year of income, so by 30 April  _(Income Tax Act (Chap. 75:01), Ministry of the Attorney General consolidation to Act 7 of 2016, s 76(1) — https://rgd.legalaffairs.gov.tt/laws2/alphabetical_list/lawspdfs/75.01.pdf)_
- **Annual corporation tax return deadline** — 30 April following the end of the income year  _(Income Tax Act (Chap. 75:01), Ministry of the Attorney General consolidation to Act 7 of 2016, s 76(1), applied to companies through the Corporation Tax Act — https://rgd.legalaffairs.gov.tt/laws2/alphabetical_list/lawspdfs/75.01.pdf)_
- **Green Fund Levy** — 0.3% of actual gross sales or receipts of companies and partnerships, with effect from 21 January 2016, paid by quarterly instalment  _(Inland Revenue Division, Corporations page — https://www.ird.gov.tt/corporations ; Miscellaneous Taxes Act (Chap. 77:01), Ministry of the Attorney General consolidation, Part XIV, ss 61 to 65 — https://rgd.legalaffairs.gov.tt/laws2/alphabetical_list/lawspdfs/77.01.pdf)_
- **Business Levy** — 0.6% of gross sales or receipts, payable where it exceeds the corporation tax or income tax liability, which is credited against it  _(Corporation Tax Act (Chap. 75:02), Ministry of the Attorney General consolidation to Act 10 of 2016, s 3A(1) and (3) — https://rgd.legalaffairs.gov.tt/laws2/alphabetical_list/lawspdfs/75.02.pdf ; Income Tax Act (Chap. 75:01), Ministry of the Attorney General consolidation to Act 7 of 2016, s 5A(1) — https://rgd.legalaffairs.gov.tt/laws2/alphabetical_list/lawspdfs/75.01.pdf ; Inland Revenue Division, Corporations page — https://www.ird.gov.tt/corporations)_
