# Sri Lanka tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the taxpayer, where income arose, residence facts and period. Check the applicable treaty and foreign-tax relief.

## Territorial reach

Residents ordinarily include income from Sri Lanka and abroad. Nonresidents include Sri Lankan-source income. Identify the type and source of each receipt and any income attributable to a Sri Lankan permanent establishment. Check applicable exemptions, treaty relief and any separate tax on remitted branch profits. [Inland Revenue Act, ss 4, 62 and 71–79](https://www.ird.gov.lk/en/publications/Acts_Income%20Tax_2017/IRA_Cons_Act_-_2025_Changes.pdf) · [Inland Revenue (Amendment) Act No. 11 of 2026, ss 10–11](https://www.ird.gov.lk/en/publications/Acts_Income%20Tax_2017/IR_Act_No_11-2026_E.pdf)

## Residence

An individual generally qualifies by residing in Sri Lanka or by being present during the assessment year as part of at least 183 days in a 12-month period beginning or ending in that year. When the day-count rule is the only basis, residence starts at the beginning of that period under section 70. Check the separate government-service and Sri Lankan ship employment tests.

From 1 April 2025, an Investor Category Residence Visa holder is excluded from residence under section 69(1A). Under section 69(1)(d), a foreign citizen or subject deemed resident through qualifying Sri Lankan ship employment is liable as a resident by that rule only on income from the employment on that ship. If ship employment is the individual's sole residence basis, residence lasts only for the employment period.

A separate nonresidence period applies when someone leaves under a qualifying contract of at least 1 year with an employer unassociated with their immediate Sri Lankan employer. It starts on the first day of the assessment year in which they leave and ends when the contract expires.

A company qualifies through Sri Lankan incorporation or formation, registration, principal office, or management and control exercised in Sri Lanka at any time in the year. A partnership qualifies through formation or management and control in Sri Lanka. [Inland Revenue Act, ss 69–70](https://www.ird.gov.lk/en/publications/Acts_Income%20Tax_2017/IRA_Cons_Act_-_2025_Changes.pdf) · [2026 amendment, ss 1 and 9](https://www.ird.gov.lk/en/publications/Acts_Income%20Tax_2017/IR_Act_No_11-2026_E.pdf)

## Taxpayer types

Identify the individual carrying on a sole business, partnership, company, trust or other taxpayer before assigning receipts. A partnership is liable for tax separately from the partners under the rules in force since January 2020. The partners also account for their allocated amounts under the partner rules, including applicable credits. [Inland Revenue Act, ss 53–59](https://www.ird.gov.lk/en/publications/Acts_Income%20Tax_2017/IRA_Cons_Act_-_2025_Changes.pdf) · [2026 amendment, ss 5–7](https://www.ird.gov.lk/en/publications/Acts_Income%20Tax_2017/IR_Act_No_11-2026_E.pdf)

## Currency

Express amounts in Sri Lankan rupees (LKR). The general conversion uses the Central Bank of Sri Lanka rate on the date the amount is taken into account. Check for a Commissioner-General specification and the rules for financial institutions and instruments. [Inland Revenue Act, s 26](https://www.ird.gov.lk/en/publications/Acts_Income%20Tax_2017/IRA_Cons_Act_-_2025_Changes.pdf)

## Assessment periods

The statutory year runs from 1 April to 31 March. A trust or company may apply for approval to use accounts for a different 12-month period in computing its assessment. The statutory payment timetable continues to apply. [Inland Revenue Act, s 20](https://www.ird.gov.lk/en/publications/Acts_Income%20Tax_2017/IRA_Cons_Act_-_2025_Changes.pdf)

## Responsible authorities

The Inland Revenue Department and its Commissioner-General administer income tax. Use the Department's registration and filing services for tax matters and Sri Lanka Customs for border charges. [Inland Revenue Department](https://www.ird.gov.lk/) · [Sri Lanka Customs](https://www.customs.gov.lk/)

## Fictional intake

A resident partner receives an allocation of business income from a Sri Lankan partnership that has already paid tax. Record the partnership income, the payment and the partner's share before applying the partnership and partner rules.
