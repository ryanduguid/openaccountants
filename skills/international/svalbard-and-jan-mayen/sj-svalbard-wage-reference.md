> **General reference only.** This source-cited Tier 2 conditional draft illustrates one annual wage-band component. It does not establish an actual payroll deduction, tax claim, refund or complete income tax engagement. Jan Mayen remains outside the calculation. ## Scope and source conditions

Load workflow-base and income-tax-workflow-base in full. Preserve all 4 universal outputs and 18 checks, all 5 income tax outputs and 12 checks, all mandatory country slots and every shared refusal. Missing applicable law, facts, tools, outputs or qualified review prevents delivery of an actual financial result.

The fictional branch stipulates an ordinary individual, one employer and complete calendar income year 2026. S is the independently established aggregate annual ordinary cash-wage income base before the annual decision's whole-krone rounding. S is already classified for the selected Svalbard wage branch. Applicable law, territorial liability, annual aggregation and the absence of any applicable youth or special treatment are premises, not inferred from a person's age, job title or address. Pension, benefits in kind, business and other income, multiple employers, mixed territories and Jan Mayen are excluded.

For these examples only, the threshold H is stipulated as NOK 1,638,588, the 12 G figure expressly reported in the tax authority's 27 May 2026 update from 1 May 2026. This is not the annual-average G multiplied by 12. The calculation is an annual reference, not a January-April correction rule, a monthly allowance or a threshold reset for each payment. Actual year-specific G timing and corrections require the principal Act and complete applicable chain.

The component rounds the complete annual S down once to whole kroner, allocates that base between the 8% and 22% bands and preserves the raw band result T. T excludes National Insurance and downstream claim, withholding, reconciliation and settlement operations. A component output is not an actual financial result.

Negative amounts, incomplete annual totals and unknown applicability are outside the illustrated branch.
4. **Round aggregate income once.** Set B = floor(S), in whole NOK, then R = S - B. Preserve S, B and R, with S = B + R and 0 <= R < 1. Do not round each receipt or payment before aggregation, use nearest or upward rounding, or apply section 4 to final T. An exact whole-krone S remains unchanged. _(L1 section 4)_
5. **Allocate the bands and compute raw T.** Set L = min(B, H) and U = max(B - H, 0). Set T = 0.08 x L + 0.22 x U. Check B = L + U, 0 <= L <= H and U >= 0. The first H remains in the low-rate band; equality creates no excess. Do not apply 22% to the full base once H is crossed, divide H by 12, reset H each payday or grant a final allowance separately to every employer. _(L1 section 3(a); L2-L3; L5)_
6. **Preserve the raw result and hold delivery.** R is discarded income precision, not a credit or refund. T is the raw wage-band component, excluding National Insurance, youth refunds, separate relief and actual deductions already made. L7 supplies separate claim-rounding guidance; its lawful application, special-rule interaction and settlement stage remain held.
