# Bangladesh Tax Overview

## Bangladesh tax system at a glance

- **Income year (tax year)** — 1 July to 30 June (banks, insurers and financial institutions use the calendar year; foreign-parent subsidiaries may follow the parent's year)  _(Income Tax Act 2023 (Bangladesh) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/bangladesh/corporate/tax-administration)_
- **Currency** — Bangladeshi Taka (BDT / Tk)  _(NBR (National Board of Revenue))_
- **Tax authority** — National Board of Revenue (NBR), under the Internal Resources Division, Ministry of Finance  _(NBR (National Board of Revenue) — https://nbr.gov.bd)_
- **Basis of taxation** — Residents are taxed on worldwide income and non-residents only on Bangladesh-source income; an individual is resident when present for 183 days or more in the income year, or for 90 days or more in the year after 365 days or more in the preceding four years  _(Income Tax Act, 2023, authentic English text published by the National Board of Revenue (SRO 404 of 16 October 2025), s 2(45), definition of 'resident' — https://nbr.gov.bd/uploads/acts/3190-SRO-404-NBR-16_October_2025(10455-10741).pdf ; scope of charge: PwC — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/bangladesh/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Headline top personal income tax rate** — 30%  _(Income Tax Act 2023 (Bangladesh), as amended by Finance Act 2024 — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/bangladesh/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **Personal tax-free threshold (general taxpayer)** — BDT 350,000 BDT (AY 2025-26 / income year 2024-25; raised to BDT 375,000 from income year 2025-26)  _(Income Tax Act 2023 (Bangladesh), as amended by Finance Act 2024 — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/bangladesh/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **Headline corporate tax rate (non-listed company)** — 27.5% (reduced to 25% where prescribed receipts/payments are made through banking channels; listed companies 20-22.5%)  _(Income Tax Act 2023 (Bangladesh), as amended by Finance Ordinance 2025 — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/bangladesh/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **VAT (value added tax)** — Yes — standard rate 15% on taxable imports and taxable supplies unless the Act provides otherwise  _(Value Added Tax and Supplementary Duty Act, 2012, NBR unofficial English translation, s 15(3) — https://nbr.gov.bd/uploads/acts/18.pdf)_
- **Individual return filing deadline (Tax Day)** — 30 November following the end of the income year (Tax Day); an individual who has never filed before has until 30 June following the income year  _(Income Tax Act, 2023, authentic English text published by the National Board of Revenue (SRO 404 of 16 October 2025), s 2(23)(a) and (c) — https://nbr.gov.bd/uploads/acts/3190-SRO-404-NBR-16_October_2025(10455-10741).pdf)_
- **Company return filing deadline** — The 15th day of the seventh month following the end of the income year, or 15 September following the income year where that 15th day would fall earlier  _(Income Tax Act, 2023, authentic English text published by the National Board of Revenue (SRO 404 of 16 October 2025), s 2(23)(b) — https://nbr.gov.bd/uploads/acts/3190-SRO-404-NBR-16_October_2025(10455-10741).pdf)_
