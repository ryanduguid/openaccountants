# Bangladesh tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the taxpayer, where income arose, residence facts and period. Check the applicable treaty and foreign-tax relief.

## Territorial reach

Residents generally account for income that arises or is received in Bangladesh or abroad. Nonresidents generally account for income that arises, is deemed to arise or is received or deemed received in Bangladesh. Check the source provisions for a Bangladesh permanent establishment and other local income. From 1 July 2026 the domestic permanent establishment definition includes certain digital or online activities or presence of a nonresident entity in Bangladesh with at least 100,000 digital or online customers or subscribers, subject to the applicable treaty provisions. [Income Tax Act 2023, ss 2(92) and 26–27](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1429.html) and [Finance Act 2026, ss 23 and 30](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1725/act-chapter-print-2816.html)

## Residence

An individual is resident for an income year if present in Bangladesh for at least 183 days that year, or for at least 90 days that year and at least 365 days in total in the 4 preceding years. A Bangladeshi company is resident, as is another company whose control and management are wholly in Bangladesh in the year. For a firm, Hindu undivided family or other association of persons, the test is wholly local control and management. A trust, fund or other juridical entity can qualify by creation under local law or wholly local control and management. [Income Tax Act 2023, s 2(45)](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1429.html)

## Taxpayer types

Individuals, companies and partnership firms are within the income tax system. A firm is a partnership within the Partnership Act. For an ordinary firm assess total income and tax at firm level, then apportion income amongst partners under the Act. Check the extended definition of company, which includes specified foreign offices and permanent establishments and certain other entities. [Income Tax Act 2023, ss 2, 18 and 187](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1429.html), [Finance Act 2026, s 23](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1725/act-chapter-print-2816.html) and [Partnership Act 1932, ss 4–6](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-157/act-chapter-print-411.html)

## Currency

The local currency is the Bangladesh taka (BDT). Income tax amounts stated in taka are domestic amounts. [Bangladesh Currency Order 1972](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-print-406.html) and [Income Tax Act 2023](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1429.html)

## Tax periods

The usual income year is 1 July to 30 June, preceding the tax year. Calendar-year treatment applies to the specified banks, insurers, finance companies and subsidiaries. Special shorter periods apply at commencement, cessation or certain partner changes. Eligible foreign-parent structures can use another 12-month period for consolidation with approval from the Deputy Commissioner of Taxes. [Income Tax Act 2023, s 2(15) and (24)](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1429.html)

## Authorities

The National Board of Revenue remains an income tax authority under the Act as amended in 2026. Assigned income tax officers administer individual cases. [Income Tax Act 2023, s 4](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1429.html), [Finance Act 2026, s 24](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-1725/act-chapter-print-2816.html) and [National Board of Revenue](https://nbr.gov.bd/)
