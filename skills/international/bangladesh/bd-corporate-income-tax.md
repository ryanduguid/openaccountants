# Bangladesh Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax rates

- **Non-publicly traded company** — 27.5% (25% if all receipts/payments above thresholds go through banking channels)  _(Income Tax Act 2023 (Bangladesh), as amended by Finance Ordinance 2025 — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/bangladesh/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Publicly traded company (IPO float > 10% of paid-up capital)** — 20% (22.5% if banking-channel conditions not met)  _(Income Tax Act 2023 (Bangladesh), as amended by Finance Ordinance 2025 — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/bangladesh/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Publicly traded company (IPO float <= 10% of paid-up capital)** — 22.5% (25% if banking-channel conditions not met)  _(Income Tax Act 2023 (Bangladesh), as amended by Finance Ordinance 2025 — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/bangladesh/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Banks, insurance and financial institutions (non-listed)** — 40% (37.5% if publicly traded) (listed rate approx — confirm)  _(Income Tax Act 2023 (Bangladesh), as amended by Finance Ordinance 2025 — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/bangladesh/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Mobile phone operators** — 45%  _(Income Tax Act 2023 (Bangladesh), as amended by Finance Ordinance 2025 — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/bangladesh/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Tobacco / cigarette manufacturers** — 45% plus 2.5% surcharge  _(Income Tax Act 2023 (Bangladesh), as amended by Finance Ordinance 2025 — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/bangladesh/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Tax base** — Resident company taxed on worldwide income; non-resident company taxed on Bangladesh-source income  _(Income Tax Act 2023 (Bangladesh) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/bangladesh/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_

## Withholding taxes and filing

- **Dividend WHT — resident company recipient** — At the rate applicable to a company, under s 117(a) of the authentic English text consolidated to October 2025; the guide's earlier source gave a flat 20%. Whether the deduction is creditable against the recipient company's own liability follows the general credit rules  _(Income Tax Act, 2023, authentic English text published by the National Board of Revenue (SRO 404 of 16 October 2025), s 117(a) — https://nbr.gov.bd/uploads/acts/3190-SRO-404-NBR-16_October_2025(10455-10741).pdf ; earlier reading of 20%: https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/bangladesh/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Dividend WHT — resident individual** — 10% where the recipient holds a Taxpayer's Identification Number and 15% where not, under s 117(b) of the authentic English text consolidated to October 2025; the guide's earlier source gave a flat 15%  _(Income Tax Act, 2023, authentic English text published by the National Board of Revenue (SRO 404 of 16 October 2025), s 117(b) — https://nbr.gov.bd/uploads/acts/3190-SRO-404-NBR-16_October_2025(10455-10741).pdf ; earlier reading of 15%: https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/bangladesh/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Dividend WHT — non-resident** — Deducted at the specified rate not exceeding 30% under s 119(1); PwC reports 20% for a company, fund or trust and 25% for any other person  _(Income Tax Act, 2023, authentic English text published by the National Board of Revenue (SRO 404 of 16 October 2025), s 119(1) — https://nbr.gov.bd/uploads/acts/3190-SRO-404-NBR-16_October_2025(10455-10741).pdf ; rates: PwC — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/bangladesh/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding from a non-resident without a PE is the final tax** — Tax deducted under s 119 is deemed the non-resident payee's minimum tax liability for that income and cannot be set off against a demand; the Board may certify a nil or reduced rate under a treaty (s 119(2))  _(Income Tax Act, 2023, authentic English text published by the National Board of Revenue (SRO 404 of 16 October 2025), s 119(3) — https://nbr.gov.bd/uploads/acts/3190-SRO-404-NBR-16_October_2025(10455-10741).pdf)_
- **Interest WHT — resident** — 10% (20% on company/trust/AoP savings and fixed deposits)  _(Income Tax Act 2023 (Bangladesh) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/bangladesh/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Interest WHT — non-resident** — 20% (subject to reduction under an applicable tax treaty)  _(Income Tax Act 2023 (Bangladesh) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/bangladesh/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Royalty WHT — resident** — 10%  _(Income Tax Act 2023 (Bangladesh) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/bangladesh/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Royalty / technical service fee WHT — non-resident** — 20% (subject to reduction under an applicable tax treaty)  _(Income Tax Act 2023 (Bangladesh) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/bangladesh/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Company return filing deadline** — Tax Day for a company is the 15th day of the seventh month following the end of the income year, or 15 September where that day would fall earlier  _(Income Tax Act, 2023, authentic English text published by the National Board of Revenue (SRO 404 of 16 October 2025), s 2(23)(b) — https://nbr.gov.bd/uploads/acts/3190-SRO-404-NBR-16_October_2025(10455-10741).pdf)_
- **Advance tax installments** — Four equal instalments of 25% on 15 September, 15 December, 15 March and 15 June of the financial year, on a minimum advance tax equal to last year's tax at current rates less tax deducted or collected at source (s 155(1) and (2))  _(Income Tax Act, 2023, authentic English text published by the National Board of Revenue (SRO 404 of 16 October 2025), ss 154 and 155 — https://nbr.gov.bd/uploads/acts/3190-SRO-404-NBR-16_October_2025(10455-10741).pdf)_
