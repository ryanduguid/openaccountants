# Switzerland Tax Overview

## The Swiss tax system at a glance

Switzerland taxes income at three levels — federal (Confederation), cantonal (26 cantons) and municipal — so the headline burden depends heavily on where a taxpayer lives or a company is based. The figures below are 2025 figures and are an unverified draft for accountant review.

- **Tax year** — Calendar year (1 January – 31 December)  _(Federal Act on Direct Federal Taxation (DBG, SR 642.11, consolidated text on Fedlex), Art. 40(1) — https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)_
- **Currency** — Swiss franc (CHF)  _(Swiss National Bank (Federal currency))_
- **Federal tax authority** — Federal Tax Administration (FTA / ESTV / AFC)  _([Federal Tax Administration](https://www.estv.admin.ch/))_
- **Cantonal/municipal taxes administered by** — 26 cantonal tax administrations (each canton and its municipalities set their own rates)  _(Federal Act on the Harmonisation of Direct Taxes of the Cantons and Communes (StHG/LHID), as reported `[RESEARCH GAP — the statute's text was not read for this change; the secondary summary that carried the figure is no longer cited]`)_
- **Residence basis** — Residents (tax domicile, or a stay of at least 30 days with gainful activity or 90 days without, Art. 3) are taxed on worldwide income except businesses, permanent establishments and real estate abroad (Art. 6(1)); non-residents are taxed on the Swiss-source items listed in Art. 4 and 5 (Art. 6(2))  _(Federal Act on Direct Federal Taxation (DBG, SR 642.11, consolidated text on Fedlex), Art. 3 and 6 — https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)_
- **Top federal personal income tax rate** — 11.5% (the single tariff reaches a flat 11.5% of the whole taxable income from CHF 794,000 under the tariff in force from 1 January 2026; cantonal and municipal tax is added on top)  _(Federal Act on Direct Federal Taxation (DBG, SR 642.11, consolidated text on Fedlex), Art. 36(1) — https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de)_
- **Headline corporate income tax (federal + cantonal/municipal, effective)** — Federal flat 8.5% on after-tax profit; combined effective rates range roughly 11.85%–21.6% by canton (national average ~14.4%) (approx — confirm)  _(Federal Act on Direct Federal Taxation (DBG/LIFD), as reported `[RESEARCH GAP — the statute's text was not read for this change; the secondary summary that carried the figure is no longer cited]`)_
- **Does VAT exist?** — Yes — VAT (MWST/TVA/IVA) at a standard rate of 8.1%, a reduced rate of 2.6% and a special rate of 3.8% on accommodation (Art. 25)  _(Federal Act on Value Added Tax (MWSTG, SR 641.20, English translation on Fedlex), Art. 25 — https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/2009/615/en ; https://www.estv.admin.ch/en/vat-rates-switzerland)_
- **Net wealth tax** — Levied at cantonal/municipal level on worldwide net assets of residents (no federal wealth tax)  _([Federal Act on the Harmonisation of Direct Taxes of the Cantons and Communes (StHG/LHID)]( (as reported)))_
- **Individual return filing deadline (ordinary)** — Generally 31 March of the year following the tax year (cantonal deadlines vary; extensions available) (approx — confirm cantonal deadline)  _(Federal Act on Direct Federal Taxation (DBG/LIFD), as reported `[RESEARCH GAP — the statute's text was not read for this change; the secondary summary that carried the figure is no longer cited]`)_
