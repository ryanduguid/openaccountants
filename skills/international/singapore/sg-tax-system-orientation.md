# Singapore tax system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the taxpayer, the source of each amount, when any foreign income is received in Singapore, the year of assessment and the basis period. Check income tax, GST, customs and CPF obligations separately.

## Territorial reach

The ordinary income tax charge includes income accruing in or derived from Singapore, foreign income received in Singapore and amounts the Act deems chargeable. Receipt under s 10(25) can include remittance, satisfying a debt of a Singapore trade or business or buying movable property brought into Singapore. IRAS applies the receipt rule to foreign income of resident individuals and entities located in Singapore. For a foreign business, establish whether it operates in or from Singapore. Check exemptions before computing the tax. For example, s 13(7A)(b) can exempt foreign income received by a resident individual outside a Singapore partnership where the Comptroller is satisfied the exemption is beneficial. Sections 13(8) and 13(9) set separate conditions for relief on specified foreign dividends, branch profits and service income. Check deemed source, any treaty and any special foreign disposal gain under s 10L separately. [Income Tax Act 1947, s 2A](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/ITA1947) · [Income Tax Act 1947, s 10](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/ITA1947?ProvIds=pr10-) · [IRAS, foreign income received in Singapore](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/corporate-income-tax/income-deductions-for-companies/taxable-non-taxable-income) · [IRAS, foreign-sourced income exemption](https://www.iras.gov.sg/media/docs/default-source/e-tax/tax-exemption-for-foreign-sourced-income285e5d43d6af4001b3ce35da1e9005b5.pdf)

## Residence

For an individual, check residence in Singapore in the year before the year of assessment, allowing reasonable temporary absences consistent with residence. The definition includes someone physically present for at least 183 days or working in Singapore for at least 183 days in that year, with work as a company director excluded from the employment limb. Check the IRAS concessions for qualifying stays or employment spanning years, including the exclusions from the 2-year employment concession. A company or body of persons is resident when control and management of its business is exercised in Singapore. For a company, locate the actual strategic decision-making and check any applicable treaty. [Income Tax Act 1947, s 2](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/ITA1947) · [IRAS, individual residence](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/individual-income-tax/basics-of-individual-income-tax/tax-residency-and-tax-rates/working-out-my-tax-residency) · [IRAS, company residence](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/corporate-income-tax/basics-of-corporate-income-tax/tax-residency-of-a-company-certificate-of-residence)

## Taxpayer types

A sole proprietor reports business income in their individual return. A company computes its own chargeable income. For an ordinary trading partnership, the precedent partner files Form P and each partner includes their share in their own return. Check special rules for the particular partnership type. For an ordinary trust, identify what income the trustee derives and which beneficiaries are entitled. A corresponding share of trustee income can be taxed at a lower rate or left untaxed at trustee level under s 43(2), subject to proof and the exceptions. Check any separate beneficiary liability. [Income Tax Act 1947, s 36](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/ITA1947?ProvIds=pr36-) · [Income Tax Act 1947, s 43](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/ITA1947?ProvIds=pr43-) · [IRAS, business income](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/individual-income-tax/self-employed-and-partnerships/calculating-my-business-income/calculating-business-income)

## Currency

The Singapore dollar (SGD or S$) is the monetary unit. Singapore transactions default to SGD unless the law provides otherwise or the parties validly agree to another currency. A company with financial statements in another functional currency prepares its income tax computation in that currency and translates the applicable amounts for reporting and tax payable in SGD. Use the IRAS rate and method for the amount and period. [Currency Act 1967, ss 11 and 12](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/CA1967?ProvIds=P13-) · [IRAS, corporate tax computation](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/corporate-income-tax/form-c-s-form-c-s-%28lite%29-form-c-filing/preparing-a-tax-computation)

## Assessment periods

The year of assessment (YA) generally draws on income from the preceding calendar year. If accounts are normally closed on another date, the Comptroller may direct the use of the year ending on that date in the preceding calendar year. For a taxpayer other than an individual, this covers statutory income from all sources. For a partnership, use the separate rule with section 36. For an individual, the direction covers the trade, business, profession or vocation to which the accounts relate. Check commencement, cessation, a change of year-end and any specific direction separately. [Income Tax Act 1947, s 35](https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/ITA1947?ProvIds=pr35-)

IRAS assesses an investment holding company's investment income on its financial-year basis. An established investment holding company with a normal 31 March 2025 year-end uses 1 April 2024 to 31 March 2025 for YA 2026 on that basis. Record both dates and the YA. [IRAS, investment holding companies](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/corporate-income-tax/specific-industries/investment-holding-companies/) · [IRAS, investment holding company computation, note 1](https://www.iras.gov.sg/docs/default-source/uploadedfiles/pdf/basic-format-of-tax-computation-for-an-investment-holding-company.pdf)

## Responsible authorities

The Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore (IRAS) administers income tax and domestic GST. Singapore Customs handles imports and collects applicable duty and import GST. Identify any separate employer and CPF work before setting the filing checklist. [IRAS, the Singapore tax system](https://www.iras.gov.sg/who-we-are/what-we-do/taxes-in-singapore/the-singapore-tax-system/) · [Singapore Customs, paying duty and GST](https://www.customs.gov.sg/doing-business/import-operations/import-procedures/paying-duty-and-gst/)

## Fictional intake

A Singapore-managed company closes its accounts on 30 June 2026, has a foreign branch and prepares its accounts in US dollars. Record who made its strategic decisions and where. Match that year-end to YA 2027, trace when foreign branch profits are received in Singapore and check the statutory exemption conditions. Retain the functional currency computation and the SGD conversion used for the return.
