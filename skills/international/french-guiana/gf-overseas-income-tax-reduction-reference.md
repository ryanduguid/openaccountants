## Intake and original unrounded component mechanics

Reuse redacted records before grouped questions. Establish the requested income year and obligation, applicable source chain, filing unit and legal decote category, full-year domicile, all income categories and special regimes, complete upstream tax computation, prior relief, currency and precision, downstream adjustments, withholding, prepayments, reviewer and required tools. Present the inferred profile and wait for confirmation. Keep tax identifiers, bank details and signatures with the professional. Missing law requires primary evidence rather than a taxpayer's guess.

1. **Establish the stage.** Let G be the independently established ordinary progressive income tax amount entering the DOM reduction, after family quotient capping and all applicable complementary family quotient relief. L1's calculation sheet shows I2 = I1 - H where complementary relief H applies. Establish that H is already applied or independently nil; never default unknown H to zero. A raw post-cap I1, post-decote amount, final tax, taxable income or payroll deduction is not an interchangeable input. _(L1, physical page 382)_
2. **Validate the hypothetical input.** These fixtures accept a non-negative finite base-10 EUR amount expressed in whole euros or cents. Preserve its exact decimal value. Reject negative, missing, non-finite, malformed, mixed-currency or ambiguous amounts; hold unsupported precision for reconciliation rather than silently rounding it. This is the fixture input contract, not a rule that every legally valid intermediate must have 2 decimal places.
3. **Calculate the uncapped amount.** U = 0.40 x G. The rate is 40% of the established tax amount, not 40% of wages or taxable income. _(L1, physical pages 62 and 373; L2, paragraph 40; L4, I.3)_
4. **Apply the cap once.** D = min(U, EUR 4,050). The cap limits the reduction, not G or the remaining tax. This component neither multiplies the cap by household parts nor treats it as a personal allowance. _(L1, physical pages 62 and 373; L2, paragraph 40; L4, I.3)_
5. **Reconcile the remainder.** P = G - D, so G = D + P. P corresponds to the amount after the DOM reduction and before decote, shown as I3 on L1's calculation sheet. Neither D nor P is the final tax, a refund, withholding credit or payment instruction. _(L1, physical page 382)_

L1 physical page 373 states nearest-euro rounding for the taxable base and tax, with fractions below EUR 0.50 discarded and EUR 0.50 or above counted as another euro, citing CGI articles 193 and 1657-1. The original G, U, D and P fixtures below preserve exact arithmetic before statutory rounding; a fractional fixture is not established as a permissible actual return intermediate. Do not feed their exact D or P into the separate rounded calculation. L7-L9 now support that bounded rounded stage, whose GR input must first be independently established with lawful upstream precision. Do not infer a complete base computation, round arbitrary intermediate products, truncate fractions or use banker's rounding.

## Separate rounded DOM and decote component

This fictional branch uses **GR**, a separately established, lawfully rounded non-negative whole-EUR ordinary progressive tax amount after family quotient capping and every applicable complementary relief H, before DOM relief. GR is not a renamed fractional G or unadjusted I1. All preceding law, facts and precision are stipulated as independently established for these checks; this guide does not calculate them. The fixture input is an ungrouped base-10 whole-EUR numeral. Hold fractional, negative, missing, non-finite, malformed, mixed-currency or wrong-stage inputs rather than coercing them.

Establish the legal assessment category independently: **S** denotes the applicable single, divorced or widowed category, with K = EUR 897; **J** denotes joint assessment, with K = EUR 1,483. Neither a marital label nor the number of household parts chooses K. L8 III.50-70 also applies the relevant S ceiling to separate spouse/PACS assessments, including a qualifying election in the marriage/PACS year, and to widows retaining the conjugal quotient. Death-year pre-death joint and post-death widow periods require separate treatment and are outside this full-year fixture branch. No category is a default. _(L4 I.4.a; L5 I.B.3 and II.A; L8 I and III)_

For non-negative amounts define **R(x)** as nearest whole EUR, with exactly EUR 0.50 rounded upwards. Apply this sequence once:

1. **Rounded DOM relief:** A = R(min(0.40 x GR, EUR 4,050)). Round the capped reduction as a whole modifying element. _(L4 I.3; L7 IV.50; L9 paragraph 1)_
2. **Tax entering decote:** I = GR - A. Exclude proportional-rate tax; do not add later reductions or credits. _(L1 physical page 382; L8 I.10-30)_
3. **Rounded decote:** if I < EUR 1,982 for S, or I < EUR 3,277 for J, set Q = min(I, R(max(0, K - 0.4525 x I))); otherwise Q = 0. Round the **whole difference** K - 0.4525 x I, not the percentage product first. Limit Q to I so relief cannot create a negative balance or refund. _(L4 I.4.a; L7 IV.50; L8 I, opening paragraph, and II.40; L1 physical page 382)_
4. **Remainder:** B = I - Q, so GR = A + Q + B and I = Q + B. Whole-EUR I and B follow from subtraction; no extra rounding operation is needed. B is before later reductions, credits, withholding, prepayments and collection rules. It is not final liability, a filing entry or an amount to pay. _(L4 I.5; L8 I.20-30)_
