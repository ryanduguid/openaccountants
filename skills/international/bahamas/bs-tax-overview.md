# Bahamas Tax Overview

## The Bahamian tax system at a glance

The Bahamas raises revenue through VAT, customs and excise duties, Business Licence tax, Real Property Tax, stamp duty and the Domestic Minimum Top-Up Tax (DMTT). [2026-27 Budget Communication](https://bahamasbudget.gov.bs/media/filer_public/81/9d/819d8c82-6e4c-471e-bf84-2e0965620327/final_budget_communication_fiscal_year_20262027-_a_budget_that_build_on_progress_.pdf)

- **Standard tax/assessment year** — Calendar year (1 January to 31 December) (approx — confirm)  _(Business Licence Act, 2023)_
- **Currency** — Bahamian dollar (BSD), pegged 1:1 to the US dollar  _(Central Bank of The Bahamas Act)_
- **Principal tax authority** — Department of Inland Revenue (DIR), Ministry of Finance  _([Department of Inland Revenue](https://inlandrevenue.finance.gov.bs/))_
- **Domestic personal income tax** — Inapplicable. The Ministry of Finance confirms that The Bahamas has no domestic personal income tax regime. The `bs-income-tax` guide gives the period and filing scope. [Bahamas–Japan MAP guidance, ‘Scope of the MAP “Limitations”’](https://cdn.bahamas.gov.bs/tenant/tenantministryoffinance/documents/All%20Documents/BAHAMAS-JAPANMAPGUIDANCEDOCUMENT-20240718120503.pdf)
- **Domestic Minimum Top-Up Tax.** The DMTT applies to Bahamian constituent entities of in-scope multinational groups for fiscal years starting in 2025 or later, with a separate commencement condition for fiscal years starting in 2024. Check group scope and the fiscal year under the Act. [Domestic Minimum Top-Up Tax Act, 2024, ss 1, 4 and 5](https://laws.bahamas.gov.bs/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/2024/2024-0058/2024-0058_1.pdf)
- **Does a VAT/GST exist?** — Yes — Value Added Tax at a standard rate of 10%  _(Value Added Tax Act, 2014, as reported `[RESEARCH GAP — the statute's text was not read for this change; the secondary summary that carried the figure is no longer cited]`)_
- **Capital gains tax** — None — there is no capital gains tax  _(a secondary summary (link removed))_
- **Inheritance / estate / gift tax** — None — there is no inheritance, estate, or gift tax  _(a secondary summary (link removed))_
- **Business licence renewal / return filing deadline** — Renew by 31 January; business licence tax payable by 31 March each year  _([Business Licence Act, 2023](https://www.mof.gov.bs/about-business-licence-and-real-property-tax))_
- **National Insurance remittance.** Employers generally pay both shares and file the monthly contribution statement by the 15th of the month following the month wages are paid. See `bs-payroll-social` for contribution classes, ceilings and exceptions. [Contributions Regulations, reg 26](https://laws.bahamas.gov.bs/cms/images/LEGISLATION/SUBORDINATE/1984/1984-0068/1984-0068_2.pdf)
