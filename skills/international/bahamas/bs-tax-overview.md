# Bahamas Tax Overview

## The Bahamian tax system at a glance

The Department of Inland Revenue (DIR) administers VAT, Business Licence and Real Property Tax. Customs handles duties on imported goods. The Financial Secretary administers the Domestic Minimum Top-Up Tax (DMTT), and the National Insurance Board administers contributions. The Bahamian dollar (BSD) has fixed parity with the US dollar. [DIR](https://inlandrevenue.finance.gov.bs/) · [Bahamas Customs](https://www.bahamascustoms.gov.bs/) · [Central Bank monetary policy](https://www.centralbankbahamas.com/monetary-policy)

The filing period follows the charge. Business Licence renewals track the calendar year. DMTT follows the multinational group's fiscal year. VAT periods follow the applicable registration and filing rules in the [VAT guide](bahamas-vat.md).

- **Personal income.** The Ministry of Finance confirms that The Bahamas has no domestic personal income tax regime. The [income tax guide](bs-income-tax.md) records the sources and scope checked through 10 November 2026. Check foreign filing and residence rules in the other jurisdiction when relevant. [Bahamas–Japan MAP guidance, ‘Scope of the MAP “Limitations”’](https://cdn.bahamas.gov.bs/tenant/tenantministryoffinance/documents/All%20Documents/BAHAMAS-JAPANMAPGUIDANCEDOCUMENT-20240718120503.pdf)
- **Business Licence.** An annual licence expires on 31 December. Section 26 sets 31 January for renewal applications and 31 March for the prior-year turnover return and unpaid prior-year tax. Estimated tax follows the separate section 35 payment rules, including approved instalments. Section 26(4) exempts licensees who have made a qualifying inactivation or cessation notification from specified obligations, with the prior-year turnover return still due. From 1 July 2026 the amendment adds a 1.5% rate on turnover for businesses above BSD 175 million under the general Part I schedule. Check the separate schedules for special business classes. [Business Licence Act 2023 ss 26 and 35](https://inlandrevenue.finance.gov.bs/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Business-Licence-Act-2023.pdf) · [Act 22 of 2026 ss 1 and 8](https://laws.bahamas.gov.bs/cms/images/LEGISLATION/AMENDING/2026/2026-0022A/2026-0022A.pdf)
- **Value added tax.** The standard rate is 10%. Since 1 April 2026, the Second Schedule exempts the wholesale and retail supply of listed goods sold unprepared by a qualifying food store and specified imports of those goods by a qualifying food store. Check the statutory goods list and food-store definition. From 1 July 2026, the government entity paying under a government supply contract withholds and remits the full VAT under section 23A(11)–(12). The July amending Act separately requires an appointed date for section 4's transit measures. [Act 3 of 2025 s 3](https://laws.bahamas.gov.bs/cms/images/LEGISLATION/AMENDING/2025/2025-0003A/2025-0003A.pdf) · [Act 4 of 2026 ss 1–3](https://laws.bahamas.gov.bs/cms/images/LEGISLATION/AMENDING/2026/2026-0004A/2026-0004A.pdf) · [Act 19 of 2026 ss 1–4](https://laws.bahamas.gov.bs/cms/images/LEGISLATION/AMENDING/2026/2026-0019A/2026-0019A.pdf)
- **Employment and property.** Employers generally remit both National Insurance shares and the monthly statement by the 15th of the month after payment of wages. Use the [payroll guide](bs-payroll-social.md) for contribution classes, ceilings and exceptions. DIR administers the separate recurrent property tax. For disposals, gifts and estates, check the specific instrument, taxpayer and property charges. For registration and operating approvals, see the [formation guide](bs-company-formation.md). [National Insurance Contributions Regulations reg 26](https://laws.bahamas.gov.bs/cms/images/LEGISLATION/SUBORDINATE/1984/1984-0068/1984-0068_2.pdf) · [DIR real property tax](https://inlandrevenue.finance.gov.bs/real-property-tax/)

## Domestic minimum top-up tax

Start with the group's scope under the incorporated GloBE Model Rules. Article 1.1 generally tests consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750 million in at least 2 of the 4 fiscal years immediately preceding the tested year. The tax applies to Bahamian constituent entities of an in-scope multinational group, with joint and several liability and a jurisdictional top-up calculation under section 5. Section 1 deems the Act in force from 1 January 2024. For a fiscal year beginning in 2024 the law applies only if an income inclusion rule (IIR) or undertaxed profits rule (UTPR) must be applied for that year in respect of each constituent entity located in The Bahamas. For later years apply the Act and incorporated rules without that transitional condition. [Act 58 of 2024 ss 1, 4 and 5](https://laws.bahamas.gov.bs/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/2024/2024-0058/2024-0058_1.pdf) · [OECD GloBE Model Rules art 1.1](https://doi.org/10.1787/782bac33-en)

For this regime, section 2(3) deems an entity other than a flow-through entity resident if formed or effectively managed in The Bahamas. Apply the GloBE entity-location rules and test group scope before concluding that the entity has a filing or payment obligation. From 1 July 2026, an entity claiming dual residence and location outside The Bahamas under GloBE article 10.3.4 must notify the Financial Secretary and provide the evidence the Secretary requires. [Act 58 of 2024 s 2](https://laws.bahamas.gov.bs/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/2024/2024-0058/2024-0058_1.pdf) · [Act 25 of 2026 ss 1 and 2](https://laws.bahamas.gov.bs/cms/images/LEGISLATION/AMENDING/2026/2026-0025A/2026-0025A.pdf)

From 1 July 2026, amended section 6 requires an in-scope Bahamian entity to file or have filed on its behalf a GloBE Information Return with applicable notifications, or a prescribed return, even if no tax is payable. Section 7(1) gives an 18-month payment period after fiscal year-end in the Bahamian transition year and 15 months otherwise. Amended section 7(2) specifies US or Bahamian dollars for taxes, penalties and interest. New section 7A requires prescribed instalments for entities liable to pay DMTT in the immediately preceding fiscal year. Confirm the prescribed periods and amounts before remitting. The DIR's pre-registration dates are distinct from statutory filing and payment dates. [Act 58 of 2024 s 7](https://laws.bahamas.gov.bs/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/2024/2024-0058/2024-0058_1.pdf) · [Act 25 of 2026 ss 1–4](https://laws.bahamas.gov.bs/cms/images/LEGISLATION/AMENDING/2026/2026-0025A/2026-0025A.pdf) · [DIR DMTT administration](https://inlandrevenue.finance.gov.bs/dmtt/) · [Corporate tax guide](bs-corporate-income-tax.md)
