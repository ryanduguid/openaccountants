# Barbados Corporate Income Tax

## Corporation tax rates and base

Barbados moved to a 9% general corporation tax rate from 1 January 2024, with reduced rates for approved small businesses, shipping, insurance and qualifying IP income. A 15% Qualified Domestic Minimum Top-Up Tax applies to large in-scope MNE groups.

- **General corporation tax rate** — 9% of taxable income, with effect from income year 2024  _(Income Tax (Amendment and Validation) Act, 2024-15 (Barbados Revenue Authority copy), s 6, inserting s 43(8) — https://bra.gov.bb/attachment?file=Attachments%2fIncome+Tax+%28Amendment+and+Validation%29+Act%2c+2024-15.pdf&name=Income+Tax+%28Amendment+and+Validation%29+Act%2c+2024-15)_
- **Approved small business rate** — 5.5% of taxable income for a company with gross income of BBD 2,000,000 or less that is registered as an approved small business under the Small Business Development Act, Cap. 318C  _(Income Tax (Amendment and Validation) Act, 2024-15 (Barbados Revenue Authority copy), s 6, inserting s 43(9)(a) — https://bra.gov.bb/attachment?file=Attachments%2fIncome+Tax+%28Amendment+and+Validation%29+Act%2c+2024-15.pdf&name=Income+Tax+%28Amendment+and+Validation%29+Act%2c+2024-15)_
- **International shipping companies** — Tiered: 5.5% on taxable income up to BBD 1,000,000; 3% on the next BBD 19,000,000; 2.5% on the next BBD 10,000,000; 1% above BBD 30,000,000  _(Income Tax (Amendment and Validation) Act, 2024-15 (Barbados Revenue Authority copy), s 6, inserting s 43(9)(c) — https://bra.gov.bb/attachment?file=Attachments%2fIncome+Tax+%28Amendment+and+Validation%29+Act%2c+2024-15.pdf&name=Income+Tax+%28Amendment+and+Validation%29+Act%2c+2024-15)_
- **Insurance companies (Class 1, 2, 3)** — 0% for class 1 insurance business and 2% for class 2 and class 3 business, unchanged by the 2024 reform  _(Barbados Revenue Authority, Income Tax: Corporations — https://bra.gov.bb/About/Tax-Types/Income-Tax/Corporations ; Ministry of Finance, Corporation Tax Reform 2024 (appendix tabled in Parliament) — https://www.barbadosparliament.com/uploads/sittings/attachments/8548dbe944a97cd2acfe22f78eade9f5.pdf)_
- **Qualifying intellectual property income** — 4.5% by election on income from qualifying intellectual property computed under s 22A (a nexus approach with a 30% uplift of qualifying expenditure), from income year 2024  _(Income Tax (Amendment and Validation) Act, 2024-15 (Barbados Revenue Authority copy), ss 3 and 7, inserting ss 22A and 43B — https://bra.gov.bb/attachment?file=Attachments%2fIncome+Tax+%28Amendment+and+Validation%29+Act%2c+2024-15.pdf&name=Income+Tax+%28Amendment+and+Validation%29+Act%2c+2024-15)_
- **Qualified Domestic Minimum Top-Up Tax (Pillar Two)** — 15% top-up tax on in-scope resident members of MNE groups with consolidated revenue of EUR 750 million or more  _([Corporation Top-up Tax Act, 2024-16](https://bra.gov.bb/About/Legislation-Regulations/Corporation-Top-up-Tax-Act-2024-16))_
- **Tax base** — Resident companies (managed and controlled in Barbados) are taxed on worldwide income; non-resident companies on Barbados-source income only  _(Barbados Revenue Authority, Income Tax: Corporations — https://bra.gov.bb/About/Tax-Types/Income-Tax/Corporations)_
- **WHT on dividends (resident recipients)** — 0% to resident companies; 15% to resident individuals (raised from 12.5% by the 2020 amendment)  _(Income Tax (Amendment) Act, 2020-10 (Barbados Revenue Authority copy), s 9(a), amending s 65(4A) — https://bra.gov.bb/attachment?file=Attachments%2fIncome+Tax+%28Amendment+%29+Act%2c+2020-10.pdf&name=Income+Tax+%28Amendment%29+Act%2c+2020-10 ; PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries, Withholding taxes — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/barbados/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **WHT on dividends (non-resident recipients)** — **0% or 5%**: 0% where the dividend is paid out of income earned from sources outside Barbados, 5% otherwise, before treaty relief. Grandfathered international business companies and ISRLs are exempt from withholding on payments to non-residents  _([Income Tax Act, Cap. 73](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/barbados/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **WHT on interest** — 15% to resident individuals (0% for pensioners aged 60 and over); 0% domestic rate to non-residents before treaty relief  _(Income Tax (Amendment) Act, 2020-10 (Barbados Revenue Authority copy), s 8, amending s 64A — https://bra.gov.bb/attachment?file=Attachments%2fIncome+Tax+%28Amendment+%29+Act%2c+2020-10.pdf&name=Income+Tax+%28Amendment%29+Act%2c+2020-10 ; PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries, Withholding taxes — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/barbados/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **WHT on royalties (non-residents)** — **0%** domestic rate before treaty relief, which is what PwC publishes. **Sources conflict and the payer carries the risk:** older summaries and some secondary pages still give 15%, the rate before the 2019 convergence of the domestic and international regimes. Confirm with the Barbados Revenue Authority before paying a royalty gross, because an under-deduction is the payer's liability, not the recipient's  _([Income Tax Act, Cap. 73](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/barbados/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **Corporate income tax prepayments** — From income year 2025, all companies (except approved small businesses) prepay monthly 1/12 of the tax payable on taxable income of the income year before the preceding year, due by the 15th of each month  _([Income Tax Act, Cap. 73](https://bra.gov.bb/Popular-Topics/Companies/Filing-and-Payment))_
- **Corporation tax return deadline** — Generally within 3-6 months of fiscal year end; BRA set 31 July 2025 for income year 2024 returns (deadlines subject to BRA extension notices — confirm current year)  _([Income Tax Act, Cap. 73](https://bra.gov.bb/Popular-Topics/Companies/Filing-and-Payment))_
