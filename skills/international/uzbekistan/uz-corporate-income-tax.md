# Uzbekistan Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax rates, base and filing (2025)

Corporate income tax (CIT) in Uzbekistan is charged at a standard 15% rate, with a 20% rate for certain sectors and a simplified turnover-tax regime for smaller businesses. Withholding taxes apply to outbound payments to non-residents. Verify figures with a licensed accountant.

- **Standard CIT rate** — 15% for taxpayers not listed in the special rows of art. 337 (row 12); dividend income of resident legal entities is taxed at 5% (row 11)  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan (lex.uz consolidated text in Uzbek Cyrillic, amendments in force to 25 July 2026), art. 337, rows 11 and 12 — https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674902)_
- **Elevated CIT rate (specified sectors)** — 20% for banks, producers of polyethylene granules, taxpayers whose main activity is mobile communication services, and profit from services in markets and trade complexes; the cement (clinker) producers line carried by this guide until October 2026 does not appear in the current table  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan (lex.uz consolidated text in Uzbek Cyrillic, amendments in force to 25 July 2026), art. 337, rows 1, 2 and 7 — https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674902)_
- **Preferential CIT for textile/footwear/leather** — 1% CIT for qualifying textile, footwear and leather industry enterprises meeting social conditions, 1 January 2025 to 1 January 2028 percent (conditional on meeting social conditions)  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan — https://www.ey.com/en_uz/technical/tax-alerts/2025/01/changes-in-taxation-in-uzbekistan)_
- **Turnover tax (simplified regime) threshold** — Art. 461(1) defines turnover tax payers as Uzbek legal entities whose total income in the tax period does not exceed **UZS 1 billion**, and individual entrepreneurs and self-employed persons whose income from sales does not exceed the same sum. PwC reports a change to **12,000 BCU** from **1 June 2026**; that figure does not appear in the Code edition with amendments in force to 25 July 2026, so treat it as unconfirmed until the amending act is located  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan (lex.uz consolidated text in Uzbek Cyrillic, amendments in force to 25 July 2026), art. 461(1), as amended by Law ЎРҚ-1108 of 25 December 2025 in force from 1 January 2026 — https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674902)_
- **What 12,000 BCU is worth, and when it changes** — The BCU was **UZS 412,000** from 1 August 2025, making the threshold **UZS 4.944 billion** when it took effect on 1 June 2026. The BCU rose to **UZS 440,000** from **1 September 2026**, taking the same 12,000 BCU to **UZS 5.28 billion**. The threshold therefore moved twice in 2026: once by rule change in June, once by indexation in September. Never carry a sum-denominated figure across a BCU change — recompute it (approx — sources give the 412,000 period as running to 31 July 2026 and the 440,000 figure as applying from 1 September 2026, which leaves August 2026 unstated; confirm that month before relying on a threshold for it)  _(Base calculation unit (bazaviy hisoblash miqdori) schedule; [Tax Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan, official text, lex.uz](https://lex.uz/en/docs/4674902))_
- **Where to confirm the threshold, and what is still unconfirmed** — The Code text at lex.uz (edition with amendments in force to 25 July 2026) keeps UZS 1 billion in art. 461(1) and sets the transition rules in art. 462; the 12,000 BCU figure and the 1 June 2026 date in the next line come from PwC and are not in that text  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan (lex.uz consolidated text in Uzbek Cyrillic, amendments in force to 25 July 2026), arts. 461 and 462 — https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674902)_
- **Turnover tax general rate** — 4% for taxpayers in all sectors unless a special row applies (row 1); retail trade 4% in cities with 100,000 or more inhabitants, 2% in other localities and 1% in remote and mountain districts (row 2.1), with 4% on tobacco sales wherever located (row 2.2); procurement, sorting, storage and packing of agricultural produce 4% of turnover or 25% of gross income (row 3); 0% for the listed associations of people with disabilities (row 4); individual entrepreneurs and the self-employed have their own row 5  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan (lex.uz consolidated text in Uzbek Cyrillic, amendments in force to 25 July 2026), art. 467 — https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674902)_
- **50% rate reduction on first crossing UZS 10 billion** — A taxpayer whose revenue **first** exceeds **UZS 10 billion** after **1 September 2022** pays CIT at a **50% reduced rate** for the period of the excess and the following period, provided revenue stays under **UZS 100 billion**. It is a one-time transitional relief keyed to the first crossing, not an annual test  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-uzbekistan/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **One-year CIT exemption on moving off the turnover tax** — From **2026**, a taxpayer newly transitioning from the turnover tax to CIT is exempt from CIT for one year, **except on dividends and interest**. Those two heads stay taxable  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-uzbekistan/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **CIT tax base** — Taxable profit = total income less deductible expenses, computed for residents on worldwide income and for non-residents on Uzbek-source income / PE profit  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-uzbekistan/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Export 0% CIT rate** — Abolished from 1 January 2025; export income now included in the standard tax base  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-uzbekistan/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends (non-residents)** — 10% (treaty relief may reduce; JSC-share dividends currently exempt to 31 Dec 2028)  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan (lex.uz consolidated text in Uzbek Cyrillic, amendments in force to 25 July 2026), art. 353, row 1 — https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674902 ; JSC-share dividend exemption: PwC — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-uzbekistan/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on interest (non-residents)** — 10% (treaty relief may reduce)  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan (lex.uz consolidated text in Uzbek Cyrillic, amendments in force to 25 July 2026), art. 353, row 1 — https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674902)_
- **Withholding tax on royalties (non-residents)** — 20%, as 'other income' not listed in rows 1 to 5 (treaty relief may reduce)  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan (lex.uz consolidated text in Uzbek Cyrillic, amendments in force to 25 July 2026), art. 353, row 6 — https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674902)_
- **Withholding tax on freight (non-residents)** — 6% on income from international transport (freight) and international telecommunications  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan (lex.uz consolidated text in Uzbek Cyrillic, amendments in force to 25 July 2026), art. 353, row 3 — https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674902)_
- **Double tax treaty network** — Uzbekistan has double tax treaties with 55 countries that may reduce withholding rates (requires tax-residency certificate to claim treaty relief)  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-uzbekistan/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Annual CIT return deadline** — 1 March following the tax period  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan (lex.uz consolidated text in Uzbek Cyrillic, amendments in force to 25 July 2026), art. 339 — https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674902)_
- **Quarterly CIT reports** — Due by the 20th of the month following the reporting period; the tax is paid by the reporting deadline  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan (lex.uz consolidated text in Uzbek Cyrillic, amendments in force to 25 July 2026), arts. 339 and 340 — https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674902)_
- **Monthly advance payments** — Taxpayers whose total income for the previous tax period exceeded UZS 20 billion pay monthly advance payments by the 23rd of each month of the reporting period  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan (lex.uz consolidated text in Uzbek Cyrillic, amendments in force to 25 July 2026), art. 340 — https://lex.uz/ru/docs/4674902)_
