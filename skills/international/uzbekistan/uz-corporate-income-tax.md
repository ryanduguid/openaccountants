# Uzbekistan Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax rates, base and filing (2025)

Corporate income tax (CIT) in Uzbekistan is charged at a standard 15% rate, with a 20% rate for certain sectors and a simplified turnover-tax regime for smaller businesses. Withholding taxes apply to outbound payments to non-residents. Verify figures with a licensed accountant.

- **Standard CIT rate** — 15% percent  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-uzbekistan/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Elevated CIT rate (specified sectors)** — 20% for commercial banks, cement (clinker) producers, polyethylene granule producers, mobile service providers, and markets/shopping malls percent  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-uzbekistan/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Preferential CIT for textile/footwear/leather** — 1% CIT for qualifying textile, footwear and leather industry enterprises meeting social conditions, 1 January 2025 to 1 January 2028 percent (conditional on meeting social conditions)  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan — https://www.ey.com/en_uz/technical/tax-alerts/2025/01/changes-in-taxation-in-uzbekistan)_
- **Turnover tax (simplified regime) threshold — changed on 1 June 2026, and it is no longer a fixed sum** — The threshold for mandatory transition from the simplified regime to the general regime was **UZS 1 billion** until **31 May 2026**, and from **1 June 2026** is **12,000 BCU** (base calculation units). Two consequences follow and the guide carried neither. A 2026 assessment straddles the change, so the month matters. And a BCU multiple **moves when the BCU moves, with no change in the law at all** — see the next line  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-uzbekistan/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **What 12,000 BCU is worth, and when it changes** — The BCU was **UZS 412,000** from 1 August 2025, making the threshold **UZS 4.944 billion** when it took effect on 1 June 2026. The BCU rose to **UZS 440,000** from **1 September 2026**, taking the same 12,000 BCU to **UZS 5.28 billion**. The threshold therefore moved twice in 2026: once by rule change in June, once by indexation in September. Never carry a sum-denominated figure across a BCU change — recompute it ((approx — sources give the 412,000 period as running to 31 July 2026 and the 440,000 figure as applying from 1 September 2026, which leaves August 2026 unstated; confirm that month before relying on a threshold for it))  _(Base calculation unit (bazaviy hisoblash miqdori) schedule; [Tax Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan, official text, lex.uz](https://lex.uz/en/docs/4674902))_
- **Where to confirm the threshold, and what is still unconfirmed** — The official text of the Tax Code is published at [lex.uz](https://lex.uz/en/docs/4674902). The 12,000 BCU figure and the 1 June 2026 date above come from PwC, **not** from the Code text, and the simplified-regime article was not located in the extract checked. Confirm both against the Code's special-regimes section before relying on them. This line exists because a guide that cites an authority has not thereby taken its figures from one  _([Tax Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan, lex.uz](https://lex.uz/en/docs/4674902))_
- **Turnover tax general rate** — 4%, varying from **0%** to 25% by type of entity and services provided. The floor is 0%, not 1% percent (rate depends on activity; confirm the applicable category)  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-uzbekistan/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **50% rate reduction on first crossing UZS 10 billion** — A taxpayer whose revenue **first** exceeds **UZS 10 billion** after **1 September 2022** pays CIT at a **50% reduced rate** for the period of the excess and the following period, provided revenue stays under **UZS 100 billion**. It is a one-time transitional relief keyed to the first crossing, not an annual test  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-uzbekistan/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **One-year CIT exemption on moving off the turnover tax** — From **2026**, a taxpayer newly transitioning from the turnover tax to CIT is exempt from CIT for one year, **except on dividends and interest**. Those two heads stay taxable  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-uzbekistan/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **CIT tax base** — Taxable profit = total income less deductible expenses, computed for residents on worldwide income and for non-residents on Uzbek-source income / PE profit  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-uzbekistan/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Export 0% CIT rate** — Abolished from 1 January 2025; export income now included in the standard tax base  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-uzbekistan/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends (non-residents)** — 10% percent (treaty relief may reduce; JSC-share dividends currently exempt to 31 Dec 2028)  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-uzbekistan/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on interest (non-residents)** — 10% percent (treaty relief may reduce)  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-uzbekistan/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on royalties (non-residents)** — 20% percent (treaty relief may reduce)  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-uzbekistan/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on freight (non-residents)** — 6% percent  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-uzbekistan/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Double tax treaty network** — Uzbekistan has double tax treaties with 55 countries that may reduce withholding rates (requires tax-residency certificate to claim treaty relief)  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-uzbekistan/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Annual CIT return deadline** — 1 March following the reporting year  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-uzbekistan/corporate/tax-administration)_
- **Quarterly CIT reports** — Due by the 20th of the month following the quarter; tax payable by the same date  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-uzbekistan/corporate/tax-administration)_
- **Monthly advance payments** — Companies with prior-year income over UZS 20 billion make monthly advance payments by the 23rd of each month UZS  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Uzbekistan — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/republic-of-uzbekistan/corporate/tax-administration)_
