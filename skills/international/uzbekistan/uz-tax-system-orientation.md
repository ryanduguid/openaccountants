# Uzbekistan tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the taxpayer, where income arose, residence facts and period. Check the applicable treaty and foreign-tax relief.

## Territorial reach

Resident individuals generally pay personal income tax on income from Uzbekistan and abroad. Nonresident individuals generally pay on Uzbek-source income. Ordinary resident legal entities come within profit tax on their income subject to the Code. A foreign entity operating through an Uzbek permanent establishment follows the rules for income attributable to the establishment, including relevant foreign-source income. Apply the source and withholding rules to other Uzbek-source payments to nonresidents. Check any treaty and foreign-tax relief before treating a foreign payment as exempt. [Tax Code, arts 294–296, 347–353 and 364–366](https://lex.uz/docs/4674902)

## Residence

An individual generally qualifies with more than 183 days in Uzbekistan during any consecutive 12-month period beginning or ending in the tax period. Article 30 also recognises some people with fewer than 183 days in the tax period who spent more time in Uzbekistan than in any other country. Check counted absences, excluded roles, any early determination on application and the applicable treaty where a person is resident in both countries.

Uzbek legal entities are resident. Foreign entities with their place of actual management in Uzbekistan are generally resident, subject to treaty rules. A treaty can also establish a foreign entity's residence for treaty purposes. Check where the executive organs regularly act or senior officers mainly direct the business under art 34. Board preparations and shareholder decisions alone do not establish actual management. [Tax Code, arts 30 and 32–36](https://lex.uz/docs/4674902)

## Taxpayer types

An individual entrepreneur carries on a registered business without forming a legal entity. Identify the applicable business regime. A registered general or limited partnership is a legal entity. For a contractual simple partnership, identify the appointed participant who must keep separate accounts, calculate and pay profit tax on the joint business. Each participant's share of distributed joint income is treated as a dividend. Check the separate obligations for value-added tax and any nonresident partner with a permanent establishment. [Civil Code, arts 39 and 58–62](https://lex.uz/docs/111181) · [Law on Business Partnerships 308-II, arts 3, 8 and 28](https://lex.uz/ru/docs/2457) · [Tax Code, arts 31, 36, 275, 294 and 319](https://lex.uz/docs/4674902)

## Currency

Ordinary tax accounting and payment use the Uzbek sum (UZS), subject to statutory exceptions. Keep amounts and exchange-rate evidence for transactions in foreign currency. [Tax Code, arts 76 and 89](https://lex.uz/docs/4674902) · [Central Bank of Uzbekistan](https://cbu.uz/ru/press_center/news/1754033/)

## Assessment periods

The calendar year is the ordinary period for profit tax and personal income tax. The reporting period for profit tax is ordinarily the quarter, whereas a personal income tax agent reports monthly. Check the first and final period rules and the reporting provisions for special cases. [Tax Code, arts 73, 338 and 384](https://lex.uz/docs/4674902)

## Responsible authorities

The Tax Committee and its territorial tax bodies administer ordinary national tax obligations. Check the relevant body for registration, returns and withholding. Refer import procedures to the customs authority. [Tax Code, arts 25–28](https://lex.uz/docs/4674902) · [Tax Committee](https://gov.uz/ru/soliq/pages/about)

## Fictional intake

Two registered businesses agree to share the proceeds of a project without creating a new legal entity. Review their written agreement to identify the appointed participant, separate accounting and the tax treatment of each distribution.
