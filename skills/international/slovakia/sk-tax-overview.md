# Slovakia Tax Overview

## Slovak tax system at a glance

Slovakia (the Slovak Republic) is an EU member state using the euro, with a self-assessment tax system administered by the Financial Administration. The 2025 tax year saw major consolidation-package changes, including a higher standard VAT rate (23%) and a three-tier corporate income tax.

- **Standard tax year** — Calendar year (1 January to 31 December)  _(Income Tax Act No. 595/2003 Coll. (zákon o dani z príjmov, consolidated text in force from 1 January 2026, Slov-Lex), § 2, definition of the tax period — https://static.slov-lex.sk/static/SK/ZZ/2003/595/20260101.html ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/slovak-republic/individual/tax-administration)_
- **Currency** — Euro (EUR) (Eurozone membership (Slovakia adopted the euro on 1 January 2009))
- **Tax authority** — Financial Administration of the Slovak Republic (Finančná správa SR), incl. tax offices and the Financial Directorate  _([Act on Tax Administration (Tax Procedure Code, Act No. 563/2009 Coll.)](https://www.financnasprava.sk))_
- **Residence basis of taxation** — Tax residents (permanent residence, habitual abode or at least 183 days in Slovakia in the calendar year, § 2(d)) are taxed on worldwide income; non-residents only on Slovak-source income listed in § 16  _(Income Tax Act No. 595/2003 Coll. (zákon o dani z príjmov, consolidated text in force from 1 January 2026, Slov-Lex), § 2(d) and § 16 — https://static.slov-lex.sk/static/SK/ZZ/2003/595/20260101.html ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/slovak-republic/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **Headline personal income tax rates** — 2025: 19% on the tax base up to 176.8 times the subsistence minimum (životné minimum, EUR 48,441.43) and 25% above. From 1 January 2026 (§ 15(a)(1) of the consolidated text): 19% up to 154.8 times, 25% to 212.4 times, 30% to 264 times and 35% above 264 times the subsistence minimum. Business income with taxable revenues up to EUR 100,000 is taxed at 15% (§ 15(a)(2))  _(Income Tax Act No. 595/2003 Coll. (zákon o dani z príjmov, consolidated text in force from 1 January 2026, Slov-Lex), § 15(a) — https://static.slov-lex.sk/static/SK/ZZ/2003/595/20260101.html ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/slovak-republic/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **Headline corporate income tax rates** — 10% where taxable revenues do not exceed EUR 100,000; 21% from EUR 100,000 to EUR 5,000,000; 24% above EUR 5,000,000 (§ 15(b)(1), unchanged in the 2026 text)  _(Income Tax Act No. 595/2003 Coll. (zákon o dani z príjmov, consolidated text in force from 1 January 2026, Slov-Lex), § 15(b)(1) — https://static.slov-lex.sk/static/SK/ZZ/2003/595/20260101.html ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/slovak-republic/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Does Slovakia have VAT?** — Yes — VAT (DPH) at a standard rate of 23% from 1 January 2025, with reduced rates of 19% and 5% on the goods and services in Annexes 7 and 7a  _(VAT Act No. 222/2004 Coll. (zákon o dani z pridanej hodnoty, consolidated text in force from 1 January 2026, Slov-Lex), § 27(1) to (3) — https://static.slov-lex.sk/static/SK/ZZ/2004/222/20260101.html)_
- **Personal income tax return deadline** — Within three calendar months after the end of the tax period (31 March for a calendar year), with payment in the same period (§ 49(2)); extendable by notice by up to three months, or six months where the income includes foreign-source taxable income (§ 49(3))  _(Income Tax Act No. 595/2003 Coll. (zákon o dani z príjmov, consolidated text in force from 1 January 2026, Slov-Lex), § 49(2) and (3) — https://static.slov-lex.sk/static/SK/ZZ/2003/595/20260101.html ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/slovak-republic/individual/tax-administration)_
- **Corporate income tax return deadline** — Within three calendar months after the end of the tax period (31 March for a calendar year), with payment in the same period (§ 49(2)); extendable by notice by up to three months, or six months where the income includes foreign-source taxable income (§ 49(3))  _(Income Tax Act No. 595/2003 Coll. (zákon o dani z príjmov, consolidated text in force from 1 January 2026, Slov-Lex), § 49(2) and (3) — https://static.slov-lex.sk/static/SK/ZZ/2003/595/20260101.html ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/slovak-republic/corporate/tax-administration)_
