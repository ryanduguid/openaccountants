# Slovakia Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax (2025)

- **Overview of CIT structure** — Slovak corporate income tax (daň z príjmov právnických osôb) moved to a three-tier rate structure from 1 January 2025, with a reduced 10% rate for small companies and a new top 24% rate for the largest taxpayers. Resident companies are taxed on worldwide income; non-residents on Slovak-source income.  _(Income Tax Act (Act No. 595/2003 Coll.))_
- **Reduced CIT rate (small companies)** — 10% percent (where taxable income does not exceed EUR 100,000 in the tax period)  _(Income Tax Act (Act No. 595/2003 Coll.) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/slovak-republic/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Standard CIT rate** — 21% percent (where taxable income is between EUR 100,000 and EUR 5,000,000)  _(Income Tax Act (Act No. 595/2003 Coll.) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/slovak-republic/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Top CIT rate (large taxpayers)** — 24% percent (where taxable income exceeds EUR 5,000,000)  _(Income Tax Act (Act No. 595/2003 Coll.) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/slovak-republic/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Tax base** — Accounting profit adjusted for tax purposes (non-deductible items added back, exempt items removed)  _(Income Tax Act (Act No. 595/2003 Coll.) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/slovak-republic/corporate/income-determination)_
- **Minimum tax (tax licence)** — Five bands by **taxable revenues** (zdaniteľné príjmy), not by taxable profit and not "EUR 340 up to EUR 1,540": **EUR 340** up to EUR 50,000, **EUR 960** from 50,001 to 250,000, **EUR 1,920** from 250,001 to 500,000, **EUR 3,840** from 500,001 to 5,000,000, and **EUR 11,520** above EUR 5,000,000. The top band is new for 2026. The base matters: a company with high revenues and little or no profit still lands in a high band, which is the point of a minimum tax. Payable whether the company profits or loses; a newly formed company does not pay it in its first tax period, and an excess over the computed tax can be credited against the next three periods  _(Income Tax Act (Act No. 595/2003 Coll.) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/slovak-republic/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **WHT on dividends to companies/non-residents** — 0% / 35% percent (Generally 0% to corporate shareholders in cooperating states; 35% to non-cooperating jurisdictions)  _(Income Tax Act (Act No. 595/2003 Coll.) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/slovak-republic/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **WHT on interest** — 19% / 35% percent (19% standard; 35% to non-cooperating jurisdictions; reduced/0% under EU Interest-Royalty Directive or treaty)  _(Income Tax Act (Act No. 595/2003 Coll.) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/slovak-republic/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **WHT on royalties** — 19% / 35% percent (19% standard; 35% to non-cooperating jurisdictions; reduced/0% under EU Interest-Royalty Directive or treaty)  _(Income Tax Act (Act No. 595/2003 Coll.) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/slovak-republic/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **CIT return filing deadline** — Within 3 months after the end of the tax period (31 March for a calendar year); extendable by 3 months (6 for foreign income)  _(Income Tax Act (Act No. 595/2003 Coll.) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/slovak-republic/corporate/tax-administration)_
- **CIT payment deadline** — Tax due payable by the filing deadline; advance payments quarterly or monthly depending on prior-year tax  _(Income Tax Act (Act No. 595/2003 Coll.) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/slovak-republic/corporate/tax-administration)_
