# Myanmar Company Formation & Entity Choice

## Company formation and annual compliance

Companies are incorporated under the Myanmar Companies Law 2017 and registered online through the DICA MyCo system. The private company limited by shares is the most common vehicle, and foreign ownership is permitted subject to sector restrictions.

- **Governing company law** — Myanmar Companies Law 2017  _(Myanmar Companies Law 2017 (https://www.lawplusltd.com/2018/01/myanmar-companies-law-2017/))_
- **Company registry / regulator** — Directorate of Investment and Company Administration (DICA), via the online MyCo registration system  _(Myanmar Companies Law 2017 (https://www.lawplusltd.com/2018/01/myanmar-companies-law-2017/))_
- **Common entity types** — Private company limited by shares; public company limited by shares; branch/overseas corporation; sole proprietorship; partnership  _(Myanmar Companies Law 2017 (https://www.lawplusltd.com/2018/01/myanmar-companies-law-2017/))_

> **The Companies Law has been read.** DICA's own registry, **Myanmar Companies Online
> (MyCO)**, publishes the ***Myanmar Companies Law 2017*** in English — 188 pages, clean
> text — alongside the Regulations 2018 and DICA Notifications 56–60/2018. Every row
> below was sourced to a law firm's blog. **`www.dica.gov.mm` itself never completes a
> TLS handshake** from here — the tunnel closes about twelve seconds after the client
> hello — which is why earlier passes recorded Myanmar formation as unreachable. Whether
> that is the host being down, filtering, or geo-blocking is not established. The
> registry subdomain is on different infrastructure and is the live one.
>
> _([Myanmar Companies Law 2017](https://myco.dica.gov.mm/documentation/mm/MCL.en-US.pdf), published at [myco.dica.gov.mm](https://myco.dica.gov.mm/))_

- **⚠ Foreign company threshold — 35%, but of ownership *interest*, and control counts** — Section 1(xiv): a *"foreign company"* means a company incorporated in the Union in which an overseas corporation or other foreign person *"(**or combination of them**) **owns or controls, directly or indirectly, an ownership interest of more than thirty-five per cent**"*. The 35% is confirmed. This guide's *"a foreign person owns more than 35% of its shares"* is narrower on four counts: the test reaches **control** as well as ownership, reaches it **indirectly**, **aggregates combinations** of foreign persons, and is expressed as an **ownership interest** rather than shares %  _([Companies Law 2017, s. 1(xiv)](https://myco.dica.gov.mm/documentation/mm/MCL.en-US.pdf))_
- **⚠ Minimum shareholders and directors — the resident-director rule is not confined to private companies** — Section 4(a) makes **every** company registered under the Law have: a name; a constitution; **at least one share in issue** (a company limited by guarantee need not have share capital); at least one member; and **"at least one director who must be ordinarily resident in the Union"**. That requirement is general, not — as this guide had it — specific to a private company limited by shares. **Section 4(a)(vi) adds a rule the guide omits entirely: a public company needs at least three directors, at least one of whom must be a Myanmar citizen ordinarily resident in the Union.** A registered office address in the Union is also required  _([Companies Law 2017, s. 4(a)](https://myco.dica.gov.mm/documentation/mm/MCL.en-US.pdf))_
- **"Ordinarily resident" is a defined term with a day count** — Section 1(xix): a person who is a **permanent resident** of the Union under an applicable law, **or is resident in the Union for at least 183 days in each 12-month period**, running from the commencement of the Law for existing companies and from incorporation otherwise. The guide used the phrase without the test  _([Companies Law 2017, s. 1(xix)](https://myco.dica.gov.mm/documentation/mm/MCL.en-US.pdf))_
- **Minimum share capital — none, and shares have no par value at all** — The phrase *"minimum share capital"* appears **nowhere in the 188-page Law**, so the guide's "(approx — confirm)" resolves in its favour. **Section 60(b) goes further: *"A share will not have a nominal or par value."*** Myanmar runs a **no-par-value** share regime, so a minimum capital figure is not merely unset but structurally absent. What section 4(a)(iii) does require is **at least one share in issue**. Sector and MIC requirements remain separate  _([Companies Law 2017, ss. 4(a)(iii), 60(b)](https://myco.dica.gov.mm/documentation/mm/MCL.en-US.pdf))_
- **Default rights attaching to a share** — Section 61(a): subject to the constitution, a share confers **one vote on a poll** at a company meeting on any resolution, and **an equal share in dividends**  _([Companies Law 2017, s. 61(a)](https://myco.dica.gov.mm/documentation/mm/MCL.en-US.pdf))_
- **Incorporation timeline** — Online registration via MyCo is typically completed within a few business days once documents are in order ((approx — confirm))  _(Myanmar Companies Law 2017 (https://www.lawplusltd.com/2018/01/myanmar-companies-law-2017/))_
- **First annual return — two months, and the statute says so** — **Section 97(a)**: every company must file a return of its particulars with the Registrar in the prescribed form *"**within 2 months from its incorporation**"*. Confirmed; the law-firm citation is replaced by the provision  _([Companies Law 2017, s. 97(a)](https://myco.dica.gov.mm/documentation/mm/MCL.en-US.pdf))_
- **Ongoing annual return — one month after the anniversary** — Same section 97(a): thereafter *"once at least in every year (**but no later than 1 month after the anniversary of its incorporation**)"*. Confirmed exactly as this guide had it  _([Companies Law 2017, s. 97(a)](https://myco.dica.gov.mm/documentation/mm/MCL.en-US.pdf))_
- **⚠ An overseas corporation files on a different clock** — Section 53 applies to **every overseas corporation conducting business in the Union**, not the two-month / one-month cycle above: an **annual return within 28 days of the end of its financial year**, and — **at least once each calendar year and at intervals of no more than 15 months** — a balance sheet to the end of its last financial year, a cash flow statement and a profit and loss statement. This guide lists *"branch/overseas corporation"* as an entity type and gives it no filing rule  _([Companies Law 2017, s. 53](https://myco.dica.gov.mm/documentation/mm/MCL.en-US.pdf))_
- **Share transfers — 21 days, and the notice must flag a change of foreign status** — Section 86: within **21 days** of registering a transfer of shares or other registrable securities the company must file notice in the prescribed form; **if the transfer makes the company become, or cease to be, a foreign company the notice must say so**. Failure does not invalidate the transfer, but section 87 makes **every director knowingly involved liable to a fine of 750,000 kyats**. Given the 35% test above, an ordinary share sale can flip foreign status and start this clock  _([Companies Law 2017, ss. 86–87](https://myco.dica.gov.mm/documentation/mm/MCL.en-US.pdf))_
- **Tax registration** — New companies must register with the Internal Revenue Department (IRD) and obtain a Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN)  _(Income Tax Law (Myanmar) (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/myanmar))_
- **Annual financial statements / audit** — Companies must prepare annual financial statements; audited accounts are filed with the annual income tax return to the IRD  _(Myanmar Companies Law 2017 (https://www.lawplusltd.com/2024/04/reports-and-filings-required-for-companies-in-myanmar/))_
