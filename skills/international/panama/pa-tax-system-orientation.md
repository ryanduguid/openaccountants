# Panama tax system

The Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) administers income tax (impuesto sobre la renta, ISR) and the tax on transfers of movable goods and services (ITBMS). Amounts are in Panamanian balboas (PAB, shown as B/.). [DGI income tax](https://dgi.mef.gob.pa/DInforme/D-IsRenta) · [DGI Form 430](https://dgi.mef.gob.pa/DInforme/Formulario430)

## Income tax

- **Residence and source.** An individual can be tax resident through presence of more than 183 days in Panama during the fiscal year or immediately preceding year, whether continuous or intermittent, or by establishing a permanent home in Panama. Entities incorporated in Panama need material means of direction and administration there. Foreign entities also need Public Registry registration. ISR generally covers income produced in Panama, wherever received. Statutory source rules also apply to certain cross-border services and payments. [Law 52 of 2012, art. 13](https://www.gacetaoficial.gob.pa/pdfTemp/27108/38920.pdf) · [Fiscal Code art. 694, as amended by Law 6 of 2005](https://dgi.mef.gob.pa/itbms/Normativa/Ley-6-2-febrero-2005.pdf)
- **Individuals.** The ordinary annual scale applies 0% through B/.11,000 of net taxable income, then 15% on the excess through B/.50,000. Above B/.50,000, pay B/.5,850 plus 25% of the excess. Check separate treatment for items such as representation allowances. [DGI rate schedules](https://dgi.mef.gob.pa/DInforme/Tarifa)
- **Companies.** The standard rate is 25% of net taxable income. Under the alternative calculation (CAIR), companies with annual taxable revenue above B/.1.5 million generally apply the corporate rate to the higher of their ordinary net taxable income and 4.67% of taxable gross revenue. A company that meets the statutory criteria can apply for permission to use the ordinary calculation alone. [DGI rate schedules](https://dgi.mef.gob.pa/DInforme/Tarifa) · [Official Gazette 29773-B, Fiscal Code art. 699 as reproduced](https://www.gacetaoficial.gob.pa/storage/gacetas/2023/05/29773_B/98221.pdf) · [DGI CAIR guidance](https://dgi.mef.gob.pa/DInforme/GD-ISR.php/GD-ISR)
- **Foreign passive income from 2027.** Law 526 introduces reporting and economic substance requirements for defined foreign passive income of covered multinational-group entities constituted or domiciled in Panama. The law starts with fiscal year 2027. Its implementing decree applies to fiscal periods starting on or after 1 January 2027. Covered income of an entity that fails to qualify is generally subject to 15% on taxable net income. [Law 526 of 2026, arts. 1 and 6](https://www.gacetaoficial.gob.pa/storage/gacetas/2026/05/30534_B/GacetaNo_30534b_20260528.pdf) · [Executive Decree 32 of 2026, art. 20](https://www.gacetaoficial.gob.pa/documento/105014)

## ITBMS and filing

- **ITBMS.** The standard rate is 7%. The small-supplier exclusion applies to qualifying producers, traders and service providers whose prior-year gross income was at most B/.3,000 on a monthly average and at most B/.36,000 for the year. Importers and specified transactions have separate rules. Monthly filers lodge and pay by the 15th of the following month. The DGI specifies quarterly filing and payment for certain independent professionals. [DGI Form 430](https://dgi.mef.gob.pa/DInforme/Formulario430)
- **Annual filing and payment.** The ordinary year ends on 31 December. An individual who must file generally lodges by 15 March and a company by 31 March. A worker who receives only salary and representation allowances from one employer and meets the withholding conditions is exempt from filing. The ordinary calendar-year balance is due by 31 March even when the DGI grants a filing extension. An approved special fiscal period can change the filing date. Check separate estimated payments and withholding. [DGI Fiscal Code art. 710](https://dgi.mef.gob.pa/DInforme/A710codigofiscal) · [DGI filing and payment dates](https://dgi.mef.gob.pa/DInforme/P-Presentacion)
