# Malawi tax overview

Identify the taxpayer, income source and year of assessment before applying Malawi's rates. The ordinary charge covers income from Malawi and income deemed to come from Malawi. The Malawi Revenue Authority (MRA) administers the tax. Amounts in this guide are in Malawi kwacha (MWK). [Taxation Act, ss 2, 11 and 27][act] · [Act 36 of 2025, s 3][act36] · [MRA][mra] · [Reserve Bank of Malawi][currency]

## Residence and assessment period

The statutory definition of resident includes an individual present in Malawi for at least 183 days in aggregate during the year of assessment, a company incorporated in Malawi and a trust, estate or partnership organised under Malawi's written law. The Act defines a year of assessment as a 12-month period for which tax is chargeable. Confirm the applicable dates, other residence provisions and any treaty for the taxpayer. The 2023 amendment added a definition of place of effective management. Check the provision that uses that term for the case at hand. [Taxation Act, s 2][act] · [Act 17 of 2023][act17]

## Ordinary rates during 2026

Use these annual personal income tax bands for periods governed by the Eleventh Schedule as amended by Act 36 of 2025. [Act 36 of 2025, s 6][act36]

| Annual taxable income | Employment income rate |
| --- | ---: |
| Up to MWK 2,040,000 | 0% |
| Next MWK 16,800,000 | 30% |
| Next MWK 101,160,000 | 35% |
| Above MWK 120,000,000 | 40% |

For ordinary taxable income other than employment income, the schedule has a 0% band up to MWK 2,040,000 and a 30% rate on the excess. Check any special charge and how the rates apply to the taxpayer's assessment period. [Act 36 of 2025, s 6][act36]

The base rate for an ordinary company incorporated in Malawi is 30%. For periods governed by Act 3 of 2026, published on 14 April 2026, an additional 10% applies to taxable income above MWK 5 billion. A separate 0.5% minimum alternative tax applies subject to the turnover and business-age tests. Confirm the company's assessment period, turnover base, comparison with ordinary tax and available credits before calculating tax payable. [MRA base rates][base] · [Act 12 of 2024, s 7(a)][act12] · [Act 36 of 2025, ss 3–4 and 6][act36] · [Act 3 of 2026, s 12][act3]

The standard VAT rate is 17.5% under the amended VAT Act. The MRA dates the parallel import VAT measure under the customs order to 10 January 2026. Confirm the tax point and applicable treatment for supplies and imports around the transition. [Act 37 of 2025, s 2][act37] · [MRA customs measures][customs]

## Returns and transition dates

From 1 April 2025 the Tax Administration Act requires returns in the prescribed form and manner when a tax law requires a return. The old Taxation Act subsection containing a 180-day income return period and an exception for PAYE-only income was deleted on that date. Confirm the operative due date, filing requirement and balance payment for the actual period with the MRA. The MRA's older brochure still quotes the deleted period. [Gazette commencement notices][commencement] · [Act 15 of 2021, schedule amending s 84][act15] · [Tax Administration Act, ss 40 and 42][taa] · [MRA return brochure][brochure]

For the 31 May accounting date, the MRA's November 2026 notice gives 30 November 2026 for income tax return submission and payment. Check the dates for other accounting periods and any applicable extension. [MRA notice, 12 November 2026][november-notice]

Registered users file listed individual and company returns electronically and receive a receipt with a unique number. Rule 9 gives the paper filing procedure when the system is unavailable on the filing date. Check the relevant form, return period and statutory conditions for an extension. [Electronic System Rules, rr 6–9 and sch 2][electronic] · [MRA MyTax][portal]

The December 2025 tax amendments were published on 30 December. MRA notices give 30 and 31 December as the effective date. The notices also give company tax treatment that differs from the enacted company rate sequence before the April 2026 amendment. Confirm transition treatment with the MRA for an affected year or transaction. [General Interpretation Act, s 9][interpretation] · [MRA 30 December notice][mra30] · [MRA 31 December notice][mra31] · [Act 36 of 2025, s 6][act36] · [Act 3 of 2026, s 12][act3]
