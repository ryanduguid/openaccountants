# Grenada employer PAYE source reference

Use this reference to identify the Inland Revenue duties of a private employer paying employees in Grenada. Section 54 of the Income Tax Act directs employers other than the Government of Grenada to deduct from remuneration under the Fourth Schedule. Check the provisions and Inland Revenue Division (IRD) instructions that apply to the actual pay period before withholding, filing or paying. Use [Grenada National Insurance contributions](gd-social-contributions.md) for the separate contributions. [Cap 149, s 54 and Fourth Schedule][INCOME]

## Employer registration, deductions and records

- Under paragraph 1 of the Fourth Schedule, an employer who pays or becomes liable to pay remuneration registers with the Comptroller. The 2010 revised edition gives a new employer 30 days after the end of the month in which they become an employer and gives 30 days to notify a change of business address or cessation. Section 12(1) of the Tax Administration Act separately gives persons who are or may become liable for tax under the named Acts 30 days after commencing business to register. Confirm each applicable registration and the current notification procedure with IRD. [Cap 149, Fourth Schedule para 1][SCHEDULE] · [Tax Administration Act 14 of 2016, s 12(1)][ADMIN]
- The 2014 amendment replaced the original deduction subparagraphs with a reference to the Fifth Schedule rates on chargeable income. The Comptroller can direct deductions under paragraphs 2(3)–(6), including concurrent employment and non-resident employees. Check the applicable Fifth Schedule, remuneration and relief rules and any direction for the employee before calculating deductions. The annual employment-income reference covers selected periods and does not calculate a pay-period deduction. [Cap 149, Fourth Schedule para 2][SCHEDULE] · [SRO 1 of 2014, para 4][ORDER] · [Grenada annual employment-income reference](gd-annual-employment-income-reference.md)
- Keep each employee's accrued remuneration and tax deducted for the calendar year available for inspection. Where a certificate is requested, paragraph 5 requires the prescribed certificate to show the employment period, total remuneration accrued and total tax deducted and gives 7 days from the request. Check IRD's current certificate format. [Cap 149, Fourth Schedule paras 5 and 7][SCHEDULE]

## Remittance and returns

| Obligation | Rule in the retained legislation |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly remittance and return | Section 96(e) makes tax deductible under section 54 payable within 7 days after the end of the month in which it was deductible. Submit the return showing tax deducted and remitted when making the payment. [Cap 149, s 96(e)][REMIT] · [Fourth Schedule paras 3 and 8][SCHEDULE] |
| Annual return | Paragraph 9 gives an employer 1 month after the calendar year ends, or 7 days after ceasing to be an employer during that year. The Comptroller may allow more time. Report the tax deducted for each employee and total payments, include copies of certificates issued under paragraph 5(1) and account for any shortfall. [Cap 149, Fourth Schedule para 9][SCHEDULE] |

The Tax Administration Act commenced on 1 May 2016. Section 44(1) refers ordinary payment timing to the applicable tax law. Under section 44(3), the Comptroller prescribes the manner and place of payment. Schedule 2 paragraph 4(c) leaves section 96 outside its repeal list and repeals section 97, the old interest provision. The 2025 Beryl relief law expressly refers to section 96 and paragraph 9 of the Fourth Schedule in granting specific employers relief for the 2024 tax period. Check any extension for the employer and period. [SRO 34 of 2016][COMMENCEMENT] · [Tax Administration Act 14 of 2016, s 44 and Schedule 2 para 4(c)][ADMIN] · [Beryl Special Measures Act 6 of 2025, ss 2, 7–8][BERYL]

When no contrary rule applies, section 51 of the Interpretation and General Provisions Act excludes the starting day from a period measured in days after an event. Count intervening Sundays and bank holidays in the 7-day PAYE period. If the last day is a Sunday or bank holiday, the period runs through the next day that is neither. For example, if the seventh day falls on a Sunday and the following Monday is a bank holiday, the period runs through Tuesday, provided Tuesday is neither and no contrary rule applies. Check the applicable bank holidays and any PAYE-specific override. [Cap 153, ss 3 and 51][TIME] · [Cap 149, s 96(e)][REMIT]

## Filing channels and reconciliation

The Ministry of Finance identified monthly PAYE filing and payment in its 26 January 2024 GTAX launch. The government services directory lists monthly PAYE under GTAX and has a separate annual PAYE entry. Confirm the current annual and back-filing routes and any portal requirements with IRD before submitting. [GTAX launch][GTAX] · [Grenada Government Online Services][SERVICES]

IRD's public GTAX onboarding guide explains how to create and activate an online profile, link an existing tax identification number or apply as a new taxpayer. Confirm that the employer's PAYE obligation is active before relying on the account for filing. [Getting Started with GTAX, pp 1–2][ONBOARDING]

For an ordinary monthly close, reconcile tax already deducted in the payroll records to the return and payment. At year end, reconcile the sum of deductions by employee to the annual return and total remittances. Investigate any difference and keep the return and payment records together. [Cap 149, Fourth Schedule paras 7–9][SCHEDULE]

## Confirm before applying this reference

- Obtain the current IRD pay-period calculation method, tables, forms and treatment of nil periods or corrections. Verify the legal version, any non-working-day rule and deadline in the employer's account for the relevant period. Use confirmed local requirements for taxable benefits, multiple employments, non-residents, tax-free remuneration agreements and public-sector payroll.
- Confirm the current annual filing channel, employee certificate and payment allocation instructions. Keep late payments, interest, special relief and retrospective corrections subject to the rules for the affected period.
