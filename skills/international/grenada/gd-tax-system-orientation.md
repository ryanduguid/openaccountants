# Grenada tax system orientation

The Inland Revenue Division administers income tax and domestic VAT. The Comptroller of Customs collects VAT on taxable imports. Amounts in this guide are in Eastern Caribbean dollars (XCD), the domestic currency used in Grenada's debt strategy. [Tax Administration Act No. 14 of 2016, ss 4–6 and Schedule I][ADMIN] · [VAT Act, s 8][VAT] · [Debt strategy, p ii][CURRENCY]

## Residence and source

The income tax residence tests for an individual include 183 days in the year of assessment, a permanent abode with presence subject to statutory exceptions for absence, and continuous presence across an adjacent year that independently meets the 183-day test. A company qualifies by incorporation in Grenada or, if incorporated abroad, by management and control in Grenada. [Income Tax Act, Cap. 149, s 2][TAX]

For residents and non-residents, the starting point is Grenada-source income, including income deemed local. Check the banking interest proviso and exemptions. Non-residents' specified non-business income follows separate withholding routes for partnership income and for payments by another person. Partnerships allocate income to their partners under section 21. [Income Tax Act, ss 7–12 and 21][TAX] · [Act No. 27 of 2015, ss 1, 3, 11 and 12][NONRESIDENT]

## Individuals and companies

For a qualifying resident individual, the statutory allowance is up to XCD 36,000. The Fifth Schedule applies 10% to the first XCD 24,000 of chargeable income and 28% to the excess. The ordinary company rate is 28%. The 10% bracket took effect from 1 January 2017 and the 28% rates from 1 January 2019. Check available exemptions and verify later applicable changes before calculating liability. [Income Tax Act, ss 42A and 56][TAX] · [SRO 1 of 2014, ss 3 and 5][ORDER2014] · [SRO 2 of 2017, ss 1 and 3][ORDER2017] · [SRO 25 of 2018, ss 1 and 3][ORDER2018]

## Transactions and filing

Section 8 of the VAT Act sets a general rate of 15% on taxable supplies and imports and a 10% rate for qualifying hotel or holiday accommodation and specified supplies. Check the precise classification, registration threshold, any special sector rates and the applicable schedule before filing. The 2026 Third Schedule order gives a separate zero-rating rule for the named electricity supplier and permits qualifying goods to be zero rated during approved VAT-free periods. Keep the rules for remote digital supplies open pending confirmation of the operative amendment. [VAT Act, ss 8–10][VAT] · [SRO 18 of 2026][ZERO] · [Parliament, 2026 VAT amendment bill][DIGITAL]

The assessment year is the calendar year. The income tax basis period normally ends on 31 December, with rules for qualifying business accounting periods and cessation. Section 65(1), as revised by the 2016 Tax Administration Act, generally requires a return within 90 days of the basis-period end. Check filing exceptions and extensions. The IRD's notice of 31 March 2026 extended that filing round for personal and corporate income tax and annual stamp tax to 30 April 2026 for filing and payment. The VAT Act sets monthly tax periods and generally requires the return and payment within 20 days of period end. [Income Tax Act, ss 2, 11 and 65][TAX] · [Tax Administration Act, Schedule 2][ADMIN] · [IRD, 31 March 2026 notice][EXTENSION] · [VAT Act, ss 2, 44 and 46][VAT]
