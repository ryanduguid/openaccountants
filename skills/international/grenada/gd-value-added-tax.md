# Grenada value added tax

Grenada's ordinary VAT rate is **15%** in 2026. For a domestic business, check which supplies count towards registration, the rate for each supply and the month in which to report it. The supplier accounts for VAT on ordinary domestic taxable supplies and the importer pays VAT on taxable imports to the Comptroller of Customs. [Value Added Tax Act, Cap 333A, ss 7–9][VAT] · [Finance Ministry, August 2026 consumer guide][GUIDE]

## Registration

At each month-end test the preceding 12 months, the preceding 4 months and the next 12 months. The ordinary threshold is **XCD 300,000** under the 2015 amendment. The 12-month tests include taxable activity supplies equal to the threshold. The 4-month test applies above XCD 100,000 unless reasonable grounds support a total below XCD 300,000 for those 4 months and the following 8 months. [Cap 333A, s 9(1)–(2)][VAT] · [Act 21 of 2015, ss 1–2][THRESHOLD]

Exclude supplies that would be non-taxable if registered, sales of capital assets and supplies made solely because the business or part of it is sold or the taxable activity permanently ceases. Include acquisitions that would require a reverse charge if the business were registered. Include supplies of a related person if the Comptroller directs it. Apply within 14 days after the ordinary registration obligation arises. Public entertainment, government bodies, accommodation, certain investments and voluntary registration have separate rules. Confirm the approved form and registration date with the Inland Revenue Division (IRD). [Cap 333A, ss 9–14][VAT] · [Act 18 of 2014, s 4][ACT2014] · [Tax Administration Act 14 of 2016, Schedule 2 para 1][ADMIN]

## Supplies and input credits

- Classify each supply before selecting a rate. The 10% accommodation provision and the 2014 accommodation amendments took effect on 1 August 2014. Check the schedules and effective instruments for zero-rated, exempt, reduced-rate and special-rate treatment for the transaction. [Cap 333A, s 8][VAT] · [Act 18 of 2014, ss 1–4 and 11][ACT2014]
- Under the ordinary timing rule, use the earliest of invoice issue, receipt of any consideration or actual supply, which is delivery or availability of the goods or performance of the services. Check the separate rules for periodic supplies and other exceptions. [Cap 333A, s 17][VAT] · [Act 18 of 2014, s 5][ACT2014]
- Subtract allowable input credits from output VAT. Check taxable use, blocked expenses and mixed-use apportionment. Subject to the statutory exceptions, a period with no taxable supplies generally gives no input credits. Hold the specified VAT invoice, eligible receipt, customs record or adjustment note as applicable. The date the documentation is held can affect which return can claim the credit. [Cap 333A, ss 20–21, 32–34 and 37–43][VAT] · [Act 7 of 2011, ss 11–12][ACT2011]
- A registered supplier normally gives a registered recipient a VAT invoice and an unregistered recipient a sales receipt. For a registered recipient the supplier can give a sales receipt if the taxable supply's consideration is paid in money and totals XCD 50 or less. The unregistered-recipient rule in s 38(1) does not apply to sales of XCD 5 or less. Check regulations for required particulars and retain VAT invoices, debit and credit notes and customs records under the tax administration rules. The general records rule is 7 years from the transaction, or until the assessment time limit expires if that is longer. [Cap 333A, ss 37–39][VAT] · [Act 7 of 2011, s 13][ACT2011] · [Tax Administration Act 14 of 2016, s 20][ADMIN]

## Return and payment

Lodge a return for each calendar month, including a month with no liability, within **20 days** after month-end. Pay any net liability by the same ordinary deadline. Sections 44(1) and 46(1)–(2) survived the amendments that took effect on 1 May 2016. The government's filing portal directs periods from 2025 onward to GTAX and earlier periods to eService. Confirm the current approved form, required attachments and payment allocation with IRD. Apply for a filing extension by the due date if needed. An extension to file affects the payment deadline only if the Comptroller expressly extends payment as well. [Cap 333A, ss 2, 44(1) and 46(1)–(2)][VAT] · [Tax Administration Act 14 of 2016, ss 22 and 26 and Schedule 2 para 1][ADMIN] · [Commencement SRO 34 of 2016][START] · [Government filing portal][FILING]

For June 2026, assume a registered trader makes XCD 10,000 of standard-rated sales before VAT and holds the required evidence for XCD 450 of eligible input credits. With no other adjustments, output VAT is XCD 1,500 and the net payment is XCD 1,050. The ordinary filing and payment deadline is 20 July 2026. [Cap 333A, ss 8, 32, 34, 44 and 46][VAT]

## Cases requiring further evidence

Check the transaction's date and the applicable instrument before applying product exemptions, electricity or telecommunications treatment, short VAT-free events, customs relief or an import classification. Check the existing imported-services rules in Cap 333A s 22 as amended. The 2026 Senate digital-services bill calls for commencement by Gazette notice. Confirm the assented text and commencement before using its proposed provisions. Confirm live forms, correction, credit and refund procedures, penalties and any case-specific IRD directions. [Cap 333A, ss 8, 22 and 49–52][VAT] · [Act 7 of 2011, s 9][ACT2011] · [Finance Ministry VAT-free materials][RELIEF] · [2026 Senate bill, cl 1(2)][BILL]
