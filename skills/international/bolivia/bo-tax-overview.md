# Bolivia tax system

The Servicio de Impuestos Nacionales (SIN) administers national income and transaction taxes. This guide covers the ordinary general regime. Amounts are in bolivianos (BOB, shown as Bs). [SIN tax obligations](https://siatinfo.impuestos.gob.bo/index.php/obligaciones-tributarias)

## Income tax

- **Source and taxpayers.** The Régimen Complementario al IVA (RC-IVA) applies to individuals and undivided estates with taxable Bolivian-source income, regardless of nationality, residence or domicile. Source generally follows the location or use of the assets, the activity or the event producing the income. The Impuesto sobre las Utilidades de las Empresas (IUE) covers Bolivian-source profits of companies, sole traders and local branches, agencies and permanent establishments. Identify the applicable withholding rules for foreign recipients and individuals domiciled abroad. [Law 843, arts 19–22, 36–37, 42 and 51](https://www.impuestos.gob.bo/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/1.-LEY-N%C2%B0-843-10-26.pdf)
- **Individuals.** RC-IVA is 13% on the applicable taxable base. The regime includes employment earnings and specified capital and independent professional income. The individual professional or trade rules moved from IUE to RC-IVA on 1 January 2023. Employers generally calculate and withhold monthly. Registered independent taxpayers generally file quarterly on Form 610. Check the applicable deductions, credits and withholding before computing payment. [Law 843, arts 19, 26 and 30–31, and Supreme Decree 21531, arts 8–11](https://www.impuestos.gob.bo/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/1.-LEY-N%C2%B0-843-10-26.pdf) · [SIN RC-IVA guidance](https://siatinfo.impuestos.gob.bo/index.php/impuesto-asunto/rc-iva)
- **Businesses.** The ordinary IUE rate is 25% of taxable net profits. File and pay within 120 days of the applicable fiscal close. The ordinary commercial and service close is 31 December. Other activities can have a different fiscal close, and SIN can extend a specified deadline. For fiscal years ending on 30 June 2026, SIN extended the IUE return and payment deadline to 30 November 2026. [SIN Resolution 102600000043, Artículo Único I](https://www.impuestos.gob.bo/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/RND-43.pdf) · [Law 843, arts 36–37, 46 and 50, and Supreme Decree 24051, art 39](https://www.impuestos.gob.bo/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/1.-LEY-N%C2%B0-843-10-26.pdf) · [SIN tax obligations](https://siatinfo.impuestos.gob.bo/index.php/obligaciones-tributarias)

## VAT and transaction tax

- **IVA.** The standard rate is 13%. Law 843 covers specified sales of movable goods, work and services in Bolivia and definitive imports. Establish liability and obtain the appropriate taxpayer registration for the activity. For an ordinary domestic invoice under the existing inclusive method, the IVA is 13% of the invoiced taxable amount. Registered businesses generally file and pay monthly by the 13th to 22nd of the following month, according to the final digit of the NIT. [Law 843, arts 1, 3, 5 and 15, and Supreme Decree 21530, art 10](https://www.impuestos.gob.bo/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/1.-LEY-N%C2%B0-843-10-26.pdf) · [SIN IVA filing guidance](https://siatanexo.impuestos.gob.bo/index.php/declaraciones-juradas-en-formato-electronico/impuesto-al-valor-agregado/200-impuesto-al-valor-agregado)
- **Invoice change.** Law 1733 requires a separate IVA amount at 13% of the pre-tax value from the first day of the month after publication of its regulating supreme decree. Resolution 102600000045, published in November 2026, extended the invoicing pilot until that publication. Check the decree and the effective date before applying the new method. [Law 1733, second additional and sole final provisions](https://www.impuestos.gob.bo/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/L17332051NCPP.pdf) · [SIN Resolution 102600000045](https://www.impuestos.gob.bo/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/RND-45.pdf)
- **Transaction tax.** The ordinary Impuesto a las Transacciones (IT) rate is 3% of gross income from specified activities, subject to exemptions and any permitted IUE offset. Businesses generally file and pay IT monthly on Form 400 using the NIT calendar. [SIN Form 400 instructions](https://siatanexo.impuestos.gob.bo/index.php/declaraciones-juradas-en-formato-electronico/impuesto-a-las-transacciones/400-impuesto-a-las-transacciones) · [Law 843, arts 72–77, and Supreme Decree 21532, art 7](https://www.impuestos.gob.bo/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/1.-LEY-N%C2%B0-843-10-26.pdf)
