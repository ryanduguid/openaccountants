# Thailand Payroll & Social Contributions

## Social security and payroll withholding

Employers must withhold personal income tax from salaries (PAYE-equivalent) and contribute, alongside employees, to the Social Security Fund administered by the Social Security Office.

- **Employee social security contribution rate** — 5% of monthly wages, the rate set by ministerial regulation under s 46 (reduced to 3% for employers and employees in nine disaster-affected southern provinces from December 2025 to May 2026)  _(Social Security Act B.E. 2533 (1990), Office of the Council of State translation on sso.go.th, s 46 — https://www.sso.go.th/wpr/assets/upload/files_storage/sso_th/6aece18d294cfea419d4b4ad946bc368.pdf ; Ministry of Labour, Cabinet approves reduction of social security contributions under sections 33 and 39 (30 December 2025) — https://www.mol.go.th/en/news/cabinet-approves-reduction-of-social-security-contributions-under-sections-33-and-39-for-6-months-from-december-2025-to-may-2026-as-proposed-by-mol-labour-minister-accelerates-relief-measures-for-employers-and-insured-persons-in-9-severely-disaster-affected-southern-provinces-expected-to-ease-costs-by-over-1-4-billion-baht)_
- **Employer social security contribution rate** — 5% of monthly wages (matching the employee), set by ministerial regulation under s 46  _(Social Security Act B.E. 2533 (1990), Office of the Council of State translation on sso.go.th, s 46 — https://www.sso.go.th/wpr/assets/upload/files_storage/sso_th/6aece18d294cfea419d4b4ad946bc368.pdf ; Ministry of Labour, Cabinet approves reduction of social security contributions under sections 33 and 39 (30 December 2025) — https://www.mol.go.th/en/news/cabinet-approves-reduction-of-social-security-contributions-under-sections-33-and-39-for-6-months-from-december-2025-to-may-2026-as-proposed-by-mol-labour-minister-accelerates-relief-measures-for-employers-and-insured-persons-in-9-severely-disaster-affected-southern-provinces-expected-to-ease-costs-by-over-1-4-billion-baht)_
- **Monthly wage base cap (2025)** — THB 15,000 per month  _(Social Security Act B.E. 2533 (1990), Office of the Council of State translation on sso.go.th, s 46 (minimum and maximum wages set by ministerial regulation) — https://www.sso.go.th/wpr/assets/upload/files_storage/sso_th/6aece18d294cfea419d4b4ad946bc368.pdf ; PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries, Other taxes — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/thailand/individual/other-taxes)_
- **Maximum monthly contribution per party (2025)** — THB 750 per month (5% of THB 15,000)  _(Social Security Act B.E. 2533 (1990), Office of the Council of State translation on sso.go.th, s 46 — https://www.sso.go.th/wpr/assets/upload/files_storage/sso_th/6aece18d294cfea419d4b4ad946bc368.pdf ; PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries, Other taxes — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/thailand/individual/other-taxes)_
- **Monthly wage base cap (from Jan 2026)** — THB 17,500 per month (max contribution THB 875), phase 1 of 2026–2028 increases THB ((future change — confirm))  _(Social Security Act B.E. 2533 (1990), Office of the Council of State translation on sso.go.th, s 46 (wage base set by ministerial regulation) — https://www.sso.go.th/wpr/assets/upload/files_storage/sso_th/6aece18d294cfea419d4b4ad946bc368.pdf ; Acclime summary of the 2025 ministerial regulation — https://thailand.acclime.com/news/new-social-security-contribution-rates-benefits/)_
- **Government contribution** — The Government also contributes to the Social Security Fund at 2.75% of wages a month, a rate the Ministry of Labour confirmed as unchanged during the December 2025 to May 2026 regional reduction  _(Ministry of Labour, Cabinet approves reduction of social security contributions under sections 33 and 39 (30 December 2025) — https://www.mol.go.th/en/news/cabinet-approves-reduction-of-social-security-contributions-under-sections-33-and-39-for-6-months-from-december-2025-to-may-2026-as-proposed-by-mol-labour-minister-accelerates-relief-measures-for-employers-and-insured-persons-in-9-severely-disaster-affected-southern-provinces-expected-to-ease-costs-by-over-1-4-billion-baht ; Social Security Act B.E. 2533 (1990), Office of the Council of State translation on sso.go.th, s 46 — https://www.sso.go.th/wpr/assets/upload/files_storage/sso_th/6aece18d294cfea419d4b4ad946bc368.pdf)_
- **Social security remittance deadline** — Within the 15th day of the month following the month of deduction, remitted to the Social Security Office with the contribution remittance form  _(Social Security Act B.E. 2533 (1990), Office of the Council of State translation on sso.go.th, s 47 — https://www.sso.go.th/wpr/assets/upload/files_storage/sso_th/6aece18d294cfea419d4b4ad946bc368.pdf)_
- **Payroll withholding (PAYE equivalent)** — Employer withholds PIT from each salary payment, computing the year's tax under s. 48 on the annualised income and dividing it by the number of payments (s. 50(1))  _(Revenue Code (Revenue Department English translation), s. 50(1) — https://www.rd.go.th/english/37749.html#section50 ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/thailand/individual/tax-administration)_
- **Payroll withholding remittance deadline** — By the 7th of the following month with Form PND.1 (s 52 sets seven days from the payment, which the ministerial regulation made under it runs from the end of the month; e-filing is typically extended)  _(Revenue Code, Chapter 3 Part 2 (ss 38 to 64), Revenue Department English translation, ss 52 and 59 — https://www.rd.go.th/english/37749.html ; PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries, Tax administration — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/thailand/individual/tax-administration)_
- **Annual payroll withholding summary** — Form PND.1 Kor filed annually under s 58, summarising the assessable income under s 40(1), (2) and (4) paid and the tax withheld during the previous year; s 58 sets January of the following year, so check the Revenue Department's current filing calendar  _(Revenue Code, Chapter 3 Part 2 (ss 38 to 64), Revenue Department English translation, s 58 — https://www.rd.go.th/english/37749.html)_
- **Workmen's Compensation Fund** — Employer-only annual contribution of 0.2% to 1.0% of wages, depending on the risk level of the business, under the Workmen's Compensation Act B.E. 2537 (1994)  _(Ministry of Labour, Labour Law summary (Workmen's Compensation Act B.E. 2537) — https://www.mol.go.th/en/labour-law)_
