# Thailand tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the taxpayer, where income arose, residence facts and period. Check the applicable treaty and foreign-tax relief.

## Territorial reach

Thailand taxes assessable income from work or business in Thailand, a Thai employer's business or property in Thailand, including amounts paid abroad. An individual's assessable foreign income earned from 1 January 2024 whilst Thai resident generally comes into charge when remitted to Thailand, including in a later tax year. Record residence in the year of earning and the amounts and dates of remittances. Check the exception for foreign income earned before 2024, applicable exemptions and treaty relief.

A Thai-incorporated company or juristic partnership generally accounts for business income from Thailand and abroad. A foreign company or juristic partnership carrying on business in Thailand is assessed on the profits of that business under the Thai rules. Check any deemed local business presence and separate withholding on specified Thai payments to foreign entities. [Revenue Code, ss 40–41](https://www.rd.go.th/5937.html) · [Corporate provisions, ss 65–76](https://www.rd.go.th/5939.html) · [Revenue Department guidance on orders P.161 and P.162](https://www.rd.go.th/fileadmin/download/news/question_p161_162.pdf)

## Residence

An individual is Thai resident for a tax year once their days in Thailand total at least 180 in that calendar year. Add separate stays in the same year. Apply the definition in the Revenue Code and check the treaty's residence rules separately.

For ordinary corporate income tax, identify a company or juristic partnership formed under Thai law or under foreign law. In the latter case, check whether it carries on business in Thailand, including relevant agency or representative arrangements. Check any specific treaty's residence and permanent establishment tests. [Revenue Code, s 41](https://www.rd.go.th/5937.html) · [Revenue Code, ss 66 and 76 bis](https://www.rd.go.th/5939.html) · [Revenue Department foreign income guidance](https://www.rd.go.th/fileadmin/user_upload/porphor/GuideTaxFromAbroad_EN.pdf)

## Taxpayer types

A sole trader generally accounts for their business income as an individual. A nonjuristic ordinary partnership or body of persons can be a separate unit under the personal income tax rules. Check the income tax treatment of amounts distributed to its partners or members. A registered juristic partnership is assessed under the corporate rules. Confirm the legal form before choosing the return. [Revenue Code, ss 39–40 and 56](https://www.rd.go.th/5937.html) · [Revenue Code, s 65](https://www.rd.go.th/5939.html) · [Revenue Department instructions for ordinary partnerships](https://www.rd.go.th/fileadmin/user_upload/kormor/970658dn149.pdf)

## Currency

Thai baht (THB) is the ordinary reporting currency. Keep source amounts, dates and exchange rates. Check the applicable conversion method and the notification or approval rules if a company or juristic partnership uses another permitted functional currency. Pay the tax in baht under the conversion rules. [Revenue Department foreign income and conversion guide](https://www.rd.go.th/fileadmin/user_upload/porphor/GuideTaxFromAbroad_EN.pdf) · [Revenue Code, ss 65 bis and 76 ter to 76 sexies](https://www.rd.go.th/5939.html)

## Assessment periods

The individual tax year is the calendar year. A company or juristic partnership normally uses a 12-month accounting period. A shorter first period, an approved change or certain cessation events may give a different period. Check the filing and instalment rules separately. [Revenue Code, ss 39 and 56](https://www.rd.go.th/5937.html) · [Corporate accounting period, s 65](https://www.rd.go.th/5939.html)

## Responsible authorities

Thailand's Revenue Department administers income tax. Use its filing services and check the competent local revenue office. Thai Customs administers import procedures and border charges. [Revenue Department](https://www.rd.go.th/english/) · [Thai Customs](https://www.customs.go.th/)

## Fictional intake

An individual spends 180 days in Thailand in 2025, when they earn foreign investment income, then remits part of it in 2026. Record the days, the source, the amount remitted and the earning and remittance years before checking the 2026 return and any treaty relief.
