# Bahrain tax system

> General orientation for 2026. Confirm the taxpayer, period and transaction with a qualified local tax professional before applying the rules.

The National Bureau for Revenue administers VAT, excise and domestic minimum top-up tax. Amounts are in Bahraini dinars (BHD). [Bahrain government, VAT](https://www.bahrain.bh/new/en/business-vat_en.html) · [National Bureau for Revenue, DMTT scope guide](https://s3-me-south-1.amazonaws.com/nbrproduserdata-bh/media/NOVozf75Slo0Rl63KY4DMsggAhiypTJrRHT74V5d.pdf)

## Income, business and residence

The Bahrain Economic Development Board's 2026 factsheet identifies no ordinary personal income tax. Check social insurance, end-of-service and foreign-country tax separately. Immigration residence, treaty residence and VAT residence can require different checks. [Bahrain Economic Development Board, 2026 manufacturing factsheet, archived in March 2026](https://web.archive.org/web/20260321041012/https://www.bahrainedb.com/app/uploads/2026/01/Bahrain-EDB-MFG-Factsheet-2026.pdf) · [National Bureau for Revenue, VAT general guide, residence and registration](https://nbrproduserdata-ir.s3.eu-west-1.amazonaws.com/media/TjifwIUNJsy20MvW1ueSYqyColOmV9V8vqxUZIK9.pdf)

For 2026, the income tax under Decree-Law 22 of 1979 applies at 46% to the defined Bahrain hydrocarbon exploration, production or refinery activities, including for an entity formed elsewhere. The tax follows the taxpayer's annual accounting period. Keep books on a Gregorian calendar-year basis unless the minister approves another basis. The separate minimum top-up tax applies to qualifying Bahrain entities of multinational groups for fiscal years starting on or after 1 January 2025 under Decree-Law 11 of 2024. Test consolidated group revenue of at least EUR 750 million in at least 2 of the 4 immediately preceding fiscal years. Adjust the threshold proportionately for each of those years that is shorter or longer than 12 months. The 15% minimum is an effective-rate test under that regime. Check any later general corporate income tax enactment and commencement for periods after 2026. [Income Tax Law, arts. 2 and 6](https://lloc.gov.bh/Legislation/HTM/L2279) · [Decree-Law 11 of 2024, arts. 1, 3 and 44](https://lloc.gov.bh/Legislation/HTM/L1124) · [National Bureau for Revenue, DMTT scope guide](https://s3-me-south-1.amazonaws.com/nbrproduserdata-bh/media/NOVozf75Slo0Rl63KY4DMsggAhiypTJrRHT74V5d.pdf)

## VAT and filing

The standard VAT rate is 10% from 1 January 2022. A resident business generally registers if relevant taxable supplies exceed BHD 37,500 in the previous 12 months or are expected to exceed that amount in the next 12 months. Check zero-rating, exemptions, non-resident registration and reverse charge separately. [Bahrain government, VAT rate](https://www.bahrain.bh/new/en/business-vat_en.html) · [National Bureau for Revenue, VAT general guide, section 3.2.2](https://nbrproduserdata-ir.s3.eu-west-1.amazonaws.com/media/TjifwIUNJsy20MvW1ueSYqyColOmV9V8vqxUZIK9.pdf)

Ordinarily, annual supplies above BHD 3 million give monthly periods and supplies at or below that level give quarterly periods, subject to the assigned period and exceptions. Submit the VAT return and pay by the last day of the month after the period ends. Check the separate returns, notifications and payment dates for the hydrocarbon and minimum top-up tax regimes. [National Bureau for Revenue, VAT general guide, sections 13.2–13.3](https://nbrproduserdata-ir.s3.eu-west-1.amazonaws.com/media/TjifwIUNJsy20MvW1ueSYqyColOmV9V8vqxUZIK9.pdf) · [National Bureau for Revenue, DMTT administrative guide](https://s3-me-south-1.amazonaws.com/nbrproduserdata-bh/media/GTZmFbeFNOZmQVoXrMkbw4liKAiMjl79b56Q7LaL.pdf)
