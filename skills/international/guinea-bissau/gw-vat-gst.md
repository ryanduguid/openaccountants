# Guinea-Bissau VAT — Imposto sobre o Valor Acrescentado (IVA)

> **Every rate and threshold in the previous version was correct.** The nine
> facts it carried from one commercial host all survived reading the statute.
> What follows adds what it did not have: the statute itself, the 5% simplified
> regime, a domestic withholding obligation, the fiscal-representative rule, the
> filing deadlines, and the 30% import surcharge.

## 1. The tax, and what it replaced

- **Código do IVA — Lei nº 4/2022** — Approved as an annex to Lei nº 4/2022,
  published in the **4th supplement to Boletim Oficial nº 8 of 25 February
  2022**. It is a harmonised consumption tax to be interpreted in conformity
  with the **UEMOA and CEDEAO** directives (art. 1.º)  _(Código do IVA, Lei nº 4/2022, arts. 1.º–2.º — https://kontaktu.mef.gw/legislation)_
- **What was repealed** — The **Código do Imposto Geral sobre Vendas e Serviços
  (IGV), approved by Lei nº 16/97 de 31 de março**, and **Decreto nº 33/89 de 27
  de dezembro** (Imposto sobre o Turismo), together with all other legislation on
  matters the IGV Code governed. References to IGV provisions are read as
  references to their IVA counterparts  _(Lei nº 4/2022, arts. 2.º and 7.º — https://kontaktu.mef.gw/legislation)_
- **The IGV's standard rate was also 19%, but it is not a like-for-like swap** —
  The repealed IGV ran **10%** on an annexed list, **15% on the import and supply
  of electricity and water**, and **19%** on everything else. The IVA has no 15%
  band, so electricity and water move from 15% to whichever IVA rate applies to
  them under Anexo I  _(Código do IGV, art. 18.º as worded by Lei nº 02/2018, now repealed — https://kontaktu.mef.gw/legislation)_
- **Commencement** — 1 January 2025 ((approx — confirm; the DGCI's consolidated
  text carries no commencement article for Lei nº 4/2022, and this date is
  carried from a secondary source, not from the statute))  _(Código do IVA (as described at [vatupdate.com](https://www.vatupdate.com/2025/01/16/guinea-bissau-introduction-of-the-vat-regime/)))_

## 2. Rates — Artigo 18.º

| Supply | Rate |
| --- | :---: |
| Imports and supplies listed in **Anexo I** to the Code | **10%** |
| All other imports, supplies of goods and services | **19%** |
| Exports | **0%** |

The rate in force is the one applying at the moment the tax becomes chargeable
(art. 18.º, final paragraph). For bundled goods forming a distinct commercial product, nº 2
decides which rate applies to the whole. Finance leases take the rate that would
apply to a transfer of the leased asset (nº 3).  _(Código do IVA, art. 18.º — https://kontaktu.mef.gw/legislation)_

## 3. Three regimes, by turnover — Artigos 37.º, 38.º and 45.º

| Annual turnover (prior calendar year) | Regime | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **≥ FCFA 40,000,000** | Normal (art. 37.º) | Full IVA: charge at 10/19/0%, deduct input tax, monthly return |
| **FCFA 10,000,000 – 40,000,000** | Simplified (art. 38.º) | **5% of the value of supplies**, and **no input deduction at all** |
| **< FCFA 10,000,000** | Small taxpayers (art. 45.º) | **Exempt from IVA**, subject instead to a single small-taxpayer tax *"em termos a fixar por lei"* |

- **The simplified regime is a turnover tax, not a reduced VAT** — 5% applied to
  the value of supplies, excluding sales of tangible investment goods; **no input
  tax is deductible from it** (nº 3); and an invoice issued under it **gives the
  buyer no right of deduction**, which must be stated expressly on the invoice
  (art. 39.º). A buyer who treats such an invoice as recoverable input tax is
  wrong on both sides  _(Código do IVA, arts. 38.º nos 1–3 and 39.º — https://kontaktu.mef.gw/legislation)_
- **A single isolated transaction can bring you into the normal regime** — Art.
  37.º nº 2 puts an art. 6.º(1)(b) person into the normal regime on one
  transaction of FCFA 40,000,000 or more  _(Código do IVA, art. 37.º nº 2 — https://kontaktu.mef.gw/legislation)_
- **Moving between regimes** — A simplified-regime taxpayer moves compulsorily to
  the normal regime once the art. 38.º amount is exceeded in two consecutive
  years; the request is filed in December and takes effect from 1 January. A
  normal-regime taxpayer meeting the art. 38.º conditions for two consecutive
  years may elect down (art. 43.º). Stock held at the start of the year may be
  deducted on moving up  _(Código do IVA, arts. 43.º–44.º — https://kontaktu.mef.gw/legislation)_
- **The Code's own cross-reference is out by one** — Art. 37.º nº 6 refers to
  *"o regime de pequenos contribuintes previstos no artigo 46º"*. The small
  taxpayer regime is **art. 45.º**; art. 46.º is *Garantias*. [RESEARCH GAP —
  reviewer to confirm against the Boletim Oficial text whether the printed
  cross-reference or the article numbering is the error.]  _(Código do IVA, arts. 37.º nº 6, 45.º and 46.º — https://kontaktu.mef.gw/legislation)_

## 4. Withholding, reverse charge and non-residents — Artigos 7.º and 33.º

- **A domestic withholding the previous version had no trace of** — A
  normal-regime taxpayer must **withhold the whole of the IVA charged to it on an
  invoice** issued by (a) a **simplified-regime** supplier, or (b) a
  normal-regime supplier the director-general has designated a **"contribuinte de
  risco"**. This is a full withholding of the tax on the invoice, not a partial
  one, and the risk list is an administrative designation a buyer has to check  _(Código do IVA, art. 7.º nº 3 — https://kontaktu.mef.gw/legislation)_
- **Reverse charge on services from non-established suppliers** — The
  art. 6.º(1)(a) **recipient** is the taxable person on services supplied in
  national territory by taxable persons without a seat here  _(Código do IVA, art. 7.º nº 1 — https://kontaktu.mef.gw/legislation)_
- **Non-residents must appoint a fiscal representative** — A non-resident without
  a permanent establishment carrying out taxable operations located in
  Guinea-Bissau **must** appoint a representative who is himself a taxable person.
  The representative meets every obligation under the Code including
  registration, is the debtor for the tax, and the appointment must be
  communicated to the counterparty **before** the operation. The represented
  person is **jointly and severally liable**  _(Código do IVA, arts. 7.º nº 2 and 33.º — https://kontaktu.mef.gw/legislation)_
- **Improperly showing IVA on an invoice makes you a taxable person** — Art.
  6.º(1)(d) brings anyone who wrongly mentions IVA on an invoice into the charge  _(Código do IVA, art. 6.º nº 1(d) — https://kontaktu.mef.gw/legislation)_
- **A 30% flat surcharge on imports by IVA non-declarants** — Imports by taxable
  persons appearing on the director-general's list of IVA non-declarants carry an
  **additional flat rate of 30% on the customs value**. The list is updated
  **monthly**  _(Lei nº 4/2022, art. 6.º — https://kontaktu.mef.gw/legislation)_

## 5. Returns and payment

- **Normal regime — monthly, by the 15th** — The *declaração periódica* is filed
  by the **15th of the month following** the operations it covers, at the
  competent office or through the authorised banking network, and the tax is paid
  within the same deadline. **The return is due even if there were no taxable
  operations in the period**  _(Código do IVA, arts. 25.º nº 1 and 31.º nos 1 and 3 — https://kontaktu.mef.gw/legislation)_
- **Simplified regime — quarterly** — Paid by *guia* on an approved form for each
  calendar quarter: **1st quarter by the last working day of April; 2nd by the
  last working day of July; 3rd by the last working day of October; 4th by the
  last working day of January following**. An annex of purchases and sales or
  services is filed with the quarterly declaration, and that declaration is also
  **mandatory even with no operations** in the period  _(Código do IVA, art. 42.º nos 1 and 3 — https://kontaktu.mef.gw/legislation)_
- **One-off operations — *declaração avulsa*** — Art. 6.º(1)(b) and (d) persons
  file by the **end of the month in which the taxable operation took place**, with
  payment on the same deadline  _(Código do IVA, arts. 25.º nº 2 and 32.º — https://kontaktu.mef.gw/legislation)_
- **On cessation** — Payment and the final declaration are due within **30 days**
  of cessation. A declaration of commencement is filed before activity starts; a
  declaration of alteration within **15 days** of any change  _(Código do IVA, arts. 28.º (Obrigações de registo) nos 2–3 and 42.º nº 2 — https://kontaktu.mef.gw/legislation)_
- **Assessment by the administration** — Where the services assess the tax, the
  taxpayer is notified to pay within **30 days**  _(Código do IVA, art. 26.º (Pagamento por iniciativa da administração) — https://kontaktu.mef.gw/legislation)_
- **Records** — Books, registers and supporting documentation are kept for the
  **five subsequent calendar years**  _(Código do IVA, art. 41.º — https://kontaktu.mef.gw/legislation)_

## 6. Deduction and exemptions

- **Input VAT recovery** — Normal-regime taxpayers deduct input tax against output
  tax. Where a proportion is used, the provisional percentage is based on the
  prior year's operations and corrected to the definitive figures for the year in
  question, with the resulting regularisation  _(Código do IVA, art. 23.º (Dedução parcial) — https://kontaktu.mef.gw/legislation)_
- **Simplified regime: no deduction** — see §3  _(Código do IVA, art. 38.º nº 3 — https://kontaktu.mef.gw/legislation)_
- **Exempt supplies** — Financial services, real estate, domestic gas/heating,
  education and medicines ((approx — confirm against **Título II, arts. 13.º–15.º**
  of the Code, which was not read line by line for this pass; the list here is
  carried from a secondary source))  _(Código do IVA, Título II (as described at [vatupdate.com](https://www.vatupdate.com/2025/03/07/)))_
- **Credit notes carried over from the IGV** — IGV credit notes issued in the four
  years before the IVA Code came into force are recognised for IVA, on the
  director-general's decision and after a prior inspection. IGV exemptions granted
  administratively were revoked when the IGV Code was repealed  _(Lei nº 4/2022, arts. 4.º–5.º — https://kontaktu.mef.gw/legislation)_

## 7. What this replaces

| The guide said | The Código says |
| --- | --- |
| 19% / 10% / 0% | **Confirmed** — art. 18.º |
| Thresholds FCFA 40,000,000 and 10,000,000 | **Confirmed** — arts. 37.º and 38.º |
| "replaced the former 19% general sales tax" | True of the headline rate; the IGV also had a **15%** band for electricity and water that the IVA does not carry |
| "simplified VAT scheme" | A **5% turnover tax with no input deduction**, whose invoices give the buyer no deduction right |
| "below FCFA 10,000,000 is exempt from VAT registration" | Exempt from IVA **and** subject to a single small-taxpayer tax to be fixed by law (art. 45.º) |
| "Monthly returns ((approx — confirm due date))" | **By the 15th** of the following month, nil returns mandatory (art. 31.º) — and the simplified regime is **quarterly** |
| "Reverse charge ((approx — confirm mechanism))" | Confirmed (art. 7.º nº 1) — and non-residents without a PE must appoint a **fiscal representative** (art. 33.º) |
| *nothing* | Normal-regime buyers **withhold the entire IVA** invoiced by simplified-regime and "risk" suppliers (art. 7.º nº 3) |
| *nothing* | **30% flat surcharge** on imports by IVA non-declarants (Lei nº 4/2022 art. 6.º) |

Every figure above except those marked "(approx — confirm)" was read from the
Direcção Geral das Contribuições e Impostos' own consolidated text.
