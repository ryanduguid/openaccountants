# Gabon Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax (Impôt sur les Sociétés)

- **Overview** — Companies operating in Gabon are subject to the Impôt sur les Sociétés (IS) on Gabon-source profits, with minimum-tax floors and instalment payments. Rates and deadlines below are a 2025 draft for accountant review.
- **Standard CIT rate** — 30%  _(Code Général des Impôts 2025 (Gabon), art. 16 — https://dgi.ga/document/code-general-des-impots-2025/)_
- **CIT rate — oil and mining sectors** — 35%  _(Code Général des Impôts 2025 (Gabon), art. 16 — https://dgi.ga/document/code-general-des-impots-2025/)_
- **Tax base** — Net profit from all operations of the period, including disposals of any asset during or at the end of the business; gains on fixed assets sold during the business can be deferred if booked to a reinvestment reserve and reinvested within three years  _(Code Général des Impôts 2025 (Gabon), arts. 8 and 9 — https://dgi.ga/document/code-general-des-impots-2025/)_
- **Corporate residence / territoriality** — IS is due on profits from operations carried out in Gabon by resident companies (registered in Gabon or with their place of effective management there) and by non-resident companies with a permanent establishment, the latter only on profits attributable to the establishment or to similar sales and activities in Gabon  _(Code Général des Impôts 2025 (Gabon), art. 5 — https://dgi.ga/document/code-general-des-impots-2025/)_
- **Impôt Minimum Forfaitaire (IMF)** — 1% of global turnover of the year (gross turnover on all operations, including ancillary products, financial income and exchange gains, rounded down to the thousand)  _(Code Général des Impôts 2025 (Gabon), arts. 24 and 25 — https://dgi.ga/document/code-general-des-impots-2025/)_
- **Minimum de Perception (CIT floor)** — XAF 500,000 — CIT cannot fall below the higher of this floor and the IMF, even with no profit; the DGI's corporate tax web page still shows XAF 1,000,000  _(Code Général des Impôts 2025 (Gabon), art. 24 as amended by the 2024 and 2025 finance laws — https://dgi.ga/document/code-general-des-impots-2025/)_; _(DGI, Impôt sur les sociétés — https://dgi.ga/imposition-des-personnes-morales/impot-sur-les-societes/)_
- **Minimum-tax relief — new/SME companies** — IS-exempt entities and newly registered companies that have never traded in Gabon are exempt from the IMF and the floor; newly registered companies are also exempt for their first two loss-making years, extended to three years for PME/PMI under Law 008/2018, unless activity began at least two years before registration  _(Code Général des Impôts 2025 (Gabon), art. 26 — https://dgi.ga/document/code-general-des-impots-2025/)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends (non-treaty)** — 20% final levy (IRCM), withheld by the paying institution and remitted within 30 days of payment; 10% on dividends received by venture capital companies during the holding period  _(Code Général des Impôts 2025 (Gabon), arts. 109, 110 and 111 — https://dgi.ga/document/code-general-des-impots-2025/)_
- **Withholding tax on interest (non-treaty)** — 20% final levy, including on interest from claims and deposits paid to persons without a real domicile in Gabon; 15% on bank bons de caisse and 10% on bonds of at least five years issued in Gabon  _(Code Général des Impôts 2025 (Gabon), arts. 109 and 110 — https://dgi.ga/document/code-general-des-impots-2025/)_
- **Withholding tax on royalties / service fees (non-treaty)** — 25% of the gross amount excluding turnover taxes, on royalties, industrial property income and services supplied or used in Gabon paid to persons or companies without a permanent professional installation in Gabon  _(Code Général des Impôts 2025 (Gabon), art. 206 — https://dgi.ga/document/code-general-des-impots-2025/)_
- **Withholding on non-resident companies without a PE** — 25% withholding by the Gabonese payer, remitted with a return within the first 15 days of the month after payment  _(Code Général des Impôts 2025 (Gabon), art. 206 — https://dgi.ga/document/code-general-des-impots-2025/)_
- **Annual CIT return filing deadline** — Before 30 April of the following year  _(Code Général des Impôts 2025 (Gabon), art. 20 — https://dgi.ga/document/code-general-des-impots-2025/)_
- **First CIT instalment** — One quarter of the tax paid for the previous year, due 30 November without notice; the company may reduce instalments by special declaration if it expects a lower liability  _(Code Général des Impôts 2025 (Gabon), art. 21 — https://dgi.ga/document/code-general-des-impots-2025/)_
- **Second CIT instalment** — One third of the tax paid for the previous year, due 30 January  _(Code Général des Impôts 2025 (Gabon), art. 21 — https://dgi.ga/document/code-general-des-impots-2025/)_
- **Final CIT balance** — Balance paid with the return by 30 April at the latest; tax paid above the amount due becomes a credit against later years  _(Code Général des Impôts 2025 (Gabon), arts. 21 and 21 bis — https://dgi.ga/document/code-general-des-impots-2025/)_
- **2026 finance law (Law 041/2025 of 29 December 2025)** — Raises the final levy on gains from share sales by non-resident shareholders from 20% to 25% and the withholding on rents from 5% to 10%, collected quarterly; e-invoicing becomes a condition of deductibility (RESEARCH GAP: the Journal Officiel text was not read; figures from a Deloitte summary)  _(Deloitte, Gabon : les principales mesures de la Loi de Finances pour 2026 — https://blog.avocats.deloitte.fr/gabon-les-principales-mesures-de-la-loi-de-finances-pour-2026/)_
