# Gabon Company Formation & Entity Choice

## Entity types, capital and incorporation

For the **2025** guide period, Gabon's national SARL capital and statutes
rules are sourced to **Loi n°028/2018 of 21 April 2020**, published in
**Journal Officiel n°65 of 1 May 2020**. Article 2 replaces articles 2 and 3
of Loi n°13/2016 of 5 September 2016. Members set total capital freely in the
statutes, divided into equal shares with a nominal value of at least
**CFA 5,000**. [Official Gazette, replacement article 2](https://journal-officiel.ga/4761-028-2018-/)

The revised OHADA AUSCGIE took effect on **5 May 2014**. Its SARL capital
default under article 311 is subject to contrary national provisions. Read
Gabon’s national capital rule above with the remaining uniform rules below.
[OHADA company law register](https://www.ohada.org/droit-des-societes-commerciales-et-du-gie/),
[AUSCGIE, article 311](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974#page=76)

- **Company law framework.** The national SARL capital and statutes provisions below come from Loi n°028/2018. The remaining subscription, pay-up and SA capital provisions cite the AUSCGIE. Current agency procedures, costs and timelines still need primary-source verification. [Loi n°028/2018](https://journal-officiel.ga/4761-028-2018-/), [AUSCGIE](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974)
- **Limited liability company** — **SARL** (Société à Responsabilité Limitée): the members answer for the company's debts only up to their contributions and their rights are represented by *parts sociales*. **It may be instituted by a single natural or legal person** (the SARLU in local usage), or between two or more. The name must carry *"société à responsabilité limitée"* or *"S.A.R.L."*  _(AUSCGIE 2014, arts. 309–310 — https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974)_
- **Public/joint-stock company** — **SA** (Société Anonyme): shareholders liable only up to their contributions, rights represented by shares, and ***"La société anonyme peut ne comprendre qu'un seul actionnaire."*** The name must carry *"société anonyme"* or *"S.A."* **and the mode of administration** under article 414  _(AUSCGIE 2014, arts. 385–386 — https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974)_
- **Simplified joint-stock company** — SAS (Société par Actions Simplifiée) — flexible governance, available under OHADA  _(OHADA Uniform Act on Commercial Companies (AUSCGIE))_
- **SARL capital.** Members freely fix total capital in the statutes under replacement article 2 of Loi n°13/2016. [Loi n°028/2018, article 2](https://journal-officiel.ga/4761-028-2018-/)
- **SARL share values.** Capital is divided into equal shares, each with a nominal value of at least **CFA 5,000**. [Loi n°028/2018, replacement article 2](https://journal-officiel.ga/4761-028-2018-/)
- **SARL pay-up on formation** — Parts sociales must be **subscribed in full**; those for contributions in kind **paid up in full**; those for cash paid up on subscription to **at least half** of nominal value, balance within **two years** of RCCM registration. A ***commissaire aux apports*** is compulsory once a contribution in kind, or all of them together, exceeds **5,000,000**  _(AUSCGIE 2014, arts. 311-1 and 312 — https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974)_
- **SA minimum share capital**: **10,000,000** in CFA francs. This is the ordinary SA minimum under article 387; public offerings and regulated activities require separate capital checks. The nominal amount of the shares is **freely fixed by the statutes** and must be a whole number XAF  _(AUSCGIE 2014, art. 387: https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974)_
- **SA subscription and pay-up** — The capital must be **entirely subscribed before the date the statutes are signed**; cash shares are paid up on subscription to **at least one quarter** of nominal value, the balance within a period not exceeding **three years** from RCCM registration  _(AUSCGIE 2014, arts. 388–389 — https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974)_
- **Registered office requirement** — A physical registered office in Gabon is mandatory  _(OHADA Uniform Act on Commercial Companies (AUSCGIE) — https://www.expanship.com/ga/blog/incorporation-requirements-in-gabon.html)_
- **Share capital deposit** — The uniform act requires the founder to deposit the paid-up funds **immediately**, against receipt, with a bank or any other duly approved credit or **microfinance** institution, into an account in the name of the company in formation, **or with a notary**. Unless national provisions say otherwise, payment and deposit are recorded by a notary of the registered-office district in a *déclaration notariée de souscription et de versement*, and the funds are **blocked until RCCM registration**. The commercial description of "a Gabonese bank account before notarisation, with a bank deposit certificate" is one way of satisfying this, not the only one the Act allows  _(AUSCGIE 2014, arts. 313–314 — https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974)_
- **Form of SARL statutes.** Replacement article 3 permits a private deed or
  notarial act. Deposit of private-deed statutes in a notary's records with
  recognition of writing and signatures is optional. Private-deed statutes
  require certification by the body responsible for enterprise creation before
  registration. Certification requires a signature at the foot of the statutes
  and the body's stamp on each page. Current submission procedures, required
  documents and fees still need verification.
  [Loi n°028/2018, replacement article 3](https://journal-officiel.ga/4761-028-2018-/)
- **Incorporation timeline** — Typically 1–2 weeks via ANPI-Gabon (approx — confirm)  _(ANPI-Gabon — https://www.expanship.com/ga/blog/incorporation-requirements-in-gabon.html)_
- **Indicative administrative formation cost** — Roughly XAF 200,000–500,000 in administrative costs (plus notary/registration fees) (approx — confirm)  _(ANPI-Gabon — https://www.expanship.com/ga/blog/incorporation-requirements-in-gabon.html)_
- **Beneficial ownership disclosure** — Ultimate beneficial owner (UBO) disclosure required at registration  _(OHADA / ANPI-Gabon registration requirements — https://www.expanship.com/ga/blog/incorporation-requirements-in-gabon.html)_
- **Annual accounts**: see [the selected AUDCIF rules below](#selected-accounting-rules-under-the-2017-audcif) for preparation, scope, accounting regime and records, and the separate AUSCGIE section for annual decisions and RCCM deposits. National submission procedures remain to be verified.
- **Annual tax compliance** — CIT return before 30 April with instalments on 30 November and 30 January, monthly VAT return by the 20th, payroll withholding paid within the first 15 days of the following month, and the patente instalment before 1 March  _(Code Général des Impôts 2025 (Gabon), arts. 20, 21, 96, 237 and 265 — https://dgi.ga/document/code-general-des-impots-2025/)_
- **Business licence tax (patente), 2025** — Annual fixed duty set by profession in the tariff annexed to the code: Tableau A fixed duties run from XAF 15,000 to 540,000, with per-unit duties for the Tableau B and C professions; companies and real-regime traders pay an instalment equal to the previous year's contribution before 1 March.  _(Code Général des Impôts 2025 (Gabon), arts. 253, 265, 266 and the tariff under art. 271 — https://dgi.ga/document/code-general-des-impots-2025/)_

## Patente provisions enacted for 2026

Loi n°041/2025, signed on **29 December 2025** and published in
**Journal Officiel n°96 Quater of 30 December 2025**, changes the patente
for the **2026** guide comparison. Replacement article 252 covers persons
carrying on a trade, industry or profession in Gabon, subject to the article
254 exemptions. Replacement article 254(18) exempts newly created companies
for their **first financial year**. Other exemptions and the precise scope
of an activity require separate checks. [Replacement articles 252 and 254](https://journal-officiel.ga/22265-041-2025-/)

Replacement article 265 uses the prior year's annual products or receipts,
as defined by article 62 for minimum-tax purposes. It sets the patente at
**0.1% of turnover excluding tax**, subject to a **CFA 150,000 minimum** and
**CFA 10,000,000 maximum**. Article 265(3) requires self-declaration and full
payment **before 1 March of the tax year**. Article 253 and articles 255
to 258 are deleted. Later amendments, the full article 62 base and current
filing procedures still need verification. [Replacement article 265](https://journal-officiel.ga/22265-041-2025-/)

The printed article 62 reference needs clarification. In the DGI's **CGI
2025** compilation, article 62 concerns securities-income exemptions,
whilst articles 24 and 25 set out the IS minimum tax and its base. That
compilation does not resolve the cross-reference in the **2026** law.
Obtain an operative correction or tax-authority clarification before
settling the full patente base. [CGI 2025, articles 24, 25 and 62](https://dgi.ga/document/code-general-des-impots-2025/)

For example, an existing taxable company with a qualifying prior-year base
of **CFA 200,000,000** has a patente of **CFA 200,000** at **0.1%**. A base
of **CFA 50,000,000** produces **CFA 50,000** before the minimum, so the
patente is **CFA 150,000**. A base of **CFA 20,000,000,000** produces
**CFA 20,000,000** before the maximum, so the patente is **CFA 10,000,000**.
These examples assume the activity is taxable and no exemption applies.
[Replacement articles 254 and 265](https://journal-officiel.ga/22265-041-2025-/)

## National SARL capital example

For the capital rule alone, **20 equal shares at CFA 5,000 each** give
**CFA 100,000** total capital. The statutes fix the total and each share meets
the national minimum nominal value. Subscription, pay-up and other formation
obligations still apply, including certification for private-deed statutes.
[Loi n°028/2018, replacement articles 2 and 3](https://journal-officiel.ga/4761-028-2018-/)

## Annual company reporting under the 2014 AUSCGIE

The revised AUSCGIE took effect on **5 May 2014**. The following rules address
annual SARL and SA decisions and commercial-company registry deposits.
[OHADA company law register](https://www.ohada.org/droit-des-societes-commerciales-et-du-gie/)

- **SARL annual meeting.** The annual ordinary general meeting must take place
  within **6 months after the financial year closes**. The managers may ask the
  competent court for an extension. [AUSCGIE, articles 347 and 348](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974#page=86)
- **SA annual meeting.** The ordinary general meeting must take place at least
  once a year, within **6 months after the financial year closes**, subject to
  an extension by court decision. [AUSCGIE, article 548](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974#page=133)
- **Sole-member companies.** The sole shareholder of an SA takes the annual
  ordinary decisions within **6 months after the financial year closes** and
  records them in minutes kept in the company's archives. Article 347 applies
  these sole-member decision rules to a SARL, with the exceptions it specifies.
  [AUSCGIE, article 347](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974#page=86),
  [articles 558 to 560](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974#page=135)
- **Deposit of annual accounts.** A commercial company must deposit the prior
  year's annual financial statements with the RCCM in the state of its
  registered office **within the month following approval** by the competent
  body. If approval is refused, a copy of the refusal decision must be deposited
  within the same period. [AUSCGIE, article 269, pages 65 and 66](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974#page=65)

For example, for a multi-member SARL with a **31 December 2025** year-end, the
ordinary annual meeting is due by **30 June 2026**, subject to a court extension.
Track the RCCM deposit deadline from the actual approval decision. Record the
national tax-return deadline separately. The current registry filing method,
fees, applicable accounting and audit rules and any sector-specific obligations
still need verification. [AUSCGIE, articles 269 and 348](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974)

## Selected accounting rules under the 2017 AUDCIF

This reference summarises selected provisions of the original AUDCIF adopted on
26 January 2017. Article 113 expressly fixes commencement at **1 January 2018**
for individual entity accounts and **1 January 2019** for consolidated accounts,
combined accounts and IFRS statements. It also contains a general
publication-based clause; the dates here come from its express commencement
paragraph. Verify later amendments, accounting-standard updates and the rules
for the entity's actual period before use.
[AUDCIF, article 113, PDF page 53](https://justice.sec.gouv.sn/wp-content/uploads/textes-reglements/OHADA/Acte-uniforme-relatif-droit-comptable-information-financiere-JAN-2017-JO.pdf#page=53)

### Scope and accounting regime

Articles 1 and 2 impose accounting duties on entities within their scope,
including commercial companies, and exclude entities governed by public
accounting rules. Article 5 establishes SYSCOHADA but excludes credit
institutions, microfinance institutions, financial-market participants, insurers
and reinsurers, social security and welfare bodies and non-profit entities
from that accounting system. Exclusion from SYSCOHADA does not by itself exempt
an entity from the AUDCIF's accounting duties. Verify the applicable sector or
non-profit framework separately.
[AUDCIF, articles 1, 2 and 5, PDF pages 16 and 17](https://justice.sec.gouv.sn/wp-content/uploads/textes-reglements/OHADA/Acte-uniforme-relatif-droit-comptable-information-financiere-JAN-2017-JO.pdf#page=16)

- **Normal system.** Article 11 makes the normal system the default, subject
  to the size exception. Its annual statements comprise a balance sheet, income
  statement, cash flow statement and notes.
  [AUDCIF, articles 8, 11 and 26, PDF pages 18, 19 and 25](https://justice.sec.gouv.sn/wp-content/uploads/textes-reglements/OHADA/Acte-uniforme-relatif-droit-comptable-information-financiere-JAN-2017-JO.pdf#page=18)
- **Minimal cash system (SMT).** Under article 13, small entities use SMT unless
  they opt otherwise. Eligibility requires annual turnover excluding tax
  **below CFA 60,000,000 for trading, CFA 40,000,000 for craft and similar
  activities or CFA 30,000,000 for services**, or the equivalent in the member
  state's legal currency. These are accounting thresholds, not tax-regime
  thresholds. Check activity classification, any election and the applicable
  local-currency equivalent before selecting SMT. Article 28 requires a balance
  sheet, income statement and notes based on cash accounting, taking changes
  in inventories, receivables and debts into account when material.
  [AUDCIF, articles 13 and 28, PDF pages 20 and 25](https://justice.sec.gouv.sn/wp-content/uploads/textes-reglements/OHADA/Acte-uniforme-relatif-droit-comptable-information-financiere-JAN-2017-JO.pdf#page=20)
- **Listed entities and public offerings.** Article 8 requires annual IFRS
  statements in addition to the other annual statements for entities whose
  securities are exchange-listed or which seek financing through a public
  offering. The IFRS statements are for financial markets and do not determine
  distributable profit. Group-accounting requirements need a separate assessment.
  [AUDCIF, article 8, PDF pages 18 and 19](https://justice.sec.gouv.sn/wp-content/uploads/textes-reglements/OHADA/Acte-uniforme-relatif-droit-comptable-information-financiere-JAN-2017-JO.pdf#page=19)

### Annual timetable and records

Article 7 sets an ordinary **12-month calendar accounting year**, with specific
first-year and liquidation exceptions. Article 23 requires annual statements to
be drawn up (*arrêtés*) **within 4 months after year-end**, with that date
mentioned whenever they are transmitted. This drawing-up date is distinct from
shareholder approval, an RCCM deposit and a national tax-return deadline.
[AUDCIF, articles 7 and 23, PDF pages 18 and 24](https://justice.sec.gouv.sn/wp-content/uploads/textes-reglements/OHADA/Acte-uniforme-relatif-droit-comptable-information-financiere-JAN-2017-JO.pdf#page=24)

Under article 71, the governing or management bodies draw up an inventory,
annual statements and a management report, with a social report where
applicable. The management report covers the past year, prospects, liquidity
and financing, and significant events after year-end up to its preparation.
Where statutory auditors exist, the documents go to them **at least 45 days
before the ordinary general meeting**. Article 70 addresses auditors appointed
voluntarily or mandatorily; it does not establish that every entity must appoint
one. Verify appointment duties and exemptions for the legal form and sector.
[AUDCIF, articles 70 and 71, PDF pages 39 and 40](https://justice.sec.gouv.sn/wp-content/uploads/textes-reglements/OHADA/Acte-uniforme-relatif-droit-comptable-information-financiere-JAN-2017-JO.pdf#page=39)

Article 16 requires an accounting procedures and organisation manual, updated
periodically. Article 17 addresses the official language, legal currency,
double-entry records, dated supporting documents and inventory controls.
Article 19 lists the journal, general ledger, general trial balance and
inventory book. SMT bookkeeping follows its own provisions in article 21.
Article 24 requires accounting books or equivalent documents and supporting
vouchers to be retained for **10 years**. It does not set the starting point
for that period in its text; verify that point and any additional national
retention rules before destroying records.
[AUDCIF, articles 16 to 24, PDF pages 20 to 24](https://justice.sec.gouv.sn/wp-content/uploads/textes-reglements/OHADA/Acte-uniforme-relatif-droit-comptable-information-financiere-JAN-2017-JO.pdf#page=20)

For an ordinary **31 December 2025** year-end, the article 23 drawing-up
deadline gives **30 April 2026**. Record shareholder approval, any auditor
communication and RCCM deposit as separate steps under their own rules.
This example does not establish the national tax deadline.
[AUDCIF, article 23](https://justice.sec.gouv.sn/wp-content/uploads/textes-reglements/OHADA/Acte-uniforme-relatif-droit-comptable-information-financiere-JAN-2017-JO.pdf#page=24)

The complete SYSCOHADA annexes, subsequent standard-setting decisions, detailed
recognition and measurement, group accounts, sector and non-profit frameworks,
legal-form audit requirements, national submission procedures and fees, and
qualified local review remain outstanding. These selected provisions do not
establish complete reporting coverage or filing readiness.
