# Gabon Tax Overview

## The Gabonese tax system at a glance

Gabon is a Central African (CEMAC) state that taxes on a largely territorial/source basis, administered by the Direction Générale des Impôts (DGI) under the Code Général des Impôts (CGI). The figures below are an UNVERIFIED 2025 draft for accountant review.

- **Standard tax year** — Calendar year ending 31 December: both the corporate tax and the IRPP are assessed on a twelve-month period matching the budget year, the calendar year  _(Code Général des Impôts 2025 (Gabon), arts. 15 and 83 — https://dgi.ga/document/code-general-des-impots-2025/)_
- **Currency** — Central African CFA franc (XAF) (CEMAC monetary framework)
- **Tax authority** — Direction Générale des Impôts (DGI), Ministère de l'Économie  _(Code Général des Impôts (Gabon))_
- **Basis of taxation** — Territorial for companies: profits from operations carried out in Gabon by resident companies (registered in Gabon or effectively managed there) and by permanent establishments of non-residents (art. 5); individuals with a habitual residence in Gabon, or present at least 183 days in the year, pay IRPP, and persons resident abroad pay it only on Gabon-source income, salaries and pensions included (art. 74)  _(Code Général des Impôts 2025 (Gabon), arts. 5 and 74 — https://dgi.ga/document/code-general-des-impots-2025/)_
- **Top personal income tax (IRPP) marginal rate** — 35% on the fraction of income per part above XAF 11,000,000  _(Code Général des Impôts 2025 (Gabon), art. 174 — https://dgi.ga/document/code-general-des-impots-2025/)_
- **Standard corporate income tax (IS) rate** — 30%  _(Code Général des Impôts 2025 (Gabon), art. 16 — https://dgi.ga/document/code-general-des-impots-2025/)_
- **CIT rate — oil and mining sectors** — 35%  _(Code Général des Impôts 2025 (Gabon), art. 16 — https://dgi.ga/document/code-general-des-impots-2025/)_
- **Does VAT (TVA) apply?** — Yes — VAT (Taxe sur la Valeur Ajoutée) applies; standard rate 18%  _(Code Général des Impôts 2025 (Gabon), art. 221 — https://dgi.ga/document/code-general-des-impots-2025/)_
- **Annual CIT return filing deadline** — Before 30 April of the year following the tax year  _(Code Général des Impôts 2025 (Gabon), art. 20 — https://dgi.ga/document/code-general-des-impots-2025/)_
- **Minimum corporate tax (Minimum de Perception)** — XAF 500,000 floor on the annual CIT, or 1% of global turnover (impôt minimum forfaitaire) if higher, even with no profit; the DGI's corporate tax web page still shows XAF 1,000,000  _(Code Général des Impôts 2025 (Gabon), art. 24 as amended by the 2024 and 2025 finance laws — https://dgi.ga/document/code-general-des-impots-2025/)_; _(DGI, Impôt sur les sociétés — https://dgi.ga/imposition-des-personnes-morales/impot-sur-les-societes/)_
