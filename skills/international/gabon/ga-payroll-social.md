# Gabon Payroll & Social Contributions

## Periods and remaining source checks

The salary-tax section describes the DGI's **CGI 2025** compilation, updated
through Loi n°033/2024 of 30 December 2024. The social sections distinguish
**2017** CNAMGS legislation, provider guidance checked on **11 November 2026**
and the CNSS decree signed on **18 December 2025**. Later amendments,
implementation, current practice and special categories still need checking.
[DGI, CGI 2025](https://dgi.ga/document/code-general-des-impots-2025/)

## CNSS: decree signed on 18 December 2025

Decree **0487/PR/MASI**, article 2, records these ordinary branch rates:

| Branch | Employer | Worker |
|---|---|---|
| Family and maternity benefits | 5% | 0% |
| Work accidents and occupational diseases | 2% | 0% |
| Old age, invalidity and death | 11% | 5% |
| Total CNSS | 18% | 5% |

The family and occupational-risk branches are exclusively employer-funded
under replacement article 28 of Law **037/2023**. Article 27 permits an
occupational-risk increase up to double for the specified prevention failures
or employer fault. The ordinary table assumes no such increase.
[Decree 0487, article 2](https://journal-officiel.ga/22255-x/) and
[Law 037/2023, replacement articles 27 to 28](https://journal-officiel.ga/20147-037-2023-/)

Article 3 includes remuneration, allowances, bonuses, commissions, cash
benefits and the value of benefits in kind. It excludes **expense
reimbursements and social benefits**. Benefits in kind use CGI valuation
rules. Article 4 sets the ceiling at **CFA 1,500,000 per month**. The text's
article 3 points to article 5 for the ceiling, although article 4 prints it.
[Decree 0487, articles 3 to 5](https://journal-officiel.ga/22255-x/)

The Gazette text does **not state a 1 January 2026 commencement date**.
Its webpage identifies issue 96 Bis as **29 November 2025**, before the
**18 December 2025** signature, and prints inconsistent underlying-law dates.
A notice on the LinkedIn account named CNSS Gabon reports application from
**1 January 2026** and the first DTS by **30 April 2026**. The matching notice
on the CNSS website, the legal commencement instrument and correction of the
Gazette metadata remain to corroborate. Do not infer the operative date from
the decree's signature alone.
[Gazette decree text](https://journal-officiel.ga/22255-x/) and
[CNSS account, new-contribution notice](https://fr.linkedin.com/posts/cnss-gabon_cnssemployeur-cnsstravailleur-cnsspluspr%C3%A8sdevous-activity-7416830583407910912-XCKD)

**Historical 2025 rates remain unresolved.** Decree **197/PR/MASSSCSN** of
20 February 1989, effective **1 April 1989**, records an employer pension rate
of **6%** and worker rate of **2.5%**. This does not establish the rates for
2025. The later amendment chain must be found before verifying the previous
2025 pension and combined-rate summaries. Decree 0487 expressly repeals that
1989 decree. [Decree 197, articles 2 and 4](https://journal-officiel.ga/9574-197-pr-massscsn/)
and [Decree 0487, article 5](https://journal-officiel.ga/22255-x/)

Law 037/2023 requires withholding the worker share at each pay and leaves
the employer share exclusively with the employer. With multiple employers,
each contributes within the ceiling in proportion to the remuneration it
pays. The employer owes the whole remittance. Replacement article 26 also
requires continuing family-benefit contributions for **3 months** after an
economic dismissal. The CNSS employer page calls for quarterly DTS filings;
the operative payment deadline and special-class rules still need the
applicable implementing instruments.
[Law 037/2023, replacement articles 26 to 30](https://journal-officiel.ga/20147-037-2023-/)
and [CNSS, employers](https://cnss.ga/entreprise/)

## CNAMGS: dated legislation and provider guidance

Decree **578/PR/MDSFPSSN** of **22 December 2016** took effect on
**1 January 2017** under Order **0010/MDSFPSSN**, article 2. Subject to the
exemptions below, it records:

| Sector | Employer | Active worker | Retired worker |
|---|---|---|---|
| Public | 5% | 2.5% | 1% |
| Private and parapublic | 4.1% | 2% | 1% |

[Decree 578, articles 3 to 4](https://journal-officiel.ga/5702-578-pr-mdsfpssn-/)
and [Order 0010, article 2](https://journal-officiel.ga/5740-0010-mdsfpssn-/)

Article 2 exempts former combatants; public agents and employees receiving
work-accident annuities and their dependants; public agents, employees and
retirees whose salary or pension is **at or below the SMIG**, with their
dependants; and public- and private-sector retirees resident abroad, with
their dependants. Check the applicable SMIG and each person's classification.
[Decree 578, article 2](https://journal-officiel.ga/5702-578-pr-mdsfpssn-/)

Article 5 uses salary, pension, bonuses, emoluments and allowances subject to
**IRPP**, with a **CFA 2,500,000 monthly ceiling** under article 6. Employers
and pension payers collect the respective shares. With multiple employers,
each is responsible within the ceiling in proportion to its remuneration.
The CNAMGS payment page still displays these rates, base and ceiling when
checked on **11 November 2026**. Law **036/2023**, replacement article 42,
retains decree-setting of rates, base and ceiling and employer responsibility
for the employer share. A complete later-amendment check remains open.
[Decree 578, articles 5 to 6](https://journal-officiel.ga/5702-578-pr-mdsfpssn-/),
[Law 036/2023, replacement article 42](https://journal-officiel.ga/20146-036-2023-/)
and [CNAMGS, employer payments](https://cnamgs.ga/payement-des-cotisations-employeurs/)

For private and parapublic payroll, the CNAMGS page gives the following
quarterly declaration and payment windows, checked on **11 November 2026**:

| Salary quarter | Declaration and payment window |
|---|---|
| January to March | 1 to 30 April |
| April to June | 1 to 31 July |
| July to September | 1 to 31 October |
| October to December | 1 to 31 January of the following year |

Submit the prescribed remuneration or pension file before the window closes.
These windows do not replace the distinct public-sector collection rule:
article 7 of Decree 578 requires transfer of collected public contributions
on the **25th of each month**. Special collection, penalties and later
instructions require separate checks.
[CNAMGS, declaration and payment calendar](https://cnamgs.ga/payement-des-cotisations-employeurs/)
and [Decree 578, articles 7 to 9](https://journal-officiel.ga/5702-578-pr-mdsfpssn-/)

## Contribution example: separate bases and ceilings

Assume a non-exempt private employee has a **CFA 2,000,000 monthly CNSS base**
and a separately established **CFA 2,000,000 CNAMGS base**. Assume the ordinary
Decree 0487 rates apply for the pay period, with no risk surcharge or special
class. This assumption does not resolve the commencement evidence above.

| Calculation | Employer, CFA | Worker, CFA |
|---|---|---|
| CNSS on the capped CFA 1,500,000 base | 18% × 1,500,000 = 270,000 | 5% × 1,500,000 = 75,000 |
| CNAMGS on CFA 2,000,000, below its ceiling | 4.1% × 2,000,000 = 82,000 | 2% × 2,000,000 = 40,000 |
| Total contributions | 352,000 | 115,000 |

Do not apply a combined percentage to one capped salary: the bases and
ceilings differ. This example excludes salary taxes and other employer levies;
rounding instructions and the operative pay-period rules remain to check.
[Decree 0487, articles 2 to 4](https://journal-officiel.ga/22255-x/) and
[Decree 578, articles 4 to 6](https://journal-officiel.ga/5702-578-pr-mdsfpssn-/)

## Salary taxes recorded in CGI 2025

- **IRPP withholding.** Article 95 requires payers of taxable employment
  income under articles 90 onwards to withhold IRPP. Article 174 supplies
  the **0% to 35%** scale per family part, reproduced in the overview.
  Salary exemptions, deductions, benefits in kind, household allocation and
  special foreign-personnel arrangements require their own checks.
  [CGI 2025, articles 90 to 96 and 168 to 175](https://dgi.ga/document/code-general-des-impots-2025/)
- **TCTS.** Articles 346 to 350 impose **5%** on the article 92 net-income
  base, exempting the first **CFA 150,000 per month**. Listed performance
  bonuses, profit-sharing payments, gratuities, thirteenth-month and year-end
  payments share an annual **CFA 4,000,000** exemption. Check the listed
  categories and allocation before applying the exemption. The employer
  withholds monthly. Article 351 makes TCTS deductible from the article 92
  gross-income amount.
  [Articles 346 to 351](https://dgi.ga/document/code-general-des-impots-2025/)
- **Remittance.** Article 96 requires payment and the prescribed declaration
  within the **first 15 days of the following month** to the employer's tax
  office. Article 350 applies that collection route to TCTS. These are the
  compiled **2025** rules; later salary-tax and filing changes remain open.
  [Articles 95 to 96 and 350](https://dgi.ga/document/code-general-des-impots-2025/)
