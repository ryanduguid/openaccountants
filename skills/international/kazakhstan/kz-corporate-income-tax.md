# Kazakhstan Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax rates and base (2025)

- **Standard CIT rate** — 20% on taxable income from activities other than those with their own rate; 25% for second-tier banks' banking activity (other than lending to businesses) and for casinos, slot halls, betting and bookmaking  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan, Code No. 214-VIII of 18 July 2025 (in force from 1 January 2026), English text on adilet.zan.kz, art. 357.2.5 — https://adilet.zan.kz/eng/docs/K2500000214)_
- **Reduced CIT rate for qualifying agricultural producers** — 3% from 1 January 2026 on income of legal entities producing agricultural or aquaculture products, including processing and sale of their own production (the 10% rate belonged to the previous Code); agricultural cooperatives 6%  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan, Code No. 214-VIII of 18 July 2025 (in force from 1 January 2026), English text on adilet.zan.kz, art. 357.2.1 and 357.2.2 — https://adilet.zan.kz/eng/docs/K2500000214)_
- **Tax base** — Taxable income = aggregate annual income less allowable deductions and carried-forward losses  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan, Code No. 214-VIII of 18 July 2025 (in force from 1 January 2026), English text on adilet.zan.kz, art. 236 (taxable income = aggregate annual income less adjustments and deductions, with loss carry-forward) — https://adilet.zan.kz/eng/docs/K2500000214)_
- **Loss carryforward** — Business losses are carried forward consecutively to the following ten tax periods and offset against the taxable income of those periods (art. 338)  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan, Code No. 214-VIII of 18 July 2025 (in force from 1 January 2026), Russian text on adilet.zan.kz, art. 338 — https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/K2500000214)_
- **Branch profits tax on non-resident permanent establishments** — 15% on the net income of a non-resident legal entity operating through a permanent establishment, after corporate income tax (treaty rates may reduce it)  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan, Code No. 214-VIII of 18 July 2025 (in force from 1 January 2026), English text on adilet.zan.kz, art. 357.1.4 — https://adilet.zan.kz/eng/docs/K2500000214)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends to non-residents** — **15%**, with a reduced **5%** where the distributed amount of dividends does **not exceed 230,000 MCI** (roughly USD 1.9 million); 15% applies to the excess above that. This line previously attributed the 5% to "qualifying holding/holding-period conditions", which is not the test the cited source states — the test is the size of the distribution, and the statute adds a holding condition: the 5% band applies to dividends paid to a person holding directly or indirectly at least 25% of the paying resident's capital  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan, Code No. 214-VIII of 18 July 2025 (in force from 1 January 2026), English text on adilet.zan.kz, art. 682.1(5) and 682.1(6) — https://adilet.zan.kz/eng/docs/K2500000214)_
- **Withholding tax on interest to non-residents** — **15%** generally, but **10%** on interest from credits, loans and debt securities. Identify the instrument before applying 15%, and note the exemptions: interest and dividends on listed securities where the "active trade" criteria are met, and certain debt-security capital gains where the holding period exceeds three years and the holder is not in a black-listed jurisdiction  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan, Code No. 214-VIII of 18 July 2025 (in force from 1 January 2026), English text on adilet.zan.kz, art. 682.1(5) (15% on remuneration) and 682.1(7) (10% on interest on loans, credits and debt securities), art. 682.3 (exemptions) — https://adilet.zan.kz/eng/docs/K2500000214)_
- **Withholding tax on royalties to non-residents** — 15% (art. 682(1)(5)), unless reduced by an applicable double tax treaty  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan, Code No. 214-VIII of 18 July 2025 (in force from 1 January 2026), Russian text on adilet.zan.kz, art. 682(1)(5) — https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/K2500000214)_
- **Withholding tax on services to non-residents** — **20%** on management, financial, consulting, engineering, marketing, auditing and legal services — five points above the dividend, interest and royalty rate and the head this guide did not carry until September 2026. A service invoice is not a 15% case  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan, Code No. 214-VIII of 18 July 2025 (in force from 1 January 2026), Russian text on adilet.zan.kz, art. 682(1)(1), the 20% rate for art. 679 income outside the named heads — https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/K2500000214)_
- **Withholding tax on other income of non-residents** — **20%** on Kazakhstan-source income that does not fall into a named category, so 20% rather than 15% is the residual  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan, Code No. 214-VIII of 18 July 2025 (in force from 1 January 2026), Russian text on adilet.zan.kz, art. 682(1)(1) — https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/K2500000214)_
- **Withholding tax on insurance premiums** — **15%** on risk insurance premiums (art. 682(1)(2)) and **5%** on risk reinsurance premiums (art. 682(1)(3))  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan, Code No. 214-VIII of 18 July 2025 (in force from 1 January 2026), Russian text on adilet.zan.kz, art. 682(1)(2) and (3) — https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/K2500000214)_
- **Withholding tax on international transportation services** — **5%** (art. 682(1)(4))  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan, Code No. 214-VIII of 18 July 2025 (in force from 1 January 2026), Russian text on adilet.zan.kz, art. 682(1)(4) — https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/K2500000214)_
- **Withholding tax on constructive dividends** — **15%** on benefits paid to shareholders or related parties, so a related-party benefit can carry dividend withholding without a declared dividend  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan, Code No. 214-VIII of 18 July 2025 (in force from 1 January 2026), Russian text on adilet.zan.kz, art. 682(1)(5) — https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/K2500000214)_
- **Withholding tax on payments to low-tax (tax-haven) jurisdictions** — 20%, regardless of income type, where the recipient is in a listed preferential-tax jurisdiction  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan, Code No. 214-VIII of 18 July 2025 (in force from 1 January 2026), English text on adilet.zan.kz, art. 682.1(1) (20% default rate) and 682.2 (20% for persons registered in a state with preferential taxation) — https://adilet.zan.kz/eng/docs/K2500000214)_
- **Advance CIT payments** — Monthly advance payments by the 25th of each month; taxpayers whose total annual income for the tax period before the previous one did not exceed 600,000 times the monthly calculation index are exempt, as are newly created taxpayers for their first two periods, non-profit organisations and the other categories in art. 348.1 (the exemptions do not apply to the digital asset sector)  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan, Code No. 214-VIII of 18 July 2025 (in force from 1 January 2026), English text on adilet.zan.kz, art. 348.1, 349 and 350 — https://adilet.zan.kz/eng/docs/K2500000214)_
- **CIT return filing deadline** — 31 March of the year following the reporting year  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan, Code No. 214-VIII of 18 July 2025 (in force from 1 January 2026), English text on adilet.zan.kz, art. 359.1 — https://adilet.zan.kz/eng/docs/K2500000214)_
- **Final CIT payment deadline** — Within 10 calendar days after the return deadline, so by 10 April; advance payments made during the year are credited  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan, Code No. 214-VIII of 18 July 2025 (in force from 1 January 2026), English text on adilet.zan.kz, art. 347.2 — https://adilet.zan.kz/eng/docs/K2500000214)_
