# Kazakhstan tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the taxpayer, where income arose, residence facts and period. Check the applicable treaty and foreign-tax relief.

## Territorial reach

Residents generally pay tax on income from Kazakhstani and foreign sources. Nonresidents generally pay tax on Kazakhstan-source income. A nonresident carrying on business through a Kazakhstani permanent establishment can also be taxed on foreign-source income attributable to that establishment and on income from identical or similar activity in Kazakhstan that is connected with the establishment and received or receivable from the date the nonresident began activities there. Check source, attribution and the treaty before classifying receipts. [Tax Code, arts 221, 679 and 688](https://www.gov.kz/org/kgd-akmola/documents/details/1084149?lang=ru)

## Residence

An individual generally meets the permanent presence test with at least 183 calendar days, including arrival and departure, during 12 consecutive months ending in the tax period. A separate test considers the individual's centre of vital interests. Check the combined citizenship or residence permit, family and available dwelling conditions. Article 222 also sets a distinct 90-day rule for qualifying Astana International Financial Centre investment residents and special rules for eligible individuals abroad.

A legal entity is resident if created under Kazakhstani law or, for a foreign-law entity, effectively managed in Kazakhstan. For a foreign-law entity, identify where the actual management body meets to exercise management or control and take business decisions. Check any applicable treaty rule. [Tax Code, arts 222–224](https://www.gov.kz/org/kgd-akmola/documents/details/1084149?lang=ru)

## Taxpayer types

An individual entrepreneur is a natural person with a business. Check the ordinary business-income rules and eligibility for any special regime. A registered business partnership is a legal entity, including general and limited partnerships. Participants in a contractual joint activity without a legal entity ordinarily account for and pay tax on their own shares. Where they appoint a tax accounting representative, check who keeps the separate records and how income and expenses are allocated under the agreement and the Code. [Civil Code, arts 58 and 63–72](https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/K940001000_) · [Tax Code, arts 215, 233 and 360](https://www.gov.kz/org/kgd-akmola/documents/details/1084149?lang=ru)

## Currency

Ordinary tax records use the tenge (KZT) and accrual accounting. Record the original currency and apply the relevant exchange-rate and recognition provisions before reporting an amount in tenge. [Tax Code, arts 21 and 204](https://www.gov.kz/org/kgd-akmola/documents/details/1084149?lang=ru)

## Assessment periods

The ordinary corporate income-tax period is the calendar year. The calendar year also normally applies to individual income tax on income an individual assesses directly. The Code sets separate rules for commencement, cessation, withholding and special regimes. [Tax Code, arts 358–359 and 415](https://www.gov.kz/org/kgd-akmola/documents/details/1084149?lang=ru)

## Responsible authorities

The State Revenue Committee of the Ministry of Finance and its territorial state revenue bodies administer national taxes and customs. Verify registration, filing and withholding with the competent state revenue body. [State Revenue Committee](https://www.gov.kz/memleket/entities/kgd?lang=en) · [Tax Code, arts 42–43](https://www.gov.kz/org/kgd-akmola/documents/details/1084149?lang=ru)

## Fictional intake

A foreign supplier has a permanent establishment in Kazakhstan and invoices several customers outside the country. Separate each receipt by activity, source and its relationship to the establishment before applying the domestic rules and treaty.
