# Kenya tax system

> General orientation for 2026. Confirm the taxpayer, accounting period and transaction with a qualified local tax professional before applying these rules.

Amounts are in Kenyan shillings (KES). The Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA) administers the taxes described here. Check the relevant year of income and the effective date of amendments. [Income Tax Act, Cap. 470, consolidated as at 1 July 2026](https://www.kra.go.ke/images/publications/Income-Tax-Act-2026.pdf) · [KRA Finance Act 2026 guidance](https://www.kra.go.ke/finance-act-2026-what-it-means-for-you/)

## Income tax

- **Individuals and residence.** Residence generally requires a permanent home in Kenya and presence during the year. A person without a permanent home can qualify by being present at least 183 days during the year, or by being present in that year and each of the previous 2 years for an average of more than 122 days a year. Income tax generally applies to Kenyan-source income. Specific rules deem some foreign employment income and income from a business carried on partly in Kenya by a resident to be Kenyan-source. The 2026 ordinary annual rates are 10% on the first KES 288,000, 25% to KES 388,000, 30% to KES 6 million, 32.5% to KES 9.6 million and 35% above that. Eligible resident individuals generally receive KES 28,800 annual personal relief. [Income Tax Act, ss. 2–5 and Third Schedule](https://www.kra.go.ke/images/publications/Income-Tax-Act-2026.pdf)
- **Companies.** Kenyan incorporation, management and control exercised in Kenya during the year, or designation by notice can establish residence. The ordinary rate is 30% for resident companies and for non-resident companies with a Kenyan permanent establishment from the 2024 year of income. Check special rates, withholding and the separate charge on repatriated income before applying the ordinary rate to a cross-border business. [Income Tax Act, ss. 2, 3 and 7B and Third Schedule, Head B, para. 2](https://www.kra.go.ke/images/publications/Income-Tax-Act-2026.pdf)

## VAT and filing

- **VAT.** The standard rate is 16%. Ordinary compulsory registration starts at KES 5 million in annual taxable supplies, with separate rules for non-resident suppliers through electronic channels. VAT returns and payment ordinarily fall due on the 20th of the following month. For the 15 November to 14 December 2026 pricing cycle, the petroleum regulator's published retail prices for super petrol, diesel and kerosene include 8% VAT. Check the operative instrument and supply date for petroleum. [KRA VAT guidance](https://www.kra.go.ke/individual/filing-paying/types-of-taxes/value-added-tax) · [Energy and Petroleum Regulatory Authority November pricing notice](https://www.epra.go.ke/sites/default/files/2026-11/FINAL%20EPRA%20PRESS%20RELEASE-%20NOVEMBER%202026%20SIGNED.pdf)
- **Filing.** From 1 January 2027, the ordinary individual income tax return deadline is the last day of the fourth month following the year of income. This gives 30 April 2027 for calendar-year individuals filing for 2026. Companies and other non-individual filers generally retain the sixth-month return deadline. The ordinary self-assessment balance falls due on the last day of the fourth month, even where the return is due later. Check any instalments separately. [KRA Finance Act 2026 guidance](https://www.kra.go.ke/finance-act-2026-what-it-means-for-you/) · [Income Tax Act, s. 92A](https://www.kra.go.ke/images/publications/Income-Tax-Act-2026.pdf)
