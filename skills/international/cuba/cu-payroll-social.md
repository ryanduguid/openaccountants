# Cuba Payroll & Social Contributions

## Social security and payroll contributions

- **Overview of social security and payroll contribution system** — Cuba funds social security through the employer Contribución a la Seguridad Social, the employee Contribución Especial a la Seguridad Social and the employer Impuesto por la Utilización de la Fuerza de Trabajo. Ley 113 defines each tribute and leaves the rate to the annual budget law; for 2026 the rates are in Ley 181/2025  _(Ley 113/2012 Del Sistema Tributario (concordada, GOC-2021-152-ES1), art. 231, 291 and 301 — https://www.gacetaoficial.gob.cu/sites/default/files/goc-2021-es1.pdf ; Ley 181/2025 Del Presupuesto del Estado para el año 2026 (GOC-2025-594-O99, Gaceta Oficial No. 99 Ordinaria, 29 Dec 2025), art. 19, 20 and 75 — https://www.gacetaoficial.gob.cu/sites/default/files/goc-2025-o99_1.pdf)_
- **Employer social-security contribution** — 14% of the salary base for the 2026 fiscal year, payable by entities, non-state economic actors and self-employed persons who employ labour; 12.5% goes to the State Budget and the remaining 1.5% stays with the entity to pay its workers' short-term social security benefits  _(Ley 181/2025 Del Presupuesto del Estado para el año 2026 (GOC-2025-594-O99, Gaceta Oficial No. 99 Ordinaria, 29 Dec 2025), art. 19.1 and 19.2 — https://www.gacetaoficial.gob.cu/sites/default/files/goc-2025-o99_1.pdf)_
- **Employee social-security contribution (base)** — 5% of monthly earnings up to 15,000 CUP (Contribución Especial a la Seguridad Social, 2026)  _(Ley 181/2025 Del Presupuesto del Estado para el año 2026 (GOC-2025-594-O99, Gaceta Oficial No. 99 Ordinaria, 29 Dec 2025), art. 20.3 — https://www.gacetaoficial.gob.cu/sites/default/files/goc-2025-o99_1.pdf)_
- **Employee social-security contribution (excess)** — 10% on the part of monthly earnings above 15,000 CUP (2026)  _(Ley 181/2025 Del Presupuesto del Estado para el año 2026 (GOC-2025-594-O99, Gaceta Oficial No. 99 Ordinaria, 29 Dec 2025), art. 20.3 — https://www.gacetaoficial.gob.cu/sites/default/files/goc-2025-o99_1.pdf)_
- **Employee social-security wage threshold** — 15,000 CUP per month (boundary between the 5% and 10% rates for 2026); the base includes salaries and other non-salary remuneration received for work  _(Ley 181/2025 Del Presupuesto del Estado para el año 2026 (GOC-2025-594-O99, Gaceta Oficial No. 99 Ordinaria, 29 Dec 2025), art. 20.1 and 20.3 — https://www.gacetaoficial.gob.cu/sites/default/files/goc-2025-o99_1.pdf)_
- **Tax on use of labour force** — 5% of remuneration paid for 2026 (Impuesto por la Utilización de la Fuerza de Trabajo), the rate Ley 113 set for the fifth year onwards; Ley 181 art. 76 exempts cooperatives, basic cooperative production units, state enterprises and other listed payers, and the Foreign Investment Law and Mariel zone rules carry their own exceptions  _(Ley 113/2012 Del Sistema Tributario (concordada, GOC-2021-152-ES1), art. 231 — https://www.gacetaoficial.gob.cu/sites/default/files/goc-2021-es1.pdf ; Ley 181/2025 Del Presupuesto del Estado para el año 2026 (GOC-2025-594-O99, Gaceta Oficial No. 99 Ordinaria, 29 Dec 2025), art. 75 and 76 — https://www.gacetaoficial.gob.cu/sites/default/files/goc-2025-o99_1.pdf)_
- **Payroll income-tax withholding** — Entities withhold their workers' Contribución Especial and pay it to the State Budget within the first 10 working days of each month for the previous month; self-employed persons pay their own contribution quarterly within the first 20 calendar days after the quarter. Personal income tax withholding on wages follows Ley 113's personal income tax rules (approx — confirm)  _(Ley 113/2012 Del Sistema Tributario (concordada, GOC-2021-152-ES1), art. 303 and 304 — https://www.gacetaoficial.gob.cu/sites/default/files/goc-2021-es1.pdf)_
- **Remittance frequency** — Monthly for the withheld Contribución Especial (first 10 working days of the following month) and for the labour force tax (monthly return); the employer Contribución a la Seguridad Social follows the payment rules of Ley 113 Title I of Libro Cuarto (approx — confirm the due date)  _(Ley 113/2012 Del Sistema Tributario (concordada, GOC-2021-152-ES1), art. 233 and 303 — https://www.gacetaoficial.gob.cu/sites/default/files/goc-2021-es1.pdf)_
- **Territorial contribution for local development** — 1% of gross income from sales of goods and services for 2026 (Contribución Territorial para el Desarrollo Local), payable by cooperatives, micro, small and medium enterprises, wholly Cuban-owned companies and national and provincial enterprises for each establishment in the municipality where it trades, within the first 45 calendar days of the following month; importers deduct the acquisition cost of the goods  _(Ley 113/2012 Del Sistema Tributario (concordada, GOC-2021-152-ES1), art. 305 to 313 — https://www.gacetaoficial.gob.cu/sites/default/files/goc-2021-es1.pdf ; Ley 181/2025 Del Presupuesto del Estado para el año 2026 (GOC-2025-594-O99, Gaceta Oficial No. 99 Ordinaria, 29 Dec 2025), art. 84 and 85 — https://www.gacetaoficial.gob.cu/sites/default/files/goc-2025-o99_1.pdf)_
