# Cuba Corporate Income Tax

## Tax on profits (Impuesto sobre Utilidades)

- **Tax on profits (Impuesto sobre Utilidades)** — Companies, MIPYMEs and other legal persons pay the Impuesto sobre Utilidades at a general rate of 35% on net taxable profit under Law No. 113. Reduced and increased rates apply to foreign-investment entities and natural-resource activities respectively.  _(Ley No. 113 del Sistema Tributario)_
- **General corporate tax rate** — 35% % on net taxable profit (General rate)  _(Ley No. 113 del Sistema Tributario)_
- **Tax base** — Net profit = total income less documented deductible expenses; settled annually by sworn declaration (Declaración Jurada) with quarterly installments during the year  _(Ley No. 113 del Sistema Tributario (https://ivacalculator.com/cuba/impuestos-mipymes-empresas-privadas/))_
- **Natural resources / mining rate** — up to 50% % on profits from mining or exploitation of natural resources (approx — confirm)  _(Ley No. 113 del Sistema Tributario)_
- **Foreign-investment (joint venture) rate** — 15% % on net profit (for joint ventures and foreign-investment entities under the Foreign Investment Law (approx — confirm))  _(Ley No. 118 de la Inversión Extranjera (https://opapeleo.com/en/news/taxes-for-foreigners-in-cuba/))_
- **Foreign-investment tax holiday** — An exemption period (commonly cited as 8 years) from the tax on profits may be granted to new joint ventures / international economic association contracts under the Foreign Investment Law (approx — confirm)  _(Ley No. 118 de la Inversión Extranjera)_
- **MIPYME / private company rate** — 35% on net profit for the 2026 fiscal year (general Impuesto sobre Utilidades rate, with the exceptions in Ley 113), settled annually with payments on account  _(Ley 181/2025 Del Presupuesto del Estado para el año 2026 (GOC-2025-594-O99, Gaceta Oficial No. 99 Ordinaria, 29 Dec 2025), art. 53.1 — https://www.gacetaoficial.gob.cu/sites/default/files/goc-2025-o99_1.pdf ; https://www.mep.gob.cu/en/node/1381)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends** — Dividends distributed to MIPYME partners are taxed via personal income tax (max marginal ~20%); a separate dividend withholding tax on foreign shareholders should be confirmed against statute (approx — confirm)  _(Ley No. 113 del Sistema Tributario)_
- **Withholding tax on interest / royalties to non-residents** — Payments of interest and royalties to non-residents may be subject to withholding; rate to be confirmed against statute and any applicable treaty (approx — confirm)  _(Ley No. 113 del Sistema Tributario)_
- **Filing / payment deadline** — Annual sworn declaration due by 31 March (corporate Declaración Jurada); quarterly advance payments during the year (approx — confirm)  _(Ley No. 113 del Sistema Tributario)_
- **Territorial contribution for local development** — 1% of gross income from sales of goods and services for 2026 (Contribución Territorial para el Desarrollo Local), payable by state and private entities for each establishment; importers deduct the acquisition cost of the goods; a deductible financial expense for the Impuesto sobre Utilidades  _(Ley 113/2012 Del Sistema Tributario (concordada, GOC-2021-152-ES1), art. 305, 308 and 312 — https://www.gacetaoficial.gob.cu/sites/default/files/goc-2021-es1.pdf ; Ley 181/2025 Del Presupuesto del Estado para el año 2026 (GOC-2025-594-O99, Gaceta Oficial No. 99 Ordinaria, 29 Dec 2025), art. 84 and 85 — https://www.gacetaoficial.gob.cu/sites/default/files/goc-2025-o99_1.pdf)_
