# Cuba VAT / GST

## Sales tax and services tax (no VAT)

- **VAT / GST status** — Cuba has no value-added tax. Indirect taxation is by the Impuesto sobre las Ventas, the Impuesto Especial a Productos and the Impuesto sobre los Servicios under Title III of Ley 113, with the year's rates set by the annual budget law  _(Ley 113/2012 Del Sistema Tributario (concordada, GOC-2021-152-ES1), Libro Segundo Título III — https://www.gacetaoficial.gob.cu/sites/default/files/goc-2021-es1.pdf ; Ley 181/2025 Del Presupuesto del Estado para el año 2026 (GOC-2025-594-O99, Gaceta Oficial No. 99 Ordinaria, 29 Dec 2025), art. 58 — https://www.gacetaoficial.gob.cu/sites/default/files/goc-2025-o99_1.pdf)_
- **Retail sales tax rate** — 10% on retail sales of goods by legal persons to the population for 2026; Ley 113 lets retail rates be set as a percentage of sales value or per physical unit, and Resolución 350/2025 (same gazette issue) applies 10% to retail sales of agricultural products in markets and fairs  _(Ley 113/2012 Del Sistema Tributario (concordada, GOC-2021-152-ES1), art. 145 — https://www.gacetaoficial.gob.cu/sites/default/files/goc-2021-es1.pdf ; Ley 181/2025 Del Presupuesto del Estado para el año 2026 (GOC-2025-594-O99, Gaceta Oficial No. 99 Ordinaria, 29 Dec 2025), art. 58 and Resolución 350/2025 — https://www.gacetaoficial.gob.cu/sites/default/files/goc-2025-o99_1.pdf)_
- **Wholesale sales tax rate** — Set by product in value per physical unit, not as a general percentage; the 2026 budget law fixes no wholesale percentage. The 2% figure carried from a secondary source is not in the texts read [RESEARCH GAP — reviewer to confirm the wholesale schedule with ONAT]  _(Ley 113/2012 Del Sistema Tributario (concordada, GOC-2021-152-ES1), art. 144 — https://www.gacetaoficial.gob.cu/sites/default/files/goc-2021-es1.pdf)_
- **Services tax rate** — 10% of total service income for natural persons and for entities serving the population; 5% for the telecommunications services of the Empresa de Telecomunicaciones de Cuba for 2026; electricity transmission by the Unión Eléctrica is not taxed  _(Ley 113/2012 Del Sistema Tributario (concordada, GOC-2021-152-ES1), art. 149 and 150 — https://www.gacetaoficial.gob.cu/sites/default/files/goc-2021-es1.pdf ; Ley 181/2025 Del Presupuesto del Estado para el año 2026 (GOC-2025-594-O99, Gaceta Oficial No. 99 Ordinaria, 29 Dec 2025), art. 58, 64 and 66 — https://www.gacetaoficial.gob.cu/sites/default/files/goc-2025-o99_1.pdf)_
- **Export of goods** — Goods destined for export are excepted from the sales, special product and services taxes, as are goods that are raw materials for industrial production  _(Ley 113/2012 Del Sistema Tributario (concordada, GOC-2021-152-ES1), art. 153 — https://www.gacetaoficial.gob.cu/sites/default/files/goc-2021-es1.pdf)_
- **Export of services** — Services destined for export are excepted from the services tax  _(Ley 113/2012 Del Sistema Tributario (concordada, GOC-2021-152-ES1), art. 153 — https://www.gacetaoficial.gob.cu/sites/default/files/goc-2021-es1.pdf)_
- **Reverse charge / input credit** — No input credit or reverse charge: these are turnover taxes on the total value of sales or of service income, with no deduction for tax borne on inputs  _(Ley 113/2012 Del Sistema Tributario (concordada, GOC-2021-152-ES1), art. 145 and 148 — https://www.gacetaoficial.gob.cu/sites/default/files/goc-2021-es1.pdf)_
- **Filing / remittance frequency** — Natural persons and retail entities pay within the 20 calendar days after the close of the month of sale or service; wholesale entities within 60 calendar days  _(Ley 113/2012 Del Sistema Tributario (concordada, GOC-2021-152-ES1), art. 152 — https://www.gacetaoficial.gob.cu/sites/default/files/goc-2021-es1.pdf)_
- **Registration threshold** — Liability attaches to every natural or legal person that sells goods or provides taxed services; there is no turnover registration threshold  _(Ley 113/2012 Del Sistema Tributario (concordada, GOC-2021-152-ES1), art. 134 and 147 — https://www.gacetaoficial.gob.cu/sites/default/files/goc-2021-es1.pdf)_
