# Togo Personal Income Tax

## Personal income tax (IRPP) bands and rates

**Every figure below is read from the consolidated *Code Général des Impôts et
Livre des Procédures Fiscales, mis à jour 2025* published by the Office Togolais
des Recettes.** Two books are bound into that one PDF and each numbers its
articles from 1, so citations here name the book: **CGI art. n** for the Code
Général des Impôts, **LPF art. n** for the Livre des Procédures Fiscales. The
current scale was set by *Loi n°2022-022 du 27 décembre 2022 portant loi de
finances, exercice 2023*.

- **IRPP band 1 (exempt)** — 0% on annual net taxable income up to XOF 900,000. The statute reads *exonéré*, not a low positive rate percent (Bands are annual XOF slices, applied cumulatively.)  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI art. 74 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **IRPP band 2** — 3% on the slice from XOF 900,001 to 3,000,000 percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI art. 74 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **IRPP band 3** — 10% on the slice from XOF 3,000,001 to 6,000,000 percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI art. 74 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **IRPP band 4** — 15% on the slice from XOF 6,000,001 to 9,000,000 percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI art. 74 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **IRPP band 5** — 20% on the slice from XOF 9,000,001 to 12,000,000 percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI art. 74 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **IRPP band 6** — 25% on the slice from XOF 12,000,001 to 15,000,000 percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI art. 74 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **IRPP band 7** — 30% on the slice from XOF 15,000,001 to 20,000,000 percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI art. 74 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **IRPP top band** — 35% on the slice above XOF 20,000,000 percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI art. 74 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Tax-free threshold** — The first XOF 900,000 of annual net taxable income is exempt XOF  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI art. 74 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Rounding, both ends of the calculation** — Annual net taxable income is rounded **down** to the nearest XOF 1,000 before the scale is applied, and the tax produced by the scale is rounded **down** to the nearest XOF 10  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI art. 74 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **What the scale covers** — The art. 74 scale applies to employment income, and is extended by name to remuneration of *gérants* and partners of the companies at CGI art. 28 (art. 75), to pensions and life annuities (art. 76), and to property income (art. 77)  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI arts. 75–77 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Business income is off the scale — flat 30%** — Taxpayers with *revenus d'affaires* (the CGI art. 29 category: commercial, artisanal, agricultural and non-commercial profits) are taxed at a flat **30%**, not on the progressive scale. Quoting the art. 74 barème for a Togolese sole trader is wrong percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI art. 78 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Standard 28% deduction on employment income** — After the deductions listed below, a lump-sum deduction of **28%** is applied to the fraction of gross employment income **not exceeding XOF 10,000,000**. It is computed on gross income after removing mandatory pension withholdings, employee social and health insurance contributions, and amounts withheld in respect of benefits in kind. Omitting it materially overstates the tax percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI art. 26 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Deductions from gross employment income** — Mandatory pension/retirement withholdings capped at 6% of gross pay; employee contributions to compulsory social and health insurance; amounts withheld against benefits in kind; job-related expenses not covered by an allowance; interest on the first ten annuities of a loan for building, buying or major repair of the taxpayer's main residence (plus façade renovation), on production of the lender-certified amortisation table; interest on loans to fund capital contributed to a Togolese industrial, commercial or agricultural business; compulsory maintenance annuities and family-code alimony; voluntary retirement savings capped at 6% of net taxable income  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI art. 26 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Life assurance premium relief** — Premiums to Togo-approved insurers on life-contingent contracts with a death benefit and an effective term of at least six years (or a deferred annuity/capital of at least six years) are deductible up to **XOF 200,000, plus XOF 30,000 per dependent child, for at most six children** XOF  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI art. 26(9) — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Dependants: a fixed XOF 10,000 per month, not a family quotient** — Net income is reduced by a fixed **XOF 10,000 per dependant per month** — XOF 120,000 a year each, and XOF 720,000 a year at the six-dependant cap. Togo does **not** use a *quotient familial* that divides income into parts, and the relief reduces income rather than tax. The reduction applies **exclusively to employment income** under CGI art. 25  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI arts. 72–73 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Who counts as a dependant** — A spouse (or spouses) without resources; children under 21, or disabled at any age; children up to 25 in study or apprenticeship; children taken into the taxpayer's own household within the same age limits; and invalid ascendants or collaterals of the taxpayer or spouse who live permanently under the taxpayer's roof and are wholly supported by them. **Capped at six dependants**  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI art. 72 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Dependants are fixed at 1 January** — Family charges are those existing at 1 January of the tax year; an increase during the year is taken into account in the declaration following the event. Spouses nominate who claims the children by a declaration to the employer no later than 15 January; failing that the claim falls to the husband, or to a wife if he is unable to act  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI arts. 5 and 73 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Spouses are taxed separately** — A spouse with their own income (and each spouse in a polygamous marriage) is taxed separately on all of their income and is treated as single for the calculation, with no art. 73 dependant reduction unless they prove exclusive personal support of a child. The same children can never give the reduction to two spouses  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI art. 6 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Residence test** — An individual is fiscally domiciled in Togo if any one of these applies: their *foyer* or principal place of stay is in Togo; they carry on a professional activity there, employed or not, unless they show it is ancillary; the centre of their economic interests is in Togo; or they are a State agent posted abroad who is not personally taxed on total income in that country  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI art. 3 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Scope of charge** — Individuals fiscally domiciled in Togo are taxed on their worldwide income, Togolese-source and foreign-source alike. Individuals not domiciled in Togo are taxed on Togo-source income, **or if they have one or more dwellings in Togo**. Both are subject to any applicable double tax treaty  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI art. 2 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Exemptions from IRPP** — Foreign-national ambassadors, diplomatic agents, consuls and consular agents, subject to reciprocity and only on their official remuneration and foreign-source private income; and individuals subject to an *impôt synthétique*  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI art. 4 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Employment income (PAYE-equivalent)** — Tax on public- and private-sector employees, and on *gérants* and partners of certain companies, is withheld at source by the employer **at each payment** of taxable sums, and shown on the payslip  _(Livre des Procédures Fiscales (Togo) 2025, LPF art. 26 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Monthly withholding remittance deadline** — Withheld IRPP is paid to the tax office by the **15th of the following month**, with a dated and signed *bordereau-avis* naming the withholding period, the payer and the total withheld. This is a fixed calendar date, not a rolling 15 days from the end of the pay period. Payment records must be kept for ten years  _(Livre des Procédures Fiscales (Togo) 2025, LPF art. 27 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Income not withheld, and withholding adjustments** — Income that was not subject to those withholdings, and any adjustments to them, must be declared and the tax paid by **15 January of the following year**  _(Livre des Procédures Fiscales (Togo) 2025, LPF art. 27 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Annual salary declaration (DAS)** — Employers file a *déclaration annuelle des salaires* by **31 January** each year, showing separately for each executive or managerial employee the tax identification number and the employment-expense, entertainment, travel, mission and other professional allowances or reimbursements of the previous year  _(Livre des Procédures Fiscales (Togo) 2025, LPF art. 28 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Domestic staff — one annual payment** — For household staff employed by individuals who are themselves employees, the withholding is remitted in a **single payment by 31 January** each year, not monthly  _(Livre des Procédures Fiscales (Togo) 2025, LPF art. 28 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Annual income tax return deadline** — A detailed declaration of the previous fiscal year's income is due by **31 March** each year, on the form prescribed by the tax administration, addressed to the assessment office for the taxpayer's fiscal domicile  _(Livre des Procédures Fiscales (Togo) 2025, LPF art. 20 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Most employees do not file at all** — Taxpayers whose only income is salaries, wages, pensions, life annuities and/or investment income are **exempt from the art. 20 filing obligation**, provided the tax has been withheld at source  _(Livre des Procédures Fiscales (Togo) 2025, LPF art. 21 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Business income return** — Taxpayers taxed on *revenus d'affaires* (CGI art. 29 and following) file by **31 March** in five copies, declaring taxable profit — or the loss, on the same deadline, if the year was loss-making. Where the profit genuinely cannot be determined in time, a provisional declaration may be filed on the same deadline and regularised within three months  _(Livre des Procédures Fiscales (Togo) 2025, LPF art. 22 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Instalments on business income** — Four instalments, each a quarter of the previous closed year's assessment rounded down to the nearest XOF 1,000, due **31 January, 31 May, 31 July and 31 October**. The balance is paid spontaneously when the return is filed  _(Livre des Procédures Fiscales (Togo) 2025, LPF art. 25; Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI arts. 114–117 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Non-resident taxation — 20% withholding, creditable but not refundable** — Sums paid by a debtor established in Togo to persons or companies with no permanent professional establishment in Togo bear a **20%** withholding on the gross amount: remuneration for non-commercial (BNC) activity carried on in or outside Togo, inventors' and authors' royalties and income from industrial or commercial property and similar rights, and payments for services of any kind supplied or used in Togo or abroad. The withholding is **credited against** the year's income tax and is **not refundable** — so it is a non-refundable credit, not a *libératoire* final tax. It is remitted within the month following the month of payment, and the payer must issue individual withholding certificates percent  _(Livre des Procédures Fiscales (Togo) 2025, LPF art. 98 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Investment income withholding is final for individuals** — For an individual, the withholding on investment income discharges the income tax on the products it applies to. Variable-income products: **13%**, or **3%** on dividends of companies listed on a CREPMF-approved WAEMU exchange. Fixed-income products: **13%**. The *libératoire* character **cannot be invoked** for products that are taken into account in determining the taxable profit of an industrial, commercial, artisanal or agricultural business or of a non-commercial profession percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI arts. 79–80 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Rental income withholding — 8.75%, and it splits** — Gross rents due to property owners bear an **8.75%** withholding, remitted by the **15th of the month following** the period the rent relates to. On proof of payment it is credited **3.75% against the taxe foncière and 5% against income tax**, once the assessment notices for those taxes are issued. Tenant and owner are jointly liable for it percent  _(Livre des Procédures Fiscales (Togo) 2025, LPF art. 100 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Not every tenant withholds** — The tenants designated to operate the rental withholding are public bodies, international organisations, legal persons of public or private law, and individuals carrying on an artisanal, commercial or non-commercial activity. A landlord whose rents do **not** suffer the withholding must instead pay 8.75% instalments on declaration, by the 15th of the month following the period the rent relates to  _(Livre des Procédures Fiscales (Togo) 2025, LPF arts. 100–101 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_

## What this replaces

The previous version of this guide gave a five-band scale sourced entirely to a
commercial summary site, and it was wrong on nearly every line. Recorded here
because the shape of the error is instructive, not to dwell on it:

| Previous claim | CGI art. 74 |
|---|---|
| up to 900,000 → 0.5% | **exonéré** |
| 900,001–3,000,000 → 7% | **3%** |
| 3,000,001–**5,500,000** → 15% | 3,000,001–**6,000,000** → **10%** |
| 5,500,001–15,000,000 → 25% | four separate bands: 15%, 20%, 25% |
| above 15,000,000 → 35% | 15,000,001–20,000,000 → **30%**; above 20,000,000 → 35% |

Five bands where the statute has eight, and the top rate starting five million
francs too early. The guide also asserted a *quotient familial* that Togo does
not operate, and omitted both the 28% standard deduction and the flat 30% on
business income — each of which changes the answer on its own.
