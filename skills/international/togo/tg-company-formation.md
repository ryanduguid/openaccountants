# Togo Company Formation & Entity Choice

## Entity types and incorporation

> **The uniform act has been read, and this guide did not match it.** Togo is
> an **OHADA** member state, so its company law is the *Acte uniforme relatif au
> droit des sociétés commerciales et du groupement d'intérêt économique* (AUSCGIE),
> adopted at Ouagadougou on **30 January 2014** and published in the **Journal
> Officiel de l'OHADA, numéro spécial du 4 février 2014**. What the Act actually
> provides:
>
> - **SARL capital — article 311.** *"**Sauf dispositions nationales contraires**,
>   le capital social doit être d'un million (1.000.000) de francs CFA au moins. Il
>   est divisé en parts sociales égales dont la valeur nominale ne peut être
>   inférieure à cinq mille (5.000) francs CFA."* So the uniform minimum **is**
>   1,000,000, the **5,000 is the minimum nominal value of one part sociale**, and
>   this is the **only** capital figure a member state may displace by its own law.
> - **SARL pay-up — article 311-1.** Parts subscribed in full; those for
>   contributions in kind paid up in full; those for cash paid up on subscription to
>   **at least half**, balance within **two years** of RCCM registration.
> - **SA capital — articles 387–389.** The **ordinary** minimum is **10,000,000**;
>   public offerings and regulated activities require separate capital checks, and
>   article 311's derogation wording does not appear here. Nominal value of shares
>   freely fixed by the statutes; capital **entirely subscribed before the statutes
>   are signed**; cash shares paid up **at least one quarter** on subscription,
>   balance within **three years** of RCCM registration.
> - **Listed or public-offering companies — article 824.** Minimum **100,000,000**
>   where the securities are listed on a member state's exchange **or** offered
>   publicly for placement in one or more member states. Any interested party may
>   seek dissolution if it is not met.
> - **One member is enough.** A SARL may be instituted by a single natural or legal
>   person (art. 309) and *"la société anonyme peut ne comprendre qu'un seul
>   actionnaire"* (art. 385).
>
>
> **This is not a theoretical gap.** Burkina Faso has exercised the derogation:
> article 1 of *Décret n°2016-314 of 3 May 2016* replaces article 3 of *Décret
> n°2014-462* with a rule letting members fix SARL capital freely in the articles,
> so the uniform 1,000,000 is **not** Burkina Faso's national minimum. Assume
> nothing about any other member state without checking its own law —
> [the decree](https://police.gov.bf/index.php/infos-utiles/textes-officiels/category/3-decrets?download=26:decret-capital-minimum).
>
> [RESEARCH GAP — what is not settled is whether **Togo** has enacted contrary
> national provisions under article 311. The uniform default is stated below as the
> default it is; a reviewer with the national company legislation should confirm
> whether it displaces the 1,000,000 figure.]
>
> _(Acte uniforme relatif au droit des sociétés commerciales et du GIE, adopté le 30 janvier 2014 — Journal Officiel de l'OHADA, n° spécial du 4 février 2014: https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974)_


- **SARL (limited liability company)** — Most common form; limited liability, one or more shareholders  _(OHADA Uniform Act on Commercial Companies and Economic Interest Groups (https://chandrawatpartners.com/llc-sarl-incorporation-in-togo-a-practical-professional-overview-for-investors-across-the-globe/))_
- **SA (public limited company) minimum capital** — **10,000,000**, no longer "approx": article 387 fixes it with no derogation clause. **100,000,000** where the securities are listed on a member state's exchange or publicly offered for placement in one or more member states (art. 824) XOF  _(AUSCGIE 2014, arts. 387 and 824 — https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974)_
- **SAS (simplified joint-stock company)** — Société par Actions Simplifiée — flexible governance, no statutory minimum capital (approx — confirm the SAS provisions specifically; the 10,000,000 in art. 387 is the **SA** figure and does not carry across)  _(OHADA Uniform Act on Commercial Companies and Economic Interest Groups)_
- **SARL minimum share capital** — **OHADA sets 1,000,000** (art. 311), not "no high statutory minimum". A figure "effectively from XOF 100,000" would be **Togolese national law** exercising the article's derogation, and needs its own citation  _(AUSCGIE 2014, art. 311 — https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974; the 100,000 figure as described at [healyconsultants.com](https://www.healyconsultants.com/togo-company-registration/))_
- **Single-member company** — One shareholder and one director permitted  _(OHADA Uniform Act on Commercial Companies and Economic Interest Groups (https://www.healyconsultants.com/togo-company-registration/))_
- **Registration body** — Centre de Formalités des Entreprises (CFE) — one-stop shop; registration on the RCCM  _(Centre de Formalités des Entreprises (Togo) (https://www.deel.com/blog/entity-setup-togo/))_
- **Incorporation steps** — Name reservation; draft/notarize statutes (French); deposit capital; CFE/RCCM registration; OTR tax/VAT and CNSS registration  _(OHADA Uniform Act / Centre de Formalités des Entreprises (Togo) (https://chandrawatpartners.com/llc-sarl-incorporation-in-togo-a-practical-professional-overview-for-investors-across-the-globe/))_
- **Incorporation timeline** — Roughly 1 to 4 weeks via the CFE (approx — confirm)  _(Centre de Formalités des Entreprises (Togo) (https://www.healyconsultants.com/togo-company-registration/))_
- **Incorporation cost** — Government, notary and professional fees roughly XOF 300,000 to 800,000+ XOF (approx — confirm)  _(Centre de Formalités des Entreprises (Togo) (https://chandrawatpartners.com/llc-sarl-incorporation-in-togo-a-practical-professional-overview-for-investors-across-the-globe/))_
- **Capital deposit** — Share capital deposited in a corporate bank account; bank certificate required for registration  _(OHADA Uniform Act on Commercial Companies and Economic Interest Groups (https://chandrawatpartners.com/llc-sarl-incorporation-in-togo-a-practical-professional-overview-for-investors-across-the-globe/))_
- **Registration duty on formation — none** — Acts forming a company, and acts increasing share capital, are on the list of acts stamped and **registered *gratis***. There is no percentage duty on the contribution itself, so the incorporation cost above is notary and agency fees, not tax. The 2% that circulates for Togo is the duty on acts **extending a company's term** (*prorogation*) containing no obligation, discharge or transfer of property  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI arts. 464(21) and 436 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **VAT registration is not automatic on incorporation** — A new company is liable to VAT only once turnover exceeds **XOF 100,000,000**, taking effect from the first day of the month the threshold is crossed — except for liberal professions, holders of *charges et offices*, and the freight-forwarding, port and maritime-expertise professions, which are liable at any turnover. Below the threshold, registration is by option before 30 November for five years  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI arts. 176–177 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **New businesses skip the minimum tax for a year** — The *Minimum Forfaitaire de Perception* is not due for a new business's **first twelve months** of operation. A company formed by transforming another legal person does not count as new  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI art. 121 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Core annual compliance** — Annual financial statements (SYSCOHADA) filed with the return — balance sheet, income statement, cash flow statement, notes, detailed overheads by category, the extra-accounting adjustments to the fiscal result, and the depreciation and provisions schedule — with the IS return by **31 March** in five copies, monthly VAT and IRPP-withholding returns by the 15th, the annual salary declaration by 31 January, and instalments on 31 January, 31 May, 31 July and 31 October  _(Livre des Procédures Fiscales (Togo) 2025, LPF arts. 22–25, 27–28 and 60 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Statutes language** — Articles of association drafted in French  _(OHADA Uniform Act on Commercial Companies and Economic Interest Groups (https://chandrawatpartners.com/llc-sarl-incorporation-in-togo-a-practical-professional-overview-for-investors-across-the-globe/))_

Togo follows the OHADA Uniform Act on Commercial Companies. Most investors use a Société à Responsabilité Limitée (SARL); incorporation runs through the Centre de Formalités des Entreprises (CFE) one-stop shop with registration on the RCCM.

## Annual company reporting under the 2014 AUSCGIE

The revised AUSCGIE took effect on **5 May 2014**. The following rules address
annual SARL and SA decisions and commercial-company registry deposits.
[OHADA company law register](https://www.ohada.org/droit-des-societes-commerciales-et-du-gie/)

- **SARL annual meeting.** The annual ordinary general meeting must take place
  within **6 months after the financial year closes**. The managers may ask the
  competent court for an extension. [AUSCGIE, articles 347 and 348](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974#page=86)
- **SA annual meeting.** The ordinary general meeting must take place at least
  once a year, within **6 months after the financial year closes**, subject to
  an extension by court decision. [AUSCGIE, article 548](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974#page=133)
- **Sole-member companies.** The sole shareholder of an SA takes the annual
  ordinary decisions within **6 months after the financial year closes** and
  records them in minutes kept in the company's archives. Article 347 applies
  these sole-member decision rules to a SARL, with the exceptions it specifies.
  [AUSCGIE, article 347](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974#page=86),
  [articles 558 to 560](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974#page=135)
- **Deposit of annual accounts.** A commercial company must deposit the prior
  year's annual financial statements with the RCCM in the state of its
  registered office **within the month following approval** by the competent
  body. If approval is refused, a copy of the refusal decision must be deposited
  within the same period. [AUSCGIE, article 269, pages 65 and 66](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974#page=65)

For example, for a multi-member SARL with a **31 December 2025** year-end, the
ordinary annual meeting is due by **30 June 2026**, subject to a court extension.
Track the RCCM deposit deadline from the actual approval decision. Record the
national tax-return deadline separately. The current registry filing method,
fees, applicable accounting and audit rules and any sector-specific obligations
still need verification. [AUSCGIE, articles 269 and 348](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974)
