# Togo Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax (Impôt sur les Sociétés)

Companies liable in Togo pay corporate income tax (*Impôt sur les Sociétés*, IS)
on net taxable profit, with a turnover-based floor — the **Minimum Forfaitaire
de Perception (MFP)**, which is what the code calls it. Free-trade-zone
enterprises benefit from holidays and reduced rates.

**Figures below are read from the consolidated *Code Général des Impôts et Livre
des Procédures Fiscales, mis à jour 2025* published by the Office Togolais des
Recettes.** That PDF binds two separately numbered books, so citations name the
book: **CGI art. n** for the Code Général des Impôts, **LPF art. n** for the
Livre des Procédures Fiscales.

- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 27% of taxable profit. Any fraction of taxable profit below XOF 1,000 is disregarded percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI art. 113 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Minimum Forfaitaire de Perception (MFP)** — **1% of turnover excluding VAT** for the last closed financial year, rising to **2%** on the import of second-hand vehicles for resale. It is due where the company is loss-making or where the tax on the fiscal result would be lower. **In no case less than XOF 20,000** for taxpayers on the *régime du bénéfice réel*. Turnover means all operations of the taxpayer's ordinary professional activity including SYSCOHADA accessory income; for imported second-hand vehicles the base is the customs value percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI art. 120 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Who is exempt from the MFP** — Cooperatives, their unions, federations and confederations whatever their activity; companies exempt from IS; **new businesses for their first twelve months** of operation (a company formed by transforming another legal person is not new); businesses approved under the investment code during their IS or income tax exemption period; businesses that ceased all professional activity before 1 January of the tax year and were not assessed to the *patente* in the previous year's assessment notices; businesses whose sole object is publishing, printing or selling periodicals; individuals on a *régime synthétique*; home-worker regime beneficiaries under CGI art. 18; and businesses approved under the SME incentive regime at CGI arts. 143–153  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI art. 121 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Who pays IS** — SAs, single-shareholder SAs, SAS, SARLs, and single-member SARLs where the sole member is a legal person, whatever their object; and also SNCs, *sociétés en commandite simple*, *sociétés en participation*, *sociétés de fait*, GIEs, cooperatives and their groupings, property-dealing intermediaries and *crédit foncier* companies, and companies subdividing and selling their own land  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI arts. 91–92 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Place of assessment** — IS is assessed under a single entry in the legal person's name for all its taxable activities in Togo, at its principal establishment. The administration may instead designate the place of effective management, or the registered office if it is in Togo. Legal persons operating in or holding property in Togo without a registered office there are assessed at their place of effective management  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI art. 112 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Capital gains — ordinary IS income, except mining titles** — Gains on assets **other than** Togolese mining titles and exploitation licences realised by companies liable to IS are **exempt from the separate *taxe sur les plus-values***, so they fall into the IS base and bear the 27% rate. Gains on Togolese mining titles and exploitation licences are taxed under the separate tax at **15%**, and CGI art. 89 excludes that income from the IS taxable profit — it is not taxed twice  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI arts. 84, 89–90 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Indirect disposals of mining interests are caught** — A disposal of shares, partnership interests or any direct or indirect participation of **10% or more** in a legal person holding a Togolese mining title or licence is treated as an indirect disposal, including by merger, demerger or partial asset contribution. The tax is borne by the transferor notwithstanding any agreement to the contrary  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI art. 83 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Separate capital gains tax rate (non-IS assets)** — 7% on disposals of immovable property or real property rights and of shares or partnership interests; 15% on Togolese mining titles and exploitation licences percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI art. 90 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Withholding tax — dividends and other variable-income products** — Legal persons: **13%** of distributed income, **7%** on dividends of companies listed on an exchange approved by the CREPMF within WAEMU. Individuals: **13%**, or **3%** on those listed dividends, and the withholding is *libératoire* of income tax. **No withholding** on dividends distributed between parent and subsidiary companies established in Togo that meet the CGI art. 107 conditions percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI art. 79 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Withholding tax — interest and other fixed-income products** — Legal persons: **6%** on prizes paid to creditors, **6%** on bond income whoever the issuer, **3%** on bonds issued by public bodies and their emanations with a term of five to ten years, **0%** where that term exceeds ten years, **3%** on gains from disposing of bonds, and **6%** on other income. Individuals: **13%**, *libératoire* of income tax percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI art. 80 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Withholding tax — payments to non-residents (including royalties)** — **20%** of the gross amount on sums paid by a debtor established in Togo to persons or companies with no permanent professional establishment there: remuneration for non-commercial (BNC) activity carried on in or outside Togo, inventors' and authors' royalties and income from industrial or commercial property and similar rights, and payments for services of any kind supplied or used in Togo or abroad. Credited against the year's income tax; **not refundable**. Remitted within the month following the month of payment percent  _(Livre des Procédures Fiscales (Togo) 2025, LPF art. 98 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Withholding tax — service fees to residents: 3% / 5% / 20%** — On fees for services of the non-commercial professions at CGI art. 35 used in Togo, and on sums paid to banking and payment-services intermediaries (insurance general agents, brokers, commission agents, business agents, exclusive agents and their agents): **3%** where the recipient produces a valid *attestation de régularité fiscale*, **5%** where they hold a tax identification number, and **20%** otherwise. Remitted by the 15th of the following month. Withholding agents are Togo-established debtors, the State, local authorities and public establishments, NGOs, and projects and programmes percent  _(Livre des Procédures Fiscales (Togo) 2025, LPF art. 99 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **No *taxe sur les salaires* in the CGI** — The consolidated 2025 code contains **no employer tax on salaries**. The only taxes assimilated to direct taxes are the *Taxe sur les Véhicules à Moteur* and the TETTIC. An employer's payroll charge in Togo is the CNSS social security contribution, which sits outside the CGI — see the payroll guide. Treat any "3% employer payroll tax" figure attributed to the Togolese tax code as unsupported until a current statute is produced for it  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI Titre Deuxième — Taxes assimilées aux impôts directs — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Telecoms turnover tax (TETTIC) — 5%** — Businesses holding a licence to establish and operate a public telecommunications network pay 5% of annual turnover excluding VAT. The tax is deductible from the income tax base percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI arts. 171 to 171 quinquies — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Company formation and capital increases are registered free of duty** — Acts forming a company and acts increasing share capital are on the list of acts stamped and **registered *gratis***. There is no percentage duty on the contribution itself. The 2% that circulates for Togo is the duty on **acts extending a company's term** (*prorogation*) that contain no obligation, discharge or transfer of property, which is a different act percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI arts. 464(21) and 436 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Merger and share transfer duties** — Mergers of SAs and SARLs bear **1%**, whether by absorption or by forming a new company; where the liabilities taken over exceed the absorbed company's assets, a fixed duty of **XOF 5,000** applies instead on that excess. Transfers of shares, founders' shares, beneficiary shares and interests in companies whose capital is not divided into shares also bear **1%**, whether partial or total percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI art. 438 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Annual IS return deadline** — **31 March**, in five copies, declaring the taxable profit of the preceding year or financial year; a loss is declared on the same deadline. Where the profit genuinely cannot be determined in time, a provisional declaration may be filed by 31 March and must be regularised within three months, failing which the late-filing penalties at LPF art. 113 apply  _(Livre des Procédures Fiscales (Togo) 2025, LPF art. 22 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Accounts filed with the return** — Balance sheet, income statement, cash flow statement, notes, a detailed list of overheads by category, a detailed statement of the extra-accounting adjustments made to reach the fiscal result, the depreciation and provisions schedule on the administration's model, and (for non-profit entities) the donor register under art. 17 of the OHADA uniform act on non-profit accounting. Benefits in kind granted to staff must be recorded explicitly in the accounts by nature and value, and the accountant or expert responsible for the books must be named  _(Livre des Procédures Fiscales (Togo) 2025, LPF arts. 23–24 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Instalments** — Four instalments, each a quarter of the previous closed year's assessment (or the MFP standing in for it), rounded down to the nearest XOF 1,000, due **31 January, 31 May, 31 July and 31 October**. Where instalments exceed the final liability the excess is refunded, net of other direct taxes owed. The balance is paid spontaneously when the return is filed. A company expecting no further liability may apply to the Commissaire des Impôts for a waiver at least fifteen days before the next due date  _(Livre des Procédures Fiscales (Togo) 2025, LPF art. 25; Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI arts. 114–117 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Free-trade-zone incentive** — Tax holidays and reduced rates for Free Trade Zone enterprises. Terms are set by the free-zone legislation, not the CGI; confirm the current schedule before relying on it  _([Togo Free Trade Zone regime](https://www.state.gov/reports/2025-investment-climate-statements/togo))_

## What this replaces

Four figures in the previous version came from a bank's country summary and did
not survive the code:

| Previous claim | Consolidated CGI 2025 |
|---|---|
| minimum tax "commonly cited minimum of XOF 3,000" | **MFP: 1% of turnover excl. VAT, floor XOF 20,000** (art. 120) |
| royalties to residents "5%/10% (with/without tax number)" | art. 99 is **service fees**, at **3% / 5% / 20%** |
| employer payroll tax "3% on gross salaries" | **no *taxe sur les salaires* in the code at all** |
| registration duty on capital contributions "2%" | formation and capital increases are **registered *gratis*** (art. 464(21)); the 2% is the *prorogation* duty (art. 436) |

The 27% rate, the 31 March deadline, the dividend rates and the interest rates
were right, and are now cited to the article rather than to the summary.
