# Togo Payroll & Social Contributions

## Payroll and social security (CNSS)

- **Employer CNSS contribution (total)** — 17.5% percent  _(Code de Sécurité Sociale (Togo) — CNSS, https://www.cleiss.fr/docs/cotisations/togo.html)_
- **Employer — old-age/invalidity/survivors pension** — 12.5% percent  _(Code de Sécurité Sociale (Togo) — CNSS, https://www.cleiss.fr/docs/cotisations/togo.html)_
- **Employer — family benefits** — 3% percent  _(Code de Sécurité Sociale (Togo) — CNSS, https://www.cleiss.fr/docs/cotisations/togo.html)_
- **Employer — occupational risk** — 2% percent  _(Code de Sécurité Sociale (Togo) — CNSS, https://www.cleiss.fr/docs/cotisations/togo.html)_
- **Employee CNSS contribution** — 4% (old-age/invalidity/survivors pension) percent  _(Code de Sécurité Sociale (Togo) — CNSS, https://www.cleiss.fr/docs/cotisations/togo.html)_
- **Combined CNSS rate** — 21.5% (17.5% employer + 4% employee) percent  _(Code de Sécurité Sociale (Togo) — CNSS, https://www.cleiss.fr/docs/cotisations/togo.html)_
- **Minimum contribution base (SMIG)** — Base may not be below the SMIG of XOF 52,500/month XOF  _(Code de Sécurité Sociale (Togo) — CNSS, https://www.cleiss.fr/docs/cotisations/togo.html)_
- **Contribution ceiling (plafond)** — A monthly ceiling applies to the contributory wage base ((approx — confirm exact XOF ceiling with CNSS)) (approx — confirm exact XOF ceiling with CNSS)  _(Code de Sécurité Sociale (Togo) — CNSS)_
- **No *taxe sur les salaires* in the tax code** — The consolidated *Code Général des Impôts, mis à jour 2025* contains **no employer tax on salaries**; the only taxes assimilated to direct taxes are the *Taxe sur les Véhicules à Moteur* and the telecoms turnover tax (TETTIC). The employer's payroll charge in Togo is the CNSS contribution above. Treat any "3% employer payroll tax" attributed to the Togolese tax code as unsupported until a current statute is produced for it  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, Titre Deuxième — Taxes assimilées aux impôts directs — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **IRPP withholding (PAYE-equivalent)** — Tax on public- and private-sector employees, and on *gérants* and partners of certain companies, is withheld by the employer **at each payment** of taxable sums, on the CGI art. 74 progressive scale, and shown on the payslip  _(Livre des Procédures Fiscales (Togo) 2025, LPF art. 26 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Monthly IRPP remittance deadline** — Withheld IRPP is paid to the tax office by the **15th of the following month**, with a dated and signed *bordereau-avis* naming the withholding period, the payer and the total withheld. Payment records must be kept for ten years. Income not withheld, and adjustments, are declared and paid by **15 January of the following year**  _(Livre des Procédures Fiscales (Togo) 2025, LPF art. 27 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Annual salary declaration (DAS) — 31 January** — Employers file a *déclaration annuelle des salaires* by 31 January, showing separately for each executive or managerial employee the tax identification number and the employment-expense, entertainment, travel, mission and other professional allowances or reimbursements of the previous year. For household staff employed by individuals who are themselves employees, the withholding is remitted **once a year, by 31 January**, not monthly  _(Livre des Procédures Fiscales (Togo) 2025, LPF art. 28 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Employee contributions are deductible from taxable pay** — Mandatory pension/retirement withholdings are deductible up to 6% of gross pay, and employee contributions to compulsory social and health insurance are deductible in full, before the 28% standard deduction and the art. 74 scale are applied  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI art. 26 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Universal health insurance (AMU)** — Separate AMU contributions apply under Decree 2023-096 ((approx — confirm rate)) (approx — confirm rate)  _(Décret 2023-096 fixant les taux de cotisations AMU, https://cnss.tg/cnss-media/2024/01/DECRET-2023-096-fixant-les-taux-de-cotisations-AMU.pdf)_
