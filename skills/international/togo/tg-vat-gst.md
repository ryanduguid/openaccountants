# Togo VAT / GST

## Value added tax (TVA)

**Figures below are read from the consolidated *Code Général des Impôts et Livre
des Procédures Fiscales, mis à jour 2025* published by the Office Togolais des
Recettes.** That PDF binds two separately numbered books, so citations name the
book: **CGI art. n** for the Code Général des Impôts, **LPF art. n** for the
Livre des Procédures Fiscales.

- **Standard VAT rate — and the only rate** — 18%. CGI art. 195 states it as a **single rate** (*un taux unique*) applying to all activities and all products except those exempted by art. 180. The paragraph that followed it in the code is marked *Abrogé* percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI art. 195 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **There is no reduced rate** — The consolidated 2025 code carries **no 10% reduced rate** for accommodation, catering, hotels, restaurants or tour operators. Charging 10% on those supplies on the strength of a summary site would under-collect by eight points. Confirm against a current *loi de finances* before applying any reduced rate to Togo percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI art. 195 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Leasing follows the asset** — For *crédit-bail* / leasing operations the rate applied to the rentals is the rate of the asset at the time it was bought percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI art. 195 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Exports — exempt, with input VAT still deductible** — Exports of goods and services assimilated to exports are exempt, and exporters keep the right to deduct the VAT charged by their suppliers under CGI arts. 196 ff. Also in this list: sale, repair or conversion of registered seagoing vessels; supplies to shipping companies and professional fishermen for incorporation in or upkeep of their vessels, and sea-fishing gear and nets; victualling of ships and aircraft bound abroad; work on aircraft of airlines whose international services are at least 60% of the routes they operate; entries into bonded or suspensive customs regimes subject to actual export; and international air and sea passenger transport percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI art. 181 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Financial and insurance services are exempt because another tax applies** — Banking and financial operations are exempt from VAT because they bear the *taxe sur les activités financières* (TAF); insurance business, and services of insurance brokers and intermediaries, are exempt because they bear the *taxe sur les conventions d'assurance*. These are not untaxed supplies — reading them as tax-free is the common error  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI art. 180(I) — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Passenger transport is exempt only on the TPU** — Passenger transport receipts are exempt only where the carrier is liable to the *taxe professionnelle unique* as a synthetic tax under CGI art. 135. There is **no general exemption for transport**: freight transport is an ordinary taxable service under art. 174. The same paragraph exempts other business of individuals on the TPU (arts. 128 ff), operations within the entertainment and gambling taxes, and transfers of bare land, goodwill or clientele that bear registration duty  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI art. 180(I) — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Other exempt supplies** — Unprocessed deliveries of farm, livestock and fishing produce by farmers, herders and artisanal fishermen, and agricultural equipment; medical consultations and care, hospital and clinic services, veterinary and paramedical care, hospitalisation costs, prostheses and laboratory analyses; school, university, technical and vocational teaching; newspapers and periodicals excluding advertising revenue; original works of art sold by their creator; services by non-profit associations and public-utility establishments at or below cost (**but accommodation, catering and running bars stay taxable**); school, university and staff canteens; products in the chapter's VAT annex; supplies by the State, local authorities and non-commercial public establishments; industrial free-zone enterprises for goods and services strictly needed to set up and operate; and postage and fiscal stamps at face value  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI art. 180 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **VAT registration threshold — XOF 100,000,000** — A person is liable to VAT when turnover **exceeds XOF 100,000,000**. The XOF 60,000,000 figure that circulates for Togo is real but belongs to a different tax: it is the ceiling of the *taxe professionnelle unique* for individuals at CGI art. 130 XOF  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI art. 177 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Liable whatever the turnover** — Liberal professions and holders of *charges et offices* are liable as of right at any turnover. So are air and maritime transport commission agents, air and sea freight agents, approved port commission agents, ship consignees and the maritime expertise professions  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI art. 177 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Crossing the threshold mid-year, and falling back under it** — Where the threshold is reached during the year, VAT liability takes effect from the **first day of the month** in which that happens. A business whose turnover falls below the threshold must keep filing and paying for **three consecutive financial years**  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI art. 177 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Voluntary registration** — Persons with turnover **below XOF 100,000,000**, and farmers, herders, fishermen and artisans making unprocessed deliveries of their produce, may opt into VAT. The option must be made **before 30 November**, runs for **five years** from 1 January of the following year, needs the Commissaire des impôts' approval, and renews by tacit reconduction unless denounced  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI art. 176 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Filing frequency** — A monthly return on the prescribed form is due to the tax office by the **15th of each month** for the preceding month, showing taxable and non-taxable turnover, gross tax computed, deductions in detail, and tax payable or credit carried. **The return is still due for a month with no taxable operations**, and the obligation expressly covers operators of foreign and local e-commerce platforms  _(Livre des Procédures Fiscales (Togo) 2025, LPF art. 60 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Non-residents: a fiscal representative first, reverse charge as the fallback** — A non-established business making imports, supplies of goods or services taxable in Togo bears the same tax as a Togolese business doing the same thing. A VAT-liable person established or domiciled outside Togo must **accredit a Togo-domiciled representative** who undertakes their formalities and pays the tax in their place. **Failing that**, the tax and any penalties must be assessed and paid by the **recipients or beneficiaries** of the taxable operation. The reverse charge is the consequence of not appointing a representative, not the primary mechanism  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI art. 185 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Accounting obligations** — A VAT-liable person must keep full accounts on the SYSCOHADA *système normal*, including a general ledger, a journal initialled by the judge of the court of first instance, an inventory book, and auxiliary sales/receipts and purchases/expenses journals  _(Livre des Procédures Fiscales (Togo) 2025, LPF art. 61 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **2025 VAT reforms** — Recent VAT reforms have increased compliance and administrative obligations for businesses. Read alongside the consolidated code above, which is the *mis à jour 2025* text  _(Loi de finances (Togo) 2025 (as described at [wts.com](https://wts.com/global/publishing-article/20250930-togo-vat-reforms~publishing-article)))_
