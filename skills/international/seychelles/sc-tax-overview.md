# Seychelles tax overview

Identify the taxpayer, income type, source and period. Business tax applies to taxable business income. For a resident outside the Eleventh Schedule company rules, the ordinary source test looks at where activities take place, goods are situated or rights are used. For non-resident business income, check attribution to a Seychelles permanent establishment and the separate statutory source and withholding rules. An Eleventh Schedule company has additional tests for particular foreign income, including passive income and economic substance. Check the company and income category separately. [Business Tax Act, ss 5–8][business-act] · [Business Tax Amendment Act, s 2(c) and Eleventh Schedule][amendment] · [Seychelles Revenue Commission tax system][tax-system]

Employment tax applies to emoluments from employment in Seychelles, including employment deemed to be in Seychelles under section 3. Check who pays or provides the amount or benefit, the employer's presence and the source. Employers ordinarily withhold income tax and report it through their Business Activity Statement. Their tax on non-monetary benefits follows separate rules. Check the applicable employee and benefit category before using the [payroll guide](sc-payroll-social.md). [Income and Non-Monetary Benefits Tax Act, ss 3–5][employment] · [Revenue Commission employment guidance][payroll]

## Residence and taxpayer type

For business tax, an individual can qualify as resident through residence in Seychelles, Seychelles domicile without a permanent place of abode abroad, or at least 183 days of presence in a 12-month period beginning or ending in the tax year. An entity can qualify through establishment in Seychelles or management and control there. Trusts and partnerships have specific tests. Confirm the statutory test for the particular tax and any treaty tie-break before using a residence classification. [Business Tax Act, s 2][business-act]

Business tax applies to business operators, including sole traders and companies. Check whether the business uses the ordinary return, the presumptive scheme or a different statutory route. Use the [business tax guide](sc-corporate-income-tax.md) to find the relevant regime. [Business Tax Act, ss 2 and 6][business-act] · [Revenue Commission business tax and presumptive tax guidance][tax-system]

For 2025 ordinary company business tax, apply 15% to the first SCR 1 million of taxable business income and 25% to the excess. Ordinary sole traders and partnerships use 0% through SCR 102,666, 15% on the portion above that up to SCR 1 million and 25% on the excess. Check the applicable sector, presumptive tax and exemptions separately. [S.I. 108 of 2021, regs 1–2][business-rates] · [Revenue Commission tax rates][tax-system]

## Currency, period and filing

Use Seychelles rupees (SCR), and check the statutory conversion method and date for foreign amounts. The ordinary 2025 business tax year ran from 1 January to 31 December. Section 26 provides for a substituted year with the Revenue Commissioner's approval and a transitional year when changing periods. [Central Bank of Seychelles Act, s 17][currency] · [Business Tax Act, ss 2 and 26][business-act]

Use Seychelles Revenue Commission (SRC) services for returns and payments. The ordinary deadline for calendar-year 2025 business tax returns and payments was 31 March 2026. Eligible tourism and related businesses could apply for an extension of payment to 30 June 2026. Check the filing obligation, actual extension and payment instructions separately. The SRC also administers VAT, with a 15% standard rate. Use the [VAT guide](sc-vat-gst.md) for the applicable supply or import and return. [SRC 2025 return notice][filing] · [SRC 2025 payment extension][extension] · [SRC tax system][tax-system]
