# Bulgaria tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the taxpayer type, residence facts, income sources and relevant period. Check any treaty that assigns residence or limits the source country's tax.

## Territorial reach

Bulgarian-resident individuals and companies generally account for domestic and foreign income under their respective laws. Nonresident individuals account for Bulgarian-source income, including work performed in Bulgaria where the source rule applies. Nonresident companies account for profit through a Bulgarian permanent establishment, including disposals of its assets, and other receipts specified as Bulgarian-source. Check which items require withholding and the treaty position. ## Residence

The individual rules consider permanent address, presence for more than 183 days in a 12-month period, sending abroad by a Bulgarian state body or enterprise, including the family branch, and centre of vital interests. Count entry and departure as separate days of presence under the statutory rule. The day-count test confers residence in the year in which the count exceeds 183. Presence solely for study or medical treatment does not count for that test. A Bulgarian permanent address alone does not establish residence if the person's centre of vital interests is abroad. For companies, check formation under Bulgarian law and the specific rules for European companies and European cooperative societies seated and registered in Bulgaria. Review any treaty outcome separately. [Personal Income Tax Act, art 4](https://nra.bg/wps/wcm/connect/nra.bg25863/e0daae38-32b9-40b7-8c21-ff7feed98935/%D0%97%D0%94%D0%94%D0%A4%D0%9B%2B2026.pdf?CACHEID=ROOTWORKSPACE.Z18_PPGAHG8009LJC0QTR5C2HH30I6-e0daae38-32b9-40b7-8c21-ff7feed98935-pKnjB9A&MOD=AJPERES) · [Corporate Income Tax Act, arts 3 and 4](https://nra.bg/wps/wcm/connect/nra.bg25863/ed10ff12-b25a-4b07-827c-915979acef42/ZAKON%2BZA%2BKORPORATIVNOTO%2BPODOHODNO%2BOBLAGANE_60.pdf?CACHEID=ROOTWORKSPACE.Z18_PPGAHG8009LJC0QTR5C2HH30I6-ed10ff12-b25a-4b07-827c-915979acef42-nEJyDbV&MOD=AJPERES)

## Taxpayer types

A sole trader accounts for business income under the personal income-tax rules for traders, which use specified corporate tax-base rules. A Bulgarian limited liability or joint-stock company is generally a corporate taxpayer. The corporate statute treats an unincorporated partnership as a legal person for its purposes. Check the entity and partner consequences separately and identify foreign entities under the rules applicable to their form. For a foreign trust, obtain the deed, rights, control and distribution records before classifying the vehicle, receipt and taxpayer. Check registration of beneficial owners separately where the anti-money-laundering rules apply. ## Currency

Bulgaria adopted the euro (EUR) on 1 January 2026. Current accounting records and annual financial statements use EUR. For a return for a completed period, normally use the official currency at the end of the period. A return for a period ending in or including the dual circulation period uses EUR. A 2025 calendar-year return remains in Bulgarian lev (BGN) even when filed in 2026. Check separately the euro rules for tax payments and corrections. [Euro Introduction Act, arts 50–53](https://evroto.bg/upload/1068/ZVERB.pdf) · [Government accounting guidance](https://evroto.bg/upload/687/Nasoki%20schetovodstvo.pdf) · [Government euro guidance](https://evroto.bg/bg/euro/business/faq)

## Assessment periods

Ordinary individual and corporate income taxation uses the calendar year. An ordinary newly formed corporate taxpayer has a first period from formation to calendar year end. Check any statutory exception, including a liquidation or special tax regime, before using the ordinary period. [Personal Income Tax Act, art 15](https://nra.bg/wps/wcm/connect/nra.bg25863/e0daae38-32b9-40b7-8c21-ff7feed98935/%D0%97%D0%94%D0%94%D0%A4%D0%9B%2B2026.pdf?CACHEID=ROOTWORKSPACE.Z18_PPGAHG8009LJC0QTR5C2HH30I6-e0daae38-32b9-40b7-8c21-ff7feed98935-pKnjB9A&MOD=AJPERES) · [Corporate Income Tax Act, art 21](https://nra.bg/wps/wcm/connect/nra.bg25863/ed10ff12-b25a-4b07-827c-915979acef42/ZAKON%2BZA%2BKORPORATIVNOTO%2BPODOHODNO%2BOBLAGANE_60.pdf?CACHEID=ROOTWORKSPACE.Z18_PPGAHG8009LJC0QTR5C2HH30I6-ed10ff12-b25a-4b07-827c-915979acef42-nEJyDbV&MOD=AJPERES)

## Responsible authorities

The National Revenue Agency administers ordinary personal and corporate income taxes. The Customs Agency administers import customs duties. Check the relevant municipality for local taxes and fees. [National Revenue Agency, taxes and services](https://nra.bg/wps/portal/nra/taxes) · [Customs Agency](https://customs.bg/)

## Fictional intake

A Bulgarian calendar-year company needs to file its 2025 return in 2026 and set up the ledger for its 2026 period. Identify the period of each return before choosing the currency. Its 2025 return figures stay in BGN and its 2026 accounting and ordinary 2026 return figures use EUR. Record the conversion basis and confirm the payment instruction separately.
