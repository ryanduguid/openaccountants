# Argentina Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax rates and base (2025)

- **Corporate rate — tier 1** — 25% on taxable income from ARS 0 up to ARS 101,679,575.26 %  _([Income Tax Law (Ley de Impuesto a las Ganancias, Ley 20.628), art. 73](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/argentina/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Corporate rate — tier 2** — ARS 25,419,893.82 + 30% on the amount exceeding ARS 101,679,575.26 up to ARS 1,016,795,752.62 %  _([Income Tax Law (Ley de Impuesto a las Ganancias, Ley 20.628), art. 73](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/argentina/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Corporate rate — tier 3 (top)** — 35% applies to taxable income above the second-tier ceiling (ARS 1,016,795,752.62), plus a fixed base amount % ((approx — confirm the fixed base amount and exact top-tier threshold for FY2025))  _([Income Tax Law (Ley de Impuesto a las Ganancias, Ley 20.628), art. 73](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/argentina/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Corporate bracket indexation** — The 25/30/35% bracket thresholds are indexed annually for inflation  _(Income Tax Law (Ley de Impuesto a las Ganancias, Ley 20.628), art. 73)_
- **Tax base** — Worldwide net taxable income of resident companies, with adjustment for inflation (ajuste por inflación impositivo) where applicable  _(Income Tax Law (Ley de Impuesto a las Ganancias, Ley 20.628))_
> **The non-resident payment regimes below generally apply 35% to presumed
> net income. Dividends and branch remittances have a separate 7% charge.**
> The presumed margin depends on what the payment is for and, for
> technology payments, by *whether the agreement is registered*. Every head is
> given its own line below so it can be found without reading the ones above
> it; the effective rate in bold is what the withholding agent actually
> deducts.

- **Withholding tax on dividends** — 7% on dividend distributions (profits arising from 2018 onward); equalisation tax may apply to pre-2018 earnings %  _([Income Tax Law (Ley de Impuesto a las Ganancias, Ley 20.628)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/argentina/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Withholding tax on branch profit remittances** — **7%**, the same rate as dividends, on profits arising from 2018 onward  _([Income Tax Law (Ley de Impuesto a las Ganancias, Ley 20.628)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/argentina/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Withholding tax on interest to non-residents** — 35% applied to a presumed net income margin, and the margin is what sets the rate. **15.05%** (35% on a presumed 43%) applies where the debtor is a local **bank**, or the creditor is a foreign **financial institution** that is under its own central bank's supervision, resident in a country that is not a low- or no-tax jurisdiction, has an information-exchange agreement with Argentina and no bank-secrecy regime. **35%** (presumed 100%) applies otherwise, which is where a related-party or non-bank lender lands. The presumed margins are **not contestable**. Do not carry 15.05% across from one loan to the next: it is the exception, its conditions are cumulative, and assuming it wrongly under-deducts by nearly twenty points with the withholding agent carrying the shortfall. Treaty rates range roughly 4%–15% and may reduce either figure %  _([Income Tax Law (Ley de Impuesto a las Ganancias, Ley 20.628), beneficiarios del exterior](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/argentina/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **Withholding tax on royalties to non-residents — the registration rule** — 35% on a presumed net income margin, and **registration of the agreement decides the rate, not the label on the invoice**. A **non-registered** agreement gets neither technology-regime margin below and falls to **31.5% or 35%**. Treaty relief may reduce any rate in this block %  _([Income Tax Law (Ley de Impuesto a las Ganancias, Ley 20.628), beneficiarios del exterior](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/argentina/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **Withholding tax on technical assistance, technology and engineering services** — **21%** (35% on a presumed 60%) where the service is **not obtainable in Argentina**; **28%** (presumed 80%) where it **is** obtainable in Argentina. Both require a **registered** agreement under the Foreign Technology / transfer-of-technology regime  _([Income Tax Law (Ley de Impuesto a las Ganancias, Ley 20.628), beneficiarios del exterior](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/argentina/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **Withholding tax on invention patent licences** — **28%** (35% on a presumed 80%) for the cession of rights or licences to exploit **invention patents**, under a registered agreement  _([Income Tax Law (Ley de Impuesto a las Ganancias, Ley 20.628), beneficiarios del exterior](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/argentina/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **Withholding tax on copyright** — **12.25%** (35% on a presumed 35%). The lowest rate on the schedule, and nineteen points below the residual — see the software caution below  _([Income Tax Law (Ley de Impuesto a las Ganancias, Ley 20.628), beneficiarios del exterior](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/argentina/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **Withholding tax on motion picture, video and sound recordings** — **17.5%** (35% on a presumed 50%)  _([Income Tax Law (Ley de Impuesto a las Ganancias, Ley 20.628), beneficiarios del exterior](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/argentina/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **Withholding tax on rental of Argentine real estate** — **21%** (35% on a presumed 60%). A rent head in its own right, not a species of royalty  _([Income Tax Law (Ley de Impuesto a las Ganancias, Ley 20.628), beneficiarios del exterior](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/argentina/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **Withholding tax on any other Argentine-source income** — **31.5%** (35% on a presumed 90%). This is the residual: it applies when nothing above characterises the payment, which is not the same as it being the right answer  _([Income Tax Law (Ley de Impuesto a las Ganancias, Ley 20.628), beneficiarios del exterior](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/argentina/corporate/withholding-taxes))_

> **Two characterisations this schedule invites getting wrong.**
>
> **Trademarks are not in the 28% band.** That band is invention patents and
> technical assistance. PwC does not enumerate trademarks at all, so a trademark
> royalty has to be characterised before a rate is quoted rather than assumed
> at 28%.
>
> **Software is not settled at 31.5%.** 31.5% is the residual that applies
> *absent* a better characterisation; a copyright characterisation would put the
> same payment at **12.25%**. That is a nineteen-point spread decided by the
> contract, not by a default. Get local advice rather than taking the residual
> because it is listed last.
- **Tax treaties** — Argentina has a network of 20+ double tax treaties that may reduce dividend, interest and royalty withholding rates  _(Double Taxation Agreements (Convenios para evitar la doble imposición))_
- **CIT filing & payment deadline** — Annual return generally due in the fifth/sixth month after fiscal year-end; extended to June of the following year for calendar-year companies under recent resolutions ((approx — confirm exact CUIT-based dates))  _(ARCA General Resolution (e.g. RG 5648/2025) (as described at [commenda.io](https://www.commenda.io/argentina/corporate-tax-rates)))_
- **Advance payments** — Companies make monthly/periodic advance income-tax payments (anticipos) during the year toward the annual liability  _(ARCA General Resolution on income-tax advances (anticipos))_
