# Argentina Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax rates and base (2025)

- **Corporate rate — tier 1** — 25% on taxable income from ARS 0 up to ARS 101,679,575.26 (fiscal years beginning in 2025); up to ARS 133,514,185.74 for fiscal years beginning from 1 January 2026  _(ARCA, Escala 2025 and Escala 2026; Ley de Impuesto a las Ganancias, texto ordenado 2019 (Decreto 824/2019), updated text on InfoLEG, art. 73(a), scale introduced by Ley 27.630 and indexed annually by the IPC from 1 January 2022 — https://servicios.infoleg.gob.ar/infolegInternet/anexos/330000-334999/332890/texact.htm)_
- **Corporate rate — tier 2** — ARS 25,419,893.82 + 30% on the amount exceeding ARS 101,679,575.26 up to ARS 1,016,795,752.62 (2025); ARS 33,378,546.43 + 30% on the amount exceeding ARS 133,514,185.74 up to ARS 1,335,141,857.38 (fiscal years beginning from 1 January 2026)  _(ARCA, Escala 2025 and Escala 2026; Ley de Impuesto a las Ganancias, texto ordenado 2019 (Decreto 824/2019), updated text on InfoLEG, art. 73(a), scale introduced by Ley 27.630 and indexed annually by the IPC from 1 January 2022 — https://servicios.infoleg.gob.ar/infolegInternet/anexos/330000-334999/332890/texact.htm)_
- **Corporate rate — tier 3 (top)** — ARS 299,954,747.02 + 35% on the amount exceeding ARS 1,016,795,752.62 (2025); ARS 393,866,847.93 + 35% on the amount exceeding ARS 1,335,141,857.38 (fiscal years beginning from 1 January 2026)  _(ARCA, Escala 2025 and Escala 2026; Ley de Impuesto a las Ganancias, texto ordenado 2019 (Decreto 824/2019), updated text on InfoLEG, art. 73(a), scale introduced by Ley 27.630 and indexed annually by the IPC from 1 January 2022 — https://servicios.infoleg.gob.ar/infolegInternet/anexos/330000-334999/332890/texact.htm)_
- **Corporate bracket indexation** — The three thresholds are adjusted every 1 January from 2022 by the variation in the INDEC consumer price index (art. 73, final paragraph); ARCA publishes the indexed amounts for each fiscal year (Escala 2025: https://www.afip.gob.ar/gananciasYBienes/ganancias/personas-juridicas/determinacion/documentos/Ganancias-Escala-PJ-2025.pdf ; Escala 2026: https://www.afip.gob.ar/gananciasYBienes/ganancias/personas-juridicas/determinacion/documentos/Ganancias-Escala-PJ-2026.pdf ; both opened 2026-11-01)  _(Ley de Impuesto a las Ganancias, texto ordenado 2019 (Decreto 824/2019), updated text on InfoLEG, art. 73, final paragraph — https://servicios.infoleg.gob.ar/infolegInternet/anexos/330000-334999/332890/texact.htm)_
- **Tax base** — Worldwide net taxable income of resident companies, with adjustment for inflation (ajuste por inflación impositivo) where applicable  _(Income Tax Law (Ley de Impuesto a las Ganancias, Ley 20.628))_
> **The non-resident payment regimes below generally apply 35% to presumed
> net income. Dividends and branch remittances have a separate 7% charge.**
> The presumed margin depends on what the payment is for and, for
> technology payments, by *whether the agreement is registered*. Every head is
> given its own line below so it can be found without reading the ones above
> it; the effective rate in bold is what the withholding agent actually
> deducts.

- **Withholding tax on dividends** — 7% on dividends and similar distributions out of profits arising from 2018 onward (art. 97); distributions that exceed the accumulated taxed profits bear the 35% equalisation tax as a single and final payment (art. 74)  _(Ley de Impuesto a las Ganancias, texto ordenado 2019 (Decreto 824/2019), updated text on InfoLEG, art. 97 and art. 74 — https://servicios.infoleg.gob.ar/infolegInternet/anexos/330000-334999/332890/texact.htm)_
- **Withholding tax on branch profit remittances** — **7%** additional tax when a permanent establishment remits profits to its head office (art. 73(b)), the same rate as dividends  _(Ley de Impuesto a las Ganancias, texto ordenado 2019 (Decreto 824/2019), updated text on InfoLEG, art. 73(b) — https://servicios.infoleg.gob.ar/infolegInternet/anexos/330000-334999/332890/texact.htm)_
- **Withholding tax on interest to non-residents** — 35% applied to a presumed net income margin, and the margin is what sets the rate. **15.05%** (35% on a presumed 43%) applies where the debtor is a local **bank**, or the creditor is a foreign **financial institution** that is under its own central bank's supervision, resident in a country that is not a low- or no-tax jurisdiction, has an information-exchange agreement with Argentina and no bank-secrecy regime. **35%** (presumed 100%) applies otherwise, which is where a related-party or non-bank lender lands. The presumed margins are **not contestable**. Do not carry 15.05% across from one loan to the next: it is the exception, its conditions are cumulative, and assuming it wrongly under-deducts by nearly twenty points with the withholding agent carrying the shortfall. Treaty rates range roughly 4%–15% and may reduce either figure  _(Ley de Impuesto a las Ganancias, texto ordenado 2019 (Decreto 824/2019), updated text on InfoLEG, art. 104(d) — https://servicios.infoleg.gob.ar/infolegInternet/anexos/330000-334999/332890/texact.htm)_
- **Withholding tax on royalties to non-residents — the registration rule** — 35% on a presumed net income margin, and **registration of the agreement decides the rate, not the label on the invoice**. A **non-registered** agreement gets neither technology-regime margin below and falls to **31.5% or 35%**. Treaty relief may reduce any rate in this block  _(Ley de Impuesto a las Ganancias, texto ordenado 2019 (Decreto 824/2019), updated text on InfoLEG, art. 104(a)(2) and 104(i) — https://servicios.infoleg.gob.ar/infolegInternet/anexos/330000-334999/332890/texact.htm)_
- **Withholding tax on technical assistance, technology and engineering services** — **21%** (35% on a presumed 60%) where the service is **not obtainable in Argentina**; **28%** (presumed 80%) where it **is** obtainable in Argentina. Both require a **registered** agreement under the Foreign Technology / transfer-of-technology regime  _(Ley de Impuesto a las Ganancias, texto ordenado 2019 (Decreto 824/2019), updated text on InfoLEG, art. 104(a)(1) and (2) — https://servicios.infoleg.gob.ar/infolegInternet/anexos/330000-334999/332890/texact.htm)_
- **Withholding tax on invention patent licences** — **28%** (35% on a presumed 80%) for the cession of rights or licences to exploit **invention patents**, under a registered agreement  _(Ley de Impuesto a las Ganancias, texto ordenado 2019 (Decreto 824/2019), updated text on InfoLEG, art. 104(a)(2) — https://servicios.infoleg.gob.ar/infolegInternet/anexos/330000-334999/332890/texact.htm)_
- **Withholding tax on copyright** — **12.25%** (35% on a presumed 35%). The lowest rate on the schedule, and nineteen points below the residual — see the software caution below  _(Ley de Impuesto a las Ganancias, texto ordenado 2019 (Decreto 824/2019), updated text on InfoLEG, art. 104(b) — https://servicios.infoleg.gob.ar/infolegInternet/anexos/330000-334999/332890/texact.htm)_
- **Withholding tax on motion picture, video and sound recordings** — **17.5%** (35% on a presumed 50%)  _(Income Tax Law (Ley de Impuesto a las Ganancias, Ley 20.628), beneficiarios del exterior, as reported `[RESEARCH GAP — the statute's text was not read for this change; the secondary summary that carried the figure is no longer cited]`)_
- **Withholding tax on rental of Argentine real estate** — **21%** (35% on a presumed 60%). A rent head in its own right, not a species of royalty  _(Income Tax Law (Ley de Impuesto a las Ganancias, Ley 20.628), beneficiarios del exterior, as reported `[RESEARCH GAP — the statute's text was not read for this change; the secondary summary that carried the figure is no longer cited]`)_
- **Withholding tax on any other Argentine-source income** — **31.5%** (35% on a presumed 90%). This is the residual: it applies when nothing above characterises the payment, which is not the same as it being the right answer  _(Income Tax Law (Ley de Impuesto a las Ganancias, Ley 20.628), beneficiarios del exterior, as reported `[RESEARCH GAP — the statute's text was not read for this change; the secondary summary that carried the figure is no longer cited]`)_

> **Two characterisations this schedule invites getting wrong.**
>
> **Trademarks are not in the 28% band.** That band is invention patents and
> technical assistance. The secondary summary does not enumerate trademarks at all, so a trademark
> royalty has to be characterised before a rate is quoted rather than assumed
> at 28%.
>
> **Software is not settled at 31.5%.** 31.5% is the residual that applies
> *absent* a better characterisation; a copyright characterisation would put the
> same payment at **12.25%**. That is a nineteen-point spread decided by the
> contract, not by a default. Get local advice rather than taking the residual
> because it is listed last.
- **Tax treaties** — Argentina has a network of 20+ double tax treaties that may reduce dividend, interest and royalty withholding rates  _(Double Taxation Agreements (Convenios para evitar la doble imposición))_
- **CIT filing & payment deadline** — The annual return is due in the fifth month after the month of fiscal year-end, on dates ARCA sets by CUIT ending; any balance is paid on the next business day. For a December 2025 year-end: 13 May 2026 (CUIT ending 0-3), 14 May 2026 (4-6) or 15 May 2026 (7-9). Use ARCA's table for the year, and check for any later extension by general resolution  _(ARCA, Impuesto a las ganancias – Personas jurídicas, Vencimientos 2026: https://www.afip.gob.ar/gananciasYBienes/ganancias/personas-juridicas/declaracion-jurada/documentos/Ganancias-vencimientos-PJ.pdf, opened 2026-11-01)_
- **Advance payments** — Companies make monthly/periodic advance income-tax payments (anticipos) during the year toward the annual liability  _(ARCA General Resolution on income-tax advances (anticipos))_
