# Taiwan tax overview

Identify the taxpayer type, residence or head office, the source of each item and the applicable period. The regular individual income tax applies to Taiwan-source income. Check the source rules by income type and the separate income basic tax for includible overseas income. For a company subject to enterprise income tax, a head office in Taiwan brings both Taiwan and overseas income into the tax base. When the company's head office is outside Taiwan, test Taiwan-source income and the applicable local filing or withholding route. Sole proprietorships and partnerships follow the individual income tax route described below. Check treaty relief and any special territorial rules separately. [Income Tax Act, arts 2–3, 8, 71 and 73][income-act] · [Income Basic Tax Act, art 12][basic-act] · [Southern Area bureau, enterprise scope and filing][enterprise-filing]

## Residence and taxpayer type

An individual is resident if domiciled in Taiwan and habitually living there, or if they have no Taiwan domicile and spend at least 183 days there in the tax year. For the domicile branch, the Ministry of Finance's order effective 1 January 2013 explains the test for a person with Taiwan household registration. They must have lived in Taiwan for at least 31 days in the year, or for 1 to 30 days with their centre of life and economic interests in Taiwan. Confirm the person's facts and applicable treaty before selecting a return or withholding treatment. [Income Tax Act, art 7, Chinese text][residence-act] · [Finance Ministry Order 10104610410][domicile] · [2025 foreign individual filing instructions][individual-filing]

The Act's profit-seeking enterprise category includes companies, sole proprietorships and partnerships. Check which ordinary annual return route applies. [Income Tax Act, arts 11, 14 and 71][income-act] · [Income Tax Act, art 71, Chinese text][annual-returns]

- **Company.** File an annual enterprise income tax return under article 71 where the annual filing obligation applies.
- **Sole proprietorship or partnership.** File an annual enterprise income tax return. Include the business income in the proprietor's or partners' individual income as income from profit-seeking activities. Article 71 exempts the business from calculating and paying enterprise tax for that annual filing.
- **Recognised small-scale sole proprietorship or partnership.** Article 71 exempts the business from the annual enterprise return. Include the income assessed by the tax authority in the proprietor's or partners' individual income as income from profit-seeking activities.

## Currency, periods and authority

Use New Taiwan dollars (NT$). Check the conversion method and date for foreign amounts. The ordinary 2025 tax year runs from 1 January to 31 December 2025. A profit-seeking enterprise can have a different fiscal year under article 23 with tax authority approval. For an ordinary calendar-year return, article 71 sets the statutory filing window in May of the following year. For the 2025 individual and calendar-year enterprise returns, the Ministry of Finance announced a deadline of 1 June 2026. Check the return and payment rules for any different period or departure case. [Central Bank, currency issuance][currency] · [Income Tax Act, arts 23, 71 and 101][income-act] · [Ministry of Finance, 2025 filing deadline][filing-deadline]

Use the relevant National Taxation Bureau under the Ministry of Finance and its eTax services for annual national income tax. Check return routing against the individual or enterprise's location and filing status. [Ministry of Finance, eTax portal][etax] · [2025 foreign individual filing instructions][individual-filing] · [Southern Area bureau, enterprise scope and filing][enterprise-filing]

## Main taxes

- **Individual income tax.** Ordinary resident progressive rates range from 5% to 40%. The highest bracket starts above NT$4,980,000 of net taxable income for 2025 and above NT$5,190,000 for 2026. See the [individual income guide](tw-income-tax.md). [Ministry of Finance resident rate tables][individual-rates]
- **Enterprise income tax.** The standard 2025 rate is 20%, subject to the exemptions and limits in article 5. See the [corporate income guide](tw-corporate-income-tax.md). [Income Tax Act, art 5][income-act] · [Taxation Administration, enterprise rates][corporate-rates]
- **Business tax.** The standard VAT rate is 5%. Exempt, zero-rated and non-value-added treatments have separate rules. See the [VAT guide](taiwan-vat.md). [Value-added and Non-value-added Business Tax Act, art 10][vat-act] · [Ministry of Finance, business tax rates as at 31 May 2025][vat-rate]
- **Income basic tax.** For 2025, enterprises within article 3 apply 12% to basic income above NT$600,000. Article 3 excludes sole proprietorships, partnerships and enterprises with neither a fixed place of business nor a business agent in Taiwan, amongst other exclusions. Individuals within article 3 apply 20% to basic income above NT$7.5 million. Non-resident individuals are excluded. Check what enters each basic income amount and any credits before calculating tax payable. The separate 15% proposal for specified multinational groups requires an operative instrument and confirmation of the group's period and eligibility before application. [Income Basic Tax Act, arts 3, 8, 12 and 13][basic-act] · [2025 thresholds][basic-thresholds] · [2025 enterprise basic tax return, attachment 6, field 09][basic-return] · [Ministry of Finance draft announcement, 28 August 2024][mne-draft] · [Legislative Yuan assessment 115249, November 2026][mne-assessment]
