# Isle of Man Payroll & Social Contributions

## National Insurance and payroll withholding (2026/27)

Payroll in the Isle of Man combines income tax withheld under the ITIP (Income Tax Instalment Payments) system with National Insurance contributions broadly aligned to the UK structure. Employers remit both to the Income Tax Division monthly.

- **Employee NI primary threshold** — IMP 176 per week (IMP 763 per month) for 2026/27; no employee NI is due below this (IMP 168 per week in 2025/26)  _(Isle of Man Treasury, Income Tax Division, National Insurance Rates and Thresholds (2026/27 column) — https://www.gov.im/categories/tax-vat-and-your-money/income-tax-and-national-insurance/national-insurance-contributions/rates-and-thresholds/)_
- **Employee Class 1 NI main rate** — 11% on weekly earnings between the primary threshold of IMP 176 and the upper earnings limit of IMP 1,082  _(Isle of Man Treasury, Income Tax Division, National Insurance Rates and Thresholds (2026/27 column) — https://www.gov.im/categories/tax-vat-and-your-money/income-tax-and-national-insurance/national-insurance-contributions/rates-and-thresholds/)_
- **Employee Class 1 NI additional rate** — 1% on weekly earnings above the upper earnings limit of IMP 1,082  _(Isle of Man Treasury, Income Tax Division, National Insurance Rates and Thresholds (2026/27 column) — https://www.gov.im/categories/tax-vat-and-your-money/income-tax-and-national-insurance/national-insurance-contributions/rates-and-thresholds/)_
- **Upper Earnings Limit** — IMP 1,082 per week (IMP 4,689 per month) for 2026/27; IMP 1,032 per week in 2025/26  _(Isle of Man Treasury, Income Tax Division, National Insurance Rates and Thresholds (2026/27 column) — https://www.gov.im/categories/tax-vat-and-your-money/income-tax-and-national-insurance/national-insurance-contributions/rates-and-thresholds/)_
- **Lower Earnings Limit** — IMP 129 per week (IMP 559 per month) for 2026/27; IMP 125 per week in 2025/26  _(Isle of Man Treasury, Income Tax Division, National Insurance Rates and Thresholds (2026/27 column) — https://www.gov.im/categories/tax-vat-and-your-money/income-tax-and-national-insurance/national-insurance-contributions/rates-and-thresholds/)_
- **Employer Class 1 NI rate** — 12.8% on all earnings above the secondary threshold  _(Isle of Man Treasury, Income Tax Division, National Insurance Rates and Thresholds (2026/27 column) — https://www.gov.im/categories/tax-vat-and-your-money/income-tax-and-national-insurance/national-insurance-contributions/rates-and-thresholds/)_
- **Employer NI secondary threshold** — IMP 176 per week for 2026/27 (IMP 168 per week in 2025/26)  _(Isle of Man Treasury, Income Tax Division, National Insurance Rates and Thresholds (2026/27 column) — https://www.gov.im/categories/tax-vat-and-your-money/income-tax-and-national-insurance/national-insurance-contributions/rates-and-thresholds/)_
- **Self-employed Class 2 NI** — IMP 6.75 per week (IMP 351.00 a year) for 2026/27, with a small earnings exception where annual earnings are below IMP 9,152  _(Isle of Man Treasury, Income Tax Division, National Insurance Rates and Thresholds (2026/27 column) — https://www.gov.im/categories/tax-vat-and-your-money/income-tax-and-national-insurance/national-insurance-contributions/rates-and-thresholds/)_
- **Self-employed Class 4 NI** — 8% on annual profits between IMP 9,152 and IMP 56,264 and 1% on profits above the upper limit (2026/27)  _(Isle of Man Treasury, Income Tax Division, National Insurance Rates and Thresholds (2026/27 column) — https://www.gov.im/categories/tax-vat-and-your-money/income-tax-and-national-insurance/national-insurance-contributions/rates-and-thresholds/)_
- **Payroll income tax withholding (ITIP)** — Employers deduct income tax from remuneration on each pay day under the Income Tax Instalment Payments (ITIP) scheme, according to the employee's tax code, and pay it to the Income Tax Division monthly  _(Isle of Man Treasury, Income Tax Division, Income Tax (ITIP) — https://www.gov.im/categories/tax-vat-and-your-money/income-tax-and-national-insurance/employers/income-tax-itip/)_
- **ITIP/NI remittance deadline** — Monthly: the T35 notification of deductions with payment of ITIP and NI is due by the 19th of the month following the month of payment (penalty 5% of the amount due, plus a further 5% after six months); the T37 employer's annual return is due within 30 days after the tax year end, by 5 May  _(Isle of Man Treasury, Income Tax Division, Compliance and Penalties — https://www.gov.im/categories/tax-vat-and-your-money/income-tax-and-national-insurance/employers/compliance-and-penalties/)_
