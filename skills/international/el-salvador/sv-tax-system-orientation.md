# El Salvador tax system

> General orientation for 2026. Confirm the taxpayer, accounting period and transaction with a qualified local tax professional before applying these rules.

Amounts are in US dollars (USD). The Directorate General of Internal Taxes (DGII) administers income tax (ISR) and domestic value added tax (IVA). [Income Tax Law, arts. 37 and 48](https://transparencia.mh.gob.sv/downloads/pdf/DC5811.pdf) · [IVA Law, arts. 54 and 94](https://transparencia.mh.gob.sv/downloads/pdf/DC9226_Ley_del_Impuesto_a_la_Transferencia_de_Bienes_Muebles_y_a_la_Prestacion_de_Servicios.pdf)

## Income tax

- **Individuals and domicile.** An individual generally meets the domestic tax domicile test by spending more than 200 consecutive days in El Salvador in a calendar year or having their principal place of business there. The principal place of business produces the largest amount of their income. A person domiciled for more than a calendar year can be absent for up to 165 days without losing that status under the presence rule. Salvadoran-source income includes services performed or used in El Salvador even if payment occurs abroad. Specified foreign-source receipts are excluded under article 3(4). [Tax Code, art. 53](https://transparencia.mh.gob.sv/downloads/pdf/DC5810.pdf) · [Income Tax Law, art. 16](https://transparencia.mh.gob.sv/downloads/pdf/DC5811.pdf) · [Legislative Decree No. 969](https://www.asamblea.gob.sv/sites/default/files/documents/decretos/86A64E36-EB7C-4F57-82BC-A289A5D94C33.pdf)
- **Individual rates.** The ordinary annual schedule for domiciled individuals exempts taxable income up to USD 6,600. The next bands apply 10% to the excess over USD 6,600 through USD 9,142.86, 20% to the excess over USD 9,142.86 through USD 22,857.14 and 30% to the excess over USD 22,857.14. Each taxable band adds the fixed amount specified in the statutory table. The law sets a separate 30% rate on net or taxable income for non-domiciled individuals under article 37, with income already subject to definitive withholding excluded from that calculation. [Legislative Decree No. 293](https://www.asamblea.gob.sv/sites/default/files/documents/decretos/E198E77D-2027-47AA-A8F6-1D70E2F46431.pdf) · [Income Tax Law, art. 37](https://transparencia.mh.gob.sv/downloads/pdf/DC5811.pdf)
- **Companies and small business.** The standard rate for legal persons is 30% of taxable income. The rate is 25% for eligible taxpayers with taxable revenues of USD 150,000 or less. Income subject to definitive withholding and distributions have separate rules. [Income Tax Law, art. 41](https://transparencia.mh.gob.sv/downloads/pdf/DC5811.pdf)

## IVA and filing

- **IVA.** The standard rate is 13%. The ordinary monthly return and payment fall due within the first 10 business days after the month ends. Check the separate treatment of imports, exports and exempt transactions. [IVA Law, arts. 54, 74 and 93–94](https://transparencia.mh.gob.sv/downloads/pdf/DC9226_Ley_del_Impuesto_a_la_Transferencia_de_Bienes_Muebles_y_a_la_Prestacion_de_Servicios.pdf)
- **Filing.** File the ordinary annual income tax return and pay any self-assessed balance within 4 months after the end of the tax period, normally by 30 April for a calendar-year filer. Some domiciled employees with only salary income are exempt from filing when correct withholding covers their liability. The exemption does not apply to income above USD 60,000 or where withholding was missing or differed from the statutory tax. [Income Tax Law, arts. 38, 48 and 51](https://transparencia.mh.gob.sv/downloads/pdf/DC5811.pdf)
