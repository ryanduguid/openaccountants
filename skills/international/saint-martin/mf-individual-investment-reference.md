# Saint-Martin individual investment reference v0.1

This conditional reference explains selected wording of local article 199 undecies D and supplies raw fictional components for a qualified reviewer's working paper. Load both declared bases. Their mandatory slots, outputs, tools, checks and professional review remain required before delivering an actual financial result. This is a dated source reference, with current eligibility and operational delivery held.

## Scope, dates and source library

Saint-Martin here means the French collectivity, jurisdiction MF. Do not apply these local provisions to Sint Maarten, jurisdiction SX, or substitute French State overseas-investment provisions for similarly numbered local articles. Corporate article 217 undecies A computations and national investment relief are outside this guide.

## Captured article D rules

All rules in this section describe **S1's dated wording**, subject to the source and application holds above.

| Branch | Captured conditions and distinctions |
|---|---|
| **D(1), D(2)(a)** | Taxpayer domiciled in Saint-Martin under article 4 B; new local immovable with the printed building-permit condition; principal-residence commitment for 5 years from completion or acquisition if later. The full residence test and permit condition must be established before application. |
| **D(2)(b)** | New housing let unfurnished within 6 months of completion/acquisition if later, for at least 5 years, as the occupant's principal residence; spouse and tax-household exclusions apply. |
| **D(2)(c)** | Subscriptions to companies whose actual exclusive object is constructing new local housing with the stated 5-year unfurnished principal-residence letting conditions. Foundations must be completed within 2 years after each annual subscription closes. Securities are retained for at least 5 years from the immovables' completion. Do not import the distinct corporation-tax condition from (e). |
| **D(2)(d)** | Enterprise rehabilitation of housing over 40 years old, excluding article 31 deductible expenses, with the printed 5-year use/letting undertaking and 6-month letting limit. A second paragraph includes substantial renovation/completion without an age condition, with structural or comfort/safety work and exclusions for minor work, maintenance and simple preservation. Removing age does not waive every other condition. |
| **D(2)(e)** | Cash subscriptions to corporation-tax companies effectively managed in Saint-Martin, investing in eligible new productive assets within 12 months after subscription close. Foundations have a separate 2-year limit. Asset use lasts 5 years after acquisition or normal life if shorter; subscriber retention lasts 5 years from subscription. Eligibility incorporates local E I; renewal investment expressly references Regulation 1407/2013. Those dependencies remain held. |
| **D(3)** | Split ownership excludes the specified property/securities, with a conditional surviving-spouse continuation on request after death. This continuation differs from recapture in D(7). |
| **D(4)** | Subscriptions under (e) in sectors referred to by local A III require prior Executive Council approval under A IV. For other paragraph 2 investments, the specified investment/programme/capital amount **strictly above EUR 500,000** requires prior notification and absence of a reasoned objection within 3 months. These are distinct routes. Exact programme aggregation, complete A III/IV conditions and actual evidence remain required. |
| **D(5)** | For (a)–(d), the printed cost limit is EUR 2,800 excluding tax per square metre of habitable area. Renewable-energy equipment acquired and installed in the dwelling enters the reduction calculation but is excluded when evaluating that limit. A separately attributed later paragraph admits ancillary non-enclosed, non-habitable area up to 20% of CCH R.111-2 habitable area, excluding excess. Its commencement and area definition remain held. |
| **D(6)** | For (a), entitlement year plus 9 following years; for (b), (c), (e), the specified completion/acquisition/subscription year plus 4 following years; for (d), rehabilitation-completion year plus 4 following years. Every annual base is printed as 20% of sums actually paid by 31 December of the year the right arose. Rate on that annual base: 25% for (a)/(d), 30% for (b)/(c)/(e). Offset is against the tax identified by article 197 I(1)–(4); excess is neither carried forward nor refunded. Complete liability, order and precision remain held. |
| **D(7)** | Failure of commitments, cession, prohibited split ownership, failure of exclusive company purpose or dissolution triggers event-year recapture, subject to the printed conditional restructuring, death and staff-housing provisions. Read the complete applicable exception before using it. No recapture amount is computed here. |
| **D(8)** | Benefit is conditional on Regulation 651/2014, with a printed SA.39295 reference. The compilation's citation does not establish current scheme coverage, complete EU conditions, aid intensity, cumulation or a project's gross grant equivalent. |

D(6)'s 10-year (a) schedule appears alongside the common 20% annual base and 25% rate. Preserve that wording; do not replace the base with 10% or derive a total saving without resolving the operative sources. The government scheme description also retains an older EUR 2,300/m² limit, differing from S1; coverage of the later local changes is unresolved. E has separate carryforward rules which must not be imported into D. The booklet's simplified E/A 10-year conservation statements cannot replace their detailed asset, share and normal-life conditions.

## Fictional components and boundary controls

These positions use invented inputs and S1's printed formula **after assuming the named branch and eligible paid basis solely for arithmetic**. They determine no entitlement, filed field, annual tax saving, refund or final balance. Raw EUR results do not supply a rounding or conversion rule.

| Position | Invented inputs and reproducible result | Held application stage |
|---|---|---|
| **1. D(2)(a) annual component** | Assumed eligible paid amount EUR 100,000. Annual base `100,000 × 20% = 20,000`; raw reduction `20,000 × 25% = 5,000`. | Residence, property, area, permit, dates, EU coverage and available tax unresolved. No 10-year aggregate is inferred. |
| **2. D(2)(b) annual component** | Assumed eligible paid amount EUR 60,000. Annual base `60,000 × 20% = 12,000`; raw reduction `12,000 × 30% = 3,600`. | Letting commencement, tenant exclusions and full 5-year undertaking unresolved. No payment or carryforward follows. |
| **3. D(2)(d) annual component** | Assumed eligible paid rehabilitation amount EUR 80,000. Annual base `80,000 × 20% = 16,000`; raw reduction `16,000 × 25% = 4,000`. | Works classification, age branch, article 31 and commencement unresolved. No current works eligibility is asserted. |
| **4. D(2)(c) annual component** | Assumed eligible paid subscription EUR 50,000. Annual base `50,000 × 20% = 10,000`; raw reduction `10,000 × 30% = 3,000`. | Exclusive purpose, foundations, letting and retention evidence unresolved; no corporate relief is calculated. |
| **5. D(5) simple area component** | Invented qualifying habitable area 40 m², cost EUR 120,000 excluding tax, no ancillary area or renewable equipment. Raw ceiling `40 × 2,800 = 112,000`; amount over that ceiling `120,000 − 112,000 = 8,000`. | This isolates the printed ceiling only. | **6. Strict notification boundary** | For the distinct D(4) notification branch alone, EUR 500,000 is not strictly above EUR 500,000; EUR 500,001 is. | No safe-harbour conclusion: sector approval, programme aggregation and other conditions must still be checked in both cases. |
| **7. Incomplete eligibility evidence** | A receipt for EUR 100,000 without branch, eligibility, dates or authority remains an unclassified held input. | Do not multiply it by a percentage or enter zero relief. Obtain the missing primary evidence. |
