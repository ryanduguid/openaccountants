# Niue registration, licensing and reporting sources

Sources checked on 11 November 2026. This is a source reference for choosing the applicable review route. Use the period and limits for each source and confirm the live requirements before preparing an application or return. See the [private company annual reporting guide](nu-company-reporting.md) for the Schedule 2 reporting branch and its required workflow bases.

## Company formation and overseas registration

The Companies Act 2006 generally commenced on 1 August 2006. The Government's 2019 reprint incorporates Act 2006/282. The Government's tables as at 31 December 2024 list the Act, the commencement regulation, the 2006 amendment and Companies Regulations 2006. Check later Acts, regulations and Registrar notices for the relevant date. [2019 reprint, Volume 1](https://www.gov.nu/media/pages/information/5977ee14d1-1764012821/niue_laws_vol1_il2022.pdf) · [Companies Office legislation](https://www.companies.gov.nu/about/legislation/) · [Government tables to 31 December 2024](https://www.gov.nu/media/pages/information/51f88605f2-1779840449/niue-legislation-supplement-2021-2024-part-1.pdf)

| Route | Statutory and registry evidence |
|---|---|
| Incorporate in Niue | Under ss 6–10, lodge Form 1, Form 2 consent from each director, the rules if they differ from the applicable model rules and the prescribed fee. Identify each initial shareholder and their share allotment, the directors and their addresses, the registered office and the postal address. A private company's rules must prohibit public offers and cap the number of shareholders at 100, and it must also meet the actual shareholder cap. The certificate establishes the incorporation date. Under s 21 shares have no nominal or par value. [Companies Act, ss 6–10 and 21](https://www.gov.nu/media/pages/information/5977ee14d1-1764012821/niue_laws_vol1_il2022.pdf) · [Domestic company forms](https://www.companies.gov.nu/help-centre/forms-and-fees/forms-for-niue-companies/) |
| Register an existing overseas company | Check the activities and exclusions in s 279. Where s 281(1) applies, submit the s 283 application within 20 working days of commencing business in Niue. The current Companies Office Form 16 asks for the directors' details, a Niue business and postal address, a person resident or incorporated in Niue who can accept service, evidence of incorporation and the company's rules. Follow the form's certified English translation requirement for non-English documents. Check any applicable exemption instrument under s 291. [Companies Act, Part 11](https://www.gov.nu/media/pages/information/5977ee14d1-1764012821/niue_laws_vol1_il2022.pdf) · [Form 16](https://www.companies.gov.nu/assets/niue-companies-office/forms/overseas/nu-co-form-16-registration-os-company.pdf) |
| Transfer incorporation to Niue | The Companies Office identifies Form 20 under s 293. Check home jurisdiction authority and compliance, evidence and any shareholder consent under s 294, and the insolvency and creditor restrictions in s 295. Registration under s 297 preserves the entity's continuity. [Companies Act, Part 12](https://www.gov.nu/media/pages/information/5977ee14d1-1764012821/niue_laws_vol1_il2022.pdf) · [Overseas company forms](https://www.companies.gov.nu/help-centre/forms-and-fees/forms-for-overseas-companies/) |

The Companies Office lists $150 for the s 6 incorporation and for the s 283 overseas registration, with separate annual-return fees. The Companies Regulations' Schedule 1 contents list and the body of its overseas forms give different numbers. Match the current form's title and Act section against the Companies Office catalogue. The Act's Part 15 ended new IBC incorporation in 2006. Review any historic registration or restoration under its own provisions. [Companies Regulations 2006](https://www.companies.gov.nu/assets/niue-companies-office/legislation/companies-regulations-2006-niue.pdf) · [Registrar fee schedule](https://www.companies.gov.nu/help-centre/forms-and-fees/fee-schedule/) · [Companies Act, Parts 14–15](https://www.gov.nu/media/pages/information/5977ee14d1-1764012821/niue_laws_vol1_il2022.pdf)

## Other structures and foreign investment

- The Companies Office distinguishes sole traders, partnerships and companies. The Business Licence Act's application route also identifies a family business. Section 5 of the Partnership Amendment Act 1994 requires the written partnership agreement or deed to be executed or signed in Niue by all partners or their appointed attorneys for formation in Niue. Check the separate special partnership regime under Part 2 of the Partnership Act 1908 and s 6 of the amendment Act, and limited liability partnership registration under ss 11–14 of the amendment Act. [Companies Office structures guide](https://www.companies.gov.nu/help-centre/registering-a-company/choosing-a-business-structure/) · [2019 reprint, Volumes 1 and 3](https://www.gov.nu/information)
- Check the foreign enterprise definition in s 3 of the Development Investment Act 1992, which includes ownership, control and entity type. If the definition applies, ss 6–9 require Cabinet registration for the proposed activity before doing business. Keep the Cabinet certificate and its conditions separate from company registration and business licensing. [2019 reprint, Volume 2](https://www.gov.nu/media/pages/information/bfc8b91513-1764012795/niue_laws_vol2_ul2022.pdf)

## Business licence and tax registration

- Under the Business Licence Act 1997 as consolidated at 31 December 2019, screen the definitions and ss 4–7 before trading. The Act provides separate treatments for foreign enterprises, the Crown and sellers covered by the stall, Alofi market or hawker provisions. Under ss 8–12, identify each activity and premises, licence class and related permits. New applications are advertised, with 10 working days for written objections. Sections 13–14 set expiry on 31 May and renewal in May. The original law linked by the Tax Office predates the change from March. [2019 reprint, Volume 1](https://www.gov.nu/media/pages/information/5977ee14d1-1764012821/niue_laws_vol1_il2022.pdf) · [Niue Tax Office licence guidance](https://mof.gov.nu/niuetax/)
- The Tax Office lists online and paper application and renewal routes for the retailer, wholesaler and service provider classes. Its company application quotes $34 for each licence type, $23 for advertising and $12.50 for a certificate, plus the $150 incorporation item on that form. The 2019 reprint of the Business Licence Regulations shows $30 per annual licence class and $10 for a copy. Establish the current authority and confirm the actual charges with the Licensor before paying. [Tax Office company application](https://mof.gov.nu/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/COMPANY_NewBL.pdf) · [2019 subsidiary legislation, Volume 4, p 29](https://www.gov.nu/media/pages/information/39290dbbd4-1764012801/niue_laws_vol4_part1_ul2022.pdf)
- The Government launched the online Business Licence Register on 5 November 2026. Use it for public licence lookup and use the application or renewal route for the corresponding submission. The Government says older licence statuses still need validation. Keep the company certificate, business licence and taxpayer identification number separate. Use the Tax Office's current registration routes and verify separate consumption tax and employer requirements when applicable. [Launch notice](https://gov.nu/media-releases/niue-launches-online-business-licence-register) · [Niue Tax Office](https://mof.gov.nu/niuetax/)

## Taxpayer identification and ownership changes from 1 July 2026

- The Tax Administration Act 2025 applies from 1 July 2026 under s 2(2). Sections 10–11 require a person who starts an activity that may lead to tax liability to apply to the Financial Secretary for a TIN within 21 days of starting the activity, subject to existing TINs and any prescribed exceptions. Section 108(5) continues earlier TINs unless changed by written notice and s 108(6) deals separately with people likely to need a TIN at transition. Confirm the approved form and prescribed evidence for the taxpayer class. [Tax Administration Act 2025, ss 2, 10–11 and 108](https://www.gov.nu/media/pages/information/b29c7ba832-1784584144/act-379-tax-adminstration-act-2025.pdf) · [Tax Office](https://mof.gov.nu/niuetax/)
- Section 12(1) requires the taxpayer to notify the Financial Secretary in writing within 14 days of a specified change. This includes changes in beneficial owners for a company, partnership or trust that is a taxpayer. Under s 12(3), an individual can qualify through an underlying ownership interest of at least 20% or through the power to make decisions about the entity's finances or operations. In either case, count the individual's interest or power alone or together with associates. Section 12(2) addresses duplicate notices across tax registrations. Check the accepted notice route and any separate initial or registrar disclosure. A shareholder register covers legal title. Apply the Act's ownership and control tests separately. [Tax Administration Act 2025, s 12 and Schedule 1](https://www.gov.nu/media/pages/information/b29c7ba832-1784584144/act-379-tax-adminstration-act-2025.pdf)

## Overseas company returns

Under the 2019 reprint of the Companies Act, s 287, a registered overseas company carrying on business in Niue uses its allocated month, subject to written changes by the Registrar. The exception in s 287(5) refers to an overseas company deemed registered under Part 11. Check that condition before applying it. The Companies Office publishes Form 18 under s 287 and a $45 fee in the Registrar's schedule. The domestic Form 10 procedure is separate. [Companies Act, ss 124 and 287](https://www.gov.nu/media/pages/information/5977ee14d1-1764012821/niue_laws_vol1_il2022.pdf) · [Overseas company forms](https://www.companies.gov.nu/help-centre/forms-and-fees/forms-for-overseas-companies/) · [Registrar fee schedule](https://www.companies.gov.nu/help-centre/forms-and-fees/fee-schedule/)
