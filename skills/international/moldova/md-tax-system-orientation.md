# Republic of Moldova tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the taxpayer, where income arose, residence facts and period. Check the applicable treaty and foreign-tax relief. This guide applies to 2026. Law 247/2026 changes certain source, currency and administration rules from 2027, with separate effective dates for specified measures. Check the date of the relevant provision for a later period. [Law 247/2026, arts III and XVIII](https://monitorul.gov.md/ro/monitor/3368)

## Territorial reach

The income-tax base depends on the taxpayer and source. Resident legal persons and ordinary resident entrepreneurs generally account for taxable domestic and foreign receipts. For resident Moldovan citizens outside business, the base includes Moldova-source income, foreign receipts for activities in Moldova and foreign investment and financial income. For resident foreign citizens and stateless people carrying on activities in Moldova, the base includes Moldova-source income and foreign receipts for activities in Moldova, with the statutory exclusion for foreign investment and financial income. Review any applicable exemptions and separate regimes.

For nonresidents, identify Moldovan-source income and whether it is attributable to a Moldovan permanent establishment. Attribution and withholding have separate rules. Check treaty relief and the evidence needed before reducing withholding. [Tax Code CF1163/1997, arts 13–14 and 70–79](https://www.legis.md/cautare/downloadpdf/149766) · [State Tax Service guidance on foreign residents](https://www.sfs.md/ro/intrebare-baza-de-date-generalizare/56) · [Nonresident guidance and certificates](https://sfs.md/ro/formular/nerezidenti)

## Residence

An individual ordinarily qualifies through a permanent domicile in Moldova or presence in Moldova for at least 183 days in the fiscal year. Some temporary absences are compatible with permanent domicile. Check the statutory exceptions for people present in diplomatic, international organisation or certain exclusively temporary capacities. The domestic day threshold comes from the Code. For treaty relief, check the treaty and the separate documents the State Tax Service requires to issue a residence certificate.

A legal person or organisational form with individual status is resident if its activity is organised or managed in Moldova or its principal place of activity is there. Check treaty residence if another country also treats the taxpayer as resident. [Tax Code, art 5(5)–(6)](https://www.legis.md/cautare/downloadpdf/149766) · [State Tax Service residence guidance](https://www.sfs.md/ro/ordinele-de-baze-de-date-de-generalizare/520) · [Residence certificate requirements](https://sfs.md/ro/pagina/certificate-de-rezidenta-persoane-fizice)

## Taxpayer types

Classify an individual, registered individual entrepreneur or legal person before choosing the income tax treatment. Registered general partnerships (societăți în nume colectiv) and limited partnerships (societăți în comandită) are legal entity forms. Classify those entities under the legal-person income tax rules and check for any special regime. The independent entrepreneur regime for eligible service providers applies from 1 January 2026 and has its own registration and tax rules. [Tax Code, arts 5 and 13–15](https://www.legis.md/cautare/downloadpdf/149766) · [Public Services Agency forms for legal entities](https://www.asp.gov.md/ro/informatii-utile/formulare-tip/4) · [Law 228/2025](https://www.legis.md/cautare/downloadpdf/150415) · [State Tax Service independent entrepreneur guidance](https://sfs.md/ro/intrebare-baza-de-date-generalizare/495)

## Currency

The Moldovan leu (MDL) is legal tender. The National Bank of Moldova sets the official exchange rate for accounting and statistical purposes. Keep the original transaction amount and check which conversion date and rule the return or accounting treatment requires. [National Bank Law, art 56](https://www.bnm.md/files/Legea%20nr_%20548_1995_ro.pdf) · [National Bank foreign exchange guidance](https://www.bnm.md/ro/content/intrebari-si-raspunsuri-domeniul-reglementarii-valutare)

## Assessment periods

Income tax ordinarily follows the calendar year. Check the shorter periods for registration or winding up. An entity entitled to use a different financial reporting year has a corresponding income tax period of 12 consecutive months. Check the notification and transitional rules when changing periods. [Tax Code, art 12¹](https://www.legis.md/cautare/downloadpdf/149766) · [State Tax Service guidance on changes to the corporate period](https://sfs.md/ro/intrebare-baza-de-date-generalizare/72)

## Responsible authorities

The State Tax Service (Serviciul Fiscal de Stat) administers income tax, taxpayer filing and tax residence certificates. Use the Public Services Agency for state registration of legal entities and registered individual entrepreneurs. Check the Customs Service for import procedures and the local authority for municipal obligations. [Tax Code, arts 131–133](https://www.legis.md/cautare/downloadpdf/149766) · [State Tax Service](https://www.sfs.md/) · [Public Services Agency legal entity and entrepreneur services](https://www.asp.gov.md/ro/servicii/persoane-juridice) · [Customs Service](https://customs.gov.md/)

## Fictional intake

A Moldovan citizen and a foreign citizen who works in Moldova are both domestic tax residents. Each receives interest on a deposit held abroad outside any business. Check the taxpayer-specific source provisions before applying the same income tax treatment to both deposits.
