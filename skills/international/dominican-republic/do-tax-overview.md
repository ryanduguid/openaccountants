# Dominican Republic Tax Overview

## The 2026 tax system at a glance

The Dirección General de Impuestos Internos (DGII) administers internal taxes, including income tax (ISR), ITBIS and asset tax. Customs administers duties and charges at import. The Dominican peso (DOP, shown as RD$) is the national monetary unit. [DGII](https://dgii.gov.do/) · [Central Bank, Constitution art 229](https://bancentral.gov.do/a/CustomView/6068-normas-sobre-billetes-y-monedas)

### Residence and income source

The Tax Code treats a person present for more than 182 days in the fiscal year as resident, counting continuous and intermittent stays. Article 12 has separate domicile grounds for companies, including incorporation under Dominican law, a principal business seat or effective management in the country. Residents and domiciliaries generally pay ISR on Dominican-source income and foreign investment and financial gains under article 269. Article 271 phases the foreign-source rules in from the third taxable period for individuals becoming resident, subject to special legislation. Non-residents generally pay on Dominican-source income, with a distinct attribution rule for permanent establishments. Determine the relationship between source and residence before applying any treaty. [Tax Code arts 12 and 269–272](https://dgii.gov.do/transparencia/marcoLegal/Documents/Leyes/11-92.pdf) · [DGII Aviso 22-25](https://dgii.gov.do/publicacionesOficiales/avisosInformativos/Documents/2025/22-25.pdf)

### Income tax rates

- **Individuals.** The 2026 DGII resolution keeps the exemption through RD$416,220 and gives marginal rates of 15%, 20% and 25%, with the top band beginning at RD$867,123.01. Use the resolution's brackets and base amounts. Law 30-26 article 10 sets a separate scale from fiscal year 2027, with a base exemption of RD$480,000 and a 27% top marginal band starting at RD$4,800,000.01. Apply any operative indexed amounts for the year. [DGII 2026 resolution DDG-AR1-2026-00001](https://dgii.gov.do/legislacion/resoluciones/Documents/DDG-%20AR1-2026-00001.pdf) · [Law 30-26 art 10](https://dgii.gov.do/transparencia/baseLegal/Documents/Leyes/30-26.pdf)
- **Companies.** The ordinary rate is 27% of taxable income. Under Law 30-26 article 15, taxpayers with income of at least RD$1 billion pay 30% of taxable income for fiscal years 2026–2028. The DGII calendar starts application with the 30 June 2026 close. At the threshold use the enacted inclusive wording, which the DGII's current ISR page also uses. The temporary provision returns those taxpayers to the ordinary rate from fiscal year 2029, subject to later law. [Law 30-26 art 15](https://dgii.gov.do/transparencia/baseLegal/Documents/Leyes/30-26.pdf) · [DGII implementation calendar](https://www.dgii.gov.do/publicacionesOficiales/avisosInformativos/Documents/2026/10-26.pdf) · [DGII ISR guidance](https://dgii.gov.do/cicloContribuyente/obligacionesTributarias/principalesImpuestos/Paginas/impuestoSobreRenta.aspx)
- **Asset tax.** For an applicable business, compare the 1% tax on taxable assets with assessed ISR. If the assessed ISR covers the asset charge, the asset tax payment obligation is extinguished. If the asset charge exceeds ISR, the excess is payable in 2 equal instalments, with the first due on the ISR deadline and the second 6 months later. Check the asset base, taxpayer and any relief separately. [Tax Code arts 401–407](https://dgii.gov.do/transparencia/marcoLegal/Documents/Leyes/11-92.pdf) · [DGII ISR and asset tax guidance](https://dgii.gov.do/cicloContribuyente/obligacionesTributarias/principalesImpuestos/Paginas/impuestoSobreRenta.aspx)

### Consumption tax and filing periods

ITBIS is 18% at the standard rate, with a 16% statutory rate for listed goods and separate exemptions. Confirm the supply category and any relief before applying a rate. Domestic IT-1 filings and payments are generally due by the 20th of the next month. On imports, DGII describes payment with customs duties and charges. [Tax Code Title III arts 341, 343 and 353](https://dgii.gov.do/legislacion/codigoTributario/Cdigo%20Tributario/Titulo3.pdf) · [DGII ITBIS](https://dgii.gov.do/cicloContribuyente/obligacionesTributarias/principalesImpuestos/Paginas/Itbis.aspx)

The ordinary individual period ends on 31 December, with IR-1 due by 31 March where required. Companies use 31 December or select a 31 March, 30 June or 30 September close under article 300. Changing an established close requires the DGII's permission. Companies file and pay IR-2 within 120 days of the close. Check the DGII calendar for non-working days and extensions. Payroll ISR withholding uses IR-3 and other applicable withholding uses IR-17. DGII lists the 10th of the next month as the ordinary deadline for these forms. Social security has a separate employer process. [Tax Code art 300](https://dgii.gov.do/legislacion/codigoTributario/Cdigo%20Tributario/Titulo2.pdf) · [DGII filing and payment calendar](https://dgii.gov.do/cicloContribuyente/obligacionesTributarias/declaracionPagoImpuestos/Paginas/PagodeImpuesto.aspx) · [Social Security Treasury](https://tss.gob.do/)

### November electronic invoicing

From 1 November 2026, DGII requires large local and medium taxpayers to issue electronic sequences type E, subject to the applicable contingency procedure. The general extension for small, micro and unclassified taxpayers ended on 15 November 2026. In its 16 November notice, DGII said it was continuing to assist businesses and would temporarily refrain from fines, penalties and blocks for incomplete onboarding. Confirm the taxpayer's category and any applicable notice or contingency before invoicing. [DGII Aviso 14-26](https://www.dgii.gov.do/publicacionesOficiales/avisosInformativos/Documents/2026/14-26.pdf) · [DGII 16 November notice](https://dgii.gov.do/noticias/Paginas/DGII-mantendra-acompa%C3%B1amiento-para-Facturacion-Electronica-sin-aplicar-momento-multas-ni-bloqueos.aspx)
