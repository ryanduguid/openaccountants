# New Caledonia Tax System Orientation

Use New Caledonia's Code des impôts for ordinary personal income tax (IRPP), company tax (IS) and the general consumption tax (TGC). Establish the applicable period and any double taxation arrangement before using a headline figure. [Code des impôts][CODE] · [Direction des services fiscaux][DSF]

## Residence

An individual has a New Caledonian tax domicile if their home or main place of stay is there, they carry on a professional activity there beyond an incidental activity, or their centre of economic interests is there. Apply the tests separately to each spouse or civil partner. [Code des impôts, art 48][CODE]

## Income and territorial scope

The resident IRPP rule starts with all income, subject to double taxation agreements and specific exclusions. Non-residents are liable on New Caledonian-source income. Article Lp. 56 can exclude certain foreign-source income taxed outside New Caledonia from the tax base, whilst taking it into account in setting the effective rate. [Code des impôts, arts 47–48, Lp. 56 and Lp. 138][CODE] · [DSF effective rate guidance][FOREIGN]

Company tax covers profits from businesses operated or headquartered in New Caledonia and profits allocated there by an agreement. Local operations can arise from an establishment, a dependent representative or a complete business cycle. Article Lp. 15 excludes profits and the related losses and costs of a local business's qualifying establishments outside New Caledonia. [Code des impôts, art Lp. 15][CODE]

## Taxpayer type

Individuals use the applicable IRPP income category. Companies and other legal entities within articles 2–4 can be liable to IS. Some partnerships and civil entities can pass taxable results to members, subject to their activities, legal form and any valid IS election. Confirm the entity and its election before deciding who accounts for the income. [Code des impôts, arts 2–4 and 49][CODE]

## Currency

Keep the figures in Pacific francs (F CFP, currency code XPF). Apply the conversion rule for the particular item before reporting a foreign amount. [Institut d'émission d'outre-mer][IEOM]

## Assessment periods

Personal income tax is annual. For 2025 income, DSF's 2026 calendar distinguishes paper returns, separate online deadlines and the non-resident timetable. Company tax follows the applicable accounting period. Confirm the relevant close date and return timetable, particularly for irregular, multiple or missing closes. [Code des impôts, arts 16–17 and 46][CODE] · [DSF individual calendar][PERSON] · [DSF business calendar][BUSINESS]

## Tax authority and headline rates

The Direction des services fiscaux administers IRPP, IS and domestic TGC. The 2025 ordinary IRPP marginal scale per part has no tax through XPF 1 million, then marginal bands of 4%, 12% and 25% and a 40% top band above XPF 4.5 million. Household parts, caps and reductions affect the final result. The ordinary company rate in article 45 is 30%, with 35% for businesses within article 3. A conditional reduced rate applies under articles Lp. 45-0 and R. 45-0. The TGC rates are 3%, 6%, 11% (standard) and 22%, with exemptions and category-specific rules. DSF's July 2025 guidance identifies the classification changes from 1 July. Check the later law for the requested transaction or income period before calculating, and check distinct solidarity contributions separately. [Code des impôts, arts 45, Lp. 45-0, R. 45-0 and 136][CODE] · [DSF 2025 IRPP notice][NOTICE] · [DSF TGC brochure][TGC] · [DSF July 2025 changes][TGC25]
