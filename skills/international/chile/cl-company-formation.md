# Chile Company Formation & Entity Choice

## Company formation & annual compliance

Most foreign founders in Chile incorporate a SpA (Sociedad por Acciones) for its flexibility and single-shareholder allowance, often via the online Empresa en un Dia platform. There is no statutory minimum share capital. Draft figures for accountant review.

- **Sociedad por Acciones (SpA)** — Flexible share-based company; single shareholder permitted; limited liability; most common for foreign founders  _(Código de Comercio, Ley Chile consolidated text, Arts 424 to 446 (added by Ley 20.190) — https://www.bcn.cl/leychile/navegar?idNorma=1974)_
- **Sociedad de Responsabilidad Limitada (SRL/Ltda.)** — Limited liability company; requires 2-50 partners; common for family/small businesses  _(Ley 3.918 sobre Sociedades de Responsabilidad Limitada, Ley Chile text — https://www.bcn.cl/leychile/navegar?idNorma=24349)_
- **Sociedad Anonima (S.A.)** — Corporation; can be closely-held or open (publicly traded); more formal governance (board required)  _(Ley 18.046 sobre Sociedades Anónimas, Ley Chile consolidated text — https://www.bcn.cl/leychile/navegar?idNorma=29473)_
- **Empresa Individual de Responsabilidad Limitada (EIRL)** — Single-owner limited liability entity for sole proprietors  _(Ley 19.857 (Empresas Individuales de Responsabilidad Limitada), Ley Chile text — https://www.bcn.cl/leychile/navegar?idNorma=207588)_
- **Minimum share capital** — None — no statutory minimum capital for SpA, SRL or S.A.  _(Codigo de Comercio / Ley 20.190 (SpA) (as described at [expanship.com](https://www.expanship.com/cl/blog/incorporation-requirements-in-chile)))_
- **Empresa en un Dia (Tu Empresa en un Dia)** — Free online incorporation platform (registroempresas.cl) run by the Ministry of Economy under Ley 20.659, covering the EIRL, Ltda., closed S.A. and SpA; the company exists once the form is signed, immediately when every partner uses an advanced electronic signature and otherwise after a notary signs it  _(Registro de Empresas y Sociedades (Ministerio de Economía), Acerca de Tu empresa en un día — https://www.registrodeempresasysociedades.cl/acercade.aspx ; Ley 20.659 (Registro de Empresas y Sociedades), Ley Chile text — https://www.bcn.cl/leychile/navegar?idNorma=1048718)_
- **Core incorporation steps** — Draft & sign bylaws (notarized or via RES); register in Commercial Registry (Registro de Comercio) and publish extract in the Diario Oficial; obtain RUT and inicio de actividades with the SII; open a bank account  _(Registro de Empresas y Sociedades (Ministerio de Economía), Acerca de Tu empresa en un día — https://www.registrodeempresasysociedades.cl/acercade.aspx ; Código de Comercio, Ley Chile consolidated text, Arts 424 to 446 — https://www.bcn.cl/leychile/navegar?idNorma=1974)_
- **Typical timeline** — About 1 day for a simple online SpA; ~6-8 weeks end-to-end including SII registration and bank account opening (approx — varies by case)  _(Ley 20.659 (RES) / SII inicio de actividades (as described at [bizlatinhub.com](https://www.bizlatinhub.com/setting-company-chile/)))_
- **Typical incorporation cost** — Registration through the Registro de Empresas y Sociedades is free; the traditional route adds notary fees and the Diario Oficial publication (commonly a few hundred USD for a simple SpA)  _(Registro de Empresas y Sociedades (Ministerio de Economía), Acerca de Tu empresa en un día — https://www.registrodeempresasysociedades.cl/acercade.aspx ; Varela Abogados — https://www.varelaabogados.cl/en/post/how-to-incorporate-a-company-in-chile-a-step-by-step-guide)_
- **Tax registration** — Obtain a RUT (tax ID) and file inicio de actividades with the SII before operating  _([Codigo Tributario Art. 66 / Art. 68 — RUT e inicio de actividades](https://www.sii.cl))_
- **Core annual compliance** — Monthly VAT (Formulario 29) and PPM; annual income tax return (Operacion Renta) each April; annual informational returns (Declaraciones Juradas); statutory accounting records  _([Codigo Tributario / LIR / DL 825 (IVA)](https://www.sii.cl))_
