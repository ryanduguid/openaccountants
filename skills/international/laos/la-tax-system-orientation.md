# Lao PDR tax-system orientation

Identify the income category and relevant period. Law 88/NA distinguishes enterprise profit tax from income tax on salaries and other receipts. The Official Gazette records publication on 19 June 2026. For a period around the change, confirm the applicable commencement and transition rules before applying Law 88/NA or its predecessor. [Official Gazette entry](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/index.php?id=2597&r=site%2Fdisplay) · [Income Tax Law 88/NA, arts 9, 31 and 74](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/88-25-6-2025_0001.pdf)

## Taxable income and source

Lao income can bring an individual, legal entity or organisation within income tax. A Lao-domiciled individual working abroad is also within the statutory charge on income from that work, subject to applicable treaty provisions. Determine the income category and relevant source rule. [Income Tax Law 88/NA, arts 32, 33 and 45](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/88-25-6-2025_0001.pdf)

Enterprise profit tax can cover profits from business in Lao PDR or abroad. Its taxpayer categories include operators that establish enterprises under Lao law and non-domiciled operators with Lao receipts who have no Lao enterprise. For the latter, distinguish a permanent place of business from operations subject to the separate profit-tax collection rule and check any treaty. [Income Tax Law 88/NA, arts 9–14 and 20](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/88-25-6-2025_0001.pdf)

## Domicile and the offshore-salary rule

The domicile definitions refer to permanent home, living, earning a livelihood and conducting business in Lao PDR. A foreign worker who works and earns income in Lao PDR is within the domestic charge. For a foreign worker working there but receiving salary abroad, article 32 brings the salary into charge if they stay in Lao PDR for more than 183 days in the year, continuously or in total. Check the exceptions for applicable tax treaties and qualifying investment agreements with the Lao government. [Income Tax Law 88/NA, arts 3 and 32](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/88-25-6-2025_0001.pdf)

## Taxpayer type

The Enterprise Law provides for ordinary and limited partnerships registered as enterprises. Use the registered enterprise and activity to determine profit-tax obligations. Check a partner's own receipts separately. The profit-tax law specifically disallows a partnership's deduction for salaries paid to partners who have no position and do no work in the enterprise. [Enterprise Law 33/NA, arts 43 and 74–75](https://www.laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/Eng_Enterprise%20Law_%20Final.pdf) · [Income Tax Law 88/NA, arts 12 and 18](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/88-25-6-2025_0001.pdf)

## Currency

Use Lao kip (LAK) for ordinary domestic computations and payments. Check the authorised exchange and conversion rules where the income or accounting records are in another currency. Limited foreign-currency arrangements can apply, including for some government obligations of businesses receiving foreign-currency income. [Income Tax Law 88/NA, art 37](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/88-25-6-2025_0001.pdf) · [Foreign Exchange Management Law 15/NA, arts 8–9](https://www.bol.gov.la/en/fileupload/18-12-2025_1766032152.pdf)

## Assessment periods

Businesses using calendar-year accounts close at year end. Where the Ministry of Finance authorises another accounting year, the business closes on its 12-month cycle and files its annual financial statements within 3 months of close. Salaries follow monthly reporting and payment rules. [Income Tax Law 88/NA, arts 21, 24 and 38](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/88-25-6-2025_0001.pdf)

## Tax authority

The Ministry of Finance is responsible for income-tax administration. The Tax Department and its capital, provincial, district and city offices administer the tax. Confirm the office responsible for the taxpayer and the applicable procedure in TaxRIS, the tax revenue information system. [Income Tax Law 88/NA, arts 61–64](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/88-25-6-2025_0001.pdf) · [Ministry of Finance TaxRIS notice](https://www.mof.gov.la/news_detail/656)

## Worked example

A foreign employee works in Lao PDR and receives salary in an overseas bank account. They stay in Lao PDR for 190 days in the year across separate visits. The offshore-salary rule in article 32 applies. Check any treaty or investment agreement exception, then identify the payroll obligation and the applicable conversion rule.
