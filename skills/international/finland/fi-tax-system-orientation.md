# Finland tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record residence, legal form, business and property locations, the income period and any treaty. Check the separate Åland municipal income-tax rules and VAT boundary where applicable. [Finnish Tax Administration, Åland resident deductions](https://www.vero.fi/en/individuals/deductions/what-can-I-deduct/deductions-for-the-residents-of-aland/) · [Finnish Tax Administration, Åland VAT border](https://vero.fi/en/businesses-and-corporations/taxes-and-charges/vat/international-commerce/value-added-taxation-of-imported-goods/%C3%A5land-islands-tax-border-and-importing-goods/)

## Territorial reach

A resident individual, Finnish corporate entity or resident foreign corporate entity generally comes within charge on worldwide income. A non-resident generally comes within charge on Finnish-source income in the statutory categories, subject to exemptions and treaties. For 2026, check all income attributable to a Finnish permanent establishment where the conditions in § 9 are met. Finlex displays amendments introducing a separate § 9 b from 1 January 2027. An individual resident for part of the year applies the full and limited liability rules to the relevant parts. [Income Tax Act 1535/1992, §§ 9–10](https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/1992/1535) · [Finnish Tax Administration, corporate tax residence](https://vero.fi/en/detailed-guidance/guidance/95551/resident-and-nonresident-tax-liability-of-corporate-entities5/)

## Residence

An individual is ordinarily resident if their actual home is in Finland or they stay there continuously for more than 6 months, including temporary absences. A Finnish citizen who leaves generally remains resident through the third calendar year following the year of departure unless they establish that they lack substantial ties in the tax year. A corporate entity founded or registered under Finnish law is ordinarily resident. A foreign entity can be resident if its effective management is in Finland. Check where the board or equivalent body makes the most important senior day-to-day decisions and the other organisational and business facts. Check the 2026 statutory exception for qualifying EEA funds and apply treaty residence separately. [Income Tax Act 1535/1992, §§ 9, 9 a and 11](https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/1992/1535) · [Finnish Tax Administration, individual tax residence](https://www.vero.fi/en/individuals/tax-cards-and-tax-returns/arriving_in_finland/individuals-residency-and-nonresidency-in-finland/)

## Taxpayer types

A sole trader reports business income for assessment in the individual's tax. Ordinary general and limited partnerships calculate their business results and allocate taxable shares to the partners after the applicable prior-loss deductions. A limited company (Oy) is taxed separately. Classify a foreign trust from its purpose, terms and participant rights before attributing income. In KHO 2024:100, the court treated the particular irrevocable trust as a separate tax entity comparable to a pool of assets set aside for a special purpose. The outcome depended on the trustee's ownership and control and the permanent nature of the arrangement. [Income Tax Act 1535/1992, §§ 3–4 and 16](https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/1992/1535) · [Finnish Tax Administration, self-employed individuals](https://www.vero.fi/en/businesses-and-corporations/taxes-and-charges/taxation-of-self-employed-individuals/) · [Supreme Administrative Court, KHO 2024:100](https://www.finlex.fi/fi/oikeuskaytanto/korkein-hallinto-oikeus/ennakkopaatokset/2024/100) · [Finnish Tax Administration, trust classification](https://www.vero.fi/en/detailed-guidance/guidance/360700/impact-of-private-international-law-on-finnish-taxes/)

## Currency

Finland uses the euro (EUR). Financial statements covered by the Accounting Act state amounts in euro. If the statements also present another currency, disclose the rate used. Record the applicable conversion method for foreign amounts in the tax return. [Council Regulation (EC) No 974/98, art 2 and Annex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/1998/974/2026-01-01/eng) · [Accounting Act 1336/1997, ch 3 s 5](https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/1997/1336#chp_3__sec_5)

## Assessment periods

The tax year generally follows the calendar year. When the accounting period differs, the tax year includes the accounting period or periods ending during that calendar year. A company files the relevant forms for each period and the Finnish Tax Administration combines the results for assessment. Check any special rules for specific income. [Tax Procedure Act 1558/1995, § 3](https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/1995/1558) · [Finnish Tax Administration, corporate income tax and periods](https://www.vero.fi/en/businesses-and-corporations/taxes-and-charges/limited-companies-and-cooperatives/income-tax/)

## Responsible authorities

Verohallinto, the Finnish Tax Administration, administers ordinary income-tax registration, reporting and assessments. Finnish Customs (Tulli) handles import declarations. The Finnish Tax Administration generally handles import VAT for VAT-registered importers, with Finnish Customs responsible in other cases under the applicable rules. [Finnish Tax Administration, tax handbook](https://www.vero.fi/en/businesses-and-corporations/business-operations/foreign-business-in-finland/your-tax-handbook-in-finland/) · [Finnish Tax Administration, import VAT procedure](https://vero.fi/en/detailed-guidance/guidance/61955/value-added-tax-procedure-for-importation-as-of-1-january-20182/)

## Fictional intake

A company managed in Finland changes its accounting year and closes 2 periods in 2026. It also trades through a branch abroad. Identify its residence and each period, record the branch income and treaty facts and prepare the separate period details for the 2026 assessment.
