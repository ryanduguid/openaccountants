# Gambia Personal Income Tax

## Personal income tax bands and rates

- **Annual tax-free threshold** — D36,000 per year (D3,000 per month) GMD  _(Gambia Revenue Authority, Employment Income Tax (PAYE) brochure — https://www.gra.gm/download-file/8d0ccc58-d217-11ed-9b31-029254d29bb1 ; Gambia Revenue Authority, Income Tax Table 2025 (monthly and half-monthly paid employees) — https://www.gra.gm/download-file/2a69ec11-c633-11ef-b086-029254d29bb1)_
- **Band 1: D0 to D36,000** — 0 percent  _(Gambia Revenue Authority, Employment Income Tax (PAYE) brochure — https://www.gra.gm/download-file/8d0ccc58-d217-11ed-9b31-029254d29bb1 ; Gambia Revenue Authority, Income Tax Table 2025 (monthly and half-monthly paid employees) — https://www.gra.gm/download-file/2a69ec11-c633-11ef-b086-029254d29bb1)_
- **Band 2: D36,001 to D46,000** — 5 percent  _(Gambia Revenue Authority, Employment Income Tax (PAYE) brochure — https://www.gra.gm/download-file/8d0ccc58-d217-11ed-9b31-029254d29bb1 ; Gambia Revenue Authority, Income Tax Table 2025 (monthly and half-monthly paid employees) — https://www.gra.gm/download-file/2a69ec11-c633-11ef-b086-029254d29bb1)_
- **Band 3: D46,001 to D56,000** — 10 percent  _(Gambia Revenue Authority, Employment Income Tax (PAYE) brochure — https://www.gra.gm/download-file/8d0ccc58-d217-11ed-9b31-029254d29bb1 ; Gambia Revenue Authority, Income Tax Table 2025 (monthly and half-monthly paid employees) — https://www.gra.gm/download-file/2a69ec11-c633-11ef-b086-029254d29bb1)_
- **Band 4: D56,001 to D66,000** — 15 percent  _(Gambia Revenue Authority, Employment Income Tax (PAYE) brochure — https://www.gra.gm/download-file/8d0ccc58-d217-11ed-9b31-029254d29bb1 ; Gambia Revenue Authority, Income Tax Table 2025 (monthly and half-monthly paid employees) — https://www.gra.gm/download-file/2a69ec11-c633-11ef-b086-029254d29bb1)_
- **Band 5: D66,001 to D76,000** — 20 percent  _(Gambia Revenue Authority, Employment Income Tax (PAYE) brochure — https://www.gra.gm/download-file/8d0ccc58-d217-11ed-9b31-029254d29bb1 ; Gambia Revenue Authority, Income Tax Table 2025 (monthly and half-monthly paid employees) — https://www.gra.gm/download-file/2a69ec11-c633-11ef-b086-029254d29bb1)_
- **Top band: above D76,000** — 25 percent  _(Gambia Revenue Authority, Employment Income Tax (PAYE) brochure — https://www.gra.gm/download-file/8d0ccc58-d217-11ed-9b31-029254d29bb1 ; Gambia Revenue Authority, Income Tax Table 2025 (monthly and half-monthly paid employees) — https://www.gra.gm/download-file/2a69ec11-c633-11ef-b086-029254d29bb1)_
- **PAYE employment income threshold** — Employees earning above D3,000 per month (D36,000 per year) are subject to employment income tax GMD  _(Income and Value Added Tax Act https://www.gra.gm/domestic-faqs)_
- **Minimum tax for individual business income** — Higher of the schedule rate or 1% of turnover (audited accounts) / 2% of turnover (otherwise)  _(Income and Value Added Tax Act https://www.gra.gm/domestic-faqs)_
- **Small business turnover tax** — 3% of turnover as a final tax where annual business turnover is under D500,000; above that, personal business income tax is the higher of the tax on chargeable income and 1% of audited turnover (2% where the accounts are unaudited)  _(Gambia Revenue Authority, Personal Income Tax brochure — https://www.gra.gm/download-file/8d0cd78a-d217-11ed-9b31-029254d29bb1)_
- **Capital gains tax (individuals)** — Higher of 15% of the gain or 5% of the consideration  _(Income and Value Added Tax Act https://www.gra.gm/domestic-faqs)_
- **Residence test** — An individual is resident if they reside in The Gambia for at least 183 days during the tax year; an entity incorporated, managed or controlled in The Gambia is resident  _(Gambia Revenue Authority, Withholding Tax on Contract Payments brochure — https://www.gra.gm/download-file/8d0d0925-d217-11ed-9b31-029254d29bb1)_
- **Individual annual return deadline** — 31 March of the following year  _(Income and Value Added Tax Act https://www.gra.gm/domestic-faqs)_
- **PAYE remittance deadline** — Within 15 days after the end of the month  _(Income and Value Added Tax Act https://www.gra.gm/domestic-faqs)_
