## Intake and selected annual mechanics

Reuse redacted records before grouped questions. Establish the requested obligation and period, the complete applicable source chain, presence and employment coverage, age throughout the period, employer count, all salary and wage components, overtime, benefit and absence facts, special arrangements, prior deductions and actual remittances. A taxpayer's guess cannot resolve missing law.

1. **Coverage, section 15.** The selected provision concerns a person in Antigua and Barbuda who is aged 16 or more, is gainfully employed or self-employed and receives annual income of at least 6,500 source dollars. This worksheet addresses ordinary employment only. The source does not support a citizenship shortcut or an automatic upper-age exemption. Partial years, age transitions, cross-border facts and special classes remain held.
2. **Annual base, section 2.** Annual income means total salary or wages including overtime payment. Let A be the complete established non-negative annual amount for the selected fictional branch. Net bank pay, basic salary alone, salary after other deductions or a multiplied sample pay period does not establish A. Detailed wage character, exclusions, benefits and irregular payments require their own rules.
3. **Threshold and amount, sections 14–16.** The first 6,500 is exempt. The selected income condition is met at exactly 6,500, whilst the band amount is zero. Section 16 requires an application and satisfaction of the Commissioner for its below-threshold exemption. A mathematical zero below 6,500 is not proof that all actual exemption, deduction or reporting duties have been discharged.
4. **Marginal bands, section 14.** Apply 2.5% only to annual income above 6,500 through 60,000 and 5% only to the portion above 60,000. The lower taxable band is 53,500 wide. Do not tax all income at the top marginal rate, grant a second exemption above 60,000 or use 60,000 as the lower taxable width.
5. **Precision.** Retain the complete established base, each band and the exact sum separately. The examples below show exact unrounded source-dollar arithmetic. They establish no cent or whole-dollar claiming rule, pay-period divisor, cumulative method, year-end adjustment, refund or employee recovery right.

For the selected source structure:

```text
lower slice = min(max(A - 6500, 0), 53500)
upper slice = max(A - 60000, 0)
exact annual band amount = 0.025 × lower slice + 0.05 × upper slice
```

At A = 72,000, the lower slice is 53,500 and the upper slice is 12,000. Their exact amounts are 1,337.5 and 600, giving 1,937.5. This is a source-band demonstration under the stated fictional premises, not a payroll deduction or accepted annual assessment.

## Payroll, calendar and adjustment holds

Section 14(2) says the levy is paid monthly or weekly as the case may be; section 14(5) addresses weekly or monthly employer deductions. That does not establish the annual period, annualisation, cumulative computation or lawful division of this annual curve. Do not derive accepted withholding by dividing the exact annual amount by 12, 52 or another assumed count.

For the Board's weekly 250 example, 2.5% of the stated 125 difference is exactly 3.125; the page displays 3.13 and a 4-week total of 12.52. For its daily 60, 3-day example, 2.5% of the stated 35 difference is exactly 0.875; the page displays 0.88 but an aggregate of 2.63. These displays do not supply a consistent rule for rounding each component versus the aggregate. The monthly exemption display of 541.67 does not replace the statutory annual 6,500. Obtain the lawful precision and ordering before actual deductions.

Retained Board Act sections 17–18 say the seventh day of each month, without express following-month wording. Current Board guidance says on or before the 14th of the following month. No retained amendment, valid regulation or actual authorised decision has reconciled them. Hold payment dates and period attribution; do not select either calendar as accepted current law. General collection approval, Tax Administration filing extensions and unrelated 14-day court or auction periods do not resolve the conflict.

Section 19's retained arrears provision states 6% per month. Fractional-month treatment, compounding, rounding, applicable later law and actual ledger facts are not established here, so do not calculate a penalty. Section 21's retained annual-list date is on or before 30 January; the later Gazette notice and 14-day response are separate events. Reporting year, current forms, actual notice and accepted filing channel remain held.

Subtracting prior withholding from an annual band amount creates an arithmetic difference only. It establishes no automatic true-up, employee refund, offset, later deduction or retrospective recovery. Verify every legal condition, payment allocation and supporting record before any adjustment.
