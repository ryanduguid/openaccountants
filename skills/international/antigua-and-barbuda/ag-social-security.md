# Antigua and Barbuda ordinary monthly Social Security reference

> **General reference only.** This is a bounded Tier 2 draft for professional review. It does not certify current law, calculate complete payroll, authorise employee recovery or prepare an accepted return or payment. Guide presence and component checks do not establish comprehensive jurisdiction acceptance.

## Scope and source gate

Load both workflow bases in full. Preserve all 4 universal outputs and 18 checks, all 5 payroll outputs and 12 checks, all 12 mandatory payroll country slots, shared refusals and the additional refusals below. This companion does not complete the country contract. No incomplete prescribed financial output is permitted.

This branch is already registered ordinary local employment outside the public service, with an independently established insured employee and employer, no age transition, no benefit/exemption period and an established full calendar-month contribution period. All included cash wages and their proper period allocation are established. No advance, arrears, termination, tax-free emolument gross-up, abnormal pay practice, concurrent employment or special Board arrangement applies. These are fictional example premises, not actual missing-fact defaults. Statutory boards fall within the retained regulation's definition of public service; private ownership labels do not establish the category.

Before an actual calculation, establish the complete direct and indirect Act, amendment, commencement, regulation, exemption, prescribed pension-age and Board-direction chain for the period. The retained Act is not a working consolidation of later amendments. The Legal Affairs website's current-year availability notice and its example do not establish which operative 2026 instruments exist. An online list, dated rate announcement or absence of a search result cannot clear the source gate.

The selected SI 60 endpoint is 7% employee and 9% employer, total 16%, for employees outside the public service from the stated 2025 step. Its separate public-service endpoint is 6% and 9%, total 15%, and is outside the calculation branch below. The schedules state wage-base ceilings of 1,500 per week and 6,500 per month; do not treat those as contribution-amount caps. The FAQ also states fortnightly and annual figures. Do not substitute those figures for a proved applicable period or turn the monthly ceiling into an annual exhaustion rule.

The principal reg 4(2) allocation proviso refers to paragraphs (d) and (m), whilst SI 45 renumbers listed paragraphs without expressly updating those proviso references. Do not silently repair the references or infer an affected allocation rule. Ambiguous separate-payment and period-allocation cases remain held.

## Intake and selected monthly mechanics

Reuse relevant redacted records before grouped questions. Establish employer/employee registration, local insured employment, public/private category, prescribed pension-age and exemption status throughout the period, actual pay dates and contribution periods, complete gross cash components, benefit/absence facts, prior deductions and recoveries, other employments, Board arrangements, actual remittances, reviewer identity and required tools. Present the profile and wait for confirmation before classification. Keep names, registration identifiers, bank details and signatures with the reviewer. A taxpayer cannot resolve missing law by guessing it.

1. **Coverage and liability.** Apply the amended Act s 19 and prescribed employment rules, not an age-60 shortcut. Section 22(1) makes the employer initially responsible for both shares unless regulations otherwise provide. A Social Security card or lifetime insured status does not prove this period's contribution liability. Age boundaries, benefit periods, overseas/reciprocal arrangements and special classes remain held.
2. **Cash wage base, regulation 4.** Use established insurable gross cash wages paid during or immediately after the relevant period. The retained rule includes listed cash earnings such as overtime, commissions, housing allowances, shift payments, bonuses and service charges. It has specific allocation provisions for payments set aside or paid separately. Net bank wages, a tax label or a payroll total is not a complete base. Noncash remuneration, uncertain exclusions, separate/irregular items, advances, arrears and abnormal practices require their complete rules and are held here.
3. **One monthly ceiling.** For the selected established monthly period, aggregate all included payments by this employer for that month before applying one ceiling. Let W be established non-negative insurable monthly cash wages and B = min(W,6,500) source units. Do not grant a fresh monthly ceiling to each instalment, deduct an allowance not prescribed here or stop a later month's contributions merely because a prior-month total is high. Weekly, fortnightly, partial and concurrent-employment mechanics remain held.
4. **Separate shares.** Under the selected established endpoint premises, employee E = 0.07 × B; employer R = 0.09 × B; total SS liability L = E + R = 0.16 × B. Section 22(2) forbids recovering the employer's share from the employee despite a contrary contract. Gross wages less E is only a partial reconciliation, not accepted cash net pay. Wages plus R is only a partial cost component, not total employer cost. Medical Benefits, Education Levy, other taxes, pensions and deductions require separate sourced treatment; no nil result is assumed for them.
5. **Deduction and recovery, regulations 5–7.** Ordinary employee deductions follow the prescribed payment rules. A prior good-faith under-deduction is not an unlimited catch-up right: the retained reg 7(2) requires prior employee notification, recovery from subsequent emoluments during the same year and an additional-amount limit tied to the ordinary deductible amount. Section 40(6) separately prevents employee recovery of amounts paid under its prosecution-recovery route. This reference calculates neither correction nor recovery; missing or disputed conditions remain held.
6. **Remittance and records differ from calculation.** Read reg 9 as amended for monthly payment of both shares and submission of the remittance form no later than 14 days after month end. A deduction, bank movement or prepared form does not establish receipt or allocation by ABSSB. Regulations 10–12 contain employer records, a separate annual return by 31 January and inspection requirements. Actual forms, relevant year, accepted channel, holiday treatment, calendar and retention duration remain unestablished here. The Fund's own accounting duties in Act s 17 are not employer retention rules.
7. **Corrections and penalties.** Regulation 9(3)'s error adjustment is conditional on the retained good-faith error, same-year limit and accounting to an employee for a corresponding over-deduction. It is not an automatic cash refund or permission to offset any debt. SI 45 amends late-remittance surcharge and adds an interest provision; do not infer current compound interest or a complete penalty amount from the FAQ. Actual refund, offset, correction, default and enforcement cases remain held.
8. **Precision and currency.** CU means a fictional source-unit dollar. Preserve wage-base precision and exact employee, employer and total components separately. An unrounded fraction is not a lawful remittance or payslip amount. Establish actual currency, conversion, rounding sequence and prescribed total reconciliation before any actual output. The payroll base requires Needs Input when an applicable rounding rule is unknown; do not substitute an invented rule.
