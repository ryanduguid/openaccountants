> **General reference only.** This bounded Tier 2 draft compares the public Medical Benefits Scheme (MBS) rate table with retained legislation. It does not certify current law, complete payroll, lawful recovery, accepted deductions, a remittance or a return. This branch concerns independently established registered ordinary local employment, one employer, an employee aged at least 16 and under 60 throughout one established full monthly contribution period and complete salary/wages whose medical-benefits classification is resolved. There is no sick or maternity leave, director/owner or self-employed status, age transition, concurrent employment, advance, arrears, termination, disputed allowance, noncash item or special Board arrangement. The monthly low-earnings test uses the same established salary/wage amount W in the fictional cases; no exclusion or period issue affects that test. These are fictional stipulated premises, not missing-fact defaults.

## Intake and selected monthly mechanics

Reuse relevant redacted records before grouped questions. Establish legal parties and registrations, ordinary employee versus director/self-employed status, age throughout the period, actual pay dates and Board-established contribution period, complete pay components and classifications, sick/maternity leave, other employment, prior deductions, payments and recoveries, special directions, required tools and reviewer identity. Present the profile and wait for confirmation before classification. Keep identifying documents, registration numbers, bank details and signatures with the reviewer. A taxpayer cannot resolve missing law by guessing it.

1. **Parties and coverage.** Act ss 32–33 require registration and employer assurance of employee registration, subject to special directions. A Social Security card alone does not establish MBS registration or liability. Regulation 2(2) includes corporation directors within its self-employed definition; do not select the ordinary employee branch solely from a contract or payslip. Actual classification and registration remain gates.
2. **Distinct earnings base.** Regulation 2(1) uses wages, salaries and similar employment payments and expressly excludes sick/maternity leave payments, severance, travelling, meal and similar allowances. Do not import Social Security's gross-cash base or assume that any item labelled allowance is excluded. The public Scheme description lists other earnings categories, but disputed inclusions, noncash values, employer-paid liabilities, irregular pay and their allocation require complete applicable rules. W is a non-negative, fully established monthly amount within this clean branch, not net bank wages.
3. **Strict low-earnings allocation.** Under Act s 40(2) and Second Schedule item 4, a registered person earning less than 400 monthly has the selected 7% contribution wholly paid by the employer. For the stipulated monthly branch with 0 ≤ W < 400, employee E = 0 and employer R = 0.07 × W. Equality is outside the special wording: at W = 400, use the ordinary selected split below. The separate weekly threshold is less than 100; no conversion, frequency choice or prorating rule is inferred. Mixed pay, leave, multiple employers and uncertain threshold measurement remain held.
4. **Ordinary selected split.** For W ≥ 400 under the established age-16-to-under-60 premises, Act s 43 and the Second Schedule with regulation 17 supply E = 0.035 × W and R = 0.035 × W. Combined component L = E + R = 0.07 × W in both selected allocations. No ordinary wage ceiling appears in this retained source set; this is not proof of uncapped current law. Do not import Social Security's 6,500 monthly ceiling, 7% employee rate or pension-age endpoint. An amount above 6,500 remains an illustrative medical-benefits base under the stipulated retained-source premises.
5. **Deductions and employer responsibility.** Act s 43(2) concerns deduction of the prescribed employee part when earnings are paid; s 43(3) makes the employer responsible for paying Scheme contributions. Section 44 discharges earnings only to the extent of a deduction made in accordance with s 43(2). It does not authorise charging the employee the employer's component. For W < 400 this selected component supplies no employee deduction. Gross W less E is only a partial reconciliation; W plus R is only a partial employer-cost component. Neither is accepted net wages or total cost.
6. **Leave, age and benefit boundaries.** Regulation 8 exempts a person during medically authorised sick/maternity leave and prohibits deductions from salary for that leave. Regulation 9 preserves benefit entitlement. Employer-share/partial-period treatment remains held. Regulation 17's employer wording covers all employees under 60, its employee wording begins at 16 and its 60-to-under-70 provision is separate. Under-16, age 60+, transitions and their interaction with low earnings are outside this branch. Do not substitute Social Security retirement age. The 26-week benefit condition and a certificate form's six-month wording do not establish a contribution holiday or benefit acceptance.
7. **Recovery and correction are separate.** Act s 57(3) bars recovering the employee portion of amounts paid under that prosecution route. The retained set does not establish an ordinary missed-deduction catch-up or refund method. Do not import Social Security's good-faith recovery conditions or treat a medical-expense claim form as a contribution-refund form. Actual under/over-deductions, recoveries, refunds and offsets remain held.
8. **Remittance and records.** Regulation 5 gives the Board the due-month/date determination; the Minister may publish it by Gazette Notice. Its employer wording is on or before the 14th day of each month for which the contribution is due, unless a different date is authorised in writing by the Chief Executive Officer. It also requires the Schedule XIV remittance form. Do not rewrite this as a proved actual following-month calendar solely from administrative guidance. Establish current directions, period linkage, forms, channel, holiday treatment and receipts. Keep liability, employee deduction, employer component, payment and authority allocation separate. The Board's accounts/reporting duties are not employer annual-return or retention rules.
9. **Precision and remaining charges.** CU means a fictional source-unit dollar. Preserve exact W, E, R and L separately, including five-decimal share fractions where produced. A fraction is not an accepted payable minor-unit amount. Unknown applicable rounding requires Needs Input under the payroll base. Establish actual currency, conversion, sequence and reconciliation before actual output. Social Security, Education Levy, income tax, pensions and every other charge require their own sourced treatment; no nil result is assumed. Regulation 7's late-payment additions and exemption application do not establish an actual penalty without the due-date, base, current-law and payment evidence.
