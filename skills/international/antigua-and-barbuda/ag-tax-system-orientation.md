# Antigua and Barbuda tax system orientation

The Inland Revenue Department (IRD) administers company income tax, unincorporated business tax and Antigua and Barbuda Sales Tax. Amounts in this guide are in Eastern Caribbean dollars (XCD). [IRD, tax services][IRD]

## Residence and source

The unincorporated business tax (UBT) uses the individual residence definition in the Income Tax Act. It includes a 183-day basis-period test, an abode and presence test and a test for presence continuous with a qualifying adjacent period. The abode test has specified exceptions for absence. Under the UBT Act, self-employment, partnership and deemed business income can come within the charge whether it arises locally or abroad. Check the exemptions for foreign income already liable to tax abroad and unrelated to local activity, and for certain temporary visitors. Non-resident property owners and recipients of specified local payments can face separate agent and withholding obligations. [Income Tax Act, Cap. 212, s 2][CAP] · [UBT Act, ss 2–4 and 16–17][UBT]

## Individuals and companies

Antigua and Barbuda repealed its general personal income tax from 1 July 2016. Employment income is exempt from UBT. Education levy, medical benefits and social security obligations are separate. UBT applies to chargeable business income at 0%, 8% and 25% in the sole-proprietor and partnership bands. Check the applicable Schedule I and IRD tables for thresholds, partnership treatment and non-resident property. [UBT Act, ss 3–4 and 47 and Schedule I][UBT] · [IRD, UBT FAQ, questions 1, 2 and 10][UBTFAQ] · [IRD, administered taxes and levies][LEVIES]

The ordinary company income tax rate is 25% of chargeable income. A separate 10% rate provision applies to specified commercial banks, registered insurers, petroleum, energy and telecommunications companies for income years from 1 January 2021. Confirm the statutory category and any other applicable provision before using that rate. For a cross-border company, check which income comes into the charge, the section 5 receipt rules and any applicable treaty. [Income Tax Act, ss 5 and 8][CAP] · [Income Tax (Amendment) Act 2008, s 4][CORP08] · [Income Tax (Amendment) Act 2022, s 3][CORP22]

## Transactions and filing

The ordinary Antigua and Barbuda Sales Tax (ABST) rate is 17%. Check registration, transaction classification, exemptions and applicable temporary reductions for the actual supply. A taxable person generally lodges the ABST return within 15 calendar days after the period ends, under the 2024 amendment applicable from the period ending 31 March 2024. [Revenue (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2023, s 2][ABST] · [ABST (Amendment) Act 2024, ss 1 and 3][ABSTRET]

The UBT annual business return and any outstanding balance are due by 31 March for the preceding year. Quarterly returns and payments are due within 15 days after quarter end. The IRD lists a company annual return and balance deadline of three months after year end, with instalments during the year. Use the IRD forms for the applicable taxpayer and period. [UBT Act, ss 11–15][UBT] · [IRD, corporation tax forms][FORMS] · [IRD, company payments and due dates][CORPRET] · [IRD, administered taxes and levies][LEVIES]
