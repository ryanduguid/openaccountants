# Wallis and Futuna Tax System Orientation

The territorial system relies primarily on customs and other indirect charges. The Institut d'émission d'outre-mer (IEOM) describes the ordinary regime as having no general personal income tax, corporate profits tax or tax on income from securities. Patente and the tax on registered companies with no local activity are separate charges. [IEOM 2025 annual economic report, fiscal regime][REPORT] · [Territorial Gazette no 596][GAZETTE]

## Residence

For ordinary local classification, start with the taxable activity, import or registration. The ordinary regime described by IEOM has no personal income tax residence schedule. Residence, income source or a business presence elsewhere can still matter under the other jurisdiction's rules. [IEOM 2025 annual economic report, fiscal regime][REPORT] · [Territorial customs formalities][IMPORT]

## Income and territorial scope

Test the patente for a profession carried on independently for profit in the territory. Test customs duty, entry tax and applicable consumption charges on imports using the goods, origin, value and applicable regime. Under the 2022 territorial deliberation, a legal person registered on the Wallis and Futuna commercial register with no activity in the territory can be liable for the separate Taxe sur les sociétés sans activité (TSSA). Confirm the taxpayer's activity classification with the customs-attached Contributions diverses office. [Territorial patente guidance][PATENTE] · [Territorial customs formalities][IMPORT] · [Deliberation 27/AT/2022][GAZETTE] · [Deliberation 82/AT/2024][OFFICE]

## Taxpayer type

The ordinary patente rules can apply to individuals and legal persons carrying on a profession for their own account. The TSSA rule in the 2022 deliberation applies to legal persons meeting the registration and activity tests, irrespective of their form or purpose. Every liable legal person must have a local representative authorised to provide domiciliation services by prefectoral order. [Territorial patente guidance][PATENTE] · [Deliberation 27/AT/2022, arts 1–3][GAZETTE]

## Currency

Use Pacific francs (F CFP, currency code XPF). Confirm the customs valuation and conversion basis for imports quoted in another currency. [Institut d'émission d'outre-mer][IEOM] · [Territorial customs formalities][IMPORT]

## Assessment periods

Declare a patente activity to Contributions diverses before starting. Establish the assessment year, any change of activity and the applicable decision or roll before paying. Under the 2022 TSSA text the full current year's charge arises on registration, with a separate first-year deposit. Later collection is by assessment roll. Customs declarations follow the relevant import. [Territorial patente guidance][PATENTE] · [Deliberation 27/AT/2022, arts 6–10][GAZETTE] · [Territorial customs formalities][IMPORT]

## Tax authority and headline rates

The Service des Douanes et Contributions diverses deals with customs and territorial revenue. The ordinary patente has fixed and proportional parts, with a separate chamber charge where applicable. Under article 4 of the 2022 TSSA text, the ordinary annual flat charge is XPF 1 million. Confirm the applicable year and any later amendments or special cases before using the amount. Determine import charges from the current tariff and eligibility instead of applying a uniform percentage. [Territorial customs and contributions service][SERVICE] · [Territorial patente guidance][PATENTE] · [Deliberation 27/AT/2022, arts 4–5][GAZETTE] · [Territorial customs formalities][IMPORT]
