# Ukraine Tax Overview

## The Ukrainian tax system at a glance

Ukraine operates a residence-based tax system administered by the State Tax Service. The tax year is the calendar year and the currency is the Ukrainian hryvnia (UAH). A temporary wartime military levy applies on top of headline income taxes.

- **Tax year** — Calendar year (1 January – 31 December)  _(Tax Code of Ukraine)_
- **Currency** — Ukrainian hryvnia (UAH)  _(Tax Code of Ukraine)_
- **Tax authority** — State Tax Service of Ukraine (Derzhavna podatkova sluzhba)  _([Tax Code of Ukraine](https://tax.gov.ua/en/))_
- **Basis of taxation** — Residents are taxed on worldwide income; non-residents on Ukrainian-source income only  _(Tax Code of Ukraine)_
- **Headline personal income tax rate** — 18% (art. 167.1); passive income 18%, except 5% on dividends paid by resident corporate income tax payers and half the basic rate (9%) on dividends from non-residents, collective investment institutions and non-payers of corporate income tax (art. 167.5)  _(Tax Code of Ukraine No. 2755-VI (Податковий кодекс України, current consolidated text on zakon.rada.gov.ua), art. 167.1 and 167.5 — https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/2755-17 ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/ukraine/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **Military levy (temporary, on top of PIT)** — 5% of the income that enters the taxpayer's total annual taxable income, for the period of martial law (raised from 1.5% by Law 4015-IX from 1 January 2025)  _(Tax Code of Ukraine No. 2755-VI (Податковий кодекс України, current consolidated text on zakon.rada.gov.ua), Section XX, subsection 10, para. 16-1 — https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/2755-17 ; https://www.ey.com/en_gl/technical/tax-alerts/ukraine-adopts-tax-increases-to-fund-defense)_
- **Headline corporate income tax rate** — 18% (25% for financial institutions other than insurers from 2024, art. 136.1-1)  _(Tax Code of Ukraine No. 2755-VI (Податковий кодекс України, current consolidated text on zakon.rada.gov.ua), art. 136.1 — https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/2755-17 ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/ukraine/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Does VAT exist?** — Yes — VAT (PDV) at 20%, with 0% on the operations listed in the Code, 7% on medicines, medical devices and listed cultural and tourism services, and 14% on listed agricultural products (art. 193.1)  _(Tax Code of Ukraine No. 2755-VI (Податковий кодекс України, current consolidated text on zakon.rada.gov.ua), art. 193.1 — https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/2755-17)_
- **Annual personal income tax declaration deadline** — By 1 May of the year following the reporting year for individuals, including the self-employed (art. 49.18.4)  _(Tax Code of Ukraine No. 2755-VI (Податковий кодекс України, current consolidated text on zakon.rada.gov.ua), art. 49.18.4 — https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/2755-17 ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/ukraine/individual/tax-administration)_
- **Annual corporate income tax return deadline** — Within 60 calendar days after the last day of the reporting year for annual filers (art. 49.18.3 and 49.18.6)  _(Tax Code of Ukraine No. 2755-VI (Податковий кодекс України, current consolidated text on zakon.rada.gov.ua), art. 49.18.3 and 49.18.6 — https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/2755-17)_
- **Statutory minimum monthly wage (2025)** — UAH 8,000 UAH  _(Law of Ukraine On the State Budget of Ukraine for 2025)_
