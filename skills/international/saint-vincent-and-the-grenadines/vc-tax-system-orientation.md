# Saint Vincent and the Grenadines tax system orientation

The Inland Revenue Department (IRD) administers personal income tax, corporate income tax and VAT. Amounts in this guide are in Eastern Caribbean dollars (XCD). [IRD, taxes][IRD]

## Residence and source

An individual can be resident under the 183-day basis-period test, the permanent abode and presence test or a continuous presence test involving a qualifying adjacent basis period. Specified absences can qualify under the abode rule. A company is resident if incorporated locally, or, if incorporated abroad, if managed or controlled locally. [Income Tax Act, Cap. 435, s 2][LAW]

Residents generally bring in worldwide income. A resident individual who is not ordinarily resident, or who meets the international ship crew rule, brings in foreign income to the extent received locally. Check the separate rule for government-sponsored contract labour abroad. Non-residents generally bring in domestic-source income subject to exemptions and the separate withholding treatment for income outside business or employment. [Income Tax Act, ss 7–10][LAW]

## Individuals and companies

The IRD lists an XCD 25,000 standard deduction for 2025 and links to PAYE tables effective from January 2024. The published progressive schedule uses 10% on the first XCD 5,000 of chargeable income, 20% on the next XCD 5,000 and 28% above XCD 10,000. Confirm the rate table and deduction for 2026 with the IRD before calculating liability or setting up payroll. For 2025 returns due 31 March 2026, the IRD directed employees to eTax and directed sole traders and self-employed professionals to submit manual returns with supporting statements. [IRD, standard deduction][DEDUCTION] · [IRD, PAYE tables][PAYE] · [Ministry of Finance, progressive rate schedule][BANDS] · [IRD, 30 March 2026 notice][FILING]

The ordinary company income tax rate is 28%. The IRD normally requires the company return and payment balance three months after financial year end. Check taxable income, special regimes and instalments independently. [Finance Act No. 10 of 2023, s 8][FINANCE] · [IRD, taxes][IRD]

## Transactions and filing

The IRD lists ordinary VAT at 16% and qualifying accommodation rooms at 11%. It lists XCD 300,000 in annual sales for business registration and separate registration categories for public entertainment promoters and accommodation providers. Check taxable turnover and registration rules for the activity. VAT registrants generally file and pay by the 15th of the month after the tax period. [IRD, taxes and registration][IRD] · [IRD, FAQs][FAQ]
