# Mauritius Corporate Income Tax

## Mauritius Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax rates and base

Resident companies are taxed at a flat 15% on net chargeable income, with reduced rates for certain export and partial-exemption activities. The following figures reflect Finance Act 2025 measures.

- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 15%  _(Income Tax Act 1995 (MRA consolidation to May 2026), s 44 and First Schedule Part IV — https://www.mra.mu/download/ITAConsolidated.pdf)_
- **Reduced rate on chargeable income from export of goods** — 3% on the chargeable income attributable to the export of goods, computed under the formula in s 44B(2)  _(Income Tax Act 1995 (MRA consolidation to May 2026), s 44B(1) and First Schedule Part II — https://www.mra.mu/download/ITAConsolidated.pdf)_
- **Partial exemption (80%) on qualifying income** — 80% of foreign-source dividends (item 6) and of interest (item 7) derived by a company other than a bank, non-bank deposit taker, money changer or foreign exchange dealer is exempt, subject to the prescribed substance conditions, leaving an effective 3% on that income  _(Income Tax Act 1995 (MRA consolidation to May 2026), Second Schedule Part II Sub-Part B items 6 and 7 — https://www.mra.mu/download/ITAConsolidated.pdf)_
- **Tax base** — Chargeable income after allowable deductions; a company incorporated in Mauritius or with its central management and control there is resident and taxed on income derived from Mauritius or elsewhere  _(Income Tax Act 1995 (MRA consolidation to May 2026), ss 5(1) and 73(1)(b) — https://www.mra.mu/download/ITAConsolidated.pdf)_
- **Fair Share Contribution (companies, other than banks)** — 5% of chargeable income for companies taxed at the 15% rate and 2% for companies taxed at the 3% export rate, where both supplies and chargeable income exceed Rs 24 million in the accounting year; banks pay 5% plus an additional 2.5% on chargeable income from transactions with residents. Applies to income derived from 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2028 and is levied under Part XC of the Value Added Tax Act. The Finance Act 2026 (s 25(o), in force from its assent on 12 August 2026) drops the supplies test, so liability turns on chargeable income above Rs 24 million alone  _(Value Added Tax Act 1998 (MRA consolidation to May 2026), Part XC, s 53N(1), (2) and (8), inserted by the Finance (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2025 — https://www.mra.mu/download/VATAct.pdf ; Finance (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2025 — https://www.mra.mu/download/FinanceAct2025.pdf ; Finance Act 2026 (Act No. 14 of 2026), s 25(o) — https://www.mra.mu/download/FinanceAct2026.pdf)_
- **Qualified Domestic Minimum Top-up Tax (QDMTT)** — Top-up to a 15% effective rate for Mauritius members of multinational groups with consolidated revenue of EUR 750 million or more in at least two of the four preceding fiscal years, from the year of assessment commencing 1 July 2025  _(Income Tax Act 1995 (MRA consolidation to May 2026), ss 50Q(1) and 50R(3) — https://www.mra.mu/download/ITAConsolidated.pdf)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends** — 0%: dividends are not among the payments subject to deduction at source under s 111B, and dividends paid by a company resident in Mauritius are exempt income  _(Income Tax Act 1995 (MRA consolidation to May 2026), s 111B and Second Schedule Part II Sub-Part B item 1 — https://www.mra.mu/download/ITAConsolidated.pdf)_
- **Withholding tax on interest** — 15% on interest paid by any person other than a bank or non-bank deposit-taking institution to any person other than a company resident in Mauritius; a treaty may reduce the rate  _(Income Tax Act 1995 (MRA consolidation to May 2026), s 111C and Sixth Schedule item 1 — https://www.mra.mu/download/ITAConsolidated.pdf)_
- **Withholding tax on royalties** — **10%** to a resident and **15%** to a non-resident; a treaty may reduce the non-resident rate  _(Income Tax Act 1995 (MRA consolidation to May 2026), s 111C and Sixth Schedule item 2 — https://www.mra.mu/download/ITAConsolidated.pdf)_
- **Withholding tax on rent** — **7.5%** to a resident and **10%** to a non-resident  _(Income Tax Act 1995 (MRA consolidation to May 2026), s 111C and Sixth Schedule item 3 — https://www.mra.mu/download/ITAConsolidated.pdf)_
- **Withholding tax on management fees** — **5%** to a resident and **10%** to a non-resident  _(Income Tax Act 1995 (MRA consolidation to May 2026), s 111C and Sixth Schedule item 9 — https://www.mra.mu/download/ITAConsolidated.pdf)_
- **Withholding tax on services to non-residents (general)** — **10%** on payments to a non-resident for services rendered in Mauritius  _(Income Tax Act 1995 (MRA consolidation to May 2026), s 111C and Sixth Schedule item 8 — https://www.mra.mu/download/ITAConsolidated.pdf)_
- **Withholding tax on payments to contractors and sub-contractors** — **0.75%** — the lowest rate in the table, and low enough that treating a construction payment as an ordinary service fee at 5% or 10% over-deducts several times over  _(Income Tax Act 1995 (MRA consolidation to May 2026), s 111C and Sixth Schedule item 4 — https://www.mra.mu/download/ITAConsolidated.pdf)_
- **Withholding tax on professional service providers** — **5%** on payments to an accountant or accounting firm, architect, attorney or solicitor, and tax adviser or representative  _(Income Tax Act 1995 (MRA consolidation to May 2026), s 111C, Fifth Schedule and Sixth Schedule item 5 — https://www.mra.mu/download/ITAConsolidated.pdf)_
- **Withholding tax on owners or agents of immovable property** — **5%**  _(Income Tax Act 1995 (MRA consolidation to May 2026), s 111C and Sixth Schedule item 7 — https://www.mra.mu/download/ITAConsolidated.pdf)_
- **Withholding tax on non-resident entertainers and sportspersons** — **10%**  _(Income Tax Act 1995 (MRA consolidation to May 2026), s 111C and Sixth Schedule item 10 — https://www.mra.mu/download/ITAConsolidated.pdf)_
- **Withholding tax at 3% — commission and specified trades** — **3%** on commission, on consultants, on security, cleaning and pest-management services, and on motor surveyors (Sixth Schedule items 11 to 14); the Finance Act 2026 adds **1%** on payments to ICT services providers and **5%** on advertising, promotional, endorsement or marketing services carried out through social media or other digital means (items 15 and 16, from the Act's assent on 12 August 2026)  _(Income Tax Act 1995 (MRA consolidation to May 2026), s 111C and Sixth Schedule items 11 to 14 — https://www.mra.mu/download/ITAConsolidated.pdf ; Finance Act 2026 (Act No. 14 of 2026), s 7(o) and (y) — https://www.mra.mu/download/FinanceAct2026.pdf)_
- **Withholding tax on government procurement payments** — **1%** on goods and services above MUR 300,000 and on goods above MUR 100,000; **3%** on services above MUR 30,000. These are threshold-based, so the size of the contract decides whether anything is withheld  _(Income Tax Act 1995 (MRA consolidation to May 2026), s 111C and Sixth Schedule item 6 — https://www.mra.mu/download/ITAConsolidated.pdf)_
- **Advance Payment System (APS)** — Companies file an APS Statement and pay tax for each of the first three quarters within three months from the end of the month in which the quarter ends (a September quarter is due two working days before the end of December), with the balance paid on the annual return  _(Income Tax Act 1995 (MRA consolidation to May 2026), s 50B(1) and (2) — https://www.mra.mu/download/ITAConsolidated.pdf)_
- **Corporate return filing & payment deadline** — Within six months from the end of the month in which the accounting period ends, paying any tax with the return; a June year-end files and pays two working days before the end of December, or by 15 January where no tax is payable or a loss is declared  _(Income Tax Act 1995 (MRA consolidation to May 2026), s 116(1), (2) and (2A) — https://www.mra.mu/download/ITAConsolidated.pdf)_
