# Indonesia tax system

> General orientation for 2026. Confirm the taxpayer, accounting period and transaction with a qualified local tax professional before applying these rules.

Amounts are in Indonesian rupiah (IDR). The Directorate General of Taxes (DGT) administers income tax (PPh) and domestic value added tax (PPN). The income tax and VAT amendments in Law No. 7 of 2021 apply on separate timetables. [Law No. 7 of 2021, arts. 3 and 4](https://pajak.go.id/index.php/id/peraturan/harmonisasi-peraturan-perpajakan)

## Income tax

- **Individuals and residence.** A person can be resident by living in Indonesia, spending more than 183 days there in a 12-month period, or being present in the tax year with intent to live there. Parts of a day count as a full day. Residents generally include Indonesian and foreign income, subject to statutory exceptions. Non-residents generally report Indonesian-source income under the applicable non-resident rules. For 2026, the ordinary annual marginal rates on taxable income after applicable deductions and allowances are 5% to IDR 60 million, 15% to IDR 250 million, 25% to IDR 500 million, 30% to IDR 5 billion and 35% above that. Check final-tax income and treaty treatment separately. [PER-23/PJ/2025, arts. 3, 4 and 6](https://www.pajak.go.id/id/peraturan/penentuan-subjek-pajak-dalam-negeri-dan-subjek-pajak-luar-negeri-0) · [Income Tax Law, arts. 4 and 17 as amended](https://pajak.go.id/index.php/id/peraturan/harmonisasi-peraturan-perpajakan)
- **Companies and small business.** An entity established or domiciled in Indonesia generally has domestic taxpayer status. The standard rate for domestic companies and permanent establishments is 22% from tax year 2022. Government Regulation No. 20 of 2026, effective 22 April 2026, amended the separate 0.5% final tax on qualifying gross turnover. The amended regime generally covers qualifying resident individuals, single-person companies and cooperatives with turnover up to IDR 4.8 billion, subject to exclusions, aggregation and transitional rules. Check entity type, business activity and the relevant tax year before applying the concession. [Income Tax Law, art. 17 as amended](https://pajak.go.id/index.php/id/peraturan/harmonisasi-peraturan-perpajakan) · [Government Regulation No. 20 of 2026, arts. I–III](https://pajak.go.id/en/node/120099)

## VAT and filing

- **VAT.** The statutory VAT rate is 12% from 1 January 2025. In 2026, PMK 131/2024 applies 12% to an alternative base of 11/12 of the applicable value for ordinary non-luxury transactions it covers, equivalent to 11% of that value. Specified luxury goods subject to luxury sales tax generally use the full base. Check separate rules for goods and services already subject to a prescribed base or amount, and confirm the treatment of exports and exempt supplies. [VAT Law, art. 7 as amended](https://pajak.go.id/index.php/id/peraturan/harmonisasi-peraturan-perpajakan) · [PMK 131/2024, arts. 2–5](https://www.jdih.kemenkeu.go.id/dok/pmk-131-tahun-2024)
- **Filing.** The ordinary annual income tax return (SPT) deadline is 3 months from the end of the tax year for individuals and 4 months for entities. For a 31 December 2026 year end, this gives 31 March and 30 April 2027 respectively, subject to any applicable extension. Check the separate periodic return and payment deadlines. [DGT filing deadlines](https://www.pajak.go.id/index.php/id/batas-waktu-lapor) · [DGT Coretax filing regulation, arts. 96–97](https://www.pajak.go.id/sites/default/files/lampiran/PER-11_PJ_2025_0.pdf)
