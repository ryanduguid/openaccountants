# Belgium tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the individual's residence, the entity's principal establishment and management, the legal form, the income source and the relevant tax period.

## Territorial reach

A Belgian-resident individual generally reports domestic and foreign income. A resident company starts with its total business result and applies the statutory adjustments and relevant treaty relief. Non-resident income tax applies to specified Belgian-source items such as qualifying work, Belgian establishment profits and Belgian real estate income. Check whether any foreign amounts must be shown on a non-resident return to apply the correct calculation. [FPS Finance, moving to Belgium](https://fin.belgium.be/en/private-individuals/international/coming-to-belgium/tax-return?wizid=359) · [FPS Finance, resident company return instructions](https://finances.belgium.be/sites/default/files/downloads/121-declaration-isoc-explications-2026.pdf) · [FPS Finance, non-resident company return instructions](https://finances.belgium.be/sites/default/files/downloads/121-declaration-inr-soc-explications-2026.pdf)

## Residence

An individual generally has Belgian tax residence when domiciled there or, in the absence of a Belgian domicile, when the centre of their financial interests is in Belgium. National Register entry gives rise to a rebuttable presumption. Check the household domicile rules for married people and legal cohabitants and apply any treaty separately. A resident company generally has its principal establishment or seat of management or administration in Belgium and is within corporate income tax. A Belgian statutory seat gives rise to a rebuttable presumption. Rebuttal requires showing that the company's tax domicile is established in another country under that country's tax law. [FPS Finance, individual residence](https://fin.belgium.be/en/private-individuals/international/coming-to-belgium/tax-return?wizid=359) · [FPS Finance, company residence](https://finances.belgium.be/sites/default/files/downloads/121-declaration-isoc-explications-2026.pdf)

## Taxpayer types

A sole trader accounts for professional income under the individual rules. A trading company within the corporate charge, such as an ordinary resident SRL/BV or SA/NV, accounts for its own result. Where a partnership or association is treated as lacking legal personality for income tax, check assessment of the participants and their respective shares. Check the entity's legal form and the tax definition before selecting a return. For a foreign trust, check whether the arrangement is a legal construction, who is treated as founder and the treatment of income or distributions. Also check the reporting requirement and the effect of the Constitutional Court's partial annulment of the Cayman tax reform. [FPS Finance, individual business return instructions](https://fin.belgium.be/sites/default/files/media/documents/explications-partie-2-2026.pdf) · [FPS Finance, company return instructions](https://finances.belgium.be/sites/default/files/downloads/121-declaration-isoc-explications-2026.pdf) · [Programme Law of 22 December 2023, arts 33–40](https://www.ejustice.just.fgov.be/eli/loi/2023/12/22/2023048600/justel) · [Constitutional Court, judgment 117/2025](https://fr.const-court.be/a/117/2025) · [FPS Finance, annex 276 CJC](https://financien.belgium.be/sites/default/files/downloads/155-276cjc-2024-fr.pdf)

## Currency

Belgium uses the euro (EUR). Belgian statutory accounts ordinarily use EUR. An eligible company with permission from FPS Economy can keep its accounts in a foreign currency and disclose that permission in the notes. Check the currency and any conversion required by the tax return separately. [National Bank of Belgium, annual account filing format](https://www.nbb.be/en/central-balance-sheet-office/preparation-and-filing/when-and-how-file/filing-format)

## Assessment periods

Ordinary personal income tax uses calendar-year income. For example, 2026 income ordinarily corresponds to assessment year 2027. A company files after its financial year closes. Check the closing date and the applicable assessment year, especially for a company whose year does not end on 31 December. [FPS Finance, income and assessment years](https://finances.belgium.be/en/private-individuals/tax-return/income/tax-rates) · [FPS Finance, corporate income tax return](https://financien.belgium.be/nl/node/1473)

## Responsible authorities

FPS Finance administers federal income taxes for individuals, companies and non-residents. Confirm the region and commune for the relevant personal income-tax components. Check the responsible authority separately for a distinct regional or local charge. [FPS Finance, General Administration of Taxes](https://finances.belgium.be/fr/node/1598) · [FPS Finance, regional return instructions](https://fin.belgium.be/fr/particuliers/declaration-impot/rentrer-declaration/declaration-impot) · [FPS Finance, communal income-tax rates](https://fin.belgium.be/sites/default/files/media/documents/taux-taxe-communale-2026.pdf)

## Fictional intake

A person moves to Belgium, retains income from a foreign rental property and trades through an SRL with a June closing date. Gather the facts about the person's domicile, identify any treaty relief for the property income and record the company's closing date separately to identify its return.
