# Bouvet Island VAT, customs and excise scope

Use this reference to decide which Norwegian territorial rule to check for goods connected with Bouvet Island. Load `workflow-base`. Identify where the goods are sold, when and where they cross the relevant boundary and who carries out each movement.

## Which territory applies?

Section 1 of the Norwegian Dependencies Act names Bouvet Island as a Norwegian dependency. Section 2 separately addresses private law, criminal law, administration of justice and the extent to which other laws apply. Check the territorial provision of the particular tax or customs statute. [Dependencies Act, ss 1–2](https://lovdata.no/lov/1930-02-27-3)

| Rule | Selected territorial reading |
|---|---|
| VAT | The VAT Act excludes the Norwegian dependencies from the VAT area. For goods moved into the VAT area, apply the import branch in section 3-29 and establish any applicable relief. [VAT Act, ss 1-2(2) and 3-29](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/2009-06-19-58) |
| Customs control and duty | Both the Goods Movement Act and the Customs Duty Act exclude the dependencies from the customs area. Movement into or out of the customs area can bring the Goods Movement Act into play. Duty on an entry into the customs area depends on the separate Customs Duty Act. The two Acts commenced on 1 January 2023. [Goods Movement Act, s 1-2](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/2022-03-11-9) · [Customs Duty Act, s 1-1](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/2022-03-11-8) |
| Goods excise from 1 January 2027 | Section 1-2 of the 2026 Excise Duty Act excludes the dependencies from the excise area, subject to the Act’s other provisions. Under Chapter 2, section 2-6 exempts goods exported from the excise area to the dependencies. Use the new Act from the appointed date and check the applicable product, documentation and procedure. [Excise Duty Act, ss 1-2, 2-6 and 26-1](https://lovdata.no/dokument/LTI/lov/2026-06-19-41) · [Commencement FOR-2026-10-30-2205](https://lovdata.no/dokument/LTI/forskrift/2026-10-30-2205) |

For an earlier movement, establish the particular excise and read the relevant parliamentary levy decision and regulations for that date. The prior regulation’s section 2-7 describes registered-business treatment of exports abroad, to Svalbard or to Jan Mayen. Its definition of an export abroad refers to another state’s land territory. Obtain authority for the actual Bouvet movement. The 2027 Act’s express reference to the dependencies applies from its appointed date. [Prior Excise Regulation, ss 2-7 and 2-8](https://lovdata.no/dokument/SF/forskrift/2001-12-11-1451/%C2%A72-7) · [Prop. 55 L (2025–2026), comments on s 2-6](https://www.regjeringen.no/no/dokumenter/prop.-55-l-20252026/id3154707/?ch=16)

## VAT on goods exported from the mainland

Section 6-21 exempts sales of goods out of the VAT area, subject to the exception for goods within the aggregate settlement under section 4-6. Verify the actual movement and applicable sales and customs procedures. An invoice with a Bouvet address needs evidence of export. [VAT Act, ss 4-6 and 6-21](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/2009-06-19-58)

VAT Regulation section 6-21-1 normally requires the sales document, declaration and export attestation. Where section 5-9-5(1) of the Goods Movement Regulation removes the declaration requirement, keep the sales document and the applicable attestation. The Norwegian Tax Directorate’s statement of 16 October 2026 explains the conditional timing of the sales document, use of pro forma invoices and retention of proof. Check the appropriate branch before invoicing or reporting the exemption. [VAT Regulation, s 6-21-1](https://lovdata.no/dokument/SF/forskrift/2009-12-15-1540/%C2%A76-21-1) · [Goods Movement Regulation, s 5-9-5](https://lovdata.no/dokument/SF/forskrift/2022-10-27-1901/%C2%A75-9-5) · [Export documentation statement](https://www.skatteetaten.no/rettskilder/type/uttalelser/uttalelser/krav-til-dokumentasjon-ved-utforsel-av-varer/)

## Evidence checks

| Fictional facts | Review action |
|---|---|
| A research instrument goes directly to Bouvet without a recorded mainland entry. | Check each actual port and transfer. The destination falls outside the Norwegian VAT and customs areas. Assess any entry or additional authority separately. |
| A mainland supplier invoices goods to Bouvet but has only a destination address. | Obtain evidence of the physical export and the applicable declaration and attestation. Check the section 4-6 exception before applying section 6-21. |
| Equipment travels from Bouvet back to mainland Norway for repair. | Check the entry into the VAT and customs areas and the applicable return or repair procedure. Obtain evidence for any relief and the subsequent movement. |
| Goods move from mainland Norway to Bouvet around the 2026–27 transition. | Identify the event that determines when the excise must be calculated. Check the rules for the relevant date and any transition provision, then verify the goods, evidence and procedure for the export. |

Record the goods, parties, places, dates, border crossings, declarations, sales records, authority and evidence of any special scheme or relief. Hold any payable amount or filing decision that depends on missing facts, rates, documentation or procedures.
