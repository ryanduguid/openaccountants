# Saudi Arabia tax system

> General orientation for 2026. Confirm the taxpayer, accounting period and transaction with a qualified local tax professional before applying these rules.

Amounts are in Saudi riyals (SAR). The Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority (ZATCA) administers the taxes and business zakat described here. Ownership, activity and residence can affect which regime applies. [Saudi Income Tax Law](https://laws.boe.gov.sa/Files/Download/?attId=8b8fa466-1e37-44ca-a876-adbb011146c2) · [ZATCA zakat regulations](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Pages/ZakatRegulations.aspx)

## Residence, income tax and zakat

- **Residence.** For income tax an individual is generally resident if they maintain a permanent residence and spend at least 30 days in the Kingdom during the tax year, or spend at least 183 days there in the tax year. Part-days count, except transit between destinations outside the Kingdom. A company can qualify through Saudi formation or central management in Saudi Arabia. [Income Tax Law, art. 3](https://laws.boe.gov.sa/Files/Download/?attId=8b8fa466-1e37-44ca-a876-adbb011146c2)
- **Ordinary income tax.** The 20% rate generally applies to the taxable base of resident capital companies in respect of non-Saudi ownership, the Saudi-source taxable business income of resident non-Saudi individuals and income attributable to a non-resident's Saudi permanent establishment. Check statutory source and withholding rules for other payments to non-residents, and the separate rules for natural resources and special regimes. [Income Tax Law, arts. 2, 4–7 and 68](https://laws.boe.gov.sa/Files/Download/?attId=8b8fa466-1e37-44ca-a876-adbb011146c2)
- **Business zakat.** Eligible Saudi and qualifying Gulf Cooperation Council ownership generally falls under the separate zakat rules. For mixed ownership, confirm each owner's classification, the corresponding business base and any exceptions. Under the 1445 H implementing regulations the general zakat rate is 2.5% on the zakat base for a Hijri year. A different fiscal year is generally prorated by actual days under article 15. Use the applicable base and year under the regulations. [Zakat implementing regulations, arts. 3, 4 and 15](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/RulesRegulations/Documents/ZAKAT%20COLLECTION.pdf)

## VAT and filing

- **VAT.** The standard rate is 15% from 1 July 2020. Ordinary resident mandatory registration applies above SAR 375,000 in relevant taxable supplies, subject to the applicable look-back and forecast tests. Monthly periods generally apply above SAR 40 million of annual taxable supplies and quarterly periods otherwise. The normal return and payment deadline is the last day of the following month. Check separate non-resident rules and any assigned Fatoora integration date. [ZATCA VAT rate](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/RulesRegulations/VAT/Pages/About-Vat.aspx) · [VAT implementing regulations](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/RulesRegulations/Taxes/Pages/VATImplementingRegulations.aspx) · [ZATCA e-invoicing phases](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/E-Invoicing/Introduction/Pages/Roll-out-phases.aspx)
- **Income tax and zakat filing.** Where required, the ordinary annual income tax and business zakat returns and amounts due are generally submitted within 120 days of the end of the relevant year. Confirm exceptions, including for cessation or information returns, and check VAT and withholding timelines independently. [Income Tax Law, art. 60](https://laws.boe.gov.sa/Files/Download/?attId=8b8fa466-1e37-44ca-a876-adbb011146c2) · [Zakat implementing regulations, art. 102](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/RulesRegulations/Documents/ZAKAT%20COLLECTION.pdf) · [ZATCA 2026 filing calendar](https://www.zatca.gov.sa/en/MediaCenter/Publications/Documents/Zakat%20Declaration%20and%20Tax%20Returns%20Calendar%20%28EN%29.pdf)
