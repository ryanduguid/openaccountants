# Maldives tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the taxpayer, where income arose, residence facts and period. Check the applicable treaty and foreign-tax relief.

## Territorial reach

Ordinary residents are within scope on income from the Maldives and abroad. Temporary residents and nonresidents are within scope on Maldivian-source income. Apply the statutory source and permanent establishment rules and check any relevant treaty. From 31 August 2026, withholding under section 55(b) is final for covered income even where it is earned through a Maldivian permanent establishment. Exclude that income when computing the establishment's taxable income under section 27(c). The establishment cannot deduct that income or any amount attributable to it when working out taxable income. [Income Tax Act, ss 2, 11, 27 and 55](https://mira.gov.mv/Legislations/View/Incometaxact) · [Second amendment and commencement](https://presidency.gov.mv/Press/Article/37277)

## Residence

An individual meets the residence test if their permanent place of living is in the Maldives, they are present or intend to be present there for at least 183 days in any 12-month period starting or ending in the tax year, or they are a Maldivian government employee or official posted abroad in the year. Classify a person meeting that test as temporarily resident if they are a foreigner permitted to stay under the Immigration Act and are unmarried to a Maldivian.

A company qualifies through incorporation, head office, or control and management in the Maldives. A partnership qualifies through formation or control and management in the Maldives. Apply the separate residence definition for trusts. [Income Tax Act, s 79(kk)–(mm)](https://mira.gov.mv/Legislations/View/Incometaxact)

## Taxpayer types

Identify an individual, company, partnership, cooperative society, trust or other body of persons. A partnership pays tax in its own name as if it were a separate legal entity. General partners are jointly and severally liable for the partnership's obligations. Check the deemed-partnership rules for businesses held in common and the rules for trusts and banks. [Income Tax Act, ss 7–9, 36–39 and 79(cc)](https://mira.gov.mv/Legislations/View/Incometaxact) · [Income Tax Regulation](https://www.mira.gov.mv/Legislations/View/Income-Tax-Regulation)

## Currency

Determine the taxpayer's functional and presentation currencies under the Income Tax Regulation. For taxpayers subject to the financial-statement rules, use Maldivian rufiyaa (MVR) as the presentation currency if the functional currency is rufiyaa and US dollars (USD) if it is another currency. Check the separate return, payment and conversion rules before filing. [Income Tax Act, s 41(d)](https://mira.gov.mv/Legislations/View/Incometaxact) · [Income Tax Regulation, ss 60 and 105–108](https://www.mira.gov.mv/Legislations/View/Income-Tax-Regulation)

## Assessment periods

The statutory tax year runs from 1 January to 31 December. Determine the relevant accounting period under the Regulation and match it to the statutory tax year before filing. [Income Tax Act, s 79(c) and (ww)](https://mira.gov.mv/Legislations/View/Incometaxact) · [Income Tax Regulation](https://www.mira.gov.mv/Legislations/View/Income-Tax-Regulation)

## Responsible authorities

The Maldives Inland Revenue Authority (MIRA) and its Commissioner General administer income tax, registration and filing. Use MIRAconnect for applicable returns and the Maldives Customs Service for border matters. [MIRA](https://www.mira.gov.mv/) · [Maldives Customs Service](https://www.customs.gov.mv/)

## Fictional intake

An individual permitted to stay under the Immigration Act lives in the Maldives for 200 days, has no Maldivian spouse and receives interest from a bank overseas. Check their temporary residence and the statutory source of the interest before preparing the return.
