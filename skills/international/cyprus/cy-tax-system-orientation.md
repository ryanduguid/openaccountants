# Cyprus tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the taxpayer type, residence facts, income sources and relevant period. Check any treaty that assigns residence or limits the source country's tax.

## Territorial reach

A Cyprus resident generally accounts for income from Cyprus and abroad. A nonresident accounts for the Cyprus-source items specified in the Income Tax Law. For a nonresident company, identify income attributable to a Cyprus permanent establishment and check the other statutory source categories and any treaty restriction. Decide whether an exemption or another tax applies to each amount. [Income Tax Law, arts 2 and 5](https://www.cylaw.org/nomoi/enop/non-ind/2002_1_118/full.html) · [2026 amendment, arts 2–3](https://cylaw.org/nomoi/arith/2025_1_244.pdf)

## Residence

An individual ordinarily qualifies with more than 183 days in Cyprus in a tax year. The alternative requires at least 60 days in Cyprus, no stay over 183 days in another single country, a permanent home owned or rented in Cyprus and the prescribed Cyprus business, employment or office. Check whether the qualifying activity or office ends during the year. From 2026, the alternative has no separate prohibition on tax residence abroad. A company is resident if its management and control are exercised in Cyprus or it is incorporated there under the Companies Law, subject to the treaty exception in the incorporation rule. A company that moves its registered office or seat to Cyprus is deemed incorporated there for this purpose. Check treaty residence and Special Defence Contribution domicile separately. [2026 amendment, art 2](https://cylaw.org/nomoi/arith/2025_1_244.pdf) · [Commencement, art 23 as corrected](https://www.mof.gov.cy/mof/gpo/gazette.nsf/203DBFC78C778160C2258DAB0020F89D/%24file/5075%2027%202%202026%20PARARTIMA%201o%20MEROS%20I.pdf) · [Tax Department residence and domicile guidance](https://www.gov.cy/mof-tax/en/documents/tax-residency-domicility/)

## Taxpayer types

Individuals generally account for their business or professional income under the individual rules, and companies account for income under the corporate rules. An ordinary partnership calculates its income and each partner includes their entitlement in their own return. Check the separate partnership return and the responsible partner. For an international trust within Law 69(I)/1992, art 12 applies to worldwide trust income and gains where the beneficiary is resident and to Cyprus-source amounts where the beneficiary is nonresident. Confirm that the trust qualifies, and apply the substantive charging provisions to the amounts. [Income Tax Law, arts 2 and 5](https://www.cylaw.org/nomoi/enop/non-ind/2002_1_118/full.html) · [Assessment and Collection of Taxes Law, art 7](https://www.cylaw.org/nomoi/enop/non-ind/1978_1_4/full.html) · [International Trusts Law, art 12](https://www.cylaw.org/nomoi/enop/ind/1992_1_69/section-sc26aeda89-4c70-447a-b82b-f411a9c506f7.html)

## Currency

Cyprus uses the euro (EUR). Prepare ordinary tax amounts in euros and retain the currency, conversion basis and records supporting foreign transactions. Check the applicable accounting rules for any different functional or presentation currency. [Euro adoption regulation](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/en/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32007R0835) · [Tax Department on books and records](https://www.gov.cy/mof-tax/en/documents/maintenance-of-books-and-records/)

## Assessment periods

The income tax year is the 12 months starting on 1 January. Identify the correct year for the personal or company income and check whether the accounting period or a start or cessation affects the reporting work. Check the assessment, filing and payment rules separately for that year and taxpayer. [Income Tax Law, arts 2 and 5](https://www.cylaw.org/nomoi/enop/non-ind/2002_1_118/full.html) · [Assessment and Collection of Taxes Law](https://www.cylaw.org/nomoi/enop/non-ind/1978_1_4/full.html)

## Responsible authorities

The Cyprus Tax Department administers ordinary income tax. Check its current registration and filing instructions for an individual, partnership or company. Check the authorities and rules for customs and Special Defence Contribution separately. [Income Tax Law, art 3](https://www.cylaw.org/nomoi/enop/non-ind/2002_1_118/full.html) · [Tax Department](https://www.gov.cy/mof-tax/en/)

## Fictional intake

An individual spends 75 days in Cyprus and holds a rented home and a continuing office with a Cyprus-resident company. They also qualify as a tax resident elsewhere but have spent fewer than 184 days in any other country. Check the 2026 domestic 60-day conditions, the relevant treaty and their domicile before classifying the receipts.
