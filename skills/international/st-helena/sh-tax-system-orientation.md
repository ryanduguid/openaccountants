# Saint Helena, Ascension and Tristan da Cunha tax system orientation

The SH catalogue entry covers three distinct tax systems. Identify the island before applying its income tax law. The St Helena and Ascension ordinances each provide for a Commissioner of Income Tax. The Tristan da Cunha Administrator collects and assesses income tax and medical contributions. [St Helena Ordinance, s 3][HELENA] · [Ascension Ordinance, s 3][ASCENSION] · [Tristan Order, paras 9–11][TRISTAN]

## St Helena

The tax year runs from 1 April to 31 March. Section 2 includes individual residence tests based on a normal abode with presence exceeding 90 days, aggregate presence exceeding 183 days, or presence to fulfil local employment under a contract of at least 183 days. Check the travel discretion and preceding and following year rules. A body can qualify through St Helena incorporation or through control and management there. The income charge starts with income earned, accrued or derived in or from St Helena. Apply the specific source rules to employment, business, property and disposals. The default tax currency is the St Helena pound (SHP), subject to section 2A. [St Helena Ordinance, ss 2, 2A, 8, 12 and 17–19][HELENA]

The ordinary personal rates are 26% on the first £18,000 of chargeable income and 31% above it. The ordinary company rate is 25%. Different rates apply to categories including qualifying primary production, dividends and capital gains. The published personal allowance is £7,000, with a monthly entitlement rule and a statutory branch for non-resident employment. The Legislative Council reported that the 2026 Bill proposing a higher allowance did not pass. Child Benefit under the specified policy became exempt on 1 November 2026. Check later resolutions and eligible deductions before a calculation. [St Helena Ordinance, ss 6–10 and Schedule 1][HELENA] · [Income Tax Office][HROFFICE] · [20 March 2026 Bill announcement][BILL] · [Legal Notice 20 of 2026][CHILD]

The Tax Office publishes 30 June for self-employed and company returns and 30 September for payment. Employee returns and PAYE follow separate rules. For employee filing, verify the required return, certificate receipt and any special notice under sections 37–39. [St Helena Ordinance, ss 37–42][HELENA] · [Income Tax Office, key dates][HROFFICE]

## Ascension

The year of assessment runs from 1 April to 31 March. The charge follows Ascension-source income, including an office, employment or services performed there regardless of payment location. Part A and Part B of Schedule I use different basis periods. The statutory company definition includes local incorporation or registration and an overseas company that carries on a business or has an office or place of business on Ascension. The unit of currency is the St Helena pound. Specified UK notes and coins are also legal tender. [Ascension Ordinance, ss 2 and 5–7 and Schedule I][ASCENSION] · [Currency (Ascension) Ordinance, ss 2–3][ACURRENCY]

From 1 April 2026, persons other than companies pay 15% on the first £6,000 of chargeable income and 27% on the balance. For years of assessment ending on or after 31 March 2027, the general company rate is 27%, with 20% for the companies named in the notice. A separate business levy has a specified payer schedule for the financial year beginning 1 April 2026. For income tax, section 16 governs notices and returns. Section 28 ordinarily allows 30 days after service of the assessment notice, subject to PAYE and extensions. [Ascension Legal Notice 1 of 2026][ARATES] · [Business Levy Amendment Ordinance 4 of 2026][LEVY] · [Ascension Ordinance, ss 16 and 28–29][ASCENSION]

## Tristan da Cunha

The assessment year is the calendar year and the local currency in use is sterling (GBP). The local Order taxes employment or office income and profits from trade, business, a profession or a vocation arising in or derived from Tristan da Cunha. It defines a resident as someone who is or will be resident for at least four months in the year, and a permanent resident as someone domiciled there and resident for at least five years. These definitions affect the separate medical contribution. [Tristan Order, paras 2–8 and Schedule 1][TRISTAN] · [Tristan da Cunha Tourism, currency][TCURRENCY]

The official 2020 consolidation sets employment rates of 0% up to £1,500, 10% on the next £1,500 and 13% on the excess. The trade or business rate is 10% on the corresponding annual taxable income. Check the distinct 4% medical contribution, exemptions, deduction and any later Administrator determination. Paragraphs 9–10 require provisional monthly instalments within seven days after month end and an annual return within 30 days of year end, with a defined exception for qualifying employment-only cases. Check current local instruments and customs rules with the relevant island authority before a transaction. [Tristan Order, paras 4–10 and Schedules 1–2][TRISTAN] · [Tristan laws index][TINDEX] · [Applied St Helena laws][TAPPLIED]

## Customs and other transaction charges

St Helena's Customs and Excise Ordinance and tariff regulations govern customs and excise duties there. Ascension has its own Customs Ordinance, tariff rules and Collector of Customs. For Tristan da Cunha, the official applied-laws list directs readers to local law and says the St Helena Customs and Excise Ordinance does not apply. Check the local import and export rules and any instrument governing the particular goods. [St Helena Customs and Excise Ordinance, ss 4–5][HCUSTOMS] · [Ascension Customs Ordinance, ss 4–5][ACUSTOMS] · [Applied St Helena laws][TAPPLIED] · [Tristan Export and Import Control Ordinance][TCUSTOMS]
