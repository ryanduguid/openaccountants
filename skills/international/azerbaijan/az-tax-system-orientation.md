# Azerbaijan tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the taxpayer, where income arose, residence facts and period. Check the applicable treaty and foreign-tax relief.

## Territorial reach

A resident individual generally accounts for taxable income from inside and outside Azerbaijan. A resident enterprise generally accounts for taxable profit from all sources. A nonresident generally accounts for Azerbaijani-source income. Determine whether receipts are attributable to a permanent establishment or governed by separate withholding rules. Check any applicable treaty and foreign-tax relief. [Tax Code, arts 13.2.16, 19, 95–97, 103–104, 125 and 127](https://frameworks.e-qanun.az/46/46948.pdf)

## Residence

An individual is generally resident if actually present in Azerbaijan for more than 182 days in total during the calendar year. Azerbaijani state service abroad can also establish residence. Where presence in neither Azerbaijan nor a foreign country exceeds the statutory threshold, check permanent home, centre of vital interests, habitual residence and Azerbaijani citizenship in that order. Check the separate year-of-departure rule if the person is nonresident the following year.

For a legal person, art 13.2.5.3 covers a person formed under Azerbaijani law and conducting business, or a person whose place of management is in Azerbaijan. The management test examines where essential commercial decisions are made and day-to-day management takes place. Check treaty residence separately. [Tax Code, art 13.2.5](https://frameworks.e-qanun.az/46/46948.pdf) · [State Tax Service terminology](https://www.taxes.gov.az/az/page/iqtisadi-terminler-lugeti)

## Taxpayer types

An individual can register as an entrepreneur and conduct business without forming a legal person. Registered general and limited partnerships are commercial organisations under the Civil Code. A legal person generally follows enterprise profit tax under the ordinary regime. Where parties conduct joint business without forming a legal person, compute the joint profit after expenses and allocate taxable profit or income under their agreement. Check the treatment of the participants and the separate VAT rule for the person accounting for the joint activity. [Civil Code, arts 43–44 and 64](https://frameworks.e-qanun.az/46/f_46944.html) · [Tax Code, arts 33, 101, 103–104, 137 and 154.5](https://frameworks.e-qanun.az/46/46948.pdf)

## Currency

The Azerbaijani manat (AZN) is the national currency. For tax accounting of a foreign-currency transaction, use the Central Bank rate on the statutory date and retain the original amounts and conversion evidence. [Central Bank Law, art 36](https://www.cbar.az/law-168/law-no-802-iiq?language=en) · [Tax Code, art 69](https://frameworks.e-qanun.az/46/46948.pdf)

## Assessment periods

Income and profit tax generally follow the calendar tax year. Self-assessed returns are generally due by 31 March of the following year for the taxpayers specified in art 149. Check withholding, current payments and the separate return rules for a taxpayer that ceases or liquidates. [Tax Code, arts 96, 104 and 149–151](https://frameworks.e-qanun.az/46/46948.pdf)

## Responsible authorities

The State Tax Service under the Ministry of Economy administers national taxes and operates taxpayer registration and filing services. Check the State Customs Committee for import customs and tax procedures and the competent municipality for municipal taxes. [Tax Code, arts 4, 6, 8 and 20–23](https://frameworks.e-qanun.az/46/46948.pdf) · [State Tax Service](https://www.taxes.gov.az/en/) · [State Customs Committee](https://customs.gov.az/en/)

## Fictional intake

Two registered entrepreneurs jointly perform an Azerbaijani contract under a written agreement without forming a legal person. Identify which participant accounts for the joint activity for VAT and allocate the taxable income under the agreement for each participant's income-tax computation.
