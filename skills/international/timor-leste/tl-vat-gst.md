# Timor-Leste VAT / GST

## Indirect taxes (no VAT/GST)

Timor-Leste does NOT have a VAT or GST system. Indirect taxation is instead through an import sales tax, a domestic services tax, excise duties, and import duties. These are unverified draft figures.

- **VAT / GST** — Timor-Leste has no VAT and no GST  _(Taxes and Duties Act (Law No. 8/2008), https://taxatlas.io/country/timor-leste/vat-sales-tax)_
- **Sales tax — imported goods** — 2.5% of the sales tax value of taxable goods imported into Timor-Leste  _(Taxes and Duties Act 2008 (Law No. 8/2008), English text published by the Autoridade Tributária Timor-Leste, s 15 and Schedule III — https://attl.gov.tl/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/Taxes_and_Duties_Act_2008_Eng.pdf)_
- **Sales tax — domestic supply of goods/services** — 0% on the sale of taxable goods or the provision of taxable services in Timor-Leste  _(Taxes and Duties Act 2008 (Law No. 8/2008), English text published by the Autoridade Tributária Timor-Leste, s 15 and Schedule III — https://attl.gov.tl/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/Taxes_and_Duties_Act_2008_Eng.pdf)_
- **Services tax — designated services** — 5% of the gross consideration received for designated services by a person whose monthly turnover from those services is USD 500 or more  _(Taxes and Duties Act 2008 (Law No. 8/2008), English text published by the Autoridade Tributária Timor-Leste, s 5 and Schedule I — https://attl.gov.tl/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/Taxes_and_Duties_Act_2008_Eng.pdf)_
- **Services tax — monthly threshold** — USD 500 of monthly turnover from designated services, counting the turnover of associates providing the same type of service; below that the rate is 0%  _(Taxes and Duties Act 2008 (Law No. 8/2008), English text published by the Autoridade Tributária Timor-Leste, Schedule I — https://attl.gov.tl/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/Taxes_and_Duties_Act_2008_Eng.pdf)_
- **Services tax — scope of designated services** — Hotel services, restaurant and bar services (food or beverages for immediate consumption, or catering of prepared food) and telecommunications services  _(Taxes and Duties Act 2008 (Law No. 8/2008), English text published by the Autoridade Tributária Timor-Leste, s 2 (definitions of designated services and restaurant and bar services) — https://attl.gov.tl/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/Taxes_and_Duties_Act_2008_Eng.pdf ; PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/timor-leste/corporate/other-taxes)_
- **Sales tax / services tax filing frequency** — Monthly; the services tax form and the sales tax form, with the tax, are delivered by the 15th day after the end of the calendar month  _(Taxes and Duties Act 2008 (Law No. 8/2008), English text published by the Autoridade Tributária Timor-Leste, ss 9.1 and 18.1 — https://attl.gov.tl/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/Taxes_and_Duties_Act_2008_Eng.pdf)_
- **Reverse charge** — No VAT reverse-charge mechanism exists, as Timor-Leste has no VAT; imported goods are instead subject to import sales tax at the border  _(Taxes and Duties Act (Law No. 8/2008), https://taxatlas.io/country/timor-leste/vat-sales-tax)_
- **Excise tax** — Levied on excisable goods listed in Schedule II (including alcohol, tobacco, fuel and motor vehicles) on removal from a registered manufacturer's premises or on importation; a registered manufacturer files by the 15th day after the end of the month  _(Taxes and Duties Act 2008 (Law No. 8/2008), English text published by the Autoridade Tributária Timor-Leste, ss 10 to 13 and Schedule II — https://attl.gov.tl/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/Taxes_and_Duties_Act_2008_Eng.pdf)_
