# Hungary tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the taxpayer's legal form and residence facts, income sources, place of management, accounts and the period covered. Check any applicable treaty before deciding which country can tax a receipt.

## Territorial reach

A Hungarian-resident individual generally accounts for worldwide income. A nonresident individual accounts for Hungarian-source receipts and receipts Hungary may otherwise tax under a treaty or reciprocity. Hungarian-resident corporate taxpayers generally account for domestic and foreign income. A foreign enterprise accounts for income from business activity at its Hungarian permanent establishment. Check separate corporate branches for specific cases such as certain disposals of interests in Hungarian real-estate holding companies. [Personal Income Tax Act, §§ 2(4)–(5) and 3](https://njt.jog.gov.hu/jogszabaly/1995-117-00-00) · [Corporate Tax and Dividend Tax Act, §§ 1(4), 2 and 3](https://njt.jog.gov.hu/jogszabaly/1996-81-00-00.164)

## Residence

Hungarian citizens generally qualify. A dual citizen with neither a registered home nor a registered place of stay in Hungary falls outside the citizenship limb. A person exercising free movement and the right to stay more than 3 months qualifies on at least 183 days in Hungary in the calendar year, counting arrival and departure days. Separate rules cover stateless people and third-country nationals with long-term stay rights. A holder of the particular status in § 83(1)(e) of Act XC of 2023 is nonresident if present for fewer than 183 days in any 12 months, also counting arrival and departure days.

For people outside the preceding residence categories, check the exclusive permanent home, then the centre of closest personal, family and economic ties if the person has no permanent home or has one in more than one country. Use the habitual abode when that centre cannot be determined. Apply the treaty if both countries claim residence. A company formed under Hungarian law is ordinarily a domestic corporate taxpayer. A foreign person whose place of management is in Hungary is a resident corporate taxpayer. [Personal Income Tax Act, § 3 points 2–3](https://njt.jog.gov.hu/jogszabaly/1995-117-00-00) · [Corporate Tax and Dividend Tax Act, § 2](https://njt.jog.gov.hu/jogszabaly/1996-81-00-00.164)

## Taxpayer types

A sole trader accounts for business income under the individual rules. Limited liability companies (*kft.*), general partnerships (*kkt.*) and limited partnerships (*bt.*) are separate taxpayers under the ordinary corporate tax regime. In a Hungarian business trust (*bizalmi vagyonkezelés*), the assets managed under the trust contract are a corporate tax subject. A trust foundation has its own statutory category. Establish the deed, trustee, settlor and beneficiary rights and the dates of contributions and distributions before applying the separate transaction or recipient rules. [Personal Income Tax Act, §§ 49/A and 65/C](https://njt.jog.gov.hu/jogszabaly/1995-117-00-00) · [Corporate Tax and Dividend Tax Act, § 2](https://njt.jog.gov.hu/jogszabaly/1996-81-00-00.164) · [National Tax and Customs Administration, business associations](https://nav.gov.hu/pfile/file?path=%2Fen%2Ftaxation%2FBooklets%2F41---key-rules-of-the-taxation-of-business-associations)

## Currency

Ordinary income-tax calculation and the corporate income-tax return use the Hungarian forint (HUF). The default annual accounts use HUF. A business can prepare EUR or USD accounts if it records the election in its accounting policy before the first day of the financial year and uses that currency in its constituting document. Other functional accounting currencies require the separate statutory conditions. Check the return conversion and any formal foreign-currency payment election separately. [Personal Income Tax Act, §§ 5 and 6](https://njt.jog.gov.hu/jogszabaly/1995-117-00-00) · [Corporate Tax and Dividend Tax Act, § 5(8)](https://njt.jog.gov.hu/jogszabaly/1996-81-00-00.164) · [Accounting Act, § 20](https://njt.jog.gov.hu/jogszabaly/2000-100-00-00) · [Rules of Taxation Act, § 66/A](https://njt.jog.gov.hu/jogszabaly/2017-150-00-00)

## Assessment periods

Individual income tax ordinarily uses the calendar year. For an ordinary corporate return based on accounts, the tax year follows the financial year. This is normally a calendar year, with a non-calendar year available where the Accounting Act permits it, including eligible business-cycle or parent-reporting cases. The ordinary accounting period is 12 months. Check the separate rules for new entities and changes of year end. [Personal Income Tax Act, §§ 1(6) and 3 point 6](https://njt.jog.gov.hu/jogszabaly/1995-117-00-00) · [Rules of Taxation Act, § 7 point 3](https://njt.jog.gov.hu/jogszabaly/2017-150-00-00) · [Accounting Act, § 11](https://njt.jog.gov.hu/jogszabaly/2000-100-00-00)

## Responsible authorities

The National Tax and Customs Administration (NAV) handles national income taxes and customs. Check each relevant municipality for local business tax. For an ordinary electronic local business tax return, file through NAV, which forwards the return to the relevant municipal tax authority once it passes its checks. [National Tax and Customs Administration](https://nav.gov.hu/en) · [Local Taxes Act, §§ 1, 35 and 42/D](https://njt.jog.gov.hu/jogszabaly/1990-100-00-00)

## Fictional intake

An employee exercising free movement and the right to stay more than 3 months spends 183 days in Hungary in 2026, including arrival and departure days. Record the dates and applicable category, then check domestic and treaty residence. For a separate Hungarian limited partnership, identify the entity's accounting period and any accounting-currency election before preparing its HUF corporate income-tax return.
