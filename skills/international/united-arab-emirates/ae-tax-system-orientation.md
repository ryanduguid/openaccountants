# United Arab Emirates tax system

> General orientation for 2026. Confirm the taxpayer, accounting period and transaction with a qualified local tax professional before applying these rules.

Amounts are in UAE dirhams (AED). The Federal Tax Authority (FTA) administers federal corporate tax and VAT. The corporate tax law generally applies to financial years beginning on or after 1 June 2023. [Ministry of Finance corporate tax overview](https://mof.gov.ae/en/public-finance/tax/corporate-tax-in-the-uae/)

## Residence and corporate tax

- **Individual residence.** Domestic residence can arise through a primary home and centre of financial and personal interests in the UAE, or through at least 183 days of physical presence in the relevant 12 consecutive months. A separate 90-day test in the same kind of period requires UAE or Gulf Cooperation Council nationality or a valid UAE residence permit, and a permanent place of residence or employment or business in the UAE. Check the separate corporate tax and treaty tests. [Cabinet Decision No. 85 of 2022, art. 4](https://tax.gov.ae/en/content/cabinet.decision.no.85.of.2022.on.determination.of.tax.residency.home.aspx)
- **Business income.** A natural person generally enters corporate tax when relevant UAE business turnover exceeds AED 1 million in a calendar year. Wages and qualifying personal investment and real estate investment income are excluded from that business test. UAE-formed or effectively UAE-managed juridical persons are generally resident for corporate tax and include worldwide taxable income. Non-resident persons can be taxed on income attributable to a UAE permanent establishment, UAE-source income or nexus as specified. [Cabinet Decision No. 49 of 2023](https://tax.gov.ae/DataFolder/Files/Legislation/05-06-2023/3/Cabinet%20Decision%20No.%2049%20of%202023%20-%20for%20publishing.pdf) · [Corporate Tax Law, arts. 11–14](https://mof.gov.ae/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Federal-Decree-Law-No.-47-of-2022-and-its-amendments-en-v13.1.26.pdf)
- **Rates and relief.** The ordinary rate is 0% on the first AED 375,000 of taxable income and 9% on the excess. Qualifying Free Zone Persons apply a separate rate structure. Under Small Business Relief, an eligible resident can elect where revenue does not exceed AED 3 million in the relevant and preceding tax periods, subject to conditions and exclusions. Ministerial Decision No. 131 of 2026 extended the AED 3 million threshold to relevant periods ending on or before 31 December 2029. [Corporate Tax Law, arts. 3, 18 and 21](https://mof.gov.ae/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Federal-Decree-Law-No.-47-of-2022-and-its-amendments-en-v13.1.26.pdf) · [Cabinet Decision No. 116 of 2022 threshold announcement](https://mof.gov.ae/en/news/ministry-of-finance-confirms-applicable-taxable-income-threshold-for-corporate-tax-abu-dhabi-uae/) · [Ministerial Decision No. 131 of 2026](https://mof.gov.ae/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Ministerial-Decision-No.-131-of-2026-Amending-Certain-Provisions-of-Ministerial-Decision-No.-73-of-2023-on-Small-Business-Relief-for-the-Purposes-of-Federal-Decree-Law-No.-47-of-2022-EN.pdf)

## VAT and filing

- **VAT.** The standard rate is 5%. For a resident business, registration is generally mandatory when taxable supplies and imports exceed AED 375,000 over the previous 12 months or are expected to exceed it within the next 30 days. Non-resident suppliers have separate registration rules. VAT returns and payments are generally due within 28 days of the end of the assigned period. Check zero-rating, exemptions and current reverse charge rules by supply. [VAT Law, art. 3](https://tax.gov.ae/Datafolder/Files/Legislation/2025/Federal-Decree-Law-No-8-of-2017-and-amendments.pdf) · [FTA VAT registration](https://tax.gov.ae/en/services/vat.registration.aspx) · [FTA VAT return and payment timelines](https://tax.gov.ae/en/taxes/Vat/vat.topics/filing.vat.returns.and.making.payments.aspx)
- **Corporate tax filing.** The ordinary corporate tax return and payment deadline is 9 months from the end of the relevant period. A 31 December 2026 year end generally gives 30 September 2027. Check earlier registration deadlines and whether the FTA has directed another filing date. [Corporate Tax Law, arts. 48, 51 and 53](https://mof.gov.ae/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Federal-Decree-Law-No.-47-of-2022-and-its-amendments-en-v13.1.26.pdf)
