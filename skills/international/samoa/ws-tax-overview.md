# Samoa Tax Overview

This guide covers ordinary national tax in the Independent State of Samoa. Identify the taxpayer, relevant period and source of each income item before choosing a return.

## Residence

An individual can be resident by having a home in Samoa, by 183 days' aggregate presence in any 12-month period starting or ending in the tax year or by being a Samoan citizen who is an officer or employee of the Government or a statutory authority. The first or last day of presence can limit the resident period in a year of arrival or departure, depending on the adjacent tax year's status. [Income Tax Act 2012, s 6][ITA]

## Income and territorial scope

A resident's ordinary assessable income generally includes Samoan and foreign amounts. A non-resident's ordinary assessable income includes only Samoa-source amounts. Section 7 contains separate source rules for wages, business income and other categories. Exempt income and amounts dealt with under the specified special charges are outside ordinary assessable income. Temporary residents may exempt foreign-source income other than salary and wages. That category requires employment in Samoa as the sole or main purpose of residence under a contract of no more than 3 years and excludes Samoan citizens and permanent residents. [Income Tax Act, ss 7 and 14 and Schedule 2 Pt A para (u)][ITA]

## Taxpayer type

A company is resident if incorporated, registered or formed in Samoa or if its central management and control is there. A partnership is resident if formed in Samoa or centrally managed and controlled there. It files a partnership return and partners account for their allocated amounts under ss 48–50. Trustees and beneficiaries apply the trust provisions. [Income Tax Act, ss 2 and 48–53][ITA]

Separate legislation governs international companies and certain international insurance, banking and special purpose entities. An amendment enacted in 2026 removes specified income tax and other direct tax exemptions in those regimes from 1 January 2028. [Miscellaneous (Removal of Tax Exemption for International Companies) Amendment Act 2026, ss 1–6][SIFA]

## Currency

Use Samoan tala (WST). The usual currency conversion is at the Central Bank of Samoa midpoint rate on the date the amount is taken into account. A business can use the tax-year average for the amounts specified in s 76 with prior written approval. [Income Tax Act, s 76][ITA] · [Central Bank of Samoa currency][CBS]

## Assessment periods

The standard tax year is the calendar year. A company carrying on business can adopt a substituted 12-month period with written approval, with a separate transitional period under s 34. Required annual return filing is generally within 3 months after the tax year ends, or 31 March for a December year-end. The Act also prescribes separate provisional tax and withholding procedures. [Income Tax Act, ss 2, 34, 80 and 85–99][ITA]

## Tax authority and headline rates

Use the Ministry's Inland Revenue Services. The ordinary resident individual bands start with nil tax through WST 15,000 and reach 27%. The standard company rate is 27%. The standard Value Added Goods and Services Tax (VAGST) rate is 15%. The Act provides for 2-month filing periods in the ordinary categories and requires return and payment within 15 working days after the period ends, subject to any applicable alternative filing date. The Ministry's published calendar lists the 21st. Check the registered period and applicable due date. [VAGST Act, ss 8, 29 and 41–42][VAGST] · [Ministry rates][RATES] · [Inland Revenue Services][REVENUE]
