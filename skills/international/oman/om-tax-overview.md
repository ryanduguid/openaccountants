# Oman Tax Overview

## Oman tax system at a glance (2025)

> **The decree has been read, and it is not only an Income Inclusion Rule.** This
> guide previously described Royal Decree No. 70/2024 as "a top-up tax (Income
> Inclusion Rule)" with the scope thresholds marked *(approx — confirm)*. The
> decree is published by the Oman Tax Authority itself, on its
> [Income Tax Law & Regulations page](https://tms.taxoman.gov.om/portal/web/taxportal/income-tax-law-regulations),
> as *قانون الضريبة التكميلية على الكيانات التابعة للمجموعات متعددة الجنسيات* —
> the **Top-up Tax Law on Entities Affiliated to Multinational Groups**, Official
> Gazette issue **1578**. Reading it:
>
> - **Naming the Income Inclusion Rule as the mechanism is the error that matters.**
>   Article 5 imposes the tax on **three** classes of payer, and the **first** is
>   the constituent entity *located in Oman* — a domestic charge owing nothing to
>   where the parent sits. The IIR limbs are articles 5(2) and 5(3), and article 8
>   confines them to a low-taxed constituent entity **not located in Oman**. An
>   Omani subsidiary of a foreign-parented group is caught by limb (1) and is not
>   reached by the IIR at all, so a reader told the mechanism is "the Income
>   Inclusion Rule" looks for a parent that need not exist.
> - **The threshold was not approximate.** Article 2 states it in digits and words
>   together — **(٧٥٠٬٠٠٠٬٠٠٠) سبعمائة وخمسين مليون يورو**, EUR 750,000,000 — on
>   the ultimate parent's consolidated statements, in **at least two of the four**
>   preceding financial years, prorated for a year that is not twelve months.
> - **The rate likewise.** Article 4: a rate sufficient to bring the original tax
>   up to **(١٥٪) خمسة عشر في المائة** — fifteen per cent.
> - **The commencement is in the decree, not the Law.** Article 4 of the decree
>   itself: published in the Official Gazette and in force **from 1 January 2025**.
>   It was signed 29 Jumada al-Akhirah 1446, corresponding to **31 December 2024**.
>
> The gazette PDF's text layer is encoded in a legacy Arabic font and extracts as
> Latin gibberish; every figure above was read from the pages rendered at 200 dpi.
> The computation mechanism and safe harbours are **not** in the Law — article 9
> delegates them to an Executive Regulation, which has not been read here.

- **Standard tax year** — Calendar year (1 January – 31 December); a company may apply to use a different accounting period  _(Income Tax Law (Royal Decree No. 28/2009) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/oman/corporate/tax-administration)_
- **Currency** — Omani rial (OMR)  _(Central Bank of Oman)_
- **Tax authority** — Oman Tax Authority (formerly Secretariat General for Taxation)  _(Oman Tax Authority — https://tms.taxoman.gov.om/portal/tax-rate)_
- **Personal income tax (currently)** — None for tax year 2025 — Oman does not currently levy tax on individuals' employment or personal income  _(Personal Income Tax Law (Royal Decree No. 56/2025) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/oman/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **Personal income tax (from 2028)** — 5% on annual gross income above OMR 42,000, effective 1 January 2028 % / OMR  _(Personal Income Tax Law (Royal Decree No. 56/2025) — https://kpmg.com/xx/en/our-insights/gms-flash-alert/flash-alert-2025-122.html)_
- **Headline corporate income tax rate — 15% confirmed from the Law** — **Article 112**: *"The tax stipulated in this Part shall be computed by applying the rate of **15 %** of the taxable income for any establishment, Omani company or permanent establishment for any tax year."* The article was **replaced by RD 9/2017**, which is the amendment that raised the rate and which this consolidation does incorporate %  _([Income Tax Law, art. 112](https://tms.taxoman.gov.om/portal/web/taxportal/income-tax-law-regulations))_
- **VAT / GST** — Yes — VAT applies at a standard rate of 5% (in force since 16 April 2021)  _(VAT Law (Royal Decree No. 121/2020) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/oman/corporate/other-taxes)_
- **Corporate residence / tax basis** — Resident companies are taxed on worldwide income; non-residents are taxed on Oman-source income (including via a permanent establishment)  _(Income Tax Law (Royal Decree No. 28/2009) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/oman/corporate/corporate-residence)_
- **Corporate tax return and payment.** Ordinary taxpayers file within four months under article 140 as replaced by RD 118/2020, measured from the earlier of the tax-year end and the relevant accounting-period end. Qualifying enterprises under Part Five bis retain three months under article 159 bis 18. Tax is due with the return under articles 150 and 159 bis 22. For a 31 December year end, these ordinarily give 30 April and 31 March. RD 118/2020 replaced the old provisional-return provisions; there is no separate ordinary provisional return. The previous rejection of four months was incorrect. [Arabic RD 118/2020 and Income Tax Law](https://tms.taxoman.gov.om/portal/ar/income-tax-law-regulations); [Tax Authority FAQ, question 2](https://tms.taxoman.gov.om/portal/income-tax-faqs). See `om-corporate-income-tax.md` for enterprise eligibility and source limitations.
- **VAT return deadline** — Quarterly — within 30 days of the end of each calendar quarter  _(VAT Law (Royal Decree No. 121/2020) — https://www.flick.network/en-om/oman-vat-registration-compliance-guide)_
- **Top-up tax — scope** — Applies to a constituent entity that is a member of a multinational group whose revenue, per the ultimate parent entity's consolidated financial statements, reaches or exceeds the Omani rial equivalent of **EUR 750,000,000** in **at least two of the four financial years** immediately preceding the year assessed. A financial year longer or shorter than twelve months is prorated  _(Top-up Tax Law, art. 2 (Royal Decree No. 70/2024) — https://tms.taxoman.gov.om/portal/web/taxportal/income-tax-law-regulations)_
- **Top-up tax — rate** — Imposed at the rate needed to bring the original tax on the entity up to **15%**  _(Top-up Tax Law, art. 4 (Royal Decree No. 70/2024))_
- **Top-up tax — who pays** — **Three separate limbs, not one.** (1) the constituent entity **located in Oman**, for any part of the financial year — this is the domestic charge, and it is the limb that reaches an Omani subsidiary of a foreign-parented group; (2) an Omani entity that is the group's **ultimate parent** and holds, directly or indirectly, an interest in a low-taxed constituent entity; (3) an Omani **intermediate parent** holding such an interest. Limbs (2) and (3) bite on low-taxed entities **not located in Oman**  _(Top-up Tax Law, arts. 5 and 8 (Royal Decree No. 70/2024))_
- **Top-up tax — parent limbs stand down** — Article 5 does not apply where a **qualified Income Inclusion Rule** must be applied for the financial year by the ultimate parent, or by another intermediate parent holding a controlling interest in the intermediate parent. A **partially-owned** Omani parent pays its allocable share under article 7 unless it is wholly owned by another partially-owned parent obliged to apply a qualified IIR  _(Top-up Tax Law, arts. 6 and 7 (Royal Decree No. 70/2024))_
- **Top-up tax — commencement** — Royal Decree No. 70/2024 was signed **31 December 2024**, published in Official Gazette issue **1578**, and is in force **from 1 January 2025** (decree, art. 4)  _(Royal Decree No. 70/2024, art. 4)_
- **Top-up tax — excluded entities** — State administrative units and other public legal persons; international organisations; non-profit bodies (associations, federations and public-benefit private bodies); pension funds; an investment fund that is an ultimate parent entity; a real-estate investment entity that is an ultimate parent entity  _(Top-up Tax Law, art. 3 (Royal Decree No. 70/2024))_
- **Top-up tax — left to the Regulation** — The computation mechanism, safe harbours and the treatment of permanent establishments as separate entities are all delegated to the Executive Regulation, which the Chairman of the Tax Authority issues. Where neither the Law nor its Regulation provides, the **Income Tax Law** applies residually  _(Top-up Tax Law, arts. 9–10 (Royal Decree No. 70/2024))_
