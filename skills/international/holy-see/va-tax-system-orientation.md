# Holy See and Vatican City tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Identify the person or entity, employer or payer, activity, territory, period and country whose law governs the obligation. VA is the catalogue label for the Holy See. Vatican City State has its own legal order. Use that State's legislation for its domestic rules and establish the basis for any special treatment outside its territorial boundary. [2026 Fundamental Law, preamble and arts 1, 4 and 25][FUNDAMENTAL] · [Lateran Treaty, arts 2–3 and 13–17][LATERAN]

## Territorial reach

The Administration of the Patrimony of the Apostolic See (APSA) reports that Vatican City State had no income taxes in its 2022 accounts and records that some of its activities abroad were taxable. The Institute for the Works of Religion (IOR) states that Vatican City State had no corporate income tax when explaining its 2025 accounts. Use the dates of these sources when assessing a later income period. Check separately for foreign income tax, pension or health contributions and Vatican administration fees. Vatican law permits supplementary use of Italian legislation subject to formal reception and conditions. [APSA, 2022 annual accounts, p 60][APSA] · [IOR, 2025 annual report, p 63][IOR] · [Law LXXI, arts 1–4][SOURCES] · [Law X, arts 1 and 8 and Table V][FEES]

Article 17 of the Lateran Treaty exempts remuneration owed by the specified Holy See and Catholic Church bodies to the stated worker categories **within Italian territory**. Verify the payer and payment before applying that exemption. The 2015 tax convention with Italy deals separately with information exchange, qualifying financial assets held by specified Italian tax residents and listed properties. It preserves Article 17. [Lateran Treaty, arts 16–17][LATERAN] · [Italy–Holy See tax convention, arts 1–2 and 5–6][ITALY]

## Residence

Vatican civil residence follows authorisation under Law CXXXI. The Governatorate records the basis, whether residence is authorised for a fixed or indefinite period, and any termination. The law distinguishes residence, citizenship and permission to enter. Establish foreign tax residence under the law and treaty relevant to the obligation. A Vatican permit or work location alone cannot resolve Italian tax residence. [Law CXXXI, arts 1–2 and 6–10][RESIDENCE] · [Italy–Holy See tax convention, art 2][ITALY]

## Persons and entities

Identify individuals, covered employers, sole activities and legal entities from their actual capacity and governing documents. Law DL governs legal persons with seats in the State and specified Holy See-related entities within its scope. It excludes named curial bodies, the Governatorate and entities carrying out financial activity professionally. A covered entity acquires Vatican civil legal personality on registration. Identify any foreign company, partnership or trust separately under the law that governs its classification. [Law DL, arts 1–5][ENTITIES]

## Currency and periods

The euro (EUR) is the official currency and euro notes and coins are legal tender. Use the conversion rule under the specific obligation. The IOR's accounts ended 31 December 2025 and are an example of a financial reporting period. They supply no general Vatican income-tax assessment calendar. Establish the event or fee date for a Vatican charge, the entity's own reporting period and any foreign tax year before applying a deadline. The 2025 fee law applies its customs Table V from 1 December 2025. [EU–Vatican City Monetary Agreement, art 1][EUR] · [IOR, 2025 annual report, pp 60–62][IOR] · [Law DL, arts 3 and 8–9][ENTITIES] · [Law X, arts 8 and 11][FEES]

## Responsible authorities

The Governatorate administers Vatican civil residence and legal-person registration. Refer customs and goods movements to its customs and goods transit office and check the current version of `va-customs-fees-reference` and its source limits. Identify the employer or pension fund for pension enrolment and withholding. For Italian tax matters use the competent Italian authority or the applicable representative under the convention. The IOR describes its Italian tax representative arrangements for eligible clients. [Law CXXXI, art 7][RESIDENCE] · [Law DL, arts 4–5][ENTITIES] · [Governatorate organisation chart][GOV] · [Vatican Pension Fund statute, arts 1–3][PENSION] · [Italy–Holy See tax convention, arts 2 and 12][ITALY] · [IOR, 2025 annual report, p 30][IOR]

## Fictional intake

An Italian resident works for a Vatican institution and holds an investment account with the IOR. Verify the named employer and remuneration against Lateran Treaty Article 17. Check pension enrolment from the employment terms. Review the investment account separately under Italian law and the 2015 convention. Establish the relevant periods and documents before deciding whether a filing or withholding obligation applies.
