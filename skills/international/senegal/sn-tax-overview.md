# Senegal Tax Overview

## Senegal tax system at a glance

- **Tax system overview** — Senegal taxes income under the Code Général des Impôts (CGI), administered by the Direction Générale des Impôts et des Domaines (DGID). The tax year is the calendar year and the currency is the West African CFA franc (XOF / FCFA).  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI))_
- **Tax year** — Calendar year (1 January to 31 December): companies declare the preceding year's result by 30 April and individuals declare the preceding year's income before 1 May  _(Code général des impôts (Loi n° 2012-31 as amended), DGID annotated edition of January 2025, Arts 30 and 60 — https://www.dgid.sn/storage/docs/CGI-2025.pdf)_
- **Currency** — West African CFA franc (XOF / FCFA)  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI))_
- **Tax authority** — Direction Générale des Impôts et des Domaines (DGID)  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI) — https://www.impotsetdomaines.gouv.sn/)_
- **Residence basis of taxation** — Individuals with their fiscal domicile in Senegal are taxed on all their income, Senegalese and foreign source; those domiciled abroad are taxed only on Senegalese-source income, subject to double taxation conventions  _(Code général des impôts (Loi n° 2012-31 as amended), DGID annotated edition of January 2025, Art. 48 — https://www.dgid.sn/storage/docs/CGI-2025.pdf)_
- **Top personal income tax (IRPP) rate** — 43% on the band above XOF 50,000,000 (scale: 0% to 630,000; 20% to 1,500,000; 30% to 4,000,000; 35% to 8,000,000; 37% to 13,500,000; 40% to 50,000,000; 43% above), and the tax after the family-quotient reduction may not exceed 43% of taxable income  _(Code général des impôts (Loi 2012-31, annotated consolidation to January 2025, DGID), art. 173(1) and art. 174 — https://www.dgid.sn/storage/docs/CGI-2025.pdf)_
- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 30 percent  _(Code général des impôts (Loi 2012-31, annotated consolidation to January 2025, DGID), art. 36 — https://www.dgid.sn/storage/docs/CGI-2025.pdf)_
- **Does Senegal levy VAT (TVA)?** — Yes — standard TVA rate of 18%  _(Code général des impôts (Loi 2012-31, annotated consolidation to January 2025, DGID), art. 369 — https://www.dgid.sn/storage/docs/CGI-2025.pdf)_
- **Annual personal income tax return deadline** — Before 1 May of the year following the income year, on the regulatory form signed and addressed to the competent tax office  _(Code général des impôts (Loi n° 2012-31 as amended), DGID annotated edition of January 2025, Art. 60 — https://www.dgid.sn/storage/docs/CGI-2025.pdf)_
- **Annual corporate income tax return deadline** — 30 April of the year following the financial year, with the financial statements  _(Code général des impôts (Loi 2012-31, annotated consolidation to January 2025, DGID), art. 30 — https://www.dgid.sn/storage/docs/CGI-2025.pdf)_
