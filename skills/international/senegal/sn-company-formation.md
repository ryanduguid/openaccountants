# Senegal Company Formation & Entity Choice

## Entity types, capital and incorporation

For the **2025** guide period, Senegal's SARL capital rule is sourced to
**Loi n°2015-07 of 9 April 2015**, published in **Journal officiel n°6844 of
18 April 2015**, page 393. Article 1 leaves total capital to the statutes and
article 2 lets the statutes fix the nominal share amounts freely. Article 3
repeals and replaces Loi n°2014-20, which had set the earlier minimum.
[Ministry of Justice Gazette copy, articles 1 to 3](https://justice.sec.gouv.sn/wp-content/uploads/textes-reglements/OHADA/Loi-n-2015-07-09-du-avril-2015-reglementation-capital-SARL-JO.pdf)

The revised OHADA AUSCGIE took effect on **5 May 2014**. A SARL may be
formed by a single natural or legal person under article 309. An SA may have a
single shareholder under article 385. [OHADA register](https://www.ohada.org/droit-des-societes-commerciales-et-du-gie/),
[AUSCGIE, articles 309 and 385](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974)

- **SARL subscription and pay-up.** Shares must be fully subscribed.
  Contributions in kind must be fully paid. Cash shares must be paid at least
  half on subscription, with the balance within **2 years of RCCM registration**.
  [AUSCGIE, article 311-1](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974#page=77)
- **Ordinary SA capital and pay-up.** Article 387 sets an ordinary minimum of
  **CFA 10,000,000**, with whole-number nominal share values freely fixed in the
  statutes. Capital must be fully subscribed before the statutes are signed.
  Cash shares must be paid at least one-quarter on subscription and the balance
  within **3 years of RCCM registration**. Article 824 sets **CFA 100,000,000**
  for securities listed on a member state's exchange or publicly offered for
  placement in one or more member states. Regulated activities require separate
  capital checks. [AUSCGIE, articles 387 to 389 and 824](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974)

- **Governing company law** — OHADA Uniform Act on Commercial Companies and Economic Interest Groups (AUSCGIE)  _(OHADA Uniform Act on Commercial Companies (AUSCGIE) — https://www.legal500.com/doing-business-in/senegal/)_
- **Common entity types** — SARL (Société à Responsabilité Limitée), SA (Société Anonyme), SAS (Société par Actions Simplifiée)  _(OHADA Uniform Act on Commercial Companies (AUSCGIE) — https://www.legal500.com/doing-business-in/senegal/)_
- **SARL capital.** The statutes fix total capital and nominal share amounts under Loi n°2015-07, articles 1 and 2. [Official Gazette, 18 April 2015](https://justice.sec.gouv.sn/wp-content/uploads/textes-reglements/OHADA/Loi-n-2015-07-09-du-avril-2015-reglementation-capital-SARL-JO.pdf)
- **SA minimum share capital** — **10,000,000**, with the quarter correctly stated: cash shares are paid up **at least one quarter** on subscription, the capital must be **entirely subscribed before the statutes are signed**, and the balance falls due within **three years** of RCCM registration. **100,000,000** where the securities are listed or publicly offered in a member state XOF  _(AUSCGIE 2014, arts. 387–389 and 824 — https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974)_
- **Incorporation channel** — Registration via the APIX one-stop shop (guichet unique), issuing RCCM (commercial registry) and NINEA (tax ID)  _(APIX one-stop shop (guichet unique) — https://www.legal500.com/doing-business-in/senegal/)_
- **Core incorporation steps** — 1) Reserve trade name with RCCM/CFE; 2) Draft statutes (notarized where required); 3) Deposit capital and obtain bank certificate where required; 4) File at APIX for RCCM + NINEA and declare existence to the tax office  _(OHADA Uniform Act on Commercial Companies (AUSCGIE) — https://www.legal500.com/doing-business-in/senegal/)_
- **Incorporation timeline** — Around 2 days via the APIX one-stop shop (approx — confirm current processing time)  _(APIX one-stop shop (guichet unique) — https://www.legal500.com/doing-business-in/senegal/)_
- **Tax identification** — NINEA (Numéro d'Identification Nationale des Entreprises et Associations) issued at registration  _(Direction Générale des Impôts et des Domaines (DGID) — https://www.legal500.com/doing-business-in/senegal/)_
- **Mandatory registrations at formation** — Employer registration with CSS (social security) and IPRES (pension) alongside RCCM and NINEA  _(Code de la Sécurité Sociale / IPRES — https://www.legal500.com/doing-business-in/senegal/)_
- **Annual accounts (SYSCOHADA)** — Prepare annual financial statements under the SYSCOHADA chart of accounts and file with the CIT return by 30 April  _(OHADA / SYSCOHADA accounting framework, as reported `[RESEARCH GAP — the statute's text was not read for this change; the secondary summary that carried the figure is no longer cited]`)_
- **Core annual tax compliance** — Annual corporate income tax return and financial statements by 30 April (Arts 30 and 31); monthly VAT and payroll filings; instalments within the first 15 days of February and by 30 April, with the balance by 15 June (Arts 213 to 215)  _(Code général des impôts (Loi n° 2012-31 as amended), DGID annotated edition of January 2025, Arts 30, 31 and 213 to 215 — https://www.dgid.sn/storage/docs/CGI-2025.pdf)_

## National SARL capital example

For the capital rule alone, members who set **20 shares at CFA 1,000 each**
have **CFA 20,000** total capital. Articles 1 and 2 leave both the total capital
and the nominal share amount to the statutes. Subscription, pay-up and other
formation obligations still apply. [Loi n°2015-07, articles 1 and 2](https://justice.sec.gouv.sn/wp-content/uploads/textes-reglements/OHADA/Loi-n-2015-07-09-du-avril-2015-reglementation-capital-SARL-JO.pdf)

## Annual company reporting under the 2014 AUSCGIE

The revised AUSCGIE took effect on **5 May 2014**. The following rules address
annual SARL and SA decisions and commercial-company registry deposits.
[OHADA company law register](https://www.ohada.org/droit-des-societes-commerciales-et-du-gie/)

- **SARL annual meeting.** The annual ordinary general meeting must take place
  within **6 months after the financial year closes**. The managers may ask the
  competent court for an extension. [AUSCGIE, articles 347 and 348](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974#page=86)
- **SA annual meeting.** The ordinary general meeting must take place at least
  once a year, within **6 months after the financial year closes**, subject to
  an extension by court decision. [AUSCGIE, article 548](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974#page=133)
- **Sole-member companies.** The sole shareholder of an SA takes the annual
  ordinary decisions within **6 months after the financial year closes** and
  records them in minutes kept in the company's archives. Article 347 applies
  these sole-member decision rules to a SARL, with the exceptions it specifies.
  [AUSCGIE, article 347](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974#page=86),
  [articles 558 to 560](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974#page=135)
- **Deposit of annual accounts.** A commercial company must deposit the prior
  year's annual financial statements with the RCCM in the state of its
  registered office **within the month following approval** by the competent
  body. If approval is refused, a copy of the refusal decision must be deposited
  within the same period. [AUSCGIE, article 269, pages 65 and 66](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974#page=65)

For example, for a multi-member SARL with a **31 December 2025** year-end, the
ordinary annual meeting is due by **30 June 2026**, subject to a court extension.
Track the RCCM deposit deadline from the actual approval decision. Record the
national tax-return deadline separately. The current registry filing method,
fees, applicable accounting and audit rules and any sector-specific obligations
still need verification. [AUSCGIE, articles 269 and 348](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974)
