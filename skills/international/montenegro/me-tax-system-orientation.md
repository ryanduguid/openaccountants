# Montenegro tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the taxpayer, where income arose, residence facts and period. Check the applicable treaty and foreign-tax relief.

## Territorial reach

Montenegro taxes resident individuals on income from Montenegro and abroad and nonresident individuals on income earned in Montenegro. Resident legal entities are taxed on profits from Montenegro and abroad. For a nonresident legal entity, identify the profits of a Montenegrin permanent establishment. Where there is no permanent establishment, check the separate charge for receipts specified in art 29. Apply the domestic source and permanent establishment rules and the relevant treaty. [Personal Income Tax Law, art 4](https://wapi.gov.me/download/b8465a42-2073-4894-9be0-3b965c2f7195?version=1.0) · [Corporate Income Tax Law, arts 3–5 and 29](https://wapi.gov.me/download/8826962a-3806-4619-afa4-0029b0a57afb?version=1.0)

## Residence

An individual is resident if they have their residence or centre of business and life interests in Montenegro, or spend more than 183 days there in the tax year. Check the separate rule for individuals posted abroad to work for a Montenegrin resident or an international organisation. A legal entity is resident if formed in Montenegro or if its place of actual management and control is there. Where another jurisdiction claims residence, make a separate treaty determination. [Personal Income Tax Law, art 3](https://wapi.gov.me/download/b8465a42-2073-4894-9be0-3b965c2f7195?version=1.0) · [Corporate Income Tax Law, art 3](https://wapi.gov.me/download/8826962a-3806-4619-afa4-0029b0a57afb?version=1.0)

## Taxpayer types

Apply the personal income tax rules to individual business income. Distinguish principal self-employment, supplementary entrepreneurial activity and occasional self-employment. Check whether a flat-rate method is available for qualifying principal activity. Apply the corporate rules to profit-making legal entities, including general and limited partnerships that acquire legal personality on registration. The exempt bodies listed in the law can become taxpayers for their profit-making activities. Check the classification of any foreign arrangement and the rules for payments to owners or beneficiaries. [Personal Income Tax Law, arts 16–17 and 49](https://wapi.gov.me/download/b8465a42-2073-4894-9be0-3b965c2f7195?version=1.0) · [Corporate Income Tax Law, arts 2, 6 and 29](https://wapi.gov.me/download/8826962a-3806-4619-afa4-0029b0a57afb?version=1.0) · [Companies Law, arts 2 and 6](https://www.sluzbenilist.me/propisi/384356/download)

## Currency

Montenegro uses the euro (EUR) as its official means of payment. Retain source-currency amounts and exchange-rate evidence for figures reported in euros. [Central Bank of Montenegro, money in circulation](https://www.cbcg.me/en/currency/money-in-circulation)

## Assessment periods

The personal income tax period is normally the calendar year. Check the shortened-period rules where an activity starts or ceases during the year. The corporate period is the financial year, ordinarily the calendar year. Check the exceptions for liquidation, reorganisation or commencement during the year. Determine the applicable filing and payment rules separately. [Personal Income Tax Law, arts 7 and 43](https://wapi.gov.me/download/b8465a42-2073-4894-9be0-3b965c2f7195?version=1.0) · [Corporate Income Tax Law, arts 39–40](https://wapi.gov.me/download/8826962a-3806-4619-afa4-0029b0a57afb?version=1.0)

## Responsible authorities

The Tax Administration (Poreska uprava) administers the national income taxes. Use its IRMS portal guidance for returns and check the current prescribed form. Consult the competent local administration for local taxes and any applicable personal tax surcharge, including whether collection is assigned to the national authority. [Tax Administration Law, arts 5–6](https://www.gov.me/dokumenta/6bf4fd6a-fa32-4c33-9f5a-3a13ae509233) · [Tax Administration](https://www.gov.me/poreskauprava) · [IRMS portal](https://irms.tax.gov.me/public/) · [Annual personal return rules, Gazette 167/2026](https://www.sluzbenilist.me/propisi/399989)

## Fictional intake

An individual spends exactly 183 days in Montenegro during the calendar year and runs a consulting business there. Test their residence and centre of interests separately from the day count. If they work through a company, check where it was formed and where its actual management and control sit before classifying its receipts.
