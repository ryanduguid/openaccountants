# Portugal tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record dates of presence and overnight stays, habitual housing, the entity's seat and effective management, each income source, the legal form and the accounting period.

## Territorial reach

Portugal generally taxes individuals resident there on worldwide income for their resident period and nonresidents on Portuguese-source income. Apply the source rules to each receipt, including during a nonresident part of a split year. Companies with their seat or effective management in Portugal generally account for worldwide income. For companies without either, identify Portuguese-source income and separate income attributable to a Portuguese permanent establishment from other domestic-source amounts. Check domestic relief and the applicable treaty. The Azores and Madeira have regional rules within the Portuguese system. [Personal Income Tax Code, arts 15–18](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/Cod_download/Documents/cirs.pdf) · [Corporate Income Tax Code, art 4](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/CIRC_2R/Pages/irc4.aspx)

## Residence

An individual normally qualifies by spending more than 183 days in Portugal in any 12-month period beginning or ending in the year, or by having a dwelling during that period in circumstances indicating an intention to keep and occupy it as a habitual home after a shorter stay. Count days that include an overnight stay. Determine the start and end of residence and test the exceptions in art 16 before splitting the year. For corporate income tax, a seat or effective management in Portuguese territory is sufficient for domestic residence. If another state also claims residence, apply the treaty separately. [Personal Income Tax Code, art 16](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/Cod_download/Documents/cirs.pdf) · [Corporate Income Tax Code, art 2](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/CIRC_2R/Pages/irc2.aspx)

## Taxpayer types

A sole trader generally reports business or professional income under the individual rules. A Portuguese limited company generally pays corporate income tax. Check art 6 for tax transparency before allocating income from a partnership-like entity. The taxable base of qualifying civil, professional and simple asset-management companies is attributed to members even without a distribution.

For a fiduciary structure, distinguish ordinary distributions, a disposal of rights and amounts on winding up, revocation or termination. An ordinary payment generally falls within investment income unless already attributed under art 20(3). On winding up, revocation or termination the settlor and other beneficiaries fall under different rules. A value assigned to the settlor can fall under the capital gains rules. A value assigned to a beneficiary who did not establish the structure is outside personal income tax under art 12(8), so check stamp duty separately. [Personal Income Tax Code, arts 3, 5(2)(t), 10(1)(b)(3) and (j), 12(8) and 20](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/Cod_download/Documents/cirs.pdf) · [Corporate Income Tax Code, arts 2 and 6](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/CIRC_2R/Pages/irc6.aspx) · [Tax and Customs Authority, binding information 24009](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/informacoes_vinculativas/rendimento/cirs/Documents/PIV_24009.pdf)

## Currency

Portugal uses the euro (EUR). Apply art 23 to foreign-currency personal income and expenses. It prescribes the official buying or selling rate and the applicable date according to the kind of amount and how it was paid. Accounting-based income follows the applicable accounting rules. Preserve the transaction dates, rates and source documents. [Personal Income Tax Code, art 23](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/cirs_rep/Pages/irs23.aspx) · [Accounting Standards Commission, NCRF 23](https://www.cnc.gov.pt/pdf/snc/2016/normas%20com%20retifica%C3%A7%C3%A3o/NCRF_23.pdf)

## Assessment periods

The ordinary individual and corporate tax period is the calendar year. Eligible resident entities and nonresident entities with a Portuguese permanent establishment can adopt a different annual period that matches their reporting period. The choice normally applies for at least the next 5 tax periods. Check the exception for entering a consolidated group with a different period and the rules for commencement, cessation and liquidation. [Personal Income Tax Code, art 143](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/Cod_download/Documents/cirs.pdf) · [Corporate Income Tax Code, art 8](https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/informacao_fiscal/codigos_tributarios/CIRC_2R/Pages/irc8.aspx)

## Responsible authorities

The Tax and Customs Authority (AT) administers national taxation and customs and provides filing services through Portal das Finanças. Check any regional adjustments and the municipal corporate surtax for the place where the activity takes place. [Tax and Customs Authority](https://www.gov.pt/entidades/autoridade-tributaria-e-aduaneira) · [Local Finance Act, art 18](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/legislacao-consolidada/lei/2013-105795409-131574593)

## Fictional intake

A worker moves to Porto during the year, continues to receive foreign fees and holds a share in a Portuguese professional company. Establish the resident and nonresident periods, source each fee, check the company's transparency conditions and obtain its allocation before preparing the return.
