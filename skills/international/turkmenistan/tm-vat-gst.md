# Turkmenistan VAT / GST

## Turkmenistan VAT / GST

## Value-added tax (VAT)

Turkmenistan levies VAT under the Tax Code of Turkmenistan at a standard rate of 15%, with a 0% rate for qualifying exports and international transport. VAT is administered and remitted to the State Tax Service.

> **The Tax Code has been read, and the two rate rows were right.** The Tax
> Directorate publishes the ***«Salgytlar hakynda» Türkmenistanyň bitewi Kanuny***
> (Unified Law of Turkmenistan "On Taxes") at
> [tax.gov.tm](https://tax.gov.tm/hukuknamalar). Article 103 confirms **15%** and the
> **0%** band, and article 105 confirms that **oil and gas are carved out of the export
> zero rate** — a claim this guide carried on a commercial source and which the statute
> bears out. What article 105 adds is **eight further zero-rated limbs** the guide did
> not list, and a definition of international carriage that excludes a case most
> readers would assume is inside it.
>
> _([«Salgytlar hakynda» Türkmenistanyň bitewi Kanuny, arts. 103, 105](https://tax.gov.tm/pdf/ckeditor_assets/attachments/10/2.pdf))_

- **Standard VAT rate** — **15 percent**. Article 103 sets the rate at **0%** for the transactions listed in article 105 and at **15%** for all other taxable transactions. Where goods, works or services are supplied at **state-fixed prices**, the applicable figure is the rate expressed as a percentage of 100 **increased by** the rate; the same computed figure applies where the tax authority determines tax under article 36 by reference to actual market prices inclusive of VAT percent  _([Bitewi Kanun, art. 103](https://tax.gov.tm/pdf/ckeditor_assets/attachments/10/2.pdf))_
- **Zero rate (0%) — exports of goods, but not hydrocarbons** — Article 105 zero-rates exports of own-produced and purchased goods, ***excluding natural (associated) gas, oil, and products obtained from processing them***. For this purpose goods are "exported" only where there is **confirmation in the established order that they crossed the Turkmen customs border** percent  _([Bitewi Kanun, art. 105](https://tax.gov.tm/pdf/ckeditor_assets/attachments/10/2.pdf))_
- **Oil and gas exports** — Excluded from the export zero rate by article 105's own words. **One route back in:** products obtained from processing **crude oil and gas condensate purchased from non-residents** of Turkmenistan are zero-rated on export, on the **mandatory condition** that the export is carried out **directly by the Turkmen resident legal persons who perform the processing**  _([Bitewi Kanun, art. 105](https://tax.gov.tm/pdf/ckeditor_assets/attachments/10/2.pdf))_
- **⚠ International carriage — leaving the country is not enough** — Article 105 zero-rates international carriage of passengers and transport of goods for individuals and legal persons, then qualifies it: where the **point of departure and the destination are both within Turkmenistan**, the carriage is ***not*** international carriage **even if the vehicle travels outside Turkmen territory** en route. A domestic journey routed through a neighbouring state stays standard-rated percent  _([Bitewi Kanun, art. 105](https://tax.gov.tm/pdf/ckeditor_assets/attachments/10/2.pdf))_
- **Zero rate — textiles sold to exporters by their producers** — Article 105 zero-rates **textile products sold by the producers themselves** to Turkmen-resident private entrepreneurs and legal persons **who carry out the direct export** of those goods, again subject to customs-border confirmation. This is a relief at the stage **before** export and is absent from this guide percent  _([Bitewi Kanun, art. 105](https://tax.gov.tm/pdf/ckeditor_assets/attachments/10/2.pdf))_
- **Zero rate — the remaining limbs** — Article 105 also zero-rates: transport, loading, unloading and transhipment services for goods dispatched abroad, and **transit of foreign cargo** through Turkmenistan; **refuelling and servicing foreign sea and air vessels** used for international carriage (including aeronavigation and take-off/landing services) and sale of goods for consumption by their crew and passengers; sale of goods placed under the customs regime **in a duty-free shop**; goods, works and services for the **official use of foreign diplomatic and equivalent missions and international organisations**; supplies **paid out of the Government's foreign-currency reserve fund**; and supplies by contractors and subcontractors under the Law *"On Hydrocarbon Resources"* percent  _([Bitewi Kanun, art. 105](https://tax.gov.tm/pdf/ckeditor_assets/attachments/10/2.pdf))_
- **Taxable transactions** — Supply of goods and services within Turkmenistan and the import of goods  _(Tax Code of Turkmenistan)_
- **VAT registration threshold** — Registration obligations apply to taxpayers carrying out taxable supplies; a precise turnover threshold must be confirmed against the Tax Code ((approx — confirm threshold))  _(Tax Code of Turkmenistan)_
- **Exempt supplies** — Certain financial, educational, medical and other socially-oriented supplies are exempt ((approx — confirm list))  _(Tax Code of Turkmenistan)_
- **VAT filing/payment frequency** — Monthly VAT returns and payment, due in the month following the reporting period ((approx — confirm exact day))  _(Tax Code of Turkmenistan)_
- **Reverse charge on imported services** — VAT on services supplied by non-residents is generally accounted for by the Turkmen recipient under a reverse-charge / withholding mechanism ((approx — confirm))  _(Tax Code of Turkmenistan)_
- **Input VAT recovery** — Registered taxpayers may offset input VAT on business purchases against output VAT  _(Tax Code of Turkmenistan)_
