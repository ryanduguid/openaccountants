# Turkmenistan Payroll & Social Contributions

## Payroll, social insurance and pension contributions

The [State Pension Insurance Law](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-law/218?lang=ru), published by Parliament, sets the pension rates, contribution bases and deadlines below. The [Tax Code](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-code/10?lang=ru) governs withholding and the personal deduction. These are separate obligations.

| Obligation | Rule | Provision |
|---|---|---|
| Employer compulsory pension | 20% for covered employers, including registered foreign branches and individual entrepreneurs employing staff | Pension Law arts. 6(1), 20(1)(1) |
| Additional compulsory professional pension | 3.5% on the covered remuneration of eligible military, aviation and full-time hazardous or especially arduous workers | Pension Law arts. 6(2), 7(4), 19(2), 20(2) |
| Voluntary pension | Minimum 2% of the applicable remuneration base for voluntary participants; this provision does not impose a universal employee deduction | Pension Law arts. 7(6), 19(3), 20(4) |
| Contribution base | Article 19 includes remuneration in cash and kind and specifies exclusions. It states no upper wage ceiling for ordinary employees; other insured categories have different bases | Pension Law art. 19 |
| Employer pension payment | Monthly, no later than the day cash is obtained from the bank for wages or wages are transferred to employees' bank accounts | Pension Law art. 23(3) |
| Employer pension declaration | By the 20th of the following month for the employers covered by article 21(2), filed with the local Pension Fund | Pension Law art. 21(2) |
| Income-tax withholding | Ordinarily 10%; calculated cumulatively each month and withheld at each payment, subject to statutory exemptions and reliefs | Tax Code arts. 187, 192(1), 195(3)–(4) |
| Standard personal deduction | One statutory base value for calculating taxes and fees per month. The current monetary value has not been verified; the former TMT 1,280 figure is unsupported by article 188 | Tax Code art. 188(1)(a) |
| Deduction in payroll | The main employer applies article 188 deductions to contractual employment remuneration, except the first incomplete month of work. Voluntary pension and medical insurance contributions are also deductible under article 188(2) | Tax Code arts. 188(2)–(4), 195(3) |
| Withholding payment | By the day bank cash is obtained or income is transferred; for income received in kind, by the following day | Tax Code art. 195(5) |
| Annual withholding information | By 20 January following the tax year, submitted to the tax authority under the approved form | Tax Code art. 195(9) |

Pension coverage in article 7(1) includes citizens and foreign citizens or stateless persons permanently resident in Turkmenistan. Do not infer identical pension treatment for every foreign worker from tax residency alone.

The 10% income-tax rate has specific reliefs. Article 187(5), for example, reduces calculated tax for eligible young graduates entering employment by 50% for the first 12 months and 25% for the next 24 months, with supporting documents required. Article 192(1) separately provides relief for qualifying youth entrepreneurs with at least 75% of workers under 35. Check eligibility before applying either relief.

The earlier absence of the Pension Insurance Law from the Tax Directorate's page did not establish that the law was unavailable from Parliament. The rates are now supported by that primary publication. The current tax base value remains a research gap.
