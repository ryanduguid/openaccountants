# Turkmenistan Company Formation & Entity Choice

## Entity types, capital and incorporation

> **The Law on Enterprises has been read, and the minimum capital is a formula, not a
> sum.** The Tax Directorate publishes ***Kärhanalar hakynda Türkmenistanyň kanuny***
> (Law of Turkmenistan on Enterprises, Mejlis Records 2000 No. 2 art. 13, as amended
> through 2015 and later) at [tax.gov.tm](https://tax.gov.tm/hukuknamalar) — 26 pages,
> clean text. Every row below was sourced to two consultancy sites.
>
> **Minimum charter capital is expressed as a multiple of the statutory minimum wage**,
> so every fixed figure in TMT or USD is wrong in kind rather than merely out of date,
> and the requirement re-bases whenever the minimum wage changes.
>
> _([Kärhanalar hakynda Türkmenistanyň kanuny, arts. 11, 16, 24, 26, 29, 30](https://tax.gov.tm/pdf/ckeditor_assets/attachments/13/5.pdf))_

- **⚠ "LLC" is not one of the statutory forms** — Article 24(1) lists **seven** organisational-legal forms for enterprises: **döwlet kärhanasy** (state enterprise), **ýekebara kärhana** (sole enterprise), **hyzmatdaşlyk kärhanasy** (partnership enterprise), **bilelikdäki kärhana** (joint venture), **jemgyýetçilik guramasynyň kärhanasy** (enterprise of a public organisation), **hojalyk şereketi** (economic company) and **paýdarlar jemgyýeti** (joint-stock company). There is no *jogapkärçiligi çäkli jemgyýet* among them. Article 24(2) allows other forms to be provided for by legislation, so the guide's "LLC" may exist under the **Civil Code**, which was **not read** — but it is not a form of the Law on Enterprises, and the nearest statutory vehicle is the *hojalyk şereketi* below  _([Law on Enterprises, art. 24](https://tax.gov.tm/pdf/ckeditor_assets/attachments/13/5.pdf))_
- **Hojalyk şereketi (economic company) — the limited-liability vehicle, and it takes two people** — Article 29: an association of **two or more** natural and/or legal persons for joint activity, formed under a contract among the founders, its property made up of participants' shares in the charter capital, its supreme body the general meeting. **Article 29(6)** allows two liability models: participants **jointly and severally liable with all their property**, or participants liable **only up to their contributions (shares) in the charter capital**. The limited-liability form is therefore a variant to be chosen and stated, not a default  _([Law on Enterprises, art. 29](https://tax.gov.tm/pdf/ckeditor_assets/attachments/13/5.pdf))_
- **⚠ Minimum charter capital — hojalyk şereketi: 100 × the statutory minimum wage** — Article 29(4): the minimum charter capital *"must consist of 100 times the minimum wage established in Turkmenistan"*. Not a TMT figure. This guide gave *"commonly cited around 5,000 TMT, with substantially higher minimums (e.g. USD 20,000) reported for foreign-invested entities"*, which is the wrong shape of answer — the statute indexes the requirement to the minimum wage  _([Law on Enterprises, art. 29(4)](https://tax.gov.tm/pdf/ckeditor_assets/attachments/13/5.pdf))_
- **⚠ Minimum charter capital — ýekebara kärhana: 25 × the statutory minimum wage** — Article 26(5) sets a quarter of the economic company's requirement for a **sole enterprise**, which belongs to **one natural person** and whose founding document is a charter approved by the owner. The owner manages it but **may delegate conduct of its affairs by contract** to a manager or executive director — and **bears full property liability** for its obligations (art. 26(7)). A one-person vehicle with a lower capital floor and unlimited personal liability is the trade-off this guide did not present  _([Law on Enterprises, art. 26](https://tax.gov.tm/pdf/ckeditor_assets/attachments/13/5.pdf))_
- **Joint-stock companies are governed by a different statute** — Article 30 does not regulate them: it defers the formation and operation of a *paýdarlar jemgyýeti* entirely to the ***Law of Turkmenistan "On Joint-Stock Companies"***, which was **not read**. Any joint-stock capital or governance figure must come from that Law  _([Law on Enterprises, art. 30](https://tax.gov.tm/pdf/ckeditor_assets/attachments/13/5.pdf))_
- **Foreign founders are permitted by the Law itself** — Article 11(2): founders may be legally capable natural and legal persons, ***including such persons of foreign states***. This supports the "no general cap on foreign ownership" row below with a provision rather than a consultancy page. Article 11(1): an enterprise is created by decision of its founder or founders; 11(3) allows creation by spin-off or division of an existing enterprise  _([Law on Enterprises, art. 11](https://tax.gov.tm/pdf/ckeditor_assets/attachments/13/5.pdf))_
- **Registration — a decision within two weeks** — Article 16: the decision to register the enterprise, **or to refuse registration**, is taken **within two weeks** (*iki hepdäniň dowamynda*) of the founders submitting the necessary documents. The Law also governs re-registration (art. 21), cancellation of registration (art. 22), the registration fee (art. 20) and registration of branches and representative offices (art. 23)  _([Law on Enterprises, arts. 16, 20–23](https://tax.gov.tm/pdf/ckeditor_assets/attachments/13/5.pdf))_
- **Limited Liability Company (LLC)** — Most common vehicle for trading, services and local operations; liability limited to capital contributions. ⚠ See the statutory-forms row above — this description does not correspond to a form named in the Law on Enterprises  _(Civil Code of Turkmenistan / Law on Enterprises — https://www.healyconsultants.com/turkmenistan-company-registration/setup-llc/)_
- **Joint-stock company (open/closed)** — Used for larger capital-raising or share offerings  _(Law of Turkmenistan on Joint-Stock Companies)_
- **Branch / representative office** — Liaison, marketing or operation under a foreign parent; not a separate legal entity  _(Civil Code of Turkmenistan)_
- **Minimum shareholders (LLC)** — At least 2 shareholders (individuals or corporate entities) ((approx — confirm; single-member may be permitted))  _(Law on Enterprises of Turkmenistan — https://chandrawatpartners.com/llc-incorporation-in-turkmenistan-a-practical-professional-overview-for-investors-across-the-world/)_
- **Minimum directors (LLC)** — At least 1 director; need not be a Turkmenistan national or resident  _(Law on Enterprises of Turkmenistan — https://chandrawatpartners.com/llc-incorporation-in-turkmenistan-a-practical-professional-overview-for-investors-across-the-world/)_
- **Minimum share capital (LLC)** — Varies by source and sector; commonly cited around 5,000 TMT, with substantially higher minimums (e.g. USD 20,000) reported for foreign-invested entities ((approx — confirm against current rules))  _(Law on Enterprises of Turkmenistan — https://www.healyconsultants.com/turkmenistan-company-registration/setup-llc/)_
- **Capital payment schedule** — Half of share capital paid up initially, balance within one year of registration ((approx — confirm))  _(Law on Enterprises of Turkmenistan — https://chandrawatpartners.com/llc-incorporation-in-turkmenistan-a-practical-professional-overview-for-investors-across-the-world/)_
- **Incorporation step 1** — Reserve a company name with the Ministry of Finance and Economy  _(Ministry of Finance and Economy of Turkmenistan)_
- **Incorporation step 2** — Prepare the company charter and obtain notarized copies  _(Civil Code of Turkmenistan)_
- **Incorporation step 3** — Open a bank account and deposit the required share capital  _(Law on Enterprises of Turkmenistan)_
- **Incorporation step 4** — Register the company with the State Tax Service  _(Tax Code of Turkmenistan)_
- **Foreign ownership** — No general legal cap on foreign ownership; wholly foreign-owned enterprises permitted in most sectors  _(Law of Turkmenistan on Foreign Investments — https://chandrawatpartners.com/llc-incorporation-in-turkmenistan-a-practical-professional-overview-for-investors-across-the-world/)_
- **Core annual compliance** — Annual financial statements, annual tax declarations (profits tax, VAT where registered), and monthly payroll/contribution reporting  _(Tax Code of Turkmenistan)_
- **Investment incentives** — Available incentives may include tax exemptions, duty-free import of machinery/equipment, and profit/dividend repatriation rights ((approx — confirm current programs))  _(Law of Turkmenistan on Foreign Investments)_

Foreign investors in Turkmenistan most commonly use a Limited Liability Company (LLC), a joint-stock company, or a branch/representative office. Registration runs through the Ministry of Finance and Economy and the State Tax Service.
