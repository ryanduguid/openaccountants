# Turkmenistan Company Formation & Entity Choice

## Entity types, capital and incorporation

The [current Law on Enterprises](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-law/301?lang=ru) and [Law on Joint-Stock Companies](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-law/308?lang=ru) published by Parliament govern the forms and capital requirements below. Minimum capital is expressed in statutory base values for calculating taxes and fees. The earlier minimum-wage formula came from an older publication and has been withdrawn. The current monetary tax base value has not been verified.

- **⚠ "LLC" is not one of the statutory forms** — Article 24(1) lists **seven** organisational-legal forms for enterprises: **döwlet kärhanasy** (state enterprise), **ýekebara kärhana** (sole enterprise), **hyzmatdaşlyk kärhanasy** (co-operative enterprise), **bilelikdäki kärhana** (joint venture), **jemgyýetçilik guramasynyň kärhanasy** (enterprise of a public organisation), **hojalyk şereketi** (economic company) and **paýdarlar jemgyýeti** (joint-stock company). There is no *jogapkärçiligi çäkli jemgyýet* among them. Article 24(2) allows other forms to be provided for by legislation, so the guide's "LLC" may exist under the **Civil Code**, which was **not read** — but it is not a form of the Law on Enterprises, and the nearest statutory vehicle is the *economic company* below  _([Law on Enterprises, art. 24](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-law/301?lang=ru))_
- **Hojalyk şereketi (economic company) — the limited-liability vehicle, and it takes two people** — Article 29: an association of **two or more** natural and/or legal persons for joint activity, formed under a contract among the founders, its property made up of participants' shares in the charter capital, its supreme body the general meeting. **Article 29(6)** allows two liability models: participants **jointly and severally liable with all their property**, or participants liable **only up to their contributions (shares) in the charter capital**. The limited-liability form is therefore a variant to be chosen and stated, not a default  _([Law on Enterprises, art. 29](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-law/301?lang=ru))_
- **Minimum charter capital, sole enterprise**: 25 times the statutory base value for calculating taxes and fees. The current monetary value remains unverified. ([Law on Enterprises, art. 26(5)](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-law/301?lang=ru))
- **Minimum charter capital, economic company**: 100 times the statutory base value for calculating taxes and fees. A fixed TMT or USD amount requires the operative base value and any applicable sector rules. ([Law on Enterprises, art. 29(4)](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-law/301?lang=ru))
- **Joint-stock company capital and payment**: At least 200 times the statutory tax base value at the time of contributions and submission of registration documents. At least half must be paid at registration and the shares must be fully paid within 12 months. Initial shares must be placed among founders; public subscription at formation is prohibited. ([Joint-Stock Companies Law, art. 12](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-law/308?lang=ru))
- **Foreign founders are permitted by the Law itself** — Article 11(2): founders may be legally capable natural and legal persons, ***including such persons of foreign states***. This supports the "no general cap on foreign ownership" row below with a provision rather than a consultancy page. Article 11(1): an enterprise is created by decision of its founder or founders; 11(3) allows creation by spin-off or division of an existing enterprise  _([Law on Enterprises, art. 11](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-law/301?lang=ru))_
- **Registration — a decision within two weeks** — Article 16: the decision to register the enterprise, **or to refuse registration**, is taken **within two weeks** (*iki hepdäniň dowamynda*) of the founders submitting the necessary documents. The Law also governs re-registration (art. 21), cancellation of registration (art. 22), the registration fee (art. 20) and registration of branches and representative offices (art. 23)  _([Law on Enterprises, arts. 16, 20–23](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-law/301?lang=ru))_
- **Limited Liability Company (LLC)** — Most common vehicle for trading, services and local operations; liability limited to capital contributions. ⚠ See the statutory-forms row above — this description does not correspond to a form named in the Law on Enterprises  _(Civil Code of Turkmenistan / Law on Enterprises — https://www.healyconsultants.com/turkmenistan-company-registration/setup-llc/)_
- **Joint-stock company (open/closed)** — Used for larger capital-raising or share offerings  _(Law of Turkmenistan on Joint-Stock Companies)_
- **Branch / representative office** — Liaison, marketing or operation under a foreign parent; not a separate legal entity  _(Civil Code of Turkmenistan)_
- **Minimum shareholders (LLC)** — At least 2 shareholders (individuals or corporate entities) ((approx — confirm; single-member may be permitted))  _(Law on Enterprises of Turkmenistan — https://chandrawatpartners.com/llc-incorporation-in-turkmenistan-a-practical-professional-overview-for-investors-across-the-world/)_
- **Minimum directors (LLC)** — At least 1 director; need not be a Turkmenistan national or resident  _(Law on Enterprises of Turkmenistan — https://chandrawatpartners.com/llc-incorporation-in-turkmenistan-a-practical-professional-overview-for-investors-across-the-world/)_
- **Capital for the limited-liability economic company**: Use the 100-times-tax-base-value formula in article 29(4), together with the liability model chosen under article 29(6). The previously quoted TMT 5,000 and USD 20,000 amounts have not been established as current statutory requirements. ([Law on Enterprises, art. 29](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-law/301?lang=ru))
- **Capital payment schedule by entity**: Law on Enterprises article 47 requires at least 50% of founders’ contributions after signing the founding documents and before the registration application, with the balance within one year after registration. Joint-stock companies have the separate article 12(3) rule above. ([Law on Enterprises, art. 47](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-law/301?lang=ru); [Joint-Stock Companies Law, art. 12(3)](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-law/308?lang=ru))
- **Incorporation step 1** — Reserve a company name with the Ministry of Finance and Economy  _(Ministry of Finance and Economy of Turkmenistan)_
- **Incorporation step 2** — Prepare the company charter and obtain notarized copies  _(Civil Code of Turkmenistan)_
- **Incorporation step 3** — Open a bank account and deposit the required share capital  _(Law on Enterprises of Turkmenistan)_
- **Incorporation step 4** — Register the company with the State Tax Service  _(Tax Code of Turkmenistan)_
- **Foreign ownership** — No general legal cap on foreign ownership; wholly foreign-owned enterprises permitted in most sectors  _(Law of Turkmenistan on Foreign Investments — https://chandrawatpartners.com/llc-incorporation-in-turkmenistan-a-practical-professional-overview-for-investors-across-the-world/)_
- **Core annual compliance** — Annual financial statements, annual tax declarations (profits tax, VAT where registered), and monthly payroll/contribution reporting  _(Tax Code of Turkmenistan)_
- **Investment incentives** — Available incentives may include tax exemptions, duty-free import of machinery/equipment, and profit/dividend repatriation rights ((approx — confirm current programs))  _(Law of Turkmenistan on Foreign Investments)_

Foreign investors in Turkmenistan most commonly use a Limited Liability Company (LLC), a joint-stock company, or a branch/representative office. Registration runs through the Ministry of Finance and Economy and the State Tax Service.
