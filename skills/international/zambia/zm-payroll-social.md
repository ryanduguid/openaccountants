# Zambia Payroll & Social Contributions

## Payroll, NAPSA and NHIMA contributions

Employers operate PAYE withholding and must contribute to the National Pension Scheme Authority (NAPSA) and the National Health Insurance scheme (NHIMA). Note the NAPSA ceiling shown is the published 2026 figure; the 2025 ceiling should be confirmed.

**Source warning — all seven contribution figures below are unverified.** Every
one of them was cited to a single page on `zambiaprice.com`. That domain no
longer serves a Zambian tax guide: it now returns online-gambling content, so
the source these figures came from cannot be read, and nothing here has been
checked against NAPSA, NHIMA or the Acts themselves. The instrument names are
kept because they are the right instruments; the destination has been removed
rather than replaced, because substituting a source that was not actually
consulted would make an unverified figure look checked. NAPSA publishes at
[napsa.co.zm](https://www.napsa.co.zm/) and NHIMA at
[nhima.co.zm](https://www.nhima.co.zm/); both render their rate pages in
JavaScript and could not be read here. **A reviewer should treat this whole
block as unsourced and confirm each figure with the two authorities.**

- **NAPSA total contribution rate** — 10% of gross earnings, shared equally (5% employer + 5% employee)  _(National Pension Scheme Authority, Formal Sector Contributions — https://www.napsa.co.zm/self-service/formal-sector)_
- **NAPSA employee contribution** — 5% of gross earnings  _(National Pension Scheme Authority, Formal Sector Contributions — https://www.napsa.co.zm/self-service/formal-sector)_
- **NAPSA employer contribution** — 5% of gross earnings  _(National Pension Scheme Authority, Formal Sector Contributions — https://www.napsa.co.zm/self-service/formal-sector)_
- **NAPSA monthly contribution ceiling** — 2025: ZMW 1,708.20 per party (ZMW 3,416.40 in total), the ceiling NAPSA set from the 2025 national average earnings of ZMW 8,541; the 2026 ceiling of ZMW 1,861.80 each (ZMW 3,723.60) reported by secondary sources is not yet confirmed against a NAPSA notice  _(National Pension Scheme Authority, Revision in Contribution Ceiling for the Year 2025 — https://www.napsa.co.zm/news/details?id=df81c3bb-5416-4a43-b521-73923e0ccf93)_
- **NHIMA total contribution rate** — 2% of basic salary (1% employer + 1% employee) (unverified — see source warning above)  _(National Health Insurance Act — source lost)_
- **NHIMA employee contribution** — 1% of basic salary (unverified — see source warning above)  _(National Health Insurance Act — source lost)_
- **NHIMA employer contribution** — 1% of basic salary (unverified — see source warning above)  _(National Health Insurance Act — source lost)_
- **PAYE withholding** — Employers deduct PAYE from emoluments under section 71 of the Income Tax Act using the 2026 monthly bands: 0% to K5,100, 20% from K5,100.01 to K7,100, 30% from K7,100.01 to K9,200 and 37% above K9,200 (37.5% was the 2025 top rate); late remittance attracts a 5% penalty plus interest at 2% above the Bank of Zambia discount rate  _(Zambia Revenue Authority, Tax Information (turnover tax, PAYE 2026 bands, income tax, VAT), PAYE example with the 2026 tax bands — https://www.zra.org.zm/tax-information/ ; Zambia Revenue Authority, Payment Due Dates, PAYE, section 71 of the Income Tax Act — https://www.zra.org.zm/payment-due-dates/)_
- **PAYE remittance deadline** — By the 10th of the month following deduction  _([Income Tax Act](https://www.zra.org.zm/payment-due-dates/))_
- **NAPSA remittance deadline** — By the 10th of the month following the contribution month  _(National Pension Scheme Authority, Formal Sector Contributions — https://www.napsa.co.zm/self-service/formal-sector)_
- **Skills Development Levy** — 0.5% of gross emoluments, payable by the employer (approx — confirm rate and base)  _(Skills Development Levy Act)_
