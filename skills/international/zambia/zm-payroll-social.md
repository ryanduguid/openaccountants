# Zambia Payroll & Social Contributions

## Payroll, NAPSA and NHIMA contributions

Employers operate PAYE withholding and must contribute to the National Pension Scheme Authority (NAPSA) and the National Health Insurance scheme (NHIMA). Note the NAPSA ceiling shown is the published 2026 figure; the 2025 ceiling should be confirmed.

**Source warning — all seven contribution figures below are unverified.** Every
one of them was cited to a single page on `zambiaprice.com`. That domain no
longer serves a Zambian tax guide: it now returns online-gambling content, so
the source these figures came from cannot be read, and nothing here has been
checked against NAPSA, NHIMA or the Acts themselves. The instrument names are
kept because they are the right instruments; the destination has been removed
rather than replaced, because substituting a source that was not actually
consulted would make an unverified figure look checked. NAPSA publishes at
[napsa.co.zm](https://www.napsa.co.zm/) and NHIMA at
[nhima.co.zm](https://www.nhima.co.zm/); both render their rate pages in
JavaScript and could not be read here. **A reviewer should treat this whole
block as unsourced and confirm each figure with the two authorities.**

- **NAPSA total contribution rate** — 10% of earnings (5% employer + 5% employee) percent (unverified — see source warning above)  _(National Pension Scheme Act — source lost)_
- **NAPSA employee contribution** — 5% of earnings percent (unverified — see source warning above)  _(National Pension Scheme Act — source lost)_
- **NAPSA employer contribution** — 5% of earnings percent (unverified — see source warning above)  _(National Pension Scheme Act — source lost)_
- **NAPSA monthly contribution ceiling** — ZMW 3,723.60 total (ZMW 1,861.80 each party) (approx — published 2026 ceiling; confirm 2025 figure; unverified — see source warning above)  _(National Pension Scheme Act — source lost)_
- **NHIMA total contribution rate** — 2% of basic salary (1% employer + 1% employee) percent (unverified — see source warning above)  _(National Health Insurance Act — source lost)_
- **NHIMA employee contribution** — 1% of basic salary percent (unverified — see source warning above)  _(National Health Insurance Act — source lost)_
- **NHIMA employer contribution** — 1% of basic salary percent (unverified — see source warning above)  _(National Health Insurance Act — source lost)_
- **PAYE withholding** — Employer must deduct PAYE from employee pay under the progressive bands (0% / 20% / 30% / 37.5%)  _([Income Tax Act](https://www.zra.org.zm/calculate-paye/))_
- **PAYE remittance deadline** — By the 10th of the month following deduction  _([Income Tax Act](https://www.zra.org.zm/payment-due-dates/))_
- **NAPSA remittance deadline** — By the 10th of the month following the contribution month (approx — confirm)  _(National Pension Scheme Act)_
- **Skills Development Levy** — 0.5% of gross emoluments, payable by the employer percent (approx — confirm rate and base)  _(Skills Development Levy Act)_
