# Zambia Tax Overview

## Zambian tax system at a glance

Zambia operates a source-based tax system administered by the Zambia Revenue Authority (ZRA), with the charge (tax) year running from 1 January to 31 December. Figures below are for the 2025 charge year and are an unverified source-cited draft.

- **Charge (tax) year** — 1 January to 31 December  _(Income Tax Act — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/zambia/individual/tax-administration)_
- **Currency** — Zambian Kwacha (ZMW / K)  _(Bank of Zambia Act)_
- **National tax authority** — Zambia Revenue Authority (ZRA)  _(Zambia Revenue Authority Act — https://www.zra.org.zm/)_
- **Basis of taxation** — Source-based; residents also taxed on foreign interest and dividends  _(Income Tax Act — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/zambia/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **Top personal income tax (PAYE) rate** — 37% on monthly chargeable income above K9,200 under the 2026 PAYE bands (0% to K5,100; 20% from K5,100.01 to K7,100; 30% from K7,100.01 to K9,200; 37% above); the 2025 top rate was 37.5% above K8,100  _(Zambia Revenue Authority, Tax Information (turnover tax, PAYE 2026 bands, income tax, VAT), PAYE example with the 2026 tax bands — https://www.zra.org.zm/tax-information/ ; 2025 bands: MJ Consultants — https://mjconsultants.co.zm/insights/zra-paye-tax-bands-for-2025-updated-rates-and-how-they-affect-your-salary/)_
- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 30% standard rate; the agriculture sector pays 10%, and other sector rates sit in the Charging Schedule to the Income Tax Act  _(Zambia Revenue Authority, Tax Information (turnover tax, PAYE 2026 bands, income tax, VAT), income tax section — https://www.zra.org.zm/tax-information/)_
- **Value-Added Tax (VAT)** — 16% standard rate; zero-rated supplies carry 0%  _(Zambia Revenue Authority, Tax Information (turnover tax, PAYE 2026 bands, income tax, VAT), VAT section — https://www.zra.org.zm/tax-information/)_
- **Annual income tax return deadline** — 21 June each year for the return and the balance of tax under section 46 of the Income Tax Act  _(Zambia Revenue Authority, Payment Due Dates, Income Tax Annual — https://www.zra.org.zm/payment-due-dates/)_
- **VAT return deadline** — Monthly, by the 18th of the month following the tax period  _(Value Added Tax Act — https://www.zra.org.zm/payment-due-dates/)_
- **PAYE remittance deadline** — By the 10th of the month following the month of deduction  _(Income Tax Act — https://www.zra.org.zm/payment-due-dates/)_
