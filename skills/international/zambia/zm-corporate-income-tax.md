# Zambia Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax rates and base

Resident companies are taxed on Zambian-source profits at a standard 30% rate, with reduced or higher rates for specific sectors. The figures below are for the 2025 charge year and are an unverified source-cited draft.

- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 30%  _(Zambia Revenue Authority, Tax Information (turnover tax, PAYE 2026 bands, income tax, VAT), income tax section — https://www.zra.org.zm/tax-information/)_
- **Telecommunication companies rate** — 35% single rate on telecommunication income (the two-tier 30% / 40% structure was replaced from the 2023 charge year)  _(ZRA, Practice Notes 2023 (Income Tax (Amendment) Act 2022: single 35% rate for the telecommunication sector) — https://www.zra.org.zm/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Practice-Notes-2023.pdf)_
- **Agriculture / chemical fertilizer manufacture rate** — 10% for the agriculture sector (ZRA tax information); the 15% figure for chemical fertiliser manufacturing keeps its the secondary summary source, so check the Charging Schedule to the Income Tax Act before relying on it  _(Zambia Revenue Authority, Tax Information (turnover tax, PAYE 2026 bands, income tax, VAT), income tax section — https://www.zra.org.zm/tax-information/ ; fertiliser manufacturing (as reported))_
- **Non-traditional exports rate** — 15%  _(ZRA, Tax Incentives (income from non-traditional exports taxed at a reduced rate of 15%) — https://www.zra.org.zm/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Tax-Incentives.pdf)_
- **Mining (mineral processing) rate** — 30% (approx — confirm; mining also subject to mineral royalty)  _(Income Tax Act — https://www.zra.org.zm/payment-due-dates/ (as reported; the figure was not re-read against this text for this change))_
- **Turnover tax (small businesses)** — 5% on monthly turnover above K2,500 (K30,000 a year), with 0% on the first K2,500 a month, for businesses with annual sales of K5,000,000 or less; a business whose annual sales exceed K5,000,000 registers for income tax instead; returns and payment are due by the 14th of the following month  _(Zambia Revenue Authority, Tax Information (turnover tax, PAYE 2026 bands, income tax, VAT), turnover tax section — https://www.zra.org.zm/tax-information/ ; Zambia Revenue Authority, Payment Due Dates, Turnover Tax — https://www.zra.org.zm/payment-due-dates/)_
- **Tax base** — Taxable business profits from Zambian sources (source-based system): revenue less the expenses allowable for tax, taxed at the standard 30% rate  _(Zambia Revenue Authority, Tax Information, Income Tax — https://www.zra.org.zm/tax-information/)_
- **WHT on dividends (resident)** — 15%  _(ZRA, Withholding Taxes guide, Dividends, s 81 (15% for residents and 20% for non-residents, final tax) — https://www.zra.org.zm/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/Withholding-Taxes.pdf ; ZRA withholding tax codes 112 to 158 — https://portal.zra.org.zm/assets/returns/whtTaxCodes.pdf)_
- **WHT on dividends (non-resident)** — 20% (Treaty relief may reduce this)  _(ZRA, Withholding Taxes guide, Dividends, s 81 (20% for non-residents, final tax) — https://www.zra.org.zm/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/Withholding-Taxes.pdf ; ZRA withholding tax code 146 — https://portal.zra.org.zm/assets/returns/whtTaxCodes.pdf)_
- **WHT on interest (resident)** — 15%  _(ZRA withholding tax code 112, Interest – Resident (15%) — https://portal.zra.org.zm/assets/returns/whtTaxCodes.pdf)_
- **WHT on interest (non-resident)** — 20% (Treaty relief may reduce this)  _(ZRA withholding tax codes 151 (Interest – Non Resident, other, 20%) and 153 (Treasury and GRZ bonds, 15%) — https://portal.zra.org.zm/assets/returns/whtTaxCodes.pdf)_
- **WHT on royalties (resident)** — 15%  _(ZRA, Withholding Taxes guide, Royalties (15% for residents and 20% for non-residents) — https://www.zra.org.zm/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/Withholding-Taxes.pdf ; ZRA withholding tax code 117 — https://portal.zra.org.zm/assets/returns/whtTaxCodes.pdf)_
- **WHT on royalties (non-resident)** — 20% (Treaty relief may reduce this)  _(ZRA, Withholding Taxes guide, Royalties (20% for non-residents) — https://www.zra.org.zm/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/Withholding-Taxes.pdf ; ZRA withholding tax code 158 — https://portal.zra.org.zm/assets/returns/whtTaxCodes.pdf)_
- **WHT on management/consultancy fees (non-resident)** — 20%  _(ZRA withholding tax code 154, Management or Consultant Fees – Non Resident (20%) — https://portal.zra.org.zm/assets/returns/whtTaxCodes.pdf ; resident fees 15%, not final: ZRA Withholding Taxes guide — https://www.zra.org.zm/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/Withholding-Taxes.pdf)_
- **Annual income tax return deadline** — 21 June following the end of the charge year, together with the balance of tax under section 46  _(Zambia Revenue Authority, Payment Due Dates, Income Tax Annual — https://www.zra.org.zm/payment-due-dates/)_
- **Provisional tax** — Provisional return by 31 March of the charge year; the tax is payable in four equal instalments due 31 March, 30 June, 30 September and 31 December, each payable by the 10th of the following month (section 46(a))  _(Zambia Revenue Authority, Payment Due Dates, Provisional Tax — https://www.zra.org.zm/payment-due-dates/ ; Zambia Revenue Authority, Tax Information (turnover tax, PAYE 2026 bands, income tax, VAT), provisional return — https://www.zra.org.zm/tax-information/)_
