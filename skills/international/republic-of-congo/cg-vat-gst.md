# Republic of Congo VAT / GST

## Republic of Congo VAT / GST

## Value added tax (Taxe sur la Valeur Ajoutée)

The Republic of Congo operates a VAT (Taxe sur la Valeur Ajoutée) at a standard 18% rate plus a 5% non-deductible surtax (centimes additionnels), giving an effective combined rate of 18.9%. Returns are filed monthly.

- **Standard VAT rate** — 18 percent on all taxable operations other than the zero-rated exports, international transport and eucalyptus chain  _(Code Général des Impôts tome 1, édition du 28 mars 2021 (Ministère des finances, du budget et du portefeuille public), loi TVA art. 17 — https://www.finances.gouv.cg/sites/default/files/documents/CGI%20Tome%20I.pdf)_
- **VAT surtax (centimes additionnels)** — 5% of the VAT, non-deductible — effective combined rate 18.9%  _(Loi n° 47-2024 du 30 décembre 2024 portant loi de finances pour 2025, Journal officiel n° 15-2024, budget annex 3.a (18.9% cumulated precompte) — https://www.sgg.cg/JO/2024/congo-jo-2024-15-sp.pdf; Loi n° 42-2025 du 31 décembre 2025 portant loi de finances pour 2026, Journal officiel édition spéciale n° 3-2026, CGI tome 1 budget annex 4.d — https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-2.pdf)_
- **Reduced VAT rate** — 5% on the everyday goods listed in annex V (milk, bakery products, rice, wheat flour and others), on gas oil and lubricants imported from neighbouring countries by resident forestry companies, and on sales and purchases by developers and enterprises in special economic zones  _(Loi n° 47-2024 du 30 décembre 2024 portant loi de finances pour 2025, Journal officiel n° 15-2024, art. 22.2.C (nouveau) — https://www.sgg.cg/JO/2024/congo-jo-2024-15-sp.pdf; Loi n° 42-2025 du 31 décembre 2025 portant loi de finances pour 2026, Journal officiel édition spéciale n° 3-2026, CGI tome 1 annex 5 — https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-2.pdf)_
- **Zero-rated supplies** — Exports and assimilated international transactions are zero-rated percent (approx — confirm)  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI) — Taxe sur la Valeur Ajoutée)_
- **VAT registration / liability threshold** — Businesses with annual turnover at or above XAF 100,000,000 are subject to the normal (réel) VAT regime (approx — confirm)  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI))_
- **VAT return frequency and deadline** — Monthly, filed with spontaneous payment within the first 15 days of the month following the taxable operations (the 20th quoted by commercial summaries was not found in the text)  _(Code Général des Impôts tome 1, édition du 28 mars 2021 (Ministère des finances, du budget et du portefeuille public), loi TVA art. 31 — https://www.finances.gouv.cg/sites/default/files/documents/CGI%20Tome%20I.pdf)_
- **Reverse charge on imported services** — VAT on services supplied by non-established providers is self-assessed by the Congolese recipient (retenue à la source de TVA) (approx — confirm)  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI) — Taxe sur la Valeur Ajoutée)_
- **Electronic invoicing** — Certified Electronic Invoicing System (SFEC) introduced for VAT management  _(Loi de Finances 2025 (as described at [clgglobal.com](https://clgglobal.com/navigating-congos-2025-financial-law-what-it-means-for-you/)))_
- **Scope of VAT** — Applies to supplies of goods and services made for consideration in Congo and to imports  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI) — Taxe sur la Valeur Ajoutée)_
