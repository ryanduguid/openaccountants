# Republic of Congo Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax (Impôt sur les Sociétés)

- **Corporate income tax overview** — Companies pay the Impôt sur les Sociétés (IS) on net taxable profit, with a standard rate of 30% to 2025 and 28% from 2026, sector-specific rates, and a minimum tax of 1% of products.
- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 30% for financial years to 31 December 2025 (CGI art. 122 as restored by Loi 47-2024); 28% from 1 January 2026 (CGI art. 86 A(2) as rewritten by Loi 42-2025)  _(Loi n° 47-2024 du 30 décembre 2024 portant loi de finances pour 2025, Journal officiel n° 15-2024, art. 122 (nouveau) — https://www.sgg.cg/JO/2024/congo-jo-2024-15-sp.pdf; Loi n° 42-2025 du 31 décembre 2025 portant loi de finances pour 2026, Journal officiel édition spéciale n° 3-2026, CGI tome 1 art. 86 A — https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-2.pdf)_
- **Rate for foreign legal entities** — 33% (personnes morales étrangères without a permanent establishment, taxed under arts 92 to 92 D)  _(Loi n° 47-2024 du 30 décembre 2024 portant loi de finances pour 2025, Journal officiel n° 15-2024, art. 122 A (nouveau) — https://www.sgg.cg/JO/2024/congo-jo-2024-15-sp.pdf; Loi n° 42-2025 du 31 décembre 2025 portant loi de finances pour 2026, Journal officiel édition spéciale n° 3-2026, CGI tome 1 art. 86 A(3)(c) — https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-2.pdf)_
- **Reduced rate — microfinance and private education companies** — 25%  _(Loi n° 47-2024 du 30 décembre 2024 portant loi de finances pour 2025, Journal officiel n° 15-2024, art. 122 A (nouveau) — https://www.sgg.cg/JO/2024/congo-jo-2024-15-sp.pdf; Loi n° 42-2025 du 31 décembre 2025 portant loi de finances pour 2026, Journal officiel édition spéciale n° 3-2026, CGI tome 1 art. 86 A(3)(a) — https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-2.pdf)_
- **Reduced rate — mining, quarrying and real-estate development** — 28% (from 2026 this equals the general rate)  _(Loi n° 47-2024 du 30 décembre 2024 portant loi de finances pour 2025, Journal officiel n° 15-2024, art. 122 A (nouveau) — https://www.sgg.cg/JO/2024/congo-jo-2024-15-sp.pdf; Loi n° 42-2025 du 31 décembre 2025 portant loi de finances pour 2026, Journal officiel édition spéciale n° 3-2026, CGI tome 1 art. 86 A(3)(b) — https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-2.pdf)_
- **Surtax on total tax due** — None found: Loi 47-2024 created centimes additionnels of 5% on the patente (art. 369 bis) and the 5% centimes additionnels on VAT continue; neither the 2025 nor the 2026 finance law adds a surtax on the corporate tax due  _(Loi n° 47-2024 du 30 décembre 2024 portant loi de finances pour 2025, Journal officiel n° 15-2024, arts 368 and 369 bis (nouveaux) — https://www.sgg.cg/JO/2024/congo-jo-2024-15-sp.pdf; Loi n° 42-2025 du 31 décembre 2025 portant loi de finances pour 2026, Journal officiel édition spéciale n° 3-2026, budget annex 4.d — https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-2.pdf)_
- **Minimum tax (minimum de perception)** — 1% of operating, financial and extraordinary products, excluding income already subject to a final withholding; declared by 15 March on the prior year's base and paid in four instalments (15 March, 15 June, 15 September, 15 December), credited against the year's CIT and kept by the Treasury when the CIT is lower; 50% penalty for non-payment. The fixed floors of XAF 1,000,000 and XAF 500,000 quoted by commercial summaries are not in the 2026 text `[RESEARCH GAP — reviewer to confirm whether a floor survives elsewhere in the code]`  _(Loi n° 42-2025 du 31 décembre 2025 portant loi de finances pour 2026, Journal officiel édition spéciale n° 3-2026, CGI tome 1 art. 86 C — https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-2.pdf)_
- **Simplified regime for foreign service providers** — Foreign legal persons are taxed on a deemed profit of 22% of their Congolese turnover excluding tax (art. 92 A), at the art. 86 A rate for foreign legal persons of 33% (art. 92 C): about 7.26% of taxable turnover; the tax is withheld at source for foreign companies whose works last no more than six months or that have no permanent professional installation (art. 92 B)  _(Loi n° 42-2025 du 31 décembre 2025 portant loi de finances pour 2026, Journal officiel édition spéciale n° 3-2026, CGI tome 1 arts 92 A to 92 C — https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-2.pdf)_
- **Tax base** — Net accounting profit (SYSCOHADA financial statements) adjusted for tax add-backs and deductions; territorial source basis  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI); OHADA SYSCOHADA accounting framework)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends** — 15% impôt sur le revenu des capitaux mobiliers (IRCM), due when the distributing enterprise is operated in Congo or the recipient is resident; 35% on undisclosed (occult) income; no treaty  _(Loi n° 42-2025 du 31 décembre 2025 portant loi de finances pour 2026, Journal officiel édition spéciale n° 3-2026, CGI tome 1 arts 107 and 110 — https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-2.pdf)_
- **Withholding tax on interest** — 15% IRCM on interest of debts, deposits, guarantees and partners' current accounts paid by an enterprise operated in Congo, whoever the recipient (arts 105 B, 107 and 110); the 20% withholding on non-residents of art. 185 ter does not apply to loan interest paid to a foreign lender by the Congolese debtor. The 0% for residents and 20% for non-residents that commercial summaries quote were not found in the texts read `[RESEARCH GAP — reviewer to confirm any exemption for bank or resident recipients]`  _(Loi n° 42-2025 du 31 décembre 2025 portant loi de finances pour 2026, Journal officiel édition spéciale n° 3-2026, CGI tome 1 arts 105 B, 107 and 110 — https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-2.pdf; Code Général des Impôts tome 1, édition du 28 mars 2021 (Ministère des finances, du budget et du portefeuille public), art. 185 ter — https://www.finances.gouv.cg/sites/default/files/documents/CGI%20Tome%20I.pdf)_
- **Withholding tax on royalties** — 20% withheld on royalties paid to non-residents by a resident company or a permanent establishment (art. 86 D), the rate art. 185 ter also sets for persons without domicile or fiscal residence in Congo; no separate withholding article was found for royalties paid to residents, which are taxed as business income  _(Loi n° 42-2025 du 31 décembre 2025 portant loi de finances pour 2026, Journal officiel édition spéciale n° 3-2026, CGI tome 1 art. 86 D — https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-2.pdf; Code Général des Impôts tome 1, édition du 28 mars 2021 (Ministère des finances, du budget et du portefeuille public), art. 185 ter — https://www.finances.gouv.cg/sites/default/files/documents/CGI%20Tome%20I.pdf)_
- **Withholding tax on foreign service fees** — 20% on services rendered by non-residents and remitted by the 15th of the month after payment (art. 86 D; art. 185 ter); 5.75% on income from contracts in the oil unitisation zone with Angola (art. 92 B(5)); domestic withholdings under art. 183: 5% on fees paid to professionals and 10% on commissions, rebates and discounts paid by wholesalers to persons outside the CIT (Loi 47-2024)  _(Loi n° 42-2025 du 31 décembre 2025 portant loi de finances pour 2026, Journal officiel édition spéciale n° 3-2026, CGI tome 1 arts 86 D, 92 B and 183 — https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-2.pdf; Code Général des Impôts tome 1, édition du 28 mars 2021 (Ministère des finances, du budget et du portefeuille public), arts 183 and 185 ter — https://www.finances.gouv.cg/sites/default/files/documents/CGI%20Tome%20I.pdf)_
- **Annual CIT return deadline** — Within four months of the close of the financial year (30 April for a 31 December year-end), with the OHADA financial statements; the 20 May date applies to individuals' business-profit (IBA) declarations  _(Loi n° 42-2025 du 31 décembre 2025 portant loi de finances pour 2026, Journal officiel édition spéciale n° 3-2026, CGI tome 1 arts 86 F and 128 to 130 — https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-2.pdf)_
- **CIT quarterly instalment dates** — The minimum de perception is paid in four instalments by 15 March, 15 June, 15 September and 15 December, credited against the CIT of the same year (the 20 February, 20 May, 25 August and 20 November dates quoted for earlier years are superseded by the 2026 text)  _(Loi n° 42-2025 du 31 décembre 2025 portant loi de finances pour 2026, Journal officiel édition spéciale n° 3-2026, CGI tome 1 art. 86 C(5) — https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-2.pdf)_
