# Cambodia tax-system orientation

Start by identifying the taxpayer, the income category and the period. Apply the Tax on Salary rules to employment income and the Tax on Income rules to business income.

## Taxable income and source

Tax on Income covers Cambodian and foreign-source income of residents and Cambodian-source income of non-residents. A Cambodian permanent establishment of a non-resident is treated as a resident legal person for its Cambodian-source income. Use the statutory source rules and check any applicable treaty. [Law on Taxation, arts 4, 5 and 33](https://www.tax.gov.kh/u6rhf7ogbi6/gdtstream/816f7e7b-e4be-4d8f-a789-e587b92dbe2e) · [Prakas 578, art 8](https://www.tax.gov.kh/u6rhf7ogbi6/gdtstream/64a95813-0066-4af4-a414-7b7f7baedc7d)

Tax on Salary covers salary received by residents in Cambodia and abroad. For non-residents, it generally covers employment exercised in Cambodia. Check the exception for qualifying technical assistance and other services paid by a payer without a Cambodian permanent establishment or fixed base, where no such establishment or base bears the salary. [Prakas 575, art 4](https://www.tax.gov.kh/u6rhf7ogbi6/gdtstream/fb78dfde-8342-4e3c-80e0-84ef81919c70)

## Residence

Check whether an individual has a residence or principal place of abode in Cambodia. The presence test is more than 182 days in any 12-month period ending in the current tax year. For Tax on Salary, any part of a day counts as a full day, and the presence test is the final criterion when the home tests are inconclusive. A legal person or partnership is resident if established or managed in Cambodia or if its principal place of business is there. [Law on Taxation, art 5](https://www.tax.gov.kh/u6rhf7ogbi6/gdtstream/816f7e7b-e4be-4d8f-a789-e587b92dbe2e) · [Prakas 575, art 4](https://www.tax.gov.kh/u6rhf7ogbi6/gdtstream/fb78dfde-8342-4e3c-80e0-84ef81919c70)

## Taxpayer type

Distinguish a legal person, an individual or sole proprietorship and a partnership. For a partnership, allocate income, gains, losses, deductions, credits and charitable contributions to each member, retaining the character of each item, whether or not distributed. The law provides a progressive regime for members' allocated taxable shares where the partnership is outside the legal-person category. Check the partnership's own registration, annual declaration and payment obligations as well as each partner's treatment. [Law on Taxation, arts 5, 10 and 20](https://www.tax.gov.kh/u6rhf7ogbi6/gdtstream/816f7e7b-e4be-4d8f-a789-e587b92dbe2e) · [Prakas 578, art 7](https://www.tax.gov.kh/u6rhf7ogbi6/gdtstream/64a95813-0066-4af4-a414-7b7f7baedc7d) · [GDT tax registration for partnerships](https://www.tax.gov.kh/u6rhf7ogbi6/gdtstream/64f5133b-e4ef-495a-9ade-df6f102a8fad)

## Currency

Use Cambodian riel (KHR) for the ordinary Tax on Income computation. Prakas 578 requires foreign-currency transactions to be recorded in riel using the National Bank of Cambodia rate on the transaction date. Check the separate rule for receivables or debts with a contracted forward exchange rate and use the applicable conversion rule for other taxes. [Prakas 578, art 5](https://www.tax.gov.kh/u6rhf7ogbi6/gdtstream/64a95813-0066-4af4-a414-7b7f7baedc7d)

## Assessment periods

A legal person normally calculates annual Tax on Income on its calendar-year results and files and pays by 31 March of the following year. An enterprise controlled by a non-resident legal person may apply for a different tax year. Establish the authorised accounting period before selecting the return. Tax on Salary has a monthly cycle. [Law on Taxation, arts 8 and 53](https://www.tax.gov.kh/u6rhf7ogbi6/gdtstream/816f7e7b-e4be-4d8f-a789-e587b92dbe2e) · [Prakas 578, art 7](https://www.tax.gov.kh/u6rhf7ogbi6/gdtstream/64a95813-0066-4af4-a414-7b7f7baedc7d)

## Tax authority

The General Department of Taxation (GDT) administers these taxes under the Ministry of Economy and Finance. Use the GDT's Tax on Income e-Filing service for annual declarations and its monthly e-Filing service for the relevant monthly declarations. [GDT](https://www.tax.gov.kh/en/) · [Official e-Tax services](https://www.tax.gov.kh/en/e-service)

## Worked example

An employee with no established home in Cambodia spends 184 days there across several visits in a 12-month period ending in their current tax year. Count each partial day once. The employee meets the domestic presence test for Tax on Salary, so identify both Cambodian and foreign salary before checking any treaty treatment.
