# Turks and Caicos Islands: formation and business licensing reference v0.3

> **General reference only.** Selected dated sources support this Tier 2 draft. A qualified local professional must establish applicable law, facts, documents and procedures before reliance. Incorporation, name registration, business licensing, sector authorisation and tax registration are separate decisions. No actual entity recommendation, approved ownership structure, fee, filing, reporting exemption or operating permission is accepted here.

## Scope

Selected company, beneficial-ownership, business-name and licensing source units with distinct historical cutoffs. Actual type choice, incorporation, ownership determination, public disclosure, fee, filing, retention deletion and operating permission remain Held. An enacted licensing amendment still requires its operative commencement and complete chain to be established.

### Dated company types and incorporation

F1 s 3 distinguishes companies incorporated under s 10, continued into the Islands and existing companies registered through Schedule 1. It excludes dissolved and continued-out companies; foreign companies are separately defined. The Companies definition of ordinary residence in s 6 is not a general tax-residence result.

Section 7 permits 5 company types: limited by shares; limited by guarantee with shares; guarantee without shares; unlimited with shares; unlimited without shares. Shares/guarantee/unlimited liability, funding, members, activities and cross-border treatment require a complete comparison before choosing a type. Protected-cell companies and non-profit companies have additional statutory branches. NPO approval, permitted purposes, guarantee form and distribution restrictions, and PCC Commission approval cannot be bypassed by ordinary-company steps. Complete incorporated NPO/PCC rules are not qualified here. [F1 ss 1–15, 23–25]

Only the proposed registered agent may file incorporation under s 9. Required content includes signed application, articles, prescribed other documents and the agent's written consent, plus the applicable Part IX information. Articles state name/type and the relevant share-class/guarantee particulars and other prescribed matters. The Registrar must be satisfied of compliance, register the documents, allot a number and issue the incorporation certificate. The certificate evidences the stated incorporation date; preparing, uploading or paying for an application is not that event. Separate legal personality does not settle tax treatment or authority to operate. [F1 ss 9–15, 23–25]

### Dated office, agent and filing rules

F1 requires a physical registered office in the Islands and an eligible registered agent licensed under Cap 16.10. Section 44(4) expressly excepts liquidation under the Insolvency Ordinance from the agent requirement. The complete agent licensing and insolvency regimes remain unqualified.

Appointment requires the prescribed resolution route, endorsed consent notice and Registrar registration. Office/agent changes have their own resolution and filing steps; the office change takes effect on registration. Agent resignation requires at least 30 days' intention notice and filing its copy within 10 working days, followed by the specified replacement/resignation events. Eligibility-loss duties and the conditional transitional-acting protection remain separate. F1 defines working day to exclude Saturday, Sunday and public holiday; do not substitute a rule from a different statute. [F1 ss 42–48]

Literal references in 42(3)(b) to office-change s 45 and 44(2) to resignation s 46 differ from the operative change/resignation headings in ss 46–47. No corrected mapping or current correction is certified.

Section 291 ordinarily routes company filings through its registered agent or appointed Insolvency Ordinance liquidator, except otherwise provided. It permits Commission notices for specified non-working-day actions; none was qualified here. Sections 303–304 require the published approved form and specified attachments and authorise guidance. An observed filename or portal link does not establish an approved current form, successful filing or Registrar acceptance. [F1 ss 291, 303–304; F7]

### Records, retention and reporting boundaries

F1 s 132 requires at the agent office articles, member/director registers or copies, beneficial ownership register, charges register or copy and notices/documents filed in the previous 10 years. Electronic availability and hard-copy reproduction conditions, copy-register location and updating within 5 working days and possible listed-company modifications must be retained. Section 133 separately requires meeting minutes and written resolutions for the stated bodies/classes/committees.

Section 134 requires records explaining transactions and allowing financial position to be determined with reasonable accuracy at any time, including invoices, receipts, contracts and underlying evidence of money, assets/liabilities and position. Section 135 permits the specified hard-copy/electronic routes, subject to the Electronic Transactions law. Where held elsewhere, provide the agent written location/controller information; offshore hard copy must be supplied on request and electronic records must be inspectable at the agent office. Section 136 has distinct director/member access and court-remedy conditions, not universal public access. [F1 ss 132–136]

### Dated beneficial ownership interpretation and information

F12 interprets the Companies Part IX criteria; it does not settle the current applicability of every company, foreign company or exemption. F2 subsequently includes foreign companies subject to listed/prescribed exemptions and substitutes natural-person registrability. Its incorporated Regulatory Act thresholds, the Financial Services Commission definition, applicable exemptions, later law and the complete amended Part IX still require reconciliation before an actual determination. [F1 ss 145–147, 160; F2; F12 Regulations 1–4]

Under F12, joint holders and qualifying joint arrangements receive the combined attribution. The shares test **exceeds 25%**, rather than including exactly 25%. Par-value shares use aggregate nominal value; no-par shares use numbers. Mixed capital tests the separate pools as specified, not an invented blended percentage. Voting rights concern all or substantially all matters and specified equivalent policy/constitutional control, excluding the entity's own rights. Majority director appointment/removal concerns directors carrying a majority of board voting rights, not a headcount alone. [F12 Regulations 5–8]

Indirect ownership/control follows the specified majority-stake chain, including voting, member appointment, joint-agreement and dominant-control branches. A share held as nominee is attributed to the person for whom it is held. Control of rights can attribute them to the controller rather than the formal holder. Conditional, temporarily incapable and insolvency-related rights have distinct rules; security rights depend on the specified instruction, ordinary-lending and interest conditions and exceptions. No blanket lender exemption is established. A member who is not a shareholder and has a right to share in more than 25% of the company's capital or profits meets the stated significant-control branch, without limiting the other control criterion. [F12 Regulations 9–14]

Professional, commercial, security, derivative, employment, director and statutory roles do not establish control **merely because** of the listed relationship; this is not a blanket exemption for an adviser or director who otherwise controls. Limited-partner exclusions have separate conditions and do not exclude the trust/firm criterion identified in Regulation 16. PCC rules distinguish a cell from the company excluding its cells. Regulation 24's nonregistrability route requires the stated solely indirect holding through other companies, each registrable in the relevant company. Later natural-person registrability and literal references remain reconciliation gates. [F12 Regulations 15–17, 24]

Regulation 18 prescribes distinct particulars for natural persons, the referenced other-person category and companies. Natural-person information includes names/former names, date of birth, nationality, residential/service addresses, identification number/country/issue/expiry, becoming/ceasing dates and criteria. Other categories have their own name, office, legal-form, governing-law, country, date and criteria fields. Incorporation/continuation has a deemed becoming date. These statutory information fields do not themselves establish identity verification, a public right to receive the fields or permission to collect actual identity documents here. Record readiness and reviewer sighting references only. [F12 Regulation 18]

Regulation 19 requires the specified no-registrable-person statement when the company knows or has reasonable cause. Its literal reference is **Part VI**. Regulation 18(2) literally refers to **s 148(b)(ii)–(v)** and Regulation 24 also points to s 148. Other printed anomalies include Regulation 3's subparagraph sequence, Regulation 9(3)(b)(i)'s 'company immediately below', Regulation 14(3)'s reference to guidance under (1) although the power appears in (2), and Regulation 18(3)'s (c)-to-(e) sequence. No silent renumbering or corrected substantive mapping is accepted. [F12]

### Dated business-name registration

F3 ss 2–4 contain independent name-registration branches. Individual/firm surname and permitted forename/initial rules are not the entire test: s 3(d) separately covers **every trader** within the statutory definition and exceptions. Nominees/trustees and general agents for foreign firms have separate registration and Schedule particulars. Specific court-receiver/manager and other provisos remain. Do not turn public guidance about self-named businesses into a universal registration exemption; coownership of property alone is not business.

Sections 5–8 prescribe particulars and signatures, initial application within **14 days after relevant commencement**, annual renewal at least **14 days before certificate expiry**, conditional timely-renewal continuation and changes within **14 days**, subject to the Registrar's extension power. Section 15 separately requires cessation notice within **6 months**. Those triggers are not the licensing deadlines below.

Registration does not authorise otherwise prohibited name use. Name restrictions/consents, public-index rules and the specific inspection/certificate routes remain statutory conditions. Default has conditional penalties and contract-enforcement consequences with reasonable-excuse/court-relief/opponent-pleading and counterclaim protections. It does not automatically void every contract. The Rules contain dated fees but later fees, approved forms and payment acceptance remain held. [F3 ss 1–22, Schedule and Rules]

### Dated 2018 business-licensing position

F4 reaches gainful business **in or from within** the Islands; its business definition excludes office/employment. Separate distinct undertakings require separate licences where not reasonably incidental in nature/degree to the main business, whether at the same premises or elsewhere. Application particulars, requested information/examination, conditions, nontransferability and licence grant are separate checkpoints. Changes, including specified corporate control/direction/director changes, and cessation require written notification within **30 days**. [F4 ss 2–9]

The included Exemption Order has 9 conditional classes, wider than older administrative shorthand: banking, insurance, trust, the specified exempted-company activity, company management/agent, investment services, mutual funds, fund administration and money services. Each needs its actual statutory licensing/status predicates. The historic exempted-company branch restricts Island business except with other exempted companies. No current exemption follows merely from incorporation, an overseas owner or a modern company label. [F4 s 23 and complete Order]

Regulations 3–7 require fees, sector authority and the specified Islander or non-Islander immigration/investor evidence. The investor declaration does not establish an immigration exemption by label. Reserved activities retain the dated 26 March 2014 grandfather branch and conditional discretionary approval; restricted activities require Cabinet consultation. The complete retained lists do not establish a current 2026 assignment. Fee-method paragraphs address selling area, excluded areas and aggregation, proration to 1 April with part months counted as whole months, annual fees and late payment. Fee Table A is not fully qualified; no exact current business fee is supplied.

Dated suspension/revocation has notice and representation conditions. Sections 13–15 provide a 30-day Magistrates Court appeal from notification, with a stay only for suspension/revocation appeals. These are selected historical rules, not a conclusion about an actual appeal or the later amended regime. [F4 ss 10–24, Regulations and selected schedules]

### Dated BO disclosure and restricted public access

F9's LN 62/2024 appoints **1 December 2024** for Ordinance 41/2024. Substituted s 159(1) specifies Police, the AML Committee, FIA, Integrity Commission, Attorney General's Chambers, the designated tax-information authority, Registrar of Lands, procurement entity and a person/authority prescribed under the BO Regulations, subject to the concluding request condition. New (2A)–(2B) address access/search and the specified functions, crime, national-security, cooperation and statistical purposes. Subsections (2C)–(2E) provide regulation-making/public-disclosure, restriction/prohibition and fee powers. They do not themselves authorise unrestricted public BO disclosure. Contravening the (2A) search restriction has a summary maximum of **$50,000, 12 months' imprisonment or both**. [F9; F1 s 159]

Unchanged parts of s 159 retain timely-access, relevant disclosure, knowledge/suspicion/prejudice, legal-advice/proceedings, criminal-purpose and defence conditions. The printed (4) starts 'Subject to subsection (4)', and (6) refers to (4) for the criminal-purpose exception. Preserve those anomalies; do not execute an affected disclosure on an assumed correction. F12 Regulation 25 originally confined the approved-form request to Police. F13 deletes only that police phrase; the approved-form and s 159(1) requirements remain. No currently approved request form is established here. [F1 s 159; F12 Regulation 25; F13 Regulation 3]

F13 expressly commences **30 June 2025**, before F2. Its restriction procedure concerns an individual who is, or is proposed to be, registrable and reasonably believes public disclosure associated with the company creates the specified serious risk of kidnapping, extortion, violence, intimidation or similar danger/harm to the individual or household. It requires evidence and an **$800 administration fee**. Notify changed information in writing without delay upon awareness. An application is made only when registered by the Commission; whilst that made application is considered, the concerned public disclosure is prohibited. Sending a draft or payment does not establish that event. [F13 Regulations 26–27]

The Commission can require further evidence and make the specified risk referrals, including the identified domestic bodies, foreign enforcement/FIU and other determined persons. Application, supporting evidence and notices are not publicly inspectable. Written determination notice is due within **7 working days after determination**, not 7 days after application. A refusal requires reasons. Approval lasts **3 years from notice**, subject to earlier written wish/revocation. The Schedule is a **restriction application**, not a public-access application: it requests sensitive identity, address/contact, conviction, control, risk, evidence and related-application information, submitter relationship and separate dated submitter/applicant signatures. Address or email is available only as accepted. No real identity material is submitted by this reference. [F13 Regulation 28 and full Schedule]

Regulation 29 permits revocation on its specified imprisonment, dishonesty, extended-UK-sanctions and newly known information grounds, including stated historical and related-individual circumstances. Notice of intention and proposed effective date, representations **28 days from the specified date**, consideration of timely representations/relevant matters and the separate no-representations power are distinct steps. Decision notice is within **7 working days after decision** and includes reasons. Following revocation, release remains subject to Part VI and the specified timing; revocation does not confer unrestricted publication. [F13 Regulation 29]

Foreign competent AML/predicate-offence/terrorist-financing authorities and law enforcement have Regulation 30's equivalent-access route. The public route in Regulations 31–33 separately requires legitimate interest through the specified journalist/bona fide academic, civil-society or actual/potential business-relationship/transaction category **and** its AML, predicate-offence or terrorist-financing purpose. An applicable granted restriction prohibits the concerned public disclosure. Electronic-portal credentials, identity, purpose/context, supporting information and reasonably required evidence plus an **$250 administration fee** are required. Multi-company requests require the specified connected allegation, not common ownership alone. These statutory requirements do not establish a currently working portal or an accepted application. [F13 Regulations 30–33]

A complete application and fee lead to evaluation and a grant with information or refusal with reasons. The written information/notice or refusal reasons are due within **7 working days after decision**; no application-to-decision deadline is established. Public individual fields exclude a child under 18 and comprise name, country of residence, nationality, month/year of birth and nature of control. The other printed category points to **s 147(b)(ii)–(vii)** and has name, office, legal form, registration number where held and nature of control. F2 subsequently replaces s 147 with natural-person wording without those subitems. Do not repair this category, disclose full birth/ID/address fields as public data or infer a general publication right. [F13 Regulations 33–34; F2]

Regulation 36 confines use to the permitted purpose and imposes confidentiality/privacy and onward-disclosure conditions. Required-by-law/legal-proceeding onward disclosure has its recipient-record and Commission-on-request conditions. The printed financial penalty for failure of (1) and (2) is **$25,000**, not a daily criminal fine. F2's later s 159(1A) business/DNFBP fields, including full birth date and government-ID information, are a different route and must not be merged with the public fields. Its access/date/time/purpose records under (2F) also remain distinct. [F13 Regulation 36; F2]

F1 ss 160 and 302 and F9 supply earlier regulatory powers; F2 later adds s 160(5)'s financial-penalty/offence provisions. F13's literal enabling recital prints '159, 160 302' without a conjunction. Do not treat a typographical anomaly as an invalidity finding, or use F2's later commencement alone to prove an earlier instrument's authority. Complete chain, incorporated definitions, consultation/current-form evidence and the unresolved cross-references remain held. No actual access, restriction or disclosure decision is authorised. [F1 ss 160, 302; F9; F13; F2]

### The enacted 2026 licensing branch remains commencement-dependent

F5 is positive enacted evidence beyond the March consultation. Its s 1 requires a day appointed by Governor's Gazette Notice; printed assent/publication alone does not establish that day. No such notice was qualified. Neither assume F5 operative from January nor assume the 2018 position current because commencement is unresolved.

Subject to commencement and complete incorporated law, F5 adds:

- Exclusive, Reserved, Restricted and General categories, with stated Islander ownership requirements of **100%, at least 60% and at least 50%** for the first 3. Category assignments require Regulations.
- The 1 April–31 March licensing cycle, grace/termination/restoration, fee/tax debt conditions and new applications for ownership changes.
- Substance-over-form/fronting rules, effective control, Reserved-category voting/management/quorum conditions, foreign-transfer approval/first-refusal and financing/disclosure rules. Labels and nominee arrangements alone do not settle compliance.
- Revised suspension/revocation, ticketing and transition/surcharge provisions. Their exceptions and interactions must be established before application.

The operative ticket rule uses **14 working days from receipt**; its form says **14 days from date**. Other literal references differ: new 6B's waiver points to 3(4), while inserted ownership is 2(4); new 6(5A) points to (1); ticket rows identify 7A rather than inserted ownership 7B and 9(4) rather than retained 9(2); savings 15 refers to 2(3) 'of this Act' and 11(2A), neither matching the identified body. Do not silently repair or choose a favourable deadline. Existing-licence saving and up-to-12-month ownership compliance/additional 100% annual surcharge need reconciliation, not a blanket transition. [F5 complete ss 1–16 and schedules]

F6 separately provides narrow **2026-27 annual renewal penalty relief**: notwithstanding its specified Schedule 2 paragraph, payment of the annual fee **in full on or before 31 May 2026** avoids that paragraph's penalty. Otherwise the unpaid fee/part increases **10% per month or part**. Its own commencement is Gazette publication, whose event remains held. It does not extend every licence, tax, filing deadline or business authorisation. A March administrative payment warning cannot override this later positive source. [F6]

## Worked cases

### Conditional surcharge and historical document-readiness checks

E1 assumes the selected dated F1/F11 rule applies for a stipulated period after every source gate has been independently established. A registered agent has 3 documents that may each be filed electronically and deliberately chooses another method: the incremental surcharge is 3 × 50 = 150. If that same agent instead files all 3 electronically, the count filed by another method is 0 and this particular surcharge is 0. The conditional cash difference is 150; all base fees, other charges and channel costs are excluded and unqualified. This is a channel comparison only, not a recommendation, actual fee, payable, filing or assurance that electronic filing works. If electronic filing is unavailable or the actor is not that registered agent, these stipulated branches do not apply. [F1 Regulation 32; F11]

E2 uses only the historical March 2018 Form 9/9A capacities in F14. For 8 invented member entries, the main form has 3 slots and each continuation has 2. Three continuations provide 9 slots in total, leaving 1 unused. The ceiling operation counts whole continuation sheets; it is not a money-rounding rule. No personal data is entered, no current form or acceptance is established and no fee follows. Neither example supplies the missing 5 complete formation positions, comparison or executed outputs. [F14]

### Limited voluntary-liquidation qualification

F1 s 238 requires the Commission's **prior written consent** for the regulated company's voluntary-liquidation resolution and approval of the proposed individual. A contravening resolution or unapproved appointment is void. Regulation 19's disqualification, age, bankruptcy, prior-director/financial-manager and close-family conditions remain independent. Regulation 20 reaches a company that holds or has ever held any of its 7 licence classes and requires the voluntary liquidator, or at least 1 where liquidators are joint, to be a licensed insolvency practitioner, subject to the amended insurance limb. [F1 s 238, Regulations 19–20]

These exceptions concern only the insurance limb: they do not remove the other licence limbs, general eligibility, prior consent or proposed-person approval. The full insurance, agent and insolvency regimes remain unqualified; no actual liquidation workflow is complete. [F11]

### BO notices and distinct retention clocks

F1 s 157 requires the applicable company to give its initial prescribed notice within **14 days after incorporation** and changes within **14 days after the recorded particulars change**; it separately allows the specified no-registrable-person statement and provides for Commission registration on receipt. Section 158 protects the register, supplied information and requests/disclosures, including the fact of a request, subject to s 159. Its summary maximum is $50,000. F12 Regulation 22 requires s 157 notices to be delivered **only by the registered agent**. These are not the company/person discovery and confirmation duties below. [F1 ss 157–160; F12 Regulations 19, 22]

F12 Regulation 21 gives the required s 153 notice contents and **4 weeks from receipt** for compliance. F2, commencing **31 July 2025**, substitutes s 153 and changes s 154. The company's notice duty depends on knowledge/reasonable cause, with the stated as-soon-as-possible requirement and no later than **30 days after it learns or has reasonable cause**. Its confirmation-request wording uses **30 days from the notice date**. The company's already-informed exception and the registrable person's separate knowledge, unchanged-register and no-notice conditions remain material. Do not flatten these into 30 days from every underlying event or treat the 2017 receipt-based period as identical to the amended notice-date period. Literal s 153(2)(a) says 'legal person' whilst amended s 147 uses natural persons. Reconcile the operative notice and incorporated law before calculating an actual deadline. [F2 complete Act; F12 Regulation 21]

F2 also adds cooperation under s 149A and extends the related offence provision. Existing foreign companies and the specified registrable-person change provisions have separate **3-month** transitions from commencement. Those transitions do not establish an exemption for a current case. The LN 24/2025 notice appoints 31 July 2025; the index label does not identify a second Companies Act numbered 24. [F2]
