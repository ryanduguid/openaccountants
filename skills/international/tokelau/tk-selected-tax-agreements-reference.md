# Tokelau and selected New Zealand tax agreements

Load `workflow-base`. Record the payment or tax period, domestic tax residence, income source, place of activity and the agreement relied on. For an information request, also record the tax and the location or control of the information.

## Establish the territorial scope

Section 3 of the Tokelau Act 1948 declares that Tokelau forms part of New Zealand. Section 6 requires express provision for New Zealand statute law to be in force in Tokelau. Under s 8(2), a reference to New Zealand in an Act generally excludes Tokelau unless a contrary intention appears. Read each agreement’s territorial definition and effective provisions for its own purposes. [Tokelau Act 1948, ss 3, 6 and 8(2)](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/1948/24/en/latest/)

## New Zealand–Australia double tax convention

Article 3(1)(b) excludes Tokelau from the convention’s definition of New Zealand. Article 4(1) separately tests whether the person is liable to tax as a resident under the law of a contracting state and excludes a person liable there solely in respect of local-source income. For dual residence, confirm the applicable Article 4 provisions, including the Multilateral Instrument modification for non-individuals. [Double Taxation Relief (Australia) Order 2010, Schedule arts 1, 3 and 4](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/secondary-legislation/pco-drafted/2010/13/en/latest/) · [Inland Revenue, agreement dates and Multilateral Instrument](https://www.taxpolicy.ird.govt.nz/tax-treaties/australia)

Article 2 covers Australian federal income tax, including resource rent tax on offshore petroleum exploration or production projects, and Australian federal fringe benefits tax. It covers New Zealand income tax, including fringe benefit tax. It also covers later identical or substantially similar taxes imposed under Australian federal or New Zealand law in addition to or in place of those taxes. Check the separate scopes of the non-discrimination, information exchange and collection assistance provisions for other taxes. [Double Taxation Relief (Australia) Order 2010, Schedule arts 2, 24, 26 and 27](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/secondary-legislation/pco-drafted/2010/13/en/latest/)

The convention entered into force on 19 March 2010. Its withholding provisions generally took effect on 1 May 2010 in both states. Check Article 30 for the separate effective dates for other taxes. Inland Revenue records later effective dates for the Multilateral Instrument. [Inland Revenue, agreement dates](https://www.taxpolicy.ird.govt.nz/tax-treaties/australia)

## New Zealand–Turks and Caicos Islands information exchange

Article 4(1)(a) of the information exchange agreement also excludes Tokelau from its definition of New Zealand. Article 2 requires the information to be in the requested party’s territory or held or controlled by a person subject to that party’s jurisdiction. The nationality and residence of the subject and holder do not determine that test. Article 1 limits exchange to information foreseeably relevant to the covered taxes. Article 3 covers New Zealand taxes of every kind and description. For the Turks and Caicos Islands it names passenger tax, stamp duties and hotel and restaurant tax. For both parties it also covers later taxes that are identical or substantially similar and are imposed in addition to or in place of those taxes, and other taxes agreed by exchange of letters. Taxes imposed by municipalities, local authorities or possessions of either party are excluded. [Double Tax Agreements (Turks and Caicos Islands) Order 2010, Schedule arts 1–4](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/secondary-legislation/pco-drafted/2010/156/en/2010-06-08.pdf)

The agreement entered into force on 23 December 2016. Article 14 applies it to taxable periods beginning on or after 1 April 2017, or charges arising from that date where there is no taxable period. Relevant information may predate entry into force. This agreement authorises exchanges between competent authorities within its terms. Determine the underlying liability and any relief separately. [Agreement art 14](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/secondary-legislation/pco-drafted/2010/156/en/2010-06-08.pdf) · [Inland Revenue, agreement dates](https://www.taxpolicy.ird.govt.nz/tax-treaties/turks-and-caicos-islands)

## Fictional file checks

- An Australian payer receives an address in Tokelau as the only evidence for a treaty withholding claim. The address alone does not establish residence in a contracting state. Obtain evidence of domestic and treaty residence and classify the payment before applying a rate.
- A record relating to a covered Turks and Caicos stamp duty charge is held by a person in New Zealand who is subject to its jurisdiction. A Tokelau address for the person the record concerns does not defeat the Article 2 test. Verify the period, relevance, custody and the competent authority’s request before reaching a conclusion.
