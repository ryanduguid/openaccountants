# Tokelau land and succession

Load `workflow-base`. Record the ownership and location of each asset, the date of any transfer or death, the person’s last habitual residence and any will. Identify the relevant village and whether its custom governs the land. This reference covers the position recorded in the 2016 compilation. Check the subsequent rules and the competent village or court for the actual matter. [Tokelau rules listed in force at 1 May 2016](https://www.paclii.org/tk/indices/legis/rules-in-force.html)

## Classify the land

Rule 15 reproduces the local Land Rules in the constitutional compilation. Subject to the governing rules, the Taupulega controls land. Customary land is held under village custom. The rule assigns ownership questions about non-customary land to the High Court under English common law. It also restricts the transfer of land or an interest in land to a person who is not Tokelauan. Confirm the interest, classification and applicable current rules before relying on the restriction. [Tokelauan 2016 compilation, r 15(1)–(5)](https://www.paclii.org/tk/legis/consol_act_2016/tfat214.pdf) · [Government of Tokelau law team, English text at pp 143–144 and land history at p 38](https://www.tokelau.org.nz/site/tokelau/files/Tokelau%20A%20History%20of%20Government%20FINAL.pdf)

The New Zealand Tokelau Amendment Act 1967 was repealed on 20 September 2007. Its historical land provisions should be read for the period in which they operated. The full self-determination constitutional package was conditional on the referendum. The archived land rule above is the local rule identified separately in the compilation. [New Zealand legislation, repeal annotation](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/1967/38/en/2007-09-20.pdf) · [Government of Tokelau, constitutional package summary](https://www.tokelau.org.nz/About%2BUs/Government/Self%2BDetermination%2BPackage/Summary%2Bof%2Bthe%2BConstitution%2Bof%2BTokelau.html)

## Identify the succession assets and route

- The Succession Rules cover movable property owned by the deceased and immovable property outside Tokelau custom. Record customary land separately and confirm the village’s rules and custom.
- If a person habitually resident in a Tokelau village dies without a will, the kaiga distributes the property in scope according to custom. The Taupulega decides a dispute about that distribution, with its decision final under r 3(2). If there is no kaiga successor, the village is the ultimate successor under r 4.
- The rules provide for a written will signed and dated at the foot or end. For the witnessed route, 2 witnesses sign and date in the testator’s presence. A beneficiary witness’s own provision has no effect under r 9(3). Apply the proxy-signature conditions in r 8 when someone signs at the testator’s direction. The holograph route requires the testator’s own handwriting, signature and date.

[Tokelau Succession Rules 2004, rr 2–10, 2016 compilation](https://www.paclii.org/tk/legis/consol_act_2016/sr2004187/)

## Identify the law for assets across borders

For a case with foreign elements, r 157 distinguishes 3 questions. Movable title follows the law where the asset was when transferred. Succession to a movable asset follows the deceased’s last habitual residence. The law where immovable property is situated governs both its title and its succession. Determine habitual residence from evidence. The Tokelau rules on procedure, proof of foreign law and public policy also apply. [Crimes, Procedure and Evidence Rules 2003, rr 153–154 and 157, 2016 compilation](https://www.paclii.org/tk/legis/consol_act_2016/cpaer2003302.pdf)

## Fictional estate inventory

Assume a person habitually resident in a Tokelau village dies without a will in the reference period. The inventory includes tools owned by the deceased, a customary land interest in Tokelau and registered land abroad. File the movable tools with the local intestacy analysis. Refer the customary land interest for village classification and custom. Verify the foreign land’s title and succession under the law of its location. Document the evidence before recognising an estate entitlement or allocating proceeds. [Succession Rules 2004, rr 2–4](https://www.paclii.org/tk/legis/consol_act_2016/sr2004187/) · [Crimes, Procedure and Evidence Rules 2003, rr 153–154 and 157](https://www.paclii.org/tk/legis/consol_act_2016/cpaer2003302.pdf)

## Remaining coverage

Verify current and village-specific law, any appointment or filing procedure, proof of title, practitioner review and the tax treatment of the actual transfer or estate. The archived property and succession rules do not establish a tax rate, valuation basis, registration fee or exemption.
