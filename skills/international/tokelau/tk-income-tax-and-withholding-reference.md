# Tokelau income tax and source withholding

Load `workflow-base`. Record the service location, whether services are performed in the service of Tokelau, payment type, residency and dates. The General Fono’s Income Tax Rules 1997 are listed in force at 1 May 2016. Sections 11–12 of the New Zealand Tokelau Amendment Act 1996 repealed the earlier Act and revoked the 1994 levy order, with a transition preserving the earlier levy until the General Fono made an income levy or tax rule. Check subsequent rules and instructions before applying this historical reference to a later period. [Tokelau Table of Rules in Force at 1 May 2016](https://www.paclii.org/tk/indices/legis/rules-in-force.html) · [Tokelau Amendment Act 1996, ss 11–12](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/1996/31/en/latest/)

## Remuneration and resident income

- Under rule 2(1), remuneration for services performed in Tokelau or in the service of Tokelau is taxed in Tokelau under the Schedule. Tax is levied at source for the Administration of Tokelau. Preserve the payment and withholding records.
- Under rule 2(2), a Tokelau resident pays the Administration tax on net income received during the financial year under the table of rates. The rule requires payment by 1 October in the following financial year. Money received as family remittances is excluded for this paragraph. Rules 1–2 of the Interpretation Rules give a default 1 July to 30 June financial year, subject to express or contextual variation. Confirm the applicable year before setting the payment date. [Interpretation Rules 2003, rr 1–2](https://www.paclii.org/tk/legis/consol_act_2016/ir2003240/) Establish the facts supporting residency, net income and each receipt’s classification.

The 2016 Schedule lists 6 annual-income rates from 5% to 25%. The 2005 compilation described the schedule by reference to gross payments of salary, wages or honorarium. The 2015–16 household survey describes the Government’s top-bracket treatment in that survey period. Confirm the operative annualisation, rate application, credit for source withholding, payment channel and period boundaries before quantifying liability. [Income Tax Rules 1997, r 2 and Schedule, 2016](https://www.paclii.org/tk/legis/consol_act_2016/itr1997165/) · [2005 compilation](https://www.paclii.org/tk/legis/consol_act_2005/itr1997165/) · [Tokelau National Statistics Office, 2015–16 household survey](https://www.tokelau.org.nz/site/tokelau/files/TokelauNSO/HIES/2015-16TokelauHIES-Report4Bprint.pdf)

For July 2024 to June 2025, the Tokelau National Statistics Office cash data label receipts from tax on individual income and profits as community services levy. Reconcile that reporting label with the operative local rules and any later instruments before identifying the legal basis for a payment in that period. [OECD Revenue Statistics in Asia and the Pacific 2026, Table 4.35](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/revenue-statistics-in-asia-and-the-pacific-2026_065aa566-en/full-report/country-tables-tax-revenues-2000-2024_b895f288.html)

## Companies and other bodies under the archived rule

The 2016 compilation of rule 2(2) applies to each person resident in Tokelau. The Interpretation Rules apply unless the rule expressly provides otherwise or its context requires another reading. The definition of 'person' includes a corporation sole and corporate or unincorporated bodies. Check the body’s legal form, residency and the applicable rule before applying the resident-income provision. The archived texts leave corporate residence, company-specific rates and filing procedure unresolved. [Income Tax Rules 1997, r 2(2)](https://www.paclii.org/tk/legis/consol_act_2016/itr1997165/) · [Interpretation Rules 2003, rr 1–2](https://www.paclii.org/tk/legis/consol_act_2016/ir2003240/)

For July 2024 to June 2025 the Tokelau National Statistics Office cash figures show zero in the OECD aggregate category for corporate income and profits. The detailed corporate profits and corporate capital gains figures are unavailable. Check liability from the governing law and the taxpayer’s circumstances. [OECD Revenue Statistics in Asia and the Pacific 2026, Table 4.35](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/revenue-statistics-in-asia-and-the-pacific-2026_065aa566-en/full-report/country-tables-tax-revenues-2000-2024_b895f288.html)

## Fictional records

In a period governed by the archived rules, a Tokelau resident receives a salary for work in Tokelau and a separately identifiable family remittance during the financial year ended 30 June 2016. Assume the default financial year applies. The resident payment date on those facts is 1 October 2016. Record the salary and any tax levied at source. Exclude the family remittance when evaluating the resident paragraph, supported by its payment records. Verify how source withholding is credited before completing the liability and payment schedule. [Income Tax Rules 1997, r 2(1)–(2)](https://www.paclii.org/tk/legis/consol_act_2016/itr1997165/)
