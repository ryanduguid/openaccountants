# Spain tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record where the taxpayer lives or operates, the legal form, each income source, the applicable treaty and the tax period. Check the competent tax authority before preparing a return.

## Territorial reach

In the common regime, personal income tax (IRPF) generally covers a resident's worldwide income. Corporate income tax generally covers all income of a resident entity subject to that tax. Non-resident income tax applies to qualifying Spanish-source income, with separate permanent establishment and other source rules. Check treaty limits, the Basque and Navarre foral regimes and the special rules for the Canary Islands, Ceuta and Melilla. The VAT territory has its own boundaries. [IRPF Law, arts 2–5](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764&p=20261118&tn=0#a2) · [Corporate Income Tax Law, arts 2 and 7](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2014-12328#a7) · [Non-Resident Income Tax Law, art 13](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2004-4527#a13) · [VAT Law, art 3](https://www.boe.es/eli/es/l/1992/12/28/37/con/20260930#a3)

## Residence

For individuals, test more than 183 days in Spain in the calendar year and the main base or centre of activities or economic interests. Check the rules for sporadic absences, the rebuttable family presumption and treaty residence. An entity is generally resident if it was formed under Spanish law, has its registered office in Spain or is effectively managed there. [IRPF Law, art 9](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764&p=20261118&tn=0#a9) · [Corporate Income Tax Law, art 8](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2014-12328#a8)

## Taxpayer types

A sole trader usually reports their economic activity in personal income tax. Companies within the corporate income tax charge report their own income. For a civil partnership, check legal personality and whether its object is commercial. A civil partnership with legal personality and a commercial object generally falls under corporate tax. In a qualifying attribution arrangement, income goes to the partners or other participants. For a foreign trust, establish the settlor, beneficiaries, governing terms and event. The central tax tribunal's resolution 00/05163/2024 discusses the attribution of trust income to the settlor in the cited consultation and the treatment of transfers on the facts. [IRPF Law, arts 8, 27 and 87–89](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764&p=20261118&tn=0#a8) · [AEAT, attribution arrangements](https://www3.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/irpf-2025/c10-regimenes-especiales-imputacion-atribucion-rentas/regimen-atribucion-rentas/entidades-regimen-atribucion-rentas.html) · [TEAC, resolution 00/05163/2024](https://serviciostelematicosext.hacienda.gob.es/TEAC/DYCTEA/textoresolucion.aspx?id=00%2F05163%2F2024%2F00%2F0%2F1)

## Currency

Spain uses the euro (EUR). Record the original currency, applicable conversion rule and conversion date for each foreign-currency amount. [Council Regulation (EC) No 974/98, art 2 and Annex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/1998/974/2026-01-01/eng)

## Assessment periods

IRPF ordinarily follows the calendar year and accrues on 31 December. Death can end the period early. The corporate tax period follows the entity's financial year and lasts at most 12 months, with statutory events that end it early. Check the non-resident and payment rules separately. [IRPF Law, arts 12–13](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764&p=20261118&tn=0#a12) · [Corporate Income Tax Law, arts 27–28](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2014-12328#a27)

## Responsible authorities

The Agencia Estatal de Administración Tributaria (AEAT) administers state taxes and customs. Check the division of responsibility with the autonomous communities for partly ceded IRPF and with the foral administrations where their rules apply. The place of activity and the tax concerned can affect which authority is competent. [AEAT, functions](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/educacion-civico-tributaria/programa-educacion-civico-tributaria/quien-recauda-impuestos-controla-aduanas/contenidos/funciones-administracion-tributaria.html) · [IRPF Law, arts 3–4](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2006-20764&p=20261118&tn=0#a3)

## Fictional intake

An individual moves to Madrid during the year and keeps a share in a civil partnership abroad. Establish presence, family and economic ties, treaty residence, the partnership's legal and tax classification and the source of each amount before choosing the taxpayer and period.
