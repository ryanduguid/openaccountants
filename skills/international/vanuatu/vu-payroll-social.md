# Vanuatu Payroll & Social Contributions

## VNPF social-security contributions and payroll

Vanuatu has no PAYE income tax withholding because there is no personal income tax. The principal payroll obligation is the compulsory contribution to the Vanuatu National Provident Fund (VNPF), a retirement savings scheme funded jointly by employer and employee. Note a legislated rate increase takes effect from January 2026.

- **Payroll income-tax withholding (PAYE)** — None — there is no personal income tax, so no PAYE withholding from wages  _(Vanuatu tax legislation (https://www.globalcitizensolutions.com/vanuatu-taxes/))_
- **VNPF total contribution rate (2025)** — 8 % (of the employee's gross remuneration; applies up to the December 2025 contribution (payable January 2026))  _(National Provident Fund Act (https://www.vnpf.com.vu/employers.html))_
- **VNPF employer contribution (2025)** — 4 % (of the employee's gross remuneration)  _(National Provident Fund Act (https://www.vnpf.com.vu/employers.html))_
- **VNPF employee contribution (2025)** — 4 % (of the employee's gross remuneration)  _(National Provident Fund Act (https://www.vnpf.com.vu/employers.html))_
- **VNPF total contribution rate (from 2026)** — 12 % (of gross remuneration, effective 1 January 2026 (first applies to the January 2026 contribution payable February 2026))  _(National Provident Fund (Amendment) Act 2025 (https://www.dailypost.vu/news/new-vnpf-contribute-rate-effective-soon/article_8c91a19e-005e-5cc7-ba35-a6b0f32ea789.html))_
- **VNPF employer contribution (from 2026)** — 6 % (of gross remuneration)  _(National Provident Fund (Amendment) Act 2025 (https://www.dailypost.vu/news/new-vnpf-contribute-rate-effective-soon/article_8c91a19e-005e-5cc7-ba35-a6b0f32ea789.html))_
- **VNPF employee contribution (from 2026)** — 6 % (of gross remuneration)  _(National Provident Fund (Amendment) Act 2025 (https://www.dailypost.vu/news/new-vnpf-contribute-rate-effective-soon/article_8c91a19e-005e-5cc7-ba35-a6b0f32ea789.html))_

### Read from the Act, which the Fund publishes itself

> The VNPF publishes the ***Vanuatu National Provident Fund Act* [CAP. 189], Consolidated
> Edition 2026** at [vnpf.com.vu](https://www.vnpf.com.vu/vnpf-act-cap-189.html) — 29
> pages, clean text. Every row above was sourced to an employer-information page or a
> newspaper report. The statute **confirms the 12% / 6% / 6% figures** and answers both
> rows this guide had marked *(approx — confirm)*.

- **12%, split in half, and the Act says so twice** — Section 25(1): on each occasion remuneration is paid, a contribution is payable at **12 percent** of that remuneration, *"of which half is to be paid by the employer and half by the employee"*. Section 25(1A), *"to avoid doubt"*: the employer pays **6 percent** and the employee **6 percent**. This is the operative rate in the 2026 consolidation; the 8% rows above are **historical and remain secondary-sourced** percent  _([VNPF Act CAP. 189, Consolidated Edition 2026, s. 25(1)–(1A)](https://www.vnpf.com.vu/uploads/9/5/0/5/95052148/vanuatu_national_provident_fund_consolidated_2026.pdf))_
- **⚠ VNPF contribution base — there is a floor, and it is per employer** — Section 25(2): **no contribution is payable** in respect of an employee in any month in which the total remuneration paid to that employee **by any one employer** is less than **VT 3,000**. This guide asked to "confirm any minimum/maximum earnings limits"; the minimum is VT 3,000 a month and it is tested **employer by employer**, so an employee with two small jobs can fall below it twice. **No maximum appears in section 25** VT  _([VNPF Act s. 25(2)](https://www.vnpf.com.vu/uploads/9/5/0/5/95052148/vanuatu_national_provident_fund_consolidated_2026.pdf))_
- **The employee's half is deducted by the employer, and no agreement can change that** — Section 25(3): *"Notwithstanding the provisions of any other law or any agreement to the contrary, the employer shall deduct"* the employee's share from remuneration  _([VNPF Act s. 25(3)](https://www.vnpf.com.vu/uploads/9/5/0/5/95052148/vanuatu_national_provident_fund_consolidated_2026.pdf))_
- **VNPF remittance deadline — by the end of the following month** — Section 26(1): the employer pays the Board the contributions payable in any month, for and by all employees, **by the end of the following month**, in the prescribed manner and form. This guide asked to "confirm exact day of month"; the Act fixes a month-end, not a day  _([VNPF Act s. 26(1)](https://www.vnpf.com.vu/uploads/9/5/0/5/95052148/vanuatu_national_provident_fund_consolidated_2026.pdf))_
- **⚠ Late payment carries a 5% surcharge per month, compounding by month started** — Section 26(2): an employer who fails to pay some or all contributions within the section 26(1) time is liable to a surcharge of **5 per cent in respect of each month or part of a month** after the due date for which they remain unpaid, subject to a proviso setting a floor on small amounts. "Part of a month" means a day's delay costs a full 5%  _([VNPF Act s. 26(2)](https://www.vnpf.com.vu/uploads/9/5/0/5/95052148/vanuatu_national_provident_fund_consolidated_2026.pdf))_
- **Contributions are split three ways inside the Fund** — Section 30(2): each member has a **retirement account**, an **investment account** and a **medi-save account**, credited with **50%**, **25%** and **25%** of contributions paid by and in respect of them, plus interest at the rate the Board declares. These accounts accumulate only in respect of contributions made **on and after 1 January 2008**  _([VNPF Act s. 30(2), (2A)](https://www.vnpf.com.vu/uploads/9/5/0/5/95052148/vanuatu_national_provident_fund_consolidated_2026.pdf))_
- **Fund income, interest and withdrawals are tax-exempt by statute** — Section 46: all income of the Fund, all interest paid or credited to members, all sums withdrawn or paid out, and **every instrument made or executed by or on behalf of the Fund** are *"exempted from all taxes, levies or charges imposed by law"*. That is a statutory exemption in its own right, not merely a consequence of Vanuatu having no income tax  _([VNPF Act s. 46](https://www.vnpf.com.vu/uploads/9/5/0/5/95052148/vanuatu_national_provident_fund_consolidated_2026.pdf))_
- **An employee's bankruptcy does not stop the deductions** — Section 47: the bankruptcy of an employee does not affect payment of contributions from their remuneration, and those payments continue notwithstanding any other law  _([VNPF Act s. 47](https://www.vnpf.com.vu/uploads/9/5/0/5/95052148/vanuatu_national_provident_fund_consolidated_2026.pdf))_
