# South Georgia and South Sandwich Islands: ordinary income tax reference v0.1

> **Conditional Tier 2 draft.** This is a source and intake reference with fictional annual-charge examples. It is not a completed return, payslip, withholding calculation, payment demand or professional sign-off. Load `workflow-base` and `income-tax-workflow-base`; their source gates, confirmed profile, refusals, outputs and checks remain mandatory.

## Scope and source conditions

The selected component concerns one individual, one calendar tax year and complete ordinary income earned in respect of territorial work. Before applying the rate, independently establish at least 183 days of presence in that year, the work condition and the absence of every applicable exemption. Employment, a services contract or another engagement can satisfy the work condition. An ordinary land-based role is an example premise, not proof that no exemption applies. Mixed land/vessel roles and uncertain exemption classes require review.

Sections 5-7 establish a charge, while sections 10-12 govern notification, expected quarterly payments and other collection. An annual reference amount does not determine a payslip deduction, refund recipient, enforceable payment, net pay or deadline. In particular, section 12(5) qualifies the employee's liability to pay when no qualifying demand is made. Keep the statutory charge and collection conclusions separately identified.

The source audit accessed the official ordinances, secondary-legislation and Gazette indexes on 30 October 2026, the complete 2016 Gazette and all 16 later Gazette files then listed for 2017-2026, ending with Gazette No. 4 of 26 August 2026. Native extraction covered all 266 later physical pages; all 10 pages flagged for OCR were processed. The selected material disclosed Collector appointments, but no amendment to the selected income-tax rate, presence test or exemptions. This bounded audit is not a consolidated-law certificate. The indexes themselves describe their lists as informal and identify Gazette publication as the record. Establish the complete law applicable to the requested period, later changes, territorial application and facts before actual work.

No UK or Falkland Islands income tax rate, allowance, rounding rule, payroll practice or filing procedure is imported. This reference does not determine companies, business profits, investment or property income, treaty relief, other jurisdictions' taxes, social contributions, formation, financial reporting or other compliance obligations. Their applicability remains to be investigated separately.

## Intake and ordered mechanics

Reuse redacted documents before grouped questions. Establish the requested obligation and calendar year; complete operative law; territorial location and work; all visits across employers; engagement and exemption classes; complete remuneration, emoluments, allowances and genuine reimbursements; earning/attribution period; currency and lawful precision; employer notifications, quarterly expectations, actual deductions/payments and demands; procedures; reviewer and required tools. Present the inferred profile and wait for confirmation. Confirmation supplies facts, not unknown law. Keep identifiers, bank details, signatures and authentication with the professional.

1. **Period and presence.** The tax year is 1 January-31 December. Count arrival and departure days as whole days, include unlawful presence and aggregate separate stays in the same tax year. Count each actual calendar day once. Do not combine years, use a rolling 183-day window or test each employer separately. The condition is **183 days or more**. The Territory refers to the South Georgia and South Sandwich Islands Order 1985 as amended; establish actual location and application rather than inventing a maritime boundary. _(L1 section 4 and section 6(1)-(2))_
2. **Territorial work.** Presence alone is insufficient: income must be earned in respect of work carried out in the Territory. Preparatory or incidental activities outside it do not necessarily remove territorial work. The contract label is not decisive. Do not treat the residence condition as a worldwide-income charge, or tax only income earned after the 183rd day. Uncertain work allocation, delayed/cross-year earnings and mixed territorial duties remain held. _(L1 section 4(2), section 6(1) and section 6(3))_
3. **Exempt individual classes.** Regulation 4 exempts individuals working as crew or observers on fishing vessels or vessels supporting/accompanying them; individuals working on tourist vessels as crew, expedition leaders or in any other capacity; crew on vessels passing through the Territory; and serving members of Her Majesty's armed forces, including reserve or auxiliary forces, using regulation 4(1)(d)'s wording. Do not extend that Crown-force category to other countries' military services without established authority. These exemptions commenced on 1 January 2017 and continue until ended by further Regulations. Establish the complete applicable class and period before using an ordinary non-exempt branch. Do not assume that every vessel worker, every visitor or every land-based worker has the same treatment. _(L1 section 8 and regulations 2-4)_
4. **Establish complete S.** S is the independently established nonnegative annual chargeable income for the qualified non-exempt branch, in established GBP. Include remuneration, emoluments and allowances of any kind; exclude genuine reimbursement of expenditure incurred. A reimbursement exclusion is not a general deduction for an employee's expenses. Do not substitute bank receipts or net pay, deduct tax from S, invent a personal allowance or apportion the annual income by days after the threshold. Unresolved valuation, expense treatment, loss, relief or income completeness prevents acceptance of S. _(L1 sections 5-7; L2 page 2)_
5. **Preserve precision and compute T.** Retain exact S and its established input precision separately. The fictional transport notation is an ungrouped base-10 numeral, optionally with a decimal point and digits. It does not establish statutory precision. T = 7 x S / 100 is the exact **unrounded annual charge reference**, before any lawful rounding, collection or settlement determination. Reject missing, malformed, ambiguous, negative, non-finite or mixed-currency inputs; do not clamp, coerce or round them. No maximum S or T is invented. _(L1 section 7; L3 section 3 for sterling from its stated commencement)_
6. **Keep delivery held.** Exact arithmetic, including an exact-pence product, does not supply missing tax-base, tax-result or payment precision. No rounding or conversion rule was established from the selected sources. Actual calculation delivery requires those rules, applicable law, complete intake, all later stages, outputs, checks and professional review. T is not a filing-ready financial result.

These source rules form an intake/control reference. They do not provide a complete calendar or collection calculation.
