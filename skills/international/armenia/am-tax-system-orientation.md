# Armenia tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the taxpayer, where income arose, residence facts and period. Check the applicable treaty and foreign-tax relief.

## Territorial reach

Resident organisations, registered individual entrepreneurs and notaries under the ordinary profit-tax regime account for domestic and foreign business income. Personal income of the entrepreneurs and notaries follows the individual income-tax rules. A resident individual generally accounts for taxable Armenian and foreign personal income. A nonresident individual generally accounts for taxable Armenian-source personal income.

For a nonresident carrying on business in or deriving income from Armenia, examine whether there is an Armenian permanent establishment and whether receipts are attributable to it. Armenian-source receipts of a nonresident organisation outside a permanent establishment can be taxed on a gross basis, with withholding where required. Check the domestic source rules and the applicable treaty before choosing the treatment. [Tax Code, arts 20, 27, 103–107, 132–133 and 140–142](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/228326/latest)

## Residence

An individual is generally resident with at least 183 days of actual presence in Armenia during the tax year. Both arrival and departure days count. A centre of vital interests in Armenia or government service for Armenia temporarily performed abroad can also establish residence. The domestic residence status applies for the full year if the person qualifies on any day of that year.

An ordinary organisation is resident if its location is in Armenia. The Code uses the place of state registration for an organisation and the place of state record registration for an institution, with a special rule for registered investment funds. Check treaty residence and the permanent establishment rules separately. [Tax Code, arts 21–27](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/228326/latest)

## Taxpayer types

Classify the individual, registered entrepreneur, notary, organisation or permanent establishment before selecting the relevant tax rules. General and limited partnerships under the Civil Code are commercial organisations. An Armenian-registered partnership generally follows the ordinary resident organisation rules, subject to any applicable special regime. Organisations and individual entrepreneurs can instead contract to conduct joint activity without a new legal person. That arrangement has separate rules for the participant responsible for reporting and the treatment of participants' shares. [Civil Code, arts 72 and 76–87](https://www.arlis.am/en/acts/216420) · [Tax Code, arts 21, 24, 31–32, 103–105 and 131](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/228326/latest)

## Currency

The Armenian dram (AMD) is the official currency. For tax withheld by a tax agent, the conversion date is the income payment date. For payments before 1 July 2026, use the Central Bank's average market rate published by 16:00 on the payment date. For payments from 1 July 2026, use the rate published on the preceding business day. An actual currency exchange uses the exchange-date average rate under the new rules. [Central Bank Law, arts 40–41](https://www.cba.am/Storage/EN/laws/cba_law.pdf) · [Prior Tax Code, art 16(1)(3) and (2)](https://www.arlis.am/en/acts/224990) · [Law HO-234-N, arts 2 and 33](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/226008/latest)

## Assessment periods

The tax year is the calendar year. Ordinary profit-tax computations and individual annual returns use the reporting year. Tax-agent remittances and returns can follow shorter reporting periods. Check the obligations for the taxpayer and for a first or closing period. [Tax Code, arts 4, 129, 134 and 156](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/228326/latest)

## Responsible authorities

The State Revenue Committee administers national taxes and customs. Use the Committee's services for taxpayer registration, filings and residence evidence. Use the State Register of Legal Entities for business formation and registration, and check the competent municipality for local obligations. [Tax Code, arts 4, 35 and 288](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/228326/latest) · [State Revenue Committee](https://www.src.am/) · [State Register](https://www.e-register.am/)

## Fictional intake

An individual spends fewer than 183 days in Armenia but maintains their main home and runs their business there. Check where their family and economic ties are centred, including whether the full-year rule applies, before selecting the tax treatment for foreign receipts.
