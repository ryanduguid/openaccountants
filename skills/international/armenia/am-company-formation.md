# Armenia Company Formation & Entity Choice

> **The formation facts are now read from the statute; the administrative ones are
> not.** Armenia's legal information system, `arlis.am`, publishes the consolidated
> Tax Code and the LLC Law in full text, and §§1–3 below are read from them. The
> registration mechanics in §4 — timeline, fee, e-invoicing — remain on commercial
> sources and are marked.

## 1. The company itself — Law on Limited Liability Companies

- **There is no minimum charter capital, and that rule has a rider the summaries
  drop** — art. 28(1) states plainly that *"Ընկերության կանոնադրական կապիտալի
  նվազագույն չափ չի սահմանվում"*: no minimum amount of charter capital is
  established. The **next sentence** provides that in particular cases, depending on
  the company's field of activity, laws and other legal acts **may** set minimum
  charter capital amounts. So "no minimum" is the default for an ordinary trading
  LLC and **not a statement about regulated sectors**  _(Law on Limited Liability Companies (ՀՕ-252), art. 28(1) — https://www.arlis.am/en/acts/218832/latest)_
- **Charter capital is denominated in dram** — the nominal values of the charter
  capital and of participants' contributions are determined in dram  _(LLC Law, art. 28(2) — https://www.arlis.am/en/acts/218832/latest)_
- **Approving the annual results has a floor as well as a ceiling** — the ordinary
  general meeting is held at least once a year, and the meeting approving the
  company's annual results *"պետք է անցկացվի ֆինանսական տարվա ավարտից ոչ շուտ,
  քան 2 ամիս և ոչ ուշ, քան 6 ամիս անց"* — **not earlier than 2 months and not
  later than 6 months** after the end of the financial year. Guides that give only
  the six-month limit omit the earliest date the meeting may be held  _(LLC Law, art. 37 — https://www.arlis.am/en/acts/218832/latest)_

## 2. The regime election, and its deadline

A newly registered company does not simply "have" a tax regime — it elects one, and
the election has a statutory deadline measured from registration.

- **By the 20th day after state registration, inclusive** — a commercial
  organisation registered during the tax year, or an individual recorded as a sole
  trader, is a turnover-tax payer from the date of registration to the end of that
  tax year **if** it files the tax authority's declaration *"մինչև ... պետական
  գրանցման ... օրվան հաջորդող 20-րդ օրը ներառյալ"*. The micro-business election
  runs on the same clock  _(Tax Code (ՀՕ-165-Ն), arts. 254(1)(2) and 267(1)(2) — https://www.arlis.am/en/acts/230455/latest)_
- **An existing company elects by 20 February** — for a regime to apply from 1
  January of a tax year, the declaration is due by **20 February** of that year,
  inclusive  _(Tax Code, arts. 254(1)(1) and 267(1)(1) — https://www.arlis.am/en/acts/230455/latest)_
- **Making no election leaves the company in the general system** — the turnover
  and micro-business systems are entered *by declaration*; nothing places a company
  in them by default  _(Tax Code, arts. 254(1) and 267(1) — https://www.arlis.am/en/acts/230455/latest)_

## 3. The three systems

### General system

- **Profit tax is 18%** — *"շահութահարկը հաշվարկվում է 18 տոկոս դրույքաչափով"*, for
  a resident payer and for a non-resident operating through a permanent
  establishment on the base attributable to that establishment  _(Tax Code, art. 125(1) — https://www.arlis.am/en/acts/230455/latest)_
- **Two exceptions sit in the same article** — registered investment funds (other
  than pension and guarantee funds) and securitisation funds are at **0.01%**, and a
  sole trader inside the turnover-tax system pays profit tax at a flat **AMD 5,000
  a month**, which is that person's final profit-tax liability for those activities  _(Tax Code, art. 125(2)–(3) — https://www.arlis.am/en/acts/230455/latest)_
- **VAT is 20%** — on the base of transactions that are objects of VAT taxation
  under art. 60, except those in arts. 64 and 65; art. 65 transactions are at **0%**  _(Tax Code, art. 63(1) and (3) — https://www.arlis.am/en/acts/230455/latest)_
- **A 16.67% embedded rate applies where the paperwork fails** — where the
  consideration already includes VAT, the VAT is extracted at a computational
  **16.67%** in four cases: the business did not treat itself as a VAT payer in
  breach of the Code; it did not state the VAT rate and amount on a separate line of
  the settlement document; it issued no settlement document at all; or it gave the
  buyer only a cash-register receipt. This is the practical cost of invoicing
  wrongly and is not a rate a compliant business chooses  _(Tax Code, art. 63(2) — https://www.arlis.am/en/acts/230455/latest)_

### Turnover tax — a schedule, not a rate

Open to a resident commercial organisation or sole trader whose **previous-year
sales turnover across all activities did not exceed AMD 115,000,000**, counting
turnover attributable to any micro-business activities as well.

| Income type | Rate |
| --- | --- |
| Trading (buy and sell), other than rows 2 and 9 | **10%** |
| Trading in secondary raw materials on the Government's list | **5%** |
| Newspapers disposed of by editorial offices | **1.5%** |
| Production activity | **7%** |
| Rent, interest, royalty | **10%** |
| Public catering | **12%** |
| Other assets and other activity of a payer that declared for public catering | **20%** |
| Activities on the Government's list of high-technology fields | **1%** |
| Disposal of other assets, including immovable property | **10%** |
| Other activity | **10%** |

_(Tax Code, arts. 254(2) and 258(1) — https://www.arlis.am/en/acts/230455/latest)_

### Micro-business

- **Ceiling AMD 24,000,000** of previous-year sales turnover across all activities  _(Tax Code, art. 267(3) — https://www.arlis.am/en/acts/230455/latest)_
- **The exemption is broad and its exceptions are the point** — a micro-business
  subject is released from calculating and paying **all state taxes** arising on the
  micro-business, including its obligations as a tax agent and the profit-tax
  advance payments under art. 135. It is **not** released from: taxes on goods
  imported into Armenia, including from EAEU member states; **income tax on taxable
  income paid to individuals who are not sole traders or notaries** — so payroll
  withholding continues; and excise, environmental and road tax  _(Tax Code, art. 269(1)–(2) — https://www.arlis.am/en/acts/230455/latest)_

## 4. Registration mechanics — still on commercial sources

These four rows were not found in the statutes read above and keep the citations
they had. [RESEARCH GAP — the Law on State Registration of Legal Entities and the
Law on State Duty are both on `arlis.am` and would settle the fee and the timeline.]

- **Common entity types** — Limited liability company (LLC / ՍՊԸ), joint-stock company (JSC), and sole proprietor / individual entrepreneur  _(Civil Code of the Republic of Armenia (https://www.repatarmenia.org/repatriate/practical-information/business/starting-a-business-in-armenia))_
- **Foreign ownership** — 100% foreign ownership permitted; no local residency requirement for founders or directors  _(Law on Limited Liability Companies of the Republic of Armenia (https://armenian-lawyer.com/immigration/registering-an-llc-in-armenia-one%E2%80%91day-incorporation-tax-registration-and-first-compliance-steps/))_
- **Registration authority** — State Register of Legal Entities, Ministry of Justice of the Republic of Armenia (e-register)  _(Law on State Registration of Legal Entities (https://www.e-register.am/en/))_
- **Incorporation timeline** — Standard LLC registration in roughly 2–3 business days (online), with simple cases possible within hours ((approx — confirm) typical processing time)  _(Law on State Registration of Legal Entities (https://armenian-lawyer.com/immigration/company-registration-in-armenia-in-2-3-days-a-realistic-day-by-day-setup-checklist/))_
- **Government registration fee** — No state registration fee for standard LLC incorporation ((approx — confirm) current fee schedule)  _(Law on State Duty of the Republic of Armenia (https://armenian-lawyer.com/immigration/registering-an-llc-in-armenia-one%E2%80%91day-incorporation-tax-registration-and-first-compliance-steps/))_

## 5. What this replaces

| The previous version said | The statute says |
| --- | --- |
| "No statutory minimum — nominal capital (e.g. AMD 1) is sufficient" | **No minimum is set** — art. 28(1) — but the same article lets other laws set sector-specific minimums, which the flat claim hides |
| "Annual report … approved by the shareholders' meeting within 6 months of the fiscal year-end" | **Not earlier than 2 months and not later than 6 months** after year end — art. 37. The floor was missing |
| "elect its tax regime … within about 20 days of incorporation" | **By the 20th day following the day of state registration, inclusive** — arts. 254(1)(2) and 267(1)(2). No longer approximate |
| "defaults to the general regime (18% CIT + 20% VAT)" | **Confirmed** — 18% profit tax (art. 125(1)), 20% VAT (art. 63(1)) — and the default holds because the other two systems are entered by declaration |
| "turnover tax" as a single alternative | A **ten-row schedule** from 1% to 20% — art. 258(1) — under an **AMD 115,000,000** ceiling (art. 254(2)) |
| "micro" as a single alternative | An **AMD 24,000,000** ceiling (art. 267(3)) with an exemption that still leaves **payroll withholding**, import, excise, environmental and road tax in place (art. 269) |
| *nothing* | Investment and securitisation funds at **0.01%**, and sole traders in the turnover system at **AMD 5,000 a month** — art. 125(2)–(3) |
| *nothing* | The **16.67%** embedded-VAT rate that applies when invoicing obligations are breached — art. 63(2) |
