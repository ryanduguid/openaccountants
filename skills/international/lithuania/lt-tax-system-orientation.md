# Lithuania tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the taxpayer type, residence facts, income sources and relevant period. Check any treaty that assigns residence or limits the source country's tax.

## Territorial reach

A Lithuanian resident individual generally accounts for Lithuanian and foreign income. Check the narrower statutory scope for qualifying noncitizen dual residents. A nonresident accounts for income attributable to a Lithuanian permanent base and specified Lithuanian-source income. A Lithuanian entity generally starts with domestic and foreign income, subject to the exclusion for qualifying taxed foreign permanent establishments. A foreign entity accounts for its Lithuanian permanent establishment and specified Lithuanian-source income outside it. [Individual Income Tax Act, art 5](https://e-tar.lt/rs/actualedition/TAR.C677663D2202/XOzdzBmAdl/) · [Corporate Income Tax Act, art 4](https://e-tar.lt/rs/actualedition/TAR.A5ACBDA529A9/hCLyLeiKmp/)

## Residence

An individual may be resident through a permanent home in Lithuania or because their personal, social or economic interests are mainly there. The stay tests are at least 183 days in a tax year, or at least 280 days in 2 consecutive tax years with at least 90 days in one of them. Check the citizen state-funding rule, statutory exceptions, departures and any treaty. For companies, the ordinary Lithuanian-entity test is registration under Lithuanian law. Special rules include domestic collective investment undertakings without legal personality and Lithuanian hybrid entities. [Individual Income Tax Act, art 4](https://e-tar.lt/rs/actualedition/TAR.C677663D2202/XOzdzBmAdl/) · [Corporate Income Tax Act, art 2](https://e-tar.lt/rs/actualedition/TAR.A5ACBDA529A9/hCLyLeiKmp/)

## Taxpayer types

An individual conducting independent activity generally uses the individual income tax rules. A Lithuanian limited liability company, small partnership or other registered legal entity generally falls within corporate income tax, with statutory exclusions. Identify the entity's obligations separately from those of its owners. For a foreign trust, check whether it is treated as a foreign entity under the Act and who derives the relevant income under the governing law and instruments. [Individual Income Tax Act, arts 2–3 and 5](https://e-tar.lt/rs/actualedition/TAR.C677663D2202/XOzdzBmAdl/) · [Corporate Income Tax Act, arts 2–4](https://e-tar.lt/rs/actualedition/TAR.A5ACBDA529A9/hCLyLeiKmp/) · [State Tax Inspectorate obligations by form](https://www.vmi.lt/evmi/lt/mokestines-pareigos-pagal-juridinio-asmens-verslo-forma)

## Currency

Express accounting documents and register entries in euros (EUR). Include the foreign currency as well where needed and keep the conversion basis with the accounting records. [Financial Accounting Act, arts 3(5) and 6](https://e-tar.lt/portal/lt/legalAct/TAR.43178AA9832E/asr)

## Assessment periods

The ordinary individual tax period is the calendar year. The corporate tax year normally follows the calendar year. The local tax administrator can approve a different 12-month period where the entity's activities warrant it. Confirm the first or last period separately for a new or closing entity or permanent establishment. [Individual Income Tax Act, art 7](https://e-tar.lt/rs/actualedition/TAR.C677663D2202/XOzdzBmAdl/) · [Corporate Income Tax Act, art 6](https://e-tar.lt/rs/actualedition/TAR.A5ACBDA529A9/hCLyLeiKmp/)

## Responsible authorities

The State Tax Inspectorate (Valstybinė mokesčių inspekcija, VMI) administers the ordinary individual and corporate income taxes. The customs authorities administer customs duties. Check the competent local tax administrator and use the applicable VMI reporting service. [Tax Administration Act, art 15](https://e-tar.lt/portal/lt/legalAct/TAR.3EB34933E485/asr) · [State Tax Inspectorate](https://www.vmi.lt/evmi/pagrindinis)

## Fictional intake

A consultant spends 140 days in Lithuania in one year and 145 the next and keeps a home abroad. Check the 2-year presence rule and its exceptions, establish the domestic residence position for the relevant years, then apply any treaty before classifying the consulting receipts.
