# Philippines tax overview

## Periods, authority and tax base

The Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) administers national internal revenue taxes. Keep amounts in Philippine pesos (PHP). Individuals use the calendar year. A corporation may account on a calendar or fiscal year basis. A change in basis requires approval. [National Internal Revenue Code (NIRC), ss 2, 22, 43, 46 and 52](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1997/ra_8424_1997.html) · [Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas currency guide](https://www.bsp.gov.ph/Coins%20and%20Notes/Educational%20Campaign/Philippine%20Currency%20Guide.pdf)

A resident Philippine citizen and a domestic corporation generally include Philippine and foreign income. Non-resident citizens, qualifying overseas contract workers, aliens and foreign corporations generally include Philippine-source income. Domestic corporations are organised under Philippine law. A foreign corporation's business activity in the Philippines distinguishes resident from non-resident foreign corporations. Check the citizenship and non-resident citizen tests, income source and any treaty before applying the domestic rules. [NIRC, ss 22–23](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1997/ra_8424_1997.html)

## Ordinary income tax and VAT

- **Individual income.** From 1 January 2023, the ordinary graduated schedule starts at 0% up to PHP 250,000 of taxable income. Marginal rates of 15%, 20%, 25% and 30% apply to the next bands, and 35% applies to taxable income above PHP 8 million. Use the applicable statutory calculation for the taxpayer. The conditional 8% election for business and professional income differs for purely self-employed and mixed-income individuals. Passive income and capital transactions have separate rules. [TRAIN Act (RA 10963), s 5](https://www.lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2017/ra_10963_2017.html) · [CMEPA (RA 12214), ss 4–5](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2025/ra_12214_2025.html)
- **Corporate income.** The general domestic corporate rate is 25%, in effect from 1 July 2020. The 20% domestic rate requires both net taxable income of PHP 5 million or less and total assets of PHP 100 million or less. Exclude from the assets test the land where the entity's office, plant and equipment are situated. The ordinary resident foreign corporate rate is 25% on taxable Philippine-source income. Check minimum corporate income tax and separate CREATE MORE incentives before computing tax. [CREATE MORE (RA 12066), ss 1–2, signed text](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2024/pdf/ra_12066_2024.pdf) · [BIR Revenue Regulations 7-2025 digest](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%207-2025.pdf)
- **VAT.** The standard rate is 12% on ordinary taxable sales of goods and services and on taxable imports. Section 109(CC) specifies a PHP 3 million gross annual sales threshold for the ordinary VAT exemption and provides for a CPI adjustment every 3 years. Section 236 handles compulsory registration separately. The usual registered taxpayer files and pays quarterly within 25 days after the quarter ends. Check current threshold adjustments, the specific VAT registration and supply rules and any special digital service, zero-rated or exempt treatment. [RA 10963, ss 31–33](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2017/ra_10963_2017.html) · [RA 11976, ss 16–18, 22 and 34](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2024/pdf/ra_11976_2024.pdf) · [RA 12023, ss 2 and 8](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2024/ra_12023_2024.html) · [BIR Form 2550Q](https://www.bir.gov.ph/bir-forms?datasetCode=3381&idTag=BIR2550Q&label=2550Q&tab=VAT%2FPercentage+Tax+Returns&type=TAB+LINK)

## Income tax filing

The ordinary annual individual filing date is 15 April following the year. Check whether substituted filing or another exception applies. A corporation ordinarily files its final adjustment return by the 15th day of the fourth month after the taxable year closes. For 2025 annual returns otherwise due 15 April 2026, BIR Circular 30-2026 extended filing, payment and associated attachments to 15 May 2026. Verify the form and payment route for the taxpayer. [NIRC, ss 51, 52 and 77](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1997/ra_8424_1997.html) · [Ease of Paying Taxes Act (RA 11976), ss 3 and 11](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2024/ra_11976_2024.html) · [BIR annual income tax forms](https://www.bir.gov.ph/bir-forms?datasetCode=3381&idTag=BIR1701&label=1701&tab=Income+Tax+Return&type=TAB+LINK) · [BIR Circular 30-2026](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RMC%20No.%2030-2026.pdf)

Check withholding, social contributions, formation, property and transaction-specific regimes in the applicable specialist guidance. Practitioner review remains pending.
