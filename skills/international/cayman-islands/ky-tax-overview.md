# Cayman Islands Tax Overview

## Tax-system scope in 2026

**Territory.** The Cayman Islands comprise Grand Cayman, Cayman Brac and Little Cayman. The territorial sea extends 12 nautical miles from the statutory baselines under the 1989 Order, in force from 1 January 1990. Check the charging law for the event and any island-specific relief. For example, the current stamp duty rules give qualifying Cayman Brac buyers a separate concession. [Interpretation Act (1995 Revision), s 3(3)](https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/1963/1963-0017/1963-0017.pdf) · [Territorial Sea Order, arts 1–5](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/1989/2397/made) · [Cayman Tax, section 5.2](cayman-tax.md#52-property-and-transfer-duties)

**Residence.** Cayman has no domestic personal or corporate income tax and no general domestic tax residence test. Residency can still determine the scope of a particular rule. Tourist accommodation tax distinguishes ordinary residents from tourists under its own definition. For the Common Reporting Standard (CRS), Cayman financial institutions collect tax residence information under the separate self-certification rules effective from 1 January 2026. An entity carrying on a relevant activity that claims foreign tax residence under the economic substance rules must provide details and supporting evidence. Determine foreign tax liability under the relevant foreign law. [Government tax overview](https://cigarchives.gov.ky/economy) · [Cayman CRS residence submission](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/topics/policy-issue-focus/aeoi/cayman_tax_residency.pdf) · [Tourist Accommodation (Taxation) Act, ss 2–3](https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/1964/1964-0168/1964-0168.pdf) · [SL 50 of 2025, regs 1 and 3](https://gov.ky/documents/35692/0/Tax%2BInformation%2BAuthority%2B%28International%2BTax%2BCompliance%29%2B%28Common%2BReporting%2BStandard%29%2B%28Amendment%29%2BRegulations%2C%2B2025.pdf/4f797d1c-5947-f6b2-6a6a-10503a6b5071?t=1764436589139) · [Economic Substance Act (2026 Revision), s 7(1)](https://gov.ky/w/international-tax-co-operation-economic-substance-act-2026-revision-lg7-s10)

**Currency.** The Cayman dollar (KYD or CI$) is divided into 100 cents. For payments or records in another currency, check the conversion rule for the particular obligation. [Monetary Authority Act (2020 Revision), ss 22–25](https://www.cima.ky/upimages/lawsregulations/MonetaryAuthorityLaw2020Revision_1579789069_1599483258.pdf) · [Department of Tourism payment guidance](https://www.ourcayman.ky/en-us/business-in-tourism/how-to/how-to-pay-taxes)

**Assessment period, liable parties and authorities.** There is no general income tax assessment period or domestic income tax return for individuals or entities. The Government's financial year ending 31 December is a public reporting period. Cayman raises revenue through duties and fees. Determine liability, timing and the authority from the transaction or activity. [DITC, general FAQs](https://www.ditc.ky/news-updates/faqs/) · [Public Management and Finance Act (2026 Revision), s 2](https://gov.ky/documents/35692/0/Public%2BManagement%2Band%2BFinance%2BAct%2B%282026%2BRevision%29%2C.pdf/3232b021-a66a-ca44-2458-4664858d2f6e?t=1770924958898) · [Government financial report to 30 September 2026, pp 24–27](https://gov.ky/documents/35692/0/Unaudited%2BQuarterly%2BFinancial%2BReport%2BNine-Month%2BPeriod%2BEnded%2B30%2B%2BSeptember%2B2026..pdf/fa95556e-4c18-d2ac-e060-4ceb2519e0db?t=1793981776634)

## Main taxes and returns
- **Personal income tax** — None — no income or withholding taxes are imposed on individuals  _(Cayman Islands Government, Finance and Economy (gov.ky archive) — https://cigarchives.gov.ky/economy)_
- **Corporate income tax** — None. See the separate transaction tax on transfers of equity in landholding corporations below. [Cayman Islands Government, Finance and Economy](https://cigarchives.gov.ky/economy)
- **VAT / GST** — None — no value-added tax, goods and services tax, or general sales tax is imposed  _(Cayman Islands Government, Finance and Economy (gov.ky archive) — https://cigarchives.gov.ky/economy)_
- **Import duty** — Classify the goods under Schedule 1, then check any relief or later tariff amendment in effect at the time of entry. [Customs Tariff Act (2026 Revision), s 3 and Schedules 1–2](https://gov.ky/documents/35692/0/Customs+Tariff+Act+%282026+Revision%29%2C.pdf/31bf00bb-42e2-e2b6-b4f3-8783076bfcc1?t=1770147513057)
- **Stamp duty on land transfers** — For ordinary instruments executed from 1 January 2026, use the higher of the price and market value. Duty is 7.5% of the full base below CI$2 million or 10% of the full base from CI$2 million. Check Caymanian buyer and Cayman Brac concessions, as well as separate rules for linked developments, leases, mortgages and filing. [Cayman Tax, Section 5.2](cayman-tax.md#52-property-and-transfer-duties) · [SL 63 of 2025, regs 1–3](https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/SUBORDINATE/2025/2025-0063/2025-0063_SL%2063%20of%202025.pdf) · [Government explanation, 2 January 2026](https://gov.ky/w/legislation-passed-to-increase-stamp-duty-on-properties-worth-2m-and-over)
- **Landholding share transfer tax** — From 1 January 2026, the ordinary tax is 7.5% of the higher of the price and statutory taxable value below CI$2 million, or 10% of that full amount from CI$2 million. The corporation files the return and pays within 31 days of transfer. Check the statutory exceptions and transition. [Cayman Tax, Section 5.2](cayman-tax.md#52-property-and-transfer-duties) · [Principal Act, ss 2–5](https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/1976/1976-0020/1976-0020_2022%20Revision.pdf) · [Act 16 of 2025, ss 1–3](https://gov.ky/w/land-holding-companies-share-transfer-tax-amendment-act-2025-lg54-s3)
- **Capital gains tax** — None  _(Cayman Islands Government, Finance and Economy (gov.ky archive) — https://cigarchives.gov.ky/economy)_
- **Inheritance, estate, gift and net wealth taxes** — None  _(Cayman Islands Government, Finance and Economy (gov.ky archive) — https://cigarchives.gov.ky/economy)_
- **Late exempted company annual return or fee** — Section 169(3) sets penalties of 33.33%, 66.67% or 100% of the annual fee when the late return is filed or the fee and penalty are paid in the second, third or fourth quarter respectively. [Companies Act (2026 Revision), ss 168–169](https://gov.ky/documents/43485/45951/Companies%2BAct%2B%282026%2BRevision%29%2C%2B%28LG4%2C%2BS7%29%2Bv2.pdf/bda7504d-2c94-2c49-3ac6-632a54fdd070?t=1770848564418)
