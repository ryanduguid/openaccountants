# Cayman Islands Tax Overview

## The Cayman tax system at a glance

- **Cayman tax system overview** — The Cayman Islands is a no-direct-tax jurisdiction: there is no income tax, no corporate income tax, no capital gains tax, no payroll tax, and no VAT/GST. Government revenue comes mainly from import duty, stamp duty, work-permit fees, and company/financial-services registration and annual fees.
- **Currency** — Cayman Islands dollar (KYD / CI$), pegged to USD at approximately CI$1 = US$1.20 (US$1 = CI$0.82) (approx — confirm peg)  _(Currency Law (Cayman Islands))_
- **Tax / revenue authority** — Cayman Islands Government: the Department for International Tax Cooperation (DITC) administers economic substance, CRS and FATCA reporting; Customs and Border Control collects import duty; the General Registry collects company registration and annual fees; the Lands and Survey Department (Valuation and Estates Office) assesses stamp duty on land transfers  _(Department for International Tax Cooperation — https://www.ditc.ky/ ; Customs and Border Control, Customs Duty and Other Fees — https://gov.ky/web/cbc/trade/imports/environmental-impact-fee ; General Registry — https://www.ciregistry.ky/ ; Cayman Islands Government, Legislation passed to increase stamp duty on properties worth $2M and over (December 2025) — https://gov.ky/w/legislation-passed-to-increase-stamp-duty-on-properties-worth-2m-and-over)_
- **Personal income tax** — None — no income or withholding taxes are imposed on individuals  _(Cayman Islands Government, Finance and Economy (gov.ky archive) — https://cigarchives.gov.ky/economy)_
- **Corporate income tax** — None. See the separate transaction tax on transfers of equity in landholding corporations below. [Cayman Islands Government, Finance and Economy](https://cigarchives.gov.ky/economy)
- **VAT / GST** — None — no value-added tax, goods and services tax, or general sales tax is imposed  _(Cayman Islands Government, Finance and Economy (gov.ky archive) — https://cigarchives.gov.ky/economy)_
- **Residence / tax basis** — Not applicable — because there is no income or corporate tax, residence has no bearing on direct tax liability for individuals or companies  _(Cayman Islands Government, Finance and Economy (gov.ky archive) — https://cigarchives.gov.ky/economy)_
- **Import duty (main consumption tax)** — Schedule 1 of the Customs Tariff Act sets a rate for each tariff heading: most headings carry 22% of the customs value, 27% and higher rates apply to specific goods, and 0%, 12% and 17% apply to others; Customs and Border Control collects the duty at import  _(Customs Tariff Act (2026 Revision), Schedule 1 — https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/2012/2012-0001/2012-0001_2026%20Revision.pdf ; Customs and Border Control, Customs Duty and Other Fees — https://gov.ky/web/cbc/trade/imports/environmental-impact-fee)_
- **Stamp duty on land transfers** — For ordinary instruments executed from 1 January 2026, use the higher of the price and market value. Duty is 7.5% of the full base below CI$2 million or 10% of the full base from CI$2 million. Check Caymanian buyer and Cayman Brac concessions, as well as separate rules for linked developments, leases, mortgages and filing. [Cayman Tax, Section 5.2](cayman-tax.md#52-property-and-transfer-duties) · [SL 63 of 2025, regs 1–3](https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/SUBORDINATE/2025/2025-0063/2025-0063_SL%2063%20of%202025.pdf) · [Government explanation, 2 January 2026](https://gov.ky/w/legislation-passed-to-increase-stamp-duty-on-properties-worth-2m-and-over)
- **Landholding share transfer tax** — From 1 January 2026, the ordinary tax is 7.5% of the higher of the price and statutory taxable value below CI$2 million, or 10% of that full amount from CI$2 million. The corporation files the return and pays within 31 days of transfer. Check the statutory exceptions and transition. [Cayman Tax, Section 5.2](cayman-tax.md#52-property-and-transfer-duties) · [Principal Act, ss 2–5](https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/1976/1976-0020/1976-0020_2022%20Revision.pdf) · [Act 16 of 2025, ss 1–3](https://gov.ky/w/land-holding-companies-share-transfer-tax-amendment-act-2025-lg54-s3)
- **Capital gains tax** — None  _(Cayman Islands Government, Finance and Economy (gov.ky archive) — https://cigarchives.gov.ky/economy)_
- **Inheritance, estate, gift and net wealth taxes** — None  _(Cayman Islands Government, Finance and Economy (gov.ky archive) — https://cigarchives.gov.ky/economy)_
- **Annual filing** — There is no domestic income tax return. Exempted companies file a company annual return and pay a fee in January after the year of registration. Relevant entities file economic substance notifications and returns with the DITC. [Companies Act (2026 Revision), ss 168–169](https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/1961/1961-0003/1961-0003_2026%20Revision.pdf) · [International Tax Co-operation (Economic Substance) Act (2024 Revision), s 7](https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/2018/2018-0045/2018-0045_2024%20Revision.pdf)
- **Company annual return / fee deadline** — An exempted company files its annual return and pays the annual fee in January of each year after the year of registration (ss 168 and 169(1)); late filing adds 33.33%, 66.67% or 100% of the annual fee when settled in the second, third or fourth quarter (s 169(3))  _(Companies Act (2026 Revision), ss 168 and 169 — https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/1961/1961-0003/1961-0003_2026%20Revision.pdf ; General Registry, Companies Act fee schedule effective 1 January 2025 — https://www.ciregistry.ky/Documents/8769/)_
