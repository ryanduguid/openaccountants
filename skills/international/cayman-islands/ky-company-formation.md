# Cayman Islands Company Formation & Entity Choice

## Company formation in the Cayman Islands

The Cayman Islands is a leading offshore incorporation centre. The most common vehicle for non-resident business is the exempted company, alongside exempted limited partnerships and LLCs. There is no minimum share capital and no corporate tax, but companies pay annual government fees and must keep a registered office and statutory registers.

- **Common entity types** — Exempted company; ordinary resident/non-resident company; exempted limited partnership (ELP); limited liability company (LLC); segregated portfolio company (SPC); foundation company  _(Companies Act (2026 Revision), Part VII, ss 163 to 177 (exempted companies) — https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/1961/1961-0003/1961-0003_2026%20Revision.pdf ; Exempted Limited Partnership Act (2025 Revision) — https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/2001/2001-0005/2001-0005_2025%20Revision.pdf ; Limited Liability Companies Act (2025 Revision) — https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/2016/2016-0002/2016-0002_2025%20Revision.pdf ; Foundation Companies Act (2025 Revision) — https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/2017/2017-0029/2017-0029_2025%20Revision.pdf)_
- **Most common offshore vehicle** — Exempted company, registered on a declaration that its operations will be conducted mainly outside the Islands (ss 163 and 165); it may not trade in the Islands with any person except in furtherance of its business carried on outside the Islands unless licensed (s 174(1)), and its annual return repeats that declaration (s 168)  _(Companies Act (2026 Revision), ss 163, 165, 168 and 174(1) — https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/1961/1961-0003/1961-0003_2026%20Revision.pdf)_
- **Minimum share capital** — None; there is no statutory minimum, and companies are commonly authorised with a nominal capital (for example US$50,000) so that the authorised capital stays at or below CI$42,000, the lowest annual fee band  _(Multilaw, Cayman Islands exempted company — https://multilaw.com/Multilaw/Multilaw/Global_Business_Entities_Guide/Cayman_Islands_-_Exempted_Company.aspx ; fee bands: General Registry, Companies Act fee schedule effective 1 January 2025 — https://www.ciregistry.ky/Documents/8769/)_
- **Minimum directors / shareholders** — At least one director and one shareholder; no residency requirement and corporate directors are permitted ((confirm current minimums))  _(Companies Act (2025 Revision) (Cayman Islands) — https://multilaw.com/Multilaw/Multilaw/Global_Business_Entities_Guide/Cayman_Islands_-_Exempted_Company.aspx)_
- **Registered office** — Required; every company must have a registered office in the Islands to which notices may be addressed, normally provided by a licensed corporate services provider, and carrying on business without one incurs a penalty of CI$10 for each day  _(Companies Act (2026 Revision), s 50(1) — https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/1961/1961-0003/1961-0003_2026%20Revision.pdf)_
- **Incorporation timeline** — Typically 1–5 business days for standard registration; expedited same/next-day service available for an additional express fee ((approx — confirm))  _(General Registry — Cayman Islands — https://www.ciregistry.ky/fees/)_
- **Name reservation fee** — CI$40 for one week, CI$60 for one month, CI$80 for two months, CI$120 for three months or CI$160 for four months (US$48.78 to US$195.12); the Registrar may reserve a name for up to four months, renewable by successive applications  _(General Registry, Companies Act fee schedule effective 1 January 2025 — https://www.ciregistry.ky/Documents/8769/ ; Companies Act (2026 Revision), s 29A(2) and (3) — https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/1961/1961-0003/1961-0003_2026%20Revision.pdf)_
- **Registration and annual government fee — band 1** — Exempted company with no authorised capital or authorised capital up to CI$42,000: annual fee CI$925 (US$1,128.05) under s 169(1)(a), up from CI$825 before 1 January 2025; the registration fee for the same band is CI$700 (US$853.66) under s 26(4)(b)(i)  _(General Registry, Companies Act fee schedule effective 1 January 2025 — https://www.ciregistry.ky/Documents/8769/ ; Companies Act (2026 Revision), ss 26(4) and 169(1) — https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/1961/1961-0003/1961-0003_2026%20Revision.pdf)_
- **Annual government fee — band 2** — Authorised capital of CI$42,001 to CI$820,000: annual fee CI$1,225 (US$1,493.90) under s 169(1)(b), up from CI$1,125 before 1 January 2025; registration fee CI$1,000 (US$1,219.51)  _(General Registry, Companies Act fee schedule effective 1 January 2025 — https://www.ciregistry.ky/Documents/8769/ ; Companies Act (2026 Revision), ss 26(4) and 169(1) — https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/1961/1961-0003/1961-0003_2026%20Revision.pdf)_
- **Annual government fee — band 3** — Authorised capital of CI$820,001 to CI$1,640,000: annual fee CI$2,209 (US$2,693.90) under s 169(1)(c); registration fee CI$1,984 (US$2,419.51)  _(General Registry, Companies Act fee schedule effective 1 January 2025 — https://www.ciregistry.ky/Documents/8769/ ; Companies Act (2026 Revision), ss 26(4) and 169(1) — https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/1961/1961-0003/1961-0003_2026%20Revision.pdf)_
- **Annual government fee — band 4** — Authorised capital above CI$1,640,000: annual fee CI$2,793 (US$3,406.10) under s 169(1)(d); registration fee CI$2,568 (US$3,131.71)  _(General Registry, Companies Act fee schedule effective 1 January 2025 — https://www.ciregistry.ky/Documents/8769/ ; Companies Act (2026 Revision), ss 26(4) and 169(1) — https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/1961/1961-0003/1961-0003_2026%20Revision.pdf)_
- **Annual return filing** — Every exempted company files its annual return and pays the annual fee in January of each year after the year of registration (ss 168 and 169(1) and (2)); late filing adds 33.33%, 66.67% or 100% of the annual fee when settled in the second, third or fourth quarter (s 169(3))  _(Companies Act (2026 Revision), ss 168 and 169 — https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/1961/1961-0003/1961-0003_2026%20Revision.pdf)_
- **Beneficial ownership register** — Most Cayman companies must maintain an adequate, accurate and current beneficial ownership register and file required particulars  _(Beneficial Ownership Transparency Act (2026 Revision) — https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/2023/2023-0013/2023-0013_2026%20Revision.pdf ; Harneys, Cayman Islands Companies Act — https://www.harneys.com/our-blogs/regulatory/cayman-islands-companies-act/)_
- **Economic substance / annual compliance** — A relevant entity notifies the Tax Information Authority annually (s 7(1)) and, where it carries on a relevant activity, files an economic substance return within twelve months after each financial year end (s 7(3)); CRS and FATCA reporting applies to financial institutions  _(International Tax Co-operation (Economic Substance) Act (2024 Revision), s 7(1) and (3) — https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/2018/2018-0045/2018-0045_2024%20Revision.pdf)_
