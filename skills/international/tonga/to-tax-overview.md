# Tonga Tax Overview

Use the Kingdom of Tonga's Income Tax Act for ordinary income tax. Identify the taxpayer, fiscal period, income category and connection to Tonga.

## Residence

An individual can be resident through a home in Tonga at any time in the year, 183 days' aggregate presence in a 12-month period starting or ending in that fiscal year or employment by the Government on an overseas posting during the year. Section 4 has part-year rules tied to the first or last day of presence and adjacent-year residence. Count part days, including arrival and departure days, as whole days. Exclude days spent solely in transit between 2 places outside Tonga. [Income Tax Act, s 4][ITA] · [Income Tax Regulations, reg 4][REGS]

## Income and territorial scope

Ordinary gross income of residents includes income from Tonga and abroad. Non-residents bring Tongan-source income into gross income. Check s 60 for statutory source and the scope of business income connected with a permanent establishment. The Act has separate provisions for specified non-resident income and foreign tax credits. [Income Tax Act, ss 6–7, 11 and 60–64][ITA]

## Taxpayer type

The Act's company definition includes partnerships. A company can be resident by incorporation, creation or formation in Tonga or by having its centre of administrative management there during the fiscal year. A partnership also qualifies if it has a resident partner at any time in the year. A trust is resident if settled or established in Tonga or if a trustee is resident during the year. Apply the separate individual, trustee and company rules before computing income. [Income Tax Act, ss 2, 4 and 55–58][ITA]

## Currency

Express statutory income-tax amounts in Tongan pa'anga (TOP). Use the National Reserve Bank of Tonga midpoint rate on the date the amount is taken into account for income tax. [Income Tax Act, s 54][ITA]

## Assessment periods

A company's fiscal year is the 12 months to its annual accounts balance date. For other persons it ends on 30 June. The ordinary income tax return is due within 4 months of year-end where required under ss 68–69. The Ministry gives 31 October for a 30 June year-end and 30 April for a 31 December year-end. [Income Tax Act, ss 2 and 68–69][ITA] · [Ministry return and payment guidance][DUE]

## Tax authority and headline rates

The Ministry of Revenue and Customs administers income tax and Consumption Tax (CT). Its employment schedule shows a nil band through TOP 12,000 and a top marginal rate of 25%. Its entity guidance gives 25% of chargeable income for ordinary companies. Standard CT is 15%. Registered businesses generally lodge and pay by the 28th of the month after their CT period, with a next-business-day rule where the 28th is a weekend or public holiday. [Ministry PAYE][PAYE] · [Ministry company guidance][COMPANIES] · [Ministry CT overview][CT] · [Ministry CT returns][CTRET]
