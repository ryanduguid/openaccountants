# Solomon Islands Tax System Orientation

Apply the Solomon Islands Income Tax Act to the taxpayer's residence, type of income and accounting period. The Tax Administration Act 2022 supplies the general administration rules from 1 January 2023. [Income Tax Act, Cap 123][ITA] · [IRD commencement notice][START]

## Residence

An individual can be resident through ordinary residence, allowing temporary absences the Commissioner considers reasonable. The Act also deems residence if the individual's presence exceeds 6 months in aggregate in the year, they satisfy the Commissioner they intend to reside for that period or they meet the conditions for employment exercised wholly or mainly in Solomon Islands under a contract of at least 6 months. The Commissioner can treat the individual as non-resident after considering the employment contract and payment method. [Income Tax Act, s 2][ITA]

## Income and territorial scope

A resident's ordinary assessable income can include amounts accrued, derived or received in Solomon Islands or elsewhere. A non-resident's ordinary assessable income includes amounts accrued in or derived from Solomon Islands. Specific source and deeming rules apply, including for a resident's business carried on partly inside and partly outside Solomon Islands. Check final withholding and approved mining rules separately. [Income Tax Act, ss 3–4, 34 and 38][ITA]

## Taxpayer type

An incorporated local body is resident. A foreign-incorporated body can be resident if it carries on business in Solomon Islands and is centrally managed and controlled there or has its voting power controlled by resident shareholders. The partnership files a return and partners account for their shares of partnership profits. Check trustee and beneficiary capacity separately. [Income Tax Act, ss 2, 4 and 57][ITA] · [IRD income tax guidance][IRD]

## Currency

Use Solomon Islands dollars (SBD). The Central Bank publishes SBD exchange rates. Check the provision for the particular tax item before translating foreign amounts. [Central Bank of Solomon Islands exchange rates][CBSI]

## Assessment periods

The ordinary income year is the calendar year and the return is due by 31 March of the following year. A business with an applicable accounting period ending after 31 December files within 3 months of that period's end. Calendar-year assessment balances are generally due by 30 September. The assessment can specify the due date for a business with a different balance date. Ordinary business instalments fall on 20 March, 20 June, 20 September and 20 December, subject to the statutory adjustments. [Income Tax Act, ss 2, 26, 57 and 83][ITA] · [IRD income tax guidance][IRD]

## Tax authority and headline rates

The Inland Revenue Division administers ordinary income tax. The annual individual exemption is SBD 30,080 subject to s 28, including part-year rules. The top marginal individual rate is 40%. Section 33 generally sets 30% for locally incorporated companies and 35% for foreign-incorporated companies, with separate treatment for specified withholding and approved mining. Check incorporation for this rate test independently of residence. Goods Tax and Sales Tax have separate Acts. The ministry still describes VAT as a reform under consultation. [Income Tax Act, ss 28, 32–34 and Fifth Schedule][ITA] · [IRD tax legislation][INDEX] · [Ministry VAT consultation][VAT]
