# Botswana VAT / GST

## VAT rates

- **Standard VAT rate** — 14%. The rise to 15% proposed for 1 April 2025 never took effect: BURS's public notice of 9 July 2026 on the revised zero-rated foodstuffs list states that food items outside the list are subject to VAT at 14%, under the Value Added Tax Act, 2026, which commenced on 1 July 2026 (the text of the 2026 Act was not read; its zero-rating and remote-services notices are cited below)  _(BURS public notice, 9 July 2026 — https://www.burs.org.bw/images/Zero-Rated%20FoodStuff-%20Under%20Vat%20Act%20of%202026.pdf)_
- **Zero-rated supplies** — 0% on exports and, from 1 July 2026, on the revised list of foodstuffs supplied in their natural state and unmixed: sorghum or maize meal (not as prepared or cooked food), millet grain and meal, wheat grain, maize cobs, sugar, brown bread, fresh potatoes, tomatoes, onions, cabbage, rape, beetroots, carrots and spinach, fresh oranges, apples and bananas, rice, samp, bread flour (white, brown and whole wheat) and cooking oil; dried, canned or bottled produce and nuts are excluded, and any food outside the list carries 14%  _(BURS public notice, 9 July 2026 — https://www.burs.org.bw/images/Zero-Rated%20FoodStuff-%20Under%20Vat%20Act%20of%202026.pdf)_
- **Exempt supplies** — Exempt (no VAT, no input credit): prescribed financial services, residential accommodation and certain education services (confirm the current exempt schedule against the 2026 Act). Medical services: BURS announced on 30 July 2026 that the charging of VAT on medical services by private healthcare providers and medical aid schemes is suspended pending consultation, so treat them as not charged until BURS lifts the suspension  _(Value Added Tax Act (Cap. 50:03) — https://www.burs.org.bw/index.php/tax/value-added-tax ; BURS public notice, 30 July 2026 — https://www.burs.org.bw/images/Value%20Added%20Tax%20on%20Medical%20Services%20Suspension.pdf)_

Botswana levies VAT under the Value Added Tax Act. The standard rate is **14%**, confirmed by BURS's 9 July 2026 notice under the Value Added Tax Act, 2026 (in force from 1 July 2026); the rise to 15% proposed for 1 April 2025 never took effect. The Value Added Tax (Amendment) Act No. 16 of 2025 commenced on 1 June 2026 and brings non-resident remote-service suppliers, government entities and large unregistered persons into the system in stages between June and October 2026. Certain supplies are zero-rated or exempt.

## Registration

- **Compulsory registration threshold** — 1,000,000 BWP (of annual taxable turnover)  _(Value Added Tax Act (Cap. 50:03))_
- **Voluntary registration** — Permitted below the BWP 1,000,000 threshold (source replaced, claim not re-verified: this was cited to a `doingbusiness.co.bw` page that now returns "Under Construction" and a shopping cart. The Botswana Unified Revenue Service publishes the domestic-tax legislation and its rulings and directives; confirm the provision there)  _(Value Added Tax Act (Cap. 50:03) — Botswana Unified Revenue Service, https://www.burs.org.bw/)_
- **Non-resident remote-services registration** — 500,000 BWP of taxable supplies over 12 months (remote/electronic services, per Andersen's reading of the 2025 amendment). Commencement per BURS: registration of non-resident remote-service suppliers from 1 June 2026, charging of VAT by them from 1 October 2026. The Value Added Tax (Remote Services) Regulations, 2026 (SI 74 of 2026) list the services covered: digital content (e-books, music, games, apps, software, streaming, gambling), hosting and cloud storage, webinars and distance learning, website design, inbound tourism products, professional services provided remotely or performed physically in Botswana, and marketplace supplies; BURS says the start date for inbound tourism products will be announced separately  _(BURS public notice, 29 May 2026 — https://www.burs.org.bw/images/Value%20Added%20Tax%20Amendment%20Act%202026.pdf ; SI 74 of 2026 — https://www.burs.org.bw/images/VAT%20Added%20Tax%20Regulations%202026.pdf ; https://bw.andersen.com/botswanas-value-added-tax-amendment-bill-2025-modernising-the-vat-landscape/)_

Compulsory VAT registration applies once taxable turnover crosses the threshold; smaller businesses may register voluntarily.

## Filing and mechanics

- **VAT return frequency** — Bi-monthly (every 2 months) for most registrants (Foreign remote-service suppliers file quarterly)  _(Value Added Tax Act (Cap. 50:03) — https://www.burs.org.bw/index.php/tax/value-added-tax)_
- **Reverse charge** — A VAT-registered recipient of imported or remote services self-accounts for VAT as output and input tax in the same return. From 1 August 2026 registered persons account for reverse-charged supplies under the new reporting rules (first returns due 28 September, 28 October and 28 November 2026 by category), and government entities and large unregistered persons (total annual turnover of P1,000,000) must register from 1 August 2026 to account for reverse-charged supplies (SI 73 of 2026: application within two months of the amendment's commencement). Services imported before the transition stay on Form VAT.017.1  _(BURS public notice, 29 May 2026 — https://www.burs.org.bw/images/Value%20Added%20Tax%20Amendment%20Act%202026.pdf ; SI 73 of 2026 — https://www.burs.org.bw/images/VAT%20Added%20Tax%20Regulations%202026.pdf)_
- **Remote/digital services** — Non-resident suppliers of remote services to Botswana register from 1 June 2026 and charge VAT from 1 October 2026 once over the threshold; the 2025 amendment's commencement was gazetted for 1 June 2026, not September 2025  _(BURS public notice, 29 May 2026 — https://www.burs.org.bw/images/Value%20Added%20Tax%20Amendment%20Act%202026.pdf)_

Most registrants file bi-monthly; reverse charge applies to imported services and remote digital services received by VAT-registered businesses.
