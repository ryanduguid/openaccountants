# Botswana Corporate Income Tax

## Company tax rates

The standard company tax rate is 24.5% under the Income Tax Act, 2026 (22% for 2025/26 under the repealed Cap. 52:01). IFSC companies keep a 15% rate on approved financial operations; the repealed Act's 15% approved manufacturing rate has no counterpart in Schedule 1 of the new Act, which instead allows development approval orders under s 94.

- **Standard company tax rate** — 24.5% from 1 July 2026 (22% for 2025/26; the 23.5% proposed in the 2025/26 budget was not enacted)  _(Income Tax Act, 2026 (Act No. 13 of 2026), Government Gazette Supplement A of 1 July 2026, BURS copy, Schedule 1 Part I para 3(d) — https://www.burs.org.bw/index.php/tax/tax-laws-2026?download=954:income-tax-act-amendment-2026 ; repealed Income Tax Act (Cap. 52:01) position for 2025/26 per PwC — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/botswana/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Approved manufacturing rate** — No separate manufacturing rate appears in Schedule 1 of the Income Tax Act, 2026; additional relief for approved projects is granted by development approval order under s 94. The 15% rate for approved manufacturers applied under the repealed Act for 2025/26  _(Income Tax Act, 2026 (Act No. 13 of 2026), Government Gazette Supplement A of 1 July 2026, BURS copy, Schedule 1 Part I para 3 and s 94 — https://www.burs.org.bw/index.php/tax/tax-laws-2026?download=954:income-tax-act-amendment-2026 ; repealed Income Tax Act (Cap. 52:01) position for 2025/26 per PwC — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/botswana/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **IFSC company rate** — 15% on taxable income from approved financial operations and 24.5% on all other taxable income (22% on other income for 2025/26)  _(Income Tax Act, 2026 (Act No. 13 of 2026), Government Gazette Supplement A of 1 July 2026, BURS copy, Schedule 1 Part I para 3(b) — https://www.burs.org.bw/index.php/tax/tax-laws-2026?download=954:income-tax-act-amendment-2026)_
- **Mining (non-diamond) rate** — For a mining company other than a diamond mining company in which the Government holds equity: the higher of 70 − (1,500 / X), where X is the profitability ratio of taxable income to gross income, and the 24.5% standard company rate (para 3(a)(ii) and (d))  _(Income Tax Act, 2026 (Act No. 13 of 2026), Government Gazette Supplement A of 1 July 2026, BURS copy, Schedule 1 Part I para 3(a) — https://www.burs.org.bw/index.php/tax/tax-laws-2026?download=954:income-tax-act-amendment-2026)_

## Tax base

Companies are taxed on Botswana-source income net of allowable expenses; the regime is source-based.

- **Tax base** — Income from a source within or deemed within Botswana, less allowable deductions  _(Income Tax Act (Cap. 52:01) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/botswana/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Additional Company Tax (ACT)** — Not part of the current law: the Income Tax Act, 2026 charges a single 24.5% company rate with 10% withholding on dividends; the historic two-tier basic 15% plus additional 7% structure and its dividend credits belong to the repealed Act  _(Income Tax Act, 2026 (Act No. 13 of 2026), Government Gazette Supplement A of 1 July 2026, BURS copy, Schedule 1 Part I paras 3(d), 6(a) and 7(a) — https://www.burs.org.bw/index.php/tax/tax-laws-2026?download=954:income-tax-act-amendment-2026 ; repealed Income Tax Act (Cap. 52:01) position for 2025/26 per PwC — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/botswana/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_

## Withholding tax rates

Botswana applies withholding tax on a range of payments; rates differ for residents and non-residents and may be reduced by double-tax treaties (Botswana has ~24 DTAs).

- **Dividends** — 10% withholding on dividends paid to residents and Botswana permanent establishments (para 7(a)) and to non-residents (para 6(a)); no withholding on dividends paid to an IFSC company or a specified collective investment undertaking (s 133(7)); treaty rates may reduce the non-resident rate  _(Income Tax Act, 2026 (Act No. 13 of 2026), Government Gazette Supplement A of 1 July 2026, BURS copy, Schedule 1 Part I paras 6(a) and 7(a), ss 132 and 133(1) and (7) — https://www.burs.org.bw/index.php/tax/tax-laws-2026?download=954:income-tax-act-amendment-2026 ; treaty rates: PwC — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/botswana/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Interest** — 10% on interest paid to residents or Botswana permanent establishments (para 7(b)); 15% on interest paid to non-residents (para 6(b)); no withholding on interest paid to an IFSC company or to a bank or financial institution in its ordinary course of business (s 133(8))  _(Income Tax Act, 2026 (Act No. 13 of 2026), Government Gazette Supplement A of 1 July 2026, BURS copy, Schedule 1 Part I paras 6(b) and 7(b), s 133(2) and (8) — https://www.burs.org.bw/index.php/tax/tax-laws-2026?download=954:income-tax-act-amendment-2026 ; treaty rates: PwC — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/botswana/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Royalties (non-residents)** — 15% (treaty rates may apply)  _(Income Tax Act, 2026 (Act No. 13 of 2026), Government Gazette Supplement A of 1 July 2026, BURS copy, Schedule 1 Part I para 6(c) and s 132 — https://www.burs.org.bw/index.php/tax/tax-laws-2026?download=954:income-tax-act-amendment-2026)_
- **Management / consultancy fees (non-residents)** — 15% withholding on technical fees paid to non-residents; the Act defines technical fees to cover administrative, managerial, technical or consulting services and the development or customisation of software (treaty rates may apply)  _(Income Tax Act, 2026 (Act No. 13 of 2026), Government Gazette Supplement A of 1 July 2026, BURS copy, Schedule 1 Part I para 6(d), s 132 and s 2 definition of 'technical fee' — https://www.burs.org.bw/index.php/tax/tax-laws-2026?download=954:income-tax-act-amendment-2026)_
- **Rent** — 5% on rent for the use or occupation of immovable property in Botswana; not withheld on rent paid by an individual outside a business, on hotel, motel, guesthouse or lodge accommodation, or where the payer's total rent for the tax year is below BWP 48,000  _(Income Tax Act, 2026 (Act No. 13 of 2026), Government Gazette Supplement A of 1 July 2026, BURS copy, Schedule 1 Part I para 7(e), Part II para 3 and s 133(3) and (4) — https://www.burs.org.bw/index.php/tax/tax-laws-2026?download=954:income-tax-act-amendment-2026)_
- **Commission / brokerage** — 10% on commission or brokerage for procuring goods or services, not withheld where the payer's total commissions for the year are below BWP 48,000  _(Income Tax Act, 2026 (Act No. 13 of 2026), Government Gazette Supplement A of 1 July 2026, BURS copy, Schedule 1 Part I para 7(f), Part II para 4 and s 133(5) and (6) — https://www.burs.org.bw/index.php/tax/tax-laws-2026?download=954:income-tax-act-amendment-2026)_
- **Construction contracts** — 3% of the gross payment under a construction contract; excluded are contracts below BWP 2,000,000 executed by construction companies in the prescribed Public Procurement Act categories and contracts exclusively for design, engineering, surveying and related professional services  _(Income Tax Act, 2026 (Act No. 13 of 2026), Government Gazette Supplement A of 1 July 2026, BURS copy, Schedule 1 Part I para 7(d), Part II para 1 and s 131 — https://www.burs.org.bw/index.php/tax/tax-laws-2026?download=954:income-tax-act-amendment-2026)_

## Filing and payment

Companies self-assess; returns are due within four months of year-end and self-assessment tax (SAT) is paid in quarterly instalments where the liability exceeds BWP 50,000.

- **Return filing deadline** — Within four months after the end of the company's accounting period  _(Income Tax Act, 2026 (Act No. 13 of 2026), Government Gazette Supplement A of 1 July 2026, BURS copy, s 123(1)(a) — https://www.burs.org.bw/index.php/tax/tax-laws-2026?download=954:income-tax-act-amendment-2026)_
- **SAT instalments** — Four equal quarterly instalments on or before the end of the 3rd, 6th, 9th and 12th months of the tax year where the estimated income tax liability is BWP 50,000 or more  _(Income Tax Act, 2026 (Act No. 13 of 2026), Government Gazette Supplement A of 1 July 2026, BURS copy, s 127(1) to (3) — https://www.burs.org.bw/index.php/tax/tax-laws-2026?download=954:income-tax-act-amendment-2026)_
- **SAT single payment** — Where the estimated liability is below BWP 50,000 no quarterly instalments are required and the tax is settled with the return within four months of the accounting period end  _(Income Tax Act, 2026 (Act No. 13 of 2026), Government Gazette Supplement A of 1 July 2026, BURS copy, s 127(2)(a) — https://www.burs.org.bw/index.php/tax/tax-laws-2026?download=954:income-tax-act-amendment-2026 ; payment with the return: PwC — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/botswana/corporate/tax-administration)_
