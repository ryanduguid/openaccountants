# British Virgin Islands Payroll & Social Contributions

## Payroll tax

Inland Revenue's June 2012 guide dates the payroll tax to 1 January 2005. It describes an employer or self-employed person's liability on actual or deemed remuneration, including benefits in cash or kind. It sets out Class 1 and Class 2 rates of 10% and 14% of the tax base, including for self-employed persons. The Financial Investigation Agency's 2024 audited accounts report a 14% employer liability, of which 8 percentage points are recoverable from employees, for its payroll. [Inland Revenue payroll guide, sections 1–6](https://www.bvi.gov.vg/sites/default/files/resources/Guide%20to%20Payroll%20Tax.pdf) · [Financial Investigation Agency 2024 accounts, note 2(p)](https://www.fiabvi.vg/Portals/0/DNNGalleryPro/uploads/2026/1/30/2024AuditedFinancialStatement.pdf)

The Inland Revenue guide dates the US$10,000 annual exemption to 1 January 2007 and excludes secondary or subsequent jobs from the exemption. The 2022 Throne Speech describes applying the exemption to the longest-held job. Confirm the current allocation when an employee changes jobs or has concurrent employers. The 27 October 2026 Budget Address proposes a US$5,000 rebate for lower earners at the end of 2027, subject to approval. [Inland Revenue payroll guide, sections 2–3 and 7](https://www.bvi.gov.vg/sites/default/files/resources/Guide%20to%20Payroll%20Tax.pdf) · [2022 Throne Speech](https://bvi.org.uk/speech-from-the-throne-2022/) · [2027 Budget Address](https://www.gov.vg/news/2027-budget-address-honourable-dr-natalio-d-wheatley)

Inland Revenue's guide records that from 1 January 2007, monthly returns and payment are due within 21 days after month-end. It gives the 30th day of the fourth month following the financial year for the annual return, which is 30 April for a calendar-year filer. Confirm the applicable filing date, class thresholds, employee deduction rules and late charges for the period with Inland Revenue. [Inland Revenue payroll guide, sections 2 and 8](https://www.bvi.gov.vg/sites/default/files/resources/Guide%20to%20Payroll%20Tax.pdf)

### Filing from 2 November 2026

Use the [eTax portal](https://efiling.gov.vg/) for payroll declarations and online tax payment by debit or credit card. Existing users can sign in and new users can register online. The Ministry describes manual entry or an import using its CSV templates, calculated totals, saved drafts and online corrections for filed periods. Confirm any distinct registration, annual return or self-employed procedure with Inland Revenue. [Ministry of Finance eTax release, 6 November 2026](https://www.gov.vg/news/ministry-finance-launches-enhanced-etax-portal-and-online-tax-payments)

## Social Security and National Health Insurance

For 2026, the Social Security Board lists private-sector contributions of 4% employee and 4.5% employer, civil service contributions of 3.5% employee and 4% government, self-employed contributions of 8.5% and voluntary contributions of 7% of earnings set by the Board. Each employer contributes for an employee with multiple jobs. The Board handles refunds if combined contributions exceed the limit. [Social Security Board contributions](https://www.vissb.vg/contributions/)

The Board's 2026 bulletin gives a US$53,400 annual Social Security earnings cap (US$4,450 monthly) and contributions due by the 14th of the following month. The Board's general contributions page and the 2026 bulletin quote different late penalties. Confirm the applicable penalty with the Board for the relevant period. [2026 Social Security Board bulletin](https://www.vissb.vg/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/SSB-Contributions-Insurable-Earnings-2026.pdf) · [Social Security Board contributions](https://www.vissb.vg/contributions/)

National Health Insurance lists a 7.5% contribution on insurable income for employed and self-employed persons. Employers and employees each contribute 3.75% for employed persons. The annual NHI earnings cap is US$106,800 in 2026. The Board announced new annual caps from 1 January 2027 of US$55,536 for Social Security and US$111,072 for NHI, with contribution rates unchanged. Check the NHI rules for dependants, pensioners and other categories separately. [NHI contribution breakdown](https://www.vinhi.vg/nhi-contribution-breakdown/) · [NHI regulations explanation](https://www.vinhi.vg/nhi-regulations-explained/) · [Social Security Board cap announcement, 9 October 2026](https://www.vissb.vg/increase-in-maximum-insurable-earnings-effective-1st-january-2027/)
