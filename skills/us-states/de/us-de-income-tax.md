# Delaware individual income tax: coverage gap

This guide has no individual income-tax computation. Its former body duplicated
the gross receipts tax guide, so it could not support the income-tax scope named
in its description.

For individual income tax, obtain the relevant year's Form PIT-RES and
instructions from the [Delaware Division of Revenue](https://revenue.delaware.gov/).
Do not substitute gross receipts tax rules. Use `us-de-gross-receipts-tax` only for
that separate tax.

> General reference only. Have a qualified Delaware tax professional review any
> working paper before filing or acting on it.
