# Gabon national SARL capital and statutes

11 November 2026. The Gabon formation guide now cites the national SARL
capital and statutes provisions in Loi n°028/2018 of 21 April 2020, published
in Journal Officiel n°65 of 1 May 2020. The selected rules address the guide's
2025 period. These instrument and publication dates establish the source
chain. The exact earlier commencement day requires separate evidence.
[Official Gazette](https://journal-officiel.ga/4761-028-2018-/)

Article 2 replaces articles 2 and 3 of Loi n°13/2016 of 5 September 2016.
Replacement article 2 lets members fix total SARL capital freely in the
statutes. Capital must be divided into equal shares with a nominal value of
at least CFA 5,000. The guide's unresolved national-capital wording is
replaced by these provisions.

Replacement article 3 permits statutes made by private deed or notarial act.
Deposit of private-deed statutes in a notary's records with recognition of
writing and signatures is optional. A private deed requires certification by
the body responsible for enterprise creation before registration. The body
signs at the foot of the statutes and stamps each page. The guide now records
this duty alongside the permitted forms of statutes.

The example uses 20 equal shares at CFA 5,000, giving total capital of
CFA 100,000. Subscription, pay-up and other formation requirements remain
applicable, including certification for private-deed statutes. Existing uniform-law payment rules and the
preceding batch's annual reporting section are preserved.

The Gazette's own public search exposed the law text after the National
Assembly register's old document link returned 404. The Gazette text gives
the law number, date, provisions, publication header and signatories.

Later amendments, current submission procedures, required documents, fees,
other entity forms, sector rules and qualified local review remain open.
The guide stays Tier 2 and pending review. Its version advances to 1.4 and
its description records the national source. This strengthens an existing partial
formation component and earns no complete-topic credit.
