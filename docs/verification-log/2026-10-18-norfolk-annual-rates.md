# Norfolk Island ordinary rates and annual waste charges

I checked the 30 June 2026 carried Council resolution against its category
descriptions and public HTML table cells, then added `nf-annual-rates` and
linked it from the Norfolk scope guide. It records the adopted 2026–27
ordinary-rate bases and dollar multipliers, annual waste charges and units,
selected valuation, instalment, relief and review rules, and fictional
calculations. It remains a Tier 2 source-cited draft pending professional
review.

The sources are the current Norfolk Island Act, Applied Laws Ordinance,
NSW Local Government Act and General Regulation, Land Valuation Act,
Waste Management Act and Regulations, Absentee Landowners Levy Act and
Continued Laws Ordinance, together with the public Council records cited
in the guide. Current Register records and selected operative provisions
were checked together. This is not a claim to have read every provision of
each whole Act.

The Council resolution adds the base to the value-based rate. Accommodation
waste charges apply per unit; vacancy relief is stated for six residential
and farmland rows. Cafe/Restaurant B and Clubs/Bars B differ by one cent.
Mining's hyphens are not evidence of a nil liability. The old base-only
transition and the Council-wide minimum revenue do not replace the current
property calculation.

I also checked the selected July and August financial motions and read all
three native PDF pages of the September operational-plan report. The latter
proposes three service-fee amendments for exhibition and later Council
consideration; it does not prove a carried amendment. No private ownership
roll, parcel-sale list, client record or citizen submission was opened.
Actual notices and any later carried amendments remain required.

The guide separates a valuation notice's issue date, a category declaration
and service of a rates notice. It preserves Norfolk's court/tribunal
substitutions and the Land Valuation Act's particular no-stay provision.
The annual section 501 waste charge is not automatically treated as a
domestic-waste charge for pensioner concessions. The Council's adopted
interest figure is recorded, but an actual interest calculation is held
until the Norfolk authority and ceiling, notice and account facts are
verified. The absentee levy ceased to have new levy days after 2016;
historical recovery provisions are not treated as debt forgiveness.
