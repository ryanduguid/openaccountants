# Ghana VAT Free Zone and imported-service scope

11 November 2026. This entry follows the [VAT and pension reconciliation](2026-11-11-ghana-vat-pension-reconciliation.md). The Ghana VAT guide remains Tier 2 and pending qualified review.

Both official PDFs have now been extracted by local OCR: all 87 pages of Act 1151 and all 22 pages of the guidelines. OCR layout can be unreliable. The selected changes were checked against rendered original pages, with independent review of the same draft and 4 images. This is not a complete review of either document or the VAT guide.

Act 1151 section 75 expressly commences the Act on 1 January 2026 (PDF page 71). Section 72, on PDF page 64, defines imported services by supplier, resident recipient and use in Ghana other than making taxable supplies. It includes qualifying supplies from a Free Zone developer or enterprise. The guide now applies that use test before calculating tax on an overseas invoice and keeps liability distinct from deduction eligibility.

The Second Schedule, goods clause (6), on PDF page 80, requires satisfactory documentation that a Free Zone developer or enterprise operates and acquires goods in accordance with Act 504. A permit alone does not establish eligibility. GRA Administrative Guideline GRA/AG/25/002, issued on 31 December 2025, section 14 (printed page 13, PDF page 15), distinguishes qualifying goods supplied to these recipients from services supplied to them, which it treats as standard-rated. These services-to and services-from cases must be considered separately. The former blanket Free Zone zero-rating statements are replaced.

The cocoa-export edge case no longer promises recovery of every input. Export zero-rating, input eligibility and refund conditions are separate checks. The remaining refund summary has not been fully reconciled by this batch.
