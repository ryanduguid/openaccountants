# Laos reduced profit-tax rates under the revised Income Tax Law

Reviewed 11 November 2026 against the Lao Official Gazette's scanned [Income Tax Law (Revised)](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/kcfinder/upload/files/88-25-6-2025_0001.pdf) and [Ministry of Finance register](https://laoofficialgazette.gov.la/index.php?r=site/listlegistioncp&agencies_id=2&old=0). The Law was adopted by Resolution No. 164/NA of 25 June 2025 and promulgated by Decree No. 145/PO of 6 August 2025. The Gazette lists it as current. Article 74 gives 1 September 2025 and the register records publication on 19 June 2026. The treatment of the intervening period remains unresolved.

Article 16(2.1) and (2.2) place the specified 5% and 7% rates after the investment-law profit-tax exemption ends. The text on printed page 7 specifies research and health activities for the 5% rate and the types of technology for the 7% rate. The 10% rate in article 16(2.3) appears at the top of printed page 8 and applies to listed companies for 10 years from listing. The earlier guide omitted that provision. Its references to a 13% rate for 4 years, to the exemption period itself and to education as an article 16(2.1) activity have been corrected. The text immediately after article 16(2.3) refers education and other activities outside the article to the investment and other applicable rules.

Confirm the investment-law qualification, historic listing treatment, sector definitions and the effect of any implementing measures with a Lao practitioner. The draft retains the guide's pending review status and its 2025 label for the older sections. The amendment is limited to the source and period text and the selected profit-tax rates.
