The Second Protocol explanation heading says 24 January 1993; its body,
operative signature and notes use 26 January. Article 25(5)'s retained
extraction refers to paragraph 2(d), while the public-trading exception is
in paragraph 3(d) and the explanation links its definition there. Note 13
refers to paragraph 3 for expected loan-related competent-authority relief,
while discretion is in paragraph 4 and the explanation discusses it there.
The operative paragraph 1(b) liability wording does not reproduce the
explanation's deductible-payment qualification. These differences are
recorded without silently rewriting the operative wording, treating the
notes as automatic entitlement or claiming a formal corrigendum. Source
images and equally authentic Hebrew text must be checked before treating
extraction anomalies as original-document errors.
