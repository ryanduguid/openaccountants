# Bermuda corporate services tax

On 20 November 2026 I checked the [Bermuda corporate services tax guide](../../skills/international/bermuda/bm-corporate-services-tax.md) against the [posted Corporate Services Tax Act 1995, ss 1–7](https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Consolidated%20Law/1995/Corporate%20Services%20Tax%20Act%201995). Sections 2 and 3 distinguish the provider from the exempted undertaking receiving the service. I checked the conditions on director, resident representative, banking, audit and insurance management work. The [1999 order](https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Annual%20Law/Statutory%20Instruments/1999/Banks%20and%20Deposit%20Companies%20%28Consequential%20Amendments%29%20Order%201999) replaces the bank licensing reference from 1 January 2000.

I checked the 4 customer classes in [Companies Act 1981, s 2(1)](https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Consolidated%20Law/1981/Companies%20Act%201981). The [Limited Liability Company Act 2016, s 21 and Schedule para 1(1)](https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Consolidated%20Law/2016/Limited%20Liability%20Company%20Act%202016) defines and adds the exempted LLC. [BR 96/2016](https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Annual%20Law/Statutory%20Instruments/2016/Limited%20Liability%20Company%20Act%202016%20Commencement%20Day%20Notice%202016) appoints 1 October 2016. I left a hold for services solely under LLC Act s 26(5). The tax statute names other resident representative appointments. Separately supported registered office or administrative work has its own service limb.

The [original Act](https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Annual%20Law/Acts/1995/Corporate%20Services%20Tax%20Act%201995) specifies 4%. The [2013 Act](https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Annual%20Law/Acts/2013/Corporate%20Services%20Tax%20Amendment%20Act%202013) and [2015 Act](https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Annual%20Law/Acts/2015/Corporate%20Services%20Tax%20Amendment%20Act%202015) set 6% from 1 April 2013 and 7% from 1 April 2015 respectively. The original cover and consolidated end note give different assent dates. Section 1 gives 31 March 1995 as the operative date in both, and section 2 starts the first period in April 1995.

I checked the 30-day provider registration clock and separate 15-day return and payment requirements in sections 5 and 6. The [government article](https://www.gov.bm/corporate-services-tax) cites a 1975 Act and abbreviates the qualifying services and customers. The linked [Form C1](https://www.gov.bm/files/media-library/20260413/e34105c4-corporate-services-tax-registration-form.pdf) and the statute identify the 1995 Act. The previous form URL returned 404. I used the form link from the current article. Section 7 applies the [Taxes Management Act 1976](https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Consolidated%20Law/1976/Taxes%20Management%20Act%201976) with necessary adaptations. I checked the general return variation, correction, extension, late penalty, objection and appeal provisions against the specific CST rules. The [government payment page](https://www.gov.bm/paying-taxes-and-bills) corroborates the ordinary quarter dates.
