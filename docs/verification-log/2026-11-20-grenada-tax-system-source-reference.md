# Grenada tax-system source reference

On 20 November 2026 I checked the [Grenada reference](../../skills/international/grenada/gd-tax-system-source-reference.md) against the retained [Cap 149](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Cap149-INCOME-TAX-ACT.pdf) and selected original amendments. Section 2 supports the separate residence branches. Sections 7–11 distinguish the tax charge, Grenada-source assessable income and the assessment and basis periods. Section 21 attributes partnership income or loss to the partners. These readings describe the retained edition. A common cutoff for the full amendment chain remains open.

I checked the bank-deposit and branch changes in [Act 41 of 2013](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Act-No.-41-of-2013-Income-Tax-Amendment.pdf) and the cash-winnings changes in [Act 22 of 2014](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Act-No.-22-of-2014-Income-Tax-Amendment-Act.pdf) against the publication rule in Cap 153 sections 6–7. [Act 27 of 2015](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Act-No.-27-of-2015-Income-Tax-Amendment.pdf) uses a payment trigger for sections 3 and 12 and a basis-period trigger for section 11. [Act 8 of 2016](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Act-No.-8-of-2016-Income-Tax-Amendment.pdf) and [SRO 26 of 2016](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/S.R.O.-26-of-2016-Income-Tax-Amendment-Act-Commencment-Order.pdf) support the 1 May 2016 investment-withholding change.

The original Act records assent on 25 August 2015. Constitution section 45(3) makes a duly submitted Bill law upon assent. Section 45(4) requires Gazette publication before operation and permits retrospective effects. Government Notice 37 confirms publication on 4 September 2015. I checked the first Gazette page with local OCR and separately checked the Constitution's native text. Application of Act 27's enactment trigger to a payment or basis-period start between the two dates remains open.
