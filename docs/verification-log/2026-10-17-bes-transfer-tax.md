# BES transfers: market value, ABB and document registration

On 17 October 2026 I read the complete transfer-tax chapter, articles 7.1–7.24,
the full registration regulation and selected general implementing provisions
for the new [transfer-tax reference](../../skills/international/caribbean-netherlands/bq-transfer-tax.md).
The reference remains Tier 2/pending_review. It adds a substantive topic without
certifying a complete property transaction, estate analysis or country pack.

The transfer chapter matches the January, February and April 2026 texts and
the fresh current capture after explicit interface-label and whitespace removal.
The current registration registry still selects 1 January 2011 and shows no
other version. Its age is not evidence that it has ceased to apply.

The authority's overview, rate and exemption pages were read. Their summaries
omit conditions supplied by the law, including the registered-ship size
definition, special partition rules and the requirement that ABB be due for
the specified new-building exemption. The six-month earlier-transfer rule
reduces the base by the qualifying prior taxable amount. It is not an
unqualified credit for all previously paid tax.

The full registration regulation supplies separate eight-day, four-day,
three-month and thirty-day document terms, their starting-day rule and the
non-working-day extension. The guide separates those duties from tax remittance
and does not invent a quarterly payment calendar. Actual remittance timing,
return mapping, fractional-cent rounding and transaction eligibility remain
held until their complete procedure and evidence are established.

The 27 June 2025 government letter was read as HTML text, including its
historical account of the 2011 changes and proposals for future reform.
It was not treated as proof that every 2026 inheritance or foreign connection
has no tax. No PDF, OCR or authentic-layout comparison was used.

Fictional examples use stipulated ordinary acquisitions and a qualifying
earlier transfer. They do not resolve a negative residual base, mixed property,
recoverable ABB, complex partition, gift, inheritance or real conveyance.
Technical checks and arithmetic are recorded against the candidate separately.
No live taxpayer, portal, engagement workbook or practitioner sign-off was
completed. No comprehensive-area completion credit is granted by this batch.
