## Corrected controls

The payroll guide separates social insurance from payroll tax and occupational pensions. It replaces the unqualified current BMD 75.30 total and age 16-64 shortcut with statutory eligibility and period-specific weekly categories. The general guide replaces unsupported BMD 35.69 employed-share and BMD 63.10 self-employed defaults. Ordinary 2026/27 employed amounts are BMD 39.25 each below 65, with only the employer amount at 65 or over; self-employed insured-person amounts are BMD 78.50 below 65 and BMD 39.25 at 65 or over, after eligibility and exceptions.

The contribution week, exact 4-hour exclusion, 26-consecutive-week non-ordinary-residence test, school-leaving/study definitions and specified student-employment route remain explicit. The [official employer page](https://socialinsurance.gov.bm/Contributions/Employers/)'s inclusive 4-hour wording does not override the Act; its 30-day employee-registration wording does not displace statutory entry-registration and immediate employer-number checks. The one-contribution, first-employer and employed/self-employed priority rules prevent multiplication of weekly contributions. Employer-share recovery is prohibited and employee recovery remains limited by s 7.

A small-income certificate is not automatic from low income. The BMD 3,000 boundary is inclusive; verify the grant, dates, evidence, continuing conditions and whole-week coverage. Backdating by up to 13 weeks requires the Director's decision. The insured-person exception does not establish exemption of the employer's separate contribution.

The applicable contributions are due by the last working day of the following month under ss 4(7) and 8, with employer-specific Director arrangements qualified. Unknown month-end week allocation is not resolved by inventing the last Friday or importing payroll dates. Registration, immediate employer number checks, self-employed registration within 7 days and contribution-record requirements remain separate controls.
