# New Zealand ordinary PAYE and student-loan calculations

12 November 2026. I extended the employer payroll guide for ordinary salary or wages paid from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027. I reused the byte-identical [Inland Revenue payroll specification](https://www.ird.govt.nz/-/media/project/ir/home/documents/digital-service-providers/software-providers/payroll-calculations-business-rules-specifications/payroll-calculations-and-business-rules-specification.pdf?modified=20260401012317), version 1.0 dated 24 March 2026. This pass reads PDF pages 6, 20-25, 28, 29 and 31 as selected native text and rendered images; the M flowchart on page 21 has little native text, so the detailed table on page 22 supplies the calculation rules. Native extraction warned about encoding and complex layouts. The earlier page 98 OCR warning remains outside this pass. No complete-PDF or amendment-chain review is claimed.

Sections 5.2-5.4 distinguish M/ME annualisation from the whole-period-dollar base used for secondary tax and student loans. M/ME includes period cents when annualising, drops annual cents, calculates tax and ACC, truncates to weekly cents and only then converts to other pay frequencies. The guide now preserves that sequence. Its $5,000.99 monthly M example gives $939.46 PAYE; direct annual-to-monthly truncation would give $939.49. A separate $15,000 monthly case checks the stated annual ACC maximum, and an eligible ME weekly case checks IETC abatement.

The current [IETC eligibility page](https://www.ird.govt.nz/income-tax/income-tax-for-individuals/individual-tax-credits/independent-earner-tax-credit-ietc) and [salary/wage instructions](https://www.ird.govt.nz/income-tax/income-tax-for-individuals/individual-tax-credits/independent-earner-tax-credit-ietc/getting-the-independent-earner-tax-credit-if-you-earn-salary-or-wages) supply the residence, benefit, Working for Families, whole-month and declaration qualifications. The payroll credit formula is not an entitlement decision. The [tax-code explanation](https://www.ird.govt.nz/income-tax/income-tax-for-individuals/tax-codes-and-tax-rates-for-individuals/about-tax-codes) supports using total estimated income for a secondary code, while specification section 5.6 supplies the standard combined PAYE/ACC rates and truncation.

Sections 2.2, 5.4 and 5.6 supply the 2026-27 student-loan thresholds and ordinary primary/secondary algorithms. The [student-loan salary/wage page](https://www.ird.govt.nz/repaying-my-student-loan-when-i-earn-salary-or-wages) explains the absence of a second ordinary threshold and special-rate options, but still labels its threshold as the 2026 tax year. I use the expressly dated specification for 2026-27 figures. The new cases cover monthly threshold cents, no positive primary excess and a secondary deduction below the primary-job threshold. An ND case uses section 5.8's combined rate without adding ACC twice.

The guide contains 7 additional required worked cases. They test arithmetic for stated synthetic scenarios, not code selection, personal eligibility, a final annual assessment or every special-payment regime. The existing required-examples path already makes all cases in this guide mandatory. No maintained code, dependency, catalogue or published guide count changes are needed.
