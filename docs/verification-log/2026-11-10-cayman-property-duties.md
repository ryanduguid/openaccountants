# Cayman Islands property and equity transfer duties

I checked the 2026 treatment in [Cayman Tax](../../skills/international/cayman-islands/cayman-tax.md#52-property-and-transfer-duties) and [Cayman Islands Tax Overview](../../skills/international/cayman-islands/ky-tax-overview.md) against the Acts, Gazette amendments and agency guidance.

| Topic | Source check |
| --- | --- |
| Ordinary land transfers | The higher of instrument price and market value sets the base. From 1 January 2026, the 10% rate applies to the whole base once it reaches CI$2 million. Reg 3 preserves the rate on the qualifying pre-commencement instrument. An earlier ad valorem payment can reduce or extinguish duty on a qualifying later conveyance under the separate Schedule credit. [Stamp Duty Act, Schedule](https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/1973/1973-0005/1973-0005.pdf) · [SL 63 of 2025, regs 1–3](https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/SUBORDINATE/2025/2025-0063/2025-0063_SL%2063%20of%202025.pdf) |
| Concessions and linked deals | SL 3 sets different thresholds and bases for first and second purchases. SL 63 changes the upper band and the linked transaction tiers. Cayman Brac has separately announced waivers and a 3% rate for qualifying developed property below CI$2 million through 31 December 2030. [SL 3 of 2025, reg 2](https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/SUBORDINATE/2025/2025-0003/2025-0003_SL%203%20of%202025.pdf) · [SL 63 of 2025, reg 2](https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/SUBORDINATE/2025/2025-0063/2025-0063_SL%2063%20of%202025.pdf) · [Cayman Brac extension](https://gov.ky/w/cayman-brac-duty-concessions-extended) |
| Instruments and filing | The Act distinguishes the transferor signature, disposition, assignment signature and obligation discharge triggers. The form checklist supplies the supporting documents. The Schedule separately provides for leases, mortgages, qualifying gifts, assents and probate instruments. [Stamp Duty Act, ss 20–22 and Schedule](https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/1973/1973-0005/1973-0005.pdf) · [Lands and Survey forms](https://www.caymanlandinfo.ky/forms-and-guidelines) |
| Equity transfers | Act 16 replaced the geographical branches from 1 January 2026. The higher of price and taxable value sets the base and tier. The 31-day return and payment duty, nominal equity valuation, statutory exclusions and interest sit in the principal Act. The Government's 2 January explanation confirms the 10% tier where either value reaches CI$2 million. [Principal Act, ss 2–5 and 7–9](https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/1976/1976-0020/1976-0020_2022%20Revision.pdf) · [Act 16 of 2025, ss 1–3](https://gov.ky/w/land-holding-companies-share-transfer-tax-amendment-act-2025-lg54-s3) · [Government explanation](https://gov.ky/w/legislation-passed-to-increase-stamp-duty-on-properties-worth-2m-and-over) |

The prescribed equity transfer return's amended line 8 refers to taxable value. The Act also compares the price. I found no published form instruction for the case where price is higher. The guide gives the statutory calculation and directs readers to confirm the return entry with the Ministry. Mixed-date credit application, interaction between concessions and the current administrative submission route for the equity return still need agency evidence. The checkable examples cover the land threshold, first versus second purchase bases and a share price that triggers the higher tier.
