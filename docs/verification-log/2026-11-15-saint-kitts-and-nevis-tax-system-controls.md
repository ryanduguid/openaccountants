# Dated Saint Kitts and Nevis tax-system classification controls

I reconciled official sources on 15 November 2026 and drafted a separate tax-system orientation covering territorial reach, residence, taxpayer types, currency, assessment periods and responsible authorities. The guide remains Tier 2 and pending_review.

## Period and source scope

The specified revised statutes state **31 December 2017**. The Inland Revenue Act states **31 December 2002**. Act 12 of 2021 is a separately identified amendment, not part of the 2017 consolidation. Its first page records assent and publication in Extraordinary Gazette 15 on **26 March 2021**; all 4 pages contain no postponement, retrospective date or appointed-day clause. Reading those records with Constitution s 42(3)–(4), physical p 36, supports operation from 26 March 2021 as an expressly labelled source-based inference. This does not establish a complete amendment chain at 2021 or 2026. ## Classification checks and repairs

Seven manual source-selection cases cover the ordinary employee exclusion, foreign registration versus management/control, the corporate services duration versus individual residence, a partnership computation election, separate Inland Revenue and Customs currencies, a non-December accounting date and Nevis island attribution. These are classification controls, not executed returns or new arithmetic fixtures. No arithmetic registry or baseline change is proposed.

Independent review identified 2 source-scope errors in an earlier local draft. The final source draft removes a resident-body branch from the mixed-trade proviso because the retained KN text names only a resident individual whose home is in the State. It also uses the amendment's agent relationship condition, 'is a related person', rather than importing a 'closely related' threshold. The exact 2 repairs were rechecked and approved.
