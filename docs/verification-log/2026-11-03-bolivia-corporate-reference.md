The ordinary-company reference separates taxable profit from accounting profit and conditions territorial-source exclusions, deductions, losses and asset allowances. Ley 843 article 50 supports the ordinary 25% rate; article 48 gives the ordinary following-3-year loss period without indexation, so the older indefinite indexed wording in DS article 32 is not applied over the later law. The owner-interest example applies both the domestic benchmark cap and the third-party-interest limit in DS article 18(k), rather than treating the latter as an EBITDA limit.
