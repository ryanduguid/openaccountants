The ordinary-company reference separates taxable profit from accounting profit and conditions territorial-source exclusions, deductions, losses and asset allowances. Ley 843 article 50 supports the ordinary 25% rate; article 48 gives the ordinary following-3-year loss period without indexation, so the older indefinite indexed wording in DS article 32 is not applied over the later law.
