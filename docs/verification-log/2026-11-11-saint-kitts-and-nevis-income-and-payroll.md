# Saint Kitts and Nevis income tax and payroll

I checked the following rules through 11 November 2026 and recorded the provisions, periods and limits in the [income tax and payroll guide](../../skills/international/saint-kitts-and-nevis/kn-social-security-reconciliation.md). It retains the original Social Security worked and held positions.

| Check | Result and limit |
|---|---|
| Personal income tax and wages | Section 3(3), inserted by Act 14 of 1980, excludes income accruing to persons other than companies from 1 May 1980. Sections 68–71 govern assessable emoluments. Ordinary personal income tax and its wage withholding are inapplicable within that exclusion. The broader topic still needs the effect of section 37 on specified deductible resident payments and the non-company permanent establishment filing guidance reconciled. [Income Tax Act, 2017 revision, ss 3, 36–37 and 68–71](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Act17TOC/Ch-20_22-Income-Tax-Act.pdf) · [Inland Revenue corporate guidance](https://www.sknird.com/corporate-income-tax/) |
| Corporate tax and reporting | The 2021 Act inserted the worldwide resident-company base and attribution to a local permanent establishment for a non-resident company. The Act's 2017 revision and Inland Revenue's instalment FAQ show 33%. The December 2023 budget announcement and government summary give 25%, with the announced start on 1 January 2024. The instrument resolving the difference remains open. Inland Revenue confirmed 15 April 2026 for return and payment on a 31 December 2025 year end. Its September 2023 CIT-101 guidelines identify separate information filers. [Act 12 of 2021, s 3](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Annual-Laws/2021/ACTs/Act-12-of-2021-Income-Tax-Amendment-Act-2021.pdf) · [Inland Revenue FAQs](https://www.sknird.com/faqs/) · [2024 budget announcement](https://www.sknis.gov.kn/2023/12/13/corporate-income-tax-rate-in-st-kitts-and-nevis-set-at-25-from-january-2024/) · [Filing notices](https://www.sknird.com/announcements-upcoming-events/) |
| Unincorporated business tax | I checked the scope, 4% charge, goods and services bases and election. Monthly statutory floors and quarterly form allowances can diverge. I recomputed the uneven-services example. The accepted calculation and the form's COVID relief line remain open. The 2026 calendar confirms the next quarterly deadline and the February 2024 SMARTS launch included UBT. Portal acceptance remains untested. [Cap. 18.46, ss 2–8](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Act17TOC/Ch-18_46-Unincorporated-Business-Tax-Act.pdf) · [UBT-01, p 2](https://www.sknird.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/UBT-Return-Updated-3.0.pdf) · [Tax calendar](https://www.sknird.com/tax-calendar/) |
| Withholding | Section 36 gives 15% on covered non-resident payments, with certificates and quarterly return and payment. The 2026 calendar matches the next quarter. Exceptions, treaty relief, branches and the resident-payment rule remain open. [Income Tax Act, ss 36–37](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Act17TOC/Ch-20_22-Income-Tax-Act.pdf) · [Tax calendar](https://www.sknird.com/tax-calendar/) |
| Wage levy and severance | The Acts published on 29 February 2024 and 11 July 2025 raised the low-wage thresholds in succession. I checked both levy shares in that band, the upper-band wording and the December 2025 waiver of both shares on bonuses paid that month. The 2017 Protection of Employment Act sets a separate employer contribution of 1% of normal wages, subject to prescribed variation. Threshold instructions, remittance cut-offs, special categories and termination remain open. [Cap. 20.21, ss 2–6](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Act17TOC/Ch-20_21-Housing-and-Social-Development-Levy-Act.pdf) · [Act 14 of 2025](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Annual-Laws/2025/ACTs/Act-14-OF-2025-The-Housing-and-Social-Development-Levy-Amendment.pdf) · [December 2025 waiver](https://www.sknird.com/government-of-saint-christopher-and-nevis-announces-temporary-waiver-of-social-services-levy-on-december-2025-bonuses/) · [Cap. 18.27, ss 13–15](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Act17TOC/Ch-18_27-Protection-of-Employment-Act.pdf) |
| Minimum wage and Social Security | The government confirms EC$12.50 hourly from July 2025. The operative 2025 order remains open. The Board booklet corroborates the 5% employee, 5% employer and additional 1% employer injury profile and EC$6,500 monthly ceiling. It dates the injury branch to January 1986. The Law Commission index lists Act 7 of 1985 as an amendment to the original Social Security Act. The exact charging instrument, base and later chain remain open. [Prime Minister's Office, 6 November 2026](https://www.sknis.gov.kn/2026/11/06/this-is-real-support-prime-minister-drew-says-as-government-investment-in-step-programme-reaches-54-million-in-2026-alone/) · [Board booklet, PDF pp 9 and 16–17](https://socialsecurity.kn/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Things-You-Should-Know-12-06-24.pdf) · [Law Commission chronological index, 1985](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Revised-Edition-Indices/2020-St-Kitts-and-Nevis-Rev-Ed-Index-as-at-31-Dec-2020.pdf) |
