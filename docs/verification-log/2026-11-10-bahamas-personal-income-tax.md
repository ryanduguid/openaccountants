# Bahamas domestic personal income tax scope

The Bahamian Ministry of Finance's June 2024 competent authority guidance confirms the absence of a domestic personal income tax regime. The current legislation index and the 2026-27 Budget Communication corroborate that position through 10 November 2026. For resident and non-resident individuals the domestic personal tax base, rates, deductions, allowances, tax year and return are inapplicable for the checked period. Check foreign tax, reporting and contributions for the facts and period concerned. [Ministry of Finance guidance, ‘Scope of the MAP “Limitations”’](https://cdn.bahamas.gov.bs/tenant/tenantministryoffinance/documents/All%20Documents/BAHAMAS-JAPANMAPGUIDANCEDOCUMENT-20240718120503.pdf) · [Legislation index](https://laws.bahamas.gov.bs/cms/legislation/index/by-alphabetical.html) · [2026-27 Budget Communication](https://bahamasbudget.gov.bs/media/filer_public/81/9d/819d8c82-6e4c-471e-bf84-2e0965620327/final_budget_communication_fiscal_year_20262027-_a_budget_that_build_on_progress_.pdf)

The Domestic Minimum Top-Up Tax Act imposes a separate charge on Bahamian constituent entities of qualifying multinational groups, with a commencement condition for fiscal years starting in 2024. Employee services to an employer by reason of employment fall outside the VAT Act under s 3(6), as shown in the 1 July 2024 reprint used for the 2025 reference. The VAT guide now refers a payment labelled ‘dividend’ for classification based on the underlying transaction. [Domestic Minimum Top-Up Tax Act, 2024, ss 1, 4 and 5](https://laws.bahamas.gov.bs/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/2024/2024-0058/2024-0058_1.pdf) · [VAT Act, 1 July 2024 reprint](https://inlandrevenue.finance.gov.bs/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Value-Added-Tax-Act.pdf)

The guides retain their pending-review status. Current NIB schedules, wider VAT rules and DMTT filing, instalments and credits require separate verification.
