# Cook Islands: ordinary CINSF contribution reference

This batch adds a bounded domestic employee classification and contribution
reference for the Cook Islands National Superannuation Fund. It separates
mandatory membership, age and historical elections, paid elective contributions,
earnings inclusions and exclusions, employer shares and remittance allocation.
Self-employed, overseas, pension and other programmes remain held.

The selected 52 current official API responses were retained and read. The
complete employer handbook was retained, with operative pages visually compared.
The handbook supports the ordinary 5% employee and 5% employer reference but
cannot establish the complete current instrument chain. Public guidance giving
a month-end payment date conflicts with section 48, which selects the 20th of
the month following the payable month. The guide preserves this conflict and
does not authorise an actual remittance calendar from the illustration.

The signed 2026 employment amendment changes leave and Crown-funded paternity
pay. It does not amend the selected CINSF provisions. Leave entitlements and
Crown-payer allocation remain outside this reference. The signed 2026 VAT
amendment changes import exemptions, outside the companion domestic VAT scope.

The component check covers 20 fictional worked or held positions, 12 hold rows
and 8 computed missing-premise holds. It rejects 34 arithmetic, input-premise
and hold mutations and reconciles all 52 retained source hashes. Exact raw
precision is preserved: the example of 1,000.01 multiplied by 5% gives 50.0005.
No remittable rounded amount is asserted without the applicable convention.

The guide loads the shared universal and social-contribution workflows and
retains all required outputs, refusal controls and checks. Its country-slot
matrix records incomplete coverage. Currency, rounding, current instruments,
forms, actual payment histories and cross-month timing remain held.

Tier 2, pending_review and verified_by pending remain. Component arithmetic
and guide presence do not demonstrate a complete contribution workflow, actual
filing acceptance, professional sign-off or comprehensive country acceptance.
