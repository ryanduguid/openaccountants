# Nauru: conditional corporate annual-return date reference

The [source guide](../../skills/international/nauru/nr-corporate-annual-return-reference.md) adds a bounded reference for a fictional mature ordinary locally incorporated trading corporation. It remains Tier 2, pending_review and verified_by pending. It does not establish an accepted formation workflow, compliance calendar, annual return, certificate renewal or comprehensive jurisdiction coverage.

The complete official Corporations, Business Names, Business Licences and Beneficial Ownership volumes were retained and natively processed: 346, 68, 120 and 60 physical pages respectively. Only selected provisions were substantively examined; this is not a complete audit of all 594 pages. Seven selected pages received offline OCR, including the 3-page original Gazette and fee/application pages with extraction or layout problems. Original-image comparison verified the commencement notice, the annual-return timing, incorporation-certificate renewal and beneficial-ownership statement provisions and selected fee/application layouts. No actual fee is quoted as payable.

The [original GN40/1972](https://ronlaw.gov.nr/assets/docs/gazettes/1972/2/Nauru%20Government%20Gazette%2C%20No.%206%20%2818%20February%201972%29.pdf) in Gazette 6 distinguishes Part II's 21 February 1972 commencement from the 8 March commencement excluding Part VI Division 2. It does not establish whether that excluded division commenced later. The retained principal Act's introductory section reference conflicts with its operative section 1(2); that editorial discrepancy is preserved. The [Revised Written Laws Act 2021](https://ronlaw.gov.nr/assets/docs/acts/2021/Revised_Written_Laws_Act_2021.pdf) separates certification, scheduled revisions and appointed commencement and excludes explanatory notes/tables from the law. The [Law Revision and Consolidation Act 2019](https://ronlaw.gov.nr/assets/docs/acts/2019/Law%20Revision%20and%20Consolidation%20Act%202019_serv6.pdf) qualifies a revised edition's evidentiary status and preserves certified originals, later amendments and original commencement.

The 12 fictional date positions distinguish 28 days before the anniversary from the made-up date's separate 28-day lower bound. They include early/on-limit/late lodgement, February and cross-year boundaries, a same-day made-up date and a future-date hold. Independent date recomputation found no discrepancy, and 10 scratch mutation challenges detected the tested arithmetic and premise errors. The 10 held positions preserve invalid dates, January/calendar-year reconciliation, first-year and 29 February cases, unsupported entities, missing documents/signatures, beneficial-ownership countersignature, expired certificates, fees, current procedures and reviewer/output gates. These are component checks and textual guard review, not deployed workflow enforcement.

The separate annual calendar occurrence, certificate-renewal, prescribed-form, fee/payment, licence/name and beneficial-ownership obligations remain material. Neither the Registrar's stated 14-day issuance period nor the holding-corporation certificate branch creates a trading grace period or audit exemption. Current applicable law, the complete amendment/subsidiary-law chain, time-counting rules, actual facts, filing acceptance, full workflow outputs and scoped professional sign-off remain held. Repository checks, independent final review and completed financial review must be assessed separately before publication and merge.
