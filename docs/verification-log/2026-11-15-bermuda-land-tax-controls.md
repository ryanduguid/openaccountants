# Bermuda land-tax assessment, calculation and payment controls

Source checks recorded on 15 November 2026, using official captures observed on 14 November UTC. This entry covers selected 2026 land-tax rules in the existing Bermuda tax guide and its overview. Both remain Tier 2 and pending_review.

## What changed

I replaced the higher-band fallback for unknown ARV and the unsupported general quarterly-payment alternative. The guide now requires the assessment instrument, statutory owner, period-start class, assessed ARV, relevant period, actual relief/adjustment decisions and demand/service facts before an amount or deadline.

The selected private-dwelling rule uses the BMD 150 annual base from 1 January 2026. Above ARV BMD 22,000, every reached Schedule band applies, including the first 2 bands, plus that base. The examples preserve the resulting threshold step. Separate other-unit and EEZ cases require established classifications and designation facts. Tourist and interest-rate conflicts stay unresolved.

The age-related concession has its own application, Commissioner-satisfaction, ownership, occupation, age, Bermudian-status and period conditions. Payment, valuation information, objections, appeals and written postponement use distinct controls. Death-related deferment, hardship relief and the announced probate policy remain separate.

## Selected control groups

| Group | Control |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Establish the unit, statutory owner, period-start class, relevant source period, assessment instrument and ARV. Purchase price or a missing ARV does not select a rate. |
| 2 | Apply the 2026 private-dwelling flat/base-plus-all-slices branches and keep the other-unit, EEZ and unresolved tourist categories separate. |
| 3 | Apply s 3A only with its application, Commissioner satisfaction and complete period-specific criteria. Above BMD 45,500, subtract ordinary tax at that ARV from ordinary full-ARV tax. |
| 4 | Distinguish half-year charge, period-start owner, specific adjustments and subsequent-owner liability. No universal daily-proration rule is supplied. |
| 5 | Establish the demand and actual/deemed service, later-date payment rule and any written s 37 postponement. A request or advertised plan is not an approval. |
| 6 | Keep information, draft-list, Director-proposal, demand-objection and appeal routes separate, including grounds, service, extension and deposit conditions. |
| 7 | Distinguish exceptional relief, individual hardship authorisation, deferred tax on death, age-related concession and the announced probate policy. |
| 8 | Keep interest-rate, order, arrears-period and execution gaps explicit; an unresolved rate does not become an actual interest amount. |
