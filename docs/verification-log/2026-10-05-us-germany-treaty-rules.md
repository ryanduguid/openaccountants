# US treaties: Germany rules

The Germany section of `us-major-partners` omitted the parent-company dividend
exemption and described interest, royalties and technical services as blanket
zero-rate categories. Its contingent-interest note asserted 30% without the
deductibility condition, its construction PE row used 12 months, and its summary
implied reciprocal treatment. This check corrects the Germany section and its
PE/summary rows for tax year 2025, starting from commit `c4b1ba7`. Version advances
to 1.3; the guide remains a tier 2 draft pending professional review.

I checked the [1989 Convention](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-trty/germany.pdf),
[original technical explanation](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-trty/germtech.pdf),
[2006 Protocol](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-trty/germanprot06.pdf) and
[2007 technical explanation](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-trty/germanyte07.pdf)
linked by the [IRS treaty index](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/international-businesses/germany-tax-treaty-documents)
on 5 October 2026. Protocol IV replaces art 10; Protocol VII deletes art 14 and
removes the corresponding fixed-base references; Protocol III includes
professional services and other independent activities in art 7 business profits.
Protocol I replaces the saving and transparent-entity provisions, II replaces
the residence definition, and XIV replaces the limitation-on-benefits article.
Protocol XVI restates the related Protocol. The original explanations apply to
unchanged provisions, subject to those amendments.

Separately governed income retains its own article under art 7(6). |

The 5% corporate dividend category retains the direct 10% voting-stock test in
art 10(2)(a). The 2007 explanation describes conditional proportionate ownership
through a fiscally transparent entity and the entitlement-date test; it does not
replace direct ownership with a general indirect-ownership test. The parent
exemption needs one of the specific routes in art 10(3)(a), including both the
ownership/base-erosion and active-business tests where that route is used.

[IRS Announcement 2012-21](https://www.irs.gov/irb/2012-19_IRB) clarifies the
eligibility of specified pension arrangements, including German contractual
trust arrangements and pension investment funds, subject to all other treaty
requirements. It preserves the German asset-owner rule in restated related
Protocol para 8(b); it does not determine a claimant's eligibility.

The English Protocol's art 10(4) expressly conditions the 15% REIT ceiling on
three ownership/diversification tests. Its first test names an individual and
the REIT sentence does not name the art 10(3)(b) pension exemption. The 2007
explanation says pension relief also requires one of the tests and includes a
pension fund in the first test. The published UN English text has the same
paragraph references. The German text was retrieved and its references and
numerical boundaries compared, but it was not independently translated. No
official reconciliation was identified in the sources checked. The guide
therefore excludes REIT pension relief from its settled exemption claims and
records the discrepancy for qualified professional review. It neither denies
nor establishes a particular taxpayer's entitlement.

The section also qualifies PE income and special-relationship excess under
arts 10(7), 11(3), (4), 12(3), (4); the specified additional company tax under
arts 10(9), (10); and the third-jurisdiction PE and German investment-fund rules
under art 28(5), (6). The performance-specific PE exception in restated related
Protocol para 3 is distinguished from general services treatment.

The [UN publication index](https://treaties.un.org/doc/Publication/Cumulative%20Index/UNTS%20Volume%20No%202501-2550/cumindex.chrono.en.pdf)
records the original force date, 21 August 1991, and the amendment's force date,
28 December 2007. The [volume 1708 contents](https://treaties.un.org/doc/Publication/UNTS/Volume%201708/v1708.pdf)
confirm the original Convention at 1708 UNTS 3; the
[volume 2504 contents and record](https://treaties.un.org/doc/Publication/UNTS/Volume%202504/v2504.pdf)
confirm the Protocol at 2504 UNTS 90. The
[Treasury announcement](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/hp753)
also confirms the amendment's force. Protocol XVII applies withholding changes
to amounts paid or credited from 1 January 2007 and other income-tax changes to
taxable years beginning on or after 1 January 2008. The former twelve-month
transition does not set a 2025 rate.

The four IRS financial PDFs extracted natively without OCR. The original UN
contents needed local offline OCR; the protocol's contents and force record
extracted natively. The published English/German dividend pages and English
joint declaration needed offline OCR, with no hosted-review recommendations on
the selected dividend or declaration pages. The declaration proposes future
retirement-income amendments; those proposals were not treated as operative
dividend or withholding rules. Financial wording is based on the native IRS
instruments, with the UN material used for publication details and comparison.

Manual source comparisons distinguish direct holdings of 10% from below 10%,
the exact 80% and twelve-month parent boundaries and the additional entitlement
routes, RIC treatment from the three REIT tests, and the unresolved REIT pension
interpretation. They cover deductible profit-participating payments, REMIC
excess, defined royalties versus excluded film/equipment rentals, PE property
and special-relationship excess, and exactly twelve months versus more than
twelve months of construction. The third-jurisdiction test is below 60%, while
the investment-fund minimum is at least 90%. These are source comparisons, not
taxpayer calculations. For the third REIT test, art 10(4) defines diversification
as no single real-property interest exceeding 10% of total real-property
interests, excludes foreclosure property and attributes partnership property
in proportion to the REIT's partnership interest. Exactly 10% satisfies this
diversification bound; an interest above 10% does not. The ordinary-interest
row cites unchanged art 11(1), read with the Protocol amendments to its
exceptions and PE wording.
