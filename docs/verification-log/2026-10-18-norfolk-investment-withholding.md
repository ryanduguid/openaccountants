# Norfolk Island dividend, interest and royalty withholding

I added `nf-investment-withholding` and linked it from the Norfolk scope
guide. It covers selected domestic liability, payer and intermediary
triggers, constructive payment, ordinary rates, rounding, remittance and
reporting branches. It remains a Tier 2 source-cited draft pending
professional review, with actual residence, treaty and specialised
arrangements held.

The source chain records twenty-two primary-source files with hashes and
reading scopes. The current 1936 Assessment Act and Agreements Act records
are effective October 2026. The 1974 withholding rate Act remains the
latest published compilation despite its older date. The Administration
Act's latest published compilation ends in September; I checked the
October amendment list and relevant targets before relying on selected
Subdivision 12-F and Division 16 provisions. The October Act's credit
timing changes concern capital gains withholding, rather than all
withholding payments.

The current Administration Regulations supply the amount and whole-dollar
rules. Liability, payer trigger, taxable component, ordinary rate, relief
and prior withholding are separate checks. The draft keeps franking and
conduit conditions, resident foreign-PE branches, joint-account treatment,
temporary residents and other exceptions distinct. It does not infer
residence from an address or label every software invoice a royalty.

The live annual report instructions are dated 4 September 2026. Their
NAT 7187 coverage, AIIR overlap, private-investor conditions and reporting
routes replace reliance on an older printable search result. The annual
report, remittance, payment summary and additional certificate have
different requirements. Interest/royalty deduction restoration is for the
otherwise eligible income year when the withholding tax is paid, rather
than an automatic deduction in the payment year.

Fictional components and boundary checks cover ordinary interest,
part-franked dividends, royalties, prior withholding, input/output
whole-dollar treatment, joint accounts and statutory remittance dates.
Repository validation includes all four generators, strict metadata and
derived-tree checks, gate checkers, country report, documentation links,
root/MCP suites and an assembled Norfolk bundle. Baselines are preserved;
advisory output is assessed as leads rather than accepted from exit status
alone.

Large statutory captures were read in selected provisions. No particular
treaty, MLI, foreign credit, PE attribution, special fund, actual form,
engagement calendar, tax submission or payment is selected. No private
client, identity or bank data was obtained. These checks confer neither a
practitioner's sign-off nor comprehensive-country acceptance.
