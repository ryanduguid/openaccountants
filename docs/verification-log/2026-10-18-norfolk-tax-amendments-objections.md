# Norfolk assessment amendments, objections and shared Australian routing

I added nf-tax-amendments-objections for selected income-tax assessment
corrections, objection requirements, delayed decisions and external review.
The scope guide links to it. The conditional business self-amendment period
is separate from ordinary ATO review and the two-year objection branch.
The new guide remains Tier 2 pending professional review.

I read the complete current section 170, sections 173-175A, Part IVC's
selected interpretive and operative provisions and the Division 255 payment
provisions. Act 135 of 2024 Schedule 3 supplies item 3A's commencement and
application conditions and expressly retains the two-year objection branch.
Item 3A also expresses a power to amend within four years. Section 170's
limited-period definition omits item 3A, so a timely request is not treated
as an automatic processing extension under subsection (5).
The live TAA compilation's October amendment targets were checked instead
of treating its 30 September interval as all-law current.

The 2025 regulations apply by income-year start and retain the old rules
for earlier-starting income years. I read both section 14 exclusion tables
and the relevant application provisions. The tables have renumbering and
specified ESS wording differences. An ordinary 2025-26 year does not use
the successor table merely because an assessment issues during 2026.
Actual specialised ESS, scheme and historical cases remain held.

The current ATO individual amendment page has a specific expiry-date
example that is not used in this draft. Current statutory after-day rules
and dated ruling examples were compared; the guide states the statutory
relative clocks and requires actual notice-giving/service, calendar and
route-specific verification. The older ruling's HTML appendix is context,
not current item 3A authority or a claim to have read its authorised PDF.
The ATO's 3 July 2026 objection page supports the ordinary original-period
versus amended-notice comparison. Multiple notices and special decisions
retain their own limits and checks.

The Australian amendment guide already contained a conditional item 3A
table, but its method directed readers back to the first two-year table
and its intake grouped the original and most recent amended notices.
Version 1.3 corrects that routing, requires the complete notice chain and
preserves the statutory limits on later amendments. It declares applicable
workflow dependencies and mandatory-fact gates. The penalty tables, older
ruling/PDF material and other financial details retain their earlier
11 October provenance; no new full Australian financial review is claimed.
The topical refusal catalogue and existing CTA are preserved.

Fictional components test two versus four-year purposes, later-of periods,
regulation commencement boundaries, inclusive monetary/count conditions,
approved-form and evidence gates, nil-assessment relief and payment
separation. They do not establish any actual due date, filing or tax result.
Repository checks cover the four generators, required validation and test
suites, counts, country report, documentation links and both affected
assembled bundles. Existing checker baselines are preserved.

No actual client, identity, bank or signing records were obtained. No
account was accessed and no tax application, objection, court document,
payment or professional sign-off was submitted. Penalty/interest amounts,
complex disputes, actual notice expiry and full compliance/country
acceptance remain open.
