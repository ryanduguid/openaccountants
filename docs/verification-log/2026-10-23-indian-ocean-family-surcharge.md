# Christmas Island and Cocos: family and period Medicare surcharge

Both selected income companions advance to v0.7 for 2026-27. This component
adds the ordinary unmarried-with-dependants, whole-year marriage and part-year
marriage mechanics to the preceding single-person reference. It separates
annual tier 2/tier 3 classification from each period's charging screen. In
particular, annual family income below the family base does not erase an
otherwise chargeable child-free unmarried period.

surcharge Act compilation 42 sections 5-8/11-16, ITAA36 compilation 193
sections 251R/251U/251V/251VA and PHI compilation 42 sections 22-30/22-40

for 2025-26 and later assessments. The ATO expressly labelled 2026-27

still unproved. Its family/dependant page updated 21 August 2026 was read.

The reference specifies qualifying-child thresholds, statutory marriage and
dependant classification, relevant-person uninsured unions, separate day
ledgers and limited listed-category cover deeming. It preserves the distinction
between an ordinary Medicare half-period concession, surcharge prescribed
status and deemed cover for a family cover test. Worked boundaries include
constant 2/3-child thresholds, own I $28,011/$28,012, full/partial cover,
part-year marriage, year-end separation, student/shared-care dependants and
below-family-base unmarried periods. The fictional cases stipulate independently
established inputs and entitlement facts; they do not certify upstream
benefit values, offsets or prescribed entitlements.

death/competing or successive relationship computations, final assessment
rounding and complete returns remain held. Both universal/domain contracts
