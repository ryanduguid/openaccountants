## What I checked

I compared the Companies Act's local, exempted and permit-company tax/fee branches, declarations and statutory capital dates with its Fifth Schedule. The interleaved native table extraction was checked against retained positioned text and visual pages 240-243. The actual 2018:6 replacement Act and selected 2023/2024/2026 instruments establish the stated period distinctions; capture is not proof of absent later amendments or orders.

The guide now distinguishes local issued capital from exempted authorised-capital-plus-premium, mutual-fund authorised capital and mutual-company reserves. It states ordinary bands, separate special/permit branches, unit-trust additions, regulatory and operated-account charges, licence and continuance clocks and selected Government Fees Regulations transactions. A fee component is not an all-in formation invoice or proof of payment acceptance.

The initial source audit found one real scope error: the special latest-filing/capital-alteration FX-date exception belongs to section 131(1)(a)(iii)(cc)'s other-case branch. It cannot replace the memorandum-filing-date information in incorporation and first-January-after-late-year-incorporation branches (aa)/(bb). The revised draft qualifies that rule and adds a late-incorporation/later-capital-alteration counterexample. The per-company operated-account cap is explicit.
