# Kiribati business-statute data-protection amendments

12 November 2026. I added a selected amending-law reference to the existing Kiribati business-tax guide. It records Data Protection Act 2025 sections 2 and 34(2)–(3), with the appointed commencement, and retains the missing principal-law context. The guide remains Tier 2 and pending qualified review. No guide or published coverage count changes.

## Selected amendments and limits

Section 34 begins on PDF page 19. Page 20 supplies 2 business-statute amendments:

- Section 34(2) adds a Data Protection Act compliance qualifier to Business Names Act 2021 section 7(2).
- Section 34(3) replaces Companies Act 2021 section 160(b) with wording addressing actual or likely commercial-position prejudice to another person, whether or not that person supplied the information to the company, or a Data Protection Act violation.

The principal sections and the Companies Act chapeau remain unavailable. The reference does not reconstruct the underlying registry-access or company-information regime, assign a right to inspect or withhold, or invent a refusal procedure. Page 20's opening rights-preservation paragraph belongs to section 34(1)(g), which amends the Digital Government Act, and is kept separate.

The 1 September 2026 request example demonstrates that boundary: being after commencement identifies the selected amending text but does not settle entitlement or permit withholding.
