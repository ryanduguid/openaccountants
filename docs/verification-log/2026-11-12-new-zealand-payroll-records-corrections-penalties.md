The source comparisons materially affect the draft:

- Regulations 4-11 distinguish repaid/agreed overpayments, other income calculations, income-tax/ACC errors, independently miscalculated deductions and reporting-only errors. The later-payday shortfall condition is strictly less than 10% of the specified withholding base; equality fails. Negative reported netting requires Commissioner permission. These are reporting rules, not authority to recover wages.
- The operational 4-year amendment page is qualified by the distinct statutory assessment powers and exceptions. A student-loan borrower can face significant adverse effects from not amending under section 108AC. The general section 113A small-error thresholds exclude ancillary taxes other than FBT and are not a PAYE shortcut.
- Selected section 22 business tax records ordinarily run for 7 years after the income-year end, subject to extensions and authorisation conditions. The guide does not authorise deletion or establish every employment/holiday/retirement-record period.
- Section 139AA counts employees affected by incorrect format/manner, whereas IR335 and the web summary abbreviate this to persons employed. Its monthly maximum is not repeated for each late payday. The late-filing and non-electronic EI penalties have distinct provisions but both use 30 days after the relevant month end for payment.
- Section 183F says $100 or less, including equality; IR335's less-than wording omits that boundary. Its late-payment/interest and non-payment limbs test different dates.
- Late-payment penalties and section 141ED non-payment penalties have separate bases. Section 141ED uses unpaid required tax before the penalty date, a conditional 5% reduction, a 150% aggregate cap and penalty-first payment allocation. Neither an instalment request nor a remission application establishes an approved outcome. Current interest rates, full mixed-debt treatment, other shortfall/criminal penalties and the complete remission regime remain outside this pass.
