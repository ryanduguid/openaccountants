# Annual company reporting in Cameroon, Côte d’Ivoire and Comoros

12 November 2026. I added separate company-law references to the existing
Cameroon VAT, Côte d’Ivoire VAT and Comoros consumption-tax guides. Their tax
workflows remain separate from annual company decisions and RCCM deposits.
The additions reuse the captured primary evidence and selected-page review
behind the earlier [OHADA reporting entry](2026-11-11-ohada-annual-reporting-and-benin-capital.md).
They provide partial reporting coverage and remain Tier 2, pending qualified review.

## Source period and applicability

The [OHADA membership register](https://www.ohada.org/les-etats-membres-de-lohada/)
lists all 3 countries. The revised AUSCGIE was adopted on 30 January 2014 and
published on 4 February 2014. Article 920 supplies its commencement rule; the
[dated company-law register](https://www.ohada.org/droit-des-societes-commerciales-et-du-gie/)
confirms 5 May 2014. The register’s introductory prose gives a conflicting
November date and was not relied on. The appendices identify the original
2014 text and explicitly leave later amendments unverified. They do not
establish the law for every subsequent company period.
[Official Gazette, article 920](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974#page=226)

## Provisions checked

- Articles 347 and 348 set the SARL annual ordinary meeting within 6 months
  after financial year-end, with a judicial-extension route for the managers.
  [Gazette, page 86](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974#page=86)
- Article 548 sets the SA annual ordinary meeting within 6 months after
  financial year-end, subject to a court extension.
  [Gazette, page 133](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974#page=133)
- Articles 558 to 560 cover the sole SA shareholder’s annual decisions and
  archived minutes. Article 347 applies those rules to a sole-member SARL
  with its specified exceptions. The references distinguish decisions from
  a multi-member meeting.
  [Gazette, page 135](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974#page=135)
- Article 269 requires the commercial company’s prior-year annual financial
  statements to be deposited with the RCCM in its registered-office state
  within the month following approval. If approval is refused, the refusal
  decision is deposited within the same period. This gives no universal
  calendar filing date.
  [Gazette, pages 65 and 66](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974#page=65)

The reused example applies the original ordinary SARL rule to a 31 December
2025 year-end, giving 30 June 2026, subject to a court extension. It tracks
the registry deposit from the actual approval decision and keeps national
tax-return deadlines separate. Applicability to an actual period still
requires the later-law check identified in each appendix.
