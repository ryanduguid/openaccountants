# Norfolk foreign income and shared Australian offset corrections

I added `nf-foreign-income` for selected foreign-income inclusion,
temporary-resident and foreign-service conditions, foreign tax eligibility,
the ordinary credit limit, translation, amendments and records. The scope
guide links to it; the new guide declares the cross-border dependency so
the assembled Norfolk package includes that coordination layer. The guide
remains Tier 2 pending professional review.

The source chain includes the current 1936 Act, the latest published 1997
Act with its October amendment targets checked, current translation
regulations and the ATO's directly inspected 2026 foreign-offset pages.
The search initially returned older pages; direct inspection established
the 2026 version. Older 2025 material is context rather than the authority
for this draft. The 2026 ATO worked tax examples concern 2025–26, so their
personal tax figures are not copied into the 2026–27 components.

The source review separates foreign tax paid from tax that counts, the
statutory limit from usable credit, refund entitlement from cash receipt,
income-year matching from the payment year, and income-date rates from
foreign-tax payment-date rates. It preserves the ordinary A$1,000 floor,
the optional short calculation, special limit increases and non-refundable
use. Debt deductions attributable to an overseas PE and other related
non-debt deductions have distinct counterfactual rules.

The related Australian guide had difference-only wording in its summary,
Rule 3 and worksheet, despite section 770-75's A$1,000 floor. Version 1.3
corrects those routes and the short-claim condition, and updates selected
translation and retention wording using the same current sources. The
earlier worked tax figures, form labels and other financial content retain
their prior provenance; this is not a new full Australian financial review.
The prior missing-fact safeguards and refusal catalogue remain in force.

Fictional component checks cover the floor, eligible tax below/above the
floor, non-refundable capacity, income matching, refund entitlement,
separate exchange-rate dates and service-day boundaries. Repository
validation includes the four generators, strict metadata/derived-tree
checks, gate checkers, country report, documentation links, root/MCP suites
and the assembled Norfolk bundle. Baselines are preserved and advisory
leads require contextual assessment.

Large statutory captures were read in selected provisions. Section 23AG's
image formulas were not treated as verified or computed. Actual residence,
foreign law, treaty entitlement, corporate branch/distribution, CFC,
minimum-tax, pension, full forex, exempt-income progression, filing and
financial-output requirements retain their own gates. No private client,
identity or bank records were obtained, and no tax submission or payment
was made. Technical checks do not confer practitioner sign-off or
comprehensive-country acceptance.
