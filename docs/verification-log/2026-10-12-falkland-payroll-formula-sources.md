## The alternative formula needs an authoritative resolution

The [Payments On Account Of Tax (Employees' Deductions) Regulations 1997 PDF, version dated 1 January 2022](https://legislation.gov.fk/download/pdf/e2e89312-74e1-4d82-8239-426a6f92b3a1/a821bb28-ccc1-4cc7-9fc1-7561f30e2046/fisl-1997-23_2022-01-01.pdf)
was captured through the current portal. Regulation 5(2)(c), on physical page 5,
prints the same lower-rate symbol on both sides of the final subtraction.
The adjacent key defines the lower rate and a separate higher rate, expressed
as decimals. The page image confirms the repeated symbol in the native text;
it cannot be dismissed as an extraction error or silently replaced.

The same page annotates the formula and key with S.R. & O. 28/03 and an effect
date of 1 January 2004. The captured [legislative-history XML](https://legislation.gov.fk/download/lh/e2e89312-74e1-4d82-8239-426a6f92b3a1/fisl-1997-23.xml)
lists the Payment on Account of Tax (Amendment) Regulations 2003, document
identifier fisl-2003-28, with commencement on 1 January 2004. The made and
1 January 2004 portal routes returned 'Title Not Found' with no PDF links.
The substantive amending text and any applicable official correction remain
missing. History metadata identifies a source to obtain; it does not resolve
the printed formula.

Regulation 4 requires the Commissioner to prepare deduction tables and make
them available free of charge to employers required to make deductions.
Regulation 5(2B) requires employer agreement for a POAT direction; paragraph
(2C) says deductions for employees covered by that direction follow it.
Paragraph (4) makes regulation 5 subject to regulations 10 and 11, which
address remuneration paid free of tax and deductions by multiple employers.
Those dependencies prevent using the page 5 expression as a universal method.

Indexed official links identified 2025 and 2026 employer guides, but both
original downloads failed because the host could not be resolved. The employer
page and one fallback scrape also failed DNS resolution. No current employer
tables, calculator instructions or operative directions were acquired.

## The zero-rate provision has a defined assessment scope

The [Medical Services Tax Ordinance 2010 PDF, version dated 10 October 2024](https://legislation.gov.fk/download/pdf/6646a2d4-9b05-4309-8ffa-28de77386442/870cfd7e-e6d0-4510-a20e-993281ff5cac/fiord-2010-13_2024-10-10.pdf)
section 5(1), on physical page 7, prints 0% separately for employees,
employers and self-employed persons. Its annotation names section 11 of
Ordinance 10/2017 and an effect date of 1 January 2018.

Section 5(2) names assessment requirements in sections 37, 38 and 39(2) that
do not apply where the section 5(1) rate is zero. That wording alone does not
establish a general exemption from every information, return or record duty.
The underlying amendment, later changes and period-specific administrative
requirements remain unverified.

The employee definition in section 3 says 'aged 17 or more'. Regulation 8A(1)
of the Employees' Deductions Regulations says 'over the age of 17'. These
wording boundaries remain unreconciled; no age-based deduction rule was chosen.
