# Christmas Island and Cocos tax-system boundaries

I added selected scope references for Christmas Island and the Cocos (Keeling)
Islands, both previously absent from the catalogue's guide inventory. They
remain Tier 2 drafts pending review. Guide presence does not complete either
territory's tax pack.

I read the territorial application provisions in the Christmas Island Act
1958 and the Cocos (Keeling) Islands Act 1955. Their Commonwealth-law extension
and applied Western Australian law provisions require attention to express
exclusions, ordinances and modifications. A Western Australian service
provider or address does not establish an unmodified state revenue regime.

The selected income-tax sources establish geographical inclusion separately
from residence and income source. I checked the ordinary/statutory income
branches, the financial-year and substituted-period distinction, selected
currency provisions and current Medicare principal/exemption provisions.
The Register's latest Income Tax Assessment Act 1997 compilation 267 still
shows a closed interval ending 30 September 2026. I retained that limit and
checked the selected October-commencing amendment targets in Acts 71 and 86
of 2026. This does not establish every operative tax regime or complete an
engagement calculation.

For GST I read the statutory indirect-tax-zone definition, the charging and
connection provisions and the Australian-consumer exception. ATO LCR 2016/1
paragraphs 25-27 corroborate the territory exclusion; its HTML was not treated
as the authorised consolidated PDF. Island goods delivery, mainland delivery,
exports and digital or mixed supplies retain their distinct tests.

Federal Customs Act section 6 does not settle the complete border position.
Both local Customs Ordinances 1993 reapply listed Commonwealth customs laws
with modifications, except provisions dealing with customs duties. Their
first Schedule 1 modification separately excludes inter-island movements
from that applied Act. I preserved the controls and the limits of that
exception. Goods sent into Australia have a conditional territory-produce
exemption; storage or transshipment alone does not prove entitlement. The
Excise Act's express geography exclusion does not establish every other
tax, charge, remission or landed cost.

I read section 102-25 of the current Income Tax (Transitional Provisions)
Act 1997. The Cocos transition requires all historical conditions, supplies
a 30 June 1991 deemed acquisition and first cost-base element, and permits
a conditional choice to disapply it. It differs from the Norfolk date
substitution and does not arise solely from current Christmas Island residence.

The guides include 10 and 12 fictional classification cases respectively,
no authorised factual defaults, topical refusals, reviewer flags without a
cash floor, the 4 universal output components and supplemental checks. I
recomputed the AUD24,000 income-inclusion component, checked the cutoff
comparison and verified the stipulated first cost-base element. These are
component checks, not model execution, completed filings or benchmark results.

The source bundle retains 32 captures with hashes and read limits. A missing
GST volume URL and an unrelated title identifier were excluded from reliance;
the correct operative texts were captured separately. The full Acts were
not reviewed clause by clause. Complete individual/entity computations,
payroll/social rules, formation/reporting, cross-border rules, property and
local charges, compliance and special regimes remain open.

At original draft preparation, independent financial review had not been
submitted because selector qualification was unavailable. The clarification
below records the completed review and its reliance limits. Publication
still requires any resulting repairs and final checks. Technical checks
cannot confer professional sign-off.

## GST delivery clarification

Independent financial-rule review found an ambiguity in case 4: customer
residence does not establish where goods are delivered. Version 0.1.1
states a mainland delivery location within the indirect tax zone and adds
the same mainland-resident customer collecting goods on the island. The
paired cases preserve the remaining charging conditions and do not compute
a GST liability. GST Act section 9-25(1) supplies the distinction.

The review found no demonstrated material misstatement in the selected
substantive rules. End-to-end model refusal and output behaviour remains
unverified, as do operative-law completeness and professional sign-off.
Those gaps prevent operational reliance or comprehensive acceptance; they
do not establish an exemption or a zero liability. Final repair review and
publication checks are tracked on the pull request.
