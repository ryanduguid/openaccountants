# Norfolk Island: selected UK treaty conditions and MLI matching

I checked the 2003 Australia-UK convention's geographic definition against
the authentic UK instrument. It expressly includes Norfolk Island. That
does not settle covered taxes, domestic or treaty residence, beneficial
ownership, income classification or relief entitlement. The individual
tie-breaker has no habitual-abode step; the modified non-individual rule
requires competent-authority agreement, with the specific dual-listed
arrangement exception retained.

I compared the selected provisions with the authentic MLI, Australia's
final deposit position, the UK's final deposit position and both
authorities' synthesised texts. Australia lists the UK as agreement 40;
the UK lists Australia as agreement 6. The UK reserves against MLI
Article 8, so its 365-day dividend condition cannot be appended to the
ordinary 5% branch. Australia's unilateral choice of Article 13 Option A
does not apply that option to the pair. The matched anti-fragmentation
provision still matters. Both countries reserve against MLI Article 12.
The new guide preserves the separate dividend zero-branch conditions,
financial-institution/back-to-back interest tests, royalty classification,
resident saving clause, purpose test and MAP effect and notification rules.

The OECD list's flattened table initially made the UK's 31 May 2024 date
look like a later notification. I withdrew that interpretation before
publication: native text coordinates, the table headers and linked document
identities show that it is an arbitration profile. Australia's 2 October
2020 notification concerns an objection to Denmark's arbitration
reservation. Neither date establishes a new UK-pair modification. The
OECD UK's scanned deposit PDF also gave unreliable table associations
under OCR; I used HMRC's native-text final-deposit publication for the
selected matching rows. I do not claim the 2 differently paginated PDFs
were compared in full.

I checked the selected Australian residence, treaty-priority and royalty
override provisions, the current C227 collection cap and C17 withholding
regulations, and the Division 770 eligibility and refund-entitlement rules.
The C267 Assessment Act 1997 text has a closed compilation interval; I
checked the relevant later October amendment targets rather than describing
that compilation alone as current law. The current 2026 ATO offset pages
and HMRC's 2026 residence/FIG guidance were also read in selected ranges.
The existing Norfolk foreign-income guide supplies the ordinary Australian
credit calculation; the new companion does not duplicate it or infer that
the former UK remittance basis continues unchanged after 6 April 2025.

Thirteen independent arithmetic assertions recompute the fictional
dividend, interest, royalty, foreign-tax-excess and transparent-income
components. Twelve manual boundary cases distinguish stipulated ceilings
from domestic liability, actual collection, credit/refund rights and
unresolved eligibility. These are reference and instruction checks,
not a model execution, actual workbook, completed engagement or named
practitioner's sign-off.

The companion supplies the universal mandatory slots and preserves all
universal, cross-border and applicable domain outputs, source conditions,
decisions, refusals and checks. Actual residence and ownership evidence,
PE attribution, UK domestic computations, Article 23/FIG interaction,
forms and procedures, full MAP/arbitration and other treaty partners remain
held pending their own evidence. Required independent financial review
remains outstanding; the draft must not be merged on repository checks
alone. No comprehensive-country acceptance is claimed.
