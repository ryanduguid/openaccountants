# Turkmenistan youth-enterprise relief and corporate reporting

In the November 2026 [Tax Code arts 171(6) and 172](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-code/10?lang=tm), the private SME regime applies 2% to a statutory gross-income base with specific exclusions and limited deductions. Article 172(1) and (4) reduce the established tax for youth enterprises by 50% in the first tax year and 25% in the second and third. The [Youth Policy Law art 1(7)](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-law/19?lang=tm) supplies the qualifying entity definition. Its state-language text includes non-state ownership. Parliament's Russian translation omits that condition. Article 1(9) defines youth entrepreneurship separately.

The [SME Support Law](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-law/260?lang=ru) as consolidated through No. 32-VII of 3 June 2023 preserves the checked article 3 and 5 eligibility rules. The [2023 amending law, part I(4)](https://turkmenistan.gov.tm/index.php/ru/post/73034/zakon-turkmenistana-o-vnesenii-izmenenij-i-dopolneniya-v-nekotorye-zakonodatelnye-akty-turkmenistana-5) changes administrative references and repeals part 3 of article 21³. The [Free Economic Zones Law art 8](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-law/116?lang=ru) remains the authority for participant registration.

[Tax Code arts 174–176](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-code/10?lang=tm) distinguish ordinary and simplified interim profit-tax settlements, add a separate private SME advance and settlement rule and link return dates to financial-statement deadlines. [Accounting and Financial Reporting Law arts 16–20](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-law/254?lang=ru), in Parliament's consolidation through 11 April 2026, set reporting periods, recipients and the 1 July public disclosure deadline for specified entities. Article 19(2) delegates financial-statement filing deadlines to the Ministry or Central Bank. The [April 2026 amending law](https://www.turkmenistan.gov.tm/ru/post/105366/zakon-turkmenistana-o-vnesenii-dopolnenij-i-izmenenij-v-zakon-turkmenistana-o-buhgalterskom-uchyote-i-finansovoj-otchyotnosti) addresses chief accountant provisions and leaves those reporting articles intact.

The checked period is the November 2026 statutory snapshot, with youth reductions tied to the specified operating years. The first effective date of the tax reduction, entity-specific filing instruments, financial reporting forms, youth-enterprise procedures and qualified local review remain open.
