# Laos enterprise social security contributions

Reviewed 11 November 2026 against the Lao text and LSSO English translation of the revised Social Security Law No. 54/NA. The [LSSO legislation register](https://www.lsso.gov.la/index.php/lo/download-la/legislation-la/90-law-la/new-low) lists the Law with a 1 May 2019 date. Article 123 provides for entry into force after promulgation and 15 days after Official Gazette publication. The register date alone does not establish that publication date. [Social Security Law, Lao](https://www.lsso.gov.la/Legislation/nssf%20law%2054.pdf) · [LSSO English version](https://lsso.gov.la/Legislation/nssf%20law%2054%20en.pdf).

Articles 74–75 give the 6% employer and 5.5% employee rates, covered earnings and monthly remittance and list. The base includes output pay, overtime and other earnings declared for income tax. Articles 77 and 106 distinguish the government minimum wage from the ceiling set through the social security system. The old LAK 4,500,000 figure comes from Decision No. 1740 and Notice No. 0824, effective 1 January 2017 under the previous law. The guide now dates that amount. The instruments inspected do not establish a numeric ceiling for November 2026. [Decision No. 1740](https://www.lsso.gov.la/Legislation/agreement1740.pdf) · [Notice No. 0824](https://www.lsso.gov.la/images/stories/Notices/notices_phadan_4500000.pdf).

Instruction No. 0060 has applied since 1 May 2023 under Notice No. 1356. Item 13 distinguishes employer and voluntary payments. Its stated following-month 15th applies to the voluntary case. Article 75(2.1) gives the enterprise monthly frequency and does not set a day. Articles 90–92 give registration scope and timing, including the 30 days after signing an employment contract. [Instruction No. 0060](https://www.lsso.gov.la/Legislation/knn_new0060.pdf) · [Notice No. 1356](https://www.lsso.gov.la/images/stories/Notices/notices_khnn_0060.pdf) · [Notice No. 3185](https://www.lsso.gov.la/images/stories/Notices/notices_regis.pdf).

Outstanding evidence includes the contribution month's ceiling and applicable minimum wage, the enterprise payment day, and any entity-specific exclusions or procedures. The income-tax provisions use a separate law. Its commencement and Gazette publication dates remain unresolved. The salary calculation rows and pending review status are retained.
