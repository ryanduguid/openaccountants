# OHADA annual decisions and registry deposits

11 November 2026. This batch adds dated company reporting references to the
14 existing formation guides for BF, BJ, CD, CF, CG, GA, GN, GQ, GW, ML, NE, SN,
TD and TG. It also resolves Benin's recorded national SARL total-capital gap.
The guides remain Tier 2 and pending review.

## Statutory reporting rules

The revised AUSCGIE was adopted on 30 January 2014, published on 4 February 2014
and took effect on 5 May 2014. Article 920 provides the publication-based
commencement rule. The OHADA register's dated header confirms 5 May. Its
introductory prose gives a conflicting November date, which was not relied on.
[OHADA register](https://www.ohada.org/droit-des-societes-commerciales-et-du-gie/),
[2014 Gazette](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974#page=226)

- Articles 347 and 348, PDF page 86, cover the SARL ordinary annual meeting and
  its 6-month deadline from year-end. Managers may seek a judicial extension.
  Article 548, page 133, gives the SA ordinary annual meeting deadline and
  judicial-extension provision. [AUSCGIE](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974#page=86)
- Articles 558 to 560, page 135, govern sole-shareholder decisions. The annual
  ordinary decisions fall within 6 months from year-end and are recorded in
  minutes kept in the company's archives. Article 347 applies those provisions
  to a sole-member SARL with the exceptions specified there. The guides keep
  these decisions distinct from a multi-member meeting.
  [AUSCGIE](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974#page=135)
- Article 269, pages 65 and 66, requires a commercial company's prior-year annual
  financial statements to be deposited with the RCCM in the registered-office
  state within the month following approval by the competent body. On refusal,
  a copy of the refusal decision is deposited within the same period. The guides
  use the statutory timing and do not infer a universal calendar filing date.
  [AUSCGIE](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974#page=65)

The worked example applies the ordinary SARL deadline to a 31 December 2025
year-end, giving 30 June 2026, subject to a court extension. A registry deposit
is tracked from the actual approval decision. National tax filings retain their
own deadlines. Current filing methods, fees, accounting, audit and sector rules
remain open. Mali's earlier approximate meeting row is replaced by the sourced
section. The statutory rules above cover the guides' 2025 period.

## Benin national capital

The signed Décret n°2014-220 of 26 March 2014, pages 1, 3 and 4, governs SARL
formation in Benin. Article 6 lets the members set total share capital freely
in the statutes, requires equal nominal share values and refers subscription
conditions to the revised AUSCGIE. Article 9 preserves other AUSCGIE provisions
not expressly addressed. Article 11 expressly starts the decree on 5 May 2014.
The former research-gap banner and conditional total-capital claim are replaced
by this national source. The unsupported approximate national tax-filing row is
replaced by an explicit evidence gap.
[Official decree](https://sgg.gouv.bj/doc/decret-2014-220/download)
