# Finland: 2025 income, contribution and business corrections

The income guide mixed 2024 and 2026 health figures with its stated 2025 tax
year. Its Helsinki example used the wrong municipal rate, omitted deductions
and supplied an unsupported total. Two bounded primary-source passes now
support the corrections below. The guides remain Tier 2/pending_review;
component verification does not complete Finland's operational country pack.

## Period-specific rates and personal deductions

[Act 701/2024](https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/2024/701) confirms the
ordinary state scale already in the guide. The unsupported decomposition of
its highest rate into a separate solidarity component was removed. Åland's
state-scale adjustment and municipal rules remain separate.

[Schedule 648/2024](https://www.finlex.fi/sv/lagstiftning/2024/648) fixes 2025
municipal and parish rates. The two language schedules each contain 308
municipality rows. Excluding the 16 Åland municipalities leaves 292 mainland
rows, whose municipal range is 4.70% to 10.90%. Helsinki is 5.30%, with 1.00%
Lutheran or 1.80% Orthodox parish rates. Mainland municipality-linked parish
ranges are 1.00% to 2.00% Lutheran and 1.75% to 2.25% Orthodox. A special parish
or disputed membership needs its own rule. The schedule's 7.50% average is
expressly for Act 627/1978 section 15(2), not a substitute taxpayer rate.

[Act 702/2024](https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/saadoskokoelma/2024/702)
first applies the EUR 3,225 ordinary work-credit maximum and EUR 4,115 basic
deduction for tax year 2025. Historical provisions and [Vero's 2025 calculation
guidance](https://www.vero.fi/syventavat-vero-ohjeet/ohje-hakusivu/49038/verotettavan-tulon-laskeminen-henkiloverotuksessa8/)
support the credit's 18% accrual, distinct phase-out slices, age/child uplifts
and allocation. The basic deduction affects both mainland state and municipal
earned-income bases after preceding deductions. It is not a municipal-only
tax credit. Current 2026 maxima cannot establish these historical amounts.

## Health contributions and YEL routes

[Act 694/2024](https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/saadoskokoelma/2024/694)
and [historical guidance VH/1986/00.01.00/2024](https://www.vero.fi/syventavat-vero-ohjeet/ohje-hakusivu/338472/vakuutetun-sairausvakuutusmaksu/)
establish the 2025 rates: ordinary medical care 1.06%, ordinary daily allowance
0.84% at combined eligible income of at least EUR 16,862, and a separate 0.20%
YEL-only supplement that also applies below that threshold. The guide now
preserves the distinct bases, coverage, age-month boundaries, increased-medical
exceptions and credit/deductibility interactions. Profit is not automatically
confirmed YEL income or a contribution base.

The historical guidance's table appears to swap its last two column headings.
Its section 3.2.3 and the annual Act support 0.20% for the supplement and 1.04%
for the combined daily rate on qualifying YEL income. The source conflict is
retained explicitly.

The income and YEL guides both incorrectly prohibited a business deduction for
compulsory YEL premiums. [Income Tax Act section 96](https://www.finlex.fi/sv/lagstiftning/1992/1535),
the historical calculation guidance and [Vero's YEL instructions](https://www.vero.fi/yritykset-ja-yhteisot/verot-ja-maksut/liikkeen-tai-ammatinharjoittaja/vahennykset/yel-maksut/)
permit business or own/spouse personal deduction, once only. Booked business
costs claimed personally require an add-back. The YEL guide's remaining
insurance and benefit content was not certified by this targeted correction.

## Business classification and expense conditions

[Business Income Tax Act 360/1968](https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/1968/360)
and [Vero's 2025 business-travel guidance](https://www.vero.fi/syventavat-vero-ohjeet/ohje-hakusivu/48192/liikkeen-ja-ammatinharjoittajien-seka-maataloudenharjoittajien-matkakustannusten-vahentaminen/)
require the correct EVL/TVL classification before business deductions. A
freelancer label does not establish EVL eligibility. Deductibility, private
allocation, accounting timing and capital treatment are separate checks.

[Vero's 2025 harmonisation guidance](https://www.vero.fi/syventavat-vero-ohjeet/ohje-hakusivu/48275/verohallinnon-yhtenaistamisohjeet-vuodelta-2025-toimitettavaa-verotusta-varten/)
supports EUR 980/490/245 workroom amounts with use tests and a combined standard
maximum. The same guidance recognises limited qualifying gifts up to EUR 850
per recipient; the personal TVL 98a donation route has separate conditions.

EVL 55 uses actual expenses plus any qualifying additional travel deduction.
The [2025 decision](https://www.vero.fi/syventavat-vero-ohjeet/paatokset/47405/verohallinnon-paatos-verovapaista-matkakustannusten-korvauksista-vuonna-2025/)
supplies domestic EUR 53/24 and EUR 0.59/km benchmarks. These are not payments
the sole trader makes to themselves. The guide now preserves temporary-trip,
distance, duration, meal, vehicle and evidence tests and prevents a second
deduction of costs already claimed. Personal commuting is not employee-only;
its [2025 valuation decision](https://www.vero.fi/sv/Detaljerade_skatteanvisningar/beslut/47204/)
has a distinct EUR 0.27/km car value under its own conditions.

Ordinary movables use pooled reducing-balance depreciation with the statutory
small/short-life exceptions. Buildings generally use 4%/7% of unrelieved cost,
not straight-line depreciation. [Amendment 1314/2022](https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/saadoskokoelma/2022/1314)
proves the temporary 50% branch's extension through 2025, but the captured base
Act supplied metadata only after one same-URL retry. Its unchanged conditions
remain unverified and that branch is held.

Income Tax Act sections 30, 30a, 38 and 41 require the 5% entrepreneur deduction,
prescribed asset valuation, 30% qualifying-wage addition, timely capital-share
requests and mandatory gain treatment. The old example's simple split omitted
these conditions. Seven synthetic component positions now replace the former
estimated personal total; none is a complete employee, trader or mixed ladder.

## Inherited YEL example arithmetic

The minimum new-entrepreneur example in fi-yel-social previously displayed
EUR 1,731.03 annually. Its own stated inputs give EUR 9,208.43 × 24.10% × 78% =
EUR 1,731.0006714, or EUR 1,731.00 at illustrative two-decimal precision.
The monthly display remains EUR 144.25. Fixed-integer checks verify that
multiplication and display only. The inherited statutory rates, eligibility,
discount, billing adjustments and legal premium rounding remain unverified.

## Verification limits

The two source passes acquired eight and seven distinct public documents.
Captured text, source periods, exact read spans, hashes and contrary evidence
were retained. The parent independently matched the selected capture hashes
and the first pass's 638 excerpt lines. Neither pass certifies every original,
correction and amendment chain. The current consolidated statutes include
historical and prospective provisions; later figures were excluded from the
2025 corrections.

The complete net-asset valuation chain, negative assets, accounting periods,
spouse allocation, detailed expense categories, temporary depreciation and
special travel/coverage branches remain open. The displayed travel decision
is an old version, though its domestic figures agree with 2025 guidance.
Personal notices, full filing and retention rules, live portal/form behaviour,
complete credit ordering/allocation, annual rounding and professional review
remain unverified. No accountant sign-off or comprehensive-country completion
is assigned. Build, test and financial-review outcomes belong to the candidate
revision's separate receipts.
