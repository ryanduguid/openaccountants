# BES compliance: distinct deadlines and older collection policy

On 17 October 2026 I checked the selected general procedure for the new
[national compliance reference](../../skills/international/caribbean-netherlands/bq-tax-compliance.md).
It remains Tier 2/pending_review, with no comprehensive-area completion credit.
Fifty selected Belastingwet BES articles match across the January, February
and April 2026 versions and the fresh current capture after explicit interface
labels and whitespace are removed. Comparison alone is not a full reading.
The cited filing, assessment, collection, record, objection and first-instance
provisions were read in context.

The full General Time Limits Act and additional-days order establish the
applicable weekend/holiday rules and 2 January and 15 May 2026 designations.
Belastingwet BES article 8.43 expressly excludes that Act for its assessment
collection terms. The fictional calendar components therefore keep payment
and objection dates separate, including the September provisional-assessment
instalments. They do not establish actual service or admissibility.

I examined implementing relief provisions and collection-policy sections 8.40,
8.44 and 8.57. The public relief page describes a director route that cannot
silently replace the objectionable decisions in the current statute and
implementing rules. The regulation and policy also use different starting
points for their twelve-month relief descriptions. Actual conflicting notices,
eligibility, exceptional extensions and payment plans remain held.

The 2018 policy's monthly interest window predates the statutory amendment
effective from 1 January 2020. The Tax Plan's article XXII and commencement
article XXV were read. Current law supplies the collection/refund windows,
6% simple rate, minimum and upward half-dollar rounding. The guide holds
actual day-count, partial-payment and method interaction. Its monetary
examples stipulate established amounts or charges and test only allocation
and amount controls; they do not infer an interest liability.

Current authority objection, payment-relief and collection pages were read
in full. No PDF, OCR, authentic-layout comparison, private taxpayer, actual
form submission, court filing, bank record or live engagement was used.
Higher appeals, special enforcement, all remission/penalty branches, local
levies, operational interest methods and practitioner sign-off remain open.
Technical checks and fictional calculations are recorded against the candidate
separately. This publication does not certify a complete country pack.
