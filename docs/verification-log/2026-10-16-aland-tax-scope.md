# Åland: territorial exceptions and operative periods

On 16 October 2026 I added Åland's first scope guide and corrected the Finland
VAT guide's unconditional statement about services. The new guide is a dated
Tier 2/pending_review reference. Every topic remains partial; neither guide
presence nor this source work establishes comprehensive country coverage.

## Territorial and service rules

I inspected the relevant competence, business-right and property provisions of
the Self-Government Act, the municipal law's adoption provisions, Vero's Åland
overview and the Commission's territorial table. National and regional rules
must be checked together. EU VAT/excise territorial exclusion does not establish
an exemption from Finnish tax or a general zero rate.

The Finland guide said that Åland services followed normal place-of-supply
rules without qualification. Current Act 1266/1996 section 7 and Vero's
tax-border guidance sections 4 and 4.9 identify exceptions for specified
intangible services. Section 4.4 also distinguishes FI VAT-number use and
EU-services reporting, including mainland fixed-establishment exceptions.
The revised passage and R-FI-4 hold affected classifications when those rules
or the necessary facts are unresolved. They do not verify the legacy Finland
guide's other figures, defaults or return-field mappings for 2026.

## Enactment and commencement

The regional 2025:30 original supplies enacted annual company-tax provisions
where Vero's older table still cites a bill. Its general section 29 provision
is deferred to tax year 2030. The 2026:10 amendment starts on 1 March 2026 and
applies from municipal tax year 2026. The separately published 2026:70 change
starts on 1 January 2027 and first applies to 2027 municipal taxation.
No national/regional company burden or taxpayer deduction was computed.

The current VAT consolidation includes identified changes under 598/2026 that
begin in 2027. It also contains a conflict: the Swedish healthcare heading
annotates amendment 316/2024 as beginning in July 2027, while the commencement
entry says July 2024. The currently linked official Swedish and Finnish
publication PDFs both give 1 July 2024. Native extraction required no OCR.
The Finnish consolidation has no corresponding future-date heading annotation.
The guide records the discrepancy and the publication evidence without
determining any particular healthcare provider's exemption. Only the first
correction-index page was inspected; no exhaustive correction-index claim is made.

Four initial amendment fetches used the wrong updated-legislation view and
returned 404. The actual consolidation links identified the original-publication
view, which supplied the Acts. An empty EUR-Lex capture was not used as Protocol 2
verification. Original regional PDFs with merged column extraction were checked
against their rendered pages in the independent source pass.

policy, six hypothetical scope positions and additions to the universal outputs
and Review Checklist Items. These examples classify facts and periods; they
do not calculate liability or establish a successful financial workbook.
The source register records twelve captured primaries, hashes and read limits.

Complete operative chains, topical tax calculations, financial-workbook
execution, live consumer adherence, portal behaviour and professional sign-off
