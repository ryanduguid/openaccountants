# BES double-tax relief: separate instruments and directional methods

On 17 October 2026 I read all operative articles of the current Netherlands/BES
arrangement (BRN) and unilateral relief decree for the new
[double-tax reference](../../skills/international/caribbean-netherlands/bq-double-tax-relief.md).
The registry selects 1 January 2025 and 5 March 2011 respectively. Their ages
do not establish that either is obsolete. The current authority's Netherlands-
income page links to the older 2019 BRN version; the draft uses current text.
The authority's historical account is not treated as an exhaustive treaty list.

The guide separates taxing rights from domestic liability, tax from insurance
premiums, BRN residence from a single incorporation address, and the two
directions of BRN relief. In particular, directors' income appears in different
relief lists by direction. Its ordinary employment exception requires the
rolling 183-day, employer and PE conditions together. The unilateral decree's
short foreign-work proof rule uses a different period and purpose.

The unilateral methods distinguish per-jurisdiction exemption from credit,
statutory denominator income from gross receipts, and separate income/loss/tax
carry decisions. The 5% dividend cap limits eligible foreign tax for relief;
it does not set foreign withholding. Fictional positive-income examples hold
eligibility and established tax/denominator inputs fixed. They test arithmetic
and limits without supplying actual foreign law or a complete return.

Selected introductory and relief-delegation Belastingwet BES articles and
both authority pages were read. No PDF/OCR, private taxpayer data, bank record,
relief submission, practitioner sign-off or live engagement was used. Other
bilateral/Kingdom instruments, delegated exemption/refund conditions, actual
forms, complex residence, complete transfer-pricing/reporting duties, rounding
and operational cases remain open. The guide is Tier 2/pending_review; neither
technical checks nor publication establishes comprehensive country coverage.
