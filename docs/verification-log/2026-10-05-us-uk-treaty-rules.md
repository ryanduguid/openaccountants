# US treaties: UK rules

The UK section of `us-major-partners` presented interest, royalties and
technical services as blanket zero-rate categories. Its parent-dividend row
omitted additional conditions, its branch-tax note gave an unconditional 5%
rate, and its construction PE row used 12 months. This check corrects the UK
section, PE row and summary row for the guide's 2025 tax year. Version advances
to 1.2; the guide remains a tier 2 draft pending professional review.

The primary sources read on 5 October 2026 were the Treasury's
[2001 Convention](https://home.treasury.gov/system/files/131/Treaty-UK-7-24-2001.pdf),
[Protocol signed 19 July 2002](https://home.treasury.gov/system/files/131/Treaty-UK-Protocol-7-19-2002.pdf),
[2001 exchange of notes](https://home.treasury.gov/system/files/131/Treaty-UK-Notes-7-24-2001.pdf)
and [technical explanation](https://home.treasury.gov/system/files/131/Treaty-UK-Protocol-TE-7-22-2002.pdf).
[IRS Announcements 2021-13 and 2021-14](https://www.irs.gov/irb/2021-33_irb#ANN-2021-13)
supply the USMCA and UK equivalent-beneficiary interpretations.
[HMRC DT19850](https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/double-taxation-relief/dt19850)
records entry into force on 31 March 2003, withholding application from
1 May 2003 and other US tax application from 1 January 2004.

| Earlier statement | Correction and primary pinpoint |
| --- | --- |
| The 5% company dividend row describes direct investment | Art 10(2)(a) permits direct or indirect ownership of at least 10% of voting power. |
| Parent dividends are exempt at 80% held for 12+ months | Art 10(3)(a) uses the twelve-month period ending on declaration and requires one of its additional conditions (i), (ii) or (iii). The technical explanation describes the period's holding as direct. |
| All interest is exempt | Art 11(5) permits specified contingent interest taxation up to 15% of gross, with paragraph 5(b)'s improvement/deterioration exception. Art 11(6) permits domestic taxation of specified securitisation excess returns without an art 11 cap. |
| Royalties and technical services are both zero-rate categories | Art 12(2) requires royalty classification; arts 3(1)(c), (d), 7(1), (6) govern services classified as enterprise business profits while preserving separately governed categories. |
| Branch profits tax is reduced to 5% | Arts 10(7), (8) provide exemptions and a conditional 5% ceiling on the specified dividend-equivalent amount. |
| Construction PE starts at 12 months | Art 5(3) requires more than twelve months. Fixed-place and dependent-agent grounds can apply independently. |
| The UK PE row has only standard construction rules | Art 21 separately addresses offshore exploration and exploitation. Only exploration has the no-more-than-30-day aggregate exception, with associated-enterprise aggregation excluding concurrent days. |
| The UK summary presents reciprocal blanket rates | The revised US-source row points to the section's category, entitlement and exception conditions. Reciprocal UK withholding was not reviewed. |

The check applies Protocol I's replacement of the saving-clause exceptions,
Protocol II's pooled-vehicle paragraph and Protocol IV's equivalent-beneficiary
definition. Parent and pension relief, pooled vehicles, PE attribution and
conduit payments are qualified in the section. The notes addressing arts 9,
11(4) and 12(4) prevent an additional domestic withholding rate on excess
interest or royalties when the described deduction adjustment is made. The
conditional remittance rule, transparent-entity derivation and non-individual
dual-resident restriction are stated without asserting current domestic
remittance law or claimant eligibility. Protocol V's replacement of the old
convention transition was read; that transition is not a 2025 rate-setting rule.

The four Treasury PDFs extracted natively with no OCR required. The
[UN cumulative index](https://treaties.un.org/doc/Publication/Cumulative%20Index/UNTS%20Volume%20No%202201-2250/cumindex.chrono.en.pdf)
identifies the Convention in volume 2224 and Protocol in volume 2226.
The scanned [volume 2224 contents](https://treaties.un.org/doc/Publication/UNTS/Volume%202224/v2224.pdf)
needed local offline OCR to confirm the Convention's starting page 247.
The official indexed [volume 2226 record](https://treaties.un.org/doc/Publication/UNTS/Volume%202226/v2226.pdf)
identifies the Protocol's starting page 465. The UN copies were used for
publication details; the Treasury instruments supply the financial text.

Manual comparisons cover exactly 10% ownership, the parent's exact 80% and
twelve-month boundaries and additional conditions, pooled-vehicle and pension
restrictions, specified contingent interest and paragraph 5(b), securitisation
excess, and related-party excess after a deduction adjustment. They distinguish
construction lasting exactly twelve months from more than twelve months,
offshore exploration at 30 and 31 aggregate days from exploitation, and entry
into force from the withholding application date. These are source comparisons,
not taxpayer calculations.
