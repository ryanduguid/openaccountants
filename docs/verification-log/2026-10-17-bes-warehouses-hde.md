# BES warehouses and HDE: permissions, tax bridges and export pointers

On 17 October 2026 I read DAB 2.98–2.106 and 5.1–5.10; decree
2.40–2.46; regulation 5.1 and 3.15; Belastingwet 4.8, 5.2, 6.10,
6.11 and 6.17; tax regulation 6.6–6.7; four complete storage/transfer
authority pages; and the February 2026 correction in HTML for the
[warehouse/HDE reference](../../skills/international/caribbean-netherlands/bq-warehouses-hde.md).
The system reference now routes to it. Existing substantive tax figures
do not change; the ABB and personal-import companions gain a source hold.

Operator and premises permits, approved goods, handling, stock accounts,
same-island movements, discharge and actual responsibility remain distinct.
An open warehouse still requires declarations and permissions. The
three-day goods-destination rule on warehouse withdrawal differs from
HDE's six-month entity-removal rule. Shortages do not become automatic
tax-free losses or change responsibility merely because title was sold.

HDE site establishment, entity admission and other/local delivery permits
are separate. Qualifying activities, excluded professional/financial
services, non-local customers, services/goods turnover floors, resident
full-time employment and investment conditions are preserved. The 80,000
services floor is not the corporate small-body turnover ceiling. Corporate
residence, property-value improvement rules and ABB have separate bridges;
no blanket tax exemption is inferred from admission.

The saved current tax regulation 6.6(1) and customs regulation 3.15(1)(a)
point to Belastingwet 6.10(2)(c) for goods/export treatment, while the
current Act places the specified producer/export/HDE goods branch in (d)
and the specified no-CO2 car/van branch in (c). I retained the mismatch
and held actual zero-rate/re-import application in the new guide and both
companions. The reviewed February correction addresses different named
matters; it does not resolve these pointers. This is not a claim that all
other amendments or interpretive authorities have been exhausted.

Twenty-four manual criteria were frozen before drafting and checked by
the implementing agent. The stock components recompute to 80 expected
units and a difference of 1. This is not an independent model benchmark
or practitioner sign-off. Actual sites/permits, transitional or mixed
admissions, disputed mappings, AGP interactions, full tax bills, external
forms and operational cases remain held. The guides stay Tier 2/pending_review.
No comprehensive-area credit, private records, identity/tax identifier or
bank submission, application, declaration, payment, PDF/OCR or engagement
workbook is claimed.
