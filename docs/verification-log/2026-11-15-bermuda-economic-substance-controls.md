# Bermuda economic substance classification and declaration controls

15 November 2026

I checked selected economic substance (ES) duties against retained official primary texts and expanded the existing company-formation guide. This adds 17 control groups and 13 hypothetical manual groups. It does not decide an actual entity's compliance, complete the ES regime or supply an accepted filing.

## Selected controls

| Control group | Check and retained boundary |
|---|---|
| Entity | Apply the statutory company/LLC/partnership definitions, permit/overseas inclusion and local-partnership exclusion |
| Non-resident | Test foreign tax residence and Annex 1 condition; separate the s 5A evidence duty |
| Activities | Classify each business activity and each relevant period; dated guidance no-revenue and cessation conditions do not erase reporting |
| Activity exceptions | Apply financing overlap, land, shipping, foreign-affiliate and statutory fund-management boundaries |
| Period | Use the entity's financial year, not an assumed calendar year or foreign tax year |
| Substance | Establish Bermuda management, CIGA, physical presence, suitable employees and expenditure; no universal numeric minimum inferred |
| Outsourcing | Retain Bermuda CIGA, entity oversight, provider capacity/qualification and no multiple counting |
| Pure equity | Apply the primary-function, no-commercial-activity and control tests, governance/records, annual declaration, adequate people and Bermuda premises |
| Local | Apply actual statutory qualification, Bermuda-only business and no-MNE conditions; retain annual company reporting and local-partnership distinction |
| IP | Distinguish ordinary and high-risk branches and their contrary-evidence conditions, including the even-CIGA presumption |
| Annual declaration | Preserve minimum period fields and the Agency's determined form/prescribed-date wording |
| Clock | Attribute six months to dated guidance and CITA publications; the recurring statutory prescribing instrument is not located |
| Residence evidence | Require objective whole-period evidence, transparent-entity conditions and English/certified translation; an opinion alone is insufficient |
| Provisional residence | Treat 12 months as a cap, subject to a shorter direction and ordinary timing for provisional-claim evidence |
| Actor | Keep 31 March 2026 transfer and prior-enforcement savings separate from corporate income tax liability |
| Agency requests | Apply the reasoned notice, reasonable period/place, privilege and evidentiary safeguards |
| Exchange | Retain the s 6 triggers, competent-authority forwarding exception and treaty/statutory safeguards |

Independent source preparation identified 2 missing guidance qualifications before drafting. The repaired map retains the no-gross-revenue condition for that activity/period whilst keeping it in scope, and cessation only from the actual cessation point whilst retaining other activities, declarations and pre-cessation review. Liquidation whilst activity continues is not cessation. The guide and 2 of the manual groups carry those repairs.

Native web access subsequently returned the full [CITA transfer notice](https://www.cita.bm/news/cita-new-changes-economic-substance-automatic-exchange-of-information), [ES page](https://www.cita.bm/economic-substance) and [filing-deadline page](https://www.cita.bm/exchange-of-information-filing-payment-deadlines). The former international-tax-compliance path remains unavailable. The successful pages are separate retained evidence; the unsuccessful captures are not relabelled as successes.

The transfer notice says existing ES filing methods/location continue until further instruction and directs declarations to the Registrar's site, with essupport@cita.bm for correspondence. The ES page and its linked [deadline PDF](https://www.cita.bm//assets/images/files/Exchange%20of%20Information%20Filing%20Deadlines.pdf), last published 5 June 2026, give six months after the last day of the relevant financial period. The PDF has one natively extracted page; no OCR was needed. Current public directions and republication are therefore supported. Current authenticated forms, subsequent instructions, actual portal operation and acceptance remain unverified.

The consolidated Regulations and 6 listed amending instruments did not locate an express recurring six-month statutory prescription. Section 12(2) requires the Agency, when determining compliance with ss 3 or 5, to consider whether the entity followed relevant Minister-issued guidance that was, at the time, published on the Agency's official website or, before 1 March 2026, published by the Registrar in an approved manner. Section 12(3) states that guidance is not a statutory instrument. The guide attributes the clock to guidance and agency publications and does not claim that reg 2 prescribes it or that no other instrument exists.

## Verification and limits

The 13 manual groups cover office/label assumptions, holding versus financing, pure-equity people/premises, unsupported residence, incomplete-period evidence, a 31 March year-end clock, shorter provisional directions, multiple counting, high-risk IP, split commencement, an MNE local-company claim, no revenue and cessation/liquidation. These are source and date-arithmetic checks, not machine-executed legal tests or actual-company results. The existing 37 formation/fee/reporting groups and 38 fee rows remain part of the guide.

Repository generation and the documented gates check package synchronisation, metadata, links, examples, baselines and software behaviour; they cannot establish legal completeness, adequacy, actual residence, an agency decision or practitioner assurance.
