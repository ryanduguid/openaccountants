# British Virgin Islands income tax, payroll and company reporting

11 November 2026. I reconciled the five British Virgin Islands guides covering
the tax overview, income tax, payroll and contributions, and company formation
and reporting. The guide metadata retains pending local accountant review.

- The Commission's 2024 audited accounts, note 20, date the ordinary individual
  and company zero rate to 1 January 2005. The 2025 SIGTAS Validation Act
  validates specified administrative acts and limits proceedings in respect of
  those acts. It lists the Income Tax Ordinance and Payroll Taxes Act separately.
  The 2026 budget estimates contain prior receipts under the personal and
  corporate income tax headings. They do not assign those amounts to assessment
  periods. The charging and zero-rate text, current returns and the powers
  applicable to earlier assessments remain to be checked. [FSC accounts](https://www.bvifsc.vg/sites/default/files/annual_report_2024.pdf) · [Act 17 of 2025, section 2](https://laws.gov.vg/sites/default/files/2025-10/Act%20No%2017%20of%202025-Miscellaneous%20Amendments%20%28SIGTAS%29%20%28Validation%29%20Act%2C%202025.pdf) · [2026 Detailed Budget Estimates, revenue codes 411110 and 411210](https://gov.vg/sites/default/files/2026-04/2026_budget_estimates_final_b.pdf)
- Section 242 exempts a section 3 company, payments by it and gains on its
  securities from the Ordinance. The stamp duty exception covers BVI land and
  securities in a company with a BVI land interest directly or through a
  subsidiary. The listed 2022–24 amendments leave section 242 in place.
  Act 15 of 2024 adds a separate return-making power in section 242A.
  The fiscal risk discussion in the 2026 estimates describes Pillar Two
  implementation conditionally. The later index search did not resolve whether
  a domestic minimum tax had been enacted or commenced. Non-BC payments,
  treaty relief, foreign top-up exposure and local property charges require
  separate checks. [2020 revised Act, sections 3 and 242](https://www.bvifsc.vg/sites/default/files/bvi_business_companies_act.pdf) · [Attorney General amendment list](https://laws.gov.vg/laws/bvi-business-companies-act) · [Act 15 of 2024, section 59](https://www.bvifsc.vg/sites/default/files/bvi_business_companies_amendment_act_2024.pdf)
- The June 2012 Inland Revenue guide dates the 21-day monthly deadline and
  US$10,000 exemption to 1 January 2007. Its annual instructions give 30 April
  for calendar-year filers. The older general guide incorrectly gave the
  pre-2007 monthly deadline and a March annual deadline. The Ministry's
  November 2026 release confirms the public eTax route and employer
  declaration and correction functions. The post-login workflow, class
  thresholds, self-employed and deemed remuneration, multi-job relief,
  assessment and current penalties still require the operative law and
  administration. [Inland Revenue payroll guide, sections 2–9](https://www.bvi.gov.vg/sites/default/files/resources/Guide%20to%20Payroll%20Tax.pdf) · [eTax release](https://www.gov.vg/news/ministry-finance-launches-enhanced-etax-portal-and-online-tax-payments)
- The Board's 2026 bulletin supports the Social Security rates for private
  employers and earnings cap. Its contribution page distinguishes public,
  self-employed and voluntary rates. The Board's October 2026 announcement
  gives the 2027 cap changes for Social Security and NHI. The 2026 bulletin
  quotes a 5% late penalty and the general page quotes 10%. The guide flags
  the discrepancy. NHI dependant, pensioner and other category rules and
  the underlying contributions and penalty instruments remain open.
  [Social Security Board contributions](https://www.vissb.vg/contributions/) · [2026 bulletin](https://www.vissb.vg/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/SSB-Contributions-Insurable-Earnings-2026.pdf) · [October 2026 announcement](https://www.vissb.vg/increase-in-maximum-insurable-earnings-effective-1st-january-2027/)
- S.I. 89 of 2022 changed the ordinary company registration and annual fees
  from 1 January 2023. Section 98A governs business company financial returns.
  The 2024 amendment permits an extension of the initial filing period by
  notice. S.I. 71 of 2026 substitutes the form from 2 November 2026 and the
  Commission directs its use for returns required to be filed from 2027.
  The formation guide records the exceptions and filing route. Limited
  partnerships, details of beneficial ownership and register filings, economic substance,
  late penalties and current transition notices need separate checks.
