# Norfolk tax interest and shared Australian compliance corrections

I added nf-tax-interest and linked it from the Norfolk scope guide. It
covers selected ordinary GIC/SIC liability and periods, current quarterly
rates, compounding, receipt/allocation, payment distinctions, incurred-year
deduction treatment, recoupments and remission. It remains Tier 2 pending
professional review and does not complete the country pack.

I checked the current TAA/1997 Act records and version grids, October
amendment targets, complete selected provisions, the 2025 deduction
Schedule and the remedial determination. The statutory SIC formula was
checked using its author-provided accessibility alternative. Current rate
pages distinguish calendar quarters and actual calendar-year divisors.
Mathematical components retain precision and show cents for presentation;
actual account rounding, allocation and notices remain separately required.
The discretionary GIC five-cent rounding power is not applied as an
automatic daily-cent or SIC rule.

The current GIC practice statement was revised on 25 June 2026. It makes
an important distinction: backdated GIC on a late original assessment can
be incurred when the assessment is served, with later charges incurred
daily. SIC is incurred on service of the amended assessment. Schedule 2
of Act 29 of 2025 commenced on 1 April but applies to assessments for
income years starting on or after 1 July 2025. Substituted accounting
periods therefore cannot be replaced by an unqualified calendar cutoff.
Current recoupment provisions test deducted or could-deduct amounts;
not claiming an available deduction does not alone make remission tax free.

The shortfall practice statement was revised on 30 July 2026. Its advance
payment rules distinguish statutory SIC accrual from subsequent remission
and early-payment interest. A payment plan, penalty relief or reasonable
care does not automatically cancel interest. The statutory SIC remission
objection threshold is more than 20% of the specified additional tax,
separate from the penalty-remission two-unit condition.

The related Australian guide omitted SAPs and could-deduct recoupments,
used a reasonable-care taxpayer-or-agent statement and described indirect
tax review too broadly. Version 0.5 corrects those points and the linked
safe-harbour, disclosure, no-shortfall/default, group and penalty-relief
conditions. It separates notice clocks and actual posting from mathematical
interest. Current section 170 and its self-amendment enactment are reused
from the earlier verified batch; GST sections 93-5 to 93-15 and TAA
155-15/155-35 distinguish credit entitlement and assessment-review clocks.

The Australian example retains its fictional 62-day component and current
rates but uses the unrounded annual-rate/365 expression. Its displayed
A$470.45 is not labelled as a final posted liability; the GIC rounding
power and possible later charges are explicit. A nil return does not
guarantee an imposed penalty, and this FTL penalty's nondeductibility is
not generalised to every statutory penalty. The updated fork footer and
workflow source/fact gates make the reference's status explicit.

The Australian guide's unchanged programme, annual/BAS calendar and
recovery summaries retain their earlier 16 September provenance; this is
not a fresh financial sign-off of the full guide. Fictional numeric and
source checks cover the changed components. Repository verification covers
the four generators, required validation/tests, inventory, country report,
documentation links and both assembled affected bundles. Existing checker
baselines are preserved.
