## Classifications and workflow

The 5 hypothetical manual cases distinguish direct equity with incidental
bank interest, non-equity assets, an entity whose sole asset is a claim
against its broker, passive or active holding management and absence of
gross relevant income. I preserved the guidance examples' ownership and
bank-account premises, the conditional ordinary-test assessment, resource
adequacy and surviving notification/reporting duties. The no-income test
treatment is attributed to DITC guidance; zero profit or cash receipts do
not establish zero gross relevant income.

The update makes the universal workflow dependency explicit. It narrows the
old blanket ES refusal so that supported reference classifications can
proceed, whilst out-of-scope compliance and filing requests retain a coded
refusal. ES intake, evidence holds, review attention, all 4 universal
outputs and self-check/reviewer additions are explicit. All ES conclusions
are flagged for review regardless of cash impact; that is an internal
workflow policy, not a statutory threshold. The remaining country sections
do not establish a complete operational engagement on their own.

The 5 cases are manual controls, not executed examples or an end-to-end
taxpayer trial. Existing numerical examples were preserved. Candidate
generation, repository checks and publication decisions are recorded
separately; this entry does not establish their results.

## Unresolved questions

The current definitions and partnership appendix support qualifying
partnerships' access to the reduced test. Section 4(5)(a) still names
applicable Companies Act filings; the reviewed sources do not establish a
substituted Partnership Act filing limb. The guide holds the final
compliance conclusion on that application and invents no equivalent rule.

The Act's Schedule paragraph (2) prints 20 June 2021 for the cited
commencement, whilst the appendix says 30 June 2021. I did not resolve that
discrepancy or use either date to decide a historical case. The appendix's
prospective portal wording also does not prove current submission fields
or general-partner return non-duplication procedures.
