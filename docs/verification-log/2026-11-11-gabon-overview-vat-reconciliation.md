# Gabon overview and historical VAT reconciliation

11 November 2026. I checked the overview and historical VAT claims against
the DGI's CGI 2025 compilation, whose header identifies amendments through
Loi n°033/2024 of 30 December 2024. The selected VAT provisions enacted for
2026 remain separately labelled and unchanged.
[DGI, CGI 2025](https://dgi.ga/document/code-general-des-impots-2025/)

## Overview and individual income

Article 7-I supplies corporate residence and territoriality, correcting the
overview's article 5 citation. Article 15 supplies the calendar-year rule
and first-period exception for a company starting in the final 6 months.
Articles 16, 20, 24 to 26, 74 and 83 support the dated corporate rates,
filing, qualified minimum tax, individual residence and income-year rules.
The undated Ministry label and stale DGI webpage comparison are removed.

Articles 168, 170 and 174 support rounding, the family-part calculation,
the marginal scale and the example. For one part and CFA 12,000,000 of
taxable income, the top-band formula gives CFA 2,531,000 before credits.
Household allocation, exemptions, special assessments and any business-income
minimum tax remain separate checks. The currency reference is separately
dated to 11 November 2026 and cites the BEAC and Banque de France.

## VAT, CSS and excise

Articles 208 and 208 bis supply the thresholds, 2-year retention and option
conditions. Articles 209 to 210 and 221 support the qualified property and
construction summary and rate lists. Articles 211 ter and 240 support the
qualified imported-services withholding. Article 237 supplies monthly
filing, schedules, nil returns and export customs references.

The annexed 2017 Finance Law articles 13 to 17 and 24 to 32 supply the CSS
charge, rate, contribution rounding, collection and income-tax deductibility.
The guide now records the public-sector and VAT-customer withholding duties
and qualifies non-resident suppliers by the absence of a Gabon permanent
establishment or permanent professional installation. Exemptions and special
mining and petroleum rules remain transaction checks.

Article 250 supports the selected excise items. The historical gambling
summary now includes CFA 100,000 per operated machine in addition to 5%.
The source's table columns were visually checked on PDF page 93; the IRPP
scale was checked on page 62. Native extraction used pdf-inspector, with
PDFium used only for rendering. The 2025 cement example gives CFA 50,000
VAT at 5% on CFA 1,000,000, separately from the enacted 2026 10% example.

## Limits

Independent review found no actionable findings after the table images
were checked. Later amendments, implementing instructions, current practice,
transaction eligibility, the remaining excise schedule and qualified local
review remain open. Both guides stay Tier 2 and pending review. This work
supports selected components and earns no complete-topic verification.
