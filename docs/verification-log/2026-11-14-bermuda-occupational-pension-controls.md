# Bermuda occupational-pension contribution controls

I checked selected membership, earnings, contribution and remittance provisions of the [National Pension Scheme (Occupational Pensions) Act 1998](https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Consolidated%20Law/1998/National%20Pension%20Scheme%20(Occupational%20Pensions)%20Act%201998) and [General Regulations 1999](https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Consolidated%20Law/1999/National%20Pension%20Scheme%20(General)%20Regulations%201999), captured on 14 November 2026. This is a bounded contribution update. The legal-site snapshot is through 31 October 2026; capture on 14 November does not establish the absence of later amendments.

The current Act PDF has 52 pages. The older Pension Commission copy has 51; their selected contribution provisions agree after extraction-format differences are removed. Later annotations elsewhere do not establish a complete contribution history. The current General Regulations PDF is byte-identical to the retained Commission copy. These sources had usable native text. The First Schedule extraction split its final row and headers, so I checked page 48 visually before distinguishing the contribution rate from the annual defined-benefit accrual column.

The inherited pension bullet gave a broad Bermudian/spouse statement and an approximate 5% employer/5% employee default. It did not establish membership, the earnings base, the plan route or the remittance period. The revised section distinguishes the restricted employee definition from the separate self-employed definition, statutory exclusions, attained age 23, 720 prior-membership hours with the relevant employer and the plan's normal retirement age. Existing membership does not cease merely for a later year below 720 hours. Normal retirement is not a universal age-65 cutoff; continued accrual after the plan date requires the stated written election.

For an established ordinary defined-contribution case, the Schedule supplies 5% each on pensionable earnings. The BMD 200,000 annual employee earnings cap and listed inclusions/exclusions are separate from payroll-tax remuneration. The collective-agreement and defined-benefit branches need their own plan/funding checks. Employer funding of the member share and voluntary extras do not create an automatic additional deduction or matching requirement. The current Schedule does not prove uninterrupted historical rates or the absence of period-specific relief.

Section 19(5) requires at least monthly withholding and payment with the employer contribution within 30 days following the withholding month. The administrator and any person authorised under s 18(3) to receive contributions must notify the employer and Commission of unpaid contributions within 15 days. This is a separate duty, not a payment grace period. The 7% interest provision is a ceiling; the operative Gazette rate remains unverified. Sections 4-5 tie a new-plan registration application to 90 days after establishment, rather than a general hiring allowance.

Regulations 20-24 separately address self-employment earnings, a strict annual amount exceeding BMD 20,000, attained age 23 and normal retirement, the conditional twice-Schedule rate, the BMD 10,000 mandatory cap and the 60-day year-end contribution clock. Optional monthly estimates and their excess are qualified. Specified existing-plan participation can remove separate registration without removing the contribution requirement. The employee 720-hour test is not imported into this route.

I checked [BR 139/2019](https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Annual%20Law/Statutory%20Instruments/2019/National%20Pension%20Scheme%20(Occupational%20Pensions)%20%20Amendment%20Act%202019%20(No.%202)%20Commencement%20Notice%202019), its [BR 10/2020 amendment](https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Annual%20Law/Statutory%20Instruments/2020/National%20Pension%20Scheme%20(Occupational%20Pensions)%20Amendment%20Act%202019%20Commencement%20Amendment%20Notice%202020) and the 2019 amending Act for the selected commencement dates. BR 139 expressly revokes BR 130/2019. The retained [2020 amending Act](https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Annual%20Law/Acts/2020/National%20Pension%20Scheme%20(Occupational%20Pensions)%20Amendment%20Act%202020), [BR 55/2020](https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Annual%20Law/Statutory%20Instruments/2020/National%20Pension%20Scheme%20(Occupational%20Pensions)%20Amendment%20Act%202019%20Commencement%20Notice%202020) and [BR 56/2020](https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Annual%20Law/Statutory%20Instruments/2020/National%20Pension%20Scheme%20(Occupational%20Pensions)%20Amendment%20Act%202020%20Commencement%20Notice%202020) resolve one outdated not-in-force entry; that narrow check does not verify the current withdrawal regime. The uncommenced broader employee definition and excluded transitional provisions are not treated as operative. The [conditional 2020 temporary suspension](https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Annual%20Law/Acts/2020/National%20Pension%20Scheme%20(Occupational%20Pensions)%20Temporary%20Amendment%20Act%202020) does not prove relief for another period. The 2019 temporary-relief instruments and uninterrupted history remain open.

The guide adds 8 grouped manual pension controls. The single-employer BMD 100,000 example is expressly limited to pensionable earnings for the applicable membership period, with category, relief and payer prerequisites. The self-employed BMD 150,000 example demonstrates the conditional BMD 10,000 cap. These are manual source and arithmetic checks, not consuming-model, taxpayer, pension, workbook or portal execution.

The general and overview guides route pension questions to these selected controls. Prior payroll and social-insurance controls remain. Defined-benefit funding, actual prescribed late interest, cross-employer cap coordination, mixed roles, historical relief, benefits/withdrawals, later law and plan-specific facts still prevent a full pension result. Tier 2 and pending review remain; no practitioner, whole-topic or country completion is claimed.
