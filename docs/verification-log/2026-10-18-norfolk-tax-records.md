# Norfolk Island tax records and retention

I added nf-tax-records and linked it from the Norfolk scope guide. It
separates business records, individual expense substantiation, CGT evidence
and mainland-connected indirect-tax records. It remains Tier 2 pending
professional review and does not complete the country pack.

I checked section 262A in current C193, selected Division 900 provisions,
CGT sections 121-20 to 121-35 and TAA sections 288-25, 382-5 and 382-20.
The current compilation records/version grids and October amendment targets
were read with the selected provisions. Earlier verified Norfolk territorial
and CGT-transition evidence was reused. Current ATO business, individual and
longer-record guidance supplies the guidance distinctions and practical
record-quality requirements. Public HTML rulings TR 96/7 and TR 2018/2 were
corroboration only; their authorised PDFs were not examined or relied on.

The starts differ. Ordinary business records generally run for five years
from the later preparation/receipt or completion event. Division 900
sections 900-25, 900-75 and 900-90 use the return due day or a later actual
lodgement, so early lodgement cannot shorten the statutory period. CGT
records can remain relevant to future events; indirect-tax records compare
completion and review clocks, and specified delayed credits add a return
giving clock. Elections and compliant certified asset registers have
separate triggers. Other applicable obligations are retained separately.

Section 900-170 extends the ordinary period until a specified dispute is
resolved. The ATO individual page recommends the later of five years from
lodgement and five years from dispute resolution. I labelled the statutory
rule and broader guidance separately and held every actual disposal decision
for reviewer reconciliation. The guide does not convert either source into
permission to delete records.

The Norfolk section 102-25(3)(b) acquisition-date exception retains every
subsection (2) condition and does not remove all CGT evidence. Division 900
copy/replacement relief is conditional and does not establish substantive
deductibility. TAA 288-25's selected 20-unit administrative penalty excludes
Division 900 documents; separate 30-unit criminal provisions are not an
automatic fine or instruction to stack penalties. Specialist records and
company/employment/super regimes retain their own source requirements.

Fictional boundary components check the distinct start choices, overlapping
endpoints, evidence conditions, exception gates and penalty exclusions.
Repository verification covers the four generators, required checks/tests,
inventory, country report, documentation links and the assembled Norfolk
bundle. Existing checker baselines are preserved.

No client records, tax identifiers, identity or bank documents, passwords,
encryption keys or signing credentials were obtained. No actual return,
notice response, reconstruction claim, penalty action or record disposal
was processed. Actual dates/notices, privilege, litigation holds, special
regimes, professional sign-off and comprehensive-country acceptance remain
open.
