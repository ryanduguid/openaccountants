Both pages were read natively and the operative first-page image was checked. Assent is printed as 11 October 2012; the commencement field has no exact day or month.

Section 2 adds section 20(1)(f): more than 6 months without employment, the Board's opinion of financial hardship and discretionary approval of an amount sufficient to alleviate it. The memorandum's at-least-six-month description differs at the boundary. The guide retains the operative condition and illustrates why duration alone does not establish a payout. Section 3 inserts the consent protection for a Board-approved amount, subject to Parts VIA and VIB and section 28. The source's Part VIA heading and Part VI insertion sentence differ; the principal text and operative scope remain needed.
