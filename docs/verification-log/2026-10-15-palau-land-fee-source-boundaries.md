# Palau: land-fee scope and filing sources remain unresolved

The captured land-fee sources do not settle its interaction with business taxes
or identify its filing form and deadline. I compared the retained statutory text
and Bureau of Revenue & Taxation pages on 15 October 2026. These observations do
not qualify an operative property-tax guide.

## The agency page adds a business-tax condition

The Bureau's [dedicated land-fee page](https://www.palaugov.pw/executive-branch/ministries/finance/brt/land-transaction-proceeds-fee/)
describes a 4% fee from 1 January 2023 on land-lease or transfer proceeds when
Business Profits Tax (BPT) or Gross Revenue Tax (GRT) does not apply. It describes
an exemption for qualifying public-land leases, a charge on subsequent subleases
and the addition of Palau Goods and Services Tax (PGST) where applicable.

The retained *Palau National Code*, title 40, s 1409, states the 4% charge, the
public-land lease exemption, subsequent-sublease rule and addition of applicable
PGST. Section 1409 itself does not state the agency's BPT/GRT condition. Its source
note cites [RPPL 11-11](https://www.palaugov.pw/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/RPPL-11-11.pdf)
s 14. I have not completed the related provisions, regulations and amendment
chain needed to reconcile that condition. This is an unresolved source question;
it does not establish that the agency page contradicts the law.

## Gross receipts and disposal gains require separate analysis

Title 40 s 1002(15)(C) excludes gross receipts from land sales from its definition
of gross revenue. BPT provisions require further reading: s 1421 imposes BPT on
the net income of PGST-registered persons; s 1431 imports the general definitions
subject to modifications; and s 1432(a) defines a business capital-asset disposal
gain by reference to consideration and net book value.

Those passages do not establish the treatment of every land transaction. In
particular, the gross-receipts exclusion alone does not establish a general
exemption for land gains. I have not selected a BPT/GRT coordination rule or a
calculation method.

## The main page's filing labels concern other taxes

Under its land-fee heading, the captured [main Bureau page](https://www.palaugov.pw/executive-branch/ministries/finance/brt/)
labels the filing instructions Tax400, international transportation, and the
payment text Tax401, non-resident withholding. The separate [forms list](https://www.palaugov.pw/executive-branch/ministries/finance/brt/tax-forms/)
also assigns Tax-400 and Tax-401 to those taxes. These labels do not qualify a
land-fee return or payment deadline. No dedicated land-fee form was identified in
the captured list; that observation does not prove that no form exists.
