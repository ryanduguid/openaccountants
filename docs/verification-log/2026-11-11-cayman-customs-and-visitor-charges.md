# Cayman Islands customs and visitor charges

I checked the 2026 position on 11 November. The [indirect tax guide](../../skills/international/cayman-islands/ky-vat-gst.md) now covers the customs entry, tourist accommodation, timeshare and departure rules. I removed the approximate tariff bands and the default to 22% from the [Cayman Tax guide](../../skills/international/cayman-islands/cayman-tax.md).

| Component | Source and period checked |
|---|---|
| Customs classification, value and entry | I checked the 2026 tariff schedule and the statutory classification rules. The guide uses 4 exact codes and distinguishes percentage duty from a per-litre duty. The Customs and Border Control Act sets the 7-day entry period, duty payment and valuation. CBC gives the evidence, exchange rate and COLS instructions. [Customs Tariff Act, ss 2–3 and Schedules 1 and 6](https://gov.ky/w/customs-tariff-act-2026-revision-lg5-s3) · [Customs and Border Control Act, ss 17 and 43–45](https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/2018/2018-0034/2018-0034_2024%20Revision.pdf) · [CBC entry instructions](https://gov.ky/web/cbc/trade/imports/import-entry) |
| Relief and additional charges | SL 33 sets the CI$800 returning resident allowance from 1 September 2026, with CI$1,000 limits for 2 specified periods. I checked the port and other package tax exceptions and the environmental vehicle tax bands in force from 1 October 2025. SL 34 commences specified sections of Act 2 of 2025 but omits s 2, which contains the new 2% temporary import rate. Current CBC guidance still describes the discretionary duty-free route. [SL 33 of 2026](https://gov.ky/w/customs-tariff-amendment-of-schedule-2-order-2026-lg34-s2) · [SL 36 of 2025](https://gov.ky/w/customs-tariff-amendment-of-schedules-order-2025-lg34-s3) · [SL 34 of 2025](https://gov.ky/w/customs-and-border-control-amendment-act-2025-commencement-order-2025-lg34-s1) · [CBC import relief](https://gov.ky/web/cbc/trade/imports) |
| Accommodation and timeshares | I checked the 13% and US$10-equivalent branches in ss 2–3, the statement on departure, the statutory 28-day deadline and the 20% surcharge. Department guidance supplies the return forms, nil filings, evidence and payment instructions. It describes a checkout practice for longer stays, which the guide distinguishes from s 7's month-of-supply language. [Tourist Accommodation (Taxation) Act, ss 2–10](https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/1964/1964-0168/1964-0168.pdf) · [Department of Tourism](https://www.ourcayman.ky/en-us/business-in-tourism/how-to/how-to-pay-taxes) |
| Departures | I checked the CI$26 and CI$6 statutory charges, the relevant traveller and tourist definitions, agency and accounting split and the separate environmental protection provisions. [Travel Act (2026 Revision), ss 2–7](https://gov.ky/documents/35692/0/Travel%2B%28Departure%2BTax%2Band%2BEnvironmental%2BProtection%2BFee%29%2BAct%2B%282026%2BRevision%29%2C.pdf/8ce52143-7c33-aeb3-eb13-4b2e8d46aee0?t=1770926366494) |
