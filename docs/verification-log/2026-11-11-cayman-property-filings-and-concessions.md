# Cayman Islands property filings, appeals and concessions

I checked the 2026 position on 11 November against the [previous rates review](2026-11-10-cayman-property-duties.md), the legislation and current public forms. I updated the [Cayman Tax guide](../../skills/international/cayman-islands/cayman-tax.md) and [indirect tax guide](../../skills/international/cayman-islands/ky-vat-gst.md).

| Component | Source and period checked |
|---|---|
| Stamp duty credit | I checked the credit for duty actually paid on a conforming purchase agreement, including minor discrepancies in the property description, and the procedure to endorse payment. SL 63 preserves the earlier rate for instruments executed before 1 January 2026. The new worked case applies each instrument's rate and deducts the earlier payment. Lands and Survey's general no-balance example does not address the change of rate. [Stamp Duty Act, s 19 and Schedule, agreement para 2](https://judicial.ky/wp-content/uploads/stamp-duty-act-2019-revision.pdf) · [SL 63 of 2025, regs 2–3](https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/SUBORDINATE/2025/2025-0063/2025-0063_SL%2063%20of%202025.pdf) · [Lands and Survey agreement guidance](https://www.caymanlandinfo.ky/services/valuation/stamp-duty/mortgages/contracts-for-sale---purchase-agreements) |
| Valuation and appeals | I checked the Lands and Survey evidence route and distinguished the assessment review window from the statutory adjudication, stamping and appeal clocks. The Act gives 15 days for the paid adjudication request, 7 days to stamp after adjudication and 30 days from adjudication to appeal after payment. The appeal uses a notice of motion and the rules specify supporting documents and service. [Lands and Survey transfer guidance](https://www.caymanlandinfo.ky/services/valuation/stamp-duty/mortgages/transfer-sale-of-land) · [Stamp Duty Act, ss 8–13 and 20–22 and Schedule](https://judicial.ky/wp-content/uploads/stamp-duty-act-2019-revision.pdf) · [Stamp Duty (Appeal) Rules, rr 3–6](https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/SUBORDINATE/1976/1976-0020/1976-0020_SL%2020%20of%201976.pdf) |
| Landholding equity return | I checked the prescribed form and its changes from 1 January 2026 against the higher-of basis in the Act. The statutory delivery to the Finance Minister and 31-day deadline are clear. The revised line 8 still refers to taxable value. I also checked the CI$5,000 fee and 2-director annual declaration for approved corporations and the valuation appeal route. [Principal Act, ss 3, 5 and 9 and Schedule](https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/1976/1976-0020/1976-0020_2022%20Revision.pdf) · [Act 16 of 2025](https://gov.ky/w/land-holding-companies-share-transfer-tax-amendment-act-2025-lg54-s3) · [1997 Regulations, reg 2 and Schedule](https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/SUBORDINATE/1997/1997-0016/1997-0016_1997%20Revision.pdf) · [SL 57 of 2025](https://gov.ky/web/govky/w/land-holding-companies-share-transfer-tax-amendment-regulations-2025-lg54-s4) |
| Property and construction concessions | I checked the April 2026 Caymanian buyer checklist and 5-year title restriction, and the Cayman Brac application and evidence. Cabinet announced extensions through 31 December 2030 and distinguishes Grand Cayman construction materials from the Sister Islands waivers. The Brac announcement measures development from the grant and the application refers to purchase. The guide calls for checking the approval date and conditions. [Caymanian buyer form](https://www.caymanlandinfo.ky/docs/default-source/laws-and-regulations/stamp-duty/application-concession-form-for-caymanian-property-buyers.pdf?sfvrsn=a01a6f6d_1) · [Cayman Brac application](https://www.caymanlandinfo.ky/docs/default-source/laws-and-regulations/stamp-duty/cayman-brac-stamp-duty-concessions.pdf?sfvrsn=ea25d4ad_1) · [Cabinet announcement, 14 January 2026](https://gov.ky/w/cayman-brac-duty-concessions-extended) · [CBC import entry](https://gov.ky/web/cbc/trade/imports/import-entry) |
