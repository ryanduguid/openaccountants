# Cayman Islands formation and reporting

I checked the principal Cayman registration and reporting routes on 11 November 2026. The [formation guide](../../skills/international/cayman-islands/ky-company-formation.md) now separates the 2026 rules from changes commencing in January 2027. The [tax guide](../../skills/international/cayman-islands/cayman-tax.md) points to the current fee schedule and economic substance law.

| Check | What I verified |
| --- | --- |
| Registration and licensing | I checked Parts 2, 7 and 9 of the Companies Act and the registration routes for LLCs, limited partnerships, ELPs, LLPs, foundation companies and SPCs. Trusts and NPOs have separate routes. Local company control and trade licensing are separate checks. The amended trade fees took effect on 28 April 2026. [Local Companies (Control) Act](https://gov.ky/documents/35692/750927/Local-Companies-Control-Act-2025-Revision-LG8-S6.pdf/038a14a3-1d49-a5c4-a6da-220788d5b319) · [Trade licensing fee amendment](https://gov.ky/documents/35692/0/TradeandBusinessLicensing%28AmendmentofSchedule1%29Regulations2026_MADE.pdf/bb91fd7f-e50a-fd48-1fd8-3812a490b632) |
| Fees and periods | Companies Act Schedule 5 sets the 2026 registration and annual bands. The March erratum corrects the merger or consolidation plan fee to CI$1,000 per constituent entity in Part 6(e)(i). SL 46 supersedes the earlier scheduled company fee changes from 1 January 2027. The LLC annual fee increases on the same date. [Companies Act erratum](https://gov.ky/documents/35692/0/Erratum%2B-%2BCompanies%2BAct%2B2026%2BRevision.pdf/94bc0855-ac88-516e-0c04-009c41db5516) · [SL 46 of 2026](https://gov.ky/w/companies-amendment-of-schedule-5-no-2-order-2026-lg48-s1) · [SL 40 of 2026](https://gov.ky/w/limited-liability-companies-fees-amendment-regulations-2026-lg39-s3) |
| Ownership | The Act uses a threshold of 25% or more and separate control tests. I checked reporting categories, alternative routes, 30-day change requirements, deposit intervals and 5-year retention against ss 2–16 and regs 3 and 8. An older Registry page gives a different threshold, so I used the Act and the March 2026 guidance. [Act](https://gov.ky/documents/35692/0/Beneficial%2BOwnership%2BTransparency%2BAct%2B%2B%282026%2BRevision%29%2C.pdf/451d0c7a-a64d-65da-974e-4fd7863af95f) · [Regulations](https://gov.ky/w/beneficial-ownership-transparency-regulations-2026-revision-lg4-s5) · [Registry guidance](https://www.ciregistry.ky/wp-content/uploads/dlm_uploads/2026-Amendments-Beneficial-Ownership-Transparency-General-Guidance.pdf) |
| Accounts and filings | I distinguished the Companies Act's January fee from the declarations under ss 41 and 168. I checked the 5-year company, LLC and ELP records rules and CIMA's 6-month filing period for audited fund accounts and the Fund Annual Return. [Companies Act](https://gov.ky/documents/43485/45951/Companies%2BAct%2B%282026%2BRevision%29%2C%2B%28LG4%2C%2BS7%29%2Bv2.pdf/bda7504d-2c94-2c49-3ac6-632a54fdd070) · [CIMA reporting requirements](https://www.cima.ky/investment-funds-reporting-requirements-and-schedule-e-reporting) |
| Tax cooperation | Economic substance notification and reporting have different triggers under s 7. For in-scope financial institutions, the guide separates filing in 2026 for 2025 from CRS due diligence effective in 2026 and reporting provisions commencing in 2027. [Economic Substance Act](https://gov.ky/documents/35692/0/International%2BTax%2BCo-operation%2B%28Economic%2BSubstance%29%2BAct%2B%282026%2BRevision%29%2C.pdf/099118d2-c42f-65b8-6d98-cb5f43cd4ad9) · [DITC deadlines](https://www.ditc.ky/press/crs-jurisdictions-lists-and-2026-reporting-deadlines/) · [CRS amendment](https://gov.ky/documents/35692/0/Tax%2BInformation%2BAuthority%2B%28International%2BTax%2BCompliance%29%2B%28Common%2BReporting%2BStandard%29%2B%28Amendment%29%2BRegulations%2C%2B2025.pdf/4f797d1c-5947-f6b2-6a6a-10503a6b5071) |
