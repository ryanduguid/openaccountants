## Customs and consumption tax

I checked import entry, duty timing, transaction value, consumption tax and the separate processing fee against the 1 January 2025 revised Acts. SRO 27/2021 replaced both schedules from 12 April 2021. The MCRS tariff reproduces both schedules. I used the operative order and tariff to resolve the revised Act's older schedule reference. [Cap. 17.04](https://www.gov.ms/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/17.04-Customs-Control-and-Management-Act.pdf) · [Cap. 17.05](https://www.gov.ms/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/17.05-Customs-Duties-and-Consumption-Tax-Act.pdf) · [SRO 27/2021](https://www.gov.ms/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/SRO-No-27-of-2021-Customs-Duties-and-Consumption-Tax-Amendment-of-Schedules-Order.pdf) · [MCRS tariff](https://mcrs.ms/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/Customs-2021-Tariff-Using-2017-HS-Code-SRO-27-of-2021.pdf)

SRO 30/2025 applies 50% to specified transport and handling for paragraph 3 transaction values in its stated period. It refers to a provision that also covers insurance, but gives no separate insurance instruction. SRO 31/2025 lists 5% duty and 5% consumption tax for specified materials. The 2025-26 budget highlights proposed 5% and 10%. The full 30 September 2025 Hansard records laying of both orders. The summary report omits them. SRO 31 cites the exemption power, whilst the rate alteration power has a separate 90-day rule that excludes time when the Assembly is dissolved. The legal basis, any required resolution, later orders and application to a particular entry remain open. [SRO 30/2025](https://www.gov.ms/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/SRO.-No.-30-of-2025-Customs-Control-and-Management-Amendment-of-Schedule-Order-2025-2.pdf) · [SRO 31/2025](https://www.gov.ms/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/SRO-No.-31-of-2025-CD-CT-Alteration-of-Rate-of-Customs-Duties-and-Consumption-Tax-Building-Materials-Order-2025.pdf) · [Hansard, meeting 10, pp 718–719](https://parliament.ms/hansard/?book=18th-parliament-2024-2025) · [Assembly summary](https://parliament.ms/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Minutes-No.-10-of-the-18th-Parliament-for-September-30th-2025.pdf)

I recomputed the fictional cement example independently in cents. It assumes both orders apply and there is no insurance. EC$1,000 plus half of EC$240 in freight and handling gives EC$1,120. Duty is EC$56. Consumption tax is EC$58.80 on EC$1,176. The two charges total EC$114.80. In the MCRS scissors example, 15% of EC$8.05 gives EC$1.2075 and the published amount is EC$1.20. The statute identifies amounts subject to the customs duty rounding rule. The separate consumption tax rule and current declaration rounding remain open.
