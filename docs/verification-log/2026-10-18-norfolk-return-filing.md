# Norfolk Island annual income-tax returns and filing controls

I added nf-return-filing for selected 2026 annual filing obligations and
linked it from the scope guide. The 2026 instruments concern the ordinary
year ended 30 June 2026 or an approved substituted period. They are kept
separate from 2026-27 income-tax planning and do not supply a 2027 calendar.
The guide remains Tier 2 pending professional review.

I read both current annual instruments in full, including the separate
child-support instrument. The main instrument contains independent
withholding, business, loss, reporting and special-entity triggers and
limited exceptions. Low taxable income alone cannot establish that no
return is required. The child-support exception uses six components, a
strictly-less-than threshold and whole-year receipt of listed payments;
its outcome does not override a separate main-instrument or notice duty.

The selected current 1936 Act provisions establish annual, further and
special returns, approved forms and assessment/reliance distinctions.
Selected TAA provisions establish the required form, declaration, delivery,
business-day adjustment and lodgment deferral. A deferral expressly does
not defer payment. The actual agent date depends on the client and confirmed
arrangement; no universal May date or automatic extension is asserted.

The latest published TAA and 1997 Act compilations have an interval ending
30 September 2026. I checked their live versions pages and the unincorporated
October Acts 71 and 86 against the selected provisions. Act 86 Schedule 7
renames ancillary funds and preserves specified instrument references.
The guide does not treat an older form name as the end of an obligation.
The current 1936 Act compilation is effective 1 October 2026.

The ordinary 31 October 2026 instrument date falls on a Saturday. Schedule 1
section 388-52, the imported statutory business-day definition and the
checked official 2026 holiday list support Monday 2 November 2026. The
regional Tasmanian Recreation Day does not meet that definition's whole-state
holiday condition. This is a derived ordinary filing date, with actual
notices, later announcements and agent arrangements checked separately.

The source ledger preserves hashes and read scopes for 20 official inputs.
Large volumes were read in selected provisions. Native search initially
returned 2011/2016 and pre-reform material; those leads are excluded from
current verification. The instrument commencement clauses say the day
after registration; the record's effective display is not substituted for
the operative clause. No commencement-date calculation is applied here.

Fictional component checks cover strict thresholds, resident months, the
part-year cap, child-support totals and whole-year receipt, substituted
period dates, weekend/holiday treatment, separate payment dates, notice and
declaration gates. Repository verification uses the four generators,
required validation and test suites, country report and assembled Norfolk
bundle. Existing checker baselines are preserved and advisories require
contextual review. No actual financial engagement benchmark was run.

The guide preserves applicable universal/domain outputs, mandatory facts,
refusals and professional review. It does not complete actual returns,
special-entity forms, general amendments/objections, penalties/remission,
debt, interest, full recordkeeping or comprehensive country coverage.
No private client, identity, bank or signing information was obtained; no
account was accessed and no declaration, tax submission or payment was made.
