# Oman social protection commencement and employer contributions

[Royal Decree 60/2025, art I](https://www.mjla.gov.om/modules/decrees/download.php?file=1345), in Official Gazette 1604 of 13 July 2025, replaces clauses 2, 3 and 5 of Royal Decree 52/2023 art VI. It moves the sick and unusual leave branch to three years after the 2023 decree, non-Omani work-injury coverage to five years and the Board-set provident contribution start to within four years. The [SPF and ILO June 2026 report, p 19 and figure 3](https://www.spf.gov.om/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/20260602-Oman_summary-report-ILO-SPF-web.pdf) identifies the revised months as July 2026, July 2028 and July 2027 respectively. The amendment itself took effect on the day after publication under art III. The report does not establish a Board-selected first provident payroll date.

[SPF Decision R/13/2026, arts 1 and 3](https://www.mjla.gov.om/modules/decisions/download.php?file=2219), in Official Gazette 1654 of 28 June 2026, makes sick and unusual leave insurance compulsory from 19 July 2026 for the specified non-Omani public employees and private employees covered by the Labour Law. The [SPF commencement notice](https://www.spf.gov.om/news/%D8%A8%D8%AF%D8%A1-%D8%AA%D8%B7%D8%A8%D9%8A%D9%82-%D9%81%D8%B1%D8%B9-%D8%AA%D8%A3%D9%85%D9%8A%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D8%AC%D8%A7%D8%B2%D8%A7%D8%AA-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%B1%D8%B6%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D9%88/) confirms the 19 July launch and the 1 August 2026 start of electronic employer compensation and settlement submissions. Earlier SPF FAQ and programme pages still show the original timetable.

The 1% sick-leave contribution is employer-only under [Social Protection Law art 123](https://www.spf.gov.om/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Social-Protaction-Law.pdf). For eligible Omanis at or below the Article 52 wage ceiling and at the ordinary Article 90 work-injury rate, it increases the employer total from 13.5% for the four branches in 2025 to 14.5% for the five branches from 19 July 2026. The employee total remains 8%. The VAT guide now points to the dated payroll treatment.

The checked scope is the phase-in and related contribution totals. The selected provident Board decision, work-injury categories for non-Omanis, employer-specific rates and contribution calculations, and local-accountant review remain open.
