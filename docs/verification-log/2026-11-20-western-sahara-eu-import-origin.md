# Western Sahara EU import origin and labelling

On 20 November 2026 I revisited the [Western Sahara reference](../../skills/international/western-sahara/eh-eu-import-origin-reference.md). The [Exchange of Letters](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/agree/2025/2025/oj/eng) and [provisional application notice](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/notice/2025/2042/oj) support the 3 October 2025 provisional start. [Delegated Regulation 2025/2652](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_del/2025/2652/oj/eng) applies from 4 October and entered into force on 24 December. Recitals 7 and 12 use 4 October for provisional application of the new agreement. Recital 6 concerns the continued effect of the annulled decision on the previous agreement. The guide uses the dedicated notice for the agreement and Article 3 for the labelling rule.

[Association Council Decision No 2/2025](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dec/2026/242/oj) was published on 28 January 2026 as 2026/242. Article 2 fixes entry into force on adoption and application from 3 October 2025. I checked Article 8 and both specimens in the annex. The guide records the EUR.1 box entries and regional origin declaration. It directs readers to the vessel and processing provisions for the applicable Convention and transitional rules. [Council Decision 2025/2023](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dec/2025/2023/oj) concerns the prior EU position.

The [Parliament file](https://oeil.europarl.europa.eu/oeil/en/procedure-file?reference=2025%2F0297%28NLE%29) listed consent as preparatory on 20 November. [InfoCuria](https://infocuria.curia.europa.eu/tabs/affair?lang=EN&searchTerm=T-907%2F25) listed T-907/25, T-908/25 and T-181/26 as pending. The guide requires transaction-date checks for later decisions and interim orders.

Four fictional cases check the instrument dates, earlier stock conditions and a misplaced EUR.1 entry. The product-specific rule, proof eligibility, tariff, quota, authority and shipment facts still need evidence. Western Sahara’s local charging rules and professional review remain open.
