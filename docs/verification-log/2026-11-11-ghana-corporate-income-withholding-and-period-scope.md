# Ghana corporate income, withholding and period scope

11 November 2026. The Ghana corporate-income guide remains a Tier 2 source-cited draft pending qualified review. Selected original tax-base, rates, minimum-income, withholding and filing components have been reconciled. No complete corporate-income, tax-system, cross-border or compliance topic is certified.

Original Income Tax Act, 2015 (Act 896), sections 2 and 3 distinguish residence/source and include income connected with a Ghanaian permanent establishment irrespective of source. Section 101(4) contains alternative incorporation under Act 179 or management/control tests. Successor-law references, PE attribution and the complete later amendment chain remain open. Selected deductions, accrual accounting and capital-allowance references are principles, not a verification of every restriction or asset class.

The original First Schedule manufacturing formula gives 75% or 50% of the ordinary reference rate. Applied to 25%, these calculate 18.75% or 12.5%. The current GRA business-exemptions page agrees, whilst its CIT page prints 18.5% for the regional-capital case. The guide records this conflict and the literal paragraph 3(6) exclusions under (1) and (3); eligibility and the interaction with the broad ordinary-rate provision remain unresolved.

Income Tax (Amendment) Act, 2023 (Act 1094), sections 1, 3 and 13 support selected minimum-chargeable-income, 5-year business-loss and rate changes. Section 13(c) supports a 5% Sixth Schedule concession rate, conflicting with GRA 1% introductory prose. This does not establish a particular concession's eligibility or duration. Assent and Gazette notification on 3 April 2023 do not independently resolve statutory commencement.

GRA practice note GRA/AG/2024/001, issued 20 March 2024, states minimum-income implementation from 1 May 2023. It explains the 5 consecutive tax-loss-year condition, first 5 operation-year/farming exclusions and the distinction between 5% turnover as an income base and tax on that income. Tax paid under this rule is not another-year tax credit; unrelieved losses remain subject to section 17. Segmented activities, carried-loss tables and subsequent law remain open.

Selected GRA income-withholding guidance captured 11 November 2026 supports the qualified 8%/15%/20% investment/service rows, resident contract 3%/5%/7.5% rows, strict exceeding GHS 2,000 per annum thresholds for the stated rows, monthly statement and payment within 15 days and non-resident contract notice within 30 days. Non-resident finality has a Ghanaian-PE exception. Individual-service and interest-recipient qualifications remain explicit. The obsolete VAT tail was not used. Complete statutes, threshold mechanics, exemptions, treaties and operative application remain open.

Original Act 896 sections 18 and 121 to 125 distinguish the assessment year, accounting basis period, instalment months and return wording. The original return rule says 4 months after year of assessment, not literally accounting year. Original Revenue Administration Act, 2016 (Act 915), section 30 provides a discretionary timely extension up to 60 days without moving the payment date. GRA's 6 October 2016 accounting-date note supports approval and transition calculations. Exact current non-calendar deadlines and later administration amendments remain open.

Native extraction was empty on all 182 Act 896, 12 Act 1094 and 16 minimum-income-note pages; bounded default OCR completed each file. Only 14 selected original pages were visually checked. Independent source and draft reviews found no actionable issue in the selected changes. All 4 fictional worked examples were recomputed, including both manufacturing cases. These checks do not verify consolidated current law or qualified local sign-off.
