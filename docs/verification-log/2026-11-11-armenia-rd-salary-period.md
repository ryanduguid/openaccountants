# Armenia R&D salary withholding period and eligibility

Reviewed 11 November 2026 against the [Armenian Tax Code consolidation from 1 September 2026](https://www.arlis.am/en/acts/230455/latest) and [Law HO-499-N](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/201054). Articles 7–8 of the amending Law introduced article 150(1.1) and give the 1 January 2025 to 31 December 2031 period. The Code requires a listed occupation, qualifying work and a positive commission opinion. If that opinion is annulled, the Code requires recalculation under the general rules.

[Decision No. 195-N as amended](https://www.arlis.am/hy/acts/203841) gives the starting salary month in clause 7(3) and the April 2025 application transition in clause 8. Annex 4 clause 4 limits the salary relief to listed employees exclusively performing the programme's covered professional work and to salary for that work. Clause 6 covers resubmissions. The consolidation applies from 7 March 2025. [Decision No. 353-N](https://www.arlis.am/en/acts/204784) entered into force on 28 March 2025 and gives the occupation list, applying to relationships arising from 1 January 2025.

Article 150(1.1) excludes foreign citizens without Armenian residence rights and also refers to stateless persons. The [earlier ARLIS English version](https://www.arlis.am/en/acts/229632) does not settle how the residence modifier applies to a stateless person. Both guides now require qualified confirmation for that case. The positive examples state a covered salary period. Contribution figures and administrative rules outside this salary-income-tax change retain their existing gaps.
