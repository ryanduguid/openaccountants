# New Zealand 2026-27 employer payroll and contribution boundaries

12 November 2026. I added a source-cited ordinary employer payroll guide for payments from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027. Inland Revenue's [KiwiSaver change notice](https://www.ird.govt.nz/kiwisaver-changes) and [employee deduction instructions](https://www.ird.govt.nz/kiwisaver/kiwisaver-for-employers/contributions-and-deductions/kiwisaver-deductions-from-employee-pay) establish the payday-based 3.5% default, other employee elections and approved temporary 3% reductions. The [employer contribution instructions](https://www.ird.govt.nz/kiwisaver/kiwisaver-for-employers/contributions-and-deductions/employer-contributions-to-kiwisaver-and-complying-funds) distinguish eligibility, contracts and the employer's choice on temporary matching.

I read the cover and pages 92, 93 and 95 of Inland Revenue's [payroll specification](https://www.ird.govt.nz/-/media/project/ir/home/documents/digital-service-providers/software-providers/payroll-calculations-business-rules-specifications/payroll-calculations-and-business-rules-specification.pdf?modified=20260401012317) as native text and images. Its cover expressly says 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027, version 1.0 dated 24 March 2026. The selected provisions distinguish the ESCT rate threshold from the whole-dollar contribution base and require truncating tax to cents. [Schedule 1 Part D of the Income Tax Act 2007](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2007/97/en/latest/parts/DLM1523173/), in the retrieved current copy dated 4 September 2026, supports the bands and the last-dollar rule. I did not review the complete Act or amendment chain.

The [IR335 employer guide](https://www.ird.govt.nz/-/media/project/ir/home/documents/forms-and-guides/ir300---ir399/ir335/ir335.pdf?modified=20250811232131) actually has a September 2026 cover. I read the cover and pages 26, 27, 71 and 72 as native text and images. Its page 27 Matthew example calculates ESCT on $204.17, conflicting with the whole-dollar instruction on page 26 and the [dedicated deduction page](https://www.ird.govt.nz/employing-staff/deductions-from-income/employer-superannuation-contribution-tax/deduct-esct-from-each-employer-contribution). The new guide follows the technical algorithm and records the conflict: at 17.5%, the whole-dollar $204 base gives $35.70 ESCT and $168.47 net. A second worked check covers truncation of $18.375 to $18.37. Contractual net contributions require gross-up and reconciliation rather than treating the promised net percentage as gross.

The [payday filing page](https://www.ird.govt.nz/payday) and [electronic filing instructions](https://www.ird.govt.nz/employing-staff/payday-filing/filing-employment-information-electronically) support separate filing deadlines and the inclusive $50,000 electronic filing threshold after the new-employer concession. The [payment page](https://www.ird.govt.nz/employing-staff/payday-filing/paying-deductions-to-inland-revenue) says 'more than' $500,000; IR335 page 71 explicitly includes exactly $500,000 in twice-monthly remittance. The guide uses that inclusive boundary, the exclusion of ACC earners' levy from the payment test and the 15 January exception for late-December payments.

I also checked the [dated employee ACC levy table](https://www.ird.govt.nz/income-tax/income-tax-for-individuals/acc-clients-and-carers/acc-earners-levy-rates), [salary/wage deductions](https://www.ird.govt.nz/deductions), [employer registration requirement](https://www.ird.govt.nz/setting-up-a-business-or-organisation) and [PAYE record types](https://www.ird.govt.nz/employing-staff/deductions-from-income/paye-records). [ACC's payer explanation](https://www.acc.co.nz/for-business/understanding-levies-if-you-work-or-own-a-business) supports correcting the existing self-employment guide's payer rows and PAYE-only warning. Its generic rates are not the source for the new guide's dated levy figures.

PDF extraction warned of encoding and complex layouts; selected page images resolved those reading issues. Payroll-specification page 98 was flagged for OCR but lies outside the selected provisions and was not used. Other pages are not claimed as reviewed. An old ESCT campaign URL returned 404 and supplies no substantive evidence; a zero-character web save also supplies none.

Both guides remain tier 2 and pending qualified review. The new guide expressly leaves special PAYE algorithms, complete contribution bases, cross-border employment, FBT, employment-law entitlements, employer ACC invoice calculations and full records/penalty rules for further checks. The inventory increase records an added guide, not comprehensive verification, new accountant sign-off or completion of any whole topic. Repository checks and canonical publication are recorded separately.
