# Cayman Islands tax-system scope

11 November 2026. I checked the six tax-system scope requirements in the [coverage plan](../COUNTRY-COVERAGE-PLAN.md) against the [Cayman Islands Tax Overview](../../skills/international/cayman-islands/ky-tax-overview.md) and [Cayman Tax](../../skills/international/cayman-islands/cayman-tax.md).

| Requirement | Source and period checked |
| --- | --- |
| Territorial reach | Section 3(3) identifies the three islands. The territorial sea order has applied since 1 January 1990. The 2026 Economic Substance Act schedule uses the same boundary for its shipping business definition. The Cayman Brac stamp duty concession is linked from the overview. | Tax residence | The government's statement on direct taxation and Cayman’s CRS submission support the absence of a general domestic tax residence test. The rules in force in 2026 use separate classifications for tourist accommodation, CRS self-certification and economic substance. The self-certification rule applies from 1 January 2026. | Taxpayer types | The absence of a personal or corporate income tax covers individuals and entities. | Currency | Section 22 establishes the Cayman dollar. I checked the Acts amending the 2020 revision through Act 22 of 2026, gazetted on 28 October. The guides now direct readers to the conversion rule for the particular payment. | Assessment period | There is no general domestic income tax assessment period. The core government financial year ends on 31 December. I checked the distinct periods for home-use customs entries, land and equity transactions, the month of supply for tourist accommodation, and company and substance reporting. Act 2 of 2025 changes temporary imports from 1 October 2025. The ordinary timing rule in s 43 remains in effect.
