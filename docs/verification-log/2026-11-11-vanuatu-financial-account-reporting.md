# Vanuatu financial account reporting and filing dates

I checked the original Applied CRS rules, the 2020 amendment and the Vanuatu Competent Authority's notices on 11 November 2026. The overview records the calendar-year, electronic and nil-return rules, the ordinary filing date and the authority's power to authorise another date. Order 154 generally commenced on 1 January 2020. The return rule refers to reporting periods from 1 January 2017. [Tax Administration Regulation Order No. 154 of 2019, reg 53 and Sch 2 Part 1 cls 1 and 4–7](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/images/TAA/Tax_Administration_Order_no154_of_2019.pdf)

The original order sets 30 March of the first reporting calendar year for electronic notification and requires updates within 14 days. Public Notice No. 001 of 2026 gives 31 March 2026 for affected institutions. The calendar 2025 filing cycle has a separate discrepancy. The portal notice gives 31 May 2026 and the reporting plan shows 31 July 2026. Both are linked from the authority's exchange of information page. The authority explicitly extended the calendar 2023 and earlier filing cycle to 30 June 2024. I could not verify a decision settling either 2026 discrepancy. [Order 154, Sch 2 Part 1 cls 5–7](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/images/TAA/Tax_Administration_Order_no154_of_2019.pdf), [Public Notice No. 001 of 2026](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/images/Forms/VCA/Vanuatu%20Competent%20Authority.pdf), [exchange of information page and filing notices](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/home/section/inland-revenue/international.html)

I checked the 5-year records rule against Order 156. It runs from the end of the calendar year in which the return for the last relevant reporting period is required. I could not retrieve the Gazette copy of Order 164 of 2024 from the retired government hostname or the corresponding path on the current site. The Global Forum reports that Vanuatu made further changes effective 3 October 2024. The exact changes and current penalty provisions still need checking against the Gazette. [Tax Administration Regulation (Amendment) Order No. 156 of 2020, cl 2 and Sch items 8–9](https://doft.gov.vu/images/2020/Tax_Policy/Tax_Administration_Regualtion_Amendment_Order_No_156_of_2020.pdf), [Global Forum 2025 legal framework review](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/peer-review-of-the-automatic-exchange-of-financial-account-information-2025-update_bbf150e4-en/full-report/vanuatu_e160e6b1.html)

The country-by-country reporting schedule has a separate commencement provision. Regulation 53(4) leaves the start date and applicable financial years to a ministerial order. I did not locate that instrument. The overview leaves current country-by-country filings, source-based withholding, treaty treatment and later CRS formats open. Local-accountant review remains pending. [Order 154, reg 53(4) and Sch 3](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/images/TAA/Tax_Administration_Order_no154_of_2019.pdf)
