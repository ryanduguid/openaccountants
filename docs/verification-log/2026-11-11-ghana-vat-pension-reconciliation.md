# Ghana VAT and pension source reconciliation

11 November 2026. This batch corrects selected claims in two Ghana guides. Both remain Tier 2 and pending qualified review.

## VAT

GRA's 2026 reform guidance preserves imported-service exemptions, requires separate imported-services reporting and describes input claims by VAT-registered taxpayers. The guide now checks liability and deduction eligibility separately and removes automatic recovery/net-zero conclusions. GRA's imports page names box 17, conflicting with the former draft's boxes 10 to 15. The prescribed current return remains unchecked, so fixed box instructions are removed. The review also removed an unsupported Box 20 instruction.

## Pension allocation

The complete SSNIT FAQ supports percentages of basic salary: employer 13%, employee 5.5%, allocation 13.5% to SSNIT and 5% to Tier 2. The guide states the bases explicitly and keeps the SSNIT earnings limit separate from unresolved Tier 2 above-ceiling treatment. Its GHS 10,000 example totals GHS 1,850, allocated GHS 1,350 and GHS 500.

SSNIT's notice expressly applies from 1 January 2026 and publishes maximum/minimum earnings GHS 69,000/GHS 587.80 and contributions GHS 9,315/GHS 79.40. Native extraction returned all 875 characters with no OCR requirement. Its minimum differs from 13.5% times GHS 587.80 = GHS 79.353; rounding instructions remain open. The FAQ is undated and was checked on 11 November 2026. NPRA's FAQ discusses historical transitions and does not establish a blanket current age rule.
