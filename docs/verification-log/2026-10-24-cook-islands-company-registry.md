# Cook Islands: domestic company registry and reporting

This batch adds a bounded draft for genuinely new ordinary domestic companies
incorporated after 1 January 2026. It covers selected registration prerequisites,
director and local-office evidence, annual return month, records and financial
statement/audit classification. It does not complete the formation country
contract or any other country topic.

The source work read selected official consolidated provisions and retained
complete original Companies amendments for 2021 and 2025 and the 2024 Financial
Transactions Reporting amendment. The 2021 historical audit and first-period
transition rules do not apply to the genuinely new company population. The
2025 amendment concerns legacy restoration. Scrambled extraction of the 2024
PDF was resolved by visually reading all 6 rendered pages. Its ultimate-principal
fallback cross-references have the same ambiguity in the original and current
consolidation; ownership classification remains held rather than silently repaired.

The selected statutory fee rows were reconciled to the exact native table cells.
The fees are NZD 75 for incorporation and NZD 50 for an annual return, already
VAT inclusive. Their NZD 125 cross-period budget subtotal is not a complete
formation or annual cost. The annual return uses the Registrar's allocated
month, with the incorporation-calendar-year exception. It is separate from
statement preparation, audit and records.

The component check recomputes exact voting-share fractions, the 9/10-holder
boundary, current-plus-completed period retention, selected fees and conditional
working-day dates. It covers 16 fictional worked positions and 14 held cases
and rejects 28 arithmetic or input-premise mutations. The April date examples
remain conditional on no additional applicable holiday declaration; real
deadline outputs remain held until the complete calendar is established.

Tier 2, pending review and verified-by pending status remain. Generated copies,
guide presence, component checks and repository checks do not establish local
professional sign-off, a completed formation engagement or comprehensive
Cook Islands acceptance. Tax, VAT registration, payroll, social, cross-border,
property, full reporting framework and other compliance work remain open.
