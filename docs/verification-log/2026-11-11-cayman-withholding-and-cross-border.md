# Cayman Islands withholding and cross-border rules

I checked the 2026 Cayman position on 11 November. The [corporate income tax guide](../../skills/international/cayman-islands/ky-corporate-income-tax.md) now distinguishes domestic withholding, limited treaty relief, information exchange and tax cooperation filings. The [Cayman Tax guide](../../skills/international/cayman-islands/cayman-tax.md) records the economic substance scope and test.

| Component | Source and period checked |
|---|---|
| Payments, rates and source | Cayman has no underlying domestic income tax on dividends, interest, royalties or ordinary service fees. Domestic income tax withholding rates, income sourcing for that charge, a Cayman withholding claim and a Cayman income tax credit are inapplicable. The Tax Concessions Act s 6 deals with protection against future taxes. For a payment that may be taxed abroad, use the taxing jurisdiction's source, rate and claim rules. [Government tax overview](https://cigarchives.gov.ky/economy) · [Tax Concessions Act, s 6](https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/1964/1964-0164/1964-0164_2018%20Revision.pdf) |
| Eligibility and relief | I checked the UK arrangement in force from 20 December 2010, with effect for corporation tax from 1 April 2011 and income and capital gains tax from 6 April 2011. The Cayman instructions for DT-Individual address the 6-year pension conditions and direct submission to HMRC. I checked the separate Japan agreement, effective for Chapter 3 withholding from 1 January 2012, and Japan's payer and refund routes. The guide links the limited Nordic agreements and uses Denmark's associated enterprises instrument as a profit adjustment example. [UK arrangement](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/cayman-islands-tax-treaties/2010-uk-cayman-islands-double-taxation-arrangement-in-force) · [Japan agreement](https://www.mof.go.jp/tax_policy/summary/international/press_release/sy230207ke_b.pdf) · [Denmark associated enterprises agreement](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/ltc/2010/12/dan/pdf) |
| Transfer pricing and CbCR | The absence of a Cayman corporate income tax leaves no general Cayman tax profit base to adjust. I checked the separate statutory CbCR threshold, notification, filing and retention rules. The August 2025 Guidelines distinguish normal filings from requests for information resembling a master file or local file. The December 2025 advisory moved filing to the DITC Portal and gave a limited extension for certain 2024 reports. [CbCR Regulations](https://www.ditc.ky/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/Tax_Information_Authority_International_Tax_Compliance_Country_by_Country_Reporting_Regulations_2017.pdf) · [DITC Guidelines](https://www.ditc.ky/wp-content/uploads/CbCR_Guidelines.pdf) · [Portal advisory](https://www.ditc.ky/wp-content/uploads/CbCR_Functionality_Now_Available_on_the_Portal.pdf) |
| Economic substance | The 2026 revision supplies the entity, activity and general test rules, the reduced pure equity holding company test and the high-risk IP presumption. I checked the foreign residence notification particulars and DITC's requirements for evidence. [Economic Substance Act, ss 4 and 7 and Schedule](https://gov.ky/w/international-tax-co-operation-economic-substance-act-2026-revision-lg7-s10) · [DITC guidance v3.2](https://www.ditc.ky/wp-content/uploads/Economic-Substance-Guidance.pdf) |
| Account information | I checked the US Model 1 agreement in force from 1 July 2014, including the distinction between Cayman reporting and US withholding. CRS new account due diligence changed from 1 January 2026. The amended return and compliance form provisions commence on 1 January 2027. [US–Cayman FATCA agreement](https://home.treasury.gov/system/files/131/FINAL-US-Cayman-Islands-Cayman-alternat.pdf) · [CRS amendment](https://www.ditc.ky/wp-content/uploads/Tax_Information_Authority_International_Tax_Compliance_Common_Reporting_Standard_Amendment_Regulations_2025.pdf) |

Withholding and cross-border coverage remains partial pending Nordic claim forms and detailed treaty branches for transport and associated enterprises. Special regimes gains partial coverage for economic substance. Sector rules, digital assets, incentives and the remaining substance exceptions still need reconciliation. Detailed financial account reporting classifications and corrections remain open. Both guides await local accountant review.
