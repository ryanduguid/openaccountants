# BES personal imports: separate reliefs, ages and indivisible goods

On 17 October 2026 I read DAB 3.25–3.47, customs regulation 3.14–3.15,
Belastingwet 6.20 and the complete authority traveller, moving and gift
pages for the
[personal-import reference](../../skills/international/caribbean-netherlands/bq-personal-import-relief.md).
The [system reference](../../skills/international/caribbean-netherlands/bq-tax-system.md)
now distinguishes succession/gift-tax scope from importing personal goods.
Existing substantive tax figures do not change.

The guide distinguishes duty, ABB and excise relief. The excise list does
not name the student provisions. Normal residence uses its customs-title
ties and exceptions; “foreign” is defined per importing public body.
Moving permits, six-month ordinary-goods conditions, the one-year vehicle
condition before embarkation, one vehicle per household and twelve-month
post-acceptance disposal restrictions retain their different triggers.
Inheritance's declaration clock starts from possession rather than death.

Postal 28, private-gift 70 and traveller 500/under-fifteen 150 ceilings
have different scope. The gift page includes carriage; disputed valuation
remains held. Indivisible goods do not gain a partial traveller allowance.
Tobacco/alcohol groups, proportional alternatives, half crew quantities,
under-seventeen restrictions and the separate under-eighteen distilled
spirits exclusion are preserved. The oral declaration limit is not a
tax exemption.

Twenty manual criteria were frozen before drafting and checked against
the primary text. The proportional tobacco and crew examples recompute
to 1 and 100. This is implementing-agent source review, not an independent
model benchmark or practitioner sign-off. The moving summary's broader
territory/tobacco wording remains explicit; disputed inter-island and
household-stock application is held rather than resolved from that page.

Actual value, classification, permit, restricted-goods and other relief
regimes, external forms, live operational cases and complete tax bills
remain open. The reference stays Tier 2/pending_review. No comprehensive-area
credit, private taxpayer records, identity/tax identifier or bank submission,
application, declaration, payment, PDF/OCR or engagement workbook is claimed.
