# BES customs debt and remedies: events, permissions and separate clocks

On 17 October 2026 I read DAB 2.51–2.97 including 2.70a, 3.144–3.154,
4.3–4.5 and 4.48; customs regulation 2.40–2.41 and 2.73–2.74;
Belastingwet 6.21 and 8.95–8.116; tax regulation 6.12; and two complete
authority pages for the
[debt and remedies reference](../../skills/international/caribbean-netherlands/bq-customs-debt-remedies.md).
The [system reference](../../skills/international/caribbean-netherlands/bq-tax-system.md)
now routes to it. Existing substantive tax figures do not change.

The guide distinguishes ordinary acceptance, irregular entry, storage,
undischarged documents and failed relief, with their own events and persons.
It preserves the knowledge tests, seven-year records, liability provisions
and specified burden consequence. Loss and debt extinction are not inferred
from missing stock. Five-year post-assessment has a criminal-act exception;
the separate below-8 duty/debt rules are not blanket import allowances.

Ten-day communication/service payment terms differ from general domestic
remittance. Monthly credit requires a permit, establishment and continuing
security, with its eight-day post-month term. The authority's direct-payment
description does not itself grant release before payment/security. Security
below 56 is discretionary; mortgage/pledge values of 1,500/1,250 for a
stipulated 1,000 security amount leave the remaining conditions intact.

The guide distinguishes request/decision, two-month objection, nine-month
objection decision, two-month first appeal and the court clerk's separate
six-week fee notice. The specific body-search non-suspension provision is
not broadened into a universal objection rule. Court appeal does not itself
suspend payment under the incorporated rule. A request for payment relief
does not establish a grant.

Refund grounds retain the five-year import-declaration trigger, per-document
minimum, identity/use/sale conditions and required pre-export decision or
permission. The import-ABB request bridge is separated from substantive
eligibility and the adjustment of an earlier producer deduction. No double
refund/input credit is inferred.

Thirty manual criteria were frozen before drafting and reviewed against the
primary inputs. This is implementing-agent source review, not independent
model execution or practitioner sign-off. Actual charge-specific threshold
and refund correspondence, service/calendar disputes, full collection and
enforcement, interest/relief methods, penalties, higher appeals, substantive
relief, external annexes, forms and live cases remain open. The reference
stays Tier 2/pending_review, with no comprehensive-area credit, private
records, identity/tax identifier or bank submission, filing, payment,
guarantee, PDF/OCR or engagement workbook claimed.
