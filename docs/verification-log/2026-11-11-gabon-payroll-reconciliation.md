# Gabon payroll and social contribution source reconciliation

11 November 2026. The draft separates the DGI CGI 2025 salary-tax rules,
CNAMGS legislation effective from 1 January 2017 and the CNSS decree signed
on 18 December 2025. Provider guidance is separately dated to the check.

## Corrected source coverage

- Law 037/2023 replacement articles 26 to 30 and decree 0487 articles 2 to 4
  supply the ordinary CNSS branches, employer/worker allocation, broad base,
  exclusion of reimbursements and social benefits and monthly ceiling.
- Decree 578 articles 2 to 9 supplies CNAMGS rates, exemptions, its distinct
  IRPP base and CFA 2,500,000 ceiling. Order 0010 fixes commencement on
  1 January 2017. Law 036/2023 replacement article 42 retains decree-setting
  and employer responsibility. The current provider page confirms the
  selected private-sector calendar, separately from public monthly transfer.
- The contribution example applies each base and ceiling separately. On the
  stated CFA 2,000,000 bases and ordinary decree rates, employer contributions
  are CFA 352,000 and worker contributions CFA 115,000. It assumes those
  decree rates apply for the pay period and does not resolve commencement.
- CGI 2025 articles 95 to 96, 174 and 346 to 351 support the selected salary
  withholding, TCTS exemptions and remittance. The full salary calculation,
  other employer levies and later changes remain open.

## Source conflicts and outstanding work

underlying-law dates and a ceiling cross-reference to article 5 rather than
article 4. A public notice on the account named CNSS Gabon reports application
from 1 January 2026 and first DTS by 30 April. Firecrawl does not support that
site; native HTTP captured the post. A matching CNSS website notice, account
corroboration and the legal commencement instrument remain open. The public
post search and short archive extraction do not prove that no notice exists.

The 1989 decree records employer pension at 6%, whilst the earlier 2025 guide
used 5% from secondary summaries. The draft does not extrapolate 1989 rates
to 2025. Find the operative later amendment chain. Order 0011 also has a
heading/signature date conflict; it was captured but its penalty provisions
are not promoted as verified current rules.
