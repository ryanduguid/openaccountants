It covers territorial reach, tax residence, taxpayer types, currency, assessment periods and responsible authorities. The guide remains Tier 2 and pending_review.

## Period and source scope

The revised statutes state a 1 January 2025 cutoff. The [Revised Edition of the Laws Order 2026, S.R.O. 8 of 2026](https://www.gov.ms/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/SRO-No.-8-of-2026-Revised-Edition-of-the-Laws-Order-.pdf#page=2), ss 2–3, distinguishes that revision date from 23 February 2026, when the edition became the sole authentic edition and authoritative version. Neither date establishes subsequent amendments for a requested liability period. A November 2026 request still requires the law and facts for that date.

## Conditional scenarios and limits

Seven manual rule-selection cases cover a 2025 assessment year with a 2024 ordinary basic year, a foreign-incorporated business, partner allocation and partnership information, the conditional trustee rate, six-month versus 183-day wording, the Customs scope exclusion and a held November 2026 liability request. These are written classification controls, not taxpayer calculations or evidence that actual conditions are met. No arithmetic fixture or required-example registry change is proposed.

The source review distinguishes legal classification, income attribution and the person responsible for filing or acting as representative. It does not infer a uniform company rate for every partnership, trust or estate. It preserves the actual statutory conjunctions, separate residence periods and limited IBC deeming.

The ECCB Act s 4 Gazette appointed-application chain remains unverified. The guide therefore qualifies Art 17 and relies separately on the Interpretation Dictionary for the statutory currency unit and Cap. 17.26 s 54 for payment within that Act’s scope. It establishes no exchange rate, actual payment channel, conversion method or universal remittance rule.

The existing company/tax and monthly social-security references retain their separately dated details and unresolved conditions. Detailed surcharge history, PAYE conflicts, contribution rounding, individual relief, sector incentives and actual filing/payment requirements belong to their respective topics. This orientation does not audit every exemption, calculation, levy, return procedure or subsequent instrument. Professional review remains pending.
