# Bermuda conveyance stamp-duty, relief and stamping controls

Source checks recorded on 15 November 2026, using official captures observed on 14 November UTC. I reconciled selected ordinary Bermuda-land conveyance, first-home and granted-notice, execution, adjudication/appeal, late-stamping and instrument-use controls in the existing main guide and overview. Both remain Tier 2 and pending_review.

## What changed

The inherited ordinary rule and BMD 2 million example used unsupported 2%, 4%, 6% and 8% bands and totalled BMD 128,000. The selected ordinary Head 14 schedule from 1 April 2023 instead uses 2.10%, 3.15%, 4.20%, 6.30% and 7.35% on successive slices, including a distinct BMD 1-1.5 million slice. The conditional ordinary BMD 2 million duty is BMD 103,950. A sale price alone does not establish the actual statutory consideration, chargeable instrument or exemption position.

I separated complete first-home conditions from the actual granted-notice schedule. The selected notice schedule exempts the first BMD 1 million, then charges 6.30% on the next BMD 500,000 and 7.35% thereafter. The old 3-year residence/construction limb was repealed in 2022. Each purchaser's status and individual interest history, the entire acquired interest and property category, fair-value/money consideration, Commissioner opinion and signed declarations still matter. A price, ownership fact, application or first-home label is not a relief decision.

Ordinary domestic stamping, overseas execution, escrow, adjudication, appeal, special permission, penalty timing and current payment/stamping are separate. The s 10 BMD 500 penalty maximum does not cap duty or the distinct s 67 maximum fine. A request does not establish clock suspension, permission, remission or a stamped instrument.

## Review repairs

A later primary-source check identified B146-SUPP-01: Interpretation Act ss 1 and 40 make specified offence punishments maxima unless a contrary intention appears. Independent source review confirmed that qualification applies to the selected s 67 wording. The main paragraph, manual case and this record now identify BMD 800 as an illustrative **maximum fine**, while preserving prosecution, conviction, unpaid duty and s 10 qualifications. No arithmetic result or other control changed.

Independent source review found 2 narrow defects in the first draft. B146-DRAFT-01: a blanket rounding hold overlooked s 76 on the already captured principal pages 41-42. I replaced it with upward whole-cent rounding of the percentage-calculated duty. The new ordinary BMD 100,000.01 case checks unrounded duty BMD 2,100.000315 and final BMD 2,100.01; the granted-notice BMD 1,000,000.01 case checks BMD 0.00063 and final BMD 0.01. The earlier exact-cent outputs stay numerically unchanged.

B146-DRAFT-02: generic intake and worksheet wording could require s 22 adjudication, first-home declarations or a notice for every ordinary conveyance. Section 22 is optional generally; Head 14(b) requires opinion/declarations for its relief route and Head 14(c) requires an actual granted notice. The intake now separates those routes, late permission and ordinary stamping, with inapplicable items recorded as N/A. Mandatory first-home and special-permission conditions remain in their own routes.
