Scales 1-4, declaration status, registration, statutory remittance cycles,
selected STP duties and the scoped annual finalisation relief. They retain
Tier 2, pending_review and verified_by pending.

territorial and selected payroll rules. The relevant TAA provisions and

payroll sources after whitespace normalisation. The complete current
withholding instrument also matches the retained instrument, including all

section 2. Neither registration nor publication dates select the pay period.

for both enterprise predicates; it does not remove employee PAYG duties.
