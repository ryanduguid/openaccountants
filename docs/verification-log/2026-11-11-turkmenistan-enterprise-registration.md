# Turkmenistan enterprise registration and joint-stock reporting

The [2025 Civil Code commencement law, arts 2 and 4](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-code/6?lang=tm), enacted as No. 162-VII on 28 June 2025, brought the Code into force on 1 January 2026 and sets transitional rules for earlier relationships. Code arts 49–53 leave formation of commercial legal entities to legislation and tie legal creation to state registration. Article 53 provides the charter rule where a sole founder is permitted. Article 56 defines branches and representative offices as units of their parent without separate legal personality.

The [Law on Enterprises, arts 24 and 29](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-law/301?lang=tm) lists seven forms and calls the economic company *hojalyk jemgyýeti*. The economic company requires at least two participants and can use contribution-limited liability under art 29(6). This replaces the guide's generic LLC description and unverified director and single-member claims. The 25, 100 and 200 capital multiples and the distinct enterprise and joint-stock payment schedules carry forward under [Law on Enterprises arts 26, 29 and 47](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-law/301?lang=tm) and [Joint-Stock Companies Law art 12](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-law/308?lang=ru).

The [2021 amending law, parts I–II](https://turkmenistan.gov.tm/index.php/tk/habar/53123/turkmenistanyn-kanuny-karhanalar-hakynda-turkmenistanyn-kanunyna-uytgetme-we-gosmaca-girizmek-hakynda) changed the enterprise registration decision period to 3 working days from receipt of the required documents, effective on official publication. It also authorised electronic document handling under a Ministry procedure. The November 2026 snapshot matches both Parliament language versions of [Law on Enterprises arts 15–17](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-law/301?lang=ru). The guide also covers the statutory submission list, appeal and re-registration in arts 12, 17–18 and 20–23.

The [Joint-Stock Companies Law, arts 56 and 58–60](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-law/308?lang=ru) supports the auditor appointment, financial statements, board approval, publication and recordkeeping duties. The [Law on Foreign Investments, arts 8–10, 12 and 21](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-law/265?lang=ru) supports qualified capital choice and investment remittance, with registration, customs and tax conditions. The stated duties and rights reflect the consolidations available in November 2026.

The operative monetary base value, registration fee and operational forms, sector and reciprocity restrictions, reporting forms and filing dates, withholding and transfer practice, and qualified local review remain open.
