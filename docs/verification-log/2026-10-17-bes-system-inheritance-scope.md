# BES tax-system scope, local powers and inheritance connections

On 17 October 2026 I read the operative territorial and residence definitions,
the currency and temporary-continuation provisions, the FinBES local-tax
framework and the complete 2010 commencement decree/explanatory note for the
new [system reference](../../skills/international/caribbean-netherlands/bq-tax-system.md).
The guide separates national taxes from island charges, enabling law from
an adopted ordinance, and a property-tax addition from a percentage of
property value. Its illustrative 20% is explicitly fictional.

For the [inheritance/gift reference](../../skills/international/caribbean-netherlands/bq-inheritance-gifts.md),
I read Successiewet articles 1, 1a, 1b, 2 and 3, AWR article 2 and article
4(1)–(2), both current authority pages, and the 2025 government letter.
The temporary continuation is in Wet geldstelsel BES articles 13a–13b,
not the current or original Invoeringswet chapter II articles II–V, which
read `[vervallen]`. Saved pre-transition liabilities and legal relationships
remain distinct from the end of temporary taxation of new events in 2011.

The former succession tax's absence does not settle European Netherlands
residence or deemed residence. The guide distinguishes the ten-year branch
for Dutch nationals from the one-year gift branch for any former resident,
and does not create a one-year inheritance rule. Eight fictional scope
cases test these distinctions and the entity/property branches. They do not
compute a live estate, tax exemption, foreign credit or filing obligation.

The fresh AWR page selected the version from 1 October 2026, later than the
version in search results. The guide cites that current version and requires
the version applicable to the actual event. A requested historical
Invoeringswet locator using 31 December 2018 returned a 404 page; it is not
legal evidence. The valid 2016 version and original publication were then
read. Search snippets and that failed locator do not support a published rule.

The separate BES relief review supplies the BRN covered-tax list; no estate
relief is inferred from an income-tax instrument. Current source references
do not establish all island ordinances, customs/excise, special regimes,
estate valuations, tariffs/exemptions, historic liabilities or forms.
