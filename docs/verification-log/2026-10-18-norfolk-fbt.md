# Norfolk Island employer fringe benefits tax

I added `nf-fbt` for selected ordinary-employer rules in the FBT year ending
March 2027 and linked it from the Norfolk scope guide. The ATO's Norfolk
business guidance affirmatively identifies employer FBT obligations; an
island GST exclusion does not remove them. The reference separates employer
FBT, GST-credit entitlement, employee reporting and the annual return cycle.
It remains a Tier 2 source-cited draft pending professional review.

I read current Register records and selected provisions of the Fringe
Benefits Tax Assessment Act and imposition Act, together with the ATO's
Norfolk, calculation, reporting and current lodging guidance. The 47% rate,
Type 1/2 factors, strict reporting and minor-benefit thresholds, inclusive
next-year instalment trigger and records requirements have separate sources.
External expense reimbursement and third-party-payment branches are not
collapsed into one recipient-contribution calculation.

The July 2026 compilation contains changed work-item and otherwise-deductible
wording. The 2026 Tax Reform Act's Schedule 4 item 20 applies those changes
only to FBT years beginning on or after April 2027. I checked the previous
section 58X and retained its earlier-year conditions alongside the new-year
transition. The existing Australian FBT reference already records this
transition, so no repair to that guide was needed.

I also read the full 2022 electric-car amending Act. Its first-held-and-used
application date and later hybrid savings are not all visible in the current
section 8A. The new reference retains those application conditions without
selecting an actual vehicle, lease, recommitment or exemption. A nil employer
FBT amount does not establish that the employee has no reportable amount.
