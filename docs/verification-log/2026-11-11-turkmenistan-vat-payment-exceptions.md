# Turkmenistan VAT reporting and payment exceptions

The November 2026 Parliament [Tax Code, arts 104, 108 and 111](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-code/10?lang=tm) separates declaration and remittance dates. The ordinary return is due by the 20th and ordinary payment by the 25th of the month after the reporting period. Article 111(5) sets 1 September and 1 March payment dates for VAT-paying individual entrepreneurs and partnerships with no legal entity as a partner, with notices by 1 August and 1 February. Article 111(6) lets the Ministry set different deadlines for designated categories within the statutory limit.

Article 108(2) ties foreign supplier VAT withholding to a taxable Turkmen transaction, the supplier's tax registration and the payer's status as an agent. Article 111(2) separates the agent's information report from the foreign supplier's return. Article 111(4) gives the agent's payment deadline and separate 15-day rules for overseas and deferred payments. The Turkmen and Russian Parliament texts agree on the reviewed provisions.

[Article 105, third paragraph](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-code/10?lang=tm) applies the processed hydrocarbon export exception to direct exports by qualifying resident processors. The provision originally applied from 1 June to 31 December 2021. The [law of 30 November 2024, parts I(2)(3) and II](https://turkmenistan.gov.tm/ru/post/90313/zakon-turkmenistana-o-vnesenii-izmenenij-i-dopolneniya-v-nekotorye-zakonodatelnye-akty-turkmenistana-7) restored it from official publication. Article 105 in the November 2026 consolidation also supports the current fund-financed supplies and own agricultural produce categories, and sets limits on petroleum work supplies. The guide clarifies the inclusive-price calculation and gives examples of exempt supplies from article 106(1).

The general statutory rules are verified for the November 2026 snapshot. Operational forms, category-specific extensions, import and input VAT documentation and qualified local review remain outstanding.
