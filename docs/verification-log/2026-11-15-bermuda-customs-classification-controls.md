# Bermuda customs classification, value and relief controls

15 November 2026

I checked selected Bermuda customs rules and replaced unsupported general-rate assumptions in the customs, tax and overview guides. The customs guide now separates ordinary headings, personal and postal/courier routes, statutory value, allowances, conditional relief and traveller declarations. Unspecified electronics valued at BMD 5,000 remain **Needs Input** instead of producing the former BMD 1,112.50 estimate at 22.25%. These controls do not complete an import engagement or decide an actual classification, relief claim or filing.

## Primary sources and periods

The [Revenue Act 1898](https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Consolidated%20Law/1898/Revenue%20Act%201898), s 24 and Second Schedule, supplies the selected value rules. The [2025 amending Act](https://bermudalaws.bm/Document/%7B3d4d351a-3001-4ec0-b789-ac5f60bd4dc2%7D) and [2026 amending Act](https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Annual%20Law/Acts/2026/Customs%20Tariff%20Amendment%20Act%202026) establish the dated BELCO changes. The later-law supplement also retained the [Bermuda Laws main-Act consolidation](https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Consolidated%20Law/1970/Customs%20Tariff%20Act%201970); its last listed amendment is 2026:10. That listing is not proof that no other instrument applies.

## Selected controls

| Group | Correction and boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Classification and source period | Require the exact product, code, unit and import date. Camera 8525.890 is 10%, portable computer 8471.300 and television 8528.720 are 25% in the selected posted ordinary rows; broad goods labels do not establish them. |
| Statutory route | Apply Chapter 98 definitions and Special Rules 1-2, including the 0% and greater-than-25% exceptions and Collector control. Personal ownership/use and postal/licensed-courier choices are separate facts. |
| Value | Apply Revenue Act s 24 and the Second Schedule transaction conditions, required additions, Part 8(2) transport/handling/insurance exclusions, alternative sequence and import-date exchange evidence. An invoice total alone is insufficient. |
| Allowances | Preserve BMD 300 for a qualifying islander's other accompanied goods and BMD 50 for a visitor, once in aggregate with part-value relief. Alcohol/tobacco quantities require age 18 or over; excess goods retain their applicable units and rates. |
| Conditional relief | Keep beneficiary, goods, end-use, CPC, approval, timing and Collector conditions. CPC 4247 does not cover every musician, CPC 4248 excludes tools/vehicles and BELCO treatment does not apply to every fuel importer. Retail capital relief has its own Act and approval/claim rules. |
| Declaration | Apply BR109's operative 1 October 2026 rules and route distinctions, including the combined categories strictly above BMD 10,000. The old 4/16 form was accepted only through 31 October 2026. |
| Separate evidence gaps | Keep VAT/GST/general-sales-tax absence, actual importer facts, other import charges, unread/later instruments, authenticated filing and practitioner assurance unresolved. |

Chapter 98 note 1, First Schedule PDF p 447, contains the personal/accompanied/unaccompanied definitions. Special Rules 1-2 are on PDF p 2. The ordinary and Chapter 98 headings cannot be substituted merely to choose the lowest rate. The companion guides link to the detailed controls and no longer supply an unknown-category 22.25% fallback or a blanket category table. Unrelated corporate, payroll, social-insurance, pension, property and treaty text retains its existing scope and holds.

## Later-law and relief findings

The [Retail Shops (Temporary Customs Duty Relief for Capital Investments) Act 2008](https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Consolidated%20Law/2008/Retail%20Shops%20%28Temporary%20Customs%20Duty%20Relief%20for%20Capital%20Investments%29%20Act%202008), ss 2-10 and Schedule, requires a qualifying scheme, listed goods and Ministerial approval before its s 6 0% ad valorem branch. Heading 84.71 is listed; the heading alone does not establish entitlement. The [2026 amendment](https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Annual%20Law/Acts/2026/Retail%20Shops%20%28Temporary%20Customs%20Duty%20Relief%20For%20Capital%20Investments%29%20Amendment%20Act%202026) took effect retrospectively from 1 April 2026 and extends the s 7(2)(b)(i) import cutoff to **31 March 2031**. The s 7(2)(b)(ii) written claim is tied to one calendar month after that statutory date, or a later date allowed by the Minister, not one month after each actual import. A later claim date does not itself extend the import cutoff. The amendment does not restart the principal Act's defined 3-year statutory period. Actual inspection/disposal application, approval/cancellation conditions, claimant facts and Collector procedure remain held.

Named later notices retain their beneficiary, goods, period and CPC conditions. [BR131/2026](https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Annual%20Law/Statutory%20Instruments/2026/Customs%20Tariff%20%28Approved%20Businesses%20-%20CPC%204237%29%20Temporary%20%28No.%207%29%20Notice%202026) is operative 12 November 2026 and concerns its named centre's specified technology infrastructure/equipment through 31 March 2028; it is not general technology relief. Positive-rate CPCs cannot be treated as zero merely because a beneficiary is named. The announced January 2027 vehicle change remains an announcement in the retained evidence, not an established enacted duty rate.

[BR109/2026](https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Document/%7B68161f46-0415-4638-9f13-8aec2993708b%7D) has native rule and instruction text, but its form-image pages 5-6 and a current standalone 3/26 form were not verified. The official allowance page still linked the older 4/16 form. A Firecrawl basic scrape of a blocked public page returned 403; that failure is retained separately and supplies no substantive evidence. Wharfage and processing guidance lacks the charging-authority/base/exemption evidence needed for a complete import-cost total. Historical 2025 numerical tariff rows and exhaustive later valuation regulations remain open.

## Examples, checks and limits

The customs guide contains 17 conditional manual groups covering unknown goods, ordinary versus personal cameras, computers/televisions, the 0% and greater-than-25% exceptions, relief approval/cutoff, ownership/use, age and aggregate allowances, value adjustments, missing valuation methods, postal goods, vehicles/building materials, CPC restrictions, declarations, other charges and VAT inference. The overview retains its 11 earlier manual screening groups and adds 2 customs groups. These are source checks and hypothetical assumptions, not actual taxpayer or portal tests.

Four executable Decimal examples encode BMD 500 for an ordinary BMD 5,000 camera, BMD 1,175 for the accompanied personal-camera assumptions with a BMD 300 available allowance, BMD 1,250 for the specified ordinary computer and BMD 475 after the stated BMD 200 transport and BMD 50 insurance exclusions. Exact inputs and eligibility assumptions appear beside each block. The required-example registry adds only the customs guide and preserves all 96 existing entries.

Repository generation and documented gates check metadata, links, generated packages, examples, baselines and software behaviour; they do not establish legal completeness, actual classification, an agency decision or practitioner assurance. The guides remain Tier 2 and pending local review. The batch supplies no verified VAT absence, full-topic completion, new guide count or practitioner-review credit.
