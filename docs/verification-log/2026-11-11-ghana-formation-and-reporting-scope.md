# Ghana formation and reporting scope

11 November 2026. The Ghana company-formation guide is a Tier 2 source-cited draft pending qualified review. Selected formation and reporting components have been checked; neither complete topic is certified.

The original Companies Act, 2019 (Act 992), sections 4, 6 to 8, 13 to 15 and 21 support the selected company types, incorporation particulars and name requirements. Sections 171 and 182 require at least 2 directors for the specified incorporation case, including one ordinarily resident in Ghana, with a continuing resident-director requirement. Citizenship alone does not prove residence. Current private-company forms, fees, stamp duty, capital requirements and processing times remain unverified. The current ORC incorporation page describes a public-company procedure and cannot supply private-company fees.

Sections 126 to 129 distinguish accounting records, financial statements and dispatch from the annual-return trigger. The first dispatch is within 18 months, followed by at least annual dispatch at intervals no longer than 15 months, subject to the statutory exceptions and Registrar powers. Section 126 contains initial-year exceptions, a 36-day completion period after the specified reports are sent and director/secretary signatures. Section 298 addresses private-company attachments and alternatives. The original 2019 text is not a consolidated amendment check.

ORC's notice of 28 April 2026 extended the stated 30 April filing deadline to 30 June 2026 for companies with a 31 December financial year end. It also describes eligible small-company relief under Directive No. 5 of 22 January 2025, using an auditor's review report in place of a full audit. The former blanket audited-statements requirement is qualified. The original directive and operative classification/conditions remain missing; its indexed PDF returns 404. No threshold or eligibility conclusion is taken from search excerpts.

The original Ghana Investment Promotion Authority Act, 2026 (Act 1173), records assent and Gazette notification on 15 July 2026. Its selected section 31 text specifies US$500,000 cash equity and at least 75% skilled Ghanaian employees for a non-citizen trading enterprise. Capital exceptions do not expressly waive the employment condition. Section 35(3) applies section 31's minimum capital to a wholly Ghanaian-owned enterprise with a non-Ghanaian beneficial owner or director, without an express trading limitation. The provider's only-trading summary does not resolve that wording. Section 59's definition includes purchase/resale as a principal activity or a material component. Section 60 preserves specified registrations, applications, benefits, permits, L.I. 1547 and technology-transfer agreements; it does not establish indefinite continuation of every former capital threshold. Exact commencement and application to particular investments remain open.

Act 992 was read through native text. Act 1173 required OCR, with interleaved layout; original PDF pages 4, 18, 20, 21, 41, 42 and 43 were visually checked. Independent source and draft reviews found no remaining actionable issue in these selected changes. Four numerical boundary comparisons for the fictional cash-equity and employee example passed. These checks do not resolve later amendments or the operative commencement question.
