# New Zealand CAE and EDW eligibility correction

I corrected the CAE and EDW eligibility rows in the New Zealand payroll guide on 13 November 2026. An independent review of the [ordinary NSW, CAE and EDW entry](2026-11-13-new-zealand-ordinary-special-codes.md) found both rows narrower than the statutory definitions. Its rates, rounding, RSE first-month exception, ND treatment and 6 worked figures were confirmed and are unchanged. The guide moves to v0.9; it remains tier 2, verified_by pending and pending_review. No topic, inventory or qualified-review count advances.

[Income Tax Act 2007 section YA 1](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2007/97/en/latest/sections/DLM1520575/), version as at 4 September 2026, defines a casual agricultural employee as a casual agricultural worker, a shearer or a shearing shed hand. The day-to-day, 3-month limit belongs only to the casual agricultural worker definition. A shearer works other than in their own business and is not permanently employed on the shed's premises; a shed hand is likewise outside a business of their own and is not a shearer, wool classer or permanent employee there. The election day worker definition covers elections and polls under or subject to the Electoral Act 1993 or the Local Electoral Act 2001, and simultaneous ones in the same premises, paid by the controlling authority exclusively for work immediately before, on or immediately after voting days. Both review findings were confirmed.

The Act's page lists amendments not yet incorporated from 13 instruments. I searched the current text of the 12 linked ones for 'casual agricultural', 'election day worker', 'shearer' and 'shearing shed' and found none; each tax Act did contain YA 1 amendments, so the search reached the amending text. The State-Owned Enterprises (Meteorological Service of New Zealand Limited and Vehicle Testing New Zealand Limited) Amendment Act 1999 had no link and predates the 2007 Act; it was not read. This is a direct-text check of the selected definitions, not certification of the whole Act.

A fresh [IR335](https://www.ird.govt.nz/-/media/project/ir/home/documents/forms-and-guides/ir300---ir399/ir335/ir335.pdf) download has SHA256 b7514882917184bf9e2c266becd567273468111bbea908e69cfc25ab68766896, matching the retained September 2026 guide, so I reused its native extraction. PDF page 57 puts shearers and shed hands on CAE, keeps CAE for a shearer's ordinary farm work when shearing is not possible and for a farmer's part-time shearing for another farmer, and treats a normal farm employee's shearing wages as ordinary pay. PDF page 51 describes election-day workers as casual workers immediately before, on or after polling day. Neither page has an OCR flag.

The [IRD tax-code page](https://www.ird.govt.nz/income-tax/income-tax-for-individuals/tax-codes-and-tax-rates-for-individuals/what-tax-code-should-i-use) still shows 'Last updated: 30 Apr 2026'. Its CAE text adds that these workers earn no other income in that time, a condition YA 1 does not state; the guide reports both. Its EDW text covers general elections in the 2-week advance-voting period and election day and sends other election work to the IR330 code that fits; the guide keeps that guidance alongside the statutory test. The current [IR330](https://www.ird.govt.nz/-/media/project/ir/home/documents/forms-and-guides/ir300---ir399/ir330/ir330.pdf), dated August 2026, gives shorter descriptions in notes 7 and 8: CAE for casual seasonal day-to-day work for up to 3 months, including shearers and shed-hands, and EDW for the advance-voting period and election day only. It does not say the worker must have no other income or give the 2-week length; pdf-inspector 1.25.2 read all 4 pages natively. The guide does not cite it.

Actual declarations, worker classification and the controlling authority's payment arrangements remain required facts. The worked cases assume eligibility and are unchanged.
