# Gabon provisions enacted for 2026

11 November 2026. This batch replaces 3 secondary-source research-gap rows
with selected provisions from Loi n°041/2025. The signed closing text dates
the law to 29 December 2025. Journal Officiel n°96 Quater is dated
30 December 2025. Its webpage heading gives the latter date for the law.
Article 1 identifies the 2026 budget year and article 4 amends the CGI.
The guide sections describe the law as enacted for 2026, with later
amendments and implementation still open.
[Official Gazette](https://journal-officiel.ga/22265-041-2025-/)

## VAT and excise

Replacement article 221 retains the standard 18% rate, lists the 10% and
5% goods and services and retains zero-rated categories. The guide records
the 10% list and selected 5% foods, with separate checks for conditional
tourism, used-goods and fishing categories. Replacement article 210 retains
conditional medical and disease-control exemptions. The previous blanket
pharmaceuticals summary is replaced by a classification check against these
provisions.

The article 250 excise table supplies selected drink and vehicle rates,
including the exception for new passenger vehicles no greater than
1,500 cm³. Replacement article 223 supplies the electronic-invoice
deduction condition and the enacted 6-month customs-document exception.
The VAT examples recompute CFA 1,000,000 of cement at 10% and CFA 20,000
of bread at 5%. Historical 2025 rows retain their own period heading.

## Corporate tax, rent and invoices

Replacement article 23(6) supplies the 25% levy and 1-month remittance duty
for the gains covered by article 7(2). That cross-referenced scope remains
a separate transaction check. Replacement article 178 bis supplies the 10%
rental withholding, tenant categories, IS-landlord exception, quarterly
remittance in the following quarter's first 15 days and refund provision.
The example assumes a covered tenant and landlord outside the exception.

Replacement article 11 adds the electronic-invoice deduction condition
and an enacted 6-month customs-document exception. Article P 832 ter(1)
to (3) supplies the invoicing obligation and approval requirements, whilst
leaving implementation details to regulations. The batch makes no claim
that later extensions or operational requirements have been checked.

## Patente

Replacement articles 252 and 254 define the charge and exemptions, including
the first financial year of newly created companies. Replacement article
265 sets 0.1% of turnover excluding tax, with a CFA 150,000 minimum and
CFA 10,000,000 maximum, and points to article 62 for the prior-year base.
The full article 62 definition still needs reconciliation. Article 265(3)
requires self-declaration and full payment before 1 March of the tax year.

The examples cover an ordinary charge, the minimum and the maximum, each
assuming a taxable activity outside the exemptions. The 2025 fixed-duty row
is separately labelled. The preceding national-capital and reporting work
is preserved.
