Specification section 5.9 supplies the ordinary tailored PAYE whole-dollar base, separate loan rate/threshold instructions, certificate periods, expiry fallback and repayment exemption operation. All 3 saved pages were read; no search snippet was used as the final evidence for a claim.

Non-termination extra pay normally uses the 4-week window ending on the payment date. End-of-employment extra pay uses the last 2 paid regular periods before the termination-payment period, skipping unpaid periods and other extra pays. Frequency changes require annualising the 2 periods separately and averaging. Other extra pay paid together with termination extra pay shares its calculation, whilst each component keeps its own ACC character. Regular bonuses follow section 5.13's normal-pay or monthly-difference method instead of automatically using the annual/special bonus method.

The PDF contains demonstrable inconsistencies. I inspected their images and did not use the printed results as arithmetic oracles:

- Pages 47, 53 and 59 use 'greater than' wording at the first dollar of some bands, leaving that dollar unassigned. Both current IRD web tables expressly include $15,601, $53,501 and $78,101 in the next band. The guide uses those inclusive ranges.
- Pages 45, 51 and 57 print 22.58% for 24% less 1.75%. The stated subtraction is 22.25%; the guide uses that conditional rate only for a confirmed levy-exempt payment under a certificate that includes the levy.
- Page 48 prints $10,366.39 on a $30,000.56 bonus after shortening the tax and levy components. Page 47 expressly says retain those components until the final cent truncation. $9,900.1848 plus $466.2175 gives $10,366.40 after final truncation. The new worked check follows that sequence.
- Pages 54–55 label $179,136 as both the grossed amount and annualised-plus-threshold amount. The latter is $139,136; $17,505 of the bonus is liable for levy. $40,000 less $13,200 tax and $306.33 final levy gives $26,493.67 before other deductions, rather than the printed $22,188.67.
- Page 63's mixed $15,000 redundancy/$500 bonus example prints net $10,377.05. $15,500 less $5,115 income tax and $8.75 levy is $10,376.25. The guide labels the payment components and recalculates them.
