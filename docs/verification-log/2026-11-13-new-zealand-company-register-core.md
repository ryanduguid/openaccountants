## Sources and period

The selected Companies Act 1993 provisions are sections 10–15, 20, 22, 26–28, 151–152, 186, 192, 214 and Schedule 4, in the 1 July 2025 consolidation. The [live Act version warnings](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/1993/105/en/latest/sections/DLM320108/?active_tab=version) list Deposit Takers Act 2023, Employment Leave Act 2026 and Companies (Address Information) Amendment Act 2025. The first 2 were screened through the selected consequential-amendment text: no change to the adopted incorporation/annual-return provisions. This does not establish whole-Act currency.

The selected Companies Act 1993 Regulations 1994, regulations 4, 7A, 10/12 and Schedule 1 Form 12, are in the 1 July 2023 consolidation. Their [live version warning](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/secondary-legislation/pco-drafted/1994/118/en/latest/sections/DLM6462000/?active_tab=version) lists the 2026 address-information regulations. The [address Act](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2025/67/en/latest/) is not yet in force at the check date; section 2 gives commencement by order or 18 November 2026. The [2026 regulations](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/secondary-legislation/pco-drafted/2026/319/en/latest/) expressly commence on 18 November 2026. That is a required revalidation boundary. The guide does not implement or approve an alternative-address application.

Companies Office public guidance was checked for incorporation, consents, addresses, director conditions, ultimate holding companies, selected fees, the separate register return, first calendar-year exception, month changes and extensions. The [fee table](https://companies-register.companiesoffice.govt.nz/help-centre/managing-your-online-account/schedule-of-fees/) states effectiveness from 1 July 2022. ## Controls and limits

The guide preserves the name-reservation clock and separate application-consent clock, including acceptance of replacements within the original period. It requires actual director eligibility, a qualifying Australian directorship when used, compliant physical office/service arrangements, all required consents and the actual incorporation certificate. Citizenship, a PO Box or a declaration cannot replace those conditions.

The annual-return rule is a calendar-year obligation with no return required in the year of registration. The statutory signature and information-date rules, conditional extension and restrictions on changing month remain explicit. Regulation 4 and current Schedule 1 Form 12 establish the prescribed particulars. Form 12 has no reporting/audit/resolution questions, so the guide does not turn the wider Schedule 4 catalogue into extra Form 12 requirements. Regulation 7A establishes online filer/company email contacts. The guide preserves additional-sheet shareholder completeness and authorised certification. No separate Gazette/approval instrument or authenticated conditional portal flow was established; verify any different applicable requirements before answering them. Separate reporting/audit obligations remain open. No penalty amount or automatic-removal date is supplied.

Eleven synthetic control cases cover fees, separate clocks, missing consent, Australian directorship evidence, disqualification, addresses and transition, distinct filing statuses, the calendar-year exception/conditional extension prescribed-form versus separate reporting work shareholder completeness and multiple required consents for one shareholder’s separate allocations. Five executable blocks check 9 expected arithmetic values. Arithmetic does not verify legal classification, a live application or practitioner sign-off. The formation base's missing mandatory country slots remain visible; this is not a complete entity-comparison or reporting guide.
