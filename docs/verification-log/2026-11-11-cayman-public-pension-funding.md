# Cayman Islands public pension funding

I checked the November 2026 Gazette instruments and the pension statutes on
11 November 2026. The [payroll and contributions guide](../../skills/international/cayman-islands/ky-payroll-social.md)
now distinguishes public service, parliamentary and judicial funding.

| Component | Source and period checked |
| --- | --- |
| Public service | SL 49 of 2026 is deemed in force from 1 January 2025 and repeals the 2024 regulations from that date. I checked the Government's defined benefit rates and past service payment, the separate employer schedule, the 6% and 0.4% employer defined contribution rates and the 6% allocation. I checked employer remittance under reg 4 and member deductions under s 17, including the separate statutory authority route. [SL 49 of 2026, regs 1–5](https://gov.ky/w/public-service-pensions-contribution-rates-regulations-2026-lg55-s1) · [Public Service Pensions Act (2023 Revision), ss 3 and 17](https://www.pspb.ky/cdn/documents/pension-law/2026-04-06-07-33-13-Public-Service-Pensions-Act-2023-Revision.pdf) |
| Parliament | SL 50 of 2026 is deemed in force from 1 January 2025 and repeals the 2024 regulations from that date. I checked the defined benefit funding and the separate defined contribution rates and allocation. The monthly deadline in reg 4 refers to defined benefit funding under reg 2. Section 18 separately governs participant deductions and simultaneous payment of the Government contribution. The 2009 defined contribution regulations describe the employer matching account. The Administrator's posting of the reg 3(4) amounts against the separate accounts remains to be confirmed. [SL 50 of 2026, regs 1–5](https://gov.ky/w/parliamentary-pensions-contribution-rates-regulations-2026-lg55-s2) · [Parliamentary Pensions Act (2016 Revision), ss 2, 18 and 27](https://www.pspb.ky/upimages/pensionslaws/ParliamentaryPensio_1496332207.PDF) · [2009 Regulations, regs 6–8 and 13](https://www.pspb.ky/upimages/pensionslaws/ParliamentaryPensio_1496331833.pdf) |
| Judicial service | I checked the 2021 Revision listed on the legislation register. The Second Schedule requires a 10% deduction for participants' service after 1 September 1997, sets the Government's 20% defined contribution rate and ties the defined benefit rate to the approved actuarial valuation. The guide distinguishes the payment rule in para 12(4)–(5). The latest approved defined benefit rate and current remittance practice remain open. [Judges’ and Magistrates’ Emoluments and Allowances Order, Second Schedule, paras 1, 10, 12 and 46](https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/SUBORDINATE/2005/2005-0003/2005-0003_2021%20Revision.pdf) · [Legislation register](https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/legislation/index/alphabetical.html) |

The self-employed 10% rate and monthly contribution period remain verified
as recorded in the [earlier payroll review](2026-11-11-cayman-payroll-and-contributions.md).
The current DLP guidance repeats the rate. I checked the enrolment and payment
options for one listed plan. The statutory calculation of
earnings from business receipts and expenses remains open. The 2025-effective
public service and parliamentary schedules also leave the treatment of
earlier remittances to be confirmed. Social contributions coverage remains
partial and local accountant review is pending.
