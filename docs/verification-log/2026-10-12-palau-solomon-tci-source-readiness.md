# Palau, Solomon Islands and Turks and Caicos: source readiness

These checks establish document identity and selected statutory observations.
They leave current law completeness and taxpayer calculations open. The work
was performed on 12 October 2026 in Australia/Sydney, using retained primary
captures from 11 October UTC.

## Palau: codification has a stated boundary

The [Code Commission's National Code page](https://www.palaulaw.gov.pw/palau-national-code)
says its documents incorporate public laws through RPPL 12-8, signed on
21 July 2026. It also says temporary provisions, including appropriations,
are excluded from codification. The retained
[Title 40, Revenue and Taxation](https://www.palaulaw.gov.pw/view/recsEBwJfyb2r7jaF?source=documents)
has 284 PDF pages; [Title 41, Social Security](https://www.palaulaw.gov.pw/view/rec1fbmec2XPeZ8fZ?source=documents)
has 61. Their footers print `10/01/2026`. The publisher's coverage statement
and that literal date do not establish a complete operative law chain.

Selected complete provisions were mapped to the following topics. This was
not a semantic review of all 345 pages.

| Topic | Retained code provisions |
|---|---|
| Scope and wages | Title 40 sections 1002 and 1101 to 1104 |
| Goods and services tax | Title 40 sections 1211 to 1215 and 1221 to 1224 |
| Business profits and non-residents | Title 40 sections 1412 to 1413 and 1421 to 1423 |
| Filing and payment | Title 40 sections 1281 to 1283 and 1461 to 1465 |
| Licensing and records | Title 40 sections 1501, 1506 and 1601 |
| Social security | Title 41 sections 702 to 703 and 741 to 745 |
| Healthcare contributions | Title 41 sections 901 to 902, 918 and 924 to 925 |

Several provisions expressly require further sources. Section 1295 permits
temporary ministerial changes to the goods and services tax rate. Section
1464 leaves business profits tax instalments to regulations. Section 1501
refers to business regulation in Title 11 and foreign investment in
Title 28. Sections 2707 to 2708 make environmental fee implementation and the
departure tax transition depend on further instruments.

The original RPPL 11-11 and 11-34 provisions, commencement exceptions,
regulations and forms still need reconciliation with the code. Identified
budget and later-law records, including RPPL 12-3, 12-7 and 12-9, have not
been fully checked. A failed retrieval of 12-9 does not establish absence.
State laws, the separate civil service pension and the substantive excise
chapter also remain outside this review.

## Solomon Islands: incentive conditions differ by schedule

The [official investment page](https://www.investsolomons.gov.sb/investment-trade-framework/special-economic-zones-act-2025)
links the full 80-page *Special Economic Zones Act 2025*, No 1 of 2025.
Its certification records passage on 26 May and assent on 12 June 2025.
[Legal Notice 246 in Gazette 94](https://solomons.gov.sb/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Gaz-No.-94-Sup-No.-49-Tuesday-29th-July-2025.pdf)
appoints 1 August 2025 as commencement. The full native Act text was read in
a separate source exploration; material provisions and the two layout issues
below were then checked against retained text and page images.

Section 56 limits Part 7 to activities not carried out elsewhere in Solomon
Islands and requires a minimum USD 5 million capital investment for a
business owned by a foreign investor. Its definition excludes plant and
machinery already used in Solomon Islands. Section 57 assigns separate schedules to
infrastructure development, domestic production and exports. Section 101
keeps incentive administration under the relevant tax laws.

Physical page 77, printed page 69, confirms Schedule 2's detached native
labels: clause (b) is the initial corporate tax exemption for ten years, (c)
the rent and dividend withholding exemption for ten years, (d) the stamp duty
exemption for ten years, and (f) a separate instrument stamp duty exemption
without an express term in that clause. Do not merge these provisions or assign a
duration by analogy.

Physical page 79, printed page 71, confirms that the words about compliance
with applicable conditions and procedures belong to Schedule 4(a)'s export
credit guarantee scheme. Native extraction split that sentence into a table
and displaced some words. The condition is not a separate introductory
sentence applying to every listed incentive.

Further interpretation remains open: sections 46 and 49 restrict export
business permits, while Schedule 3 addresses domestic sales; Schedule 2's
stamp duty clauses overlap; Schedule 3(k) expressly gives foreign personnel
PAYE relief for ten years, while Schedule 4(k) has no express duration.
Applicable regulations, rules, designations, licence conditions, incentive
start dates and current general tax laws must be checked before a guide
calculates relief. The captured Act and commencement notice alone do not
complete that chain.

## Turks and Caicos: preserve the printed substitution discrepancy

The [6 February 2026 Gazette, volume 177, No 7](https://drive.google.com/uc?export=download&id=1ZHZjxR4iE7yJF3kO0mWGdbK-jMGdvmCH)
contains the *National Insurance (Contributions) (Amendment) Regulations
2026*, Legal Notice 3, on physical pages 18 to 21. Their complete native
text was read. Regulation 1 appoints 1 April 2026 as commencement; the
making text on page 20 prints 4 February 2026. The maker/signature area was
not checked against an image.

Regulation 3 repeals principal regulation 14, but the quoted replacement
is printed as `4`. The page 19 image confirms that discrepancy and the
printed self-employed contribution of 11% of weekly income elected under
regulation 15. Preserve the numbering as evidence; do not silently repair
it or extend this provision to employees and employers.

The [Attorney General's revised-law index](https://www.gov.tc/agc/2021-revised-laws)
and subsidiary-legislation indexes identify the principal National Insurance
Ordinance and intervening amendments. Four exact linked law endpoints
returned HTTP 404. The principal regulations, elected-income rules,
applicable ceilings and complete later amendment chain remain unresolved.
These failed links do not prove that the instruments are unavailable
elsewhere or that the captured amendment alone governs a current payment.
