# Cross-border coordination: source precedence and unresolved conditions

On 17 October 2026 I compared the cross-border workflow with the universal
contract and the current BES double-tax reference. The earlier cross-border
text instructed the agent to assume legal statuses, charges and obligations
when law was unknown, and to prefer a cross-border guide over country content
on coordination matters. Those instructions conflicted with the universal
source conditions and the actual-instrument approach in the BES reference.

The revised workflow uses the applicable primary authority for the actual
jurisdictions, taxes and period. It preserves expressly permitted factual
defaults, with their evidence and reviewer disclosure, while holding unknown
law and unsupported facts. It separates risk flags from established liability,
payroll from insurance and withholding from relief. It permits an empty risk
list where completed checks support it. Generic rates, day tests and pre-set
foreign-country answers no longer act as workflow law.

The ten coordination steps now map to universal intake, profile confirmation,
refusal, substantive work, ambiguity resolution, output preparation, checks
and delivery. All four universal outputs, applicable domain outputs/checks
and five map components remain identifiable. All seven hard-refusal rows were
retained byte-for-byte. The existing quoted catalogue is not an updated
sanctions roster; current applicability still requires sources.

The BES relief guide now explicitly declares the cross-border base so its
upload package carries the required coordination layer. Its substantive tax
rules and calculations are unchanged. The current BRN's article 1.4, already
read in the preceding source batch, is the concrete counterexample to imposing
a universal model-treaty residence cascade. This change does not reverify
every specialist reference or country's law.

Ten synthetic cases were defined before editing for paired manual instruction
review and package checks. The evidence distinguishes instruction/file checks
from model behaviour. No fresh model comparison, runtime-discovery test,
practitioner sign-off or live engagement was completed. This documentation
correction grants no new country-presence or comprehensive-completion credit.
