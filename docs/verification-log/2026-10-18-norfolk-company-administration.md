# Norfolk Island company registration, records and reporting

Added a Tier 2, pending-review company administration reference and linked
it from the scope guide. It supplies selected Corporations Act application,
registration, officer/ownership records, size tests, financial reporting,
audit, annual review and fee rules. It retains the universal and formation
workflow gates and open country slots.

Read selected provisions in current Corporations Act compilation 149,
including the express Norfolk territorial definition, new registration
and the Part 10.51 transferred-company branch. Read the reform Act's
commencement provision: the corporate change commenced on 2 August 2021,
not on assent. Actual director-ID application periods and the special
Norfolk transition remain subject to their operative instruments.

Read the current Regulation 1.0.02B prescribed size thresholds and its
2019-20 application provision in 10.30.01. The Act's lower fallback amounts
are not selected. Consolidation, standards, FTE, strict small-company
boundaries, two-of-three tests and reporting/audit exceptions remain
explicit. Sustainability reporting has separate phased conditions;
financial size alone does not select a 2026 reporting duty.

Read current financial record, year, member-delivery and ASIC-lodgement
provisions, and annual review/extract/solvency rules. The extract-response
clock runs from issue, while solvency has its own review-date clock.
Fee payment does not pass the directors' required resolution.

Read selected expanded ASIC INFO 30 company fee tables, marked from
1 July 2026 and reissued in July. Current fees and late-fee branches are
separate from private costs and actual liability. The collapsed annual
review page was read for its overview; it did not expose its detailed
accordion text in that capture. The operative Act and expanded fee tables
support the detailed rules. The old Norfolk reform page returned 404 and
its search snippet was not treated as a current source.

Forty-seven manual criteria were frozen before authoring. Before
publication, fictional numerical boundary checks, the four generators,
all required check components, scoped review and exact bundle/canonical
comparison must pass on the immutable candidate. No baselines, executable
repository code or dependencies change. No real ownership/identity data,
registration, payment, model engagement benchmark, practitioner sign-off
or comprehensive-country credit is claimed.
