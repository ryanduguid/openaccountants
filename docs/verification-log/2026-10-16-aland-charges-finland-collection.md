# Åland charges and Finland collection corrections

The Åland income reference now includes ordinary church membership and parish
rates, the insured-residence test and the personal media-fee comparison. The
related Finland guides correct the unknown-membership default, advance bands,
assessment-credit reconciliation, completion-based dates and penalty bases.
These changes remain Tier 2/pending_review and do not complete either country pack.

## Åland membership, coverage and media fee

[The source register](../../skills/international/aland/references.md) adds ten
primary captures as AX50–59 and extends the read scope of the retained health
Act and guidance. Act 1013/2012 supplies Lutheran prior-year-end membership and
the municipal taxable-income base. The Orthodox Act supplies its municipal
base; Vero's general guidance corroborates the membership date for both churches.
The existing annual decision supplies all sixteen geographical parish rows.
Special parishes, foreign membership, estates and relief or appeal cases remain held.

Two church-source issues remain explicit. The general guidance, updated
4 January 2022, gives both the preceding-year-end membership test and a
next-to-last-day exit-notification deadline. No exit deadline is prescribed by
this draft. Act 1013/2012 section 3 still cites former Church Law 1054/1993,
while current Church Law 652/2023 repeals that law and refers back to 1013/2012
for the tax rules. This pass does not certify every transition or remedy.

For ordinary insured residence, Health Insurance Act chapter 1 section 2
refers to Act 16/2019. Sections 4–5 require lawful presence, a valid permit
where required, an actual Finnish dwelling and home, and continuous predominant
presence. Kela determines status. Income-tax residence and a fiscal municipality
alone do not settle this test. The 2026 worker-route amendment does not replace
the ordinary residence test with an earnings threshold. Special and cross-border
coverage, benefit entitlements and contribution-base exceptions remain separate.

Regional Act 2019:103 and Vero's 2026 media guidance establish the personal age,
residence and income-measure tests. Compare combined state net earned and capital
income with confirmed YEL plus MYEL work income. The fixed fee applies only
when the higher measure exceeds EUR 14,000. Work-income and household credits
do not reduce it. The death-year media charge remains payable by the estate,
unlike the insured health contribution's death-year exemption.

The original ÅFS 2025:54 fixes EUR 128 for tax year 2026 but expressly commences
on 1 October 2026. Native positioned text and the rendered original agree on
that date. Vero guidance effective 1 January 2026 cites this decision. No official
correction or full earlier-2026 collection treatment was verified. Both dates
remain recorded; the guide neither silently changes the original year nor
infers an earlier-period exemption.

## Shared Finnish collection and penalty rules

The Finland corrections reuse retained national sources AX15 and AX38–49.
Decision VH/6503/00.01.00/2022 has an ordinary EUR 170 initial advance minimum
and 2/3/6/12 instalment bands. An individual in their first business year has no
automatic exemption from an advance decision. The prescribed collection months
do not establish the person's notice, a requested variation or processing time.

Assessment Procedure Act sections 34 and 50 distinguish imposed advances from
cash paid and later reductions. Decree 1010/2019 sets ordinary residual and
refund dates by assessment-completion month, with separate changed-assessment
rules. Interest has reduced-settlement and overdue branches, and only the
ordinary residual reduced interest receives the capped EUR 20 reduction.
Calendar-2026 rates are labelled separately from tax year 2025.

The natural-person EUR 50 late-filing fee is not EUR 50 plus a percentage.
Sections 32a–32b distinguish added-income percentages from alternative
increased-tax percentages. Non-imposition grounds precede the EUR 75 minimum.
The apparent wording conflicts in Vero's 20 May 2026 guidance are preserved.
An insufficient advance alone does not establish a return-disclosure penalty.

The old Finland prepayment example also misstated six EUR 1,000 payments plus
six EUR 583 payments as EUR 8,498. Their sum is EUR 9,498. The replacement uses
explicit hypothetical decision inputs and separates imposed, paid and unpaid
amounts instead of deriving an unsupported whole annual tax estimate.

## Pension, healthcare and small-advance clarification

The retained [Income Tax Act section 124(1), (4)](https://www.finlex.fi/sv/lagstiftning/1992/1535)
also establishes a 5.85% additional state charge on pension income after the
pension deduction above EUR 60,000 for 2026. The previous EUR 47,000 threshold
is kept separate. The Åland guide now states this component beside the
progressive scale and continues to hold a complete pension assessment.

[Health Insurance Act chapter 18 section 20(3)–(4)](https://www.finlex.fi/fi/lainsaadanto/2004/1224)
requires a qualifying income trigger and excludes section 14(2) income from
the additional healthcare charge. Its 0.39% component uses the positive
healthcare-base minus daily-base difference. The guide now states that
condition and tests positive, zero, negative and excluded-income branches.
The 1.49% combined rate cannot be applied indiscriminately to a mixed base.

Both collection guides also distinguish [Prepayment Act section 24](https://www.finlex.fi/sv/lagstiftning/1996/1118)'s
below-EUR-10 non-imposition condition from the ordinary EUR 170 initial
minimum and the amended-advance division rule. An existing small balance
does not establish the amount imposed by the amendment decision. Unresolved
decision attribution remains held; no existing debt is automatically cancelled.

## Remaining verification

A further six-document source pass did not establish annual money rounding.
Vero names a regular-assessment processing-rule description but its detailed
current rules were not obtained. Public calculator, API and Incomes Register
reporting material does not supply the missing annual algorithm.

The component positions use synthetic inputs and do not complete employee,
self-employed or mixed settlement ladders. Final credit allocation and rounding,
specialist chains, personal notices, bank-day calendars, live consumer/workbook
and portal behaviour and professional review remain open. The inherited Finland
2025 rates, deductions and income-tax examples were not certified by these
targeted corrections. No accountant sign-off or comprehensive-country credit
is assigned.
