# New Zealand exempt ESS share and trustee meanings

I added 2 statutory definitions to the exempt employee share scheme (ESS) conditions in the New Zealand payroll guide on 13 November 2026. An independent second reading of the [exempt ESS entry](2026-11-13-new-zealand-exempt-employee-share-schemes.md) found that the condition table omitted CW 26F, so a scheme offering shares outside that meaning, such as partly paid shares, would have passed it, and that the trust dividends row used 'trustee' without the CW 26G meaning. The guide moves to v1.1; it remains tier 2, verified_by pending and pending_review. No topic, inventory or qualified-review count advances.

[Income Tax Act 2007 section CW 26F](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2007/97/en/latest/sections/LMS32498/), version as at 4 September 2026, defines share for CW 26C as a fully paid ordinary share that ranks equally with, and has the same designation as, an existing ordinary voting share in the company. The new 'Meaning of share' row states that test and the one exclusion its words give directly, a partly paid share. It does not restate the rank and designation test in the negative: a company can have more than one class of ordinary voting share, and a share need match only one of them. It says nothing about options, which CW 26F does not mention. [Section CW 26G](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2007/97/en/latest/sections/LMS32500/), same version, defines trustee for CW 26C as a group of persons appointed to administer an exempt ESS of a company that employs an employee, and to hold shares under that scheme on trust for the employee during any CW 26C(7) restriction period; the trust dividends row now gives that meaning. I read both section pages on 13 November 2026: each shows 'Latest version as at 04 September 2026', and its section text matches the Part C capture used for the earlier entry. Their history notes record both sections as inserted on 29 March 2018 by section 35 of 2018 No 5.

Case 75 described the loan as repaid in equal monthly payments. CW 26C(4)(d) requires repayment by regular instalments of a month or less, not equal ones, so the case now states equal payments as its assumption. Its figures and check block are unchanged.

On 14 November 2026 the Act's [Versions page](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2007/97/en/latest/parts/DLM1512301/?active_tab=version) listed 13 instruments whose amendments the 4 September 2026 version does not yet incorporate. I searched the full text of each for CW 26F and CW 26G: none mentions either section. The only CW 26 change amongst them is section 27 of 2025 No 9, which amends CW 26C(2) and is covered by the earlier entry. This is a direct-text check, not a check of every definition the 2 sections rely on. The other rows, cases and sources are unchanged.
