# Christmas Island and Cocos: surviving-spouse Medicare surcharge

Both selected income companions advance to v0.8 for 2026-27. This component
supports a proved marriage existing on 1 July until death whilst married,
with no later or competing spouse, independently established ordinary annual
inputs and the survivor nonprescribed and statutorily uncovered all year.
It retains whole-year marriage rather than creating single days on death.

Selected MLA compilation 53 sections 3(2)/(3A)/8D, benefits surcharge Act
compilation 42 sections 5/7(3)/14-16 and PHI compilation 42 sections
22-30/22-40 support the timeline, combined annual family income, own-I
$28,011 screen and conditional rates. The actual deceased spouse annual
income is included without annualisation. The ATO day worksheet published
1 June 2026 applies to 2025-26; its condition of a spouse on 1 July with no replacement spouse
corroborates the timeline, but its historical monetary thresholds do not
supply 2026-27 values. The current expressly labelled ATO table does.

The taxpayer's own uncovered status satisfies the family cover condition
only after both statutory taxpayer cover deeming rules are excluded. A
physical lack of a policy plus nonprescribed status is insufficient. This
branch therefore avoids assuming the deceased's post-death status as a
cover-test dependant. Potential days before income screening and post-screen
C/B days are distinct; a failed strict income screen gives zero days.

Constant qualifying-child counts and a proved count never above 1 retain
the existing threshold basis. Relationship, residence, maintenance and
age/study qualification must remain proved after death; deceased parentage
alone establishes none of those facts. Sixteen fictional numeric instances
cover strict own/combined threshold equality, rate and 2/3-child boundaries
and separate C/B bases. They stipulate established input and status facts.

Other death, separation, later/competing spouse, changing 2+ child counts,
unknown cover/prescribed status, special adjustments, benefit valuation,
final assessment rounding and complete returns remain held. The historical
worksheet's Example 10 overlaps 15 December in two periods; those endpoints
are not imported. Universal/domain contracts and incomplete-country slots
remain. Tier 2/pending_review/verified_by pending remains. No operational,
professional or comprehensive-country acceptance is established.
