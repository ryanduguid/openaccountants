# Niue registration, licensing and ownership notices

I checked the [Niue source register](../../skills/international/niue/references.md) on 11 November 2026. The existing Schedule 2 annual reporting guide and its mirror matched the published revision before adding the link to the register. The existing reporting source period still applies to that guide.

- I read the domestic incorporation, overseas branch, transfer and overseas return provisions in the Companies Act 2006 as consolidated to 31 December 2019. I checked the current Companies Office form titles and their Act sections because the Companies Regulations' contents list differs from the numbering on the overseas forms themselves. The 2024 legislation tables support the selected law history through their cutoff. Current notices and any later amendments remain open. The current application quotes fees that differ from the 2019 regulations. The register separates the Tax Office's administrative figures from the historical regulation. The authority for the current charges and individual licence status need further verification. I recorded the ownership and control limbs in s 12(3). Any separate initial disclosure, current implementation and complete reporting coverage remain open.
