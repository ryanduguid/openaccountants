# Ghana overview and property source scope

11 November 2026. The Ghana tax-overview guide remains a Tier 2 source-cited draft pending qualified review. Selected tax-system and capital/property components were reconciled; no complete topic or consolidated current-law position is certified.

The overview now distinguishes the original Act 896 calendar year of assessment from a company's accounting basis period, qualifies the annual-return rule by section 125 and preserves the Ghanaian-PE income limb irrespective of source. The previous Ghana-source-only non-resident statement and accounting-year deadline were too broad. Selected PAYE, CIT and VAT provider positions are labelled with their periods and linked to the detailed guides. Original Act 1178 remains missing; the captured GRA PDF is an implementation announcement.

Original Act 896 sections 35 to 38 distinguish gains, tax cost, consideration and realisation beyond cash sales. Tax cost excludes consumption and excluded expenditure, directly deducted amounts and amounts already in another asset's cost. The selected text does not settle all transfer, rollover, valuation or later amendment conditions.

Act 1094 section 13(a) permits defined individual 25% elections for specified investment-asset gains net of the stated losses and for gifts outside business/employment. Neither is a universal rate for all gains or gifts. GRA's capital-gains page retains a 15% individual summary; its two gift pages disagree at 15%/25% and 25%/30%. These summaries do not establish the operative election after the reviewed amendment or resolve the missing later-law chain.

Act 1094 section 5 inserts section 39A's asset/liability realisation return within 30 days; it does not itself state a payment deadline. Sections 8, 9 and 13 distinguish section 115's resident-payer/source/exception route from section 116A's resident non-individual payer route. The specified added rate rows use consideration, with 3% for a resident realiser and 10% for a non-resident. Section 116A's separate category/rate interaction, exceptions, finality and credits remain open. The guide does not apply withholding to every disposal automatically.

GRA's rent page gives 8% residential and 15% commercial/non-residential rates and a receipt-based 30-day payment summary. Original section 119 conditions final rental withholding by recipient, property/location and activity, with business-related exclusions and a separate non-resident PE exception. The withholding agent's monthly statement/payment clock remains distinct. Full rent, gift and succession treatment remains unverified.

GRA's stamp-duty page gives generic ranges, whilst its implementation notice says Act 1109 increased rates from 1 January 2024. The revised instrument-specific schedule was not retrieved and no generic range is certified. Its property-platform notice describes implementation from 1 January 2023, without proving the current collector, local rate, valuation or exemptions for every property in 2026. Local resolutions and current administration evidence remain outstanding.

All 7 new primary-page captures returned 200. Twelve new original PDF pages were visually checked and 4 previously checked images were reused unchanged. Independent source and draft reviews identified 2 draft issues: cost exclusions needed absolute wording and 2 publication dates were absent from retained full captures. Both were corrected; implementation dates remain. Corrected-draft readback found no remaining actionable issue. All 3 fictional worked comparisons recompute, and crosslinks and the footer were checked. These reviews do not establish qualified local sign-off or complete current law.
