## Selected procedure and workflow

I separated annual ESN, Registry annual return and ES return, financial
start-year selection, notification routes, responsible submitters,
per-activity returns, zero-income reporting, submission status and
correction routes. The update adds 4 hypothetical manual filing-plan
controls and the corresponding intake, working-paper, reviewer and
checklist fields. The existing 5 classification controls and numerical
examples remain. No new control is an executed portal or taxpayer test;
generation, checks and publication results are recorded separately.

Guidance Appendix B.2 requires both declarations for the stated GP
non-duplication treatment: no different own relevant activity and relevant
income for that activity only from income received for the stated
partnerships. An additional qualifying partnership is not itself contrary
to the second condition. The manual GP case expressly uses own-account
income outside any such partnership. No blanket general-partnership
exemption or current implementation of both return controls is established.

## Holds and source distinctions

The June 2026 bulletin and advisory withdraw RP courtesy reminder emails
from ESN Year 2025 onward and move ES reminders to the portal banner.
Their CRS dates and temporary CRS closure do not establish ES deadlines.
A bounded search of 6 official pages plus native searches found no current
ESN 2025/2026 amendment calendar or public confirmation of both GP-return
controls. This is a bounded no-result, not proof that no notice or control
exists.

The ESN guide gives 31 December 2021 for amendment of ESN Year 2020,
whilst the November 2024 Practice Points give 31 December 2022. The
Practice Points' availability table covers ESN Years 2023/2024 and closing
dates in 2024/2025. These are not evidence of current CAP availability or
extensions. Section 7(2) delegates notification timing, form and manner.

The portal guide permits retrieval of a submitted ES return only before
the applicable deadline has passed. Practice Points II.19 discuss
post-deadline contact for TRO forms; that passage does not establish an
ES-return amendment route. I retained the latter as an evidence hold.
The zero-income form-section instructions do not override the guidance's
substantive no-income test treatment.

The partnership Companies Act filing limb and 20 June/30 June 2021
historical discrepancy remain held. No real taxpayer data, filing,
payment, credential use, authenticated portal trial or practitioner
engagement was assessed. Other country figures were not re-verified.
