# Senegal and Congo national SARL capital rules

11 November 2026. This batch replaces the national SARL capital research gaps
in the Senegal and Republic of Congo formation guides with their published
legislation. The selected national rules address the guides' 2025 period.
The instrument and Gazette dates below record the publication chain. Earlier
commencement and transition questions require separate evidence.

## Senegal

Loi n°2015-07 was promulgated on 9 April 2015 and published in Journal officiel
n°6844 of 18 April 2015, page 393. Article 1 leaves total SARL capital to the
statutes. Article 2 leaves nominal share amounts to the statutes. Article 3
repeals and replaces Loi n°2014-20. The Ministry of Justice copy contains the
Gazette header, adopted and promulgated text and signatories. It completes the
publication chain missing from the earlier parliamentary scan. The former
uniform-default and national-research-gap language is replaced by this source.
[Official Gazette copy](https://justice.sec.gouv.sn/wp-content/uploads/textes-reglements/OHADA/Loi-n-2015-07-09-du-avril-2015-reglementation-capital-SARL-JO.pdf)

The capital example sets 20 shares at CFA 1,000, giving CFA 20,000. Both amounts
are chosen in the statutes under articles 1 and 2. The example addresses the
capital rule alone and preserves separate subscription, pay-up and formation
obligations.

## Republic of Congo

Décret n°2017-41 of 28 March 2017 appears in Journal officiel n°14-2017 of
6 April 2017, printed pages 360 and 361, PDF pages 4 and 5.

- Article 2 permits SARL statutes by private deed or notarial act. Deposit of
  private-deed statutes in a notary's records is optional.
- Article 4 lets members set total capital freely in the statutes. Article 5
  requires equal shares with nominal value of at least CFA 5,000.
- Article 6 requires immediate deposit of paid-up funds with a bank or approved
  credit or microfinance institution, against receipt, in an account for the
  company in formation. Article 7 allows the founders or a notary of the
  registered-office district to record payment and deposit using the specified
  declarations. The guide's mandatory-notarisation and bank/notary-deposit
  shorthand is replaced by these national provisions.

[Official Gazette](https://www.sgg.cg/JO/2017/congo-jo-2017-14.pdf#page=4)

The example uses 20 equal shares at CFA 5,000, giving total capital of
CFA 100,000. It satisfies the stated nominal-value floor and the freely fixed
total-capital rule. Other formation and payment obligations remain separate.

## Scope and remaining evidence

The common OHADA rules on ordinary SA capital, public offerings, subscription,
pay-up and single-member formation remain source-cited. The preceding batch's
annual reporting section is preserved exactly. Both guide versions advance
because their update dates already record 11 November 2026.

Current registry methods, required documents, fees, sector rules, other entity
forms, later national amendments and qualified local review remain open. The
guides stay Tier 2 and pending review. This strengthens existing partial
formation components and earns no complete-topic credit.
