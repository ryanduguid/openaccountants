# Bahamas National Insurance rates, ceilings and reporting

The ordinary rates, insurable wage ceilings, filing basis and arrears provisions below were checked through 10 November 2026. This entry covers `bs-payroll-social`, the National Insurance sections of `bs-income-tax` and the remittance summary in `bs-tax-overview`.

| Component | Basis and period |
| --- | --- |
| Ordinary contributions | S.I. 37 took effect on 1 July 2024. Its Fifth Schedule and amendments to regs 23, 30 and 35 give the ordinary employee and employer shares of 4.65% and 6.65%, 10.3% for ordinary self-employed people under 65 without retirement benefit and 6.5% for eligible voluntary contributors. The NIB rates table confirms these figures. [S.I. 37 of 2024, regs 1, 4–7 and 9](https://laws.bahamas.gov.bs/cms/images/LEGISLATION/AMENDING/2024/2024-0037S/2024-0037S.pdf) · [NIB rates sheet](https://www.nib-bahamas.com/UserFiles/HTMLEditor/Contribution-Rates-and-Insurable-Ceilings-2024.pdf) |
| July 2026 change | The NIB gave BSD 830 weekly and BSD 3,597 monthly from 1 July, with the prior BSD 810 cap for the workweek beginning 29 June and the new weekly cap from the workweek beginning 6 July. The authority kept the rates unchanged. Regs 2 and 52 supply the contribution week and biennial cap rule. [NIB July 2026 notice](https://www.nib-bahamas.com/uploads/2026_biennial_adjustments_f9ad1d917d.pdf) · [Contributions Regulations, regs 2 and 52](https://laws.bahamas.gov.bs/cms/images/LEGISLATION/SUBORDINATE/1984/1984-0068/1984-0068_2.pdf) |
| Wage and income basis | Regs 2 and 23 distinguish basic wages and other wage cases. Reg 43 counts basic wages before qualifying gratuities. For continuing self-employment, reg 30 uses prior-year qualifying profit under reg 2 and the First Schedule. First-year transitions and concurrent employment have separate rules. [Contributions Regulations, regs 2, 23, 30, 42–43 and First Schedule](https://laws.bahamas.gov.bs/cms/images/LEGISLATION/SUBORDINATE/1984/1984-0068/1984-0068_2.pdf) |
| Registration and remittance | NIB publishes a 10-working-day registration window, Form R1 for employers and an Employer Self Service filing and payment route. The C10(A) instructions count actual contribution weeks for weekly payroll and use insurable monthly wages for monthly payroll. The August 2015 form instructions contain older rates and ceilings. Their statement instructions are used with the later rates and ceilings. Reg 26 governs employer payment and reporting. Reg 32 governs self-employed payment. [NIB employer guidance](https://www.nib-bahamas.com/_m1758/Employers) · [NIB portals](https://www.nib-bahamas.com/portals) · [C10(A) instructions](https://www.nib-bahamas.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/C10-A-INSTRUCTIONS-FOR-COMPLETING-C10-A-FORM-Revised-August-2015.pdf) · [Contributions Regulations, regs 26 and 32](https://laws.bahamas.gov.bs/cms/images/LEGISLATION/SUBORDINATE/1984/1984-0068/1984-0068_2.pdf) |
| Arrears | Section 55(1) applies annual compounding at the prime rate subject to regulations and other prescribed rates and dates. Act 27 added the ministerial interest waiver, employer recovery interest and garnishee mechanism from 1 July 2024. An actual arrears calculation requires the applicable prescription and the Board's balance and dates. [National Insurance Act, ss 2 and 54–55](https://laws.bahamas.gov.bs/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/1972/1972-0021/1972-0021.pdf) · [Act 27 of 2024, ss 1 and 5–7](https://laws.bahamas.gov.bs/cms/images/LEGISLATION/AMENDING/2024/2024-0027A/2024-0027A.pdf) |
| Labour standards | The Industrial Tribunal applied the BSD 260 weekly minimum to a private employer from 1 January 2023. The general work hours and overtime provisions are in Employment Act ss 8–10. Verify the applicable wage order, pay basis, class and any industry variation for individual cases. [Industrial Tribunal, NR/NES/041, paras 124–125](https://www.industrialtribunal.bs/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/NR24-041-DEC.pdf) · [Employment Act, ss 8–10](https://laws.bahamas.gov.bs/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/2001/2001-0027/2001-0027_2.pdf) |

The 2025 examples use unrounded rate products for estimates and count actual contribution weeks for the C10 cases. Payment rounding, the complete later amendment chain for the special classes, current special-class amounts and live statement and payment results remain open. The published 2024 NIB table differs from the Gazette schedule and the older Seventh Schedule for some workers aged 65 or older and gratuities. The guides keep these cases separate and remain pending practitioner review.
