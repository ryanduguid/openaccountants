17 guides carrying the earlier [ethics and quality-tool reference](2026-11-12-ohada-ethics-quality-tools.md).

## Selected provisions

- Sections 200 to 280: acceptance, conflicts, second opinions, fees,
  promotion, gifts, custody and objectivity.
- Sections 300 to 350: pressures on employed professionals, preparation of
  information, competence, financial interests and inducements.
- Section 290: selected audit-independence boundaries, interests,
  relationships, employment, other services and fees. The lending passage
  preserves banks or financial institutions, guarantees, outgoing loans to
  any audit client and immateriality to all specified parties.
- Section 291: selected independence rules for assurance other than audits
  and limited reviews, including relationships, other services, fees and
  breach responses.
- Selected glossary entries: firms, networks, engagement and assurance teams,
  family, financial interests, related entities and engagement definitions.

The preparation maps contain 73 selected claims across these sections.
Their source reviews were reused for identical inputs. Integration changes
only 2 remaining-work passages to acknowledge the selected sections now
appearing below them and repairs a broken line in 'normal commercial terms'.
No exception, materiality condition or substantive source conclusion is
changed by those editorial adjustments.

The file's rotation periods, fee threshold, transition references and
