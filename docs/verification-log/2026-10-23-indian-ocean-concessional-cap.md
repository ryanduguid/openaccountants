# Christmas Island and Cocos: ordinary concessional-cap and excess worksheet

Both income companions advance to v1.0 for 2026-27. The new ordinary
worksheet takes the full statutory section 291-25 contribution amount Q
and applies the period-labelled $32,500 general cap. It does not substitute
gross deposits or RESC + DedSuper for Q. Defined-benefit, protected,
notional/transitional and Commissioner modifications remain held.

Section 291-20 permits eligible previously unapplied amounts from 2021-22
through 2025-26 only when the statutory prior total superannuation balance
is less than $500,000. The worksheet applies the oldest year first and only
the amount needed, records remainders before expiry and excludes 2020-21
and current/future amounts. Equality at $500,000 does not qualify.

A Q at or below the general cap establishes nil ECC without prior balance
or ledger evidence. A proved balance at or above $500,000 makes the ledger
immaterial. A proved nil eligible ledger also makes the prior balance
immaterial to this component. A bypassed unknown remains unknown. Unknown
required classifications, balance, entitlement, prior use, year or special
adjustments remain Needs Input.

The current ATO page explicitly gives $32,500 from 1 July 2026. Current
ITAA97 sections 960-265, 960-280 and 960-285 and the release-specific ABS
Original full-time adult ordinary-time earnings series A85002148L
corroborate the index: November 2025 $2,051.10 divided by November 2016
$1,533.40 rounds to 1.338; $25,000 multiplied by 1.338 is $33,450, rounded
down to the $2,500 multiple $32,500. Retained workbooks and their pre-release
internal timestamps corroborate those inputs, without proving immutable
publication history. The cap relies on the current authoritative ATO label.
Adjacent quarterly SG payment wording is not adopted as a 2026-27 rule.

Thirteen fictional cap cases cover equality, a $0.01 excess, partial use,
exact exhaustion, insufficient carry-forward, balance equality, proved nil
ledgers, expired amounts and last-year amounts. Two further cases reconcile
ECC through RSC and C into I using independently established RESC and actual
DedSuper. Seven held-input rows preserve remaining prerequisites and final
outputs. Actual deduction entitlement, special-case ECC, offset ordering,
release instructions and final assessments remain outside this worksheet.

Shared workflow outputs and checks are unchanged. Both guides remain
Tier 2, pending_review and verified_by pending. These components do not
establish operational model performance, professional sign-off, complete
income-tax returns or comprehensive country acceptance.
