# Anguilla income tax scope and Social Security guidance

I checked the [Anguilla source register](../../skills/international/anguilla/references.md) on 10 November 2026. The register distinguishes the ordinary income tax system from sector charges, payroll, Social Security and the separate tax residence programme.

- The government's 2021 white paper describes the absence of general personal and business income taxes. Select Anguilla confirms the ordinary scope and describes its separate tax residence programme. The 2026 recurrent revenue estimates show sector charges in the corporate revenue classification. The estimates support screening for these charges. Their operative charging provisions and entity scope need further verification. [Government white paper, pp 6–7](https://www.gov.ai/document/GST%20White%20Paper%20for%20Anguilla-Revised_v2_09.03.2021%20%28002%29.pdf) · [2026 estimates](https://www.gov.ai/document/2026-03-18-011937_1898942126.pdf) · [Select Anguilla, Business & Tax](https://www.selectanguilla.com/business-tax/)
- I read ss 4–5 and 11 of the Economic Residence Act and the regulations' Schedule in the revision as at 31 December 2022. The register distinguishes ordinary income tax from the programme's annual lump-sum tax, property conditions, annual declarations and certificate renewal. The online law is a dated revision. The 2024 amendment and subsequent law and programme administration remain open. [Economic Residence Act](https://www.gov.ai/Legislation/Acts/A064-Anguilla%20Economic%20Residence%20Act.pdf) · [Economic Residence Regulations](https://laws.gov.ai/storage/pdfs/2022%20AXA%20Revised%20Statutes%20and%20Regulations/PDF%20%28Regulations%29/A-R.R.A.s/A064-Anguilla%20Economic%20Residence%20Regulations.pdf) · [Select Anguilla, Tax Residency](https://www.selectanguilla.com/tax-residency/)
- The Social Security Board's 2026 guidance confirms 5.75% for each of the employee and employer and identifies the CR-1 contribution certificate. I recorded those components separately from the Universal Social Levy. The operative contribution regulations, current ceilings and special classes remain open. [Social Security Board rate notice](https://www.ssbai.com/post.php?post_id=107) · [Employer contribution guidance](https://ssbai.com/contributions.php?type=employer) · [Board forms](https://www.ssbai.com/resources.php?type=forms)

I checked the retained source hashes and guide bodies against the published GST and USL components. The 2025 GST and 31 December 2022 USL source periods still apply to those references. Current amendment chains, full calculations and practitioner review remain open.
