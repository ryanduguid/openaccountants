# Norfolk Island local and federal review routes

Expanded the existing property transfer reference after current federal
tribunal regulations identified a separate Norfolk review route. The local
Administrative Review Tribunal Act 1996 remains in force; the federal
Administrative Review Tribunal Act 2024 does not by its name abolish it.

Read the current publisher records and selected operative provisions of
both Acts, federal Regulations 27–28 and Rules 5–6. The federal list covers
Business Transactions Administration Act decisions, selected Levy
Imposition and Land Titles decisions, Land Fees section 5 and Land
Valuation section 79(1). Land Fees section 8 calculation review is a
different provision. The reference now preserves that distinction and the
separate general federal timing, reasons, extension and stay rules.

The existing local objection/appeal text remains identified as its own
statutory route. Actual forum, interaction of routes, applicable period,
standing, fees and complete procedure require the particular decision and
current law; no blanket 28-day or 30-day instruction is supplied. No actual
application or deadline is selected.

Twelve criteria were frozen before authoring. The change advances source
version 0.1 to 0.2 and retains Tier 2 pending professional review. Transfer
rates, valuation branches and numerical examples are unchanged. Before
publication, the four generators, all required check components, scoped
review and exact bundle/canonical comparison must pass on the immutable
candidate. No baseline, executable repository code, dependency, real case
record, filing or payment changes are included. Annual rates work remains
a separate unfinished batch.
