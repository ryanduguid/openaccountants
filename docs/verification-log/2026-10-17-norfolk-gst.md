# Norfolk Island GST: connection, registration and exports

Added a Tier 2, pending-review reference for selected ordinary Norfolk
GST transactions and linked it from the scope guide. The guide distinguishes
the indirect tax zone from income-tax territory, current/projected GST
turnover, registration, actual supplier export and deemed recipient export.
It does not certify a complete country pack or an actual BAS.

Read the cited parts of GST Act compilation 96 (1 January 2026), the exact
registration and low-value-invoice provisions in regulations compilation
5 (1 November 2025), and the ATO Norfolk mainland-transactions page.
Live Register records identified both compilations as latest on 17 October.
The 75,000 and 150,000 thresholds come from the regulations; the Act's
lower fallback figures are not the operative amounts. The meet-or-exceed
boundary resolves the older page's inconsistent use of “over” and “or more”.

The export section retains the earlier invoice/payment clock, qualifying
instalment conditions, export evidence, recipient registration restriction,
external-Territory declaration and distinct reimportation rules. It does
not infer export from the buyer's address. GSTR 2002/6 HTML was acquired
and read in selected scope as a lead, but its authorised consolidated PDF
was not verified, so no administrative extension is selected from it.

Native HTML text extraction omitted formula images. Their DOM alternative
text recovered the value/price, partly creditable acquisition and net-amount
formulas without an OCR or image fallback. Read the relevant statutory
context and retained the native extraction and alternative text separately.
Four-year registration backdating and input-credit expiry retain their
different starting events and exceptions.

Thirty-two manual criteria were frozen before authoring. Fictional rate,
credit, low-value-invoice and threshold cases require independent arithmetic
and boundary checks. The guide preserves universal and VAT-domain source,
profile, refusal, output and country-slot requirements. Special schemes,
actual attribution/returns, disputed GST and further import/customs work
remain held rather than inferred from the ordinary cases.

All four generators, required repository check components, the dated
country-presence report, the assembled bundle and the scoped diff must
pass on the accepted immutable revision before publication. Existing
review queues remain separate from new findings. No baseline changes,
private records, identity submission, filing or payment are authorised by
this verification entry.
