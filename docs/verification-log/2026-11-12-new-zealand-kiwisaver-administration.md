The guide uses age 18 inclusive and under 65 for ordinary automatic enrolment, separately from eligible members' age-16 employer-contribution rule. General web phrases such as 'over 18' or '18 to 65' are not precise boundary definitions. The 28-day temporary-employment test concerns continuous employment; its exclusion ends on day 29 when the employment continues. Existing members and statutory exceptions require their own treatment.

Section 64(1)(ab) expressly retains a historical 4% default where section 60 first applied to this employer in respect of the employee before 1 April 2009, subject to the statutory alternatives and overriding elections/reductions. It is not a rule for all people who joined before that date. The guide requires historical employment/rate evidence and records the separate transitional/pre-2013 limbs. The general IRD/KS4 3.5% default summaries omit this surviving limb. An independent source check confirmed the distinction; actual employee applicability remains unproven without records.

Section 101L's requested temporary-reduction period is 92 days to 1 year. Section 101S separately permits revocation for an employer before 92 days if that employer agrees, resuming the applicable ordinary/default/elected rate. The specification's page 13 sample letter omits that exception; KS4 PDF page 18 and current [IRD guidance](https://www.ird.govt.nz/kiwisaver/kiwisaver-individuals/making-changes-to-my-kiwisaver/changing-my-kiwisaver-contribution-rate/temporary-rate-reduction) support earlier revocation by agreement. I did not adopt an absolute first-3-month ban.

The base table distinguishes KiwiSaver gross salary/wages from complying-fund gross base wages and preserves payment classification and trust-deed conditions. The employer minimum can be shared across qualifying funds. Section 101D's other-contribution/hybrid offsets remain a detailed actual-case check; the guide does not grant every other retirement contribution an automatic credit.

The dedicated [special-circumstances page](https://www.ird.govt.nz/kiwisaver/kiwisaver-employers/contributions-and-deductions/employees-with-special-circumstances), updated 1 October 2026, correctly calculates a voluntary unpaid-leave contribution: $1,500 × 3.5% = $52.50; whole-dollar $52 × 30% = $15.60 ESCT; net $36.90. The specification page 19 table switches to a $45 tax base and is inconsistent. Its earlier direct-provider/payment-code wording was not adopted; the current web page supplies the employment-information/IRD and return-from-leave options.

KS4 PDF page 15 rounds $91 × 17.5% to $15.93, whereas the explicit specification and dedicated ESCT instructions require cent truncation, giving $15.92 and $75.08 net. I retained the existing explicit algorithm. Old 3% or other historical-rate illustrations in the specification are not 2026 minimum-rate authority.

The [opt-out page](https://www.ird.govt.nz/kiwisaver/kiwisaver-employers/employees-who-want-to-opt-out-of-kiwisaver) distinguishes the timely days 14–56 process from IRD approval of late requests. Its separate ESCT amendment/request instruction is more specific than KS4's compressed refund wording. Employee deductions already forwarded are refunded by IRD, avoiding a duplicate employer refund. Employer contributions and ESCT are separate employer-side streams, subject to processing and any amounts owing. Mixed or partly forwarded balances require reconciliation, not a guessed payroll offset.
