# Norfolk Island tax penalties, disclosure and remission

I added nf-tax-penalties-remission and linked it from the Norfolk scope
guide. It covers selected Australian administrative late-filing and
statement penalties, default assessments, safe harbour, disclosure,
remission, notice requirements and distinct objection rights. It remains
Tier 2 pending professional review and does not complete the country pack.

I checked the current TAA record and October amendment targets, selected
complete operative provisions, current Crimes Act record, the 2026 penalty
unit publication and the complete remission remedial determination. The
published unit is A$364 from 1 July 2026. TAA section 286-80(5) values each
28-day period at its own start; applying one current amount to every older
period would be wrong. PS LA 2011/19 paragraph 16 explains counting through
the date before receipt. The examples test 28/29 days, the five-unit cap,
ordinary multipliers and their strict/inclusive monetary boundaries.

The legal withholder definitions use historical periods, group conditions
and effective Commissioner determinations. Neither a current payroll total
nor one income threshold proves the entire penalty classification. Unknown
required facts remain Needs Input. Administrative non-imposition practice
for selected nil/refund or isolated late returns is not a statutory
exemption. Agent engagement alone does not establish either safe harbour.

I checked the statutory penalty formula using its author-provided
accessibility text and the current practice statement. Percentage increases
and reductions use the common BPA, rather than sequential multiplication.
Worked components distinguish tax shortfall from omitted income, the
behaviour rates, exact disclosure and reasonably-arguable thresholds,
no-shortfall statements, prior history and remission. The nil-disclosure
component stipulates no increase; mixed disclosures and actual aggregation
retain their own full evidence requirements. Selected HTML ruling material
was contextual research only, not a claim to have read its authorised PDF.

Section 298-20 remission, the FTL-specific remedial power, Division 284
assessment objections and objections against remission decisions are
separate. Exactly two remaining penalty units does not meet the statutory
more-than-two condition. A notice's payment date, reasons, later increase,
underlying debt, interest and dispute position must be reconciled separately.
No request is treated as an automatic extension, remission or recovery stay.
