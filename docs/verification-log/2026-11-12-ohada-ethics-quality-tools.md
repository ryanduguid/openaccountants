## Sources, periods and provenance

ethics Code, practical-guide Tomes 1 and 2 and the quality manual
Parts 1 and 2. Together they contain 5,408,829 bytes. The
[7 March 2018 issuer notice](https://www.ohada.org/mise-en-ligne-des-outils-d-harmonisation-des-pratiques-des-professionnels-de-la-comptabilite-et-de-l-audit/)
reports CNC adoption of the named tools on 8 December 2017, without
identifying adopted file hashes. Issuer hosting does not prove that the
retrieved bytes are those adopted or remain applicable to a later period.
Upload-directory dates are not legal commencement dates.

The Code preamble states a May 2015 IESBA basis and April 2015 changes
applicable from April 2016. Its footer says ‘Version après atelier’. The
quality manual cover is dated January 2018. Final adopted versions,
OHADA commencement of these exact bytes and applicable later editions
remain unverified. The existing framework’s 1 January 2018 commencement
is not asserted as proof of commencement of these files.
