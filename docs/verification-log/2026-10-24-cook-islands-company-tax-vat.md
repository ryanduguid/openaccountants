# Cook Islands: ordinary company tax and VAT components

This batch adds selected ordinary company residence, source and basic-rate
working papers and domestic VAT components. It distinguishes all 3 company
residence branches, concurrent resident directors and the natural-person
home/day conditions. The basic company rate applies only to an independently
supported taxable-income input, before credits, provisional payments or a
final assessed balance.

Company-tax work loads corporate-income-tax-workflow-base and preserves all
26 intake fields, 12 refusal codes, 7 reviewer-brief sections and 18 corporate
self-checks. The country slot table identifies unsupported adjustments,
losses, credits, filing, anti-avoidance and overlay rules as held. A reviewed
taxable-income input or illustrative rate result cannot waive these controls.

The VAT component separates invoice and payments basis, ordinary time of
supply, inclusive/exclusive value, partial receipts/payments and documentary
and acquisition-purpose conditions. It preserves the written Collector
determination for a partially taxable car or pick-up, the later-invoice
deduction route and both sides of credit-note adjustments. Quarterly filing
needs its election, overdue-return check and Collector date; an excess ends
quarterly filing after the current quarter.

Forty-six selected current official responses were retained and read. Complete
original 2021 residence, 2013 income-tax and 2014 VAT amendments were retained,
with selected complete pages visually compared after unreliable OCR reading
order. Those selections do not establish a complete current-law chain. The
2014 tourism transition is historical; later VAT amendments and their
return-period changes must be read together with the current provisions.

The company payment calendar remains held: section 179 and Schedule 3 give
a different ordinary sequence from MFEM's published company calendar. A
filing extension or additional-tax grace period does not establish the missing
authority. Compulsory VAT registration also remains held until the applicable
section 12 threshold Order position is established. Negative metadata/index
searches do not prove that no instrument exists. The separate voluntary
amount is not described as having the same express substitution power.

The component check covers 23 fictional worked or held positions, 14 hold
rows and 6 computed missing-premise holds. It rejects 35 arithmetic, input
premise or corporate-contract mutations and reconciles all 46 raw current-source
hashes. This is bounded reference validation, not a complete tax engine or a
completed return.

Tier 2, pending_review and verified_by pending remain. Complete taxable-income
reconciliation, returns, country/domain slots, prescribed forms, supplier
catalogues, operational outputs and professional sign-off remain open.
Guide presence and passing component or repository checks do not establish
comprehensive Cook Islands or worldwide acceptance.
