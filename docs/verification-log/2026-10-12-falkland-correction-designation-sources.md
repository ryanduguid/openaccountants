## The 2024 amendment changes the source needed for defence employment

All five native pages of the [Taxes and Duties (Special Exemptions) (Amendment) Ordinance 2024 PDF](https://www.legislation.gov.fk/download/pdf/64f027c6-db18-493a-a7c3-9214d8be345c/31123030-05ad-4b65-a864-18547ee85fa3/fiord-2024-2_2024-06-12.pdf)
were read. Section 2 provides for commencement on publication in the Gazette;
the document labels publication as 8 May 2024. Section 5 replaces section 9A

The replacement section 9A(1) names the Commissioner acting on the advice
of the Standing Finance Committee. Subsections (2) to (7) contain eligibility,
exclusion, scope and duration conditions. Subsection (8) addresses employer
designation. Subsection (9) requires an annual return concerning qualifying
employees' relevant income, with information and lodgement time specified
by the Commissioner.

Subsections (10) and (11) require a public list showing whether the exemption
covers tax, retirement pension or both, its effective date and its duration.
Section 6 of the amending Ordinance separately preserves exemptions under
the named 2023 Order for the period stated in that Order. The substantive
2023 Order was not acquired in this follow-up.

The [principal legislative history](https://legislation.gov.fk/download/lh/4917fe43-d47f-4ec5-8f81-9abecdc262e3/fiord-1987-26.xml)
