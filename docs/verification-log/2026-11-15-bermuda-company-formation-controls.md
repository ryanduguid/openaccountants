# Bermuda company-formation and Registrar controls

I checked selected Companies Act 1981, Beneficial Ownership Act 2025 and Economic Substance Act 2018 provisions captured on 14 November 2026, with the actual 2025-26 commencement and amending instruments. This is a bounded company-formation correction. Capture does not establish the absence of later law or verify every historical period.

The inherited guide claimed no meaningful capital minimum, BMD/USD 1 shares, typical BMD 12,000 capital, a universal January annual fee, general BMA beneficial-owner pre-clearance and a few-business-day turnaround. The retained primary provisions do not establish those general shortcuts. The revised guide distinguishes Companies Act legal forms from local/exempted status, requires the actual local-business or exempted-business permission branch and keeps LLC, partnership, segregated-account, sectoral and exchange-control decisions open.

The [Companies Act, ss 6-7, 14, 62, 64, 91-92 and 130](https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Consolidated%20Law/1981/Companies%20Act%201981) supports the selected name-reservation/local-advertisement, memorandum, capital-subscription, Registrar-consent, physical-office and officer controls. The resident director, resident secretary and resident representative routes for an exempted company are alternatives; the general secretary requirement remains. The AGM dispensing election under s 71A preserves accrued defaults and a qualified member requisition right. This is not a complete accounts, audit or annual-return checklist.

I checked the [3 November 2025 BO commencement](https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Annual%20Law/Statutory%20Instruments/2025/Beneficial%20Ownership%20Act%202025%20Commencement%20Day%20Notice%202025), [30 December 2025 repeal of BO s 16](https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Annual%20Law/Acts/2025/Beneficial%20Ownership%20Amendment%20Act%202025) and [14 September 2026 amendment](https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Annual%20Law/Acts/2026/Beneficial%20Ownership%20Amendment%20Act%202026). Repeal of the former BO approval provision is distinct from the later Companies Act s 14 incorporation-consent requirement. The [BO Act, ss 2-11 and 15](https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Consolidated%20Law/2025/Beneficial%20Ownership%20Act%202025) separately covers scope/exemptions, ownership and other control tests, identity verification, the register and Registrar filing. Initial filing is tied to the statutory registration or other named event. Later register updates, change filings and exemption changes have distinct triggers and 30-day requirements; confirmation rules must also be satisfied. The official limited-companies page updated 4 September 2026 still describes some 14-day requirements, so the later primary amendment governs the selected events. Trust-law, regulations and exemption-order cases remain open.

The [Companies Act, s 121](https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Consolidated%20Law/1981/Companies%20Act%201981) requires the local-company declaration/payment within 1 month of memorandum filing and then by 31 March. [Section 131](https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Consolidated%20Law/1981/Companies%20Act%201981) instead uses filing/January for exempted companies. Applicable fees, annual tax, capital bases and separate corporate regulatory charges must be classified. The ordinary exempted shares-company BMD 12,000 authorised capital plus BMD 3,000 premium example gives BMD 15,000 assessable capital; it does not determine a fee. After-31-August reductions are limited to the named statutory charges. No monetary fee table is published: full fee instruments, amendments, special Schedule layout and actual business/measurement facts remain to be reconciled.

The [Economic Substance Act, ss 2-5A](https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Consolidated%20Law/2018/Economic%20Substance%20Act%202018) and [2026 amending Act, ss 4 and 14](https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Annual%20Law/Acts/2026/Economic%20Substance%20Amendment%20Act%202026) distinguish formation information from ongoing substance and non-resident duties. The Agency transfer took effect on 31 March 2026. Current Regulations, prescribed date/period, activity-specific conditions and operational filing remain unverified; the Corporate Income Tax Agency's name is not a tax-scope decision.

The draft adds 6 grouped hypothetical manual controls. They are source and arithmetic checks, not machine-executed legal tests, accepted filings or practitioner/consuming-model execution. All 7 official PDFs and 4 public pages were available directly with native text; no Firecrawl, OCR or paid route was needed. Unsupported prevalence, overall cost and turnaround claims are removed rather than estimated.
