# Selected OHADA accounting regimes, timetables and records

12 November 2026. I added the same bounded accounting reference to 17 existing
guides for Benin, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Chad,
Comoros, Côte d’Ivoire, DR Congo, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Guinea,
Guinea-Bissau, Mali, Niger, Republic of Congo, Senegal and Togo. All remain
Tier 2, pending qualified local review. The existing
[2014 annual-decision and deposit reference](2026-11-11-ohada-annual-reporting-and-benin-capital.md)
and [Cameroon, Côte d’Ivoire and Comoros addition](2026-11-12-ohada-reporting-cameroon-cote-divoire-comoros.md)
remain distinct from accounts preparation and national tax filing.

## Selected provisions checked

- Articles 1, 2 and 5 distinguish accounting duties, public-accounting
  entities and the sectors excluded from SYSCOHADA. A SYSCOHADA exception
  does not itself remove all AUDCIF duties.
- Articles 8, 11, 13, 26 and 28 distinguish normal statements, SMT and
  additional IFRS statements for listed or public-offering entities.
  Article 13’s annual turnover excluding tax must be strictly below
  CFA 60,000,000 for trading, CFA 40,000,000 for craft and similar activities
  or CFA 30,000,000 for services, or the legal-currency equivalent. These
  accounting thresholds do not determine a national tax regime.
- Articles 7 and 23 distinguish the ordinary calendar accounting year and
  drawing-up within 4 months from shareholder approval, an RCCM deposit
  or a national tax return. The ordinary 31 December 2025 example gives
  30 April 2026 for drawing up the statements.
- Articles 70 and 71 address management documents and communication to
  existing statutory auditors at least 45 days before the ordinary
  general meeting. They do not establish universal auditor appointment.
- Articles 16 to 24 address procedures, books, supporting documents and
  10-year retention. Article 24 does not state the retention period’s
  starting point, which remains to be established before destruction.

The guides cite the operative articles and PDF pages beside these claims.

## Integration and remaining work

I qualified blanket accounting-regime statements in the formation guides,
including those beside existing national filing claims. The latter claims
and their existing gaps are not reverified by this addition. Guide
descriptions identify the selected reporting scope, versions advance and
generated packages remain identical to their source guides. Catalogue
entries, published coverage figures and excluded guides are retained.

Later amendments, subsequent standard-setting decisions, the complete
SYSCOHADA annexes, recognition and measurement, consolidated and combined
accounts, sector and non-profit frameworks, legal-form audit eligibility,
local-currency equivalents, national submission procedures and fees and
qualified local review remain outstanding. The official non-profit register’s
metadata identifies a separate later framework, but its operative Act has
not been reviewed for these additions. These selected original provisions
do not establish complete reporting coverage or filing readiness.
