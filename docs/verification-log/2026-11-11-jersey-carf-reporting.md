# Jersey crypto-asset reporting from 1 January 2026

On 11 November 2026 I checked the enacted [Jersey CARF Regulations 2025, R&O.96/2025](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/ro_96_2025) and updated the [cryptocurrency tax guide](../../skills/international/jersey/je-crypto-tax.md). Regulation 18 establishes commencement on 1 January 2026. The [Revenue Jersey overview](https://www.gov.je/TaxesMoney/InternationalTaxAgreements/IGAs/pages/cryptoassetreporting.aspx) also gives a 2026 period and 30 June 2027 deadline, whilst retaining conditional implementation wording.

I checked the provider and user scope, the annual return and the 30 June deadline against regs 1, 2 and 5 and the Schedule. Regulation 3 provides the separate due diligence exception and notification where its partner-jurisdiction conditions are met. Regulation 4 starts the 5-year record period the day after the relevant calendar year ends. The guide keeps tax treatment, provider reporting, partner status and reportable status separate.
