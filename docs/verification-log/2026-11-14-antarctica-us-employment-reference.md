# Antarctica: US employee pay, sourcing and the foreign earned income exclusion

I added a Tier 2 reference on how US federal income tax treats a US citizen employee's pay for work in Antarctica. It rebuilds an unpublished 3 November draft, whose local copy was lost, on that draft's design, with every source re-read on 14 November 2026.

I read 26 USC 863(c)-(e), 911(a), (b) and (d) and 7701(a)(30) on the OLRC site, current through Public Law 119-142. I read 26 CFR 1.863-8 and 1.911-2 in the official edition revised as of 1 April 2025; govinfo had no 2026 edition of either section. Both matched today's eCFR text sentence for sentence. The IRS now publishes the 2026 Form 2555 instructions, which exclude 'the Antarctic region' from 'foreign country'. I read Articles IV, VI and VIII of the Antarctic Treaty from the Treaty Secretariat.

The guide states plainly that no Antarctic tax law is relied on, that Antarctic pay cannot be excluded as foreign earned income, that Antarctic days do not count towards the 330-day test and that section 863(d) generally makes a US person's Antarctic service income US source. It makes no sovereignty finding and does not cover any other state's law. Both fictional examples are executable checks. The later regulatory amendment chain, actual facts, other relief, filing and professional review remain open.
