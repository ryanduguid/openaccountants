## Controls and limits

Initial section 41(a) consideration depends on section 46A and actual constitution/contracts. Later issues keep authority, constitutional restrictions, pre-emption, interest groups, major transactions, supported non-cash value, director certificates, new-liability consent, share-register entry and separate notice clocks distinct. Regulation 4 and Form 7 supply the event notice particulars. Conversion/options and unanimous assent remain separate routes, without implying a blanket waiver.

Records distinguish years, completed accounting periods and the current period. Separate shareholder inspection duties prevent treating the 7-year communication category as an automatic destruction permission. Overseas accounting records require sufficient New Zealand accounts/returns and location notices; divided share registers have separate constitutional, location and copying conditions. Transfer refusal conditions are cumulative, and ordinary unlisted certificates follow a request rather than an automatic rule.

Shareholder decisions distinguish meeting votes actually cast from general written-resolution headcount plus eligible-vote tests and the specific 95% written mechanism. The guide does not classify reporting/audit opt-out eligibility. Annual-meeting exceptions require all their conditions or all required annual business, and written resolutions retain non-signer notices.

Source review corrected the poll right to cover any company resolution, removed an implied need for express constitutional permission for different classes, used section 50's 'a person' wording and corrected a GST guide reference. No authenticated filing flow, live transaction, actual company constitution, reporting classification or full buy-out procedure was assessed. No full formation or reporting topic credit follows from this selected guide.
