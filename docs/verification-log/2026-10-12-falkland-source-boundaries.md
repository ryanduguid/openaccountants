## The pension webpage and later regulations state different amounts

The captured [government pension webpage](https://www.gov.fk/pensions/retirement-pension-scheme/)
states a full weekly pension of £197.19 for 2026. The retained
[Retirement Pensions (Prescribed Rates) Regulations 1996 PDF, version dated 1 July 2026](https://legislation.gov.fk/download/pdf/9598b106-0064-4837-9d41-774a34fc0e4e/c5589ace-6b61-4730-9c11-f4ad37c12d68/fisl-1996-39_2026-07-01.pdf)
prints £202.51 in regulation 3(a). Images of physical pages 2 and 3 confirm
the amount and the annotation referring to Ordinance 4/2026 with effect from
1 July 2026. The portal states publication on 14 July 2026.

Regulation 5 separately prints £23.90 for an employee and £23.90 for an
employer, with £47.80 for a self-employed or retired person. Physical page 3
was image checked. These contribution entries must retain their distinct
labels; they are separate from regulation 3's pension benefit. The applicable
period, principal-law conditions, exemption orders and later amendments need
verification before using any amount for an engagement.

## The pension payment provision contains questionable wording in the PDF

The [Retirement Pensions Ordinance 1996 PDF, version dated 1 January 2026](https://legislation.gov.fk/download/pdf/53aa8cd0-53e8-4333-ab6a-7648adc17498/a2aa126a-867a-45bc-be37-59ce19aa66e2/fiord-1996-20_2026-01-01.pdf)
section 14(2), on physical page 17, repeats *employee* in the references to
the person entitled to recover, the person from whom recovery occurs and the
person who paid the contribution. It also prints *form* where the sentence
appears to require *from*. The image matches these features of the native
extraction. They cannot be dismissed as extraction errors or silently repaired
into an employer deduction rule.

Section 14(1) addresses initial employer payment on behalf of an employee,
while section 14(3) separately prohibits recovery of an employer's section 11
contribution. Those neighbouring provisions were read. The original enactment,
applicable amendments, any official correction and payment/collection
regulations remain required before resolving section 14(2)'s wording or
writing a payroll method.

## The 2026 employment guidance retains a 2020 exemption expiry

The [Taxation Office's Taxation at MPA for Employees PDF](https://www.gov.fk/taxation/wp-content/uploads/sites/25/2026/02/Taxation-at-MPA-for-Employees-2026-2.pdf)
is labelled tax year 2026 and has a 19 January 2026 footer. Physical page 2
states that the exemption order for current designated employers is effective
until 31 December 2020, subject to annual review and possible extension.
That page was image checked. The document also says its guidance is not
binding in law.

The retained guidance does not establish whether a later order extended the
exemption. A [later source review](2026-10-12-falkland-correction-designation-sources.md)
captures the 2024 change to the defence-employment procedure and a dated
designated-employer list. Period-specific grant scope, conditions and
eligibility remain required before selecting treatment for Mount Pleasant
Airport employment.

## Company formation requires the applied Act and local amendments

Section 2 of the [Companies and Private Partnership Ordinance 1922 PDF, version dated 1 July 2026](https://legislation.gov.fk/download/pdf/940d29d6-4de9-42da-a2c4-209781ab9969/b8250e53-0b46-4b89-9ae9-a351702cf851/fiord-1922-7_2026-07-01.pdf)
extends the UK Companies Act 1948, as at 24 July 1950, subject to the local
Ordinance and in so far as applicable. The physical page 6 image confirms
these qualifications and the annotation naming the Companies (Auditors)
Ordinance 2003, Companies Amendment Ordinance 2006 and Finance Ordinance 2015
section 7(2).

The underlying applied Act and those amendments were not captured in this
packet. The government's formation and annual-return webpages alone cannot
establish the complete formation and reporting requirements. A formation guide needs the
applied legislation, filing instruments and relevant later changes.
