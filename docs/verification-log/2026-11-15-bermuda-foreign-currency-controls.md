## What changed

FCPT is a separate charge on a resident's non-exempt purchase of foreign currency from a licensed bank. The selected rate is 1.25% from 1 April 2019, applied to Bermuda currency paid for that purchase. It is not a withholding rate on the underlying dividend, interest, royalty or other payment. I retained the separate withholding-absence and treaty-operation gaps.

The new controls separate purchaser, currency and bank predicates, exchange-control residence, the statutory BMD base, exact exemption conditions, bank collection/remittance and any actual written periodic-return authorisation. Incorporation, CIT residence, a USD peg, a foreign face amount, an exemption label or a general quarter-end webpage cannot replace those facts.

Criminal consequences remain separate from tax. The selected offence punishments are maxima unless a contrary intention appears under Interpretation Act ss 1 and 40. An illustration does not establish an offence, conviction, minimum or mandatory sentence, actual fine or tax discharge. Missing operative facts and permissions remain Needs Input.
