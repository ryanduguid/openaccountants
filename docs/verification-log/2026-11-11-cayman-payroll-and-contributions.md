# Cayman Islands payroll and mandatory contributions

I checked the 2026 rules on 11 November 2026 against the September review and
the current instruments. The [payroll and contributions guide](../../skills/international/cayman-islands/ky-payroll-social.md)
covers the 6 payroll components in the [coverage plan](../COUNTRY-COVERAGE-PLAN.md).
Social contributions coverage is partial.

| Payroll component | Evidence and period |
| --- | --- |
| Employer registration | The 2026 employer setup uses a registered pension plan or participation in a registered multi-employer plan, an approved health insurer and WORC where a permit is needed. Cayman has no wage income tax registration. [DLP pension guidance](https://gov.ky/web/dlp/frequently-asked-questions) · [Health Insurance Act, ss 4A, 5 and 11](https://hsa-cayman.imgix.net/images/HealthInsuranceAct_2021-Revision.pdf) · [Caymanian Protection Act, Part 7](https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/2018/2018-0033/2018-0033_2022%20Revision.pdf) · [Tax-system verification](2026-11-11-cayman-tax-system-scope.md) |
| Withholding | Cayman PAYE is inapplicable because there is no domestic wage income tax. The 2026 payroll deductions include the applicable employee pension amount, statutory health premium recoveries and other deductions allowed under Labour Act s 29. Section 67(3) prohibits recovery of sponsored worker permit fees and security. [Labour Act, s 29](https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/1987/1987-0030/1987-0030_2021%20Revision.pdf) · [Caymanian Protection Act, s 67](https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/2018/2018-0033/2018-0033_2022%20Revision.pdf) |
| Benefits | The current rules cover pension enrolment, rates and limits and the standard, high-risk and dependant health premium treatments. I checked the 2026 health amendments against the 2021 Act and 2017 Regulations. They concern medical indigence and leave the employer premium provisions in ss 7–8 and reg 6 intact. [National Pensions Act, ss 3, 25 and 47](https://www.procure.gov.ky/upimages/commonfiles/1751266504NationalPensionsAct2024Revision_1751266507.pdf) · [Act 2 of 2026](https://gov.ky/w/health-insurance-amendment-act-2026-lg19-s1) · [SL 9 of 2026](https://gov.ky/documents/35692/0/HealthInsurance%28Amendment%29Regulations%2C2026_MADE.pdf/5c98d324-76ec-8a30-ce27-8b64ca393838) |
| Reporting | For 2026, written pay statements, employer work accounts, service employers' gratuity reports and the insurer notice apply on their respective schedules. Pension member statements and arrears notifications belong to the administrator. [Labour Act, ss 32–38](https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/1987/1987-0030/1987-0030_2021%20Revision.pdf) · [National Pensions Act, ss 22 and 48](https://www.procure.gov.ky/upimages/commonfiles/1751266504NationalPensionsAct2024Revision_1751266507.pdf) · [Health Insurance Act, s 11](https://hsa-cayman.imgix.net/images/HealthInsuranceAct_2021-Revision.pdf) |
| Payment | The amended minimum wage applies from 1 January 2026. Pension remittance follows the month of service under s 48(2), irrespective of the pay period. Standard health premiums fall due on the first day of the month covered. The work permit schedule applies from 1 May 2026 subject to transitional provisions. The Gazette erratum corrects the fee regulations' number to SL 20 of 2026. [SL 60 of 2025](https://gov.ky/documents/43485/45951/Labour%28NationalMinimumBasicWage%29%28Amendment%29Order%2C2025_MADE.pdf/c898f82a-aefb-c72f-2e42-2ab50359665c) · [Health Insurance Regulations, reg 6](https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/SUBORDINATE/1997/1997-0011/1997-0011_2017%20Revision_g.pdf) · [Fee schedule](https://gov.ky/web/govky/w/caymanian-protection-fees-regulations-2026-lg23-s1) · [E2 of 2026](https://gov.ky/documents/43485/45951/Notice%2Bof%2BErratum%2Bin%2Brespect%2Bof%2Bthe%2BCaymanian%2BProtection%2B%28Fees%29%2BRegulations%2C%2B2026.pdf/04e318b8-931a-07bb-e2d6-2cd69915708f) |
| Reconciliation | The guide gives a monthly payroll control using the wage register, year-to-date pension earnings, plan receipts and statements and health invoices. Section 48 governs recovery and reporting of arrears. A PAYE year-end reconciliation is inapplicable because there is no Cayman wage income tax. [National Pensions Act, ss 22 and 47–48](https://www.procure.gov.ky/upimages/commonfiles/1751266504NationalPensionsAct2024Revision_1751266507.pdf) · [General Regulations, regs 15 and 29](https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/SUBORDINATE/1998/1998-0003/1998-0003_2018%20Revision.pdf) |

The CI$87,000 ordinary annual pension ceiling comes from s 3. DLP still uses the
9-month rule and the consolidation records the 6-month amendment as
uncommenced. The separate public service contribution regulations published
6 November 2026 apply from 1 January 2026. [DLP guidance](https://gov.ky/web/dlp/frequently-asked-questions) ·
[SL 50 of 2026](https://gov.ky/w/public-service-pensions-contribution-rates-regulations-2026-ex81-s1)

I verified the self-employed 10% rate, the annual cap and payments at least
monthly on earnings since the last payment. Section 3 refers to a prescribed
income calculation. Regulation 29(3) establishes the contribution period. I
did not locate a current primary rule for which business receipts and expenses
enter that base. The contribution cell remains partial pending that evidence
and reconciliation of special public pension regimes. Local accountant review
