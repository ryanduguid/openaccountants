# Vanuatu VAT, local company incorporation and reporting

I checked the Vanuatu VAT, formation and tax overview guides on 11 November 2026.

## VAT

Cap 247 sets the standard rate at 15%, requires registration above VUV 4 million under the past or forecast test and permits approved 3-month periods at or below the statutory VUV 8 million amount, subject to the exceptions and any ministerial order. Sections 16 and 20 join the ordinary return and remittance dates. For a period ending 30 November, the statutory date is 5 January. Both ordinary dates move to the next working day where necessary. Section 11 of the Tax Administration Act distinguishes individual filing extensions from extensions initiated by the Director for all taxpayers or a class. [VAT Act, ss 11–12, 15–17 and 20](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/images/Laws_Regulations/VAT/247_Value_Added_Tax_Consolidated_2020.pdf), [Tax Administration Act, s 11](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/images/TAA/Tax_Administration_Act_No_37_of_2018.pdf), [DCIR VAT guidance](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/taxes-and-licensing/taxes/value-added-tax-vat/introduction.html)

I applied the 22 July 2021 changes to the statutory residency and non-profit references. The amendment gives its separate 2020 stimulus-payment provision effect from 1 April 2020. The guide covers selected ordinary supply categories. It leaves amended export deduction conditions, the visiting-vessel rule and historic stimulus-return adjustments for separate review. The import section records the statutory components and Customs valuation route. A worked calculation requires confirmation of the treatment of freight and insurance already included in the customs value, the applicable tariff and the exemption schedules. [VAT Amendment Act, s 2 and Sch items 1–5 and 9–10](https://doft.gov.vu/images/2021/TaxPolicy/Tax/Value_Added_Tax_Amendment_Act_No_13_of_2021.pdf), [Tax Administration Amendment Act, Sch items 2–3](https://doft.gov.vu/images/2021/TaxPolicy/Tax/Tax_Administration_Amendment_Act_No_14_of_2021.pdf), [DCIR import charges](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/customs/customs-valuation/how-customs-duty-charges-apply-at-import.html)
