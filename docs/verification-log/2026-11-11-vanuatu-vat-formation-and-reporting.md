# Vanuatu VAT, local company incorporation and reporting

I checked the Vanuatu VAT, formation and tax overview guides on 11 November 2026.

## VAT

Cap 247 sets the standard rate at 15%, requires registration above VUV 4 million under the past or forecast test and permits approved 3-month periods at or below the statutory VUV 8 million amount, subject to the exceptions and any ministerial order. Sections 16 and 20 join the ordinary return and remittance dates. For a period ending 30 November, the statutory date is 5 January. Both ordinary dates move to the next working day where necessary. Section 11 of the Tax Administration Act distinguishes individual filing extensions from extensions initiated by the Director for all taxpayers or a class. [VAT Act, ss 11–12, 15–17 and 20](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/images/Laws_Regulations/VAT/247_Value_Added_Tax_Consolidated_2020.pdf), [Tax Administration Act, s 11](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/images/TAA/Tax_Administration_Act_No_37_of_2018.pdf), [DCIR VAT guidance](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/taxes-and-licensing/taxes/value-added-tax-vat/introduction.html)

I applied the 22 July 2021 changes to the statutory residency and non-profit references. The amendment gives its separate 2020 stimulus-payment provision effect from 1 April 2020. The guide covers selected ordinary supply categories. It leaves amended export deduction conditions, the visiting-vessel rule and historic stimulus-return adjustments for separate review. The import section records the statutory components and Customs valuation route. A worked calculation requires confirmation of the treatment of freight and insurance already included in the customs value, the applicable tariff and the exemption schedules. [VAT Amendment Act, s 2 and Sch items 1–5 and 9–10](https://doft.gov.vu/images/2021/TaxPolicy/Tax/Value_Added_Tax_Amendment_Act_No_13_of_2021.pdf), [Tax Administration Amendment Act, Sch items 2–3](https://doft.gov.vu/images/2021/TaxPolicy/Tax/Tax_Administration_Amendment_Act_No_14_of_2021.pdf), [DCIR import charges](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/customs/customs-valuation/how-customs-duty-charges-apply-at-import.html)

Cap 91 Schedule 2 clause 4 includes qualifying freight and insurance in the transaction value. DCIR's current no-excise clearance calculation includes them once. The December 2023 Act moved import concessions from the cited parts of Schedule 1 into a new Schedule 3. I kept the VAT consolidation, the concession amendment and the agency calculation distinct because the operative import VAT interpretation and complete concession crosswalk need more evidence. [Import Duties Act, s 2 and Sch 2 cl 4](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/images/legislations/Customs/Import_Duties/Import_DutiesConsolidationCAP_91.pdf), [Import Duties Amendment Act No. 24 of 2023, s 2 and Sch items 3–4](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/images/Customs_Tariff/2024/IMPORT%20DUTIES%20CONSOLIDATION%20AMENDMENT%20ACT%20NO.%2024%20OF%202023.pdf), [DCIR cargo clearance](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/index.php/customs/customs-revenue/customs-clearance)

## Local companies

I matched the local registration and annual return fees to their section 6 and section 119 entries in the VFSC schedule checked on 11 November 2026. The earlier capital-based range and processing estimate had no matched source for this route. The original regulation commenced on 31 August 2015 and prescribed VUV 30,000 for each online filing and VUV 35,000 offline. The current VFSC schedule lists VUV 35,000 for each in either channel. The later instrument supporting the online increase remains open. [Companies Regulations Order No. 114 of 2015, part 1 PDF p 9 and part 2 PDF p 45](https://www.vfsc.vu/regulations/), [VFSC fees](https://www.vfsc.vu/companies-fees-and-penalties/)

I checked the allocated-month return, calendar-year incorporation exception, accounting-records duty and the removal trigger introduced on 5 January 2018. I used DCIR's 2026 application guidance for the separate business licence process. Detailed licence fees and exemptions, overseas and international company reporting, statutory audit branches and ownership filings remain open. [Companies Act, ss 6–9, 119, 124–129 and 142](https://www.vfsc.vu/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Companies-Act-2012-Official-Gazetted-Version-1.pdf), [Companies Amendment Act, Sch items 2 and 16](https://www.vfsc.vu/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Companies-Amendment-Act-No.-32-of-2017-1.pdf), [DCIR 2026 business licensing](https://vanuatucustoms.gov.vu/taxes-and-licensing/licensing/business-licencing)

The guides remain pending local-accountant review. The catalogue and published coverage totals are unchanged.
