# Gabon corporate tax reconciliation against CGI 2025

11 November 2026. I checked the corporate guide's historical 2025 rules
against the DGI-hosted CGI 2025 compilation. Its header identifies updates
through Loi n°033/2024 of 30 December 2024. The selected provisions enacted
for 2026 remain separately labelled and unchanged.
[DGI, CGI 2025](https://dgi.ga/document/code-general-des-impots-2025/)

## Corrections and checks

The residence and territoriality rule is in article 7-I(1) to (3), whilst
article 5 identifies taxable entities. The guide now cites article 7 and
records its treaty reservation and separate permanent-establishment check.

Articles 8 and 9 support the net-profit summary and reinvestment relief.
The relief summary now includes the undertaking attached to the return,
the 3-year period from the financial year's close, reinvestment of both
the gain and original cost, the share-acquisition exclusion, portfolio
holding period and consequences of successful or failed reinvestment.

Articles 16 and 20 to 28 support the rates, rounding, filing, instalments,
credit and minimum tax. The article 26 summary now preserves the exclusion
for a mere name or management change with continued activity. The guide
distinguishes its separate 2-year loss-making relief and 3-year PME/PMI
derogation. The old undated comparison with a DGI webpage is removed.

Articles 103 to 111 support the qualified investment-income and interest
summaries. The paying establishment withholds before payment, rather than
simply on payment. Exemptions and recipient tax status require checks.
Article 206 supports the 25% withholding only for listed payments to covered
persons without a permanent professional installation in Gabon. The former
separate broad statement about all non-resident companies without a PE is
replaced by that statutory scope. The 15-day remittance window is retained.

The example compares CFA 300,000 ordinary IS, CFA 1,200,000 IMF and the
CFA 500,000 floor for expressly stated qualifying bases without exemptions
or other relief. The resulting liability is CFA 1,200,000 before credits.

## Patente reference

Replacement article 265 in Loi n°041/2025 cites article 62 for its base.
Both the captured Gazette HTML and government-hosted Gazette PDF print that
reference. In the DGI's 2025 compilation, article 62 concerns securities
income exemptions, whilst articles 24 and 25 concern the IS minimum tax.
The formation guide records the discrepancy. No inferred replacement
reference or complete patente base is published.
[Gazette, replacement article 265](https://journal-officiel.ga/22265-041-2025-/)

The focused searches found no operative correction or DGI clarification.
That search result does not prove that none exists. Later amendments,
implementation, transaction-specific exceptions, current practice and
qualified local review remain open. The guides stay Tier 2 and pending
review. This batch supports partial topic components, with no complete-topic
verification claim.
