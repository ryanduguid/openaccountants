## Controls and limits

Current-year income/loss offset precedes carryforward deduction. The ordinary 80% and combined 100% ceilings use the verified positive s 5(1)(a) amount, with prescribed exceptions and the shock-origin/election conditions preserved. Designations cannot exceed each BCE's available pool. The draft requires actual Filing BCE designations and introduces no mandatory ordinary-versus-shock priority. A form's nil result for excessive designations is a correction signal. The same allocated loss cannot enter both pools in full.

The draft separates a reduced current-year offset (s 5(2)), a reduced annual deduction retaining the undeducted amount (s 6(1)(b)) and permanent disregard of the specified amounts (s 6(6)). It prevents repeating a permanent opening reduction already reflected in the input balance. Shock origin, actual E40 and the workflow's sector permission are separate requirements.
