Schedule 3, PDF page 13, provides fees of AUD500 up to and including AUD25,000, AUD1,000 above AUD25,000 through AUD250,000 and AUD1,500 above AUD250,000. The original fee base is share capital or other capital. The Act's Registrar/Cabinet boundary instead uses total capital input; the guide keeps the tests separate. The ministry's standalone 2019 form, page 8, corroborates the fee table. The administrative start-up guide's AUD25,000 overlap is retained as a source defect but no longer treated as an ambiguity in the original 2019 schedule. Current fees remain held.

The guide distinguishes the Act's qualified processing provisions, certificate duration/cancellation, sector amendment and savings controls, review rights and transfers subject to Kiribati law. It gives conditional fee/approval boundary examples without treating an original fee as a current quote or transfer permission as a tax exemption.

Original Schedule 2 Form 2 calls for yearly financial statements within 60 days of the enterprise's financial year-end and 'bi-annual' progress reports, without an exact calendar here. These are certificate conditions to confirm against an issued certificate and any Registrar-modified form. They are distinguished from the Registrar's section 6(6) administration report. General company returns, accounting standards, audit/retention requirements and the employment condition's wording remain open.
