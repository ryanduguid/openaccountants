# Norfolk Island tax application and employer super

Added two Tier 2, pending-review references for NF, which previously had
no indexed guide. They establish Australian tax application with specific
territorial exceptions and selected 2026–27 employer SG rules. They do not
complete Norfolk's mandatory country topics or confer accountant review.

Read the complete 2015 reform and 2016 CGT exemption Acts and current
section 102-25. The 2016 Act replaces the original June 2016 rebasing
approach with the conditional October 2015 date substitution. The guides
preserve all subsection (2) conditions and distinguish the Cocos election
in subsection (4). They separate island GST scope, mainland connections,
ABN supplier rules and employee PAYG withholding.

Read the ATO Norfolk collection, including the current employer page, and
current Payday Super deadline guidance. Verified the SG Act's selected
definitions, sections 10A, 12, 17A–17B and 18A–18C against compilation 78
(1 July 2026). The live Register identified it as latest on 17 October.
Read the Payday Act's commencement and selected application/transitional
items, including the one-twelfth Norfolk adjustment in item 189 and the
end of the former quarterly transition in item 150. The Norfolk guide
uses qualifying earnings and payment day, separate fund receipt and the
annual base; it does not transplant old quarterly payment rules.

The ATO's summary employer matrix does not list every statutory residence
combination. The guide includes item 189(3)(b) explicitly and applies the
adjustment only to the established Norfolk subset. For the business-day
definition it retains the Act's precise State/ACT/NT wording rather than
turning the ATO's broader territory summary into a local-only calendar.
Actual calendar mapping, delegated exclusions and special due-date
instruments remain held. No actual financial method was selected for an
unknown taxpayer or live payroll.

The source PDFs were extracted with pdf-inspector; only blank imprint
pages required default OCR. Native table order in the ATO PDF was
insufficient for its rate/residence matrices, so the corresponding HTML
tables were read and retained as the authoritative alignment check.
No hosted OCR, private data, identifier, filing or payment was used.

Thirty-two manual criteria were frozen before authoring. Fictional
qualifying-earnings examples were independently recomputed, with annual
base, certificate, fund and allocation assumptions stated. Source hashes,
read scopes and the manual ledger are retained in the dated evidence
packet. The four generators, coverage refresh, bundle integrity, required
validation/checker/test components and scoped diff review must pass on the
accepted immutable revision before publication.

All broader Norfolk income/corporate tax, consumption/imports, payroll,
other social programmes, formation/reporting, cross-border, capital/local
charges, compliance and special-regime gaps remain open. This adds guide
presence only. The existing standing review queues remain separate from
new findings; no baseline entries are authorised by this note.
