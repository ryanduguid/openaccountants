# Selected OHADA statutory audit appointment rules

12 November 2026. I added the same bounded original-law audit reference to
17 existing guides for Benin, Burkina Faso, Cameroon, Central African Republic,
Chad, Comoros, Côte d’Ivoire, DR Congo, Equatorial Guinea, Gabon, Guinea,
Guinea-Bissau, Mali, Niger, Republic of Congo, Senegal and Togo. All remain
Tier 2 and pending qualified local review. The existing annual-decision/RCCM
and [AUDCIF accounting reference](2026-11-12-ohada-accounting-regimes-records.md)
remain separate from these auditor-appointment tests.

## Primary source and period

The [original revised AUSCGIE Gazette](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974)
was adopted on 30 January 2014, published on 4 February 2014 and took effect
on 5 May 2014 under article 920. The captured PDF has 240 pages and is
6,288,638 bytes, SHA-256
`143aab11d2fe189d30eb49f125e6457662d0d010f4a3acca48a6c0f695f8b613`.
The selected appointment rules are labelled as original 2014 provisions.
They do not establish a consolidated current-law position.

## Selected provisions checked

- SARL article 376 requires at least 2 strict year-end excesses: balance
  sheet total above CFA 125,000,000, annual turnover above CFA 250,000,000
  or permanent workforce above 50. Equality does not satisfy a condition.
  The original passage does not specify an excluding-tax turnover basis.
  Its cessation, voluntary appointment and court-request routes remain
  distinct. Article 379 supplies the selected SARL term and voting rules.
- SA articles 694 and 702 require an auditor and distinguish principal and
  substitute counts for public-offering and other SAs. Articles 703 to 709
  distinguish initial 2-year and later 6-year appointments, expiry,
  replacements and an omitted renewal or replacement.
- SAS article 853-13 adds control relationships to its size test, includes
  voluntary and court-request routes and addresses separate transaction
  certification. Article 174 supplies the selected control definition.
  No conclusion about an SAS appointment term is added.
- Articles 377, 378 and 694 to 700 supply the original professional-order,
  list and independence framework. These references do not verify an
  individual practitioner or a current national register.

The guides cite the articles and PDF pages beside each material claim.
The hypothetical 31 December 2025 SARL with CFA 126,000,000 balance sheet
total, CFA 250,000,000 turnover and 51 permanent staff meets 2 conditions,
not the turnover condition at equality. It illustrates the original test,
not actual 2025 applicability or a legal-currency conversion.

Native extraction of all 240 pages returned 607,136 characters but omitted
parts of articles 691, 696 and 699. Direct French-image checks on pages 45,
93, 94, 161 to 164 and 208 to 210 control the selected claims and were
independently reviewed. Pages 227 to 240 were not legally reviewed.
Extraction does not establish full review of the Act or detailed audit standards.

## Integration and remaining work

I replaced 4 approximate blanket audit lines in the Benin, Chad, Mali and
Republic of Congo guides with pointers to the selected provisions. The
separate Congo national tax-filing line and all other national claims were
retained and are not reverified by this addition. Guide descriptions identify
audit appointments, versions advance and generated packages match their sources.
Catalogue entries, published coverage figures and excluded guides are retained.

Later amendments, actual-period applicability, national professional and
sector rules, non-CFA legal-currency treatment, current practitioner
registration, detailed audit standards and procedures and qualified local
review remain outstanding. The official 2017 professional-practice Regulation
notice supplied metadata, but its attachment was unavailable in this pass;
no substantive rule from it is asserted. These selected original appointment
rules do not establish complete reporting coverage or filing readiness.
