# Christmas Island and Cocos employer super operations

I expanded both 2026-27 SG companions with selected employer procedures:
valid fund-choice implementation, further choice/form requests, ABN/WPN
and fund readiness, current choice/stapled channels, SuperStream, member
verification, rejected contributions and STP reporting/corrections.
Both companions remain tier 2 drafts pending review.

The source check uses the previously captured SG Act compilation 78 and
Regulations compilation 8, both effective 1 July 2026, with selected
additional choice provisions. Payday Super Act Schedule 1 item 178
supports the reporting scope change and labour-contract distinction.
The new operational evidence is 12 complete current ATO HTML captures.
NAT 13080 and NAT 75404 references use those instructions; their PDFs,
complete technical standards and every provider-specific field were not
audited. No portal, form, request, identity check or payment was submitted.

The current ATO pages retain some older instructions. The guides exclude
the SuperStream page's old quarterly wording and historical Small Business
Superannuation Clearing House concession for new QE days. Its general
SMSF/default shorthand does not override the current chosen/stapled/default
ordering. MVR support and the new-employee page's software condition remain
explicit. An unavailable feature, rejection or correction creates no
statutory payment extension.

A choice becomes effective under section 32F after 2 months or the earlier
time the employer determines, whilst SG deadlines continue. A further
choice within 12 months may be refused; it is not universally prohibited.
A failed stapled account requires a lawful alternative or ATO assistance,
not an automatic switch to an arbitrary default. Sensitive identifiers
remain in the employer or practitioner's controlled system; the workflow
uses readiness references only.

STP QE YTD and super liability YTD are distinct. The guides cover the
annual-base reporting option, verified certificate periods and additional
award/enterprise-agreement super on non-QE payments. The conditional OTE
reporting acceptance ends on 30 June 2027 or when QE reporting starts.
It does not defer QE-based SG payments. Corrections preserve actual pay
dates and contribution history; an update event does not create new cash
pay merely because it is lodged. The ATO's finalisation data exception is
stated separately.

Each guide adds 25 fictional operational cases to its 26 existing cases,
including cap/YTD crossings, separate agreement liability, labour
contractors, fund changes, rejection and correction boundaries. Local
component checks and repository tests do not establish an executed model,
full payroll or complete contribution engagement. Employer registration,
all-programme coverage, actual holiday calendars, complete SGC filing and
penalty machinery, deductions and fractional-cent rules still have gaps.
No comprehensive-country acceptance or accountant sign-off is added.

negative case with targeted notifying-party checks. Section 299C in
