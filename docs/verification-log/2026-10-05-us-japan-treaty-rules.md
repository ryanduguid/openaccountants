# US treaties: Japan rules

The Japan section of `us-major-partners` attached a six-month holding test to
its 5% corporate dividend category, described the parent exemption as new in
2013, and presented interest and services as unconditional zero-rate categories.
This source check corrects that section, its PE row and summary row for the
guide's 2025 tax year. The guide remains a tier 2 draft awaiting professional
review; its metadata version advances to 1.1.

The primary sources read on 5 October 2026 were the Treasury's
[2003 convention](https://home.treasury.gov/system/files/131/Treaty-Japan-11-6-2003.pdf),
[2003 Protocol](https://home.treasury.gov/system/files/131/Treaty-Japan-Protocol-11-10-2003.pdf),
[2003 exchange of notes](https://home.treasury.gov/system/files/131/Treaty-Japan-Notes-11-6-2003.pdf),
[2013 Protocol](https://home.treasury.gov/system/files/131/Treaty-Japan-Pr2-1-24-2013.pdf)
and [technical explanation](https://home.treasury.gov/system/files/131/Treaty-Japan-Pr2-TE-10-29-2015.pdf).
The [IRS document index](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/international-businesses/japan-tax-treaty-documents)
identifies the published treaty instruments. The
[State Department record](https://2021-2025.state.gov/japan-19-830) and
[IRS 2004 bulletin](https://www.irs.gov/irb/2004-29_IRB) supply force dates.
The [GovInfo copy of TIAS 19-830](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-S-PURL-gpo188375/pdf/GOVPUB-S-PURL-gpo188375.pdf)
contains the 2013 notes and Japanese-language correction.

| Earlier statement | Correction and primary pinpoint |
| --- | --- |
| The 5% dividend category requires a 10% holding for six months | Art 10(2)(a) requires a company beneficial owner with at least 10% of voting stock on the entitlement date. It has no six-month dividend holding test; art 22 still applies. |
| The 2013 Protocol added the parent exemption | Art 10(3)(a) already provided it. Protocol art III changes more than 50% to at least 50%, and twelve months to six months ending on the entitlement date. The specified ownership chain and entitlement routes remain conditions. |
| All interest is exempt | Amended art 11(2) permits specified contingent interest taxation up to 10% of gross, and domestic taxation of specified securitisation excess returns. Arts 11(5), (6) and (7) address PE connection, special-relationship excess and equivalent-debt arrangements. |
| All royalty and technical-service payments are zero-rate payments | Arts 12(1) and (2) require royalty classification, with PE, excess-payment and anti-conduit exceptions. Arts 3(1)(l), 7 and 7(7) require services to be classified before applying the enterprise-profit rule. |
| Construction PE starts at 12 months | Art 5(3) requires more than 12 months. Art 5's fixed-place and agency rules can apply without a separate duration-based services PE provision. |
| 30 August 2019 is the withholding application date | Protocol art XV(2) starts the withholding amendments on 1 November 2019. The other income-tax provisions covered here apply to taxable years beginning from 1 January 2020. |

The section includes the parent exemption's specific art 22 routes, the pension
fund conditions, US RIC/REIT exceptions, remittance and transparent-entity rules,
the saving clause and the amended dual-resident-company rule. It states the 5%
gross excess-payment cap in amended art 11(6) and art 12(4), distinguishes treaty
interest source from domestic taxability, and identifies the additional company
tax ceiling on the dividend-equivalent amount under amended art 10(9) and (10).
The summary heading now describes selected ceilings and exemptions; Japan's row
links to its conditions and identifies its US-source direction.

The notes were checked alongside the instruments. The 2013 notes delete
paragraphs 5, 7 and 8 of the 2003 notes and renumber paragraph 6 as paragraph 5.
The surviving notes include PE capital attribution and Japan's dividend
entitlement date. The 2013 correction concerns the Japanese text of art 11;
the English article used here is the published English Protocol text. Local
offline OCR supplied missing text in the original notes and the scanned TIAS
copy. The Japanese-language correction was not independently translated.
The State PDF download returned an HTML Technical Difficulties page, so the
GovInfo publication supplied the notes instead.

Manual source comparisons covered the 10% holding with no dividend-duration
test, the parent category's exact 50% and six-month boundaries plus specified
entitlement, pension and RIC/REIT restrictions, contingent and securitisation
interest, related-party excess, and construction lasting exactly 12 months
versus more than 12 months. The 31 October and 1 November 2019 application
boundary was checked separately from entry into force. These are comparisons
against the instruments, not taxpayer-specific calculations.
