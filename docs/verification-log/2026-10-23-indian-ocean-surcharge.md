# Christmas Island and Cocos: ordinary single-person Medicare surcharge

Both selected income companions advance to v0.6 for 2026-27. The new
component covers a full-year resident adult who is unmarried and has no
surcharge dependants throughout the year. It distinguishes annual statutory
threshold income, taxable-income charge base and reportable-benefits charge
base, verifies the separate charging provisions and uses exact day fractions.

surcharge Act in force with no commenced unincorporated amendments.

thresholds and rates. PHI's statutory tier/index mechanism was read. A
