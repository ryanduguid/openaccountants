# Gibraltar: ordinary weekly Social Insurance published-profile reference

This batch adds the first Gibraltar guide: a bounded reference for the published ordinary earnings-related (ER) weekly Social Insurance profile, for fictional complete Monday to Sunday weeks wholly within July to December 2026. It does not establish an actual contribution base, complete payroll, an actual deduction or remittance, or comprehensive country acceptance. The fictional branch stipulates one ordinary registered employee aged 30, one employer, an ordinary contract of service and at least 15 service hours in the week; D1 detached workers and every other class are excluded.

Eleven complete official PDFs totalling 156 pages, including the original uncorrected 2025 Order for comparison, are retained by byte count and hash outside this repository. Selected provisions were read and 9 selected original pages were visually compared. Neither complete acquisition nor selected reading establishes the complete current or saved law. The [guide](../../skills/international/gibraltar/gi-weekly-social-insurance-reference.md) identifies the primary sources and reading limits in its primary library table.

The 2025 contribution Order (LN120/2025) commences 1 July 2025; its 19 June 2025 corrigendum (LN124/2025) replaces the employee minimum 14.33 with 14.34 and the employer minimum 31.97 with 31.98, and the stale minima are not used. The Income Tax Office class table labelled effective 1 July 2026 states the same selected ordinary ER weekly cells: employee 10%, minimum 14.34, maximum 40.79; employer 18%, minimum 31.98, maximum 56.22. Each floor and ceiling applies to its own share's percentage result, not to one common earnings band, and the employee and employer amounts stay separate. Exact rational crossings are preserved without inferred rounding.

The older combined Employment Injuries table printing a 20% employer rate is treated as replaced, not as an additional charge: LN2008/042 regulation 2 replaces its Schedule 1 with the Insurance Act Schedule 1 'as amended from time to time'. The actual earnings basis remains held: Insurance Act section 2(7) delegates the calculation of earnings, Benefits regulation 3 expressly invokes it and limits the calculation to net remuneration after specified deductions and expenses, whilst the later contribution Schedule says 'Gross Earnings', and the guide does not resolve that relationship. Annual and weekly minima and maxima are not interchangeable by 52, and no annual reconciliation, period allocation, rounding, recovery of the employer's share or filing calendar is derived. The source anomalies in the incorporated Employment Injuries and Insurance regulations remain explicit.
