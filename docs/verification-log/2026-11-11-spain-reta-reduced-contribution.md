# Spain's reduced RETA contribution in 2025 and 2026

11 November 2026. I rechecked the initial reduced contribution, extension, income bands and related examples in [es-social-contributions](../../skills/international/spain/es-social-contributions.md), [es-autonomous-worker](../../skills/international/spain/es-autonomous-worker.md) and [spain-tax-optimization](../../skills/international/spain/spain-tax-optimization.md).

Article 38 ter of Ley 20/2007 sets the eligibility and application rules, the initial and extension periods, the earnings test and the categories with special treatment. Qualifying company partners can apply. The amended transitional provision sets the EUR 80 and EUR 160 amounts applicable in 2023–2025 before MEI and refers later amounts to each year's State Budget Act. The ordinary initial period is exempt from annual earnings regularisation. The extension tests each calendar year separately. The statute expressly addresses earnings above the SMI and TGSS includes equality in its guidance, including its bulletin about the 2024 regularisation. RD 126/2026 sets the 2026 annual SMI at EUR 17,094 with effect from 1 January 2026. Importass shows EUR 88.64 including 0.90% MEI in its 2026 guidance. The 2026 order puts the minimum of the first general band at EUR 950.98. The calculation on that base is EUR 8.56 of MEI. The published total implies EUR 8.64. The order's preamble explains the budget rollover and RDL 3/2026 art. 3(4) carries forward the ordinary income-band tables with a higher maximum for the top 2 general bands. The reason for the MEI difference, the statutory continuation of the reduced amount and its application to extension, special-category and cross-year assessments remain open. The assessment and matching payment establish the amount for a particular period. The updated examples use stated assessments or assumptions and round contributions to cents.
