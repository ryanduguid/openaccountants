# Cook Islands: selected ordinary PAYE reference

This batch adds selected ordinary Rarotonga wage withholding, declaration,
payment and reporting references. It uses the statutory weekly table and
longer-period method rather than annual income tax divided into pay periods.
An expressly established no-declaration case retains the statutory dollar-or-part
quantum. Unknown records do not create a primary or no-declaration default.

The source intake retains 46 current official provision/schedule responses,
including 5 definition, records and electronic cross-references. Six complete
current MFEM PDFs cover tables, forms and employer guidance. Selected signed
2020 and 2023 amendment and commencement pages were visually compared; the
2016 and 2017 reconciliation amendments were also independently compared.
The bulk 2020 OCR attempt timed out and supplies no reading evidence.

The current API table shows 17.76 at weekly wages of 1,034, whilst the signed
2020 Act's 2021 table and MFEM PDF show 173.76. Four further selected rows differ
by a cent between the API and MFEM. The guide holds affected calculations and
preserves both readings. It does not silently repair or regenerate a table.
The secondary-method wording is present in the original 2023 amendment, so it
is held without labelling it an extraction error.

The reconciliation limb and following shortfall proviso print different dates.
The original amendments do not conclusively resolve their scope. Public forms
also conflict with current employee-certificate wording. These source conflicts,
current variations, year-end assembly and final deadlines remain held.

The component check covers 20 worked or held positions, 12 held-case rows and
17 computed missing-premise or unsupported-input holds. It rejects 44 document,
arithmetic and premise mutations and reconciles 46 API and 6 PDF hashes. It
checks selected reference arithmetic, not a deployed payroll engine. The
certificate example expressly uses qualifying weekly payments.

Both mandatory bases, all required outputs and checks and all 12 payroll
country slots remain binding. Currency, rounding, complete operative law,
other programmes, employment entitlements, special payments, prescribed

verified_by pending remain. No final payroll, filing or country acceptance
