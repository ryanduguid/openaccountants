# BES customs clearance: separate declarations, storage and permissions

On 17 October 2026 I read customs-law articles 2.1–2.50; decree article 2.1,
2.9–2.13 and 2.21–2.25; regulation articles 2.1–2.34; and five complete
authority pages for the
[clearance reference](../../skills/international/caribbean-netherlands/bq-customs-clearance.md).
The [system reference](../../skills/international/caribbean-netherlands/bq-tax-system.md)
now separates its customs route from general tax compliance. The substantive
tax figures in existing guides do not change.

Pre-arrival notice, arrival notification, entry clearance, destination
declaration, acceptance, release and document discharge remain separate.
The ordinary 48-hour destination clock starts from the entry declaration;
RTO storage uses 21 days with a permitted maximum of 30 days total. A move
between temporary stores does not reset that clock. Incomplete declarations
need permission, minimum data, security and completion within the inspector's
term, capped at one month; replacement does not simply change acceptance.

The guide distinguishes amendment before control, extension before expiry,
revival within 60 days after expiry, the conditional 15-calendar-day
wrong-destination route and four-week discharge notice. None is labelled a
general appeal deadline. Government storage and qualifying official-service
fees have separate scope and units. The fictional components recompute to
80 and 27; no fee is imposed on every private store or import.

Eighteen manual criteria were frozen before drafting and reviewed against
the primary provisions. This is implementing-agent source review, not an
independent model benchmark or practitioner sign-off. The current entry
page's closing-time cross-reference points to paragraph 6; the reviewed
operative rule is regulation 2.8(12). The declaration page's field-37 examples
are inconsistent, so actual code mapping remains held.

Regulation 2.8(6) and 2.14(5) also leave a competing pre-arrival
cross-reference for exempt craft. Actual special-craft treatment is held
outside the ordinary commercial scope. Full customs debt, collection,
penalties, remedies, enforcement, controlled-goods, origin/AEO and relief,
external form annexes, current client software and operational cases remain
open. The guide stays Tier 2/pending_review. No comprehensive-area credit,
private records, identity/tax identifiers, bank details, PDF/OCR, submission,
engagement workbook or practitioner sign-off is claimed.
