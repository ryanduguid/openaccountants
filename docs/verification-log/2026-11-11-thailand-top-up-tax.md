# Thailand top-up tax scope, reporting and payment

The Revenue Department hosts the [Emergency Decree on Top-up Tax, B.E. 2567 (2024)](https://www.rd.go.th/fileadmin/user_upload/kormor/newlaw/prk2567.pdf) as printed in Royal Gazette vol 141, part 82 A, 26 December 2024. The PDF's Thai text layer was garbled. The review used rendered pages 2, 14–15 and 29–31. This resolves the access gap in the [12 September verification entry](2026-09-12-thailand-the-authority-s-own-account-and-three-different.md).

- Sections 2 and 26 and paragraph 2 of [Revenue Department News No. 6/2025](https://www.rd.go.th/fileadmin/user_upload/news/2567eng/englishnews_6_2025.pdf) establish application from periods beginning on or after 1 January 2025 and the two-of-four-period threshold. Section 26 also covers proration and delegates special rules for restructurings.
- Section 27 delegates the characteristics of the excluded classes. The [Royal Decree on excluded constituent entities, B.E. 2569 (2026)](https://www.rd.go.th/fileadmin/user_upload/kormor/newlaw/dctut.pdf), Royal Gazette vol 143, part 47 A, 7 August 2026, addresses exclusions under s 27(7). Sections 2 and 5 distinguish commencement on 8 August 2026 from application to periods beginning on or after 1 January 2025. Sections 3–4 give the ownership condition and pension services definition. The [December 2025 Cabinet announcement](https://www.rd.go.th/fileadmin/user_upload/news/2568eng/englishnews_5_2026.pdf) described 4 draft instruments at that date. Review each separately for its subsequent publication and application.
- Sections 53–56 distinguish the notification, local GIR and conditions for foreign GIR filing. Sections 57–58 establish filing and payment where tax is due, the relevant joint-venture period end and the first-period extension from 15 to 18 months.

Outstanding checks include Director-General filing procedures and live forms, the effective exchange relationship by period, the remaining computation and restructuring instruments, any instalment rules and local-accountant review. The Revenue Department's September 2026 announcement records Thailand's signature of the GloBE agreement. Applying s 56 still requires evidence of an effective exchange relationship with the filing jurisdiction for the period. [Revenue Department English news index](https://rd.go.th/english/)
