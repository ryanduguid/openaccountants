# BES penalties: statutory ceilings, policy and correction boundaries

On 17 October 2026 I read Belastingwet 8.6a and 8.19–8.37 including
insertions; DAB 2.107–2.125; customs regulation 2.76–2.80; decree 2.15,
2.17, 2.43, 2.47–2.49 and 2.53;
all current penalty-policy articles; and the complete original 2015 and
2023 amendment publications in HTML for the
[penalties reference](../../skills/international/caribbean-netherlands/bq-penalties-corrections.md).
The system and general-compliance references now route to it. Existing
substantive tax figures do not change.

The guide separates statutory ceilings from policy amounts, defaults from
culpable offences, filing from payment and general tax from customs.
Reminder, recidivism, loss/base, notice, hearing/interview, credit,
proportional reduction and limitation conditions remain specific.
General policy is not applied automatically to DAB penalties. The customs
three-year penalty-power clock is not the debt's post-assessment term.

Voluntary correction requires the stated awareness, disclosure and payment
conditions. An ABB balance in accounts is insufficient. The inclusive
3,000 condition differs from a strict below-10% comparison. Actual period,
denominator and refund signs require separate establishment. Mandatory
disclosure under 8.6a requires its delegated case rather than a guessed
general deadline.

Policy 8(8)'s assessment-correction label differs from statute 8.33's
replacement wording. The original publication contains that wording;
the 2023 amendment changes scope and the accounts reference. Actual
assessment-correction classification and policy rates for new statutory
branches remain held. No legal reconciliation is invented.

Before merge I found and corrected a decree cross-reference error: 2.53
concerns the decree's own warehouse prohibitions, not the Act's document
provision. I read the underlying clauses, added the related delegated
origin/warehouse defaults and froze four repair criteria before editing.
Administrative/criminal concurrence and actual charging remain distinct.
The initial manual review did not catch that reference error; its earlier
passing technical checks did not establish source accuracy.

Twenty-two manual criteria were frozen before drafting. The conditional
amounts were recomputed and compared with the cited rules by the implementing
agent, not an independent model or practitioner. Actual culpability,
defences, disputed mappings, special regimes, criminal procedure,
rounding, forms and complete operational cases remain open. The guide
stays Tier 2/pending_review. No comprehensive-area credit, private
taxpayer data, identity/tax identifier or bank submission, correction,
payment, engagement workbook or practitioner sign-off is claimed.
