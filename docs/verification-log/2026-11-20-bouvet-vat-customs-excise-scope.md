# Bouvet Island VAT, customs and excise scope

On 20 November 2026 I checked the [Bouvet Island guide](../../skills/international/bouvet-island/bv-vat-customs-excise-scope.md) against the [Dependencies Act, sections 1–2](https://lovdata.no/lov/1930-02-27-3). Section 1 lists Bouvet Island as a dependency. The [VAT Act, sections 1-2(2), 3-29 and 6-21](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/2009-06-19-58) excludes the dependencies from the VAT area and distinguishes imports from sales of goods out of that area. Section 6-21 keeps its exception for goods included in an aggregate settlement under section 4-6. The [VAT Regulation, section 6-21-1](https://lovdata.no/dokument/SF/forskrift/2009-12-15-1540/%C2%A76-21-1) sets the export documentation branches and refers to [Goods Movement Regulation section 5-9-5(1)](https://lovdata.no/dokument/SF/forskrift/2022-10-27-1901/%C2%A75-9-5) for the declaration exception. The [Norwegian Tax Directorate statement of 16 October 2026](https://www.skatteetaten.no/rettskilder/type/uttalelser/uttalelser/krav-til-dokumentasjon-ved-utforsel-av-varer/) explains invoicing timing and the records required.

I checked [Goods Movement Act section 1-2](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/2022-03-11-9) and [Customs Duty Act section 1-1](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/2022-03-11-8). Both exclude the dependencies from the customs area. The commencement instruments [FOR-2022-09-16-1574](https://lovdata.no/dokument/LTI/forskrift/2022-09-16-1574) and [FOR-2022-09-16-1575](https://lovdata.no/dokument/LTI/forskrift/2022-09-16-1575) appoint 1 January 2023.

I checked the enacted [Excise Duty Act, sections 1-2, 2-6 and 26-1](https://lovdata.no/dokument/LTI/lov/2026-06-19-41) and the appointed start of 1 January 2027 in [FOR-2026-10-30-2205](https://lovdata.no/dokument/LTI/forskrift/2026-10-30-2205). Section 2-6 expressly includes exports to dependencies. The [previous Excise Regulation, sections 2-7 and 2-8](https://lovdata.no/dokument/SF/forskrift/2001-12-11-1451/%C2%A72-7) uses different terms. I did not infer the treatment of an earlier Bouvet movement from the new Act. The [July 2026 consultation on draft regulations](https://www.skatteetaten.no/nn/rettskjelder/type/horinger/horing--forslag-til-forskrift-til-ny-lov-om-saravgifter/) is a proposal. The guide leaves product, transition and documentary requirements to the applicable instruments for the actual movement.

Four fictional cases exercise direct delivery, export proof, a mainland return and the excise transition. Rates, goods classification, licences, actual declarations, consuming-model execution and professional review remain open. This check does not establish income tax, payroll or social contribution treatment for Bouvet Island.
