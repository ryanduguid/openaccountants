# Andorra minimum-wage periods and CASS supplement limits

12 November 2026. I reconciled the payroll guide's 2025 minimum wage and its 2026 reference with 3 published BOPA decrees. [Decret 4/2025](https://www.bopa.ad/Documents/Detall?doc=GD_2025_01_15_12_19_56) fixes €8.35/hour and €1,447.33/month with financial effect from 1 January 2025. [Decret 8/2026](https://www.bopa.ad/Documents/Detall?doc=GD_2026_01_14_12_20_08) fixes €8.80/hour and €1,525.33/month from 1 January 2026. [Decret 253/2026](https://www.bopa.ad/Documents/Detall?doc=GD_2026_07_02_14_45_22) replaces the conflicting amount with €9.05/hour and €1,568.67/month from 1 July 2026. The guide distinguishes financial effect, publication and commencement. Its annual 2025 cash figure is explicitly 12 rounded monthly payments, not a separately prescribed annual rate.

The updated [CASS factsheet dated 9 July 2026](https://www.cass.ad/sites/default/files/arxius/CASS_COMPLEMENT%20ART99%20ASS%20fitxa%2009-07-2026.pdf) uses the July threshold. I read both pages through native text and page images. It is expressly informational and has no legal validity. It supports the stated registration, declaration/payment, aggregation and special-case instructions, without automatically increasing the employer's ordinary base.

Selected legal copies add the Article 99 framework in [Llei 18/2014, article 25](https://portaljuridicandorra.ad/L2014018), the employee's 10% General Branch shortfall charge and other-benefit-entitlement exception in [regulation article 19](https://portaljuridicandorra.ad/DR20150527A) and selected qualification changes in [Llei 4/2022, final provision 6(15)](https://www.portaljuridicandorra.ad/L2022004_0) and [Llei 1/2024, final provision 4(10)](https://www.portaljuridicandorra.ad/L2024001_0). I did not reconcile the complete amendment chain or exact BOPA publication identities/dates of those law copies.

A material eligibility conflict remains. The [CASS web page](https://www.cass.ad/complement-de-salari) says the relevant low-paid workers are not indirectly insured, whilst the July factsheet and article 224 title refer to indirectly insured workers. The reproduced 2024 eligibility paragraph also omits the below-minimum words in its title. The guide records those discrepancies and requires applicable BOPA text and CASS confirmation before relying on that exemption. It makes no eligibility decision from age or low pay alone.

The guide remains tier 2 and pending qualified review. No catalogue, published coverage figure or whole-topic verification changes. Checks and canonical publication are recorded separately; this entry does not establish complete current-law verification.
