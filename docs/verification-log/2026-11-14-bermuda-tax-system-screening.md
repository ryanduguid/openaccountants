## Corrections and surviving gaps

The system guide checks MNE definitions/consolidation, the 2-of-4-year revenue lookback, entity exclusions/elections, limited-footprint conditions and timing, grouping, the 15% charge and assurance treatment. It separates the statutory fiscal period from payroll April–March. The companion guides must neither prohibit all entity income-tax work nor deny every indirect-tax return. The corporate companion also separates the shared workflow controls from Bermuda law: the revenue threshold does not import GloBE calculations, sector refusals remain and non-corporate statutory screening is not permission to compute through a corporation-only workflow. The 11 screening controls are hypothetical manual cases; no new executed calculation or portal test is claimed. An independent reading identified a retained unqualified VAT assertion in the import example and a payroll-route hold bypass. Both are corrected in the repaired draft; the general intake and working paper now ask for CIT group/election/period facts. The independent reader also identified a source tension: CIT s 13(4)’s ‘more than 5 years’ wording versus General Guidance v2’s 5-fiscal-year description and 2030 example. An actual endpoint/classification decision is held pending authoritative clarification, rather than silently choosing the illustration.

Current affirmative primary evidence for no VAT/GST/general sales tax, no separate CGT and each dividend/interest/royalty withholding category remains missing. Neither the PIT proposition, departmental inventory nor a missing provision proves those negatives. The retained figures and detailed corporate/payroll/social/customs/land/stamp examples remain topic-specific Needs Input items. The whole tax-system topic is not complete. A final source review identified 2 further scope/hold defects: s 13(1)(c) concerns any group constituent entity located in Bermuda, including a relevant entity outside the defined Bermuda Constituent Entity category, and the social-insurance remittance deadline was not covered by the earlier calculation-only hold. Both are corrected. Added manual desk cases trace the 79%-owned entity/IIR condition, the withheld social-insurance deadline and the non-corporate/shared-workflow boundary; they are not executed taxpayer or portal tests.
