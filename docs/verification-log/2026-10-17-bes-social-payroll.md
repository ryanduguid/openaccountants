# BES contributions and payroll: separate bases and source discrepancies

On 17 October 2026 I read the selected current and period-specific legislation,
rate instruments and payroll guidance for the new
[contribution](../../skills/international/caribbean-netherlands/bq-social-contributions.md)
and [payroll](../../skills/international/caribbean-netherlands/bq-payroll.md)
references. Both remain Tier 2/pending_review. The references add substantive
topics to the BES pack; they do not establish comprehensive country coverage
or complete operational workflow companions.

## Insurance, rates and periods

The selected AOV January/July 2026 texts retain the same insured-person and
premium provisions. The original July amendment adds allowance-administration
provisions and punctuation, rather than a contribution-rate change. The
separate ministerial regulation changed on 22 July: its specified Canadian/IBM,
English/Barclays and Saipem exclusions were removed. The earlier version,
current registry and original amendment were read; no past premium or benefit
consequence was selected from the repeal alone.

AOV, AWW and healthcare have separate insured-circle conditions. AOV's upper-age
condition cannot be copied to AWW. The healthcare decree separately charges the
insured person's taxable sum and an employer's wage base. The annual
USD 38,390 insured-person ceiling is not an employer-premium ceiling. Employee
insurance daily-wage benefit limits also do not cap the employer premiums.
The annual rate instrument and healthcare rate regulation supply the 2026
percentages used in the references.

The annual examples keep the matching contribution credits, remaining income
tax and separate premiums visible. Employer contributions remain an employer
cost, rather than an additional employee deduction. Example bases and insured
status are fictional stipulations; the reference does not establish a real
person's eligibility.

## Native PDF and table checks

I downloaded the official six-page explanation, two-page calculation sheet,
27-page monthly table and 92-page manual. pdf-inspector extracted native text;
no page required OCR. The explanation and calculation sheet were read in full.
Selected monthly rows and manual sections were examined, rather than treating
extraction of every page as a complete read.

Positioned native cells in the actual monthly table show USD 562.71 in the
no-allowance column at the USD 1,914 row. The explanatory example prints
USD 526.71. The explanation's last-row narrative also uses USD 1,416.33 while
its own table and total use USD 1,425.91; the actual monthly table confirms the
latter cell. These differences are present in native source text, rather than
OCR substitutions. The references use the checked table cells and do not
silently repair an entire table or software algorithm.

The calculation sheet and manual show a USD 1,677 elderly allowance, while the
current statute and indexation instrument show USD 1,678. The manual's
birth-date shortcut also disagrees with the statutory age-at-year-start test
for 2026. The calculation sheet's monthly end amount differs from the actual
table's final row. These issues remain explicit; dependent elderly payroll
calculations and a general payroll-software algorithm are not approved.

The website's quarter-table label points to the special-payment document.
That link was not treated as a read quarterly table. It is separate from the
quarterly **return** period established by the tax implementing regulation.

## Records and minimum wage

The full payroll implementing regulation was read. Employee statements,
collective wage statements, cessation notifications and the specific retention
periods remain separate. The reference preserves both the mandatory
termination statement and the Act's request-based duty. Identification records
and actual payment details remain with the employer/reviewer outside the agent.

The current Minimum Wages Act and annual indexation establish the adult island
hourly amounts and the default upward whole-dollar conversion for weekly and
monthly minima. The youth decree supplies the 2026 age percentages. The
September 2026 amendment adds an age-15 band from 1 January 2027; publication
during 2026 does not make it operative earlier. The adult example uses
stipulated ordinary hours, rather than asserting a standard legal working week.
