Native extraction found 3 scanned pages and no text. Default offline OCR

## Selected provisions checked

- Articles 1 and 2 establish the scope and exclude organisation and
  functioning of professional orders; national registration and practice
  eligibility are not established.
- Article 3 requires ISA for contractual and statutory audits, alongside
  the AUDCIF and relevant additional AUSCGIE requirements. These performance
  standards do not determine when an auditor must be appointed.
- Articles 4 and 5 distinguish Tomes 1 and 2, audit and other engagements,
  full standards reading and applicable additional AUSCGIE requirements.
- Article 6 establishes an ethics code drawing on IFAC and AUSCGIE. It does
  not by itself establish automatic adoption of the latest international code.
- Articles 7 and 8 distinguish quality assurance responsibilities of orders
  and professionals and require regular updating of the tools.

Article/page citations appear beside material claims. The hypothetical
contractual-audit versus limited-review example applies original
classifications after commencement; it does not verify current law or
practitioner eligibility.
