# Mauritius Company Formation & Entity Choice

## Entity types and incorporation

- **Private company limited by shares** — Most common domestic vehicle; a private company may not have more than 50 shareholders (25 before the Finance (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2019) and may not offer its shares or debentures to the public; a small private company is one with turnover below Rs 100 million that holds no Global Business Licence  _(Companies Act 2001 (Corporate and Business Registration Department consolidation to 31 August 2026), ss 2(5) and 270(a) and (b) — https://companies.govmu.org/cbrd/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Companies-Act-200131-08-2026.pdf)_
- **Public company** — Every company is a public company unless its application or constitution states that it is private; a public company may offer shares to the public and has no shareholder limit  _(Companies Act 2001 (Corporate and Business Registration Department consolidation to 31 August 2026), s 21(4) and (5) — https://companies.govmu.org/cbrd/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Companies-Act-200131-08-2026.pdf)_
- **Global Business Company (GBC) / Authorised Company (AC)** — Licensed vehicles for cross-border/international business, regulated by the Financial Services Commission  _(Financial Services Act (https://www.finsburey.com/products/ac/))_
- **Foreign ownership** — A domestic company may be 100% foreign-owned; the Act lets any person apply for incorporation  _(Companies Act 2001 (Corporate and Business Registration Department consolidation to 31 August 2026), s 22 — https://companies.govmu.org/cbrd/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Companies-Act-200131-08-2026.pdf ; practice: https://www.sovereigngroup.com/mauritius/domestic-companies/)_
- **Minimum share capital** — No statutory minimum share capital: a company limited by shares needs only one or more shares and one or more shareholders (approx — licensed and global business entities carry their own capital and substance conditions; confirm)  _(Companies Act 2001 (Corporate and Business Registration Department consolidation to 31 August 2026), s 21(1) — https://companies.govmu.org/cbrd/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Companies-Act-200131-08-2026.pdf)_
- **Minimum directors** — At least one director, who must be ordinarily resident in Mauritius; directors must be natural persons  _(Companies Act 2001 (Corporate and Business Registration Department consolidation to 31 August 2026), ss 132 and 133(1)(a) — https://companies.govmu.org/cbrd/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Companies-Act-200131-08-2026.pdf)_
- **Company secretary** — Every company other than a small private company (turnover below Rs 100 million) or an Authorised Company must have one or more secretaries, each a natural person ordinarily resident in Mauritius  _(Companies Act 2001 (Corporate and Business Registration Department consolidation to 31 August 2026), ss 163(1) and 2(5) — https://companies.govmu.org/cbrd/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Companies-Act-200131-08-2026.pdf)_
- **Incorporation step — name reservation** — A company name may be reserved by application to the Registrar of Companies in the approved form before incorporation  _(Companies Act 2001 (Corporate and Business Registration Department consolidation to 31 August 2026), s 34 — https://companies.govmu.org/cbrd/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Companies-Act-200131-08-2026.pdf)_
- **Incorporation step — application** — File the incorporation application with the Registrar (Corporate and Business Registration Department) in the approved form, signed by each applicant, with each director's and secretary's signed consent and certificate of non-disqualification  _(Companies Act 2001 (Corporate and Business Registration Department consolidation to 31 August 2026), s 23 — https://companies.govmu.org/cbrd/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Companies-Act-200131-08-2026.pdf)_
- **Incorporation timeline** — Typically about 1 to 3 weeks for a domestic company (faster online) (approx — confirm current CBRD processing times)  _(Companies Act 2001 (https://www.sovereigngroup.com/mauritius/company-formation/))_
- **Annual compliance — annual return** — File an annual return with the Registrar within 28 days of the annual meeting date; a small private company with annual turnover not exceeding Rs 100 million is exempt unless its shareholding, board or other registered particulars changed  _(Companies Act 2001 (Corporate and Business Registration Department consolidation to 31 August 2026), s 223(1), (1B) and (2) — https://companies.govmu.org/cbrd/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Companies-Act-200131-08-2026.pdf)_
- **Annual compliance — financial statements** — Prepare financial statements within 6 months after the balance sheet date; an auditor is appointed at each annual meeting, except that a small private company (turnover below Rs 100 million) need not appoint one  _(Companies Act 2001 (Corporate and Business Registration Department consolidation to 31 August 2026), ss 195, 209(1) and 210(1) — https://companies.govmu.org/cbrd/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Companies-Act-200131-08-2026.pdf)_

Companies are formed under the Companies Act 2001 and registered with the Corporate and Business Registration Department (CBRD). The most common vehicle is a private company limited by shares; Global Business and Authorised Companies are used for international structuring.
