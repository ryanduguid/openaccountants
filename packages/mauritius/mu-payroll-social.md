# Mauritius Payroll & Social Contributions

## Payroll contributions and PAYE

Employers operate PAYE withholding and remit social contributions (CSG, NSF) and a training levy via the monthly MRA return. Rates below reflect the 2025/26 year; bands and ceilings should be confirmed against the current MRA employer guide.

- **CSG — employee rate (monthly remuneration up to Rs 50,000)** — 1.5%  _(Social Contribution and Social Benefits Act 2021 (Act 14/2021), s 4(1)(a) and (b) and First Schedule Part II (Rs 50,000) — https://www.mra.mu/download/SocialContributionAndSocialBenefitsAct2021.pdf)_
- **CSG — employee rate (monthly remuneration above Rs 50,000)** — 3%  _(Social Contribution and Social Benefits Act 2021 (Act 14/2021), s 4(1)(a) and (b) and First Schedule Part II (Rs 50,000) — https://www.mra.mu/download/SocialContributionAndSocialBenefitsAct2021.pdf)_
- **CSG — employer rate (monthly remuneration up to Rs 50,000)** — 3% (the guide previously showed the combined employee and employer figure of 4.5%)  _(Social Contribution and Social Benefits Act 2021 (Act 14/2021), s 4(1)(a) and (b) and First Schedule Part II (Rs 50,000) — https://www.mra.mu/download/SocialContributionAndSocialBenefitsAct2021.pdf)_
- **CSG — employer rate (monthly remuneration above Rs 50,000)** — 6% (the guide previously showed the combined employee and employer figure of 9%)  _(Social Contribution and Social Benefits Act 2021 (Act 14/2021), s 4(1)(a) and (b) and First Schedule Part II (Rs 50,000) — https://www.mra.mu/download/SocialContributionAndSocialBenefitsAct2021.pdf)_
- **National Savings Fund (NSF) — employee rate** — 1% of basic wage or salary, deducted by the employer, up to the ceiling in the Fifth Schedule to the National Pensions Act (approx Rs 28,570 a month for the standard sector; confirm the current ceiling)  _(National Savings Fund Act 1995 (MRA consolidation), s 5(2) and s 2 (basic wage capped at the Fifth Schedule to the National Pensions Act) — https://www.mra.mu/download/TheNationalSavingsFundAct1995.pdf)_
- **National Savings Fund (NSF) — employer rate** — 2.5% of basic wage or salary, subject to the same monthly ceiling  _(National Savings Fund Act 1995 (MRA consolidation), s 5 — https://www.mra.mu/download/TheNationalSavingsFundAct1995.pdf)_
- **Training levy (HRDC) — employer** — 1.5% of each employee's basic wage or salary, excluding overtime, bonuses and allowances; household workers are excluded  _(Human Resource Development Act 2003 (MRA consolidation), s 18(1) and (2) and First Schedule — https://www.mra.mu/download/TheHumanResourceDevelopmentAct2003.pdf)_
- **PAYE withholding** — Employers withhold income tax, including the individual Fair Share Contribution, from emoluments when they are paid or made available (s 93(1)) and remit it within 20 days from the end of the month, or by the end of the following month where the PAYE return and payment are made electronically (s 100(1) and (1B))  _(Income Tax Act 1995 (MRA consolidation to May 2026), ss 93(1) and 100(1) and (1B) — https://www.mra.mu/download/ITAConsolidated.pdf)_
- **PAYE exempt-person monthly threshold** — Rs 38,462 per month  _(Income Tax Act 1995 (MRA consolidation to May 2026), s 2, definition of 'exempt person', as amended by the Finance (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2025 from 1 July 2025 — https://www.mra.mu/download/ITAConsolidated.pdf)_
- **Monthly PAYE/CSG/NSF return & payment deadline** — By the end of the month following the month in which contributions/tax were withheld (May and November returns are due 2 working days before end of June and December respectively)  _(Income Tax Act 1995 (MRA consolidation to May 2026), s 100(1B) — https://www.mra.mu/download/ITAConsolidated.pdf ; Social Contribution and Social Benefits Act 2021 (Act 14/2021), ss 6(2)(a) and 7(1) — https://www.mra.mu/download/SocialContributionAndSocialBenefitsAct2021.pdf ; MRA important dates — https://www.mra.mu/eservices1/individual/monthly-paye-csg-nsf-return)_
