# Isle of Man Corporate Income Tax

## Company income tax rates and base

The Isle of Man taxes most company profits at 0%, with higher rates reserved for banking/large-retail, Isle of Man land income, and (from 2024) certain extraction activities. A 15% Pillar Two top-up applies to in-scope multinational groups. There are no withholding taxes on dividends, interest or royalties.

- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 0% for all resident and non-resident companies  _(Isle of Man Treasury, Income Tax Division, Corporate Tax Rates — https://www.gov.im/categories/tax-vat-and-your-money/income-tax-and-national-insurance/business-and-corporations/corporate-tax-rates/)_
- **10% rate (banking and large retail)** — 10% on banking business income and on retail business profits above IMP 500,000 (a 15% rate applied to these sources for the 2024/25 year of assessment only)  _(Isle of Man Treasury, Income Tax Division, Corporate Tax Rates — https://www.gov.im/categories/tax-vat-and-your-money/income-tax-and-national-insurance/business-and-corporations/corporate-tax-rates/)_
- **20% rate (Isle of Man land/property and extraction)** — 20% on income from Isle of Man land and property from all sources, including petroleum extraction activities or rights from 6 April 2024  _(Isle of Man Treasury, Income Tax Division, Corporate Tax Rates — https://www.gov.im/categories/tax-vat-and-your-money/income-tax-and-national-insurance/business-and-corporations/corporate-tax-rates/)_
- **Pillar Two domestic top-up rate** — 15% effective minimum rate for in-scope large multinational groups (OECD Pillar Two) (approx — confirm Act title)  _(Global Minimum Tax (Pillar Two) Act 2024)_
- **Voluntary election to 10%** — Certain 0%-rate companies may elect to be taxed at 10%  _(Income Tax Act 1970, as reported `[RESEARCH GAP — the statute's text was not read for this change; the secondary summary that carried the figure is no longer cited]`)_
- **Tax base** — Resident companies are taxed on their worldwide income and non-resident companies on their Manx-source income only; companies have been assessed on an accounting period basis since 6 April 2007  _(Isle of Man Treasury, Income Tax Division, Business and Corporations — https://www.gov.im/categories/tax-vat-and-your-money/income-tax-and-national-insurance/business-and-corporations/ ; Isle of Man Treasury, Income Tax Division, Corporate Tax Rates — https://www.gov.im/categories/tax-vat-and-your-money/income-tax-and-national-insurance/business-and-corporations/corporate-tax-rates/)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends** — 0%: dividends paid to a company or an individual carry no withholding  _(Isle of Man Treasury, Income Tax Division, Withholding Tax — https://www.gov.im/categories/tax-vat-and-your-money/income-tax-and-national-insurance/business-and-corporations/withholding-tax/)_
- **Withholding tax on interest** — 0% on loan interest paid by a 0% company; loan interest paid to an individual by a company with Manx land and property income taxed at 20% bears 20%  _(Isle of Man Treasury, Income Tax Division, Withholding Tax — https://www.gov.im/categories/tax-vat-and-your-money/income-tax-and-national-insurance/business-and-corporations/withholding-tax/)_
- **Withholding tax on royalties** — 0% on payments to a company other than Manx property rent, which bears 20%; the Division's table charges 20% on other Manx-source income paid to a non-resident individual  _(Isle of Man Treasury, Income Tax Division, Withholding Tax — https://www.gov.im/categories/tax-vat-and-your-money/income-tax-and-national-insurance/business-and-corporations/withholding-tax/)_
- **Distribution/loan charge** — Historic 'distributable profits charge' / attribution regimes abolished; relevant Manx-resident shareholders taxed on distributions (approx — confirm)  _(Income Tax Act 1970, as reported `[RESEARCH GAP — the statute's text was not read for this change; the secondary summary that carried the figure is no longer cited]`)_
- **Corporate tax return deadline** — 12 months and one day after the end of the accounting period; a late return penalty is charged, with a second penalty if the return is still outstanding six months after the due date  _(Isle of Man Treasury, Income Tax Division, Pay and File — https://www.gov.im/categories/tax-vat-and-your-money/income-tax-and-national-insurance/business-and-corporations/pay-and-file/)_
- **Corporate tax payment deadline** — Income tax is due on the return due date, 12 months and one day after the accounting period end; interest runs from the due date  _(Isle of Man Treasury, Income Tax Division, Pay and File — https://www.gov.im/categories/tax-vat-and-your-money/income-tax-and-national-insurance/business-and-corporations/pay-and-file/)_
