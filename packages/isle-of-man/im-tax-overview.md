# Isle of Man Tax Overview

The Isle of Man Treasury's Income Tax Division administers income tax, and the Customs and Excise Division administers VAT. Amounts below are in Manx pounds, at par with sterling. The personal tax year runs from 6 April to 5 April. [Currency Act 1992][CURRENCY] · [Income Tax Division, rates and allowances][RATES] · [Customs and Excise, VAT][VAT]

## Residence and income source

Individuals resident on the Island generally declare worldwide income. Individuals who are non-resident generally come within the charge on Manx-source income, subject to statutory exceptions. Check the individual's circumstances, any applicable treaty and any foreign tax relief separately. Company residence has its own tests under section 2N, including incorporation, central management and control and the conditions of the foreign residence exception. [Income Tax Act 1970, ss 2N and 9–11][LAW] · [Income Tax Division, new residents][RES]

## Individuals and companies

For 2026–27, the ordinary resident rate is 10% on the first £6,500 of taxable income and 21% on the remainder. The lower-rate band for joint assessment is £13,000. The standard allowances are £17,000 for an individual and £34,000 for a jointly assessed couple, subject to income-related reductions. The annual tax cap requires an election. The rates table gives £220,000 for an individual and £440,000 for a jointly assessed couple for 2026–27. The Isle of Man does not levy a separate capital gains tax or inheritance tax. [Income Tax Division, rates and allowances][RATES] · [Income Tax Division, new residents][RES]

The ordinary company rate is 0%. A 10% rate applies to banking business and qualifying large retail business. Manx land and property income and certain petroleum income are taxed at 20%. Under the retail order, 0% applies to the entire taxable retail income up to the small company limit. When taxable retail income exceeds that limit, the 10% rate applies to the entire taxable retail income. The £500,000 limit is subject to short-period and associated-company adjustments. The temporary 15% banking and retail rule concerned 2024–25 and excludes accounting periods starting on or after 1 January 2025. The separate domestic top-up tax concerns in-scope multinational groups. [Income Tax Division, corporate rates][CORP] · [SD 2013/0013, arts 3–7][RETAIL] · [Treasury memorandum, SD 2024/0243][TEMP] · [Tynwald, approval of SD 2024/0243][APPROVAL]

## VAT and filing

The Isle of Man operates VAT under its Value Added Tax Act 1996. The standard rate is 20%, with reduced or zero rates and exemptions for qualifying supplies. Check the nature and place of the supply, the VAT accounting period and the treatment of movements of goods under the Isle of Man and UK customs arrangements. [Value Added Tax Act 1996][VATLAW] · [Customs and Excise, VAT][VAT]

The 2025–26 personal return was due on 6 October 2026. For company income tax the ordinary pay-and-file deadline is 12 months and one day after the end of the accounting period. Check the period, any exit or cessation rule and the return instructions before filing. [Income Tax Division, 2025–26 personal deadline][PERSONAL] · [Income Tax Division, company pay and file][FILE]
