# Nepal tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the taxpayer, where income arose, residence facts and period. Check the applicable treaty and foreign-tax relief.

## Territorial reach

Residents generally include income from employment, business and investment regardless of source. Nonresidents generally include Nepal-source income in those categories. A Nepal permanent establishment of a nonresident is separately recognised for tax on its business income and may face a separate charge on income remitted abroad. Check the statutory source rules, any foreign tax credit and any relevant treaty. [Income Tax Act 2058, ss 3, 5–6, 67–68, 71 and 73](https://www.moljpa.gov.np/public/uploads/%E0%A4%86%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%95%E0%A4%B0%20%E0%A4%90%E0%A4%A8%2C%20%E0%A5%A8%E0%A5%A6%E0%A5%AB%E0%A5%AE-2.pdf)

## Residence

An individual is resident if their normal place of abode is in Nepal, they spend at least 183 days in Nepal during a continuous 365-day period, or the Nepal Government posts them abroad during the income year. A company is resident if formed under Nepalese law or effectively managed in Nepal in the income year. The definition also includes partnership firms, certain trusts and the Nepal permanent establishment of a nonresident. [Income Tax Act 2058, s 2(कङ)](https://www.moljpa.gov.np/public/uploads/%E0%A4%86%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%95%E0%A4%B0%20%E0%A4%90%E0%A4%A8%2C%20%E0%A5%A8%E0%A5%A6%E0%A5%AB%E0%A5%AE-2.pdf)

## Taxpayer types

An individual accounts for their business income. A partnership firm is an entity for income tax and is responsible for its tax separately from its beneficiaries, as is a company. Check the distribution rules when the entity pays out income. The Partnership Act separately governs the legal constitution of registered firms. [Income Tax Act 2058, ss 2, 5, 7 and 52–54](https://www.moljpa.gov.np/public/uploads/%E0%A4%86%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%95%E0%A4%B0%20%E0%A4%90%E0%A4%A8%2C%20%E0%A5%A8%E0%A5%A6%E0%A5%AB%E0%A5%AE-2.pdf) and [Partnership Act 2020, s 3](https://lawcommission.gov.np/content/12140/partnership-act--2020/)

## Currency

Convert amounts used to calculate taxable income into Nepalese rupees (NPR). Use the rate when the amount is received, spent, paid, borne or otherwise recognised for tax. A department-approved average rate can apply with written permission. [Income Tax Act 2058, s 28](https://www.moljpa.gov.np/public/uploads/%E0%A4%86%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%95%E0%A4%B0%20%E0%A4%90%E0%A4%A8%2C%20%E0%A5%A8%E0%A5%A6%E0%A5%AB%E0%A5%AE-2.pdf)

## Tax periods

The statutory income year begins on 1 Shrawan and ends on the last day of Ashadh in the next Bikram Sambat year. Use the 2083/84 income-year provisions for the current period, including the amendments made by Finance Act 2083. [Income Tax Act 2058, s 2(झ)](https://www.moljpa.gov.np/public/uploads/%E0%A4%86%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%95%E0%A4%B0%20%E0%A4%90%E0%A4%A8%2C%20%E0%A5%A8%E0%A5%A6%E0%A5%AB%E0%A5%AE-2.pdf) and [Finance Act 2083, ss 1 and 57](https://www.moljpa.gov.np/public/uploads/%E0%A4%86%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A5%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%95%20%E0%A4%90%E0%A4%A8,%20%E0%A5%A8%E0%A5%A6%E0%A5%AE%E0%A5%A9-1.pdf)

## Authorities

The Inland Revenue Department in the Ministry of Finance administers the Act. Check the taxpayer's assigned office for registration and compliance. [Income Tax Act 2058, ss 2 and 72](https://www.moljpa.gov.np/public/uploads/%E0%A4%86%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%95%E0%A4%B0%20%E0%A4%90%E0%A4%A8%2C%20%E0%A5%A8%E0%A5%A6%E0%A5%AB%E0%A5%AE-2.pdf) and [Inland Revenue Department](https://ird.gov.np/)
