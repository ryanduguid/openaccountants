# Mainland China tax system

> General orientation for 2026. Confirm the taxpayer, domicile, source of income and type of supply with a qualified mainland Chinese tax professional before applying these rules.

Amounts are in renminbi (CNY). This guide applies to mainland China. Check any other jurisdiction involved and the relevant treaty or arrangement.

## Income tax

- **Individuals and residence.** An individual domiciled in mainland China, or a non-domiciled individual present for at least 183 days in a tax year, is generally resident. Residents generally include worldwide income. Non-residents generally include China-source income. For non-domiciled residents, check relief for foreign-source income paid outside China, the preceding 6 tax years, any single absence longer than 30 days and the applicable filing conditions. [Individual Income Tax Law, arts. 1 and 7](https://www.chinatax.gov.cn/n810219/n810744/n3752930/n3752974/c3970366/content.html) · [MOF and STA Announcement No. 34 of 2019](https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c102416/c5202334/content.html)
- **Individual rates and filing.** The ordinary progressive rates on residents’ comprehensive income are 3%, 10%, 20%, 25%, 30%, 35% and 45%. The usual annual basic deduction for that category is CNY 60,000 before allowable special deductions. Check the separate schedules for business income and other categories. For calendar 2026, a resident who must reconcile comprehensive income ordinarily files between 1 March and 30 June 2027. Individual business income has a separate settlement deadline by 31 March of the following year. Check payer withholding and any return exemption. [Individual Income Tax Law, arts. 2–3, 6, 11–14 and Schedule I](https://www.chinatax.gov.cn/n810219/n810744/n3752930/n3752974/c3970366/content.html)
- **Enterprise tax.** An enterprise established under Chinese law or with its actual management institution in China is generally resident and taxable on worldwide income. For a non-resident enterprise, identify China-source income and any foreign income effectively connected with its establishment in China. The ordinary enterprise income tax rate is 25%. Preferential and non-resident withholding rules can change the result. The ordinary annual enterprise return and settlement are due within 5 months after year-end, with monthly or quarterly prepayments as applicable. [Enterprise Income Tax Law, arts. 2–4, 27–28 and 53–54](https://gtm-cn-2r42yzjvn0s.jiangsu.chinatax.gov.cn/art/2018/12/29/art_23636_1724.html)

## VAT

- **Rate and method.** The VAT Law and State Council implementing regulation commenced on 1 January 2026. Ordinary rates are 13% for most goods and specified services, 9% for listed categories such as transport and construction, and 6% for other taxable services and intangibles unless another rule applies. The statutory simplified levy rate is 3%, with separate concessions. Check the applicable method, recoverable input tax, exemptions and zero-rating using the actual supply. [VAT Law, arts. 10–16 and 38](https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c100009/c5237365/content.html) · [State Council Decree No. 826](https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c100010/c5246349/content.html)
- **Specified services supplied by individuals.** From 1 November 2026 a mainland unit paying an individual for a service listed in Announcement No. 28, such as software or consulting, may have to withhold VAT and associated levies under the measures. The first filing period opens 1 December 2026. Check the scope and thresholds before treating the payer as liable. [MOF and STA Announcement No. 28 of 2026](https://tianjin.chinatax.gov.cn/11200000000/0300/030004/03000418/20260907101101261.shtml) · [STA November guidance](https://www.chinatax.gov.cn/chinatax/c102414/c5253526/5253526/files/%E5%A2%83%E5%86%85%E5%8D%95%E4%BD%8D%E4%BB%A3%E6%89%A3%E4%BB%A3%E7%BC%B4%E8%87%AA%E7%84%B6%E4%BA%BA%E5%A2%9E%E5%80%BC%E7%A8%8E%E6%94%BF%E7%AD%96%E5%8D%B3%E9%97%AE%E5%8D%B3%E7%AD%94-20261113161625338.pdf)
