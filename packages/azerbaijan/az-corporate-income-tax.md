# Azerbaijan Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate profit tax rate and base

- **Standard corporate profit tax rate** — 20% on the profit of enterprises (art. 105.1), applying to resident enterprises and to the permanent establishments of non-residents  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Azerbaijan (e-qanun.az consolidated text, Azerbaijani, with amendments to May 2026), art. 105.1 — https://e-qanun.az/framework/46948)_
- **Tax base — resident enterprise** — Worldwide gross income less deductible business expenses (taxable profit)  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Azerbaijan — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/azerbaijan/corporate/income-determination)_
- **Tax base — non-resident with a PE** — Income attributable to the Azerbaijan permanent establishment, taxed at 20%  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Azerbaijan — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/azerbaijan/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Simplified tax (turnover-based) alternative** — 2% of receipts from goods, works and services and of non-sales income (art. 220.1), for persons not registered for VAT whose taxable transactions are AZN 200,000 or less in every month of a consecutive 12-month period (art. 218.1.1); certain activities are excluded (art. 218.4). The 4% Baku rate quoted by older secondary sources is no longer in the Code  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Azerbaijan (Vərgi Məcəlləsi, consolidated text on e-qanun.az), art. 218.1.1, 218.4 and 220.1 — https://e-qanun.az/framework/46948 ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/azerbaijan/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends** — 5% at source on dividends paid by a resident enterprise (art. 122.1)  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Azerbaijan (e-qanun.az consolidated text, Azerbaijani, with amendments to May 2026), art. 122.1 — https://e-qanun.az/framework/46948)_
- **Withholding tax on interest** — 10% at source on interest paid to non-residents and to individuals (art. 123.1)  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Azerbaijan (e-qanun.az consolidated text, Azerbaijani, with amendments to May 2026), art. 123.1 — https://e-qanun.az/framework/46948)_
- **Withholding tax on royalties** — 14% at source on royalties from an Azerbaijani source (art. 124.1)  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Azerbaijan (e-qanun.az consolidated text, Azerbaijani, with amendments to May 2026), art. 124.1 — https://e-qanun.az/framework/46948)_
- **Withholding on other non-resident Azerbaijan-source income / services** — **10%** on Azerbaijani-source income of a non-resident not connected with a permanent establishment where no more specific category applies. Management and service fees are not a separate head in the Tax Code, so they land here at 10%. Treaty relief may reduce it  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Azerbaijan (e-qanun.az consolidated text, Azerbaijani, with amendments to May 2026), art. 125.1 — https://e-qanun.az/framework/46948)_
- **Withholding on rent (movable and immovable property)** — **14%** on rent (icarə) from an Azerbaijani source, the same rate as royalties; letting of housing to individuals (kirayə) carries 10% (art. 124.1). The characterisation of an equipment payment as rent rather than a service therefore matters  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Azerbaijan (e-qanun.az consolidated text, Azerbaijani, with amendments to May 2026), art. 124.1 — https://e-qanun.az/framework/46948)_
- **Withholding on risk insurance and reinsurance premiums** — **4%** (art. 125.1)  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Azerbaijan (e-qanun.az consolidated text, Azerbaijani, with amendments to May 2026), art. 125.1 — https://e-qanun.az/framework/46948)_
- **Withholding on telecommunications and international transport services** — **6%** (art. 125.1)  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Azerbaijan (e-qanun.az consolidated text, Azerbaijani, with amendments to May 2026), art. 125.1 — https://e-qanun.az/framework/46948)_
- **Payments to favourable-tax-regime jurisdictions** — A direct **or indirect** payment to a person in a country with a favourable tax regime is **deemed to be Azerbaijani-source income** and withheld at **10%**. This catches payments that would otherwise sit outside the charge entirely, so the payee's jurisdiction has to be checked before concluding no Azerbaijani withholding arises  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Azerbaijan (e-qanun.az consolidated text, Azerbaijani, with amendments to May 2026), arts. 13.2.16.14-1 and 125.1.9 — https://e-qanun.az/framework/46948)_
- **Annual profit tax return filing deadline** — 31 March of the year following the reporting year (art. 149.2)  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Azerbaijan (e-qanun.az consolidated text, Azerbaijani, with amendments to May 2026), art. 149.2 — https://e-qanun.az/framework/46948)_
- **Final profit tax payment deadline** — By the return deadline, 31 March, when the final computation is made (art. 152.1)  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Azerbaijan (e-qanun.az consolidated text, Azerbaijani, with amendments to May 2026), art. 152.1 — https://e-qanun.az/framework/46948)_
- **Quarterly advance (current) payments** — Within 15 days after the end of each quarter; each payment is one quarter of the prior year's tax, or under the alternative method the tax on the quarter's actual profit, and the year's current payments must reach at least 75% of the tax due (arts. 151.1 and 151.5.2)  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Azerbaijan (e-qanun.az consolidated text, Azerbaijani, with amendments to May 2026), arts. 151.1 and 151.5.2 — https://e-qanun.az/framework/46948)_
- **Corporate property tax** — 1% per annum on the average annual net book value of fixed assets (art. 198)  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Azerbaijan (e-qanun.az consolidated text, Azerbaijani, with amendments to May 2026), art. 198 — https://e-qanun.az/framework/46948)_
