# Tokelau incorporated associations and annual accounts

Load `workflow-base`. Use this reference for associations under the Incorporation Rules 2003 listed in force at 1 May 2016. Rule 1 defines an association as 2 or more persons incorporated under the rules. Schedule 2 sets additional governing-document requirements for specified purposes, including charitable, commercial and cooperative purposes. Verify the current rules and decide which entity and licensing route fits the actual operations before applying. [Incorporation Rules 2003, rr 1 and 3 and Schedule 2](https://www.paclii.org/tk/legis/consol_act_2016/ir2003224/) · [Table of Rules in Force at 1 May 2016](https://www.paclii.org/tk/indices/legis/rules-in-force.html)

## Apply and establish the association

Use Form 1 and attach the proposed rules covering the matters in Form 2 and applicable Schedule 2 requirements. At least one applicant must be Tokelauan and ordinarily resident in Tokelau. Supply any relevant further information the Registrar requires. Rule 3(6) refers to relevant New Zealand law in applying the Schedule 2 requirements. Identify that law for the purpose and period before settling the proposed rules. [Incorporation Rules 2003, r 3 and Schedules 1–2](https://www.paclii.org/tk/legis/consol_act_2016/ir2003224/)

The Registrar consults the relevant Taupulega before deciding whether to grant or refuse the application. Under the 2016 text, a grant takes effect as incorporation on payment of the $100 fee. The Registrar issues a Form 3 certificate and may impose conditions. Rule 2 separately provides for inspection of the register for $10. The archived Interpretation Rules identify the New Zealand dollar and Tokelau dollar as local currency units. Confirm the current fee denomination and method before payment. [Interpretation Rules 2003, r 12](https://www.paclii.org/tk/legis/consol_act_2016/ir2003240/) From incorporation the association has perpetual succession and can sue and be sued in its own name. The association’s liability is limited to its assets. Document officer authority and consider the protection in rule 5(3) on its stated conditions of lawful action, good faith and pursuit of the association’s objects. [Incorporation Rules 2003, rr 2 and 4–6 and Schedule 1](https://www.paclii.org/tk/legis/consol_act_2016/ir2003224/)

## Set up the annual reporting file

- Follow the accounting procedures specified by the Registrar on incorporation. Nominate an annual accounting date and retain evidence that money received is held in the Tokelau Savings System as specified by rule 8(4).
- Within 3 months of the nominated annual accounting date, deliver to the Registrar the statement of income and expenditure for the accounting year and assets and liabilities at its end. Include a certificate signed by 2 officers confirming submission to and approval by members at a meeting.
- Present the annual record of accounts audited by a person approved by the Registrar no later than 6 months after the financial year ends. Confirm the nominated accounting date and financial year end. Track the statement and audit submissions separately.

Use Form 4 to apply to change the rules. An approved change takes effect when the Registrar issues the Form 5 certificate. Confirm the Form 6 dissolution process, payment of debts and destination of remaining property before closing an association. [Incorporation Rules 2003, rr 8–10 and Schedule 1](https://www.paclii.org/tk/legis/consol_act_2016/ir2003224/)

## Fictional reporting file

A registered association governed by the archived rules has a nominated accounting date and financial year end of 30 June. At a September meeting the members approve the statements. On 15 September the association delivers them with the approval certificate signed by 2 officers. It presents the audited annual accounts on 15 December after audit by a Registrar-approved person. On those facts both submissions fall within their respective periods. Retain the approval minutes, signed certificate, auditor approval, audited accounts and proof of the 2 submissions. [Incorporation Rules 2003, r 8(2)–(5)](https://www.paclii.org/tk/legis/consol_act_2016/ir2003224/)

## Remaining coverage

Confirm the current forms, fees, registrar and filing procedure, requirements for the association’s stated purpose, current accounting and audit obligations, entity taxation, sector licences and practitioner review. Apply village corporation rules separately.
