# Tokelau national accounts and audit

Load `workflow-base`. Identify whether the entity is the Tokelau Administration, a national department or another body corporate. Confirm the governing rule and financial year before setting its timetable. The national Finance Rules in the 2016 compilation use a financial year ending 30 June. The separate village incorporation rules give the villages a 31 December year end. [Finance Rules 1998, rr 2 and 14](https://www.paclii.org/tk/legis/consol_act_2016/fr1998115/) · [Village incorporation rules, r 15](https://www.paclii.org/tk/legis/consol_act_2016/vir1986345/) · [2016 table of rules in force](https://www.paclii.org/tk/indices/legis/rules-in-force.html)

## Close the national accounts

Maintain the Administration’s accounts on an accrual basis. Under the archived rule, prepare annual financial statements for the General Fono by 1 September following year end and send all statements to the Auditor by 30 September. Rule 9 specifies generally accepted accounting practice and items including financial position and performance, cash flows, overdrafts, commitments, contingent liabilities, unappropriated and emergency expenditure or liabilities, policies and trust money. Check the specified budget figures and comparative actuals for each statement. [Finance Rules 1998, r 9](https://www.paclii.org/tk/legis/consol_act_2016/fr1998115/)

For a department, prepare the previous month’s statement by the 20th of each month starting with 20 September in each financial year. Include financial and service performance and unappropriated expenditure, expenses or liabilities. The officer responsible for each government agency also submits the end-of-year agency report to the General Fono. [Finance Rules 1998, r 10](https://www.paclii.org/tk/legis/consol_act_2016/fr1998115/)

## Record the audit and its authority

The archived rules assign the Auditor-General of New Zealand the audit of accounts containing public money and, unless legislation otherwise directs, of Tokelau bodies corporate. They provide for access to records and requests for explanations. Rule 13 sets 31 December as the deadline for the auditor’s report to the General Fono. Rule 14 directs the report for a body corporate to its governing body and applies the national practices and audit requirements subject to express law. Confirm the entity’s own instrument before applying any of those duties. [Finance Rules 1998, rr 11–14](https://www.paclii.org/tk/legis/consol_act_2016/fr1998115/)

In November 2022 the General Fono sent proposed Finance Rules amendments for further village discussion. The accompanying draft states different dates for annual submission and audit. The decision and draft do not supply a commencement date for enacted changes. Obtain the later final text before applying the archived or proposed dates to an actual year. [General Fono decisions, 1–3 November 2022, item 3 and draft rr 9–12](https://www.mfat.govt.nz/assets/OIA/OIA-2023/OIA-28717-Tokelau-Briefings-27-July-2023.pdf)

## Fictional national close

Assume a 30 June national close governed by the 2016 text. Record 1 September for preparation, 30 September for forwarding to the Auditor and 31 December for the audit report. Keep the date of each completed step separately. The New Zealand Auditor-General’s 2023/24 annual report records later completion of the audit for Tokelau’s year ended 30 June 2017. The 2025/26 annual report still describes clearing the backlog as work in progress. Check the signed records for the actual year before recording completion. [Finance Rules 1998, rr 9 and 13](https://www.paclii.org/tk/legis/consol_act_2016/fr1998115/) · [New Zealand Auditor-General, annual report 2023/24, Part 3](https://oag.parliament.nz/2024/annual-report/docs/annual-report.pdf) · [Audit Office annual report 2025/26, Part 2](https://ao.parliament.nz/2026/annual-report/part2.htm)

## Remaining coverage

Verify the current national and entity-specific rules, applicable accounting standards, the latest reporting instructions, actual filings, budget and expenditure authority and practitioner review. Obtain the signed audit report and any subsequent public record for the year.
