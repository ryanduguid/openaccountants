# Tokelau village accounts and audit

Load `workflow-base`. Confirm that the reporting entity is one of the 3 villages. Rule 3 of the text reproduced in Tulafono a Tokelau 2016 establishes Atafu, Fakaofo and Nukunonu as bodies corporate with perpetual succession. The Taupulega manages its village. The 2016 table calls the instrument the Village Incorporation Rules 1986 and lists it in force at 1 May 2016. Rule 1 of the reproduced text calls it the Village Incorporation Regulations 1986. Confirm the governing rules separately for a village store, a department or a private business. [Tokelau 2016 compilation, rr 1 and 3–7](https://www.paclii.org/tk/legis/consol_act_2016/vir1986345/) · [Table of Rules in Force at 1 May 2016](https://www.paclii.org/tk/indices/legis/rules-in-force.html)

## Keep the village records

The Failautuhi serves as secretary and treasurer. Keep the village’s financial records and receipts for payments to and from the village. Keep confirmed minutes with the record of the Taupulega’s decisions. Rule 14 provides for the village’s accounts with the Tokelau Savings System, term deposits and the Administrator’s prior approval for village borrowing. Verify the current account arrangements and any instrument affecting authority before using them. [Tokelau 2016 compilation, rr 12–14](https://www.paclii.org/tk/legis/consol_act_2016/vir1986345/)

## Close the year and prepare for audit

- The village’s financial year ends on 31 December. Within 3 months the Failautuhi prepares the income and expenditure account and a statement of assets and liabilities at year end.
- The village submits those statements to an auditor it appoints as soon as practicable after year end. Provide access to the village books and papers and respond to the auditor’s information requests.
- The auditor sends a written report to the Faipule. After presentation, deposit the report and the statements at the village Administration Office. Village members may inspect them at reasonable times without a fee.

The Tokelau compilation uses ‘balance sheet’. The New Zealand reprint dated 1 October 1997 uses ‘statement of financial position’. The 2016 table explains the General Fono’s treatment of pre-August 1996 regulations. Use the Tokelau compilation for this period and confirm the required terminology, formats and local procedure for the actual year. [Tokelau 2016 compilation, r 15](https://www.paclii.org/tk/legis/consol_act_2016/vir1986345/) · [Table of Rules in Force at 1 May 2016](https://www.paclii.org/tk/indices/legis/rules-in-force.html) · [New Zealand reprint, reg 15](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/secondary-legislation/pco-drafted/1986/319/en/latest/)

## Fictional year-end file

A village closes its books on 31 December. At the March review, the Failautuhi has prepared both financial statements. The appointed auditor has received them and is still conducting the audit. Record the preparation and submission dates separately. Keep the written report and Administration Office deposit open until each occurs. Apply the 3-month rule to preparation of the statements and record the separate audit completion date. [Tokelau 2016 compilation, r 15(1)–(6)](https://www.paclii.org/tk/legis/consol_act_2016/vir1986345/)

## Remaining coverage

Confirm the current village rules, statutory changes, appointments, accounting basis, supporting forms and practitioner review for the actual year. Obtain the relevant authority and dates before advising on borrowing, a late audit or access to records. Private entity formation, tax returns and public-sector consolidation need their own governing rules.
