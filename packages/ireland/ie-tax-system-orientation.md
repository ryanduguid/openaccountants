# Ireland tax system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the taxpayer, the source and nature of each amount, residence, the applicable period and the authority responsible for each tax.

## Territorial reach

An individual who is both resident and domiciled in Ireland is generally chargeable on worldwide income and gains. An individual who is neither resident nor domiciled is generally chargeable on Irish-source income, foreign employment income for duties performed in Ireland and gains on specified Irish assets. Check ordinary residence, remittances and treaty relief where relevant. A resident company generally accounts for worldwide profits. For a non-resident company, check profits attributable to an Irish trading branch or agency and Irish rental profits or gains under Schedule D Case V accruing on or after 1 January 2022. Specified exploration or exploitation profits, rights and offshore employment income can be treated as Irish under ss 13 and 23. Check the activity, the designated area and the particular tax. [Revenue, individual residence](https://www.revenue.ie/en/jobs-and-pensions/tax-residence/index.aspx) · [Revenue, Taxes Consolidation Act 1997 Part 2 notes, ss 23 and 25–27](https://www.revenue.ie/en/tax-professionals/documents/notes-for-guidance/tca/part02.pdf) · [Finance Act 2021, s 18](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/eli/2021/act/45/section/18/enacted/en/html) · [Taxes Consolidation Act 1997, s 13](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/eli/1997/act/39/section/13/enacted/en/html) · [Maritime Jurisdiction Act 2021, s 29](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/eli/2021/act/28/section/29/enacted/en/html)

## Residence

The ordinary individual tests are at least 183 days in the year or at least 280 days across that year and the preceding year. A year with 30 days or fewer is disregarded for the 2-year calculation. Presence for part of a day generally counts. Check the transit and unavoidable-departure rules, any arrival-year election and separate ordinary residence and domicile. An Irish-incorporated company is generally resident unless a tax treaty treats it as resident elsewhere and outside Ireland. The older incorporation transition ended on 31 December 2020. For a foreign-incorporated company, check where central management and control takes place and then any applicable treaty. [Revenue, individual residence tests](https://www.revenue.ie/en/jobs-and-pensions/tax-residence/resident-for-tax-purposes.aspx) · [Finance Act 2014, s 43](https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/eli/2014/act/37/section/43/enacted/en/html) · [Revenue, company residence rules](https://www.revenue.ie/en/companies-and-charities/corporation-tax-for-companies/corporation-tax/company-residency-rules.aspx)

## Taxpayer types

A sole trader reports their own trading profits and a company accounts for its own corporation tax. The precedent partner files Form 1 (Firms) for an ordinary trading partnership and each partner accounts for their share under their applicable tax rules, including s 1009 for a corporate partner. For a trust, identify who is assessed on the income and whether a beneficiary paid from that income may claim credit for tax already paid. Check the trust instrument and separate trust registration, capital gains and discretionary trust rules where relevant. [Revenue, business forms](https://www.revenue.ie/en/starting-a-business/what-is-a-business/index.aspx) · [Revenue, Taxes Consolidation Act 1997 Part 43 notes, ss 1008–1009](https://www.revenue.ie/en/tax-professionals/documents/notes-for-guidance/tca/part43.pdf) · [Revenue, trustees](https://www.revenue.ie/en/life-events-and-personal-circumstances/death-and-bereavement/information-on-tax-after-a-bereavement/what-is-a-trustee.aspx)

## Currency

Ireland uses the euro (EUR). The EU regulation lists 1 January 1999 as Ireland's adoption date and 1 January 2002 as the cash changeover. Record amounts in their original currency and retain the conversion basis and evidence for the applicable return. [Council Regulation (EC) No 974/98, arts 1a and 2 and Annex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/1998/974/2026-01-01/eng)

## Assessment periods

The individual tax year runs from 1 January to 31 December. Corporation tax is assessed by accounting period and a period cannot exceed 12 months. Check when the company comes into charge and any earlier end caused by its accounts, trade, residence or cessation of charge. Keep PAYE, VAT and other return periods separate. [Revenue, the tax system in Ireland](https://www.revenue.ie/en/life-events-and-personal-circumstances/moving-to-or-from-ireland/moving-or-returning-to-ireland/the-tax-system-in-ireland.aspx) · [Revenue, Taxes Consolidation Act 1997 Part 2 notes, s 27](https://www.revenue.ie/en/tax-professionals/documents/notes-for-guidance/tca/part02.pdf)

## Responsible authorities

The Office of the Revenue Commissioners administers taxes and customs duties and carries out customs controls. Revenue also collects PRSI on behalf of the Department of Social Protection. Identify the correct Revenue registration and filing service for the taxpayer and obligation. [Revenue, core business](https://www.revenue.ie/en/corporate/information-about-revenue/role-of-revenue/core-business.aspx) · [Revenue, employers’ PAYE guide, s 1.8.5](https://www.revenue.ie/en/tax-professionals/tdm/income-tax-capital-gains-tax-corporation-tax/part-42/42-04-35a.pdf)

## Fictional intake

A company incorporated abroad makes its strategic decisions in Dublin and closes its accounts on 30 September 2026. Determine its domestic residence and check any treaty before classifying profits. Confirm the corporation tax accounting period, review the source of foreign currency receipts and keep the conversion evidence. Assess the directors' personal residence separately using their own days of presence.
