# Saint Lucia tax system orientation

The Inland Revenue Department (IRD) administers income tax and domestic VAT. Customs collects VAT on imported goods. Amounts in this guide are in Eastern Caribbean dollars (XCD). [Income Tax Act, s 3][AGENCY] · [VAT Act, s 22][IMPORT] · [ECCB, payment system strategy][CURRENCY]

## Residence and source

An individual can be resident under the 183-day test, the permanent-abode and presence test or continuous presence across a qualifying adjacent income year. The abode test accommodates specified absences. A company is resident if incorporated in Saint Lucia or, if incorporated abroad, managed and controlled in Saint Lucia. [Income Tax Act, s 2][RESIDENCE]

A resident individual's starting point is worldwide income. A resident individual who is not ordinarily resident brings in foreign income to the extent received in Saint Lucia. A resident company excludes foreign-source income within the section 10A definition. Non-residents generally bring in Saint Lucia-source income, with separate withholding rules for income outside employment and business through a permanent establishment. [Income Tax Act, ss 7, 8 and 10A][SOURCE] · [Foreign-source definition][FOREIGN]

## Individuals and companies

The individual bands introduced in 2023 apply 15% to the first XCD 15,000 of chargeable income, 20% to the next XCD 15,000 and 30% to the rest. A resident individual's personal allowance is XCD 25,000 from 1 January 2023. From 1 January 2025 the aggregate cap for the deductions and allowances covered by section 44(4) is XCD 40,000, with separate treatment for the personal allowance and medical deductions and an additional solar exclusion for income year 2025. Act No. 10 of 2026 adds a separate interest deduction outside the cap for qualifying first-time residential landowners whose deed is registered from 2 May 2026. [Income Tax Act, Schedules 5 and 6][RATES] · [Personal allowance][ALLOWANCE] · [Act No. 2 of 2026, s 4][ACT2] · [Act No. 10 of 2026, s 3][ACT10]

The IRD identifies 30% as the ordinary company income tax rate. Schedule 5 records 30% for companies within its paragraph 2(a)(vi) conditions from the 2005 income year. Check company category, compliance conditions and applicable relief before using the rate. [Income Tax Act, Schedule 5][RATES] · [IRD, Know Your Taxes][IRDTAX]

## Transactions and filing

The standard VAT rate is 12.5%. The tourism order sets 7% for qualifying tourism accommodation and 10% for specified restaurant, water sports, domestic tour and attraction supplies. Check the activity definition, registration and any time-limited relief. Taxable persons generally file VAT returns within 21 calendar days after each tax period. [VAT Act, ss 10 and 37][VAT] · [Tourism sector order, paragraph 3][TOURISM] · [VAT return deadlines][VATFILE]

For income tax, section 84 gives a general filing period of 3 months after the year of income. The 2025 filing round received an extension to 10 April 2026 for the taxpayers listed in the IRD notice. The July 2026 amendment permits qualifying company filing extensions up to 5 months from the end of the year of assessment, subject to the new section 96(3) conditions. Confirm the company's applicable filing date and its payment date separately. [Income Tax Act, ss 84 and 96][FILE] · [IRD, 31 March 2026 notice][EXTENSION] · [Act No. 10 of 2026, s 4][ACT10]
