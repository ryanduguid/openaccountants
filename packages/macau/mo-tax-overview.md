# Macao tax overview

Identify where the work or business activity took place, the type of income and the tax period. The Macao Special Administrative Region has its own tax system. The Fiscal Code generally applied from 1 January 2026. Its tax-residence definition applied from 1 January 2025. [Law 24/2024, art 29 and Fiscal Code art 24][code]

## Scope and taxpayers

Complementary income tax applies to the combined income from commercial or industrial activity earned in Macao by individuals or legal entities, regardless of where they reside or have their head office. From 2026, the separate rule for Macao-resident constituent entities of multinational groups also brings specified foreign dividends, interest, royalties and capital gains into scope. Check the entity definition, source and any applicable credit separately. For complementary tax, determine whether the taxpayer is in Group A, assessed on profits supported by organised accounts, or Group B, assessed on presumed profits. [Complementary Income Tax Regulation, arts 1-A, 2–4 and 51-A][profits] · [Law 24/2024, arts 10 and 29][code]

Professional tax covers remuneration from work undertaken in Macao, including remuneration paid in kind or paid outside Macao. Group 1 covers employed individuals and Group 2 covers self-employed individuals carrying on the listed liberal or technical professions. For other self-employment, check the trade's classification and the complementary tax rules. [Professional Tax Regulation, arts 2, 3 and 5][professional] · [Financial Services Bureau salaries tax guidance][salaries]

## Residence and currency

An individual is resident if they spend at least 183 days in Macao during the calendar year to which the income relates. Whole or partial days count. An individual with fewer days can qualify if they have a home in Macao on 31 December in circumstances showing an intention to maintain and occupy it as their habitual home. Companies and equivalent entities are resident if their head office or effective management is in Macao. Confirm the facts and any treaty rule before relying on this classification. [Fiscal Code, art 24][code]

The currency is the pataca (MOP). Enter M/1 amounts in patacas and check which exchange rate and date apply to foreign amounts. [Monetary Issuance Law 10/2023, art 2][currency] · [Financial Services Bureau Group A guidance][group-a]

## Period and filing authority

Use the Financial Services Bureau, known as DSF, and separate the calendar year of the income from the following filing year. For 2025 complementary income, the 2026 M/1 filing windows were January to March for Group B and April to July for Group A. The 2026 Budget Law sets the 2025 complementary income exemption at MOP 600,000, with 12% on the amount above it. Check the measure for the income year at issue. [Complementary Income Tax Regulation, art 10][profits] · [2025 Group B return notice][group-b] · [Group A filing guidance][group-a] · [Budget Law 13/2025, art 22][budget]

For 2025 professional income, the order extending the article 10 M/5 deadline to 31 March 2026 covered Group 1 taxpayers with multiple payers and Group 2 taxpayers without organised accounts. Group 2 taxpayers with organised accounts had until 15 April 2026 under article 11. Check whether an annual declaration is required for the actual case. [Order 35/2026][order] · [DSF March notice][march] · [DSF April notice][april]

For 2025 professional income, the ordinary exemption was MOP 144,000 and the tax calculated on income above the exemption was reduced by 30%. The 2026 Budget Law sets the same exemption and tax reduction for 2026 professional income. In both years, the exemption is MOP 198,000 for employees and wage earners over 65 or with a certified permanent disability of at least 60%. [Budget Law 25/2024, art 19][budget-2025] · [Budget Law 13/2025, art 18][budget]

Use the [complementary income tax guide](mo-corporate-income-tax.md), [payroll guide](mo-payroll-social.md) and [indirect tax guide](mo-vat-gst.md) for specialist treatment. Confirm actual filing, payment, relief, property and registration separately.
