# Monaco tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the taxpayer, activity, location of operations and accounting period. Check the rules of any other country that claims taxing rights.

## Territorial reach

Monaco has no general personal income tax. French nationals covered by art 7 of the 1963 convention are subject to French income tax as if domiciled or resident in France, subject to that article's exceptions. Monaco's business profits tax (ISB) applies to industrial or commercial enterprises operating in Monaco that earn at least 25% of turnover from operations outside Monaco and to companies whose Monaco activity consists of receiving specified industrial, literary or artistic property income. Determine outside turnover under arts 2–3. Exclude profits and losses of foreign permanent establishments, habitual complete operations abroad and the specified foreign representative activity from the tax base. [Monaco Government guide, ch 2](https://monservicepublic.gouv.mc/content/download/48067/file/welcomeofficeFR.pdf?inLanguage=fre-FR&version=1) · [France–Monaco Tax Convention, art 7](https://legimonaco.mc/tai/convention/1963/05-18-tai1l000235/) · [ISB Ordinance 3.152, arts 1–3](https://legimonaco.mc/tnc/ordonnance/1964/03-19-3.152/index.html)

## Residence

For a residence certificate for tax formalities, a Monegasque applicant establishes identity and residence. Other applicants establish a valid residence card and housing and document their principal stay, household or main centre of activity, subject to applicable agreements. The principal-stay test includes at least 183 days in Monaco in a year or a shorter stay that exceeds the stays in other countries. Consider the household only if the principal stay cannot be determined. For business profits tax, apply the enterprise's activity and turnover or specified property-income test, including for an enterprise whose registered office is abroad. [Residence Certificate Ordinance 8.566, arts 3–4](https://legimonaco.mc/tnc/ordonnance/1986/03-28-8.566/) · [ISB Ordinance 3.152, arts 1 and 23](https://legimonaco.mc/tnc/ordonnance/1964/03-19-3.152/index.html)

## Taxpayer types

The business profits tax can apply across legal forms. The assessment is made in the name of the entity, association or group without legal personality for its taxable profits, or in the sole trader's name for an individually operated enterprise. A sole trader whose enterprise meets the charging conditions is liable for ISB. Check any exemption for the particular body and activity. [ISB Ordinance 3.152, arts 1 and 4–5](https://legimonaco.mc/tnc/ordonnance/1964/03-19-3.152/index.html)

## Currency

Monaco uses the euro (EUR). Retain foreign-currency source amounts and the conversion support for the figures used in the tax return and accounts. [EU–Monaco Monetary Agreement, art 1](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A02012A1013%2801%29-20260324) · [ISB Ordinance 3.152, art 23](https://legimonaco.mc/tnc/ordonnance/1964/03-19-3.152/index.html)

## Assessment periods

The ISB assessment uses the tax year's profits or the results of the accounting period that closed in that year, including a shorter or longer period. If no accounts close during the year, assess to 31 December from the last taxed period's end or from commencement for a new business. Deduct the amounts already assessed when the accounts later include them. Combine results of successive accounts closed in the same year. Check filing dates separately. [ISB Ordinance 3.152, arts 6 and 23](https://legimonaco.mc/tnc/ordonnance/1964/03-19-3.152/index.html)

## Responsible authorities

Submit business profits returns to the Direction des Services Fiscaux. For periods opening from 1 January 2026, a statutory information request to a business liable for ISB also serves as formal notice to submit the return. The Direction de la Sûreté Publique handles requests for an individual's residence certificate for tax formalities. Check French administration requirements separately for a taxpayer covered by the bilateral convention. [ISB Ordinance 3.152, art 23](https://legimonaco.mc/tnc/ordonnance/1964/03-19-3.152/index.html) · [Ordinance 12.211 of 2026, arts 1 and 4](https://journaldemonaco.gouv.mc/fr/Journaux/2026/Journal-8823/Ordonnance-Souveraine-n-12.211-du-23-octobre-2026-modifiant-l-Ordonnance-Souveraine-n-3.152-du-19-mars-1964-instituant-un-impot-sur-les-benefices-modifiee) · [Residence Certificate Ordinance 8.566, art 4](https://legimonaco.mc/tnc/ordonnance/1986/03-28-8.566/)

## Fictional intake

A Monaco sole trader sells equipment to customers abroad and carries out local retail sales for cash. Classify turnover under the ordinance before testing whether at least 25% comes from operations outside Monaco. If the enterprise meets the ISB test, identify the relevant accounting period and assess the profits in the operator's name.
