# Dominica tax system orientation

The Inland Revenue Division (IRD) administers income tax and VAT in the Commonwealth of Dominica. Amounts in this guide are in Eastern Caribbean dollars (XCD). [IRD, tax rates][RATES]

## Residence and source

The Income Tax Act includes a 183-day basis-period test for individual residence, alongside tests based on permanent abode and presence or presence continuous with a qualifying adjacent period. A company can be resident by local incorporation, or, if incorporated abroad, by management and control in Dominica. Check the statutory basis period and any treaty separately. [Income Tax Act, Chap. 67:01, ss 2 and 11][LAW]

Residents generally bring in income from all sources. A resident individual who is not ordinarily resident generally brings in foreign income to the extent received in Dominica. Non-residents generally bring in Dominica-source income. Specific non-resident income outside local business or employment can fall under withholding instead. [Income Tax Act, ss 7–10][LAW]

## Individuals and companies

For 2026, the ordinary resident personal allowance is XCD 30,000. The progressive rates after allowances are 15% on the first XCD 20,000 of chargeable income, 25% on the next XCD 30,000 and 35% on the balance. Parliament approved a 10% flat personal rate from January 2027. Confirm the gazetted provisions and the treatment of foreign income for a 2027 engagement. [IRD, current rates][RATES] · [IRD, personal return and rate computation][FORM] · [Office of the Prime Minister, November 2026 passage][REFORM]

Companies ordinarily pay 25% on chargeable income. The IRD requires the company return and financial statement within three months of financial year end. Check instalments, sector rules and applicable relief separately. Personal returns are generally due by 31 March after the calendar year, subject to the filing rules and exceptions. [IRD, company income tax][CORP] · [Income Tax Act, s 66][LAW] · [IRD, personal income tax][PERSONAL]

## Transactions and filing

The ordinary VAT rate on local taxable supplies is 15%. Qualifying accommodation and diving activity are subject to 10%. Check registration and any exemptions or zero rating before invoicing. VAT can also apply to imports. The Office of the Prime Minister reports that Parliament passed amendments ending the compounding of VAT and excise on imports with effect from 1 October 2026. Verify the amended valuation provisions and applicable Customs treatment for the transaction. [IRD, VAT][VAT] · [Value Added Tax Act 2005, s 9][VATLAW] · [Office of the Prime Minister, import amendments][IMPORT]
