# Qatar Payroll & Social Contributions

## Social insurance and payroll contributions

- **Payroll income tax withholding (PAYE-equivalent)** — None — there is no income tax withholding on employee salaries  _([Income Tax Law No. 24 of 2018](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/qatar/corporate/other-taxes))_
- **Social insurance scope** — Applies to Qatari (and GCC) national employees in public and private sectors; expatriate (non-Qatari) employees are excluded  _([Social Insurance Law No. 1 of 2022](https://www.tamimi.com/news/new-social-security-law/))_
- **Total social insurance contribution** — 21% percent (of the contributory wage)  _([Social Insurance Law No. 1 of 2022](https://www.tamimi.com/news/new-social-security-law/))_
- **Employee social insurance contribution (Qatari nationals)** — 7% percent (of the contributory wage)  _([Social Insurance Law No. 1 of 2022](https://www.tamimi.com/news/new-social-security-law/))_
- **Employer social insurance contribution (Qatari nationals)** — 14% percent (of the contributory wage)  _([Social Insurance Law No. 1 of 2022](https://www.tamimi.com/news/new-social-security-law/))_
- **Contributory wage base** — Basic salary plus social and housing allowance  _([Social Insurance Law No. 1 of 2022](https://www.tamimi.com/news/new-social-security-law/))_
- **Contributory wage cap** — QAR 100,000 per month (existing enrolments above this amount may be grandfathered) QAR/month  _([Social Insurance Law No. 1 of 2022](https://www.tamimi.com/news/new-social-security-law/))_
- **Employer contributions for non-Qatari (expat) employees** — None — no social security contributions are due for expatriate employees (end-of-service gratuity is a separate Labour Law obligation)  _([Labour Law No. 14 of 2004](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/qatar/corporate/other-taxes))_
- **Contribution remittance deadline** — By the 5th day of the month following the month for which contributions are payable  _([Social Insurance Law No. 1 of 2022](https://www.tamimi.com/news/new-social-security-law/))_
