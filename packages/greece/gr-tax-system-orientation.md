# Greece tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the taxpayer's residence and presence, the place of effective management, the legal form, each income source and the accounting period.

## Territorial reach

Greek tax residents generally account for taxable domestic and foreign income. Nonresidents account for taxable Greek-source income. Article 5 lists the domestic-source categories, including specified work performed in Greece, Greek real estate and business income from activities through a Greek permanent establishment. Apply the regime, exemption or treaty that applies to the specific receipt. [Income Tax Code, arts 3–6](https://minfin.gov.gr/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/%CE%9A%CE%A9%CE%94%CE%99%CE%9A%CE%9F%CE%A0%CE%9F%CE%99%CE%97%CE%A3%CE%97-%CE%A4%CE%9F%CE%A5-%CE%9A%CE%A9%CE%94%CE%99%CE%9A%CE%91-%CE%A6%CE%9F%CE%A1%CE%9F%CE%9B%CE%9F%CE%93%CE%99%CE%91%CE%A3-%CE%95%CE%99%CE%A3%CE%9F%CE%94%CE%97%CE%9C%CE%91%CE%A4%CE%9F%CE%A3-%CE%9C%CE%95%CE%A7%CE%A1%CE%99-%CE%A4%CE%9F-%CE%9D-5264-2025.pdf) · [Independent Authority for Public Revenue, Greek-source income](https://www.aade.gr/en/greeks-abroad-non-residents/income-taxation/taxation-greek-sourced-income-obtained-non-residents-pit)

## Residence

An individual is generally resident if they have a permanent or principal home, habitual abode or centre of vital interests in Greece. Presence for more than 183 days in total in any 12-month period generally establishes residence from the first day of presence, subject to the personal-purpose exception for qualifying stays of up to 365 days. Check the other residence tests separately. A legal person or entity generally qualifies under art 4(3) if formed under Greek law, if its statutory seat is in Greece or if its effective management is in Greece during the tax year. Test management using the actual arrangements and decisions and apply any treaty separately. [Income Tax Code, art 4](https://minfin.gov.gr/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/%CE%9A%CE%A9%CE%94%CE%99%CE%9A%CE%9F%CE%A0%CE%9F%CE%99%CE%97%CE%A3%CE%97-%CE%A4%CE%9F%CE%A5-%CE%9A%CE%A9%CE%94%CE%99%CE%9A%CE%91-%CE%A6%CE%9F%CE%A1%CE%9F%CE%9B%CE%9F%CE%93%CE%99%CE%91%CE%A3-%CE%95%CE%99%CE%A3%CE%9F%CE%94%CE%97%CE%9C%CE%91%CE%A4%CE%9F%CE%A3-%CE%9C%CE%95%CE%A7%CE%A1%CE%99-%CE%A4%CE%9F-%CE%9D-5264-2025.pdf) · [Independent Authority for Public Revenue, individual residence](https://aade.gr/en/greeks-abroad-non-residents/income-taxation/tax-residence-natural-persons-itc)

## Taxpayer types

An individual sole trader accounts for business profits under the personal income tax rules. Ordinary capital companies and personal companies, including Greek general and limited partnerships, are within the entity income tax rules. Check any subsequent profit allocation or distribution separately.

The Income Tax Code includes trusts in its legal-entity definition. The revenue authority's foreign-trust guidance distinguishes the trust's own Greek-source income from money or assets received by a beneficiary. Obtain the deed and determine who contributed the capital and whether the payment is a profit distribution or a transfer of property. Apply the current tax provisions for the period before relying on the classification. [Income Tax Code, arts 2, 21(1), 45 and 47](https://minfin.gov.gr/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/%CE%9A%CE%A9%CE%94%CE%99%CE%9A%CE%9F%CE%A0%CE%9F%CE%99%CE%97%CE%A3%CE%97-%CE%A4%CE%9F%CE%A5-%CE%9A%CE%A9%CE%94%CE%99%CE%9A%CE%91-%CE%A6%CE%9F%CE%A1%CE%9F%CE%9B%CE%9F%CE%93%CE%99%CE%91%CE%A3-%CE%95%CE%99%CE%A3%CE%9F%CE%94%CE%97%CE%9C%CE%91%CE%A4%CE%9F%CE%A3-%CE%9C%CE%95%CE%A7%CE%A1%CE%99-%CE%A4%CE%9F-%CE%9D-5264-2025.pdf) · [Independent Authority for Public Revenue, entity return instructions](https://www.aade.gr/sites/default/files/2026-03/odigies_entypoy_%20N_fe2025.pdf) · [Independent Authority for Public Revenue, circular POL.1114/2017](https://www.aade.gr/egkyklioi-kai-apofaseis/pol-1114-24-07-2017)

## Currency

Greece uses the euro (EUR). Under the Greek Accounting Standards, convert foreign-currency transactions to the applicable reporting currency under art 27. Retain the transaction and closing rates used for business records. [Greek Accounting Standards, Law 4308/2014, art 27](https://www.aade.gr/sites/default/files/2022-02/n4308_2014.pdf) · [Independent Authority for Public Revenue, foreign-currency records guidance](https://www.aade.gr/sites/default/files/2018-12/1190552_2018.pdf)

## Assessment periods

The default tax year is the calendar year. Legal persons or entities with double-entry books may use a year ending 30 June. A Greek-resident entity more than 50% owned by a foreign entity can use that foreign entity's tax year under art 8(2). Check the maximum period and the applicable rules on starting or ceasing business. [Income Tax Code, art 8](https://minfin.gov.gr/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/%CE%9A%CE%A9%CE%94%CE%99%CE%9A%CE%9F%CE%A0%CE%9F%CE%99%CE%97%CE%A3%CE%97-%CE%A4%CE%9F%CE%A5-%CE%9A%CE%A9%CE%94%CE%99%CE%9A%CE%91-%CE%A6%CE%9F%CE%A1%CE%9F%CE%9B%CE%9F%CE%93%CE%99%CE%91%CE%A3-%CE%95%CE%99%CE%A3%CE%9F%CE%94%CE%97%CE%9C%CE%91%CE%A4%CE%9F%CE%A3-%CE%9C%CE%95%CE%A7%CE%A1%CE%99-%CE%A4%CE%9F-%CE%9D-5264-2025.pdf)

## Responsible authorities

The Independent Authority for Public Revenue (AADE) administers national tax and customs. Use the relevant myAADE service or tax office for registration and income tax returns. [Independent Authority for Public Revenue, responsibilities](https://www.aade.gr/en/aade/mission-responsibilities) · [Independent Authority for Public Revenue, entity returns](https://www.aade.gr/dilosi-forologias-eisodimatos-nomikon-prosopon-ontotiton)

## Fictional intake

A person spends part of the year in Greece, retains an overseas home and receives a payment from a foreign trust. Establish the dates of presence and the facts for the other residence tests, then trace the payment under the trust documents before classifying it and applying any treaty.
