# Belize Personal Income Tax

## Personal income tax rates and thresholds

- **Non-resident income tax rate** — 25 percent (25% on Belize-source income (personal reliefs generally restricted for non-residents) (approx — confirm non-resident relief treatment))  _(Income and Business Tax Act (Cap. 55))_
- **Tax-free threshold (employees)** — 29,000 BZD (Employment income of BZD 29,000 or less is exempt (2025 amendment — confirm enacted figure))  _(Income and Business Tax Act (Cap. 55))_
- **Personal relief — earnings above BZD 29,000** — 19,600 BZD (personal relief (approx — confirm figure))  _(Income and Business Tax Act (Cap. 55))_
- **Tapered relief — earnings BZD 26,001 to 27,000** — 24,600 BZD (relief (approx — confirm figure))  _(Income and Business Tax Act (Cap. 55))_
- **Tapered relief — earnings BZD 27,001 to 29,000** — 22,600 BZD (relief (approx — confirm figure))  _(Income and Business Tax Act (Cap. 55))_
- **Non-resident income tax rate** — 25 percent (25% on Belize-source income (personal reliefs generally restricted for non-residents) (approx — confirm non-resident relief treatment))  _(Income and Business Tax Act (Cap. 55))_
- **Charitable donations relief** — Approved charitable donations deductible (subject to limits) (approx — confirm cap)  _(Income and Business Tax Act (Cap. 55))_
- **Education / dependent allowances** — Allowances for spouse, children and education may apply within the personal relief framework (approx — confirm current allowances)  _(Income and Business Tax Act (Cap. 55))_
- **Residence test** — Generally based on physical presence in Belize; a common benchmark is 183 days in the tax year (approx — confirm day-count rule in statute)  _(Income and Business Tax Act (Cap. 55))_
- **Employer withholding (PAYE)** — Employers deduct income tax at source from chargeable employment income and remit to BTS  _(Income and Business Tax Act (Cap. 55))_
- **Annual return filing deadline** — 31 March following year-end  _([Income and Business Tax Act (Cap. 55)](https://bts.gov.bz/))_
