# Andorra tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the taxpayer type, residence facts, income sources and relevant period. Check any treaty that assigns residence or limits source-country tax.

## Territorial reach

Andorra's individual income tax (IRPF) covers a resident's income from Andorra and abroad. The ordinary company income tax (IS) covers worldwide income of resident entities, subject to the applicable exemptions and reliefs. Nonresident individuals and entities fall under the nonresident income tax (IRNR) for income within its Andorran source rules. If they operate through an Andorran permanent establishment, include all income attributable to it wherever earned. Otherwise, apply IRNR separately to each taxable item. [IRPF Law, arts 1–4](https://www.portaljuridicandorra.ad/L2014005) · [IS Law, arts 1–4 and 21](https://www.portaljuridicandorra.ad/L2010095) · [IRNR Law, arts 1–4 and 8–11](https://www.portaljuridicandorra.ad/L2010094)

## Residence

An individual is resident if they spend more than 183 days in Andorra in the calendar year or have their main activities or economic interests based there, directly or indirectly. Include sporadic absences in the day count unless the taxpayer establishes residence abroad. Check the rebuttable presumption for a spouse who is not legally separated and minor children resident in Andorra, and the statutory exception for qualifying daily commuters from France or Spain. An entity is resident if it is formed under Andorran law, has its registered office or effective management there, or completes a transfer of its domicile to Andorra under company law. Check any applicable treaty separately. [IRPF Law, art 8](https://www.portaljuridicandorra.ad/L2014005) · [IS Law, art 7](https://www.portaljuridicandorra.ad/L2010095)

## Taxpayer types

A resident sole trader generally reports economic activity under IRPF. A resident company generally accounts for its own income under IS. The statutes attribute income of civil partnerships, undivided estates, co-ownerships and other specified arrangements to their members or beneficiaries. Apply the governing rules or agreements, or equal shares where neither applies, and identify the tax applicable to each recipient. [IRPF Law, arts 7 and 14–17](https://www.portaljuridicandorra.ad/L2014005) · [IS Law, art 6](https://www.portaljuridicandorra.ad/L2010095)

## Currency

Andorra uses the euro (EUR). Entrepreneurs must express bookkeeping entries and items in annual accounts in euros. Retain original currencies, conversion records and supporting documents. [EU–Andorra Monetary Agreement, art 1](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A02011A1217%2801%29-20260324) · [Entrepreneurs' Accounting Law, arts 6–7](https://www.portaljuridicandorra.ad/L2007030)

## Assessment periods

IRPF uses the calendar year, shortened at death if earlier than 31 December. For a company, the tax period follows its financial year and cannot exceed 12 months. Check statutory early termination events such as deregistration or a transfer of residence from Andorra. An Andorran permanent establishment uses its declared accounting period, up to 12 months, or the calendar year by default. Check the filing and payment calendar for each tax separately. [IRPF Law, arts 40–41](https://www.portaljuridicandorra.ad/L2014005) · [IS Law, arts 39–40](https://www.portaljuridicandorra.ad/L2010095) · [IRNR Law, arts 21 and 32](https://www.portaljuridicandorra.ad/L2010094)

## Responsible authorities

Use the Government of Andorra's Departament de Tributs i de Fronteres for national tax registration, assistance and returns. Check the relevant tax and reporting channel for the taxpayer's case. Check parish tax obligations separately with the relevant commune. [Government tax service](https://www.govern.ad/ca/impost-sobre-la-renda-de-les-persones-f%C3%ADsiques) · [IS Law, arts 47 and 57–58](https://www.portaljuridicandorra.ad/L2010095)

## Fictional intake

A consultant spends 140 days in Andorra but directs their main business there. They also share income from a civil partnership. Check the economic-interests residence test and any treaty before assigning the partnership income under the governing agreement and identifying the return that includes it.
