# Malaysia tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the taxpayer, where income arose, residence facts and period. Check the applicable treaty and foreign-tax relief.

## Territorial reach

The Income Tax Act charges income accruing in or derived from Malaysia and income received in Malaysia from abroad, subject to statutory exclusions and exemptions. Identify the activities and statutory source rule for each receipt. Check how and when foreign income was received and whether the taxpayer and income qualify for an exemption. An eligible resident individual's foreign-income exemption has separate conditions and an exception for income from a Malaysian partnership business. Check any applicable treaty and the separate rules for nonresident business and withholding. [Income Tax Act, ss 3–4 and 12–15A](https://lom.agc.gov.my/ilims/upload/portal/akta/outputaktap/3345910_BI/Act%2053%20%28Online%202026%29.pdf) · [HASiL individual return notes](https://www.hasil.gov.my/media/brdldjmx/explanatorynotes_b2023_2.pdf) · [Foreign income exemption extension from 2027](https://lom.agc.gov.my/ilims/upload/portal/akta/outputp/2584396/PUA%20451_2024.pdf)

## Residence

An individual ordinarily meets the first residence test with at least 182 days in Malaysia in the basis year. Also check the linked period of at least 182 consecutive days in an adjacent year, including allowed temporary absences. Another route applies after at least 90 days in the basis year if the individual met the residence or 90-day condition in at least 3 of the preceding 4 years. A separate route uses residence in each of the preceding 3 basis years and the following basis year. Check the special rule for certain citizens in public service abroad.

A trading company or body of persons generally qualifies if management and control of at least one business is exercised in Malaysia at any time during the basis year. For a nontrading entity within section 8(1)(c), check where the directors or other controlling authority manage and control its affairs. Check the separate limited liability partnership and trust provisions. [Income Tax Act, ss 7–8](https://lom.agc.gov.my/ilims/upload/portal/akta/outputaktap/3345910_BI/Act%2053%20%28Online%202026%29.pdf) · [HASiL residence guidance](https://www.hasil.gov.my/en/individu/taraf-mastautin/?isStampsSite=false&lang=en)

## Taxpayer types

Classify the individual, company, ordinary partnership, limited liability partnership or other taxpayer. A sole trader reports business income as an individual. Compute the ordinary partnership income and allocate each partner's share for assessment to the partner. A limited liability partnership is separately assessed under its own rules. [Income Tax Act, ss 2, 6 and 55–59](https://lom.agc.gov.my/ilims/upload/portal/akta/outputaktap/3345910_BI/Act%2053%20%28Online%202026%29.pdf) · [Public Ruling 8/2021, partnership income](https://phl.hasil.gov.my/pdf/pdfam/PR_08_2021.pdf) · [Public Ruling 3/2014, limited liability partnerships](https://phl.hasil.gov.my/pdf/pdfam/PR_3_2014.pdf)

## Currency

Report the relevant amounts in Malaysian ringgit (MYR). Keep the transaction currency, date and rate used. For business exchange differences, check the treatment of revenue versus capital items and the realisation rule before recognising a gain or loss. [HASiL individual rate tables in ringgit](https://www.hasil.gov.my/en/individu/kadar-cukai/) · [Public Ruling 12/2019, foreign exchange](https://phl.hasil.gov.my/pdf/pdfam/PR_12_2019.pdf)

## Assessment periods

The year of assessment is a calendar year. Individuals generally use the corresponding basis year. For a company, limited liability partnership, trust body or cooperative, check the actual accounting period ending in that basis year and the specific rules for commencement and changes of accounting date. [Income Tax Act, ss 2 and 20–21A](https://lom.agc.gov.my/ilims/upload/portal/akta/outputaktap/3345910_BI/Act%2053%20%28Online%202026%29.pdf)

## Responsible authorities

The Inland Revenue Board of Malaysia (HASiL) and the Director General administer income tax. Use MyTax for applicable registration and returns. Use the Royal Malaysian Customs Department for border taxes and the sales and service tax system. [HASiL](https://www.hasil.gov.my/) · [Royal Malaysian Customs Department](https://www.customs.gov.my/)

## Fictional intake

An individual spends 170 days in Malaysia during the assessment year and links this stay to 190 consecutive days in Malaysia beginning in the adjacent year. Check the linked periods and any temporary absences before determining residence. Then check the source and receipt of each income item.
