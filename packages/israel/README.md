# Israel — Tax Skills Package

## What's in this folder

### Core skills
1. `foundation.md` — How the system works (conservative defaults, classification contract, output requirements)
2. `intake.md` — Onboarding questions and document collection for Israel
3. `israel-vat.md` — VAT (Ma'am — מע"מ) return preparation, supplier patterns, filing mechanics

### References
- `references.md` — Related open-source Israeli tax projects (license compatibility notes, integration approach)

### Complementary skills
4. `il-freelancer-ops.md` — Freelancer operations: Osek Murshe/Patur/Esek Za'ir, invoice requirements, deadline calendar, threshold monitoring, Bituach Leumi, accountant packages
5. `il-tax-withholding.md` — Withholding tax (Nikui Mas BeMakor — ניכוי מס במקור): rates, certificates, Form 102, Form 856, cross-border payments
6. `il-income-tax-returns.md` — Annual income tax returns: Form 1301/1214, brackets, Nekudot Zikui, pension credits, surtax, capital gains, Mikdamot
7. `il-corporate-tax.md` — Corporate tax strategy: salary vs dividend vs shareholder loan vs management fees, Section 3(tet), Chevra Me'atim rules
8. `il-employee-tax-refund.md` — Employee tax refund claims: Form 135, Form 106, 6-year window, refund triggers, reserve duty credits, donations
9. `il-crypto-tax.md` — Cryptocurrency tax reporting: FIFO cost basis, 25% capital gains, DeFi classification, Forms 1322/1325, voluntary disclosure
10. `il-customs-duty.md` — Customs and import duties: personal import threshold, HS codes, CIF valuation, cascading tax calculation, FTA preferences

## How to use

1. Upload ALL files in this folder to your AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, etc.)
2. Attach your 2025 bank statement (CSV or PDF)
3. Say: **"Help me with my 2025 Israel taxes. Here's my bank statement."**

The AI will:
- Ask a few onboarding questions to confirm your situation
- Classify every transaction on your bank statement
- Produce working papers for each tax obligation
- Flag anything that needs your qualified tax professional's attention

## Important

**This is not tax advice.** Everything produced must be reviewed and signed off by a qualified qualified tax professional before filing.

## Found an error? Improve this skill.

Tax rules change. Rates get updated. Thresholds move. If something in these files is wrong for your country:

1. Use Claude or ChatGPT with deep research to verify: *"Search [country] tax authority website for current VAT rate and compare against this skill"*
2. Fork the repo: [github.com/openaccountants/openaccountants](https://github.com/openaccountants/openaccountants)
3. Fix the error in `skills/` (the source files)
4. Submit a PR — your name goes on the skill as a verified contributor

Know a vendor pattern we're missing? Know how your local bank formats statements? Every pattern you add saves the next user from a misclassification.
