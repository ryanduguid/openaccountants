# Papua New Guinea Payroll & Social Contributions

## Salary and wages tax (PAYE-equivalent)

- **Salary and wages tax overview** — Employers withhold salary or wages tax (SWT) from employee pay using fortnightly tax tables and remit it to the IRC. SWT operates as PNG's pay-as-you-earn equivalent.
- **Salary and wages tax (SWT)** — Every fortnight the employer totals each employee's salary, wages, benefits and allowances, calculates the tax from the fortnightly tables and deducts it; the deduction finalises the employee's liability for that fortnight  _(Internal Revenue Commission, Salary and Wages Tax (rates from 1 January 2024) — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/know-your-taxes/salary-wages-tax)_
- **SWT remittance deadline** — Deductions are remitted monthly by the 7th of the following month; an employer who fails to remit pays a 20% penalty plus 20% a year calculated daily  _(Internal Revenue Commission, Key Tax Dates — https://irc.gov.pg/key-tax-dates ; Internal Revenue Commission, Salary and Wages Tax (rates from 1 January 2024) — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/know-your-taxes/salary-wages-tax)_
- **SWT rate range** — Resident tables (Part 7 Table 1, from 1 January 2024): 0% to K20,000, then 30% to K33,000, 35% to K70,000, 40% to K250,000 and 42% above. Non-resident tables for 2025 (Table 2A, from 1 January 2025): 30% from the first kina to K33,000, then the same 35%, 40% and 42% bands; the 22% band on the first K20,000 that the IRC's 2024 page still shows ended on 31 December 2024. From the 2026 tax year the Income Tax Act 2025 sets the fortnightly rates in the Act itself: residents 0% to K770, 30% to K1,270, 35% to K2,693, 40% to K9,615 and 42% above; non-residents the same bands with 22% on the first K770  _(Income Tax (Salary or Wages Tax) (Rates) (2024 Budget) (Amendment) Act 2023 (No. 25 of 2023), Schedule 1 Part 7, PacLII — https://www.paclii.org/pg/legis/num_act/itowt2024ba2023500/ ; Income Tax (Salary or Wages Tax) (Rates) (2025 Budget) (Amendment) Act 2024 (No. 18 of 2024), s 2 and Table 2A, IRC copy — https://static.irc.gov.pg/2025/January/9RMCiB-media-incometax-salaryorwagestaxratesact2024.pdf ; Income Tax Act 2025, Schedule 1 Part I cl (3) and (4) and Schedule 6 cl 2, IRC certified copy — https://static.irc.gov.pg/2025/October/0uvVvY-media-certifiedincometaxact2025.pdf ; Internal Revenue Commission, Salary and Wages Tax (rates from 1 January 2024) — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/know-your-taxes/salary-wages-tax)_
- **Dependant rebate in the fortnightly tables** — The 2024 Budget's repeal of the dependant rebate was put on hold in January 2024 and the IRC did not administer it, so the dependant columns of the fortnightly tables continued to apply; the Income Tax Act 2025 keeps a dependant tax credit (15% of tax for the first dependant up to K450, 10% up to K300 for each other dependant, capped at the greater of 35% of tax or K1,050) and divides it by 26 in the fortnightly withholding formula  _(Internal Revenue Commission, Public Notice: 2024 Budget - Employee Tax-Free Threshold and Dependant Rebate, 19 January 2024, and Salary and Wages Tax Table Text Effective 1 January 2024, both linked from https://irc.gov.pg/pages/know-your-taxes/salary-wages-tax ; Income Tax Act 2025, s 56, Schedule 1 Part II cl (8) to (12) and Schedule 6 cl 2(3), IRC certified copy — https://static.irc.gov.pg/2025/October/0uvVvY-media-certifiedincometaxact2025.pdf)_
- **Annual reconciliation** — By 14 February the employer returns the statements of earnings for the year with a reconciliation of SWT deducted against SWT remitted; a fine of K500 to K5,000 applies for failing to issue or return them  _(Internal Revenue Commission, Salary and Wages Tax — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/know-your-taxes/salary-wages-tax ; Internal Revenue Commission, Information for Employers — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/know-your-taxes/information-for-employers)_

## Superannuation contributions

- **Superannuation contributions overview** — Employers with 15 or more employees must enrol staff in an Authorised Superannuation Fund (ASF, such as Nambawan Super or Nasfund) and contribute on their behalf. Both employer and employee contributions are mandatory above the size threshold; mandatory contributions do not apply to non-citizens, and smaller employers and their employees may contribute voluntarily. The Bank of Papua New Guinea regulates the funds and prosecutes non-compliance (s 114: a fine of up to K500,000 or imprisonment of up to 10 years, or both).
- **Employer superannuation contribution** — 8.4% of the employee's base salary, paid out of the employer's own funds; a contract term allowing less is void and the employer may contribute more  _(Superannuation (General Provisions) Act 2000, s 76 (rate prescribed by regulation), PacLII — https://www.paclii.org/pg/legis/num_act/spa2000396/ ; Bank of Papua New Guinea, Public Notice: Employer and Employee Superannuation Contributions, 21 February 2025 — https://bankpng.gov.pg/sites/default/files/2025-02/20250221%20-%20Public%20Notice%20to%20Employer%20and%20Employee%20Superannuation%20Contribution.pdf)_
- **Employee superannuation contribution** — 6.0% of base salary, deducted from pay by the employer at the time of payment; the employee may contribute more  _(Superannuation (General Provisions) Act 2000, s 77, PacLII — https://www.paclii.org/pg/legis/num_act/spa2000396/ ; Bank of Papua New Guinea, Public Notice: Employer and Employee Superannuation Contributions, 21 February 2025 — https://bankpng.gov.pg/sites/default/files/2025-02/20250221%20-%20Public%20Notice%20to%20Employer%20and%20Employee%20Superannuation%20Contribution.pdf)_
- **Superannuation employer threshold** — The Act applies to every employer employing or engaging 15 or more employees (s 4(1)(a) sets 20 "or such other number as prescribed by regulation", and the Superannuation (Amendment) Regulation 2004 prescribes 15) and to each of their employees continuously employed for three months or more. Mandatory contributions do not apply to non-citizens (ss 76(6) and 77(6), inserted in 2013)  _(Superannuation (General Provisions) Act 2000, s 4, PacLII — https://www.paclii.org/pg/legis/num_act/spa2000396/ ; Superannuation (General Provisions) (Amendment) Act 2013, ss 6 and 7, PacLII — https://www.paclii.org/pg/legis/num_act/spa2013476/ ; Bank of Papua New Guinea, Public Notice: Employer and Employee Superannuation Contributions, 21 February 2025 — https://bankpng.gov.pg/sites/default/files/2025-02/20250221%20-%20Public%20Notice%20to%20Employer%20and%20Employee%20Superannuation%20Contribution.pdf)_
- **Contribution base** — "Pay" is remuneration of any kind, including gross salary, wages and commission, earned while on duty or on paid leave and paid in cash, but excludes overtime pay, allowances, bonuses, compensation and gifts (s 3); the Bank's notice calls this base salary  _(Superannuation (General Provisions) Act 2000, s 3 definition of "pay", PacLII — https://www.paclii.org/pg/legis/num_act/spa2000396/ ; Nasfund, What is superannuation — https://www.nasfund.com.pg/superannuation/about-superannuation/what-is-superannuation)_
- **Superannuation remittance deadline** — Employer contributions are remitted to the ASF within 14 days of the end of each calendar month; employee contributions deducted from pay are remitted within 14 days of the date of deduction. A defaulting employer owes the arrears plus penal interest at the prescribed rate (s 79)  _(Superannuation (General Provisions) Act 2000, ss 78 and 79, PacLII — https://www.paclii.org/pg/legis/num_act/spa2000396/ ; Bank of Papua New Guinea, Public Notice: Employer and Employee Superannuation Contributions, 21 February 2025 — https://bankpng.gov.pg/sites/default/files/2025-02/20250221%20-%20Public%20Notice%20to%20Employer%20and%20Employee%20Superannuation%20Contribution.pdf)_
- **Training levy — abolished** — The training levy under Division III.14D of the Income Tax Act 1959 ceased to apply after 31 December 2017 (s 196ZB), and the double deduction for staff training ended for expenditure after 1 January 2018 (s 72A(6)); the Income Tax Act 2025 carries no training levy. Payroll guides that still list a 2% levy are out of date  _(Income Tax (2018 Budget) (Amendment) Act 2017, ss 6 and 16, PacLII — https://www.paclii.org/pg/legis/num_act/it2018ba2017249/ ; Income Tax Act 2025, Part II, IRC certified copy — https://static.irc.gov.pg/2025/October/0uvVvY-media-certifiedincometaxact2025.pdf)_
