# Papua New Guinea Tax Overview

## PNG tax system at a glance

Papua New Guinea taxes income under the Income Tax Act, administered by the Internal Revenue Commission (IRC). It operates a calendar tax year, has both personal income tax and a 10% GST, and uses a residence-based system under which residents are taxed on worldwide income.

- **Tax year** — 1 January to 31 December; a company lodges on a 31 December year end unless the Commissioner General permits another period  _(Internal Revenue Commission, Taxation of Companies — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/taxes/businesses-and-employers/taxation-of-companies)_
- **Currency** — Papua New Guinea kina (PGK / K)  _(Central Banking Act)_
- **Tax authority** — Internal Revenue Commission (IRC)  _(Income Tax Act — https://irc.gov.pg/)_
- **Basis of taxation** — Residents are taxed on worldwide income; non-residents only on Papua New Guinea-source income, with passive income subject to withholding  _(Internal Revenue Commission, Taxation of Companies — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/taxes/businesses-and-employers/taxation-of-companies ; Internal Revenue Commission, Corporate Tax Rates — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/taxes/businesses-and-employers/corporate-tax-rates)_
- **Top personal income tax rate** — 42% on the part of taxable income above K250,000. Residents pay 0% to K20,000, then 30%, 35% and 40% (tables from 1 January 2024). Non-residents pay 30% from the first kina to K33,000 in 2025 (Table 2A, which replaced the 22% band on the first K20,000 from 1 January 2025); the Income Tax Act 2025 restores the 22% band on a non-resident's first K20,000 from the 2026 tax year  _(Internal Revenue Commission, Salary and Wages Tax (rates from 1 January 2024) — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/know-your-taxes/salary-wages-tax ; Income Tax (Salary or Wages Tax) (Rates) (2025 Budget) (Amendment) Act 2024 (No. 18 of 2024), s 2 and Table 2A, IRC copy — https://static.irc.gov.pg/2025/January/9RMCiB-media-incometax-salaryorwagestaxratesact2024.pdf ; Income Tax Act 2025, Schedule 1 Part I cl (1) and (2), IRC certified copy — https://static.irc.gov.pg/2025/October/0uvVvY-media-certifiedincometaxact2025.pdf)_
- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 30% of taxable income for a resident company and 48% for a non-resident company in the 2025 tax year (Rates Act 1984, Schedule 4). From the 2026 tax year the Income Tax Act 2025 taxes every company other than a superannuation fund or commercial bank at 30%, and a non-resident company's PNG permanent establishment pays 30% on its taxable income plus 15% non-resident tax on its repatriated profit  _(Internal Revenue Commission, Corporate Tax Rates — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/taxes/businesses-and-employers/corporate-tax-rates ; Income Tax, Dividend (Withholding) Tax and Interest (Withholding) Tax Rates Act 1984 (consolidated to 2021), s 6 and Schedule 4, listed on Internal Revenue Commission, Legislation Home — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/know-your-taxes/legislation-home ; Income Tax Act 2025, ss 14(1)(b) and 71 and Schedule 1 Part I cl (5)(d) and (9), IRC certified copy — https://static.irc.gov.pg/2025/October/0uvVvY-media-certifiedincometaxact2025.pdf)_
- **Does PNG have GST/VAT** — Yes; Goods and Services Tax at 10% of the value of goods and services sold or imported, with registration compulsory above K250,000 annual turnover and monthly returns due on the 21st  _(Internal Revenue Commission, Goods and Services Tax — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/taxes/businesses-and-employers/goods-services-tax ; Internal Revenue Commission, Key Tax Dates — https://irc.gov.pg/key-tax-dates)_
- **Individual income tax return deadline** — For 2024 and earlier years the Commissioner General's annual gazette notice under s 223 of the Income Tax Act 1959 set 28 February following the year of income, or a later date under the tax agent lodgement extension program (the 2024 returns were due on or by 28 February 2025); the notice for 2025 returns is gazetted each January. From the 2026 tax year the Income Tax Act 2025 fixes the dates in the Act: individuals and partnerships within 3 months after year end (31 March), companies within 6 months, and companies whose return a registered tax agent prepares within 9 months  _(National Gazette No. G7 of 7 January 2025, Lodgement of 2024 Income Tax Returns, Part E, PacLII — http://www.paclii.org/pg/other/PGGovGaz/2025/7.pdf ; Income Tax Act 2025, s 135, IRC certified copy — https://static.irc.gov.pg/2025/October/0uvVvY-media-certifiedincometaxact2025.pdf)_
- **Personal tax-free threshold** — K20,000: the first K20,000 of a resident's annual salary or wages is taxed at 0% under the tables from 1 January 2024, then 30% to K33,000, 35% to K70,000, 40% to K250,000 and 42% above  _(Internal Revenue Commission, Salary and Wages Tax (rates from 1 January 2024) — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/know-your-taxes/salary-wages-tax)_
