# Papua New Guinea Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate tax rates

- **Corporate tax rates overview** — Resident companies are taxed at a flat 30% on trading profits. Non-resident companies operating through a branch face the same 30% plus a branch remittance tax. Commercial banks and certain resource projects have special rates.  _(Income Tax Act)_
- **Resident company tax rate** — 30% of taxable income  _(Income Tax, Dividend (Withholding) Tax and Interest (Withholding) Tax Rates Act 1984 (consolidated to 2021), IRC copy, s 6 and Schedule 4 — https://media.irc.gov.pg/2021/December/S8b5xv-document-Income-Tax-Dividend-Withholding-Tax-and-Interest-Withholding-Tax-Rates-Act-Consolidat.pdf ; Internal Revenue Commission, Corporate Tax Rates — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/taxes/businesses-and-employers/corporate-tax-rates ; PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/papua-new-guinea/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Non-resident company (branch) rate** — 30% on attributable profits, plus a 15% branch remittance tax on repatriated profits (combined effective rate approximately 40.5%) %  _(Income Tax Act — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/papua-new-guinea/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Commercial bank rate** — 35% on taxable income below PGK 300 million; above that, an additional levy applies that reduces progressively from 43% toward 35% year-by-year through to 2034 %  _(Income Tax Act — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/papua-new-guinea/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Overseas shippers (non-resident transport)** — 2.4% of gross income derived from PNG %  _(Income Tax Act — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/papua-new-guinea/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Mining / petroleum / gas project rate** — Resource projects are taxed under special regimes; mining is generally 30% and petroleum/gas project rates vary by project agreement % (approx — confirm rate per the specific resource project agreement and current rate schedule)  _(Income Tax Act — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/papua-new-guinea/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_

## Tax base, withholding taxes and administration

- **Tax base and administration overview** — The tax base is worldwide income for resident companies, less allowable deductions. Companies file annually following the calendar year. PNG's withholding regime is **not** limited to dividends, interest and royalties — see the non-resident tax heads below  _(Income Tax Act)_

> **Read this before computing any PNG withholding: from the 2026 tax year the
> question is not "what kind of payment is this?" but "does the recipient have a
> PNG permanent establishment?"**
>
> The Income Tax Act 2025 replaces the old Act for tax years commencing on or
> after **1 January 2026** (the 2026 calendar year for a standard taxpayer; a
> substituted accounting period approved by the IRC shifts that). It splits
> non-residents in two:
>
> - **With a PNG permanent establishment** — taxed by **assessment at 30% on the
>   net taxable income attributable to the PE**, not by withholding on gross.
>   This is the change that catches people: a non-resident service provider who
>   used to suffer 15% Foreign Contractors Withholding Tax on gross revenue from
>   a prescribed contract may now be filing a PNG return instead.
> - **Without a PNG permanent establishment** — **Non-Resident Tax (NRT)**,
>   withheld on the **gross** amount as a final tax.
>
> The Act sets presence limits that decide the question, and three of them are
> short enough to be crossed by an ordinary engagement: a **consulting services
> PE** at more than **183 days** in any 12-month period; a **substantial
> equipment PE** at more than **90 days**; and a **construction, assembly or
> installation PE** — including supervisory activity, and including connected
> activities performed by an **associate** — at more than **90 days**. A fixed
> place of business (office, factory, warehouse, mine site) and an agent who
> regularly negotiates or signs contracts also create a PE.
>
> Count the days before quoting a rate. (PwC PNG, *2025 Income Tax Act
> Commentary*; Income Tax Act 2025, https://www.parliament.gov.pg/uploads/acts/25A-11.pdf)
- **Corporate tax base** — Resident companies are taxed on worldwide income less allowable deductions; non-resident companies on PNG-source income  _(Income Tax Act — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/papua-new-guinea/corporate/income-determination)_
- **Withholding / non-resident tax on dividends** — 15% dividend (withholding) tax on dividends (other than unit trust dividends) paid by a resident company, as replaced by No. 3 of 2017; treaty rates may apply  _(Income Tax, Dividend (Withholding) Tax and Interest (Withholding) Tax Rates Act 1984 (consolidated to 2021), IRC copy, s 10 — https://media.irc.gov.pg/2021/December/S8b5xv-document-Income-Tax-Dividend-Withholding-Tax-and-Interest-Withholding-Tax-Rates-Act-Consolidat.pdf ; PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/papua-new-guinea/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding / non-resident tax on interest** — 15% of the gross interest for resident and non-resident recipients; treaty relief (often 10%) may apply  _(Income Tax, Dividend (Withholding) Tax and Interest (Withholding) Tax Rates Act 1984 (consolidated to 2021), IRC copy, s 10A — https://media.irc.gov.pg/2021/December/S8b5xv-document-Income-Tax-Dividend-Withholding-Tax-and-Interest-Withholding-Tax-Rates-Act-Consolidat.pdf ; PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/papua-new-guinea/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding / non-resident tax on royalties** — 10% of the assessable income (or 48% of taxable income if lower) where the recipient is not an associated person; 30% where the recipient is an associated person; treaty rates may apply  _(Income Tax, Dividend (Withholding) Tax and Interest (Withholding) Tax Rates Act 1984 (consolidated to 2021), IRC copy, s 7 — https://media.irc.gov.pg/2021/December/S8b5xv-document-Income-Tax-Dividend-Withholding-Tax-and-Interest-Withholding-Tax-Rates-Act-Consolidat.pdf ; PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/papua-new-guinea/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding / non-resident tax on technical fees** — 15% of the gross contract income of a foreign contractor, the head most often missed on technical and service contracts; the 48% rate was replaced by 15% in 2016  _(Income Tax, Dividend (Withholding) Tax and Interest (Withholding) Tax Rates Act 1984 (consolidated to 2021), IRC copy, s 6A (foreign contractor income under s 196C of the Income Tax Act 1959) — https://media.irc.gov.pg/2021/December/S8b5xv-document-Income-Tax-Dividend-Withholding-Tax-and-Interest-Withholding-Tax-Rates-Act-Consolidat.pdf ; PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/papua-new-guinea/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding / non-resident tax on annuities** — 15% ((approx — rate from PwC's PNG withholding-taxes page; confirm against Schedule 1 Part 1 of the Act))  _(Income Tax Act 2025 — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/papua-new-guinea/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding / non-resident tax on natural resource amounts** — 15% ((approx — rate from PwC's PNG withholding-taxes page; confirm against Schedule 1 Part 1 of the Act)). A distinct NRT head, separate from the resource-project income tax regimes  _(Income Tax Act 2025 — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/papua-new-guinea/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding / non-resident tax on insurance premiums** — 3% of the **gross premium** on premiums relating to PNG risks ((approx — rate from PwC's PNG withholding-taxes page; confirm against Schedule 1 Part 1 of the Act)). The low headline rate is on gross, not on profit  _(Income Tax Act 2025 — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/papua-new-guinea/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding / non-resident tax on entertainment fees** — 10% ((approx — this head appears on PwC's PNG withholding-taxes page but is not in the Act commentary's list of NRT heads; confirm it survives into the 2026 regime before relying on it))  _(https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/papua-new-guinea/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Foreign Contractors Withholding Tax (FCWT) — being displaced, not simply continued** — Under the **old** Act a non-resident executing a *prescribed contract* suffered **15% FCWT on gross** income, administered by the PNG entity receiving the services. Under the Income Tax Act 2025 a non-resident acting through a **permanent establishment** is instead assessed at **30% on net taxable income** of that PE. Do not quote 15% FCWT for a 2026 engagement without first testing the PE day-counts above  _(PwC PNG, *2025 Income Tax Act Commentary*)_
- **Business income payments withholding — a resident-facing head** — Payments to **local** contractors in prescribed industries carry **10%** withholding unless the payee produces a **nil-withholding authority** from the IRC. The prescribed industries are building and construction, road transport, motor vehicle repairs, security services, and construction of items of joinery. This is not a non-resident tax and is easily missed by a payer who has checked only the NRT table  _(Income Tax Act — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/papua-new-guinea/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Resident recipients are not all at the non-resident rate** — Resident **companies**: 0% on dividends and royalties. Resident **individuals**: 15% on dividends, 0% on royalties and technical fees. Read the recipient's residence and entity type before applying a rate from the NRT table ((approx — from PwC's PNG withholding-taxes page; confirm against the Act))  _(https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/papua-new-guinea/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Treaty dividend cap** — PNG's tax treaties generally cap dividend withholding at 15%, reduce interest to around 10% and set royalties commonly at 10% (Australia, Canada, China, Korea, Singapore among others). Treaty relief applies to NRT on gross; it does not help a non-resident who has crossed into PE taxation on net  _(PNG Double Taxation Agreements — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/papua-new-guinea/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Capital gains tax — new from 1 January 2026** — PNG had **no CGT regime at all** before the Income Tax Act 2025. The Act introduces a narrow, transaction-based CGT at **15%** on gains on resource rights, information relating to a resource right, and membership interests in an entity where more than 50% of the interest's value derives from such assets. It is filed on a **transactional return**, not through the annual income tax return, and the obligation sits with the **seller**. Assets acquired before 1 January 2026 take a market-value cost base at commencement, and there is **no indexation**, so nominal inflationary gains are taxed. See `pg-income-tax` for the individual-side treatment  _(PwC PNG, *2025 Income Tax Act Commentary*)_
- **Corporate tax year** — Calendar year ending 31 December; a substituted accounting period requires the Commissioner General's permission  _(Internal Revenue Commission, Taxation of Companies — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/taxes/businesses-and-employers/taxation-of-companies)_
- **Corporate return deadline** — 28 February following the year of income (extended to 30 June where lodged by a registered tax agent) (approx — confirm against current IRC lodgment program)  _(Income Tax Act — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/papua-new-guinea/corporate/tax-administration)_
- **Corporate tax payment** — Provisional tax is assessed by the Commissioner General and paid in three equal instalments on or before 30 April, 31 July and 31 October, credited against the year's assessment; the balance of assessed tax is due within 30 days of the notice  _(Internal Revenue Commission, Taxation of Companies — https://irc.gov.pg/pages/taxes/businesses-and-employers/taxation-of-companies ; PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/papua-new-guinea/corporate/tax-administration)_
