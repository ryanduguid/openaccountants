# Paraguay tax system

The Dirección Nacional de Ingresos Tributarios (DNIT) administers national taxes. This guide covers the ordinary rules for 2026. Amounts are in Paraguayan guaraníes (PYG). [DNIT tax deadlines](https://www.dnit.gov.py/web/portal-institucional/w/vencimiento-ley-n-6380/19)

## Income tax

- **Residence and source.** The personal income tax (IRP) applies to taxable Paraguayan-source income of resident individuals. For an individual entering from abroad, check permanent residence under the migration rules for the relevant period. An entity constituted under Paraguayan law is resident. A foreign entity establishing domicile in Paraguay is treated as resident from registration in the applicable public register. Apply the source tests separately. They include specified services rendered abroad to Paraguayan IRE or IRP taxpayers and certain foreign investment income of resident business taxpayers. Non-residents with covered Paraguayan-source income generally fall under the INR regime, subject to business establishment and withholding rules. [Law 6380/2019, arts 2, 5–6, 47–49 and 71–73](https://www.dnit.gov.py/en/web/portal-institucional/w/ley-n-6380-19) · [Decree 3181/2019, art 2](https://www.dnit.gov.py/documents/d/global/decreto-n-3181-19)
- **Individuals.** The IRP personal-services registration trigger is more than PYG 80 million in gross taxable receipts from 1 January. A registered taxpayer at or below the payment threshold may still need to file. On net taxable personal-service income, the marginal rates are 8% up to PYG 50 million, 9% on the portion above PYG 50 million through PYG 150 million and 10% on the excess. For the first year, art 62 sets the statutory base using all gross taxable personal-service receipts for the year and qualifying expenses incurred from the day after crossing the threshold. For payment, DNIT tests the PYG 80 million threshold again over the period from that following day through 31 December. Taxable capital income and gains have a separate 8% rate. Dividends covered by IDU are outside that capital-income charge. [Law 6380/2019, arts 47 and 57–69](https://www.dnit.gov.py/en/web/portal-institucional/w/ley-n-6380-19) · [DNIT Resolution 69/2020, art 11](https://www.dnit.gov.py/documents/d/global/resolucion-general-n-69_2020_-irp)
- **Businesses.** The general business income tax (IRE) is 10% of net taxable income. SIMPLE and RESIMPLE have separate eligibility and base rules. Check also the separate dividend and profits tax (IDU) when a distribution is made or deemed made. [Law 6380/2019, arts 21, 26–27 and 40–45](https://www.dnit.gov.py/en/web/portal-institucional/w/ley-n-6380-19)

## VAT and filing

- **IVA.** The standard rate is 10%, with a statutory 5% rate for listed transactions, such as exclusive residential leases, transfers of immovable property, specified food and agricultural products and registered human medicines. Verify exceptions and special rate or base rules. Liability follows the taxable activity and taxpayer category, including independent personal services and imports. Sole traders actually using RESIMPLE are outside IVA under art 27. The PYG 80 million IRP services threshold does not set IVA liability. [Law 6380/2019, arts 27, 69, 80–82, 85 and 90](https://www.dnit.gov.py/en/web/portal-institucional/w/ley-n-6380-19)
- **Filing and payment.** The ordinary IRP return and payment fall in the third month after fiscal year end. General-regime IRE is due in the fourth month after the fiscal close. IVA returns and payment fall in the following month. Use the last digit of the taxpayer's RUC before the check digit to find the exact day in DNIT's standing calendar. Check whether any withholding or separate deadline applies. [DNIT Resolution 38/2020, arts 2–3](https://www.dnit.gov.py/web/portal-institucional/w/resolucion-general-n-38-20)
