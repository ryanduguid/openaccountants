# Burundi Personal Income Tax

## Burundi Personal Income Tax

## Personal income tax (Impôt sur les revenus)

- **Progressive scale overview** — Burundi applies a progressive scale to individual income (employment, business and rental income) administered by the OBR. The monthly bands below are widely cited for 2025; an accountant should confirm exact thresholds against the current General Tax Code.
- **Monthly taxable income up to BIF 150,000** — 0% %  _(Law No. 1/02 of 24 January 2013 (income tax law) — https://remotepeople.com/countries/burundi/hire-employees/payroll-tax/)_
- **Monthly taxable income BIF 150,001 to 300,000** — 20% %  _(Law No. 1/02 of 24 January 2013 (income tax law) — https://remotepeople.com/countries/burundi/hire-employees/payroll-tax/)_
- **Monthly taxable income over BIF 300,000** — 30% %  _(Law No. 1/02 of 24 January 2013 (income tax law) — https://remotepeople.com/countries/burundi/hire-employees/payroll-tax/)_
- **Tax-free threshold** — BIF 150,000 per month (income below this is taxed at 0%) BIF  _(Law No. 1/02 of 24 January 2013 (income tax law) — https://remotepeople.com/countries/burundi/hire-employees/payroll-tax/)_
- **Non-resident individual income (Burundi-source)** — 15% flat (withholding on Burundi-source payments) % ((approx — confirm))  _(Law No. 1/02 of 24 January 2013 (income tax law) — https://remotepeople.com/countries/burundi/hire-employees/payroll-tax/)_
- **Residence test for individuals** — An individual is resident if they have their tax domicile in Burundi, carry out their main professional activity in Burundi, or have the centre of their economic interests there  _(Law No. 1/02 of 24 January 2013 (income tax law) — https://www.jarniascyril.com/expatriation/moving-to-burundi-as-an-expat-complete-guide/taxation-income-tax-property-tax-burundi-expatriates/)_
- **Scope of taxation** — Residents are taxed on worldwide income; non-residents only on Burundi-source income  _(Law No. 1/02 of 24 January 2013 (income tax law))_
- **Agricultural income exemption** — Smallholder/agricultural income is exempt (commonly cited up to BIF 1,800,000 per year) BIF ((approx — confirm))  _(Law No. 1/02 of 24 January 2013 (income tax law) — https://remotepeople.com/countries/burundi/hire-employees/payroll-tax/)_
- **Employment income collection mechanism** — Tax on salaries is withheld at source by the employer (PAYE-equivalent) and remitted monthly  _(Law No. 1/02 of 24 January 2013 (income tax law))_
- **Monthly salary tax remittance deadline** — 15th of the following month  _(OBR filing rules under the General Tax Code — https://remotepeople.com/countries/burundi/hire-employees/payroll-tax/)_
- **Annual individual income tax return deadline** — Generally by 31 March of the following year ((approx — confirm))  _(Law No. 1/02 of 24 January 2013 (income tax law))_
