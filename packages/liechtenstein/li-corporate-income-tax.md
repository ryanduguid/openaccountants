# Liechtenstein Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate profit tax rates and base

- **Standard corporate profit tax rate** — 12.5% flat on taxable net income (Ertragssteuer)  _(Steuergesetz (SteG, LGBl. 2010 Nr. 340), consolidated version in force from 1 July 2026 on gesetze.li, Art. 61 — https://www.gesetze.li/konso/pdf/2010340000?version=38 ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/liechtenstein/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Minimum profit tax (Mindestertragssteuer)** — CHF 1,800 a year for legal persons whose tax is not settled by deduction at source, charged regardless of the length of the tax period and credited against the profit tax; not levied on entities whose sole purpose is running a commercial business and whose average balance sheet total over the last three financial years did not exceed CHF 500,000  _(Steuergesetz (SteG, LGBl. 2010 Nr. 340), consolidated version in force from 1 July 2026 on gesetze.li, Art. 62 Abs. 1 to 3 — https://www.gesetze.li/konso/pdf/2010340000?version=38 ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/liechtenstein/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Annual corporate minimum tax** — CHF 1,800, fully creditable against profit tax CHF  _(Tax Act (Steuergesetz, SteG) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/liechtenstein/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **OECD global minimum tax (QDMTT / IIR)** — 15% for in-scope ultimate parent / large multinational groups under the GloBE rules %  _(GloBE Tax Act (GloBE-Steuergesetz / FL GlobE Tax Law) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/liechtenstein/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Tax base** — Net taxable profit per the statutory financial statements, with tax adjustments  _(Tax Act (Steuergesetz, SteG) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/liechtenstein/corporate/income-determination)_
- **Notional interest deduction on equity (Eigenkapital-Zinsabzug)** — A deduction equal to the Sollertrag (4%, Art. 5) on modified equity, which is equity less own shares, participations and 6% of other assets as adjusted under Art. 54 Abs. 2; the deduction cannot create or increase a loss  _(Steuergesetz (SteG, LGBl. 2010 Nr. 340), consolidated version in force from 1 July 2026 on gesetze.li, Art. 54 and Art. 5 — https://www.gesetze.li/konso/pdf/2010340000?version=38 ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/liechtenstein/corporate/income-determination)_
- **Dividends received from subsidiaries** — Generally tax-exempt  _(Tax Act (Steuergesetz, SteG) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/liechtenstein/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Dividends from low-taxed passive-income foreign subsidiaries** — Taxable at the 12.5% profit tax rate where the payer is a foreign, low-taxed subsidiary earning predominantly passive income %  _(Tax Act (Steuergesetz, SteG) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/liechtenstein/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Capital gains on the sale of participations** — Capital gains on the sale or liquidation of participations in legal persons, and unrealised appreciation, are excluded from taxable net income, subject to the anti-abuse conditions in Art. 48 Abs. 3 to 5  _(Steuergesetz (SteG, LGBl. 2010 Nr. 340), consolidated version in force from 1 July 2026 on gesetze.li, Art. 48 Abs. 1 Bst. f and Abs. 3 to 5 — https://www.gesetze.li/konso/pdf/2010340000?version=38 ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/liechtenstein/corporate/income-determination)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends** — 0% — no withholding tax on distributions regardless of recipient's residence %  _(Tax Act (Steuergesetz, SteG) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/liechtenstein/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Withholding tax on interest** — 0% %  _(Tax Act (Steuergesetz, SteG) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/liechtenstein/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Withholding tax on royalties** — 0% %  _(Tax Act (Steuergesetz, SteG) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/liechtenstein/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Corporate return filing deadline** — The filing period is set each year by the Steuerverwaltung, not in the Act; in practice 1 July following the tax year, with an extension of up to 6 months on substantiated written request  _(Steuergesetz (SteG, LGBl. 2010 Nr. 340), consolidated version in force from 1 July 2026 on gesetze.li, Art. 95 Abs. 1 — https://www.gesetze.li/konso/pdf/2010340000?version=38 ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/liechtenstein/corporate/tax-administration)_
- **Loss carryforward** — Losses carried forward reduce taxable net income by at most 70% of that year's taxable net income; the unused balance carries forward  _(Steuergesetz (SteG, LGBl. 2010 Nr. 340), consolidated version in force from 1 July 2026 on gesetze.li, Art. 57 — https://www.gesetze.li/konso/pdf/2010340000?version=38 ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/liechtenstein/corporate/deductions)_

Corporations, foundations and establishments pay a flat 12.5% profit tax, subject to an annual CHF 1,800 minimum tax that is creditable against profit tax. Large multinational groups face a 15% effective minimum under the OECD/EEA GloBE rules. Liechtenstein levies no withholding tax on outbound dividends, interest or royalties.
