# Liechtenstein tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the taxpayer type, residence facts, income sources and relevant period. Check any treaty that assigns residence or limits the source country's tax.

## Territorial reach

Individuals with unlimited liability generally account for worldwide assets and taxable income under the wealth and income rules, including the statutory standard yield on taxable assets. Individuals with limited liability account for the listed domestic assets and income. Legal persons with unlimited liability generally account for worldwide income under the corporate profit-tax rules. A foreign company outside that scope accounts for the specified Liechtenstein-source amounts, including the taxable profit of a Liechtenstein permanent establishment. Check exemptions and any treaty allocation. [Tax Act, arts 5–6, 14 and 44–48](https://www.gesetze.li/konso/pdf/2010340000?version=38)

## Residence

An individual generally has unlimited liability with a Liechtenstein domicile or habitual abode. Domicile requires an intention to remain permanently. A connected stay exceeding 6 months generally establishes habitual abode from the start, ignoring short interruptions. Check the statutory rules for study, institutional and temporary stays and qualifying diplomats. A legal person generally has unlimited liability if its seat or place of actual management is in Liechtenstein. Actual management turns on where the enterprise's senior management is centred. Check treaty residence separately. [Tax Act, arts 2, 6–7 and 44](https://www.gesetze.li/konso/pdf/2010340000?version=38)

## Taxpayer types

A sole trader uses the individual income rules. Allocate the wealth and income of a partnership without legal personality to its partners. Companies, establishments, foundations and trust enterprises with legal personality can fall within the corporate regime. For foundations or other dedicated assets, check revocability, legal personality, the identity and tax position of the beneficiary or settlor and any separate assessment under art 9. Asset arrangements without legal personality also have specific rules under arts 44 and 65, including the minimum profit tax where applicable. [Tax Act, arts 9, 14, 44 and 65](https://www.gesetze.li/konso/pdf/2010340000?version=38)

## Currency

The Swiss franc (CHF) is the legal currency. Identify the currency of the financial statements and check the tax form or instructions for translating balances and income. Keep the rates used with the supporting records. [Franken Currency Act, art 1](https://www.gesetze.li/konso/pdf/1924008000) · [Tax Administration exchange-rate guidance](https://www.llv.li/de/unternehmen/steuern-abgaben-versicherung/steuern-juristische-personen/kurse-devisen-gold-und-edelmetalle-kryptowaehrungen)

## Assessment periods

Individual income and corporate profit taxes are annual and ordinarily use the calendar year. Use the relevant period of liability if it applies for only part of the year. For an eligible individual business or company with an off-calendar financial year, use the results of the completed financial year under the applicable provision. Check the return deadline and any extension separately. [Tax Act, arts 14(5) and 47(6)](https://www.gesetze.li/konso/pdf/2010340000?version=38)

## Responsible authorities

The Liechtenstein Tax Administration (Steuerverwaltung) administers ordinary individual and corporate taxes. Individuals should check the municipal surcharge and use the competent office and forms. The Tax Administration publishes return and exchange-rate guidance. [Tax Act, art 75](https://www.gesetze.li/konso/pdf/2010340000?version=38) · [Tax Administration](https://www.llv.li/de/landesverwaltung/steuerverwaltung)

## Fictional intake

A consultant moves to Liechtenstein in April and maintains an office there through December. Record the date and purpose of arrival, absences and any continuing home abroad. Then determine the period of domestic liability and check whether a treaty changes which receipts are taxable.
