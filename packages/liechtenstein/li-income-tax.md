# Liechtenstein Personal Income Tax

## Personal income tax rates and bands

Liechtenstein applies a progressive national income tax topped up by a communal surcharge of 150%–180%, producing combined effective rates of roughly 2.5% to 22.4%. Net wealth is taxed through the income tax via a notional yield rather than a separate wealth tax.

- **National income tax rate range (before communal surcharge)** — 1% to 8% progressive under the Art. 19 tariff  _(Tax Act of 23 September 2010 (Steuergesetz, SteG, LR 640.0; LILEX consolidated text), Art. 19(1) — https://www.gesetze.li/konso/pdf/2010340000)_
- **Combined effective rate range (national + communal surcharge)** — National rates of 1% to 8% above the CHF 15,855 basic allowance, plus a communal surcharge that each municipal council sets between 150% and 250% of the national tax: 2.5% to 28% at the statutory extremes, and about 2.5% to 22.4% at the surcharges municipalities currently apply  _(Steuergesetz (SteG), LR 640.0, LILEX consolidated text, Art. 19 Abs. 1 and Art. 75 Abs. 3 — https://www.gesetze.li/konso/pdf/2010340000)_
- **Communal surcharge (Gemeindesteuerzuschlag)** — Set each year by each municipal council as a percentage of the national tax, within the statutory band of 150% to 250% (Art. 75(3)); the municipalities' current rates of 150% to 180% keep their existing source  _(Tax Act of 23 September 2010 (Steuergesetz, SteG, LR 640.0; LILEX consolidated text), Art. 75(3) — https://www.gesetze.li/konso/pdf/2010340000 ; current municipal rates (as reported))_
- **1% national bracket threshold (single)** — 1% applies from CHF 15,856 to CHF 21,140 of taxable income, above the CHF 15,855 basic exemption (Art. 19(1)(a))  _(Tax Act of 23 September 2010 (Steuergesetz, SteG, LR 640.0; LILEX consolidated text), Art. 19(1) — https://www.gesetze.li/konso/pdf/2010340000)_
- **8% (top) national bracket threshold (single)** — Taxable income of CHF 211,401 or more (Art. 19(1)(a)); communal surcharges of 150% to 250% of the national tax sit on top  _(Tax Act of 23 September 2010 (Steuergesetz, SteG, LR 640.0; LILEX consolidated text), Art. 19(1) — https://www.gesetze.li/konso/pdf/2010340000)_
- **Tax-free personal exemption (single)** — CHF 15,855 (Grundfreibetrag, Art. 19(1)(a))  _(Tax Act of 23 September 2010 (Steuergesetz, SteG, LR 640.0; LILEX consolidated text), Art. 19(1) — https://www.gesetze.li/konso/pdf/2010340000)_
- **Tax-free personal exemption (married, joint)** — CHF 31,710 (joint Grundfreibetrag, Art. 19(1)(c))  _(Tax Act of 23 September 2010 (Steuergesetz, SteG, LR 640.0; LILEX consolidated text), Art. 19(1) — https://www.gesetze.li/konso/pdf/2010340000)_
- **Tax-free personal exemption (single parent)** — CHF 23,783 (Grundfreibetrag, Art. 19(1)(b))  _(Tax Act of 23 September 2010 (Steuergesetz, SteG, LR 640.0; LILEX consolidated text), Art. 19(1) — https://www.gesetze.li/konso/pdf/2010340000)_
- **Net wealth taxed via notional yield (Sollertrag)** — Taxable net wealth × 4% notional yield is added to taxable income and taxed at income tax rates  _(Tax Act of 23 September 2010 (Steuergesetz, SteG, LR 640.0; LILEX consolidated text), Arts. 5 and 14(2)(l) — https://www.gesetze.li/konso/pdf/2010340000)_
- **Investment income on wealth-taxed assets** — Interest and dividend income on assets subject to wealth tax is exempt from income tax; instead a notional yield (Sollertrag) of 4% of the taxable wealth is added to taxable income  _(Steuergesetz (SteG), LR 640.0, LILEX consolidated text, Art. 5, Art. 14 Abs. 2 Bst. l and Art. 15 Abs. 1 Bst. a — https://www.gesetze.li/konso/pdf/2010340000)_
- **Capital gains on movable private assets (e.g. securities)** — Exempt from the Erwerbssteuer: capital gains on the sale of movable and immovable private assets (Bst. m), gains on the sale or liquidation of participations in legal persons and their unrealised appreciation (Bst. o), profit distributions from participations (Bst. n) and gains on foreign real estate (Bst. l); business assets follow Art. 48 where its conditions apply  _(Steuergesetz (SteG, LGBl. 2010 Nr. 340), consolidated version in force from 1 July 2026 on gesetze.li, Art. 15 Abs. 2 Bst. l, m, n and o — https://www.gesetze.li/konso/pdf/2010340000?version=38)_
- **Tax residence test** — Permanent or habitual residence (domicile or customary abode) in Liechtenstein  _(Tax Act of 23 September 2010 (Steuergesetz, SteG, LR 640.0; LILEX consolidated text), Art. 6(1) — https://www.gesetze.li/konso/pdf/2010340000)_
- **Lump-sum (expenditure-based) taxation for non-working foreigners** — Available on application to persons who take up residence in Liechtenstein for the first time (or after at least ten years' absence), are not Liechtenstein citizens, do not work in Liechtenstein and live off the income from their wealth; the tax is assessed on their total expenditure  _(Steuergesetz (SteG), LR 640.0, LILEX consolidated text, Art. 30 Abs. 1 and Art. 32 — https://www.gesetze.li/konso/pdf/2010340000)_
- **Filing deadline** — Set each year by the Steuerverwaltung rather than in the Act; in practice mid to late April following the tax year, with extensions of up to 5 months (80% prepayment required for extensions beyond one month); taxpayers leaving the country file before departure (Art. 95 Abs. 2)  _(Steuergesetz (SteG, LGBl. 2010 Nr. 340), consolidated version in force from 1 July 2026 on gesetze.li, Art. 95 Abs. 1 — https://www.gesetze.li/konso/pdf/2010340000?version=38)_
- **Default / late-payment charge rate** — 4% a year, the default interest the Government sets by ordinance under Art. 114 Abs. 3, running from the end of the 30-day payment period  _(Steuergesetz (SteG), LR 640.0, LILEX consolidated text, Art. 114 Abs. 3 — https://www.gesetze.li/konso/pdf/2010340000)_
