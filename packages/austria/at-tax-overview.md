# Austria Tax Overview

## Austrian tax system at a glance

- **Tax year** — Calendar year (1 January to 31 December); companies may elect a deviating fiscal year  _(Einkommensteuergesetz (EStG) / Körperschaftsteuergesetz (KStG) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/austria/individual/tax-administration)_
- **Currency** — Euro (EUR)  _(Austrian national currency (Eurozone member))_
- **Tax authority** — Finanzamt Österreich (Federal Ministry of Finance / Bundesministerium für Finanzen), filings via FinanzOnline  _(Bundesabgabenordnung (BAO) — https://www.bmf.gv.at)_
- **Residence basis of taxation** — Residents taxed on worldwide income; non-residents taxed on Austrian-source income only  _(Einkommensteuergesetz (EStG) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/austria/individual/residence)_
- **Top personal income tax rate** — 55 percent (on income above EUR 1,000,000)  _(Einkommensteuergesetz (EStG) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/austria/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **Personal income tax-free threshold** — 13,308 EUR  _(Einkommensteuergesetz (EStG) — 2025 indexation (Progressionsabgeltung) — https://www.thelocal.at/20241220/how-austrias-income-tax-brackets-will-change-in-2025)_
- **Corporate income tax (CIT) rate** — 23 percent (flat)  _(Körperschaftsteuergesetz (KStG) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/austria/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **VAT (Umsatzsteuer) in force** — Yes — standard rate 20%, reduced rates 13% and 10%  _(Umsatzsteuergesetz (UStG 1994) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/austria/corporate/other-taxes)_
- **Annual personal income tax return deadline** — 30 April of the following year (30 June if filed electronically via FinanzOnline); extensions available where a tax adviser files  _(Bundesabgabenordnung (BAO) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/austria/individual/tax-administration)_
- **Annual corporate income tax return deadline** — 30 April of the following year (30 June via FinanzOnline); represented taxpayers can extend (typically into the following year)  _(Bundesabgabenordnung (BAO) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/austria/corporate/tax-administration)_
- **Flat tax on capital investment income (KESt)** — 27.5% (25% on bank deposits and cash savings) percent  _(Einkommensteuergesetz (EStG) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/austria/individual/income-determination)_

Austria taxes residents on worldwide income and operates a self-assessment-with-assessment system administered through FinanzOnline. The figures below are 2025 headline parameters; an Austrian accountant should confirm each before reliance.
