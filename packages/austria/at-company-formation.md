# Austria Company Formation & Entity Choice

## Entity types and company formation

The most common Austrian business vehicles are the GmbH (limited liability company), the AG (stock corporation), and the FlexCo/FlexKapG (flexible company introduced in 2024). Companies are entered in the commercial register (Firmenbuch) and require a notarial deed of incorporation.

- **GmbH (Gesellschaft mit beschränkter Haftung)** — Private limited liability company — the most common form for SMEs  _(GmbH-Gesetz (GmbHG), https://startup.usp.gv.at/en/gruendung)_
- **AG (Aktiengesellschaft)** — Stock corporation, suitable for larger enterprises and listing  _(Aktiengesetz (AktG), https://startup.usp.gv.at/en/gruendung/ueberlegungen-im-vorfeld/weitere-informationen/aktiengesellschaft)_
- **FlexCo / FlexKapG (Flexible Kapitalgesellschaft)** — Flexible company form in force since 1 January 2024 (BGBl. I Nr. 179/2023) and designed for startups; GmbH law applies unless the FlexKapGG provides otherwise, and the articles may authorise enterprise value shares (Unternehmenswert-Anteile) totalling less than 25% of the share capital  _(Flexible-Kapitalgesellschafts-Gesetz (FlexKapGG), RIS consolidated text, § 1 — https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/i/2023/179/P1/NOR40258596 ; Flexible-Kapitalgesellschafts-Gesetz (FlexKapGG), RIS consolidated text, § 9 Abs. 1 — https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/i/2023/179/P9/NOR40258604)_
- **GmbH minimum share capital** — EUR 10,000 (reduced from EUR 35,000 from 1 January 2024); at least one quarter of each cash contribution, and at least EUR 5,000 in total, must be paid in on formation EUR  _(GmbH-Gesetz (GmbHG), RIS consolidated text, § 6 Abs. 1 — https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/rgbl/1906/58/P6/NOR40258368 ; GmbH-Gesetz (GmbHG), RIS consolidated text, § 10 Abs. 1 — https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/rgbl/1906/58/P10/NOR40258370)_
- **FlexCo minimum share capital** — EUR 10,000, the GmbH minimum applied through FlexKapGG § 1 Abs. 2; each shareholder's contribution may be as low as EUR 1, at least one quarter of each cash contribution (and never less than EUR 1) must be paid in, and the GmbHG's EUR 5,000 minimum total cash payment continues to apply EUR  _(Flexible-Kapitalgesellschafts-Gesetz (FlexKapGG), RIS consolidated text, § 1 Abs. 2 — https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/i/2023/179/P1/NOR40258596 ; Flexible-Kapitalgesellschafts-Gesetz (FlexKapGG), RIS consolidated text, § 3 — https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/i/2023/179/P3/NOR40258598 ; Flexible-Kapitalgesellschafts-Gesetz (FlexKapGG), RIS consolidated text, § 5 — https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/i/2023/179/P5/NOR40258600 ; GmbH-Gesetz (GmbHG), RIS consolidated text, § 10 Abs. 1 — https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/rgbl/1906/58/P10/NOR40258370)_
- **AG minimum share capital** — EUR 70,000 (at least one quarter paid in on formation) EUR  _(Aktiengesetz 1965 (AktG), RIS consolidated text, § 7 — https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/bgbl/1965/98/P7/NOR12040509 ; USP Aktiengesellschaft page — https://startup.usp.gv.at/en/gruendung/ueberlegungen-im-vorfeld/weitere-informationen/aktiengesellschaft)_
- **Incorporation deed** — Articles of association must be executed as a notarial deed (a simplified electronic single-shareholder GmbH founding is available)  _(GmbH-Gesetz (GmbHG), https://startup.usp.gv.at/en/gruendung)_
- **Commercial register (Firmenbuch) entry** — Legal existence arises on registration in the Firmenbuch at the competent regional court  _(Firmenbuchgesetz (FBG) / Unternehmensgesetzbuch (UGB), https://startup.usp.gv.at/en/gruendung)_
- **Typical incorporation timeline** — Around 1–3 weeks once notarial deed, bank capital confirmation and Firmenbuch filing are in place (approx — confirm)  _(GmbH-Gesetz (GmbHG), https://startup.usp.gv.at/en/gruendung)_
- **Typical formation cost** — Notary, court and registration fees commonly EUR 1,000–3,000 for a standard GmbH (excluding share capital) (approx — confirm) EUR  _(Gerichtsgebührengesetz (GGG) / notarial tariff, https://startup.usp.gv.at/en/gruendung)_
- **Annual financial statements filing** — The legal representatives of corporations must file the annual accounts with the Firmenbuch court within nine months of the balance sheet date  _(Unternehmensgesetzbuch (UGB), RIS consolidated text, § 277 Abs. 1 — https://ogd.ris.bka.gv.at/eli/drgbl/1897/219/P277/NOR40275593)_
- **Tax registration on formation** — New companies register with the tax office for a tax number and (if applicable) a VAT identification number (UID) via FinanzOnline  _(Bundesabgabenordnung (BAO) / Umsatzsteuergesetz (UStG 1994), https://www.usp.gv.at)_
