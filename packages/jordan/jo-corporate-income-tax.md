# Jordan Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax rates and base

- **Standard CIT rate (most companies)** — 20% percent  _(Income Tax Law No. 34 of 2014 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jordan/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **CIT rate — banks** — 35% percent  _(Income Tax Law No. 34 of 2014 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jordan/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **CIT rate — telecom, insurance/reinsurance, financial intermediation, electricity, mining** — 24% percent  _(Income Tax Law No. 34 of 2014 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jordan/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **CIT rate — industrial companies** — Reduced rate of 10%-14% depending on sector and location percent ((approx — confirm))  _(Income Tax Law No. 34 of 2014 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jordan/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Development / free zone rate** — Reduced flat CIT of 5% or 10% for qualifying activities, plus sales tax and customs exemptions percent  _(Investment Environment Law / Development and Free Zones legislation (https://www.flick.network/en-jo/corporate-tax-jordan))_
- **Tax base** — Territorial — resident corporations are generally not taxed on worldwide income; only Jordanian-source income is taxed  _(Income Tax Law No. 34 of 2014 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jordan/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Foreign branch net income** — 10% percent (Taxed at a fixed rate)  _(Income Tax Law No. 34 of 2014 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jordan/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **National contribution tax (companies)** — Banks & electricity 3%; mining 7%; financial intermediation/leasing 4%; major telecom & insurance 2%; other companies 1% percent  _(Income Tax Law No. 34 of 2014 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jordan/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Withholding tax on dividends** — Generally exempt from WHT for both resident and non-resident recipients percent  _(Income Tax Law No. 34 of 2014 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jordan/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **Withholding tax on interest/commissions (paid by banks/financial companies)** — 5% for individuals; 7% for legal entities percent  _(Income Tax Law No. 34 of 2014 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jordan/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **Withholding tax on non-resident services (imported services / royalties)** — 10% plus national contribution tax percent  _(Income Tax Law No. 34 of 2014 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jordan/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **Withholding tax on resident professional services** — 5% on services by resident professionals (doctors, lawyers, engineers, etc.) percent  _(Income Tax Law No. 34 of 2014 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jordan/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **Advance income tax payments** — Two advance payments due within 30 days of the end of each half of the tax period where gross income exceeds JOD 1,000,000 JOD  _(Income Tax Law No. 34 of 2014 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jordan))_
- **CIT return filing and final payment deadline** — Before the end of the fourth month following the end of the tax period (30 April for calendar-year companies)  _(Income Tax Law No. 34 of 2014 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/jordan))_
