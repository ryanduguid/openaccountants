# Mali Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax (Impôt sur les Sociétés)

Companies pay the Impôt sur les Sociétés (IS) on net taxable profit; industrial, commercial and professional profits are computed under the IBIC/IBNC rules of the CGI. A turnover-based minimum tax ensures a floor liability even in loss years.

- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 30%, on a base rounded down to the thousand francs (art. 85 CGI, Loi n°11-078)  _(Code Général des Impôts (Mali), art. 85 — https://www.dgi.gouv.ml/CGI/)_
- **Mining sector rate** — 25% under the Mining Code (Code Minier) (approx — confirm; mining fiscal terms depend on the convention vintage)  _(Code Minier du Mali)_
- **Minimum tax (Impôt Minimum Forfaitaire)** — The tax of a taxpayer under the régime du bénéfice réel cannot be less than 1% of turnover excluding tax (art. 86 CGI)  _(Code Général des Impôts (Mali), art. 86 — https://www.dgi.gouv.ml/CGI/)_
- **Tax base** — Net taxable profit = accounting profit adjusted for non-deductible expenses and exempt income  _(Code Général des Impôts (Mali) — IBIC/IS)_
- **Accounting framework for the base** — SYSCOHADA (OHADA accounting system) financial statements  _(OHADA Uniform Act on Accounting and Financial Reporting (SYSCOHADA))_
- **Withholding tax on dividends (IRVM)** — 10% on distributed dividends, 7% for companies listed on a CREPMF-approved exchange; 6% on bond income, 3% on bonds issued by the State or public bodies with a term of five to ten years (art. 42 CGI)  _(Code Général des Impôts (Mali), art. 42 — https://www.dgi.gouv.ml/CGI/)_
- **Withholding tax on interest** — 15% (IRC — Impôt sur le Revenu des Créances) (approx — confirm rate and instrument scope)  _(Code Général des Impôts (Mali) — Impôt sur le Revenu des Créances)_
- **Withholding tax on royalties/service fees to non-residents** — Up to 30% on gross royalties paid to non-residents (service fees commonly 15%) (approx — confirm; treaty relief may reduce)  _(Code Général des Impôts (Mali))_
- **Corporate payment deadline** — The balance of IS (and IBIC) is paid no later than 30 April of the year following the income year, or the next business day (art. 248 LPF)  _(Livre de Procédures Fiscales (Mali), art. 248 — https://www.dgi.gouv.ml/CGI/)_
- **Advance payments** — IS is paid by instalments (acomptes) during the year, with a final balancing payment on filing (approx — confirm instalment schedule with DGI)  _(Code Général des Impôts (Mali))_
