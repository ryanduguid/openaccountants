# Equatorial Guinea Payroll & Social Contributions

## Social security and payroll contributions

- **Employer INSESO contribution** — 21.5% of gross salary percent  _(Social Security Law of Equatorial Guinea (INSESO), https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/equatorial-guinea/corporate/other-taxes)_
- **Employer Work Protection Fund (WPF) contribution** — 1% of gross salary percent  _(Work Protection Fund regulations, Equatorial Guinea, https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/equatorial-guinea/corporate/other-taxes)_
- **Employee INSESO contribution** — 4.5% of net salary percent  _(Social Security Law of Equatorial Guinea (INSESO), https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/equatorial-guinea/corporate/other-taxes)_
- **Employee Work Protection Fund (WPF) contribution** — 0.5% of net salary percent  _(Work Protection Fund regulations, Equatorial Guinea, https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/equatorial-guinea/corporate/other-taxes)_
- **Total employer social contribution** — 22.5% of gross salary (21.5% INSESO + 1% WPF) percent  _(Social Security Law of Equatorial Guinea; Work Protection Fund regulations, https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/equatorial-guinea/corporate/other-taxes)_
- **Total employee social contribution** — 5% of net salary (4.5% INSESO + 0.5% WPF) percent  _(Social Security Law of Equatorial Guinea; Work Protection Fund regulations, https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/equatorial-guinea/corporate/other-taxes)_
- **Contribution wage base / cap** — No statutory ceiling published in consulted sources (approx — confirm whether a contribution ceiling applies)  _(Social Security Law of Equatorial Guinea (INSESO))_
- **Payroll income-tax withholding (PAYE-equivalent)** — Employer withholds personal income tax on salaries at source per the IRPF progressive scale  _(General Tax Code of Equatorial Guinea (Personal Income Tax), https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/equatorial-guinea/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **Payroll / social contribution remittance** — Monthly remittance to the tax authority and INSESO, generally within 15 days of the following month (approx — confirm exact monthly deadline)  _(General Tax Code of Equatorial Guinea; Social Security Law of Equatorial Guinea)_

Employers and employees contribute to the National Institute of Social Security (INSESO) and the Work Protection Fund (WPF). Employment income tax is withheld by the employer at source.
