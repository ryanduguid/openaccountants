# Guernsey: personal allowance withdrawal reference v0.1

> **General reference only.** This source-cited Tier 2 conditional draft illustrates the remaining personal-allowance basis under selected 2026 schedules. It establishes no actual entitlement, tax reduction, tax payable, coding allowance, return or refund. Guide presence, source preparation and passing component checks do not establish comprehensive jurisdiction acceptance.

## Scope and source conditions

Load workflow-base and income-tax-workflow-base in full. Preserve all 4 universal outputs and 18 checks, all 5 income tax outputs and 12 checks, all mandatory country slots and every shared refusal. Missing applicable law, facts, tools, outputs or qualified review prevents delivery of an actual financial result.

The fictional branch stipulates an individual with established sole or principal residence, entitlement and claim/proof conditions for the complete 2026 year of charge. There is no arrival, departure, special pension proration, transfer or other allowance or deduction. C is independently established calculated income under the applicable law, before this personal allowance is withdrawn. C is not automatically salary, gross receipts, a bank balance, assessable income or income after deducting the personal allowance.

The selected First Schedule specifies relief at the individual standard rate on an allowance basis of GBP 15,200, subject to conditions. This reference preserves that basis without applying a rate. A remaining basis of 15,200 when C is zero does not create a tax credit, refund or automatically transferable sum. The output is neither tax saved nor a complete income tax computation.

These examples use exact non-negative whole GBP inputs. Positive excess over 85,000 must be divisible by 5, so every illustrated stage is integral without an assumed rounding rule. This is a fixture restriction, not evidence that legal claims or income must be expressed in whole pounds. Fractional results and actual precision rules remain held.

Guernsey source evidence and catalogue code GG do not establish identical treatment throughout the Bailiwick, or the law of Jersey or the United Kingdom. Establish the actual territorial and personal application separately.

## Intake and component mechanics

Reuse redacted records before grouped questions. Establish territory, year and basis period, applicable primary law, residence and day-count evidence, exceptions, full-year entitlement and claim/proof, filing unit, all income and deduction categories, calculated-income reconciliation, other allowances, pension and mortgage items, transfers, precision, reviewer and required tools. Present the inferred profile and wait for confirmation. Confirmation cannot establish law. Keep identifiers, bank details and signatures with the professional.

1. **Establish the selected branch.** The examples stipulate full-year sole or principal residence, an individually established personal basis P = 15,200 and threshold H = 85,000. Arrival/departure, pensions subject to special proration, non-resident/dual-resident cases and unresolved territorial application require separate authority. _(L1 First Schedule; L2 resolution 22; L5)_
2. **Establish calculated income C and the complete pool.** L1 defines C as income net of deductions but gross of withdrawable deductions. Reconcile income, each deduction and each add-back before using C. Do not deduct the personal allowance to determine its own taper. In this branch all other allowances, deductions, pension contributions, mortgage relief and transfers are independently nil. Nil is an express fictional fact, never a default from missing records. _(L1 First Schedule)_
3. **Validate the fictional encoding.** Use finite, non-negative, exact base-10 whole GBP amounts. If C exceeds H, C - H must be divisible by 5. Preserve the original input without truncation, coercion, clipping or premature rounding. Negative income, missing values, pennies or unsupported precision require reconciliation or another branch; no actual legal rounding rule is inferred.
4. **Compute excess and uncapped reduction.** Set X = max(C - H, 0) and U = X / 5. At equality X and U are zero. Apply the ratio to excess over H, not to all C. H is the stipulated full-year individual threshold, not a separate threshold for each receipt or employer. _(L1 First Schedule condition (iii))_
5. **Limit reduction to the available basis.** Set W = min(P, U), then A = P - W. Check W >= 0, 0 <= A <= P and A + W = P. U is the uncapped mathematical reduction; U above P does not make A negative or create additional tax. This isolates the personal basis only because every other pool item is nil. _(L1 First Schedule condition (iii) and personal-allowance row)_
6. **Preserve the component and hold actual delivery.** Record C, P, H, X, U, W and A separately. The zero-basis boundary is H + 5 x P = 161,000 under these premises. It is not a universal salary threshold, tax-free limit or coding balance. Apply no tax rate and infer no actual tax saving, withholding, prepayment, claim, payment or refund. _(L1-L2; L4; L6)_

For a different pool, stop this component and establish the complete combined treatment. L1 protects an aggregate 2,500 of specified pension contributions from withdrawal and from the withdrawable-deduction category; it is not protection for each scheme or a 2,500 cash credit. Its interaction with deductions and calculated-income add-backs needs the full qualifying law. Do not allocate withdrawal among reliefs without authority, count retirement annuity contributions twice or reduce C by withdrawable deductions without the required add-back. The table's short pension wording cannot replace the schedule. Mortgage, other allowances and transfers are outside this P-only calculation. _(L1 First/Second Schedules; L2-L4)_
