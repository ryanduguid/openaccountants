# Chile Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax (First Category Tax)

- **Overview of First Category Tax** — Chile taxes corporate profits via the First Category Tax (Impuesto de Primera Categoria, FCT), which is partly integrated with shareholders' personal/Additional Tax. SMEs use the simplified ProPyme regime with a temporarily reduced rate. Draft figures for accountant review.
- **Standard corporate income tax rate (semi-integrated regime)** — 27% for companies in the general semi-integrated regime (Art. 14 A), from commercial year 2020  _(SII, Tasas de Impuesto de Primera Categoría (FAQ 4708) — https://www.sii.cl/preguntas_frecuentes/declaracion_renta/001_140_4708.htm ; Ley sobre Impuesto a la Renta (DL 824), Ley Chile consolidated text, Art. 14 A and Art. 20 — https://www.bcn.cl/leychile/navegar?idNorma=6368)_
- **ProPyme (SME) regime rate, 2025** — 12.5% for commercial years 2025, 2026 and 2027 and 15% for 2028 under art. 25 transitorio of Ley 21.755 (Pro Pyme general companies, Art. 14 D N° 3); the rate returns to 25% afterwards  _(SII Circular 53 of 3 September 2025 (Ley 21.755, art. 25 transitorio) — https://www.sii.cl/normativa_legislacion/circulares/2025/circu53.pdf ; SII, Tasas de Impuesto de Primera Categoría (FAQ 4708) — https://www.sii.cl/preguntas_frecuentes/declaracion_renta/001_140_4708.htm)_
- **ProPyme regime baseline rate (outside the temporary reduction)** — 25%  _(Ley sobre Impuesto a la Renta (DL 824), Ley Chile consolidated text, Art. 14 D N° 3 — https://www.bcn.cl/leychile/navegar?idNorma=6368 ; SII, Tasas de Impuesto de Primera Categoría (FAQ 4708) — https://www.sii.cl/preguntas_frecuentes/declaracion_renta/001_140_4708.htm)_
- **ProPyme eligibility threshold** — Average gross income of the business over the last three commercial years not exceeding 75,000 UF (exceedable once, with no single year above 85,000 UF) and capital contributed at the start of activities not exceeding 85,000 UF  _(SII, Requisitos para optar al Régimen Pro Pyme (FAQ 7530) — https://www.sii.cl/preguntas_frecuentes/declaracion_renta/001_140_7530.htm ; Ley sobre Impuesto a la Renta (DL 824), Ley Chile consolidated text, Art. 14 D N° 1 — https://www.bcn.cl/leychile/navegar?idNorma=6368)_
- **Tax base** — Net taxable profit (Renta Liquida Imponible) — worldwide income for resident companies, with deduction of necessary business expenses and inflation adjustment (correccion monetaria)  _(Ley sobre Impuesto a la Renta (DL 824), SII text, Arts 29 to 33 — https://www.sii.cl/normativa_legislacion/dl824.pdf)_
- **Integration with shareholder tax** — Semi-integrated regime: shareholders credit 65% of the FCT paid against their IGC/Additional Tax (full 100% credit under treaties)  _(Ley sobre Impuesto a la Renta (DL 824), Ley Chile consolidated text, Art. 14 A N° 5, Art. 56 N° 3 and Art. 63 — https://www.bcn.cl/leychile/navegar?idNorma=6368 ; SII, Régimen General (Semi Integrado) (FAQ 7535) — https://www.sii.cl/preguntas_frecuentes/declaracion_renta/001_140_7535.htm)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends to non-residents** — 35% Additional Tax, reduced by the creditable portion of the First Category Tax already paid  _(Ley sobre Impuesto a la Renta (DL 824), SII text, Art. 58 N° 2 — https://www.sii.cl/normativa_legislacion/dl824.pdf ; Ley sobre Impuesto a la Renta (DL 824), Ley Chile consolidated text, Art. 63 — https://www.bcn.cl/leychile/navegar?idNorma=6368)_
- **Withholding tax on interest to non-residents (general)** — 35%  _(Ley sobre Impuesto a la Renta (DL 824), SII text, Art. 59 N° 1 — https://www.sii.cl/normativa_legislacion/dl824.pdf)_
- **Withholding tax on interest — qualifying foreign bank/financial-institution loans** — 4% on interest on foreign-currency deposits with authorised institutions and on loans from foreign or international banks and financial institutions (and qualifying foreign insurers and pension funds)  _(Ley sobre Impuesto a la Renta (DL 824), SII text, Art. 59 N° 1 letras a) and b) — https://www.sii.cl/normativa_legislacion/dl824.pdf)_
- **Withholding tax on royalties to non-residents** — 30% general; 15% for invention patents, utility models, industrial designs, layout designs, new plant varieties, copyright and edition rights and software; standard (non-customised) software may be exempt  _(Ley sobre Impuesto a la Renta (DL 824), SII text, Art. 59 incisos primero to tercero — https://www.sii.cl/normativa_legislacion/dl824.pdf)_
- **Monthly provisional payments (PPM)** — Monthly advance payments of FCT based on prior-year tax burden; SME PPM rates are halved for FY2025-2027  _(Ley sobre Impuesto a la Renta (DL 824), SII text, Art. 84 — https://www.sii.cl/normativa_legislacion/dl824.pdf ; Orbitax summary of the SME PPM reduction — https://orbitax.com/news/country/article/Chile-Extends-Reduced-Tax-Rate-59395)_
- **Corporate annual return filing & payment deadline** — April of the year following the commercial year (Operación Renta), on the dates the SII fixes each year, with the monthly PPM credited against the tax  _(Ley sobre Impuesto a la Renta (DL 824), SII text, Art. 69 — https://www.sii.cl/normativa_legislacion/dl824.pdf ; SII, Regímenes tributarios, Operación Renta 2025 — https://www.sii.cl/destacados/renta/2025/regimenes_renta2025.html)_
