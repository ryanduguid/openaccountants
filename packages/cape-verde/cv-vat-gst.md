# Cape Verde VAT / GST

## Value Added Tax (IVA)

- **Standard VAT rate** — 15%  _(Código do IVA (Lei n.º 21/VI/2003, republication), art 17(1) — https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/54571/64715/Lei+n%C2%BA+21+-+2003+-+Republica%C3%A7%C3%A3o+o+C%C3%B3digo+do+IVA+com+altera%C3%A7%C3%B5es.pdf/39072edf-457a-adc8-3133-554a65079487)_
- **Reduced VAT rate** — 8% on the supply of electricity and water to final consumers, set each year by the budget law (Lei 69/X/2025 art 84 for 2026); practitioner summaries also report a reduced rate for certain tourism services  _(Lei n.º 69/X/2025, de 31 de dezembro (Orçamento do Estado para 2026), art 84 — https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/198414/5793427/Lei+que+aprova+o+OE+2026.pdf/f9c15473-419c-0817-2d7d-dd46b4912fbc)_
- **Zero-rated supplies** — Exports, assimilated operations and international transport are exempt with credit (art 13); listed imports, including diplomatic and consular goods and qualifying re-imports, are exempt at import (art 12)  _(Código do IVA (Lei n.º 21/VI/2003, republication), arts 12 and 13 — https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/54571/64715/Lei+n%C2%BA+21+-+2003+-+Republica%C3%A7%C3%A3o+o+C%C3%B3digo+do+IVA+com+altera%C3%A7%C3%B5es.pdf/39072edf-457a-adc8-3133-554a65079487)_
- **VAT registration threshold** — Businesses outside the REMPE micro/small regime are within the normal VAT regime; the REMPE turnover ceiling is CVE 5,000,000 (approx — confirm)  _(Codigo do IVA (VAT Code))_
- **Filing frequency** — Monthly VAT return (declaracao periodica) under the normal regime (approx — confirm)  _(Codigo do IVA (VAT Code))_
- **VAT return and payment deadline** — By the last day of the month following the tax period (approx — confirm)  _(Codigo do IVA (VAT Code))_
- **Reverse charge** — Reverse charge applies to services acquired from non-resident suppliers; the Cape Verde recipient self-accounts for IVA (approx — confirm)  _(Codigo do IVA (VAT Code))_
- **VAT for micro/small companies** — Micro and small companies under the REMPE regime pay the 4% TEU in place of VAT and do not charge or recover VAT separately  _(Regime Jurídico Especial das Micro e Pequenas Empresas (Lei n.º 70/VIII/2014) as republished by Lei n.º 116/IX/2021, art 24(3) — https://www.mf.gov.cv/documents/54571/64342/Altera%C3%A7%C3%B5es+C%C3%B3digos+Tribut%C3%A1rios_Lei116-IX-2021%2C+de+2+fevereiro.pdf/26a33c81-cb9a-a813-a842-f4152024574c)_
