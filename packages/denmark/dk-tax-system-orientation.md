# Denmark tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record residence or establishment, legal form, income source, tax period and any treaty. Denmark, the Faroe Islands and Greenland have separate tax rules. [Danish Tax Agency, international tax agreements](https://info.skat.dk/data.aspx?oid=2082596)

## Territorial reach

A fully liable individual generally comes within Danish income tax on worldwide income, subject to domestic rules and treaty relief. A person with limited liability falls within charge on the Danish items specified by law, including qualifying employment income, property income and permanent establishment profits. Companies within the ordinary resident corporate charge start from the general income rules, with an exclusion for income and expenses of foreign permanent establishments and real estate. Check statutory exceptions, including international joint taxation and special shipping and aviation rules. A foreign company comes within the corporate charge on the items specified in § 2, including qualifying Danish permanent establishment profits and real estate income. [State Tax Act, § 4](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/1922/149/) · [Tax at Source Act, §§ 1–2](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2024/460) · [Corporate Tax Act, §§ 1–2 and 8](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2026/984/pdf)

## Residence

An individual ordinarily comes within full liability through residence in Denmark or a stay of at least 6 months including brief trips abroad for holidays or similar purposes. A person who acquires a residence before taking up a stay generally enters full liability when the stay begins. Short holiday-style visits do not count as taking up residence for this purpose. Full liability based on the qualifying 6-month stay generally begins on the first day of that stay. Check special rules for qualifying tourists and students and separate treaty residence. A Danish registered A/S or ApS is ordinarily within the resident company rules. For the other entities listed in § 1(1), nos 2–6, check Danish registration or the seat of management and the statutory exception for certain foreign-resident entities. [Tax at Source Act, §§ 1 and 7–8](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2024/460) · [Corporate Tax Act, § 1(1) and (6)](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2026/984/pdf) · [Danish Tax Agency, full and limited liability](https://skat.dk/en-us/individuals/taxation-in-denmark/tax-liability)

## Taxpayer types

A sole trader reports business profit through the individual tax rules. An ordinary general partnership (I/S) or limited partnership (K/S) is transparent for income tax. Check each partner's share and income classification and whether the statutory reclassification rule applies. An A/S or ApS generally pays corporation tax on its own result. For a foreign trust, check whether assets have been definitively and effectively separated from the settlor and who controls them. Check § 16 K separately for attribution of positive trust income to qualifying contributors, including the timing and exception rules. [Danish Tax Agency, starting a business](https://skat.dk/en-us/businesses/own-business/starting-a-business) · [Danish Tax Agency, transparent partnerships](https://info.skat.dk/data.aspx?oid=1976630) · [Danish Tax Agency, partner income classification](https://info.skat.dk/data.aspx?oid=2048527) · [Corporate Tax Act, §§ 1 and 2 C](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2026/984/pdf) · [Danish Tax Agency, foreign trusts](https://info.skat.dk/data.aspx?oid=1946424) · [Tax Assessment Act, § 16 K](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2025/1500)

## Currency

Denmark uses the Danish krone (DKK). Statutory amount disclosures to the Danish tax authorities use DKK. Businesses with eligible foreign-currency accounts can use the statutory conversion route where its conditions are met. Keep the exchange rate, conversion method and any required notification with the records. [Danish National Bank, exchange rates](https://www.nationalbanken.dk/da/vores-arbejde/stabile-priser-pengepolitik-og-dansk-oekonomi/valutakurser) · [Tax Control Act, §§ 28–35](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2024/12#P28) · [Danish Tax Agency, foreign-currency accounts](https://info.skat.dk/data.aspx?oid=2284510)

## Assessment periods

Individuals generally use the calendar year, or the period of liability when liable for only part of the year. An eligible self-employed person can choose a different income year under § 22. Companies generally use the calendar year, with a permitted different income year under § 10. Identify which calendar year's rules apply and check the separate rules for shorter periods or a change of year. [Tax at Source Act, § 22](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2024/460) · [Corporate Tax Act, § 10](https://www.retsinformation.dk/eli/lta/2026/984/pdf)

## Responsible authorities

Skattestyrelsen is the Danish Tax Agency and handles ordinary income tax, VAT and tax returns. Toldstyrelsen is the Danish Customs Agency and handles customs procedures. Confirm responsibility for the obligation in question. [Danish Tax Agency, agencies and responsibilities](https://skat.dk/en-us/about-us/about-us)

## Fictional intake

A person takes up residence in Denmark part-way through the year, continues a trade and receives foreign property income. Record the date full liability starts, the source and legal owner of each amount, the applicable individual income year and any treaty relief. If a company owns the property, check its corporate rules separately.
