# Turkmenistan tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the taxpayer, where income arose, residence facts and period. Check the applicable treaty and foreign-tax relief.

## Territorial reach

A resident individual generally accounts for taxable income from domestic and foreign sources. A nonresident individual generally accounts for Turkmenistan-source income. Resident legal entities generally account for profit and income from Turkmenistan and abroad. A nonresident legal entity can be taxed on profit or income from its local permanent establishment or Turkmenistan sources. Identify the source, profit attribution and treaty treatment for each receipt. [Tax Code, arts 18, 147–149, 179 and 182–185](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-code/10?lang=ru)

## Residence

An individual generally qualifies with at least 183 calendar days of presence in Turkmenistan during the tax year. Count part-days of actual presence and exclude transit-only presence. Check the exception for specified foreign diplomatic, consular and international organisation staff and their families. A legal entity qualifies if formed under Turkmen law or if its principal governing body is in Turkmenistan. Check any applicable treaty. [Tax Code, arts 5, 14 and 17](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-code/10?lang=ru)

## Taxpayer types

Identify the individual, individual entrepreneur or legal entity before choosing the tax treatment. Tax registration irregularities do not by themselves prevent treatment as an entrepreneur. For a contractual joint activity, the Code can put the payment obligation on one partner carrying on activity in Turkmenistan. The partners remain liable for their respective shares. Determine the payments and accounting from the contract and the applicable tax provisions. [Tax Code, arts 12, 15, 147 and 182](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-code/10?lang=ru) · [Civil Code, arts 1012–1018](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-code/6?lang=ru)

## Currency

Calculate and ordinarily pay taxes in the Turkmen manat (TMT). Use the Central Bank of Turkmenistan's official exchange rate on the transaction date, or the US-dollar cross-rate when there is no official rate for that currency, subject to Code exceptions. A nonresident legal entity may be able to pay in freely convertible currency, including through a tax agent. [Tax Code, art 9](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-code/10?lang=ru)

## Assessment periods

The tax year is ordinarily the calendar year. Ordinary corporate profit tax has cumulative first-quarter, half-year, nine-month and annual reporting periods. The individual tax period is the tax year, with half-year and annual reporting for some income requiring a return. Check the special rules for tax agents, foreign citizens and prescribed activities. [Tax Code, arts 5, 173, 191 and 195–197](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-code/10?lang=ru)

## Responsible authorities

The tax bodies of the Ministry of Finance and Economy administer ordinary domestic taxes. Use the relevant tax office for registration, declarations and documentary confirmation of residence. The State Customs Service has separate customs functions. [Tax Code, arts 13–14](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-code/10?lang=ru) · [Ministry of Finance and Economy](https://fineconomic.gov.tm/) · [State Customs Service](https://customs.gov.tm/)

## Fictional intake

A visitor spends part of several days in Turkmenistan on business and also changes planes there on transit-only days. Separate the actual business visits from the transit days before counting residence days.
