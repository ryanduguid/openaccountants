# Turkmenistan Personal Income Tax

## Personal income tax rates and residence

The [Tax Code published by Parliament](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-code/10?lang=ru) sets the ordinary 10% rate, deductions and collection rules. Article 188(1)(a) makes the standard monthly deduction one statutory base value for calculating taxes and fees. The monetary value has not been established here, so the former TMT 1,280 figure has been removed.

- **Personal income tax rate — residents** — **10% flat**. Article 192(1): *"Şu Bitewi kanunda başgaça bellenilmedik bolsa, salgyt möçberi 10 göterim derejede bellenýär"* — unless otherwise established in this Unified Law, the rate is set at 10 percent percent  _([Bitewi Kanun, art. 192(1)](https://tax.gov.tm/pdf/ckeditor_assets/attachments/10/2.pdf))_
- **Income from operating gambling activities** — Article 192(2) takes individuals' income from operating gambling activities **out of the flat rate** and charges it at the **fixed daily amounts in article 172(3)** — TMT 30 per gaming machine, TMT 370 per gaming table, TMT 7 per seat, TMT 3 per square metre of premises or TMT 38 per cash desk, per day, with the highest-yielding measure applying where more than one could. This is the *"unless otherwise established"* limb of article 192(1) in operation, and it was absent from this guide  _([Bitewi Kanun, arts. 172(3) and 192(2)](https://tax.gov.tm/pdf/ckeditor_assets/attachments/10/2.pdf))_
- **Personal income tax rate — non-residents** — 10% on Turkmenistan-source income percent ((approx — confirm))  _(Tax Code of Turkmenistan)_
- **Specific tax reductions**: Article 187(5) reduces calculated tax for eligible young graduates entering employment by 50% for the first 12 months and 25% for the next 24 months, with supporting documents required. Article 192(1) separately reduces calculated tax for qualifying youth entrepreneurs with at least 75% of workers under 35 by 50% in the first tax year of activity and 25% in the second and third. These are conditional reductions of tax, not a replacement progressive scale. ([Tax Code, arts. 187(5), 192(1)](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-code/10?lang=ru))
- **Tax bands** — None — Turkmenistan applies a single flat rate, not progressive brackets  _(Tax Code of Turkmenistan)_
- **Standard monthly personal deduction**: One statutory tax base value per month, subject to documentary requirements. Voluntary pension and medical insurance contributions are also deductible. The current monetary base value remains unverified. ([Tax Code, art. 188](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-code/10?lang=ru))
- **Income within scope** — Employment income, business and professional income, interest, royalties, real-estate income, and capital gains  _(Tax Code of Turkmenistan (as described at [gsl.org](https://gsl.org/en/taxes/turkmenistan/)))_
- **Capital gains (individuals)** — Taxed at the ordinary 10% personal income tax rate percent ((approx — confirm))  _(Tax Code of Turkmenistan)_
- **Residence test** — An individual is generally treated as resident if present in Turkmenistan for 183 days or more in the tax year ((approx — confirm))  _(Tax Code of Turkmenistan)_
- **Collection on employment income** — Employer withholds the 10% PIT at source from salary (PAYE-equivalent)  _(Tax Code of Turkmenistan (as described at [rivermate.com](https://rivermate.com/guides/turkmenistan/taxes)))_
- **⚠ Individual declaration deadline — the 25th, and 1 April for foreign citizens** — Article 197(1): taxpayers within article 196(1), ***other than foreign citizens***, file **no later than the 25th of the month following the reporting period**, at the tax office where they are registered. **Foreign citizens file no later than 1 April of the year following the past tax year.** The "31 March" stated here is in neither limb  _([Bitewi Kanun, art. 197(1)](https://tax.gov.tm/pdf/ckeditor_assets/attachments/10/2.pdf))_
- **The reporting period is a half-year, not a month** — Article 191: the **tax period is the tax year**; the **reporting period**, for separate types of income where a declaration is required during the tax year, is **the first half-year and the tax year**. So article 197's "25th of the month following the reporting period" falls after each half-year, not monthly  _([Bitewi Kanun, art. 191](https://tax.gov.tm/pdf/ckeditor_assets/attachments/10/2.pdf))_
- **⚠ A departing foreign individual files before leaving, not after** — Article 197(2): where a foreign individual **ceases** the activity taxed under article 196 during the tax year **and leaves Turkmenistan**, the declaration of income actually received while in the country during the current reporting period must be filed **no later than one month before departure**. Tax additionally computed on a declaration is payable **within fifteen days** of filing it  _([Bitewi Kanun, art. 197(2)](https://tax.gov.tm/pdf/ckeditor_assets/attachments/10/2.pdf))_
- **Filing voluntarily to get tax recomputed** — Article 197(1) also gives individuals **not obliged to file** the right to submit an income tax declaration to the tax office for their place of residence once the tax period ends, **so that the tax for the whole period is recomputed**  _([Bitewi Kanun, art. 197(1)](https://tax.gov.tm/pdf/ckeditor_assets/attachments/10/2.pdf))_
- **Withholding payment and reporting**: Tax is due by the day bank cash is obtained or income is transferred; tax on income received in kind is due by the following day. Annual income and withholding information is due by 20 January following the tax year. ([Tax Code, art. 195(5), (9)](https://mejlis.gov.tm/single-code/10?lang=ru))
