# Saint Pierre and Miquelon Tax System Orientation

Saint Pierre and Miquelon administers income and business taxes under its local tax code. Determine domicile, the source and type of income and the taxpayer's legal form before choosing a return. [Organic statute, art LO6414-1][CGCT] · [Direction des Services Fiscaux, local tax code][CLI]

## Residence and source

Under article 2, local fiscal domicile can arise from a principal residence, a principal place of stay, an employed or self-employed activity in the archipelago or the centre of economic interests. Local household income assessment takes account of income from outside the archipelago under the applicable code and convention rules. For taxpayers domiciled elsewhere, article 2 brings specified Saint Pierre and Miquelon-source income into local tax. Check the source category, any applicable exception and treaty treatment. [Local tax code, arts 2, 80 B and 81][CLI] · [France–Saint Pierre and Miquelon convention][TREATY]

## Taxpayer type

Individuals generally declare annual income by household. The treatment of a business depends on its form and any valid election. Article 106 lists entities within corporate income tax. Article 109 generally includes profits of enterprises operated in Saint Pierre and Miquelon and profits allocated to the territory under a double taxation convention. Article 118 has reporting provisions for certain entities outside the corporate regime. [Local tax code, arts 2, 4, 106, 109 and 118][CLI]

## Currency and assessment periods

The official currency is the euro (EUR). The annual individual income timetable in article 81 is 31 March for ordinary paper returns, 30 April for qualifying electronic returns and 15 May for non-residents within its scope. Corporate returns are generally due within three months after the accounting close or before 1 April of the following year when there is no close in the year. Verify the applicable period, annexes and filing method. [IEDOM, currency][CURRENCY] · [Local tax code, arts 81 and 117][CLI]

## Headline taxes and authorities

- **Personal income tax.** The progressive scale applies to an income share under the household quotient rules. Consult article 95 for the income year's scale and article 96 for the applicable non-resident calculation. [Local tax code, arts 88–96][CLI]
- **Corporate income tax.** Article 113 sets the ordinary rate at 23% for periods beginning on or after 1 January 2022. For periods beginning on or after 1 January 2024 the 15% rate can apply to up to EUR 42,500 of taxable profit per 12 months, with turnover below EUR 600,000 on a 12-month basis and conditions on paid-up capital and ownership. The code also provides for minimum annual taxation subject to exemptions. [Local tax code, art 113][CLI]
- **Indirect taxes.** IEDOM's 2025 report describes the absence of a general VAT and documents customs and import levies. Check the current tariff, classification and restrictions with the local customs service. [IEDOM, 2025 economic report][IEDOM]

The Direction des Services Fiscaux handles assessment and declarations. The Direction des Finances Publiques handles collection. [Tax administration, 2026 orientation for arrivals][DSF] · [IEDOM, 2025 economic report][IEDOM]
