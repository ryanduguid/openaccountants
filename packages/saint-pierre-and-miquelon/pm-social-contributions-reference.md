# Saint Pierre and Miquelon: social contributions reference v0.4

> **Conditional Tier 2 draft.** Load workflow-base and social-contributions-workflow-base. Their source gates, confirmed profile, refusals, outputs, checks and professional review remain mandatory. The fictional pension components below are not total contributions due, payslips, returns or payment instructions.

## Scope and source limits

This reference preserves selected employee old-age contribution sources and annual reporting interfaces for Saint Pierre and Miquelon. It does not establish a complete contribution regime or certify the law applicable to an actual period. The CPS January 2026 sheet includes other programmes and categories whose complete legal bases, eligibility and later changes have not been reconciled here. Full payroll, income tax, cross-border coordination, formation, financial reporting and property obligations require separate investigation.

The official July 2025 consolidated dataset supplied complete bodies and closed dated structures for law 87-563, ordinance 77-1102 and decree 2017-1000. Their selected structures contained 10, 64 and 13 date-active article references respectively. This is snapshot source capture, not proof of present legal effect: dated links can include abrogated records. Later July 2026 captures disclose material changes. No complete amendment search through an actual requested period, current national cross-reference certificate or operational CPS acceptance is established. An open-ended metadata date, current link label or successful download does not close that gap.

### Professional income provisionals and regularisation

S17 supplies the following dated, conditional component. Establish contributor, programme, requested-period law, ceiling/units and lawful precision before use. Article 2 literally refers to 'employeurs et travailleurs indépendants' and the ordinance article 3 risks: their professional income retained for income-tax calculation, within the PM ceiling. Do not turn that wording into a profit-based ordinary employee payroll rule or assume it resolves every old-age programme. Keep law article 4's separate old-age base/rate chain.

1. Keep contribution year Y, the professional income from Y-2 and the year of regularisation separate. Article 6 uses the penultimate year's professional income for the 4 quarterly provisional fractions. It does not state that those fractions are equal. Establish tax-income adjustments, the applicable programme/base/rate and qualifying fractions independently; a salary-sheet percentage is insufficient.
2. Once Y's professional income is definitive, establish its definitive contribution and the total provisional fractions used in the regularisation. Preserve due, declared and paid amounts separately and reconcile discrepancies; a declared amount alone is not proof of a qualifying payment.
3. Article 6 pays a positive balance in 2 equal parts with the third- and fourth-quarter provisional fractions of the 'année en cours'. A negative difference is applied half against each of those fractions; any remaining balance is refunded at the last fraction's due date. K below denotes that legally established current year, not an arbitrary chosen year or a notification-trigger default. Late processing after the relevant dates remains unresolved. Keep adjustment, fraction allocation, scheduled cash and actual settlement separate. Do not import S14's employee BRA notification instruction into this branch.
4. Article 8 specifies a provisional first activity income base of one-third of the PM ceiling. Numerical use needs an independently established applicable ceiling, units, status and programme. Its contribution starts with the quarter containing commencement and continues through 31 December of the following year. The low-income refund may be made on request under its stated conditions; it is not automatic. Do not equate 'minimum soumis à cotisation' with the one-third base without establishing that interpretation. Changed operating conditions and restarting in the cessation year or following year are excluded from 'beginning activity'.
5. Article 9 stays collection for quarters following cessation until annual regularisation. It does not cancel the liability or make the cessation quarter zero.

S17 article 3 describes CPS income forms sent by 1 April and completed, signed returns by 1 May; article 5 describes payment within the first 15 days of each calendar quarter's first month. These are captured text observations, not verified current deadlines. Article 4 literally refers to article 5, whilst article 3 describes the income declaration.
