# Fiji Tax Overview

Use Fiji's Income Tax Act 2015 for ordinary national income tax. Establish the taxpayer, relevant period, source of income and whether a special regime or treaty applies.

## Residence

An individual meets a domestic test if they live in Fiji, are domiciled there without a permanent home abroad, are present for 183 days in aggregate in a 12-month period or are a Fiji government employee posted overseas. The first or last day of presence can start or end part-year residence where the adjacent year is non-resident. [Income Tax Act, s 6][ITA]

## Income and territorial scope

A resident person's gross income generally includes income from Fiji and abroad. A non-resident person's gross income is limited to Fiji-source income. Check the source rules for the class of income, including the permanent-establishment rules for business income. Exempt amounts and specified separately taxed amounts are outside ordinary gross income. A non-citizen without Fiji permanent residence who is resident mainly or solely to work in Fiji under an employment contract of at most 3 years can qualify as a temporary resident. The exemption for that category covers foreign income other than employment income. [Income Tax Act, ss 2, 7 and 14 and Exempt Income Regulations, Schedule Pt 3 para 13][ITA]

## Taxpayer type

A company is resident if incorporated, formed or settled in Fiji or if any part of its central management and control is there. A partnership can be resident by formation or by the same management test. The partnership files an income return and the partners account for their shares as applicable. Trustees and beneficiaries have separate rules under ss 55–57. [Income Tax Act, ss 2, 51–58 and 104][ITA]

## Currency

Use Fiji dollars (FJD). Ordinary foreign-currency amounts use the relevant financial institution's telegraphic transfer rate on the date the amount is taken into account. Section 139 allows a tax-year average for the business amounts it specifies with prior written approval. [Income Tax Act, s 139][ITA] · [Reserve Bank of Fiji exchange rates][RBF]

## Assessment periods

The ordinary tax year is the calendar year. A business company can adopt a different 12-month period with written approval, and the transition inherits the rules in s 36. Annual returns required by s 104 are due within 3 months after year-end, ordinarily 31 March for a December balance date. Check the individual filing exceptions in s 105. [Income Tax Act, ss 2, 36 and 104–105][ITA]

## Tax authority and headline rates

The Fiji Revenue and Customs Service (FRCS) administers the taxes. Its resident individual table has a nil band through FJ$30,000 and a top marginal rate of 39% on chargeable income above FJ$1 million. The standard company rate is 25%. The separate Social Responsibility Tax ended on 31 December 2023 and the relevant income moved into the income tax bands. Check the published schedule for the taxpayer and period. [FRCS rates][RATES] · [FRCS interpretation guideline on individual rates][SRT]

Standard VAT fell from 15% to 12.5% on 1 August 2025. Use the time of supply to identify the rate at the change and apply point-of-sale label G to supplies at 12.5%. The 22 items listed as zero-rated were unaffected by the standard-rate change. Check the applicable schedule for the date of supply. FRCS lists the former ECAL services label as removed and uses label H for the separate 5% Tourism Services Tax from 1 September 2026 where that tax applies. [FRCS VAT calculation notice][VATCHANGE] · [FRCS time-of-supply notice][TIME] · [FRCS VAT and tax label guide][VAT] · [FRCS zero-rated items][ZERO] · [FRCS Tourism Services Tax notice][TST]
