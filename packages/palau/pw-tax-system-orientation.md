# Palau Tax System Orientation

Palau's Title 40 taxes wages, registered businesses' net profits, specified gross revenues and taxable consumption under separate rules. Start with the activity, wage or business income, and whether the person is registered or required to register for Palau Goods and Services Tax (PGST). The national Bureau of Revenue and Taxation administers the taxes covered here. [Palau National Code, Title 40, chs 10–12, 14 and 15][PNC] · [Bureau of Revenue and Taxation][BRT]

## Residence

For Title 40's tax division, an individual is resident if domiciled in Palau or if present for a purpose other than a temporary or transitory one. Presence for more than 60 days in aggregate in the tax year raises a presumption of residence. The Director can determine residence based on the individual's work or profession in Palau regardless of day count. For the business profits tax provisions, a company incorporated or formed in Palau is resident. The definition also covers partnerships, trusts and other listed entities formed, settled or otherwise established there. [Title 40, ss 1002(31) and 1412(19)][PNC]

## Income and territorial scope

Wages and salary tax generally applies to compensation as an employee for personal services performed in Palau, subject to statutory exclusions. For business tax source, s 1414 generally treats a resident's gross revenue as Palau-source except revenue attributable to a permanent establishment outside Palau. A non-resident's Palau-source revenue includes revenue attributable to a Palau permanent establishment and specified similar local sales or activities. Section 1414(c) gives separate source rules for listed receipts. Sections 1422 and 1423 impose separate charges on specified non-resident receipts and international transport, with exceptions. [Title 40, ss 1002(35), 1101 and 1412–1423][PNC]

## Taxpayer type

PGST-registered persons, including qualifying commercial banks, pay business profits tax on net income at the top wages and salary rate, currently 12%. A non-registered person carrying on a business with annual gross income above USD 50,000 generally pays 4% gross revenue tax after the prescribed salary deductions. A non-registered business at or below that amount generally pays the additional USD 100 annual business licence fee under s 1506, collected by quarterly instalments. Check the separate PGST registration rules in ss 1221–1222 before classifying the business. A sole proprietor's own drawings do not constitute employee wages. [Title 40, ss 1101, 1221–1222, 1298, 1421 and 1506][PNC] · [Bureau circular on sole proprietors][SOLE]

## Currency

Title 40 expresses PGST and the relevant business profits tax amounts in US dollars (USD). For PGST, use the customs conversion rule on imports and generally the Federal Reserve mid-exchange rate on the recognition date for other amounts, subject to s 1293. For business profits tax, s 1471 states the recognition-date rate and allows annual averages with the Director's prior written permission. [Title 40, ss 1293 and 1471][PNC]

## Assessment periods

The general tax year is the calendar year. For business profits tax a company's tax year ends on the date of annual balance of its accounts, and other taxpayers generally use the calendar year. The business profits tax return and balance are due within 3 months after year end, with instalments under the regulations. Employer wage withholding is generally paid within 30 days after disbursement and the return within 30 days after quarter end. For PGST, the period is a calendar month and returns and domestic payments are due within 30 days after period end. PGST on a taxable import is payable at import. [Title 40, ss 1002(34), 1102, 1202(30), 1281–1282, 1412(24) and 1462–1464][PNC]

## Headline rates

Ordinary wages and salary tax is 6% on the first USD 8,000, 10% on the next USD 32,000 and 12% on the excess. Citizen-employee refunds require an application and follow separate eligibility and income rules. PGST is generally 10%, with zero-rated and exempt supplies distinguished in Title 40. See the existing Palau PGST charge and value reference for classification and calculation. [Title 40, ss 1101, 1104 and 1211–1215][PNC] · [Bureau of Revenue and Taxation][BRT]
