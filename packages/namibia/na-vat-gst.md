# Namibia VAT / GST

## VAT rates, registration and filing

- **VAT overview** — Namibia levies Value-Added Tax (VAT) on taxable supplies of goods and services and on imports, administered by NamRA under the Value-Added Tax Act. The standard rate is 15%, with certain zero-rated and exempt categories.
- **Standard VAT rate** — 15%  _(Value-Added Tax Act 10 of 2000 (NamRA copy of the annotated consolidation including Act 5 of 2024), s 6(1) — https://www.namra.org.na/documents/cms/uploaded/valueadded-tax-act-10-of-2000-0d8c7ed7fc.pdf ; Namibia Revenue Agency, VAT page — https://www.namra.org.na/tax-types/page/value-added-tax-vat-30120)_
- **Zero-rated supplies** — 0%: exports, international transport services, supplies to export processing zone enterprises, fuel subject to the fuel levy (petrol, diesel, kerosene), sanitary pads and the listed basic foods: mahango, mahango meal and maize meal (para 2(s)), fresh and dried beans, sunflower cooking oil, animal fat for food, bread and cake flour, bread, sugar and fresh milk  _(Value-Added Tax Act 10 of 2000 (NamRA copy of the annotated consolidation including Act 5 of 2024), s 9 and Schedule III para 2 — https://www.namra.org.na/documents/cms/uploaded/valueadded-tax-act-10-of-2000-0d8c7ed7fc.pdf)_
- **Exempt supplies** — Financial services, residential accommodation in a dwelling, education services, medical services in registered hospitals and similar institutions, and public transport services  _(Value-Added Tax Act 10 of 2000 (NamRA copy of the annotated consolidation including Act 5 of 2024), s 10 and Schedule IV — https://www.namra.org.na/documents/cms/uploaded/valueadded-tax-act-10-of-2000-0d8c7ed7fc.pdf)_
- **Compulsory VAT registration threshold** — N$1,000,000 of taxable supplies in any 12-month period (NamRA's web page still shows the earlier N$500,000 figure)  _(Value-Added Tax Act 10 of 2000 (NamRA copy of the annotated consolidation including Act 5 of 2024), s 15(1), as amended by Act 5 of 2024 — https://www.namra.org.na/documents/cms/uploaded/valueadded-tax-act-10-of-2000-0d8c7ed7fc.pdf)_
- **Voluntary VAT registration threshold** — N$200,000: a person whose taxable supplies exceed or are expected to exceed N$200,000 in a 12-month period may apply to register  _(Value-Added Tax Act 10 of 2000 (NamRA copy of the annotated consolidation including Act 5 of 2024), s 15(3) — https://www.namra.org.na/documents/cms/uploaded/valueadded-tax-act-10-of-2000-0d8c7ed7fc.pdf)_
- **VAT return filing frequency** — Tax periods of two calendar months for registered persons  _(Value-Added Tax Act 10 of 2000 (NamRA copy of the annotated consolidation including Act 5 of 2024), s 23(2)(b) — https://www.namra.org.na/documents/cms/uploaded/valueadded-tax-act-10-of-2000-0d8c7ed7fc.pdf)_
- **VAT return and payment due date** — Not later than the 25th day of the month following the end of the tax period, whether or not tax is payable  _(Value-Added Tax Act 10 of 2000 (NamRA copy of the annotated consolidation including Act 5 of 2024), s 24(1) — https://www.namra.org.na/documents/cms/uploaded/valueadded-tax-act-10-of-2000-0d8c7ed7fc.pdf ; Namibia Revenue Agency, VAT page — https://www.namra.org.na/tax-types/page/value-added-tax-vat-30120)_
- **Import VAT** — 15% on the value of imported goods and imported services other than exempt imports  _(Value-Added Tax Act 10 of 2000 (NamRA copy of the annotated consolidation including Act 5 of 2024), s 6(1) — https://www.namra.org.na/documents/cms/uploaded/valueadded-tax-act-10-of-2000-0d8c7ed7fc.pdf)_
- **Imported services (reverse charge basics)** — VAT on imported services is paid by the recipient where the services are acquired by a non-registered person or for making exempt supplies; a registered person importing services for taxable supplies treats the import as exempt  _(Value-Added Tax Act 10 of 2000 (NamRA copy of the annotated consolidation including Act 5 of 2024), ss 6(1) and 14 — https://www.namra.org.na/documents/cms/uploaded/valueadded-tax-act-10-of-2000-0d8c7ed7fc.pdf ; Namibia Revenue Agency, VAT page — https://www.namra.org.na/tax-types/page/value-added-tax-vat-30120)_
