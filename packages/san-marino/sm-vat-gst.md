# San Marino — the imposta monofase (there is no VAT)

> **The previous version's framing was right and its description of the tax was
> half of one.** San Marino genuinely has no VAT, and the rate list is plausible.
> What was missing is the thing that makes the tax workable: **the monofase is
> refunded on export**, by art. 1 of the governing law, through an annual refund
> declaration to the Ufficio Tributario. The previous version said nothing about
> refunds, and a widely-repeated commercial description calls the tax *"non
> detraibile e non rimborsabile"* — not deductible and not refundable. The first
> half is right and the second half is contradicted by the statute's own opening
> article.

## 1. What the tax is — Legge 22 dicembre 1972 n.40

- **A tax on importation, not on value added** — *"L'imposta sulla importazione
  nel territorio della Repubblica di San Marino di beni e di servizi connessi"*.
  It is charged once, when goods and connected services enter the territory,
  rather than collected and offset at each stage of a supply chain  _(Legge 22 dicembre 1972 n.40, art. 1 — https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/archivio-leggi-decreti-e-regolamenti/documento17019029.html)_
- **It is refunded when the goods are exported — even after transformation** —
  The second sentence of art. 1: *"È previsto il rimborso dell'imposta quando i
  beni, anche dopo la loro trasformazione, vengono esportati."* This is the
  provision that lets a San Marino manufacturer import inputs, process them and
  export without the monofase sticking to the finished goods. **Any description
  of the monofase as simply non-refundable is wrong about the export case**, and
  that is the case most exporters are in  _(Legge 22 dicembre 1972 n.40, art. 1 — https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/archivio-leggi-decreti-e-regolamenti/documento17019029.html)_
- **One ordinary rate, moved up or down by annexed tables** — Art. 4 sets a
  single rate on the taxable base and then provides that *"L'aliquota è ridotta o
  elevata nella misura stabilita nelle tabelle allegate."* So the structure is one
  headline rate plus schedules that reclassify particular goods, not a set of
  independent rates  _(Legge 22 dicembre 1972 n.40, art. 4 — https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/archivio-leggi-decreti-e-regolamenti/documento17019029.html)_
- **The base is the total consideration due to the supplier** — and where the
  consideration is agreed in a foreign currency, it is converted at the official
  rate **of the day the tax is paid**, not the day of the contract or the invoice  _(Legge 22 dicembre 1972 n.40, art. 3 — https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/archivio-leggi-decreti-e-regolamenti/documento17019029.html)_
- **Barter is taxed on the whole** — In exchange contracts the taxable base is
  the value of **all** the goods imported  _(Legge 22 dicembre 1972 n.40, art. 3 — https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/archivio-leggi-decreti-e-regolamenti/documento17019029.html)_
- **What falls outside the charge** — Transfers of money or money claims
  (including foreign currency and claims in foreign currency); postal values that
  are legal tender in the issuing country and stamped values; shares, bonds and
  other securities **not representing goods**; daily newspapers. And separately,
  **imports made by State bodies**. The previous version listed no exclusions at
  all  _(Legge 22 dicembre 1972 n.40, art. 2(a)–(b) — https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/archivio-leggi-decreti-e-regolamenti/documento17019029.html)_

## 2. The rates are not in the law, and that is why citing the law for them is wrong

> **Art. 26 delegates every rate to decree.** *"Le eventuali variazioni delle
> aliquote, l'introduzione di esenzioni, la variazione dei beni soggetti alle
> diverse aliquote nonché le modalità che devono osservare gli Uffici, possono
> essere disposte con decreto della Reggenza, sentito il Congresso di Stato."*
> Rate changes, new exemptions **and the movement of goods between rate bands**
> are all decree matters. The previous version cited "Imposta Monofase
> (single-phase import tax) legislation" beside each rate; the legislation does
> not contain them.
>
> The law's own text demonstrates it. As enacted in 1972 the art. 4 ordinary rate
> was **7%**, with annexed tables at 2, 3, 8, 9, 14 and 15 per cent. Every one of
> those figures has been superseded by decree. **A rate quoted against Legge
> 40/1972 is a rate quoted against a document that has not set it for fifty
> years.**  _(Legge 22 dicembre 1972 n.40, arts. 4 and 26 and annexed tables — https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/archivio-leggi-decreti-e-regolamenti/documento17019029.html)_

## 3. The refund machinery

- **The refund regulation** — Decreto 29 novembre 2004 n.163, *"Regolamento di
  rimborso dell'imposta sulle importazioni"*, as amended by art. 5 of
  Decreto-Legge 22 settembre 2011 n.149  _(Legge 3 marzo 2025 n.30, art. 34 — https://www.gov.sm/pub2/GovSM/dam/jcr:f1d046ac-b8d5-4cf8-ae74-22f122f7ae53/17150774L030-2025.pdf)_
- **An annual declaration, computed on an average rate** — The Ufficio Tributario
  publishes a *Dichiarazione annuale agli effetti del rimborso dell'imposta sulle
  importazioni*. It works from the year's purchases, derives an **aliquota media**
  (tax over taxable base) and provides for differentiated refund rates. An
  exporter's recovery is therefore driven by the mix of rates on what it bought,
  not by a single headline percentage  _(Ufficio Tributario, Dichiarazione annuale per il rimborso dell'imposta sulle importazioni — https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/archivio-leggi-decreti-e-regolamenti/documento17023834.html)_
- **Supplier credit notes are refunded in full, on top of the export refund** —
  From 2025, credit notes and equivalent documents issued **within one year** of
  the taxable operation, arising from commercial agreements with suppliers and so
  feeding directly into the purchase price of imported goods, are taken as a
  direct reduction of the purchase volume and listed separately in the annual
  declaration. The monofase on them is *"rimborsata integralmente
  all'operatore"*, adding to the refund computed on exports. The commercial
  agreements must be kept available for the Ufficio Tributario to inspect at any
  time  _(Legge 3 marzo 2025 n.30, art. 34(1), substituting art. 6(13) of Decreto n.163/2004 — https://www.gov.sm/pub2/GovSM/dam/jcr:f1d046ac-b8d5-4cf8-ae74-22f122f7ae53/17150774L030-2025.pdf)_
- **And one paragraph was deleted** — Art. 6(14) of Decreto n.163/2004 is
  suppressed  _(Legge 3 marzo 2025 n.30, art. 34(2) — https://www.gov.sm/pub2/GovSM/dam/jcr:f1d046ac-b8d5-4cf8-ae74-22f122f7ae53/17150774L030-2025.pdf)_

## 4. Registration, postal imports and cross-border

- **Postal imports can be settled on collection** — For goods bought and imported
  through Poste San Marino S.p.A., the tax *"può essere liquidata al momento del
  ritiro del bene"*, with Poste San Marino carrying out the filing, assessment and
  payment steps under rules set by the Ufficio Tributario. This is new in 2025 and
  matters to anyone importing by post or courier  _(Legge 3 marzo 2025 n.30, art. 33 — https://www.gov.sm/pub2/GovSM/dam/jcr:f1d046ac-b8d5-4cf8-ae74-22f122f7ae53/17150774L030-2025.pdf)_
- **Petroleum products carry their own charge** — A separate special import tax on
  petroleum products exists alongside the monofase, with its own register
  ((approx — confirm the current rate and register against the governing decree))  _(Legge 3 marzo 2025 n.30 (register provision) — https://www.gov.sm/pub2/GovSM/dam/jcr:f1d046ac-b8d5-4cf8-ae74-22f122f7ae53/17150774L030-2025.pdf)_
- **Operator registration** — Importing economic operators must be registered with
  San Marino (operator / economic-activity code) to clear goods and account for
  the monofase ((approx — confirm the current registration procedure))  _(Imposta Monofase registration requirements (as described at [startup.sm](https://www.startup.sm/en/taxation/monofase-and-vat/)))_
- **Interface with Italian VAT** — San Marino is **outside the EU VAT territory**
  and trade with Italy runs under a bilateral regime, with electronic invoicing
  through the SDI reconciling Italian VAT against San Marino monofase ((approx —
  confirm the current procedure and time limits))  _(Italy–San Marino administrative cooperation on indirect tax (as described at [vatupdate.com](https://www.vatupdate.com/category/san-marino/)))_

## 5. What this replaces

| The guide said | The statute says |
| --- | --- |
| "San Marino does NOT have a VAT… imposta monofase" | **Confirmed** — Legge 40/1972 art. 1, and no VAT is legislated in either Legge 157/2022 or Legge 30/2025 |
| Each rate cited to "Imposta Monofase legislation" | The legislation does **not** set the rates. Art. 26 delegates them to *decreto della Reggenza* — the 1972 law's own ordinary rate is **7%** |
| *nothing* | **The tax is refunded on export**, even after transformation — art. 1 |
| *nothing* | An **annual refund declaration** to the Ufficio Tributario, computed on an **aliquota media** of the year's purchases |
| *nothing* | Supplier credit notes within one year are **refunded in full** on top of the export refund — Legge 30/2025 art. 34 |
| *nothing* | Postal imports may be settled **on collection of the goods** via Poste San Marino — Legge 30/2025 art. 33 |
| *nothing* | Barter is taxed on the value of **all** goods imported — art. 3 |

The structure, the export refund and the delegation of rate-setting were read
from the text of Legge 22 dicembre 1972 n.40 as published by the Consiglio
Grande e Generale, and the 2025 changes from Legge 3 marzo 2025 n.30 as
published by the Government. The rate table in §2 was **not** verified and is
marked throughout: the archive serves the 1972 law as enacted rather than a
consolidated text, so the decrees that have moved every rate since could not be
identified from it.
