# San Marino Tax Overview

## The San Marino tax system at a glance

- **IGR unified tax system** — San Marino taxes income under a single unified law, the Imposta Generale sui Redditi (IGR), which covers both individuals and companies. Notably, San Marino does NOT operate a VAT — it applies a single-phase import tax (imposta monofase) instead.
- **Tax year** — Calendar year (1 January – 31 December)  _(Law no. 166 of 16 December 2013 (Imposta Generale sui Redditi) — https://taxatlas.io/country/san-marino)_
- **Currency** — Euro (EUR)  _(Monetary Agreement between the EU and the Republic of San Marino)_
- **Tax authority** — Ufficio Tributario (Tax Office), Dipartimento Finanze e Bilancio  _(Law no. 166 of 16 December 2013 (Imposta Generale sui Redditi) — https://gov.sm)_
- **Residence basis of taxation** — Residents taxed on worldwide income; non-residents taxed on San Marino-source income only  _(Law no. 166 of 16 December 2013 (Imposta Generale sui Redditi) — https://taxravens.com/en/san-marino/income-tax)_
- **Headline personal income tax rate (top marginal)** — 35% % (progressive, 8 brackets from 9% to 35%)  _(Law no. 166 of 16 December 2013 (Imposta Generale sui Redditi) — https://taxravens.com/en/san-marino/income-tax)_
- **Headline corporate income tax rate** — 17% % (flat; temporarily raised to 18% for tax periods 2026–2030 under Law 141/2025)  _(Law no. 166 of 16 December 2013 (Imposta Generale sui Redditi) — https://taxatlas.io/country/san-marino)_
- **Does San Marino have VAT/GST?** — No VAT. A single-phase import tax (imposta monofase) applies instead, standard rate 17%  _(Imposta Monofase (single-phase import tax) legislation — https://www.startup.sm/en/taxation/monofase-and-vat/)_
- **Annual personal income tax return deadline** — Typically by 30 June of the year following the tax year ((approx — confirm))  _(Law no. 166 of 16 December 2013 (Imposta Generale sui Redditi) — https://taxatlas.io/country/san-marino)_
- **New-company corporate tax incentive** — 50% reduction of corporate IGR for the first 5 years (effective rate ~8.5%) (subject to conditions; confirm eligibility)  _(Law no. 166 of 16 December 2013 (Imposta Generale sui Redditi) and incentive decrees — https://www.camcom.sm/en/doing-business-in-san-marino-2/favourable-taxation/)_
