# San Marino tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the taxpayer type, residence facts, income sources and relevant period. Check any treaty that assigns residence or limits source-country tax.

## Territorial reach

San Marino's general income tax (IGR) covers resident individuals' and entities' income from San Marino and abroad. For nonresidents, identify income produced in San Marino under the statutory source rules. The rules include income from property in San Marino and business conducted through a San Marino permanent establishment. Classify the receipt and check the relevant treaty and tax relief. [IGR Law 166/2013, arts 1 and 4–5, coordinated text](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/documento17086436.html) · [Delegated Decree 156/2026, arts 2 and 19](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/archivio-leggi-decreti-e-regolamenti/documento17160902.html)

## Residence

An individual qualifies if they have registered residence in San Marino for most of the tax period, are actually living there for most of the period or have their centre of vital interests there. The third test takes account of economic, property, social and family interests. A legal entity or assimilated body qualifies if its registered office or place of effective management is in San Marino for most of the tax period. Check treaty residence if more than one jurisdiction claims the taxpayer. [IGR Law 166/2013, arts 2, 10 and 42, coordinated text](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/documento17086436.html)

## Taxpayer types

Individuals account for their sole-trade and other income under Title II of the IGR Law. Legal entities and assimilated bodies are subject to Title III. Ordinary partnerships attribute income to partners by their profit shares even if the cash stays in the partnership. Associations of professionals receive the same treatment as partnerships. Check source, entity classification and any separate withholding obligations for each participant. [IGR Law 166/2013, arts 2, 10–11 and 42, coordinated text](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/documento17086436.html)

## Currency

San Marino uses the euro (EUR). Record the amounts used for the tax return in euro and keep evidence of the conversion basis for foreign-currency receipts, expenses and tax payments. Check the conversion and accounting treatment for the item and period. [EU–San Marino Monetary Agreement, art 1](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A02012A0426%2801%29-20260324) · [IGR Law 166/2013, arts 3 and 44, coordinated text](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/documento17086436.html)

## Assessment periods

Each calendar year is a separate IGR tax period. Apply the income category's timing rules and check filing and payment deadlines separately. The October 2026 decree applies its amendments from the 2026 tax period and preserves the earlier rules for earlier periods, including assessment of those periods. [IGR Law 166/2013, art 3, coordinated text](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/documento17086436.html) · [Delegated Decree 156/2026, art 19](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/archivio-leggi-decreti-e-regolamenti/documento17160902.html)

## Responsible authorities

The Ufficio Tributario collects, checks and assesses IGR. Use its direct taxes service for registration and reporting. If final tax was not withheld, check the ISS code for a nonresident individual and the economic operator code for a nonresident operator. A nonresident economic operator can appoint a fiscal representative in San Marino to obtain the code on its behalf and assume the associated obligations. [IGR Law 166/2013, art 2, coordinated text](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/documento17086436.html) · [Delegated Decree 156/2026, art 2](https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/archivio-leggi-decreti-e-regolamenti/documento17160902.html) · [Ufficio Tributario](https://www.gov.sm/pub1/GovSM/Dipartimenti/Dipartimento-Economia-e-Finanze/Ufficio-Tributario.html)

## Fictional intake

A partnership in San Marino earns income in 2026 and retains the cash. A partner is registered abroad but lives mainly in San Marino. Determine that partner's residence under domestic law and any treaty, then identify their share of the partnership income for the 2026 calendar year.
