# San Marino Company Formation & Entity Choice

## Entity types, capital and incorporation

- **SRL (limited liability company)** — Società a responsabilità limitata — quota capital; can have a sole member  _(San Marino Company Law (https://lawstrust.com/en/company-formation-preferential-taxation/san-marino))_
- **SPA (joint-stock company)** — Società per azioni — share capital; can have a sole member  _(San Marino Company Law (https://lawstrust.com/en/company-formation-preferential-taxation/san-marino))_
- **SRL minimum capital** — EUR 25,500 EUR  _(San Marino Company Law (https://lawstrust.com/en/company-formation-preferential-taxation/san-marino))_
- **SRL capital payment timing** — Half paid within 60 days of registration, balance within 3 years; if sole member, full amount within 60 days  _(San Marino Company Law (https://lawstrust.com/en/company-formation-preferential-taxation/san-marino))_
- **SPA minimum capital** — EUR 77,000 EUR  _(San Marino Company Law (https://lawstrust.com/en/company-formation-preferential-taxation/san-marino))_
- **SPA capital deposit** — Full share capital deposited into a corporate bank account prior to incorporation  _(San Marino Company Law (https://lawstrust.com/en/company-formation-preferential-taxation/san-marino))_
- **Sole proprietorship** — Lavoro autonomo individuale / impresa individuale — available for individuals carrying on business or a profession  _(San Marino Company / Economic Activity Law (https://www.deel.com/blog/sole-proprietorship-san-marino/))_
- **Director residence requirement** — Generally at least one director must be resident in San Marino ((confirm current rule with local counsel))  _(San Marino Company Law (https://www.healyconsultants.com/san-marino-company-registration/setup-llc/))_
- **Incorporation procedure** — Articles of incorporation drafted and executed by notary, registered with the tax registration office, then entered in the companies register at the Office for Economic Activities; an economic-activity licence is required to operate  _(San Marino Company Law (https://lawstrust.com/en/company-formation-preferential-taxation/san-marino))_
- **Minimum members / directors** — One shareholder and one director sufficient for both SRL and SPA  _(San Marino Company Law (https://lawstrust.com/en/company-formation-preferential-taxation/san-marino))_
- **Core annual compliance** — Keep statutory accounting records, file annual financial statements, file the annual IGR return, and operate ISS/payroll obligations  _(San Marino Company Law and Law no. 166 of 16 December 2013 (IGR) (https://www.camcom.sm/en/doing-business-in-san-marino-2/favourable-taxation/))_
