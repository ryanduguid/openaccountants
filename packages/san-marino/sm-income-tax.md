# San Marino Personal Income Tax

## Personal income tax (IGR) bands and rates

Individuals pay the Imposta Generale sui Redditi (IGR) on a progressive scale of eight brackets from 9% to 35%, set in Allegato C art. C1 of Legge 166/2013 (coordinated text to 22 December 2025, published by the Consiglio Grande e Generale); the bracket boundaries are revalued every two years against the cost-of-living index from tax period 2028. Earlier drafts of this guide carried intermediate boundaries from firm summaries that disagreed with one another; the schedule below is the statute's.

- **Income up to EUR 10,000** — 9%  _(Legge 16 dicembre 2013 n. 166 (Imposta Generale sui Redditi), testo coordinato al 22 dicembre 2025, Allegato C art. C1 — https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/archivio-leggi-decreti-e-regolamenti/documento17086436.html)_
- **Income EUR 10,000.01 – 18,000** — 13%  _(Legge 16 dicembre 2013 n. 166 (Imposta Generale sui Redditi), testo coordinato al 22 dicembre 2025, Allegato C art. C1 — https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/archivio-leggi-decreti-e-regolamenti/documento17086436.html)_
- **Income EUR 18,000.01 – 28,000** — 17%  _(Legge 16 dicembre 2013 n. 166 (Imposta Generale sui Redditi), testo coordinato al 22 dicembre 2025, Allegato C art. C1 — https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/archivio-leggi-decreti-e-regolamenti/documento17086436.html)_
- **Income EUR 28,000.01 – 38,000** — 21%  _(Legge 16 dicembre 2013 n. 166 (Imposta Generale sui Redditi), testo coordinato al 22 dicembre 2025, Allegato C art. C1 — https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/archivio-leggi-decreti-e-regolamenti/documento17086436.html)_
- **Income EUR 38,000.01 – 50,000** — 25%  _(Legge 16 dicembre 2013 n. 166 (Imposta Generale sui Redditi), testo coordinato al 22 dicembre 2025, Allegato C art. C1 — https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/archivio-leggi-decreti-e-regolamenti/documento17086436.html)_
- **Income EUR 50,000.01 – 65,000** — 28%  _(Legge 16 dicembre 2013 n. 166 (Imposta Generale sui Redditi), testo coordinato al 22 dicembre 2025, Allegato C art. C1 — https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/archivio-leggi-decreti-e-regolamenti/documento17086436.html)_
- **Income EUR 65,000.01 – 80,000** — 31%  _(Legge 16 dicembre 2013 n. 166 (Imposta Generale sui Redditi), testo coordinato al 22 dicembre 2025, Allegato C art. C1 — https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/archivio-leggi-decreti-e-regolamenti/documento17086436.html)_
- **Income over EUR 80,000** — 35%  _(Legge 16 dicembre 2013 n. 166 (Imposta Generale sui Redditi), testo coordinato al 22 dicembre 2025, Allegato C art. C1 — https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/archivio-leggi-decreti-e-regolamenti/documento17086436.html)_
- **Bracket indexation** — The brackets are adjusted automatically every two years for the rise in the cost-of-living index, by at most 6 percentage points; the first adjustment is in tax period 2028 (Allegato C art. C1(2))  _(Legge 16 dicembre 2013 n. 166 (Imposta Generale sui Redditi), testo coordinato al 22 dicembre 2025, Allegato C art. C1 — https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/archivio-leggi-decreti-e-regolamenti/documento17086436.html)_
- **Tax-free threshold / personal allowance** — No general zero-rate exemption band; first EUR 10,000 taxed at 9%. Personal/family deductions apply instead (confirm exact deduction amounts with accountant)  _(Law no. 166 of 16 December 2013 (Imposta Generale sui Redditi) — https://taxravens.com/en/san-marino/income-tax)_
- **SMaC card / traceable expense deduction** — Qualifying expenses incurred in San Marino paid via SMaC card or traceable instruments are deductible from employment-income tax  _(Law no. 166 of 16 December 2013 (Imposta Generale sui Redditi) — https://toccacelibronzetti.sm/en/taxation-in-san-marino/)_
- **Tax residence test for individuals** — Resident if (a) registered residence in San Marino, OR (b) physical presence exceeds 183 days (6 months) in the calendar year  _(Law no. 166 of 16 December 2013 (Imposta Generale sui Redditi) — https://taxravens.com/en/san-marino/income-tax)_
- **Non-resident taxation** — Non-residents taxed only on San Marino-source income; certain income subject to final withholding (see withholding facts)  _(Law no. 166 of 16 December 2013 (Imposta Generale sui Redditi) — https://taxravens.com/en/san-marino/income-tax)_
- **Annual return filing deadline** — By 31 July of the year following the tax year (art. 93); a return filed within thirty days after that is valid but penalised under art. 139(1)(d)  _(Legge 166/2013, art. 93, testo coordinato — https://www.consigliograndeegenerale.sm/on-line/home/archivio-leggi-decreti-e-regolamenti/documento17086436.html)_
