# Guyana Corporate Income Tax

## Corporation tax rates and base

- **Commercial company rate** — 40% of chargeable profits, or the 2% minimum corporation tax on turnover where that is higher  _(Corporation Tax Act (Cap 81:03); GRA, Rates & Calculations — https://gra.gov.gy/quick-links-2/rates-calculations/)_
- **Non-commercial company rate** — 25% of chargeable profits; a company with both kinds of activity pays 25% on the non-commercial activity and 40% on the commercial activity  _(Corporation Tax Act (Cap 81:03); GRA, Rates & Calculations — https://gra.gov.gy/quick-links-2/rates-calculations/)_
- **Minimum corporation tax (MCT)** — 2% of turnover for commercial companies, charged where the corporation tax on chargeable profits is less than 2% of the year's turnover  _(Corporation Tax Act (Cap 81:03); GRA, Rates & Calculations — https://gra.gov.gy/quick-links-2/rates-calculations/)_
- **Telephone company rate** — 45% of chargeable profits  _(Corporation Tax Act (Cap 81:03); GRA, Rates & Calculations — https://gra.gov.gy/quick-links-2/rates-calculations/)_
- **Exempt institutions** — Private corporate educational institutions and private corporate medical healthcare institutions are exempt from corporation tax from 1 January 2020  _(GRA, Rates & Calculations — https://gra.gov.gy/quick-links-2/rates-calculations/)_
- **Tax base** — Chargeable profits = accounting profit adjusted for tax (non-deductibles added back, capital allowances and exempt income removed)  _(Corporation Tax Act (Cap 81:03), as reported `[RESEARCH GAP — the statute's text was not read for this change; the secondary summary that carried the figure is no longer cited]`)_
- **Capital gains** — 20% capital gains tax on the net chargeable capital gain of the year; gains realised within twelve months of the change of ownership are treated as income chargeable to income tax, and no tax is charged where the year's net chargeable gain does not exceed G$500,000  _(Capital Gains Tax Act (Cap 81:20), Guyana Revenue Authority consolidated text (revised 15 September 2022), s 12 (rate), charging proviso (G$500,000 exemption and twelve-month rule) — https://www.gra.gov.gy/storage/2022/09/Capital-Gains-Tax-Act-revised-Sept.-15-2022.pdf)_
- **WHT on dividends to non-residents** — 20% withholding tax on gross distributions to non-residents, subject to reduction under a double taxation treaty  _(Income Tax Act (Cap 81:01), Guyana Revenue Authority consolidated text (revised to January 2019), s 39(1)(a) and Third Schedule — https://www.gra.gov.gy/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/IncomeTaxActrevDec18Jan19final.pdf)_
- **WHT on interest / royalties / management fees / rent to non-residents** — 20% final withholding tax on gross payments (interest, rent, royalties, management charges, fees and similar) to non-residents not engaged in trade or business in Guyana, with no withholding on income arising outside Guyana; payments to non-resident companies under contracts carry a 10% corporation withholding tax, accounted for within 30 days  _(Income Tax Act (Cap 81:01), Guyana Revenue Authority consolidated text (revised to January 2019), s 39(1)(b) and Third Schedule — https://www.gra.gov.gy/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/IncomeTaxActrevDec18Jan19final.pdf ; Corporation Tax Act (Cap 81:03) with return guidance, Guyana Revenue Authority text (revised 1 April 2026), s 10B — https://www.gra.gov.gy/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Corporation-Tax-Revised-April-1.-2026.pdf)_
- **WHT on local bank/savings interest** — 20% final withholding tax on interest earned on savings accounts at commercial banks and other financial institutions and on interest on loans secured by bonds and similar instruments, whether the recipient is resident or not percent  _(Income Tax Act (Cap 81:01), Guyana Revenue Authority consolidated text (revised to January 2019), s 39(1)(c) and (d) and Third Schedule — https://www.gra.gov.gy/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/IncomeTaxActrevDec18Jan19final.pdf)_
- **WHT return and remittance deadline** — Due by the 14th day of the month following the month of payment  _(Income Tax Act (Cap 81:01) — withholding provisions, as reported `[RESEARCH GAP — the statute's text was not read for this change; the secondary summary that carried the figure is no longer cited]`)_
- **Corporation tax return & payment deadline** — 30 April of the year of assessment for every company registered or doing business in Guyana, with the balance of tax paid by the same date  _(Guyana Revenue Authority, File Corporation Tax Return — https://gra.gov.gy/quick-links-2/file-corporation-tax-return/ ; Guyana Revenue Authority, Income Tax: Who Must File a Return — https://gra.gov.gy/business/tax-operations-and-services/income-tax/submission-of-returns/)_
- **Advance (quarterly) corporation tax** — Corporate advance taxes are due on 15 March, 15 June, 15 September and 15 December, with the balance payable with the 30 April return  _(Guyana Revenue Authority, File Corporation Tax Return — https://gra.gov.gy/quick-links-2/file-corporation-tax-return/)_
