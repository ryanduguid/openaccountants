# Iran Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate tax rate and base

- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 25% of net taxable profit (flat) percent  _([Direct Taxes Act (DTA), Article 105](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/))_
- **Tax base** — Net taxable profit = total income less deductible expenses and allowable depreciation, per the DTA  _(Direct Taxes Act (DTA), Article 106)_
- **Corporate residence / scope** — Iranian legal entities are taxed on worldwide income; foreign companies are taxed on income derived from Iran (e.g. Iranian operations, contracts, or a permanent establishment)  _(Direct Taxes Act (DTA))_
- **Reduced rate — listed companies** — Companies listed on the Tehran Stock Exchange / Iran Fara Bourse may receive a tax rate reduction (commonly a 10% reduction of the applicable rate, increasing if free-float conditions are met) percent ((approx — confirm current discount percentage and conditions))  _(Direct Taxes Act (DTA), Article 143)_
- **Free trade zone exemption** — Businesses operating in designated Free Trade-Industrial Zones may enjoy a long-term income tax holiday (historically 20 years from start of operations) ((approx — confirm current holiday length))  _(Law on the Administration of Free Trade-Industrial Zones)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends** — 0% — no withholding tax on dividends distributed by Iranian companies (profits are taxed at the company level only) percent  _([Direct Taxes Act (DTA)](https://orbitax.com/taxhub/countrychapters/IR/Iran/))_
- **Withholding tax on interest (to non-residents)** — 5% on interest paid by Iranian entities to non-residents percent  _([Direct Taxes Act (DTA)](https://orbitax.com/taxhub/countrychapters/IR/Iran/))_
- **Withholding tax on royalties (to non-residents)** — Effective 5% in qualifying cases (e.g. manufacturing/mining, government payers, or cinematographic rights); otherwise an effective 7.5% percent  _([Direct Taxes Act (DTA)](https://orbitax.com/taxhub/countrychapters/IR/Iran/))_
- **Filing & payment deadline** — Annual corporate return and payment due within 4 months after fiscal year-end (~22 July for a 20 March year-end) ((approx — confirm exact date))  _(Direct Taxes Act (DTA), Article 110)_
