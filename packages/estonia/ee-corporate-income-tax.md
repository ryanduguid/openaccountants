# Estonia Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax base and rates

Estonia's corporate income tax is levied only on distributed (and deemed-distributed) profits, not on accrued earnings. Retained and reinvested profits are taxed at 0%, making the timing of distribution the key tax event.

- **Tax on retained / reinvested profits** — 0%  _(Income Tax Act (Tulumaksuseadus) § 50(1): a resident company pays income tax on profit when it is distributed — https://www.riigiteataja.ee/en/eli/504092025004/consolide)_
- **Tax on distributed profits** — 22% of the gross amount, levied as 22/78 of the net distribution  _(Income Tax Act (Tulumaksuseadus) § 4(1) and (1¹), in force from 1 January 2025 — https://www.riigiteataja.ee/en/eli/504092025004/consolide)_
- **Distribution tax formula** — CIT = net distribution × 22/78 (e.g. 78 EUR dividend → 22 EUR CIT)  _(Income Tax Act (Tulumaksuseadus) § 4(1¹): the taxable amount is divided by 0.78 before the 22% rate applies — https://www.riigiteataja.ee/en/eli/504092025004/consolide; EMTA, Tax rates (2026) — https://www.emta.ee/en/business-client/taxes-and-payment/income-and-social-taxes/tax-rates)_
- **Reduced 14% rate on regular dividends** — Abolished from 1 January 2025 (previously 14/86 on regularly distributed profits)  _(Income Tax Act (Tulumaksuseadus) — https://www.ey.com/en_ee/insights/tax/significant-tax-changes-in-estonia-in-2025-2026)_
- **Tax base** — Profit distributions, fringe benefits, gifts/donations, non-business expenses, and other deemed distributions — not annual accounting profit  _(Income Tax Act (Tulumaksuseadus) §§ 48–53 — https://www.riigiteataja.ee/en/eli/504092025004/consolide)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends to non-residents** — 0% (no WHT; the 22% CIT is borne at company level on distribution). Transitional exception: 7% is withheld when a company redistributes to a natural person dividends it received before 2025 that were taxed at 14/86  _(Income Tax Act (Tulumaksuseadus) § 41, whose list of withholding payments has no dividend clause since clause 7² was repealed from 1 January 2025, and § 61(68) — https://www.riigiteataja.ee/en/eli/504092025004/consolide; EMTA, Tax rates (2026) — https://www.emta.ee/en/business-client/taxes-and-payment/income-and-social-taxes/tax-rates)_
- **Withholding tax on interest to non-residents** — 0% (generally no WHT on ordinary interest payments; withholding reaches only interest tied to a real-estate fund or pool under § 29(7))  _(Income Tax Act (Tulumaksuseadus) § 41 clause 4 and § 29(7) — https://www.riigiteataja.ee/en/eli/504092025004/consolide)_
- **Withholding tax on royalties to non-residents** — **10%** under domestic law, often reduced under double tax treaties. The definition reaches **payments for the use of industrial, commercial or scientific equipment**, so an equipment rental to a non-resident lessor is a royalty here and not an untaxed service payment  _(Income Tax Act (Tulumaksuseadus) § 16(3), § 41 clause 8, § 43(1) clause 2 and § 43(2) — https://www.riigiteataja.ee/en/eli/504092025004/consolide)_
- **Withholding tax on certain services performed in Estonia** — **10%** on payments to non-resident companies for services **provided in Estonia**, management and consultancy fees included; treaty relief may reduce or remove it. **22%** applies instead where the recipient is a legal person located in a non-cooperative jurisdiction for tax purposes, and then on services provided to an Estonian resident wherever they were performed. The territorial test is real and is the answer to the hedge this line used to carry: a service provided outside Estonia is outside this withholding  _(Income Tax Act (Tulumaksuseadus) § 29(3), § 41 clauses 10 and 11, § 43(1) — https://www.riigiteataja.ee/en/eli/504092025004/consolide)_
- **CIT return and payment deadline** — Monthly via Form TSD by the 10th of the following month (tax due only when a distribution is made)  _(Income Tax Act (Tulumaksuseadus) § 54(2) and (4); Form TSD — https://www.riigiteataja.ee/en/eli/504092025004/consolide)_
- **Advance income tax for credit institutions** — Resident credit institutions and Estonian branches of non-resident credit institutions pay an advance of 18% on the previous quarter's profit by the 10th day of the third month of each quarter, and deduct it from the income tax later due on distributions (in force from 1 January 2025; checked against the current Estonian text on 6 October 2026)  _(Income Tax Act (Tulumaksuseadus) § 4(5), § 47¹, § 54(2¹) and (4¹) — https://www.riigiteataja.ee/en/eli/504092025004/consolide; current Estonian text — https://www.riigiteataja.ee/akt/103122024005)_
