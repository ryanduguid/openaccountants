## Primary sources and reading limits

- **Principal classification and contributions:** Retirement Pensions Ordinance 1996 sections 2 and 9 to 16. Section 2 defines an employee, ordinary residence, temporary absence and a 7-day week starting Monday. Reaching an age is taken to occur at the beginning of that week. Mixed employee/self-employment has its own rule and aggregate-earnings condition. Sections 10 and 11 distinguish the employee and employer obligations. Section 10(3A)'s 2,200 made/deemed contribution rule does not, by itself, establish that the employer's separate contribution ends. Section 11(2) and prescribed regulation 4(2) expressly refer to the section 10(4) earnings-limit exception. [Current principal](https://legislation.gov.fk/download/pdf/53aa8cd0-53e8-4333-ab6a-7648adc17498/a2aa126a-867a-45bc-be37-59ce19aa66e2/fiord-1996-20_2026-01-01.pdf).
- **Earnings, amounts and allocation:** Prescribed Rates Regulations 1996 regulations 2 and 4 to 7. Earnings are the earned income payable in respect of the period, computed under the Taxes Ordinance 1997 before deductions, whether or not subject to income tax. The employee and employer figures are fixed weekly amounts. Regulation 6 is an earnings-limit exemption, not an earnings-base ceiling or a tax band. Regulation 7 preserves aggregation and exact calendar-day allocation for longer pay periods and split-period weeks. [Current prescribed rates](https://legislation.gov.fk/download/pdf/9598b106-0064-4837-9d41-774a34fc0e4e/c5589ace-6b61-4730-9c11-f4ad37c12d68/fisl-1996-39_2026-07-01.pdf).
- **Commencement and forward change:** Finance Ordinance 2025 section 2(2), 2(4)(e) and the pension row in Schedule Part B establish the selected earnings-limit text and the 1 January 2026 contribution-rate change. Its original says assent 21 July and publication 24 July 2025. Finance Ordinance 2026 section 2(2)(d) defers regulation 5/6 changes to **1 January 2027**, including employee/employer 24.55 and the 396.80 earnings limit. Do not apply them to wholly 2026 weeks, or infer a year-boundary-week rate from a generic cover year. The as-made download filenames' 2026-10-24 suffix is not enactment or commencement evidence. [Finance 2025 original](https://www.legislation.gov.fk/download/pdf/840d0247-0491-4d60-8502-4f474ae0d089/1ba5d047-81f2-4685-8dba-127f5080d3b8/fiord-2025-5_2026-10-24.pdf), [Finance 2026 original](https://www.legislation.gov.fk/download/pdf/4984e9a5-08ba-44f7-8536-2ceb3e338d69/8817c27c-4f8d-4da2-ab8c-bf947597c3c4/fiord-2026-4_2026-10-24.pdf).
- **2025 principal/regulation amendment:** the Retirement Pensions (Amendment) Ordinance 2025 section 2 commences it on 1 January 2026. Sections 4, 6 to 8 and its Schedule establish pensionable age 65, updated earnings cross-references and corresponding wording. The annual earnings limit definition does not create an annual employee contribution ceiling. [Amendment original](https://www.legislation.gov.fk/download/pdf/1c2e6853-c2a6-4b8e-bc45-d5017071a69b/7f792714-9ff1-4188-ab09-fb6cb2de0cb9/fiord-2025-9_2026-10-24.pdf).

Regulation 4 separately supplies the selected recovery rule; section 14(3) prohibits recovery of the employer's own contribution. [Current General Provisions](https://www.legislation.gov.fk/download/pdf/e4e09bec-48c1-44fc-a023-1818449d4620/68dd2718-c65f-4fc1-9511-f0090d0804c0/fisl-1996-38_2026-01-01.pdf).

1. **Select each week independently.** Record its actual Monday and Sunday, employment/status facts and applicable rates. This branch accepts only complete weeks wholly within 2026. Do not select a week from the annual income guide's assessment-year label, assume 52 liable weeks or infer cross-year treatment. A contribution in respect of the whole or part of a week is not automatically prorated by days worked.
2. **Establish weekly earnings W before deductions.** For ordinary weekly pay, use the independently established earnings attributable to that week. Combine all included ordinary salary components before testing the threshold. Net bank receipts, a tax-free salary label or a contribution deduction do not establish or reduce W. A missing component or legal classification holds the dependent result.
3. **Longer ordinary pay periods.** For a complete week lying within one established longer pay period, regulation 7(2)-(3) gives W = 7 × A/B, where A is earnings payable in respect of that period and B its number of calendar days. Use calendar days, not working days, 4 weeks per month or 52/12. Where the week falls partly in the first period and partly in the second, regulation 7(4) gives W = d1 × A1/B1 + d2 × A2/B2, with d1 + d2 = 7. Establish both periods and amounts. Missing next-period earnings does not permit a same-rate default. Use these allocation formulae only where payment less frequently than weekly is independently established. Dates overlapping a statutory week do not establish that condition. Short periods, misaligned weekly pay records without independently established earnings for the exact statutory week and other allocation patterns remain held.
4. **Apply the earnings-limit condition.** For a selected wholly 2026 week, W **not exceeding 386.40** meets the section 10(4)/regulation 6 earnings-limit exemption. Under the selected ordinary premises, employee reference E = 0 and employer reference R = 0. Equality is exempt. For W **greater than 386.40**, E = 23.90 and R = 23.90. Combined reference P = E + R = 47.80. These are fixed amounts, not percentages of W or its excess. Preserve the exact rational comparison in fictional examples; no lawful rounding sequence is inferred. An unknown actual precision rule remains a source gate.
5. **Aggregate established weeks only.** Add each employee share and employer share separately over the selected weeks. Preserve a row for each week and its evidence. A month with 4 liable weeks gives 95.60 for each share; 5 gives 119.50. Do not replace actual week counts with a monthly salary threshold or an annualised average. The principal's 2,200-count exemption, widow/widower exclusion, special exemption, mixed work and age transitions are separate held branches, not an invented annual earnings ceiling.
6. **Keep recovery and collection distinct.** Subject to the regulations, section 14(1) requires the employer to pay the employee contribution in the first instance on the employee's behalf. Regulation 4 requires deduction from remuneration for that week or part. If the employer omits all or part, it may instead deduct that amount from remuneration for a later week, with no other recovery route. Multiple employment requires the employer first worked for that week and is outside this branch. The employer's own contribution cannot be deducted or otherwise recovered from the worker under section 14(3). A reference amount is not proof of a lawful actual deduction, recovery or remittance.
7. **Preserve the statutory calendar.** General Provisions regulation 5 concerns contributions in respect of **weeks beginning in the contribution month**. The monthly return is due **before the 14th day** of the following month and accompanying remittance covers the required contributions. The annual return is due **before 1 April** of the following pension year. Do not change 'before' to 'by' or substitute a POAT deadline. Current Board forms, submission method, payment evidence, complete calendar and any applicable qualification remain gates. Principal section 9's employer notification within 14 days of employment is separate from monthly returns and does not establish registration by itself.
8. **Stop at the reference.** Do not compute net pay, POAT, a full payroll journal, an accepted remittance, an annual tax deduction, a refund or a pension benefit from these illustrations. The annual income guide consumes only independently qualified actual contributions; these weekly examples do not establish an amount paid or eligible under Taxes Ordinance section 15. The unresolved printed POAT TR1 minus TR1 term remains held. No annual income tax divided by 12 or 52, or invented nil withholding, completes payroll.
