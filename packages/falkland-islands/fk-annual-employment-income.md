# Falkland Islands annual employment income reference

> **General reference only.** This bounded Tier 2 draft illustrates selected annual salary mechanics for professional review. It does not establish complete operative law, payroll withholding, a filing amount, payment, credit, refund or complete return. ## Scope and period gate

Preserve all 4 universal outputs and 18 checks, all 5 income tax outputs and 12 checks, all 13 mandatory income tax country slots, shared refusals and every additional refusal below. The fictional branch is one living individual whose resident and ordinarily resident status and full allowance entitlement are independently established throughout income year 2025. The individual is present in the Falkland Islands for all 365 days of that year, has ordinary domestic cash salary from one or more direct employers and is not a company director. All relevant salary is established under the statutory timing rules. There is no foreign service, treaty claim, special exemption, agency or intermediary arrangement, business, pension income, rent, investment income, chargeable gain, noncash benefit, gratuity, redundancy or other unusual payment. Employment expenses, losses, other deductions and credits are independently nil, except for the specifically established contributions, subscriptions and cash donations below. Those are stipulated fictional facts, not defaults for an actual missing record.

**Use both year labels.** Taxes Ordinance section 2 defines the assessment year as the calendar year; section 9(1) charges the immediately preceding year's chargeable income. This reference therefore uses **income year 1 January to 31 December 2025 and assessment year 1 January to 31 December 2026**. Calendar-year salary received or arising in 2026 is not automatically part of this 2026 assessment branch. Its ordinary next assessment year is 2027 and needs its own applicable-law check. POAT payment years are separate. Do not infer annual year mapping from an employer guide's cover date. ## Intake and annual steps

Reuse redacted records before grouped questions. Establish the individual, both year labels, physical-presence and ordinary-residence decisions, complete gross salary and entitlement/payment dates, director status, all employers, every other receipt, qualifying contribution records, approved subscription evidence, expense/loss records, charitable evidence, prior returns, notices, actual deductions and payments, reviewer and calculation/output tools. The taxpayer cannot supply a guess to fill a legal source gap.

1. **Scope and residence.** Section 200(2)'s resident test is at least 183 days in the preceding year; arrival and departure days count under subsection (3). Ordinary residence is separately defined by habitual residence and the Commissioner's temporary-absence view, including the government-service provision. Immigration status or 183 days alone does not establish every required condition. Partial residence, death, dual residence and all apportionment cases are held. [Taxes Ordinance, s 200](https://www.legislation.gov.fk/download/pdf/05a29220-06c3-4560-867c-99d6c5200d0d/15f327cc-37ae-4304-a080-e3d54a80bd43/fiord-1997-14_2026-06-12.pdf); [Apportionment Rules, rr 2–4](https://www.legislation.gov.fk/getdata?ds=TTFL-FragViewDataSource&collection=TTFL.fragment&expression=VersionSeriesId%3D%225acd2843-c1cb-4a5d-a8d7-4f9a78dc45aa%22%20AND%20VersionDescId%3D%22cd270fe2-049b-4e2e-a3d0-c22f73ea9e2e%22%20NOT%20id_p%3D(%22fisl-1997-22%22)&subset=F&start=1&count=1).
2. **Gross salary G.** Reconcile all ordinary salary from every employer before any single annual allowance or bands. Use gross income, not net bank receipts. Under section 9(1A), take the earlier applicable actual payment or entitlement time; director rules have additional events and are held here. A salary entitlement arising on 31 December 2025 is not shifted to 2026 merely because paid in January 2026. Sections 8 and 206 cover more than base salary; establish the exclusions in the stated scope rather than silently discarding another item. A salary paid abroad is not automatically foreign source or exempt. [Taxes Ordinance, ss 2, 8, 9, 13 and 206](https://www.legislation.gov.fk/download/pdf/05a29220-06c3-4560-867c-99d6c5200d0d/15f327cc-37ae-4304-a080-e3d54a80bd43/fiord-1997-14_2026-06-12.pdf).
3. **Qualifying retirement contribution R.** Use only an independently verified amount made by the individual on their own behalf under section 10 or section 12 of the Retirement Pensions Ordinance 1996, as required by Taxes Ordinance section 15. Reconcile payment and period evidence, legal qualification and the employee's own amount. No employer share, generic private pension, other person's contribution, duplicated deduction or guessed weekly/yearly amount is included. Unknown eligibility or amount holds the dependent calculation. [Taxes Ordinance, s 15](https://www.legislation.gov.fk/download/pdf/05a29220-06c3-4560-867c-99d6c5200d0d/15f327cc-37ae-4304-a080-e3d54a80bd43/fiord-1997-14_2026-06-12.pdf).
4. **Approved annual subscription S.** Use only an annual subscription actually paid to a Commissioner-approved body whose activities are relevant to the duties of the office or employment under section 15A. Membership, a professional title, relevance alone or a foreign approval does not prove that statutory approval. Preserve evidence of approval, payment, period, employment relevance and no duplicate deduction. Other employment expenses under section 58 require all of wholly, necessarily and exclusively incurred during the period in producing that income; section 59 imposes disallowances. This branch requires those other expenses and losses independently nil. A positive or unknown item is a separate source and calculation gate, not a default denial. [Taxes Ordinance, ss 15A, 58–59A](https://www.legislation.gov.fk/download/pdf/05a29220-06c3-4560-867c-99d6c5200d0d/15f327cc-37ae-4304-a080-e3d54a80bd43/fiord-1997-14_2026-06-12.pdf).
5. **One personal allowance.** With G, R and S nonnegative and R + S not exceeding G, set N = G − R − S. Apply the established full allowance A = 16,860 once: allowance used = min(N,A), and C0 = max(N − A,0). Map the generic workflow's taxable-income rung explicitly to local **chargeable income** after relevant deductions. No unused allowance becomes negative chargeable income, a cash credit or a refund in this component. Other deductions, expense claims, loss relief or apportionment require a complete separate ordering check. [Taxes Ordinance, ss 2 and 16](https://www.legislation.gov.fk/download/pdf/05a29220-06c3-4560-867c-99d6c5200d0d/15f327cc-37ae-4304-a080-e3d54a80bd43/fiord-1997-14_2026-06-12.pdf); [Finance Ordinance, s 2 and Schedule Part A](https://legislation.gov.fk/download/pdf/63ec8e1c-da09-4070-8b6c-f1a161d7901f/0502d8c8-afa1-4050-a954-a7997a81f0ea/fiord-2024-6_2024-07-21.pdf).
6. **Cash charitable donations last.** For an ordinary individual, establish each claimed money donation's amount, payment in the relevant period, qualifying recipient status at donation time under section 57A(2)(b) and, where relevant, the section 57B Gazette-approved list. The donation must not be below 50 source pounds; do not combine separate sub-threshold gifts to invent a qualifying gift. There must be a claim and the Commissioner must receive the charity's written evidence stating that person's donations for the period. A receipt merely held by the taxpayer does not establish that condition. Exclude known nonqualifying gifts with their reason; unknown evidence remains held. No amount is deducted twice under section 58 or another provision. Let Q be the total of independently established qualifying money donations. Section 57A(6) places the donation deduction **after all other deductions and allowances, including loss relief**, and prevents it reducing income below zero. For this branch only, donation used = min(Q,C0) and C = C0 − donation used. No unused donation amount becomes a cash credit or a proved carryforward here. In-kind gifts, business donations and unresolved recipient/evidence cases are held. [Taxes Ordinance, ss 57A–57B](https://www.legislation.gov.fk/download/pdf/05a29220-06c3-4560-867c-99d6c5200d0d/15f327cc-37ae-4304-a080-e3d54a80bd43/fiord-1997-14_2026-06-12.pdf).
7. **Annual rate slices.** Apply 21% only to the first 18,000 of C and 26% only to its remainder: **T = 0.21 × min(C,18,000) + 0.26 × max(C − 18,000,0)**. T is an exact unrounded annual reference charge before credits, adjustments, surcharges or settlement. With R, S and Q nil and a full allowance, the gross-salary band boundary is 34,860, not 18,000. Deductions can move the boundary; do not apply 26% to all C. [Taxes Ordinance, s 10](https://www.legislation.gov.fk/download/pdf/05a29220-06c3-4560-867c-99d6c5200d0d/15f327cc-37ae-4304-a080-e3d54a80bd43/fiord-1997-14_2026-06-12.pdf); [Finance Ordinance, s 2 and Schedule Part A](https://legislation.gov.fk/download/pdf/63ec8e1c-da09-4070-8b6c-f1a161d7901f/0502d8c8-afa1-4050-a954-a7997a81f0ea/fiord-2024-6_2024-07-21.pdf).
8. **No automatic settlement.** POAT sums are on account of income tax under section 89 and may be allocated to earlier liabilities. Sections 88–90 contain distinct credit, non-remittance, direction, repayment and set-off conditions; an employer certificate or arithmetic difference does not alone establish a credit or refund. Section 93's non-resident branch is outside this scope. Keep annual reference charge, tax deducted, tax paid, authority allocation, accepted credit and any repayment separate. No annual-T division by 12 or 52 supplies POAT. [Taxes Ordinance, ss 83–93](https://www.legislation.gov.fk/download/pdf/05a29220-06c3-4560-867c-99d6c5200d0d/15f327cc-37ae-4304-a080-e3d54a80bd43/fiord-1997-14_2026-06-12.pdf).
9. **Filing and payment remain distinct.** Section 12(1) permits a notice requiring a return within 60 days of issue or a longer specified period; subsection (3) separately requires a chargeable person's notice within 4 months after the assessment year begins unless the statutory prior-notice/return condition applies. Section 11(1) makes assessed tax payable before expiry of 30 days from service of the assessment notice or, if later, 1 October in that year. These are different issue/service/chargeability events, not one universal filing date. Section 162 has departure/recovery-risk powers. Actual notices, forms, channels, calendar interpretation, penalties and other exceptions remain held. [Taxes Ordinance, ss 11–12A and 162](https://www.legislation.gov.fk/download/pdf/05a29220-06c3-4560-867c-99d6c5200d0d/15f327cc-37ae-4304-a080-e3d54a80bd43/fiord-1997-14_2026-06-12.pdf).
