# Falkland Islands tax system orientation

The Falkland Islands Government Taxation Office administers income and corporation tax. Its Commissioner acts under the Taxes Ordinance 1997. The local currency is the Falkland Islands pound (FKP), at parity with sterling. [Taxes Ordinance, s 5][TAX] · [Finance Department, currency][CURRENCY]

## Residence and income source

For an ordinary individual assessment, the statutory residence test looks to at least 183 days of physical presence in the year immediately preceding the year of assessment. Arrival and departure days count. Ordinary residence follows habitual residence, allowing absences the Commissioner considers temporary. Residents and ordinary residents generally report worldwide income. Section 13 excludes foreign-source income for someone who is neither resident nor ordinarily resident when it accrues, subject to the statutory source and allocation rules. Employment duties in the Islands and the split of duties across jurisdictions can affect source. [Taxes Ordinance, ss 8–9, 13 and 200][TAX] · [Taxation Office, 2026 individual guide, parts 3–4][INDGUIDE]

An incorporated Falkland Islands company is generally resident there. A foreign-incorporated company looks to the location of central management and control. Resident companies generally enter the corporation tax charge on profits wherever arising, subject to statutory exceptions. For a non-resident company, check section 34 for local and designated-area sources and profits connected with a local branch or agency. [Taxes Ordinance, ss 23–25, 34 and 201][TAX]

## Ordinary rates and filing

For the 2026 individual income period, the annual personal allowance is £16,860, subject to residence and any applicable apportionment. After deductions and allowances, the first £18,000 is taxed at 21% and the balance at 26%. The calendar-year income period ends on 31 December. The Taxation Office gives the ordinary individual return date as the later of 31 July after the income year and 60 days from issue. It gives the ordinary payment date as the later of 1 October and 30 days from assessment. Check departure and non-resident refund rules separately. [Taxes Ordinance, ss 9–12, 16 and 195][TAX] · [Taxation Office, individual tax][INDIVIDUAL] · [Taxation Office, 2026 individual guide, parts 4 and 6][INDGUIDE]

Corporation tax applies at 21% up to the relevant threshold and 26% above it. The starting threshold is £500,000 before adjustments for associated companies and short periods. Ring-fence petroleum profits use their own rules, including the 26% rate. Tax is generally payable the day after eight months from the end of the accounting period. The return and accounts are ordinarily due within nine months from the end of the period of account. [Taxes Ordinance, ss 26–30][TAX] · [Taxation Office, companies][COMPANY]

## Transactions

The Taxation Office's 2026 individual guide lists no VAT, sales tax or stamp duties and no general capital gains tax. Check statutory charges on particular disposals and applicable customs duties separately. Payments on account deducted by employers, medical services tax and retirement pension contributions have separate rules. [Taxation Office, 2026 individual guide, parts 2, 5 and 6][INDGUIDE] · [Taxation Office, 2024 guide, tax structure][STRUCTURE] · [Taxes Ordinance, s 100A and Part VI, Chapter II][TAX] · [Customs and Immigration, FAQs][CUSTOMS]
