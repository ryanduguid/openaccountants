# Bouvet Island land and activity permits

Load `workflow-base`. Record the proposed rights, activity, dates, site, aircraft or vehicles and any construction before advising on a Bouvet Island project.

## Land status

Section 3 of the Norwegian Dependencies Act vests land in the State except land the State disposes of to private parties. Title and rights of use over State land cannot be acquired by prescription. The Norwegian Government identifies Bouvet Island as State land. Before treating an ordinary private land purchase as inapplicable, confirm the parcel remains State land and has no documented prior State disposition. Record the State land status as the reason. For a proposed State disposition or grant, obtain express authority, the title or grant instrument and terms before determining taxes or registration. [Dependencies Act, ss 1–3](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/1930-02-27-3) · [Justice Ministry white paper, ch 6.2.2](https://www.regjeringen.no/no/dokumenter/meld.-st.-33-20142015/id2415895/?ch=6)

## Nature reserve permissions

The nature reserve regulation has covered Bouvet Island and adjacent territorial waters since 17 December 1971. The consolidated text includes the amendment effective 1 January 2007.

| Proposed activity | Authority to check |
|---|---|
| Building, road or other physical works | Part III item 1 protects the landscape from physical works and activities that disturb the terrain or natural environment. It makes provision for necessary supervision accommodation. Obtain the applicable determination for any other works before committing funds. |
| Aircraft landing or off-road vehicle | Item 5 requires special permission, subject to the stated supervision, police and ambulance exceptions. The Norwegian Polar Institute publishes the helicopter application routes. |
| Research or other special project | Item 7 allows the responsible ministry or its delegate to grant a dispensation on application where the measure is compatible with the purpose of the reserve. Keep the actual decision and conditions with the project records. |

[Nature reserve regulation, pt III items 1, 5 and 7](https://lovdata.no/dokument/SF/forskrift/1971-12-17-9) · [Norwegian Polar Institute permit guidance](https://npolar.no/en/regulations-bouvetoya-nature-reserve/)

## Nyrøysa

The separate regulation effective 24 June 2005 identifies the Nyrøysa area in section 2. From 1 November to 15 March, travel and stays require an approved environmental monitoring purpose unless a section 4 dispensation or section 5 exception applies. From 1 November to 1 May, aircraft must maintain at least 500 metres of vertical and horizontal separation unless special overflight or landing permission applies. An overflight or landing permission specifies the altitude, approach and landing site. [Nyrøysa regulation, ss 2–5 and 7](https://lovdata.no/dokument/SF/forskrift/2005-06-24-687)

The Norwegian Polar Institute may grant a section 4 research dispensation where the work cannot take place elsewhere and does not conflict with approved monitoring. Section 4 also allows a general-stay dispensation in the specified southern part where compatible with the purpose of the regulation. Section 5 covers necessary ambulance, police, rescue and supervision activity. Confirm site boundaries, dates and permit conditions before any expenditure that depends on access. [Nyrøysa regulation, ss 2–5](https://lovdata.no/dokument/SF/forskrift/2005-06-24-687)

## Remaining coverage

Obtain the actual grant or permit, accepted application route, terms and any fees before giving a project-specific answer. Assess ownership and movement of equipment, capital transactions involving the operator, entity registration and corporate tax from their own source evidence.
