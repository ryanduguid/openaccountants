# Bouvet Island wage tax and payroll in 2026

Use this reference for ordinary wages earned on Bouvet Island during the 2026 Norwegian income year by workers aged 20 or over at the end of that year. Load `workflow-base`. Record the actual work location, dates of each stay, departures, pay dates, remuneration by work location, benefits and National Insurance coverage before applying any rate.

## Wages covered by the Norwegian rules

The Norwegian Dependencies Act names Bouvet Island as a dependency. Sections 1–2 of the Jan Mayen Tax Act apply Svalbard wage withholding provisions, including the corresponding section 2-2 scope test, and the annual rates to qualifying work earnings in the Norwegian Antarctic dependencies. Section 3 allocates the tax to the municipality determined under the ordinary Norwegian residence rules. For a worker resident on the mainland, the Tax Administration uses the municipality of residence on 1 January of the income year. Resolve mainland residence and other income under the applicable rules for the worker. [Dependencies Act, s 1](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/1930-02-27-3) · [Jan Mayen Tax Act, ss 1–3](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/1996-11-29-69) · [Skatte-ABC J-1-1 to J-1-3](https://oppslag.rettskilder.skatteetaten.no/rettskilder2/type/handboker/skatte-abc/gjeldende/skatteabc-J-1)

Check that the remuneration relates to work during a continuous stay of at least 30 days. Exclude travel days to and from Bouvet. An interruption ends the stay and starts a separate count on return. Count days of presence regardless of how many days the person worked. A continuous stay can qualify across year-end. Establish the relevant periods before using the scheme for any time aboard a vessel or away from the island. Qualifying sickness benefits have additional requirements. [Svalbard Tax Act, s 2-2](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/1996-11-29-68) · [Skatte-ABC J-1-2](https://oppslag.rettskilder.skatteetaten.no/rettskilder2/type/handboker/skatte-abc/gjeldende/skatteabc-J-1) · [Skatte-ABC S-23-3.4](https://oppslag.rettskilder.skatteetaten.no/rettskilder2/type/handboker/skatte-abc/gjeldende/skatteabc-S-23/skatteabc-S-23.041/skatteabc-S-23.045)

## Income tax and contributions

For 2026, calculate the ordinary wage tax at 8% up to 12 times the National Insurance basic amount (G) and 22% on the excess. The annual threshold uses the last G set for the income year. G from 1 May 2026 is NOK 136,549, giving an annual threshold of NOK 1,638,588. The employer's withholding calculation under section 5-2 has a separate rule for cumulative payments to each worker. Confirm the timing and settlement rules for split periods or payers before applying the annual threshold to a pay run. [Svalbard Tax Act, ss 3-1 and 5-2](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/1996-11-29-68) · [2026 parliamentary tax decision, s 3(a)](https://lovdata.no/STV/forskrift/2025-12-19-2780) · [NAV basic amounts](https://www.nav.no/grunnbelopet)

Work from the gross value of qualifying remuneration. In 2026, the Tax Administration includes NOK 151 per day for free board and lodging combined. Its handbook describes ordinary withholding as settling the wage tax and says the qualifying personal income carries no bracket tax. Statutory holiday pay, ordinary pay during holidays and pre-Christmas pay remain subject to withholding under this scheme. Resolve any disputed deduction, adjustment or settlement separately. [2026 valuation regulation](https://lovdata.no/dokument/SF/forskrift/2025-12-15-2559) · [Skatte-ABC J-1-4 and J-1-5](https://oppslag.rettskilder.skatteetaten.no/rettskilder2/type/handboker/skatte-abc/gjeldende/skatteabc-J-1)

For a worker subject to Norwegian National Insurance, the ordinary employee contribution rate on this wage income is 7.6% in 2026. Establish coverage and any applicable exception before withholding it. Employer National Insurance contributions are 0% on wages taxed under the Jan Mayen Act. Assess other wages separately. [2026 National Insurance contribution decision, ss 3 and 7](https://lovdata.no/dokument/LTI/forskrift/2025-12-18-2748) · [Jan Mayen contribution regulation, s 1](https://lovdata.no/dokument/SF/forskrift/1997-12-22-1432) · [Skatte-ABC J-1-5.2 and J-1-6](https://oppslag.rettskilder.skatteetaten.no/rettskilder2/type/handboker/skatte-abc/gjeldende/skatteabc-J-1)

## A-melding reporting

Report the relevant pay and employment details in the Norwegian a-melding. For qualifying earnings, the Tax Administration's instructions use the Jan Mayen and dependencies designation with separate labelling for wage withholding and employee contributions. Include the stay ID, start date, end date and stay designation at least once per stay. The reporting guide says ‘more than 30 days’ whilst the law and the current tax handbook say ‘at least 30 days’. For a stay of exactly 30 days, confirm the reporting treatment before submitting. [Tax Administration a-melding guide for Jan Mayen and the dependencies](https://www.skatteetaten.no/nn/bedrift-og-organisasjon/arbeidsgiver/a-meldinga/rettleiinga-til-a-meldinga/sarskilde-grupper/jan-mayen-og-dei-norske-bilanda-i-antarktis/) · [Svalbard Tax Act, s 2-2](https://lovdata.no/dokument/NL/lov/1996-11-29-68) · [Skatte-ABC J-1-2 and J-1-7](https://oppslag.rettskilder.skatteetaten.no/rettskilder2/type/handboker/skatte-abc/gjeldende/skatteabc-J-1)

## Evidence checks

| Fictional facts | Review action |
|---|---|
| A technician works on Bouvet for one continuous 36-day stay. | Verify the actual stay and wage allocation, the payer and National Insurance coverage before classifying the wages and contribution. |
| A worker spends 17 days on the island, departs, then returns for another 17 days. | Check each stay separately and identify the applicable treatment outside the special wage withholding rules. |
| A laboratory team records a continuous stay of exactly 30 days after excluding travel days. | Verify the count and apply the statutory wage scope. Hold the filing route for confirmation. |
| A worker records 30 calendar days including 2 travel days, one each way. | The 28 days of continuous presence fall short of the 30-day test. |
| An uninterrupted stay includes 18 days of presence in December and 14 in January. | The 32 days meet the duration test. Determine the tax treatment of wages in each income year. |

## Remaining coverage

Resolve residence at the start of the income year, non-resident and sickness cases, deduction and rounding, split-period apportionment, payment and holiday deadlines, netting and settlement before calculating a real pay run. Keep this reference out of automated payroll determinations until those inputs and the consuming system are verified.
