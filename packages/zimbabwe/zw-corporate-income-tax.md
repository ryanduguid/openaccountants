# Zimbabwe Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax rates and base (2025)

Companies are taxed on Zimbabwe-source income at a standard rate of 25% from 1 January 2025, plus a 3% AIDS levy on the tax. Special regimes apply to mining and certain incentivised sectors.

- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 25% percent  _([Finance Act, 2025](https://www.zimra.co.zw/domestic-taxes/corporate/tax-rates))_
- **AIDS levy on corporate tax** — 3% of the corporate income tax chargeable (effective combined rate approximately 25.75%) percent  _(Finance Act (Chapter 23:04))_
- **Tax base** — Taxable income from a Zimbabwe source (or deemed source), being gross income less exemptions and allowable deductions  _(Income Tax Act (Chapter 23:06))_
- **Mining company rate** — Mining operations are taxed at a special rate (historically 24%/25%); separate royalty regime applies percent ((approx — confirm 2025 mining rate))  _(Income Tax Act (Chapter 23:06))_
- **Special Economic Zone / incentivised investors** — Reduced or 0% rates for approved investors in designated zones for an initial period (per investment licence terms) ((approx — confirm current SEZ incentive terms))  _(Finance Act (Chapter 23:04))_
- **Withholding tax on dividends (ZSE-listed)** — 10% on dividends from Zimbabwe Stock Exchange-listed shares percent  _(Income Tax Act (Chapter 23:06))_
- **Withholding tax on dividends (unlisted / to non-resident)** — 15% non-resident shareholders' tax on dividends from unlisted companies percent  _(Income Tax Act (Chapter 23:06))_
- **Withholding tax on dividends (VFEX-listed)** — 5% on dividends from Victoria Falls Stock Exchange-listed securities percent  _(Income Tax Act (Chapter 23:06))_
- **Withholding tax on royalties to non-residents** — 15%, payable within 10 days of the date of payment percent  _(Income Tax Act (Chapter 23:06))_
- **Withholding tax on interest to non-residents** — **15%** non-residents' tax on interest, reintroduced with effect from **1 January 2026** by the 2026 national budget (presented 27 November 2025). Interest paid before that date falls outside it, so check the payment date before applying this rate. **Withhold the tax and remit the return within 30 days of the payment date.** A payer who fails to withhold is **personally liable for the tax due plus a further 15% of that tax** — the penalty is on the payer, not the recipient. Exempt: interest payable by licensed investors in respect of their special economic zone business, and interest payable by the Infrastructure Development Bank of Zimbabwe to non-resident institutional shareholders  _(2026 national budget statement; Finance Act, 2025; EY Zimbabwe post-budget tax spotlight, November 2025)_
- **Non-residents' tax on fees** — **15%** of the gross where a person pays a non-resident for services performed in Zimbabwe. This is a separate head from royalties and interest and was missing from this guide until September 2026  _(Income Tax Act (Chapter 23:06); ZIMRA REV 5 return for the remittance of withholding taxes)_
- **Non-residents' tax on remittances** — **15%** on remittances to a non-resident  _(Income Tax Act (Chapter 23:06); ZIMRA REV 5)_
- **Tax on non-executive directors' fees** — A separate withholding head in its own right on ZIMRA's remittance return. Confirm the current rate with ZIMRA before paying a non-executive director, and do not treat the fee as an ordinary service payment  _(ZIMRA REV 5)_
- **Withholding tax on tenders and on contracts without a tax clearance** — Zimbabwe withholds where the payee cannot produce a valid **tax clearance certificate (ITF263)**, and the sources consulted do not agree on the rate: one gives **30%** on payments above US$1,000 a year under a contract, another **10%** of the contract value for each payment of $250 or more to a supplier without a clearance. Both may be live rules covering different payers. Until ZIMRA settles it, withhold at the **higher** figure where no ITF263 is produced — an under-deduction is the payer's own liability, an over-deduction is the payee's to reclaim — and ask for the clearance first, which removes the question entirely  _(Income Tax Act (Chapter 23:06), Seventeenth Schedule; ZIMRA REV 5)_
- **Other heads on ZIMRA's own remittance return** — REV 5, the return a payer files to remit withholding taxes, enumerates fourteen heads. Besides those above it lists **resident shareholders' tax**, **resident tax on interest**, **capital gains withholding tax** on immovable property and on marketable securities separately, **value added withholding tax**, the **automated financial transaction tax**, tax on the exercise of share options granted before 1 February 2009, and the **tobacco levy**. A payment that fits none of the rates in this guide may still fit one of those; check the return before concluding nothing is withheld  _(ZIMRA REV 5 return for the remittance of withholding taxes)_
- **Double tax treaty relief** — Reduced WHT rates (often 10% or less) under Zimbabwe's DTAs; relief must be claimed, it is not automatic  _(Income Tax Act (Chapter 23:06))_
- **Annual return filing** — Self-assessment return (ITF12C) due 30 April after year-end ((approx — confirm filing date))  _(Income Tax Act (Chapter 23:06))_
- **Provisional tax (QPDs)** — Quarterly Payment Dates: 25 March (10%), 25 June (25%), 25 September (30%), 20 December (35%) ((approx — confirm QPD percentages/dates))  _(Income Tax Act (Chapter 23:06))_
