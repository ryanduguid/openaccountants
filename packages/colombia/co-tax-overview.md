# Colombia Tax Overview

The Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN) administers national income tax and VAT. The ordinary income tax year is the calendar year. Tax bands are often expressed in unidades de valor tributario (UVT). DIAN set **2025 UVT at COP 49,799** and **2026 UVT at COP 52,374**. Select the value for the period the rule refers to. [Decree 1625/2016, art. 1.6.1.5.7](https://normograma.dian.gov.co/dian/compilacion/docs/decreto_1625_2016.htm#1.6.1.5.7). [DIAN Resolution 193/2024](https://normograma.dian.gov.co/dian/compilacion/docs/resolucion_dian_0193_2024.htm). [DIAN Resolution 238/2025](https://normograma.dian.gov.co/dian/compilacion/docs/resolucion_dian_0238_2025.htm).

## Income tax

- **Individuals** — Residents are generally taxed on income and occasional gains from inside and outside Colombia. Non-residents are generally taxed on Colombian-source income and occasional gains. One residence test is more than 183 days within a 365-day period, counting entry and departure days. Where the qualifying period spans 2 tax years, residence under that test starts in the second. Colombian nationals may meet other tests, subject to statutory exceptions. [Tax Statute, arts. 9–10](https://normograma.dian.gov.co/dian/compilacion/docs/estatuto_tributario.htm#9).
- **Personal rates** — The ordinary resident scale in article 241 starts at 0% through 1,090 UVT of taxable income and reaches a 39% marginal rate above 31,000 UVT. Apply the scale to the relevant taxable base. Dividends and occasional gains have distinct rules. [Tax Statute, art. 241](https://normograma.dian.gov.co/dian/compilacion/docs/estatuto_tributario.htm#241).
- **Companies** — The general corporate rate is **35%**. Paragraph 6 of article 240 sets a 15% Tasa de Tributación Depurada floor for covered taxpayers. It uses statutory adjustments to income tax and accounting profit, subject to exceptions and group rules. Check the specific regime and sector before applying the general rate or the floor. [Tax Statute, art. 240](https://normograma.dian.gov.co/dian/compilacion/docs/estatuto_tributario.htm#240).

## VAT

The standard IVA rate is **19%**. Listed supplies may be taxed at 5%, exempt or excluded. Determine the classification before computing tax or claiming credits. [Tax Statute, arts. 424, 468, 468-1, 468-3, 477 and 481](https://normograma.dian.gov.co/dian/compilacion/docs/estatuto_tributario.htm#468).

Ordinary registered taxpayers generally file bimonthly if they are large taxpayers, meet the prior-year 92,000 UVT income threshold or fall within the classes in articles 477 and 481. Other ordinary registered taxpayers below the threshold generally file every 4 months. New registrants and taxpayers in a special regime should check their rules separately. For the 2026 period test, 92,000 UVT of 2025 is COP 4,581,508,000. Use the calendar for the period and final digit of the NIT. [Tax Statute, art. 600](https://normograma.dian.gov.co/dian/compilacion/docs/estatuto_tributario.htm#600). [DIAN 2026 calendar notice](https://www.dian.gov.co/Prensa/Paginas/NG-Comunicado-de-Prensa-128-2025.aspx).

## Annual income tax returns

- **Ordinary companies** — For the 2025 tax year, ordinary companies outside the large taxpayer schedule submitted the return and paid the first instalment from 12 to 26 May 2026. The second instalment fell from 9 to 24 July, from NIT ending in 1 to NIT ending in 0. [DIAN July deadline adjustment](https://www.dian.gov.co/Prensa/Paginas/NG-Comunicado-de-Prensa-092-2026.aspx).
- **Individuals and undivided estates** — For the 2025 tax year, the ordinary 2026 filing and payment window was 12 August to 26 October, using the final 2 digits of the NIT.

Use the [DIAN 2026 calendar](https://www.dian.gov.co/Calendarios/Calendario_Tributario_2026.pdf) for those dated examples and the [current DIAN calendar](https://www.dian.gov.co/Paginas/CalendarioTributario.aspx) for other years. Confirm the filing obligation, RUT and electronic filing requirements for the taxpayer.

Check SIMPLE, minimum tax computation, sector surcharges, municipal ICA, property and wealth taxes, payroll, foreign income and treaty relief as separate questions.
