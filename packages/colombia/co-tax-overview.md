# Colombia Tax Overview

## Colombian tax system at a glance

- **National tax authority** — Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN)  _(Estatuto Tributario Nacional (Decreto 624 de 1989), https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/colombia)_
- **Tax year** — Calendar year (1 January to 31 December)  _(Estatuto Tributario Nacional, https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/colombia)_
- **Currency** — Colombian peso (COP)  _(Estatuto Tributario Nacional)_
- **Tax value unit (UVT) for 2025** — 49,799 COP; for 2026 the UVT is 52,374 COP  _(DIAN Resolución 000193 de 2024 (2025) and Resolución 000238 de 15 de diciembre de 2025 (2026) — https://incp.org.co/agendatributariaincp/noticias/2025/11/dian-fijaria-en-52-374-el-valor-de-la-uvt-para-2026/ ; Estatuto Tributario (Decreto 624 de 1989, consolidated text on the Función Pública gestor normativo), art. 868 — https://www.funcionpublica.gov.co/eva/gestornormativo/norma.php?i=6533)_
- **Basis of taxation** — Residents taxed on worldwide income; non-residents taxed on Colombian-source income only  _(Estatuto Tributario Nacional, https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/colombia/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **Top personal income tax rate (residents)** — 39%  _(Estatuto Tributario Nacional, art. 241, https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/colombia/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **General corporate income tax rate** — 35%  _(Estatuto Tributario (Decreto 624 de 1989, consolidated text on the Función Pública gestor normativo), art. 240 — https://www.funcionpublica.gov.co/eva/gestornormativo/norma.php?i=6533 ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/colombia/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Does Colombia have VAT?** — Yes — IVA (Impuesto sobre las Ventas), standard rate 19%, with 5% and 0% (exento) schedules and excluded goods and services  _(Estatuto Tributario (Decreto 624 de 1989, consolidated text on the Función Pública gestor normativo), art. 468 — https://www.funcionpublica.gov.co/eva/gestornormativo/norma.php?i=6533)_
- **Minimum effective tax rate (METR) for resident corporations** — 15% (Tasa de Tributación Depurada floor, applies from FY 2023 onward)  _(Estatuto Tributario (Decreto 624 de 1989, consolidated text on the Función Pública gestor normativo), art. 240 parágrafo 6 — https://www.funcionpublica.gov.co/eva/gestornormativo/norma.php?i=6533 ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/colombia/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Annual income tax return (Declaración de Renta)** — Filed electronically with DIAN; deadlines run mid-year by NIT last digit (individuals typically Aug–Oct; companies typically Apr–May) (approx — confirm exact calendar in DIAN annual deadline decree)  _(DIAN annual tax deadline decree (Decreto de plazos tributarios), https://medellinguru.com/income-taxes/)_
