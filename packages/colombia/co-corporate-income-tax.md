# Colombia Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax rates and surcharges

- **General corporate income tax rate** — 35%  _(Estatuto Tributario (Decreto 624 de 1989, consolidated text on the Función Pública gestor normativo), art. 240 — https://www.funcionpublica.gov.co/eva/gestornormativo/norma.php?i=6533)_
- **Overview of corporate taxation** — Colombian-incorporated companies are taxed on worldwide income at a general 35% rate; foreign entities are taxed on Colombian-source income. Sector surcharges apply to financial institutions and extractive industries, and a 15% minimum effective tax rate (METR) acts as a floor.  _(Estatuto Tributario (Decreto 624 de 1989, consolidated text on the Función Pública gestor normativo), art. 240 and parágrafo 6 — https://www.funcionpublica.gov.co/eva/gestornormativo/norma.php?i=6533)_
- **Surcharge for financial institutions** — Five additional points (total 40%) for taxable years 2023 to 2027 for financial institutions, insurers, broker-dealers and market infrastructure providers whose taxable income is at least 120,000 UVT, with a 100% advance payment of the surcharge  _(Estatuto Tributario (Decreto 624 de 1989, consolidated text on the Función Pública gestor normativo), art. 240 parágrafo 2 (Ley 2277 de 2022) — https://www.funcionpublica.gov.co/eva/gestornormativo/norma.php?i=6533)_
- **Surcharge for oil/coal extraction** — Variable surtax from 5% up to 10% (up to 15% for oil extraction), depending on price levels, where taxable income is at least ~50,000 UVT  _(Estatuto Tributario (Decreto 624 de 1989, consolidated text on the Función Pública gestor normativo), art. 240 parágrafo 3 (Ley 2277 de 2022) — https://www.funcionpublica.gov.co/eva/gestornormativo/norma.php?i=6533)_
- **Minimum effective tax rate (METR)** — 15% floor: where the Tasa de Tributación Depurada falls below 15%, the difference is added to the income tax (group computation for consolidated groups)  _(Estatuto Tributario (Decreto 624 de 1989, consolidated text on the Función Pública gestor normativo), art. 240 parágrafo 6 (Ley 2277 de 2022) — https://www.funcionpublica.gov.co/eva/gestornormativo/norma.php?i=6533)_
- **Tax base** — Worldwide income for Colombian national companies; Colombian-source income for foreign entities/PEs  _(Estatuto Tributario (Decreto 624 de 1989, consolidated text on the Función Pública gestor normativo), art. 12 — https://www.funcionpublica.gov.co/eva/gestornormativo/norma.php?i=6533)_
- **WHT on dividends to non-residents (from taxed earnings)** — 20%  _(Estatuto Tributario (Decreto 624 de 1989, consolidated text on the Función Pública gestor normativo), art. 245 (Ley 2277 de 2022, art. 4) — https://www.funcionpublica.gov.co/eva/gestornormativo/norma.php?i=6533)_
- **WHT on dividends to non-residents (from untaxed earnings)** — Taxed first at the art. 240 rate (35%), then 20% on the remainder (48% in total)  _(Estatuto Tributario (Decreto 624 de 1989, consolidated text on the Función Pública gestor normativo), art. 245 parágrafo 1 — https://www.funcionpublica.gov.co/eva/gestornormativo/norma.php?i=6533)_
- **WHT on interest to non-residents** — 20% in general; 15% on interest from credits of one year or more and on finance lease interest; 5% on 8-year-plus financing of infrastructure projects  _(Estatuto Tributario (Decreto 624 de 1989, consolidated text on the Función Pública gestor normativo), art. 408 — https://www.funcionpublica.gov.co/eva/gestornormativo/norma.php?i=6533)_
- **WHT on royalties to non-residents (incl. software)** — 20%  _(Estatuto Tributario (Decreto 624 de 1989, consolidated text on the Función Pública gestor normativo), art. 408 — https://www.funcionpublica.gov.co/eva/gestornormativo/norma.php?i=6533)_
- **WHT on technical assistance, consulting and technical services** — **20%**, the same as royalties, whether the service is rendered in Colombia or from abroad. This head was missing from the guide until September 2026 and it is the one most cross-border service contracts land in  _(Estatuto Tributario (Decreto 624 de 1989, consolidated text on the Función Pública gestor normativo), art. 408 — https://www.funcionpublica.gov.co/eva/gestornormativo/norma.php?i=6533)_
- **WHT on administrative expenses** — **33%**, the highest rate in the Colombian table and thirteen points above technical services. The line between an administration or management charge and a technical service is therefore worth thirteen points, and it is not a distinction a payer makes by instinct  _(Estatuto Tributario (Decreto 624 de 1989, consolidated text on the Función Pública gestor normativo), art. 408 (with art. 124) — https://www.funcionpublica.gov.co/eva/gestornormativo/norma.php?i=6533)_
- **WHT on other payments to non-residents (residual)** — **15%** of the gross payment for Colombian-source payments that fall into no named category (art. 415, not art. 408); occasional gains are withheld at 10%. Note the direction: the residual here is **below** the royalty and service rate, unlike Georgia or Kazakhstan where it sits above  _(Estatuto Tributario (Decreto 624 de 1989, consolidated text on the Función Pública gestor normativo), art. 415 — https://www.funcionpublica.gov.co/eva/gestornormativo/norma.php?i=6533)_
- **WHT on leasing** — **1%** on aircraft and ship leasing, and **15%** on standard leasing agreements with a term of a year or more. The 1% is the lowest rate in the table by a wide margin  _(Estatuto Tributario (Decreto 624 de 1989, consolidated text on the Función Pública gestor normativo), art. 408 — https://www.funcionpublica.gov.co/eva/gestornormativo/norma.php?i=6533)_
- **WHT on significant economic presence (SEP) payments** — **10%** where a non-resident with a significant economic presence in Colombia does **not** voluntarily register. Registration changes the treatment, so establish the payee's registration status before withholding — this is the head a digital or cross-border platform business meets  _(Estatuto Tributario (Decreto 624 de 1989, consolidated text on the Función Pública gestor normativo), art. 408 inciso 8 (Decreto 2039 de 2023) — https://www.funcionpublica.gov.co/eva/gestornormativo/norma.php?i=6533)_
- **Staleness warning on the source, not the guide** — The rates above were read against the Estatuto Tributario text on the Función Pública gestor normativo on 4 October 2026; the secondary source's table is headed "FY 2023". Colombian withholding rates move with each tax reform, so confirm the article text before a payment rather than relying on either table  _(Estatuto Tributario (Decreto 624 de 1989, consolidated text on the Función Pública gestor normativo), art. 240, 245, 408 and 415 — https://www.funcionpublica.gov.co/eva/gestornormativo/norma.php?i=6533)_
- **Treaty relief** — Reduced WHT rates may apply under Colombia's double-tax treaties  _(Applicable double taxation conventions (Convenios para evitar la doble imposición))_
- **CIT return filing and payment** — Annual return filed electronically with DIAN; deadlines (typically Apr–May) staggered by NIT, with an advance payment installment for large taxpayers (approx — confirm exact dates in DIAN annual deadline decree)  _(DIAN annual tax deadline decree (Decreto de plazos) — https://en.tpcgroup-int.com/news/2026-tax-calendar-in-colombia-transfer-pricing-and-key-obligations/)_
