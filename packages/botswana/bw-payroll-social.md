# Botswana Payroll & Social Contributions

## PAYE withholding

Employers operate PAYE — withholding income tax from employee remuneration using the individual tax tables — and remit monthly to BURS.

- **PAYE** — Employers withhold income tax from salary, wages and taxable benefits using the BURS resident and non-resident tax deduction tables effective 1 July 2026  _(Income Tax Act, 2026 (Act No. 13 of 2026), Government Gazette Supplement A of 1 July 2026, BURS copy, s 129(1) — https://www.burs.org.bw/index.php/tax/tax-laws-2026?download=954:income-tax-act-amendment-2026 ; BURS Tax Table and Guidance Notes for Employee's Income, effective 1 July 2026, Part I tax deduction tables for resident and non-resident employees — https://www.burs.org.bw/index.php/tax/tax-downloads?download=953:tax-table-2026)_
- **PAYE applies above** — BWP 48,000 per year for residents (tax-free band); non-residents pay 5% from the first pula  _(Income Tax Act, 2026 (Act No. 13 of 2026), Government Gazette Supplement A of 1 July 2026, BURS copy, Schedule 1 Part I para 1 — https://www.burs.org.bw/index.php/tax/tax-laws-2026?download=954:income-tax-act-amendment-2026 ; BURS Tax Table and Guidance Notes for Employee's Income, effective 1 July 2026, Part II annual tables — https://www.burs.org.bw/index.php/tax/tax-downloads?download=953:tax-table-2026)_

## Social security / pensions

Botswana has no general mandatory state social-security or national pension contribution. There is no statutory employer/employee social-security payroll levy beyond PAYE.

- **Mandatory social security** — None — no general statutory social-security or national pension contribution for employees or employers  _([PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries — Botswana](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/botswana/individual/other-taxes))_
- **Occupational pensions** — Workplace pension/provident funds are voluntary/employer-arranged and regulated, not a universal state scheme ((approx — confirm)) (approx — confirm)  _([Retirement Funds Act](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/botswana/individual/other-taxes))_

## Vocational Training Levy

A separate employer/turnover levy funds vocational training; it is not a payroll deduction but is a standing employer obligation.

- **Vocational Training Levy — up to BWP 2bn turnover** — 0.2% of annual turnover (turnover up to BWP 2 billion)  _([Vocational Training Act / Value Added Tax Act administration](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/botswana/corporate/other-taxes))_
- **Vocational Training Levy — above BWP 2bn turnover** — 0.05% on turnover above BWP 2 billion  _([Vocational Training Act / Value Added Tax Act administration](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/botswana/corporate/other-taxes))_

## Employer obligations and deadlines

Employers remit PAYE monthly and file annual reconciliations and employee tax certificates after the 30 June year-end.

- **Monthly PAYE remittance** — Within 14 days after the end of the month in which the employment income was paid, with a withholding tax remittance return (the repealed Act's 15th-of-month deadline no longer applies)  _(Income Tax Act, 2026 (Act No. 13 of 2026), Government Gazette Supplement A of 1 July 2026, BURS copy, s 138(1) and (2) — https://www.burs.org.bw/index.php/tax/tax-laws-2026?download=954:income-tax-act-amendment-2026)_
- **Annual PAYE reconciliation (Form ITW10)** — An annual withholding tax return showing total tax deducted and total payments made is due within 28 consecutive days after the 30 June tax year-end, so by 28 July; ITW10 is the BURS form used for it under the repealed Act  _(Income Tax Act, 2026 (Act No. 13 of 2026), Government Gazette Supplement A of 1 July 2026, BURS copy, s 139(1) — https://www.burs.org.bw/index.php/tax/tax-laws-2026?download=954:income-tax-act-amendment-2026)_
- **Employee tax certificate (Form ITW8)** — A withholding tax certificate showing the payment and tax withheld must be furnished by the due date for paying the withheld tax under s 138, that is within 14 days after the month end; the former annual ITW8 timing of 31 March rests on the repealed Act (approx — confirm how BURS applies s 137 to annual employee certificates)  _(Income Tax Act, 2026 (Act No. 13 of 2026), Government Gazette Supplement A of 1 July 2026, BURS copy, s 137 — https://www.burs.org.bw/index.php/tax/tax-laws-2026?download=954:income-tax-act-amendment-2026 ; ITW8 practice under the repealed Act: https://headoffice.app/botswana/blog/botswana-payroll-tax)_
