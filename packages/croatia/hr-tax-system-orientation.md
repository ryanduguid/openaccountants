# Croatia tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the taxpayer type, residence facts, income sources and relevant period. Check any treaty that assigns residence or limits the source country's tax.

## Territorial reach

Croatian residents generally account for Croatian and foreign income under the applicable individual or profit-tax law. Nonresident individuals account for income arising in Croatia. A foreign business's Croatian permanent establishment is within the profit-tax regime on its attributable profits. Check withholding separately for specified Croatian payments and apply any treaty relief. [Personal Income Tax Act, arts 4–6](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/full/2016_12_115_2525.html) · [Profit Tax Act, arts 2–5 and 31](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2004_12_177_3067.html) · [2026 profit-tax changes](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/full/2025_12_151_2261.html)

## Residence

An individual can be resident with a Croatian domicile or habitual abode. For tax domicile, a qualifying dwelling continuously owned or possessed for at least 183 days in one or 2 calendar years can suffice without physical occupation. The habitual-abode test considers non-temporary presence and a continuous or connected stay of at least 183 days in one or 2 calendar years. Check the statutory treatment of interruptions, multiple homes and state-service wages. For business residence, test the registered seat or place of actual management and business supervision. For ties abroad, confirm the current treaty position and document the residence facts. [Personal Income Tax Act, arts 3–4](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/full/2016_12_115_2525.html) · [General Tax Act, art 43](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/full/2016_12_115_2519.html) · [Profit Tax Act, art 3](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2004_12_177_3067.html)

## Taxpayer types

An individual operating a trade or profession ordinarily starts with the personal income rules for independent activity. Check whether the operator opts or is required to move into profit tax. When individuals jointly carry out a qualifying independent activity, calculate the common income, allocate the shares and identify the designated operator responsible for records and filings. A Croatian limited liability company is ordinarily a profit-tax taxpayer. For a trust created under foreign law, separate the beneficial ownership registration test from the income-tax analysis. Identify who owns and derives each amount, then check the charging provisions and any treaty. The profit-tax law includes a residual category for entrepreneurs who fall outside the listed categories and personal income tax and whose profit is untaxed elsewhere. [Personal Income Tax Act, arts 29 and 79](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/full/2016_12_115_2525.html) · [Profit Tax Act, art 2, as amended](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/full/2025_12_151_2261.html) · [Trust registration amendments](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2022_12_151_2335.html)

## Currency

Use euros (EUR) for ordinary Croatian tax reporting, statutory books and financial statements. An eligible IFRS reporter using a different functional currency keeps the required additional records and also prepares the statements in euros. Keep the currency basis and exchange rates with the records. [Euro Adoption Act, arts 70–74](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/full/2022_05_57_803.html) · [Accounting Act, arts 38–39](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/full/2024_07_85_1474.html)

## Assessment periods

The ordinary individual and corporate tax periods follow the calendar year. The Tax Administration can approve a different profit-tax period of up to 12 months on request. Check the rules for a shortened period when starting, changing status or winding up. Align the return period with the approval and keep the underlying accounts. [Personal Income Tax Act, art 7](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/full/2016_12_115_2525.html) · [Profit Tax Act, art 29](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/2004_12_177_3067.html)

## Responsible authorities

The Tax Administration (Porezna uprava) administers ordinary national income and profit taxes and operates ePorezna. Check the responsible office and any municipal decision affecting personal income tax. The Customs Administration handles customs and excise. [Tax Administration](https://porezna-uprava.gov.hr/) · [Customs Administration](https://carina.gov.hr/customs-administration/6676) · [2025 personal income-tax amendments](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/full/2024_12_152_2505.html)

## Fictional intake

A consultant visits Croatia for 100 days and holds a Zagreb flat for 190 consecutive days. Record the right to occupy the flat and other household facts. Test domestic domicile separately from the physical stay, then check any treaty before deciding which foreign receipts enter the Croatian return.
