# Kazakhstan Tax Overview

## Kazakhstan tax system at a glance (2025)

Kazakhstan operates a worldwide-income system for residents administered by the State Revenue Committee. For tax year 2025 a flat personal income tax and a standard corporate income tax apply; a new Tax Code signed 18 July 2025 changes many rates from 1 January 2026, but those are out of scope for the 2025 year.

- **Tax year** — Calendar year, 1 January to 31 December (art. 358(1) for corporate income tax)  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan, Code No. 214-VIII of 18 July 2025 (in force from 1 January 2026), Russian text on adilet.zan.kz, art. 358(1) — https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/K2500000214)_
- **Currency** — Kazakhstani tenge (KZT)  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kazakhstan)_
- **Tax authority** — State Revenue Committee of the Ministry of Finance of the Republic of Kazakhstan  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan — https://krg.kgd.gov.kz/en)_
- **Monthly Calculation Index (MCI) for 2025** — 3,932 KZT per MCI  _(Law on the Republican Budget for 2025-2027 — https://krg.kgd.gov.kz/en/news/application-monthly-calculation-index-mci-11-73424)_
- **Residence basis of taxation** — Tax residents are taxed on worldwide income; non-residents are taxed only on Kazakhstan-source income  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan, Code No. 214-VIII of 18 July 2025 (in force from 1 January 2026), Russian text on adilet.zan.kz, arts. 222, 223 and 225 — https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/K2500000214)_
- **Headline personal income tax rate (resident)** — 10% on taxable income up to 8,500 times the monthly calculation index in the calendar year and 15% on the excess, from 1 January 2026 under Code No. 214-VIII (flat 10% for 2025 under the previous Code); private practitioners 9%  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan, Code No. 214-VIII of 18 July 2025 (in force from 1 January 2026), English text on adilet.zan.kz, art. 363.1 — https://adilet.zan.kz/eng/docs/K2500000214 ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kazakhstan/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **Headline corporate income tax rate** — 20% (25% for second-tier banks' banking activity and gambling; 3% for agricultural producers)  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan, Code No. 214-VIII of 18 July 2025 (in force from 1 January 2026), English text on adilet.zan.kz, art. 357.2.5 — https://adilet.zan.kz/eng/docs/K2500000214 ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kazakhstan/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Does VAT exist** — Yes — 16% from 1 January 2026 (art. 503(1)), with 5% in 2026 and 10% from 2027 for the supplies listed in art. 503(2) and 10% for domestic periodicals (art. 503(3)); 12% applied in 2025 under the previous Code  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan, Code No. 214-VIII of 18 July 2025 (in force from 1 January 2026), Russian text on adilet.zan.kz, art. 503 — https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/K2500000214)_
- **CIT return filing deadline** — 31 March of the year following the reporting year (art. 359(1))  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan, Code No. 214-VIII of 18 July 2025 (in force from 1 January 2026), Russian text on adilet.zan.kz, art. 359(1) — https://adilet.zan.kz/rus/docs/K2500000214)_
- **Individual annual declaration deadline (where required)** — 15 September of the year following the reporting year under the 2026 Code (31 March applied under the previous Code)  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan, Code No. 214-VIII of 18 July 2025 (in force from 1 January 2026), English text on adilet.zan.kz, art. 418.1 — https://adilet.zan.kz/eng/docs/K2500000214 ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kazakhstan/individual/tax-administration)_
- **New Tax Code** — A new Tax Code was signed 18 July 2025 and takes effect 1 January 2026 (VAT to 16%, progressive PIT, etc.) — does not affect tax year 2025  _(New Tax Code of the Republic of Kazakhstan (signed 18 July 2025) — https://www.ey.com/en_kz/technical/tax-alerts/kazakhstan-tax-legislation-update)_
