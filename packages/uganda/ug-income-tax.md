# Uganda Personal Income Tax

## Personal income tax bands — resident individuals

- **Monthly income not exceeding UGX 335,000** — 0% (tax-free threshold) from 1 July 2026 under the Income Tax (Amendment) Act, 2026 (resident individual, monthly chargeable income; UGX 235,000 for 2025/26)  _(Uganda Revenue Authority, PAYE rates page (resident and non-resident scales) — https://ura.go.ug/en/domestic-taxes/paye-rates/ ; Uganda Revenue Authority, Changes to PAYE return form following the Income Tax (Amendment) Act, 2026 (new resident rates from 1 July 2026) — https://ura.go.ug/en/changes-to-paye-return-form-following-the-income-tax-amendment-act-2026/)_
- **Monthly income UGX 335,001 – 410,000** — 20% of the amount over UGX 335,000 (resident individual; for 2025/26 this band ran from UGX 235,001 to 335,000 at 10%)  _(Uganda Revenue Authority, PAYE rates page (resident and non-resident scales) — https://ura.go.ug/en/domestic-taxes/paye-rates/ ; Uganda Revenue Authority, Changes to PAYE return form following the Income Tax (Amendment) Act, 2026 (new resident rates from 1 July 2026) — https://ura.go.ug/en/changes-to-paye-return-form-following-the-income-tax-amendment-act-2026/)_
- **Monthly income UGX 410,001 – 485,000** — UGX 15,000 plus 25% of the amount over UGX 410,000 (resident individual; a new band from 1 July 2026, when UGX 10,000 plus 20% applied between UGX 335,001 and 410,000 for 2025/26)  _(Uganda Revenue Authority, PAYE rates page (resident and non-resident scales) — https://ura.go.ug/en/domestic-taxes/paye-rates/ ; Uganda Revenue Authority, Changes to PAYE return form following the Income Tax (Amendment) Act, 2026 (new resident rates from 1 July 2026) — https://ura.go.ug/en/changes-to-paye-return-form-following-the-income-tax-amendment-act-2026/)_
- **Monthly income UGX 485,001 – 10,000,000** — UGX 33,750 plus 30% of the amount over UGX 485,000 (resident individual, monthly chargeable income; UGX 25,000 plus 30% over UGX 410,000 for 2025/26)  _(Uganda Revenue Authority, PAYE rates page (resident and non-resident scales) — https://ura.go.ug/en/domestic-taxes/paye-rates/ ; Uganda Revenue Authority, Changes to PAYE return form following the Income Tax (Amendment) Act, 2026 (new resident rates from 1 July 2026) — https://ura.go.ug/en/changes-to-paye-return-form-following-the-income-tax-amendment-act-2026/)_
- **Additional surcharge on monthly income exceeding UGX 10,000,000** — An extra 10% on the amount over UGX 10,000,000, in addition to the 30% band (resident individual, monthly chargeable income)  _(Uganda Revenue Authority, PAYE rates page (resident and non-resident scales) — https://ura.go.ug/en/domestic-taxes/paye-rates/ ; Uganda Revenue Authority, Changes to PAYE return form following the Income Tax (Amendment) Act, 2026 (new resident rates from 1 July 2026) — https://ura.go.ug/en/changes-to-paye-return-form-following-the-income-tax-amendment-act-2026/)_
- **Annual tax-free threshold (resident)** — UGX 4,020,000 per year (UGX 335,000 x 12) from 1 July 2026; UGX 2,820,000 (UGX 235,000 x 12) for 2025/26  _(Uganda Revenue Authority, PAYE rates page (resident and non-resident scales) — https://ura.go.ug/en/domestic-taxes/paye-rates/ ; Uganda Revenue Authority, Changes to PAYE return form following the Income Tax (Amendment) Act, 2026 (new resident rates from 1 July 2026) — https://ura.go.ug/en/changes-to-paye-return-form-following-the-income-tax-amendment-act-2026/)_
- **Resident PAYE scale basis** — Resident individuals are taxed on a progressive monthly PAYE scale set in the Third Schedule of the Income Tax Act, with a tax-free threshold and a high-earner surcharge; the Income Tax (Amendment) Act, 2026 lifted the threshold to UGX 335,000 and added a 25% band from 1 July 2026  _(Uganda Revenue Authority, PAYE rates page (resident and non-resident scales) — https://ura.go.ug/en/domestic-taxes/paye-rates/ ; Uganda Revenue Authority, Changes to PAYE return form following the Income Tax (Amendment) Act, 2026 (new resident rates from 1 July 2026) — https://ura.go.ug/en/changes-to-paye-return-form-following-the-income-tax-amendment-act-2026/)_

## Personal income tax bands — non-resident individuals

- **Monthly income not exceeding UGX 335,000** — 10% (non-resident individual, monthly chargeable income)  _(Uganda Revenue Authority, PAYE rates page (resident and non-resident scales) — https://ura.go.ug/en/domestic-taxes/paye-rates/)_
- **Monthly income UGX 335,001 – 410,000** — UGX 33,500 plus 20% of the amount over UGX 335,000 (non-resident individual, monthly chargeable income)  _(Uganda Revenue Authority, PAYE rates page (resident and non-resident scales) — https://ura.go.ug/en/domestic-taxes/paye-rates/)_
- **Monthly income UGX 410,001 – 10,000,000** — UGX 48,500 plus 30% of the amount over UGX 410,000 (non-resident individual, monthly chargeable income)  _(Uganda Revenue Authority, PAYE rates page (resident and non-resident scales) — https://ura.go.ug/en/domestic-taxes/paye-rates/)_
- **Additional surcharge on monthly income exceeding UGX 10,000,000** — An extra 10% on the amount over UGX 10,000,000 (non-resident individual, monthly chargeable income)  _(Uganda Revenue Authority, PAYE rates page (resident and non-resident scales) — https://ura.go.ug/en/domestic-taxes/paye-rates/)_
- **Non-resident basis** — Non-resident individuals do not get the tax-free threshold; the first band is taxed at 10%. Bands are monthly chargeable income in UGX.  _(Uganda Revenue Authority, PAYE rates page (resident and non-resident scales) — https://ura.go.ug/en/domestic-taxes/paye-rates/)_

## Residence test, deductions and filing

- **Residence and filing overview** — Residence is determined chiefly by physical presence in Uganda. Employees taxed only under PAYE generally have no further filing obligation, while individuals with other income file an annual return.  _(Income Tax Act (Cap 340))_
- **Residence test (physical presence)** — Present in Uganda for 183 days or more in a financial year, or has a permanent home in Uganda, or is employed by the Government of Uganda and posted abroad  _(Uganda Revenue Authority, PAYE rates page (resident and non-resident scales) — https://ura.go.ug/en/domestic-taxes/paye-rates/)_
- **Individual annual return deadline** — Within six months after the end of the financial period, so by 31 December for a 30 June year-end  _(Uganda Revenue Authority, Returns page (filing deadlines) — https://ura.go.ug/en/domestic-taxes/returns/)_
- **PAYE as final tax for employees** — Employment income taxed under PAYE is generally a final tax where the employee has no other income  _(Income Tax Act (Cap 340) — https://ura.go.ug/en/domestic-taxes/paye-rates/)_
- **Treatment of employee NSSF contributions** — Employee NSSF contributions are not deductible for PAYE; PAYE is computed on gross employment income (confirm current rule) ((approx — confirm))  _(Income Tax Act (Cap 340))_
- **Mortgage interest relief** — No general personal deductions or family allowances; relief is limited to specific items in the Act (confirm any owner-occupier mortgage relief) ((approx — confirm))  _(Income Tax Act (Cap 340))_
