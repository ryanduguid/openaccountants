# Uganda Tax Overview

This guide covers ordinary Ugandan income tax and VAT for 2026-27. The Uganda Revenue Authority (URA) administers both taxes. The ordinary income year runs from 1 July to 30 June, and the Commissioner General can approve a substituted year. Figures are in Ugandan shillings (UGX). [URA](https://ura.go.ug/) · [Income Tax Act, Cap. 338, ss 2 and 37](https://ulii.org/en/akn/ug/act/1997/11/eng%402024-12-23) · [Bank of Uganda Act](https://ulii.org/en/akn/ug/act/statute/1993/5/eng%402023-12-31)

## Tax residence and taxable income

- **Individuals.** A person is resident for an income year if they have a permanent home in Uganda, are present there for at least 183 days in a 12-month period beginning or ending in that year, or are present in that year and each of the 2 preceding years for an average of more than 122 days per year. A Ugandan government employee or official posted abroad during the year also qualifies. Check the first and last year rules when residence changes. [Income Tax Act, s 9](https://ulii.org/en/akn/ug/act/1997/11/eng%402024-12-23#part_III__sec_9)
- **Companies.** A company is resident if it was incorporated or formed under Ugandan law, its affairs were managed and controlled in Uganda at any time during the year, or it undertook most of its operations in Uganda during the year. [Income Tax Act, s 10](https://ulii.org/en/akn/ug/act/1997/11/eng%402024-12-23#part_III__sec_10)
- **Income scope.** Residents ordinarily include income from all sources, subject to exemptions. Non-residents ordinarily include Uganda-source income. Confirm source, exemptions, foreign tax credit and any applicable treaty for the facts and period. [Income Tax Act, ss 17 and 78–80](https://ulii.org/en/akn/ug/act/1997/11/eng%402024-12-23)

## Ordinary income tax rates from 1 July 2026

The resident individual scale applies to annual chargeable income. The amendment was published on 28 August 2026 with effect from 1 July. URA instructed employers to amend affected PAYE returns already filed on the former bands. [Income Tax (Amendment) Act 2026, ss 1 and 16](https://ulii.org/en/akn/ug/act/2026/21/eng@2026-08-28) · [URA PAYE change notice](https://ura.go.ug/en/changes-to-paye-return-form-following-the-income-tax-amendment-act-2026/)

| Annual chargeable income | Resident income tax |
| --- | --- |
| Up to UGX 4,020,000 | Nil |
| Over UGX 4,020,000 to UGX 4,920,000 | 20% of the excess over UGX 4,020,000 |
| Over UGX 4,920,000 to UGX 5,820,000 | UGX 180,000 plus 25% of the excess over UGX 4,920,000 |
| Over UGX 5,820,000 to UGX 120,000,000 | UGX 405,000 plus 30% of the excess over UGX 5,820,000 |
| Over UGX 120,000,000 | UGX 405,000 plus 30% of the excess over UGX 5,820,000 plus 10% of the excess over UGX 120,000,000 |

The standard company rate is 30% of chargeable income for companies outside the mining rate rules. Check any applicable small-business basis, exemption, incentive or sector rule separately. [Income Tax Act, ss 4 and 7 and Schedule 4 Part III](https://ulii.org/en/akn/ug/act/1997/11/eng%402024-12-23)

## Income tax returns and payment

- **Annual return and balance.** The ordinary return is due within 6 months of income year end. Pay the ordinary self-assessed balance by the return due date. For an ordinary 2026-27 year ending 30 June 2027, the statutory date is 31 December 2027. Check approved extensions and any dated URA notice. A filing extension does not extend the payment deadline. [Income Tax Act, ss 118–120](https://ulii.org/en/akn/ug/act/1997/11/eng%402024-12-23) · [Tax Procedures Code Act, Cap. 343, ss 16, 19 and 22](https://ulii.org/en/akn/ug/act/2014/14/eng%402025-07-01) · [URA returns](https://ura.go.ug/en/domestic-taxes/returns/)
- **Return exceptions.** Unless URA requires one by written notice, a return can be unnecessary for a resident individual with only employment income from a single employer and the required PAYE withheld. Conditions include the additional employment income rule in s 4(7) and the diplomatic or prescribed organisation exception in s 119. Section 119 also addresses resident individuals with only zero-rated chargeable income and specified non-residents. [Income Tax Act, ss 4(4), 4(7) and 119](https://ulii.org/en/akn/ug/act/1997/11/eng%402024-12-23)
- **Provisional tax.** A liable non-individual provisional taxpayer pays by the last day of months 6 and 12 of its income year. A liable individual provisional taxpayer pays by the last day of months 3, 6, 9 and 12. [Income Tax Act, s 121](https://ulii.org/en/akn/ug/act/1997/11/eng%402024-12-23)

## Value added tax

- **Rate and monthly return.** The standard rate is 18%. For ordinary domestic VAT, file within 15 days after the end of the calendar month and pay by that return due date. Specific rules apply to non-resident services and assessments. [VAT Act, Cap. 344, ss 2, 24, 31, 32 and 51](https://ulii.org/en/akn/ug/act/statute/1996/8/eng%402024-12-23) · [URA 2026-27 VAT factsheet](https://ura.go.ug/storage/2026/11/VALUE-ADDED-TAX-FY-2026-27ENG-2.pdf)
- **Ordinary registration.** From 1 July 2026 the annual threshold is UGX 300 million. Under the 3-calendar-month tests, apply within 20 days of the period end if VAT-exclusive taxable supplies exceeded UGX 75 million, or at the start if there are reasonable grounds to expect supplies to exceed UGX 75 million. Before that change the thresholds were UGX 150 million annually and UGX 37.5 million for 3 calendar months. Section 7 has separate provisions for specified cases, including a longer-period forecast registration route limited by s 7(7). [VAT Act, s 7](https://ulii.org/en/akn/ug/act/statute/1996/8/eng%402024-12-23#part_III__sec_7) · [VAT (Amendment) Act 2026, ss 1, 3 and 5](https://ulii.org/en/akn/ug/act/2026/14/eng@2026-06-05) · [URA threshold notice](https://ura.go.ug/en/revised-value-added-tax-registration-threshold/)
- **Existing registrations.** URA says businesses below the updated threshold can apply to deregister or remain voluntarily registered and continue meeting VAT obligations. Confirm the applicable threshold and effective date before changing treatment. [URA threshold notice](https://ura.go.ug/en/revised-value-added-tax-registration-threshold/)
