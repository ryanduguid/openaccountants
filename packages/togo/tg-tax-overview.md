# Togo Tax Overview

## Togo tax system at a glance

- **Togo tax overview summary** — Togo is a WAEMU/OHADA member state whose taxes are administered by the Office Togolais des Recettes (OTR) under the Code Général des Impôts (CGI). The currency is the West African CFA franc (XOF) and the tax year is the calendar year.  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo))_
- **Tax year** — Calendar year. Income tax is due each year on the profits or income the taxpayer realises or has at their disposal during that year  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI art. 12 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Currency** — West African CFA franc (XOF / FCFA)  _(WAEMU monetary union)_
- **Tax authority** — Office Togolais des Recettes (OTR)  _([Office Togolais des Recettes](https://www.otr.tg/))_
- **Residence / taxation basis** — Individuals fiscally domiciled in Togo are taxed on worldwide income, Togolese-source and foreign-source alike; those not domiciled there are taxed on Togo-source income, **or where they have one or more dwellings in Togo**. Fiscal domicile follows from any of: *foyer* or principal place of stay in Togo; a professional activity carried on there that is not shown to be ancillary; the centre of economic interests in Togo; or being a State agent posted abroad and not personally taxed on total income there  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI arts. 2–3 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Headline personal income tax (IRPP)** — Progressive **0% to 35%** in eight bands on annual net taxable income: exempt to XOF 900,000, then 3%, 10%, 15%, 20%, 25%, 30%, and 35% above XOF 20,000,000. The first band is *exonéré*, not a low positive rate percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI art. 74 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Business income is off that scale** — Individuals with *revenus d'affaires* (CGI art. 29: commercial, artisanal, agricultural and non-commercial profits) pay a flat **30%**, not the progressive scale percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI art. 78 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Headline corporate income tax (IS)** — 27% of taxable profit, with a *Minimum Forfaitaire de Perception* of 1% of turnover excluding VAT (floor XOF 20,000 on the real regime) where the company is loss-making or the computed tax would be lower percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI arts. 113 and 120 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **VAT (TVA) in force** — Yes — a **single rate of 18%** applying to all activities and products except those exempted by CGI art. 180. There is no reduced rate in the consolidated 2025 code. Registration threshold: turnover above **XOF 100,000,000** percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI arts. 177 and 195 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Small individual traders pay one tax instead of four** — Individuals with annual turnover up to **XOF 60,000,000** fall under the *Taxe Professionnelle Unique*, which discharges IRPP on business income, the MFP, the *patente* and **VAT**. It does not apply to individuals on non-commercial profits (BNC), industrial bakers, or individuals who import or export  _(Code Général des Impôts (Togo) 2025, CGI arts. 129–131 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Annual IRPP return deadline** — 31 March. Taxpayers whose only income is salaries, wages, pensions, life annuities and/or investment income are **exempt from filing** where the tax was withheld at source  _(Livre des Procédures Fiscales (Togo) 2025, LPF arts. 20–21 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Annual IS return deadline** — 31 March of the following year, in five copies  _(Livre des Procédures Fiscales (Togo) 2025, LPF art. 22 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Monthly remittance (IRPP withholding and VAT)** — By the **15th of the month following** the period, for both employer-withheld IRPP and the monthly VAT return. CNSS contributions run on their own statute and calendar — see the payroll guide  _(Livre des Procédures Fiscales (Togo) 2025, LPF arts. 27 and 60 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
- **Instalments on business and corporate income** — Four instalments due 31 January, 31 May, 31 July and 31 October, each a quarter of the previous closed year's assessment  _(Livre des Procédures Fiscales (Togo) 2025, LPF art. 25 — https://www.otr.tg/index.php/fr/impots/reglementations-fiscales/code-general-des-impots/600-code-general-des-impots-livre-des-procedures-fiscales-mis-a-jour-2025/file.html)_
