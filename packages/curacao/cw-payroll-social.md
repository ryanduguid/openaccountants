# Curacao Payroll & Social Contributions

## Social security contributions and payroll withholding

Curacao social premiums are administered by the Social Insurance Bank (SVB) and cover old-age pension (AOV), widows/orphans (AWW), and basic health insurance (BVZ), plus accident/sickness insurance for employees. Employers withhold wage tax (loonbelasting) and premiums via payroll. Rates below reflect the 2025 SVB schedule.

- **AOV (old-age pension) — employer share** — 9.0% percent  _([National Ordinance on Old-Age Insurance (Landsverordening Algemene Ouderdomsverzekering, AOV)](https://svbcur.org/en/))_
- **AOV (old-age pension) — employee share** — Employee AOV/AWW premium applies on capped wages (combined AOV/AWW employee share around 6.5%) percent ((approx — confirm 2025 employee AOV split))  _(National Ordinance on Old-Age Insurance (Landsverordening Algemene Ouderdomsverzekering, AOV))_
- **AWW (widows/orphans) — employer share** — 0.5% percent  _([National Ordinance on Widows and Orphans Insurance (Landsverordening Algemene Weduwen- en Wezenverzekering, AWW)](https://svbcur.org/en/))_
- **AOV/AWW wage cap** — Premium income base capped at ANG 100,000 per year ANG ((approx — confirm 2025 cap))  _(National Ordinances AOV / AWW)_
- **BVZ (basic health insurance) — total rate** — 13.6% total (9.3% employer + 4.3% employee) percent  _([National Ordinance on Basic Health Insurance (Landsverordening basisverzekering ziektekosten, BVZ)](https://svbcur.org/en/))_
- **BVZ — employer share** — 9.3% percent  _([National Ordinance on Basic Health Insurance (Landsverordening basisverzekering ziektekosten, BVZ)](https://svbcur.org/en/))_
- **BVZ — employee share** — 4.3% percent  _([National Ordinance on Basic Health Insurance (Landsverordening basisverzekering ziektekosten, BVZ)](https://svbcur.org/en/))_
- **BVZ wage cap** — Premium income base capped at ANG 150,000 per year ANG ((approx — confirm 2025 cap))  _(National Ordinance on Basic Health Insurance (BVZ))_
- **Sickness (ZV) and accident (OV) insurance** — Employer-only premiums for sickness (ziekteverzekering) and accident (ongevallenverzekering) on wages up to the ZV/OV ceiling; rates vary by risk class percent ((approx — confirm class-based rates))  _(National Ordinance on Sickness Insurance (ZV) / Accident Insurance (OV))_
- **Wage tax (loonbelasting) withholding** — Employers withhold wage tax monthly on the progressive PIT scale as an advance on income tax  _(Wage Tax Ordinance (Landsverordening op de loonbelasting 1976))_
- **Payroll remittance deadline** — Wage tax and social premiums are filed and remitted monthly, generally by the 15th day of the following month ((approx — confirm exact due day))  _(Wage Tax Ordinance (Landsverordening op de loonbelasting 1976))_
