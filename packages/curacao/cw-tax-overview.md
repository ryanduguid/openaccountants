# Curaçao Tax Overview

Curaçao administers its own income, profit, wage and turnover taxes within the Kingdom of the Netherlands. Start with the taxpayer's residence, the activity or receipt and the relevant year. The Inspectorate of Taxes handles returns and assessment. The Receiver handles collection. [Belastingdienst, individual income tax][IB] · [Belastingdienst, profit tax][WB] · [Belastingdienst, filing guidance][OBFAQ]

## Residence and source

Determine an individual's residence from the circumstances, including temporary absences and any applicable agreement. Resident individuals generally report worldwide income. Non-residents report the Curaçao sources specified by the income tax ordinance. Check the business, employment, property or other source and applicable relief separately. [Income Tax Ordinance, arts 1, 3 and 17][IBLAW] · [Belastingdienst, individual income tax][IB]

From 2020, the ordinary profit tax base is net profit from domestic business under article 4 of the Profit Tax Ordinance. For mixed operations, substantiate the foreign share and the location of activities and their associated costs. The tax authority generally treats business profit as domestic unless the taxpayer substantiates the foreign share. Check separate substance, permanent establishment and passive income rules before excluding any amount. [2019 tax reform, art I][WBLAW] · [Profit tax decree, P.B. 2021 no. 19, annex G][WBG] · [Belastingdienst, territoriality guidance][WB]

## Taxpayer type

A sole trader generally returns business income on the individual return. Companies with capital divided into shares, cooperatives and certain other entities fall under profit tax. Employers assess wage withholding separately. The turnover tax (omzetbelasting, OB) can apply to supplies and services across legal forms. Check registration, the transaction and any exemption. [Belastingdienst, sole traders][SOLE] · [Belastingdienst, profit tax][WB] · [Belastingdienst, turnover tax FAQ][OBFAQ]

## Currency

Use the Caribbean guilder (Cg, currency code XCG) for current guilder amounts. The central bank introduced it on 31 March 2025. The Netherlands Antillean guilder (ANG or NAf) remained legal tender through 30 June 2025 and exchanges one for one with XCG. Confirm the applicable conversion and reporting rule for receipts in other currencies. [Centrale Bank van Curaçao en Sint Maarten][CBCS]

## Assessment periods

Individual income tax is annual. Check the dated invitation and extensions. For the 2025 return, the Inspector extended the general deadline from 16 July to 2 November 2026. Ordinary profit tax uses the calendar or other accounting year, with provisional and final returns generally due within 3 and 6 months of the close respectively. For turnover tax, check the assigned monthly, quarterly or annual period in the portal. [Belastingdienst, individual income tax][IB] · [2025 income tax return extension][DUE2025] · [Belastingdienst, profit tax][WB] · [Belastingdienst, 2026 filing periods][OBFAQ]

## Headline rates

The 2026 individual table has 6 marginal rates from 9.75% to 46.5%, before applicable credits. Belastingdienst publishes ordinary profit tax of 15% on the first Cg 500,000 of taxable profit and 22% on the excess. The two-band treatment originates in the ministerial direction published in issue 38 of the 2023 Gazette, retroactive to 1 January 2023. The ordinary OB rate is 6%, with separate 7% and 9% categories and exemptions. Check the tax period and special regimes before selecting the table. [P.B. 2025 no. 226][TABLE] · [2023 Gazette, issue 38, pp 12–13][GAZ] · [Belastingdienst, profit tax][WB] · [Belastingdienst, turnover tax rates][OBFAQ]
