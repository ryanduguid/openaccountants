# United States tax system

> General orientation for tax year 2026. Confirm the taxpayer, entity, income, tax year and jurisdictions with a qualified US tax professional before applying these rules.

The Internal Revenue Service (IRS) administers federal internal revenue taxes. Amounts use US dollars (USD). State and local income, franchise, sales and use, and property taxes can have separate registration, tax bases and filing dates. Identify the relevant state and municipality before applying a local rule. Separately identify any US possession or territory involved. [IRS filing](https://www.irs.gov/individual-tax-filing) · [USAGov state and local taxes](https://www.usa.gov/state-taxes)

## Federal income tax

- **Individuals and residence.** US citizens and resident aliens generally report worldwide income. For an alien, check lawful permanent residence and the substantial presence test. Under the latter, the ordinary test is at least 31 days in the current year and at least 183 weighted days across that year and the 2 preceding years, counting all included days of the current year, one-third of the previous year's and one-sixth of the second previous year's. Check excluded days, closer connection, treaty residence and split-year rules. For a non-resident alien, distinguish income effectively connected with a US trade or business from other US-source income and check the withholding and treaty rules. [26 USC § 7701(b)](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/t26/s7701/) · [IRS international tax FAQs](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/frequently-asked-questions-about-international-individual-tax-matters) · [IRS characterisation of nonresident income](https://www.irs.gov/individuals/international-taxpayers/characterization-of-income-of-nonresident-aliens) · [Residency workflow](us-tax-residency.md)
- **2026 individual rates.** The 7 ordinary federal marginal rates are 10%, 12%, 22%, 24%, 32%, 35% and 37%. The ordinary 2026 standard deduction is USD 16,100 for single or married filing separately, USD 24,150 for head of household and USD 32,200 for married filing jointly or a qualifying surviving spouse. Confirm filing status and eligibility, especially for dependants, non-residents and spouses filing separately when one itemises. Use the 2026 brackets for the applicable status, and check separate capital gains rates, adjustments and credits. [IRS Rev. Proc. 2025-32](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rp-25-32.pdf) · [IRS Publication 505](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p505)
- **Business entities.** The ordinary domestic C corporation federal tax rate is 21% of taxable income. Determine federal classification before picking the return. A domestic eligible single-member LLC is generally disregarded for income tax and an eligible LLC with multiple members generally defaults to a partnership, unless a valid election changes the classification. For an S corporation, confirm the election and shareholder rules. Apply any corporate alternative minimum tax, state taxes, foreign-owner reporting and entity-specific provisions separately. [26 USC § 11](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/t26/s11/) · [IRS entity guidance](https://www.irs.gov/faqs/small-business-self-employed-other-business/entities) · [C corporation workflow](us-form-1120-c-corp.md)

## Payroll, sales and filing

- **Payroll.** Federal wage withholding is separate from Social Security and Medicare. For ordinary covered 2026 wages, the employee and employer each pay 6.2% Social Security up to the USD 184,500 wage base and 1.45% Medicare without that cap. An employer generally adds 0.9% employee Additional Medicare withholding on wages it pays above USD 200,000 in the calendar year. The worker's ultimate Additional Medicare liability has filing-status rules. Determine federal and state unemployment obligations and deposit schedules separately. [IRS Publication 15](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p15) · [Federal payroll workflow](us-form-941-940-payroll.md)
- **Sales and use.** Check state and local sales or use tax using the place of supply, the seller's presence or economic nexus, the item and the transaction. Federal excise is a separate category. [USAGov state and local taxes](https://www.usa.gov/state-taxes) · [US Supreme Court, 585 U.S. 162](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/preliminaryprint/585US1PP_Web.pdf) · [Sales tax workflow](us-sales-tax.md)
- **Filing.** The ordinary 2026 Form 1040 filing and payment date is 15 April 2027. An ordinary calendar-year domestic C corporation files Form 1120 by the same date. Partnerships and S corporations ordinarily use the 15th day of the 3rd month after year-end, and C corporations the 15th day of the 4th month. Check weekend and holiday adjustments, special foreign and overseas filer rules, the transition for certain June year-ends and any disaster relief. A normal Form 4868 or Form 7004 filing extension leaves the ordinary payment due date in place. [IRS 2026 individual filing](https://www.irs.gov/individual-tax-filing) · [26 USC § 6072](https://uscode.house.gov/us/usc/t26/s6072/) · [IRS Publication 509](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p509)
