# Oman tax overview

Identify the taxpayer, legal form, residence, income source and accounting period. For an Omani company, consider domestic and foreign income and check any relief for tax paid abroad. For a foreign person, distinguish income attributable to an Oman permanent establishment from the specified Oman-source payments subject to the separate charge. Apply each treaty, attribution and exemption rule to the actual facts. [Income Tax Law, arts 1–3, 35, 39–40, 52 and 120–125][income-law]

## Residence and taxpayer type

For the ordinary Income Tax Law in 2025, a natural person is resident if present in Oman for at least 183 days, continuously or intermittently during the tax year. A legal person is resident if established under Omani law or if its actual management or headquarters is in Oman. Check domestic residence and treaty status separately. [Income Tax Law, art 18 bis][income-law] · [Royal Decree 118/2020, art 2][return-amendment]

The Income Tax Law distinguishes enterprises, establishments, Omani companies and permanent establishments. Identify business carried on by a sole owner separately from ordinary personal wages. The ordinary rate for an establishment, Omani company or permanent establishment is 15% of taxable income, subject to the specific regimes in the law. See the [corporate income tax guide](om-corporate-income-tax.md) for qualifying enterprises. [Income Tax Law, arts 1, 39 and 112 and Part Five bis][income-law]

The separate personal income tax under Royal Decree 56/2025 starts on 1 January 2028. It uses a 5% rate on taxable income where total annual income exceeds OMR42,000. Check the personal law's residence, income, deduction and transition rules for the actual period. [Royal Decree 56/2025, Gazette 1602][personal-law] · [Tax Authority personal income tax guidance][personal-faq]

## Currency, periods and filing authority

Use Omani rials (OMR) and verify the conversion rule for each foreign amount. The ordinary 2025 tax year is 1 January to 31 December 2025. Confirm any permitted different accounting period and the first or final period. Submit the return through the Oman Tax Authority's applicable service. [Central Bank of Oman, currency][currency] · [Income Tax Law, arts 1, 44 and 48–51][income-law] · [Tax Authority return services][filing]

Ordinary income tax returns are due within four months of the earlier of the tax year-end and the relevant accounting period-end. Qualifying Part Five bis enterprises generally file within three months. Pay the tax with the applicable return. For a 31 December year-end these ordinarily give 30 April and 31 March respectively. Verify case-specific timing, eligibility and the actual submission and payment instructions. [Income Tax Law, arts 140, 150, 159 bis 18 and 159 bis 22][income-law] · [Royal Decree 118/2020][return-amendment] · [Tax Authority income tax FAQs][income-faq]

## VAT and multinational top-up tax

- **VAT.** The standard rate is 5%. The ordinary resident business thresholds are OMR38,500 for mandatory registration and OMR19,250 for voluntary registration. Check the statutory period and categories of taxable supplies or expenses, special registration rules and any exemption or zero rate. The ordinary return is due within 30 days after the end of the assigned tax period. See the [VAT guide](oman-vat.md). [VAT Law][vat-law] · [Tax Authority registration guidance][vat-registration] · [VAT return filing guide][vat-filing]
- **Multinational top-up tax.** Royal Decree 70/2024 brought the charge into force on 1 January 2025. Royal Decree 101/2026 replaced the original scope and parent-entity provisions for the specified periods beginning with the tax year starting 1 January 2025. Other changes generally began on 2 November 2026. Use amended article 2 for the Omani rial equivalent of EUR750 million in at least 2 of the 4 immediately preceding financial years, subject to prorating where needed. Article 4 retains the 15% effective-rate target. Check amended article 3 for exclusions and article 3 bis for express exemptions. [Royal Decree 70/2024, Tax Authority collection][top-up-original] · [Royal Decree 101/2026 and attachment, Gazette 1672][top-up-amended]
- **Top-up reporting.** Amended article 6 sets registration within 120 days of coming within the charge and provides for appointing a reporting entity. Article 7 states a 15-month deadline after the reporting fiscal year for the top-up and Pillar Two information returns. Article 5 connects payment to the return deadline. Establish implementation, transitional dates and case-specific filing, payment and computation under the applicable regulations before applying these rules. [Royal Decree 101/2026 and attachment, arts 5–9][top-up-amended]
