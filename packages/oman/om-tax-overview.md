# Oman Tax Overview

## Oman tax system at a glance (2025)

- **Standard tax year** — Calendar year (1 January – 31 December); a company may apply to use a different accounting period  _(Income Tax Law (Royal Decree No. 28/2009) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/oman/corporate/tax-administration)_
- **Currency** — Omani rial (OMR)  _(Central Bank of Oman)_
- **Tax authority** — Oman Tax Authority (formerly Secretariat General for Taxation)  _(Oman Tax Authority — https://tms.taxoman.gov.om/portal/tax-rate)_
- **Personal income tax (currently)** — None for tax year 2025 — Oman does not currently levy tax on individuals' employment or personal income  _(Personal Income Tax Law (Royal Decree No. 56/2025) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/oman/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **Personal income tax (from 2028)** — 5% on annual gross income above OMR 42,000, effective 1 January 2028 % / OMR  _(Personal Income Tax Law (Royal Decree No. 56/2025) — https://kpmg.com/xx/en/our-insights/gms-flash-alert/flash-alert-2025-122.html)_
- **Headline corporate income tax rate** — 15% %  _(Income Tax Law (Royal Decree No. 28/2009) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/oman/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **VAT / GST** — Yes — VAT applies at a standard rate of 5% (in force since 16 April 2021)  _(VAT Law (Royal Decree No. 121/2020) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/oman/corporate/other-taxes)_
- **Corporate residence / tax basis** — Resident companies are taxed on worldwide income; non-residents are taxed on Oman-source income (including via a permanent establishment)  _(Income Tax Law (Royal Decree No. 28/2009) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/oman/corporate/corporate-residence)_
- **Corporate tax return deadline** — Within 4 months of the financial year-end (annual return) (approx — confirm; sources vary between 4 and 6 months — verify against current Executive Regulations)  _(Income Tax Law (Royal Decree No. 28/2009) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/oman/corporate/tax-administration)_
- **VAT return deadline** — Quarterly — within 30 days of the end of each calendar quarter  _(VAT Law (Royal Decree No. 121/2020) — https://www.flick.network/en-om/oman-vat-registration-compliance-guide)_
- **Pillar Two / Domestic Top-up Tax** — Oman introduced a top-up tax (Income Inclusion Rule) effective 1 January 2025 for in-scope multinational groups (approx — confirm scope thresholds)  _(Top-up Tax (Royal Decree No. 70/2024))_
