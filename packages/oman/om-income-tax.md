# Oman Personal Income Tax

## Personal income tax in Oman

For tax year 2025 Oman levies NO personal income tax on individuals — there are no bands, no thresholds and no individual filing obligation. A new 5% personal income tax has been enacted but does not take effect until 1 January 2028; the figures below describe that future regime.

- **Personal income tax for 2025** — None; the Personal Income Tax Law issued by Royal Decree 56/2025 applies from 1 January 2028, so individuals pay no tax on salaries, wages or other personal income for 2025 and file no individual return  _(Oman Tax Authority, Personal Income Tax Law and Regulation (Royal Decree 56/2025) — https://tms.taxoman.gov.om/portal/web/taxportal/personal-income-tax-law-and-regulation ; Royal Decree 56/2025 issuing the Personal Income Tax Law (Official Gazette 1602, Arabic), Tax Authority copy — https://tms.taxoman.gov.om/portal/documents/d/taxportal/-56_2025-1)_
- **Tax-free threshold (from 2028)** — OMR 42,000 of total annual income; tax and the filing obligation apply only where total income exceeds that amount  _(Oman Tax Authority, Personal Income Tax FAQs, Q1, Q9 and Q12 — https://tms.taxoman.gov.om/portal/web/taxportal/personal-income-tax-faqs)_
- **Personal income tax rate (from 2028)** — 5% on taxable income where total income exceeds OMR 42,000  _(Oman Tax Authority, Personal Income Tax FAQs, Q13 — https://tms.taxoman.gov.om/portal/web/taxportal/personal-income-tax-faqs)_
- **Scope for residents (from 2028)** — A tax resident's total income counts all cash amounts and in-kind benefits received in Oman or abroad; a non-resident's counts only amounts received in Oman; residents with total income above OMR 42,000 file a return  _(Oman Tax Authority, Personal Income Tax FAQs, Q4, Q9 and Q10 — https://tms.taxoman.gov.om/portal/web/taxportal/personal-income-tax-faqs)_
- **Individual residence test (from 2028)** — A person who stays in Oman for more than 183 days in the year, continuous or intermittent, is a tax resident whether Omani or not  _(Oman Tax Authority, Personal Income Tax FAQs, Q10 and Q11 — https://tms.taxoman.gov.om/portal/web/taxportal/personal-income-tax-faqs)_
- **Exempt income types (from 2028)** — Gain on sale of a primary residence, certain foreign salary, gifts, and inheritance are expected to be excluded (approx — confirm; detailed exemptions await Executive Regulations)  _([Personal Income Tax Law (Royal Decree No. 56/2025)](https://www.deloitte.com/middle-east/en/services/tax/perspectives/oman-personal-income-tax-law-issued.html))_
- **Deductions / reliefs (from 2028)** — Net income is total income less exemptions, costs and losses; foreign tax is creditable only where it relates to income taxable in Oman; a self-employed person may deduct 15% of gross self-employment income or documented actual expenses; the law's personal reliefs (education, healthcare, housing, zakat and donations) await the Executive Regulations  _(Oman Tax Authority, Personal Income Tax FAQs, Q5, Q7 and Q16 — https://tms.taxoman.gov.om/portal/web/taxportal/personal-income-tax-faqs ; Deloitte summary of the law — https://www.deloitte.com/middle-east/en/services/tax/perspectives/oman-personal-income-tax-law-issued.html)_
- **Executive Regulations timing — the deadline has passed** — The PIT Law was published in the Official Gazette on **30 June 2025**, so the Executive Regulations were due within twelve months, by **29 June 2026**. That date is behind us and **no Executive Regulations have been identified as issued as at September 2026**. Every "(approx — confirm; awaits Executive Regulations)" hedge above is therefore still live rather than about to resolve, and should not be read as a formality that has since been settled. The Oman Tax Authority also announced phased guidance manuals — **residency rules (Q4 2025)**, **deductions and exemptions (Q2 2026)** and **payroll withholding standards (Q4 2026)** — which are a separate track from the Regulations and may answer some of these points sooner. Check the Tax Authority portal for both before advising  _([Personal Income Tax Law (Royal Decree No. 56/2025), Official Gazette 30 June 2025](https://kpmg.com/xx/en/our-insights/gms-flash-alert/flash-alert-2025-122.html); Oman Tax Authority phased guidance schedule)_
- **Non-resident individual tax (2025)** — None in 2025; from 2028 a non-resident natural person is taxable only on income received in Oman, with employers withholding the tax  _(Oman Tax Authority, Personal Income Tax Law and Regulation (Royal Decree 56/2025) — https://tms.taxoman.gov.om/portal/web/taxportal/personal-income-tax-law-and-regulation ; Oman Tax Authority, Personal Income Tax FAQs, Q4 and Q8 — https://tms.taxoman.gov.om/portal/web/taxportal/personal-income-tax-faqs)_
- **Capital gains (individuals, 2025)** — No separate capital gains tax on individuals' personal investments in 2025 (approx — confirm)  _(Personal Income Tax Law (Royal Decree No. 56/2025), as reported `[RESEARCH GAP — the statute's text was not read for this change; the secondary summary that carried the figure is no longer cited]`)_
