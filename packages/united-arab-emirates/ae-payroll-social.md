# United Arab Emirates Payroll & Social Contributions

## Social security (GPSSA) and payroll

- **Overview of payroll tax and social security applicability** — There is no payroll/PAYE income tax in the UAE because there is no personal income tax. Mandatory social security (pension) contributions apply only to UAE and GCC national employees, administered by the General Pension and Social Security Authority (GPSSA); expatriate employees are not covered but are entitled to an end-of-service gratuity.
- **Payroll income tax (PAYE)** — None — no income tax is withheld from wages because the UAE has no personal income tax  _(The Official Portal of the UAE Government, Taxation — https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/finance-and-investment/taxation)_
- **Who social security covers** — Mandatory only for UAE national (and GCC national) employees; expatriate employees are not subject to social security contributions  _(Federal Law by Decree No. 57 of 2023 (Pension and Social Security), https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/jobs/working-in-uae-government-sector/pensions-and-social-security-for-uae-citizens)_
- **Total contribution rate (new members, from Oct 2023)** — 26% percent of the subscription account salary (11% insured plus 15% employer)  _(Federal Law by Decree No. 57 of 2023 Concerning Pension and Social Security, UAE Legislation portal, Art. 4 (1) — https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/2254 ; The Official Portal of the UAE Government, Pensions and social security for UAE citizens — https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/jobs/Sector-of-employment/working-in-uae-government-sector/pensions-and-social-security-for-uae-citizens)_
- **Employee share (new members)** — 11% percent of the subscription account salary, deducted by the employer  _(Federal Law by Decree No. 57 of 2023 Concerning Pension and Social Security, UAE Legislation portal, Art. 4 (1)(a) — https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/2254)_
- **Employer share (new members)** — 15% of the subscription account salary; for private-sector employers the government bears 2.5% of that share for national employees whose subscription account salary is below AED 20,000, so the employer pays 12.5%  _(Federal Law by Decree No. 57 of 2023 Concerning Pension and Social Security, UAE Legislation portal, Art. 4 (1)(b) — https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/2254 ; The Official Portal of the UAE Government, Pensions and social security for UAE citizens — https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/jobs/Sector-of-employment/working-in-uae-government-sector/pensions-and-social-security-for-uae-citizens)_
- **Total contribution rate (existing members, pre-Oct 2023)** — 20% percent of the subscription account salary (5% insured plus 15% employer)  _(Federal Law No. 7 of 1999 Promulgating the Pensions and Social Security Law, UAE Legislation portal, Art. 2 — https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/1111)_
- **Employee share (existing members)** — 5% percent of the subscription account salary  _(Federal Law No. 7 of 1999 Promulgating the Pensions and Social Security Law, UAE Legislation portal, Art. 2 — https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/1111)_
- **Employer share (existing members)** — 15% of the subscription account salary, of which the government bears 2.5% for private-sector employers (so the employer pays 12.5%)  _(Federal Law No. 7 of 1999 Promulgating the Pensions and Social Security Law, UAE Legislation portal, Art. 2 — https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/1111)_
- **Contribution salary cap (new members)** — AED 70,000 per month in the private sector, with an AED 3,000 floor; the limits may be revised as the Authority's finances allow  _(Federal Law by Decree No. 57 of 2023 Concerning Pension and Social Security, UAE Legislation portal, Art. 5 — https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/2254)_
- **Contribution salary cap (existing members)** — AED 50,000 per month in the private sector, with an AED 1,000 floor  _(Federal Law No. 7 of 1999 Promulgating the Pensions and Social Security Law, UAE Legislation portal, Art. 1 (subscription account salary) — https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/1111)_
- **Contribution remittance deadline** — Monthly — payable from the 1st to the 15th day of each month  _(Federal Law by Decree No. 57 of 2023 / GPSSA contribution rules, https://gpssa.gov.ae/pages/en/media-center/news/gpssa-insureds-contribution-payment-may-be-extended-15th-day-each-month)_
- **End-of-service gratuity (expatriates)** — 21 days' basic wage per year for the first 5 years of service, and 30 days' basic wage per year thereafter (capped at 2 years' total wage)  _(Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 (UAE Labour Law), Art. 51)_
