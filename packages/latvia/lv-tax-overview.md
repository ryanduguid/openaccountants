# Latvia Tax Overview

## The Latvian tax system at a glance

Latvia is an EU member state with a euro-denominated tax system administered by the State Revenue Service (Valsts ieņēmumu dienests, VID). Personal income tax became progressive again from 2025 and corporate tax uses a distribution-based model.

- **Tax year** — Calendar year (1 January to 31 December) for personal income tax; corporate income tax is assessed by calendar month  _(Law On Personal Income Tax (Par iedzīvotāju ienākuma nodokli), consolidated text on likumi.lv, 2. pants — https://likumi.lv/ta/id/56880#p2 ; Corporate Income Tax Law (Uzņēmumu ienākuma nodokļa likums), consolidated text on likumi.lv, 3. pants (2) — https://likumi.lv/ta/id/292700#p3)_
- **Currency** — Euro (EUR)  _(Law On Taxes and Duties (Par nodokļiem un nodevām))_
- **Tax authority** — State Revenue Service (Valsts ieņēmumu dienests, VID); electronic filing via the EDS system  _(Law On the State Revenue Service (Par Valsts ieņēmumu dienestu) — https://www.vid.gov.lv/en)_
- **Residence basis of taxation** — Residents (declared place of residence in Latvia, or presence of 183 days or more in any 12-month period) are taxed on worldwide income; non-residents are taxed only on Latvian-source income  _(Law On Taxes and Fees (Par nodokļiem un nodevām), consolidated text on likumi.lv, 14. pants (2) — https://likumi.lv/ta/id/33946#p14 ; Law On Personal Income Tax (Par iedzīvotāju ienākuma nodokli), consolidated text on likumi.lv, 2. pants — https://likumi.lv/ta/id/56880#p2)_
- **Headline personal income tax rates (2025)** — 25.5% up to EUR 105,300; 33% above EUR 105,300; plus an additional 3% on total annual income over EUR 200,000  _(Law On Personal Income Tax (Par iedzīvotāju ienākuma nodokli) — https://www.vid.gov.lv/en/personal-income-tax-rates)_
- **Headline corporate income tax** — 20% of the taxable base, which is the distributed profit divided by 0.8 (20/80 of the net distribution, an effective 25% rate); 0% while profits are retained or reinvested  _(Corporate Income Tax Law (Uzņēmumu ienākuma nodokļa likums), consolidated text on likumi.lv, 3. pants (1) and 4. pants (9) — https://likumi.lv/ta/id/292700#p3)_
- **Does VAT/GST exist?** — Yes — VAT (pievienotās vērtības nodoklis, PVN) at a standard rate of 21%  _(Value Added Tax Law (Pievienotās vērtības nodokļa likums), consolidated text on likumi.lv, 41. pants (1) — https://likumi.lv/ta/id/253451#p41)_
- **Annual personal income declaration deadline** — Generally by 1 June of the following year (by 1 July if total annual income exceeds EUR 78,100); refund-only declarations may be filed within 3 years (approx — confirm)  _(Law On Personal Income Tax (Par iedzīvotāju ienākuma nodokli) — https://www.vid.gov.lv/en/due-dates-filing-returns-and-paying-taxes)_
- **Corporate income tax return deadline** — Monthly, by the 20th day of the following month where a tax base arises; tax paid by the 23rd  _(Corporate Income Tax Law (Uzņēmumu ienākuma nodokļa likums) — https://www.vid.gov.lv/en/corporate-income-tax)_
- **Annual financial statements deadline** — Micro and small companies file the approved annual report with the State Revenue Service within five months of the financial year end; medium and large companies, and parents preparing consolidated accounts, within seven months  _(Law On the Annual Financial Statements and Consolidated Financial Statements (Gada pārskatu un konsolidēto gada pārskatu likums), consolidated text on likumi.lv, 97. pants (1) — https://likumi.lv/ta/id/277779#p97)_
