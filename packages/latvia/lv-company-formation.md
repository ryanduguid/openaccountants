# Latvia Company Formation & Entity Choice

## Entity types and incorporation

- **Common entity types** — SIA (private limited liability company), AS (public stock company), individual merchant (IK), partnerships, and branches of foreign companies  _(Commercial Law (Komerclikums) — https://www.ur.gov.lv/en/register/company-or-merchant/limited-liability-company-sia/)_
- **SIA minimum share capital (standard)** — EUR 2,800 EUR  _(Commercial Law (Komerclikums), consolidated text on likumi.lv, 185. pants — https://likumi.lv/ta/id/5490#p185)_
- **Reduced-capital (micro) SIA minimum capital** — From EUR 1: the capital may be below EUR 2,800 where the founders and shareholders are natural persons (at most five), every board member is a shareholder and each shareholder holds shares in only one such company; the company must set aside at least 25% of each year's profit as a mandatory reserve EUR  _(Commercial Law (Komerclikums), consolidated text on likumi.lv, 185.1 pants — https://likumi.lv/ta/id/5490#p185)_
- **Registration authority** — Enterprise Register of the Republic of Latvia (Uzņēmumu reģistrs)  _(Commercial Law (Komerclikums) — https://www.ur.gov.lv/en/)_
- **Core incorporation steps** — Reserve company name; prepare founding documents and articles of association; deposit share capital; submit the application to the Enterprise Register; register with VID (and for VAT/employees as needed)  _(Commercial Law (Komerclikums) — https://www.ur.gov.lv/en/register/company-or-merchant/limited-liability-company-sia/founding/)_
- **Typical incorporation timeline** — The Enterprise Register official decides on the entry, a refusal or a deferral within three working days of receiving the application, and sends the decision within a further three working days  _(Law On the Enterprise Register of the Republic of Latvia (Par Latvijas Republikas Uzņēmumu reģistru), consolidated text on likumi.lv, 8. pants — https://likumi.lv/ta/id/72847#p8)_
- **State registration fee** — Standard state fee plus official publication fee (a few tens of euros); expedited registration available for a higher fee ((approx — confirm current amounts)) (approx — confirm current amounts)  _(Commercial Law (Komerclikums) — https://www.ur.gov.lv/en/register/company-or-merchant/limited-liability-company-sia/founding/)_
- **VAT registration on or after formation** — Required once taxable turnover exceeds EUR 50,000 in a calendar year; voluntary registration available earlier EUR  _(Value Added Tax Law (Pievienotās vērtības nodokļa likums) — https://www.ur.gov.lv/en/register/company-or-merchant/limited-liability-company-sia/founding/registration-with-the-srs/)_
- **Core annual compliance** — File the annual report with the State Revenue Service (within five months of year end for micro and small companies, seven for medium and large); keep accounting records; file the monthly CIT declaration by the 20th of the following month for months with a taxable base and always for the last month of the financial year, and VAT returns where registered; file the monthly employer report on contributions by the 17th  _(Law On the Annual Financial Statements and Consolidated Financial Statements (Gada pārskatu un konsolidēto gada pārskatu likums), consolidated text on likumi.lv, 97. pants (1) — https://likumi.lv/ta/id/277779#p97 ; Corporate Income Tax Law (Uzņēmumu ienākuma nodokļa likums), consolidated text on likumi.lv, 17. pants (1) and (3) — https://likumi.lv/ta/id/292700#p17 ; Law On State Social Insurance (Par valsts sociālo apdrošināšanu), consolidated text on likumi.lv, 23. pants (1) — https://likumi.lv/ta/id/45466#p23)_
- **Micro-enterprise tax regime** — Optional micro-enterprise tax of 25% on turnover, open to individual merchants, individual undertakings, farm or fishing enterprises and natural persons registered with the State Revenue Service as economic operators that are not registered for VAT; the definition of a micro-enterprise no longer includes limited liability companies %  _(Micro-enterprise Tax Law (Mikrouzņēmumu nodokļa likums), consolidated text on likumi.lv, 6. pants and 1. pants 3) punkts — https://likumi.lv/ta/id/215302#p6)_
