# Guinea Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax (Impôt sur les Sociétés)

Guinea taxes company profits under the Impôt sur les Sociétés (IS), with the base derived from OHADA/SYSCOHADA financial statements. A minimum tax applies where the IS computed on profit is low.

> **The authority publishes its own rate table, and this guide was missing a rate.**
> The Direction Nationale des Impôts publishes *Système Fiscal Guinéen au 31 mars
> 2021*, a 32-row table of every Guinean tax with the rate and the CGI article for
> each, and the DGI publishes the CGI's Impôt sur les Sociétés chapter in full.
> Read together they **confirm** the 25% and 30% rows below and supply one the guide
> did not have at all: **telecommunications companies, banks, insurers and petroleum
> companies pay 35%**, ten points above the rate this guide called "standard".
>
> **Two DGI documents, and the later one governs rates.** The chapter reproductions
> are headed *"Source : Code Général des Impôts, Edition 2015"* and carry the DGI's
> own warning that *"certaines dispositions des articles ont fait l'objet de
> modification"* directing readers to the 2018 and 2019 finance laws. The IS chapter
> accordingly still shows the **pre-2018 flat 35%**; the 2021 rate table shows the
> three rates LF 2018 put in its place. Where they differ on a rate, the table is the
> later statement and is followed here. **Loi de finances 2025 amends neither article
> 229 (rates) nor article 244 (minimum tax)**, so the 2021 table is not displaced.
>
> _(Direction Nationale des Impôts, [Système Fiscal Guinéen au 31 mars 2021](https://dgi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/Syst%C3%A8me-Fiscal-Guin%C3%A9en-mars-2021.pdf); DGI, [Impôt sur les Sociétés](https://dgi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/IMPOT-SUR-LES-SOCIETES.pdf))_

- **⚠ Corporate income tax rate — telecoms, banks, insurance and petroleum** — 35% of taxable profit. The authority's rate table lists this as a separate line from the general rate, referred to **article 229 CGI (LF 2018)**. This guide previously gave one "standard" rate of 25% and did not mention the 35% line at all percent  _([Système Fiscal Guinéen au 31 mars 2021, row 1 — art. 229 CGI (LF 2018)](https://dgi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/Syst%C3%A8me-Fiscal-Guin%C3%A9en-mars-2021.pdf))_
- **Corporate income tax rate — mining companies** — 30% of taxable profit. The authority refers this to **article 229 CGI (LF 2018)**, not to the Mining Code as this guide previously did; the figure itself is confirmed percent  _([Système Fiscal Guinéen au 31 mars 2021, row 1 (*Impôt sur sociétés minières*) — art. 229 CGI (LF 2018)](https://dgi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/Syst%C3%A8me-Fiscal-Guin%C3%A9en-mars-2021.pdf))_
- **Corporate income tax rate — all other companies** — 25% of taxable profit (*Impôt sur autres sociétés*), the residual rate once the 35% and 30% lines are taken out percent  _([Système Fiscal Guinéen au 31 mars 2021, row 2 — art. 229 CGI (LF 2018)](https://dgi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/Syst%C3%A8me-Fiscal-Guin%C3%A9en-mars-2021.pdf))_
- **Rounding of the base** — Any fraction of taxable profit below **GNF 1,000** is disregarded in computing the tax  _([DGI, Impôt sur les Sociétés, *Section II : Taux de l'impôt*](https://dgi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/IMPOT-SUR-LES-SOCIETES.pdf))_
- **⚠ Minimum tax (Impôt Minimum Forfaitaire) — 3%, but on prior-year turnover and inside a floor and a cap** — The rate is **3% of chiffre d'affaires**, and the authority's table gives the base as **turnover of the preceding year (N-1)**, not the current year. It is bracketed at both ends, by taxpayer service: medium enterprises **minimum GNF 15,000,000 / maximum GNF 45,000,000**; large enterprises **minimum GNF 75,000,000 / maximum GNF 100,000,000**. The guide gave the 3% alone. Referred to **article 244 CGI (LF 2017, 2018 and 2019)**  _([Système Fiscal Guinéen au 31 mars 2021, row 3 — art. 244 CGI](https://dgi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/Syst%C3%A8me-Fiscal-Guin%C3%A9en-mars-2021.pdf))_
- **The minimum tax is a credit, not an addition** — The *imposition forfaitaire annuelle* of article 244 **is creditable against the IS due for the year in which it falls due**, and the balance of IS is settled net of it. So it operates as a floor on the year's liability rather than a separate charge  _([DGI, Impôt sur les Sociétés, *Section III : Imputation d'impôts acquittés* and *Section V-III*](https://dgi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/IMPOT-SUR-LES-SOCIETES.pdf))_
- **Tax base** — Net accounting profit per OHADA/SYSCOHADA accounts, adjusted for non-deductible items and tax rules  _(OHADA Uniform Act on Accounting Law (SYSCOHADA); Code Général des Impôts (CGI) (as described at [icaew.com](https://www.icaew.com/technical/by-country/africa/guinea/tax-in-guinea)))_
- **Withholding tax on dividends** — 10%. The authority's table puts *revenu des actions* and *revenu des parts sociales* inside the **Impôt sur les revenus des capitaux mobiliers (IRCM)** at 10%, referred to **article 170 CGI** percent (before treaty relief)  _([Système Fiscal Guinéen au 31 mars 2021, row 7 — art. 170 CGI](https://dgi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/Syst%C3%A8me-Fiscal-Guin%C3%A9en-mars-2021.pdf))_
- **Withholding tax on interest** — 10%. Same IRCM head at **article 170 CGI**, covering fixed-return placements: *revenus de créances, dépôts, cautionnements, comptes courants* and *revenus de bons de caisse* percent (before treaty relief)  _([Système Fiscal Guinéen au 31 mars 2021, row 7 — art. 170 CGI](https://dgi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/Syst%C3%A8me-Fiscal-Guin%C3%A9en-mars-2021.pdf))_
- **Withholding tax on royalties (to non-residents)** — 15%, and the provision is **article 198 CGI**. It applies where a debtor established in Guinea pays a person or company with **no permanent professional establishment in Guinea**, and reaches three separate limbs: (a) liberal-profession fees for activity carried on in Guinea; (b) **sums received by inventors, or by way of copyright or similar rights, and all products of industrial or commercial property or similar rights**; and (c) **services of any kind materially supplied or effectively used in Guinea**. Royalties sit in limb (b). Rate **15% of the gross amount** (LF 1992 art. 18; LF 2012 art. 23) percent (before treaty relief)  _([DGI, Taxes sur les Salaires, *Retenue à la source sur certains revenus non salariaux* — art. 198 CGI](https://dgi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/TAXES-SUR-LES-SALAIRES.pdf))_
- **The article 198 withholding is creditable but never refundable** — It is credited against the income tax or corporate tax assessed on the recipient of the sums; *"l'excédent éventuel de retenue n'est pas restituable"*. It is operated by the payer and remitted to the Treasury with a *bordereau-avis* **by the 15th of the month following payment**; a payer who fails to withhold, or knowingly under-withholds, incurs **a fine equal to the amount not withheld** and remains liable for the amount itself  _([DGI, Taxes sur les Salaires — art. 198 CGI](https://dgi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/TAXES-SUR-LES-SALAIRES.pdf))_
- **⚠ Capital gains — the "around 15%" figure does not correspond to any Guinean gains rate** — The authority's table places **plus-values de cession d'actions** inside IRCM at **10%** (article 170), alongside dividends. Business-asset gains form part of taxable profit and bear the ordinary IS rate. The two 15% rates in the table are the article 198 withholding on non-resident payments and the article 268 withholding on rents — neither is a capital gains rate. This guide's "standalone rate around 15% cited for certain gains" is withdrawn  _([Système Fiscal Guinéen au 31 mars 2021, rows 5–7 — arts. 170, 198, 268 CGI](https://dgi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/Syst%C3%A8me-Fiscal-Guin%C3%A9en-mars-2021.pdf))_
- **Merger relief** — Gains other than on trading stock, arising from the free allotment of shares on a merger of companies limited by shares or by guarantee, are **exempt from IS**. Relief is conditional on the receiving company being **of Guinean nationality** and on an undertaking in the merger deed to compute future depreciation and gains on the **transferor's original cost**, less depreciation already taken  _([DGI, Impôt sur les Sociétés, *Fusions de sociétés et opérations assimilées*](https://dgi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/IMPOT-SUR-LES-SOCIETES.pdf))_
- **Parent–subsidiary regime** — Net income from a parent's participation in a subsidiary is deducted from total net profit, less a **flat 5% quote-part for costs and charges**, itself capped at the parent's actual costs for the period. Conditions: both companies are limited by shares or by guarantee; the parent has its **registered office in Guinea** and is subject to IS; the holding is in registered form or deposited with an approved institution and represents **at least 10% of the subsidiary's capital**; and the shares were **subscribed at issue** (no minimum percentage applies to shares received for partial contributions admitted to the merger regime)  _([DGI, Impôt sur les Sociétés, *Régime des sociétés mères et filiales*](https://dgi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/IMPOT-SUR-LES-SOCIETES.pdf))_
- **Annual IS return filing deadline — 30 April, and now electronic** — The IS chapter fixes the profit-or-loss return at **"au plus tard le 30 avril de chaque année"**, and **article 142.I CGI as re-enacted by loi de finances 2025, article 24**, requires the annual return to be filed **electronically on the e-Tax/SAFIG platform by 30 April**, with the accountant's visa dematerialised through the same platform. The guide's "(approx — confirm)" is resolved, and filing on paper is no longer contemplated  _([DGI, Impôt sur les Sociétés, *Déclaration de bénéfices ou de déficit*](https://dgi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/IMPOT-SUR-LES-SOCIETES.pdf); [Loi de finances 2025, art. 24 — art. 142.I CGI](https://dgi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/LOI-DE-FINANCES-INITIALE-2025_compressed.pdf))_
- **⚠ Payment of corporate tax — two instalments of one third each, 15 June and 15 September** — IS is paid by **two acomptes**, each rounded down to the nearest thousand Guinean francs and each equal to **one third of the IS due on the last assessed year's results** — so two thirds of the reference year's tax falls due in advance and the balance on filing. The instalment dates are **15 June and 15 September** (LFI 2015). **New companies are exempt from the instalments for the first twelve months** of activity, whenever in the calendar year they were formed and whatever the length of their first period. The balance is settled by the return deadline, net of the instalments and of the minimum tax for the same year  _([DGI, Impôt sur les Sociétés, *Section V : Modalités de paiement de l'impôt*](https://dgi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/IMPOT-SUR-LES-SOCIETES.pdf))_
- **Relief from the second instalment must be applied for in advance** — A company expecting an instalment to equal or exceed its final liability may seek a reduction or waiver **of the second instalment only**, by reasoned request to the Directeur National des Impôts **at least thirty days before the instalment falls due**; he acknowledges receipt and answers within 30 days (LF 1992, art. 21)  _([DGI, Impôt sur les Sociétés, *Section V-IV*](https://dgi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/IMPOT-SUR-LES-SOCIETES.pdf))_
- **Late payment — a 10% surcharge and 1% a month, with any month begun counted whole** — Instalments not paid in full by the prescribed dates attract a **10% majoration**. Separately, and without prejudice to assessment penalties, any shortfall, delay or omission in payment bears **late interest of 1% per month**, and *"tout mois commencé est considéré comme entier"* — a single day's delay costs a full month (LF 1992, art. 18)  _([DGI, Impôt sur les Sociétés, *Section V-V* and *Section VI : Recouvrement*](https://dgi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/IMPOT-SUR-LES-SOCIETES.pdf))_
- **Cessation or disposal — fifteen days** — On a disposal or cessation of business the results return must be produced **within fifteen days** of the effective date, and IS on profits not yet assessed, **including gains**, is charged immediately  _([DGI, Impôt sur les Sociétés, *Cession ou cessation d'activité*](https://dgi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/IMPOT-SUR-LES-SOCIETES.pdf))_
- **Foreign tax credits are lost, not carried, in a loss year** — Credits attaching to investment income included in the results of a **loss-making period** *"ne peuvent être imputés, ni reportés, ni restitués et tombent donc en non-valeur"*. Parent companies cannot claim the credit on subsidiary dividends already deducted under the participation regime  _([DGI, Impôt sur les Sociétés, *Section III : Imputation d'impôts acquittés*](https://dgi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/IMPOT-SUR-LES-SOCIETES.pdf))_
