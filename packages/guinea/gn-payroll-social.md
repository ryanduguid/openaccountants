# Guinea Payroll & Social Contributions

## Social security (CNSS) and payroll withholding

Employers in Guinea register with the Caisse Nationale de Sécurité Sociale (CNSS) and withhold both the employee social-security share and IRPP at source. The exact branch split and salary ceiling vary by regulation and are flagged for confirmation.

- **Total employer social-security contribution** — 18% of gross salary (CNSS branches combined) percent (approx — confirm)  _(Code de la Sécurité Sociale — Caisse Nationale de Sécurité Sociale (CNSS) (https://tradingeconomics.com/guinea/social-security-rate))_
- **Total employee social-security contribution** — 5% of gross salary percent (approx — confirm)  _(Code de la Sécurité Sociale — Caisse Nationale de Sécurité Sociale (CNSS) (https://tradingeconomics.com/guinea/social-security-rate-for-employees))_
- **Employee pension (old-age) share** — 2.5% of gross salary percent (approx — confirm)  _(Code de la Sécurité Sociale — Caisse Nationale de Sécurité Sociale (CNSS))_
- **Employer pension (old-age) share** — 4% of gross salary percent (approx — confirm)  _(Code de la Sécurité Sociale — Caisse Nationale de Sécurité Sociale (CNSS))_
- **Employer health/medical branch share** — Around 6.5% of gross salary percent (approx — confirm)  _(Code de la Sécurité Sociale — Caisse Nationale de Sécurité Sociale (CNSS))_
- **Employer family allowances + work-injury share** — Around 6% + 4% of gross salary (family allowances and occupational risk) percent (approx — confirm)  _(Code de la Sécurité Sociale — Caisse Nationale de Sécurité Sociale (CNSS))_
- **Monthly contribution ceiling (salary cap)** — GNF 2,500,000 per month (cap on the wage base for CNSS contributions) GNF (approx — confirm)  _(Code de la Sécurité Sociale — Caisse Nationale de Sécurité Sociale (CNSS))_
- **Payroll income-tax withholding (PAYE-equivalent)** — Employer withholds tax from salaries at source under the **retenue sur traitements et salaires (RTS)** scale of **article 63 CGI (LF 2019)**. The authority's own table gives five bands: **0%** to GNF 1,000,000; **5%** from 1,000,001 to 5,000,000; **10%** from 5,000,001 to 10,000,000; **15%** from 10,000,001 to 20,000,000; and **20%** above 20,000,000. ⚠ This scale **conflicts on every row** with the one in `gn-income-tax`, which is commercially sourced — see that guide  _([Système Fiscal Guinéen au 31 mars 2021, row 4 — art. 63 CGI (LF 2019)](https://dgi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/Syst%C3%A8me-Fiscal-Guin%C3%A9en-mars-2021.pdf))_
- **⚠ Remittance of withheld tax — within ten days of the following month, not the 15th** — **Article 72.I CGI, as re-enacted by loi de finances 2025, article 13**: withholdings on payments made in a given month must be paid **"dans les dix (10) jours du mois suivant"** to the Receveur Spécial des Impôts or the Trésor public for the place of the employer's establishment or the payer's domicile. This guide's "generally by the 15th" was five days late. **The 15th is a real Guinean deadline, but for other levies** — the article 198 non-resident withholding and the 10% insurance-commission withholding are both remitted by the 15th of the following month, which is a likely source of the confusion  _([Loi de finances 2025, art. 13 — art. 72.I CGI](https://dgi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/LOI-DE-FINANCES-INITIALE-2025_compressed.pdf); [DGI, Taxes sur les Salaires](https://dgi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/TAXES-SUR-LES-SALAIRES.pdf))_
- **⚠ Employer payroll levy — versement forfaitaire, 6% of the total payroll** — Missing from this guide entirely. Beyond their obligations under articles 61 and 71–78, individuals and companies employing salaried staff in Guinea must pay the National Budget **6% of the global amount of salaries, wages, allowances and emoluments actually paid to the whole staff**, **including benefits in cash and in kind**, after deducting family-allowance contributions where applicable. Benefits in kind are valued under article 59 (LF 1994, art. 21). It is paid to the tax accountant monthly or quarterly under articles 72–74, on a *bordereau-avis* **separate from the one used for withholdings**, and is followed by a *rôle de régularisation*. This is an employer cost **on top of** CNSS, referred by the authority to **article 201 CGI**  _([DGI, Taxes sur les Salaires, *Versements forfaitaires sur les salaires*](https://dgi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/TAXES-SUR-LES-SALAIRES.pdf); [Système Fiscal Guinéen au 31 mars 2021, row 12 — art. 201 CGI](https://dgi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/Syst%C3%A8me-Fiscal-Guin%C3%A9en-mars-2021.pdf))_
- **⚠ Employer payroll levy — apprenticeship tax, 3% of gross payroll** — Also missing from this guide. The *taxe d'apprentissage* is charged at **3%** on the gross amount of salaries, allowances and emoluments actually paid, referred to **article 204 CGI**. With the versement forfaitaire this is **9% of payroll in taxes alone**, before any social-security contribution  _([Système Fiscal Guinéen au 31 mars 2021, row 13 — art. 204 CGI](https://dgi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/Syst%C3%A8me-Fiscal-Guin%C3%A9en-mars-2021.pdf))_
- **A payer who fails to withhold pays twice over** — A payer who does not operate a required withholding, or who knowingly withholds too little, incurs **a fine equal to the amount not withheld** and **remains liable for the withholding itself**, which may be assessed on them personally by *voie de rôle*  _([DGI, Taxes sur les Salaires — art. 198 CGI](https://dgi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/TAXES-SUR-LES-SALAIRES.pdf))_
- **Contribution wage base** — Gross salary including most cash benefits, up to the monthly ceiling (approx — confirm)  _(Code de la Sécurité Sociale — Caisse Nationale de Sécurité Sociale (CNSS))_

### The CNSS rows above remain unverified

> Every social-security row in this guide rests on a single commercial macro-data
> site. The **Code de la Sécurité Sociale** was not reached: the CNSS is a separate
> body from the DGI and does not publish its contribution schedule on
> `dgi.gov.gn`. The tax rows above are now sourced to the authority; **the CNSS
> rates, branch splits and the GNF 2,500,000 ceiling are not**, and keep their
> "(approx — confirm)" marks. [RESEARCH GAP — a reviewer should check the branch
> rates and the ceiling against the Code de la Sécurité Sociale or a current CNSS
> circular.]
