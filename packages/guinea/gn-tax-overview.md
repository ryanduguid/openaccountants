# Guinea Tax Overview

## Guinea tax system at a glance

Guinea (Conakry) operates a French-style tax system administered by the national tax directorate, with taxes assessed in Guinean Francs over a calendar tax year. This is an unverified source-cited draft pending review by a licensed Guinea accountant.

- **Tax year** — 1 January to 31 December (calendar year)  _([Code Général des Impôts (CGI) de Guinée](https://taxatlas.io/country/guinea))_
- **Currency** — Guinean Franc (GNF)  _([Code Général des Impôts (CGI) de Guinée](https://taxatlas.io/country/guinea))_
- **Tax authority** — Direction Nationale des Impôts (DNI), Ministère du Budget  _([Code Général des Impôts (CGI) de Guinée](https://taxatlas.io/country/guinea))_
- **Residence / taxation basis** — Residents are taxed on worldwide income; non-residents on Guinea-source income only ((approx — confirm))  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI) de Guinée)_
- **Top personal income tax (IRPP) rate** — 35 percent  _([Code Général des Impôts (CGI) — Impôt sur le Revenu des Personnes Physiques (IRPP)](https://taxatlas.io/country/guinea))_
- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 25 percent  _([Code Général des Impôts (CGI) — Impôt sur les Sociétés (IS); Loi de Finances 2018](https://www.icaew.com/technical/by-country/africa/guinea/tax-in-guinea))_
- **VAT / GST — Value Added Tax (TVA) standard rate** — 18 percent (Yes — Value Added Tax (TVA) applies at a standard rate)  _([Code Général des Impôts (CGI) — Taxe sur la Valeur Ajoutée (TVA)](https://quaderno.io/guides/guinea-vat-guide/))_
- **Annual return filing deadline** — 31 March of the year following the tax year ((approx — confirm))  _([Code Général des Impôts (CGI) de Guinée](https://taxatlas.io/country/guinea))_
- **VAT (TVA) return frequency** — Monthly — filed and paid within 15 days after month-end  _([Code Général des Impôts (CGI) — Taxe sur la Valeur Ajoutée (TVA)](https://quaderno.io/guides/guinea-vat-guide/))_
- **Accounting framework for the tax base** — OHADA SYSCOHADA Uniform Act on Accounting Law  _([OHADA Uniform Act on Accounting Law (SYSCOHADA)](https://www.icaew.com/technical/by-country/africa/guinea/tax-in-guinea))_
