# Guinea Tax Overview

## Guinea tax system at a glance

Guinea (Conakry) operates a French-style tax system administered by the national tax directorate, with taxes assessed in Guinean Francs over a calendar tax year. This is an unverified source-cited draft pending review by a licensed Guinea accountant.

- **Tax year** — 1 January to 31 December (calendar year)  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI) de Guinée (as described at [taxatlas.io](https://taxatlas.io/country/guinea)))_
- **Currency** — Guinean Franc (GNF)  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI) de Guinée (as described at [taxatlas.io](https://taxatlas.io/country/guinea)))_
- **Tax authority — now the Direction Générale des Impôts (DGI)** — The body this guide called the *Direction Nationale des Impôts (DNI)* publishes under the name **Direction Générale des Impôts** and is referred to as such in **loi de finances 2025, article 12**. DNI was the correct name as recently as the authority's own March 2021 rate table, whose letterhead reads *Ministère du Budget — Direction Nationale des Impôts*; the name has since changed and older citations keep the old form  _([Loi de finances 2025, art. 12](https://dgi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/LOI-DE-FINANCES-INITIALE-2025_compressed.pdf); [dgi.gov.gn](https://dgi.gov.gn/))_
- **Residence / taxation basis** — Residents are taxed on worldwide income; non-residents on Guinea-source income only ((approx — confirm))  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI) de Guinée)_
- **⚠ Top personal income tax rate — the authority's salary scale tops at 20%, not 35%** — The DNI rate table gives the **retenue sur traitements et salaires (RTS)** under **article 63 CGI (LF 2019)** as five bands topping at **20% above GNF 20,000,000**. No band in the authority's table is 35%. The 35% carried here, and the whole scale in `gn-income-tax`, come from a commercial source and match the authority's scale on no row. See that guide for the conflict in full; the figure is **not** restated as fact here percent (disputed — see gn-income-tax)  _([Système Fiscal Guinéen au 31 mars 2021, row 4 — art. 63 CGI (LF 2019)](https://dgi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/Syst%C3%A8me-Fiscal-Guin%C3%A9en-mars-2021.pdf))_
- **⚠ Corporate income tax — three rates, not one** — **35%** for telecommunications companies, banks, insurers and petroleum companies; **30%** for mining companies; **25%** for all others. All three are referred by the authority to **article 229 CGI (LF 2018)**. Describing 25% as *the* standard rate understates the charge on the financial, telecoms, petroleum and mining sectors percent  _([Système Fiscal Guinéen au 31 mars 2021, rows 1–2 — art. 229 CGI (LF 2018)](https://dgi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/Syst%C3%A8me-Fiscal-Guin%C3%A9en-mars-2021.pdf))_
- **VAT / GST — Value Added Tax (TVA) standard rate** — 18 percent, with **0% on exports and on international transport**, referred to **article 373 CGI (LF 2016 and 2017)** (Yes — Value Added Tax (TVA) applies at a standard rate)  _([Système Fiscal Guinéen au 31 mars 2021, row 15 — art. 373 CGI](https://dgi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/Syst%C3%A8me-Fiscal-Guin%C3%A9en-mars-2021.pdf))_
- **⚠ Annual return filing deadline — 30 April, not 31 March** — **Article 142.I CGI, as re-enacted by loi de finances 2025, article 24**, requires the annual return to be filed electronically on e-Tax/SAFIG **"au plus tard le 30 avril"**, and the CGI's IS chapter fixes the profit-or-loss return at the same date. The 31 March given here was unsourced beyond a commercial site and **contradicted this repository's own `gn-corporate-income-tax`**, which already said 30 April  _([Loi de finances 2025, art. 24 — art. 142.I CGI](https://dgi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/LOI-DE-FINANCES-INITIALE-2025_compressed.pdf))_
- **⚠ VAT (TVA) return frequency — monthly, by the 10th, not within 15 days** — **Article 373 sexies I CGI, as re-enacted by loi de finances 2025, article 13**, requires taxable persons outside the article 359 franchise to file a VAT return with their assessing office **"au plus tard le 10 de chaque mois"**. The "within 15 days after month-end" stated here gave five days that do not exist  _([Loi de finances 2025, art. 13 — art. 373 sexies I CGI](https://dgi.gov.gn/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/LOI-DE-FINANCES-INITIALE-2025_compressed.pdf))_
- **Accounting framework for the tax base** — OHADA SYSCOHADA Uniform Act on Accounting Law  _(OHADA Uniform Act on Accounting Law (SYSCOHADA) (as described at [icaew.com](https://www.icaew.com/technical/by-country/africa/guinea/tax-in-guinea)))_
