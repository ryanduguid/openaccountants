# Egypt tax system

> General orientation as at 21 November 2026. Confirm the legal provision, tax period and taxpayer facts with an Egyptian tax professional before applying these rules.

The Egyptian Tax Authority (ETA) administers income tax and VAT. Amounts below use Egyptian pounds (EGP). Identify the taxpayer, the relevant tax period and the nature and source of the income before selecting a return or rate. [ETA income tax laws](https://www.eta.gov.eg/ar/content/qwanyn-aldrybt-ly-aldkhl) · [ETA VAT laws](https://www.eta.gov.eg/ar/content/qwanyn-aldrybt-ly-alqymt-almdaft)

## Income tax

- **Residence and source.** Under article 2 of Law 91/2005, individual residence can arise through a permanent home in Egypt, presence exceeding 183 days in a 12-month period, whether continuous or intermittent, or specified Egyptian-paid employment abroad. A legal person can be resident through Egyptian incorporation, its principal or effective centre of management in Egypt or the specified public majority-ownership test. For individuals, check Egyptian source and the rules for income from abroad where Egypt is the centre of a trade or profession. Resident legal persons generally include profits from Egypt and abroad. A foreign company's Egyptian permanent establishment has a separate profits and attribution analysis. Check treaty residence and relief by case. [Law 91/2005 arts 2 and 6](https://www.eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2024-03/law_no.91-2005.pdf) · [Law 30/2023, including amended arts 3 and 4](https://eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2024-03/law_no.30-2023.pdf) · [ETA guidance on the income base](https://eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2022-03/mohtawa2_2.pdf)
- **Individuals.** Use the income-level columns of amended article 8. The ordinary progressive schedule includes 0%, 10%, 15%, 20%, 22.5%, 25% and 27.5%, with the top rate applying to the excess above EGP 1.2 million in the highest income column. Lower bands drop out at specified total income levels. The separate EGP 20,000 exemption in article 13(1) applies in the salaries chapter. Law 7/2024 has a separate commencement for salaries from 1 March 2024 and applies to the specified business, professional and real-estate income starting with the tax period ending after publication on 21 February 2024. [Law 7/2024](https://www.eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2024-03/law_no.7-2024.pdf)
- **Companies.** The ordinary rate is 22.5% of annual net taxable profit under amended article 49. Check special industries, incentives, permanent establishment attribution and withholding independently. Law 151/2026 generally commenced on 29 July 2026. It changes selected bad-debt, investment, distribution, financing and property-disposal rules. Check the corrected text, the relevant period, applicable regulations and any transition before relying on those amendments. For example, the August corrigendum puts the property-disposal amendment in article 42 and changes the start year in the specified past-period assessment rule in article 4 of Law 151/2026 from 2022 to 2023. [Law 96/2015](https://www.eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2024-03/law_no.96-2015.pdf) · [Law 151/2026](https://eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2026-08/law.no_.151.of_.2026.pdf) · [8 August corrigendum](https://eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2026-08/law.no_.149.150.151.of_.2026.retractation.pdf)
- **Simplified regime.** From 1 March 2025 qualifying businesses with annual turnover up to EGP 20 million may apply under Law 6/2025. The income tax rates of 0.4% to 1.5% apply to turnover in the applicable band. Conditions include timely returns and compliance with the relevant electronic invoicing or receipt phase. The law excludes specified concentrated professional consultancies and artificial fragmentation. It has a limited one-time excess-turnover rule. Check that regime's separate forms and periods. [Law 6/2025 arts 1–4, 10, 12 and 16](https://eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2025-02/law_no.6.of_.2025.pdf) · [Simplified regime workflow](eg-sme-tax.md)

## VAT and filing

- **VAT.** The standard rate is 14% from the start of fiscal year 2017-18. Ordinary registration is triggered when relevant turnover reaches EGP 500,000, with different rules for specified categories including importers and scheduled goods or services. Check exemptions, the schedule tax, place of supply and electronic records individually. From 29 July 2026, Law 149/2026 revises specified medical-device and industrial equipment rules and VAT exemptions. Its amended article 3 applies 5% to equipment and medical devices within the stated category, subject to exclusions and separate relief rules. Use the July Act and August corrigendum together. [VAT Law 67/2016](https://www.eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2024-02/Law-english-no.67-2016.pdf.pdf) · [ETA registration FAQ](https://portal.eta.gov.eg/ar/alasylt-alshayt) · [Law 149/2026](https://eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2026-08/law.no_.149.of_.2026.pdf) · [Corrigendum](https://eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2026-08/law.no_.149.150.151.of_.2026.retractation.pdf)
- **Income tax returns.** The ordinary deadlines are 31 March following the year for individuals and 30 April following a calendar-year company period, or 4 months from the end of a different company financial year. The ETA confirms these dates for the 2025 return season and identifies which of its electronic systems to use. Confirm the published dates for the actual filing season, registration, VAT and invoicing category. [ETA filing notice](https://www.eta.gov.eg/ar/news/tysyrat-mwst-wdm-fny-shaml-ltshyl-tqdym-alaqrarat-aldrybyt)
