# Slovakia tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the taxpayer's home, presence, legal form and place of management, the sources of income and the period covered. Keep the dated facts used to determine residence and treaty treatment.

## Territorial reach

Slovakia generally taxes residents on Slovak and foreign income. Nonresidents account for Slovak-source income. Section 16 identifies sources including income from a Slovak permanent establishment, employment performed in Slovakia and income from Slovak immovable property. Check whether a treaty changes residence or Slovakia's right to tax a receipt. [Income Tax Act, §§ 2 and 16](https://static.slov-lex.sk/pdf/SK/ZZ/2003/595/ZZ_2003_595_20260101.pdf)

## Residence

An individual generally qualifies through permanent residence, a dwelling available for more than occasional use with circumstances showing an intention to live there permanently or presence for at least 183 days in the calendar year. Combine separate stays and count each started day in Slovakia. Check the limited-liability rule for a person habitually present solely to study or receive medical treatment. A legal person qualifies through its registered seat or place of effective management. Effective management means where major management and business decisions for the entity as a whole are developed or taken, regardless of whether the address appears in the commercial register. [Income Tax Act, § 2(d)–(e)](https://static.slov-lex.sk/pdf/SK/ZZ/2003/595/ZZ_2003_595_20260101.pdf) · [Financial Administration, individual residence](https://www.financnasprava.sk/sk/obcania/dane/dan-z-prijmov/obcania-sr-v-zahranici)

## Taxpayer types

A sole trader accounts for business profits under the individual rules. Limited liability and joint-stock companies generally account for their own corporate tax. A general commercial partnership (*verejná obchodná spoločnosť*) calculates the common tax base or loss. The partners normally account for their shares in the agreed profit-sharing proportion or equally if the agreement sets no proportion. A limited partnership (*komanditná spoločnosť*) normally reduces its entity tax base by the portion attributable to general partners, who account for their shares. Check the reverse-hybrid rules for both forms where cross-border classification leaves income untaxed. [Income Tax Act, §§ 6, 12, 14 and 17j](https://static.slov-lex.sk/pdf/SK/ZZ/2003/595/ZZ_2003_595_20260101.pdf)

For a foreign trust or comparable arrangement, establish the deed, taxpayers, rights, type of receipt and where the arrangement and participants are taxed. An arrangement without legal personality can enter the controlled foreign company analysis for a Slovak-resident corporate taxpayer. Check the control and low-tax conditions and whether any exception applies before attributing income. [Income Tax Act, §§ 2(ad) and 17h](https://static.slov-lex.sk/pdf/SK/ZZ/2003/595/ZZ_2003_595_20260101.pdf)

## Currency

Slovak accounting records and financial statements use the euro (EUR). For specified foreign-currency items, including receivables and liabilities, accounting records show both the euro and foreign currency. Check the applicable exchange-rate rule and measurement date before converting an amount. [Accounting Act, §§ 4(7) and 24](https://static.slov-lex.sk/pdf/SK/ZZ/2002/431/ZZ_2002_431_20260601.pdf)

## Assessment periods

The ordinary tax period is the calendar year. An eligible corporate taxpayer may use a non-calendar accounting year consisting of 12 consecutive calendar months. Notify the competent tax office in writing within 30 days of formation for a new accounting entity or at least 15 days before an existing entity changes its period. A change can create a shorter accounting and tax period, so retain the notice and dates of the old, transition and new periods. [Income Tax Act, §§ 2(l), 41 and 49](https://static.slov-lex.sk/pdf/SK/ZZ/2003/595/ZZ_2003_595_20260101.pdf) · [Accounting Act, § 3](https://static.slov-lex.sk/pdf/SK/ZZ/2002/431/ZZ_2002_431_20260601.pdf) · [Financial Administration, 2026 corporate tax periods, paragraphs 49–50](https://www.financnasprava.sk/_img/pfsedit/Dokumenty_PFS/Zverejnovanie_dok/Dane/Metodicke_pokyny/Priame_dane_uct/2026/2026.02.13_006_DZPaU_2026_MP.pdf)

## Responsible authorities

The Financial Administration includes the Financial Directorate, tax offices and customs offices. Identify the competent tax office for income tax and the customs office for import duties. Use the official portal's filing service for the rule, return and period that apply. [Financial Administration, structure and responsibilities](https://www.financnasprava.sk/en/financial-administration)

## Fictional intake

A consultant stays in Slovakia for 145 days in 2026 and has accommodation available throughout the year. Ask about registered residence, how the home is used, the intention to stay and the person's ties before deciding residence. Identify where the work was performed and check treaty positions if another country claims residence. For an incorporated business, record the seat, actual decision-making location and financial-period notice separately.
