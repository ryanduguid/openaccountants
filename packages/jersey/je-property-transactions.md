# Jersey capital and property transactions: 2026

## Classify the transaction

The [taxable income guidance](https://www.gov.je/TaxesMoney/IncomeTax/Individuals/TypesTaxableIncome/Pages/TaxableNonTaxableIncome.aspx) lists capital gains as non-taxable unless the activity is trading. The [migration tax overview](https://www.gov.je/lifeevents/movingtojersey/livinginjersey/pages/moneytax.aspx) states that Jersey has no capital gains or inheritance tax. These statements do not remove income tax on trading or property income, transaction duties, GST or probate charges.

Determine the asset, transaction date, beneficial interest, consideration, valuation, use of the dwelling, purchaser and legal structure. Property rental and development income require separate income-tax treatment; see [property income guidance](https://www.gov.je/taxesmoney/incometax/individuals/typestaxableincome/pages/propertyincome.aspx).

## Share-transfer land transaction tax

[LTT rates guidance](https://www.gov.je/TaxesMoney/PropertyTransactionTaxes/LandTransaction/Pages/LandTransactionTaxRates.aspx) applies to the relevant share-transfer transaction. For transfer of ownership, value is the higher of consideration and gross land value. The ordinary and higher rates each add GBP 90. The higher rate generally applies where the dwelling is acquired for a use other than the buyer's main residence; developer trading-stock and other relief conditions require checking.

The following reproduces the bands needed for the examples only. The [complete schedule](https://www.gov.je/TaxesMoney/PropertyTransactionTaxes/LandTransaction/Pages/LandTransactionTaxRates.aspx) must be used for other values and reliefs. The [2026 Budget summary](https://www.gov.je/taxesmoney/incometax/technical/guidelines/pages/2026budgetsummary.aspx) confirms the temporary 2026 higher-rate difference of 2 percentage points.

| Slice of value (GBP) | Standard | Higher: 2026 |
|---|---:|---:|
| First 50,000 | 0.5%, minimum GBP 10 | 2.5%, minimum GBP 10 |
| 50,000 to 300,000 | 1.5% | 3.5% |
| 300,000 to 500,000 | 2% | 4% |
| 500,000 to 700,000 | 3% | 5% |

For an established GBP 650,000 transaction with no relief, standard LTT is GBP 90 plus GBP 250 plus GBP 3,750 plus GBP 4,000 plus GBP 4,500, totalling GBP 12,590. Higher 2026 LTT is GBP 90 plus GBP 1,250 plus GBP 8,750 plus GBP 8,000 plus GBP 7,500, totalling GBP 25,590. Both are the [published worked examples](https://www.gov.je/TaxesMoney/PropertyTransactionTaxes/LandTransaction/Pages/LandTransactionTaxRates.aspx), independently recomputed. They do not establish freehold stamp duty or a mortgage charge.

First-time buyers have residence-status and prior-ownership conditions plus their own relief bands. Security interests, matrimonial transfers, estates, charity and group transactions have separate rules. Establish eligibility and use the specific schedule before calculation.

## Other charges

- Freehold contracts and mortgages fall under [Stamp Duties and Fees Law](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/l_8_1998), including its 2026 schedule. Statutory increments and rounding must be checked; the LTT example cannot substitute for that calculation.
- Acquisition of a significant interest in a property-owning structure can engage enveloped property transaction tax (EPTT). Its [rate guidance](https://www.gov.je/TaxesMoney/PropertyTransactionTaxes/EnvelopedPropertyTransactionTax/pages/epttrates.aspx) distinguishes freehold residential, commercial, higher-rate and contract-lease cases. Determine the statutory scope before using a schedule.
- Probate and administration can carry stamp duty on the personal estate. [Stamp Duties and Fees Law, Article 5](https://www.jerseylaw.je/laws/current/l_8_1998) uses worldwide personal estate for Jersey domicile and estate within the court's jurisdiction for domicile abroad. No inheritance tax does not mean no probate fee.

## Limits and provenance

Full freehold and mortgage schedules, EPTT scope and returns, probate fee bands, valuation disputes, annual parish rates, land development reliefs and transaction filing/payment deadlines remain research gaps. No corresponding payable amount is supplied for those cases.

Check legal structure and date before selecting a schedule, apply bands progressively where required, and establish relief rather than assuming it. The cited primary sections were read on 11 October 2026. See `references.md`; the pack remains pending professional review and incomplete.
