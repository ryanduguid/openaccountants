# Jersey GST and imports: 2026

## Registration and classification

The [business GST guidance](https://www.gov.je/Industry/BusinessAdviceHub/BusinessTaxesSocialSecurity/Pages/GoodsServicesTaxBusinesses.aspx) sets the standard rate at 5%. Registration is mandatory when taxable turnover reaches or is expected to reach GBP 300,000 in a 12-month period. Voluntary registration is available below that level. A business making zero-rated supplies may apply for exemption from registration; zero rating does not automatically remove registration obligations.

Determine the place of supply, supplier and customer location, registration status and statutory classification before charging GST. The [place-of-supply guidance](https://www.gov.je/taxesmoney/gst/businesses/introduction/pages/placesupply.aspx) explains domestic goods, exports, services related to land and other service categories. Received services within Schedule 3 can affect registration and reverse charging. Overseas services are not uniformly outside GST.

Zero-rated, exempt and outside-scope supplies have different return and input-tax treatment. Identify the relevant Schedule 5 or 6 rule and evidence; do not infer exemption from the customer's location or from an invoice without GST. Partial exemption and international services entity concessions require their specific guidance.

## Return computation

Use the [GST return instructions](https://www.gov.je/TaxesMoney/GST/Businesses/SubmittingReturn/Pages/Return.aspx):

1. Reconcile total sales, zero-rated and remitted sales, domestic purchases and imports with the GST account.
2. Record actual output tax on taxable supplies using the applicable tax point. Standard-rated amounts excluding GST use 5%; GST-inclusive standard-rated amounts contain GST equal to gross x 5 / 105.
3. Claim only deductible input GST supported by invoices or import evidence. A purchase value alone is not input tax.
4. Subtract deductible input tax from output tax, including required adjustments. Treat credits and repayment eligibility according to the return instructions.
5. A Customs-approved trader does not claim import GST it has not paid; it still reports the import value in the appropriate box.

Illustration: an established standard-rated domestic sale of GBP 100 excluding GST has GBP 5 output GST and GBP 105 gross consideration. A period with GBP 1,000 output GST and GBP 400 of verified deductible input GST has GBP 600 payable before other adjustments. These examples assume ordinary invoice accounting and no partial exemption or special scheme. The [business guidance](https://www.gov.je/Industry/BusinessAdviceHub/BusinessTaxesSocialSecurity/Pages/GoodsServicesTaxBusinesses.aspx) supplies the rate and the [return instructions](https://www.gov.je/TaxesMoney/GST/Businesses/SubmittingReturn/Pages/Return.aspx) supply the net calculation.

## Imports

Import GST valuation includes relevant purchase price, insurance, freight and applicable taxes and duties. A registered overseas retailer collects GST at purchase regardless of the order value; GST already collected should not be paid again on declaration. See [import payment guidance](https://www.gov.je/taxesmoney/gst/gstcustomers/pages/declaringpaying.aspx).

[Direction 2026/03](https://www.gov.je/TaxesMoney/GST/Businesses/LawRegulationDirection/Pages/GSTDirection202304MovementofGoods.aspx), effective from 11 May 2026, gives a GBP 60 de minimis for qualifying unaccompanied personal-use goods from unregistered retailers. It excludes business imports and artificial splitting. At or below GBP 60, qualifying goods receive relief; above GBP 60, GST applies to the whole value, subject to any other applicable relief. Accompanied luggage uses separate rules. Do not extend this relief to all imports.

## Filing and records

The registration letter establishes the return periods, normally quarterly. A return and its payment are due by the last day of the following month. For January to March, the ordinary deadline is 30 April. See the [return instructions](https://www.gov.je/TaxesMoney/GST/Businesses/SubmittingReturn/Pages/Return.aspx).

Retain the GST account, invoices, adjustments and Customs import/export evidence for 6 years after the relevant period under the [recordkeeping guidance](https://www.gov.je/TaxesMoney/GST/Businesses/SubmittingReturn/pages/keepingbooksrecords.aspx). Company accounting records can have a longer statutory requirement; see `je-company-formation`.

## Limits and checks

Check supply classification before using 5%, verify deductible input tax, reconcile tax already collected on imports, and distinguish business imports from personal relief. Detailed exemption schedules, partial exemption calculations, Customs tariffs and excise, retail schemes, bad debt relief and penalty amounts remain research gaps. No arbitrary import or mixed-supply calculation is provided.
