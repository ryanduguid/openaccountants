# Jersey tax system orientation

Revenue Jersey administers income tax and local GST. Jersey issues its own pound at par with sterling, and corporate returns are expressed in pounds sterling. [Government of Jersey London Office][CURRENCY] · [Revenue Jersey, company tax return guidance][CURRENCYFILING]

## Residence and income source

An individual resident and ordinarily resident in Jersey is generally taxed on worldwide income. An individual resident but not ordinarily resident is generally taxed on Jersey-source income and foreign income remitted to Jersey. Non-residents generally pay on Jersey-source income subject to statutory exceptions. Residence can depend on the duration and frequency of visits, available accommodation and the circumstances of a move. Check any relevant tax treaty and the rules for apportioning allowances on arrival or departure. [Income Tax (Jersey) Law 1961, arts 118B, 126 and 129A–129B][LAW] · [Revenue Jersey, residence][RES]

## Individuals and companies

For 2026, Revenue Jersey uses the lower of the standard calculation at 20% after allowable expenses and pension contributions and the marginal relief calculation at 26% after those deductions and the applicable exemption threshold. The ordinary low income threshold is £21,250, with conditional additions. Independent taxation applies from 1 January 2026, with a compensatory allowance for couples meeting the transition conditions. [Revenue Jersey, 2026 allowances and reliefs][RATES]

Determine company residence under article 123, including the incorporation and central management and control rules and foreign residence conditions. The ordinary company rate is 0%. Specified financial services companies pay 10%. The 20% rate applies to qualifying utilities and specified Jersey-source activities, including designated property income and development. A separate large corporate retailer regime tests local residence or permanent establishment and both the amount and share of retail turnover. Its ordinary 12-month income thresholds are £500,000 and £750,000 for the transition between 0% and 20%, subject to aggregation and period rules. Check any separate in-scope multinational tax and filing obligations. [Income Tax (Jersey) Law 1961, arts 123–123L][LAW] · [Government of Jersey, company tax][CORP]

## GST and filing

In 2026 the general GST rate is 5%. Check the place and character of the supply, registration, any zero rating or exemption and the goods-import rules. The supplier generally accounts for tax on a taxable supply, and the importer is generally liable on imported goods. [Goods and Services Tax (Jersey) Law 2007, arts 4–9][GST]

Income tax uses a calendar year of assessment. For 2025 the Gazette set 31 May 2026 for ordinary individual paper returns, 31 July 2026 for individual electronic returns and 30 November 2026 for electronic company returns. Check the paper-filing concessions and any separate statements or payment deadlines. A Jersey-incorporated company generally files even where dormant or taxed at 0%. [Income Tax (Jersey) Law 1961, arts 1 and 16][LAW] · [Gazette, 2025 returns and statements][DATES] · [Revenue Jersey, corporate filing][CORPRET]
