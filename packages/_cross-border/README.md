# Cross-Border Tax & Incorporation Skills

This package contains **international, multi-jurisdiction** skills for AI agents helping users with cross-border company formation, tax residency planning, foreign exchange controls, and VAT/GST compliance.

Unlike country-specific packages (e.g., `brazil/`, `us/`), these skills span multiple jurisdictions and are designed for founders, freelancers, and digital nomads operating across borders.

## Skills in this package

| File | Topic | Key Jurisdictions |
|------|-------|-------------------|
| `international-incorporation.md` | Jurisdiction selection for company formation | US (Wyoming/Delaware), HK, SG, Estonia, UK, Dubai |
| `tax-residency-planning.md` | Personal tax residency rules, 183-day rule, digital nomad visas | UAE, Panama, Georgia, Portugal, Spain, Croatia, Thailand |
| `forex-controls.md` | Foreign exchange restrictions by country | China, India, Brazil, Taiwan, South Korea, Japan |
| `cross-border-vat-gst.md` | VAT/GST compliance for international digital businesses | EU, UK, Australia, Singapore, Japan, Canada, US |

## Jurisdiction code

All skills in this package use `jurisdiction: INTL` in their YAML frontmatter.

## Disclaimer

These skills provide general tax and legal information for educational purposes. They do not constitute tax, legal, or financial advice. Users must consult qualified local advisors (CPAs, tax attorneys, chartered accountants) before making decisions involving company formation, tax residency changes, or cross-border structuring. Tax rules change frequently — verify all thresholds, rates, and deadlines against current official sources.
