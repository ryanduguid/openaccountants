# Thailand Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax rates and base

- **Tax on worldwide/Thai-source profit** — Companies incorporated in Thailand are taxed on worldwide net profit; foreign companies are taxed on Thai-source profit. The standard rate is 20%, with reduced rates for qualifying SMEs.
- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 20% of net profit  _(Revenue Department, Corporate Income Tax guide, part 5 Tax Rates — https://www.rd.go.th/english/6044.html ; Revenue Code, Chapter 3 Part 3 (ss 65 to 76), Revenue Department English translation, s 67 and the Income Tax Rates Schedule — https://www.rd.go.th/english/37764.html)_
- **Tax base (Thai company)** — Net profit of the accounting period from worldwide sources, computed on the accrual basis under s. 65 and the conditions in ss. 65 bis and 65 ter  _(Revenue Code (Revenue Department English translation), s. 65 — https://www.rd.go.th/english/37764.html#section65 ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/thailand/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Tax base (foreign company)** — Net profit from business carried on in Thailand (Thai-source profit)  _(Revenue Code (Revenue Department English translation), s. 66 para 2 — https://www.rd.go.th/english/37764.html#section66 ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/thailand/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **SME rate — net profit THB 0 – 300,000** — 0% (exempt) for a company or juristic partnership with paid-up capital not exceeding THB 5 million and income not exceeding THB 30 million, for accounting periods starting on or after 1 January 2013  _(Royal Decree issued under the Revenue Code regarding tax rate reduction and exemption (No. 530) B.E. 2554, as amended by Nos 555, 564 and 583, Revenue Department English translation, s 7(2) — https://www.rd.go.th/fileadmin/user_upload/kormor/eng/RD_530.pdf ; Revenue Department, Corporate Income Tax guide, part 5 Tax Rates — https://www.rd.go.th/english/6044.html)_
- **SME rate — net profit THB 300,001 – 3,000,000** — 15%, for accounting periods starting on or after 1 January 2015  _(Royal Decree issued under the Revenue Code regarding tax rate reduction and exemption (No. 530) B.E. 2554, as amended by Nos 555, 564 and 583, Revenue Department English translation, s 6(3) — https://www.rd.go.th/fileadmin/user_upload/kormor/eng/RD_530.pdf ; Revenue Department, Corporate Income Tax guide, part 5 Tax Rates — https://www.rd.go.th/english/6044.html)_
- **SME rate — net profit over THB 3,000,000** — 20%  _(Royal Decree issued under the Revenue Code regarding tax rate reduction and exemption (No. 530) B.E. 2554, as amended by Nos 555, 564 and 583, Revenue Department English translation, s 6(3) — https://www.rd.go.th/fileadmin/user_upload/kormor/eng/RD_530.pdf ; Revenue Department, Corporate Income Tax guide, part 5 Tax Rates — https://www.rd.go.th/english/6044.html)_
- **SME qualification test** — Paid-up capital not exceeding THB 5 million on the last day of the accounting period AND income from sales of goods and services not exceeding THB 30 million in the accounting period  _(Royal Decree issued under the Revenue Code regarding tax rate reduction and exemption (No. 530) B.E. 2554, as amended by Nos 555, 564 and 583, Revenue Department English translation, ss 6 and 8 — https://www.rd.go.th/fileadmin/user_upload/kormor/eng/RD_530.pdf ; Revenue Department, Tax for Small and Medium Enterprises — https://www.rd.go.th/fileadmin/download/Tax-for-SMEs.pdf)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends (to Thai company/individual)** — 10% (s. 50(2)(e) for payments to individuals)  _(Revenue Code (Revenue Department English translation), s. 50(2)(e) — https://www.rd.go.th/english/37749.html#section50 ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/thailand/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends (to non-resident)** — 10% (may be reduced by tax treaty)  _(Revenue Code (Revenue Department English translation), s. 70 — https://www.rd.go.th/english/37764.html#section70 ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/thailand/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on interest (to non-resident)** — 15% (may be reduced by tax treaty)  _(Revenue Code (Revenue Department English translation), s. 70 — https://www.rd.go.th/english/37764.html#section70 ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/thailand/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on royalties (to non-resident)** — 15% (may be reduced by tax treaty)  _(Revenue Code (Revenue Department English translation), s. 70 — https://www.rd.go.th/english/37764.html#section70 ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/thailand/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on service and professional fees (to non-resident)** — **15%** under Section 70 on service income, professional services and property rentals (assessable income under s. 40(2) to (6)) paid from or in Thailand to a foreign company not carrying on business in Thailand. Treaty relief may reduce it  _(Revenue Code (Revenue Department English translation), s. 70 — https://www.rd.go.th/english/37764.html#section70)_
- **Withholding tax on professional and service fees (domestic)** — **3%** where a Thai payer pays a service or professional fee — lawyers, consultants, engineers, accountants, auditors — to a resident  _(Revenue Code (Revenue Department English translation), s. 3 tredecim; Revenue Department withholding orders — https://www.rd.go.th/english/37695.html)_
- **Withholding tax on rent (domestic)** — **5%** on rent for office space, land, buildings or equipment paid to a resident  _(Revenue Code (Revenue Department English translation), s. 3 tredecim; Revenue Department withholding orders — https://www.rd.go.th/english/37695.html)_
- **Withholding tax on advertising fees (domestic)** — **2%**  _(Revenue Code (Revenue Department English translation), s. 3 tredecim; Revenue Department withholding orders — https://www.rd.go.th/english/37695.html)_
- **Withholding tax on transport (domestic)** — **1%** on payments to a transport company or private transport operator  _(Revenue Code (Revenue Department English translation), s. 3 tredecim; Revenue Department withholding orders — https://www.rd.go.th/english/37695.html)_
- **Where these rates come from, and what to confirm** — The dividend, interest and royalty figures are PwC-sourced, and s. 70 is the charging section for payments to a foreign company. The four domestic heads rest on s. 3 tredecim and the Revenue Department's withholding orders; their percentages come from Thai practitioner sources that agree with each other. Confirm each rate against the current withholding order before a payment is made  _(Revenue Code (Revenue Department English translation), s. 3 tredecim and s. 70 — https://www.rd.go.th/english/37695.html)_
- **Annual CIT return deadline (PND.50)** — Within 150 days from the last day of the accounting period, with payment (s. 68)  _(Revenue Code (Revenue Department English translation), s. 68 — https://www.rd.go.th/english/37764.html#section68 ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/thailand/corporate/tax-administration)_
- **Half-year CIT return (PND.51)** — Within 2 months from the last day of the first six months of the accounting period, paying half the tax on the estimated annual net profit; listed companies, banks and finance companies pay on actual half-year profit (s. 67 bis)  _(Revenue Code (Revenue Department English translation), s. 67 bis — https://www.rd.go.th/english/37764.html#section67bis ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/thailand/corporate/tax-administration)_

## Top-up tax — a second charge, in force since accounting periods beginning 1 January 2025

`th-tax-overview.md` carried this and the file a reader opens for Thai corporate
tax did not. The facts below come from the **Revenue Department's own English
news releases**, not from a commercial summary.

- **The instrument** — *Emergency Decree on Top-up Tax, B.E. 2567 (2024)*. The
  Revenue Department proposed it through the Ministry of Finance; the Cabinet
  approved it on **11 December 2024** and it was **promulgated in the Royal Gazette
  on 26 December 2024**  _(Revenue Department, News No. 6/2025, 27 December 2024 — https://www.rd.go.th/fileadmin/user_upload/news/2567eng/englishnews_6_2025.pdf)_
- **Who it applies to** — Large multinational enterprise (MNE) groups with
  **consolidated financial statement revenues of at least EUR 750 million**  _(Revenue Department, News No. 6/2025)_
- **From when** — **Accounting periods commencing on or after 1 January 2025**  _(Revenue Department, News No. 6/2025; confirmed again in News No. 5/2026)_
- **Filing** — Through a **fully electronic system**. The Revenue Department's
  enquiry address for the charge is `topuptax@rd.go.th`  _(Revenue Department, News No. 6/2025)_
- **The detailed scope rules were still in draft a year after the charge began** —
  On **30 December 2025** the Cabinet approved **in principle** four draft
  instruments of secondary legislation under the Emergency Decree, prescribing the
  rules for **determining which MNE groups are subject to the Top-up Tax** and for
  **adjusting income, expenses and covered taxes** in computing it. They are drawn
  from the OECD GloBE Model Rules, Commentary and Administrative Guidance. So the
  charge has applied since 1 January 2025 while the instruments fixing its precise
  scope and computation were still draft — **check their current status before
  relying on any scope conclusion**  _(Revenue Department, News No. 5/2026, 30 December 2025 — https://www.rd.go.th/fileadmin/user_upload/news/2568eng/englishnews_5_2026.pdf)_

> **What has not been read, and why.** The text of the Emergency Decree itself has
> **not** been read for this guide. Everything above is the Revenue Department's own
> English account of it. The Royal Gazette (`ratchakitcha.soc.go.th`) sits behind a
> Cloudflare interstitial that does **not** clear even for a browser that waits the
> challenge out, and `krisdika.go.th` — the Council of State's law database — serves
> a **self-signed certificate**, so neither was fetched with verification left on.
>
> **Deliberately not stated:** Oman, Kuwait and the Netherlands each express the
> EUR 750 million threshold as *at least two of the four immediately preceding*
> periods. The Revenue Department's release states the threshold **without** that
> qualification, and the decree has not been read, so the two-of-four wording is
> **not** asserted for Thailand. It is the first thing to check in the decree.
