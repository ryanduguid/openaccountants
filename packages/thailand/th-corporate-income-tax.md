# Thailand Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax rates and base

- **Tax on worldwide/Thai-source profit** — Companies incorporated in Thailand are taxed on worldwide net profit; foreign companies are taxed on Thai-source profit. The standard rate is 20%, with reduced rates for qualifying SMEs.
- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 20% of net profit  _(Revenue Department, Corporate Income Tax guide, part 5 Tax Rates — https://www.rd.go.th/english/6044.html ; Revenue Code, Chapter 3 Part 3 (ss 65 to 76), Revenue Department English translation, s 67 and the Income Tax Rates Schedule — https://www.rd.go.th/english/37764.html)_
- **Tax base (Thai company)** — Net profit of the accounting period from worldwide sources, computed on the accrual basis under s. 65 and the conditions in ss. 65 bis and 65 ter  _(Revenue Code (Revenue Department English translation), s. 65 — https://www.rd.go.th/english/37764.html#section65)_
- **Tax base (foreign company)** — Net profit from business carried on in Thailand (Thai-source profit)  _(Revenue Code (Revenue Department English translation), s. 66 para 2 — https://www.rd.go.th/english/37764.html#section66)_
- **SME rate — net profit THB 0 – 300,000** — 0% (exempt) for a company or juristic partnership with paid-up capital not exceeding THB 5 million and income not exceeding THB 30 million, for accounting periods starting on or after 1 January 2013  _(Royal Decree issued under the Revenue Code regarding tax rate reduction and exemption (No. 530) B.E. 2554, as amended by Nos 555, 564 and 583, Revenue Department English translation, s 7(2) — https://www.rd.go.th/fileadmin/user_upload/kormor/eng/RD_530.pdf ; Revenue Department, Corporate Income Tax guide, part 5 Tax Rates — https://www.rd.go.th/english/6044.html)_
- **SME rate — net profit THB 300,001 – 3,000,000** — 15%, for accounting periods starting on or after 1 January 2015  _(Royal Decree issued under the Revenue Code regarding tax rate reduction and exemption (No. 530) B.E. 2554, as amended by Nos 555, 564 and 583, Revenue Department English translation, s 6(3) — https://www.rd.go.th/fileadmin/user_upload/kormor/eng/RD_530.pdf ; Revenue Department, Corporate Income Tax guide, part 5 Tax Rates — https://www.rd.go.th/english/6044.html)_
- **SME rate — net profit over THB 3,000,000** — 20%  _(Royal Decree issued under the Revenue Code regarding tax rate reduction and exemption (No. 530) B.E. 2554, as amended by Nos 555, 564 and 583, Revenue Department English translation, s 6(3) — https://www.rd.go.th/fileadmin/user_upload/kormor/eng/RD_530.pdf ; Revenue Department, Corporate Income Tax guide, part 5 Tax Rates — https://www.rd.go.th/english/6044.html)_
- **SME qualification test** — Paid-up capital not exceeding THB 5 million on the last day of the accounting period AND income from sales of goods and services not exceeding THB 30 million in the accounting period  _(Royal Decree issued under the Revenue Code regarding tax rate reduction and exemption (No. 530) B.E. 2554, as amended by Nos 555, 564 and 583, Revenue Department English translation, ss 6 and 8 — https://www.rd.go.th/fileadmin/user_upload/kormor/eng/RD_530.pdf ; Revenue Department, Tax for Small and Medium Enterprises — https://www.rd.go.th/fileadmin/download/Tax-for-SMEs.pdf)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends (to Thai company/individual)** — 10% (s. 50(2)(e) for payments to individuals)  _(Revenue Code (Revenue Department English translation), s. 50(2)(e) — https://www.rd.go.th/english/37749.html#section50)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends (to non-resident)** — 10% (may be reduced by tax treaty)  _(Revenue Code (Revenue Department English translation), s. 70 — https://www.rd.go.th/english/37764.html#section70)_
- **Withholding tax on interest (to non-resident)** — 15% (may be reduced by tax treaty)  _(Revenue Code (Revenue Department English translation), s. 70 — https://www.rd.go.th/english/37764.html#section70)_
- **Withholding tax on royalties (to non-resident)** — 15% (may be reduced by tax treaty)  _(Revenue Code (Revenue Department English translation), s. 70 — https://www.rd.go.th/english/37764.html#section70)_
- **Withholding tax on service and professional fees (to non-resident)** — **15%** under Section 70 on service income, professional services and property rentals (assessable income under s. 40(2) to (6)) paid from or in Thailand to a foreign company not carrying on business in Thailand. Treaty relief may reduce it  _(Revenue Code (Revenue Department English translation), s. 70 — https://www.rd.go.th/english/37764.html#section70)_
- **Withholding tax on professional and service fees (domestic)** — **3%** where a Thai payer pays a service or professional fee — lawyers, consultants, engineers, accountants, auditors — to a resident  _(Revenue Code (Revenue Department English translation), s. 3 tredecim; Revenue Department withholding orders — https://www.rd.go.th/english/37695.html)_
- **Withholding tax on rent (domestic)** — **5%** on rent for office space, land, buildings or equipment paid to a resident  _(Revenue Code (Revenue Department English translation), s. 3 tredecim; Revenue Department withholding orders — https://www.rd.go.th/english/37695.html)_
- **Withholding tax on advertising fees (domestic)** — **2%**  _(Revenue Code (Revenue Department English translation), s. 3 tredecim; Revenue Department withholding orders — https://www.rd.go.th/english/37695.html)_
- **Withholding tax on transport (domestic)** — **1%** on payments to a transport company or private transport operator  _(Revenue Code (Revenue Department English translation), s. 3 tredecim; Revenue Department withholding orders — https://www.rd.go.th/english/37695.html)_
- **Where these rates come from, and what to confirm** — The dividend, interest and royalty figures were taken from a secondary practitioner summary, and s. 70 is the charging section for payments to a foreign company. The four domestic heads rest on s. 3 tredecim and the Revenue Department's withholding orders; their percentages come from Thai practitioner sources that agree with each other. Confirm each rate against the current withholding order before a payment is made  _(Revenue Code (Revenue Department English translation), s. 3 tredecim and s. 70 — https://www.rd.go.th/english/37695.html)_
- **Annual CIT return deadline (PND.50)** — Within 150 days from the last day of the accounting period, with payment (s. 68)  _(Revenue Code (Revenue Department English translation), s. 68 — https://www.rd.go.th/english/37764.html#section68)_
- **Half-year CIT return (PND.51)** — Within 2 months from the last day of the first six months of the accounting period, paying half the tax on the estimated annual net profit; listed companies, banks and finance companies pay on actual half-year profit (s. 67 bis)  _(Revenue Code (Revenue Department English translation), s. 67 bis — https://www.rd.go.th/english/37764.html#section67bis)_

## Top-up tax for multinational groups

- **Start date and revenue test.** The Emergency Decree on Top-up Tax, B.E. 2567 (2024), was published on 26 December 2024 and took effect on 1 January 2025. It applies for accounting periods beginning on or after that date. A Thai constituent entity is generally in scope if its multinational group had revenue of at least the Thai-currency equivalent of EUR 750 million in the ultimate parent's consolidated financial statements in at least 2 of the 4 accounting periods immediately preceding the period under review. Prorate the threshold for a period other than 12 months. Check the rules applicable to any group restructuring. [Emergency Decree, ss 2 and 26](https://www.rd.go.th/fileadmin/user_upload/kormor/newlaw/prk2567.pdf) · [Revenue Department, News No. 6/2025](https://www.rd.go.th/fileadmin/user_upload/news/2567eng/englishnews_6_2025.pdf)
- **Excluded entities.** Section 27 lists categories subject to qualifying conditions, including government entities, pension funds and investment funds that are ultimate parents. The Royal Decree on excluded constituent entities, B.E. 2569 (2026), adds an exclusion for entities owned by qualifying entities under s 27(1)–(6), with a carve-out for pension services entities as owners. It entered into force on 8 August 2026 and applies when determining liability for accounting periods starting on or after 1 January 2025. Check the Director-General's published characteristics and any election before applying the exclusion. [Emergency Decree, s 27](https://www.rd.go.th/fileadmin/user_upload/kormor/newlaw/prk2567.pdf) · [Royal Decree, ss 2–5](https://www.rd.go.th/fileadmin/user_upload/kormor/newlaw/dctut.pdf) · [Director-General's notices](https://www.rd.go.th/68005.html)

### Reporting and payment

- **Notification and information return.** Section 54 requires information identifying the ultimate parent and the entity filing the GloBE Information Return (GIR), amongst other particulars. Section 55 separately requires the GIR. Both ordinarily fall due within 15 months of the last day of the ultimate parent's accounting period. A Thai constituent entity of the same group can file on behalf of another if it completes the relevant filing and identifies the delegating entity and its taxpayer identification number. [Emergency Decree, ss 53–55](https://www.rd.go.th/fileadmin/user_upload/kormor/newlaw/prk2567.pdf)
- **Foreign filing of the GIR.** Section 56 exempts a Thai constituent entity from local GIR filing if the ultimate parent or its designated constituent entity has filed the GIR in its country and an agreement for automatic exchange of GIR information between the competent authorities of that country and Thailand is effective for the relevant period. Check the exchange relationship for that period and handle the s 54 notification separately. [Emergency Decree, ss 54–56](https://www.rd.go.th/fileadmin/user_upload/kormor/newlaw/prk2567.pdf)
- **Top-up tax return and payment.** A Thai constituent entity with top-up tax payable must file and pay within 15 months of the last day of the ultimate parent's accounting period. For a joint venture or joint-venture subsidiary liable under s 52, use the period end of the relevant joint venture or the joint venture deemed to be the ultimate parent. [Emergency Decree, s 57](https://www.rd.go.th/fileadmin/user_upload/kormor/newlaw/prk2567.pdf)
- **First period in scope.** Section 58 extends the s 54 notification, s 55 GIR and s 57 tax return deadlines to 18 months for the group's first accounting period in scope. For a group's first in-scope period ending 31 December 2025, the extended deadline for these filings is 30 June 2027. Pay any top-up tax due when filing the return. [Emergency Decree, ss 57–58](https://www.rd.go.th/fileadmin/user_upload/kormor/newlaw/prk2567.pdf)

The Revenue Department announced electronic filing. Check its current forms, filing instructions and applicable secondary rules, including any rules for instalments under s 57. [Revenue Department, News No. 6/2025](https://www.rd.go.th/fileadmin/user_upload/news/2567eng/englishnews_6_2025.pdf) · [Top-up tax legislation index](https://www.rd.go.th/68020.html)
