# India tax system

> General orientation for tax year 2026-27. Confirm the taxpayer, entity, place of supply and relevant period with a qualified Indian tax professional before applying these rules.

Amounts are in Indian rupees (INR). A lakh is INR 100,000 and a crore is INR 10 million. The Income-tax Act, 2025 generally commenced on 1 April 2026. Tax year 2026-27 normally runs from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027. Income of financial year 2025-26 is still handled under the Income-tax Act, 1961 for assessment year 2026-27. Choose the period before selecting the form or applying section numbers. [Income-tax Act, 2025, §§ 1, 3 and 536](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/documents/d/guest/income_tax_act_2025_as_amended_by_fa_act_2026-pdf) · [Income Tax Department transition FAQs](https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/help/all-topics/e-filing-services/objective-and-scope-new-act-faq)

## Income tax

- **Residence and income scope.** An individual normally meets the Indian residence test with at least 182 days in India in the tax year, or at least 60 days in it and 365 in the preceding 4 years. Check the exceptions for Indian citizens leaving for employment abroad or as crew, eligible visiting citizens and persons of Indian origin, and deemed residence. For eligible visitors with more than INR 15 lakh of income other than foreign-source income, the second test can use 120 days instead of 60. A company is resident if it is an Indian company or its place of effective management is in India in the tax year. Income received or deemed received in India, or accruing or deemed to accrue there, is generally within scope in each residence category. A resident and ordinarily resident taxpayer is generally taxed on worldwide income. For a resident but not ordinarily resident taxpayer, foreign accrual is generally included only if derived from a business controlled in India or a profession set up there. Check treaty residence and relief separately. [Income-tax Act, 2025, §§ 5–6](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/documents/d/guest/income_tax_act_2025_as_amended_by_fa_act_2026-pdf) · [Residence workflow](in-tax-residency.md)
- **Default individual rates.** For ordinary taxable income under section 202 in tax year 2026-27, the rate is nil through INR 4 lakh. Rates of 5%, 10%, 15%, 20% and 25% apply to the next 5 bands of INR 4 lakh each. The rate above INR 24 lakh is 30%. An eligible resident individual's section 156 rebate is capped at INR 60,000 when total income is at most INR 12 lakh, with marginal relief just above that limit and a cap tied to tax under the section 202 scale. Check income subject to special rates, eligibility for an old regime election, deductions, surcharge and the 4% health and education cess separately. [Income-tax Act, 2025, §§ 156 and 202](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/documents/d/guest/income_tax_act_2025_as_amended_by_fa_act_2026-pdf) · [Finance Act, 2026, § 3](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/documents/d/guest/finance-act-2026-pdf-1)
- **Domestic companies.** The ordinary tax year 2026-27 rate is 25% if turnover or gross receipts in 2024-25 were at most INR 400 crore, and 30% otherwise. Section 200 offers a 22% base rate to domestic companies that elect it and meet its conditions, including restrictions on deductions and related losses. Apply the relevant surcharge and the 4% cess separately. Check the different rates and withholding rules for foreign companies and special income. [Finance Act, 2026, first schedule, part I-B, paragraph E](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/documents/d/guest/finance-act-2026-pdf-1) · [Income-tax Act, 2025, § 200](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/documents/d/guest/income_tax_act_2025_as_amended_by_fa_act_2026-pdf)

## GST and filing

- **GST.** Intra-state supplies generally attract central GST and the applicable state or union territory GST. Inter-state supplies generally attract integrated GST. The notified goods schedules in force from 22 September 2025 include combined rates of 5%, 18% and 40%, with other rates and exemptions depending on classification. Check the current schedule and any amendments for the exact goods or service, together with place of supply, registration, reverse charge, input credits and return obligations. [CGST Act, § 9](https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content-page/explore-act/1000278/1000001) · [IGST Act, §§ 5 and 7–8](https://upload.indiacode.nic.in/showfile?actid=AC_CEN_2_2_00043_201713_1517807328116&filename=a2017-13.pdf&type=actfile) · [Central Tax (Rate) notification 09/2025](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/266209.pdf) · [Integrated Tax (Rate) notification 09/2025](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2025/266211.pdf) · [CBIC current notifications](https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/) · [GST workflow](india-gst.md)
- **Filing.** For tax year 2026-27, the ordinary section 263 due dates in 2027 are 31 July for other assessees, 31 August for qualifying non-audit business or professional cases, 31 October for companies and applicable audit cases outside section 172, and 30 November for section 172 cases. Confirm the statutory category and any official extension. Check advance tax and tax deducted or collected at source separately from the return deadline. [Income-tax Act, 2025, § 263 and ch. XIX](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/documents/d/guest/income_tax_act_2025_as_amended_by_fa_act_2026-pdf) · [Income Tax Department e-filing portal](https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/)
