> **General reference only.** This Tier 2 draft illustrates nominal, unrounded arithmetic on expressly stipulated fictional premises. It establishes no actual contribution, lawful deduction, net wages, remittance, filing, benefit or complete country output. Every numerical share below remains nominal and unrounded, including whole-dollar examples. Guide presence and passing component checks do not establish comprehensive jurisdiction acceptance.

## Scope and stop conditions

Load workflow-base and social-contributions-workflow-base in full. Preserve the 4 universal outputs and 18 checks, the 5 social-contributions outputs and 12 checks, all mandatory country slots and shared refusals. This reference leaves the country contract incomplete. Actual payroll also requires payroll-workflow-base and a complete Montserrat payroll companion. The fictional branch stipulates one independently established covered ordinary private employee aged 30 throughout an entire calendar month in 2026, one employer, employment wholly in Montserrat, regular cash salary fixed monthly and paid for that same complete month. All relevant coverage, wage character and period allocation are independently established. G is the complete non-negative regular monthly cash earnings input before any employee deduction; it is not inferred from net cash, an account label or a missing record. No actual registration or current-law acceptance is supplied by the stipulation.

The branch excludes self-employment, voluntary contributions, government or other special employment, a director or shareholder whose status needs analysis, foreign service, agreements, vessel/aircraft work, unpaid apprenticeship, age transitions, pensions, benefit weeks, bonuses, gratuities, tax paid on behalf, non-cash items, arrears, partial or irregular periods, multiple employers, corrections and disputed components. Their actual treatment remains held. Do not infer zero liability or exemption from an exclusion from this illustration.

**Rounding is a material open gate.** SRO 8/2022 prints 'calculated to the nearest dollar'. The Fund's 2026 monthly tables show cents. The object, order and operational effect of the statutory rounding wording have not been established. Do not select cent rounding, whole-dollar rounding, truncation or the table value as the lawful result. This draft intentionally stops at nominal unrounded arithmetic. Even E = 7, R = 8 and T = 15 in P06 do not become accepted amounts because they are integers.

## Conditional nominal mechanics

1. **Establish the fictional premises independently.** Use only the ordinary single-employer, age-30, full-calendar-month 2026 branch above. G must be the expressly established complete non-negative regular monthly cash earnings in the printed source-scale dollar units. Missing, negative, malformed, disputed or unsupported-precision inputs stay held. No status, period, zero, currency, rate or current-law default is allowed.
2. **Preserve the raw input and nominal ceiling separately.** For this illustration alone, B = min(G, 4000). B is the nominal monthly capped earnings reference. Preserve G even above 4000. Do not use the weekly 923 limit, annualise, pro-rate or give each payment a fresh monthly ceiling. Multiple-employer and other-period rules are outside this branch and remain held.
3. **Calculate nominal unrounded shares only.** E = 0.07 × B and R = 0.08 × B, with nominal unrounded T = E + R = 0.15 × B. R already contains the 1% employment-injury element; never add another 1% or treat it as employee money. Preserve B, E, R and T separately. These are retained-source illustrations before the unresolved statutory rounding construction, not contribution amounts due.
4. **Do not substitute cash or another programme.** An unrelated salary cash deduction does not reduce independently established G or B. E is not lawful employee withholding, R is not recoverable from the employee and T is not a net-pay deduction. Income tax, net wages, benefits, total employer cost and other programmes remain separate and uncomputed. No contribution is credited merely because an equal bank payment exists.
5. **Stop before operational rounding or outputs.** No rounding of G, B or a share is chosen. The static fixture's bounded decimal parsing is a test capability, not legal precision authority. Unsupported precision remains held rather than truncated. Establish the complete current law, actual currency, period, rounding object/order, forms, payment history, output runtime and qualified review before actual payroll, deduction, remittance, filing or reliance.
