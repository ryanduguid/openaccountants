## Incorporation

1. Classify the proposed entity under S1 ss 3–4. The Act allows companies limited by shares, companies limited by guarantee with or without authority to issue shares and non-profit companies. A company limited by shares may register as public. A non-profit company cannot issue shares and requires the Governor's prior written consent before filing under s 5(5).
2. Under S1 ss 78–80, secure a physical registered office in Montserrat and a locally eligible registered agent. Reporting companies and, subject to prescribed exemptions, other companies carrying on activity outside Montserrat require a licensed company manager as agent. For other companies the Act also allows an individual director normally and habitually resident in Montserrat. If the registered office is at the agent's office, disclose that in the address description.
3. The proposed agent alone files the application under S1 s 5. The Registrar may reserve a proposed company's name for 45 days on its agent's application under s 20. For an incorporation, prepare the approved application, signed articles complying with s 7, the agent's signed consent and any prescribed additional document. The application includes the entity type, public status, office and agent details, proposed directors and consents, proposed shareholders and allocations and applicable control disclosures. Under s 6 the Registrar registers compliant documents and issues the incorporation certificate. The date on the certificate establishes the incorporation date.
4. Keep the S1 s 5(3)(i) initial disclosure separate from the s 95 notice. For a relevant company, the application includes registrable-person information and verification evidence. Within 14 days after incorporation, the s 95 notice gives the information and evidence or states that the company has no registrable person. Apply the full tests in ss 85–89 and SRO 15. In s 86(2), the share and voting-right tests use **more than** 25%. Appointment rights, other control and indirect structures can also be relevant.
5. File the particulars of members and directors under S1 s 373 within 14 days after incorporation or continuation. That provision also sets a separate 14-day rule for changes. Confirm the statutory time computation and operative forms before calculating an actual deadline.

The original SRO 16/2024 Sch 3, pp. 95–96, lists `C-IN1` for incorporation, `C-NR` for name reservation, `C-MD` for member and director particulars and `C-PSC-1` for the initial control notice. It lists signed agent consent and the articles with the application. Resolve the current approved forms with the registry before preparing a submission. [Schedule 3](https://www.gov.ms/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/SRO-No.-16-of-2024-Companies-Regulations-2024.pdf#page=95)

## Foreign company registration

A place of business in Montserrat is within the Part 12 test. Under s 216, activities such as maintaining a bank account or holding property do not by themselves trigger this test. Section 217 permits carrying on business if the company is registered under Part 12 or its registration application remains undecided. Other activity licences and tax obligations require separate checks. [Cap. 11.12, ss 216–217](https://www.gov.ms/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/11.12-Companies-Act.pdf#page=139)

Under ss 218–221, the application goes to the Registrar with incorporation evidence, certified constitutional documents, particulars of members and directors, the registered agent's consent and certified English translations where required. The registered agent must be a licensed company manager. The Registrar issues the foreign company registration certificate. Section 220 requires notice in the approved form within one month after specified changes, including a change of directors or registered agent. The original SRO 16 Sch 3 lists the application as C-IN-O. Check the operative form, filing channel and any separate licence before acting. [Cap. 11.12, ss 218–221](https://www.gov.ms/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/11.12-Companies-Act.pdf#page=140) · [SRO 16/2024, Sch 3](https://www.gov.ms/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/SRO-No.-16-of-2024-Companies-Regulations-2024.pdf#page=100)

Section 230 separately requires a registered foreign company to file any prescribed financial statements and financial and other returns. Establish the operative prescriptions for the foreign company. [Cap. 11.12, s 230](https://www.gov.ms/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/11.12-Companies-Act.pdf#page=147)

## Company records and annual filings

- **Company documents.** Under s 100, a company whose director acts as its registered agent keeps the articles, member and director registers and copies of filings from the previous 10 years at its registered office. Other companies keep them at the agent's office. A company required to appoint a licensed company manager may keep register copies there, subject to the separate change and location notices in s 100(4)–(5). Section 101 separately governs minutes and resolutions. [Cap. 11.12, ss 100–101](https://www.gov.ms/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/11.12-Companies-Act.pdf#page=76)

- **Records.** S1 s 105 requires underlying documents that explain transactions, allow the financial position to be established with reasonable accuracy and support required statements, returns and any audit. Retain the records for at least 7 years after the end of the financial year to which they relate. A company required to appoint a licensed company manager may keep them at its agent's office or another place, including outside Montserrat, selected by the directors. If records are elsewhere, it must keep at the agent's office the records needed to show its financial position at intervals of at most 3 months and a written location record. Other companies keep the records at the registered office or at a place in Montserrat chosen by the directors. [Cap. 11.12, ss 105–106](https://www.gov.ms/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/11.12-Companies-Act.pdf#page=79)
- **Company annual return.** Under S1 s 106(3)–(4), send the prescribed return and fee to the Registrar no later than 1 April in each year after incorporation or continuation, made up to the preceding 31 December and certified by a director. The original SRO 16 Sch 4 item 5 lists EC$200 for the return filed on or before 1 April. Confirm the permitted filer under s 374 and the actual return and fee before filing. [Cap. 11.12, s 106](https://www.gov.ms/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/11.12-Companies-Act.pdf#page=80) · [Schedule 4](https://www.gov.ms/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/SRO-No.-16-of-2024-Companies-Regulations-2024.pdf#page=102)
- **Financial statements and audit.** S1 s 106(1)–(2) distinguishes reporting companies and prescribed non-profit obligations. The Act defines a reporting company as a large or public company. Section 2(9) uses either a gross revenue or asset value **exceeding** a prescribed threshold. SRO 16 reg 21 states gross revenue of $4 million or gross assets of $2 million and includes affiliates. It cross-refers to s 2(8), which addresses preferential creditors in both the enacted and revised Acts. Resolve the cross-reference and operative thresholds before classifying a company. For reporting companies, ss 107–112 cover financial statements, director approval, the auditor's report, sending copies to members and filing with the Registrar. Section 119 addresses appointment of an auditor, subject to s 120. Section 120 limits dispensing to a company other than a reporting company and requires all members' consent. Confirm the effect of the timing language in s 120(2) before relying on a resolution. [Cap. 11.12, s 2](https://www.gov.ms/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/11.12-Companies-Act.pdf#page=23) · [Cap. 11.12, ss 106–112](https://www.gov.ms/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/11.12-Companies-Act.pdf#page=80) · [Cap. 11.12, ss 119–120](https://www.gov.ms/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/11.12-Companies-Act.pdf#page=86) · [SRO 16, Part 3](https://www.gov.ms/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/SRO-No.-16-of-2024-Companies-Regulations-2024.pdf#page=17)

### Individual annual income tax

- **Residence and income.** Cap. 17.01 s 2 uses alternative tests for individuals. Check permanent abode and presence, including permitted absences, physical presence of at least 183 days in the basic year or the continuous-presence branch spanning a qualifying adjacent year. Section 3 covers specified income from Montserrat and elsewhere, with remittance, temporary presence and partly offshore trade provisos. Determine ordinary residence, domicile, source, remittance and any treaty entitlement before calculating foreign income. Section 4 generally uses the 12 months ending on 31 December immediately before the assessment year, subject to the Comptroller's provision for a different trade accounting date. Keep the annual assessment and the pay period separate. [Cap. 17.01, ss 2–4](https://www.gov.ms/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/17.01-Income-and-Incorporation-Tax-Act.pdf#page=10)
- **General deduction.** The amendment assented to on 31 July 2024 inserted EC$18,000 for assessment years commencing on or after 1 January 2024 in then s 34(1). The numbering in the 2025 revision is s 33(1)(g), following deletion of old s 21. It applies to a resident individual who completes the return and supplies the prescribed particulars. Check the conditions for any further allowances, including eligible social security contributions and residential mortgage interest. The 2024 return subtracts the EC$18,000 allowance before applying the printed bands. [Act 20 of 2024, ss 3–4](https://www.gov.ms/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Act-No.-20-of-2024-Income-and-Corporation-Tax-Amendment-Act.pdf#page=3) · [Cap. 17.01, ss 33–34](https://www.gov.ms/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/17.01-Income-and-Incorporation-Tax-Act.pdf#page=36) · [MCRS 2024 return, pp. 2 and 4](https://mcrs.ms/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Individual-Income-Tax-Return-Form-2024.pdf)
- **Pensions.** Section 35(b) sends pension income of a resident pensioner to Schedule 3. That schedule prints 0% up to EC$60,000 and 5% on the excess for assessment years starting on or after 1 January 2008. Establish the pension's statutory classification, any exemption, foreign pension treatment and the effect of other income separately. [Cap. 17.01, s 35 and Sch 3](https://www.gov.ms/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/17.01-Income-and-Incorporation-Tax-Act.pdf#page=56)

### Tax administration and outstanding evidence

- **Registration and records.** Cap. 17.26 ss 9–10 cover the TIN and registration no later than 30 days after commencing a covered activity if the person is unregistered. Section 20 requires qualifying taxpayers to keep sufficient records and underlying documents in Montserrat. Retain transaction records for 7 years from the transaction or longer if the assessment time limit remains open. Check the place-of-business rule and any approval. This retention clock is separate from the company accounts rule above. [Cap. 17.26, ss 9–10 and 20](https://www.gov.ms/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/17.26-Tax-Administration-Act.pdf#page=16)
- **Filing dates.** Cap. 17.01 s 43(1) sets 31 March unless another date is prescribed, and s 43(4) prints 28 February for employment or pension income. Income Tax Rules r 3 retains the older s 50 reference and separately prints 31 January for the public-revenue portion of public officers' and pensioners' income and 31 March for other cases. MCRS's individual page describes 31 January for all employment-only income and 31 March otherwise. Verify the operative prescription, taxpayer branch and current notice before setting a deadline. [Cap. 17.01, s 43](https://www.gov.ms/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/17.01-Income-and-Incorporation-Tax-Act.pdf#page=41) · [Income Tax Rules, r 3](https://www.gov.ms/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/17.01-Income-and-Incorporation-Tax-Act.pdf#page=71) · [MCRS individual guidance](https://mcrs.ms/individual-tax-information/)
- **Filing and payment mechanics.** Cap. 17.26 ss 19 and 22 cover closed office days, receipt rules, prescribed filing, signatures and amended returns within 6 years after the tax period ends.
