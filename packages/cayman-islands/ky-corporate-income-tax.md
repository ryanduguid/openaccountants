# Cayman Islands Corporate Income Tax

## Domestic corporate income and withholding in 2026

Cayman does not impose a domestic corporate income tax or a branch profits
tax. There is no domestic income tax withholding charge on dividends,
interest, royalties or ordinary fees for services paid to residents or
non-residents. A Cayman income tax rate, source test, return and foreign tax
credit are inapplicable to these payments because there is no underlying
Cayman income tax. This follows from the Government's statement on direct
taxation and the current statutory framework. The Tax Concessions Act
allows certain companies to apply for an undertaking against future taxes,
including withholding on defined payments. The undertaking can run for up
to 30 years. [Government tax overview][government] ·
[Tax Concessions Act, s 6][concessions]

Determine the character of the payment, where it arises and the recipient's
residence under the law of the jurisdiction that would impose the tax.
Check that jurisdiction's withholding rule, whether a specific agreement
covers the payment and who can claim relief. The absence of a Cayman charge
does not determine foreign withholding on payments to Cayman recipients.
Cayman company registration fees and property transaction duties are
separate charges. [UK–Cayman Arrangement, paras 2, 4 and 10–11][uk] ·
[Formation and annual fees](ky-company-formation.md) ·
[Property and transfer duties](cayman-tax.md#52-property-and-transfer-duties)

## Agreements and relief

| Agreement | Scope and effective period |
| --- | --- |
| United Kingdom | In force from 20 December 2010. HMRC gives 1 April 2011 for corporation tax, 6 April 2011 for income and capital gains tax and 20 December 2010 for other covered taxes. Paragraph 4 defines residence and treats a dual resident as UK resident for the arrangement. Paragraphs 5–9 cover specified individual income. Paragraph 10 permits source taxation of other income. The arrangement gives no specific dividend, interest or royalty rate. Paragraphs 11–12 govern credits and mutual agreement. [Arrangement][uk] · [HMRC effective dates][uk-status] |
| Japan | In force from 13 November 2011. Chapter 3 applies to amounts subject to withholding from 1 January 2012 and to other income for tax years beginning from that date. It covers individuals and addresses pensions, government service and students. Apply the Article 12 residence definition and Article 16 for mutual agreement. Chapter 2 concerns information exchange. [Agreement, arts 1–18][jp] · [Entry into force][jp-status] |
| Denmark, Faroe Islands, Finland, Greenland, Iceland, Norway and Sweden | Each has a separate limited arrangement for specified individual income. Check the relevant residence, income and commencement articles. There are separate instruments for related-party profit adjustments and, in some cases, international shipping and aviation. [Denmark, No. 10 of 2010][dk] · [Faroe Islands, Act No. 182, ss 10–13][fo] · [Finland, Treaty Series 34/2010][fi] · [Greenland agreement][gl] · [Iceland treaty register][is] · [Norway individual agreement][no] · [Sweden, SÖ 2009:49][se] |

For eligible UK-source pensions, use HMRC's DT-Individual form and its
Cayman instructions. Explain the basis of Cayman residence and address UK
residence in Part B.2. Leave question 2.b blank and send the claim directly
to HMRC. Paragraphs 7(2) and 8(2) distinguish private and
government pensions. For full UK pension relief under those provisions,
check continuous Cayman residence for the 6 years immediately before
pension payments began or the 6 years immediately before the relevant
employment began. For eligible Japanese payments, use the National Tax
Agency's prescribed application through the payer by the day before the
first payment and its refund procedure where withholding has already
occurred. Check the applicable article and required evidence for the
particular income. [UK–Cayman Arrangement, paras 4, 7 and 8][uk] ·
[HMRC DT-Individual, Appendix 1 box A][uk-claim] ·
[Japan agreement, arts 13–14][jp] · [NTA application][jp-claim] ·
[NTA refund][jp-refund]

For a taxation dispute, identify the applicable agreement's mutual agreement
procedure, competent authority and filing period. Article 16 of the Japan
agreement directs a claimant to the competent authority of their party of
residence within 3 years of first notification of the action. The Denmark
associated enterprises agreement also sets a 3-year period for the
procedure covering certain profit adjustments.
Ordinary tax information exchange is conducted between authorities under
its own instruments. The Tax Information Authority is the Cayman competent
authority and DITC carries out its functions. [Denmark associated
enterprises agreement, arts 3–4][dk-map] ·
[Japan agreement, art 16][jp] · [Sweden agreements][se-overview] ·
[Tax Information Authority Act][tia] · [DITC information exchange][eoir]

## Related parties and country-by-country reporting

There is no general Cayman corporate income tax base to adjust for transfer
pricing. Apply any relevant foreign transfer pricing law and check whether
a limited agreement gives access to an adjustment or mutual agreement
procedure. DITC does not require the OECD master file or local file in the
ordinary Cayman country-by-country reporting cycle. It can request similar
information in particular cases. [Government tax overview][government] ·
[Denmark associated enterprises agreement][dk-map] ·
[DITC CbCR Guidelines v1.3, IV.E][cbcr-guide]

- **Who files.** For a group with a Cayman ultimate parent, check the
  US$850 million consolidated group revenue threshold for the preceding
  fiscal year. An in-scope Cayman ultimate parent files the report.
  A Cayman surrogate parent files where the conditions in reg 4(2) apply.
  Other Cayman constituent entities have notification duties.
- **When and where.** For current periods, the initial or changed
  notification is due by the group's fiscal year end and the report
  within 12 months after that year end. DITC's guidance assigns performance
  of the notification to the reporting entity. Reporting moved to the
  DITC Portal on 3 December 2025. Keep relevant records for 6 years.
  A specific extension to 27 February 2026 covered 2024 fiscal year reports
  originally due from 31 July to 31 December 2025.
  [CbCR Regulations, regs 2, 4–6 and 10][cbcr] ·
  [DITC Guidelines, II–IV][cbcr-guide] · [DITC Portal advisory][cbcr-portal]

## Economic substance and financial account information

An entity carrying on a relevant activity that claims tax residence outside
Cayman must report that jurisdiction, financial year end and prescribed
parent and beneficial owner particulars in its annual notification and
provide supporting evidence. DITC's current guidance asks for evidence
of foreign tax residence and that the relevant income falls within that
jurisdiction's corporate income tax system. It describes separate evidence
for US disregarded entities and foreign branches. Check the current law
and DITC requirements for the year and activity. Cayman substance rules
can apply where the claim fails. [Economic Substance Act, s 7 and
Schedule][es] · [DITC guidance v3.2, II.A.2(c)][es-guide]

Cayman financial institutions classify themselves and their account holders
separately for CRS and US FATCA. Under the amended CRS due diligence rules
from 1 January 2026, obtain a valid self-certification for a new account at
or before opening, subject to the limited exception in CRS Section VII.
Check the entity's tax residences and status and, where required, obtain
controlling person information. Keep the required information or records
of the steps taken for at least 6 years from the end of the relevant year.
The amended return and compliance form provisions start on 1 January
2027. [SL 50 of 2025, regs 1 and 3–6][crs] ·
[DITC amended CRS quick guide][crs-quick]

Under the US Model 1 agreement in force from 1 July 2014, a reporting
Cayman financial institution identifies US reportable accounts under
Annex I and reports through the Cayman Tax Information Authority.
Check the registration, reporting and applicable US withholding
conditions in Article 4 separately. FATCA withholding on US-source
withholdable payments operates under US law. See the
[formation and reporting guide](ky-company-formation.md#accounts-audits-and-tax-cooperation)
for the 2026 DITC filing dates. [US Treasury status][fatca-status] ·
[US–Cayman agreement, arts 1–5 and Annex I][fatca]
