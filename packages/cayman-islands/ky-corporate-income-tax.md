# Cayman Islands Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate taxation in the Cayman Islands

The Cayman Islands imposes no corporate income tax, no capital gains tax, no withholding tax, and no branch profits tax. Companies instead pay annual government registration fees and, where in scope, must meet economic-substance and CRS/FATCA reporting obligations.

- **Corporate income tax rate** — 0% (no corporate income, capital gains, or other direct tax is imposed on corporations)  _(Cayman Islands Government, Finance and Economy (gov.ky archive) — https://cigarchives.gov.ky/economy ; PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cayman-islands/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Special / reduced corporate rates** — Not applicable (there is no corporate tax and therefore no industry or size-based rate variations)  _(Cayman Islands Government, Finance and Economy (gov.ky archive) — https://cigarchives.gov.ky/economy ; PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cayman-islands/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Corporate tax base** — Not applicable (no taxable income concept exists for corporations under Cayman law)  _(Cayman Islands Government, Finance and Economy (gov.ky archive) — https://cigarchives.gov.ky/economy ; PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cayman-islands/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends** — 0% (no withholding tax on dividends paid to residents or non-residents)  _(Cayman Islands Government, Finance and Economy (gov.ky archive) — https://cigarchives.gov.ky/economy ; PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cayman-islands/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Withholding tax on interest** — 0% (no withholding tax on interest payments)  _(Cayman Islands Government, Finance and Economy (gov.ky archive) — https://cigarchives.gov.ky/economy ; PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cayman-islands/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Withholding tax on royalties** — 0% (no withholding tax on royalty payments)  _(Cayman Islands Government, Finance and Economy (gov.ky archive) — https://cigarchives.gov.ky/economy ; PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cayman-islands/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Corporate income tax return / payment** — None (no corporate income tax return is filed and no corporate income tax is paid)  _(Cayman Islands Government, Finance and Economy (gov.ky archive) — https://cigarchives.gov.ky/economy ; PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cayman-islands/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Tax exemption certificate (exempted company)** — An exempted company may apply to the Financial Secretary for an undertaking that no later law imposing tax on profits, income, gains or appreciations will apply to it (s 6(1)); the undertaking may run for up to 30 years from approval (s 6(5)), and the standard grant is 20 years  _(Tax Concessions Act (2018 Revision), s 6(1) and (5) — https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/1964/1964-0164/1964-0164_2018%20Revision.pdf ; standard 20-year grant: PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/cayman-islands/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Economic substance obligation** — A relevant entity carrying on a relevant activity must satisfy the economic substance test (s 4(1)), notify the Tax Information Authority annually (s 7(1)) and file an economic substance return within twelve months after the end of each financial year (s 7(3)); the penalty for failing the test is CI$10,000, rising to CI$100,000 for a subsequent year (s 8(2) and (4))  _(International Tax Co-operation (Economic Substance) Act (2024 Revision), ss 4, 7 and 8 — https://legislation.gov.ky/cms/images/LEGISLATION/PRINCIPAL/2018/2018-0045/2018-0045_2024%20Revision.pdf)_
- **Annual government fee (in lieu of corporate tax)** — Exempted company with registered capital up to CI$42,000 (US$50,000): CI$925 annual fee (effective 1 January 2025); higher capital bands pay more CI$  _([Companies Act (2025 Revision) — Fees Regulations (Cayman Islands)](https://www.ciregistry.ky/fees/))_
