# Mongolia Payroll & Social Contributions

## Social insurance and payroll withholding

- **Employer social insurance contribution** — 12.5% to 14.5% of payroll, depending on industry/occupation risk classification percent  _(Social Insurance Law of Mongolia — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/mongolia/corporate/other-taxes)_
- **Employer contribution breakdown** — 8.5% pension, 1% benefit, 2% health, 0.2% unemployment, 0.8%–2.8% industrial accident/occupational disease percent  _(Social Insurance Law of Mongolia — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/mongolia/corporate/other-taxes)_
- **Employee social insurance contribution** — 11.5% of salary percent  _(Social Insurance Law of Mongolia — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/mongolia/corporate/other-taxes)_
- **Employee contribution monthly cap** — MNT 910,800 per month (cap applies once monthly salary exceeds MNT 7,920,000) MNT  _(Social Insurance Law of Mongolia — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/mongolia/corporate/other-taxes)_
- **Payroll PIT withholding (PAYE-equivalent)** — Employers withhold PIT at the progressive resident scale (10% to MNT 120 million, 15% to 180 million, 20% above, through 2026) and at a flat 20% for non-residents. The law of 26 June 2026 re-enacts art. 21.1: from 1 January 2027 a 0% band to MNT 9,504,000 is added (then 10% to 120 million, MNT 11,049,600 plus 15% to 180 million, MNT 20,049,600 plus 20% above), and from 1 January 2028 the band from MNT 9,504,000 to 24 million is taxed at 1%  _(Law on Personal Income Tax (Хувь хүний орлогын албан татварын тухай, 22 March 2019, consolidated on legalinfo.mn), art. 21.1 and 21.2.5 — https://legalinfo.mn/mn/detail?lawId=14410 ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/mongolia/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **PIT withholding remittance deadline** — By the 10th day of the following month  _(Personal Income Tax Law of Mongolia — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/mongolia/individual/tax-administration)_
- **Social insurance remittance** — Contributions are reported and paid monthly to the social insurance authority ((approx — confirm exact monthly due date))  _(Social Insurance Law of Mongolia — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/mongolia/corporate/other-taxes)_
