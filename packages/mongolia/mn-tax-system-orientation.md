# Mongolia tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the taxpayer, where income arose, residence facts and period. Check the applicable treaty and foreign-tax relief.

## Territorial reach

Individuals resident in Mongolia and enterprises based there generally account for taxable income earned in Mongolia, sourced from Mongolia and earned abroad. Nonresident individuals and foreign enterprises outside Mongolia generally account for income earned in or sourced from Mongolia. For a foreign enterprise with a Mongolian permanent establishment, identify the Mongolian income connected with that establishment's activities. Check the applicable treaty and foreign-tax relief. [Personal Income Tax Law, arts 5–6](https://legalinfo.mn/mn/detail?lawId=14410) · [Corporate Income Tax Law, arts 4–7 and 23](https://legalinfo.mn/mn/detail?lawId=14407)

## Residence

Apply the personal residence tests in statutory order. First check for at least 183 days of presence in Mongolia in 12 consecutive months, counting repeated stays in calendar days under art 5. Then check whether at least 50% of taxable income is earned in or sourced from Mongolia. Mongolian civil servants posted abroad and specified diplomatic or international organisation personnel have separate rules. An employment contract and permit showing the expected 183-day work period can support early registration.

An enterprise formed under Mongolian law is based in Mongolia. A foreign entity can also qualify if its governing body is treated as situated there. Check whether it meets at least 3 of the 5 statutory criteria covering shareholder residence, location of shareholder meetings and financial records, board residence and Mongolia-related sales. [Personal Income Tax Law, art 5](https://legalinfo.mn/mn/detail?lawId=14410) · [Corporate Income Tax Law, art 5](https://legalinfo.mn/mn/detail?lawId=14407)

## Taxpayer types

Identify the individual, business activity or enterprise before choosing the charge. A registered partnership that conducts business is an enterprise within the Corporate Income Tax Law. The enterprise definition can also cover entities without legal personality. Check the legal form, state registration and the relevant charging or exemption provisions. [Personal Income Tax Law, arts 5, 8 and 15](https://legalinfo.mn/mn/detail?lawId=14410) · [Corporate Income Tax Law, arts 4–5](https://legalinfo.mn/mn/detail?lawId=14407) · [Partnership Law](https://legalinfo.mn/mn/detail/419)

## Currency

Use Mongolian tögrög (MNT) for tax reporting. Transactions settled in another currency and recorded for the tax return are converted at the Bank of Mongolia's official rate on the transaction date. Keep the original amount and exchange-rate evidence. [General Tax Law, art 29(1)(6)](https://legalinfo.mn/mn/detail?lawId=14403)

## Assessment periods

Use the calendar year for ordinary annual income reporting. Enterprise financial statements run from 1 January to 31 December. Corporate interim reporting usually follows quarterly or half-year periods according to the prior year's taxable income and the applicable regime. Individual withholding and self-reported income have separate deadlines. [Accounting Law, art 10](https://legalinfo.mn/mn/detail?lawId=11191) · [Corporate Income Tax Law, art 26](https://legalinfo.mn/mn/detail?lawId=14407) · [Personal Income Tax Law, arts 25–27](https://legalinfo.mn/mn/detail?lawId=14410)

## Responsible authorities

The Mongolian Tax Administration and its territorial tax offices administer ordinary domestic taxes. Check the office responsible for registration and returns. Use the Mongolian Customs General Administration for import procedures. [General Tax Law, arts 29 and 76–77](https://legalinfo.mn/mn/detail?lawId=14403) · [Mongolian Tax Administration](https://mta.gov.mn/) · [General Administration of Customs](https://customs.gov.mn/)

## Fictional intake

A consultant comes to Mongolia for a short contract and receives enough local income that it could be half of their taxable income. Record days and source classifications separately and apply the personal residence tests in their statutory order.
