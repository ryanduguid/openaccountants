# Republic of Congo Payroll & Social Contributions

## Payroll, social security and salary taxes

- **Employee old-age pension (CNSS)** — 4% of gross salary, on a monthly ceiling of XAF 1,200,000  _(CLEISS, Les cotisations au Congo (taux au 1er janvier 2023) — https://www.cleiss.fr/docs/cotisations/congo.html)_
- **Employer old-age / invalidity / death insurance (CNSS)** — 8% of gross salary, on a monthly ceiling of XAF 1,200,000 (XAF 14,400,000 a year)  _(CLEISS, Les cotisations au Congo (taux au 1er janvier 2023) — https://www.cleiss.fr/docs/cotisations/congo.html)_
- **Employer family allowance contribution** — 10.03% of gross salary (family and maternity benefits), on a monthly ceiling of XAF 600,000 (XAF 7,200,000 a year); commercial summaries print 10.035%  _(CLEISS, Les cotisations au Congo (taux au 1er janvier 2023) — https://www.cleiss.fr/docs/cotisations/congo.html)_
- **Employer work-accident contribution** — 2.25% of gross salary, on a monthly ceiling of XAF 600,000 (XAF 7,200,000 a year)  _(CLEISS, Les cotisations au Congo (taux au 1er janvier 2023) — https://www.cleiss.fr/docs/cotisations/congo.html)_
- **Single Tax on Remuneration (employer)** — Taxe unique sur les salaires (TUS), created by Loi 39-2023 (finance law for 2024); the finance laws for 2025 and 2026 fix its split (27% to the State and the rest to FIGA, FONEA, ACPE, FNH, ADPME, ACPCE, the university and ANIRSJ) and have the CNSS collect the agency shares like social contributions. Rates of 7.5% for resident employees, 6% for non-resident non-seafarers and 2.5% for oil companies are those quoted by commercial summaries `[RESEARCH GAP — the rate text of Loi 39-2023 was not read; the gazette issue carrying it is not on sgg.cg]`  _(Loi n° 47-2024 du 30 décembre 2024 portant loi de finances pour 2025, Journal officiel n° 15-2024, point 26, art. 8 (nouveau) — https://www.sgg.cg/JO/2024/congo-jo-2024-15-sp.pdf; Loi n° 42-2025 du 31 décembre 2025 portant loi de finances pour 2026, Journal officiel édition spéciale n° 3-2026, CGI tome 1 point 23, art. 8 (nouveau) — https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-2.pdf)_
- **Pension contribution wage ceiling** — XAF 1,200,000 per month (XAF 14,400,000 a year) for the pension branch; XAF 600,000 per month for the family and work-accident branches  _(CLEISS, Les cotisations au Congo (taux au 1er janvier 2023) — https://www.cleiss.fr/docs/cotisations/congo.html)_
- **Salary tax withholding (PAYE-equivalent)** — IRPP and the Single Tax on Remuneration are withheld monthly by the employer at source  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI))_
- **Payroll tax / contribution remittance deadline** — CNSS contributions are declared before the 15th of each month (CNSS employer page); the ITS is withheld monthly by the employer (CGI art. 116 G(3), Loi 42-2025)  _(CNSS, Immatriculation employeur — https://www.cnss.cg/#/employeur; Loi n° 42-2025 du 31 décembre 2025, CGI tome 1 art. 116 G — https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-2.pdf)_
- **Regional (local) tax (taxe régionale)** — Annual per-capita tax on every person aged 18 or more habitually resident in Congo on 1 January; the rate is set each year by the commune, district or regional council (art. 326) and employers withhold it from January salaries (art. 327 b); the flat XAF 2,000 quoted by commercial summaries is not in the code `[RESEARCH GAP — reviewer to confirm the local tariff]`  _(Code Général des Impôts tome 1, édition du 28 mars 2021 (Ministère des finances, du budget et du portefeuille public), arts 321 to 327 — https://www.finances.gouv.cg/sites/default/files/documents/CGI%20Tome%20I.pdf)_
