# Republic of Congo Personal Income Tax

## Personal income tax (IRPP) bands and rates

- **IRPP overview** — Individuals are taxed under the Impôt sur le Revenu des Personnes Physiques (IRPP), levied on a progressive scale. Residents are taxed on worldwide income; non-residents on Congolese-source income.
- **IRPP schedule in the 2021 code (per family-quotient part)** — 1% to XAF 200,000; 15% from 200,001 to 800,000; 30% from 800,001 to 2,500,000; 45% from 2,500,001 to 8,000,000; 50% above 8,000,000  _(Code Général des Impôts tome 1, édition du 28 mars 2021 (Ministère des finances, du budget et du portefeuille public), art. 95 — https://www.finances.gouv.cg/sites/default/files/documents/CGI%20Tome%20I.pdf)_
- **IRPP schedule quoted for 2025 by commercial summaries** — 1% to XAF 464,000; 10% from 464,000 to 1,000,000; 25% from 1,000,000 to 3,000,000; 40% above 3,000,000 `[RESEARCH GAP — not read in a gazette text; the 2022 to 2024 finance laws that would carry the change are not on sgg.cg]`  _(Code Général des Impôts tome 1, édition du 28 mars 2021 (Ministère des finances, du budget et du portefeuille public), art. 95 (2021 text) — https://www.finances.gouv.cg/sites/default/files/documents/CGI%20Tome%20I.pdf)_
- **ITS schedule from 1 January 2026 (per family-quotient part, after the 20% deduction of art. 116)** — XAF 1,200 for the fraction to 615,000; 10% from 615,001 to 1,500,000; 15% from 1,500,001 to 3,500,000; 20% from 3,500,001 to 5,000,000; 30% above 5,000,000; minimum XAF 1,200 a year when the gross salary is below the SMIG; withheld monthly by the employer  _(Loi n° 42-2025 du 31 décembre 2025 portant loi de finances pour 2026, Journal officiel édition spéciale n° 3-2026, CGI tome 1 arts 116, 116 A, 116 B and 116 G — https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-2.pdf)_
- **IBA (business profits of individuals) from 1 January 2026** — 30% of taxable profit, with a minimum de perception of 1.5% of operating, financial and extraordinary products; declared by 20 May  _(Loi n° 42-2025 du 31 décembre 2025 portant loi de finances pour 2026, Journal officiel édition spéciale n° 3-2026, CGI tome 1 arts 93, 95 and 128 to 130 — https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-2.pdf)_
- **Tax-free threshold** — No fully exempt band — income from XAF 0 is taxed at 1%; family-quotient relief reduces tax for dependants (approx — confirm)  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI) — IRPP)_
- **Family quotient (quotient familial)** — Tax is computed by dividing taxable income by the number of family shares (parts) based on marital status and dependants (approx — confirm)  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI) — quotient familial)_
- **Standard deduction for employment income** — Flat deduction for professional expenses applied to gross salary before applying the barème (approx — confirm)  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI) — IRPP)_
- **Tax residence test** — Resident if habitual abode, principal home, or centre of economic interests is in Congo, or present more than 183 days (approx — confirm)  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI) — domicile fiscal)_
- **Withholding on income of non-residents** — 20% withheld at source on income of persons without domicile or fiscal residence in Congo (art. 96 of the 2021 code; art. 185 ter), and on services and royalties paid to non-residents from 2026 (art. 86 D)  _(Code Général des Impôts tome 1, édition du 28 mars 2021 (Ministère des finances, du budget et du portefeuille public), arts 96 and 185 ter — https://www.finances.gouv.cg/sites/default/files/documents/CGI%20Tome%20I.pdf; Loi n° 42-2025 du 31 décembre 2025 portant loi de finances pour 2026, Journal officiel édition spéciale n° 3-2026, CGI tome 1 art. 86 D — https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-2.pdf)_
- **Rate for foreign employees without Congolese domicile** — 20% final withholding in place of the schedule for persons taxable under art. 2 §3 (no domicile or fiscal residence in Congo); from 2026 salaries of non-domiciled employees are taxable when the work is done in Congo for an employer established there (art. 114 D(1)(b)), with no separate secondment rate in the new text  _(Code Général des Impôts tome 1, édition du 28 mars 2021 (Ministère des finances, du budget et du portefeuille public), art. 96 — https://www.finances.gouv.cg/sites/default/files/documents/CGI%20Tome%20I.pdf; Loi n° 42-2025 du 31 décembre 2025 portant loi de finances pour 2026, Journal officiel édition spéciale n° 3-2026, CGI tome 1 art. 114 D — https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-2.pdf)_
- **Flat regime for small taxpayers (régime du forfait, impôt global forfaitaire)** — Individuals whose turnover is below the VAT registration threshold are taxed under the forfait unless they opt for VAT or the real regime; regulated professions, bakers, building contractors, hardware dealers, wholesalers and importers are excluded; the 2021 text sets the impôt global forfaitaire at 8% of declared turnover with a multiplier of 1.00, 1.17 or 1.18 by VAT status; the 5% of turnover or 8% of margin quoted by commercial summaries was not found `[RESEARCH GAP — reviewer to confirm the current IGF rate]`  _(Loi n° 42-2025 du 31 décembre 2025 portant loi de finances pour 2026, Journal officiel édition spéciale n° 3-2026, CGI tome 1 arts 96 to 99 — https://sgg.cg/JO/2026/congo-jo-2026-3-2.pdf; Code Général des Impôts tome 1, édition du 28 mars 2021 (Ministère des finances, du budget et du portefeuille public), loi IGF art. 5 — https://www.finances.gouv.cg/sites/default/files/documents/CGI%20Tome%20I.pdf)_
- **Annual IRPP return filing deadline** — 20 March following the tax year (approx — confirm)  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI))_
- **Employment income collection** — IRPP on salaries is withheld monthly at source by the employer (retenue à la source)  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI))_
