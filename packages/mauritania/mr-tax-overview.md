# Mauritania Tax Overview

## Mauritania tax system at a glance

Mauritania taxes on a calendar-year basis in Mauritanian ouguiya (MRU). Direct and indirect taxes are governed by the General Tax Code (Code Général des Impôts), recently reformed to introduce a standalone Corporate Income Tax (IS) and a Business Profits Tax for Individuals (IBAPP).

- **Tax year** — Calendar year (1 January to 31 December)  _([Code Général des Impôts (General Tax Code)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/mauritania/corporate/tax-administration))_
- **Currency** — Mauritanian ouguiya (MRU)  _(Code Général des Impôts (General Tax Code))_
- **Tax authority** — Direction Générale des Impôts (DGI), Ministry of Finance ((approx — confirm exact authority name))  _(Code Général des Impôts (General Tax Code))_
- **Residence basis of taxation** — Individuals domiciled in Mauritania (a permanent home, or the centre of vital interests, art. 111(3)) are taxed on their remuneration even where the work is done abroad; employees domiciled abroad are taxed only on work done in Mauritania (art. 111(2)); PwC reports foreign-source income as exempt where it was already taxed abroad  _(General Tax Code, Law 2019-018 of 29 April 2019 as amended to the Finance Law for 2022 (Ministry of Finance official version, January 2023), art. 111(2) and (3) — https://finances.gov.mr/sites/default/files/2023-03/CGI-Fr-2023.pdf ; foreign income rule: PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/mauritania/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **Top personal/salary income tax rate** — 40% on monthly taxable remuneration above MRU 21,000 (art. 114)  _(General Tax Code, Law 2019-018 of 29 April 2019 as amended to the Finance Law for 2022 (Ministry of Finance official version, January 2023), art. 114 — https://finances.gov.mr/sites/default/files/2023-03/CGI-Fr-2023.pdf)_
- **Standard corporate income tax (IS) rate** — 25% of net taxable profit, or the turnover-based alternative where higher (art. 51)  _(General Tax Code, Law 2019-018 of 29 April 2019 as amended to the Finance Law for 2022 (Ministry of Finance official version, January 2023), art. 51 — https://finances.gov.mr/sites/default/files/2023-03/CGI-Fr-2023.pdf)_
- **Value Added Tax (VAT / TVA)** — Yes — standard rate 16% (art. 230)  _(General Tax Code, Law 2019-018 of 29 April 2019 as amended to the Finance Law for 2022 (Ministry of Finance official version, January 2023), art. 230, as amended by the Finance Law for 2022 — https://finances.gov.mr/sites/default/files/2023-03/CGI-Fr-2023.pdf)_
- **Annual tax return and financial statements deadline** — 31 March of the following year, filed with the first instalment (art. 58 for companies, art. 90 for individual business profits)  _(General Tax Code, Law 2019-018 of 29 April 2019 as amended to the Finance Law for 2022 (Ministry of Finance official version, January 2023), arts. 58 and 90 — https://finances.gov.mr/sites/default/files/2023-03/CGI-Fr-2023.pdf)_
- **Minimum corporate tax (normal real regime)** — IS due is the higher of 25% of net taxable profit and 2% of taxable products (excluding transfers and reversals of charges) for the normal real regime, or 2.5% for the intermediate regime, in the 2023 consolidated Code (art. 51); PwC reports 2.5% for the normal regime, which that edition does not show, so confirm against the latest finance law; the minimum payment for the normal regime is MRU 100,000 (art. 51)  _(General Tax Code, Law 2019-018 of 29 April 2019 as amended to the Finance Law for 2022 (Ministry of Finance official version, January 2023), art. 51 — https://finances.gov.mr/sites/default/files/2023-03/CGI-Fr-2023.pdf ; PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/mauritania/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
