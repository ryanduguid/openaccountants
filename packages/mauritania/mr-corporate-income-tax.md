# Mauritania Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax (IS) rates and base

- **IS overview** — Mauritania's reformed Corporate Income Tax (Impôt sur les Sociétés, IS) replaces the former industrial and commercial profits tax (BIC). Tax is generally the higher of a profit-based or a turnover-based amount, subject to a fixed minimum.
- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — The higher of 25% of net taxable profit and 2% of taxable products (excluding transfers and reversals of charges) for the normal real regime, or 2.5% for the intermediate regime, in the 2023 consolidated Code (art. 51); practitioner summaries report 2.5% for the normal regime, which that edition does not show, so confirm against the latest finance law  _(General Tax Code, Law 2019-018 of 29 April 2019 as amended to the Finance Law for 2022 (Ministry of Finance official version, January 2023), art. 51 — https://finances.gov.mr/sites/default/files/2023-03/CGI-Fr-2023.pdf)_
- **Minimum tax payment (normal real regime)** — MRU 100,000 (art. 51)  _(General Tax Code, Law 2019-018 of 29 April 2019 as amended to the Finance Law for 2022 (Ministry of Finance official version, January 2023), art. 51 — https://finances.gov.mr/sites/default/files/2023-03/CGI-Fr-2023.pdf)_
- **Turnover-based minimum tax (IMF)** — 2% of taxable products (excluding transfers and reversals of charges) for the normal real regime and 2.5% for the intermediate regime in the 2023 consolidated Code, due where it exceeds 25% of net profit (art. 51); practitioner summaries report 2.5% for the normal regime, so confirm against the latest finance law before relying on either figure  _(General Tax Code, Law 2019-018 of 29 April 2019 as amended to the Finance Law for 2022 (Ministry of Finance official version, January 2023), art. 51 — https://finances.gov.mr/sites/default/files/2023-03/CGI-Fr-2023.pdf)_
- **Normal real regime turnover threshold** — Annual turnover excluding taxes above MRU 5,000,000 (art. 44(1)); a company whose turnover falls below the limit moves to the intermediate regime only after two consecutive years below it (art. 44(2))  _(General Tax Code, Law 2019-018 of 29 April 2019 as amended to the Finance Law for 2022 (Ministry of Finance official version, January 2023), art. 44 — https://finances.gov.mr/sites/default/files/2023-03/CGI-Fr-2023.pdf)_
- **Tax base** — Net taxable profit (accounting profit adjusted for tax) under the actual-income regime  _(Code Général des Impôts (General Tax Code))_
- **Withholding tax on dividends** — 10%, the general rate of the tax on income from movable capital (art. 126); 20% applies instead to gains on securities where the seller is established in a privileged tax regime state  _(General Tax Code, Law 2019-018 of 29 April 2019 as amended to the Finance Law for 2022 (Ministry of Finance official version, January 2023), art. 126 — https://finances.gov.mr/sites/default/files/2023-03/CGI-Fr-2023.pdf)_
- **Withholding tax on interest** — 10% (art. 126)  _(General Tax Code, Law 2019-018 of 29 April 2019 as amended to the Finance Law for 2022 (Ministry of Finance official version, January 2023), arts. 121 and 126 — https://finances.gov.mr/sites/default/files/2023-03/CGI-Fr-2023.pdf)_
- **Withholding tax on royalties paid to non-residents** — 15%, within the withholding on sums due to persons not resident or established in Mauritania (art. 133(1))  _(General Tax Code, Law 2019-018 of 29 April 2019 as amended to the Finance Law for 2022 (Ministry of Finance official version, January 2023), art. 133(1) — https://finances.gov.mr/sites/default/files/2023-03/CGI-Fr-2023.pdf)_
- **Withholding tax on services paid to non-residents** — 15% of the sums due to persons not resident or established in Mauritania for services, including accessory costs (art. 133(1))  _(General Tax Code, Law 2019-018 of 29 April 2019 as amended to the Finance Law for 2022 (Ministry of Finance official version, January 2023), art. 133(1) — https://finances.gov.mr/sites/default/files/2023-03/CGI-Fr-2023.pdf)_
- **Withholding tax on liberal-profession services (residents)** — 2.5% on sums paid to liberal professionals subject to the individual business profits tax (art. 131)  _(General Tax Code, Law 2019-018 of 29 April 2019 as amended to the Finance Law for 2022 (Ministry of Finance official version, January 2023), art. 131 — https://finances.gov.mr/sites/default/files/2023-03/CGI-Fr-2023.pdf)_
- **Withholding tax on housing/commercial rents** — 18% of the rent: 10% property income tax plus 8% contribution on built property (art. 137(2))  _(General Tax Code, Law 2019-018 of 29 April 2019 as amended to the Finance Law for 2022 (Ministry of Finance official version, January 2023), art. 137(2) — https://finances.gov.mr/sites/default/files/2023-03/CGI-Fr-2023.pdf)_
- **Annual return and financial statements deadline** — 31 March of the following year, filed with the first instalment (art. 58)  _(General Tax Code, Law 2019-018 of 29 April 2019 as amended to the Finance Law for 2022 (Ministry of Finance official version, January 2023), art. 58 — https://finances.gov.mr/sites/default/files/2023-03/CGI-Fr-2023.pdf)_
- **CIT payment installments** — 40% with the return by 31 March, 30% by 30 June and the balance by 30 September (art. 58)  _(General Tax Code, Law 2019-018 of 29 April 2019 as amended to the Finance Law for 2022 (Ministry of Finance official version, January 2023), art. 58 — https://finances.gov.mr/sites/default/files/2023-03/CGI-Fr-2023.pdf)_
