# Czech Republic Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax rates and base

Czech resident companies are taxed on worldwide profits at a flat corporate income tax rate; the rate rose to 21% for periods starting in 2024. Certain entities and income types have special rates.

- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 21% of the tax base after deductible items, rounded down to whole thousands  _(Zákon č. 586/1992 Sb., o daních z příjmů (consolidated text on e-Sbírka), § 21 odst. 1 — https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/1992/586)_
- **Basic investment fund rate** — 5% (applies to qualifying basic investment funds)  _(Income Tax Act (Act No. 586/1992 Coll.), § 21(2) (5% for a basic investment fund), consolidated text on e-Sbírka — https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/1992/586)_
- **Pension fund rate** — 0%  _(Income Tax Act (Act No. 586/1992 Coll.), § 21(3) (0% for a pension company fund or pension insurance institution), consolidated text on e-Sbírka — https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/1992/586)_
- **Separate tax base (certain foreign-source dividends/income)** — 15%  _(Income Tax Act (Act No. 586/1992 Coll.), § 16a(1) and § 21(4) (15% on the separate tax base), consolidated text on e-Sbírka — https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/1992/586)_
- **Tax base** — Accounting profit per Czech accounting standards, adjusted for non-deductible items and tax adjustments  _(Income Tax Act (Act No. 586/1992 Coll.), § 23(1)-(2) (income less expenses for the period, derived from the accounting result and adjusted under the Act), consolidated text on e-Sbírka — https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/1992/586)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends** — 15% general; 35% to non-EU/EEA residents without a tax treaty or TIEA; 0% under EU Parent-Subsidiary exemption for qualifying holdings  _(Zákon č. 586/1992 Sb., o daních z příjmů (consolidated text on e-Sbírka), § 36 odst. 1 — https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/1992/586)_
- **Withholding tax on interest** — 15% general; 35% to non-EU/EEA residents without a treaty or TIEA; reductions/exemptions under treaties and EU Interest & Royalties Directive  _(Income Tax Act (Act No. 586/1992 Coll.), § 36(1)(a) and (c) (15%; 35% for payees who are not residents of an EU or EEA state or of a treaty or information-exchange partner), consolidated text on e-Sbírka — https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/1992/586)_
- **Withholding tax on royalties** — 15% general; 35% to non-EU/EEA residents without a treaty or TIEA; reductions/exemptions under treaties and EU Interest & Royalties Directive. Lower rates often apply to cultural royalties under particular treaties  _(Income Tax Act (Act No. 586/1992 Coll.), § 36(1)(a) and (c) (15%; 35% for payees outside the EU, EEA and treaty or information-exchange partners), consolidated text on e-Sbírka — https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/1992/586)_
- **Withholding tax on services provided in the Czech Republic** — Payments to a non-resident for **services provided within the Czech Republic** are subject to withholding unless a treaty provides otherwise. This head was missing from the guide until September 2026. The cited source states the obligation without stating a default rate; the 15% / 35% structure above is the natural reading but is **not confirmed here** — establish the rate against the Income Tax Act before deducting  _(Income Tax Act (Act No. 586/1992 Coll.), § 22(1)(c) (income from services, consultancy, management and intermediation performed in the Czech Republic) with § 36(1)(a), consolidated text on e-Sbírka — https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/1992/586)_
- **Withholding on gifts and consideration-free transfers** — Withholding also applies to the **provision of gifts and similar consideration-free transfers** out of a non-resident's Czech-source income, unless a treaty stipulates otherwise. An unusual head, and one a payer would not think to look for  _(Income Tax Act (Act No. 586/1992 Coll.), as reported `[RESEARCH GAP — the statute's text was not read for this change; the secondary summary that carried the figure is no longer cited]`)_
- **Exemptions on interest** — Beyond the EU Interest and Royalty Directive exemptions adopted into Czech law, interest on **government bonds** and on **foreign-issued bonds held by unrelated parties** is exempt  _(Income Tax Act (Act No. 586/1992 Coll.), as reported `[RESEARCH GAP — the statute's text was not read for this change; the secondary summary that carried the figure is no longer cited]`)_
- **Tax loss carryforward / carryback** — Losses may be carried forward 5 years; carryback allowed for up to 2 preceding years (capped at CZK 30 million)  _(Income Tax Act (Act No. 586/1992 Coll.), § 34(1) (loss deductible in the 2 preceding or the 5 following tax periods), consolidated text on e-Sbírka — https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/1992/586)_
- **Filing deadline** — 3 months after the end of the tax period; 4 months if filed electronically; 6 months if filed by a tax advisor or where a statutory audit is required  _(Tax Procedure Code (Act No. 280/2009 Coll.), § 136(1)-(2), consolidated text on e-Sbírka — https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2009/280)_
- **Payment deadline and advances** — Final tax due by the return deadline; advance payments (quarterly or semi-annual) required based on the prior period's tax liability  _(Tax Procedure Code (Act No. 280/2009 Coll.), § 135(3) (tax payable on the last day of the return deadline) — https://e-sbirka.gov.cz/sb/2009/280 ; advances: Income Tax Act § 38a (as reported; not re-read for this change))_
