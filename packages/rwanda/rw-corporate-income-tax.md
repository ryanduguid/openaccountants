# Rwanda Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax rates, base and deadlines

The standard corporate rate was reduced to 28% under the 2025 reform package, with reduced regimes for small businesses, micro-enterprises and registered investors. Companies make quarterly advance payments and file an annual return within three months of year-end.

- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 28% percent (Article 48 as replaced by Law 051/2023, in force on its publication on 14 September 2023; 30% before that)  _(Law nº 051/2023 of 05/09/2023 amending Law nº 027/2022 (Official Gazette nº Special of 14/09/2023), Rwanda Revenue Authority copy, Art. 2 (replacing Art. 48) — https://www.rra.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/LAW_AMENDING_THE_INCOME_TAX_LAW_of_2022.pdf ; Law nº 027/2022 of 20/10/2022 establishing taxes on income (Official Gazette nº Special of 28/10/2022), Rwanda Revenue Authority copy, Art. 48 — https://www.rra.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Income_Tax_law_of_2022.pdf)_
- **Small business turnover tax (turnover RWF 12m – 20m)** — 3% of annual turnover (lump-sum regime for small businesses with turnover between RWF 12,000,001 and RWF 20,000,000; the taxpayer may apply to opt into the real profit regime) percent  _(Law nº 027/2022 of 20/10/2022 establishing taxes on income (Official Gazette nº Special of 28/10/2022), Rwanda Revenue Authority copy, Arts 3 (5°), 14 and 49 (1°) — https://www.rra.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Income_Tax_law_of_2022.pdf)_
- **Micro-enterprise flat tax, turnover RWF 2m – 4m** — RWF 60,000 per year RWF  _(Law nº 027/2022 of 20/10/2022 establishing taxes on income (Official Gazette nº Special of 28/10/2022), Rwanda Revenue Authority copy, Arts 3 (6°), 14 (flat tax table) and 49 (2°) — https://www.rra.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Income_Tax_law_of_2022.pdf)_
- **Micro-enterprise flat tax, turnover RWF 4m – 7m** — RWF 120,000 per year RWF  _(Law nº 027/2022 of 20/10/2022 establishing taxes on income (Official Gazette nº Special of 28/10/2022), Rwanda Revenue Authority copy, Arts 3 (6°), 14 (flat tax table) and 49 (2°) — https://www.rra.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Income_Tax_law_of_2022.pdf)_
- **Micro-enterprise flat tax, turnover RWF 7m – 10m** — RWF 210,000 per year RWF  _(Law nº 027/2022 of 20/10/2022 establishing taxes on income (Official Gazette nº Special of 28/10/2022), Rwanda Revenue Authority copy, Arts 3 (6°), 14 (flat tax table) and 49 (2°) — https://www.rra.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Income_Tax_law_of_2022.pdf)_
- **Micro-enterprise flat tax, turnover RWF 10m – 12m** — RWF 300,000 per year RWF  _(Law nº 027/2022 of 20/10/2022 establishing taxes on income (Official Gazette nº Special of 28/10/2022), Rwanda Revenue Authority copy, Arts 3 (6°), 14 (flat tax table) and 49 (2°) — https://www.rra.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Income_Tax_law_of_2022.pdf)_
- **Reduced rate for company listing 30%+ of shares to the public** — 25% for five years from the date of listing on the Rwandan capital market percent  _(Law nº 051/2023 of 05/09/2023 amending Law nº 027/2022 (Official Gazette nº Special of 14/09/2023), Rwanda Revenue Authority copy, Art. 2 (Art. 48 (2)(b)) — https://www.rra.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/LAW_AMENDING_THE_INCOME_TAX_LAW_of_2022.pdf)_
- **Reduced rate for company listing 40%+ of shares to the public** — 20% for five years from the date of listing on the Rwandan capital market percent  _(Law nº 051/2023 of 05/09/2023 amending Law nº 027/2022 (Official Gazette nº Special of 14/09/2023), Rwanda Revenue Authority copy, Art. 2 (Art. 48 (2)(a)) — https://www.rra.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/LAW_AMENDING_THE_INCOME_TAX_LAW_of_2022.pdf)_
- **Corporate tax base** — Business profit of taxpayers other than individuals; a resident company (established under Rwandan law or with its place of effective management in Rwanda) is taxed on its profit wherever earned, with a credit for foreign tax, while non-residents and permanent establishments are taxed on Rwandan-source profit only  _(Law nº 027/2022 of 20/10/2022 establishing taxes on income (Official Gazette nº Special of 28/10/2022), Rwanda Revenue Authority copy, Arts 4, 6, 7 and 43 — https://www.rra.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Income_Tax_law_of_2022.pdf)_
- **Domestic withholding tax on dividends, interest, royalties and service fees** — 15% percent (standard non-treaty rate on dividends, interest, royalties, service fees and the other payments listed in Article 60; dividends covered by Article 47 (2) and distributions by collective investment schemes are excepted)  _(Law nº 027/2022 of 20/10/2022 establishing taxes on income (Official Gazette nº Special of 28/10/2022), Rwanda Revenue Authority copy, Art. 60 — https://www.rra.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Income_Tax_law_of_2022.pdf)_
- **Reduced WHT on dividends/interest from listed securities** — 5% percent (dividends and interest on securities listed on the Rwandan capital market where the beneficiary is a resident taxpayer or a resident of an East African Community partner state, and interest on treasury bonds of three years or more)  _(Law nº 027/2022 of 20/10/2022 establishing taxes on income (Official Gazette nº Special of 28/10/2022), Rwanda Revenue Authority copy, Art. 60 — https://www.rra.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Income_Tax_law_of_2022.pdf)_
- **WHT on public tender / public procurement payments** — 3% of the invoice amount excluding VAT (15% if the supplier is not registered with the Tax Administration or has no recent income tax declaration) percent  _(Law nº 027/2022 of 20/10/2022 establishing taxes on income (Official Gazette nº Special of 28/10/2022), Rwanda Revenue Authority copy, Art. 63 — https://www.rra.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Income_Tax_law_of_2022.pdf)_
- **Quarterly advance CIT instalments** — Paid by 30 June, 30 September and 31 December of the year of activity (the last day of the sixth, ninth and twelfth month for an approved non-calendar tax period), each instalment computed from the previous year's tax and turnover  _(Law nº 027/2022 of 20/10/2022 establishing taxes on income (Official Gazette nº Special of 28/10/2022), Rwanda Revenue Authority copy, Art. 33 — https://www.rra.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Income_Tax_law_of_2022.pdf)_
- **Annual CIT return and final payment deadline** — Declaration and payment of the balance not later than 31 March of the year following the tax period (within three months after the end of an approved non-calendar period)  _(Law nº 027/2022 of 20/10/2022 establishing taxes on income (Official Gazette nº Special of 28/10/2022), Rwanda Revenue Authority copy, Arts 9 and 10 — https://www.rra.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Income_Tax_law_of_2022.pdf)_
