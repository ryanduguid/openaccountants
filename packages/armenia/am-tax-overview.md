# Armenia Tax Overview

## Armenian tax system at a glance

- **Overview narrative** — Armenia operates a calendar-year tax system administered by the State Revenue Committee. Personal income tax is a flat 20%, corporate income tax is 18%, and a 20% VAT applies. Residents are taxed on worldwide income; non-residents on Armenian-source income only.  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Armenia (HO-165-N), English text on the Armenian Legal Information System (ARLIS), Arts 6, 22, 25, 63, 125 and 150 — https://www.arlis.am/en/acts/205620)_
- **Tax year** — Calendar year (1 January – 31 December)  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Armenia (HO-165-N), English text on the Armenian Legal Information System (ARLIS), Art. 13 — https://www.arlis.am/en/acts/205620)_
- **Currency** — Armenian dram (AMD)  _(Central Bank of Armenia Law — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/armenia)_
- **Tax authority** — State Revenue Committee of the Republic of Armenia (SRC)  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Armenia (HO-165-N), English text on the Armenian Legal Information System (ARLIS), Art. 4(12) — https://www.arlis.am/en/acts/205620)_
- **Residence basis of taxation** — Resident organisations (registered in Armenia) and resident natural persons (183 or more days of presence, or a centre of vital interests in Armenia) are taxed on worldwide income; non-residents on Armenian-source income only  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Armenia (HO-165-N), English text on the Armenian Legal Information System (ARLIS), Arts 22, 25, 104 and 105 — https://www.arlis.am/en/acts/205620)_
- **Headline personal income tax rate** — 20% (flat rate since 1 January 2023, after 23% in 2020, 22% in 2021 and 21% in 2022)  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Armenia (HO-165-N), English text on the Armenian Legal Information System (ARLIS), Art. 150(1) — https://www.arlis.am/en/acts/205620)_
- **Headline corporate income tax rate** — 18%  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Armenia (HO-165-N), English text on the Armenian Legal Information System (ARLIS), Art. 125(1) — https://www.arlis.am/en/acts/205620)_
- **Does VAT exist** — Yes; VAT applies at a standard rate of 20%  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Armenia (HO-165-N), English text on the Armenian Legal Information System (ARLIS), Art. 63(1) — https://www.arlis.am/en/acts/205620)_
- **Annual corporate tax return deadline** — 20 April (inclusive) following the tax year  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Armenia (HO-165-N), English text on the Armenian Legal Information System (ARLIS), Art. 134(1) — https://www.arlis.am/en/acts/205620)_
- **Annual personal income tax return deadline** — 1 November 2026 for tax year 2025, a transitional window running from 2 March. From tax year 2026 the window closes on **1 July** of the following year. Universal declaration reaches every adult resident from 2026, so the population filing this return is far larger than the "where a return is required" case  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Armenia; State Revenue Committee universal declaration schedule — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/armenia/individual/tax-administration)_
- **Net wealth, inheritance and gift taxes** — None; the Code's closed list of state and local taxes has no net wealth, inheritance or gift tax  _(Tax Code of the Republic of Armenia (HO-165-N), English text on the Armenian Legal Information System (ARLIS), Art. 6 — https://www.arlis.am/en/acts/205620)_
