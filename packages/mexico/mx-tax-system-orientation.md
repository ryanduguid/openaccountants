# Mexico tax system

> General orientation for 2026. Confirm the taxpayer, accounting period and transaction with a qualified local tax professional before applying these rules.

Amounts are in Mexican pesos (MXN). The Servicio de Administración Tributaria (SAT) administers federal income tax (ISR) and value added tax (IVA). Use the tax year and taxpayer category to select the applicable rule. [Income Tax Law](https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/pdf/LISR.pdf) · [VAT Law](https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/pdf/LIVA.pdf)

## Income tax

- **Individuals and residence.** An individual with a home in Mexico is generally resident. If the individual also has a home in another country, Mexico tests the centre of vital interests, including whether more than half of calendar-year income is Mexican-source or the person's principal professional activity is in Mexico. Special residence rules can also apply. Residents are generally taxed on worldwide income. Non-residents are taxed on profits attributable to a Mexican permanent establishment and on other Mexican-source income under the applicable rules. For the ordinary 2026 individual annual calculation, use Annex 8 section C.II, with marginal rates from 1.92% to 35%. The 35% band starts at taxable income of MXN 5,107,703.93. Use the applicable schedule for periodic provisional payments and withholding. [Federal Tax Code, art. 9](https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/pdf/CFF.pdf) · [Income Tax Law, arts. 1 and 152](https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/pdf/LISR.pdf) · [2026 Miscellaneous Tax Resolution, Annex 8 C.II](https://www.sat.gob.mx/minisitio/NormatividadRMFyRGCE/documentos2026/rmf/anexos/Anexo-8-RMF-2026_DOF-28122025.pdf)
- **Companies.** Legal entities with their principal business administration or effective management in Mexico are generally resident. For a company under the ordinary regime the 2026 annual income tax rate is 30% of its taxable result. Special regimes and incentives have separate conditions. [Federal Tax Code, art. 9](https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/pdf/CFF.pdf) · [SAT, Income Tax Law art. 9](https://wwwmat.sat.gob.mx/articulo/93578/articulo-9)

## VAT and filing

- **VAT.** The standard rate is 16% on covered sales, independent services, temporary use of goods and imports. Check separate zero-rating and exemptions. Qualifying northern and southern border transactions may receive a credit that produces an 8% rate, subject to the respective decrees and SAT requirements. The 31 December 2025 decree continued those incentives through 31 December 2026. Ordinary monthly domestic VAT returns are generally due by the 17th of the following month. [VAT Law, arts. 1, 2-A and 5-D](https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/pdf/LIVA.pdf) · [Border extension decree, arts. 1 and 2](https://www.sat.gob.mx/minisitio/EstimulosFiscalesFronteraNorteSur/documentos/Decreto_modifica_diversos_estimulos_fiscales_region_fronteriza_norte_sur.pdf) · [SAT border invoicing guidance](https://wwwmat.sat.gob.mx/cs/Satellite?c=ConsultaInfo&childpagename=SatTyR%2FConsultaInfo%2FSAT_LandingConsultaInformacion&cid=1462233322035&packedargs=d%3DTouch&pagename=TySWrapper)
- **Income tax filing.** An ordinary company files and pays within 3 months of year end. Individuals required to file generally do so in April of the following year. For calendar 2026 this usually means by 31 March 2027 for an ordinary company and during April 2027 for an individual, subject to any applicable filing exception or extension. Check separate provisional payments and employer withholding. [SAT, Income Tax Law art. 9](https://wwwmat.sat.gob.mx/articulo/93578/articulo-9) · [SAT, Income Tax Law art. 150](https://wwwmat.sat.gob.mx/articulo/83662/articulo-150)
