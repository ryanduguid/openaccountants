# Turks and Caicos Islands destination management fee reference

Load `workflow-base`. Identify the arrival date, transport mode and carrier, and establish each person's status from the journey records. This guide traces the original fee and the 2 specified 2026 amendments. Apply the applicable version and any later instruments to the actual journey. [Ordinance 12 of 2023](https://agc.gov.tc/agc/component/edocman/12-of-2023-destination-management-fee-ordinance-2023/fdocument?Itemid=9999) · [Act 4 of 2026](https://gov.tc/agc/component/edocman/4-of-2026-destination-management-fee-amendment-act-2026/fdocument?Itemid=9999) · [Act 14 of 2026](https://gov.tc/agc/component/edocman/14-of-2026-destination-management-fee-amendment-no-2-act-2026/fdocument?Itemid=9999)

## Select the law by arrival date

The original Ordinance was published on 30 June 2023. Section 1 required a separate commencement notice, and Legal Notice 75 of 2023 appointed 27 November 2023. Act 4 of 2026 commenced on Gazette publication on 16 March 2026. Act 14 of 2026 commenced on Gazette publication on 27 July 2026. Its historical validation provisions address specified fees collected or deducted during earlier periods. [Ordinance 12 of 2023 s 1](https://agc.gov.tc/agc/component/edocman/12-of-2023-destination-management-fee-ordinance-2023/fdocument?Itemid=9999) · [Legal Notice 75 of 2023](https://agc.gov.tc/agc/component/edocman/75-of-2023-destination-management-fee-ordinance-2023-commencement-notice-2023/fdocument?Itemid=9999) · [Act 4 of 2026 s 1](https://gov.tc/agc/component/edocman/4-of-2026-destination-management-fee-amendment-act-2026/fdocument?Itemid=9999) · [Act 14 of 2026 ss 1 and 4](https://gov.tc/agc/component/edocman/14-of-2026-destination-management-fee-amendment-no-2-act-2026/fdocument?Itemid=9999)

## Classify passengers before counting the fee

1. Section 3 and Schedule 1 specify $10 per chargeable arriving passenger on an air carrier, vessel or general aviation flight. Section 5 excludes qualifying cruise passengers arriving on a cruise ship at the dock. Check that the terms in the referenced cruise ship law cover the actual journey. [Ordinance 12 of 2023 ss 3–5 and Schedule 1](https://agc.gov.tc/agc/component/edocman/12-of-2023-destination-management-fee-ordinance-2023/fdocument?Itemid=9999)
2. Under the original Schedule 2, the exemptions cover a child under 10 and domestic travel by flight or vessel within the Islands. From 16 March 2026, Act 4 substitutes under 2 for under 10 and adds transit in the Islands of up to and including 3 hours. A child aged exactly 2 does not qualify for the age exemption in the amended schedule. Test the separate domestic and transit exemptions. [Ordinance 12 of 2023 s 10 and Schedule 2](https://agc.gov.tc/agc/component/edocman/12-of-2023-destination-management-fee-ordinance-2023/fdocument?Itemid=9999) · [Act 4 of 2026 s 5](https://gov.tc/agc/component/edocman/4-of-2026-destination-management-fee-amendment-act-2026/fdocument?Itemid=9999)
3. From 16 March 2026, section 2 defines a passenger separately from a crew member and section 3(2) excludes crew. Establish whether the person is actually employed in the working or service of the aircraft or vessel arriving in the Islands. The definition includes the captain, master or officer and expressly covers general aviation. A crew list alone does not settle the test. [Act 4 of 2026 ss 3–4](https://gov.tc/agc/component/edocman/4-of-2026-destination-management-fee-amendment-act-2026/fdocument?Itemid=9999)

## Collection, daily returns and remittance

Under section 4, the air carrier, owner, master or agent of a vessel, or general aviation handling agent collects the fee under the applicable branch. When collected with ticket issuance, account for the fee and show it separately from the fare. Section 6 keeps the proceeds separate from the carrier's funds. An IATA member air carrier remits via IATA, from which the Airports Authority collects. Otherwise the carrier remits to the fixed base operator or the Collector referred to in customs law, as applicable. Verify the current recipient and submission procedure for the particular route. [Ordinance 12 of 2023 ss 4 and 6](https://agc.gov.tc/agc/component/edocman/12-of-2023-destination-management-fee-ordinance-2023/fdocument?Itemid=9999)

Under sections 7–8, submit a daily return and passenger manifest for each arriving flight or vessel, using the particulars in Schedule 3. Section 7 names the Airports Authority, the Collector under the Customs Ordinance or the fixed base operator as recipient, according to the applicable route. Confirm the competent recipient and current submission channel. Reconcile the counts and charges to the separate collection account. Under section 6(5), the remittance period is 30 days from the report under section 7. Section 9 provides for adjustments, including a collection or refund, when a change to the itinerary requires them. Section 11 requires the carrier to retain collection records for 7 years. [Ordinance 12 of 2023 ss 6–11 and Schedule 3](https://agc.gov.tc/agc/component/edocman/12-of-2023-destination-management-fee-ordinance-2023/fdocument?Itemid=9999)

## Fund deposits and historical validation

Act 14 amended section 12 from 27 July 2026. The proceeds go to the Destination Enhancement Fund. Before deposit, the carrier or IATA may deduct reasonable administrative fees incurred in collecting, remitting or administering the fee where Cabinet has approved them. Fees collected from IATA by the Airports Authority under section 6(3) are subject to the Permanent Secretary, Finance's instructions under public finance law and go into the Fund. Obtain the applicable approval and instructions before computing a net deposit. [Act 14 of 2026 s 3](https://gov.tc/agc/component/edocman/14-of-2026-destination-management-fee-amendment-no-2-act-2026/fdocument?Itemid=9999)

Section 4 validates specified amounts already collected or deducted. Keep each category separate from the liability and refund analysis for the underlying journey. [Act 14 of 2026 s 4](https://gov.tc/agc/component/edocman/14-of-2026-destination-management-fee-amendment-no-2-act-2026/fdocument?Itemid=9999)

| Validated category | Period in section 4 |
| --- | --- |
| Fees collected for arriving passengers | From 1 August 2023 until 27 November 2023 |
| Fees collected for children over 2 and under 10 | From 27 November 2023 until 16 March 2026 |
| Administrative fees deducted in collecting and remitting under section 6 | From 1 April 2025 to the date of the Act |

Section 4(4) bars the stated claims, proceedings or refund demands for the amounts validated against the named parties. Check the fact of collection or deduction, exact dates, age boundaries and party before applying it. Keep the ordinary section 9 itinerary adjustments separate. [Act 14 of 2026 s 4](https://gov.tc/agc/component/edocman/14-of-2026-destination-management-fee-amendment-no-2-act-2026/fdocument?Itemid=9999) · [Ordinance 12 of 2023 s 9](https://agc.gov.tc/agc/component/edocman/12-of-2023-destination-management-fee-ordinance-2023/fdocument?Itemid=9999)

## Fictional calculations

The examples isolate the charge under the selected enactments. They assume Schedule 1 applies with no other instrument changing the amount for the journey.

### Original age exemption

On 1 February 2025, 4 passengers on a qualifying international flight arrive for a stay in the Islands. They are aged 1, 9, 10 and 35. The first 2 qualify for the original under-10 exemption. The other 2 pay $10 each, for $20. Check the later validation separately if a payment was already collected from a child in a validated class. [Ordinance 12 of 2023 ss 3 and 10 and Schedules 1–2](https://agc.gov.tc/agc/component/edocman/12-of-2023-destination-management-fee-ordinance-2023/fdocument?Itemid=9999) · [Act 14 of 2026 s 4](https://gov.tc/agc/component/edocman/14-of-2026-destination-management-fee-amendment-no-2-act-2026/fdocument?Itemid=9999)

```oa-check
name: Original age threshold on international arrival
inputs:
  arriving_passengers: 4
  under_ten: 2
  fee_per_passenger: 10
steps:
  - chargeable_passengers: arriving_passengers - under_ten
  - destination_fee: chargeable_passengers * fee_per_passenger
expected:
  chargeable_passengers: 2
  destination_fee: 20
```

### Amended age, transit and crew rules

On 2 April 2026, a qualifying international flight carries 4 passengers and 1 working crew member on arrival. The passengers include a 1-year-old, a 2-year-old, an adult in transit in the Islands for exactly 3 hours and an adult staying in the Islands. The 1-year-old and the transit passenger qualify for separate exemptions. After excluding the crew member, 2 passengers pay $10 each, for $20. [Ordinance 12 of 2023 ss 3 and 10 and Schedules 1–2](https://agc.gov.tc/agc/component/edocman/12-of-2023-destination-management-fee-ordinance-2023/fdocument?Itemid=9999) · [Act 4 of 2026 ss 3–5](https://gov.tc/agc/component/edocman/4-of-2026-destination-management-fee-amendment-act-2026/fdocument?Itemid=9999)

```oa-check
name: Amended eligibility at the age and transit boundaries
inputs:
  persons_on_arrival: 5
  working_crew: 1
  under_two: 1
  transit_within_limit: 1
  fee_per_passenger: 10
steps:
  - chargeable_passengers: persons_on_arrival - working_crew - under_two - transit_within_limit
  - destination_fee: chargeable_passengers * fee_per_passenger
expected:
  chargeable_passengers: 2
  destination_fee: 20
```

## Remaining coverage

Confirm any later changes, the cruise and customs cross-references, acceptable records and the current return and payment channels. Obtain the actual instructions and approvals before applying fund deductions. The examples do not determine the liability, refund rights or remittance amount for an actual journey.
