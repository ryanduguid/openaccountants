#### Self-employed choices and literal boundaries

NI Contributions 15 offers elected weekly incomes 100, 200, 400 or 600, subject to the appointed-day age-over-50 restriction, age-54 freeze/later-entry restriction, annual election deadline, whole-year effect and adjacent-category movement rule. Missing election can invoke the specified prior-election or Director-investigation rule; it does not permit choosing the greater of profit and a guessed default. Preserve the complete restriction and actual election evidence before calculating.

NHI Contributions 12 permits the CEO, after investigation/financial statement/nature of activity or other means, to allow 150 where monthly net income is at least 2,000 but below 3,000; 75 where at least 1,000 but below 1,999; or 50 where at most 999. These are discretionary alternatives to 250, not automatic progressive bands. The intervals above 999 but below 1,000 and from 1,999 to below 2,000 are not covered by those literal concessions. Do not close them by rounding or rewriting a boundary. The net-income method and actual allowance must be established.

### Source and category gates

Before selecting a method, establish requested period, applicable source versions and later amendments, contribution week/month, contributor status, age, legal residence, actual program registration and any election, certificate, concession or determination. Keep only redacted documents and stable contributor references in agent workpapers. Identifiers, identity documents, portal access and original evidence remain with the reviewer; record readiness and sighting status.

NI entry between ages 16 and 65 and lifetime insured status do not by themselves settle contribution liability. Principal s 2 says 'over' an age means the person has reached it. Schedule 1 covers the specified service/apprenticeship, ship/aircrew and House-member branches, subject to all 6 exceptions. These include the specified foreign-force/diplomatic, spouse-service, nonresident employer/worker, occasional charitable/school and unpaid family-employment cases. Schedule 2 covers the specified own-account/control/spouse-business branches and excludes ordinary work under 5 hours per contribution week. Do not infer employee status from a director title, nationality or a registration number. [S1 ss 2–4 and Schedules 1–2]

NI public officer means the Government employee meeting the full-salary sick-leave condition of at least 3 months in the specified 12-month period. The temporary class requires all residence/immigration, not-over-180-days-in-12-months, outside-residence and employer-election conditions. Its definition points literally to reg 4(4), whilst the election appears in 3(4); that election points to Registration 5 whilst the temporary notification is in Registration 4. A notification is not automatically a valid election. Exceeding 180 days can restore ordinary contributions and reg 24 charges from the initial employment date. [S1 Contributions 2–3, Registration 4–5]

For NHI, each employer, employee and self-employed person/partnership partner follows its own statutory branch. Employer includes Government. Dependant, Islander, legal-resident, pensioner and student categories have separate predicates. Benefit Eligibility regulations do not establish a universal exemption from contribution liability for every excluded benefit category. Employed spouses contribute separately. NI and NHI categories cannot be substituted for each other. [S7 ss 2–7; Contributions 3 and 11–19; Eligibility 1–3]

#### NI age conflict

S2 substitutes Contributions 4(4) with 1.2% employer-only for an insured person 'over the age of sixty years'. The retained Contributions 22 still uses under 16/over 65, with its employed-over-65 exception; the earlier 4(4) used 65. The 60–64 overlap must be referred for authoritative reconciliation. Do not silently replace 60 with 65, select the larger rate as a legal default or generalise a reduced-rate rule to all programs. [S1 Contributions 22; S2 replacement 4(4)]
