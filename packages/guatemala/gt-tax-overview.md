# Guatemala Tax Overview

The Superintendencia de Administración Tributaria (SAT) administers national taxes in Guatemalan quetzales (GTQ). The ordinary income tax year runs from 1 January to 31 December. [Tax Update Law, Decree 10-2012, book I](https://www.minfin.gob.gt/images/archivos/despacho/decreto10_ag134_ag133.pdf) and [SAT filing guidance](https://en.portal.sat.gob.gt/portal/preguntas-frecuentes/cumplimiento-tributario/).

## Residence and income source

The income tax (ISR) applies to Guatemalan-source income earned by residents and non-residents. For ordinary residence tests, an individual is resident if present for more than 183 days in the calendar year, even if those days are non-consecutive, or if Guatemala is the centre of their economic interests, subject to proof of residence or fiscal domicile abroad under that test. Article 4 includes specified cross-border receipts in Guatemalan-source income. Check the source rule for the type of income before deciding how it is taxed. [Decree 10-2012, arts 3–6](https://www.minfin.gob.gt/images/archivos/despacho/decreto10_ag134_ag133.pdf).

## Income tax and filing

- **Employment.** The annual rate is 5% on taxable employment income up to GTQ 300,000, then GTQ 15,000 plus 7% of the excess. Article 72 allows an ordinary GTQ 48,000 personal deduction without proof, with further deductions subject to their own requirements. Employers project annual earnings and withhold ISR. An employee who received no withholding or insufficient withholding must file and pay the balance within the first 3 months of the following calendar year. [Decree 10-2012, arts 72–75 and 81](https://www.minfin.gob.gt/images/archivos/despacho/decreto10_ag134_ag133.pdf).
- **2026 employment deduction.** Add GTQ 3,024 to the deductions permitted under article 72 for the January–December 2026 tax period. This additional deduction requires no proof. Article 4 took effect on 23 May 2026 and requires the employer to update the annual projection in the following month. Articles 1–3 take effect from 1 January 2027. [Decree 13-2026, arts 4–5](https://www.congreso.gob.gt/detalle_pdf/decretos/13702) and [official gazette notice](https://dca.gob.gt/noticias-guatemala-diario-centro-america/oficializan-que-salario-minimo-esta--exento-de-isr/).
- **Business profit regime.** The ordinary taxable-profit regime for business activities applies 25% to net taxable profits. The annual return and settlement fall within the first 3 months of the next calendar year, ordinarily by 31 March. The optional simplified regime applies separately at 5% up to GTQ 30,000 of monthly taxable revenue and 7% on the excess. Check the taxpayer's registration and the applicable withholding and return rules. [Decree 10-2012, arts 36–39 and 43–46](https://www.minfin.gob.gt/images/archivos/despacho/decreto10_ag134_ag133.pdf) and [SAT filing guidance](https://en.portal.sat.gob.gt/portal/preguntas-frecuentes/cumplimiento-tributario/).

## Value added tax

The standard IVA rate is 12% of the taxable base, included in the sale price. The ordinary monthly return and any payment are due within the calendar month after the period. Check separately for exemptions, special collection obligations and small taxpayer regimes. [IVA Law, Decree 27-92, arts 10 and 40](https://www.minfin.gob.gt/images/archivos/leyes/tesoreria/Decretos/DECRETO%20DEL%20CONGRESO%2027-92.pdf).

Confirm the tax period and regime before using a rate or deadline. Use the separate country guides for payroll, social contributions, company formation and detailed computations.
