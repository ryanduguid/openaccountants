# British Virgin Islands Company Formation & Entity Choice

## Entity types, incorporation and annual compliance

The most common BVI vehicle is the Business Company (BC) incorporated under the BVI Business Companies Act, 2004, used widely for holding and investment structures. There is no minimum share capital requirement, incorporation is typically completed in a few business days through a licensed registered agent, and ongoing compliance centres on annual government fees, a registered agent, economic substance and the annual financial return.

- **Most common entity type** — BVI Business Company (BC) — limited by shares  _(BVI Business Companies Act, 2004)_
- **Other available structures** — Limited partnership, segregated portfolio company (SPC), restricted purpose company, and trusts/VISTA trusts  _(Limited Partnership Act, 2017; BVI Business Companies Act, 2004; Virgin Islands Special Trusts Act (VISTA))_
- **Minimum share capital** — None — no minimum paid-up or authorised capital is required  _(BVI Business Companies Act, 2004)_
- **Minimum directors and shareholders** — At least one director and one shareholder (may be the same person; corporate directors permitted)  _(BVI Business Companies Act, 2004)_
- **Registered agent / registered office** — Mandatory at all times — must be a BVI-licensed registered agent with a registered office in the BVI  _(BVI Business Companies Act, 2004)_
- **Incorporation timeline** — Typically 1–3 business days once due diligence and documents are in order (approx — confirm)  _(BVI Business Companies Act, 2004)_
- **Core incorporation steps** — Appoint a licensed registered agent, complete KYC/due diligence, file Memorandum & Articles of Association with the Registry, pay incorporation/government fee, receive Certificate of Incorporation  _(BVI Business Companies Act, 2004)_
- **Government incorporation fee** — For an ordinary company authorised to issue up to 50,000 shares, US$550 from 1 January 2023. Check the applicable share and company category. [S.I. 89 of 2022, section 2(a)(i)](https://www.bvifsc.vg/sites/default/files/bvi_business_companies_amendment_of_schedule_1_no_2_order_2022.pdf)
- **Annual government fee** — For an ordinary share company, US$550 for up to 50,000 authorised shares and US$1,350 above that threshold from 1 January 2023. [S.I. 89 of 2022, section 2(a)(xiii)](https://www.bvifsc.vg/sites/default/files/bvi_business_companies_amendment_of_schedule_1_no_2_order_2022.pdf)
- **Annual fee due dates** — 31 May for companies incorporated in January–June and 30 November for companies incorporated in July–December, starting in the following year. [2020 revised Act, Schedule 1 Part III paragraph 5](https://www.bvifsc.vg/sites/default/files/bvi_business_companies_act.pdf)
- **Economic substance reporting** — Annual reporting to the registered agent within 6 months of the financial period end; substance required where relevant activities are carried on  _(Economic Substance (Companies and Limited Partnerships) Act, 2018)_
- **Company registers and beneficial ownership** — From 2 January 2025, sections 43A and 96A generally require filing the register of members and beneficial ownership information with the Registrar. Check the exceptions, change notifications and transitional rules with the registered agent. Director filings have separate rules under section 118B. [Act 15 of 2024, sections 10, 16 and 25](https://www.bvifsc.vg/sites/default/files/bvi_business_companies_amendment_act_2024.pdf) · [Commencement notice](https://www.bvifsc.vg/sites/default/files/si_no_75_of_2024-notice_to_bring_into_force_the_bvi_business_companies_amendment_act_2024.pdf)

## Annual financial return

Section 98A generally requires a BVI business company to file a financial return with its registered agent within 9 months of its year-end. The Commission can extend the initial filing period by notice under the amendment deemed effective from 1 September 2024. The registered agent must supply a copy on request to the Commission or another competent authority and generally notify the Registrar within 30 days of a missed filing deadline. [Act 6 of 2022, section 17](https://www.bvifsc.vg/sites/default/files/act_no_6_of_2022-_bvi_business_companies_amendment_act_2022_1.pdf) · [Act 30 of 2024, section 2](https://www.bvifsc.vg/sites/default/files/bvi_business_companies_amendment_no._2_act_2024.pdf)

The 2023 order exempts listed companies, regulated companies that submit the required financial statements to the Commission and companies that submit an annual tax return and financial statements to Inland Revenue. The liquidation exception applies unless the return became due before liquidation starts. For this exception, distinguish the date the return first becomes due from the last day to file. [S.I. 18 of 2023, section 3](https://www.bvifsc.vg/sites/default/files/bvi_business_companies_financial_return_order_2023.pdf)

S.I. 71 of 2026 replaced the form from 2 November 2026. The Commission directs companies to use it for returns required to be filed from 2027 onwards. It asks for more detail on assets, revenue and expenses and retains the balance sheet and income statement. Consult the Commission's guidance for the accounting basis, currency and disclosures. Confirm any transition or extension with the registered agent. [S.I. 71 of 2026, sections 1–2 and schedule](https://www.bvifsc.vg/sites/default/files/bvi_business_companies_financial_return_amendment_order_2026.pdf) · [FSC Circular 29 of 2026](https://www.bvifsc.vg/news/industry-updates/industry-circular-29-2026-amendments-bvi-business-companies-financial-return) · [FSC guidance, sections 1 and 3–4](https://www.bvifsc.vg/sites/default/files/guidance_on_completing_annual_financial_returns_final_06.11.26.pdf)
