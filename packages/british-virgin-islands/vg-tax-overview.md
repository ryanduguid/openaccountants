# British Virgin Islands Tax Overview

The Inland Revenue Department administers payroll, property, stamp duty, hotel accommodation and self-drive motor vehicle taxes and charges. Customs administers import duties. The International Tax Authority has distinct international reporting functions. The territory uses United States dollars (USD). [Inland Revenue Department][IRD] · [Customs Management and Duties Act][CUSTOMS] · [Legal Tender (Adoption of United States Currency) Act, ss 2–3][CURRENCY] · [ITA, CRS information][ITA]

## Ordinary income, entity and residence

The Financial Services Commission's audited 2024 accounts describe the ordinary personal and corporate income tax rates as zero from 1 January 2005. The 2025 SIGTAS Validation Act names the Income Tax Ordinance (Cap. 206) and the Payroll Taxes Act separately. The 2020 income tax amendment provides for exemption and good standing certificates from the Commissioner on specified conditions. Earlier revenue receipts appear in the 2026 budget without identifying the originating assessment year. Verify the operative provisions and any notice before deciding a return, assessment or exemption. [FSC 2024 accounts, note 20][FSC] · [SIGTAS Validation Act 2025, s 2][SIGTAS] · [Income Tax Amendment Act 2020, ss 1–4][CERTIFICATES] · [2026 budget estimates, revenue codes 411110 and 411210][BUDGET]

Section 242 of the BVI Business Companies Act provides a company exemption under Cap. 206, including specified payments and gains. Check entity status, local land exceptions for stamp duty and foreign source and withholding rules separately. For international reporting, the ITA's CRS guidance addresses entities without a tax residence and relevant trust and financial institution branches. Use the applicable income, residence and source rules in each relevant jurisdiction when the taxpayer has foreign connections. The domestic minimum tax discussion in the 2026 budget is a policy proposal; verify enacted law and commencement before using it. [BVI Business Companies Act, ss 3 and 242][COMPANIES] · [ITA CRS guidance, pp 6–7][CRS] · [2026 budget estimates, fiscal risks][BUDGET]

## Payroll, transactions and filing periods

The published commencement of payroll tax is 1 January 2005. The June 2012 Inland Revenue guide gives 10% and 14% for employer or self-employed classes 1 and 2, subject to the relevant classification and remuneration rules. It describes a US$10,000 annual exemption and an ordinary monthly return and payment period of 21 days from month end. Check the applicable amendment chain, employee deduction, concurrent employment and actual return period before calculating. The enhanced eTax service launched on 2 November 2026 for payroll declarations and tax payments, including property tax. Social Security and National Health Insurance retain separate rates and periods. [Government laws index, Payroll Taxes Act][INDEX] · [Inland Revenue payroll guide, parts 2–8][PAYROLL] · [Ministry of Finance, eTax release][ETAX]

Import duties depend on the Customs Management and Duties Act and the tariff and relief instruments in force for the goods and entry date. The 2027 budget address identifies payroll, international trade, hotel accommodation and stamp duty amongst the territory's revenue streams. Apply the separate accommodation, property and business requirements before a transaction or filing. [Customs Management and Duties Act and related legislation][CUSTOMS] · [2027 budget address, 27 October 2026][BUDGET2027]
