# Bermuda Personal Income Tax

## Personal income tax in Bermuda

- **Domestic personal income tax in 2026.** The Government of Bermuda confirms that individuals do not pay personal income tax. There is no Bermuda personal income tax computation, band, allowance, return or payment deadline for an individual. [Government of Bermuda, Types of taxes in Bermuda, updated 14 July 2026](https://www.gov.bm/articles/types-taxes-bermuda)
- **Employment and self-employment.** Bermuda payroll tax can apply to remuneration paid to employees and deemed employees and to amounts self-employed people draw for personal use. The employer may deduct the employee portion and remains responsible for paying both portions. Separate social insurance rules also apply. [Office of the Tax Commissioner, Payroll Tax, updated 3 November 2026](https://www.gov.bm/payroll-tax) · [2026-27 payroll calculation](https://www.gov.bm/calculating-payroll-tax-2026) · [Department of Social Insurance](https://www.gov.bm/department/social-insurance)
- **Other liabilities.** Screen an incorporated business separately under Bermuda's corporate income tax rules. Check stamp duty on estate instruments and check whether another country taxes the individual or the income under its own rules. [Bermuda Corporate Income Tax](https://www.gov.bm/CIT) · [Office of the Tax Commissioner](https://www.gov.bm/department/tax-commissioner)
