# Bermuda Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax (Pillar Two CIT)

Historically Bermuda imposed no corporate income tax. The Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 introduced a 15% CIT effective for fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025, but it applies only to Bermuda constituent entities of multinational enterprise (MNE) groups with annual consolidated revenue of EUR 750 million or more. Companies outside that scope remain untaxed on profits.

- **Corporate income tax rate (in-scope entities)** — 15% percent (In-scope entities)  _(Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (https://www.gov.bm/CIT))_
- **CIT effective date** — Fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025  _(Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (https://www.gov.bm/CIT))_
- **In-scope revenue threshold** — An MNE Group is in scope for a fiscal year beginning on or after 1 January 2025 when its annual revenue in the ultimate parent's consolidated financial statements is EUR 750 million or more in at least two of the four preceding fiscal years  _(Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (2023:35), Bermuda Laws Online, s 11(1) — https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Annual%20Law/Acts/2023/Corporate%20Income%20Tax%20Act%202023 ; Government of Bermuda, Bermuda Corporate Income Tax — https://www.gov.bm/cit)_
- **Companies outside scope** — The tax is chargeable only to a Bermuda Constituent Entity Group of an In Scope MNE Group; every other company pays no corporate income tax on profits, income, capital gains, dividends, interest or royalties  _(Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (2023:35), Bermuda Laws Online, ss 4 and 9 — https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Annual%20Law/Acts/2023/Corporate%20Income%20Tax%20Act%202023 ; PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/bermuda/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **CIT tax base** — 15% of the net taxable income of the Bermuda Constituent Entity Group, less tax credits; taxable income or loss starts from financial accounting net income or loss adjusted under Part 6, and a tax loss carryforward deduction cannot exceed 80% of the positive amount  _(Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (2023:35), Bermuda Laws Online, ss 4(2), 5 and 20 — https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Annual%20Law/Acts/2023/Corporate%20Income%20Tax%20Act%202023)_
- **Design alignment** — CIT is designed to align with the OECD GloBE (Pillar Two) rules and to qualify as a Covered Tax  _(Government of Bermuda, Bermuda Corporate Income Tax — https://www.gov.bm/cit ; EY tax alert — https://www.ey.com/en_gl/technical/tax-alerts/bermuda-parliament-passes-legislation-to-enact-a-15--corporate-i)_
- **Economic Transition Adjustment (ETA)** — A Bermuda Constituent Entity included in a group in its first fiscal year beginning on or after 1 January 2025 adjusts taxable income for the fair value of its assets and liabilities (excluding goodwill) at 30 September 2023, with the adjustments capped at 80% of the relevant amount and intangibles amortised over the first ten fiscal years  _(Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (2023:35), Bermuda Laws Online, s 33 — https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Annual%20Law/Acts/2023/Corporate%20Income%20Tax%20Act%202023)_
- **Out-of-scope entities** — Excluded entities (including investment funds, real estate investment vehicles and entities they wholly or almost wholly own) and entities less than 80% owned by value by the ultimate parent entity are not Bermuda Constituent Entities, unless an election under s 9(3) brings an under-80% entity in  _(Corporate Income Tax Act 2023 (2023:35), Bermuda Laws Online, ss 9(2) and 10 — https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Annual%20Law/Acts/2023/Corporate%20Income%20Tax%20Act%202023)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends** — 0% (none) percent  _(PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries — Bermuda, Corporate withholding taxes (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/bermuda/corporate/other-taxes))_
- **Withholding tax on interest & royalties** — 0% (none) percent  _(PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries — Bermuda, Corporate withholding taxes (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/bermuda/corporate/other-taxes))_
- **CIT filing & payment** — Each Bermuda Constituent Entity Group files a CIT return for every fiscal year beginning on or after 1 January 2025 through CITA Online (Form 100 with the standard S-101 or simplified S-102 schedules); the Agency collects instalments during the fiscal year and charges interest on late instalments  _(Corporate Income Tax Agency, Corporate Income Tax Return — https://www.cita.bm/corporate-income-tax-return1 ; Corporate Income Tax Agency, home page (instalment deadlines) — https://www.cita.bm/ ; EY tax alert — https://www.ey.com/en_gl/technical/tax-alerts/bermuda-releases-final-cit-administrative-regulations)_
