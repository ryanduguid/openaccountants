# Bermuda Tax Overview

## Scope and evidence boundary

Identify the tax and applicable period before calculating or selecting a filing route. This guide covers system orientation and screening, checked through 14 November 2026. It does not complete a corporate, payroll, contributions, customs, property, formation, reporting or treaty engagement. The named evidence gaps below prevent a whole-topic completion claim.

Bermuda is a British Overseas Territory. The Government's [15 May 2026 constitutional-reform statement](https://www.gov.bm/articles/consultation-proposed-process-constitutional-reform) describes a proposed process, not an enacted constitutional change.

## Currency and periods

- **Currency:** the Bermudian dollar, divided into 100 cents under [Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969, s 9](https://cdn.bma.bm/documents/2026-01-16-16-27-35-Bermuda-Monetary-Authority-Act-1969.pdf). BMA's [CPIS guide, Annex C](https://cdn.bma.bm/documents/2025-05-07-11-44-02-CPIS-Guide-Third-Edition.pdf) records code BMD.
- **Parity:** [Bermudian Dollar Parity Order 1981, cl 3](https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Consolidated%20Law/1981/Bermudian%20Dollar%20Parity%20Order%201981), operative 22 May 1981, sets BMD 1 equivalent to USD 1. Statutory parity is not a promise of cost-free retail conversion: BMA Act s 14 qualifies the Authority's issue/redemption operations and [foreign currency purchase tax](https://www.gov.bm/foreign-currency-tax), bank charges and transaction exemptions need separate checks. No transaction tax or retail exchange calculation is supplied here.
- **CIT fiscal year:** use the accounting period for which the ultimate parent prepares consolidated financial statements. The s 2 definition provides a calendar-year fallback for paragraph (d) deemed consolidated statements. Do not assume every group's fiscal year is the calendar year. [CIT Act, s 2](https://www.cita.bm/assets/documents/2026-10-13-09-31-29-Corporate%20Income%20Tax%20Act%202023%20NEW.pdf)
- **Payroll reference period:** the official [2026-27 payroll calculation page](https://www.gov.bm/calculating-payroll-tax-2026) identifies 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027. This is separate from the CIT fiscal year and from this guide's reference date. Detailed payroll rates and filing mechanics remain a separate reconciliation.

## Tax landscape and responsible bodies

| Area | Supported orientation and public route | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Domestic personal income tax | The Government says individuals do not pay personal income tax. [Types of taxes, updated 14 July 2026](https://www.gov.bm/articles/types-taxes-bermuda) | This does not remove payroll, social insurance, property, estate-instrument or foreign-country obligations. Use [personal income scope](bm-income-tax.md). |
| Corporate income tax | Conditional CIT under the screening below; [CITA](https://www.cita.bm/) administers it. | No blanket exemption for a Bermuda company or an assurance certificate. |
| OTC taxes and duties | [Office of the Tax Commissioner](https://www.gov.bm/department/tax-commissioner): payroll, corporate services, betting, land, foreign currency purchase, financial services, hotel occupancy, cruise departure, transport infrastructure, yacht arrival, stamp duties and the timesharing taxes. | This is a departmental inventory, not proof that every charge applies or that unlisted taxes are absent. Confirm the charge, taxpayer, base, period and return separately. |
| Customs | [Customs Department](https://www.gov.bm/department/customs) assesses duty and other taxes on arriving goods and passengers. | Customs declarations and tariff treatment are separate from VAT. Existing 22.25%/25% general-rate claims in companion material are not reconciled here. |
| Social insurance | [Department of Social Insurance](https://www.gov.bm/department/social-insurance). | Contribution coverage, base, rates and remittance remain separate. |
| Vacation rentals | Government's [fact sheet](https://www.gov.bm/articles/vacation-rentals-fact-sheet-and-forms), updated 14 July 2026, identifies a fee remitted to the Bermuda Tourism Authority and a VRBO collection arrangement from 1 February 2026. | A sector fee is not a general sales tax. Eligibility, collection route and numerical liability require their own check. |
| Company administration | [Registrar of Companies](https://www2.gov.bm/department/registrar-companies). | Incorporation, annual reporting and economic-substance obligations need their own source and period checks. |

CIT must be included in any current revenue description. The Government's [6 November 2026 mid-year review](https://www.gov.bm/news/202627-mid-year-review-stronger-finances-meaningful-relief-greater-investment) projects FY2026-27 revenue of $2.09 billion, including $800 million CIT and $650 million payroll tax. These are forecasts, not collected revenue or liability rules. Its announced relief for 1 January 2027 is not applied at this guide's cutoff.

## Corporate income tax screening

Use the [CIT Act 2023 consolidated copy](https://www.cita.bm/assets/documents/2026-10-13-09-31-29-Corporate%20Income%20Tax%20Act%202023%20NEW.pdf), including the listed amendments through 2026:25, and [General Guidance Notes v2, dated 5 November 2026](https://www.cita.bm/assets/documents/2026-11-13-13-18-25-CIT%20Guidance%20Notes%20(General)%20V2.pdf). The live [amendments index](https://www.cita.bm/technical-references?sort_id=6) states that amendments were consolidated as of 13 September 2026. Upload dates do not establish commencement. The separately read [2026 Amendment Act](https://www.cita.bm/assets/documents/2026-08-26-15-58-28-Corporate%20Income%20Tax%20Amendment%20Act%202026.pdf) records assent and operation on 29 July 2026. Consultation papers are not enacted law.

1. **Identify the group and period.** Obtain the ultimate parent, group structure, consolidated financial statements and fiscal-year dates. Apply the s 2 definitions of group, MNE Group, constituent entity, controlling interest and consolidated financial statements. A standalone domestic entity is not automatically an MNE Group; permanent establishments and deemed consolidation can matter. Guidance section 7 addresses line-by-line consolidation. Missing ownership or accounts means **Needs Input**.
2. **Test group revenue.** Section 11(1) generally requires at least EUR 750 million annual revenue in the ultimate parent's consolidated statements in at least 2 of the 4 immediately preceding fiscal years. Record all 4 years, currency and applicable conversion evidence. Apply ss 11(2)–(7) for mergers, acquisitions, demergers and consolidated excluded-entity revenue. Do not replace the lookback with current-year revenue. Unusual accounting periods or restructuring need provision-specific review.
3. **Check the limited international footprint rule.** Section 13 can exclude a group where all its conditions are met: no more than 5 jurisdictions outside the reference jurisdiction, no more than EUR 50 million net book value of relevant tangible assets outside it and no parent entity required to apply an IIR with respect to any constituent entity of that MNE Group located in Bermuda. This population is wider than the defined Bermuda Constituent Entity category: an entity excluded under s 9(2)(b) can still affect s 13(1)(c). Apply its location and entity/asset exclusions. Under s 13(4), the exclusion ceases for a fiscal year beginning more than 5 years after the later of the first day of the group’s first fiscal year beginning on or after 1 January 2025 and the first day of the first fiscal year in which it meets s 11(1), tested without s 13. Once started, that period is not suspended. **Endpoint evidence conflict:** General Guidance v2 page 25 describes 5 fiscal years and its page 36 example puts a calendar-year group in scope from 1 January 2030. For a 1 January 2025 statutory start, that endpoint is not reconciled with s 13(4)’s literal ‘more than 5 years’ wording. Hold an actual endpoint/classification decision **Needs Input** pending authoritative clarification; do not silently choose the guidance illustration. Do not treat the revenue threshold alone as decisive. Sections 11(8)–(9) also permit a qualifying previously in-scope group to elect continued in-scope treatment; obtain the election history.
4. **Check each Bermuda entity.** Under s 9, a Bermuda Tax Resident Entity or Bermuda Permanent Establishment must be a constituent entity of the in-scope group. Apply s 10 excluded-entity definitions, ownership/activity conditions and any election to opt in. The under-80%-by-value exclusion in s 9(2)(b) is subject to a s 9(3) election. Not every investment fund, investment subsidiary or real-estate vehicle is automatically excluded. Sections 14–15A address location, transparency and changes in entity status. Record outstanding elections and classification facts instead of assuming exemption.
5. **Record grouping and charge.** Section 8 governs Bermuda Constituent Entity Groups and grouping elections. Section 4 charges 15% of group net taxable income less applicable foreign tax credits under Part 4. Section 4(3) extends joint and several liability across Bermuda constituent entities within the same in-scope group. Taxable income, losses, other credit regimes and return/payment computations require separate detailed verification; 15% is not a tax on gross receipts.
6. **Apply the correct commencement and assurance treatment.** Section 53 commenced specified provisions on 1 January 2024 and the remaining provisions, including the charge, on 1 January 2025. Section 11 addresses fiscal years beginning on or after commencement. Section 4(4) applies despite an Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act assurance; s 51 makes pre-1 January 2024 assurances subject to CIT and excludes CIT from later assurances. An assurance is not a CIT exemption.

CITA's functions follow [Agency Act 2024, s 4](https://www.cita.bm/assets/documents/2026-10-13-09-32-03-Corporate%20Income%20Tax%20Agency%20Act%202024%20NEW.pdf). The public [CITA Online overview](https://www.cita.bm/overview-cita-online) links to [CITA Online](https://online.cita.bm/) for TIN, returns, payments and authorised representatives. The [return page](https://www.cita.bm/corporate-income-tax-return1) concerns each fiscal year beginning on or after 1 January 2025. These public routes do not establish a taxpayer's registration, successful submission, applicable deadline or authenticated workflow. Use the [CIT registration, filing and payment procedure](bm-corporate-income-tax.md#cit-registration-filing-and-payment) for supported planning, with its fiscal-year/form, election, notice and evidence boundaries. Detailed computations and authenticated execution remain separate verification.

## Claims still requiring evidence

The inherited text reported no VAT/GST/general sales tax, no separate capital gains tax and no withholding on dividends, interest or royalties. Current affirmative primary evidence for each statement has not been established in this reconciliation. Treat them as **Research Gap / Needs Input**, not zero rates or verified inapplicability. A departmental tax list, a missing charging provision in one Act or the absence of personal income tax does not prove these negatives. Capital gains can require analysis under the CIT financial-accounting-income and adjustment rules even if no separate CGT is established.

The [official payroll page](https://www.gov.bm/articles/payroll-tax), checked on 14 November 2026, states payment dates of 15 January, 15 April, 15 July and 15 October and requires all exempted companies and companies with annual remuneration greater than BMD 200,000 to submit online. It links the Electronic Tax System as www.etax.gov.bm. Start from that official page; the destination’s secure sign-in, submission workflow and successful filing have not been tested. The prior annual-reconciliation date of 15 March remains unverified and must not supply a deadline.

## Treaties and international reporting

Bermuda has double-taxation agreements. The Government's [22 April 2026 Bahrain announcement](https://www.gov.bm/news/bermuda-signs-amendment-double-taxation-agreement-kingdom-bahrain) records a protocol signed 17 April 2026 to the 2010 DTA. It does not establish that protocol's entry into force or application dates. The official [20 January 2022 inventory](https://www.gov.bm/media/019f1ea9-0b84-722c-bb88-65a37c07e154) distinguishes DTCs from TIEAs; it is historical, not a complete current treaty count. Check subsequent instruments, entry into force, tax periods, eligibility and any termination before claiming relief.

The [U.S.A.–Bermuda Tax Convention Act 1986](https://www.cita.bm//assets/images/files/U.S.A.-Bermuda-Tax-Convention-Act-1986.pdf) concerns insurance-enterprise taxation and mutual tax assistance. Its Third Schedule records the 2 December 1988 ratification exchange and US reservations, including preservation of US foreign-insurer premium excise taxes effective 1 January 1990. Do not infer a general income-tax treaty or current insurance exemption from its title. Case-specific US relief and subsequent instruments remain unverified.

The [Government CIT page, updated 10 November 2026](https://www.gov.bm/CIT), says CITA became competent authority for CRS and CbCR on 13 March 2026 and the prior content migrated on 22 June 2026. Information exchange, economic substance and treaty relief are different questions; use the current CITA material for the applicable reporting obligation.

## Manual screening controls

These are hypothetical desk checks, not executed taxpayer or portal tests.

- Current revenue EUR 900 million with the 4-year history missing: **Needs Input**, not automatically in scope.
- Exactly EUR 750 million in 2 of the 4 relevant preceding years: the ordinary revenue limb is met; still test group/entity definitions, s 13 and elections before a CIT conclusion.
- UPE owns 79% by value: check s 9 and the five-year election history; do not assume permanent exemption from the percentage alone.
- Group meets the ordinary revenue limb but asserts limited footprint: require every s 13 condition and the running 5-year period, plus any s 11(8) election.
- A group’s first statutory start is 1 January 2025 and the question concerns 1 January 2030: **Needs Input** on the s 13 endpoint tension, even if the other footprint facts are known.
- An assurance certificate or an old instruction saying Bermuda has no income tax: neither overrides s 4 CIT screening.
- A question about an indirect-tax declaration: identify customs or a sector charge; do not infer that no return exists from the unverified VAT absence claim.
- A Bahrain protocol signature or the US convention title: neither establishes relief for a particular payment or period.
- A 2026 group otherwise satisfies the footprint and running-period conditions, with no continued-scope election. Its consolidated 79%-owned Bermuda constituent entity is excluded under s 9(2)(b), but an intermediate parent is required to apply an IIR to it. A wholly-owned Bermuda sibling is also present. The s 13(1)(c) condition fails; continue ordinary CIT screening of the sibling instead of excluding the group.
- A social-insurance remittance-deadline query: **Needs Input**, rather than the inherited last-Friday claim. The separately sourced payroll quarterly payment dates remain available; they do not establish social-insurance dates.
- An above-threshold non-corporate entity within a potentially relevant MNE group: continue the Act’s entity/group screening, with missing classification or election facts marked **Needs Input**. The corporation-only workflow does not authorise its computation, sector refusals remain and the threshold alone does not import a GloBE calculation.

## Required handoff and self-check

Record the taxpayer/entity and group, tax/question, fiscal period, source version and effective period, screening result, unanswered facts, elections, responsible authority and verified public route. Hand detailed computations to the relevant topic with its unresolved evidence clearly attached. Output a source-cited orientation memo, screening working paper, **Needs Input** list and reviewer checklist. Before release, check that no VAT/CGT/withholding unknown became zero, no general income-tax refusal blocked conditional CIT, no calendar-year default displaced the statutory period and no signing date became a treaty commencement date. Obtain practitioner review before relying on a liability or filing.
