# Bermuda Tax Overview

## Scope and evidence boundary

Identify the tax and applicable period before calculating or selecting a filing route. This guide covers system orientation and screening using the stated sources and effective periods, with the latest selected checks recorded on 21 November 2026. It does not complete a corporate, payroll, contributions, customs, property, formation, reporting or treaty engagement. The named evidence gaps below prevent a whole-topic completion claim.

Bermuda is a British Overseas Territory. The Government's [15 May 2026 constitutional-reform statement](https://www.gov.bm/articles/consultation-proposed-process-constitutional-reform) describes a proposed process, not an enacted constitutional change.

## Currency and periods

- **Currency:** the Bermudian dollar, divided into 100 cents under [Bermuda Monetary Authority Act 1969, s 9](https://cdn.bma.bm/documents/2026-01-16-16-27-35-Bermuda-Monetary-Authority-Act-1969.pdf). BMA's [CPIS guide, Annex C](https://cdn.bma.bm/documents/2025-05-07-11-44-02-CPIS-Guide-Third-Edition.pdf) records code BMD.
- **Parity:** [Bermudian Dollar Parity Order 1981, cl 3](https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Consolidated%20Law/1981/Bermudian%20Dollar%20Parity%20Order%201981), operative 22 May 1981, sets BMD 1 equivalent to USD 1. Statutory parity is not a promise of cost-free retail conversion: BMA Act s 14 qualifies the Authority's issue/redemption operations and [foreign currency purchase tax](https://www.gov.bm/foreign-currency-tax), bank charges and transaction exemptions need separate checks. Route a currency purchase to the [selected FCPT controls](bermuda-tax.md#foreign-currency-purchase-tax-scope-and-source-periods). Parity alone establishes neither that charge/exemption nor the retail conversion cost.
- **CIT fiscal year:** use the accounting period for which the ultimate parent prepares consolidated financial statements. The s 2 definition provides a calendar-year fallback for paragraph (d) deemed consolidated statements. Do not assume every group's fiscal year is the calendar year. [CIT Act, s 2](https://www.cita.bm/assets/documents/2026-10-13-09-31-29-Corporate%20Income%20Tax%20Act%202023%20NEW.pdf)
- **Payroll reference period:** the official [2026-27 payroll calculation page](https://www.gov.bm/calculating-payroll-tax-2026) identifies 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027. This is separate from the CIT fiscal year and from this guide's reference date. Use the [payroll guide](bm-payroll-social.md) for rates, effective quarters and filing mechanics.

## Foreign-currency purchase tax screening

Under the [FCPT Act 1975, ss 1A–4 and both Schedules](https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Consolidated%20Law/1975/Foreign%20Currency%20Purchase%20Tax%20Act%201975), a resident's non-exempt purchase of foreign currency from a bank is charged on the established BMD currency paid for that purchase. The [enacted 2019 amendment, ss 2–3](https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Annual%20Law/Acts/2019/Foreign%20Currency%20Purchase%20Tax%20Amendment%20Act%202019), sets **1.25% from 1 April 2019**. Establish exchange-control residence under [regs 2–3](https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Consolidated%20Law/1973/Exchange%20Control%20Regulations%201973), the bank/currency/purchase/base and any transaction exemption before computing. CIT residence, a company label, USD parity or the gross underlying payment cannot substitute. See the [conditional calculations and source limits](bermuda-tax.md#foreign-currency-purchase-tax-scope-and-source-periods).

Listed-body exemptions require **own-account** transactions; specified insurance/pension remittances need **Controller permission in each case**. The particular Saturn Solar project wording is not a general solar exemption or an express expiry after 2020. The bank's periodic return/remittance route depends on the actual **written s 4 authorisation**; the agency's quarter-end wording does not establish every bank's period/deadline or a purchaser's return duty. Preserve s 4(4)'s customer-account information restriction. Under [Interpretation Act 1951, ss 1 and 40](https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Consolidated%20Law/1951/Interpretation%20Act%201951#page=23), specified offence punishments are maxima unless a contrary intention appears. Keep those limits and the actual sentence separate from tax and automatic late additions.

The [2025 consequential Order, paras 5–6](https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Annual%20Law/Statutory%20Instruments/2025/Beneficial%20Ownership%20%28Consequential%20Amendments%29%20Order%202025), and current Regulations leave an unresolved malformed permit-company reg 3(3) sentence. Hold that classification pending authoritative clarification and actual directions. Actual status/permissions/notices, prescribed-currency treatment, final fractional-cent rounding and exhaustive later-law currency remain open where relevant. These FCPT controls do not establish zero withholding or complete treaties, foreign-source-country tax or exchange-control coverage.

## Corporate income tax screening

Use the [CIT Act 2023 consolidated copy](https://www.cita.bm/assets/documents/2026-10-13-09-31-29-Corporate%20Income%20Tax%20Act%202023%20NEW.pdf), including the listed amendments through 2026:25, and [General Guidance Notes v2, dated 5 November 2026](https://www.cita.bm/assets/documents/2026-11-13-13-18-25-CIT%20Guidance%20Notes%20(General)%20V2.pdf). The live [amendments index](https://www.cita.bm/technical-references?sort_id=6) states that amendments were consolidated as of 13 September 2026. Upload dates do not establish commencement. The separately read [2026 Amendment Act](https://www.cita.bm/assets/documents/2026-08-26-15-58-28-Corporate%20Income%20Tax%20Amendment%20Act%202026.pdf) records assent and operation on 29 July 2026. Consultation papers are not enacted law.

1. **Identify the group and period.** Obtain the ultimate parent, group structure, consolidated financial statements and fiscal-year dates. Apply the s 2 definitions of group, MNE Group, constituent entity, controlling interest and consolidated financial statements. A standalone domestic entity is not automatically an MNE Group; permanent establishments and deemed consolidation can matter. Guidance section 7 addresses line-by-line consolidation. Missing ownership or accounts means **Needs Input**.
2. **Test group revenue.** Section 11(1) generally requires at least EUR 750 million annual revenue in the ultimate parent's consolidated statements in at least 2 of the 4 immediately preceding fiscal years. Record all 4 years, currency and applicable conversion evidence. Apply ss 11(2)–(7) for mergers, acquisitions, demergers and consolidated excluded-entity revenue. Do not replace the lookback with current-year revenue. Unusual accounting periods or restructuring need provision-specific review.
3. **Check the limited international footprint rule.** Section 13 can exclude a group where all its conditions are met: no more than 5 jurisdictions outside the reference jurisdiction, no more than EUR 50 million net book value of relevant tangible assets outside it and no parent entity required to apply an IIR with respect to any constituent entity of that MNE Group located in Bermuda. This population is wider than the defined Bermuda Constituent Entity category: an entity excluded under s 9(2)(b) can still affect s 13(1)(c). Apply its location and entity/asset exclusions. Under s 13(4), the exclusion ceases for a fiscal year beginning more than 5 years after the later of the first day of the group’s first fiscal year beginning on or after 1 January 2025 and the first day of the first fiscal year in which it meets s 11(1), tested without s 13. Once started, that period is not suspended. **Endpoint evidence conflict:** General Guidance v2 page 25 describes 5 fiscal years and its page 36 example puts a calendar-year group in scope from 1 January 2030. For a 1 January 2025 statutory start, that endpoint is not reconciled with s 13(4)’s literal ‘more than 5 years’ wording. Hold an actual endpoint/classification decision **Needs Input** pending authoritative clarification; do not silently choose the guidance illustration. Do not treat the revenue threshold alone as decisive. Sections 11(8)–(9) also permit a qualifying previously in-scope group to elect continued in-scope treatment; obtain the election history.
4. **Check each Bermuda entity.** Under s 9, a Bermuda Tax Resident Entity or Bermuda Permanent Establishment must be a constituent entity of the in-scope group. Apply s 10 excluded-entity definitions, ownership/activity conditions and any election to opt in. The under-80%-by-value exclusion in s 9(2)(b) is subject to a s 9(3) election. Not every investment fund, investment subsidiary or real-estate vehicle is automatically excluded. Sections 14–15A address location, transparency and changes in entity status. Record outstanding elections and classification facts instead of assuming exemption.
5. **Record grouping and charge.** Section 8 governs Bermuda Constituent Entity Groups and grouping elections. Section 4 charges 15% of group net taxable income less applicable foreign tax credits under Part 4. Section 4(3) extends joint and several liability across Bermuda constituent entities within the same in-scope group. Taxable income, losses, other credit regimes and return/payment computations require separate detailed verification; 15% is not a tax on gross receipts.
6. **Apply the correct commencement and assurance treatment.** Section 53 commenced specified provisions on 1 January 2024 and the remaining provisions, including the charge, on 1 January 2025. Section 11 addresses fiscal years beginning on or after commencement. Section 4(4) applies despite an Exempted Undertakings Tax Protection Act assurance; s 51 makes pre-1 January 2024 assurances subject to CIT and excludes CIT from later assurances. An assurance is not a CIT exemption.

CITA's functions follow [Agency Act 2024, s 4](https://www.cita.bm/assets/documents/2026-10-13-09-32-03-Corporate%20Income%20Tax%20Agency%20Act%202024%20NEW.pdf). The public [CITA Online overview](https://www.cita.bm/overview-cita-online) links to [CITA Online](https://online.cita.bm/) for TIN, returns, payments and authorised representatives. The [return page](https://www.cita.bm/corporate-income-tax-return1) concerns each fiscal year beginning on or after 1 January 2025. These public routes do not establish a taxpayer's registration, successful submission, applicable deadline or authenticated workflow. Use the [CIT registration, filing and payment procedure](bm-corporate-income-tax.md#cit-registration-filing-and-payment) for supported planning, with its fiscal-year/form, election, notice and evidence boundaries. Detailed computations and authenticated execution remain separate verification.

## Claims still requiring evidence

The inherited text reported no VAT/GST/general sales tax, no separate capital gains tax and no withholding on dividends, interest or royalties. Current affirmative primary evidence for each statement has not been established in this reconciliation. Treat them as **Research Gap / Needs Input**, not zero rates or verified inapplicability. A departmental tax list, a missing charging provision in one Act or the absence of personal income tax does not prove these negatives. Capital gains can require analysis under the CIT financial-accounting-income and adjustment rules even if no separate CGT is established.

For payroll, [Payroll Tax Act ss 21-22](https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Consolidated%20Law/1995/Payroll%20Tax%20Act%201995) establish ordinary 3-month periods beginning 1 April, 1 July, 1 October and 1 January, with conditional written monthly permissions. [Taxes Management Act ss 7 and 15](https://www.bermudalaws.bm/Laws/Consolidated%20Law/1976/Taxes%20Management%20Act%201976) require the return within 15 days after the period closes and payment when the return is furnished, unless the Taxes Acts require or permit another time. Normal quarterly return dates are 15 January, 15 April, 15 July and 15 October; check individual return notices, certificates, assessments and payment extensions first. Payroll Act s 19(5) has a separate deducted-employee-tax clock, using 'within fifteen days beginning on the last day' of the period. Do not assume the ordinary return dates resolve that wording; its precise counting interaction remains **Needs Input** pending clarification. No additional 15-day grace period is established.

The [official payroll landing page](https://www.gov.bm/articles/payroll-tax), updated 3 November 2026, says all exempted companies and companies with annual remuneration greater than BMD 200,000 must submit online. The [June 2026 calculation page](https://www.gov.bm/articles/calculating-payroll-tax-2026) instead says all taxpayers with payroll BMD 200,000 or more. The exact boundary and taxpayer-description conflict remains unresolved; obtain current official clarification for an affected case and do not infer an exemption at exactly BMD 200,000. Start from the landing page, which links www.etax.gov.bm; secure sign-in, submission and successful filing are untested. The former 15 March annual-reconciliation claim remains unverified. Use the [payroll guide](bm-payroll-social.md) for the bounded rate and classification controls; annualisation and relief applications retain their stated gaps. Social insurance has separate selected weekly controls in that guide. Its [occupational-pension controls](bm-payroll-social.md#occupational-pensions-selected-contribution-controls) separately check selected membership, earnings, contribution and remittance rules. Plan-specific funding, historical relief and a full pension engagement remain open.

The [customs guide](bm-vat-gst.md#classification-route-and-source-periods) replaces unsupported general-rate assumptions with selected posted 2026 tariff rows and conditional route, value, allowance and relief controls. Its later-law check includes the 2026 retail capital-investment extension and BR109 declaration rules from 1 October 2026. It does not establish every later instrument, actual classification or relief eligibility, a complete import-cost amount or an accepted declaration. The old 4/16 traveller form was accepted only through 31 October 2026; current form images and authenticated filing remain unverified. A zero-duty heading or approved relief cannot settle the separate VAT/GST/general-sales-tax absence gap.

The [land-tax controls](bermuda-tax.md#land-tax-scope-and-source-periods) reconcile the selected 2026 ordinary private-dwelling base and bands, other-unit/EEZ distinctions, qualified age-related concession, half-year liability and separate service/payment/objection clocks. The appointed 2025 service amendment date is supported by BR 125/2025. Establish the applicable assessment instrument, statutory owner and classification, assessed ARV, period and relief/demand facts before an amount. A zero ARV, age alone or an advertised payment arrangement is not an actual tax or relief decision. The announced 2027 list, tourist-rate and interest-rate conflicts, full exemptions/later law and authenticated action remain unresolved.

The [stamp-duty controls](bermuda-tax.md#stamp-duty-scope-and-source-periods) replace the old ordinary bands and BMD 128,000 example with source-qualified Head 14 calculations. Established ordinary BMD 2 million consideration gives conditional duty BMD 103,950; a genuinely granted and fully qualifying first-home notice instead uses its nil-first-million, 6.30% and 7.35% schedule. Complete property/status/history, fair-value/money consideration, opinion and signed declaration conditions remain. The former 3-year residence/construction limb was repealed in 2022. Ordinary domestic execution timing, overseas and escrow exceptions, adjudication, appeal, special permission and distinct penalties use separate controls. Section 76 rounds a fractional-cent percentage-duty result upwards to the nearest whole cent. Actual facts, applicable notice/permission/remission, fee/form/payment acceptance, other instruments and exhaustive later law remain held.

## Treaties and international reporting

Bermuda has double-taxation agreements. The Government's [22 April 2026 Bahrain announcement](https://www.gov.bm/news/bermuda-signs-amendment-double-taxation-agreement-kingdom-bahrain) records a protocol signed 17 April 2026 to the 2010 DTA. It does not establish that protocol's entry into force or application dates. The official [20 January 2022 inventory](https://www.gov.bm/media/019f1ea9-0b84-722c-bb88-65a37c07e154) distinguishes DTCs from TIEAs; it is historical, not a complete current treaty count. Check subsequent instruments, entry into force, tax periods, eligibility and any termination before claiming relief.

The [U.S.A.–Bermuda Tax Convention Act 1986](https://www.cita.bm//assets/images/files/U.S.A.-Bermuda-Tax-Convention-Act-1986.pdf) concerns insurance-enterprise taxation and mutual tax assistance. Its Third Schedule records the 2 December 1988 ratification exchange and US reservations, including preservation of US foreign-insurer premium excise taxes effective 1 January 1990. Do not infer a general income-tax treaty or current insurance exemption from its title. Case-specific US relief and subsequent instruments remain unverified.

The [Government CIT page, updated 10 November 2026](https://www.gov.bm/CIT), says CITA became competent authority for CRS and CbCR on 13 March 2026 and the prior content migrated on 22 June 2026. Information exchange, economic substance and treaty relief are different questions; use the current CITA material for the applicable reporting obligation.

### Original Bahrain agreement controls

The [official NBR English publication of the original 2010 agreement](https://www.nbr.gov.bh/treaties/bermuda_dtts_en) supports [selected original-text controls](bermuda-tax.md#56-original-bahrain-agreement-selected-conditional-controls), with the instrument, tax and application period still to be established for a current case. The NBR network capture cached 14 November 2026 records original signature on 22 April 2010 and entry on 29 January 2012, and a separate 17 April 2026 protocol signature with no entry date. The table's dash does not prove non-operation. Obtain the protocol, notifications/application, subsequent instruments, relevant MLI matching, termination and current implementation evidence.

Original Art 2 covers income and capital taxes and names the Bahrain oil tax and Bermuda taxes except payroll tax, with later-tax and notification provisions. It does not by itself settle today's CIT coverage. Read the original Art 4 introductory national/ordinary-residence wording together with its country limbs, definitions and tie-breakers. Bahrain's 183-day limb also requires the stated nationality condition; it is not a general Bermuda residence test. Treaty, CIT and FCPT residence require separate analysis. PE screening includes fixed-place, more-than-12-month construction, hydrocarbon, agency and exclusion rules; exactly 12 months failing one limb does not settle the rest. The original's Art 5(6) paragraph-reference discrepancy remains recorded.

Original Art 10 sets a conditional 0% gross-dividend source ceiling for both the direct-at-least-25% qualifying-company branch and other cases. Art 11 sets a conditional 0% gross-debt-claim-income ceiling. Beneficial ownership, treaty residence, payment character, source, PE connection, special relationships, application machinery and any separate specified-government/body exemption require their own facts. These ceilings do not exempt the payer's profits, establish domestic withholding absence or settle a current payment. Late-payment penalty charges are excluded from Art 11's income definition. **Art 12 gives no percentage ceiling and does not establish zero royalties.**

Original Art 22 distinguishes qualified exemption, the actual-tax-paid credit capped at attributable resident tax, progression and paragraph 4's exemption switch-off. Do not infer residence exemption or a deemed credit from a zero source ceiling. The original MAP deadline is 3 years from first notification of the offending action, using the residence or Art 23(1) nationality route. Its 2-year arbitration clock runs from presentation to the **other** competent authority and retains request, unresolved-issue, court/tribunal, acceptance and mutual-implementation conditions. Neither clock establishes a domestic deadline or collection stay. Complete Arts 27–28 and actual diplomatic facts are needed for their awkward combined commencement/cessation period wording.

The official publication's final clause identifies the authentic languages and English precedence on divergence; physical execution/handwritten signatures are not independently authenticated. The guidance DOCX has material scope, definition, numbering, debt-claim and MAP errors. The Arabic ratification law has no qualified substantive review in this reconciliation. See the [source comparison and review limits](https://github.com/ryanduguid/openaccountants/blob/main/docs/verification-log/2026-11-15-bermuda-bahrain-original-treaty-controls.md). These selected controls retain current-law, taxpayer-fact, domestic-withholding, other-payment/treaty, practitioner and consuming-model execution gaps.


## Manual screening controls

These are hypothetical desk checks, not executed taxpayer or portal tests.

- Current revenue EUR 900 million with the 4-year history missing: **Needs Input**, not automatically in scope.
- Exactly EUR 750 million in 2 of the 4 relevant preceding years: the ordinary revenue limb is met; still test group/entity definitions, s 13 and elections before a CIT conclusion.
- UPE owns 79% by value: check s 9 and the five-year election history; do not assume permanent exemption from the percentage alone.
- Group meets the ordinary revenue limb but asserts limited footprint: require every s 13 condition and the running 5-year period, plus any s 11(8) election.
- A group’s first statutory start is 1 January 2025 and the question concerns 1 January 2030: **Needs Input** on the s 13 endpoint tension, even if the other footprint facts are known.
- An assurance certificate or an old instruction saying Bermuda has no income tax: neither overrides s 4 CIT screening.
- A question about an indirect-tax declaration: identify customs or a sector charge; do not infer that no return exists from the unverified VAT absence claim.
- A Bahrain protocol signature or the US convention title: neither establishes relief for a particular payment or period.
- A 2026 group otherwise satisfies the footprint and running-period conditions, with no continued-scope election. Its consolidated 79%-owned Bermuda constituent entity is excluded under s 9(2)(b), but an intermediate parent is required to apply an IIR to it. A wholly-owned Bermuda sibling is also present. The s 13(1)(c) condition fails; continue ordinary CIT screening of the sibling instead of excluding the group.
- A social-insurance remittance query: match the contribution weeks to the account schedule, then use the last working day of the following month under Act ss 4(7) and 8, subject to Director employer arrangements. Missing month-end allocation or arrangement facts remain **Needs Input**. Do not substitute the last Friday or payroll quarterly dates.
- An above-threshold non-corporate entity within a potentially relevant MNE group: continue the Act’s entity/group screening, with missing classification or election facts marked **Needs Input**. The corporation-only workflow does not authorise its computation, sector refusals remain and the threshold alone does not import a GloBE calculation.

- A BMD 5,000 import described only as electronics: **Needs Input**. Identify the tariff code, import date, statutory value, route, allowance and any approved relief before selecting a rate.
- A computer within the retail capital-investment Schedule: the heading alone does not establish 0% treatment. Check the actual scheme/goods, written approval, source period, 31 March 2031 import cutoff and claim requirements in the [customs relief control](bm-vat-gst.md#conditional-relief-and-later-notices). Do not start the written-claim clock from each import or restart the principal Act's defined 3-year period.

- A home purchase price is known but assessed ARV or statutory category is missing: **Needs Input**, not a higher-band land-tax amount.
- An ordinary private dwelling has assessed ARV BMD 22,100 and no relief or adjustment: the selected 2026 annual amount is BMD 439.50, using all reached bands and the base. Confirm the period and other stated premises before using it.
- An owner turns 65 or dies during a tax period: establish every s 3A condition and the Commissioner's satisfaction for that period. Neither age alone nor death during the period establishes the annual result.
- A land-tax objection or payment-plan request has been made: identify actual demand/service and any written postponement; neither request automatically stays payment.
- A BMD 2 million sale price is known, but the chargeable instrument/date or statutory consideration is not: **Needs Input** for actual stamp duty. Only an established ordinary Head 14 case without relief/special rules gives the conditional BMD 103,950 amount.
- A purchaser claims a first home, previously held exactly 25% or jointly acquires the entire interest: test each statutory limb separately. Neither price/status alone, a partial-history fact nor a new application establishes relief or a granted notice.
- An actually granted qualifying first-home notice applies to established consideration BMD 1.2 million: conditional duty is BMD 12,600, not the ordinary BMD 48,300 or restarted low bands. Keep the opinion, declarations and remaining duty separate from relieved advances.
- A stamp-duty instrument was executed overseas, delivered in escrow or sent for adjudication: establish its actual statutory event and any separate permission. A generic 30 days from the last signature, automatic clock suspension or the BMD 500 cap for every consequence is unsupported.

- **FCPT control:** A resident non-exempt purchase has an established BMD base 1,000: the 1.25% product is BMD 12.50, subject to the stated operative period. A foreign face amount or unexplained all-in quote is not automatically that base.
- **FCPT control:** An exempted-company label, 79.99% or exactly 80% ownership, or a permit-company question: apply the separate statutory residence routes/directions. Preserve the 80% boundary and hold the malformed permit-company default; do not infer zero FCPT or CIT residence.
- **FCPT control:** A listed body acts for a customer/affiliate, an insurance/pension category lacks actual per-case permission or a general solar project claims the specific exemption: no category-only exemption; establish remaining charge/exemption facts.
- **FCPT control:** A quarter-end webpage replaces an absent bank notice, a purchaser is asked to file the bank return or a fractional-cent product is rounded under the Stamp Duties Act: reject those substitutions. Keep actual return authority, current instructions and precision open, preserve s 4(4) and do not turn any FCPT outcome into zero withholding.

- **Original Bahrain control:** The original entry date, a 2026 protocol signature or a cached blank date does not settle current application. Obtain the operative instruments, notification/application, relevant MLI and termination evidence.
- **Original Bahrain control:** A 25%/24.99% holding, zero source ceiling or public-sector label does not establish residence, beneficial ownership, PE treatment or entitlement, and does not exempt the payer company's profits. Keep the original Art 10 branches and specified-recipient conditions.
- **Original Bahrain control:** A royalty described as interest, or a late-payment penalty described as a debt return, requires characterisation. Art 12 supplies no zero royalty ceiling and Art 11 excludes late-payment penalty charges.
- **Original Bahrain control:** A source ceiling of zero does not create resident exemption or a deemed foreign credit. Apply the relevant Art 22 route, actual tax paid, attributable-tax cap, progression and paragraph 4 switch-off, resolving disputed levies/refunds separately.
- **Original Bahrain control:** First notification, presentation to each competent authority and a court decision are different facts. Preserve the Art 23(1) nationality route, 3-year MAP clock and qualified 2-year arbitration clock; complete diplomatic facts and Arts 27–28 are needed for an operative-period result.

## Required handoff and self-check

Record the taxpayer/entity and group, tax/question, fiscal period, source version and effective period, screening result, unanswered facts, elections, responsible authority and verified public route. Hand detailed computations to the relevant topic with its unresolved evidence clearly attached. Output a source-cited orientation memo, screening working paper, **Needs Input** list and reviewer checklist. Before release, check that no VAT/CGT/withholding unknown became zero, no general income-tax refusal blocked conditional CIT, no calendar-year default displaced the statutory period and no signing date became a treaty commencement date. For customs, also record the code/unit, statutory value, route, available allowance and approval/CPC evidence; check that no unknown goods label became a rate and no historical schedule or form became current by assumption. For land tax, also record the assessment instrument and ARV, statutory owner and period-start class, applicable relief/adjustment decision, demand/service and any written postponement. Check that a purchase price did not become ARV, a missing input did not become a higher band or nil tax, and an information, objection or payment clock did not replace another. For stamp duty, also record the instrument/head/date/location, statutory BMD consideration and related transactions, property/interest and applicable exemptions; each purchaser's status/history, opinion/declarations and granted notice where the first-home route requires them; any required late permission/remission; execution/escrow and applicable assessment/appeal events, stamped instrument and receipts. Record inapplicable documents as N/A. Check that price or a first-home label did not establish relief, a request did not suspend a clock and the late-penalty cap did not become a cap on duty or conviction consequences. For FCPT, also record the purchaser/capacity and exchange-control residence route/directions, bank/currency/purchase, statutory BMD base and precision question, Schedule route and actual permissions/project facts; record the written s 4 notice and reporting/remittance requirements when preparing bank returns. Check that a category, peg, foreign face amount, quarter-end page or stamp-duty rounding rule did not substitute for those facts, and preserve s 4(4). Obtain practitioner review before relying on a liability or filing.
