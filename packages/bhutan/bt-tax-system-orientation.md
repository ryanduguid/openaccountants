# Bhutan tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the taxpayer, where income arose, residence facts and period. Check the applicable treaty and foreign-tax relief.

## Territorial reach

A resident's assessable business income includes income from Bhutan and abroad, with separate rules for calculating domestic and foreign business income and using losses. For employment, investment and other income, the ordinary rule includes Bhutan-source amounts. The same territorial rule applies to a nonresident's business income. Check the statutory source and permanent establishment attribution provisions for cross-border business. An applicable double tax agreement can override domestic treatment. [Income Tax Act 2025, ss 19–20 and 236–248](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf)

## Residence

An individual is resident if domiciled in Bhutan, unless they have a permanent home abroad for the whole income year and have no permanent home in Bhutan. Residence also applies to a person present during the income year whose presence is part of at least 183 days in a 12-month period beginning or ending in that year, or to a Bhutan government employee or official posted abroad during the year. Part-days count. For an individual who qualifies only by presence, residence starts at the beginning of the qualifying period. A company qualifies by Bhutanese formation, registration or principal office, or management and control exercised in Bhutan during the year. Partnerships qualify by local formation or management and control, and trusts have separate tests. [Income Tax Act 2025, ss 226–232](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf)

## Taxpayer types

An individual reports their sole-trader income as business income. A partnership allocates its income or loss to the partners and has withholding obligations. A company is taxed separately from its shareholders. A trust is generally taxed separately from its beneficiaries, subject to the Act's rule for a beneficiary presently entitled to the trust income. [Income Tax Act 2025, ss 25, 163–176 and 181–195](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf)

## Currency

The Bhutanese ngultrum (BTN) is the monetary unit. Specific rules apply to foreign-currency amounts on financial instruments. Check the applicable accounting standard and any translation method approved in writing by the department. [Royal Monetary Authority Act 2010, s 87](https://www.rma.org.bt/media/RMA%20Act%202010.pdf) and [Income Tax Act 2025, ss 122–123](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf)

## Tax periods

The ordinary income year runs from 1 January to 31 December. The department can approve a different 12-month period. The 2025 Act applies from 1 January 2026, with savings rules for earlier obligations. [Income Tax Act 2025, ss 2–8, 290 and 706(40)](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf)

## Authorities

The Department of Revenue and Customs in the Ministry of Finance administers ordinary income tax through its headquarters and regional and branch offices. [Department of Revenue and Customs](https://www.drc.gov.bt/) and [Income Tax Act 2025, s 706(22)](https://www.drc.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Income-Tax-Act-of-Bhutan-2025.pdf)
