# Senegal Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax (Impôt sur les sociétés)

- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 30 percent of taxable profit  _(Code général des impôts (Loi 2012-31, annotated consolidation to January 2025, DGID), art. 36 — https://www.dgid.sn/storage/docs/CGI-2025.pdf ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/senegal/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Reduced rate — Free Export Enterprise (Entreprise Franche d'Exportation)** — 15% percent ((approx — confirm current regime status))  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI) / Free Export Enterprise regime — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/senegal/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Minimum corporate tax (Impôt Minimum Forfaitaire, IMF)** — 0.5% of the previous year's turnover excluding tax, capped at XOF 5,000,000 (art. 40, as amended by Loi 2019-17)  _(Code général des impôts (Loi 2012-31, annotated consolidation to January 2025, DGID), art. 40 — https://www.dgid.sn/storage/docs/CGI-2025.pdf ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/senegal/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Tax base** — Profits of businesses operating in Senegal; non-residents taxed on Senegal-source profits via a permanent establishment  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/senegal/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends (to non-residents)** — 10 percent on dividends and other distributions of companies subject to corporate income tax, withheld at source and final for individuals (unless reduced by tax treaty)  _(Code général des impôts (Loi 2012-31, annotated consolidation to January 2025, DGID), art. 173(2) and art. 203 — https://www.dgid.sn/storage/docs/CGI-2025.pdf ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/senegal/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on interest** — 16 percent on interest and other fixed-income investment products; 8 percent on deposit and current account interest; 13 percent on bonds, or 6 percent on bonds of at least five years issued in Senegal (unless reduced by tax treaty)  _(Code général des impôts (Loi 2012-31, annotated consolidation to January 2025, DGID), art. 173(2) and art. 208 — https://www.dgid.sn/storage/docs/CGI-2025.pdf ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/senegal/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on royalties / services paid to non-residents** — 20 percent effective: 25 percent on the net amount after a 20 percent deduction from the gross sums paid for services supplied or used in Senegal, royalties and professional activity (unless reduced by tax treaty)  _(Code général des impôts (Loi 2012-31, annotated consolidation to January 2025, DGID), art. 202 — https://www.dgid.sn/storage/docs/CGI-2025.pdf ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/senegal/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on services by certain residents not subject to CIT** — 5%  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/senegal/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Annual CIT return + financial statements filing deadline** — 30 April of the year following the financial year  _(Code général des impôts (Loi 2012-31, annotated consolidation to January 2025, DGID), art. 30 — https://www.dgid.sn/storage/docs/CGI-2025.pdf ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/senegal/corporate/tax-administration)_
- **CIT first installment (acompte)** — One third of the tax on the last assessed year's results, due within the first fifteen days of February  _(Code général des impôts (Loi 2012-31, annotated consolidation to January 2025, DGID), art. 214 — https://www.dgid.sn/storage/docs/CGI-2025.pdf ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/senegal/corporate/tax-administration)_
- **CIT second installment (acompte)** — One third of the tax on the last assessed year's results, due by 30 April  _(Code général des impôts (Loi 2012-31, annotated consolidation to January 2025, DGID), art. 214 — https://www.dgid.sn/storage/docs/CGI-2025.pdf ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/senegal/corporate/tax-administration)_
- **CIT final balance payment** — The balance on the declared results is paid spontaneously by 15 June  _(Code général des impôts (Loi 2012-31, annotated consolidation to January 2025, DGID), art. 214 — https://www.dgid.sn/storage/docs/CGI-2025.pdf ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/senegal/corporate/tax-administration)_
- **Newly incorporated companies** — Exempt from installments in the first year; full CIT due by 15 June following the first financial year  _(Code Général des Impôts (CGI) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/senegal/corporate/tax-administration)_
