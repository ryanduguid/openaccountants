# Nigeria tax system

> General orientation for 2026. Confirm the taxpayer, accounting period and transaction with a qualified local tax professional before applying these rules.

Amounts are in Nigerian naira (NGN). The Nigeria Tax Act 2025 and the Nigeria Tax Administration Act 2025 commenced on 1 January 2026. The Nigeria Revenue Service (NRS) and the relevant state or other tax authority have different responsibilities. Check which authority administers the tax for the taxpayer concerned. [Nigeria Tax Act 2025, Act No. 7](https://nass.gov.ng/documents/download/11249) · [Nigeria Tax Administration Act 2025, Act No. 5, s. 3](https://nass.gov.ng/documents/download/11250) · [State House commencement statement](https://statehouse.gov.ng/new-tax-laws-will-commence-on-january-1-2026-as-planned/)

## Income tax

- **Individuals and residence.** Residence may arise from domicile, a permanent home available for domestic use, habitual abode, substantial economic and immediate family ties, at least 183 days in a 12-month period including specified absences, or Nigerian diplomatic service abroad. Residents are generally subject to tax on income wherever it arises, and non-residents on Nigerian-source income under the Act. Section 58 and the Fourth Schedule apply 0% to the first NGN 800,000 of ordinary taxable income after applicable reliefs, then 15% to NGN 3 million, 18% to NGN 12 million, 21% to NGN 25 million, 23% to NGN 50 million and 25% above that. The Act separately addresses national minimum-wage earners. [Nigeria Tax Act 2025, ss. 12, 13, 17, 58 and 201 and Fourth Schedule](https://nass.gov.ng/documents/download/11249)
- **Companies.** Nigerian companies generally include worldwide profits. Section 56 sets the ordinary rate at 30% from commencement and provides a 0% rate for qualifying small companies. Section 59 imposes a separate 4% development levy on relevant assessable profits, with exclusions for small and non-resident companies and profits computed for hydrocarbon tax. The copies available from the National Assembly and the Lagos State Internal Revenue Service give different small-company turnover tests. Confirm the operative text and any rate order before applying a concession or projecting a later rate. [National Assembly text, ss. 6, 56, 59 and 201](https://nass.gov.ng/documents/download/11249) · [Lagos State published gazette, ss. 56 and 202](https://lirs.gov.ng/assets/docs/Gazette%20-%20NIGERIA%20TAX%20ACT%2C%202025.pdf)

## VAT and filing

- **VAT.** The standard rate is 7.5% on taxable supplies. Check place of supply, exemptions and zero-rating before charging VAT. The ordinary monthly VAT return is due by the 21st day of the following month, subject to the small-business and other exceptions in the administration Act. [Nigeria Tax Act 2025, ss. 143–148 and 185–186](https://nass.gov.ng/documents/download/11249) · [Nigeria Tax Administration Act 2025, s. 22](https://nass.gov.ng/documents/download/11250)
- **Filing.** Check the relevant state authority's individual return deadline, payment and any extension. The Lagos authority initially notified 31 March 2026 for returns covering the preceding calendar year and subsequently announced a local extension to 14 April 2026. A company in business for more than 18 months generally files within 6 months of its accounting year end. A newly incorporated company generally files by the earlier of 18 months from incorporation and 6 months from its first period end. Confirm separate payroll, VAT and payment obligations. [Lagos State filing notices](https://www.lirs.gov.ng/publications) · [Nigeria Tax Administration Act 2025, ss. 13, 14 and 16](https://nass.gov.ng/documents/download/11250)
