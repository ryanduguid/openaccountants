# Saint Vincent and the Grenadines: persons abroad contributions reference v0.1

**Tier 2 source-cited draft. Financial delivery is held.** This reference explains complete retained instruments and their limits. It is not a complete country companion under either declared workflow base. The missing 2024 and 2025 operative amendments, all later changes, applicable pensionable age, legal interpretation, operational outputs and professional review remain required before actual use. Loading the bases supplies none of that missing country content.

> **General reference only.** No person's eligibility, contributions, benefits, application, payment or filing is approved here. A qualified practitioner in Saint Vincent and the Grenadines must establish the requested-period law and review the complete position before reliance.

## Scope

A source reading and schedule-quotation method for the National Insurance (Persons Abroad and Voluntary Contributors) Regulations. S1 is the complete Issue 1/2009 compilation, S2 the complete SR&O 28/2013 and S3 the complete retained archived SR&O 13/2026. These readings do not prove the intervening or later legal chain. The principal compilation records SRO 8/1997 as amended by SRO 6/2003; the separate 1997 and 2003 originals were not independently read.

The quarterly wages schedule refers to regulation 8(1C). That provision points to 8(1B), which proceeds under 8(1A), the citizen residing in another state branch. Do not extend these amounts to every domestic voluntary contributor, every person working abroad, ordinary employees, employers or self-employed persons. Ordinary payroll, income tax, VAT and other programmes need separate country methods.

## Ask the client first

Read redacted records and ask only for missing facts. Share at most 10 factual questions across the declared bases. These are 5 grouped topics, not 5 mandatory questions. Keep real identifiers, identity documents, credentials and bank details with the reviewer; use stable redacted references and readiness flags. A client answer cannot establish missing law.

1. Requested contribution weeks or whole quarters and the complete legal versions for those periods.
2. Citizenship, residence, employment and self-employment history, age and separately established applicable pensionable age; which statutory branch the reviewer has confirmed.
3. Redacted contribution history, any Board certificate, determination, category selection and approved application or election evidence.
4. Income and category evidence, overlapping compulsory contributions and the Board's instructions for remittance and any currency conversion.
5. Applicable form, submission and payment route, receipt/reference evidence, precision rules and qualified local reviewer.

## The method, step by step

1. Fix the requested period and obtain the complete operative chain, including SR&O 31/2024 and 2/2025. A Gazette notice or an Assembly register does not reproduce either body. For an actual eligibility, payment or benefit result, stop with Needs Input until these legal gates close.
2. Separate the continuation-employment, resident voluntary and citizen-abroad branches. An overseas address alone does not select a schedule.
3. For a quotation of retained sources only, identify the instrument, provision, printed amount and stated effective date. Keep historical text separate from current applicability.
4. Preserve published cells. Do not replace them with percentage calculations, infer rounding or currency-conversion rules, or prorate a partial quarter without authority.
5. Record gaps in the reviewer brief. Actual delivery requires all universal and domain outputs, checks and qualified professional review together.
