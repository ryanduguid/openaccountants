# Madagascar Tax Overview

## Madagascar tax system at a glance

Madagascar levies income tax, corporate tax, salary tax (IRSA) and VAT (TVA) under the Code Général des Impôts (CGI), administered by the Direction Générale des Impôts. This is an unverified draft for tax year 2025 and must be reviewed by a licensed Malagasy accountant.

- **Currency** — Malagasy ariary (MGA)  _(Code Général des Impôts (Madagascar) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/madagascar)_
- **Tax authority** — Direction Générale des Impôts (DGI), Ministère de l'Économie et des Finances  _(Code Général des Impôts (Madagascar) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/madagascar/corporate/tax-administration)_
- **Standard tax / fiscal year** — Calendar year (31 December); companies may close on another 12-month period (e.g. 30 June) without prior authorisation  _(Code Général des Impôts (Madagascar) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/madagascar/corporate/tax-administration)_
- **Basis of taxation** — Residents are taxed on Madagascar-source income; salary income received in Madagascar is taxable even if paid by a foreign employer ((approx — confirm))  _(Code Général des Impôts (Madagascar) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/madagascar/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **Headline top personal salary-tax (IRSA) rate** — 20% %  _(Code Général des Impôts (Madagascar) — IRSA — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/madagascar/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **Headline corporate income tax (IR) rate** — 20% %  _(Code Général des Impôts (Madagascar) — Impôt sur les revenus (IR) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/madagascar/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Does VAT/GST exist?** — Yes — Taxe sur la Valeur Ajoutée (TVA) at a standard rate of 20% %  _(Code Général des Impôts (Madagascar) — TVA — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/madagascar/corporate/other-taxes)_
- **Annual corporate/individual income tax return deadline (31 Dec year-end)** — 15 May following the close of the financial year  _(Code Général des Impôts (Madagascar) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/madagascar/corporate/tax-administration)_
- **VAT filing frequency** — Monthly — return and payment due by the 15th of the following month  _(Code Général des Impôts (Madagascar) — TVA — https://manao.mg/fr/declaration-mensuelle-tva)_
- **Turnover threshold for the 'real' tax regime / VAT liability** — MGA 400,000,000 annual turnover MGA  _(Code Général des Impôts (Madagascar) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/madagascar/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
