# Brunei Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax rates and incentives

Brunei's standard corporate income tax rate is 18.5% for resident and non-resident companies. Generous start-up and MSME reliefs apply, and oil and gas upstream profits are taxed at a much higher special rate. Figures should be confirmed against the current Income Tax Act and MoFE guidance.

- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 18.5% of chargeable income for the year of assessment 2015 and later years  _(Income Tax Act (Chapter 35), Revised Edition 2024, Ministry of Finance copy, s 35(1)(f) — https://www.mof.gov.bn/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/TR_Relevant-Acts_Income-Tax-Act-Chapter-35.pdf ; Ministry of Finance, Revenue Division, Income Tax — https://www.mof.gov.bn/div_revenue_typesoftaxes_incometax/)_
- **Threshold relief on first BND 100,000 of chargeable income** — Only 25% of every dollar of the first BND 100,000 of chargeable income is charged to tax, an effective rate of 4.625%  _(Income Tax Act (Chapter 35), Revised Edition 2024, Ministry of Finance copy, s 35(4)(a) — https://www.mof.gov.bn/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/TR_Relevant-Acts_Income-Tax-Act-Chapter-35.pdf ; Ministry of Finance, Revenue Division, Income Tax — https://www.mof.gov.bn/div_revenue_typesoftaxes_incometax/)_
- **Threshold relief on next BND 150,000 of chargeable income** — Only 50% of every dollar of the next BND 150,000 of chargeable income (BND 100,001 to BND 250,000) is charged to tax, an effective rate of 9.25%  _(Income Tax Act (Chapter 35), Revised Edition 2024, Ministry of Finance copy, s 35(4)(b) — https://www.mof.gov.bn/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/TR_Relevant-Acts_Income-Tax-Act-Chapter-35.pdf ; Ministry of Finance, Revenue Division, Income Tax — https://www.mof.gov.bn/div_revenue_typesoftaxes_incometax/)_
- **Chargeable income above BND 250,000** — Taxed in full at the 18.5% statutory rate  _(Income Tax Act (Chapter 35), Revised Edition 2024, Ministry of Finance copy, s 35(4) — https://www.mof.gov.bn/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/TR_Relevant-Acts_Income-Tax-Act-Chapter-35.pdf ; Ministry of Finance, Revenue Division, Income Tax — https://www.mof.gov.bn/div_revenue_typesoftaxes_incometax/)_
- **MSME turnover exemption** — Companies with gross sales / turnover not exceeding BND 1,000,000 are exempt from corporate income tax  [Income Tax Act, s 10(4)–(5)](https://www.mof.gov.bn/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/TR_Relevant-Acts_Income-Tax-Act-Chapter-35.pdf)
- **Newly incorporated company exemption** — For each of the first three years of assessment of a qualifying company (incorporated and registered in Brunei, resident, and not limited by guarantee), the first BND 100,000 of chargeable income is exempt and only 50% of the next BND 150,000 is charged to tax; the first three years run from the year of assessment of the basis period in which the company is incorporated  _(Income Tax Act (Chapter 35), Revised Edition 2024, Ministry of Finance copy, s 35(5) and (7) — https://www.mof.gov.bn/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/TR_Relevant-Acts_Income-Tax-Act-Chapter-35.pdf ; Ministry of Finance, Revenue Division, Income Tax — https://www.mof.gov.bn/div_revenue_typesoftaxes_incometax/)_
- **Oil and gas exploration and production rate** — 55% on the profits of companies engaged in the exploration and production of oil and gas, under the Income Tax (Petroleum) Act (Chapter 119)  _(Ministry of Finance, Revenue Division, Income Tax — https://www.mof.gov.bn/div_revenue_typesoftaxes_incometax/)_
- **Tax base.** Check income accruing in, derived from or received in Brunei Darussalam. Apply any exemption, allowable deductions and capital allowances. [Income Tax Act, ss 8 and 10–11](https://www.mof.gov.bn/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/TR_Relevant-Acts_Income-Tax-Act-Chapter-35.pdf)
- **Withholding tax on dividends** — 0%; dividends are not among the specified payments to non-residents that attract withholding under ss 35(2) to (2B) and 37  _(Ministry of Finance, Revenue Division, Withholding of Tax: tax rates — https://www.mof.gov.bn/div_revenue_withholdingoftax_whtrate/)_
- **Withholding tax on interest to non-residents** — 2.5% of the gross interest, commission, fee or other payment in connection with a loan or indebtedness paid to a non-resident without a Brunei permanent establishment  _(Income Tax Act (Chapter 35), Revised Edition 2024, Ministry of Finance copy, ss 35(2) and 37(1) — https://www.mof.gov.bn/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/TR_Relevant-Acts_Income-Tax-Act-Chapter-35.pdf ; Ministry of Finance, Revenue Division, Withholding of Tax: tax rates — https://www.mof.gov.bn/div_revenue_withholdingoftax_whtrate/)_
- **Withholding tax on royalties to non-residents** — 10% of the gross royalty or lump sum for the use of movable property, and of payments for the use of scientific, technical, industrial or commercial knowledge  _(Income Tax Act (Chapter 35), Revised Edition 2024, Ministry of Finance copy, s 35(2A)(a) — https://www.mof.gov.bn/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/TR_Relevant-Acts_Income-Tax-Act-Chapter-35.pdf ; Ministry of Finance, Revenue Division, Withholding of Tax: tax rates — https://www.mof.gov.bn/div_revenue_withholdingoftax_whtrate/)_
- **Withholding tax on technical fees / management fees to non-residents** — 10% on payments for technical assistance or services, 10% on management fees, 10% on rent for movable property and 10% on remuneration paid to a non-resident director  _(Income Tax Act (Chapter 35), Revised Edition 2024, Ministry of Finance copy, ss 35(2A) and (2B) — https://www.mof.gov.bn/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/TR_Relevant-Acts_Income-Tax-Act-Chapter-35.pdf ; Ministry of Finance, Revenue Division, Withholding of Tax: tax rates — https://www.mof.gov.bn/div_revenue_withholdingoftax_whtrate/)_
- **Withholding tax remittance deadline** — The payer files the withholding tax return through STARS and pays the amount deducted to the Collector of Income Tax within 14 days after the payment to the non-resident, counting weekends and public holidays; late payment adds 5% of the tax and a further 1% for each completed month after 30 days, capped at 15%  _(Income Tax Act (Chapter 35), Revised Edition 2024, Ministry of Finance copy, s 37(1) and (4) — https://www.mof.gov.bn/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/TR_Relevant-Acts_Income-Tax-Act-Chapter-35.pdf ; Income Tax Public Ruling PR 04/2017, Withholding of Tax on Specified Nature of Payments, paras 7.1 and 7.2 — https://www.mof.gov.bn/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/PR_004-2017_Public_Ruling_on_Witholding_of_Tax.pdf)_
- **Estimated Chargeable Income (ECI) deadline** — A company that has not filed its return must furnish an estimate of its chargeable income within three months after the end of the accounting period for the year of assessment  _(Income Tax Act (Chapter 35), Revised Edition 2024, Ministry of Finance copy, s 52A(1) — https://www.mof.gov.bn/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/TR_Relevant-Acts_Income-Tax-Act-Chapter-35.pdf ; Ministry of Finance, Revenue Division, Income Tax — https://www.mof.gov.bn/div_revenue_typesoftaxes_incometax/)_
- **Corporate return and payment.** The ordinary 2026 return was due by 30 June 2026. Self-assessed tax is due by the applicable return or estimate deadline. Check the separate assessed payment route. [Income Tax Act, ss 52 and 69A–70](https://www.mof.gov.bn/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/TR_Relevant-Acts_Income-Tax-Act-Chapter-35.pdf) · [Income Tax Amendment Order 2024, ss 3 and 9–10](https://www.agc.gov.bn/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/S-46_2024_E-INCOME-TAX-ACT-AMENDMENT-ORDER-2024.pdf) · [Revenue Division 2026 notice](https://www.ocp.mofe.gov.bn/news/)
- **Amended return and payment.** The November 2024 amendment allows one qualifying amendment within 6 months after the original section 52(1) time allowed expires. Section 69B requires payment by the date the amended return is required. Refer actual amendments to a Brunei tax specialist for eligibility, the Collector's form and manner, the deadline and increases under section 52B(4). [Income Tax Act (Amendment) Order 2024, ss 4, 6 and 10](https://www.agc.gov.bn/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/S-46_2024_E-INCOME-TAX-ACT-AMENDMENT-ORDER-2024.pdf)
- **Late payment penalty** — 5% of the tax is added when tax is not paid within the prescribed period; if the tax is still outstanding 60 days after that penalty, a further 1% of the outstanding tax is added for each completed month, capped at 12%  _(Income Tax Act (Chapter 35), Revised Edition 2024, Ministry of Finance copy, s 72(1) — https://www.mof.gov.bn/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/TR_Relevant-Acts_Income-Tax-Act-Chapter-35.pdf ; Ministry of Finance, Revenue Division, Corporate Tax FAQ — https://www.mof.gov.bn/div_revenue_faq_corporatetax/)_
