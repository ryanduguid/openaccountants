# Lebanon Tax Overview

## Lebanon Tax Overview

## The Lebanese tax system at a glance

Lebanon operates a schedular tax system administered by the Ministry of Finance, with separate taxes on corporate profits, employment income, business income, and movable capital. Taxation rests on a principle of territoriality: profit is taxable if it arises from an effort or activity exerted in Lebanon.

- **Tax year** — Calendar year (1 January to 31 December) (Companies may request approval to use a different accounting year)  _([Income Tax Law (Decree-Law No. 144 of 1959)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/lebanon/corporate/tax-administration))_
- **Currency** — Lebanese pound (LBP / LL)  _([PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries — Lebanon](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/lebanon))_
- **Tax authority** — Ministry of Finance — Directorate General of Taxation ((approx — confirm exact directorate name))  _(Ministry of Finance — Directorate General of Taxation)_
- **Basis of taxation** — Territorial — income taxed if generated through activity exerted in Lebanon  _([Income Tax Law (Decree-Law No. 144 of 1959)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/lebanon/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 17% percent  _([Income Tax Law (Decree-Law No. 144 of 1959), Chapter 1](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/lebanon/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Top personal/payroll tax rate** — 25% percent (Progressive payroll rates run 2% to 25%; business income 4% to 25%)  _([Income Tax Law (Decree-Law No. 144 of 1959)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/lebanon/individual/taxes-on-personal-income))_
- **Does VAT exist? / Standard VAT rate** — Yes — standard VAT rate 11% percent  _([VAT Law (Law No. 379 of 2001)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/lebanon/corporate/other-taxes))_
- **Withholding tax on dividends** — 10% percent  _([Tax on Movable Capital (Decree-Law No. 146 of 1959)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/lebanon/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **Corporate tax return filing deadline** — 31 March (entities) / 31 May (capital companies) of the following year  _([Income Tax Law (Decree-Law No. 144 of 1959)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/lebanon/corporate/tax-administration))_
- **Structure of income taxation** — Schedular — separate taxes on business profits, salaries, and movable capital income  _([Income Tax Law (Decree-Law No. 144 of 1959)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/lebanon/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
