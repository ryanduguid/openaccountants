## Selected CFAE scope and amounts

**S3 Article 7** replaces the old opening scope wording. It includes an enterprise with its registered office or effective management seat in Saint Barthélemy, including a dormant enterprise. It separately addresses French enterprises without a BL registered office or effective management seat that have establishments carrying on activity in BL, and foreign enterprises with establishments carrying on activity there. Do not retain S1's deleted 'activité régulière' condition as the amended test.

The enterprise definition covers natural and legal persons, with the literal exception 'à l’exception des propriétaires-bailleurs', and the printed activity, charge or office and appropriate registration conditions. The listed activities include industrial, commercial, civil, craft, agricultural, liberal, artistic, sporting and services activities. The proprietor-landlord wording does not establish the absence of other taxes or settle a mixed business case. Deleting the old Article 8 reservation from Article 7 does not itself repeal Article 8; actual exemption analysis remains held.

Where different activities within the same enterprise each have a distinct establishment, Article 7 subjects each such activity to the contribution. Where the same activity is carried on through several establishments, the contribution is due once. Article 7 refers to NAF/APE activity identification. Actual activity classification, the number of chargeable contributions and employee allocation must be evidenced. S5's instruction to complete a form for each establishment does not itself authorise multiplying the tax for establishments carrying on the same activity.

**S1 Article 9** states a fixed part of **EUR 350** plus a variable part of **EUR 100 per employee**. S5 prints the same amounts in its 2025 table. These 2 dated sources support the stated historical arithmetic; they do not prove unchanged 2026 rates, no cap, no exemption or a particular employee-counting method. No currency conversion is made.

**S1 Article 144** rounds tax contribution bases, majorations, reductions and reliefs to the nearest euro, with exactly EUR 0.50 counted as one euro, at the computation of each individual tax or duty. This is the scope of that selected older provision. It does not establish rounding of individual employees, intermediate calculations or every accounting amount, or resolve its present application after later law.

## Article 10: September amendment and December replacement

S4 Article 1 replaces exactly these 5 quoted opening paragraphs of the September version: the annual dates and preceding full-year social-body attestation; remunerated contract categories; manager/accountant attestation based on the Registre Unique du Personnel; production of that register on control; and the general Article 158 majoration cross-reference. Its single replacement paragraph makes CFAE exigible from **1 January**, payable by **31 March** of the tax year, using employees appearing on the **Déclaration Sociale Nominative established at 31 December of the preceding year**. S4's recital about returning to earlier wording does not restore the whole older Article 10.

Thus the December replacement does not prescribe the replaced full-calendar-year attestation or retain its specific lists of directors and contracts as the employee-counting authority. The DSN wording still needs its applicable year, actual record and counting/allocation method. S5 corroborates a DSN reference of **31 December 2024 for its 2025 form**. Its footnote describes all employees, whatever contract type, including CDI, CDD, apprentices and paid trainees, declared for the stated period. It does not establish a new full-year cumulative or average method.

The following S3 Article 10 provisions lie outside S4's quoted replacement:

- The old bank value-date wording is struck and a bank-operation clause is inserted. The printed wording includes 'd’opération du virement par la banque émettrice, en cas de virement tardif enregistré sur le compte de dépôt de fonds de la régie de la collectivité.' Its grammar and application require clarification before choosing an actual payment date. The taxpayer bears the proof burden; the debtor's SIRET must appear in the transfer reference. Transfers without a reference are rejected and not taken into account under this selected text.
- The old cheque-receipt/encashment paragraph and its rejected-cheque 80% passage are struck. The old non-declaration/non-payment paragraph providing for a titre de recettes is also struck. S4 does not restore them. These deletions do not abolish independently printed Article 158, other collection powers or all penalties.
- The inserted electronic-payment provision, including credit card, uses the electronic transaction date. S5 still offers cheque, card and transfer options administratively. The printed cheque option does not revive the deleted statutory cheque timing paragraph or settle the actual current accepted methods.
- For an enterprise created between **1 January and 30 September**, the **fixed part** is due and payable within **90 days following the declared activity-start date**, under the specified CFE, commercial registry or eligible online formalities. This is not a variable-part proration rule and supplies no October–December treatment.
- For radiation, the retained date is the **registration date of the formality**, not merely a date stated on a declaration. The selected wording refers to the competent CFE or eligible online route. A present operational route is not established by this dated text.
- Within the same tax year, CFAE is due once in the specified legal-form changes: a company transforms into another legal form; or a sole enterprise creates a company **and transfers its fonds de commerce and closes the sole enterprise**. This is not unconditional relief for every new company, business transfer or group of entities.

**S3 Article 11** uses 'entreprises' and ties its dissolution exemption to the date of the dissolution entry shown on the K-bis or another official document evidencing legal existence, giving URSSAF as an example. It covers only sums not already paid. It does not establish a refund of paid sums or an exemption from undocumented cessation alone.

**S3 Article 12** refers CFAE complaints to Articles 184 and following. Those complete procedural units have not been qualified here. No complaint deadline, tribunal, suspension, refund right or procedural step is derived from that cross-reference or from the decisions' separate challenge boilerplate.

## Selected penalty provisions

**S3 Article 156**, complete across pp 32–33, retains **0.75% per month** for late interest and strikes out 'capitalisés'. It applies to sums charged to the taxpayer or whose payment has been deferred. Its printed period runs from the first day of the month following the month in which the principal should have been paid to the last day of the payment month. Do not treat the struck word as authority for compounding.

**S3 Article 158 III**, within the complete Article 158 read on p 33, expressly includes CFAE under Articles 7–12 amongst the listed taxes. Without prejudice to Article 156, default or insufficiency of payment attracts a majoration equal to **80% of the tax amount** in this selected wording. This is distinct from the struck cheque passage in Article 10. Paragraph I's parenthetical reference to a dock-duty sanctions regime awaiting State approval is not assumed to extend to CFAE paragraph III. Paragraphs II, IV and V concern other taxes and do not supply CFAE penalty conditions here.

These selected provisions are insufficient for an actual penalty assessment. Articles 154, 155 and 157, the full relevant collection and sanction chain, current applicability, possible relief and case-specific conditions remain unqualified. Do not infer that non-filing alone always triggers the paragraph III payment majoration, add interest and 80% automatically, compound interest, apply an assumed cap or calculate an actual sanction from this reference. Pure percentage arithmetic below is not a legal assessment.

## The official 2025 form

S5 cites Articles 7–12 and 157 in its header; Article 157 remains unqualified here. It asks for a declaration for each establishment, all identification fields, the establishment's SIRET, activity code and legal representative's declaration of employees according to the personnel annex. Its table is fixed part EUR 350, variable part employee count multiplied by EUR 100 and a total. The personnel page asks for identification, employees' surnames, given names and dates of birth and a total, with the DSN dated 31 December 2024. Do not collect or enter real personal data merely to illustrate this reference.

For transfer, S5 instructs the filer to put the **14-digit SIRET for each establishment and the CFAE year paid** in the transfer label, then return the declaration and transfer proof, described as an avis d'opéré, to its printed CFAE contact. This is a description of that complete 2025 form, not permission to submit anything or a verified 2026 filing instruction. Obtain and verify the applicable form, destination, payment instructions and account details through the current official channel before any authorised filing or payment preparation. No account identifier is reproduced here.

## Seven hypothetical positions

| --- | --- | --- |

| **D. | **E. Distinct activities** | Assume 2 distinct activities each have a distinct establishment within the Article 7 rule.
