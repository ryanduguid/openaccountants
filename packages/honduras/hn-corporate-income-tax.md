# Honduras Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax

Resident companies pay corporate income tax (ISR) at 25% of net taxable income on a territorial basis, plus a 5% solidarity contribution on taxable income above HNL 1 million. Several minimum-tax and anti-evasion measures can override the regular calculation.

- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 25% of net taxable income for legal persons  _(Ley del Impuesto sobre la Renta, texto consolidado al 26 de junio de 2018 (SAR), art 22(a) — https://www.sar.gob.hn/download/consolidado-ley-impuesto-sobre-la-renta-25-junio-2018/ ; SAR, Generalidades del Impuesto sobre la Renta — https://www.sar.gob.hn/download/generalidades-impuesto-sobre-la-renta/)_
- **Solidarity contribution (aportacion solidaria)** — 5% surcharge (sobretasa) on net taxable income above HNL 1,000,000, paid by legal persons other than those in the export and tourism special regimes; it is not deductible from income tax and follows the pagos a cuenta regime, with settlement on Form 103 with the annual return due 30 April  _(SAR, Generalidades de los impuestos con vencimiento al 30 de abril (June 2026) — https://www.sar.gob.hn/download/generalidades-impuestos-con-vencimiento-al-30-de-abril/)_
- **Tax base** — Net taxable income = Honduran-source gross income less allowable deductions and costs incurred to produce that income  _(Ley del Impuesto sobre la Renta (Income Tax Law))_
- **Minimum tax on gross income** — 1% of gross income for taxpayers whose prior-year gross income exceeded HNL 1,000,000,000, payable where the art 22 tariff would yield less (from fiscal year 2020); taxpayers at or below that figure are outside art 22-A  _(Ley del Impuesto sobre la Renta, texto consolidado al 26 de junio de 2018 (SAR), art 22-A — https://www.sar.gob.hn/download/consolidado-ley-impuesto-sobre-la-renta-25-junio-2018/ ; SAR, Generalidades del Impuesto sobre la Renta — https://www.sar.gob.hn/download/generalidades-impuesto-sobre-la-renta/ ; SAR, Generalidades de los impuestos con vencimiento al 30 de abril (June 2026) — https://www.sar.gob.hn/download/generalidades-impuestos-con-vencimiento-al-30-de-abril/)_
- **Reduced minimum tax (special sectors)** — 0.50% of gross income for cement, steel, construction-derivative, pharmaceutical, public-service and coffee production/export activities percent  _(Ley del Impuesto sobre la Renta, texto consolidado al 26 de junio de 2018 (SAR), art 22-A — https://www.sar.gob.hn/download/consolidado-ley-impuesto-sobre-la-renta-25-junio-2018/ ; PwC Worldwide Tax Summaries — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/honduras/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Anti-evasion measure (loss-making entities)** — 1% of gross income for taxpayers with gross income of HNL 100,000,000 or more reporting losses in two consecutive years (or two of five years); creditable against ISR percent  _(Ley del Impuesto sobre la Renta (Income Tax Law) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/honduras/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Foreign transport entities deemed base** — Foreign air, land and sea transport companies operating in Honduras are taxed on a deemed net taxable income equal to 10% of their Honduran-source gross income  _(Ley del Impuesto sobre la Renta, texto consolidado al 26 de junio de 2018 (SAR), art 22(c) — https://www.sar.gob.hn/download/consolidado-ley-impuesto-sobre-la-renta-25-junio-2018/ ; SAR, Generalidades del Impuesto sobre la Renta — https://www.sar.gob.hn/download/generalidades-impuesto-sobre-la-renta/)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends** — 10% on dividends and any other distribution of profits or reserves to residents and non-residents, withheld and remitted by the distributing company; capitalised reserves are exempt  _(Ley del Impuesto sobre la Renta, texto consolidado al 26 de junio de 2018 (SAR), arts 5(5) and 25 — https://www.sar.gob.hn/download/consolidado-ley-impuesto-sobre-la-renta-25-junio-2018/)_
- **Withholding tax on interest (non-resident)** — 10% on interest from commercial operations, bonds, securities and other obligations paid to non-residents  _(Ley del Impuesto sobre la Renta, texto consolidado al 26 de junio de 2018 (SAR), art 5(7) — https://www.sar.gob.hn/download/consolidado-ley-impuesto-sobre-la-renta-25-junio-2018/)_
- **Withholding tax on royalties (non-resident)** — 25% on royalties for patents, designs, secret processes and formulas, trademarks and copyright, and on mining and natural resource royalties, paid to non-residents  _(Ley del Impuesto sobre la Renta, texto consolidado al 26 de junio de 2018 (SAR), art 5(2) and 5(6) — https://www.sar.gob.hn/download/consolidado-ley-impuesto-sobre-la-renta-25-junio-2018/)_
- **Branch profit remittance withholding** — 10% on the income or profits obtained by foreign companies through branches, subsidiaries, agencies or representatives operating in Honduras  _(Ley del Impuesto sobre la Renta, texto consolidado al 26 de junio de 2018 (SAR), art 5(4) — https://www.sar.gob.hn/download/consolidado-ley-impuesto-sobre-la-renta-25-junio-2018/)_
- **Annual corporate return filing and payment deadline** — 30 April following the close of the calendar tax year (Form 103, filed through the Oficina Virtual with the Informe de Estado de Situación Financiera); the fourth instalment, the balance per the taxpayer's own computation, is paid with it  _(SAR, Generalidades de los impuestos con vencimiento al 30 de abril (June 2026) — https://www.sar.gob.hn/download/generalidades-impuestos-con-vencimiento-al-30-de-abril/ ; Ley del Impuesto sobre la Renta, texto consolidado al 26 de junio de 2018 (SAR), arts 28 and 34 — https://www.sar.gob.hn/download/consolidado-ley-impuesto-sobre-la-renta-25-junio-2018/)_
- **Advance income tax payments** — Four quarterly instalments (pagos a cuenta), each one quarter of the previous year's self-computed tax: the first three are paid by 30 June, 30 September and 31 December of the tax year and the fourth is the balance paid with the annual return  _(Ley del Impuesto sobre la Renta, texto consolidado al 26 de junio de 2018 (SAR), art 34 — https://www.sar.gob.hn/download/consolidado-ley-impuesto-sobre-la-renta-25-junio-2018/ ; SAR, Generalidades de los impuestos con vencimiento al 30 de abril (June 2026) — https://www.sar.gob.hn/download/generalidades-impuestos-con-vencimiento-al-30-de-abril/)_
