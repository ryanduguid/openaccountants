# Niger Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax rates and base

- **Overview of corporate income tax regime** — Companies in Niger are subject to the Impôt sur les Bénéfices (corporate income tax) under the Code Général des Impôts, with a minimum tax based on turnover. The figures below are an unverified draft and must be confirmed by a licensed Niger accountant.  _(Code Général des Impôts (Niger))_
- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 30 percent  _([Code Général des Impôts (Niger) — Impôt sur les Bénéfices](https://www.impots.gouv.ne/media/telech/LIVRE%201%20ET%202%20ENTIER%20CGI%202025.pdf))_
- **Minimum tax (Impôt Minimum Forfaitaire / IMF)** — Approximately 1% of annual turnover, subject to a floor amount percent (approx — confirm)  _(Code Général des Impôts (Niger) — Impôt Minimum Forfaitaire (IMF))_
- **Tax base** — Net taxable profit (accounting profit per SYSCOHADA adjusted for tax purposes); territorial source basis (approx — confirm)  _(Code Général des Impôts (Niger))_
- **Withholding tax on dividends (IRVM)** — 10 percent (approx — confirm)  _(Code Général des Impôts (Niger) — Impôt sur le Revenu des Valeurs Mobilières (IRVM))_
- **Withholding tax on interest (IRVM/IRCM)** — Generally 13% to 25% depending on the instrument (e.g. ~13% on long-term bond interest, higher on other products) percent (approx — confirm)  _(Code Général des Impôts (Niger) — Impôt sur le Revenu des Valeurs Mobilières (IRVM))_
- **Withholding tax on royalties / service fees to non-residents (BNC withholding)** — Withholding applies on payments to non-residents (commonly ~16%); rate may be reduced by treaty percent (approx — confirm)  _(Code Général des Impôts (Niger))_
- **Capital gains (professional/business)** — Taxed as business income; a 20% rate is referenced for certain professional gains percent (approx — confirm)  _(Code Général des Impôts (Niger))_
- **Annual corporate return filing deadline** — Generally 30 April (or by 30 April / within 4 months of year-end) — confirm exact date (approx — confirm)  _(Code Général des Impôts (Niger))_
- **Advance/instalment payments** — Corporate tax is paid via instalments/advances during the year with a final balancing payment on filing (approx — confirm)  _(Code Général des Impôts (Niger))_
