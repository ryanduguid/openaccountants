# Benin Company Formation & Entity Choice

## Entity types and company formation

The revised OHADA AUSCGIE took effect on **5 May 2014**. Benin's
**Décret n°2014-220 of 26 March 2014** also took effect on **5 May 2014** under
article 11. Article 6 lets the members determine the SARL's total share capital
freely and record it in the statutes. It requires equal nominal share values
and refers subscription conditions to the revised AUSCGIE. Article 9 preserves
the other AUSCGIE provisions that the decree does not expressly address.
[Benin decree, articles 6, 9 and 11, pages 3 and 4](https://sgg.gouv.bj/doc/decret-2014-220/download),
[OHADA register](https://www.ohada.org/droit-des-societes-commerciales-et-du-gie/)

- **SARL subscription and pay-up.** The shares must be fully subscribed.
  Contributions in kind must be fully paid. Cash shares must be paid at least
  half on subscription, with the balance within **2 years of RCCM registration**.
  [AUSCGIE, article 311-1](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974#page=77)
- **Ordinary SA capital and pay-up.** Article 387 sets an ordinary minimum of
  **CFA 10,000,000**, with whole-number nominal share values freely fixed in the
  statutes. Capital must be fully subscribed before the statutes are signed.
  Cash shares must be paid at least one-quarter on subscription and the balance
  within **3 years of RCCM registration**. Article 824 sets **CFA 100,000,000**
  for securities listed on a member state's exchange or publicly offered for
  placement in one or more member states. Regulated activities require separate
  capital checks. [AUSCGIE, articles 387 to 389 and 824](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974)
- **Single-member formation.** A SARL may be formed by a single natural or legal
  person. An SA may have a single shareholder. [AUSCGIE, articles 309 and 385](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974)

Company law in Benin follows the OHADA Uniform Act on Commercial Companies (Acte Uniforme OHADA relatif au droit des sociétés commerciales). The most common forms are the SARL and SA. Registration runs through the APIEx one-stop shop (guichet unique).

- **Common entity types** — SARL (private limited), SA (public limited), SAS (simplified), SUARL (single-member SARL), succursale (branch), entreprise individuelle / entreprenant (sole trader)  _(Acte Uniforme OHADA relatif au droit des sociétés commerciales — https://chandrawatpartners.com/incorporation-of-limited-liability-company-llc-in-benin/)_
- **SARL total share capital.** Members fix the total freely in the statutes under Décret n°2014-220, article 6, effective **5 May 2014**. [Benin decree, articles 6 and 11](https://sgg.gouv.bj/doc/decret-2014-220/download)
- **SA minimum share capital.** The ordinary minimum is **CFA 10,000,000**. Article 824 requires **CFA 100,000,000** for listed or publicly offered securities within its scope. Check any additional regulated-sector requirements. [AUSCGIE, articles 387 and 824](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974)
- **Registration body** — APIEx (Agence de Promotion des Investissements et des Exportations) — guichet unique / one-stop shop  _(APIEx guichet unique de formalisation des entreprises — https://tradecouncil.org/country-guides/doing-business-with-benin/)_
- **Core incorporation steps** — 1) Draft articles of association; 2) deposit capital and obtain bank certificate (if capital contributed in cash); 3) register at the APIEx one-stop shop (RCCM registration); 4) obtain tax ID (IFU/NIF)  _(APIEx guichet unique; Acte Uniforme OHADA — https://chandrawatpartners.com/incorporation-of-limited-liability-company-llc-in-benin/)_
- **Incorporation timeline** — As fast as 8 business hours to a few days via the APIEx one-stop shop  _(APIEx guichet unique — https://tradecouncil.org/country-guides/doing-business-with-benin/)_
- **Registration cost** — Approximately USD 100 (registration fee) plus notary/professional fees; reduced fees for SARL (approx — confirm current fee schedule)  _(APIEx guichet unique — barème des frais — https://chandrawatpartners.com/incorporation-of-limited-liability-company-llc-in-benin/)_
- **Tax identification number** — Companies must obtain an IFU (Identifiant Fiscal Unique) / NIF from the DGI  _(Code Général des Impôts (Bénin) — IFU — https://taxdo.com/resources/global-tax-id-validation-guide/benin)_
- **Accounting framework** — SYSCOHADA (OHADA accounting system) financial statements required  _(Acte Uniforme OHADA relatif au droit comptable (SYSCOHADA))_
- **National tax compliance remains to be verified.** Confirm the IS return and financial-statement submission to the DGI, the applicable tax deadlines and VAT and payroll filings against current Beninese legislation and DGI guidance.
- **Statutory auditor (commissaire aux comptes)** — Required for SA, and for SARL exceeding OHADA size thresholds (balance sheet, turnover, headcount) (approx — confirm thresholds)  _(Acte Uniforme OHADA relatif au droit des sociétés commerciales)_

## Annual company reporting under the 2014 AUSCGIE

The revised AUSCGIE took effect on **5 May 2014**. The following rules address
annual SARL and SA decisions and commercial-company registry deposits.
[OHADA company law register](https://www.ohada.org/droit-des-societes-commerciales-et-du-gie/)

- **SARL annual meeting.** The annual ordinary general meeting must take place
  within **6 months after the financial year closes**. The managers may ask the
  competent court for an extension. [AUSCGIE, articles 347 and 348](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974#page=86)
- **SA annual meeting.** The ordinary general meeting must take place at least
  once a year, within **6 months after the financial year closes**, subject to
  an extension by court decision. [AUSCGIE, article 548](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974#page=133)
- **Sole-member companies.** The sole shareholder of an SA takes the annual
  ordinary decisions within **6 months after the financial year closes** and
  records them in minutes kept in the company's archives. Article 347 applies
  these sole-member decision rules to a SARL, with the exceptions it specifies.
  [AUSCGIE, article 347](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974#page=86),
  [articles 558 to 560](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974#page=135)
- **Deposit of annual accounts.** A commercial company must deposit the prior
  year's annual financial statements with the RCCM in the state of its
  registered office **within the month following approval** by the competent
  body. If approval is refused, a copy of the refusal decision must be deposited
  within the same period. [AUSCGIE, article 269, pages 65 and 66](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974#page=65)

For example, for a multi-member SARL with a **31 December 2025** year-end, the
ordinary annual meeting is due by **30 June 2026**, subject to a court extension.
Track the RCCM deposit deadline from the actual approval decision. Record the
national tax-return deadline separately. The current registry filing method,
fees, applicable accounting and audit rules and any sector-specific obligations
still need verification. [AUSCGIE, articles 269 and 348](https://biblio.ohada.org/doc_num.php?explnum_id=3974)
