# Benin Personal Income Tax

## Personal income tax (IRPP) bands and rates

**Benin taxes salary income and business income on two different scales.** The
bands below are the *salary* scale (art. 142 CGI), which is what an employee
faces; an individual with industrial, commercial, non-commercial, artisanal or
agricultural income is taxed instead under the art. 136 barème, which tops out
at 40% — see "Business and professional income" below. Quoting a single "top
IRPP rate" for Benin without saying which scale is meant is what produced the
errors this table used to carry.

- **IRPP band 1 (tax-free)** — 0% on the slice of monthly income up to XOF 50,000 percent (Bands are monthly XOF slices, applied cumulatively.)  _(Code Général des Impôts (Bénin) 2021, art. 142(1) — https://www.impots.finances.gouv.bj/code-general-des-impots/)_
- **IRPP band 2** — 10% on the slice from XOF 50,001 to 130,000 percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (Bénin) 2021, art. 142(1) — https://www.impots.finances.gouv.bj/code-general-des-impots/)_
- **IRPP band 3** — 15% on the slice from XOF 130,001 to 280,000 percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (Bénin) 2021, art. 142(1) — https://www.impots.finances.gouv.bj/code-general-des-impots/)_
- **IRPP band 4** — 20% on the slice from XOF 280,001 to 530,000 percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (Bénin) 2021, art. 142(1) — https://www.impots.finances.gouv.bj/code-general-des-impots/)_
- **IRPP top band** — 30% on the slice above XOF 530,000 percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (Bénin) 2021, art. 142(1) — https://www.impots.finances.gouv.bj/code-general-des-impots/)_
- **Tax-free threshold** — First XOF 50,000 of monthly salary is not taxed XOF  _(Code Général des Impôts (Bénin) 2021, art. 142(1) — https://www.impots.finances.gouv.bj/code-general-des-impots/)_
- **Business and professional income — separate barème, top rate 40%** — Industrial, commercial, non-commercial, artisanal and agricultural income is taxed at 30% on the slice from 0 to XOF 10,000,000, 35% from 10,000,001 to 20,000,000, and **40% above XOF 20,000,000** (annual). Note the first band starts at 30%: there is no tax-free slice on this scale  _(Code Général des Impôts (Bénin) 2021, art. 136 — https://www.impots.finances.gouv.bj/code-general-des-impots/)_
- **Minimum IRPP on business income** — Not less than 1% of receipts (produits encaissables), and in no case less than XOF 250,000  _(Code Général des Impôts (Bénin) 2021, art. 137(1) — https://www.impots.finances.gouv.bj/code-general-des-impots/)_
- **Dependent-children reduction (not a family quotient)** — The tax from the salary barème is reduced by a flat percentage set by the number of dependent children: 0% for one child, 5% for two, 10% for three, 15% for four, 20% for five, 23% for six or more. Benin uses this reduction, **not** a quotient familial that divides income into parts  _(Code Général des Impôts (Bénin) 2021, art. 142(2) — https://www.impots.finances.gouv.bj/code-general-des-impots/)_
- **Dependent child definition** — A child (including legally adopted) with no income separate from the taxpayer's, under 21, or under 25 if in full-time study, or disabled with no age limit  _(Code Général des Impôts (Bénin) 2021, art. 142(2) — https://www.impots.finances.gouv.bj/code-general-des-impots/)_
- **ORTB broadcasting levy on salaries** — On top of the barème: XOF 1,000 deducted from the March salary and XOF 3,000 from the June salary. Employees whose taxable income does not exceed the first band are exempt from the XOF 3,000 charge  _(Code Général des Impôts (Bénin) 2021, art. 142(3) — https://www.impots.finances.gouv.bj/code-general-des-impots/)_
- **Deductible employee social contributions** — Mandatory employee CNSS pension contributions are deductible from taxable salary income  _(Code Général des Impôts (Bénin) — IRPP; Code de Sécurité Sociale (as described at [rivermate.com](https://www.rivermate.com/guides/benin/taxes)))_
- **Professional expenses allowance** — A standard deduction for professional expenses is applied to salary income before the IRPP barème (approx — confirm rate/cap)  _(Code Général des Impôts (Bénin) — IRPP)_
- **Residence test** — An individual is generally resident if their principal home or main place of abode is in Benin, or they carry on their principal professional activity there (approx — confirm exact criteria)  _(Code Général des Impôts (Bénin) — domicile fiscal)_
- **Non-resident taxation** — Non-residents are taxed only on Benin-source income, often by final withholding at source (approx — confirm)  _(Code Général des Impôts (Bénin) — IRPP)_
- **Salary withholding is final (libératoire)** — Tax withheld from salaries discharges the liability, so an employee with only salary income is not left with a balance to settle on an annual return  _(Code Général des Impôts (Bénin) 2021, art. 181(3) — https://www.impots.finances.gouv.bj/code-general-des-impots/)_
- **Foreign-paid salaries — the employee files** — A Benin-resident paid by an employer established outside Benin must file a salary declaration each month and compute and remit the withholding themselves, on the same terms and deadlines as an employer  _(Code Général des Impôts (Bénin) 2021, art. 181(2) — https://www.impots.finances.gouv.bj/code-general-des-impots/)_
- **Annual IRPP filing deadline** — Annual personal income declaration generally due by 30 April of the following year. Relevant to taxpayers with business or non-salary income; salary withholding is final under art. 181(3) (approx — confirm which taxpayers must file)  _(Code Général des Impôts (Bénin) — IRPP)_
- **Monthly salary tax remittance** — Employer-withheld IRPP on salaries is remitted within the first ten days of the month following the month the salary relates to, in cash or by certified cheque payable to the Directeur Général des Impôts, with a monthly return in duplicate (electronic filing permitted)  _(Code Général des Impôts (Bénin) 2021, art. 182 — https://www.impots.finances.gouv.bj/code-general-des-impots/)_
