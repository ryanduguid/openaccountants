# Benin Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax (Impôt sur les Sociétés)

- **Standard IS rate** — 30% percent (art. 156 charges 30% on "les personnes morales autres que les industries" — i.e. 30% is the residual rate, not a rate that applies to industrial companies)  _(Code Général des Impôts (Bénin) 2021, art. 156 — https://www.impots.finances.gouv.bj/code-general-des-impots/)_
- **Reduced IS rate — industrial / manufacturing / mining companies** — 25% percent (industrial companies; and mining companies on profits arising exclusively from working a mineral deposit)  _(Code Général des Impôts (Bénin) 2021, art. 156 — https://www.impots.finances.gouv.bj/code-general-des-impots/)_
- **Hydrocarbon (oil & gas) sector IS rate** — Between 35% and 45% depending on contractual clauses percent (research, working, production and sale of natural hydrocarbons, including incidental transport in Benin. Where the petroleum title convention sets its own assessment and collection rules, the Code applies only so far as the convention leaves it unmodified)  _(Code Général des Impôts (Bénin) 2021, art. 156, 2° — https://www.impots.finances.gouv.bj/code-general-des-impots/)_
- **Minimum tax (Impôt Minimum) rate** — 0.75% of receipts (produits encaissables) for industrial companies; **1%** for mining companies and for all legal persons other than industries percent (**AUDIT FLASH POINT** — the 0.75% rate is often quoted as if it were the single minimum-tax rate. It is the industrial rate. A non-industrial company applying 0.75% underpays by a quarter of the charge)  _(Code Général des Impôts (Bénin) 2021, art. 156, 1° — https://www.impots.finances.gouv.bj/code-general-des-impots/)_
- **Minimum tax floor** — Not less than XOF 250,000 XOF (art. 156 states the XOF 250,000 floor for the general minimum and again for the service-station minimum, and art. 137 sets the same floor for individuals' business income)  _(Code Général des Impôts (Bénin) 2021, art. 156, 1° and 3° — https://www.impots.finances.gouv.bj/code-general-des-impots/)_
- **Minimum tax — service stations** — Notwithstanding the general minimum, a single rate of XOF 0.60 per litre of petroleum product sold, again subject to the XOF 250,000 floor. The same treatment applies to non-importing distributors supplying from approved importers at regulated prices  _(Code Général des Impôts (Bénin) 2021, art. 156, 3° — https://www.impots.finances.gouv.bj/code-general-des-impots/)_
- **New-company IS reduction** — IS on newly and regularly incorporated companies is reduced by 25% in the first year of activity, 25% in the second and 50% in the third  _(Code Général des Impôts (Bénin) 2021, art. 156 bis — https://www.impots.finances.gouv.bj/code-general-des-impots/)_
- **Rounding** — Any fraction of taxable profit below XOF 1,000 is disregarded in computing the tax  _(Code Général des Impôts (Bénin) 2021, art. 156 — https://www.impots.finances.gouv.bj/code-general-des-impots/)_
- **Tax base** — Net accounting profit (per SYSCOHADA accounts) adjusted for tax add-backs and deductions  _(Code Général des Impôts (Bénin) — détermination du bénéfice imposable)_
- **Territorial basis** — IS applies to profits realised in Benin (territoriality); foreign-branch profits generally outside scope ((approx — confirm))  _(Code Général des Impôts (Bénin) — territorialité de l'IS)_
- **WHT on dividends (standard)** — 15% (10% for regularly distributed dividends) percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (Bénin) — IRCM / retenue sur dividendes (https://www.lloydsbanktrade.com/en/market-potential/benin/taxes))_
- **WHT on dividends — WAEMU-listed company** — 7% for dividends distributed by companies listed on an approved WAEMU stock exchange percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (Bénin) — IRCM (https://orbitax.com/taxhub/withholdingtaxrates/TG/BJ/Togo/Benin))_
- **WHT on interest** — 15% ((approx — some guides cite 10% to non-residents; confirm)) percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (Bénin) — IRCM / retenue sur intérêts (https://www.lloydsbanktrade.com/en/market-potential/benin/taxes))_
- **WHT on royalties (paid abroad)** — 10% to foreign individuals; 12% to foreign companies percent  _(Code Général des Impôts (Bénin) — retenue sur redevances (https://www.lloydsbanktrade.com/en/market-potential/benin/taxes))_
- **Treaty relief** — Benin's double tax treaties (incl. WAEMU/UEMOA multilateral convention) may reduce WHT rates on dividends, interest and royalties  _(Conventions fiscales / WAEMU multilateral tax convention (https://orbitax.com/taxhub/countrychapters/BJ/Benin/f422ca9b24bb422f820ed2741b8b2b00/Withholding-Taxes-594))_
- **IS annual return deadline** — Before 1 May of each year (i.e. by 30 April), declaring the profit of the preceding year or accounting period, filed with the tax inspector for the place of the registered office or principal establishment in Benin  _(Code Général des Impôts (Bénin) 2021, art. 159(1) — https://www.impots.finances.gouv.bj/code-general-des-impots/)_
- **Advance payments (acomptes)** — IS is paid by advance installments (acomptes) during the year, with the balance settled on filing ((approx — confirm number/dates of installments))  _(Code Général des Impôts (Bénin) — acomptes IS)_

Companies in Benin pay the Impôt sur les Sociétés (IS) on net taxable profit. A minimum tax (Impôt Minimum, IM/IMF) applies where the IS computed on profit is low. Withholding taxes apply to dividends, interest, and royalties.
