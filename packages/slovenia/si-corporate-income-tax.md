# Slovenia Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax rates and base (2025)

Slovenian-resident companies pay corporate income tax on worldwide profits at a flat rate. The standard rate was temporarily raised to 22% for tax years 2024-2028.

- **Standard CIT rate (2024-2028)** — 22% for the 2024 to 2028 tax years under ZORZFS art. 64; the general rate in ZDDPO-2 art. 60 remains 19%  _(Corporate Income Tax Act (ZDDPO-2, consolidated text, PisRS), art. 60 — https://pisrs.si/pregledPredpisa?id=ZAKO4687 ; Financial Administration of the Republic of Slovenia, corporate income tax page (rate under ZORZFS art. 64) — https://www.fu.gov.si/davki_in_druge_dajatve/podrocja/davek_od_dohodkov_pravnih_oseb_ddpo/)_
- **Pre-2024 standard rate** — 19% (art. 60, the general rate the Act still states)  _(Corporate Income Tax Act (ZDDPO-2, consolidated text, PisRS), art. 60 — https://pisrs.si/pregledPredpisa?id=ZAKO4687)_
- **Reduced rate — qualifying investment funds, pension funds, insurers** — 0% for alternative investment funds under sections 3.1 and 3.2 of the Alternative Investment Funds Act that distribute at least 75% of the previous year's business profit by 30 November (art. 61(1)), for pension funds established under the pension and disability insurance law (art. 61(2)) and for insurers' pension plans filed on a separate return (art. 61(3))  _(Corporate Income Tax Act (ZDDPO-2, consolidated text, PisRS), art. 61(1) to (3) — https://pisrs.si/pregledPredpisa?id=ZAKO4687)_
- **Tax base** — Profit, being the excess of revenue over expenses as the Act defines them, taken from the income statement under the applicable accounting standards and adjusted by the Act's rules on non-deductible expenses, exempt income and tax depreciation (art. 12)  _(Corporate Income Tax Act (ZDDPO-2, consolidated text, PisRS), art. 12(1) to (3) — https://pisrs.si/pregledPredpisa?id=ZAKO4687)_
- **Minimum effective tax base limitation** — Prior-year tax losses may reduce the tax base by at most 50% of the year's tax base (art. 36(4)); ZDDPO-2 has no separate revenue-based floor, and the 1% of revenue reference this guide carried until October 2026 was not found in the Act  _(Corporate Income Tax Act (ZDDPO-2, consolidated text, PisRS), art. 36(4) — https://pisrs.si/pregledPredpisa?id=ZAKO4687)_
- **Tax loss carryforward** — Losses may be set against the tax base of the following five tax periods, oldest first, and may reduce any year's base by at most 50% (art. 36(2) to (4)); this guide said 'indefinitely' until October 2026  _(Corporate Income Tax Act (ZDDPO-2, consolidated text, PisRS), art. 36(2) to (4) — https://pisrs.si/pregledPredpisa?id=ZAKO4687)_
- **Dividend participation exemption** — Dividends and similar income are excluded from the tax base where the payer is a Slovenian taxpayer, an EU-resident company covered by the Parent-Subsidiary regime, or a company subject to a comparable profit tax that is not resident in a listed low-tax jurisdiction (art. 24(1)); 5% of the dividends received is treated as non-deductible expense (art. 26(1)), so the exemption is 95% in effect  _(Corporate Income Tax Act (ZDDPO-2, consolidated text, PisRS), arts. 24(1) and 26(1) — https://pisrs.si/pregledPredpisa?id=ZAKO4687)_
- **Pillar Two global minimum tax** — 15% effective minimum tax (IIR, UTPR, QDMTT) for in-scope groups; first reporting for FY2024  _([Minimum Tax Act (ZMD)](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/slovenia/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Withholding tax on dividends (non-residents)** — 15% (may be reduced or removed by tax treaty or the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive)  _(Corporate Income Tax Act (ZDDPO-2, consolidated text, PisRS), art. 70(1) — https://pisrs.si/pregledPredpisa?id=ZAKO4687)_
- **Withholding tax on interest (non-residents)** — 15% (may be reduced or removed by treaty or the EU Interest and Royalties Directive)  _(Corporate Income Tax Act (ZDDPO-2, consolidated text, PisRS), art. 70(1) — https://pisrs.si/pregledPredpisa?id=ZAKO4687)_
- **Withholding tax on royalties (non-residents)** — 15% (may be reduced or removed by treaty or the EU Interest and Royalties Directive)  _(Corporate Income Tax Act (ZDDPO-2, consolidated text, PisRS), art. 70(1) — https://pisrs.si/pregledPredpisa?id=ZAKO4687)_
- **Withholding tax — services to low-tax jurisdictions** — 15% on payments for services to persons in non-EU countries whose general or average nominal profit tax rate is below 12.5% and that the Minister of Finance has placed on the published list (arts. 8.a and 70(1))  _(Corporate Income Tax Act (ZDDPO-2, consolidated text, PisRS), arts. 8.a and 70(1) — https://pisrs.si/pregledPredpisa?id=ZAKO4687)_
- **CIT return filing deadline** — Within three months after the end of the tax period (31 March for calendar-year taxpayers; the same three months after a business year that differs from the calendar year)  _(Tax Procedure Act (ZDavP-2, consolidated text, PisRS), art. 358(2) and (3) — https://pisrs.si/pregledPredpisa?id=ZAKO4703)_
- **Advance CIT payments** — Advance tax equal to the prior period's tax base at the current year's rate, paid in monthly instalments where the annual advance exceeds EUR 400 and quarterly otherwise; each instalment falls due on the last day of its period and is paid within 20 days (art. 371); the balance is settled with the annual return  _(Tax Procedure Act (ZDavP-2, consolidated text, PisRS), art. 371(2) to (4) — https://pisrs.si/pregledPredpisa?id=ZAKO4703)_
