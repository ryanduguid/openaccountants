# Grenada property tax and property transfer tax

Check the annual property tax on owning the property and the separate tax on transferring it. Match the assessment and demand notice to the parcel, tax year and owner. For a transfer, establish the parties' citizenship under the transfer tax definitions, the type of property and its fair market value. [Property Tax Act, Cap 257B, ss 3–8, 17 and 40][PROPERTY] · [Property Transfer Tax Act, Cap 257C, ss 2 and 4–8][TRANSFER]

## Annual property tax

The annual charge generally falls on the owner on 1 January. Where different people own the land and any building, each pays for what they own unless one discharges both liabilities under an agreement. Unpaid annual tax can remain a charge on the property and affect a buyer. [Cap 257B, ss 7–8 and 41][PROPERTY]

The valuation officer classifies the property by use. The statutory starting point is the market value of unimproved land and, where there is a building, separate site and building values. Check the recorded use, any mixed-use issue, the taxable values and eligibility for relief. In the published Cap 257B text, a qualifying owner-occupied dwelling occupied by its owner for at least 9 months of the year gets a XCD 100,000 deduction from building value, subject to minimum tax. Agricultural exemptions have certification requirements. Confirm the applicable rules for the year before using a deduction or exemption. [Cap 257B, ss 5–6, 11 and 13–17][PROPERTY] · [SRO 30 of 2013][CLASSES]

The Minister sets rates by gazetted order, subject to affirmative resolution. Section 4 carries forward the existing rate when no new rate is determined. Act 40 of 2013 moved the rate-setting deadline for the following year to 31 December and amended the statutory minimum to XCD 40 for bare land or a separately owned building and XCD 60 for land and building owned together, subject to the total exemption rule. Confirm the governing rate order and minimum for the year on the demand. [Cap 257B, ss 3–4 and 66][PROPERTY] · [Act 40 of 2013, ss 3–4][ACT2013]

For the **2014 tax year**, SRO 31 of 2013 sets 0.5% on commercial land and 0.3% on commercial buildings. For one owner with taxable commercial site value of XCD 100,000 and taxable building value of XCD 200,000, the amounts are XCD 500 and XCD 600 respectively. The combined XCD 1,100 exceeds the XCD 60 minimum before rebates or adjustments. Verify the applicable order before calculating another year's charge. [SRO 31 of 2013, s 2 and Schedule][RATES] · [Act 40 of 2013, s 4][ACT2013]

## Notices, payment and objections

Check the demand's issue date, due date, taxable values, classification, rates and arrears. The published text of Cap 257B s 40 provides for instalments and a separate 60-day rule for a demand issued after 31 March. From 1 May 2016, the Tax Administration Act repealed the former property notice service section and provides for service, including a specific property notice rule. Confirm the payment terms for the year and the notice served on the owner. [Cap 257B, ss 38–40][PROPERTY] · [Tax Administration Act 14 of 2016, ss 15–17 and Schedule 2 para 6][ADMIN] · [SRO 34 of 2016][ADMINSTART]

For **2026**, the Inland Revenue Division (IRD) announced 31 August 2026 as the payment deadline for Grenada, Carriacou and Petite Martinique. Its August notice listed the government's online payment service and IRD or District Revenue offices. Use the notice for that year and confirm the live payment reference and available channel for the parcel. [IRD, 17 August 2026 payment notice][NOTICE]

Identify whether you are challenging a draft valuation list or seeking a change to an existing list. The published Act provides different procedures and limits the existing-list route where the objection or appeal against the draft list could have secured relief. An appeal does not generally stop collection, although the amount recoverable can differ where the earlier valuation was lower. The IRD's 2026 notice directs owners seeking reassessment, including those affected by Hurricane Beryl, to submit a Notice of Objection. Confirm the form, grounds, service date, deadline and amount payable for the particular notice. [Cap 257B, ss 21–31 and 68–74][PROPERTY] · [IRD, 17 August 2026 payment notice][NOTICE]

The Ministry of Finance's Beryl relief notice described a property tax waiver for **2025**, covering all properties in Carriacou and Petite Martinique. On mainland Grenada, owners of affected property had to request relief and have the IRD assess eligibility. Verify the legal basis and the property's decision before treating a balance as waived. The ministry's subsequent fiscal report said the waiver would be phased out from 2026. [Finance Ministry, Beryl incentives Phase II][BERYL] · [Finance Ministry, 2026–28 fiscal strategy][FISCAL]

## Property transfers

Cap 257C reaches sales, gifts and other lifetime transfers of a property interest, including qualifying long leases and shares. For ordinary land sales, the published s 5(1) specifies 5% for a citizen seller and 15% for a non-citizen seller. It separately specifies 10% for a non-citizen purchaser. For land other than a deed of gift, s 5(3) applies tax to value above XCD 20,000. The Act defines citizenship for this purpose and the 2015 amendment aggregates specified parcels or connected transactions for the thresholds. Check the special rules and exemptions before calculating either party's liability. [Cap 257C, ss 2 and 4–6][TRANSFER] · [Act 23 of 2015, s 2][TRANSFER2015]

Use market value and check whether the Comptroller requires a valuation. Agree who pays each liability and what evidence the registrar requires before recording the deed. Confirm the separate stamp duty, non-citizen landholding licence, clearance and registration requirements. [Cap 257C, ss 7–9, 14, 18 and 20][TRANSFER]

Check for tourism or investment relief before applying the ordinary transfer rates. Act 23 of 2015 replaced the tourism provisions and saved the old rules for developments already approved by Cabinet. From 1 May 2016, the qualifying investment waiver depends on the priority sector, qualifying costs and sometimes parish. The Comptroller can recover waived tax if qualifying conditions fail. Check the instrument and the transaction's approval. [Act 23 of 2015, s 3][TRANSFER2015] · [Act 9 of 2016, s 2][TRANSFER2016] · [SRO 27 of 2016][TRANSFERSTART]

Confirm the later rate orders, the 2012 property tax amendment, current minimums and concessions, the objection form and service requirements, interest, transfer exemptions and operational filing before relying on this reference for a transaction. [Grenada Parliament, 2012 Acts register][ACTS2012] · [Cap 257B, ss 39–43 and 64–66][PROPERTY] · [Tax Administration Act 14 of 2016, s 55 and Schedule 2][ADMIN]
