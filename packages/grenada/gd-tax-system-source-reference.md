# Grenada tax-system source reference

Use this reference to select the source and period for a historical Grenada tax question. Load `workflow-base`. Confirm the enacted law for the actual period before making an assessment, withholding, filing or payment decision.

## Source, residence and taxpayer type

The retained [Income Tax Act, Cap 149](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Cap149-INCOME-TAX-ACT.pdf) lists amendments through 2010. Act 41 of 2013 identifies the principal Act as Cap 149 of the 2010 Continuous Revised Edition. The table reads selected provisions of that edition. Check later amendments and operative dates for each selected rule.

| Question | Reading from the retained edition |
|---|---|
| What income enters the assessment? | Section 7 charges tax on chargeable income for the assessment year and directs readers to Part VI, subject to Part VII. Section 8 starts from Part V amounts from sources in Grenada, subject to exemptions and the non-resident branch. Check section 10 for deemed sources and the proviso to section 8 for interest accruing to a bank, bank branch or person engaged in banking in Grenada. |
| Is an individual resident? | Section 2 has separate permanent-abode, presence of at least 183 days in the assessment year and continuous adjacent-year branches. The abode branch ordinarily requires some presence in the year. For absence throughout the year, establish whether the Comptroller accepts a statutory reason. For continuity, establish that the immediately adjacent year independently qualifies under the 183-day branch. |
| What about entities and estates? | Section 2 tests where a trust or body of persons was established. For an estate, it asks whether the deceased qualified immediately before death. For a company, establish Grenada incorporation or, for an externally incorporated company, management and control in Grenada. |
| Who can be charged and who acts for them? | Sections 12–24 route charges by legal capacity and attribution, including representatives, trustees and estates. Under section 21, charge partnership income on its partners rather than in the partnership’s name. A partner adds their share of partnership assessable income or deducts their share of assessed loss. Check separate withholding obligations and the version for the relevant period. |

The retained section 7(5) routes specified non-business income of non-residents to section 50. Sections 3 and 12 of Act 27 of 2015 replace section 7(5) and amend section 51A(1). The new section 7(5) distinguishes income from a partnership and from a person, with sections 51A and 50 respectively. Section 1(4) applies those changes to payments on or after enactment. Section 11 separately replaces parts of section 50 and follows the basis-period trigger in section 1(2). The original records assent on 25 August and a printed date of 4 September 2015. Confirm the enactment date and applicable trigger before selecting a version. [Act 27 of 2015, ss 1, 3 and 11–12](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Act-No.-27-of-2015-Income-Tax-Amendment.pdf)

Earlier changes to section 50 include the bank deposit and branch provisions in Act 41 of 2013, published on 31 December 2013, and the treatment of cash winnings in Act 22 of 2014, published on 30 May 2014. The default publication rule in Cap 153 sections 6–7 applies to those Acts. Act 8 of 2016 added the qualifying-investment waiver and recovery provisions in section 50(4A)–(4C), with commencement on 1 May 2016. Check each version and relevant exception for the applicable period. [Act 41 of 2013](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Act-No.-41-of-2013-Income-Tax-Amendment.pdf) · [Act 22 of 2014](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Act-No.-22-of-2014-Income-Tax-Amendment-Act.pdf) · [Act 8 of 2016](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Act-No.-8-of-2016-Income-Tax-Amendment.pdf) · [SRO 26 of 2016](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/S.R.O.-26-of-2016-Income-Tax-Amendment-Act-Commencment-Order.pdf)

## Assessment periods and currency

Section 2 of [Cap 149](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Cap149-INCOME-TAX-ACT.pdf) defines the assessment year as a calendar year. Sections 9 and 11 address accrual and the basis period. The ordinary period ends on 31 December of that assessment year. Section 11 gives separate routes for qualifying alternative business accounts, a later change with written Comptroller approval and cessation. Establish the actual accounting dates and the applicable period before allocating income or credits.

Grenada's [2025–2027 debt strategy, printed p. ii](https://cdn.eccb-centralbank.org/documents/2025-04-08-12-52-30-Grenada---Medium-Term-Debt-Management-Strategy-2025-2027.pdf) identifies the Eastern Caribbean dollar (XCD) as its domestic currency. Section 3(1) of the retained [Interpretation and General Provisions Act, Cap 153](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Cap153-INTERPRETATION-AND-GENERAL-PROVISIONS-ACT.pdf) qualifies its currency definitions by legal tender status. Confirm any period-specific legal tender, conversion or tax payment rule separately. The strategy gives currency context for its stated period.

## Responsible authorities

The retained [Cap 149, ss 3–4](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Cap149-INCOME-TAX-ACT.pdf) assigns the Act's administration to the Comptroller of Inland Revenue and defines the scope of written delegation. [Tax Administration Act 14 of 2016, ss 4–6 and Schedules I–II](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Act-No.-14-of-2016-Tax-Administration-Act.pdf) establishes the Inland Revenue Division and Comptroller framework for listed tax laws and other enactments that assign general administration to the Comptroller. It preserves the Customs collection and recovery carve-out and gives precedence to another tax law where it is inconsistent. Schedule I includes Cap 149 and has separate Customs exceptions for VAT and excise. Schedule II repeals Cap 149 sections 3–6 from 1 May 2016 under [SRO 34 of 2016](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/S.R.O.-34-of-2016-Tax-Administration-Act-Commencement-Order-2016.pdf). Use the authority in force for the relevant duty.

Customs Act 9 of 2015 section 4 gives the Comptroller of Customs responsibility for administering the Act and for duties assigned under other enactments. SRO 19 of 2015 fixes commencement at 1 July 2015. Check the charge and collection provision for the transaction concerned. [Customs Act 9 of 2015, s 4](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Act-No.-9-of-2015-Customs-Act.pdf) · [SRO 19 of 2015](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/S.R.O.-19-of-2015-Customs-Act-Commencment-Notice.pdf)

In the retained Cap 205 text, section 4 vests the Fund in the National Insurance Board. Section 9 sets the Director’s responsibilities, including contribution assessment and collection and payment of benefits, under the Board’s general direction. Confirm the operative version, regulations and process before assigning a contribution or benefit. [National Insurance Act, Cap 205, ss 4 and 9](https://grenadaparliament.gd/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/Cap205-NATIONAL-INSURANCE-ACT.pdf)

## Evidence checks

| Fictional facts | Source routing |
|---|---|
| An individual spends 175 days in Grenada this year and remains there continuously for the first 183 days of the next year. | The adjacent-year branch requires continuous presence and independent qualification in the adjacent year. Confirm the years, dates and applicable version before recording residence. |
| A company incorporated outside Grenada has a local sales office. | Establish where it is managed and controlled. A local office alone does not decide the selected residence test. Keep the business-source and chargeability review separate. |
| A partner’s worksheet charges the partnership in its own name under the retained text. | Section 21 assigns the partnership income or loss to partners. Check the shares, version and any separate withholding duty before assessing a partner. |
| A legacy worksheet cites Cap 149 section 3 for an administrative action from 1 May 2016. | Check the Tax Administration Act transition and the tax-specific provision. The old section was repealed by the transition. |

Keep the source instrument and version, operative date, actual location, residence, payer and payee capacities, basis period and responsible agency in the evidence record. Hold any result that depends on missing current law, applicable payment facts, fees, forms, rates, relief, legal tender or professional review.
