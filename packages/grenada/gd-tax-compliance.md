# Grenada tax administration and compliance

Use this guide for ordinary domestic Inland Revenue Division (IRD) administration. Identify the tax and period before applying the procedure. Section 4 gives the relevant tax law priority to the extent of an inconsistency and excludes collection and recovery assigned to the Comptroller of Customs. The 2016 Act commenced on 1 May 2016. Read it with effective amendments and the notice for the case. [Tax Administration Act 14 of 2016, ss 1, 2 and 4 and Schedule 1][ADMIN] · [SRO 34 of 2016][START]

## Registration, filing and records

- **Register and identify the representative.** Section 12 requires persons liable or potentially liable for income tax and annual stamp tax to register within 30 days after commencing business. Use the assigned tax identification number on returns and correspondence with the Comptroller. A company carrying on business in Grenada must have a principal officer residing there or, if there is none, an authorised agent residing there. Notify the Comptroller of the designated representative within one month of starting business and within one month after that person ceases to act. The representative must keep records, file returns and other documents and pay the relevant taxes under s 45(6). From 31 December 2020, a body corporate issued a registration certificate under s 12(5) must display the original at its principal place of activity and a certified copy at each other place from which it operates. Check tax-specific registration, including [VAT](gd-value-added-tax.md), and obtain current filing access. [2016 Act, ss 11–12 and 45(4), (6)][ADMIN] · [Act 10 of 2020, s 2][AMEND2020] · [SRO 75 of 2020][START2020]
- **Set filing and payment clocks separately.** Section 22 requires a return in the prescribed form, with the specified information and attachments and the appropriate signatures. Section 19 recognises the next day the Division is open if the last day falls on a day it is closed to the public. For documents within s 19(2), record the Division's receipt stamp or the postmark when filing by mail. Check the prescribed channel and the evidence of timely electronic submission for the tax and period. [2016 Act, ss 16, 19, 22, 23 and 44][ADMIN]
- **Use the applicable GTAX instructions.** The Ministry announced exclusive GTAX submission or processing from 1 January 2026 for listed matters including personal income tax, corporate income tax filing, annual PAYE, withholding tax and annual stamp tax. The IRD's 31 March 2026 notice separately extended filing and payment for personal income tax, corporate income tax and annual stamp tax to 30 April for that round. Record the submitted return, payment allocation and acknowledgement. Check newer directions and any separate channel for prior periods or other taxes. [Ministry of Finance notice, 24 November 2025, republished by NOW Grenada][GTAX2026] · [IRD notice, 31 March 2026][EXTENSION2026]
- **Keep the source records.** Section 20 requires up-to-date records in Grenada and generally at the place of business, with written approval for another place under s 20(3). It separately addresses the location of electronic storage devices, underlying documents, VAT records and English-language requirements. Retain required transaction records for 7 years after the transaction or until the applicable assessment time limit expires if later. Confirm the electronic storage arrangement and applicable assessment window before disposing of records. [2016 Act, ss 20 and 31][ADMIN]

## Assessments and objections

Record the tax period, the notice issue date, the service method and actual or deemed receipt date, the reasons and calculation and the demand and objection instructions. Keep the Comptroller's decision date separately. Section 15 distinguishes service on individuals, companies and partnerships and deems registered mail served after 7 days for addresses in Grenada and 30 days for addresses outside it. Check the actual service route and applicable Electronic Transactions Act rules before setting the clock. [2016 Act, ss 2(3), 15 and 33][ADMIN]

The Comptroller can make a best-judgement assessment when a required return is missing. A self-assessing return can have the effect of an assessment and remains subject to revision. The original s 31 sets a general 6-year limit from the end of the tax period with separate rules for fraud or wilful neglect, missing or late returns and changes giving effect to an appeal or court decision. Section 22(3) provides an amended-return route. Check s 22(7) if a revised assessment is outstanding and s 42 if the assessment is final. [2016 Act, ss 22, 29–33 and 42][ADMIN]

1. **Request administrative review.** For an assessment or other reviewable decision, send the Comptroller a written request with detailed grounds within 30 days after receipt of the notice. Where the assessment was made without the required return, lodge the objection with a completed return. Keep evidence of service, the request and its receipt. The taxpayer bears the burden of showing an assessment is incorrect. [2016 Act, ss 35–37 and 40][ADMIN]
2. **Check the Appeal Commissioners gateway.** Section 38 requires a prior s 37 review request and either receipt of the Comptroller's decision or 90 days since the request. For a decision under s 37, give written notice of appeal to the Comptroller within 90 days from the date of the decision under s 38(5). Section 38(6) gives the Commissioners a limited late-appeal route where reasonable cause prevented timely notice and there was no unreasonable delay. If there is no decision, confirm the deadline and service procedure for that route. Section 43 addresses High Court appeals on a point of law. Obtain the operative text and procedural advice before relying on a High Court gateway or deadline. [2016 Act, ss 38–39 and 43][ADMIN]
3. **Check what remains payable.** Section 41(1) provides that undisputed tax remains due during administrative review or appeal unless the applicable extension is granted. The printed text refers to s 51, whilst the payment extension provision is s 50. Verify the cross-reference and collection position for disputed tax. Keep the tax and appeal ledgers current whilst seeking any payment relief. [2016 Act, ss 41, 44 and 50–51][ADMIN]

## Payment relief, refunds and late charges

- **Apply for the right extension.** Apply for a filing extension by the filing due date under s 26. Payment stays due unless expressly extended. For an ordinary payment extension, s 50 requires an application in the prescribed form before the tax due date. Section 50(1) refers to s 47, which concerns winding-up. Confirm how that cross-reference applies in the case. Section 50(3) permits extensions pending appeal and arrangements including instalments. A valid application is deemed granted if no written decision is given within 30 days. Confirm validity and the effective terms before relying on that provision. Interest continues under Part VII. Default on an approved instalment can bring the full balance due immediately. [2016 Act, ss 26, 44, 47 and 50][ADMIN]
- **Reconcile payments and overpayments.** Section 52 applies payment of a specific tax to interest, late fees, penalties and then principal. Identify the tax and period and check how IRD posted the payment. Under the non-VAT route in s 49, the Comptroller first offsets assessed liabilities. Remaining amounts may be applied to advance tax due within 6 months with consent, or refunded. A taxpayer must apply within 6 years of payment. The same period applies where the Comptroller initiates the refund or credit. Verify the current form, payment date and offsets. [2016 Act, ss 49 and 52][ADMIN]
- **Separate interest, civil late fees and offence notices.** Sections 53–55 address interest, including the ordinary due date before a payment extension and the original due date for revised assessments. The Minister can vary the interest rate by Gazette order. Sections 56–57 distinguish late filing, late payment and missed income tax instalments, including extension effects. Calculate only after checking the operative rules and any waiver for the period. The fixed penalty process in Part IX Division II was replaced from 31 December 2020 and serves a separate offence procedure. [2016 Act, ss 53–58][ADMIN] · [Act 10 of 2020, ss 3–4][AMEND2020] · [SRO 75 of 2020][START2020]
- **Check the terms of any waiver.** Act 4 of 2023 was published on 19 May 2023 and deemed in force on 31 December 2022. It inserted s 58A, which lets the Minister waive some or all Part VII interest, late fees and other penalties on outstanding tax due and payable where there is good cause. Check the Gazette order for the period, assessment year and persons it covers. The order is subject to negative resolution. If the underlying tax remains outstanding after the prescribed period, the charges become payable as if the waiver had never applied. [Tax Administration Amendment Act 4 of 2023, ss 1–2, official Acts listing][AMEND2023] · [Government Gazette No. 25 of 2023, Notice 20][GAZETTE2023]
- **Date any special relief.** The 2023 waiver order under s 58A waived 100% of qualifying Part VII interest, late fees and penalties from 1 January to 31 December 2023 on outstanding tax due for the assessment year ending 31 December 2021 or an earlier year. For example, if qualifying 2021 tax remained outstanding after 31 December 2023, the charges that would have been payable on that outstanding tax became due under s 58A(2). Act 6 of 2025 was deemed in force on 1 July 2024 and defines a separate relevant period of 1 June 2024 to 31 January 2025. Its VAT, corporate advances, annual stamp tax and PAYE measures have distinct conditions and savings. Check the provision for the tax, location and period and preserve the pre-period debts it leaves payable. [SRO 20 of 2023, regs 1 and 3][WAIVER] · [Act 4 of 2023, s 2 inserting s 58A(2)][AMEND2023] · [Act 6 of 2025, ss 1–8][BERYL]

## Worked example

A domestic company receives an ordinary income tax assessment in person on 7 July 2026 and plans to dispute one adjustment. Under the published s 37 clock and the general reckoning rule, count from 8 July. Day 30 is 6 August 2026. This assumes no special rule or Division closure. Prepare the detailed written grounds and confirm the filing channel and receipt date. If the assessment was made without the required return, include it. Check the undisputed balance and any express payment extension separately. [2016 Act, ss 19, 37, 41 and 50][ADMIN] · [Interpretation and General Provisions Act, Cap 153, s 51][TIME]
