# Grenada company accounts and annual reporting

For an ordinary domestic private company limited by shares, schedule the annual return, shareholder reporting, Registrar copies or solvency certificates and beneficial ownership filings. Check the incorporation or continuance date, financial year, governing documents, most recent accounts including affiliates, ownership changes and any exemptions. The rules below trace the published legislation and selected amendments. Confirm the law in force and the applicable registry procedure before preparing a filing. [Companies Act Cap 58A, ss 107, 149–155 and 194][ACT] · [Corporate Affairs and Intellectual Property Office Act Cap 69A, ss 3–4][REGISTRAR] · [Act 5 of 2022, ss 4 and 7][BO2022]

## Annual return to the Registrar

Section 194 requires the return by 1 April each year after incorporation or continuance. Make it up to the preceding 31 December, have a director or officer certify it and send it with the prescribed fee. The 2014 regulations require business activities on the annual return to use a classification consistent with ISIC. [Cap 58A, s 194][ACT] · [SRO 36 of 2014, reg 3][REG2014]

The 2010 revised regulations show Form 28 for a company for profit and a filing fee of XCD 25 under reg 28(1)(p). Use these as historical references. The 2014 amendment repealed SRO 36 of 2011, which had prescribed Form 27A. SRO 15 of 2023 amended the incorporation fee in reg 28(1)(a). Confirm the accepted annual return form, fee and delivery method with the Corporate Affairs and Intellectual Property Office. [Companies Regulations reg 28 and Third Schedule][ACT] · [SRO 36 of 2011, reg 6][REG2011] · [SRO 36 of 2014, regs 4–5][REG2014] · [SRO 15 of 2023, reg 2][REG2023] · [CAIPO][CAIPO]

## Annual accounts, solvency certificates and audit

1. Call the first annual shareholders' meeting within 18 months of incorporation and each later meeting within 15 months of the previous one. Prepare the comparative statements for the periods specified in s 149, the auditor's report if applicable and any additional information required by the company's governing documents. Check any Registrar adjustment or permission to omit information. Obtain directors' approval and a director's signature. Subject to the exceptions in s 153, send the documents to shareholders at least 21 days before the meeting or signature of a resolution in lieu. [Cap 58A, ss 107, 130 and 149–153][ACT]
2. Apply the Registrar-copy test in s 154. The published thresholds cover a private company whose most recent statements show gross revenue above XCD 2 million or assets above XCD 1 million, including affiliates, subject to any higher prescribed amounts. Send the s 149 documents at least 21 days before the annual meeting or immediately after the resolution in lieu. The outer limit is 15 months after the last date the previous annual meeting or resolution was due. Check the Registrar's exemption process and whether the holding company has filed qualifying consolidated or combined accounts that include the subsidiary. [Cap 58A, s 154 and Companies Regulations Part VI][ACT]
3. If s 154(1) does not require the documents, check whether s 155 requires 2 solvency certificates within the same period. A director signs both on behalf of the board, along with the auditor if appointed. The certificates address the prescribed asset and liability amounts, solvency and consistency with the financial statements. A subsidiary qualifying under s 154(5) is exempt under s 155(6). [Cap 58A, s 155][ACT]
4. Appoint an eligible auditor by ordinary resolution at the first and each subsequent annual meeting. A company outside s 154(1) may dispense with the auditor until the next annual meeting if every shareholder consents, including those without voting rights. Check the auditor's eligibility and independence under ss 158–161 where an appointment is required. [Cap 58A, ss 158–163][ACT]

The published reg 10 lists a balance sheet, retained earnings statement, income statement and statement of changes in financial position as minimum components and permits other titles. Check the applicable accounting and auditing standards before preparing the statements or engaging the auditor. The published reg 9 cites standards approved by an institute but the institute's full name is incomplete in the copy. The Institute of Chartered Accountants of the Eastern Caribbean Agreement Act sets out the Institute's rule-making authority. Verify the operative standard and edition for the entity and period. [Companies Regulations regs 9–10][ACT] · [Cap 150B, s 3 and Schedule art 5][ICAEC]

Keep the company's governing documents, statutory notices, registers, minutes and adequate accounting records. If accounts are kept overseas, retain records at the registered office or another place designated in Grenada that let directors determine the financial position with reasonable accuracy each quarter. Keep the tax record retention and company income tax return on their own timetable. [Cap 58A, ss 177–178 and 187–189][ACT] · [Grenada corporate income tax](gd-corporate-income-tax.md)

## Beneficial ownership

Act 5 of 2022, published on 14 April 2022, inserted Part VIA. Check any s 136B exemption. Take reasonable steps to identify beneficial owners and relevant legal entities. The amended s 543 definition includes individuals with direct or indirect ownership or control of at least 10% of shares or votes in a non-listed legal person. Also check control of management and relevant legal arrangements. Keep the register at the registered office or another suitable place in Grenada after giving the Registrar written notice. Follow the notice and confirmation rules in ss 136D–136G. [Act 5 of 2022, ss 4 and 7][BO2022]

For register changes affecting an entry, s 136F generally requires an update as soon as practicable and within 14 days after notification. Section 136F(8) requires confirmation before you update the register. Give the minimum required ownership information to the Grenada Authority for the Regulation of Financial Institutions at incorporation or continuance. Section 136I requires reporting changes promptly and within 14 days once the company is aware or notified and has confirmed the required information. Record the dates of notification and confirmation and verify the applicable deadline, form and submission channel. [Act 5 of 2022, ss 4 and 7][BO2022]

## Worked reporting checks

A company incorporated on 10 August 2026 has its first s 194 annual return due on 1 April 2027 under the published timetable, using information as at 31 December 2026. Confirm the filing calendar for that return. If its most recent statements show XCD 1.8 million of gross revenue and its affiliate adds XCD 300,000, the combined XCD 2.1 million exceeds the printed s 154 gross revenue threshold of XCD 2 million. Check any higher prescribed threshold or applicable exemption before choosing the financial statement filing route. [Cap 58A, ss 154 and 194][ACT]

## Evidence to confirm before filing

Check the complete amendment chain, including SRO 23 of 2022, and obtain the current annual return, solvency and beneficial ownership forms, fees, delivery channels and receipt procedures. Check prescribed reporting thresholds, accounting standards, auditor eligibility, retention periods and entity-specific exemptions. Use the [Grenada company formation guide](gd-company-formation.md) for incorporation. Public, external, non-profit and regulated companies require a separate analysis.
