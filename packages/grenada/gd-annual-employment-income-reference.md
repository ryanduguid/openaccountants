## Intake and selected annual mechanics

Reuse redacted records before grouped questions. Establish the requested year and obligation, complete applicable source chain, filing unit, residence and Grenada employment/source facts, all earned remuneration, employer count, exclusions, deductions and losses, contribution treatment, credits, withholding and prepayments, reviewer and tools. Present the inferred profile and wait for confirmation. Keep identifiers, bank details and signatures with the professional. Missing law requires primary evidence rather than a taxpayer's guess.

1. **Period and source.** L1 section 2 defines the year of assessment as the calendar year; section 11(1) uses the 12 months ending 31 December. Employment income accrues when earned under section 9(1)(a). Section 8 addresses Grenada sources for residents; do not import a blanket worldwide-income rule. Full residence/source classification and special timing remain separate gates.
2. **Remuneration.** L1 section 31 includes wages, salary, leave pay, fees, commissions, bonuses, gratuities, allowances and benefits subject to the Act's qualifications. This reference uses only independently established ordinary cash remuneration A, non-negative and complete for the selected year. Gross salary alone, net pay or omitted bonuses do not establish A. Special exemptions and noncash valuation are held.
3. **One relief.** L1 sections 25(1)(a)(iii) and 42A coordinate the resident-employment exemption with the statutory allowance. L2 section 3 changes all 3 section 42A references to 36,000. For this salary-only fictional branch, exempt slice E = min(A, 36000) and chargeable slice C = max(A - 36000, 0). Do not exempt 36,000 and then deduct another 36,000 from the same remuneration. No unused relief becomes a refund, carryforward or transfer under this illustration.
4. **Annual bands.** L1 section 56(1) charges annual chargeable income at the Fifth Schedule rates. L2-L4 give the selected structure effective from 1 January 2019: 10% on the first 24,000 of C and 28% on the excess. The upper transition is A = 60,000 only under this branch's single 36,000 relief and no other adjustments. C = 24,000 is the band boundary, not a second gross-income exemption.
5. **Precision.** Preserve A, E, C, each band slice and each exact tax component separately. No lawful cash, cent or whole-dollar rounding method is established here. The examples deliberately retain sub-cent values and make no claim about a payable or returnable amount. Reject malformed, missing, negative, non-finite or wrong-currency inputs; do not silently coerce them.

```text
E = min(A, 36000)
C = max(A - 36000, 0)
lower slice = min(C, 24000)
upper slice = max(C - 24000, 0)
exact annual band amount = 0.10 × lower slice + 0.28 × upper slice
```

An independently established C may be used to examine the 2 annual bands, but it does not prove that the A-to-C relief or any earlier ladder is correct. Do not substitute A directly for C. For A = 72,000, E is 36,000, C is 36,000, the slices are 24,000 and 12,000 and their exact amounts are 2,400 and 3,360, totalling 5,760. This is not a balance due.
