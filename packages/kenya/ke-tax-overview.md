# Kenya Tax Overview

## Kenya tax system at a glance

- **Tax system administration** — Kenya operates a self-assessment tax system administered by the Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA) through the iTax portal. The headline taxes are individual income tax (PAYE), corporation tax, and value added tax (VAT).
- **Standard tax year (year of income) for individuals** — 1 January to 31 December (calendar year)  _(Income Tax Act (Cap 470) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kenya/individual/tax-administration)_
- **National currency** — Kenyan Shilling (KES / Ksh)  _(Central Bank of Kenya Act)_
- **National tax authority** — Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA), filing via the iTax portal  _(Kenya Revenue Authority Act — https://www.kra.go.ke/)_
- **Basis of taxation** — Source-based: income accrued in or derived from Kenya is taxable; residents are also taxed on certain foreign employment income  _(Income Tax Act (Cap 470) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kenya/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **Top marginal personal income tax (PAYE) rate** — 35 percent  _(Income Tax Act (Cap 470), Third Schedule (as amended by Finance Act 2023) — https://www.kra.go.ke/individual/filing-paying/types-of-taxes/paye)_
- **Resident company corporation tax rate** — 30 percent  _(Income Tax Act (Cap 470), Third Schedule — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kenya/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Does Kenya levy VAT?** — Yes — VAT at a standard rate of 16 percent  _(VAT Act, 2013 — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kenya/corporate/other-taxes)_
- **Monthly PAYE remittance deadline** — By the 9th day of the following month  _(Income Tax Act (Cap 470); Tax Procedures Act, 2015 — https://www.kra.go.ke/individual/filing-paying/types-of-taxes/paye)_
- **Annual individual income tax return deadline** — 30 June following the end of the year of income  _(Tax Procedures Act, 2015 — https://www.kra.go.ke/file-my-returns)_
- **Annual corporation tax return deadline** — Within 6 months of the company's financial year end (e.g. 30 June for a 31 December year end)  _(Tax Procedures Act, 2015 — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/kenya/corporate/tax-administration)_
- **Monthly VAT return and payment deadline** — By the 20th day of the following month  _(VAT Act, 2013 — https://www.kra.go.ke/)_
