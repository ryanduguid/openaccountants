# Kenya Tax Overview

## Kenya tax system at a glance

- **Tax system administration** — Kenya operates a self-assessment tax system administered by the Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA) through the iTax portal. The headline taxes are individual income tax (PAYE), corporation tax, and value added tax (VAT).
- **Standard tax year (year of income) for individuals** — 1 January to 31 December (calendar year)  _(Income Tax Act (Cap. 470), as at 1 July 2026 on Kenya Law, s. 2, definition of 'year of income' — https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/1973/16/eng@2026-07-01)_
- **National currency** — Kenyan Shilling (KES / Ksh)  _(Central Bank of Kenya Act)_
- **National tax authority** — Kenya Revenue Authority (KRA), filing via the iTax portal  _(Kenya Revenue Authority Act — https://www.kra.go.ke/)_
- **Basis of taxation** — Source-based: income accrued in or derived from Kenya is taxable; residents are also taxed on certain foreign employment income  _(Income Tax Act (Cap. 470), as at 1 July 2026 on Kenya Law, s. 3(1) — https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/1973/16/eng@2026-07-01)_
- **Top marginal personal income tax (PAYE) rate** — 35 percent on annual income over KES 9,600,000  _(Income Tax Act (Cap. 470), as at 1 July 2026 on Kenya Law, Third Schedule Head B para 1 — https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/1973/16/eng@2026-07-01 ; https://www.kra.go.ke/individual/filing-paying/types-of-taxes/paye)_
- **Resident company corporation tax rate** — 30 percent  _(Income Tax Act (Cap. 470), as at 1 July 2026 on Kenya Law, Third Schedule Head B para 2(a)(ix) — https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/1973/16/eng@2026-07-01)_
- **Does Kenya levy VAT?** — Yes — VAT at a standard rate of 16 percent (s. 5(2)(b)), with zero-rated supplies in the Second Schedule and exempt supplies in the First Schedule  _(Value Added Tax Act, 2013 (Cap. 476), as at 1 July 2026 on Kenya Law, s. 5(2)(b) — https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/2013/35/eng@2026-07-01)_
- **Monthly PAYE remittance deadline** — By the 9th day of the following month  _(Income Tax Act (Cap 470); Tax Procedures Act, 2015 — https://www.kra.go.ke/individual/filing-paying/types-of-taxes/paye)_
- **Annual individual income tax return deadline** — 30 June following the end of the year of income (the last day of the sixth month)  _(Income Tax Act (Cap. 470), as at 1 July 2026 on Kenya Law, s. 52B(1)(a) — https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/1973/16/eng@2026-07-01 ; https://www.kra.go.ke/file-my-returns)_
- **Annual corporation tax return deadline** — By the last day of the sixth month after the company's financial year end (30 June for a 31 December year end)  _(Income Tax Act (Cap. 470), as at 1 July 2026 on Kenya Law, s. 52B(1)(b) — https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/1973/16/eng@2026-07-01)_
- **Monthly VAT return and payment deadline** — By the 20th day after the end of the tax period (s. 44(1))  _(Value Added Tax Act, 2013 (Cap. 476), as at 1 July 2026 on Kenya Law, s. 44(1) — https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/2013/35/eng@2026-07-01 ; https://www.kra.go.ke/)_
