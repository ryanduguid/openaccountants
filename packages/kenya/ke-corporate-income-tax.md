# Kenya Corporate Income Tax

## Corporation tax rates, base and deadlines

- **General taxation approach** — Resident companies are taxed at a flat 30% on worldwide income attributable to Kenya, and branches of non-resident companies have paid the same 30% since the year of income 2024, plus 15% on repatriated income under section 7B. Tax is paid in quarterly instalments with a balancing payment after year end.
- **Resident company corporation tax rate** — 30 percent (6.00 shillings in each 20 shillings from the year of income 2021)  _(Income Tax Act (Cap. 470), as at 1 July 2026 on Kenya Law, Third Schedule Head B para 2(a)(ix) — https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/1973/16/eng@2026-07-01)_
- **Branch of a non-resident company (permanent establishment) rate** — 30 percent from the year of income 2024 (6.00 in each 20 shillings); the 37.5 percent rate ran from 2000 to 2023. A permanent establishment also pays 15 percent on repatriated income under section 7B  _(Income Tax Act (Cap. 470), as at 1 July 2026 on Kenya Law, Third Schedule Head B para 2(b)(viii) and para 3(s); s. 7B — https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/1973/16/eng@2026-07-01)_
- **Reduced rate — start-ups certified by the Nairobi International Financial Centre Authority** — 15 percent for the first three years and 20 percent for the succeeding four years (para 2(nb)); other NIFCA-certified companies that invest at least KES 3 billion in Kenya in their first three years pay 15 percent for ten years and 20 percent for the next ten, subject to the Kenyan senior-management conditions in para 2(na)  _(Income Tax Act (Cap. 470), as at 1 July 2026 on Kenya Law, Third Schedule Head B para 2(na) and 2(nb) — https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/1973/16/eng@2026-07-01)_
- **Tax base** — Net taxable business profit (accounting profit adjusted for disallowed expenses, capital allowances and exempt income); income accrued in or derived from Kenya  _(Income Tax Act (Cap 470))_
- **Capital Gains Tax (CGT) on transfer of property/securities** — 15% on the net gain, a final tax; 5% where the Nairobi International Financial Centre Authority certifies an investment of at least KES 3 billion held for five years  _(Income Tax Act (Cap. 470), as at 1 July 2026 on Kenya Law, Third Schedule Head B para 14; Eighth Schedule — https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/1973/16/eng@2026-07-01)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends to residents** — 5% on a qualifying dividend, a final tax (para 5(e)); 15% on other resident dividends (para 5(a)); a dividend to a resident company that controls 12.5% or more of the payer's voting power is not income (s. 7(2))  _(Income Tax Act (Cap. 470), as at 1 July 2026 on Kenya Law, Third Schedule Head B para 5(a) and 5(e); s. 7(2) — https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/1973/16/eng@2026-07-01)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends to non-residents** — 15%, whatever the size of the holding (para 3(d)). This line read 10% until September 2026. The 5% rate for East African Community citizens that stood in para 3(d) in the 27 December 2024 text is absent from the 1 July 2026 text  _(Income Tax Act (Cap. 470), as at 1 July 2026 on Kenya Law, Third Schedule Head B para 3(d) — https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/1973/16/eng@2026-07-01)_
- **Withholding tax on interest** — 15% is the general rate for residents and non-residents alike (para 5(b)(ii) and 3(e)(i)), and the instrument moves it: bearer instruments 25% (para 5(b)(i) and 3(e)(ii)), bearer bonds with a maturity of ten years or more 10% for residents (para 5(b)(iii)), bearer bonds issued outside Kenya 7.5% for non-residents (para 3(e)(ia)). Qualifying interest paid to resident individuals bears a final 15%, or 10% on housing bonds (para 5(h)). Identify the instrument before applying the general rate  _(Income Tax Act (Cap. 470), as at 1 July 2026 on Kenya Law, Third Schedule Head B para 3(e), 5(b) and 5(h) — https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/1973/16/eng@2026-07-01)_
- **Withholding tax on royalties — residents** — 5 percent  _(Income Tax Act (Cap. 470), as at 1 July 2026 on Kenya Law, Third Schedule Head B para 5(g) — https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/1973/16/eng@2026-07-01)_
- **Withholding tax on royalties — non-residents** — 20 percent (5 percent where paid by a Special Economic Zone enterprise, developer or operator)  _(Income Tax Act (Cap. 470), as at 1 July 2026 on Kenya Law, Third Schedule Head B para 3(b) — https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/1973/16/eng@2026-07-01)_
- **Withholding tax on management and professional fees** — **5% to residents** where the fees total KES 24,000 or more in a month (para 5(f)(i)); **20% to non-residents** (para 3(a)), or 15% on consultancy fees paid to East African Community citizens (para 3(a)(ii)). This head was absent from the guide until September 2026 and is the one a service business meets first  _(Income Tax Act (Cap. 470), as at 1 July 2026 on Kenya Law, Third Schedule Head B para 3(a) and 5(f)(i) — https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/1973/16/eng@2026-07-01)_
- **Withholding tax on training fees** — **5% to residents** above the KES 24,000 monthly threshold (para 5(f)(i)); **20% to non-residents** (para 3(a))  _(Income Tax Act (Cap. 470), as at 1 July 2026 on Kenya Law, Third Schedule Head B para 3(a) and 5(f)(i) — https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/1973/16/eng@2026-07-01)_
- **Withholding tax on contractual fees** — **3% to residents** where the fees total KES 24,000 or more in a month (para 5(f)(ii)); **20% to non-residents** (para 3(a)). The resident rate is the lowest in the table  _(Income Tax Act (Cap. 470), as at 1 July 2026 on Kenya Law, Third Schedule Head B para 3(a) and 5(f)(ii) — https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/1973/16/eng@2026-07-01)_
- **Withholding tax on rent paid to non-residents** — **30% on immovable property** and **15% on other property** (para 3(c)). The 30% is the highest rate in Kenya's withholding table and a non-resident landlord is not a 15% case. Residents pay 7.5% on immovable property rent (para 5(ja))  _(Income Tax Act (Cap. 470), as at 1 July 2026 on Kenya Law, Third Schedule Head B para 3(c) and 5(ja) — https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/1973/16/eng@2026-07-01)_
- **Withholding tax on insurance and reinsurance premiums** — **5% on premiums paid to non-residents** (para 3(p)). The resident 5% head is on commissions paid by an insurance company for providing insurance cover, not on premiums (para 5(c)); this guide read '5% to residents' until October 2026  _(Income Tax Act (Cap. 470), as at 1 July 2026 on Kenya Law, Third Schedule Head B para 3(p) and 5(c) — https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/1973/16/eng@2026-07-01)_
- **Other withholding heads to check before concluding none applies** — Kenya also withholds on **winnings (20%)** (para 3(y) and 5(r)), withdrawals by punters (5%, para 3(i) and 5(i)), **digital content monetisation (5% residents, 20% non-residents)** (para 5(m) and 3(t)), digital marketplace income (5% residents, 20% non-residents, para 5(o) and 3(v)), sales promotion, marketing and advertising (5% residents, 20% non-residents, para 5(l) and 3(q)), supplies of goods to a public entity (0.5% residents, 5% non-residents, para 5(n) and 3(u)) and **scrap metal sales (1.5%)** (para 5(q) and 3(x)). A payment under one of these heads is not outside the system  _(Income Tax Act (Cap. 470), as at 1 July 2026 on Kenya Law, Third Schedule Head B para 3 and 5 — https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/1973/16/eng@2026-07-01)_
- **Withholding tax remittance deadline** — Within five working days after the deduction is made, with a return to the Commissioner and a certificate to the payee (s. 35(5)). This guide read 'by the 20th day of the following month' until October 2026  _(Income Tax Act (Cap. 470), as at 1 July 2026 on Kenya Law, s. 35(5) — https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/1973/16/eng@2026-07-01)_
- **Instalment (advance) tax** — Four instalments of 25% each, due by the 20th day of the 4th, 6th, 9th and 12th months of the accounting period (Twelfth Schedule, accounting periods from 1 January 1996); each year's amount is the lesser of the current-year estimate and 110% of the preceding year's assessment (s. 12(2))  _(Income Tax Act (Cap. 470), as at 1 July 2026 on Kenya Law, s. 12 and Twelfth Schedule — https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/1973/16/eng@2026-07-01)_
- **Corporation tax return filing deadline** — By the last day of the sixth month after the end of the accounting period (s. 52B(1)(b))  _(Income Tax Act (Cap. 470), as at 1 July 2026 on Kenya Law, s. 52B(1)(b) — https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/1973/16/eng@2026-07-01)_
- **Balance of tax payment deadline** — By the last day of the fourth month after the end of the accounting period (s. 92(1))  _(Income Tax Act (Cap. 470), as at 1 July 2026 on Kenya Law, s. 92(1) — https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/1973/16/eng@2026-07-01)_
