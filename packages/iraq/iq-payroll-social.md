# Iraq Payroll & Social Contributions

## Social security and payroll

Iraqi private-sector social security is governed by the pension and social security framework, with combined employer and employee contributions on salaries. Employers also withhold income tax from payroll and remit it to the General Commission for Taxes.

- **Employer social security contribution** — 12% of total wages and allowances (8% retirement, 1% work injuries and occupational disease, 1% unemployment, 2% health insurance, social services and maternity); the State adds 8%, which the employer bears instead for a foreign worker  _(Workers' Retirement and Social Security Law No. 18 of 2023 (Official Gazette 4734 of 28 August 2023, in force 90 days after publication; it repeals Law No. 39 of 1971), Ministry of Justice English translation, art. 14 Second (b)(1) and (c) — https://moj.gov.iq/upload/pdf/%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%B9%D8%AF%20%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B6%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%86%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D8%AC%D8%AA%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%B9%D9%8A%20-%20Copy_102.pdf)_
- **Employer social security — oil and hydrocarbon employers** — 25% of wages for private and mixed-sector employers whose profits come from the sale of oil and other hydrocarbons produced in and exported from Iraq, or of related rights and interests (15% retirement, 3% work injuries, 3% unemployment, 4% health insurance and social services). This line previously described the class as 'prime' employers  _(Workers' Retirement and Social Security Law No. 18 of 2023 (Official Gazette 4734 of 28 August 2023, in force 90 days after publication; it repeals Law No. 39 of 1971), Ministry of Justice English translation, art. 14 Second (b)(2) — https://moj.gov.iq/upload/pdf/%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%B9%D8%AF%20%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B6%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%86%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D8%AC%D8%AA%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%B9%D9%8A%20-%20Copy_102.pdf)_
- **Employee social security contribution** — 5% of total wages and allowances received, deducted by the employer for the retirement branch (the previous text said 5% of net salary)  _(Workers' Retirement and Social Security Law No. 18 of 2023 (Official Gazette 4734 of 28 August 2023, in force 90 days after publication; it repeals Law No. 39 of 1971), Ministry of Justice English translation, art. 14 Second (a) — https://moj.gov.iq/upload/pdf/%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%B9%D8%AF%20%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B6%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%86%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D8%AC%D8%AA%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%B9%D9%8A%20-%20Copy_102.pdf)_
- **Contribution base** — Total wages and allowances received in cash and in kind; the wage used may not be less than the minimum wage for the worker's profession or the general minimum wage, whichever is higher (the previous text said after deduction of allowances)  _(Workers' Retirement and Social Security Law No. 18 of 2023 (Official Gazette 4734 of 28 August 2023, in force 90 days after publication; it repeals Law No. 39 of 1971), Ministry of Justice English translation, arts 1 Eleventh and 15 First — https://moj.gov.iq/upload/pdf/%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%B9%D8%AF%20%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B6%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%86%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D8%AC%D8%AA%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%B9%D9%8A%20-%20Copy_102.pdf)_
- **Wage ceiling / cap on contributions** — The wage used to calculate contributions may not exceed five times the minimum wage (the previous text reported no statutory cap)  _(Workers' Retirement and Social Security Law No. 18 of 2023 (Official Gazette 4734 of 28 August 2023, in force 90 days after publication; it repeals Law No. 39 of 1971), Ministry of Justice English translation, art. 15 First — https://moj.gov.iq/upload/pdf/%D9%82%D8%A7%D9%86%D9%88%D9%86%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%82%D8%A7%D8%B9%D8%AF%20%D9%88%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B6%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%86%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D8%AC%D8%AA%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%B9%D9%8A%20-%20Copy_102.pdf)_
- **Payroll income tax withholding (PAYE-equivalent)** — Employers withhold income tax from salaries and wages by direct deduction at the progressive personal rates, after the legal allowances  _(Direct Deduction Instructions No. 1 of 2007 under art. 61(4) of the Income Tax Law, General Commission for Taxes copy, arts. 1 and 2 — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/uploads/pdf/Law/%D8%AA%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA%20%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%201%20%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9%202007.pdf ; General Commission for Taxes, income tax guide (daleel dharibat al-dakhl: rates, allowances and filing) — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/%D8%AF%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%B6%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D8%AE%D9%84/)_
- **Payroll tax remittance deadline** — Within 15 days of the month following the month in which the salaries were paid  _(Direct Deduction Instructions No. 1 of 2007 under art. 61(4) of the Income Tax Law, General Commission for Taxes copy, chapter 9, art. 9 — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/uploads/pdf/Law/%D8%AA%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA%20%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%201%20%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9%202007.pdf)_
- **Employee social security registration** — Employers must register employees for social security as part of company set-up and ongoing compliance  _(Pensions and Social Security Law for Workers No. 39 of 1971 — https://bridgewest.eu/company-formation-iraq/)_
