# Iraq Personal Income Tax

## Personal income tax rates and bands

- **Band 1 — up to IQD 250,000** — 3% on annual taxable income up to IQD 250,000  _(General Commission for Taxes, income tax guide (daleel dharibat al-dakhl: rates, allowances and filing) — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/%D8%AF%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%B6%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D8%AE%D9%84/ ; Income Tax Law No. 113 of 1982 as amended, Arabic text published by the General Commission for Taxes, art. 13 (rates) — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/uploads/Najwan/law/Income-Tax-Law-No.-113-of-1982-Ar.pdf)_
- **Band 2 — IQD 250,000 to 500,000** — 5% on the slice from IQD 250,000 to 500,000  _(General Commission for Taxes, income tax guide (daleel dharibat al-dakhl: rates, allowances and filing) — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/%D8%AF%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%B6%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D8%AE%D9%84/ ; Income Tax Law No. 113 of 1982 as amended, Arabic text published by the General Commission for Taxes, art. 13 (rates) — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/uploads/Najwan/law/Income-Tax-Law-No.-113-of-1982-Ar.pdf)_
- **Band 3 — IQD 500,000 to 1,000,000** — 10% on the slice from IQD 500,000 to 1,000,000  _(General Commission for Taxes, income tax guide (daleel dharibat al-dakhl: rates, allowances and filing) — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/%D8%AF%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%B6%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D8%AE%D9%84/ ; Income Tax Law No. 113 of 1982 as amended, Arabic text published by the General Commission for Taxes, art. 13 (rates) — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/uploads/Najwan/law/Income-Tax-Law-No.-113-of-1982-Ar.pdf)_
- **Band 4 — over IQD 1,000,000** — 15% on annual taxable income above IQD 1,000,000  _(General Commission for Taxes, income tax guide (daleel dharibat al-dakhl: rates, allowances and filing) — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/%D8%AF%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%B6%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D8%AE%D9%84/ ; Income Tax Law No. 113 of 1982 as amended, Arabic text published by the General Commission for Taxes, art. 13 (rates) — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/uploads/Najwan/law/Income-Tax-Law-No.-113-of-1982-Ar.pdf)_
- **Non-resident taxation** — Income arising in Iraq is taxed in a non-resident's hands at the same progressive rates, whether or not it is received in Iraq; non-resident employees receive no personal allowances  _(Income Tax Law No. 113 of 1982 as amended (Arabic), General Commission for Taxes copy, Art. 5(2) — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/uploads/Najwan/law/Income-Tax-Law-No.-113-of-1982-Ar.pdf ; Direct Deduction Instructions No. 1 of 2007 (Arabic), General Commission for Taxes copy, Arts 1(1)(d) and 5(2) — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/uploads/Najwan/law/%D8%A3%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%82%D8%B7%D8%A7%D8%B9%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B6%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A8%D8%A9%20%D8%A8%D8%B7%D8%B1%D9%8A%D9%82%D8%A9%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%82%D8%B7%D8%A7%D8%B9%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%B4%D8%B1%20%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%201%20%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9%202007.pdf)_
- **Annual personal allowance — single (bachelor)** — IQD 2,500,000 for the taxpayer  _(General Commission for Taxes, income tax guide (daleel dharibat al-dakhl: rates, allowances and filing) — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/%D8%AF%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%B6%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D8%AE%D9%84/ ; Income Tax Law No. 113 of 1982 as amended, Arabic text published by the General Commission for Taxes, art. 12 (allowances) — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/uploads/Najwan/law/Income-Tax-Law-No.-113-of-1982-Ar.pdf)_
- **Annual personal allowance — married** — IQD 4,500,000: the IQD 2,500,000 personal allowance plus IQD 2,000,000 for a wife who is a housewife (a divorced or widowed woman receives IQD 3,200,000)  _(General Commission for Taxes, income tax guide (daleel dharibat al-dakhl: rates, allowances and filing) — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/%D8%AF%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%B6%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D8%AE%D9%84/ ; Income Tax Law No. 113 of 1982 as amended, Arabic text published by the General Commission for Taxes, art. 12 (allowances) — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/uploads/Najwan/law/Income-Tax-Law-No.-113-of-1982-Ar.pdf)_
- **Age allowance (taxpayer over 63 years)** — IQD 300,000 per year for a taxpayer who has reached 63  _(General Commission for Taxes, income tax guide (daleel dharibat al-dakhl: rates, allowances and filing) — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/%D8%AF%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%B6%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D8%AE%D9%84/ ; Income Tax Law No. 113 of 1982 as amended, Arabic text published by the General Commission for Taxes, art. 12 (allowances) — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/uploads/Najwan/law/Income-Tax-Law-No.-113-of-1982-Ar.pdf)_
- **Child allowance** — IQD 200,000 per child per year, however many children (child must have no income, be under 18, and be in full-time study)  _(General Commission for Taxes, income tax guide (daleel dharibat al-dakhl: rates, allowances and filing) — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/%D8%AF%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%84-%D8%B6%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A8%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AF%D8%AE%D9%84/ ; Income Tax Law No. 113 of 1982 as amended, Arabic text published by the General Commission for Taxes, art. 12 (allowances) — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/uploads/Najwan/law/Income-Tax-Law-No.-113-of-1982-Ar.pdf)_
- **Risk allowance exemption** — Housing, accommodation, transport, food, clothing and risk allowances paid to private sector employees are exempt up to a combined 30% of the monthly salary or wage; for state, public and mixed sector employees all allowances within the same 30% are exempt  _(Direct Deduction Instructions No. 1 of 2007 (Arabic), General Commission for Taxes copy, Art. 6(13) — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/uploads/Najwan/law/%D8%A3%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%82%D8%B7%D8%A7%D8%B9%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B6%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A8%D8%A9%20%D8%A8%D8%B7%D8%B1%D9%8A%D9%82%D8%A9%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%82%D8%B7%D8%A7%D8%B9%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%B4%D8%B1%20%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%201%20%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9%202007.pdf)_
- **Social security contributions deductible** — Pension deductions and social security contributions under the pension and social security laws are deductible from employment income; a foreign employee's home-country contributions are deductible up to the Iraqi contribution rate  _(Direct Deduction Instructions No. 1 of 2007 (Arabic), General Commission for Taxes copy, Art. 3(10) and (11) — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/uploads/Najwan/law/%D8%A3%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%82%D8%B7%D8%A7%D8%B9%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B6%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A8%D8%A9%20%D8%A8%D8%B7%D8%B1%D9%8A%D9%82%D8%A9%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%82%D8%B7%D8%A7%D8%B9%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%B4%D8%B1%20%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%201%20%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9%202007.pdf)_
- **Allowance documentation requirement** — The employee completes the allowance form for the year with supporting documents (marriage, birth and death certificates); until it is filed only the single person's allowance is granted  _(Direct Deduction Instructions No. 1 of 2007 (Arabic), General Commission for Taxes copy, Art. 11(1)(a) — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/uploads/Najwan/law/%D8%A3%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%82%D8%B7%D8%A7%D8%B9%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B6%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A8%D8%A9%20%D8%A8%D8%B7%D8%B1%D9%8A%D9%82%D8%A9%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%82%D8%B7%D8%A7%D8%B9%20%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%B4%D8%B1%20%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%201%20%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9%202007.pdf)_
- **Individual return filing deadline** — 31 March of the assessment year  _(Income Tax Law No. 113 of 1982 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/iraq))_
- **Employer payroll withholding remittance** — Withheld tax is paid to the Ministry of Finance accounts within 15 days of the month following the month of deduction, with a 5% addition for late payment  _(Direct Deduction Instructions No. 1 of 2007 under art. 61(4) of the Income Tax Law, General Commission for Taxes copy, chapter 9, art. 9 (withholding and payment dates) — https://tax.mof.gov.iq/uploads/pdf/Law/%D8%AA%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%AA%20%D8%B1%D9%82%D9%85%201%20%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%86%D8%A9%202007.pdf)_

Individuals are taxed at progressive rates from 3% to 15% under Income Tax Law No. 113 of 1982. Income derived from Iraq is taxable regardless of the individual's residence. Note: source figures for the bands vary; an Iraqi accountant should confirm the current thresholds and whether they are annual or monthly.
