# Afghanistan Payroll & Social Contributions

## Payroll withholding and social contributions

- **Employer withholding and remittance obligation** — Employers withhold monthly salary tax (PAYE-equivalent) from wages under the Income Tax Law 2009 and remit it to the Afghanistan Revenue Department. Afghanistan has no broad mandatory social-security/pension scheme for private-sector employees; a contributory pension exists chiefly for government employees.  _(Income Tax Law 2009)_
- **Salary tax withholding (PAYE-equivalent)** — 0% / 2% / 10%+150 / 20%+8,900 (Employer withholds monthly per the salary tax brackets)  _(Income Tax Law 2009)_
- **Withholding remittance deadline** — Generally within 10 days after the end of the month in which tax was withheld (approx — confirm exact remittance window)  _(Income Tax Law 2009)_
- **Private-sector social security / payroll contributions** — No general mandatory employer/employee social-security or payroll insurance contributions for private-sector workers (approx — confirm; limited schemes may apply)  _(Labour Law of Afghanistan)_
- **Government employee pension contribution** — ~8% of salary (Contributory pension scheme applies to civil servants (employee contribution commonly ~8% of salary) percent (approx — confirm rate and applicability))  _(Pensions Law / Civil Servants Law)_
- **Wage base for salary tax** — Gross monthly cash salary and most taxable benefits/allowances  _(Income Tax Law 2009)_
- **Exempt salary components** — Certain reimbursements and statutory exemptions may be excluded from taxable salary (approx — confirm specific exemptions)  _(Income Tax Law 2009)_
- **Employer reporting** — Employers file salary withholding tax returns and maintain payroll records for ARD  _(Income Tax Law 2009)_
- **Salary tax ceiling/cap** — No upper cap — top 20% band applies to all salary above 100,000 AFN per month AFN  _(Income Tax Law 2009)_
