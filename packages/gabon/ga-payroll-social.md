# Gabon Payroll & Social Contributions

## Social security and payroll contributions

- **Total employer social contribution rate** — 20.1% of capped salary for 2025: CNSS 16% (family allowances 8%, work injuries 3%, pensions 5%) plus CNAMGS 4.1%; from 1 January 2026, 22.1% (CNSS 18% plus CNAMGS 4.1%) under Decree 0487/PR/MASI  _(CLEISS contribution table in force to 31 December 2025 (archived 6 May 2025) — https://web.archive.org/web/20250506125305/https://www.cleiss.fr/docs/cotisations/gabon.html; https://gabonmediatime.com/cnss-vers-la-hausse-du-taux-de-cotisation/)_; _(Decree 0487/PR/MASI of 18 December 2025 (in force 1 January 2026, not read in the Journal Officiel; rates as reported by CLEISS and the Gabonese press) — https://www.cleiss.fr/docs/cotisations/gabon.html; https://gabonmediatime.com/cotisations-sociales-ce-que-le-nouveau-decret-change-concretement-pour-les-travailleurs/)_
- **Employer — CNSS family allowances** — 8% for 2025; 5% (family and maternity benefits) from 1 January 2026  _(CLEISS contribution table in force to 31 December 2025 (archived 6 May 2025) — https://web.archive.org/web/20250506125305/https://www.cleiss.fr/docs/cotisations/gabon.html; https://gabonmediatime.com/cnss-vers-la-hausse-du-taux-de-cotisation/)_; _(Decree 0487/PR/MASI of 18 December 2025 (in force 1 January 2026, not read in the Journal Officiel; rates as reported by CLEISS and the Gabonese press) — https://www.cleiss.fr/docs/cotisations/gabon.html; https://gabonmediatime.com/cotisations-sociales-ce-que-le-nouveau-decret-change-concretement-pour-les-travailleurs/)_
- **Employer — CNSS work injuries** — 3% for 2025; 2% from 1 January 2026  _(CLEISS contribution table in force to 31 December 2025 (archived 6 May 2025) — https://web.archive.org/web/20250506125305/https://www.cleiss.fr/docs/cotisations/gabon.html; https://gabonmediatime.com/cnss-vers-la-hausse-du-taux-de-cotisation/)_; _(Decree 0487/PR/MASI of 18 December 2025 (in force 1 January 2026, not read in the Journal Officiel; rates as reported by CLEISS and the Gabonese press) — https://www.cleiss.fr/docs/cotisations/gabon.html; https://gabonmediatime.com/cotisations-sociales-ce-que-le-nouveau-decret-change-concretement-pour-les-travailleurs/)_
- **Employer — CNSS retirement pensions** — 5% for 2025 (old age, invalidity and death branch at 7.5% with the employee's 2.5%); 11% from 1 January 2026 (branch at 16%)  _(CLEISS contribution table in force to 31 December 2025 (archived 6 May 2025) — https://web.archive.org/web/20250506125305/https://www.cleiss.fr/docs/cotisations/gabon.html; https://gabonmediatime.com/cnss-vers-la-hausse-du-taux-de-cotisation/)_; _(Decree 0487/PR/MASI of 18 December 2025 (in force 1 January 2026, not read in the Journal Officiel; rates as reported by CLEISS and the Gabonese press) — https://www.cleiss.fr/docs/cotisations/gabon.html; https://gabonmediatime.com/cotisations-sociales-ce-que-le-nouveau-decret-change-concretement-pour-les-travailleurs/)_
- **Employer — CNAMGS health insurance** — 4.1% of the IRPP base for private and parapublic employers (5% for the State as employer)  _(Decree 578/PR/MDSFPSSN of 22 December 2016 and Ordinance 0022/PR/2007 art. 38, as published by the CNAMGS — https://cnamgs.ga/payement-des-cotisations-employeurs/)_
- **Employee — CNSS contribution** — 2.5% for 2025 (pensions branch); 5% from 1 January 2026  _(CLEISS contribution table in force to 31 December 2025 (archived 6 May 2025) — https://web.archive.org/web/20250506125305/https://www.cleiss.fr/docs/cotisations/gabon.html; https://gabonmediatime.com/cnss-vers-la-hausse-du-taux-de-cotisation/)_; _(Decree 0487/PR/MASI of 18 December 2025 (in force 1 January 2026, not read in the Journal Officiel; rates as reported by CLEISS and the Gabonese press) — https://www.cleiss.fr/docs/cotisations/gabon.html; https://gabonmediatime.com/cotisations-sociales-ce-que-le-nouveau-decret-change-concretement-pour-les-travailleurs/)_
- **Employee — CNAMGS contribution** — 2% of the IRPP base for private and parapublic employees; pensioners above the SMIG pay 1% of the pension  _(Decree 578/PR/MDSFPSSN of 22 December 2016, as published by the CNAMGS — https://cnamgs.ga/immatriculation-secteur-prive-et-parapublic/)_
- **Total employee social contribution rate** — 4.5% for 2025 (2.5% CNSS + 2% CNAMGS); 7% from 1 January 2026 (5% CNSS + 2% CNAMGS)  _(CLEISS contribution table in force to 31 December 2025 (archived 6 May 2025) — https://web.archive.org/web/20250506125305/https://www.cleiss.fr/docs/cotisations/gabon.html; https://gabonmediatime.com/cnss-vers-la-hausse-du-taux-de-cotisation/)_; _(Decree 0487/PR/MASI of 18 December 2025 (in force 1 January 2026, not read in the Journal Officiel; rates as reported by CLEISS and the Gabonese press) — https://www.cleiss.fr/docs/cotisations/gabon.html; https://gabonmediatime.com/cotisations-sociales-ce-que-le-nouveau-decret-change-concretement-pour-les-travailleurs/)_
- **CNSS contribution wage ceiling** — XAF 1,500,000 per month (XAF 18,000,000 per year), unchanged by the 2026 decree, which also widens the base to bonuses, allowances, commissions and the value of benefits in kind, leaving out only reimbursed professional expenses  _(CLEISS contribution table in force to 31 December 2025 (archived 6 May 2025) — https://web.archive.org/web/20250506125305/https://www.cleiss.fr/docs/cotisations/gabon.html; https://gabonmediatime.com/cnss-vers-la-hausse-du-taux-de-cotisation/)_; _(Decree 0487/PR/MASI of 18 December 2025 (in force 1 January 2026, not read in the Journal Officiel; rates as reported by CLEISS and the Gabonese press) — https://www.cleiss.fr/docs/cotisations/gabon.html; https://gabonmediatime.com/cotisations-sociales-ce-que-le-nouveau-decret-change-concretement-pour-les-travailleurs/; https://www.gabonreview.com/cnss-le-doublement-de-la-part-salariale-garantira-t-elle-la-survie-du-modele-social-au-prix-du-pouvoir-dachat/)_
- **CNAMGS contribution wage ceiling** — XAF 2,500,000 per month  _(Decree 578/PR/MDSFPSSN of 22 December 2016, as published by the CNAMGS — https://cnamgs.ga/payement-des-cotisations-employeurs/)_
- **Salary IRPP withholding (PAYE-equivalent)** — Every employer paying salaries withholds IRPP at source using the progressive scale of art. 174 (0% to 35% per part)  _(Code Général des Impôts 2025 (Gabon), arts. 95 and 174 — https://dgi.ga/document/code-general-des-impots-2025/)_
- **Complementary salary tax (TCTS) withholding** — 5% on the part of net salary above XAF 150,000 a month, withheld monthly by the employer; performance bonuses, thirteenth month and similar payments are exempt up to XAF 4,000,000 a year, and the tax is deductible from gross salary income  _(Code Général des Impôts 2025 (Gabon), arts. 346 to 351 — https://dgi.ga/document/code-general-des-impots-2025/)_
- **Payroll withholding remittance deadline** — IRPP and TCTS withheld in a month are paid with a return within the first 15 days of the following month to the employer's tax office; CNAMGS contributions are declared and paid quarterly by the end of the month after the quarter (30 April, 31 July, 31 October, 31 January); CNSS salaries are declared quarterly on the DTS (payment date not read in the Social Security Code)  _(Code Général des Impôts 2025 (Gabon), arts. 96 and 350 — https://dgi.ga/document/code-general-des-impots-2025/)_; _(CNAMGS, Payement des cotisations employeurs — https://cnamgs.ga/payement-des-cotisations-employeurs/)_; _(CNSS, Déclaration trimestrielle des salaires — https://cnss.ga/entreprise/)_
