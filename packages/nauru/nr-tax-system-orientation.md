# Naoero Tax System Orientation

The Employment and Services Tax Act 2014 and Business Tax Act 2016 distinguish employment, independent services and business income. Begin with the activity, income source and taxpayer type. The current RONLAW texts use the name Naoero, whilst some public forms retain Nauru. [Employment and Services Tax Act][EST] · [Business Tax Act][BTA] · [Department of Finance][FIN]

## Residence

An individual is generally resident if they are a citizen without a permanent home outside the Republic, reside in the Republic as a qualifying resettled refugee or asylum seeker, or are married to a citizen who is resident under the citizenship test. A citizen employed by the Government of Naoero and posted abroad is also resident. Under the Business Tax Act, a company, partnership or trust incorporated, formed, settled or otherwise established or created in the Republic is a resident person. [Employment and Services Tax Act, s 7][EST] · [Business Tax Act, ss 3 and 8][BTA]

## Income and territorial scope

Employment and services tax applies to Naoero-source employment income and independent service fees. Source includes local work regardless of payment location and can also arise through a resident payer or a non-resident's local permanent establishment, subject to s 9. For business profits tax, resident business gross revenue can include worldwide amounts and non-resident gross revenue is limited to Naoero sources. Sections 7 and 9 govern permanent establishment and source. Section 13 sets a separate charge for specified non-resident interest, royalties and insurance premiums, with exceptions. [Employment and Services Tax Act, ss 8–12][EST] · [Business Tax Act, ss 7, 9, 11, 13 and 16–18][BTA]

## Taxpayer type

Business profits tax applies to the person conducting the business, including companies, individuals, partnerships and trusts. Schedule 1 has distinct rates and conditions for each. In particular, the per-member allowance for a partnership or trust requires solely Naoero-resident partners or beneficiaries, respectively. An eligible non-resident sole trader can instead fall within the quarterly small business tax regime. Check eligibility based on s 12, including the prior-year tests and any approval. [Business Tax Act, ss 3 and 11–12, Schedules 1–2][BTA]

## Currency

Both Acts require amounts in Australian dollars (AUD). Translate a foreign amount using the Reserve Bank of Australia rate for the date it is taken into account under the respective Act. [Business Tax Act, s 45][BTA] · [Employment and Services Tax Act, s 27][EST]

## Assessment periods

The usual business tax year ends on 30 June, with a company's annual balance date recognised as a substituted year under the definition. The annual business profits tax return and balance are normally due within 3 months of year end. Quarterly small business tax returns and payment are normally due within 15 days of the quarter's end. Employment and services withholding returns and payment are generally due within 15 days after month end. Check any applicable exceptions, instalments and filing extensions separately. [Business Tax Act, ss 3 and 39–41][BTA] · [Employment and Services Tax Act, ss 21–22 and 25][EST] · [Revenue Administration Act, ss 15 and 56][RAA]

## Tax authority and headline rates

The Nauru Revenue Office administers these taxes. The employment and services schedule includes resident pay-period bands of 0% and 20%, and separate rules for non-residents and specified activities. Business profits tax company categories range from 20% to 30%. Schedule 1 sets 3.5% for qualifying small business tax, though the older administration summary still states 2.5%. Use the applicable law and current form together. The Regional Processing Centre and public-sector branches require their own classification. [Revenue Office][NRO] · [Employment and Services Tax Act, Schedule][EST] · [Business Tax Act, Schedule 1][BTA] · [Older administration summary][OLD]
