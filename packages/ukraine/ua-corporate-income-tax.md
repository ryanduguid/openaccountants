# Ukraine Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax rates

- **Overview of CIT rate structure** — The standard corporate income tax (CIT) rate is 18%, with elevated rates applying to financial-sector entities from 2025. A simplified single-tax regime is available to qualifying small businesses as an alternative to CIT.  _(Tax Code of Ukraine)_
- **Standard CIT rate** — 18% (the basic rate, art. 136.1)  _(Tax Code of Ukraine No. 2755-VI (Податковий кодекс України, current consolidated text on zakon.rada.gov.ua), art. 136.1 — https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/2755-17 ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/ukraine/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **CIT rate for financial institutions (excluding insurers)** — 25% basic rate for the profit of financial institutions other than insurers, in force from 1 January 2024 (art. 136.1-1)  _(Tax Code of Ukraine No. 2755-VI (Податковий кодекс України, current consolidated text on zakon.rada.gov.ua), art. 136.1-1, inserted by Law 3474-IX of 21 November 2023 and amended by Law 4015-IX of 10 October 2024 — https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/2755-17 ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/ukraine/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **One-off elevated CIT rate on banks' 2024 profits** — 50% on banks' profit for the 2024 reporting year percent ((approx — confirm applicable year))  _(Tax Code of Ukraine)_
- **Insurance companies** — 3% / 0% levy on insurance premiums in addition to / in lieu of standard CIT depending on contract type ((approx — confirm))  _(Tax Code of Ukraine)_

## Tax base, withholding and deadlines

- **Overview of tax base and withholding** — CIT is computed on accounting profit adjusted by tax differences set out in the Tax Code. Passive income paid to non-residents is generally subject to a 15% withholding tax unless reduced by treaty.  _(Tax Code of Ukraine)_
- **Tax base** — Financial accounting profit (under national or international standards) adjusted by the tax differences in the Tax Code  _(Tax Code of Ukraine)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends paid to non-residents** — 15% (reducible by treaty)  _(Tax Code of Ukraine No. 2755-VI (Податковий кодекс України, current consolidated text on zakon.rada.gov.ua), art. 141.4.1 and 141.4.2 — https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/2755-17 ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/ukraine/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on interest paid to non-residents** — 15% (reducible by treaty)  _(Tax Code of Ukraine No. 2755-VI (Податковий кодекс України, current consolidated text on zakon.rada.gov.ua), art. 141.4.1 and 141.4.2 — https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/2755-17 ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/ukraine/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on royalties paid to non-residents** — 15% (reducible by treaty)  _(Tax Code of Ukraine No. 2755-VI (Податковий кодекс України, current consolidated text on zakon.rada.gov.ua), art. 141.4.1 and 141.4.2 — https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/2755-17 ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/ukraine/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on engineering services to non-residents** — **15%**. Engineering is named in the Tax Code as a withholdable category; most other services are not, so the characterisation of the contract decides whether anything is withheld at all  _(Tax Code of Ukraine No. 2755-VI (Податковий кодекс України, current consolidated text on zakon.rada.gov.ua), art. 141.4.1 and 141.4.2 — https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/2755-17 ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/ukraine/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on agency and brokerage fees to non-residents** — **15%**  _(Tax Code of Ukraine No. 2755-VI (Податковий кодекс України, current consolidated text on zakon.rada.gov.ua), art. 141.4.1 and 141.4.2 — https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/2755-17 ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/ukraine/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on freight paid to non-residents** — **6%**, including sea freight — the one rate that is not 15%, and low enough that applying the general rate over-deducts by nine points  _([Tax Code of Ukraine](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/ukraine/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **Withholding tax on proceeds from the sale of Ukrainian real estate and securities** — **15%** on income from the sale of real estate and on profits from the sale of securities. Share disposals are 15% unless the shares trade on a qualifying exchange or a treaty exempts them; capital gains on interest-free or discounted bonds are **18%**  _([Tax Code of Ukraine](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/ukraine/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **Withholding on bond yields** — Interest on Ukrainian government and municipal bonds is **exempt**; qualifying Eurobond yields are **5%**  _([Tax Code of Ukraine](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/ukraine/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **Deemed dividends** — **15%** on amounts treated as dividends, including overstatements identified in controlled transactions and distributions on a reduction of share capital. A transfer-pricing adjustment can therefore create a withholding liability where no dividend was declared  _([Tax Code of Ukraine](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/ukraine/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **What is NOT withheld** — Payments for most other services to non-residents are **not** subject to Ukrainian withholding tax. This is worth stating because the reflex in most jurisdictions is the opposite: here the default for a service fee is no withholding, and the named categories above are the exceptions  _([Tax Code of Ukraine](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/ukraine/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **Advance CIT on dividend distributions** — An advance CIT payment may be required on dividends, creditable against CIT liability  _(Tax Code of Ukraine)_
- **Annual return deadline (annual filers)** — Within 60 calendar days following the end of the reporting year days  _([Tax Code of Ukraine](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/ukraine/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **Quarterly return deadline (quarterly filers)** — Within 40 calendar days following the end of the reporting quarter days  _(Tax Code of Ukraine)_
- **CIT payment deadline** — Within 10 calendar days after the filing deadline for the relevant return days ((approx — confirm))  _(Tax Code of Ukraine)_
