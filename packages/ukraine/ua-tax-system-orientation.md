# Ukraine tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the taxpayer, where income arose, residence facts and period. Check the applicable treaty and foreign-tax relief.

## Territorial reach

A resident individual generally accounts for taxable Ukrainian and foreign income. A nonresident individual generally accounts for taxable Ukrainian-source income. An ordinary resident company starts from its accounting profit and applies the corporate tax adjustments. A foreign company within the effective-management rule in art 133.1.5 excludes foreign-source profit under art 134.1.1.

For a nonresident business, check the Ukrainian permanent establishment and source definitions, attributable profit and withholding on specified Ukrainian-source payments. A foreign company carrying on business through a Ukrainian permanent establishment must register with the tax authority before starting that activity. Check the applicable treaty for limits and relief. [Tax Code, arts 133, 134 and 141](https://tax.gov.ua/nk/spisok3/) · [Tax Code, arts 162–164 and 170.11](https://tax.gov.ua/nk/rozdil-iv--podatok-na-dohodi-fizichnih-o/) · [State Tax Service guidance on foreign receipts](https://tax.gov.ua/en/new-about-taxes--news-/print-1058326.html)

## Residence

Start with whether the individual lives in Ukraine. If they also live abroad, check for a permanent home in Ukraine. If they have a permanent home in both countries, check where their personal and economic ties are closer. If their centre of vital interests cannot be determined or they have no permanent home in either country, check for at least 183 days in Ukraine during the tax year, including arrival and departure days. The Code has a citizenship fallback and recognises specified self-identification of a principal home or registration as self-employed as sufficient grounds. Check any applicable treaty separately.

Legal persons formed and operating under Ukrainian law fall within the domestic definition, including where located abroad. A foreign company can fall within the special corporate rule for effective management in Ukraine. Any of 3 tests can establish effective management in Ukraine. The executive body meets more regularly there than in any other country, the officers mainly make decisions and carry out operations from there or the business is mainly managed from there in practice. Where another country may also be the place of effective management, check the further rules about bank accounts, accounting and managing personnel. The Code provides a separate procedure to declare resident status. [Tax Code, art 14.1.213](https://tax.gov.ua/nk/spisok1/) · [State Tax Service 2026 residence guidance](https://zp.tax.gov.ua/media-ark/news-ark/985777.html) · [Tax Code, art 133.1.5](https://tax.gov.ua/nk/spisok3/)

## Taxpayer types

Classify employees, individual entrepreneurs, independent professionals and legal persons. A registered general or limited partnership is a company form. Where participants instead sign a joint activity agreement without forming a legal entity, each participant subject to corporate income tax includes its part of the profit in its own computation. Check separate registration and filing rules for an agreement within the special tax accounting provisions, including where relevant for VAT. [Civil Code, arts 83–84, 113, 119, 133 and 1130](https://data.rada.gov.ua/ogd/zak/perv/text/d119580.htm) · [Tax Code, arts 133, 177 and 178](https://tax.gov.ua/nk/) · [State Tax Service joint activity guidance](https://zir.tax.gov.ua/main/bz/view/?id=30151&src=ques)

## Currency

The Ukrainian hryvnia (UAH) is the domestic currency. For personal income tax, convert foreign-currency income at the National Bank of Ukraine rate in effect when the income is accrued or received, as applicable. Retain the original currency record and the basis for the conversion. [National Bank Law, arts 32 and 35–36](https://bank.gov.ua/admin_uploads/law/Law_NBU_eng.pdf?v=10) · [Tax Code, art 164.4](https://tax.gov.ua/nk/rozdil-iv--podatok-na-dohodi-fizichnih-o/)

## Assessment periods

Individual income tax generally follows the calendar year. The standard corporate periods are the quarter, half-year, first 3 quarters and year, using cumulative returns. Annual corporate reporting applies to specified taxpayers, including newly registered entities and those whose previous annual accounting revenue, excluding indirect taxes, does not exceed UAH 40 million. Qualifying agricultural producers can elect the 1 July to 30 June year. Check the rules for registration, liquidation and any wartime filing relief that applies to the taxpayer and period. [Tax Code, arts 137.4–137.5 and 179](https://tax.gov.ua/nk/) · [State Tax Service 2026 corporate period guidance](https://svp.tax.gov.ua/media-ark/news-ark/print-1015004.html)

## Responsible authorities

The State Tax Service (Державна податкова служба України) and its territorial bodies administer national income tax. Identify the competent office and current payment details through the State Tax Service. Customs authorities handle duties and other tax matters within their customs jurisdiction. Check the relevant local authority's decisions for local tax settings. [Tax Code, arts 12 and 41](https://tax.gov.ua/nk/) · [State Tax Service](https://tax.gov.ua/en/) · [State Tax Service payment accounts for 2026](https://www.tax.gov.ua/en/mass-media/news/971659.html)

## Fictional intake

A company incorporated abroad moves most of its operational decisions to Kyiv. Its largest contract earns profit from a foreign source. Test the company's effective management, any treaty rules and the source of that profit before choosing how to include it in a Ukrainian return.
