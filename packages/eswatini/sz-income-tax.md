# Eswatini Personal Income Tax

## Personal income tax bands and rates (2025)

- **Band 1 — SZL 0 to 100,000** — 20% of taxable income  _(Income Tax Order, 1975 (as amended), Eswatini Revenue Service consolidation, Third Schedule Part II — https://www.ers.org.sz:8000/documents/IncomeTaxOrder1975.pdf ; Eswatini Revenue Service, Rates and Thresholds — https://www.ers.org.sz/IncomeTax/RatesandThres)_
- **Band 2 — SZL 100,001 to 150,000** — SZL 20,000 plus 25% of the excess over SZL 100,000  _(Income Tax Order, 1975 (as amended), Eswatini Revenue Service consolidation, Third Schedule Part II — https://www.ers.org.sz:8000/documents/IncomeTaxOrder1975.pdf ; Eswatini Revenue Service, Rates and Thresholds — https://www.ers.org.sz/IncomeTax/RatesandThres)_
- **Band 3 — SZL 150,001 to 200,000** — SZL 32,500 plus 30% of the excess over SZL 150,000  _(Income Tax Order, 1975 (as amended), Eswatini Revenue Service consolidation, Third Schedule Part II — https://www.ers.org.sz:8000/documents/IncomeTaxOrder1975.pdf ; Eswatini Revenue Service, Rates and Thresholds — https://www.ers.org.sz/IncomeTax/RatesandThres)_
- **Band 4 — above SZL 200,000** — SZL 47,500 plus 33% of the excess over SZL 200,000  _(Income Tax Order, 1975 (as amended), Eswatini Revenue Service consolidation, Third Schedule Part II — https://www.ers.org.sz:8000/documents/IncomeTaxOrder1975.pdf ; Eswatini Revenue Service, Rates and Thresholds — https://www.ers.org.sz/IncomeTax/RatesandThres)_
- **Standard annual tax rebate (primary)** — SZL 8,200 per year of assessment, deducted from the tax payable by a natural person; it does not apply to redundant or retiring individuals taxed at the concessionary rates  _(Income Tax Order, 1975 (as amended), Eswatini Revenue Service consolidation, s 8(1)(a) and Third Schedule Part I(b)(i) — https://www.ers.org.sz:8000/documents/IncomeTaxOrder1975.pdf ; Eswatini Revenue Service, Rates and Thresholds — https://www.ers.org.sz/IncomeTax/RatesandThres)_
- **Senior citizen rebate (age 60+)** — An additional SZL 2,700 for a person over 60 on the last day of the year of assessment, giving SZL 10,900 in total  _(Income Tax Order, 1975 (as amended), Eswatini Revenue Service consolidation, Third Schedule Part I(b)(ii) — https://www.ers.org.sz:8000/documents/IncomeTaxOrder1975.pdf ; Eswatini Revenue Service, Rates and Thresholds — https://www.ers.org.sz/IncomeTax/RatesandThres)_
- **Effective tax-free threshold** — SZL 41,000 of annual taxable income, the point at which the SZL 8,200 rebate offsets tax at the 20% lowest rate  _(Eswatini Revenue Service, PAYE Quick Guide — https://www.ers.org.sz/IncomeTax/Paye ; Eswatini Revenue Service, Rates and Thresholds — https://www.ers.org.sz/IncomeTax/RatesandThres)_
- **Non-resident rate treatment** — Non-residents are taxed on Eswatini-source income at the same graduated rates, but the rebate cannot reduce their tax below 10% of taxable income, or 3% of any part that is a pension  _(Income Tax Order, 1975 (as amended), Eswatini Revenue Service consolidation, Third Schedule Part I(c) — https://www.ers.org.sz:8000/documents/IncomeTaxOrder1975.pdf)_
- **Residence concept** — Eswatini taxes on source rather than residence; an individual is broadly treated as resident if ordinarily resident or physically present in Eswatini for a substantial part of the year (approx — confirm day-count test)  _(Income Tax Order, 1975)_
- **Employment income collection** — Employment income is collected through monthly PAYE withholding by employers  _([Income Tax (Employees' Tax) provisions / Income Tax Order, 1975](https://www.ers.org.sz/IncomeTax/Paye))_
- **PAYE remittance deadline** — Within 7 days after the end of the month in which remuneration was paid  _([Income Tax Order, 1975](https://www.ers.org.sz/IncomeTax/Paye))_
- **Annual return filing** — For the 2025 year of assessment, individuals with income beyond employment under the final deduction system, and employees earning SZL 3 million or more, file and pay by 30 November 2025; an employee whose only income is remuneration taxed under the final deduction system need not file  _(Eswatini Revenue Service, Income Tax Return Submission and Payment Notice for tax year end 2025, paras 1, 2 and 4 — https://www.ers.org.sz:8000/whatsnew-file/1753959572719-FULL_PAGE___Income_Tax_Returns_Notice_2025.pdf ; Eswatini Revenue Service, Income Tax Returns — https://www.ers.org.sz/IncomeTax/IncomeTaxReturn)_
