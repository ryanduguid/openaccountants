# Eswatini Company Formation & Entity Choice

## Entity types, incorporation and compliance

- **Overview of company formation regulation** — Company formation in Eswatini is governed by the Companies Act, 2009, administered by the Registrar of Companies under the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Trade. The private company limited by shares is the most common vehicle.  _(Companies Act, 2009)_
- **Governing company law** — Companies Act, 2009 (replacing the Companies Act of 1912)  _(Companies Act, 2009 — https://eswatinilii.org/akn/sz/act/2009/8/eng@1998-12-01)_
- **Registration authority** — Registrar of Companies, Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Trade  _(Companies Act, 2009 — https://www.gov.sz/index.php/departments-sp-1596706154?id=522)_
- **Private company (Pty Ltd)** — Most common entity — has share capital, restricts share transfers, and prohibits public offer of shares  _(Companies Act, 2009 — https://eswatinilii.org/akn/sz/act/2009/8/eng@1998-12-01)_
- **Public company (Ltd)** — Company that may offer shares to the public; subject to additional disclosure and governance requirements  _(Companies Act, 2009)_
- **Other vehicles** — External (branch of foreign) company, sole proprietorship, partnership, and non-profit association  _(Companies Act, 2009)_
- **Minimum directors** — At least one director ((approx — confirm minimum for private vs public company))  _(Companies Act, 2009 — https://eswatinilii.org/akn/sz/act/2009/8/eng@1998-12-01)_
- **Minimum share capital** — No statutory minimum share capital prescribed for a private company  _(Companies Act, 2009 — https://eswatinilii.org/akn/sz/act/2009/8/eng@1998-12-01)_
- **Core incorporation documents** — Memorandum and Articles of Association lodged with the Registrar of Companies, plus prescribed registration forms  _(Companies Act, 2009 — https://eswatinilii.org/akn/sz/act/2009/8/eng@1998-12-01)_
- **Incorporation steps** — Name reservation, prepare and lodge Memorandum & Articles, register with the Registrar of Companies, then obtain a Trading Licence and register for tax (TIN/VAT) and ENPF ((approx — confirm current portal/process))  _(Companies Act, 2009 / Trading Licences Order)_
- **Typical timeline and cost** — Generally a few weeks; registration fees are modest, but figures vary — confirm current Registrar fee schedule ((approx — confirm))  _(Companies Act, 2009)_
- **Core annual compliance** — File annual returns with the Registrar, maintain statutory records, prepare audited financial statements, hold an AGM, and renew the Trading Licence ((approx — confirm audit/AGM thresholds))  _(Companies Act, 2009)_
- **Tax registration on formation** — New companies must obtain a Taxpayer Identification Number from the ERS and register for VAT if turnover meets the threshold  _(Income Tax Order, 1975 / Value Added Tax Act, 2011 — https://www.ers.org.sz/Registration)_
