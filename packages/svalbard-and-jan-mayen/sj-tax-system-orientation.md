# Svalbard and Jan Mayen tax system orientation

Skatteetaten, the Norwegian Tax Administration, administers the Svalbard tax system and Jan Mayen employment withholding. The 2026 income year is the calendar year. Amounts in the annual Svalbard decision are in Norwegian kroner (NOK). [Svalbard Tax Act, Chapters 1 and 5][SVALBARD] · [Storting 2026 decision][RATES] · [Jan Mayen employer reporting][JREPORT]

## Svalbard residence and source

A person taking permanent residence in Svalbard is generally taxable on worldwide income and wealth, subject to statutory exclusions. A stay of at least 12 months counts as residence from its start. A person without Norwegian tax residence, including Svalbard, in the preceding 10 years generally has a restricted scope for the first five resident years. The tax office may set aside that limit in a year in the circumstances specified in section 2-1. Temporary work or business falls within section 2-2 from at least 30 consecutive days of qualifying stay. Svalbard property can give rise to separate liability. [Svalbard Tax Act, ss 2-1 and 2-2][SVALBARD]

A company is Svalbard-resident if it has an office there and is managed from there. Its income is generally in scope, with exceptions for business taxed elsewhere and immovable property or installations outside Svalbard. A non-resident company's income from qualifying business carried on in Svalbard for at least 30 consecutive days is taxable there. Test entity and individual liability separately. The business guidance also uses more-than-30-day wording in places. Use the statutory threshold and confirm the filing treatment for exactly 30 days. [Svalbard Tax Act, ss 2-3 and 2-4][SVALBARD] · [Skatteetaten, business in Svalbard][BUSINESS]

## Svalbard 2026 rates and compliance

The payroll withholding regime charges 8% up to 12 times the National Insurance base amount (G) and 22% on the excess. The Act specifies the last G fixed for the income year. Sole-proprietor income under section 3-2(4)(b) follows the wage rates through assessment. The 2026 decision applies 16% to other income under section 3(c), with a NOK 20,000 deduction for individuals in that category. A special 22% company rate applies to the part of profit that exceeds both NOK 15 million and the statutory sum of 10 times qualifying wage costs plus 20% of qualifying local capital's tax value at year end. Check wealth tax, National Insurance and the youth exemption or refund separately. [Svalbard Tax Act, ss 3-1 to 3-3 and 5-2][SVALBARD] · [Storting 2026 decision, ss 2–3][RATES] · [Skatteetaten, withholding guidance][WITHHOLDING]

Employers report and withhold through the payroll system. Income and wealth subject to assessment have a separate return route; business filers allocate the Svalbard result in their return. Check the appropriate annual notice and filing date for the taxpayer type. [Svalbard Tax Act, Chapters 4–5][SVALBARD] · [Skatteetaten, tax in Svalbard][SREPORT] · [Skatteetaten, business in Svalbard][BUSINESS]

## Jan Mayen and VAT geography

The Jan Mayen Tax Act applies the Svalbard payroll rates to remuneration for work there. Section 1 incorporates the Svalbard Act's section 2-2, which says at least 30 consecutive days. Employer reporting guidance says more than 30 days and excludes travel days. Confirm reporting with Skatteetaten for exactly 30 qualifying days. Section 3 allocates tax to the municipality where the taxpayer is treated as resident under the general tax law. The Jan Mayen Act provides a work-income regime. Use the applicable general rules for other taxpayer and income types. [Jan Mayen Tax Act, ss 1–3][JAN] · [Svalbard Tax Act, s 2-2][SVALBARD] · [Jan Mayen employer reporting][JREPORT]

Norway's VAT Act excludes Svalbard and Jan Mayen from the VAT area. Check imports, supplies and services that cross into mainland Norway under the applicable rules. [VAT Act, s 1-2, and Skatteetaten handbook][VAT]
