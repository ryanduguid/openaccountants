# Montenegro Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax rates

- **CIT structure and residence scope** — Montenegro applies a progressive corporate profit tax with three brackets. Resident companies are taxed on worldwide profit; non-residents on Montenegrin-source income or PE profit.  _(Corporate Profit Tax Law (Zakon o porezu na dobit pravnih lica), arts 3 and 28(1) (progressive rates; residents taxed on profit earned in and outside Montenegro, non-residents on profit earned in Montenegro), consolidated text with amendments to Official Gazette 125/23 on gov.me — https://wapi.gov.me/download/5e2364b3-d03c-4ceb-afff-7069b05e4034?version=1.0)_
- **CIT bracket — profit up to €100,000** — 9%  _(Corporate Profit Tax Law, art. 28(2)(1) (9% up to EUR 100,000.00), consolidated text on gov.me — https://wapi.gov.me/download/5e2364b3-d03c-4ceb-afff-7069b05e4034?version=1.0)_
- **CIT bracket — profit €100,000.01 to €1,500,000** — €9,000 + 12% on profit above €100,000  _(Corporate Profit Tax Law, art. 28(2)(2) (EUR 9,000.00 plus 12% on the amount above EUR 100,000.01), consolidated text on gov.me — https://wapi.gov.me/download/5e2364b3-d03c-4ceb-afff-7069b05e4034?version=1.0)_
- **CIT bracket — profit above €1,500,000** — €177,000 + 15% on profit above €1,500,000  _(Corporate Profit Tax Law, art. 28(2)(3) (EUR 177,000.00 plus 15% on the amount above EUR 1,500,000.01), consolidated text on gov.me — https://wapi.gov.me/download/5e2364b3-d03c-4ceb-afff-7069b05e4034?version=1.0)_

## Tax base and residence

- **Tax base description** — The tax base is accounting profit adjusted for tax purposes under the Corporate Profit Tax Law.  _(Corporate Profit Tax Law, arts 8 and 10 (income and expenses recognised in the amounts shown in the income statement under the accounting law, except where the Law provides otherwise), consolidated text on gov.me — https://wapi.gov.me/download/5e2364b3-d03c-4ceb-afff-7069b05e4034?version=1.0)_
- **CIT tax base** — Accounting profit per financial statements, adjusted for non-deductible/exempt items per the tax law  _(Corporate Profit Tax Law, arts 8 and 10 (income statement amounts adjusted as the Law provides), consolidated text on gov.me — https://wapi.gov.me/download/5e2364b3-d03c-4ceb-afff-7069b05e4034?version=1.0)_
- **Resident company scope** — Worldwide profit  _(Corporate Profit Tax Law, art. 3(1) (a resident is taxed on profit earned in Montenegro and outside Montenegro), consolidated text on gov.me — https://wapi.gov.me/download/5e2364b3-d03c-4ceb-afff-7069b05e4034?version=1.0)_
- **Non-resident company scope** — Montenegrin-source income or profit attributable to a Montenegrin permanent establishment  _(Corporate Profit Tax Law, art. 3(2) (a non-resident is taxed on profit earned in Montenegro), consolidated text on gov.me — https://wapi.gov.me/download/5e2364b3-d03c-4ceb-afff-7069b05e4034?version=1.0)_

## Withholding tax (corporate)

- **WHT overview** — A 15% withholding tax applies at the moment of payment to residents and non-residents on dividends, and to non-residents on a range of other income; double-tax treaties may reduce these rates.  _(Corporate Profit Tax Law, art. 29(1) and (4) (withholding at 15% of the gross amount at the moment of payment), consolidated text on gov.me — https://wapi.gov.me/download/5e2364b3-d03c-4ceb-afff-7069b05e4034?version=1.0)_
- **WHT on dividends / profit distributions** — 15% (to residents and non-residents)  _(Corporate Profit Tax Law, art. 29(1)(1) and (4) (dividends and profit shares paid to resident and non-resident legal persons; 15%), consolidated text on gov.me — https://wapi.gov.me/download/5e2364b3-d03c-4ceb-afff-7069b05e4034?version=1.0)_
- **WHT on interest (to non-residents)** — 15% (Montenegrin tax residents are exempt from tax on interest)  _(Corporate Profit Tax Law, art. 29(1)(2) and (4) (interest paid to a non-resident legal person; 15%), consolidated text on gov.me — https://wapi.gov.me/download/5e2364b3-d03c-4ceb-afff-7069b05e4034?version=1.0)_
- **WHT on royalties / IP fees (to non-residents)** — 15%  _(Corporate Profit Tax Law, art. 29(1)(2) and (4) (fees for copyright and other intellectual property rights paid to a non-resident legal person; 15%), consolidated text on gov.me — https://wapi.gov.me/download/5e2364b3-d03c-4ceb-afff-7069b05e4034?version=1.0)_
- **WHT on consulting/market research/audit fees and rental (to non-residents)** — 15%  _(Corporate Profit Tax Law, art. 29(1)(2) and (4) (lease of movable and immovable property, consulting, market research and audit services paid to a non-resident legal person; 15%), consolidated text on gov.me — https://wapi.gov.me/download/5e2364b3-d03c-4ceb-afff-7069b05e4034?version=1.0)_
- **Treaty relief** — A double-tax treaty may reduce or eliminate WHT where the non-resident proves treaty residency and beneficial ownership  _(Corporate Profit Tax Law (Zakon o porezu na dobit pravnih lica), as reported `[RESEARCH GAP — the statute's text was not read for this change; the secondary summary that carried the figure is no longer cited]`)_

## Filing and payment

- **Corporate tax year and filing overview** — The corporate tax year is the calendar year; the annual return and balancing payment fall at the end of March.  _(Corporate Profit Tax Law, art. 39(2) (the financial year is the calendar year) and art. 40(3) (return filed within three months of the end of the period), consolidated text on gov.me — https://wapi.gov.me/download/5e2364b3-d03c-4ceb-afff-7069b05e4034?version=1.0)_
- **CIT return filing deadline** — End of March of the following year  _(Corporate Profit Tax Law, art. 40(3) (return filed no later than three months after the end of the period for which the tax is computed), consolidated text on gov.me — https://wapi.gov.me/download/5e2364b3-d03c-4ceb-afff-7069b05e4034?version=1.0)_
- **CIT payment deadline** — End of March of the following year (for the previous year)  _(Corporate Profit Tax Law (Zakon o porezu na dobit pravnih lica), as reported `[RESEARCH GAP — the statute's text was not read for this change; the secondary summary that carried the figure is no longer cited]`)_
- **Statute of limitations on assessment** — 5 years from year-end when tax should have been assessed; absolute limit 10 years  _(General Tax Administration Law (Zakon o poreskoj administraciji), as reported `[RESEARCH GAP — the statute's text was not read for this change; the secondary summary that carried the figure is no longer cited]`)_
