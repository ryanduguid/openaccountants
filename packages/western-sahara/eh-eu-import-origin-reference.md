# Western Sahara EU import origin and labelling

Use this reference to trace the published EU provisions and identify what a shipment review still needs. Load `workflow-base`. Establish the import date, origin, goods and authorities separately. The [United Nations listing](https://www.un.org/dppa/decolonization/en/nsgt/western-sahara) records Western Sahara as a Non-Self-Governing Territory. EU import treatment does not establish a local charging power.

## Preference and labelling dates

| Question | Selected reading | Source |
|---|---|---|
| When did the agreement begin applying provisionally? | 3 October 2025, the date in the Official Journal notice. Recitals 7 and 12 of the delegated regulation refer to 4 October. Use the dedicated notice for the agreement's provisional start and Article 3 of the delegated regulation for the labelling dates. | [Provisional application notice 2025/2042](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/notice/2025/2042/oj) · [Delegated Regulation 2025/2652](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_del/2025/2652/oj/eng) |
| What does the preference provision cover? | The Joint Declaration extends the Association Agreement's preferences to originating products from Western Sahara that are subject to Moroccan customs control. Protocol 4 governs originating status and proof. The declaration provides for identifying the covered goods by region of origin on the proof. Establish each condition before selecting a preference. | [Exchange of letters, Joint Declaration paragraphs 1–6](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/agree/2025/2025/oj/eng) |
| Which labelling rule applies? | The amendment was published on 23 December 2025, entered into force on 24 December and applies from 4 October 2025. It inserts Article 5(6) in Delegated Regulation 2023/2429. For goods within Article 1(2) of that regulation that originate in Western Sahara, are under Moroccan customs control and are imported and marketed in the EU, use the region shown on the accompanying certificate of origin. | [Delegated Regulation 2025/2652, Arts 1 and 3](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_del/2025/2652/oj/eng) |
| What about earlier stock? | Article 2 allows the specified goods from Western Sahara under Moroccan customs control to remain on the EU market until stocks run out if they were lawfully imported before entry into force and labelled as originating in Western Sahara. All other applicable EU marketing standards still apply. | [Delegated Regulation 2025/2652, Art 2](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_del/2025/2652/oj/eng) |

## Regional proof of origin

The EU–Morocco Association Council adopted Decision No 2/2025 on 3 October 2025. Article 2 fixes entry into force on adoption and application from 3 October. The Official Journal published it on 28 January 2026. Article 1 adds Title III and Article 8 to Protocol 4. The earlier Council Decision 2025/2023 sets the EU position for adoption. [Association Council Decision No 2/2025, Arts 1–2](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dec/2026/242/oj) · [Council Decision 2025/2023](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dec/2025/2023/oj)

| Origin proof | Article 8 entries to check |
|---|---|
| EUR.1 movement certificate | Box 2 identifies the agreement in the form of an exchange of letters of 3 October 2025. Leave box 4 blank. Box 7 gives the applicable region, ‘Dakhla Oued Ed-Dahab’ or ‘Laâyoune-Sakia El Hamra’. |
| Origin declaration | Include the applicable region in connection with footnote (2) of the specimen declaration. Check the applicable rules for which goods qualify and who can make the declaration. |

The annex contains both specimens. Article 8 also defines the scope of ‘its vessels’ and ‘its factory ships’ and addresses working, processing, modification and consignments in Morocco under the Convention and transitional rules. Check the applicable product rules and evidence before accepting any proof. [Association Council Decision No 2/2025, Art 1 and Annex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dec/2026/242/oj)

### Proof eligibility and verification

Decision No 1/2025 sets the underlying Protocol 4 text from 2 October 2025. Protocol 4 applies the published PEM Convention and permits the Appendix A transitional rules as an alternative. Check whether that alternative still applies on the consignment date. It ends on 31 December 2027 or on earlier publication in Morocco’s Official Gazette of the latest Convention published in the EU Official Journal. For covered Western Sahara goods, apply the regional adaptations above from 3 October 2025. [Association Council Decision No 1/2025, Art 2 and Protocol 4 Arts 1–2](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dec/2025/2664/oj) · [Association Council Decision No 2/2025, Arts 1–2](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dec/2026/242/oj)

From 1 January 2026, the European Commission identifies the transitional rules for exports from Morocco to the EU pending Morocco’s ratification of the revised Convention. For a covered Western Sahara shipment under Moroccan customs control, check the regime for the movement date and how the regional entries apply alongside any other required remarks. [European Commission customs guidance from 1 January 2026](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/document/download/c4ee0731-c1d2-40bc-a65a-6251f2a5b8af_en?filename=TARIC+codes+to+be+used+in+the+customs+declarations+as+of+1+January+2026+in+relation+to+the+agreements+with+PEM+Contracting+Parties.pdf)

| Check | Selected provisions under the revised Convention and Appendix A |
|---|---|
| Who can make out the origin declaration? | An approved exporter may make out the declaration. An exporter without approval may use a declaration for an eligible consignment whose originating products total EUR 6,000 or less. Check the proof and region entries, authorisation where required and supporting origin records. |
| When is the proof due? | Ordinarily, submit it to importing customs within 10 months of issue or making out. Check late-submission exceptions and the separate rules for retrospective proofs, duplicate certificates and electronic proofs. |
| How can customs verify origin? | Importing customs can request verification at random or when it has reasonable doubts and the exporting customs authority carries out the verification. In a reasonable-doubt case, if no reply arrives within 10 months of the request or the reply gives too little information to determine the document’s authenticity or the products’ actual origin, importing customs must refuse the preference except in exceptional circumstances. |
| What if the product is a Chapter 7 vegetable? | The annex requires all Chapter 7 materials used to be wholly obtained. Match the product to the current applicable heading and record the evidence of harvest and origin. Check whether a specific tolerance, cumulation provision or other exception affects the result. |

[Revised PEM Convention, Appendix I Arts 18–19, 23 and 34 and Annex II](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dec/2024/390/oj/eng) · [Protocol 4 Appendix A, Arts 18–19, 23 and 34 and Annex II](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dec/2025/2664/oj)

### Customs declaration codes

Dutch Customs directs importers to use U179 for the regional EUR.1 or U180 for the regional origin declaration from 29 March 2026. It specifies origin code EH in field 16 08 and preference code 300 in field 14 11. Spain’s Tax Agency confirms U179, U180 and EH and directs importers to enter group code 2000 in the separate preferential-origin field 16 09. Confirm the relevant declaration fields and movement date with the importing authority. [Dutch Customs notice](https://www.douane.nl/wijziging-bescheidcodes-westelijke-sahara/) · [Spain Tax Agency notice, 4 June 2026](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/aduanas/novedades/2026/junio/4/recordatorio-declaracion-codigo-2000-pais-occidental.html)

## Procedure and holds

The European Parliament’s Legislative Observatory listed the consent procedure as preparatory on 27 November 2026. Verify conclusion and entry into force separately for the relevant period. [Procedure 2025/0297(NLE)](https://oeil.europarl.europa.eu/oeil/en/procedure-file?reference=2025%2F0297%28NLE%29)

The Official Journal records challenges to Decision 2025/2023 (the EU position on the Protocol 4 amendment), Decision 2025/2022 (signature and provisional application) and Delegated Regulation 2025/2652 (labelling). InfoCuria listed the 3 main proceedings as pending on 20 November 2026. Check for later judgments and any applicable interim relief before relying on a disputed measure for a shipment. [T-907/25](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/C/2026/1227/oj) · [T-908/25](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/C/2026/1228/oj) · [T-181/26](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/C/2026/2525/oj) · [InfoCuria case results](https://infocuria.curia.europa.eu/tabs/affair?lang=EN&searchTerm=T-907%2F25)

Confirm the applicable version of Protocol 4, the specific product rule, eligibility of the person making the proof and any verification outcome. Confirm the customs classification, declaration document codes, tariff, quota and licensing rules for the period. A preference claim and a marketing label require separate checks.

## Intake and reviewer output

Record the actual production location and region, goods description and classification, processing, route, entry and marketing dates, customs authority and control, and each certificate or other proof of origin. For earlier stock, record the import entry, original label, remaining stock and evidence of legal import and other compliance.

| Fictional facts | Bounded conclusion |
|---|---|
| Goods entered on 3 October 2025. | The date reaches the agreement's provisional application. It precedes the published labelling application date. Origin, control, eligibility and proof still need evidence. |
| A covered consignment bears a regional certificate and was entered on 4 October 2025. | The date reaches Article 3's application date. Check the exact scope and certificate under the applicable instruments before relying on the label or preference. |
| A covered consignment was lawfully imported on 23 December 2025 and labelled Western Sahara. | The stated date falls before the labelling regulation entered into force. The stock transition still depends on the other Article 2 conditions and evidence. |
| A EUR.1 puts the region in box 4 and leaves box 7 blank. | That proof fails the selected Article 8 entries. Seek the competent customs authority’s direction on correction before relying on it. |

For each position, record the instrument, operative provision and period, the established facts, the remaining source and procedure checks and the person responsible for closing them. Hold any calculation, preference declaration, relabelling or compliance decision until those checks and qualified review are complete.
