# Zambia Company Formation & Entity Choice

## Entity types and incorporation

Companies in Zambia are registered with the Patents and Companies Registration Agency (PACRA) under the Companies Act. The most common vehicle is a private company limited by shares.

- **Company registry** — Patents and Companies Registration Agency (PACRA)  _([Patents and Companies Registration Agency Act No. 4 of 2020](https://www.pacra.org.zm/))_
- **Private company limited by shares** — Most common entity; max 50 shareholders, restricts share transfers ((approx — confirm shareholder cap))  _(Companies Act)_
- **Public company limited by shares** — May offer shares to the public; higher disclosure and capital requirements  _(Companies Act)_
- **Company limited by guarantee** — Typically used for non-profit / NGO structures  _(Companies Act)_
- **Business name / sole proprietorship** — Registered business name for sole traders and partnerships (not a separate legal person)  _([Registration of Business Names Act](https://www.deel.com/blog/sole-proprietorship-zambia/))_
- **Minimum nominal share capital (private company)** — ZMW 15,000 ((approx — sources also cite ZMW 20,000 for local companies; confirm)) ZMW  _([Companies Act](https://www.elidge.com/zambia-company-registration/))_
- **Incorporation steps** — Name clearance with PACRA, file incorporation documents (Articles of Association), obtain Certificate of Incorporation, then register for TPIN with ZRA  _([Companies Act](https://www.pacra.org.zm/))_
- **Incorporation timeline** — Approximately 1 to 5 business days via PACRA's online eRegistry ((approx — confirm))  _([Companies Act](https://www.elidge.com/zambia-company-registration/))_
- **Tax registration (TPIN)** — Company must obtain a Taxpayer Identification Number (TPIN) from ZRA after incorporation  _([Income Tax Act](https://www.zra.org.zm/tax-information/))_
- **Annual return to PACRA** — Companies must file an annual return with PACRA each year  _([Companies Act](https://www.pacra.org.zm/))_
- **Annual income tax return** — Due by 21 June following the end of the charge year  _([Income Tax Act](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/zambia/corporate/tax-administration))_
- **Record retention** — Tax records must be kept for 6 years  _([Tax Procedures Act](https://quaderno.io/guides/zambia-vat-guide/))_
