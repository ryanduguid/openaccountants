# Malawi Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax rates and base

- **Standard company rate** — 30% on the taxable income of an ordinary company. Act 14 of 2025 removed the separate 5% surcharge for companies incorporated outside Malawi from the Eleventh Schedule on publication on 23 May 2025. Verify the branch’s taxable presence and the period to which the amendment applies. [MRA ordinary rate table](https://www.mra.mw/assets/upload/downloads/NEW_INCOME_TAX_RATES_EFFECTIVE_APRIL_2024.pdf), [Act 14 of 2025, s 3](https://elibrary.rat.gov.mw/server/api/core/bitstreams/fb807c0f-1335-442a-a1ce-9d6644186525/content) and [General Interpretation Act, s 9](https://media.malawilii.org/media/legislation/11801/source_file/b9d613ffd27bbbd3/1966-36.pdf).
- **Additional company tax** — For periods governed by Act 3 of 2026, published on 14 April 2026, a company pays an additional 10% on taxable income above MWK 5 billion. Check the assessment period and the law applicable to earlier or transitional periods. [Act 3 of 2026, s 12](https://media.malawilii.org/media/legislation/15519/source_file/2e96ab6e73cdf961/mw-act-2026-3-publication-document.pdf).
- **Minimum alternative tax** — Under Act 36 of 2025 a company with total turnover above MWK 5 billion in the year and at least 3 years of assessment in business compares ordinary income tax with 0.5% of total turnover and pays the higher amount. The turnover base includes exempt amounts and excludes payments subject to final withholding. Section 66B gives a limited, non-refundable credit for the excess of minimum alternative tax over ordinary income tax, usable in the next 6 years of assessment. [Act 36 of 2025, ss 3, 4 and 6](https://elibrary.rat.gov.mw/server/api/core/bitstreams/f9c5b41b-5dce-4764-b7c2-923c808bda48/content).
- **Tax base** — Taxable income = gross income from a Malawi source less allowable deductions and capital allowances  _(Taxation Act (Chapter 41:01))_
- **Capital gains** — Capital gains are included in taxable income and taxed at the normal company rate (30%) (approx — confirm treatment of indexed/inflation-adjusted gains)  _(Taxation Act (Chapter 41:01))_
- **Dividend withholding** — Section 70A sets a 10% final withholding on a distribution by a Malawi-incorporated company, subject to the specified related-company exception. For a non-resident recipient, confirm how s 70A, s 76A and any applicable treaty interact before applying a rate. [Taxation Act, ss 70A and 76A](https://www.mra.mw/assets/upload/downloads/Taxation_Act.pdf) and [Act 15 of 2021, schedule](https://elibrary.rat.gov.mw/server/api/core/bitstreams/e43a8644-e1e1-46d8-82d7-7e80afd5fd50/content).
- **Withholding tax on interest** — The Fourteenth Schedule lists a 20% rate on gross interest. Confirm whether an exemption, final treatment, the non-resident rule or a treaty applies. [Act 6 of 2022, s 7](https://media.malawilii.org/media/legislation/12931/source_file/mw-act-2022-6-publication-document.pdf) and [Taxation Act, s 76A](https://www.mra.mw/assets/upload/downloads/Taxation_Act.pdf).
- **Withholding tax on royalties** — The Fourteenth Schedule lists a 20% rate on gross royalties. Check the recipient and the non-resident rule before applying it. [Act 6 of 2022, s 7](https://media.malawilii.org/media/legislation/12931/source_file/mw-act-2022-6-publication-document.pdf) and [Taxation Act, s 76A](https://www.mra.mw/assets/upload/downloads/Taxation_Act.pdf).
- **Non-resident withholding** — Section 76A generally imposes a final 15% charge on gross Malawi-source income payable to a non-resident when the income is not attributable to that recipient’s permanent establishment in Malawi. Check statutory exceptions, any payment-specific provision, including the dividend rule, and applicable treaty treatment. [Taxation Act, s 76A](https://www.mra.mw/assets/upload/downloads/Taxation_Act.pdf) and [Act 15 of 2021, schedule](https://elibrary.rat.gov.mw/server/api/core/bitstreams/e43a8644-e1e1-46d8-82d7-7e80afd5fd50/content).
- **WHT on non-resident payments (mining project income)** — 10% on interest, royalty, independent personal services or dividend from a mining project  _(Taxation Act (Chapter 41:01))_
- **Corporate return and balance payment** — The provision containing the old 180-day return deadline was deleted with effect from 1 April 2025. A registered electronic user files the listed company return through the MRA system. For the 31 May accounting date, the MRA November 2026 notice gives 30 November 2026 for income tax return submission and payment. Confirm dates for other accounting periods and any applicable extension. [Act 15 of 2021, schedule](https://elibrary.rat.gov.mw/server/api/core/bitstreams/e43a8644-e1e1-46d8-82d7-7e80afd5fd50/content), [commencement notices](https://malawilii.org/akn/mw/act/gn/2025/21/eng@2025-03-21/publication), [Tax Administration Act, s 40](https://media.malawilii.org/media/legislation/14866/source_file/42da79119f043581/2021-13.pdf) and [Electronic System Rules, rr 6–9 and sch 2](https://media.malawilii.org/media/legislation/15001/source_file/da04be97455ad8f1/tax-administration-electronic-system-rules-2025.pdf) and [MRA notice, 12 November 2026](https://www.mra.mw/press/reminder-to-pay-taxes-and-submit-tax-returns-due-in-november-2026).
- **Provisional (quarterly) tax** — Provisional tax paid quarterly; instalments must total at least 90% of the final liability (approx — confirm exact quarterly due dates)  _(Taxation Act (Chapter 41:01) (as described at [globallawexperts.com](https://globallawexperts.com/commercial-lawyers-malawi-2026-paye-withholding-tax-corporate-tax-compliance-deadlines/)))_
