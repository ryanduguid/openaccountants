# Kuwait Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax — scope and rate

The ordinary 15% rate applies to the net taxable income of foreign corporate bodies carrying on business in Kuwait. Confirm the Kuwait activity and taxable foreign corporate profit share. Check Zakat and other contributions under their own rules. [Income Tax Decree, art 1 and Executive Bylaw, arts 2 and 9](https://mof.gov.kw/Desicions/Decree/PDF/Q3_1955e.pdf) · [Ordinary scope and source references](kw-tax-overview.md)

- **Corporate income tax rate.** 15% of net taxable income. [Income Tax Decree, art 1 and Executive Bylaw, art 9](https://mof.gov.kw/Desicions/Decree/PDF/Q3_1955e.pdf)
- **Ordinary ownership treatment.** A GCC-incorporated entity wholly owned by GCC nationals generally falls outside the ordinary charge. Confirm the foreign corporate profit share in a mixed-ownership company. [Ordinary scope and source references](kw-tax-overview.md)
- **Domestic minimum top-up tax.** For periods starting on or after 1 January 2025, in-scope multinational groups calculate a top-up against a 15% effective rate. The law disapplies specified older charges and provisions for applicable groups and periods, including ordinary corporate income tax and Zakat. [Decree-Law No. 157 of 2024, issuing art 1 and annexed arts 6–7 and 12](https://mof.gov.kw/MOFServices/PDF/TaxMultinationalLegislation.pdf)
- **DMTT revenue threshold.** The ultimate parent's consolidated revenue must reach EUR 750 million in at least 2 of the 4 immediately preceding periods. Confirm the Kuwait connection, special group and entity rules and exclusions. [Decree-Law No. 157 of 2024, annexed arts 1 and 3–4](https://mof.gov.kw/MOFServices/PDF/TaxMultinationalLegislation.pdf)
- **Tax base** — Net profits and capital gains attributable to the foreign body's activities in Kuwait (Kuwait-source income)  _(Executive Regulations to Income Tax Decree (Law No. 2 of 2008))_
- **Zakat.** Law No. 46 of 2006 imposes a 1% annual net-profit charge on public and closed Kuwaiti shareholding companies and excludes companies subject to Decree No. 3 of 1955. Check special government company exclusions, the calculation base, GCC filing and DMTT coverage. [Law No. 46 of 2006, arts 1 and 4](https://www.mof.gov.kw/Desicions/Decree/PDF/Q46-2006.pdf)
- **National Labour Support Tax (NLST)** — 2.5% percent (of annual net profit (listed Kuwaiti shareholding companies on Boursa Kuwait))  _(Law No. 19 of 2000 (National Labour Support Tax))_
- **KFAS contribution** — 1% percent (of net profit (after transfers to statutory reserve and loss offsets) for Kuwaiti shareholding companies)  _(Kuwait Foundation for the Advancement of Sciences (KFAS) contribution rules — https://www.crowe.com/kw/insights/kuwait-corporate-tax-framework)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends** — No withholding tax on dividends; however payers may apply the 5% retention rule until tax clearance is obtained  _(Law No. 2 of 2008 (Amendment to Income Tax Decree No. 3 of 1955))_
- **Withholding tax on interest / royalties** — No formal WHT regime; tax is collected via the 5% contract retention mechanism rather than a fixed-rate WHT on interest or royalties  _(Law No. 2 of 2008 (Amendment to Income Tax Decree No. 3 of 1955))_
- **5% retention rule** — Payers must retain 5% of each contract/payment to a beneficiary until the beneficiary presents a tax clearance certificate from the DIT  _(Ministerial Resolution No. 44 of 2010 (Tax Retention))_
- **Ordinary declaration.** File by the 15th day of the 4th month after period end. Check the filing exception for income of at most KWD 5,250, filing by exempt bodies and any approved extension of up to 60 days. [Income Tax Decree, art 8 and Executive Bylaw, arts 13–14](https://mof.gov.kw/Desicions/Decree/PDF/Q3_1955e.pdf)
- **Ordinary payment.** Pay in full or in 4 equal instalments due on the 15th day of months 4, 6, 9 and 12 after period end. Confirm changed payment terms when an extension applies. [Income Tax Decree, art 8 and Executive Bylaw, art 28](https://mof.gov.kw/Desicions/Decree/PDF/Q3_1955e.pdf)
- **Late payment penalty** — 1% of unpaid tax per 30-day period (or part thereof) of delay  _(Executive Regulations to Income Tax Decree (Law No. 2 of 2008))_
