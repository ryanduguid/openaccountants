# Philippines social contributions for self-employed individuals

Use this guide for ordinary domestic self-employment and professional practice. Identify the member's category, actual income and applicable months with each agency. SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG use distinct bases and payment references. Employment, overseas work, household employment and other special statuses require their own agency schedules.

| Scheme | Standard self-employed basis | Monthly contribution |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Social Security System (SSS) | Look up Monthly Salary Credit (MSC) from actual earnings in the self-employed schedule | ₱760 to ₱5,280 including Employees' Compensation |
| PhilHealth | 5% of documented monthly income, using the ₱10,000 floor and ₱100,000 ceiling | ₱500 to ₱5,000 |
| Pag-IBIG Fund (HDMF) | Mandatory members pay both shares on fund salary capped at ₱10,000 | 1% employee share up to ₱1,500 or 2% above, plus 2% employer share |

## Social Security System

Check compulsory self-employed status and register or update the existing membership in My.SSS. Use actual monthly earnings to identify the applicable MSC bracket under Circular 2024-008, effective January 2025. Members under 55 may change MSC as actual earnings change, subject to the current minimum. A member aged 55 or over may increase MSC only once per calendar year and by one bracket from the last posted MSC. When a new schedule raises the maximum, a member whose last posted MSC was the immediately preceding maximum may move to the new maximum. Decreases remain subject to the prevailing minimum. The 15% rate applies to MSC from ₱5,000 to ₱35,000. Allocate MSC up to ₱20,000 to regular Social Security and the excess up to ₱35,000 to the Mandatory Provident Fund (MPF). Add Employees' Compensation (EC) of ₱10 for MSC up to ₱14,500 or ₱30 from ₱15,000. [SSS Circular 2024-008, self-employed schedule](https://www.sss.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/2025-SSS-Contribution-Table-rev.pdf). [SSS self-employed registration and coverage](https://www.sss.gov.ph/self-employed).

Generate a Payment Reference Number (PRN) through My.SSS or the SSS app and pay through an accredited channel. The ordinary self-employed monthly or calendar-quarter deadline is the last day of the following month, or the next working day if that deadline falls on a Saturday, Sunday or holiday. Confirm the posting and retain the receipt. Section 11-A removes the contribution requirement for any month in which the self-employed member earns no income. Missed months generally remain gaps under the published individual-member payment rules. Ask SSS about any period-specific relief or a change of membership category. [Social Security Act of 2018, s 11-A](https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra2019/ra_11199_2019.html). [SSS contribution payment rules](https://www.sss.gov.ph/pay-contribution).

## PhilHealth

Use the existing PhilHealth Identification Number and update the Member Data Record with the applicable status and income evidence. For self-earning direct contributors and professional practitioners, the member pays the whole premium on documented monthly income. The 5% rate applies from January 2025, with a ₱500 minimum premium and ₱5,000 maximum. Use the current PhilHealth guidance for acceptable income records and any special contributor category. [Universal Health Care Act implementing rules, s 10](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/about_us/UHC-IRR_Signed.pdf). [PhilHealth Circular 2020-0005, V.E](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/circulars/2020/circ2020-0005.pdf). [PhilHealth Advisory 2026-0042](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/advisories/2026/PA2026-0042.pdf).

From 1 April 2026, the listed self-paying categories, including self-earning individuals and practicing professionals, must have a Statement of Premium Account (SPA) to pay. Check the member portal for the applicable months, amount and due date, and use an accredited channel. Sea-based migrant workers are excepted from this advisory. [PhilHealth Advisory 2026-0016](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/advisories/2026/PA2026-0016.pdf).

For missed periods, confirm the balance and the rules for the member's category with PhilHealth. Circular 2026-0010 provides a conditional one-time waiver of interest for specified self-paying members' missed contributions from November 2019 to December 2025, with payment arrangements for qualifying principal. Check eligibility and the date window before assuming relief. [PhilHealth Circular 2026-0010](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/circulars/2026/PC2026-0010.pdf).

## Pag-IBIG Fund

Self-employed persons with monthly income of at least ₱1,000 and aged 60 or younger are within the mandatory class in Circular 274, B.1.1.4. Circular 460, C.1.1.3 requires a mandatory self-employed member to pay both shares. From February 2024, the maximum fund salary is ₱10,000 and the ordinary combined contribution at that ceiling is ₱400. At or below ₱1,500 of fund salary, the shares total 3%. Above ₱1,500 they total 4%, subject to the fund salary cap. Other Earning Groups have their own rules. [HDMF Circular 274, official ONAR filing](https://naro.law.upd.edu.ph/issuances/5476). [HDMF Circular 460, official ONAR filing](https://naro.law.upd.edu.ph/issuances/3217). [Pag-IBIG regular savings guidance](https://pagibigfund.gov.ph/Membership_RegularSavings.html).

Register or update the membership category using the Fund's current registration process. Use the member's existing MID. Circular 274 sets the ordinary direct remittance deadline for self-employed members at the 10th day of the following month. The optional quarterly payment is due by the 10th day of the quarter's first month. Check for period-specific instructions, pay through an approved channel and verify posting in Virtual Pag-IBIG. Voluntary upgrades and Modified Pag-IBIG 2 savings are separate from the required regular savings. [HDMF Circular 274, remittance of contributions](https://naro.law.upd.edu.ph/issuances/5476). [Pag-IBIG regular savings guidance](https://pagibigfund.gov.ph/Membership_RegularSavings.html).

## Worked monthly contributions

These examples assume the stated amount is the qualifying base for each of the 3 schemes, ordinary local self-employment and no membership or age restriction that changes the applicable MSC. The total is a cash-planning figure before any applicable adjustments.

| Monthly income | SSS regular | SSS MPF | SSS EC | PhilHealth | Pag-IBIG | Total |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| ₱15,000 | ₱2,250 | ₱0 | ₱30 | ₱750 | ₱400 | ₱3,430 |
| ₱30,000 | ₱3,000 | ₱1,500 | ₱30 | ₱1,500 | ₱400 | ₱6,430 |
| ₱100,000 | ₱3,000 | ₱2,250 | ₱30 | ₱5,000 | ₱400 | ₱10,680 |

Confirm treatment with the relevant agency for gaps, no-income periods, concurrent employment or a transition to voluntary membership. Determine any tax exclusion or deduction separately under the applicable BIR regime and keep receipts for the amounts posted.
