# Tunisia Tax Overview

## Tunisia Tax Overview

## Tunisia tax system at a glance (2025)

Tunisia operates a residence-based tax system administered centrally, with a progressive personal income tax, a flat-band corporate income tax, and a standard-rate VAT. This is an unverified source-cited draft for tax year 2025; a licensed Tunisia accountant should confirm all figures.

- **Standard tax year** — Calendar year (1 January – 31 December)  _(Code de l'IRPP et de l'IS, 2026 edition (Direction Générale des Impôts, jibaya.tn), art. 7 (tax established each year on the income of the preceding year) — https://jibaya.tn/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Code_IRPP_IS_2026_fr.pdf)_
- **National currency** — Tunisian dinar (TND)  _(Banque Centrale de Tunisie statute)_
- **Tax authority** — Direction Générale des Impôts (DGI) of the Ministère des Finances, which publishes the tax system overview, finance laws and forms  _(Ministère des Finances, Aperçu général sur la fiscalité (système fiscal tunisien) — https://www.finances.gov.tn/fr/apercu-general-sur-la-fiscalite)_
- **Basis of taxation** — Personal income tax is due by any individual with their habitual residence in Tunisia; non-residents are taxed on Tunisian-source income, with withholding at source operating as a final tax for persons neither resident nor established in Tunisia  _(Ministère des Finances, Aperçu général sur la fiscalité (système fiscal tunisien) — https://www.finances.gov.tn/fr/apercu-general-sur-la-fiscalite)_
- **Top marginal personal income tax (IRPP) rate** — 40% on taxable income above TND 70,000 under the scale in force for income from 1 January 2025, plus the 0.5% Social Solidarity Contribution for returns due in 2023 to 2026  _(Finance Law for 2025, Law No. 2024-48 of 9 December 2024 (Official Gazette No. 149 of 10 December 2024; Ministry of Finance PDF, Arabic), art. 36 — https://www.finances.gov.tn/sites/default/files/2024-12/LF2025.pdf ; Finance Law for 2026, Law No. 2025-17 of 12 December 2025 (Official Gazette No. 148 of 12 December 2025; Ministry of Finance PDF, Arabic), art. 87(2) — https://www.finances.gov.tn/sites/default/files/2026-01/115725.pdf)_
- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 20%  _(Finance Law for 2025, Law No. 2024-48 of 9 December 2024 (Official Gazette No. 149 of 10 December 2024; Ministry of Finance PDF, Arabic), art. 37(1), amending art. 49(I) of the IRPP and IS Code — https://www.finances.gov.tn/sites/default/files/2024-12/LF2025.pdf)_
- **Does Tunisia levy VAT?** — Yes — VAT (TVA) at a 19% standard rate, with 13% and 7% reduced rates  _(Code de la TVA, 2026 edition, Arabic (Direction Générale des Impôts, jibaya.tn), art. 7 — https://jibaya.tn/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Code-TVA-2026.pdf)_
- **Social Solidarity Contribution (CSS)** — 1% of taxable profit as the base rate for companies (0.5% of taxable income for individuals); for companies taxed at 35% or 40% (banks, financial institutions, insurers and the other entities listed in art 49 of the Code), the contribution equals the difference between the tax at the sector rate plus four points and the tax at the sector rate, with a TND 500 minimum (art 37(11) of the Finance Law for 2025)  _(Loi n° 48-2024 du 9 décembre 2024 portant loi de finances pour l'année 2025 (JORT n° 149, Arabic text), Ministry of Finance, art 37(11) — https://www.finances.gov.tn/sites/default/files/2024-12/LF2025.pdf ; base rates: art. 53 of Loi n° 2017-66 (Finance Law for 2018) as restated by art. 87 of the Finance Law for 2026 — https://www.finances.gov.tn/sites/default/files/2026-01/115725.pdf)_
- **Annual corporate tax return deadline** — 25 March of the following year (25 June for companies subject to statutory audit)  _(Code de l'IRPP et de l'IS, 2026 edition (Direction Générale des Impôts, jibaya.tn), art. 60 I.2 — https://jibaya.tn/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Code_IRPP_IS_2026_fr.pdf)_
- **Main VAT filing frequency** — Monthly declaration and payment, within the first 15 days of the month for natural persons and the first 28 days for legal persons  _(Code de la TVA, 2026 edition, Arabic (Direction Générale des Impôts, jibaya.tn) (monthly declaration deadlines) — https://jibaya.tn/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Code-TVA-2026.pdf)_
