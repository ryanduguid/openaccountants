# Pitcairn Islands tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the person or legal entity, activity, island, dates, transaction and any foreign connections. The Pitcairn Constitution covers Pitcairn, Henderson, Ducie and Oeno. The Governor makes local Ordinances. English common law, equity and statutes of general application can apply subject to local circumstances, jurisdiction and local Ordinances. For a current liability, establish the applicable legislation and commencement with the Pitcairn Islands Office. Its laws index describes the status of the online copies and how to obtain the authoritative text. [Pitcairn Constitution Order 2010, art 2 and Schedule 2, ss 36–42][CONSTITUTION] · [Pitcairn government laws index][LAWS]

## Income and territory

The government-hosted 2014 Economic Review reported that islanders did not then pay income tax or customs duties. A 2026 United Nations working paper records budget assistance and receipts from landing fees, stamps, coins and domain names, based in part on information from the United Kingdom. Read those sources for their stated periods. Confirm any present local income, import or business obligation from the current law and obtain the foreign jurisdiction's rules where income, residence or trade crosses a border. The official UK travel advice requires declaration of goods that may be prohibited or subject to tax or duty. [2014 Economic Review, p 13][ECONOMIC] · [2026 UN working paper, paras 8–11][UN] · [UK travel advice, customs rules][CUSTOMS]

## Residence and entities

Pitcairn's immigration materials distinguish permanent residence, settlement visas and seasonal residence. Establish the person's status from their approval and dates. Check tax residence separately under the relevant law, including any foreign tax residence. Record the actual operator, business name and legal form. Chapter 16 provides for business-name registration within its scope. Confirm company incorporation and the applicable company law from the entity's own documents. [Pitcairn government immigration guidance][IMMIGRATION] · [Registration of Business Names Ordinance, ss 2–4][BUSINESS]

## Local charges

The Land Tenure Reform Ordinance provides an Annual Land Tax under s 9. It sets a mechanism for the President of the Court to prescribe the standard rate with the Governor's approval. Check the title, applicable clause and rate instrument with the Lands Court Registrar. Section 8(a)(ii) contains a 31 December payment rule for the specified absentee house-land case. Tourist and vessel charges follow their own event, passenger and authority rules. See `pn-landing-fee-passenger-levy-reference` for the source chain and the limits on calculating those charges. [Land Tenure Reform Ordinance, ss 8–9][LAND] · [Immigration fees and levies notice][FEES]

## Currency, periods and authorities

The Currency Ordinance recognises New Zealand notes and specified coins as legal tender subject to its terms. The immigration authority quotes fees in NZD. Establish the currency, charge date and conversion method for the relevant payment. The government's published 2019 accounts use a 31 March year-end. Establish each current charge or filing period from its own instrument. Contact the Pitcairn Islands Office for local law, the Lands Court Registrar for land matters and Immigration for arrival charges. Review cross-border filings with the applicable foreign tax authority. [Currency Ordinance, s 3][CURRENCY] · [Government financial information][FINANCIAL] · [Government immigration guidance][IMMIGRATION] · [Land Tenure Reform Ordinance, s 8][LAND]

## Fictional intake

A Pitcairn house-land holder settles abroad indefinitely and starts selling locally made goods online. Check whether the land was held under a Land Allocation Title and confirm the current land tax instrument. Assess the business registration and income, import and destination-country tax questions for the relevant facts and period.
