# Gibraltar tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the taxpayer, source of receipts, residence facts and period. Check any applicable international agreement and relief.

## Territorial reach

The Income Tax Act charges the categories of income in Schedule 1 that accrue in or derive from Gibraltar. For business profits, identify where the activities giving rise to profits occur and check the statutory deeming rules. For a nonresident company trading through a Gibraltar branch or agency, identify income from that trade and chargeable income from property or rights used or held by or for the branch or agency. The Act also brings specified foreign income in Tables B and C of Schedule 1 into charge for ordinarily resident persons other than companies, subject to the Act and Rules. Check the exemptions and the rule for an equivalent benefit obtained in Gibraltar. [Income Tax Act, ss 11 and 74 and Schedule 1](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/legislations/income-tax-act-2010-2627/download) · [Income Tax Office guidance](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/income-tax-office)

## Residence

An individual is ordinarily resident if present in Gibraltar for at least 183 days in the year of assessment, or if present in Gibraltar in that year and their total Gibraltar presence over 3 consecutive assessment years including that year exceeds 300 days. Any part of a day counts. A company is ordinarily resident if managed and controlled in Gibraltar or if managed and controlled outside Gibraltar by persons ordinarily resident there. An investment company under section 74 is also ordinarily resident when ordinarily resident persons control it, wherever it is resident. Check whether shares, voting rights, articles or other governing documents give those persons power to direct its affairs. Apply any relevant treaty or international agreement separately. [Income Tax Act, s 74](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/legislations/income-tax-act-2010-2627/download) · [Income Tax Office international agreements](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/income-tax-office)

## Taxpayer types

Classify an individual's sole-trade profit under the non-company assessment rules and a company's income under the rules for its accounting period. A partner's share is their entitlement to the partnership profit for that period and is assessed under the rules applicable to the partner. Trusts and foundations have separate residence and assessment rules that depend in part on who can benefit. Check both the taxable income of the arrangement and the rules for matching receipts to taxable income for a beneficiary. [Income Tax Act, ss 12–13A and 15–18](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/legislations/income-tax-act-2010-2627/download)

## Currency

Gibraltar uses sterling and Gibraltar pound notes and coins. Keep the amounts and conversion evidence that support the tax figures reported in pounds. [Government of Gibraltar key indicators](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/statistics/key-indicators) · [Government of Gibraltar fact sheets](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/press/gibraltar-fact-sheets)

## Assessment periods

The non-company year of assessment runs from 1 July to 30 June. Check the start and cessation rules where accounts are required. A company's tax accounting period ordinarily ends at its accounting date or after 12 months, whichever occurs first. Apply the special rules for a long set of accounts, an uncertain period or winding up. Check filing and payment deadlines separately, including the rule for the end of an extended accounting period. [Income Tax Act, ss 15–16, 29 and 74](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/legislations/income-tax-act-2010-2627/download) · [Income Tax Office company guidance](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/uploads/Income%20Tax%20Office/Corporate/Guidance%20Notes%20for%20Companies%2C%2008-2025.pdf)

## Responsible authorities

The Income Tax Office administers income tax and corporation tax under the Act. Use its filing and registration guidance for the taxpayer type and period. The Commissioner of Income Tax is responsible for assessment and collection. [Income Tax Act, s 2](https://www.gibraltarlaws.gov.gi/legislations/income-tax-act-2010-2627/download) · [Office of Fair Trading income tax service description](https://www.oft.gov.gi/business-support/single-point-of-contact/income-tax) · [Income Tax Office](https://www.gibraltar.gov.gi/income-tax-office)

## Fictional intake

A consultant spends 100 days in Gibraltar in each of 3 consecutive assessment years. Check the 183-day test for each year and count any additional part-days before applying the separate test that requires more than 300 days in total. Determine where the profit-generating activities took place and check the rules for any foreign receipts.

An investment company earns mainly passive income and its directors manage it abroad. Gibraltar ordinarily resident shareholders hold voting rights that let them direct how the company is run. It is ordinarily resident under section 74(c). Record the voting rights and confirm its income falls within the statutory investment-company definition.
