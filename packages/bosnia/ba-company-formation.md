# Bosnia and Herzegovina Company Formation & Entity Choice

## Entity types, capital and incorporation

The most common business vehicle is the limited liability company (d.o.o.). Registration is done at the competent court / registry in the relevant entity and involves notarisation, capital deposit, and registrations with the tax authority and municipality.

- **Limited liability company** — Drustvo s ogranicenom odgovornoscu (d.o.o.) — the most common form for SMEs and foreign investors  _(FBiH / RS Companies (Business Entities) Laws)_
- **Joint stock company** — Dionicko drustvo (d.d.) — used for larger enterprises and public companies  _(FBiH / RS Companies (Business Entities) Laws)_
- **Sole proprietorship** — Samostalni preduzetnik / obrt — individual trader, no separate legal personality  _(Entity Laws on Crafts / Self-Employment)_
- **Minimum share capital (d.o.o.)** — BAM 1,000 in FBiH and BAM 1 (cash part) in RS, paid into a temporary account before registration  _(Zakon o privrednim društvima FBiH (Sl. novine FBiH 81/15), art 307(1) — https://www.pufbih.ba/v1/public/upload/zakoni/2d6ea-zakon-o-privrednim-drustvima.pdf ; Zakon o privrednim društvima RS (Sl. glasnik RS 127/08, 58/09, 100/11 and 67/13), consolidated text on the judiciary portal, art 107(1) — https://portalfo2.pravosudje.ba/vstvfo-api/vijest/download/17283)_
- **Minimum share capital (d.d.)** — BAM 50,000 for an FBiH joint stock company; the RS Law on Companies sets separate minimums for open and closed joint stock companies  _(Zakon o privrednim društvima FBiH (Sl. novine FBiH 81/15), art 125(1) — https://www.pufbih.ba/v1/public/upload/zakoni/2d6ea-zakon-o-privrednim-drustvima.pdf)_
- **Incorporation steps** — Notarise founding act, deposit initial capital, register with the competent court, obtain company stamp, open a bank account, register with the tax administration and municipality  _(FIPA — How to Establish a Business in BiH (https://www.usemultiplier.com/bosnia-herzegovina/company-registration))_
- **Notarisation requirement** — Founding act and registration documents must be certified by a public notary  _(Law on Notaries (https://www.usemultiplier.com/bosnia-herzegovina/company-registration))_
- **Incorporation timeline** — Approximately 10 to 15 business days for court registration  _(FIPA — How to Establish a Business in BiH (https://www.usemultiplier.com/bosnia-herzegovina/company-registration))_
- **VAT registration threshold** — BAM 100,000 of taxable supplies (including exempt supplies with credit) in the previous year, or likely to be exceeded (raised from BAM 50,000 with effect from 2 December 2023); voluntary registration is possible below the threshold  _(Law on Value Added Tax (BiH), ITA English translation, art 57(1) — https://www.uino.gov.ba/portal/wp-content/uploads/95-ENGLESKI-PROPISI/1-VAT-PDV/1-Law_on_VAT_BiH.pdf ; Zakon o izmjeni Zakona o porezu na dodatu vrijednost (Sl. glasnik BiH 80/23), art 1 — https://www.uino.gov.ba/portal/wp-content/uploads/PROPISI/2_Porezi/1_PDV/1_Zakon_o_PDV/B/B-H-S-1-5-Z-o-PDV-sl-list-80-23-24-11-23.pdf)_
- **Core annual compliance** — Annual financial statements; the CIT return within 30 days after the financial statement deadline (FBiH) or within 90 days after year end (RS); monthly CIT advances; monthly VAT returns by the 10th of the following month; monthly payroll and contribution filings  _(Zakon o porezu na dobit FBiH (Sl. novine FBiH 15/16 and 15/20), Porezna uprava FBiH consolidated text, arts 49, 52 and 53 — https://www.pufbih.ba/v1/public/upload/zakoni/9080b-zakon-o-porezu-na-dobit-precisceni.pdf ; Zakon o porezu na dobit RS (Sl. glasnik RS 94/15), art 38(1) — https://www.poreskaupravars.org/dokumenti/zakoni/Zakon%20o%20porezu%20na%20dobit%20od%2001_01_2016.pdf ; Law on Value Added Tax (BiH), ITA English translation, arts 38 and 39 — https://www.uino.gov.ba/portal/wp-content/uploads/95-ENGLESKI-PROPISI/1-VAT-PDV/1-Law_on_VAT_BiH.pdf)_
