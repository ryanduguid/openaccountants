# Lithuania Tax Overview

## Lithuanian tax system at a glance

Lithuania is a euro-area EU member state. Taxes are administered by the State Tax Inspectorate (VMI) and the State Social Insurance Fund Board (Sodra). The figures below are for the 2025 tax year; note that a 2026 reform raises several rates.

- **Tax year** — Calendar year (1 January to 31 December); a company may be given a different 12-month tax period on request (Corporate Income Tax Law art. 6(2))  _(Law on Corporate Income Tax IX-675 (Pelno mokesčio įstatymas, consolidated text in force from 19 May 2026, e-seimas), art. 6(1) — https://e-seimas.lrs.lt/portal/legalAct/lt/TAD/TAIS.157066/asr ; Law on Personal Income Tax IX-1007 (Gyventojų pajamų mokesčio įstatymas, consolidated text in force from 11 June 2026, e-seimas), art. 7(1) — https://e-seimas.lrs.lt/portal/legalAct/lt/TAD/TAIS.171369/asr)_
- **Currency** — Euro (EUR)  _(Law on the Euro Adoption in the Republic of Lithuania)_
- **Tax authority** — State Tax Inspectorate (Valstybine mokesciu inspekcija, VMI); social insurance by Sodra  _(Law on Tax Administration (Mokesciu administravimo istatymas), https://www.vmi.lt/)_
- **Residence / taxation basis** — Residents (permanent place of residence, centre of personal, social or economic interests, or 183 or more days in Lithuania in the tax period, art. 4) are taxed on worldwide income; non-residents on Lithuanian-source income (art. 5)  _(Law on Personal Income Tax IX-1007 (Gyventojų pajamų mokesčio įstatymas, consolidated text in force from 11 June 2026, e-seimas), arts. 4 and 5 — https://e-seimas.lrs.lt/portal/legalAct/lt/TAD/TAIS.171369/asr ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/lithuania/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **Headline personal income tax rate** — 20% on annual income up to 36 average monthly wages (VDU), 25% from 36 to 60 VDU and 32% above 60 VDU under art. 6(1) as amended by Law XV-680 of 16 December 2025, in the text in force for 2026; dividends and the other art. 6(6) income 15%. For 2025 the scale was 20% up to 60 VDU (EUR 101,094) and 32% above  _(Law on Personal Income Tax IX-1007 (Gyventojų pajamų mokesčio įstatymas, consolidated text in force from 11 June 2026, e-seimas), art. 6(1) and 6(6) — https://e-seimas.lrs.lt/portal/legalAct/lt/TAD/TAIS.171369/asr ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/lithuania/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)_
- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 17% from 1 January 2026 (16% applied for 2025)  _(Law on Corporate Income Tax IX-675 (Pelno mokesčio įstatymas, consolidated text in force from 19 May 2026, e-seimas), art. 5(1)(1), as amended by Law XV-285 of 17 June 2025 — https://e-seimas.lrs.lt/portal/legalAct/lt/TAD/TAIS.157066/asr ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/lithuania/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Does VAT exist?** — Yes — standard VAT rate 21% (art. 2(32)); reduced rates of 12% for accommodation, scheduled passenger transport and admission to cultural events (art. 19(3) as amended by Law XV-287 of 17 June 2025, previously 9%) and 5% for medicines, medical and disability aids, periodicals and books (art. 19(4))  _(Law on Value Added Tax IX-751 (Pridėtinės vertės mokesčio įstatymas, consolidated text, e-seimas), art. 2(32) and art. 19 — https://e-seimas.lrs.lt/portal/legalAct/lt/TAD/TAIS.163423/asr)_
- **Annual personal income tax return deadline** — 1 May of the following year  _(Law on Personal Income Tax IX-1007 (Gyventojų pajamų mokesčio įstatymas, consolidated text in force from 11 June 2026, e-seimas), art. 27(1) — https://e-seimas.lrs.lt/portal/legalAct/lt/TAD/TAIS.171369/asr ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/lithuania/individual/tax-administration)_
- **Annual corporate income tax return deadline** — 15th day of the 6th month after year-end (15 June for calendar-year companies)  _(Law on Corporate Income Tax IX-675 (Pelno mokesčio įstatymas, consolidated text in force from 19 May 2026, e-seimas), art. 51(2) — https://e-seimas.lrs.lt/portal/legalAct/lt/TAD/TAIS.157066/asr ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/lithuania/corporate/tax-administration)_
- **VAT return deadline** — By the 25th of the month following a monthly tax period (quarterly and half-yearly periods: the 25th of the first month after the period)  _(Law on Value Added Tax IX-751 (Pridėtinės vertės mokesčio įstatymas, consolidated text, e-seimas), art. 85(1) — https://e-seimas.lrs.lt/portal/legalAct/lt/TAD/TAIS.163423/asr)_
