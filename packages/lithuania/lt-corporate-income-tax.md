# Lithuania Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax (pelno mokestis) - 2025

The standard corporate income tax rate is 17% from 1 January 2026 (it was 16% for 2025). Small companies and start-ups can qualify for reduced or 0% rates. Tax is on worldwide profit for resident companies.

- **Standard CIT rate** — 17% from 1 January 2026 (16% applied for 2025)  _(Law on Corporate Income Tax IX-675 (Pelno mokesčio įstatymas, consolidated text in force from 19 May 2026, e-seimas), art. 5(1)(1), as amended by Law XV-285 of 17 June 2025 — https://e-seimas.lrs.lt/portal/legalAct/lt/TAD/TAIS.157066/asr)_
- **Reduced rate for small companies** — 7% for units whose tax-period income does not exceed EUR 300,000 (art. 5(2) as amended by Law XV-285 of 17 June 2025; 6% applied for 2025, when the text also required fewer than 10 employees). Not available where related units' combined income exceeds EUR 300,000 or the ownership tests in art. 5(3) are met  _(Law on Corporate Income Tax IX-675 (Pelno mokesčio įstatymas, consolidated text in force from 19 May 2026, e-seimas), art. 5(2) and 5(3) — https://e-seimas.lrs.lt/portal/legalAct/lt/TAD/TAIS.157066/asr)_
- **Start-up 0% rate (first period)** — 0% for the first and second tax periods of a unit with income up to EUR 300,000 whose participants are natural persons, subject to the art. 5(2) and 5(3) conditions (one period before the 2025 amendment)  _(Law on Corporate Income Tax IX-675 (Pelno mokesčio įstatymas, consolidated text in force from 19 May 2026, e-seimas), art. 5(2) — https://e-seimas.lrs.lt/portal/legalAct/lt/TAD/TAIS.157066/asr)_
- **Tax base** — Resident companies: worldwide taxable profit (income less allowable deductions); non-resident: Lithuanian-source / PE profit  _(Law on Corporate Income Tax IX-675 (Pelno mokesčio įstatymas, consolidated text in force from 19 May 2026, e-seimas), art. 4(1) to 4(4) — https://e-seimas.lrs.lt/portal/legalAct/lt/TAD/TAIS.157066/asr)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends** — **17%** on distributed profits from 1 January 2026 (art. 5(1)(3)); 15% applied for 2025. The participation exemption in arts. 33 and 34 of the Law can remove it; check its holding conditions before relying on it  _(Law on Corporate Income Tax IX-675 (Pelno mokesčio įstatymas, consolidated text in force from 19 May 2026, e-seimas), art. 5(1)(3), as amended by Law XV-285 of 17 June 2025 — https://e-seimas.lrs.lt/portal/legalAct/lt/TAD/TAIS.157066/asr)_
- **Withholding tax on interest** — 0% on interest paid to entities registered in an EEA state or a treaty country; 10% otherwise (art. 5(1)(2)). Government securities, deposit interest and qualifying subordinated loan interest are outside the source-income list (art. 4(4)(1))  _(Law on Corporate Income Tax IX-675 (Pelno mokesčio įstatymas, consolidated text in force from 19 May 2026, e-seimas), art. 4(4)(1) and 5(1)(2) — https://e-seimas.lrs.lt/portal/legalAct/lt/TAD/TAIS.157066/asr)_
- **Withholding tax on royalties** — 10% standard (art. 5(1)(2)); 0% to associated EU companies that meet the Interest and Royalties Directive conditions in art. 37-1 (25% holding for two years)  _(Law on Corporate Income Tax IX-675 (Pelno mokesčio įstatymas, consolidated text in force from 19 May 2026, e-seimas), art. 4(4)(3), 5(1)(2) and 37-1 — https://e-seimas.lrs.lt/portal/legalAct/lt/TAD/TAIS.157066/asr)_
- **Withholding tax on Lithuanian immovable property** — **17%** on proceeds from the sale or transfer of title to immovable property located in Lithuania, and on the **lease** of it. The lease limb is the one that surprises: renting Lithuanian premises from a non-resident owner carries withholding at the same rate as selling them  _(Law on Corporate Income Tax IX-675 (Pelno mokesčio įstatymas, consolidated text in force from 19 May 2026, e-seimas), art. 4(4)(4) and 5(1)(2) — https://e-seimas.lrs.lt/portal/legalAct/lt/TAD/TAIS.157066/asr)_
- **Withholding tax on performers and sportspersons** — **17%** on income from sports activities or performers' activities  _(Law on Corporate Income Tax IX-675 (Pelno mokesčio įstatymas, consolidated text in force from 19 May 2026, e-seimas), art. 4(4)(6) and 5(1)(2) — https://e-seimas.lrs.lt/portal/legalAct/lt/TAD/TAIS.157066/asr)_
- **Withholding tax on board members' annual payments (tantiems)** — **17%** on annual payments to members of the board or supervisory board  _(Law on Corporate Income Tax IX-675 (Pelno mokesčio įstatymas, consolidated text in force from 19 May 2026, e-seimas), art. 4(4)(7) and 5(1)(2) — https://e-seimas.lrs.lt/portal/legalAct/lt/TAD/TAIS.157066/asr)_
- **Withholding tax on copyright infringement indemnities** — **10%** on indemnities received for infringement of copyright or neighbouring rights — the rate follows royalties rather than the 17% group  _(Law on Corporate Income Tax IX-675 (Pelno mokesčio įstatymas, consolidated text in force from 19 May 2026, e-seimas), art. 4(4)(5) and 5(1)(2) — https://e-seimas.lrs.lt/portal/legalAct/lt/TAD/TAIS.157066/asr)_
- **Treaty WHT relief** — Reduced/exempt WHT under double-tax treaties; residence certificate (Form DAS-1) required  _(Form DAS-1 (residence certificate))_
- **Annual CIT return deadline** — 15th day of the 6th month after year-end (15 June for calendar-year taxpayers)  _(Law on Corporate Income Tax IX-675 (Pelno mokesčio įstatymas, consolidated text in force from 19 May 2026, e-seimas), art. 51(2) — https://e-seimas.lrs.lt/portal/legalAct/lt/TAD/TAIS.157066/asr)_
- **Advance CIT payments** — Advance returns by the 15th day of the 3rd month of the tax period for the first six months and by the 15th day of the 9th month for the second half when computed on prior-year results, or by the 15th day of the 3rd month when computed on the expected result, which must reach 80% of the final tax (art. 47 and 51(3)); typically 15 March and 15 September  _(Law on Corporate Income Tax IX-675 (Pelno mokesčio įstatymas, consolidated text in force from 19 May 2026, e-seimas), art. 47 and 51(3) — https://e-seimas.lrs.lt/portal/legalAct/lt/TAD/TAIS.157066/asr)_
- **Final CIT payment deadline** — By the 15th day of the sixth month of the following tax period, with the annual return (15 June for calendar-year companies)  _(Law on Corporate Income Tax IX-675 (Pelno mokesčio įstatymas, consolidated text in force from 19 May 2026, e-seimas), art. 53(1) — https://e-seimas.lrs.lt/portal/legalAct/lt/TAD/TAIS.157066/asr)_
