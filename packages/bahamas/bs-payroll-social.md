# Bahamas Payroll & Social Contributions

## Bahamas Payroll & Social Contributions

## National Insurance (social security) contributions

- **Overview** — The Bahamas has no income tax PAYE, but employers must withhold and remit National Insurance Board (NIB) contributions on insurable wages. Rates and the insurable wage ceiling were increased effective 1 July 2024.
- **Employee contribution rate (employed persons)** — 4.65% of insurable wages %  _(National Insurance Act — https://www.nib-bahamas.com/about-nib/contributions/)_
- **Employer contribution rate (employed persons)** — 6.65% of insurable wages %  _(National Insurance Act — https://www.nib-bahamas.com/about-nib/contributions/)_
- **Combined contribution rate (employed persons)** — 11.3% (4.65% employee + 6.65% employer) %  _(National Insurance Act — https://www.nib-bahamas.com/about-nib/contributions/)_
- **Insurable wage ceiling (weekly)** — BSD 810 per week BSD  _(National Insurance Act — https://www.nib-bahamas.com/2024-adjustments-to-the-insurable-wage-ceiling-pensions-and-grants/)_
- **Insurable wage ceiling (monthly)** — BSD 3,510 per month BSD  _(National Insurance Act — https://www.nib-bahamas.com/2024-adjustments-to-the-insurable-wage-ceiling-pensions-and-grants/)_
- **Self-employed contribution rate** — 8.8% of insurable income (self-employed persons pay both portions) % ((approx — confirm))  _(National Insurance Act — https://www.nib-bahamas.com/about-nib/contributions/)_
- **Income tax withholding (PAYE)** — None — no income tax is withheld from wages; only NIB contributions are deducted  _(National Insurance Act)_
- **Contribution remittance deadline** — By the 15th day of the month following the contribution month  _(National Insurance Act — https://www.nib-bahamas.com/about-nib/contributions/)_
- **Employer registration** — Employers must register with the National Insurance Board and obtain an employer registration number before paying wages  _(National Insurance Act — https://www.nib-bahamas.com/about-nib/contributions/)_
