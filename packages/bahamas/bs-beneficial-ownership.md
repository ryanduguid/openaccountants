# Bahamas beneficial ownership

A legal entity subject to section 8 must identify who ultimately owns or controls it. It has 15 days from identifying a beneficial owner or registrable legal entity to notify its registered agent. If the entity has no registered agent, notify the registrar. The Registrar of Companies assumed the Registrar General's legal-entity functions on 1 February 2025. Confirm the current filing channel and form with the registry. [2020 amendment, s 4][BO-2020] · [Registrar of Companies Act, s 6][REGISTRAR] · [Registrar commencement notice][REGISTRAR-DATE]

## Who to identify

The Act covers legal entities established under the Companies Act, International Business Companies Act, Partnership Act, Partnership Limited Liability Act, Exempted Limited Partnership Act and Segregated Accounts Companies Act. It expressly includes non-profit companies that are not limited by shares. The Registrar General described the framework as applicable in February 2021. [Principal Act, ss 2–3][BO] · [2019 amendment, s 2][BO-2019] · [2020 amendment, s 2][BO-2020] · [Registrar General notice, February 2021][REGISTRY-2021]

Under section 5(1), identification includes a natural person who directly or indirectly owns or controls at least 10% of shares or voting rights in a legal person whose securities are unlisted. It also includes a natural person exercising management control beyond acting solely as a director, adviser or professional manager. The amended definition also covers directors of non-profit companies not limited by shares, natural-person partners in limited liability or exempted limited partnerships and beneficial owners of each segregated account. In insolvency proceedings it covers appointed liquidators, administrators and receivers. It also covers a creditor that appoints a receiver over at least 25% of shares or voting rights and an executor or personal representative of a deceased shareholder who would otherwise qualify. Section 5 also addresses joint holdings, disputed ownership, security interests and exposure through derivatives. [Principal Act, s 5][BO] · [2020 amendment, s 3][BO-2020]

Use section 6 to assess a legal person, affiliate or wholly owned subsidiary that would qualify as a beneficial owner if it were a natural person. It must also be an exempt entity, an entity listed on a regulated exchange, a statutory licensee, a foreign regulated person, a foreign state or a wholly owned subsidiary of a foreign state. Section 7 exempts regulated exchange listed entities, statutory licensees, subsidiaries wholly owned by those licensees and entities covered by ministerial regulations from the specified section 8 and section 9 duties. The recordkeeper still records the exempt person's particulars and exemption basis under section 9(2)(d). Confirm the basis for the particular entity before applying an exemption. [Principal Act, ss 6–9][BO]

## Maintain and update the records

The registered agent or registrar takes reasonable verification and recordkeeping steps under section 8 and maintains the database under section 9. Check section 8(3) if a beneficial owner holds an interest through a verified registrable legal entity. The entity has 15 days from becoming aware of a change to report the changed particulars and the date of the change under section 11(1). The recipient updates the database immediately on receiving the notice or otherwise becoming aware of a change. Section 11(2A) and (2B) add accuracy duties. From 19 January 2026, section 11(2C) also requires the agent or registrar to verify the section 9 particulars on an ongoing basis and regularly review records for higher-risk entities. [Principal Act, ss 8–11][BO] · [2020 amendment, ss 4–6][BO-2020] · [2022 amendment, s 3][BO-2022] · [2025 amendment, s 2][BO-2025] · [Appointed Day Notice][BO-DATE]

Section 10 requires the administrator, liquidator or other person involved in dissolution to keep the entity's database information for 5 years after dissolution, cessation as a legal entity or cessation of the appointment as registered agent, as applicable. A historic exception applies to an agent's records for an entity that had ceased to be a legal entity or was struck off before the Act commenced. [Principal Act, s 10][BO]

From 19 January 2026, section 11A(1) treats the person for whom a nominee holds shares or other interests in a legal entity as a beneficial owner. Section 11A(4) defines a nominee as someone who holds shares in their own name on another person's behalf under an express or implied agreement or arrangement. Apply that definition when assessing other interests and confirm the treatment of an arrangement involving only non-share interests. The entity and its agent identify and collect the applicable section 9(2)(a) to (c) particulars for each. Keep details of the arrangement current, including any declaration of trust or agreement. The 5-year retention duty applies when the underlying person changes or when a nominee arrangement starts or ends. [2025 amendment, s 3][BO-2025] · [Appointed Day Notice][BO-DATE]

## Secure-search requests

Section 12(5) gives the designated person 24 hours from an ordinary request for information to provide the search results from 19 January 2026. For an urgent request the period is 1 hour, or the period agreed by the designated person and requesting authority. The entity's 15-day initial notification and update periods remain governed by sections 8 and 11. [Principal Act, ss 8, 11 and 12(5)][BO] · [2020 amendment, ss 4 and 6][BO-2020] · [2025 amendment, s 4][BO-2025] · [Appointed Day Notice][BO-DATE]

## Check before filing

Confirm the entity's legal form, the current method for notifying the registry if there is no registered agent, the required form and the evidence the recipient accepts. The registry's public CARS overview describes its broader records service. The February 2021 notice is evidence of the regime at that date. Use current registry instructions for submission. [CARS overview][CARS] · [Registrar General notice, February 2021][REGISTRY-2021]
