# Japan tax system

> General orientation for 2026. Check the tax year, business year, place, taxpayer and transaction with a Japanese tax professional before applying the rules.

The National Tax Agency (NTA) administers national taxes in Japanese yen (JPY). Individual national income tax uses the calendar year. Prefectural and municipal taxes have separate bases, administration and deadlines. Identify both the relevant local government and the national office before filing. [NTA return guidance](https://www.nta.go.jp/english/taxes/individual/12011.htm) · [Ministry of Finance corporate tax summary](https://www.mof.go.jp/tax_policy/summary/corporation/c01.htm)

## Income and residence

- **Individuals.** A person with a domicile in Japan or a residence there continuously for at least 1 year is generally resident. A resident with no Japanese nationality and at most 5 years of Japanese domicile or residence in the preceding 10 years is a non-permanent resident for income tax. Other residents generally bring worldwide income into scope. Non-permanent residents bring in income other than foreign-source income plus foreign-source income paid in or remitted to Japan, subject to the source and remittance rules. Non-residents are generally taxed on Japanese-source income. Check treaty residence and any separate filing or withholding rules. [NTA No. 2010](https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/shotoku/2010.htm) · [Non-permanent resident workflow](jp-nonpermanent-resident.md)
- **Individual rates.** The ordinary national progressive scale has 7 rates from 5% to 45%. Separate taxation and additional rules for very high income can apply. The 2026 basic deduction is up to JPY 1.04 million, depending on total income. The 2026 basic deduction and payroll changes generally commence on 1 December 2026 and apply to 2026 income, with the December year-end adjustment affected and payroll withholding through November based on the previous procedures. For 2026 income, the reconstruction surtax is generally 2.1% of the relevant national income tax. For income arising from 1 January 2027, the reconstruction rate falls to 1.1% and the 1% special income tax for defence begins. Determine local inhabitant tax separately. [NTA national scale](https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/shotoku/2260.htm) · [NTA basic deduction](https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/shotoku/1199.htm) · [NTA 2026 payroll transition](https://www.nta.go.jp/users/gensen/2026kiso/index.htm)
- **Companies.** Locate the head or principal office and check foreign corporate obligations and any permanent establishment. The ordinary national corporation tax rate is 23.2%. A lower first tier can apply to qualifying small companies on the first JPY 8 million of annual income. Confirm exclusions, capital, ownership and the relevant income year. For business years starting on or after 1 April 2026, corporations subject to national corporation tax on their business-year income must file a separate defence surtax return, including when its amount is nil. In the ordinary calculation the surtax is 4% of the statutory corporation-tax amount remaining after the JPY 5 million annual deduction. Confirm adjustments, short years and group rules before calculating. The interim filing requirement starts for business years beginning on or after 1 April 2027 where applicable. [NTA residence definitions](https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/gensen/2875.htm) · [NTA company rates](https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/hojin/5759.htm) · [NTA defence surtax calculation](https://www.nta.go.jp/publication/pamph/pdf/0025004-109_1.pdf) · [e-Tax filing notice](https://www.e-tax.nta.go.jp/topics/2026/topics_20260417.htm)

## Consumption tax and filing

- **Consumption tax.** The combined national and local rates are 10% standard and 8% for qualifying items such as eligible food and drinks excluding alcohol and restaurant meals. An ordinary business with base-period taxable sales of JPY 10 million or less is generally exempt, subject to exceptions such as invoice registration and the specified-period and new-entity rules. Record the applicable invoice and retention requirements separately. [NTA reduced rates](https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/shohi/6102.htm) · [NTA exemption rules](https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/shohi/6501.htm)
- **Invoice transition.** For qualifying purchases from a non-registered supplier, the general transitional input-tax proportion was 80% through 30 September 2026. It is 70% from 1 October 2026 through 30 September 2028, then 50% for the next 2 years and 30% for the following year. For tax periods beginning from 1 October 2026, the JPY 100 million tax-inclusive limit for the applicable annual purchases from each supplier applies to that transition. Check acquisition dates, record retention and any other credit procedure. Apply the revised NTA timetable when reading an earlier consumption-tax guide. [NTA invoice reform](https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/zeimokubetsu/shohi/keigenzeiritsu/invoice-review/index.htm) · [NTA invoice Q&A, question 113](https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/zeimokubetsu/shohi/keigenzeiritsu/pdf/qa/01-01.pdf)
- **Returns.** The ordinary filing and payment deadline for a required 2026 individual income tax return is 15 March 2027. For 2026 individual business consumption tax it is 31 March 2027. Ordinary corporate income and consumption tax final returns and payments are generally due within 2 months from the day after the applicable year or period ends. Confirm any valid extensions and distinguish return extensions from relief on payment. e-Tax provides national filing. [NTA deadlines](https://www.nta.go.jp/publication/pamph/koho/kurashi/pdf/19.pdf) · [e-Tax corporate returns](https://www.e-tax.nta.go.jp/tetsuzuki/shinkoku/shinkoku02.htm)
