# Kosovo tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the taxpayer, source of income, residence facts and tax period. Check treaty treatment where applicable.

## Territorial reach

Kosovo taxes residents on taxable Kosovo and foreign-source income and nonresidents on taxable Kosovo-source income. Check whether a nonresident's business has a Kosovo permanent establishment and whether a payment is subject to withholding. Apply the relevant income and treaty rules to each receipt. [Personal Income Tax Law 05/L-028, arts 2–4](https://gzk.rks-gov.net/ActDocumentDetail.aspx?ActID=11014) · [Corporate Income Tax Law 06/L-105, arts 3–5 and 30–34](https://gzk.rks-gov.net/ActDocumentDetail.aspx?ActID=20988)

## Residence

An individual is resident if their principal residence is in Kosovo or they are physically present there for more than 183 days in any 12-month period. Check treaty residence separately. Corporate tax law defines a resident entity by establishment in Kosovo. The personal income tax law separately tests whether a sole business, partnership or grouping was established in Kosovo or has its place of effective management there. Identify which law applies before classifying the taxpayer. [Personal Income Tax Law, art 2(1.18–1.19)](https://gzk.rks-gov.net/ActDocumentDetail.aspx?ActID=11014) · [Corporate Income Tax Law, art 3(1.13)](https://gzk.rks-gov.net/ActDocumentDetail.aspx?ActID=20988)

## Taxpayer types

Individual businesses fall within personal income tax. Corporations and other commercial legal persons generally fall within corporate income tax. Kosovo general and limited partnerships have no separate legal personality. A partnership within the personal income tax rules files its annual income declaration and each partner reports their allocated share under the rules applicable to that partner. Check the partnership's own filing and payment duties for payroll and other taxes. [Personal Income Tax Law, arts 3 and 42](https://gzk.rks-gov.net/ActDocumentDetail.aspx?ActID=11014) · [Corporate Income Tax Law, art 4](https://gzk.rks-gov.net/ActDocumentDetail.aspx?ActID=20988) · [Business Organisations Law 06/L-016, arts 48–49 and 64](https://gzk.rks-gov.net/ActDocumentDetail.aspx?ActID=16426) · [Tax Administration partnership guidance](https://crmm.atk-ks.org/en/Public/InteractiveTaxGuide?ParentId=7369d6bb-efa4-4a46-2fda-08dba4862e41&StartingQuestionId=e4dea38e-c11a-4376-2fc7-08dba4862e41)

## Currency

The euro (EUR) is the currency for domestic cash payments and payments through Kosovo's national payment system. The Tax Administration accepts electronic tax payments in euros. Preserve foreign-currency amounts and the supporting conversion records. [Central Bank cash operations guidance](https://bqk-kos.org/news/the-cbk-publishes-the-document-frequently-asked-questions-on-the-regulation-on-cash-operations/?lang=en) · [Tax Administration payment guidance](https://crmm.atk-ks.org/en/Public/InteractiveTaxGuide?ParentId=a27f7663-052f-4a61-2fca-08dba4862e41&StartingQuestionId=e4dea38e-c11a-4376-2fc7-08dba4862e41)

## Assessment periods

The ordinary annual reference period is the calendar year. Both income tax laws recognise other reporting periods prescribed by the respective law. Determine which annual declarations, quarterly advances and withholding statements apply. [Personal Income Tax Law, art 2(1.27)](https://gzk.rks-gov.net/ActDocumentDetail.aspx?ActID=11014) · [Corporate Income Tax Law, art 3(1.19)](https://gzk.rks-gov.net/ActDocumentDetail.aspx?ActID=20988) · [Tax Administration filing guidance](https://www.atk-ks.org/en/portfolio/informata-te-pergjithshme-per-tatimet-ne-kosove/)

## Responsible authorities

The Tax Administration of Kosovo (Administrata Tatimore e Kosovës) administers central government income taxes. Check its filing guidance for the applicable form and the EDI channel. Refer immovable property tax matters to the municipal property tax office. [Tax Administration guidance](https://www.atk-ks.org/en/portfolio/informata-te-pergjithshme-per-tatimet-ne-kosove/) · [Tax Procedures Law 08/L-257](https://gzk.rks-gov.net/ActDetail.aspx?ActID=85269&langid=1) · [Consolidated Immovable Property Tax Law](https://gzk.rks-gov.net/ActDetail.aspx?ActID=106048)

## Fictional intake

A consultant whose principal residence is outside Kosovo spends exactly 183 days there during a 12-month period. Those days alone fall short of the residence threshold. Check other relevant 12-month periods and then assess which receipts have a Kosovo source, including the treatment of any local business presence.
