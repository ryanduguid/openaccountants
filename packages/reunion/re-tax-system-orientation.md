# Réunion tax system orientation

Réunion is a French overseas department and region under Article 73. French income taxes apply with overseas adaptations. Use the euro (EUR), identify the taxpayer and period and check the location of each operation. [French Constitution, art 73][CONSTITUTION] · [Guide de légistique][STATUS] · [IEDOM, currency][CURRENCY]

## Fiscal domicile and income source

French fiscal domicile generally brings worldwide income within French income tax. Individuals domiciled outside France are generally taxed on French-source income. Examine the home or principal place of stay, main professional activity and centre of economic interests. A relevant treaty can override domestic domicile criteria and determine which income France can tax. Check the French tax domicile and the specific overseas domicile and transfer conditions separately. [Code général des impôts (CGI), arts 4 A, 4 B and 4 bis][RES] · [BOFiP, overseas reduction and transfers][MOVE]

## Individuals and businesses

For 2025 income assessed in 2026, the ordinary individual schedule applies per family quotient share. Marginal bands above EUR 11,600 start at 11%, with 30%, 41% and 45% on the slices above EUR 29,579, EUR 84,577 and EUR 181,917 respectively. The Réunion domicile reduction is 30% of the tax at the relevant stage, capped at EUR 2,450. Determine the family quotient and its cap first and check the applicable domicile, transfer and assessment conditions. [CGI, art 197][IR] · [DGFiP, 2026 brochure][IRGUIDE] · [BOFiP, reduction stages][MOVE]

Identify whether business profits go on an individual's return, pass to taxable partners or belong to an entity within corporation tax. French corporation tax generally covers profits from enterprises operated in France, specified French-source amounts and profits allocated to France by treaty. The ordinary rate is 25%. Eligible businesses with turnover no greater than EUR 10 million can pay 15% on up to EUR 42,500 of taxable profit per 12 months, subject to the capital and group conditions where applicable. [CGI, arts 209 and 219][ISBASE] · [Art 219][IS] · [DGFiP, taxation of businesses][TYPES] · [DGFiP, group turnover][ISGROUP]

Réunion businesses can qualify for the overseas profit allowance. The ordinary 50% allowance is capped at EUR 150,000 per exercise. The enhanced 80% allowance, capped at EUR 300,000, can apply in listed sectors or to otherwise eligible establishments in communes specified under Décret n° 2026-421. Check the activity, location and period. [CGI, art 44 quaterdecies][ZFA] · [Décret n° 2026-421][ZFARE]

## VAT and octroi de mer

For 2026, ordinary local VAT is 8.5% and listed reduced-rate transactions are 2.1%. The specific exemption for rice sales and imports in Réunion is one separate product rule. Treat goods from metropolitan France, Guadeloupe and Martinique under the import rules and verify any exemptions and the distinct *octroi de mer*. [CGI, arts 294 and 296][VATBASE] · [Art 296][VATRATE] · [BOFiP, specific VAT exemptions][VATEX]

The *octroi de mer* can apply to imports and to sales of locally produced goods by their producers. For local production, check whether the independent producer's preceding calendar-year turnover from that activity reached EUR 550,000 before VAT and *octroi de mer*. Check the product, tariff and territory for any regional levy or exemption. Confirm the VAT provisions in force for transactions from 1 January 2027, when the VAT code changes. [Law 2004-639, arts 1–2 and 27–37][OM] · [French Customs, local tariffs][CUSTOMS] · [RITA tariff guidance][TARIFF] · [VAT recodification][VAT2027]

## Assessment periods and authorities

The ordinary income tax period for individuals is the calendar year. DGFiP's 2026 resident campaign for 2025 income set 4 June 2026 for electronic filing in Réunion and 19 May for eligible paper filings. Check each year's calendar, return channel and circumstances. [DGFiP, 2026 individual return dates][DATES]

Entities within corporation tax normally file form 2065 electronically. The ordinary statutory deadline is three months after a non-calendar-year close or the second working day after 1 May of the following year for a 31 December close or a year without a close. Verify any electronic filing allowance and separately confirm payment deadlines. DGFiP administers direct taxes and French Customs handles goods import formalities. [CGI, arts 209 and 223][ISBASE] · [Art 223][CFP] · [DGFiP, company filing][TYPES] · [French Customs][CUSTOMS]
