# Myanmar Personal Income Tax

> **Every band and relief in the previous version was correct.** All fourteen
> figures survived reading the Internal Revenue Department's own page. They were
> sourced to a commercial summary and are now sourced to the statute, section by
> section. What follows adds **three separate charges the guide did not mention
> at all**: capital gains, rental income, and the scale that applies when the
> source of funds cannot be shown.

## 1. Rates and bands — Union Taxation Law 2025, s.19(c)

| Taxable income from (MMK) | to (MMK) | Rate |
| ---: | ---: | :---: |
| 1 | 2,000,000 | **0%** |
| 2,000,001 | 10,000,000 | **5%** |
| 10,000,001 | 30,000,000 | **10%** |
| 30,000,001 | 50,000,000 | **15%** |
| 50,000,001 | 70,000,000 | **20%** |
| 70,000,001 | and above | **25%** |

_(2025 Union Taxation Law, s.19(c) — https://www.ird.gov.mm/tax-knowledge/taxes/individual)_

- **Salary exemption — MMK 4,800,000 a year** — No income tax is payable where
  annual salary income does not exceed 48 lakh (MMK 4,800,000, i.e. MMK 400,000
  a month)  _(2025 Union Taxation Law, s.19(a) — https://www.ird.gov.mm/tax-knowledge/taxes/individual)_
- **Non-resident foreigners: same bands, no reliefs** — The previous version
  hedged this ("some guides cite a 25% flat treatment in earlier years"). The
  IRD states it plainly: a non-resident foreigner does **not** receive the s.6
  reliefs below. The rate scale is the same; the reliefs are what differ  _(2025 Union Taxation Law, s.24 — https://www.ird.gov.mm/tax-knowledge/taxes/individual)_

## 2. Reliefs — Income Tax Law, s.6

| Relief | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Basic relief | **20% of total income**, capped at 100 lakh = **MMK 10,000,000** |
| Spouse (one only, non-earning) | **MMK 1,000,000** |
| Each qualifying child | **MMK 500,000** |
| Each co-residing parent | **MMK 1,000,000** |

_(Income Tax Law, s.6, as stated by the IRD — https://www.ird.gov.mm/tax-knowledge/taxes/individual)_

- **SSB contributions** — Employee Social Security Board contributions are
  deductible (2% of salary, capped at MMK 72,000 a year) ((approx — confirm; not
  stated on the IRD's individual-tax page, carried from a secondary source))  _(Income Tax Law (as described at [taxatlas.io](https://taxatlas.io/country/myanmar/income-tax)))_

## 3. Capital gains — a charge the guide did not carry

- **10% on gains from the sale of capital assets** — Income tax is payable at
  **10 per cent** on the gain arising on a sale of capital assets. A non-resident
  foreigner pays **in the currency in which the proceeds were received**  _(2025 Union Taxation Law, s.27(b) — https://www.ird.gov.mm/tax-knowledge/taxes/individual)_
- **Exempt where the total does not exceed MMK 10,000,000** — No income tax is
  charged even though a gain arises, if the total does not exceed 100 lakh  _(2025 Union Taxation Law, s.28 — https://www.ird.gov.mm/tax-knowledge/taxes/individual)_
- **Return within 30 days of the sale** — The return for a capital gain is filed
  within **30 days** of the disposal, on form ပတခ(ဝင)-၁  _(IRD, Registering & Filing — https://www.ird.gov.mm/tax-knowledge/taxes/individual)_

## 4. Rental income is taxed separately at 10%, not on the scale above

- **A separate 10% income tax on rent** — On total rent received, the s.6 reliefs
  are deducted and the **remainder is charged to a separate income tax at 10%**.
  A reader who put rental income on the progressive scale in §1 would compute a
  different, and usually lower, figure  _(IRD, Income & Obligation — https://www.ird.gov.mm/tax-knowledge/taxes/individual)_
- **Commercial tax on rent above MMK 50,000,000** — Where gross rent exceeds 500
  lakh a year, **commercial tax at 5%** is due; it is collected from the tenant
  and paid on advance-commercial-tax challan ပတခ(ကသခ)-၇. Rent up to MMK
  50,000,000 is exempt from commercial tax  _(2025 Union Taxation Law, ss.14(e) and 15(b) — https://www.ird.gov.mm/tax-knowledge/taxes/individual)_
- **Stamp duty on the lease** — 0.5% of the rent for a term of one year or less;
  0.5% of the average rent for a term up to three years; **2% of the average rent
  for a term over three years**  _(Myanmar Stamp Act, Schedule 1 item 35 — https://www.ird.gov.mm/tax-knowledge/taxes/individual)_

## 5. Income escaping assessment — Union Taxation Law 2025, s.25

Where a person cannot show the source of the income used to **buy or build
immovable property, a motor vehicle, a vessel, or shares**, income tax is charged
on that income at these rates. This is a second scale, entirely absent from the
previous version, and its bands are two orders of magnitude larger than the
ordinary ones:

| Income from (MMK) | to (MMK) | Rate |
| ---: | ---: | :---: |
| 1 | 300,000,000 | **3%** |
| 300,000,001 | 600,000,000 | **5%** |
| 600,000,001 | 1,000,000,000 | **10%** |
| 1,000,000,001 | 3,000,000,000 | **15%** |
| 3,000,000,001 | and above | **30%** |

_(2025 Union Taxation Law, s.25 — https://www.ird.gov.mm/tax-knowledge/taxes/individual)_

- **Property purchase also attracts 4% stamp duty** — On the value assessed by
  the relevant valuation body, with the contract drawn at that value  _(IRD, Income & Obligation; valuation Norms — https://www.ird.gov.mm/norms/real-estate/union-territory)_

## 6. Filing and payment

- **Assessment and payment — within 21 days** — Where the tax office issues the
  demand form ပတခ(ဝင)-၈ with income-tax challan ပတခ(ဝင)-၁၉, payment is due
  within **21 days** of their issue  _(IRD, Payment — https://www.ird.gov.mm/tax-knowledge/taxes/individual)_
- **Salary withholding (PAYS)** — Employers withhold income tax monthly from
  salaries and remit it to the IRD; an annual salary statement is filed after
  year-end  _(Income Tax Law (as described at [taxsummaries.pwc.com](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/myanmar/individual/taxes-on-personal-income)))_
- **Annual return deadline** — Within three months of the end of the financial
  year ((approx — confirm; not stated on the IRD's individual-tax page, and
  Myanmar has changed its financial year twice since 2018, so confirm the year-end
  before converting this to a date))  _(Income Tax Law (as described at [taxsummaries.pwc.com](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/myanmar)))_
- **Individual residence test** — A foreigner is resident if present in Myanmar
  for 183 days or more in the tax year, or domiciled or principally resident there
  ((approx — confirm against the Income Tax Law; not stated on the IRD page read))  _(Income Tax Law (as described at [taxsummaries.pwc.com](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/myanmar/individual/residence)))_

Internal Revenue Department's own statement of the law, which cites the 2025
Union Taxation Law section by section.
