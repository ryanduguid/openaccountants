# Greenland Tax System Orientation

Greenland administers its own income and import duties. Identify the taxpayer's full or limited tax liability, tax municipality and income category before selecting a return or rate. [Greenland Income Tax Act, ss 1–2 and 59–68][ACT] · [Greenland Tax Agency, rates][RATES]

## Residence and source

An individual generally has full tax liability through residence in Greenland or a stay of at least six months, including short interruptions such as holidays. Companies and other entities within section 1 can be fully liable through their Greenland domicile. The Greenland Tax Agency includes registered domicile or place of management in its company guidance. Limited liability under section 2 is confined to specified sources, including relevant work, permanent establishments and Greenland real estate. [Income Tax Act, ss 1–2][ACT] · [Greenland Tax Agency, guide for arrivals][ARRIVAL]

Full liability generally extends to worldwide income. Section 15(4) excludes specified income and expenses relating to foreign real estate. Check treaty treatment if the taxpayer, work or income connects with another jurisdiction. [Income Tax Act, ss 13–15][ACT] · [Greenland Tax Agency, guide for arrivals][ARRIVAL]

## Taxpayer type

Individuals can receive income subject to A-tax withholding or B-tax assessed outside ordinary payroll withholding. Independent business income and a company's own taxable profit require different assessment. Separate rules apply to dividend withholding and some activities outside municipal jurisdiction. [Income Tax Act, ss 59–68, 73–75 and 86–92][ACT] · [Greenland Tax Agency, business returns][RETURNS]

## Currency and assessment periods

Use Danish kroner (DKK). The income year is ordinarily the calendar year, with shorter liability periods and permitted alternative accounting periods. The administration law gives 1 May as the ordinary return deadline, 15 May for eligible electronic self-employed returns and 15 June for company and similar returns filed electronically in the prescribed manner. Check the filing channel, extension and prior estimated assessment rules. [Danmarks Nationalbank, banknotes][CURRENCY] · [Income Tax Act, s 12][ACT] · [Tax Administration Act, s 16][ADMIN] · [Greenland Tax Agency, return timetable][RETURNS]

## Headline taxes and authorities

- **Individual income tax.** The 2026 Greenland Tax Agency table gives combined ordinary rates of 42% or 44% depending on the municipality, made up of national, municipal and joint municipal components. These are rates on the applicable base after relevant deductions. Check the assigned municipality and any special regime. [Income Tax Act, ss 61–68][ACT] · [Greenland Tax Agency, rates][RATES]
- **Corporate income tax.** Section 60 sets the ordinary rate at 25%. The company regime permits voluntary advance tax under section 85 a. A separate 6% addition can apply under the tax administration provisions when final tax exceeds advance tax and the statutory threshold applies. Check the actual balance and assessment. [Income Tax Act, ss 60 and 85 a][ACT] · [Inatsisartut Act 12/2021, s 1(3)][SURCHARGE]
- **Indirect taxes.** Greenland does not impose a general VAT. Imported products can be subject to separate duties administered by the Greenland Tax Agency. Check the applicable product and relief. [Greenland Tax Agency, VAT refund guidance][VAT] · [Greenland Tax Agency, duties][DUTIES]
