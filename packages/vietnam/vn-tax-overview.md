# Vietnam Tax Overview

## Vietnam Tax Overview

## Vietnam tax system at a glance

Vietnam levies tax at the national level through the Tax Department (Cục Thuế) of the Ministry of Finance. The tax year is the calendar year and the currency is the Vietnamese Dong (VND). Residents are taxed on worldwide income; non-residents on Vietnam-source income only.

- **Standard tax year** — Calendar year (1 January - 31 December) by default; an enterprise may elect a fiscal year under the Corporate Income Tax Law, and annual tax statements fall due three months after the calendar or fiscal year end  _(Law on Corporate Income Tax No. 67/2025/QH15 (in force 1 October 2025, applying from the 2025 tax period), art. 5 — https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Doanh-nghiep/Law-67-2025-QH15-on-Corporate-Income-Tax-664714.aspx; Law on Tax Administration No. 38/2019/QH14, art. 44.2 — https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/thue-phi-le-phi/Law-38-2019-QH14-Tax-administration-425672.aspx)_
- **Currency** — Vietnamese Dong (VND); tax is declared and paid in dong, and taxpayers who keep books in a foreign currency convert them at the applicable exchange rate  _(Law on Tax Administration No. 38/2019/QH14, art. 7 — https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/thue-phi-le-phi/Law-38-2019-QH14-Tax-administration-425672.aspx)_
- **National tax authority** — The Tax Department (Cục Thuế) of the Ministry of Finance, which replaced the General Department of Taxation (Tổng cục Thuế) when the ministry was restructured from 1 March 2025 under Decree 29/2025/NĐ-CP; the Law on Tax Administration defines the tax authorities and their powers `[RESEARCH GAP — the decree text was not read; the rename rests on Ministry of Finance announcements]`  _(Law on Tax Administration No. 38/2019/QH14, art. 2 — https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/thue-phi-le-phi/Law-38-2019-QH14-Tax-administration-425672.aspx; Decree 29/2025/NĐ-CP of 24 February 2025 on the functions, tasks, powers and structure of the Ministry of Finance)_
- **Basis of taxation** — Residents taxed on worldwide income; non-residents taxed on Vietnam-source income only; a resident is present 183 days or more in a calendar year or 12 consecutive months, or has a permanent residence in Vietnam  _(Law on Personal Income Tax No. 04/2007/QH12 as amended (consolidated text 103/VBHN-VPQH, 2025), art. 2; Law on Personal Income Tax No. 109/2025/QH15 (in force 1 July 2026, applying to residents' employment and business income from the 2026 tax year), art. 2 — https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Thue-Phi-Le-Phi/Law-109-2025-QH15-personal-income-tax-687756.aspx)_
- **Headline corporate income tax rate** — 20 percent; 15 percent for enterprises with total annual revenue up to VND 3 billion and 17 percent from over VND 3 billion to VND 50 billion, 25 to 50 percent for oil and gas, from the 2025 tax period. From the 2026 tax period, enterprises with total annual revenue of VND 1 billion or less are exempt (see `vn-corporate-income-tax`)  _(Law on Corporate Income Tax No. 67/2025/QH15 (in force 1 October 2025, applying from the 2025 tax period), art. 10 — https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Doanh-nghiep/Law-67-2025-QH15-on-Corporate-Income-Tax-664714.aspx; art. 4.14a, added by Law No. 09/2026/QH16, art. 3 — https://datafiles.chinhphu.vn/cpp/files/vbpq/2026/5/09-qh.signed.pdf; Decree 141/2026/NĐ-CP, art. 2 — https://datafiles.chinhphu.vn/cpp/files/vbpq/2026/4/141-ndcp.signed.pdf)_
- **Top personal income tax rate (residents, employment income)** — 35 percent, the top band of the progressive schedule (seven brackets under the 2007 law for 2025; five brackets under the 2025 law from the 2026 tax year)  _(Law on Personal Income Tax No. 04/2007/QH12 as amended (consolidated text 103/VBHN-VPQH, 2025), art. 22; Law on Personal Income Tax No. 109/2025/QH15 (in force 1 July 2026, applying to residents' employment and business income from the 2026 tax year), art. 9 — https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Thue-Phi-Le-Phi/Law-109-2025-QH15-personal-income-tax-687756.aspx)_
- **Non-resident personal income tax rate (employment income)** — 20 flat percent on salaries and remuneration for work performed in Vietnam  _(Law on Personal Income Tax No. 04/2007/QH12 as amended (consolidated text 103/VBHN-VPQH, 2025), art. 26; Law on Personal Income Tax No. 109/2025/QH15 (in force 1 July 2026, applying to residents' employment and business income from the 2026 tax year), art. 21 — https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Thue-Phi-Le-Phi/Law-109-2025-QH15-personal-income-tax-687756.aspx)_
- **Does Vietnam have VAT?** — Yes - VAT applies with a standard rate of 10% on goods and services outside the 0% and 5% lists  _(Law on Value-Added Tax No. 48/2024/QH15 (in force 1 July 2025), art. 9 — https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/Thue-Phi-Le-Phi/Law-48-2024-QH15-on-Value-added-Tax-637020.aspx)_
- **Temporary VAT reduction** — 2% reduction (standard rate effectively 8%) for eligible goods/services from 1 July 2025 to 31 December 2026. Extended to end-2026 by National Assembly Resolution 204/2025/QH15, with implementing guidance in Decree 174/2025/ND-CP, which also brought transport, logistics and IT goods and services into scope. Supplies outside the eligible list stay at the 10% standard rate.  _(Resolution 204/2025/QH15; Decree 174/2025/ND-CP)_
- **Annual CIT return and final payment deadline** — Last day of the 3rd month after the calendar or fiscal year-end; the payment deadline is the filing deadline  _(Law on Tax Administration No. 38/2019/QH14, arts 44.2(a) and 55.1 — https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/thue-phi-le-phi/Law-38-2019-QH14-Tax-administration-425672.aspx)_
- **Annual PIT self-filing deadline (individuals filing directly)** — Last day of the 4th month after the end of the calendar year  _(Law on Tax Administration No. 38/2019/QH14, art. 44.2(b) — https://thuvienphapluat.vn/van-ban/thue-phi-le-phi/Law-38-2019-QH14-Tax-administration-425672.aspx)_
