# Pakistan tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record the taxpayer, where income arose, residence facts and period. Check the applicable treaty and foreign-tax relief.

## Territorial reach

Resident persons ordinarily account for Pakistan-source and foreign-source income. Nonresidents account for Pakistan-source income. Apply the statutory source rules to each receipt. For a foreign enterprise, check any Pakistani permanent establishment and the income attributable to it. Check treaty relief and the specific rules for foreign income of short-term and returning residents. [Income Tax Ordinance, ss 2(41), 11(5)–(6), 50–51 and 101](https://download1.fbr.gov.pk/Docs/2026724177725705IncomeTaxOrdinanace2001.pdf)

## Residence

An individual is resident if present in Pakistan for at least 183 days in the tax year or if a federal or provincial government employee or official is posted abroad during that year. A Pakistani citizen also qualifies under section 82(d) if present in no other country for more than 182 days during the tax year or if they are a resident taxpayer in no other country.

A company is resident if formed or incorporated under Pakistani law, if its affairs are wholly controlled and managed in Pakistan at any time during the year, or if it is a Pakistani provincial or local government. An association of persons is resident if its control and management is wholly or partly in Pakistan at any time in that year. [Income Tax Ordinance, ss 81–84](https://download1.fbr.gov.pk/Docs/2026724177725705IncomeTaxOrdinanace2001.pdf)

## Taxpayer types

Classify the taxpayer as an individual, company or association of persons under the Ordinance. An ordinary firm is an association of persons, with separate tax from its members. Check the rules for a member's distributions after the association pays tax and for company members, whose shares are excluded from the association's total income and taxed separately. Verify a limited liability partnership's treatment under the current provisions before applying the rules for an ordinary firm. [Income Tax Ordinance, ss 80 and 92](https://download1.fbr.gov.pk/Docs/2026724177725705IncomeTaxOrdinanace2001.pdf)

## Currency

Express amounts in Pakistani rupees (PKR). Convert foreign currency at the State Bank of Pakistan rate applying on the date the amount is taken into account under the Ordinance. [Income Tax Ordinance, s 71](https://download1.fbr.gov.pk/Docs/2026724177725705IncomeTaxOrdinanace2001.pdf) · [State Bank of Pakistan](https://www.sbp.org.pk/)

## Assessment periods

The normal tax year is the 12 months ending 30 June and is named for that calendar year. Tax year 2027 therefore ordinarily runs from 1 July 2026 to 30 June 2027. Check any approved special tax year and transition period. [Income Tax Ordinance, s 74](https://download1.fbr.gov.pk/Docs/2026724177725705IncomeTaxOrdinanace2001.pdf)

## Responsible authorities

The Federal Board of Revenue and Inland Revenue authorities administer federal income tax. Use the Board's Iris services for tax registration and returns. Check the appropriate provincial or territorial authority for obligations outside federal income tax. [Income Tax Ordinance, s 207](https://download1.fbr.gov.pk/Docs/2026724177725705IncomeTaxOrdinanace2001.pdf) · [Federal Board of Revenue](https://www.fbr.gov.pk/)

## Fictional intake

An ordinary partnership has 3 individual members and one corporate member. Record the member types and agreed shares, then identify the amounts assessed separately to the association and the company before preparing the returns.
