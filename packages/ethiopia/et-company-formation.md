# Ethiopia Company Formation & Entity Choice

## Entity types, capital and incorporation

- **Incorporation overview** — The most common vehicle is the Private Limited Company (PLC) under the 2021 Commercial Code, including a one-member PLC. Incorporation runs through name reservation, notarised constitutive documents, TIN registration, commercial registration and a business licence.
- **Common entity types** — Private Limited Company (PLC), One-Member PLC, Share Company (S.C.), branch of a foreign company, sole proprietorship, partnership  _(Commercial Code of Ethiopia Proclamation No. 1243/2021 (https://ysalawoffice.com/2025/03/20/company-law-in-ethiopia-a-comprehensive-guide/))_
- **PLC membership limits** — Minimum 1 (one-member PLC) or 2 members; maximum 50 members  _(Commercial Code of Ethiopia Proclamation No. 1243/2021 (https://www.apexhab.com/2025/08/15/one-person-plc-procedures-and-requirements-in-ethiopia/))_
- **PLC minimum capital** — ETB 15,000, fully subscribed and paid up at incorporation ETB  _(Commercial Code of Ethiopia Proclamation No. 1243/2021 (https://hulunem.com/ethiopia-business-guides/how-to-start-a-plc/))_
- **Share Company minimum capital** — ETB 50,000 with a minimum of 5 shareholders (approx — confirm under 2021 Code) ETB (approx — confirm under 2021 Code)  _(Commercial Code of Ethiopia Proclamation No. 1243/2021 (https://ysalawoffice.com/2025/03/20/company-law-in-ethiopia-a-comprehensive-guide/))_
- **Minimum capital for foreign investors** — Generally USD 200,000 per investment project (USD 150,000 for joint ventures); lower thresholds for certain sectors (approx — confirm current Investment Regulation) USD (approx — confirm current Investment Regulation)  _(Investment Proclamation No. 1180/2020 and Investment Regulation (https://www.healyconsultants.com/ethiopia-company-registration/setup-llc/))_
- **Step 1 — Name reservation** — Reserve a company name (submit several options); approval reserves the name for ~30 days  _(Commercial Registration and Business Licensing Proclamation (https://www.2merkato.com/articles/starting-a-business/82-how-to-start-a-plc-private-limited-company-in-ethiopia-particularly-in-addis-ababa))_
- **Step 2 — Constitutive documents** — Prepare and notarise the Memorandum of Association (MOA) and Articles of Association (AOA), in Amharic or English  _(Commercial Code of Ethiopia Proclamation No. 1243/2021 (https://hulunem.com/ethiopia-business-guides/how-to-start-a-plc/))_
- **Step 3 — Tax Identification Number** — Obtain a Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) from the Ministry of Revenues  _(Tax Administration Proclamation No. 983/2016 (https://hulunem.com/ethiopia-business-guides/how-to-start-a-plc/))_
- **Step 4 — Commercial registration and licence** — File for commercial registration and obtain a business licence specifying authorised activities (Ministry of Trade and Regional Integration or Ethiopian Investment Commission for foreign investors)  _(Commercial Registration and Business Licensing Proclamation (https://www.2merkato.com/articles/starting-a-business/82-how-to-start-a-plc-private-limited-company-in-ethiopia-particularly-in-addis-ababa))_
- **Typical incorporation timeline** — Roughly 2–4 weeks for a domestic PLC once documents are ready (approx — confirm) (approx — confirm)  _(Commercial Registration and Business Licensing Proclamation (https://www.healyconsultants.com/ethiopia-company-registration/setup-llc/))_
- **Core annual compliance** — Annual business-licence renewal, annual income tax return within 4 months of year-end, monthly VAT and payroll filings, and statutory bookkeeping  _(Commercial Registration and Business Licensing Proclamation; Tax Administration Proclamation No. 983/2016 (https://hulunem.com/ethiopia-business-guides/tax-regulations/))_
