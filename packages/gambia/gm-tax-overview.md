# Gambia Tax Overview

## Gambia Tax Overview

## The Gambian tax system at a glance

The Gambia taxes income, consumption (VAT), and payroll, administered by the Gambia Revenue Authority (GRA). The tax year is the calendar year and the currency is the Gambian Dalasi (GMD/D). Figures below reflect the 2025 budget reforms and are an unverified draft pending review by a licensed Gambian accountant.

- **Tax year** — Calendar year (1 January to 31 December); a taxpayer may apply to the Commissioner General for a special tax year  _(Gambia Revenue Authority, Personal Income Tax brochure — https://www.gra.gm/download-file/8d0cd78a-d217-11ed-9b31-029254d29bb1 ; Gambia Revenue Authority, Corporation Income Tax brochure — https://www.gra.gm/download-file/8d0c7954-d217-11ed-9b31-029254d29bb1)_
- **Currency** — Gambian Dalasi (GMD / D)  _(Income and Value Added Tax Act)_
- **Tax authority** — Gambia Revenue Authority (GRA)  _([Gambia Revenue Authority Act](https://www.gra.gm/))_
- **Basis of taxation** — Residents are taxed on worldwide income; non-residents on Gambian-source income only (approx — confirm)  _(Income and Value Added Tax Act)_
- **Individual residence test** — An individual is resident if they reside in The Gambia for at least 183 days during the tax year; an entity incorporated, managed or controlled in The Gambia is resident  _(Gambia Revenue Authority, Withholding Tax on Contract Payments brochure — https://www.gra.gm/download-file/8d0d0925-d217-11ed-9b31-029254d29bb1)_
- **Top personal income tax rate** — 25% percent on annual employment income above D76,000, after bands of 0% to D36,000, 5% to D46,000, 10% to D56,000, 15% to D66,000 and 20% to D76,000 (the GRA's rate table in force since January 2018, reflected in its 2025 income tax table)  _(Gambia Revenue Authority, Employment Income Tax (PAYE) brochure — https://www.gra.gm/download-file/8d0ccc58-d217-11ed-9b31-029254d29bb1 ; Gambia Revenue Authority, Income Tax Table 2025 (monthly and half-monthly paid employees) — https://www.gra.gm/download-file/2a69ec11-c633-11ef-b086-029254d29bb1)_
- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 27%  _([Income and Value Added Tax Act](https://www.gra.gm/domestic-faqs))_
- **Does VAT exist** — Yes — VAT applies at a standard rate of 15%  _(Gambia Revenue Authority, Value Added Tax brochure — https://www.gra.gm/download-file/8d0cfa28-d217-11ed-9b31-029254d29bb1)_
- **Corporation tax return deadline** — 31 March of the year following the tax year  _(Income and Value Added Tax Act — https://www.gra.gm/domestic-faqs)_
- **Monthly schedule (PAYE, VAT, withholding) deadline** — Within 15 days after the end of the month  _([Income and Value Added Tax Act](https://www.gra.gm/domestic-faqs))_
