# Angola Tax Overview

## Angola Tax Overview

## Angola tax system at a glance

- **Tax system structure** — Angola taxes through a set of separate schedular codes (personal income, corporate, investment income, VAT) administered by the AGT. The tax year is the calendar year and the currency is the Angolan kwanza (AOA).
- **Standard tax year** — Calendar year (1 January to 31 December): the Imposto Industrial is due for each financial year, which coincides with the civil year, with shorter periods on commencement and cessation  _(Código do Imposto Industrial (Lei n.º 19/14 as amended by Lei n.º 26/20), art. 77 — https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2014/lei-n-o-19-14-de-22-de-outubro/)_
- **National currency** — Angolan kwanza (AOA)  _(Banco Nacional de Angola)_
- **National tax authority** — Administracao Geral Tributaria (AGT)  _(Administracao Geral Tributaria (AGT))_
- **Corporate tax basis** — Companies and other entities with their seat or effective management in Angola are taxable persons on their profits; entities without seat or effective management in Angola are taxed on Angolan-source profits, with a 15% final tax on accidental services they render  _(Código do Imposto Industrial (Lei n.º 19/14 as amended by Lei n.º 26/20), arts. 5, 71 and 73 — https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2020/lei-n-o-26-20-de-20-de-julho/; AGT, Impostos — https://agt.minfin.gov.ao/PortalAGT/#!/servicos-fiscais//impostos)_
- **Top personal income tax (IRT) rate** — 25% on Group A income above AOA 10,000,000 (12.º escalão of the table annexed to the IRT code as replaced for 2025 by Lei n.º 18/24, Anexo I)  _(Código do Imposto sobre os Rendimentos do Trabalho (Lei n.º 18/14 as amended by Lei n.º 28/20), art. 16 — https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2020/lei-n-o-28-20-de-22-de-julho/; Lei n.º 18/24, Anexo I — https://www.ucm.minfin.gov.ao/cs/groups/public/documents/document/aw41/mdm1/~edisp/minfin5035043.pdf)_
- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 25% (10% for agriculture, aquaculture, beekeeping, poultry, livestock, fishing and forestry other than timber; 35% for banking, insurance, telecommunications operators and Angolan oil companies)  _(Código do Imposto Industrial (Lei n.º 19/14 as amended by Lei n.º 26/20), art. 64 — https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2020/lei-n-o-26-20-de-20-de-julho/)_
- **Does Angola have VAT** — Yes — VAT (Imposto sobre o Valor Acrescentado, IVA) applies at a standard 14%  _(Código do IVA (Lei n.º 7/19 as amended and republished by Lei n.º 14/23), art. 19 — https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2023/lei-n-o-14-23-de-28-de-dezembro/)_
- **Standard VAT rate** — 14%  _(Código do IVA (Lei n.º 7/19 as amended and republished by Lei n.º 14/23), art. 19 n.º 1 a) — https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2023/lei-n-o-14-23-de-28-de-dezembro/)_
- **Corporate income tax annual return deadline (general regime)** — Last business day of May of the following year (Declaração Modelo 1); last business day of April for the simplified regime  _(Código do Imposto Industrial (Lei n.º 19/14 as amended by Lei n.º 26/20), arts. 51, 61, 69 and 70 — https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2020/lei-n-o-26-20-de-20-de-julho/)_
- **VAT return frequency** — Monthly: the periodic declaration is submitted electronically by the last day of the month following the month it covers  _(Código do IVA (Lei n.º 7/19 as amended and republished by Lei n.º 14/23), art. 44 — https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2019/lei-n-o-7-19-de-24-de-abril/)_
- **Personal income tax collection method** — PAYE — the paying entity assesses Group A tax monthly and remits it by the end of the month following payment; the employer files the annual Modelo 2 in February and employees with only Group A income file no return of their own  _(Código do Imposto sobre os Rendimentos do Trabalho (Lei n.º 18/14 as amended by Lei n.º 28/20), arts. 10, 11 and 12 — https://lex.ao/docs/assembleia-nacional/2014/lei-n-o-18-14-de-22-de-outubro/)_
