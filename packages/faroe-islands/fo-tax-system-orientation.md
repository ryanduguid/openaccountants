# Faroe Islands Tax System Orientation

The Faroe Islands have their own tax legislation. TAKS administers national, municipal and church income taxes and Faroese VAT. Establish whether the taxpayer has full or limited liability before choosing the return and applicable rate. [Income Tax Act, ss 1–2 and 54–55][ACT] · [TAKS, general income tax guidance][GUIDE]

## Residence and source

Full liability generally applies to individuals resident in the Faroe Islands or present for at least 180 days in a 12-month period, including interrupted stays within the test. Fully liable taxpayers generally report worldwide income. Limited liability applies to the listed Faroese sources under section 2, which include covered employment, real estate and permanent establishments. Check seafarer, special categories and the applicable treaty before deciding cross-border treatment. [Income Tax Act, ss 1–2][ACT] · [TAKS, general income tax guidance][GUIDE]

## Taxpayer type

Registered limited liability companies and other listed entities can have full liability in their own right under section 1. An individual behind a sole proprietorship or ordinary partnership generally reports their own business income. Payroll is generally A-income subject to withholding, with separate B-income assessment outside withholding. The applicable company rate depends on legal form and statutory conditions. [Income Tax Act, ss 1–2 and 54–60][ACT] · [TAKS, business forms and tax returns][RETURNS]

## Currency and assessment periods

Faroese króna has a 1:1 value relationship with Danish kroner (DKK). The ordinary personal income cycle follows the calendar year. TAKS lists 1 May for ordinary individual and self-employed returns and 1 July for limited company returns. VAT is ordinarily reconciled each quarter. TAKS publishes specific due dates, including a separate August date for the second quarter. Check extensions and special periods. [Danmarks Nationalbank, Faroese banknotes][CURRENCY] · [Income Tax Act, s 58][ACT] · [TAKS, return timetable][RETURNS] · [TAKS, quarterly VAT dates][VATDATES]

## Headline taxes

- **Personal income tax.** The 2026 national scale is progressive, with a zero band and marginal rates from 13% to 30%. Municipal income tax is separate and the municipal and church rates depend on the relevant location and circumstances. TAKS describes a separate 42% rate for the ordinary limited-liability employment branch, subject to the applicable conditions. [TAKS, 2026 national and municipal tables][NATIONAL] · [TAKS, general income tax guidance][GUIDE]
- **Company income tax.** Section 5(1) of the Tax Assessment Act sets 49% for the entities in Income Tax Act section 1 numbers 8–16 and for the companies and other entities within section 2 that have limited tax liability. Section 5(2) sets 18% for its listed share companies, private limited companies, limited partnerships, savings banks and cooperatives with at least DKK 50,000 of qualifying capital. It also separately names entrepreneur companies under chapter 19a. Section 5(3) sets 18% for the businesses, insurers and institutions it specifies. Check the entity, capital and tax status, including any separate hydrocarbon rules. [Tax Assessment Act, s 5(1)–(4)][ASSESS]
- **VAT.** The usual Faroese VAT rate is 25% on taxable sales and imports. Check exemption, registration and import provisions for the transaction. Use TAKS for the applicable return and payment timetable. [VAT Act, chs I–II, VI and X][VAT] · [TAKS, starting a business][BUSINESS] · [TAKS, quarterly VAT dates][VATDATES]
