# Faroe Islands: national income tax table reference v0.1

> **General reference only.** This source-cited Tier 2 conditional draft illustrates one raw national-table computation. It does not complete national tax, combined income tax, PAYE or an income tax engagement. Guide presence, source preparation and passing component checks do not establish comprehensive jurisdiction acceptance.

## Scope and source conditions

Load workflow-base and income-tax-workflow-base in full. Preserve all 4 universal outputs and 18 checks, all 5 income tax outputs and 12 checks, all mandatory country slots and every shared refusal. Missing applicable law, facts, tools, outputs or qualified review prevents delivery of an actual financial result.

The fictional branch stipulates a full-year individual for calendar income year 2026, with ordinary national-table applicability and taxable table income G independently established. G is after all required income-base adjustments. Residence, liability class, income classification, period and base construction are hypothetical premises, not defaults. A person's address or profile confirmation does not establish legal residence or source applicability.

This reference starts with G, not gross salary or receipts. The first 65,000 kr. lies in the published zero-rate band; do not subtract another 65,000 from an already established table input. Municipal allowances, municipal rounding, church tax and contributions are separate matters. No national reduction, credit, coordination or payment result is determined here.

The numerical authority is the complete dated TAKS 2026 national table, L1. A printed boundary discrepancy remains in the consolidated rate law and original amending Act: limb 4 prints `450.0000`, whilst the next limb and L1 use 450,000. The original actually contains the extra zero; it is not an extraction failure. This draft does not silently correct legislation or settle its legal effect. Hold actual applicability until the discrepancy and complete operative law are reconciled by qualified review.

## Intake and component mechanics

Reuse redacted records before grouped questions. Establish the income year and obligation, operative source chain, liability class, residence and period, household and income classification, income-base adjustments and G, reduction entitlements, municipality and church position, combined-ceiling inputs, currency and lawful precision, withholding/prepayments, professional reviewer and required tools. Present the inferred profile and wait for confirmation. Keep identifiers, bank details and signatures with the professional. Missing law requires primary evidence, not a taxpayer's guess.

1. **Establish the branch and input.** Accept G only as independently established applicable ordinary annual taxable table income for 2026. L4 sections 23-24 distinguish annual personal income and subsequent assessment and require a separate annualisation branch for shorter periods. Do not relabel 2025 income as 2026 income or derive G from gross salary without the complete applicable base rules.
2. **Validate the fictional input.** These fixtures use a non-negative finite base-10 whole-kr amount. Preserve it exactly. Negative, missing, malformed, ambiguous, non-finite, mixed-currency and fractional inputs require reconciliation under this fixture contract. Whole-kr fixtures are not a proven statutory precision rule.
3. **Select one L1 row.** Equality at every positive cutoff belongs to the next row: G is at least lower bound L and strictly below any upper bound. The first row covers stipulated non-negative G below 65,000; the last has no upper limit. This is L1 table selection, not a legal correction of L2/L3. _(L1, complete 2026 table)_
4. **Calculate the raw amount.** Select cumulative B and marginal r. Set E = G - L and U = B + r x E. Apply r only to E and add B unchanged. Do not apply r to all G, multiply B by r, subtract the zero band twice or add a municipal allowance. Preserve the exact result. _(L1, complete 2026 table)_
5. **Preserve precision and coordination holds.** U remains unrounded; no national rounding rule is established here. L4 section 47's municipal taxable-base rounding down to a multiple of 100 cannot be transferred to national tax. L2 sections 2-3 reductions and L4 section 54(2) combined national, municipal and church ceiling require separate established inputs and ordering. Unknown adjustments are not zero.
6. **Reconcile without delivering liability.** Check U = B + r x (G - L), non-negative E/U and continuity at each cutoff. U is before reductions, ceiling coordination and legal precision. It is not final national tax, combined tax, PAYE, a balance due, refund or instruction to file/pay.
