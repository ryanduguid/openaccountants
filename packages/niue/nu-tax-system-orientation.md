# Niue tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Establish the taxpayer, activity, payment type, income source and relevant period. The Income Tax Act 1961 and Niue Consumption Tax Act 2009 set out the principal taxes. The Tax Administration Act 2025 applies from 1 July 2026. The 2025 income tax and consumption tax amendments link their application to that date. Check the transition for earlier liabilities and proceedings. [Tax Administration Act 2025, ss 2 and 108 and Schedule 3][ADMIN] · [Income Tax Amendment Act 2025, s 2][AMENDMENT] · [Gazette No. 3 of 2025, pp 16–17][GAZETTE]

## Residence and income source

Under s 2 of the Income Tax Act, the individual tests address domicile in Niue during the year, presence for an aggregate of 183 days in a 12-month period beginning or ending during the year, and Niue government employees posted abroad. Check the first-year and last-year provisions before assigning the residence period. A company falls within the residence definition through incorporation, creation or formation in Niue, or a centre of administrative management there, as specified for the year. The same section gives separate tests for partnerships and trusts. [Income Tax Act 1961, s 2][INCOME]

Subject to the Act, income derived whilst resident is assessable whether earned in Niue or abroad. Niue-derived income is assessable regardless of residence. Section 84 exempts a resident’s foreign-source income to the extent the Financial Secretary is satisfied that the income arises outside Niue and is chargeable there to income tax of substantially the same nature. Apply the statutory source and apportionment provisions to business activity, employment and other receipts with more than one territorial connection. [Income Tax Act, ss 80 and 82–84][INCOME]

## Income tax, PAYE and withholding

Schedule 1 gives a 30% company basic rate and individual marginal bands of 10%, 20% and 30% before applicable adjustments. Check PAYE separately for employee earnings. From 1 July 2026 the amended s 113R includes 10% withholding on qualifying gross service fees paid to a non-resident supplier by a resident or a non-resident with a fixed place of business in Niue, subject to the statutory exceptions. Check whether the supplier has a fixed place of business in Niue before treating the withholding as final. [Income Tax Act, s 39 and Schedule 1][INCOME] · [Income Tax Amendment Act, ss 2 and 12][AMENDMENT] · [Niue Tax Office][TAX-OFFICE]

## Niue consumption tax

The NCT Act establishes separate rules for taxable supplies and imports. The Gazette identifies Act 381 as a consequential amendment linked to the 2025 administration law. The online legislation index's download entry for Act 381 leads to Act 380. Obtain the operative NCT text, regulations and current notice before determining a current rate, registration threshold, deadline or conversion method. The Tax Administration Act lists an NCT return as a self-assessment return. [NCT Act 2009, 2019 reprint, ss 4–9 and 68][NCT] · [Gazette No. 3 of 2025, p 17][GAZETTE] · [Government legislation index][INDEX] · [Tax Administration Act, s 24 and Schedule 2][ADMIN]

## Currency, periods and authority

The standard income year runs from 1 April to 31 March. Section 6 permits a different annual balance date with the Financial Secretary's consent. The NCT reprint expresses amounts in New Zealand dollars (NZD). Confirm the applicable current conversion rule for each tax and period. [Income Tax Act, ss 2 and 6][INCOME] · [NCT Act, 2019 reprint, s 68][NCT]

The Financial Secretary administers the tax laws. Use approved forms and confirm deadlines with the Niue Tax Office and applicable notices. The income tax amendment removes the old s 5(2)–(7) provisions, including the small-business return threshold. Section 5(1) retains annual returns by taxpayers. Check the transition before applying either version to an earlier return. The Treasury website lists form-specific dates and payment dates. [Income Tax Act, ss 5 and 12][INCOME] · [Income Tax Amendment Act, s 6][AMENDMENT] · [Tax Administration Act, ss 6, 20 and 108][ADMIN] · [Treasury filing dates][TREASURY]

## Fictional intake

A Niue company pays a foreign consultant after 1 July 2026 and imports equipment. Establish company residence and the income year. Classify the consultant's payment under the amended withholding rules. Review the equipment separately under the current customs and consumption tax rules before processing the import.
