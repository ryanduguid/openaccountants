# Anguilla tax system orientation

The Inland Revenue Department administers domestic taxes and the Universal Social Levy. Customs administers duties and Goods Tax on goods at import. The Social Security Board administers separate contribution obligations. Anguilla uses the Eastern Caribbean dollar (XCD). Some statutory investment programme amounts are stated in US dollars. [Inland Revenue, taxes and laws][IRD] · [Goods Tax Act 2025][GOODS] · [Government, Anguilla facts][CURRENCY]

## Ordinary income and residence

The Government describes an ordinary system without personal, corporate or partnership income taxes. Check separate sector, remuneration, property and transaction charges. The 2026 estimates record Bank Deposit Levy, ANGLEC and petroleum revenue under corporate revenue classifications. Check each operative charging provision before applying it to an entity. [Government submission to Parliament, tax system][PARLIAMENT] · [Select Anguilla, business and tax][BUSINESS] · [2026 revenue estimates, p 56][BUDGET]

Ordinary income-tax scope does not determine immigration permission, residence in another jurisdiction or programme eligibility. The Economic Residence Act sets out a separate tax residence programme and annual certification requirements. The regulations' schedule sets a US$75,000 annual lump sum for the tax residence option. Act 18 of 2024 commenced on assent and amended the dependant definition and real-estate application and approval provisions. Check later amendments, conditions and current authorisation before relying on a certificate. [Economic Residence Act, ss 4–5 and 11][RESIDENCE] · [Economic Residence Regulations, Schedule][RESIDENCE-REGS] · [Act 18 of 2024, ss 2–4][RESIDENCE-2024] · [Select Anguilla, tax residence][PROGRAMME]

## Services and goods

The General Services Tax Act took effect on 1 August 2025. Its ordinary enacted rate is 13% on taxable local services, subject to zero-rating, exemptions and special rules. The ordinary actual and expected turnover tests are XCD 300,000 over the prescribed periods. Imported services can create a separate reporting and payment obligation. The ordinary calendar-month return and payment are generally due within 20 days after period end. Imports of goods follow the separate Goods Tax Act and its tariff classification. Confirm the operative amendment and tariff period before a transaction. [General Services Tax Act 2025, ss 8, 10, 21–22, 30–31, 43 and 107][SERVICES] · [Inland Revenue, General Services Tax][SERVICES-IRD] · [Goods Tax Act 2025][GOODS]

## Payroll and filing periods

The published 2022 revision and Inland Revenue guidance set the ordinary employee and employer Universal Social Levy rates at 3%, subject to the statutory remuneration exemption and employer cap. Inland Revenue gives the ordinary monthly return and payment deadline as 10 days after month end. Check later enactments, exemptions, multiple-employer, pay-period and self-employed rules before calculation. For 2026 the Social Security Board publishes 5.75% each for employer and employee, with the next scheduled rate change from 1 January 2027. Determine the specific return period for each obligation. [Universal Social Levy Act, ss 4, 6 and 8][USL] · [Inland Revenue, levy guidance][USL-IRD] · [Social Security Board, rate notice][SSB]
