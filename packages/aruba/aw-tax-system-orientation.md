# Aruba Tax System Orientation

Aruba administers its own income, profit and turnover taxes. Establish the taxpayer, the source and the taxable period before selecting the applicable return. [Departamento di Impuesto, business tax guidance][AUTH]

## Residence

An individual's tax residence is determined by the circumstances. Article 1 gives separate rules for temporary absences, departure followed by a return within a year and some temporary stays by residents of other countries in the Kingdom. A body is established in Aruba according to the facts. A legal form governed by Aruban law is deemed established there for profit tax. [Income Tax Ordinance, art 1][IB] · [Profit Tax Ordinance, art 1][WB]

## Income and territorial scope

Aruban residents generally account for worldwide income. Non-residents account for the Aruban income sources listed in article 17. The profit tax can apply to non-resident bodies with an Aruban permanent establishment, local real estate or related rights, or specified mortgage-secured receivables. Check the corresponding relief and source rules for the income or business. [Income Tax Ordinance, arts 3 and 17][IB] · [Departamento di Impuesto, 2025 return guidance][RETURN] · [Profit Tax Ordinance, art 1][WB]

## Taxpayer type

Identify whether the business activity belongs to an individual or to an entity. A sole trader generally includes business income in the individual return. An in-scope company, partnership or other body files under profit tax. An employer withholds wage tax under a separate regime. The BBO, BAVP and BAZV business turnover charges can apply regardless of legal form. Check the separate small sole-trader and border rules. [Profit Tax Ordinance, arts 1–2][WB] · [Departamento di Impuesto, business tax guidance][AUTH] · [Small business registration guidance][SMALL] · [BBO at the border][BORDER]

## Currency

Use the Aruban florin (Afl., currency code AWG). The Centrale Bank van Aruba states the USD peg is Afl. 1.79 per US dollar. Check the applicable tax conversion rule for the receipt or cost. [Centrale Bank van Aruba, the florin][CBA] · [Centrale Bank van Aruba, currency code][CODES]

## Assessment periods

Individual tax is ordinarily assessed for the calendar year. Use the filing deadline for the relevant return and residence period. Profit tax uses the accounting year, or the calendar year when there are no regular annual accounts. The tax authority's ordinary business timetable gives 5 months after the close to file and pay. BBO, BAVP and BAZV returns are usually monthly. Check extensions, earlier periods and irregular closes separately. [Income Tax Ordinance, art 12][IB] · [Departamento di Impuesto, individual return guidance][RETURN] · [Profit Tax Ordinance, arts 3 and 25][WB] · [Departamento di Impuesto, business tax guidance][AUTH]

## Tax authority and headline rates

The Departamento di Impuesto administers these domestic taxes. The department publishes a 2026 table with marginal rates of 0%, 21%, 42% and 52% on table income, which is taxable income after the applicable tax-free amount. Confirm the legislative rate for the case period before using the table in a calculation. The ordinary profit tax in article 15 is 22%. The standard BBO, BAVP and BAZV rates from January 2023 are 2.5%, 1.5% and 3% respectively. Check special profit regimes, turnover exemptions and any border deduction before calculating. [2026 income tax table][TABLE] · [Profit Tax Ordinance, art 15][WB] · [Turnover rate timeline][BBO]
