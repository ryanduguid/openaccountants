# Jamaica Tax Overview

## 2026 tax year

Tax Administration Jamaica (TAJ) administers income tax and General Consumption Tax (GCT). The ordinary year of assessment is the calendar year. The Commissioner can accept a different accounting date for measuring business profit. [Income Tax Act, section 6](https://laws.moj.gov.jm/legislation/statutes/I/The%20Income%20Tax%20Act_0.pdf)

### Personal income tax

Section 5 generally brings the worldwide income of Jamaican residents into charge and taxes specified Jamaican income of non-residents, subject to statutory and treaty rules. Determine residence and the source and type of income before applying exemptions or withholding. [Income Tax Act, section 5](https://laws.moj.gov.jm/legislation/statutes/I/The%20Income%20Tax%20Act_0.pdf) · [TAJ non-resident guidance](https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/documents/10181/123967/non-residents-and-income-tax-final-document.pdf/7bfdd5c6-eb64-926e-e5c3-ea296e5f4831)

The 25% and 30% marginal rates come from section 30 of the Income Tax Act. [Income Tax (Amendment) Act 2025, section 4](https://lipj.gov.jm/cms/uploads/No_3_The_Income_Tax_Amendment_Act_2025_bdbb20a1f2.pdf)

| Ordinary resident individual statutory income band, 2026 | Marginal rate |
| --- | ---: |
| First JMD 1,876,614 | 0% |
| Over JMD 1,876,614 through JMD 6,000,000 | 25% |
| Over JMD 6,000,000 | 30% |

The 2026 annual tax-free amount blends 3 months at the JMD 1,799,376 annual rate with 9 months at the JMD 1,902,360 rate in force from 1 April 2026. For example, JMD 2,500,000 of income subject to these bands before the threshold, with no other adjustments, gives income tax of JMD 155,846.50. [TAJ April 2026 threshold notice](https://jis.gov.jm/increase-in-income-tax-threshold-now-in-effect/)

### Corporate income tax

The ordinary rate is 25% for unregulated companies and 33 1/3% for regulated companies. Life assurance companies have separate rules, and building societies have a 30% rate. The 2025 Act also provides 25% treatment for qualifying independent power producers and trust and corporate service providers. Check the company category before applying a rate. [Fiscal Incentives (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2013, Schedule](https://www.japarliament.gov.jm/attachments/341_Fical%20Incentives%20%28Miscellaneous%20Provisions%29%20Act%2C2013.pdf) · [Income Tax (Amendment) Act 2025, section 4](https://lipj.gov.jm/cms/uploads/No_3_The_Income_Tax_Amendment_Act_2025_bdbb20a1f2.pdf) · [TAJ income tax FAQ](https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/income-tax-faq)

### Returns and payments

- From the 2025 year of assessment, an individual who must file ordinarily submits the final income tax return and pays any balance by 15 March of the following year, subject to the applicable calendar. Use the TAJ instructions for the applicable return, including S04 for ordinary self-employed filers. [Income Tax (Amendment) Act 2026, section 4](https://lipj.gov.jm/cms/uploads/No_4_The_Income_Tax_Amendment_Act_2026_b91cadadc0.pdf) · [TAJ filing dates](https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/web/guest/home/-/blogs/government-introduces-separate-filing-dates-for-corporate-and-individual-incometaxreturns-effective-year-of-assessment-2025)
- From the 2025 year of assessment, a body corporate that must file submits its final return and pays any balance by 15 April of the following year. Ordinary companies file form IT02. The Commissioner General can extend the filing deadline in writing on application. Where a form is used by more than one category of taxpayer, check the deadline for the entity with TAJ. [Income Tax (Amendment) Act 2026, section 4](https://lipj.gov.jm/cms/uploads/No_4_The_Income_Tax_Amendment_Act_2026_b91cadadc0.pdf) · [TAJ April 2026 filing reminder](https://taj-astro.vercel.app/news/just-two-days-remaining-to-file-corporate-income-tax-and-assets-tax-returns-deadline-april-15)
- Where estimated returns and tax instalments apply, the estimated return (S04A for self-employed individuals or IT07 for companies) is ordinarily due by 15 March of the assessment year. Ordinary instalments are due on 15 March, 15 June, 15 September and 15 December. Check the start-up rule in section 65 for a new source. [Income Tax Act, sections 65 and 66](https://laws.moj.gov.jm/legislation/statutes/I/The%20Income%20Tax%20Act_0.pdf) · [TAJ filing dates](https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/web/guest/home/-/blogs/government-introduces-separate-filing-dates-for-corporate-and-individual-incometaxreturns-effective-year-of-assessment-2025)

### General Consumption Tax

The standard GCT rate is 15%. Since 1 April 2025, a person carrying on a taxable activity must apply for registration when gross supplies reach JMD 15,000,000 over 12 months or average at least JMD 1,250,000 a month over a shorter period. For a mixed activity, TAJ includes exempt supplies when testing gross supplies. The ordinary registered taxpayer files the monthly return and pays the balance by the last working day of the following month. [General Consumption Tax (Amendment of Rate) Order 2020](https://japarliament.gov.jm/attachments/article/339/The%20General%20Consumption%20Tax%20%28Amendment%20of%20Rate%29%20Order%2C%202020.pdf) · [General Consumption Tax (Amendment) Act 2020, section 3](https://www.japarliament.gov.jm/attachments/article/339/The%20General%20Consumption%20Tax%20%28Amendment%29%20Act%2C%202020%20No.%202.pdf) · [TAJ GCT registration guidance](https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/general-consumption-tax1) · [TAJ monthly GCT deadline](https://jis.gov.jm/new-filing-due-date-monthly-gct-returns-now-effect/)

For example, a standard-rated JMD 100,000 sale before GCT has JMD 15,000 of GCT and an invoice total of JMD 115,000. Apply the supply classification before using the standard rate. [General Consumption Tax (Amendment of Rate) Order 2020](https://japarliament.gov.jm/attachments/article/339/The%20General%20Consumption%20Tax%20%28Amendment%20of%20Rate%29%20Order%2C%202020.pdf)
