# Jamaica Payroll & Social Contributions

## Payroll statutory deductions and social security

Jamaican employers deduct PAYE income tax plus several statutory payroll contributions (NIS, NHT, Education Tax) and pay an employer-only HEART/NSTA Trust levy. Rates below are 2025 and should be confirmed by a licensed accountant.

- **NIS — employee contribution** — 3% of gross emoluments up to the JMD 5,000,000 ceiling  _(Tax Administration Jamaica, Payroll Taxes and Statutory Contributions (Employee / Employer), April 2025 edition — https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/documents/10194/53547865/Payroll+Taxes+and+Statutory+Contributions+%28Employee+Employer%29.pdf/bef9095b-fb2d-db4a-5de4-b19405bd8631)_
- **NIS — employer contribution** — 3% of gross emoluments up to the JMD 5,000,000 ceiling  _(Tax Administration Jamaica, Payroll Taxes and Statutory Contributions (Employee / Employer), April 2025 edition — https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/documents/10194/53547865/Payroll+Taxes+and+Statutory+Contributions+%28Employee+Employer%29.pdf/bef9095b-fb2d-db4a-5de4-b19405bd8631)_
- **NIS wage ceiling** — JMD 5,000,000 of gross emoluments per annum (each side, 3%)  _(Tax Administration Jamaica, Payroll Taxes and Statutory Contributions (Employee / Employer), April 2025 edition — https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/documents/10194/53547865/Payroll+Taxes+and+Statutory+Contributions+%28Employee+Employer%29.pdf/bef9095b-fb2d-db4a-5de4-b19405bd8631)_
- **NHT — employee contribution** — 2% of gross emoluments  _(Tax Administration Jamaica, Payroll Taxes and Statutory Contributions (Employee / Employer), April 2025 edition — https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/documents/10194/53547865/Payroll+Taxes+and+Statutory+Contributions+%28Employee+Employer%29.pdf/bef9095b-fb2d-db4a-5de4-b19405bd8631)_
- **NHT — employer contribution** — 3% of gross emoluments  _(Tax Administration Jamaica, Payroll Taxes and Statutory Contributions (Employee / Employer), April 2025 edition — https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/documents/10194/53547865/Payroll+Taxes+and+Statutory+Contributions+%28Employee+Employer%29.pdf/bef9095b-fb2d-db4a-5de4-b19405bd8631)_
- **Education Tax — employee** — 2.25% of statutory income (gross emoluments after NIS and approved superannuation)  _(Tax Administration Jamaica, Payroll Taxes and Statutory Contributions (Employee / Employer), April 2025 edition — https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/documents/10194/53547865/Payroll+Taxes+and+Statutory+Contributions+%28Employee+Employer%29.pdf/bef9095b-fb2d-db4a-5de4-b19405bd8631)_
- **Education Tax — employer** — 3.5% of statutory income (gross emoluments after NIS and approved superannuation)  _(Tax Administration Jamaica, Payroll Taxes and Statutory Contributions (Employee / Employer), April 2025 edition — https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/documents/10194/53547865/Payroll+Taxes+and+Statutory+Contributions+%28Employee+Employer%29.pdf/bef9095b-fb2d-db4a-5de4-b19405bd8631)_
- **HEART/NSTA Trust — employer** — 3% of gross emoluments, employer only, where the employer's payroll costs exceed JMD 173,328 per annum; no employee contribution  _(Tax Administration Jamaica, Payroll Taxes and Statutory Contributions (Employee / Employer), April 2025 edition — https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/documents/10194/53547865/Payroll+Taxes+and+Statutory+Contributions+%28Employee+Employer%29.pdf/bef9095b-fb2d-db4a-5de4-b19405bd8631)_
- **PAYE income tax withholding** — 0% up to the threshold, 25% on statutory income above it up to JMD 6,000,000 and 30% above JMD 6,000,000; the threshold is JMD 1,799,376 from 1 April 2025 (effective JMD 1,774,554 for 2025) and JMD 1,902,360 from 1 April 2026  _(Tax Administration Jamaica, Payroll Taxes and Statutory Contributions (Employee / Employer), April 2025 edition — https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/documents/10194/53547865/Payroll+Taxes+and+Statutory+Contributions+%28Employee+Employer%29.pdf/bef9095b-fb2d-db4a-5de4-b19405bd8631 ; Tax Administration Jamaica, Technical Advisory 042025/01/IT-TA, Increase in Income Tax Threshold effective April 1, 2025 to December 31, 2028 — https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/documents/10194/52231399/Technical_Advisory_Threshold+_042025.pdf/c4adbf81-8ddd-9a1b-9e8b-8bdc431162df)_
- **Statutory payroll deductions remittance deadline** — Within 14 days after the end of the month of deduction, on the combined S01 monthly remittance covering ITIP, NIS, NHT, Education Tax and HEART (January deductions by 14 February)  _(Tax Administration Jamaica, Employer's Guide (December 2019) — https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/documents/10194/14559577/Employers_guide_13122019.pdf/9d09b82e-e215-6aaa-72c8-7b8a3dd7f061)_
- **Annual employer payroll return** — Form S02 employer's annual return, filed online no later than 31 March of the following year; a late return attracts JMD 5,000 per month or part month  _(Tax Administration Jamaica, Employer's Guide (December 2019) — https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/documents/10194/14559577/Employers_guide_13122019.pdf/9d09b82e-e215-6aaa-72c8-7b8a3dd7f061)_
