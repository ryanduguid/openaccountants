# European Netherlands tax-system orientation

Load `workflow-base`. Record where the person lives or the entity is established, the type and source of each amount, the taxable period and any treaty. This reference covers the European Netherlands. Check the separate tax administration and laws for Bonaire, Sint Eustatius and Saba. [Caribbean Netherlands Tax Administration](https://english.belastingdienst-cn.nl/topics/tax-individuals/income-tax)

## Territorial reach

Resident individuals report worldwide income, subject to the applicable relief rules. Chapter 7 of the Income Tax Act limits the ordinary non-resident charge to Dutch income as defined there. Resident companies generally start from their worldwide profits, with adjustments including the applicable exemption for foreign business profits. Non-resident entities fall within the corporate charge where their form and Dutch-source income meet the statutory rules, including the relevant business presence rules. Apply any treaty separately. [Income Tax Act 2001, arts 2.1 and 2.4 and ch 7](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-02-21#Hoofdstuk2_Afdeling2.1_Artikel2.1) · [Corporate Income Tax Act 1969, arts 2, 3, 7, 15e and 17](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01#HoofdstukI_Artikel2) · [Belastingdienst, foreign income](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/en/individuals/content/deductions-when-living-in-the-netherlands-with-income-from-abroad)

## Residence

Domestic residence of an individual and establishment of an entity depend on the circumstances. Check applicable statutory residence or establishment fictions and treaty residence separately. [General Tax Act, art 4](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002320/2026-01-01#HoofdstukI_Artikel4) · [Income Tax Act 2001, art 2.2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-02-21#Hoofdstuk2_Afdeling2.1_Artikel2.2) · [Corporate Income Tax Act 1969, art 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01#HoofdstukI_Artikel2)

## Taxpayer types

A sole trader who qualifies as an entrepreneur includes business profit in the work-and-home category (Box 1). For an ordinary general partnership (vof), assess each partner's status and share separately. A Dutch limited partnership (cv) is generally transparent from 1 January 2025, subject to separate grounds of corporate liability such as reverse hybrid status. A Dutch BV or NV generally pays its own corporate tax. For a trust or similar arrangement, test article 2.14a separately. A qualifying separated private fund (APV) generally attributes the assets, debts, receipts and outgoings to the person who transferred them or, after death, to the heirs, subject to statutory exceptions. [Income Tax Act 2001, arts 2.14a and 3.2–3.4](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-02-21#Hoofdstuk2_Afdeling2.4_Artikel2.14a) · [Corporate Income Tax Act 1969, art 2](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01#HoofdstukI_Artikel2) · [Belastingdienst, vof](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/rechtsvorm/vennootschap-onder-firma-vof) · [Belastingdienst, cv](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/ondernemen/onderneming_starten/rechtsvorm/commanditaire-vennootschap-cv)

## Currency

The European Netherlands uses the euro (EUR). Dutch corporate tax returns generally use euros. Check whether the tax administration has approved a functional-currency request and its effective period. [Council Regulation (EC) No 974/98, art 2 and Annex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/1998/974/2026-01-01/eng) · [Belastingdienst, functional currency](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/vennootschapsbelasting/functionele_valuta/functionele_valuta)

## Assessment periods

Individual income tax uses the calendar year for its 3 categories: work and home, substantial interest and savings and investments. Corporate tax generally uses the financial year, or the calendar year where there are no regular annual accounting closings. Check short periods and fiscal-unity changes separately. [Income Tax Act 2001, art 2.3](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0011353/2026-02-21#Hoofdstuk2_Afdeling2.2_Artikel2.3) · [Corporate Income Tax Act 1969, art 7](https://wetten.overheid.nl/BWBR0002672/2026-01-01#HoofdstukII_Afdeling2.1_Artikel7)

## Responsible authorities

Belastingdienst administers ordinary Dutch national income and corporate taxes. The Dutch Customs Administration (Douane) handles customs controls and import duties. Confirm the competent authority separately for local charges or a Caribbean Netherlands obligation. [Ministry of Finance, services and institutions](https://www.government.nl/ministries/ministry-of-finance/services-and-institutions) · [Caribbean Netherlands Tax Administration](https://english.belastingdienst-cn.nl/topics/tax-individuals/income-tax)

## Fictional intake

An individual lives in the Netherlands and owns shares of a Dutch BV, receives foreign income and joins a vof. Classify the individual's income and the BV's result separately, identify which country's rules apply to each foreign amount and record each reporting period before calculating tax.
