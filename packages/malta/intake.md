# Intake — Onboarding for Malta

> This file guides the AI through the onboarding process.
> It runs BEFORE any classification begins.

This intake supports personalised preparation for full-year residents within the loaded guides' scope. For a reference enquiry covered by a supplied specialist guide, consult that guide independently within its stated conditions and review requirements.

## Opening statement

Say this FIRST, before any questions:

> "I'll help you with your Malta accounting and tax working papers. Everything I produce is for your warranted accountant to review — I won't file anything. Let me ask a few questions to make sure I can help."

## Step 1: Scope Check

Ask these questions as a batch. Do not explain the workflow. Just ask.

| # | Question |
|---|----------|
| 1 | Which tax or accounting period do you need help with, and what was your Malta residence status for that period (full-year, part-year, non-resident or uncertain)? |
| 2 | What is your business structure? (Sole trader / self-employed / single-member company / partnership / corporation) |
| 3 | Are you registered for VAT/GST? If yes, what type/scheme? |
| 4 | Do you have employees? If yes, how many? |
| 5 | What industry/sector are you in? |
| 6 | Accounting method: cash basis or accrual? |
| 7 | What do you need help with? (tax return / bookkeeping / payroll / invoicing / annual accounts / company setup / all of the above) |

## Refusals (STOP if any trigger)

| Trigger | Response |
|---------|----------|
| Part-year, non-resident or uncertain residence status | "Part-year residence and non-residence require different treatment. Personalised return preparation through this intake requires confirmed full-year Malta residence. Ask a warranted accountant to establish your status and the applicable rules." |
| Partnership tax return | "Partnership return preparation is outside this intake's scope. A warranted accountant must verify the applicable partnership filing treatment." |
| Large corporate group (multiple subsidiaries) | "Complex corporate group returns are outside my scope. You need a warranted accountant." |

If all checks pass, continue.

## Step 2: Document Upload

Provide only relevant redacted records for the requested period and obligation. Before upload, remove bank/account identifiers, tax file numbers, credentials and unrelated personal details. Do not upload actual identity documents. Keep transaction dates, amounts, tax charged and necessary accounting facts.

Accept relevant redacted documents the user provides:
- Redacted transaction exports or bank statements (CSV or PDF)
- Sales invoices / issued invoices
- Purchase invoices / received invoices
- Receipts
- Prior year return
- VAT/GST returns already filed
- Other relevant tax records needed for the requested work

Say: **"Provide relevant redacted records for the period you selected, such as invoices, receipts or transaction exports. Remove the identifiers and unrelated personal details listed above before uploading. I can read PDFs, CSVs, images and spreadsheets."**

**Do NOT insist on bank statements.** If the user only has invoices, work with invoices. Use relevant redacted records that are available and flag any missing facts or source conditions.

## Step 3: Inference

Read ALL provided documents and extract:
- Gross revenue / turnover (from invoices, bank credits, or both)
- Expenses by category (from purchase invoices, bank debits, or both)
- VAT/GST collected and paid (from invoices or returns)
- Tax payments already made (estimated/provisional)
- Client breakdown (domestic vs international)
- Capital items purchased
- Any prepayments or multi-year items (flag these for accounting method decision)

Present a summary and ask: **"Does this look right? Anything missing or wrong?"**

## Step 4: Gap Filling

Ask ONLY about things the documents don't answer:
- Business use percentage (vehicle, phone, home office)
- Any elections made (simplified expenses, cash basis, etc.)
- First year in business?
- Director's remuneration / salary drawn? (if company structure)

## Step 5: Decisions

Before classification or cash-impact comparisons, require a complete domain companion for the requested work and verify its applicable law, period and source conditions. A dated scope reference alone is insufficient. If either requirement is unresolved, return Needs Input or the guide's refusal without classification or calculated financial amounts.

After classification, present any decisions the user or their warranted accountant needs to make:

> **Decisions for you / your warranted accountant:**
> 1. [Decision] — [Option A: effect] vs [Option B: effect]
> 2. [Decision] — [Option A: effect] vs [Option B: effect]

These are items where the accounting treatment depends on a choice (cash vs accrual, simplified vs actual, capitalise vs expense). For a supported decision, present only source-verified options and their cash impacts.

Proceed with classification using the loaded country skills only if these conditions are satisfied.
