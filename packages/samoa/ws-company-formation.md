# Samoa Company Formation & Entity Choice

## Entity types and incorporation

Domestic businesses incorporate under the Companies Act 2001, which is modelled on New Zealand's Companies Act 1993. A separate offshore International Company regime exists under the International Companies Act 1987. The figures below cover domestic local companies.

- **Common entity types** — Local company limited by shares, sole trader, partnership, branch of a foreign company, and (offshore) International Company  _(Companies Act 2001 (https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Companies-Act-2001.pdf))_
- **Incorporating statute (domestic)** — Companies Act 2001  _(Companies Act 2001 (https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Companies-Act-2001.pdf))_
- **Registry** — Companies Office / Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Labour (MCIL) ((confirm registering body))  _(Companies Act 2001)_
- **Minimum share capital** — No statutory minimum share capital for a local company ((approx — some incorporation agents quote a nominal WST 2,000; confirm))  _(Companies Act 2001)_
- **Minimum directors** — At least 1 director; directors need not be Samoa-resident ((approx — confirm))  _(Companies Act 2001)_
- **Minimum shareholders** — At least 1 shareholder ((approx — confirm))  _(Companies Act 2001)_
- **Incorporation steps** — Reserve a unique company name, submit constitution/application with director and shareholder details to the Companies Office, then obtain a business licence and register for tax/VAGST as needed  _(Companies Act 2001)_
- **Typical incorporation timeline** — Around 1–2 weeks for a domestic company ((approx — confirm))  _(Companies Act 2001)_
- **Business licence** — A business licence from the Ministry of Customs and Revenue is generally required to trade, renewed annually ((approx — confirm))  _(Business Licences Act 1998)_
- **Core annual compliance** — Annual return to the Companies Office, annual income tax return (within 3 months of balance date), VAGST and PAYE returns where registered, and business-licence renewal  _(Companies Act 2001 / Tax Administration Act 2012)_
- **Offshore International Company tax rate on offshore profits** — 0% (International Companies under the International Companies Act 1987 are exempt from Samoa income tax on offshore profits but cannot generally carry on domestic business ((offshore regime; confirm restrictions)))  _(International Companies Act 1987 (https://ovza.com/samoas-tax-regime-for-offshore-companies/))_
