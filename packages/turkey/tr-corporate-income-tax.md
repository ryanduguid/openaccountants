# Turkey Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax (2025)

Turkish corporate income tax applies a flat 25% rate (30% for banks and certain financial institutions). From 2025 a domestic minimum corporate tax and Pillar Two top-up rules also apply. Resident companies are taxed on worldwide income.

- **Standard corporate income tax rate** — 25%  _(Corporate Tax Law No. 5520 (Kurumlar Vergisi Kanunu, consolidated text, mevzuat.gov.tr), art. 32(1) — https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.5.5520.pdf)_
- **Financial-sector corporate income tax rate** — 30% for banks, companies under Law No. 6361 (financial leasing, factoring, financing and savings finance), electronic payment and money institutions, authorised foreign exchange institutions, asset management companies, capital market institutions, insurance, reinsurance and pension companies, and companies party to build-operate-transfer and public-private partnership project contracts  _(Corporate Tax Law No. 5520 (Kurumlar Vergisi Kanunu, consolidated text, mevzuat.gov.tr), art. 32(1) — https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.5.5520.pdf)_
- **Domestic minimum corporate tax** — From the 2025 period, corporate tax computed under arts. 32 and 32/A cannot be less than 10% of corporate income before deductions and exemptions, after the exclusions listed in art. 32/C(2); pay the higher of the standard and minimum computations  _(Corporate Tax Law No. 5520 (Kurumlar Vergisi Kanunu, consolidated text, mevzuat.gov.tr), art. 32/C (added by Law 7524, 28 July 2024) and its application table — https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.5.5520.pdf)_
- **Pillar Two global minimum tax** — 15% QDMTT for MNE groups with consolidated revenue of EUR 750m or more; IIR from 2024 fiscal years, UTPR from 2025  _(Corporate Tax Law No. 5520 (Pillar Two / GloBE rules) (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/turkey/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income))_
- **Tax base** — Net corporate income for the accounting period, determined under the Income Tax Law's business income rules (art. 6) and adjusted for non-deductible expenses, exemptions, deductions and prior-year loss carryforwards  _(Corporate Tax Law No. 5520 (Kurumlar Vergisi Kanunu, consolidated text, mevzuat.gov.tr), art. 6 — https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.5.5520.pdf)_
- **Loss carryforward** — Losses shown in prior years' returns may be deducted for up to five years, each year shown separately in the return (art. 9(1)(a)); the law provides no carryback  _(Corporate Tax Law No. 5520 (Kurumlar Vergisi Kanunu, consolidated text, mevzuat.gov.tr), art. 9(1)(a) — https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.5.5520.pdf)_
- **Withholding tax — dividends** — 15% on dividends paid to resident/non-resident individuals and non-resident companies (raised from 10% by Presidential Decree No. 9286, 22 Dec 2024)  _(Corporate Tax Law No. 5520; Presidential Decree No. 9286 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/turkey/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **Withholding tax - interest** - For loans to a Turkish company, qualifying foreign-bank and other qualifying lender interest can receive 0%; other loan interest generally attracts 10%, subject to treaty relief. Bonds, deposits and other instruments need their own rules. Do not apply a general two-year 10%/15% split. _([GIB ruling on Corporate Tax Law section 30 and Decision 2009/14593](https://gib.gov.tr/mevzuat/kanun/436/ozelge/28372))_
- **Withholding tax — royalties** — 20% on royalties paid to non-residents (treaty rates may reduce)  _(Corporate Tax Law No. 5520 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/turkey/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **Withholding tax — professional services** — **20%** on professional services (consulting, audit, legal and similar) paid to a non-resident, reduced to **17%** for certain copyright-related work  _(Corporate Tax Law No. 5520 art. 30)_
- **Withholding tax — commercial rent** — **20%** on rent for a workplace, applied to the **gross** rent rather than the net  _(Corporate Tax Law No. 5520 art. 30; Income Tax Law No. 193 art. 94)_
- **Withholding tax — progress payments on multi-year construction** — **5%** on progress payments to contractors, domestic and foreign alike, for construction and repair work spanning more than one calendar year; this applies to payments made from 1 April 2025 for projects outside the exceptions. A **1%** rate applies to certain long-term construction and repair projects — confirm whether a given project qualifies before applying it, because the difference is four points on every progress payment  _(Corporate Tax Law No. 5520 arts. 15 and 30; Income Tax Law No. 193 art. 94)_
- **Withholding tax — payments to harmful-tax-competition jurisdictions** — **30%** on all sorts of payments to corporations established or operating in countries the Turkish Council of Ministers regards as undermining fair tax competition. This overrides the ordinary rates above and applies by reference to the payee's jurisdiction, so check the payee's country before concluding a lower rate applies  _(Corporate Tax Law No. 5520 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/turkey/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **Where these rates come from, and what to confirm** — The dividend, interest, royalty and 30% lines are PwC-sourced. The professional services, rent and construction progress payment heads are not: PwC's Turkey withholding page covers only dividends, interest and royalties plus a treaty matrix, so the rest were taken from Turkish practitioner sources and PwC Turkey's own bulletins. Turkish rates sit in Presidential Decrees and move often, so confirm each against the decree in force on the payment date  _(Corporate Tax Law No. 5520 art. 30; Presidential Decrees)_
- **Inter-company dividends (resident to resident)** — Dividends between resident companies are generally exempt (participation exemption); no dividend WHT on distributions to resident corporations  _(Corporate Tax Law No. 5520 (https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/turkey/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **Quarterly advance (provisional) tax** — Advance tax at the corporate rate on each quarter's profit, declared by the 14th and paid by the 17th of the second month after the quarter and credited against the annual liability (KVK art. 32(2); GVK mükerrer art. 120). Law 7566 (Resmî Gazete 19 December 2025) removed the nine-month limit applied since 2022, so the fourth-quarter return applies again from the 2025 period  _(Corporate Tax Law No. 5520 (Kurumlar Vergisi Kanunu, consolidated text, mevzuat.gov.tr), art. 32(2) — https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.5.5520.pdf ; Income Tax Law No. 193 (Gelir Vergisi Kanunu, consolidated text with the 2026 amounts, mevzuat.gov.tr), mükerrer art. 120 and the Law 7566 application table — https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.4.193.pdf)_
- **Annual CIT return & payment deadline** — Return filed from the 1st to the 25th of the fourth month after the accounting period ends (1 to 25 April for calendar-year companies, art. 14(3)); tax paid by the end of that month (art. 21(1)). GİB has extended the filing date to month end by circular in past years; confirm the current circular before relying on 30 April. This guide gave 'by end of the 4th month' for filing until October 2026  _(Corporate Tax Law No. 5520 (Kurumlar Vergisi Kanunu, consolidated text, mevzuat.gov.tr), arts. 14(3) and 21(1) — https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.5.5520.pdf)_
