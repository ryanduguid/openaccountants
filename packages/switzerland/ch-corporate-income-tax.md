# Switzerland Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax rates, base and withholding

- **Federal corporate income tax rate** — 8.5% of net profit (Reingewinn) for companies and cooperatives (Art. 68); because the tax itself is deductible, this is about 7.83% of pre-tax profit  _(Federal Act on Direct Federal Taxation (DBG, SR 642.11, consolidated text on Fedlex), Art. 68 — https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/switzerland/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Combined effective CIT rate (federal + cantonal + municipal)** — Approx. 11.85% to 21.6% depending on canton; national average ≈14.4% (2025) (approx — confirm)  _(Federal Act on the Harmonisation of Direct Taxes of the Cantons and Communes (StHG/LHID) — https://kpmg.com/ch/en/media/press-releases/2025/05/clarity-swiss-taxes.html)_
- **Lowest-rate canton (illustrative)** — Zug, combined effective ≈11.85% (approx — confirm)  _(Federal Act on the Harmonisation of Direct Taxes of the Cantons and Communes (StHG/LHID) — https://kpmg.com/ch/en/media/press-releases/2025/05/clarity-swiss-taxes.html)_
- **Tax base** — Worldwide net profit per the statutory accounts with tax adjustments (Art. 58); businesses, permanent establishments and real estate abroad are excluded from the Swiss base (Art. 52)  _(Federal Act on Direct Federal Taxation (DBG, SR 642.11, consolidated text on Fedlex), Art. 52 and 58 — https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/switzerland/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Cantonal capital tax** — Annual cantonal/municipal tax on equity (taxable capital); rate varies by canton, no federal capital tax  _(Federal Act on the Harmonisation of Direct Taxes of the Cantons and Communes (StHG/LHID) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/switzerland/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Participation relief on dividends/capital gains** — The profit tax is reduced in the ratio of net participation income to total net profit where the company holds at least 10% of another company's capital, or at least 10% of its profit and reserves, or participations worth at least CHF 1 million (Art. 69); net participation income is the yield less attributable financing costs and a 5% administration charge (Art. 70)  _(Federal Act on Direct Federal Taxation (DBG, SR 642.11, consolidated text on Fedlex), Art. 69 and 70 — https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1991/1184_1184_1184/de ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/switzerland/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **TRAF tax reliefs** — OECD-compliant patent box and R&D super-deduction available at cantonal level (relief capped, generally at 70% of taxable profit) (approx — confirm cap)  _(Federal Act on Tax Reform and AHV Financing (TRAF) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/switzerland/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **OECD Pillar Two / QDMTT** — Swiss Qualified Domestic Minimum Top-up Tax in force from 1 January 2024 for groups with revenue ≥EUR 750 million  _(Ordinance on the Minimum Taxation of Large Corporate Groups (Pillar Two / OECD GloBE) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/switzerland/corporate/taxes-on-corporate-income)_
- **Withholding tax on dividends** — 35% federal withholding (Verrechnungssteuer); refundable to Swiss residents and reducible under tax treaties for non-residents  _(Federal Act on Withholding Tax (VStG, SR 642.21, consolidated text on Fedlex), Art. 4(1)(b) and Art. 13(1)(a) — https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1966/371_385_384/de ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/switzerland/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on interest** — 35% on interest from bonds and similar debt instruments issued by Swiss residents and on customer deposits with Swiss banks (Art. 4(1)(a) and (d)); ordinary loan interest is outside the Act's object  _(Federal Act on Withholding Tax (VStG, SR 642.21, consolidated text on Fedlex), Art. 4(1)(a) and (d), Art. 13(1)(a) — https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1966/371_385_384/de ; https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/switzerland/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Withholding tax on royalties** — 0% — Switzerland levies no withholding tax on royalty payments  _(Federal Act on Withholding Tax (VStG/LIA) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/switzerland/corporate/withholding-taxes)_
- **Filing & payment** — Annual return filed with the cantonal tax administration; deadline and provisional/final tax billing set per canton (approx — confirm cantonal deadline)  _(Federal Act on Direct Federal Taxation (DBG/LIFD) — https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/switzerland/corporate/tax-administration)_
