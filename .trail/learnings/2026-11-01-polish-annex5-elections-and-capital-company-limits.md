# Polish Annex 5 elections and capital-company limits

An ordinary small-but-not-micro sp. z o.o. needs separate size, deemed-large and approving-body election checks before using Annex 5. Audit liability does not itself add cash flow and equity movement statements. See `skills/international/poland/pl-small-company-financial-statements.md` for the scoped draft and its source/currentness holds.

The [Accounting Act, Dz.U. 2026 poz. 522](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2026/522/text.pdf), has a 26 March 2026 legal cutoff. Capital companies cannot use the small-entity prudence, tax-depreciation and employee-benefit accrual reliefs in Articles 7(2b)/(2c), 32(7)/(8) and 39(6)/(7). The separate tax lease-classification option in Article 3(6) and deferred-tax option in 37(10) have no corresponding capital-company exclusion there, but their eligibility gates and actual policy choices still matter.

Do not extend ordinary Article 69(1)/(2) filing from 15 to 60 days. Complete Article 2 of [2026 poz. 1206](https://api.sejm.gov.pl/eli/acts/DU/2026/1206/text.pdf), commencing on 11 January 2027 under Article 57, changes excluded bank/financial/insurance branch and upper-parent group routes. The principal Act's metadata revealed this amendment even though the consolidation's later-amendment list omitted it. Metadata discovery is not an exhaustive current-law opinion.

Under Article 53(3), an entity requiring audit can allocate or cover net result only after approval preceded by an unmodified or qualified audit opinion; the contrary allocation/coverage is void by law. A completed audit or approval alone does not establish that gate. KSH distribution restrictions remain separate.
