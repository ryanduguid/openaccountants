In skills/international/australia/au-not-for-profit.md, the Waratahs illustration intentionally rounds the non-member ratio to 20.9% before applying it to both sales and shared expenses. The ATO Mutuality guide permits a reasonable and accurate apportionment method and illustrates rounded intermediate percentages. Do not label the preserved $47,530 result an arithmetic defect solely because an exact ratio gives a different amount. Disclose the convention, apply it consistently and distinguish whole-dollar taxable-income reporting from intermediate calculation precision. Current company rates must come from the pinned current Income Tax Rates Act, because the older ATO mutuality publication still displays superseded BRE rates and thresholds.
