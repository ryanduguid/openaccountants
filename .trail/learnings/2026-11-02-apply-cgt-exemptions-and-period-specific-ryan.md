In skills/international/australia/au-capital-gains.md, keep the small business 15-year exemption before loss allocation: section 152-1 disregards that gain entirely. The ATO how-to page read on 2 November 2026 says at Step 5 to subtract prior-year capital losses first, but section 102-5 and the dedicated ATO capital-losses page require current-year losses before eligible prior-year losses, oldest origin year first. Do not change the statutory method to match that summary. Schedule 1 item 82 of Act No. 49 of 2026 defers the new Division 102 gain-category order until the income year including 1 July 2027, despite its appearance in compilation 268. In au-crypto-tax.md, missing acquisition records do not establish a zero cost base or an event classification: reconstruct records or hold the affected gain and dependent totals as Needs Input. A statutory zero-cost acquisition is a separate fact pattern. Preserve source scope and pending professional review; arithmetic cases do not establish eligibility or final tax.
