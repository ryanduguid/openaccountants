In au-fbt.md and au-fbt-year.md, follow the current ATO employer guide chapter 2 illustration: round each grossed-up aggregate to the nearest dollar before applying 47%. Type 1 values of $8,800 produce $18,306, and the car-only $7,000 example produces $6,843.67 tax. Taxable benefits excluded from RFBA remain in employer aggregates under FBTAA s 5C. Section 149A tests the provider's or its GST group member's credit entitlement, not whether a credit was claimed.

For annual item 20, s 105 credits instalments payable net of s 112A credits. Cash payments and unpaid instalment debt are separate. The 2026 ATO instructions confirm reported amounts, excluding penalties and other years. Cases stipulate valuation and eligibility; they do not verify client facts. The approved 2027 form and qualified review of the whole guides remain open. Check downloaded titles and years: SAV/FBTGEMP/00003 is chapter 2, whilst 00021 is chapter 20, and a search-returned 2018 PDF was not the 2026 instructions.
