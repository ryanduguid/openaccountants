## Decided
French Polynesia salary CST is a tax reference, distinct from CPS social insurance. The 3 July 2026 Lexpol compilation supplies 11 marginal bands, monthly aggregation and deferred-income allocation. Article 193-26 contains the strict monthly contribution-below-750 rule; LP193-28 concerns assessment-roll regularisation.

## Tried and ruled out
The original law was not obtainable through the old Lexpol download locally.
