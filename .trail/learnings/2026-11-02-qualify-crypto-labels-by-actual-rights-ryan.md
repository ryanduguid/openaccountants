In skills/international/australia/au-crypto-tax.md, a token label, exchange export category or economic exposure does not establish income character or a CGT event. The former migration/hard-fork zero-basis row confused a qualifying new investor chain-split asset with unrelated replacement-rights transactions. Read protocol rights and activity before selecting the event and replacement basis. TD 2026/D2 is a draft for specified smart-contract wrapping and unwrapping, not custodial transfers, business transactions or every bridge. The current ATO personal-use page says less than $10,000, but ITAA 1997 s 118-10(3) includes exactly $10,000; amount alone never establishes personal use, and short holding is evidence rather than a mandatory statutory condition. Use the statute and keep the source discrepancy visible. Fixed CoinSpot fees and net proceeds plus a cost-base disposal fee can double-count the same cost; use actual gross consideration and eligible fees once. The guide's six cases test stated arithmetic only, not protocol law, client eligibility or final tax.
