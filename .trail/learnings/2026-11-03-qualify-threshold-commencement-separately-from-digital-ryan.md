## Decided
Use the separate official commencement notice to qualify the audit threshold. Reading the amending resolution alone missed SIN's later clarification that Bs 1,700,000 applies to fiscal closes from 2027. A current upload date or a 2026 resolution date does not establish the first eligible close.

Do not replace the premature threshold with the older Bs 1,200,000 automatically. Historical article 4 is expressly repealed, and the complete earlier-close reporting package needs a period-specific amendment and taxpayer-history review. Preserve that hold. The threshold notice does not itself defer separate digital transmission changes.
