## Decided
Keep the country's verified contribution method separate from the limit type, limit period and aggregation scope. A period calculation can track an annual assessable-base limit without recomputing prior periods. A monetary contribution cap is not an assessable-base ceiling. Year-to-date data supplies evidence; its availability cannot choose the method or authorise aggregation.

## Tried and ruled out
The social base's Step 6 already preserved the verified country method, but Section 4 instructed a cumulative switch whenever year-to-date data existed. Correct the shared source and regenerate its package copy and index; a country override would leave the shared contradiction in place. Do not substitute a single ceiling-times-rate check when rates or periods change.

## Watch out
Unknown method, annual-limit basis or aggregation scope requires Needs Input before the affected calculation. Preserve country-specific prior-period figures, correction, part-period and rounding rules. Source inventories and refusal examples do not complete the universal and social operational outputs or replace computed examples.
