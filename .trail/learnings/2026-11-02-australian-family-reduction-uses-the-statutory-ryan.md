For au-medicare-levy.md and australia-payroll.md, do not derive the family reduction cut-off by adding rounded upper guide figures. Medicare Levy Act 1986 compilation 53 section 8(2) reaches zero at exactly 1.25 times the unrounded family threshold. The ATO publishes rounded upper figures; retain their attribution but calculate using the statutory formula and spouse allocation. With 3 independently qualifying children the threshold is 60,252 and the zero point is 75,315. Spouses earning 37,000 each pay 687.40 each under the stated ordinary-liability assumptions. The old payroll range implied 740 each. Plain text extraction omitted the section 8(2) formula image; the original registered HTML alternative text supplied it. These arithmetic checks do not establish child eligibility, exemptions or a final assessment.
